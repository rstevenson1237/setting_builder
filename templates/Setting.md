# Setting.md

## Purpose
Defines the setting's name, thematic tags, and a short referee-facing outline - the foundation every other document builds on.

## Context
Read first:
- `GENRE.md`
- `patterns/setting/Setting.md`

## Instructions
Give the setting a name and three thematic tags, then a two or three sentence outline for the referee.

**Three sentences is a ceiling, not a target, and the outline is prose the referee reads
once.** Its job is to let a referee decide things at the table: what this place is, how it
is run, and what the party is doing here. Anything that is going to be said again in
`History.md`, `Truths.md` or a Region Overview is said *there* and not here - per GENRE.md,
a fact belongs at the highest level where it is true and nowhere else.

**The outline states the situation, never its meaning.** "The wind through the high pass
never really stops" is a mood; "the pass-gates shut the instant the wind turns, and anyone
still outside them waits until morning, no exceptions" is the same fact with a handle on
it. Where a sentence is doing atmosphere rather than telling a referee something they will
act on, cut it - the tags carry the atmosphere, which is what the tags are for.

*(This example is illustrative only - don't reuse its imagery, domain, or vocabulary as
your own setting's hook. Draw tags from `GENRE.md`'s Tags section instead - see **Be
specific, not generic** under its **What a line has to earn**.)*

## Template
```
[Setting Name] *three, thematic, tags*

*[Setting Outline - two or three sentences outlining the setting for the referee]*
```
