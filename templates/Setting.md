# Setting.md

## Purpose
Defines the setting's name, thematic tags, and a short referee-facing outline - the foundation every other document builds on.

## Context
Read first:
- `GENRE.md`
- `patterns/setting/Setting.md`

## Instructions
Give the setting a name and three thematic tags, then a two or three sentence outline for
the referee.

## Template
```
[Setting Name] *three, thematic, tags*

*[Setting Outline - two or three sentences outlining the setting for the referee]*
```
