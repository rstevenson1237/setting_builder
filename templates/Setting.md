# Setting.md

## Purpose
Defines the setting's name, its tag line and a short referee-facing outline - the
foundation every other document builds on.

## Context
Read first:
- `GENRE.md`
- `BRIEF.md` - this build's design requests, which replace this template's defaults where they speak
- `patterns/setting/Setting.md`

## Instructions
Give the setting a name, a tag line, then a two or three sentence outline for the referee.

## Template
```
[Setting Name]
[Tag line - one phrase, the setting one step past its name]

*[Setting Outline - two or three sentences outlining the setting for the referee]*
```
