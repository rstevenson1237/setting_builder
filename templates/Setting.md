# Setting.md

## Purpose
Defines the setting's name and a short referee-facing outline - the foundation every other
document builds on. Thematic tags live in `setting/Tags.md`, not here - see step 1a.

## Context
Read first:
- `GENRE.md`
- `patterns/setting/Setting.md`

## Instructions
Give the setting a name, then a two or three sentence outline for the referee.

## Template
```
[Setting Name]

*[Setting Outline - two or three sentences outlining the setting for the referee]*
```
