# Setting.md

## Purpose
Defines the setting's name, its tag line and a short referee-facing outline - the
foundation every other document builds on.

## Context
Read first:
- `GENRE.md`, `STYLE.md` and `BRIEF.md`, as `python3 tools/validate_setting.py --brief setting` prints it
- `patterns/Common_Schema.md` and `patterns/Setting_Schema.md`

## Instructions
Give the setting a name and its Place_Form, a tag line, then a two or three sentence outline
for the referee. A `tongue` name is recorded first in `setting/Language.md`.

## Template
```
[Setting Name]
Form: [PLACE_FORM]
[Tag line - one phrase, the setting one step past its name]

*[Setting Outline - two or three sentences outlining the setting for the referee]*
```
