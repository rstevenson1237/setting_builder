# Factions.md

## Purpose
The factions present in the setting - the powers a party may treat with, oppose, or ignore.

## Context
Read first:
- `GENRE.md`, `STYLE.md` and `BRIEF.md`
- `patterns/Common_Schema.md` and `patterns/Setting_Schema.md`
- `setting/Setting.md`, `setting/History.md`, `setting/Truths.md`
- `setting/Bestiary.md`

## Instructions
List the factions `BRIEF.md` names, each following its fields in `patterns/Setting_Schema.md`.

The file opens with the Relations table below, copied as written. Each entry's Relations
lists every other faction once, by the Relation it holds toward that faction; where
`BRIEF.md` names one, that is the Relation. A parenthetical in the table is a second name
the line may add: `- The Zhakuk - Hatred (fear)`.

## Template
```
Factions of [Setting Name]

| Level | Relation | Die | Stance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Friendly | d12 | Will look to advance the causes of their allies. |
| 4 | Tolerated | d10 | Will look for equal gain between parties. |
| 3 | Neutral (or Ambivalence) | d8 | Will only cooperate with some advantage to themselves. |
| 2 | Antipathy | d6 | Will only cooperate with great advantage to themselves. |
| 1 | Hatred (or Fear) | d4 | Will only cooperate under duress, may take an opportunity to disadvantage one of their opponents, otherwise Hostility or Escape are preferred. |

### [Faction Name]
Form: [NAME_FORM, of the name]
AD: [Xd6]
Want: [WANT]
Identity: [what a party sees before anyone names the faction - a colour worn, a device on seals and crates, a way of tying a knot or marking a door, a phrase. Repeated identically everywhere this faction appears, so it can be recognised two regions apart]
Resources: [material assets, territory, coin, or manpower they can spend or leverage]
Knowledge: [what they know that others don't - secrets, locations, or rivals' intentions]
Tactics: [their characteristic move when contested - how they typically act on a Faction Turn]
Reactions: [how they respond when players or rival factions interfere with them, at three intensities - noticed, crossed, injured]
Goals: [one or more concrete objectives currently driving their turns]
Relations:
- [Other Faction Name] - [RELATION]
Fields: [a setting/Bestiary.md entry by name, or `none`]
```
