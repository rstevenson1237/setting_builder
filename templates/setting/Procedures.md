# Procedures.md

## Purpose
The setting's shared mechanics - the rules cited from every level rather than restated at
each one. Seeded with working defaults, tailored once the setting is written, and extended only
if generated content turned out to need something the seed did not cover.

## Context
Read first:
- `GENRE.md`, `STYLE.md` and `BRIEF.md`
- `patterns/Common_Schema.md` and `patterns/Setting_Schema.md`
- `setting/Setting.md` and `setting/Truths.md`, once written

## Instructions
Sections, in order: Tests and Consequences, Hazards, Wounds and Madness, Conditions,
Searching, Time, Scaling, Region Dice.

Tests and Consequences defines every Test a hazard Feature cites in
`templates/region/Location.md` (Constitution and Fate, and Sanity only where `BRIEF.md` asks
for madness), and Hazards the damage Types they name. `GENRE.md`'s Lethality answer is a
rule a hazard can cite: what one failed Test costs a first-level character.
