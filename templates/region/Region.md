# Region.md

## Purpose
A region's seed, `setting/region/[Region Code].md`: what its locations are written from -
how a party gets in, who is there and how they answer a stranger, and what is changing.

## Context
Read first:
- `GENRE.md`
- `BRIEF.md`
- `setting/Setting.md`, `setting/History.md`, `setting/Truths.md`, `setting/Bestiary.md`, `setting/Factions.md`
- `setting/region/Regions.md`
- `patterns/region/Safe.md`, `patterns/region/Wild.md`, or `patterns/region/Dangerous.md` - read only the one that matches this region's rating.
- `setting/Procedures.md`

## Instructions
Write the SEED block of this region's rating file, every field filled. Each rating has its
own field set, shown below; write only this region's.

People names the roster `BRIEF.md` sizes. The Overview names who lives here, by
`setting/Bestiary.md` or `setting/Factions.md` name; the tables place them.

A field that holds several things - people - writes each as its own `- ` line
under the field's label. Every other field is prose, and short.

No location exists yet: name no location code, and place nothing in a room.

A question `setting/Truths.md` holds open as a mystery, or a gap it leaves to the
referee, is cited there, never answered here.

Cut, before finishing:
- anything repeated from `setting/region/Regions.md` or `setting/History.md`;
- any sentence stating what the referee should conclude or the players should do;
- any sentence that carries no name, number, price, place, ruling or consequence.
