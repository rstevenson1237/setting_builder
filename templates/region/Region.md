# Region.md

## Purpose
A region's seed, `setting/region/[Region Code].md`: what its locations are written from -
how a party gets in, who is there and how they answer a stranger, and what is changing.

## Context
Read first:
- `GENRE.md`, `STYLE.md` and `BRIEF.md`, as `python3 tools/validate_setting.py --brief [Region Code]` prints it
- `setting/Setting.md`, `setting/History.md`, `setting/Truths.md`, `setting/Bestiary.md`, `setting/Factions.md`
- `setting/region/Regions.md`
- `patterns/Common_Schema.md`, and `patterns/Region_Schema.md` - this region's rating's `seed`
- `setting/Procedures.md`'s Time and Distance and Weather

## Instructions
Write every field of the rating's `seed`, in its order. The Overview names who lives
here, by `setting/Bestiary.md` or `setting/Factions.md` name; the tables place them.

No location exists yet: name no location code, and place nothing in a room. A question
`setting/Truths.md` holds open, or a gap it leaves to the referee, is cited there, never
answered here.

Cut anything repeated from `setting/region/Regions.md` or `setting/History.md`, and any
sentence stating what the referee should conclude or the players should do.

## Template
```
[Code] [Region Name]

[Field]: [prose, short]

[List field]:
- [item]
```
