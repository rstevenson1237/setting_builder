# Treasure.md

## Purpose
Five reusable random treasure tables (Treasure Tables I-V), each a d20 table of found valuables, for the referee to roll on when a location's content calls for treasure.

## Context
Read first:
- `GENRE.md`
- `patterns/setting/Treasure.md`
- `setting/Bestiary.md`
- `setting/Setting.md`, `setting/History.md`, `setting/Truths.md`

Do not consult location, region, or faction files - these tables are setting-level and reusable everywhere, not tied to any specific place, hoard, or owner. Do not decide what container holds the treasure, where it is found, or how it is moved or used - that is the location entry's job, not this table's.

## Instructions
One file per table: `setting/Treasure1.md` through `setting/Treasure5.md`, corresponding to Treasure Tables I-V in order. Each table is a d20 list, numbered 1-20, of item name, valuation in standard silver coins (cn), and weight in slots (wt). 100 standard cn weighs 1 wt - use this as the table's average value-to-weight relationship, not a fixed conversion. Mundane bulk goods may run as low as 5 cn per wt (a bag of copper pennies); there is no upper bound on value-dense items (a single pure diamond may be worth 5000 cn at 0 wt).

For every table:
- Result 1 is always significantly below the table's average value.
- Results 2 and 3 are each either slightly below average value, or roughly double the average weight for their value - vary which.
- Results 4-20 carry the remaining spread of the table's value range in randomized order - do not arrange them ascending or descending, so the roll can't be read for the best result.
- Do not describe how an item is stored, discovered, or carried, and do not assume what it will be used for.

### Table I - Scavenged Loot
Everyday debris, minor coinage, tools, and scraps. Average value under 500 cn.

### Table II - Equipment and Armaments
Weaponry, armor, and interesting or mundane adventuring gear. Adds two columns beyond item/value/weight:
- **Quality** - poor (~10%), normal (~50%), fine (~30%), masterwork (~3%), cursed (~5%).
- **Effect** - whether the item carries a mechanical effect beyond its quality: ~30% of fine items do, all masterwork items do (always positive), all cursed items do (always negative). Cursed items always present outwardly as fine or masterwork - the curse is the trap, not a giveaway.

Average value for a normal item is 100 cn. Fine items run 3-10x normal value; masterwork items run 10-100x normal value. Cursed items are valued and described as whatever quality (fine or masterwork) they present as.

### Table III - Gems and Jewelry
Average value 1000 cn, minimal weight (0 wt for nearly every entry).

### Table IV - Luxury and Trade Goods
Furniture, artwork, silks and fine clothing, and valuable bulk commodities. Average value 1000-3000 cn.

### Table V - Treasure Cache
A mix of coinage - not only standard silver, include other denominations or foreign/exotic coin - and other valuables. Average value 5000 cn.
