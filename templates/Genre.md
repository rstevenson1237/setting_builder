# Genre.md

## Purpose
The top-level thematic spine, customized at step 1a: era/tone/magic-level/structure hints
that constrain every artifact generated afterward. Read before every other file, and re-read
at every generation step - not skimmed once and relied on from memory. `STYLE.md` carries
the fixed three-test contract every artifact at every level must pass; it is not customized
here and not part of this template's output.

## Context
Read first:
- `patterns/setting/Genre.md`
- Nothing else - GENRE.md is the first artifact, alongside the generic seeds
  `setting/Procedures.md` and `setting/Language.md`.

## Instructions
Run the flow in `patterns/setting/Genre.md`: starting from a pool of 20-30 discrete, specific
genre references (authors, single books or series, and TTRPG lines), three rounds of
narrowing questions take the user from a general request down to one chosen reference; Q2
then answers a set of binary/dial axis questions, defaulted to what the reference implies
and dialed elsewhere only where the user wants to diverge. Build the genre-identity
paragraph and the 3-5 axis bullets from the chosen reference and Q2 together.

Tags and genre-specific examples are not authored here; step 1b handles both, per STEPS.md.

- **[Axis name]** - [one-line elaboration]
- **[Axis name]** - [one-line elaboration]
