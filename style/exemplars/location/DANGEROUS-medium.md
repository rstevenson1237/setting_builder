X.9 **The Cage Head** (medium) - *old workings, set for intruders*
A timbered **shaft** drops out of the floor at the room's centre, four feet square and dark. An iron **cage** hangs on its chain above the mouth, high enough to walk under. The **windlass** that holds it up is bolted to the north wall.
*Twenty-five feet square, ceiling fourteen to take the hoist frame. The head of the works' main shaft, still standing and still hung, with the frame sound. It smells of wet rope, and the chain knocks against the frame whenever air moves in the shaft.*
**Hoist Cage:** Hung on a chain above the shaft mouth and counterweighted to the windlass. The treadle plank at the lip stands a thumb's width proud of the floor. Trodden -> the cage comes down over whoever stands at the mouth (Test of Fate, Snared). The pawl has been filed bright and oiled, and the oil is still wet.
**Windlass:** Mounted on the north wall, its rope sound and its crank handle missing. A bar through the crank socket turns the drum, and two turns lift the cage. One person cannot hold it alone, and a spear shaft fits the socket.
**Shaft Mouth:** Four feet square at the room's centre, timbered, dropping out of lantern reach. A charge basket hangs wedged against the timbering ten feet down and lies on its side, still holding (Treasure II, d20). The shaft goes on past it, and a stone dropped in is heard twice.
**Exits:** plank door - elm, braced, warped off its latch, south wall -> X.8 The Weighing Floor, hoist shaft - timbered, four feet square, no ladder, room's centre -> X.14 The Sump, low opening - brick, sooted, knee height, east wall -> a rubble slope out to the hillside
