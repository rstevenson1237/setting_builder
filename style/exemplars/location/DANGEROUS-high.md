X.1 **The Kiln Hall** (high) - *ironworks, held ground*
A hall the size of a barn, floored in black slag that crunches and gives underfoot. The far wall is one stone **hood** stepped up to a hole of grey daylight. **Four men** in ash-grey cloth stand away from a small fire beneath it.
*Sixty feet by forty, ceiling eighteen at the hood and ten elsewhere. The works' smelting hall, decayed and not wrecked, with the hood still carrying its draw. It smells of boiled bone from the fire, and the slag carries every step the length of the hall.*
**Toll Crew:** Four Ash Road men at a fire beneath the hood, boiling bone down for glue (Wary, 4, Bestiary : Ash Road Toll Crew). Their smoke stands out of the vent and is seen from the yard below. They hold the hall for the road beneath it, and a third of a party's load buys passage. Threatened by numbers they do not have -> they give up the hall and take the toll on the road.
**Smoke Hood:** Corbelled stone twenty feet across, opening on a vent of grey sky. An iron ring-beam carries its lowest course and has rusted to a third of its thickness. Struck hard enough to ring -> grit and a course of stone come down (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Tap Channel:** Runs from the furnace mouth across the floor to a sand pit at the east wall. Its last three feet are plugged with fired clay where the rest lies open and swept. The plug knocked through -> a dry cavity the length of a forearm, and what was put in it (Treasure III, d20).
**Barred Slot:** Shoulder height in the hood's west face, its iron shutter dropped and pinned. The pin's head is cut to a three-lobed profile and turns to nothing in this hall (Keys: Three-Lobe Pin). Forced with a bar -> the shutter buckles in its track and jams shut for good.
**Exits:** double doors - oak, iron-strapped, one leaf off its hinges, south wall -> X.2 The Casting Floor, arched opening - brick, sooted, no door, east wall -> X.3 The Charge Ramp, vent in the hood - open to the sky, twenty feet up -> the works yard on the crown
