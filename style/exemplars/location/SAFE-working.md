X.4 **The Burning Yard** - *hard bargains, smoke and ash*
A fenced yard of stacked **charcoal** behind an open-fronted shed, the ground black and crunching underfoot. **Ilsa Rook** is raking out a **kiln** and does not stop when anyone comes through the gap.
*Thirty feet by forty of beaten ground, hurdle-fenced, the shed running the whole west side. Built as a tannery and taken over for burning; the soaking pits are filled level with ash. It smells of wet ash and the raking carries across the green. The burners call it the yard; on the tithe roll it is still the tannery.*
**Fuel Stack:** Charcoal in head-high stacks along the shed wall beneath a pitched hurdle roof. Two barrels of pitch and a crock of lamp oil stand at the near end. Charcoal 1 cn the sack, pitch 4 cn the barrel, oil 2 cn the crock. No lamps, no tinder and no rope; the tinker's cart brings those on the eighth day.
**Ilsa Rook:** Raking a cooled kiln when the party arrives. Her apron is burned through in three places and she does not take it off indoors. Coin first, and no credit to anybody who cannot be found again. Wants a back to carry for her, and takes a quarter off the sack for a day's hauling.
**Cold Kiln:** Domed clay at the yard's centre, banked and still warm to the hand. Its draw-hole is a foot across and the raking iron leans beside it. Ilsa fires anything that goes through the hole for 3 cn, and opens it when the burn ends.
**Scraper Rod:** Bronze, a foot long, squared at one end and notched three times behind it (Keys: Kiln-Yard Scraper). Ilsa scrapes clinker with it and pulled it out of a burnt works two days upriver. Six cn to anybody who asks after it, and she will not haggle.
**Exits:** hurdle gate - split ash, lashed, dragging on its foot, south side -> X.5 The Wash Green, plank door - oak, pegged, sagging on its lower hinge, west end of the shed -> X.3 Rook's House, cart gap in the fence - open, rutted deep, north corner -> the river road upstream
