X.12 **The Wood Store** (low) - *old workings, nothing kept here*
A narrow room stacked head-high along one side with split **firewood**, grey and light as cork. The end wall is laid in a **different bond** from the other three. The rest of the room is bare floor.
*Twenty feet by eight, ceiling eight, cut into rock on three sides and built up on the fourth. A firewood store for the ranges above, emptied and left standing. It smells of dry rot, and carries no sound but the party's own.*
**Firewood Stack:** Split cordwood head-high along the north side, rotted dry and light to lift. The bottom two courses have stuck together and have to be broken apart. Behind them at floor level is a canvas roll somebody pushed in and left (Treasure I, d20).
**Barrow Ruts:** Two grooves worn into the floor from the doorway, a barrow's width apart and deep as a thumb. They run the length of the room and stop a foot short of the end wall. The floor between their ends and the wall is worn smooth and clean of grit.
**Stretcher Bond:** Laid in stretcher bond where the other three walls are cut rock and header courses. Its third course from the floor carries one block with a finger-groove worn in the underside. Hooked out by hand -> the block draws free and the four above it swing back into X.13.
**Exits:** plank door - oak, unbarred, off its top hinge, south wall -> X.11 The Range Head
