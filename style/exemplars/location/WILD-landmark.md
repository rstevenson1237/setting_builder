X.6 **The Long Tower** (landmark) - *drove road, subsided ground*
A stone drum tower lies on its side across the slope, whole, its **sheared base** open to the grass like a pipe. The barrel runs twenty yards into the dark, and the near length is smoke-black from a **drovers' fire**. Rooks go up off the top when anyone comes over the rise.
*Twenty yards long and seven across, lying east to west on open slope two miles north-east of the ford, the beck running below it. A watch-tower of the road-builders, taken down whole when the marl under its north footing washed out. It smells of sheep and old smoke, and a word said inside it comes back twice.*
**Fallen Drum:** Twenty yards of mortared stone on its side, the sheared base standing open at the west end and walkable in. What were floors are now upright partitions across the barrel, each pierced by the stair-hole. The inner face is dry to head height and green with weed above that.
**Drovers' Camp:** Fire-ring of tumbled coping stones a third of the way in, the ash cold and rained into. Mutton bones, a broken hurdle and a tally of scratches cut into the plaster beside it. The tally runs to forty-one marks and the last four are cut bright.
**Sideways Stair:** Newel treads stand out of the north side like shelves and run level into the barrel. Forty feet along them the undercroft door lies face-up and half open, dropping into the dark below. Nothing bars it and nothing has to be done to it.
**Silt Line:** Waist-high around the inside of the lower end, and the floor below it is soft. The beck backs up into the tower each spring and drains away through the stair-hole. Anything set down in the lower twenty feet is standing in mud by morning.
**Exits:** stair-hole in the fifth partition - mortared brick, broken through, north side of the barrel -> X.7 The Undercroft, sheared base - open, no door, west end -> the drove road over the rise
