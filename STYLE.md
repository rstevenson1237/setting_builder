# Style

Every line at every level passes these tests; they outrank every template.

- **It reaches the players.** A setting fact becomes something players can see, hear, be
  told, pick up or decide about; a referee instruction changes how something is run.
  Anything else is cut.
- **It can be acted on.** A fact names what a party can do about it, and where.
- **It leaves the decision to the players.** State what is true and visible - never what
  the party will conclude, what they should do, or how events will resolve.
- **It is said once**, at the highest level where it is true.

The genre's conventions are free to use: a familiar shape costs a few words, and the
detail saved goes to what makes this instance its own.
