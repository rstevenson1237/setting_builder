# wealth-contents

What a strongroom holds.

1. coin and plate too heavy to move casually
2. a founder's or a family's kept wealth
3. tithes or tolls banked rather than spent
4. something taken and never returned
5. a temple's or guild's reserve
6. an item in an older or dead tongue nobody here can read
7. the one thing in this settlement everyone has heard of and nobody has seen
