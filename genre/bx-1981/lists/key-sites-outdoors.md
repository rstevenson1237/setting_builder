# key-sites-outdoors

Where a key sits out in the country.

1. in a barrow, with its holder
2. in a lair, because something took it
3. under water
4. set into a marker as decoration
5. held by a hermit or trapper who does not know what it is
6. in a cache with unrelated things
7. in plain sight and unrecognisable as a key at all
