# faction-positions

What a faction holds a place for.

1. watching an approach
2. watching a crossing or a ford
3. holding a way open
4. holding a way shut
5. working something out of the ground
6. working a resource out of the water, or the herd that passes through
7. storing what has been taken
8. keeping something alive
9. keeping something contained
10. a forward camp for something they hold more securely elsewhere
11. a foothold in country they do not yet hold outright
