# mechanisms-damaging

Built mechanisms that cost the party something they have to spend to get back.

1. a covered pit, shallow
2. a dart or needle from a wall
3. a spear trap at chest height
4. a hanging weight on a trip line
5. choking gas, spores or dust
6. a chest that gasses, sprays, or fouls what it holds
7. a fixture whose ornament is wired to its base
8. a turning trapdoor mounted on an axis
