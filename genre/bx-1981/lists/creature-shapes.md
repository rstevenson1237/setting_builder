# creature-shapes

How a creature is arranged in a room, and what the party reads before they read a species.

1. swarm - it arrives as a sound, a surface that is moving, or a smell; it does not have a front
2. pack - spread out, one of them seen first and the rest where the first one is looking
3. lone hunter - the kills found before the thing is, and no second set of tracks anywhere
4. patrol - a beaten route, a turn-around point, something carried the same way every time, and a gap in the interval
5. sentry - it is between the party and a thing, it does not follow far, and it goes back
6. part of the terrain - a growth, a mound, a slick of stone, a hanging thing that has been in the room the whole time
7. searchers - spoil turned over, a room already taken apart, marks left to say which rooms are done
8. rivals - a camp, a rope already fixed, a body of theirs, a door already opened
