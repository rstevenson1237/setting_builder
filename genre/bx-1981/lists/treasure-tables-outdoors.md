# treasure-tables-outdoors

Which of the five tables a WILD find comes from.

1. I Scavenged Loot - the default: what washes up, what is dropped, what is left at a camp, what is worth stooping for and not worth a detour
2. II Equipment and Armaments - a body and what it still carries; a cache left by somebody who meant to come back; what a predator dragged home with its meal
3. III Gems and Jewelry - grave goods; a purse buried at a boundary; something that came out of the ground with the spoil and was never noticed
4. IV Luxury and Trade Goods - a load that never arrived: a wreck, an overturned cart, a pack animal's burden, a caravan's abandoned surplus
5. V Treasure Cache - rare out here, and always attached to a Secret-tier location or a lair with a long tenancy
