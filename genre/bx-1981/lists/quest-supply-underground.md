# quest-supply-underground

What a dangerous place holds that somebody elsewhere wants.

1. a substance only found in a living thing here
2. a body, or proof of one
3. an object taken from somewhere else and brought here
4. a name, a word, or a phrase recorded here and nowhere else
5. a person, alive or otherwise
6. a thing that must be destroyed rather than retrieved
7. a measurement, a reading, a confirmation that something is true
