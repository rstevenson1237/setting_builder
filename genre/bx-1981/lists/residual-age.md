# residual-age

How an effect dates itself.

1. an effect with a clear edge, and the edge closer in than the marks say it once was
2. something strong in one room and barely detectable two rooms out
3. a warning left by whoever found it after the maker had already gone
4. a mark that has been repaired over more than once, each repair shorter than the last
5. something that has stopped in one place and has not stopped in the next
