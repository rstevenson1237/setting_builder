# doors

What an exit is made of, and what is in the opening where it is not a door at all.

1. banded oak
2. bronze-shod
3. iron-strapped
4. a slab of dressed limestone pivoting on a socket
5. a lintel of a single stone
6. a curtain of hide over a hacked opening
7. mud brick gone soft at the bottom course
8. a bronze grille green with age
9. plank-and-batten pinned with wooden trenails
10. a cast bronze leaf too heavy for its own hinges
11. iron banding over a core that has rotted out from behind it
12. a hurdle of withies
13. a hide stretched on a frame
14. a plug of piled rubble
15. a portcullis down and silted in
16. a hatch in a floor with a ring pulled off it
17. a bulkhead laid flat over a stair
18. a hanging heavy enough to stop light
19. a screen of stacked stone built dry
20. nothing, and a drop
21. nothing, and the opening is the wrong shape for anything that walks
