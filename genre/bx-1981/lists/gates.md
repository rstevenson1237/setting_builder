# gates

What holds a way through, stated as the thing that opens it.

1. locked - a lock wanting a key held elsewhere
2. wanting a fitted object - a socket cut to a profile nothing here matches
3. wanting power - a mechanism wanting what the level no longer produces
4. wanting weight or numbers - a counterweight no single person shifts
5. barred - a bar seated from the far side
6. barred - a brace jammed under the leaf
7. barred - wedges driven at the sill
8. barred - spikes through the jamb, set by somebody keeping something out, or in
9. stuck - timber swollen with damp
10. stuck - hinges seized solid
11. stuck - a lintel settled onto the leaf
12. stuck - silt or spoil banked against the far face
13. stuck - the leaf sprung in its frame and binding at one corner
14. warded - a mark cut across the threshold and kept clean when nothing else here is
15. warded - a line of fittings buried at the sill
16. warded - a name cut into the lintel in a tongue from setting/Language.md
17. warded - an opening that takes some things through and stops others
