# faction-friction

What is going wrong inside a faction's position, or between it and a rival.

1. orders that no longer make sense
2. orders that do not fit country this far out
3. a shortage
4. somebody skimming
5. a feud with the position next door
6. a captive who has been here too long
7. the region itself eating them slowly
8. a rival testing the edge of the claim
9. the country pushing back - weather, distance, a creature that does not recognise the claim
10. somebody here who would rather be posted anywhere else
11. an agreement with a rival that neither will state
12. a line neither side crosses
13. a neutral party both use
14. an incident both are pretending did not happen
15. a bidding war for the same person or the same goods
