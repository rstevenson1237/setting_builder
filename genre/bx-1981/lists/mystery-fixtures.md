# mystery-fixtures

What was built or placed with purpose underground. Filtered by the fixture kind drawn.

1. statue - a statue whose pose or gaze means something
2. statue - a carved figure worn smooth in one place and nowhere else
3. altar - an altar with a fitting, a channel, or a stain
4. altar - an offering stone with what was last left on it still on it
5. water - a fountain, basin or cistern that still runs or conspicuously does not
6. water - a pool that takes what is dropped in it and does not give it back
7. water - a basin whose level does not answer to the weather
8. water - standing water clear over a floor that should have silted
9. water - a cistern with a channel cut to somewhere
10. water - water that is warm, or is not, and what that says about what it came through
11. machinery - a mechanism whose linkage runs into the wall and out somewhere else in the region
12. machinery - a drum, capstan or windlass with its rope gone
13. machinery - a shaft that turns and does nothing visible
14. machinery - a counterweight hanging at the wrong height
15. machinery - a thing wanting power the region no longer produces
16. machinery - a fitting worn only on one side, which says which way it was worked
17. machinery - a housing with the part taken out of it, and the part is somewhere
18. machinery - a mechanism part-visible in a wall
19. ward - a mark cut across a threshold and kept clean where nothing else here is
20. ward - a line of fittings buried at a sill, one of them missing
21. ward - a name cut into a lintel in a tongue from setting/Language.md
22. ward - a ring of something laid down that has not been disturbed by anything that walks
23. ward - a doorway that takes some things through and stops others
24. ward - what is lying just short of the line, and how long it has been there
25. ward - a door held by something other than a lock
26. ward - a way barred from the side the party is on
27. illusion - a wall a draught crosses
28. illusion - a floor that carries sound from further than it goes
29. illusion - a room that shows itself furnished in a region where nothing else has survived
30. illusion - a figure that does not turn
31. illusion - a way on that dust does not settle in
32. illusion - a thing that is only wrong from one side of the room
33. illusion - a door with no handle and a made surface
34. illusion - a floor laid in a pattern that is not decoration
35. illusion - a mural that disagrees with the room it is in
