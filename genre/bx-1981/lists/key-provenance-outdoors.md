# key-provenance-outdoors

How a key came to be out in the country.

1. carried by somebody who died on the way
2. hidden at a distance on purpose, because keeping it with the lock defeated the point
3. buried as a grave good
4. taken as loot and dropped
5. given to a person out here for safekeeping, who still has it
6. built into something out here as though it were a component
7. lost, and it is a matter of looking in the right place
