# key-forms

What a key physically is.

1. an actual key, and the lock it fits is nothing like a door
2. a rod, pin, or bar cut to a profile
3. a stone, gem, or disc fitted to a socket
4. a seal, signet, or stamp
5. a token given as proof
6. a specific bone
7. a phrase or name recorded on something portable
8. a measured length of something
9. a piece broken off a larger thing, still here
