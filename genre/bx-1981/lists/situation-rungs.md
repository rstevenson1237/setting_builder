# situation-rungs

The ladder a situation climbs, written at region level and drawn per location. Shown here for armed men; the shape is the same for any situation.

1. they are paying for what they take, and the locals are selling winter stores
2. they are drinking too much, and the locals are swallowing insults
3. a delegation has gone to the captain about something specific
4. somebody local has been hurt, and the captain is deciding how much to care
5. somebody local is dead, and discipline is visibly slipping
6. they are taking what they want, and the settlement is deciding whether to fight
