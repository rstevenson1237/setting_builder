# environmental

What a place does to whoever is in it, with nobody behind it. Filtered by the kind the region admits.

1. water - standing water that never drained
2. footing - a floor slick with what grows on it
3. footing - silt that takes a boot and keeps it
4. footing - ice where a draught crosses water
5. footing - stone worn smooth by whatever has been dragged down it
6. footing - a surface that holds a person and not a person carrying something
7. air - air that will not carry a flame
8. air - a heaviness that settles into the lowest part of the room
9. air - dust that hangs where something dropped it
10. air - a smell strong enough to be a fact rather than dressing
11. air - what grows on the walls, breathed
12. cold - a cold that comes up through the floor rather than through the air
13. cold - a draught that takes warmth faster than still air at the same reading
14. cold - damp, which makes every other condition here worse
15. heat - heat still in stone long after whatever made it is gone
16. heat - stone too hot to put a hand on, and hotter with depth
17. heat - a crust over molten rock that carries a person and not a person running
18. heat - a vent that breathes on an interval somebody could have timed
19. heat - steam where water reaches it, and the water is coming from somewhere
20. heat - metal in the room that has slumped
21. dark and sound - dark that swallows a light source's useful radius
22. dark and sound - glare off water or pale stone that hides what is under it
23. dark and sound - a room that carries every sound made in it somewhere else
24. dark and sound - a room that carries none, so nothing can be heard coming
25. collapse - a ceiling carrying its load on one prop, one pillar, or one beam, and the party can see which
26. collapse - a fall already down to the waist of a corridor with a gap over it
27. collapse - a floor eaten thin from below
28. collapse - a fresh fall where the dust has not settled
29. collapse - a roof that drops grit whenever something heavy moves
30. collapse - a shaft whose collar has gone
31. collapse - a wall out of plumb far enough to read from the doorway
32. collapse - a working propped by whoever cut it, in timber that has since been eaten
33. growth - a root mass that has opened a wall and is holding what it opened
34. growth - thorn grown across the only way through, thick enough to be a job rather than a step
35. growth - a stand that closes behind whatever passes through it
36. growth - something that grips what touches it and does not choose to
37. growth - a bloom or a spore bed that answers to being disturbed
38. growth - a mat over standing water that reads as floor
39. growth - growth that has taken the walls and is load-bearing now
40. growth - a colony or a nest old enough to be terrain rather than a creature
