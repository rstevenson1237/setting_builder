# mystery-placed

What somebody set down out in the country and left.

1. standing stones, singly or in a ring
2. a monolith with a worked face
3. a cairn that is not a grave
4. an altar or offering stone in the open
5. a carved boundary marker whose terms are still legible
6. a hand-print or figure cut into rock
7. something set into a tree that the tree has grown around
8. a thing balanced in a way weather should have undone
