# wealth-protection

What stands between the party and a settlement's kept wealth. Filtered by the protection drawn.

1. hidden - a wall thinner than it should be
2. hidden - a floor that does not match the joists below it
3. hidden - a chest bricked into a foundation
4. hidden - a compartment behind an altar or a hearth
5. hidden - a stair with one step too many
6. hidden - a room smaller inside than out
7. gated - a phrase known only to a family or an order
8. gated - a password changed every season
9. gated - a condition tied to a date or event: a saint's day, a full granary, market day
10. gated - a permission that must come from one specific living person, not an office
11. gated - a price only a founder's line can pay
12. gated - proof of a claim rather than a key
13. guarded - a temple's kept beast
14. guarded - something that came with the vault and was never removed because nobody living knows how
15. guarded - a creature already a standing problem elsewhere in the region, and dealing with it has always cost more than the wealth is worth
16. trapped - an alarm bell or released animal that alerts a specific person
17. trapped - a marking dye or scent that gives a thief away later
18. trapped - a lock that takes a finger rather than a life
19. trapped - a lid weighted to hold shut from the inside
20. trapped - a mechanism old enough to half-work, and worse for it
