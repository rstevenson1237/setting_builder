# lore-forms-settlement

What a settlement keeps.

1. a ledger of debts, tolls, or tithes
2. a register of births and deaths
3. a court roll
4. a rent book
5. a map drawn for a purpose and kept because it was expensive
6. a charter or grant
7. correspondence somebody did not burn
8. a merchant's manifest
9. an inventory of what a temple holds
10. a book bought, stolen, or inherited by somebody who cannot read it
11. an apprentice's copybook
12. a survey
