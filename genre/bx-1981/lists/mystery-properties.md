# mystery-properties

A property of the place itself, with no fixture to point at.

1. a space where a sense does not work
2. a cold that has a boundary
3. a sound with no source that changes with position
4. light that falls wrong
5. a thing that does not decay in a room where everything else has
6. water that will not mix
7. a spring that changes what is put into it
8. ground that will not hold what is buried
9. a direction that reads wrong
10. a silence with an edge to it
11. an echo that answers late or differently
12. light that arrives from the wrong quarter
