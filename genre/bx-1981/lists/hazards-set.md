# hazards-set

What somebody put here on purpose, out in the country.

1. a snare or spring-noose on a game trail
2. a pit with a covered mouth
3. a deadfall log or stone
4. a spiked pit at a gap in a wall
5. a line strung at ankle or throat height
6. an alarm - stones on string, a bell, a pile that falls
7. a poisoned water source
8. a gate or barrier rigged to fall
9. a bait pile with something waiting near it
10. a marked crossing that has been re-marked wrongly
