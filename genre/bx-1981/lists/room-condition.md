# room-condition

Where a room sits on the line from still in use to gone entirely.

1. active - still used for its purpose, by whoever is here now
2. abandoned - left, not fought over or reclaimed; intact but empty
3. decayed - standing, but failing on its own: rot, rust, damp, subsidence, vermin
4. ruined - a structural failure has happened, and the room reads as an event rather than neglect
5. destroyed - the original form is no longer legible; what is left is debris, footprint, or absence
