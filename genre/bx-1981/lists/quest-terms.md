# quest-terms

What the giver offers, in their own words.

1. a flat fee, and half up front
2. a flat fee, and nothing up front
3. a share, stated as a share
4. payment in goods, favour, standing, or a debt forgiven
5. the loan of something for the duration
6. a promise that will be honoured
7. a price that is generous because the giver expects not to pay it
