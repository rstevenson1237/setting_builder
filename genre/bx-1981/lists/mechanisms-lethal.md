# mechanisms-lethal

Built mechanisms that can kill outright, and the clue already said so.

1. a spiked pit, deep, with a beam across it
2. a deadfall or collapsing floor
3. a floor that gives onto something living below
4. a swinging blade, scythe or pendulum
5. a small catapult loaded with a ball-and-chain
6. a flooding chamber
7. a rockslide or cave-in
8. boiling oil from a heated cauldron
9. walls that close
