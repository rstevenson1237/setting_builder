# treasure-claims

Who or what has a claim on it.

1. a creature that has nested on it without knowing what it is
2. an occupant who knows exactly what it is
3. a trap set by someone long dead
4. a mystery that must be answered first
5. something that will follow whoever takes it
6. the people who buried it, and mark the spot
7. a faction that considers this country theirs
8. whoever is coming back for it, and how soon
9. the person it was taken from, still findable
