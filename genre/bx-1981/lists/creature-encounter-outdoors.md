# creature-encounter-outdoors

How something out in the country meets the party.

1. it is seen first, at distance
2. it sees them first and they do not know
3. both at once, badly
4. it is heard and not seen
5. it is following, and has been for a while
6. it is fleeing something else
7. it is feeding and will defend the kill
8. it is between the party and where they were going
