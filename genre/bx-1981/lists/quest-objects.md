# quest-objects

What is actually wanted.

1. something retrieved
2. someone found, or confirmed dead
3. something delivered where the giver cannot go
4. something destroyed
5. something verified - is it still standing, is she still alive, is the way open
6. something taken from a person
7. a debt collected
8. a body brought back for burial
9. a message carried to somebody who will not receive the giver
