# quest-obstacles-underground

What stands between the party and taking it.

1. it is inside something living
2. it is guarded by something that will not negotiate
3. taking it breaks it
4. taking it is noticed by something else
5. another party is already here for it
6. it is not where the giver said it would be
7. the giver's description is wrong in a way that matters, and they know it
