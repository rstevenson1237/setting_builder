# residual-causes

What left it, and is gone. Drawn against an event in setting/History.md.

1. a death - ground that will not take a body
2. a death - cold that keeps to the spot somebody died on
3. a death - a sound repeating the last thing that happened here
4. a death - a mark that comes back through every repair
5. a working - tailings, slag, or runoff from a working nobody has run in a lifetime
6. a working - heat that never went out of a furnace floor
7. a working - water that came up when the digging stopped and has been coming up since
8. a working - what a craft used daily and poured away
9. a rite - a boundary that still holds, which nobody remembers agreeing to
10. a rite - a prohibition the place enforces on its own
11. a rite - something that still answers to a name nobody living uses
12. a rite - ground consecrated or cursed well past whoever did it
13. a tenancy - a nest, a shed skin, a spore bed, or a scent mark still doing its work after the animal is gone
14. a tenancy - what a body left in the water it fell into
15. a tenancy - something bred or kept that has outlived its keeper
