# containers

What a cache is in, under, or behind.

1. a chest with its lock on the inside
2. a coffer banded and too heavy to carry whole
3. a jar sealed under pitch
4. a niche plugged with a single dressed stone
5. a course of masonry laid over it
6. a body, still wearing it
7. a sack gone to fibre that will not lift
8. a cistern, and the water is still in it
9. a floor void under a flagstone
10. a beam hollowed and capped
11. an animal's cache, sorted the way an animal sorts
12. something built around it that would have to come down first
