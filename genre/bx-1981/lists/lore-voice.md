# lore-voice

Who wrote it, and what they were wrong about.

1. someone keeping a record for an office that no longer exists
2. someone who did not know what they were describing
3. someone making a claim, and overstating it
4. someone marking a boundary, which is an argument rather than a fact
5. someone warning whoever came next, and wrong about what the danger was
6. someone self-interested, writing for whoever would read it later
7. someone partial, who was only ever told half of it
8. someone counting, who recorded the count and not the reason
