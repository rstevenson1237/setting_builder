# creature-wants

What a creature wants that is not a fight, and what it is tied to elsewhere.

1. what it eats, and where that comes from
2. what eats it, and where that came from
3. what it will not go near, and why
4. what it tolerates that the party would not expect
5. who it answers to elsewhere in the region
6. what it would trade for
7. its young, and somewhere safer to put them
8. a thing taken from it that it wants back
9. to be let past, rather than fought
