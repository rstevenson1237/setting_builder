# lore-forms-underground

What a written find underground physically is.

1. a ledger or tally
2. a work roster
3. a letter never sent, or never delivered
4. a journal that stops
5. an inventory that does not match what is here
6. a map that is wrong, or was right
7. an inscription cut by someone who expected to be read
8. a marker or grave good
9. a tally scratched by someone counting days
10. a legal instrument - a deed, a writ, a manumission
11. a receipt
12. a warning left for the next people through
