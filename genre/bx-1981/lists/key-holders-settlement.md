# key-holders-settlement

Who in a settlement has it.

1. a moneylender, as collateral
2. a trader who cannot price it
3. the authority, in a chest of things nobody has looked at in years
4. a family, as an heirloom
5. a temple, as a relic of something else
6. a drunk who will not say where they got it
7. somebody using it as an ordinary object, who would sell it cheaply
