# authority-postings

Where notice is given, and what is posted now.

1. a board at the market or the gate
2. a stone or post where notices are nailed
3. the door of the hall
4. a crier, in a place big enough to afford one
5. a bounty
6. a summons
7. a debt called in
8. a warning
9. a hiring
10. a death
11. a list of who owes what
