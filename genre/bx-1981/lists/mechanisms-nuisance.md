# mechanisms-nuisance

Built mechanisms that cost time, ground, noise, position, or a piece of gear.

1. an alarm, bell, or released creature that alerts something elsewhere
2. a marking trap - dye, scent, residue - that draws attention later
3. a snare or net
4. a trap that ruins a resource rather than a person
5. a lid weighted to hold something shut from the inside
6. a container that destroys its contents when opened wrong
7. a mechanism so rusted it half-works and is worse for it
