# faction-signs

What identifies a faction without anyone naming it.

1. a colour worn, hung, or flown
2. a device, blazon, or mark repeated on gear, carts, walls or markers
3. a way of cutting, tying, stacking, piling, or repairing
4. a shared weapon or armour tradition
5. a mark left on doors they have cleared
6. a mark chalked on doors they have called at
7. a cairn or post at the edge of their claim
8. a device on a seal, a crate, a coin, a boot
9. a way of writing a number or tying a knot
10. a rank shown in something small
11. a phrase they use and nobody else here does
12. a tongue they speak among themselves, from setting/Language.md
