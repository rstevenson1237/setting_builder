# lair-signs

What is readable before the lair itself is reached.

1. tracks, and how fresh
2. a kill, and how it was killed
3. scat, pellets, or castings
4. a shed skin, feather, or antler
5. scrape marks, rubbing posts, or scent marks
6. bones arranged rather than scattered
7. a silence where there should be birds
8. a smell that arrives before the place does
9. prey behaving wrongly
10. something the occupant has dragged and left
