# lore-effects

What a find does to what the party already thinks.

1. names a place the party has not found
2. explains a feature they have already seen and misread
3. connects an event in setting/History.md to something physical here
4. contradicts a rumour they arrived with
5. establishes that the current occupants are not the first
6. gives a name to something they have only seen the effects of
7. gives the older name of somewhere they know by a newer one
8. fixes a date against setting/History.md
9. establishes that this country was claimed, worked, or fought over
10. records a warning that is still good
11. records a payment for something that should not have needed paying for
12. establishes who owned what before the current claim
13. lists people who went somewhere and how many came back
14. contradicts what the settlement says about itself
