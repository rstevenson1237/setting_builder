8. secret - a pivot seam in dressed stone
9. secret - a counterweighted slab
10. secret - a passage behind a fitting nobody would move
11. vertical - a shaft with staples
12. vertical - a ladder well
13. vertical - a stair spiralling in a chimney
14. vertical - a hole broken through a floor
