# Setting Judgement Check - Fellward, all three regions complete (A, B, C)

## Setting-level
- Discrete and discoverable, not vague: Confirmed - every Feature across all 32 locations names an object, person, or trigger a party can act on (a chalk-marked sack, a loose flagstone, a construct's worn floor-semicircle), rather than resting on mood alone.
- Genre held across levels: Confirmed - Low Magic (Nazirith is the setting's one unique treasure, and it costs the wearer the Barrow-Kin's goodwill for good), Points of Light (Fellward is the only real settlement; B and C are true wilderness/ruin), Mythic Underworld (Ingaraz predates the keep by three centuries and answers to none of its authority), Nominal Authority (the relief column never arrived; Charter Agents visit on no schedule anyone's seen kept), and Undermanned Frontier (every named person at Fellward - Corlan, Weyland, Wrey, Petra, Bettan, Ness - is doing a duty they were pressed into, not chose) all still read true at the location level, not just in `GENRE.md` itself.
- Recurring elements actually recur: Confirmed - the Quartermaster's Ring's crossed-tally chalk mark appears at A.1 and A.2; the Diggers' scratched spiral appears at A.2 and is their identity mark through region C; the Barrow-Kin's closed-ring glyph threads C.1, C.2, C.7, C.8, and C.17; Nazirur and Nazirith cite each other; the Foundation Seal (found B.7) and its lock (C.2) and the Threshold Warning that describes it (also C.2) all cite consistently.

## Region A (Fellward, SAFE d6)
- Region reinforces the setting: Confirmed - the region overview's Situation (relief column overdue, granary rationed, token loss erasing service) is the same pressure `History.md`'s "Still resolving" entry and `Truths.md`'s token truth state at the setting level; nothing here reads as bolted on.
- Locations reinforce this region: Needs Attention - see Room to Grow. The region's Treasure claim and its service-token motif (stated three times at the region level: Situation, Secrets, and the Events table) don't yet land in any of the six location files.

## Region B (Pelren, WILD d8)
- Region reinforces the setting: Confirmed - Pelren's claim to be "the only fixed thing... the boundary stones" pays off exactly at B.2, and its Secrets (the Diggers' scouted barrow, the Trapper Clans' known route) both land at real locations (B.8, B.6).
- Locations reinforce this region: Confirmed - B.1's washed-out road, B.2's Dead Line, B.3/B.5's toll stretch and the ledge overlooking it, B.6/B.8's trapper thread, and B.7's collapsed tower all visibly deliver what the region overview claims. Landmark/Hidden/Secret tracks correctly: B.1-B.4 landmark, B.5/B.6/B.7 hidden off their parents, B.8 secret off B.7, each connection traceable to a stated Feature at its parent (B.7's Fresh Cart-Ruts, B.2's Watchtower Shell, B.3's goat-track).

## Region C (Ingaraz, DANGEROUS d6)
- Region reinforces the setting: Confirmed - the region's central fact (three centuries older than the keep, still watched by what its builders left) is exactly what C.1/C.2's Old-Ward Sentinel and Nazirur deliver, and the Diggers-vs-Grave-Robbers race named in the overview plays out at C.3/C.4/C.15.
- Locations reinforce this region: Needs Attention - see Room to Grow. The overview's Treasure claim names Table V (Treasure Cache); no C location cites it.

## Room to Grow
Claims made upward with no location under them yet - not defects, just named openings for
the next location or revision to grow into. Two of five Truths and both of the two remaining
History gaps already closed above; what's left:

- **The oath truth** (`Truths.md`, 1st entry) - "a written oath sworn at Fellward's gate holds until the swearer passes back through it." No location lets a party swear, witness, or break an oath at the gate. A location at or near Fellward's own gate (not yet built - region A has no gate-specific room) is the natural home for this.
- **The service-token truth** (`Truths.md`, 2nd entry) - "losing a tally-marked token erases someone's service." Repeated at the region level three times over (A's Situation, A's Secrets "ghost entries," A's Events table #5 "a forged service token surfaces for sale") and cashed out nowhere. This is the setting's most-stated, least-delivered claim. A.2 The Quartermaster's Stores (Weyland issues keep-issue gear) or A.3 The Barracks Block (Watch Board, roster) are the closest existing rooms to carry it, if a future revision adds a Feature there rather than opening a new location.
- **The magic-draws-flame truth** (`Truths.md`, 4th entry) - no location shows a working of magic or its effect on nearby fire. Consistent with Low Magic being rare - this may be intentionally unspent rather than a gap to fill on the next pass, but it's named here so that judgement is made on purpose rather than by default.
- **Region A's Treasure claim** - the overview states it "draws on `Treasure1.md` (Scavenged Loot) and `Treasure2.md` (Equipment and Armaments)"; zero A locations cite either table. A.1, A.2, or A.4 (Forge) are the plainest fits for a Treasure I or II citation.
- **Region A's boar claim** - the overview says the Bramble-Back Boar has "rooted through the kitchen garden and grain stores"; only A.5 (Kitchen Garden) shows Boar Sign. A.1 (Granary) has no boar presence. Either add a boar sign to A.1 or narrow the region's claim to the garden alone.
- **Region A's "ghost entries" secret** - the overview's Secrets field claims "which names still drawing pay are ghost entries the Ring is carrying for a cut"; no location shows a name on the roster that shouldn't be. A.3's Watch Board shows names struck, not names ghost-carried - a related but different fact.
- **Region C's Treasure claim** - the overview states it "draws on `Treasure3.md` (Gems and Jewelry) and `Treasure5.md` (Treasure Cache)"; C.3 and C.4 both cite Table III correctly, but no C location cites Table V. C.1 (the region's one high-weight chamber) is the plainest fit for a Treasure V citation, or the claim should drop Table V.
- **`Language.md` coinage** - Confirmed, not a gap: every coined proper noun (Fellward, Pelren, Ingaraz, Nazirur, Nazirith) is both recorded and used at least once; nothing orphaned to report this pass.

- Confirm on the next pass whether the oath and magic-flame truths are meant to stay unspent (Low Magic, deliberately rare) or are waiting on a location neither region has room for yet.
