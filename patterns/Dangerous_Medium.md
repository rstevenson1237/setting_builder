# Dangerous - Medium Patterns

Thematic patterns for medium-weight locations within DANGEROUS regions - rooms with one major reactive element: a trap, a monster, or a puzzle to solve.

- Dungeon room with a creature performing some task.
- Dungeon room with a creature acting as a guardian of some treasure.
- Dungeon room with a room trap.
- Dungeon room with some sort of lore pertaining to the dungeon.
- Dungeon room with hidden treasure.
- Dungeon room with a puzzle blocking further progress.
- Dungeon room with a hazard tied to the region's theme.
- Dungeon room with a minor faction presence.
