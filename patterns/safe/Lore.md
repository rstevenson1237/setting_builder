# Safe - Lore

## Provides
What written record a settlement holds, who controls access to it, and what it costs to
read.

Also drawn by `safe/Wealth.md`, where the contents of a cache are lore rather than
treasure. Criteria are in `patterns/setting/Lore.md`.

## Spec

```
LORE
  1     Physical form - a kept document, not a found one: a ledger, register,
        charter, correspondence, or survey, made or kept for an administrative
        reason
  1     Who holds it, and why they have it - an institution's own record, an
        heirloom proving a claim, or something that should not be in this holder's
        hands
  1     What it takes to be allowed to read it - a fee, a vouching, a service, or
        reading it on the spot under watch
  1     Whose voice, and what they were wrong about
  30%   It is incomplete, and the holder knows where the rest went
```

**SAFE lore is obtainable rather than discovered.** Records survive here because somebody
keeps them, and that somebody is the obstacle: a ledger is shown or not shown, a register
is copied for a fee, a temple's book is read under supervision. That is a different
proposition from a dungeon's abandoned journal, and the entry is about the *access*, not
the finding.

**A useful record does one concrete thing.** It names a place by an older name, records a
payment that should not have needed paying, establishes who owned what before the current
claim, dates an event against `setting/History.md`, or contradicts what the settlement says
about itself - never several at once.

## Constraints

- **Spoken word is not Lore.** What a person tells the party is a rumour, and belongs in
  `safe/Social.md` and `setting/Rumours.md`. Lore is an object.
