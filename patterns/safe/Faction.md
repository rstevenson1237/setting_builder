# Safe - Faction

## Provides
How a power in `setting/Factions.md` shows itself inside a settlement, where it cannot
simply take what it wants.

Distinct from `dangerous/Faction.md`: a held position at depth is a garrison; in a
settlement it is influence, and influence is visible in different things.

## Spec

```
FACTION PRESENCE - in a settlement
  1     Which faction, and what it wants from this place - a route, a supply,
        recruits, a person watched, local knowledge, legitimacy, or to keep a
        rival out
  1     Something visible that identifies them without naming them - repeated on
        dress, goods, or work, rather than stated, per `setting/Language.md` where
        it is a tongue
  1     Who here is theirs, and whether the settlement knows - open about it, or
        thought to be dealing at arm's length and is not
  1     What the settlement gets in return for tolerating it - coin, protection,
        a market, news, or a rival kept off
  40%   A rival's presence, and how the two avoid open trouble - an agreement
        neither will state, a line neither crosses, or a bidding war for the same
        thing
  30%   Something they are doing here that they would rather not be seen doing
  20%   Somebody who used to be theirs
```

**A faction in a settlement trades rather than holds.** It is here because it wants
something, and it is tolerated because the settlement gets something for it. State both
halves. A faction present with no exchange is an occupation, and that is a Situation, not a
presence.

**Identity is visual and repeated.** A party should recognise the same power two regions
apart from its marks alone, before anyone names it. Use the same signs here as anywhere
else it appears - that consistency is the whole point of a faction having a look.
