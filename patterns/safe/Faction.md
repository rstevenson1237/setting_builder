# Safe - Faction

## Decides
How a power in `setting/Factions.md` shows itself inside a settlement, where it cannot
simply take what it wants.

## Read at
Step 4c, where a location carries a faction's interest. Distinct from
`patterns/dangerous/Faction.md`: a held position at depth is a garrison; in a settlement it
is influence, and influence is visible in different things.

## Spec

```
FACTION PRESENCE - in a settlement
  1     Which faction, and what it wants from this place
  1     Something visible that identifies them without naming them
  1     Who here is theirs, and whether the settlement knows
  1     What the settlement gets in return for tolerating it
  40%   A rival's presence, and how the two avoid open trouble
  30%   Something they are doing here that they would rather not be seen doing
  20%   Somebody who used to be theirs
```

**A faction in a settlement trades rather than holds.** It is here because it wants
something - a route, a supply, a person, a hearing, a foothold - and it is tolerated
because the settlement gets something for it. State both halves. A faction present with no
exchange is an occupation, and that is a Situation, not a presence.

**Identity is visual and repeated.** A party should recognise the same power two regions
apart from its marks alone, before anyone names it. Use the same signs here as anywhere
else it appears - that consistency is the whole point of a faction having a look.

## Patterns

**What a faction wants from a settlement** - a route kept open; a supply bought up before
anyone else sees it; recruits; a person watched; a place to leave things; local knowledge;
legitimacy, by being seen to be dealt with; a debt held over somebody useful; to keep a
rival out.

**Identification** - a colour worn or hung; a device on a seal, a crate, a coin, a boot; a
way of writing a number or tying a knot; a shared weapon or armour tradition; a phrase; a
mark chalked on doors they have called at; a tongue they use among themselves, per
`setting/Language.md`.

**Whose they are** - a factor who is open about it; a trader who is not; somebody in debt to
them; somebody's child, placed; an official who takes their money; a temple that shares
their interest; somebody who thinks they are dealing at arm's length and is not.

**What the settlement gets** - coin; protection from something real; a market for what it
produces; news; a rival kept off; a debt carried; work.

**Friction with a rival** - an agreement neither will state; a line neither crosses; a
neutral party both use; an incident both are pretending did not happen; a bidding war for
the same person or the same goods; a party useful to both, and dangerous to neither yet.
