# Safe - Authority

## Provides
Where a claim to authority is exercised, on what basis, and what a stranger has to do to
get anything out of it.

## Spec

```
AUTHORITY
  1     Who holds it here, and by what claim             (genre: authority-claims)
  1     What actually gets settled here, as opposed to what is claimed
                                                         (genre: authority-matters)
  1     The limit of the claim - where it stops being obeyed
  1     What it takes to get a hearing                   (genre: authority-hearing)
  40%   A rival claim, and who backs it
  30%   Something posted, current and specific, and who posted it
                                                         (genre: authority-postings)
  20%   A custom a stranger will break without knowing   (genre: customs)
```

## Constraints

- **The limit of the claim is the line that makes authority usable.** An office obeyed
  everywhere is scenery; where it stops being obeyed is where a party has something to do
  about it.
