# Safe - Dressing

## Provides
The physical reality of a SAFE location, how much of it its prominence earns, and how its
parts read as one place. Units, the Exits syntax and the citation formats are in
`templates/Location.md`.

## Spec

```
DRESSING - every SAFE location
  1     Size and shape - feet indoors, yards for a yard, green, or market ground
  1     Condition - built for its current use, or repurposed from something else, evidenced
        by signs of use: who has been here, and how often  (genre: settlement-reuse)
  1     Purpose - per its Kind; not restated here
  1     Ambiance - smell and sound, attributable to Condition or Purpose
  1     Terms for what is here, named rather than described
                                                        (genre: vocabulary-settlement)
  1     Every exit typed and positioned, and who may use it (genre: settlement-access)
DETAIL BUDGET
  liner note   baseline only. It looks like what it is, and nothing invites a second look
  working      one or two details out of spec - a repair, a disused fixture, an odd stock,
               something out of keeping with the trade
  central      dressing becomes material. Several such details, and where one gives the
               party something to act on it is promoted to its own Feature line
```

**Settlements reuse, so state which it is - built for this, or repurposed - before
reaching for signs of use.** Where the older stone came from is somewhere the party may be
going.

**SAFE is the rating where buildings recur**, so the settlement's shared way of building -
its roofing, its walls, its window shape - is stated in the Region Overview's Ambiance
field and honoured here. A building that departs from it is older, foreign, richer, or was
built in a hurry.

## Constraints

- **Purpose is supplied by the Kind, not restated here.** Whichever Kind the classifier
  drew has already stated what a location of that Kind is for. Dressing does not reopen
  that question, it dresses the answer.

- **A fact equally true of the whole settlement belongs in the Region Overview.**
  Settlements are the noisiest and smelliest places in the setting, so the Ambiance that
  earns a line here is the one only this building has.

- **Everything here belongs to the same household, trade, or institution.** Where one part
  could not plausibly share a building with the others, change it rather than explaining it.
