# Region - Wild

## Provides
One WILD Region Overview: the stretch of country as a referee runs a crossing of it
without opening a location - how it is entered, what moving through it costs, who is out
here, and what is worth carrying home. How many locations it holds, in what
classification mix, and how they connect is `templates/wild/Locations.md`'s and
`templates/region/Region_Connections.mmd`'s; what one location holds is its classification's own
file.

## Spec

```
WILD REGION
  1     Overview - what a party sees crossing into it, and what brings anyone out here
  1     Approach - where it is entered from, and how long a crossing takes by the best
        route and off it
  1     Terrain - the ground                                              {TERRAIN}
        and what one action buys in distance on a road, off it, and through the worst
        of it; what gets a party lost and what finds the way again; what weather,
        night and a lit fire each change - stated as rulings, per
        setting/Procedures.md's Time
  1     Inhabitants - each beast, band or faction out here, by setting/Bestiary.md or
        setting/Factions.md name: how many, where it ranges, what it is doing, and what
        it does on meeting a party
                            {hunting | watching | avoiding | following | trading}
  1     Places - every Landmark by code, one line each: what of it shows from a
        distance, and what a party goes there for
  1     Situation - what is changing out here, and the next two rungs: what each is, and
        what sets it off
  1     Loot - what is worth carrying home: forage and game by local name, with what the
        nearest SAFE region pays for each; and which treasure tables a find here draws on
  1     Secrets - each a fact the referee holds as true, and the act or place that
        brings it out
  1     Compositions - each coordinated build across this region's rooms: its part rows in
        the order met, its chain link by link, its way round and that way's price, and any
        part beyond its room's class; none where the region has none
  1     Tables - a d6 Encounter table, rolled on each failed Difficulty roll; and a d6
        table of things met between Landmarks, each something a party can stop for
```

```
TERRAIN - one, or two in combination
  hills        - rolling high ground, cut by valleys
  mountains    - high ground that has to be climbed or gone around
  plains       - open, level ground with little cover
  forest       - ground under trees
  swamp        - standing water and ground that will not bear weight
  desert       - ground without water
  jungle       - ground under growth too dense to see through
  coast        - ground at the edge of the sea
```

## Constraints

- **Every field sentence carries something usable at the table** - a name, a distance, a
  price, a place, a ruling. A sentence about why the country is empty or how it feels is
  planning, and is cut.

- **Never a secret without its answer.** The referee holds the truth even where the
  players never learn it.

- **Game is Loot's, danger is Inhabitants'.** Anything dangerous enough to be an encounter
  is cited from the Bestiary under Inhabitants, never listed as game.

- **Never write terrain as a location.** A slope, a brook, a field is what the region looks
  like, and belongs in Terrain; a location is somewhere that can be named, revisited and
  connected to.

- **Places is an index, never a description.** What a Landmark holds is its own entry's.
