# Region - Safe

## Provides
One SAFE Region Overview: the settlement as a whole, which its locations hang off. How
many locations it holds and how they connect is `templates/Location_Gazetteer.md`'s and
`templates/Region_Connections.mmd`'s; what one location holds is
`safe/Settlement.md`'s.

## Spec

```
SAFE REGION
  1     Settlement - decided once, here, and stated first in Layout       {SETTLEMENT}
  1     Overview - three sentences: what it is for and why here rather than a mile away,
        what it lives on, and what it trades away to stand where GENRE.md's Population
        density answer puts it
  1     Ambiance - what it looks, sounds and smells like, and the one shared way of
        building every location in it honours
  1     People - the roster every location draws its cast from: the trades present, the
        trades conspicuously absent, who holds standing, their customs, and how they meet
        outsiders
  1     Situation - the standing state affecting every location now: who is responsible,
        who is affected, which rung of its ladder it is on, and what the next rung looks
        like
  1     Layout - its shape, its approaches, its defences, and where its locations and its
        notable Features and Dangers sit relative to each other
  1     Features - what it offers that is not a location: its law such as it is, its
        prices against elsewhere, what it will and will not trade for, its gate hours,
        where outsiders are allowed
  1     Dangers - what its own people threaten a stranger with, since the wilderness is
        kept out
  1     Creatures - who lives here besides its people, and whether each is working,
        penned, or a problem
  1     Factions - which of setting/Factions.md hold influence here, over which trade,
        gate or office, or `none`
  1     Secrets - what the settlement is not saying, and who knows it
  1     Treasure - what is for sale or in stock, which tables local trade draws on, and
        what money looks like here
  1     Tables - a d6 Events table, rolled on entry and each week after: what happens to
        the settlement, on top of its Situation
```

```
SETTLEMENT - exactly one
  steading     - one household and the land it works
  thorp        - a few households, with no trade beyond their own
  village      - a gathering place and a handful of trades
  town         - a market, many trades, and an authority that sits in it
  seat         - a town where a power larger than the town sits
  hold         - a garrisoned stronghold, whose non-soldiers live under its walls
```

## Constraints

- **A Situation is a condition, not a plot.** It is true whether or not the party engages,
  and it moves on its own.

- **Never a survey of every house and person.** The Overview carries the settlement; its
  locations are the doors a party goes to for something.
