# Region - Safe

## Provides
One SAFE region's overview, in two parts: the seed, the settlement as its locations are
written from - who is here, what can be had and for what, what gets a stranger thrown out,
and what is moving; and the brief, written once the locations exist, which indexes them
for a referee running the settlement without opening a location. What one location holds
is `patterns/Schema.md`'s.

## Spec

```
SAFE SEED
  1     Settlement - decided once, here, and named in the Overview's first sentence
                                                                     {SETTLEMENT}
  1     Overview - what a party sees as it arrives, and what it comes here to do
  1     Approach - every way in: what stands at it, what it takes to pass - a toll, a
        name, an hour - and what happens to anyone turned away
  1     People - the named roster every location draws its cast from: each person's
        role, what they want from a stranger, and what they will give or sell for it
  1     Services - what can be bought, hired or sold here, each with its price in
        setting/Treasure1.md's coin; what it buys from a party, at what rate; and what
        cannot be had here at any price
  1     Law - what gets a stranger fined, locked up or put out of the gate, who
        enforces it, and what each costs
  1     Situation - what is going wrong now, who is behind it and who is hurt by it,
        and the next two rungs: what each is, and what sets it off
```

```
SAFE BRIEF
  1     Places - every location by code, one line each: what a party goes there for,
        and who of the roster is found there
  1     Secrets - each a fact the referee holds as true, who knows it, and the entry that
        brings it out
  1     Leads - each rumour pointing here, by number, and each quest beginning or ending
        here, by name
  1     Tables - a d6 Events table, rolled on arrival and each week after; and a d6
        table of people and trouble, for anywhere in the settlement no location keys
```

```
SETTLEMENT - exactly one
  steading     - one household and the land it works
  thorp        - a few households, with no trade beyond their own
  village      - a gathering place and a handful of trades
  town         - a market, many trades, and an authority that sits in it
  seat         - a town where a power larger than the town sits
  hold         - a garrisoned stronghold, whose non-soldiers live under its walls
```

## Constraints

- **Every field sentence carries something usable at the table** - a name, a number, a
  price, a place, a consequence. A sentence about why the settlement exists, what it
  lives on or how it feels is planning, and is cut.

- **Never a secret without its answer.** The players may never learn it; the referee
  always holds it, and a secret the Overview leaves undecided is a decision handed to
  whoever improvises first.

- **A Situation is a condition, not a plot.** It is true whether or not the party engages,
  and it moves on its own.

- **Places is an index, never a description.** It says what a party goes to a location
  for; what the location holds is its own entry's.
