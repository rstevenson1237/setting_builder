# Dangerous - Environmental

## Provides
A hazard that is a condition of the place itself, set by nobody and maintained by nothing.
Distinct from `dangerous/Trap.md`, which has an owner and an intent, and from
`dangerous/Residual.md`, which something left behind.

## Spec

```
ENVIRONMENTAL
  1     Kind, and what the place is doing - the physical condition, stated plainly
        {collapse | air | water | footing | heat | cold | growth | dark and sound}
                                                        (genre: environmental)
  1     What it is a consequence of, in the location's own Dressing
  1     Whether it is constant, or worsens while the party is in it
  1     Trigger - what being in it, or doing something in it, costs
  1     Damage - the expression the tier allows, per setting/Procedures.md
  30%   Somewhere in this location it does not reach, and why
```

**The kind is drawn against the region, not the room.** What the region is cut into and what
water and air do in it rule out most of the list before it is read.

**Nothing here is aimed, so the trigger is a threshold, not an act**: being in it at all, or
doing in it the one thing it will not take - moving quickly, carrying a full load, striking
a light, staying past the turn it takes to work.

**A safe pocket is worth more than a bigger number**, because it turns endurance into a
decision about how long to stay out of it.

**Its damage types are the ones a place produces without help**: Frost and Fire from
temperature, Poison from air, Crushing from collapse or footing, Blast only where the place
is holding something back.

## Constraints

- **A fall that has finished is Dressing, not a hazard.** What this file draws is still
  able to act: something holding that can stop holding, ground still moving, water still
  rising.

- **Growth that acts on its own account is a creature.** Something that reaches, follows,
  wants, or can be driven off is `dangerous/Creature.md`'s, terrain-shaped.

- **A condition the Dressing does not already account for has an owner.** It came from
  somebody building it or something leaving it, and filing it here strips it of the maker
  that made it worth finding.

- **Sanity is rarely this file's.** A condition with no author is grim, not maddening.
