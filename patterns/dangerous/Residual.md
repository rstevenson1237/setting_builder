# Dangerous - Residual

## Provides
A hazard something left behind that is still acting, after whatever made it has gone.

Distinct from `dangerous/Trap.md`, which is still doing the job it was built for, and
from `dangerous/Environmental.md`, which the place does on its own account.

## Spec

```
RESIDUAL
  1     What made it - an event from setting/History.md - and that the maker is gone
  1     What it is still doing, and to what
  1     Whether it is spreading, holding, or fading
  1     Trigger - what crossing it or disturbing it does
  1     Damage - the expression the tier allows, per setting/Procedures.md
  30%   What it was meant to protect, and whether that is still there
```

**A residual hazard is History with a Left mark that still bites.** Per
`patterns/setting/History.md` every event owes a mark findable now; this is the case where
the mark is not a wall or a name but an effect that has outlasted its cause. That is the
line to draw it from - a residual hazard invented with no event behind it is a trap with
atmosphere.

**The maker being gone is the whole character of it.** Nothing is maintaining this and
nothing will switch it off. It cannot be reasoned with, and it cannot be argued out of
doing what it does, because whatever set it going has no further say.

**Fading is the most useful state**, because it dates itself: something weaker than it
clearly once was tells a party how long ago, without a word of exposition.

**The trigger is an edge, not a mechanism.** Nothing here was built to catch anyone, so what
sets it off is reaching the ground it still holds, or handling the thing it is still in. An
effect with a clear edge - the boundary, the room, the object - is what gives the party
something to stay outside of.

**Sanity is this file's own type**, where the others rarely touch it: what a rite or a death
left is the case where the harm is to the mind rather than the body. Poison is its other -
what a working left in the water or the ground. Per `GENRE.md`'s Low Magic, a region where
several rooms force a Test of Sanity has made the extraordinary routine.

## Design patterns

**What a death left** - ground that will not take a body; cold that keeps to the spot
somebody died on; a sound repeating the last thing that happened here; a mark that comes
back through every repair.

**What a working left** - tailings, slag, or runoff from a working nobody has run in a
lifetime; heat that never went out of a furnace floor; water that came up when the digging
stopped and has been coming up since; what a craft used daily and poured away.

**What a rite left** - a boundary that still holds, which nobody remembers agreeing to; a
prohibition the place enforces on its own; something that still answers to a name nobody
living uses; ground consecrated or cursed well past whoever did it.

**What something living here left** - a nest, a shed skin, a spore bed, or a scent mark
still doing its work after the animal is gone; what a body left in the water it fell into;
something bred or kept that has outlived its keeper.

**How it shows its age** - an effect with a clear edge, and the edge closer in than the
marks say it once was; something strong in one room and barely detectable two rooms out; a
warning left by whoever found it after the maker had already gone.

**Vocabulary.** What decay and working leave behind is named technically, never
atmospherically: spall, efflorescence, salt-bloom, verdigris, patina, clinker, slag,
tailings, sinter, rime, midden, char, calcined, leached. Each carries a process as well as a
deposit, which is what makes one worth a Feature at all.

## Constraints

- **Low Magic is a constraint here too.** Per `GENRE.md` a residual hazard is rare,
  mysterious, and costly. A region where several rooms carry lingering effects has made the
  extraordinary routine, which is the drift this category is most likely to cause.
