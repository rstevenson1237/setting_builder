# Dangerous - Residual

## Provides
A hazard something left behind that is still acting, after whatever made it has gone.
Distinct from `dangerous/Trap.md`, still doing the job it was built for, and from
`dangerous/Environmental.md`, which the place does on its own account.

## Spec

```
RESIDUAL
  1     What made it - an event from setting/History.md - and that the maker is gone
                                                        (genre: residual-causes)
  1     What it is still doing, and to what
  1     Whether it is spreading, holding, or fading     (genre: residual-age)
  1     Trigger - what crossing it or disturbing it does
  1     Damage - the expression the tier allows, per setting/Procedures.md
  30%   What it was meant to protect, and whether that is still there
```

**Draw it from a History event's Left mark** - the case where the mark is an effect that has
outlasted its cause. Invented with no event behind it, it is a trap with atmosphere.

**Fading is the most useful state**, because it dates itself without a word of exposition.

**The trigger is an edge, not a mechanism** - reaching the ground it still holds, or handling
the thing it is still in. That edge is what the party can stay outside of.

**Sanity is this file's own type**, where the others rarely touch it, and Poison is its
other.

## Constraints

- **The maker being gone is the whole character of it.** Nothing is maintaining this and
  nothing will switch it off; it cannot be argued out of doing what it does.

- **Low Magic is a constraint here too.** Per `GENRE.md` a residual hazard is rare,
  mysterious and costly. A region where several rooms carry lingering effects has made the
  extraordinary routine, which is the drift this category is most likely to cause.
