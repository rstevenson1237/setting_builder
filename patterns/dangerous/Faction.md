# Dangerous - Faction

## Provides
What it means for a DANGEROUS location to be *held* rather than merely inhabited. What
lives in a room on its own account is `dangerous/Creature.md`'s.

## Spec

```
FACTION PRESENCE
  1     Which faction, and what this position is for     (genre: faction-positions)
  1     Something visible that identifies them without naming them  (genre: faction-signs)
  1     What happens elsewhere if this position is lost or alarmed
                                                          (genre: faction-offsite)
  50%   A standing order they are following, which the party can read or overhear
  30%   Friction - with another faction, with the region itself, or within the group
                                                          (genre: faction-friction)
  20%   Someone here who would rather be somewhere else
```

The difference between a lair and a held position is **off-site consequence**. A beast
killed in its den is a beast killed; a picket killed at its post is noticed, missed,
replaced, or avenged.

## Constraints

- **Identity is visual and repeated, never announced.** A party should recognise the same
  faction two regions apart from its signs alone, before anyone tells them who it is.
