# Dangerous - Faction

## Decides
What it means for a DANGEROUS location to be *held* rather than merely inhabited.

## Read at
Step 4c, where a location's occupants belong to a power in `setting/Factions.md`. Distinct
from `dangerous/Creature.md`, which covers what lives in a room; a faction presence
implies things outside the room.

## Spec

```
FACTION PRESENCE
  1     Which faction, and what this position is for
  1     Something visible that identifies them without naming them
  1     What happens elsewhere if this position is lost or alarmed
  50%   A standing order they are following, which the party can read or overhear
  30%   Friction - with another faction, with the region itself, or within the group
  20%   Someone here who would rather be somewhere else
```

The difference between a lair and a held position is **off-site consequence**. A beast
killed in its den is a beast killed. A picket killed at its post is noticed, missed,
replaced, or avenged. State what happens elsewhere - that is the whole reason this file
exists separately from `Creature.md`.

## Patterns

**What a position is for** - watching an approach; holding a way open; holding a way shut;
working something out of the ground; storing what has been taken; keeping something alive;
keeping something contained; a foothold in a region they do not yet hold.

**Identification without naming** - a colour worn or flown; a device, blazon, or mark
repeated on gear and walls; a way of cutting, tying, stacking, or repairing; a shared
weapon or armour tradition; a mark left on doors they have cleared; a rank shown in
something small; a tongue they speak among themselves, from `setting/Language.md`.

A party should be able to recognise the same faction two regions apart from these signs
alone, before anyone tells them who it is. Faction identity is visual and repeated, and
consistency across appearances is the point.

**Off-site consequence** - a patrol due back; a signal expected; a superior who will come
looking; a supply run this position exists to protect; a rival who has been waiting for
exactly this; a reprisal against somebody the party has met.

**Friction** - orders that no longer make sense; a shortage; somebody skimming; a feud
with the position next door; a captive who has been here too long; the region itself
eating them slowly.
