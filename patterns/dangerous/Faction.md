# Dangerous - Faction

## Provides
What it means for a DANGEROUS location to be *held* rather than merely inhabited.

Distinct from `dangerous/Creature.md`, which covers what lives in a room on its own
account; a faction presence implies things outside the room.

## Spec

```
FACTION PRESENCE
  1     Which faction, and what this position is for - to watch, to hold a way open
        or shut, to work or store something, to keep something alive or contained,
        or as a foothold not yet held
  1     Something visible that identifies them without naming them - repeated on
        gear, dress, or work, rather than stated
  1     What happens elsewhere if this position is lost or alarmed
  50%   A standing order they are following, which the party can read or overhear
  30%   Friction - with another faction, with the region itself, or within the group
  20%   Someone here who would rather be somewhere else
```

The difference between a lair and a held position is **off-site consequence**. A beast
killed in its den is a beast killed. A picket killed at its post is noticed, missed,
replaced, or avenged. State what happens elsewhere - that is the whole reason this file
exists separately from `Creature.md`.

A party should be able to recognise the same faction two regions apart from its
identifying sign alone, before anyone tells them who it is. Faction identity is visual and
repeated, and consistency across appearances is the point.
