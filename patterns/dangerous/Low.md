# Dangerous - Low

## Decides
What a low-weight location guarantees, and what its node role requires of it.

## Read at
Step 4c, for a location its gazetteer stub marks low. Read with `dangerous/Dressing.md`
and `dangerous/Secrets.md`, and with the node role taken from the region's
`Connections.mmd`, written at 4b.

## Spec

```
DANGEROUS - LOW                        (parameterized by node role)
  1     Purpose - what this space was for, and whether that use still holds
  1     Ambiance - one sensory fact beyond the visual
  1     Node role honoured, per the Patterns below
  14%   Treasure - unguarded, cited from a table
  20%   A detail that rewards looking without demanding action
  10%   A detail that foreshadows a HIGH location elsewhere in the region
```

Low weight means the location presents as unremarkable. It does not mean the location is
empty: a low-weight room may hide a secret door, hold a corpse worth searching, or open
onto the region's worst decision. What makes it low is that **it looks like nothing**.

That is the whole value of the class. If a low-weight room could never repay attention,
players would learn to walk through them, and the region would lose the only thing that
makes attention a real cost.

## Patterns

The node role comes from the graph, not from a pick here. Five roles, each asking the
location for something different.

- **Empty** - the party's question is *is it actually empty, or is something hiding?* Give
  enough dressing that searching is tempting and not obviously futile. Something to look
  behind, under, or inside.
- **Dead end** - the question is *is there a secret door here?* Give a reason to suspect
  one: a draft, a wall that does not match its neighbours, a floor worn toward a blank
  face. See `dangerous/Secrets.md` for the rate at which there genuinely is.
- **Branch** - the question is *which way first?* Everything returns here, so the choice
  is reversible and the cost is time. The exits must be told apart by more than direction.
- **Loop leg** - the question is *where am I relative to where I have been?* Give one
  detail distinctive enough that the party recognizes the loop closing. This is the only
  role whose payoff is spatial understanding rather than content.
- **Divide** - the question is *which way, with no coming back?* The highest-stakes choice
  in the region and the one most often written as a coin flip. Both directions need
  something legible to choose on - a sound, a smell, a mark, a difference in the air.
