# Dangerous - Dressing

## Provides
The physical reality of a DANGEROUS location, and how its parts read as one place. Units,
the Exits line syntax and the citation formats are `templates/Location.md`'s.

## Spec

```
DRESSING - every location
  1     Size and shape
  1     Condition, stated before Purpose - how far the room sits from still in use to
        gone, which decides how legible its Purpose still is
                                {active | abandoned | decayed | ruined | destroyed}
  1     Purpose - what it was for: its family, then the room's own use within it, and
        what that use left in the fabric of the room, drawn from the region's
        Architecture and its occupants' needs
                                                                 (dangerous/Block.md)
  1     Ambiance - smell and sound, caused by Condition or Purpose; temperature and
        footing follow from Condition
  1     Every exit typed and positioned                 (dangerous/Door.md)
```

## Constraints

- **Never a detail the Region Overview already gives.** A location's Dressing states what
  is specific to it; a motif claimed at the Overview and carried by a handful of rooms
  exists nowhere a party can touch it.

- **Do not reuse a purpose already used in this block.** A block with three storerooms has
  told the party that rooms do not matter. A room destroyed past legibility still states
  what it was, and lets Condition explain why it no longer shows.

- **Do not let every room in a region land at the same Condition.** Variance is what lets a
  party read, room to room, how the place answers intrusion.

- **Never leave a feature that could not share the room with the rest.** Everything in it
  was put there by the same history; change what does not fit rather than explaining it.

- **Never describe an architectural term, trade or material that has a name.** Name it.
