# Dangerous - Lore

## Provides
When a DANGEROUS location's find is written rather than valuable, and what form it takes.
What earns a `setting/Lore.md` row is `patterns/setting/Lore.md`'s.

## Spec

```
LORE
  1     Physical form - it is an object, not a thing someone knows
                                                (genre: lore-forms-underground)
  1     Why it survived where it did
  1     Whose voice it is in, and what they were wrong about   (genre: lore-voice)
  1     What it does to what the party already thinks          (genre: lore-effects)
  50%   A detail that only makes sense once another location is seen
```

At low and medium weight, lore **substitutes** for treasure; at high weight it sits
**alongside** the payload.

Record a stub row in `setting/Lore.md` now - name and this location only. The content is
written at 4d, with every location that cites it in view.

## Constraints

- **A document that knows everything is a briefing, not a find.** It is written by someone
  in the setting, so it is partial, biased, mistaken, or self-interested.
