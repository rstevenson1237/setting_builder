# Dangerous - Low Patterns

Thematic patterns for low-weight locations within DANGEROUS regions - connective areas and empty rooms that carry detail but do not demand action.

- Dungeon room that is empty but reinforces the region's theme through detail.
- Dungeon room serving as a connective corridor, junction, or stairwell.
- Dungeon room with an environmental detail hinting at what lies deeper.
- Dungeon room that once served a mundane purpose, now decayed or abandoned.
- Dungeon room safe enough to serve as a resting point.
- Dungeon room with debris or remains that reward careful looking without demanding action.
- Dungeon room with a passive sensory detail - a sound, a smell, a light - and nothing more.
