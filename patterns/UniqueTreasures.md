# Unique Treasure Patterns

Guidance for what belongs in `setting/UniqueTreasures.md` rather than a roll on `setting/Treasure[I-V].md`, stubbed during location generation and fully written afterward (see `templates/UniqueTreasures.md`).

- A Unique Treasure is a specific artifact or magical item with its own name, history, and effect - not a random find. It should feel like the exception the setting's Low Magic makes room for, not routine equipment (see `GENRE.md`).
- State what it does plainly, and what it costs - Low Magic means power carries a price; a Unique Treasure with no downside, limitation, or complication reads as generic loot with a fancy name.
- Tie it to the setting where possible - a `setting/History.md` event, a `setting/Truths.md` entry, a Faction, or a Named Creature - rather than inventing an origin disconnected from anything else established.
- A Unique Treasure belongs to the location it's found at, same as any other Treasure, but its location Feature only names and cites it - the full description lives in `setting/UniqueTreasures.md`, written in step 4d, not in the location entry.

## Where It Fits
- DANGEROUS, high weight: the natural pairing - a high-weight room's primary treasure or objective is usually worth making a Unique Treasure instead of a generic table roll, since the room is already built to be a destination.
- DANGEROUS, low or medium weight: uncommon - a plain Treasure citation almost always fits better; reach for this only when a specific artifact genuinely belongs there.
- SAFE or WILD: valid anywhere, but at a relatively low chance of occurring - most landmarks don't hide an artifact.
