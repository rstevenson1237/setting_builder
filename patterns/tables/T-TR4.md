Treasure Table IV: working devices. The band where a find does something. Magic
in this setting is a found technology nobody now understands, and this is the
table it is found in.

**Columns:** `ID | Entry | Mechanics`. A parameter table. Base band: roughly
`{VALUE: 2000 cn}` to `{VALUE: 6000 cn}` a piece.

## Patterns

**Every device half-works, works differently than it is recorded to work, or
works on the wrong thing.** That is the setting's standing condition and this
table is where a party meets it. State what it actually does, then state what it
was for, and let the two disagree.

**Each device carries instructions that are wrong in one particular.** Written
on it, with it, or known to whoever had it last. One particular, not all of
them: a party that can trust none of the instructions ignores them, and a party
that can trust all of them is reading a manual.

**Say what it costs to run.** Fuel, time, noise, light, warmth, a thing consumed,
a thing given up. A device with no running cost is an ability and it changes the
game permanently in a setting built on what a trip costs.

**It is already used, and the wear says by whom.** Nothing here is new, nothing
is replaceable, and none of it enchants on request. A device that shows no use
has no history, and its history is most of its content.

**Devices are the reason to go down.** Depth is a direction: the further down, the
fewer of the surface's rules a place keeps. A party that comes back with one of
these has extracted something the world cannot make any more.

**Eight rows.** `{QUALITY: Masterwork}` mostly, with value and weight on every
row, and one `{QUALITY: Cursed}` that reads as ordinary until it is run.

## Excluded patterns

- **A device with an unlimited use.** Everything has a running cost.
- **A device that solves a whole class of problem.** Light with no fuel, safe
  water without limit, and travel without distance each delete a pillar of play.
- **A spell, a scroll, or anything that is a rule rather than an object.**
  Written works are `T-TOM`.
- **A device whose instructions are correct.** One particular is wrong.
- **A named unique device.** `T-TRE`.
- **A device a party can repair.** Nobody now understands these.

## Design questions

1. **What did the fallen power build machines to do?**
2. **For each device: what does it actually do, and what was it for?**
3. **Which particular of the instructions is wrong, and what does that cost the
   first time?**
4. **What does running it consume?**
5. **Who used this one last, and did they stop by choice?**
6. **Which device would change how a party travels, and is that change worth
   what it takes to run?**
