Treasure Table III: fine work and instruments. The band where a find stops being
worth its material and starts being worth the hands that made it.

**Columns:** `ID | Entry | Mechanics`. A parameter table. Base band: roughly
`{VALUE: 500 cn}` to `{VALUE: 1500 cn}` a piece.

## Patterns

**Craft is the value and the craft is described.** How it was made, by what
method, to a tolerance nobody now holds. A referee should be able to answer
"why is this worth that" from the row.

**Instruments measure something.** A rule, a level, a sounding line, a gauge, a
plumb, a counter. Half this band should be things that took a measurement,
because the fallen power measured everything and a party who works out what an
instrument measures has learned something about the world.

**An instrument is not a device.** It reports; it does not act. Something that
does a thing belongs in the band above. The difference is worth holding, because
it is where a party's curiosity gets rewarded without anything being handed to
them.

**Fine work is portable and it is fragile.** Weight is low and the risk is
damage. State what breaks it: cold, a drop, water, being taken apart to fit a
pack.

**One row in this band should be incomplete.** A pair with one half missing, a
set with a piece gone. The missing half is a hook that costs nothing and points
at somewhere else in the setting.

**Eight rows.** `{QUALITY: Fine}` and `{QUALITY: Masterwork}` across the band,
with value and weight on every row.

## Excluded patterns

- **Anything that works on its own.** Higher band.
- **Jewellery whose only content is its value.** If it is only a number, it is
  coin with extra steps. Give it a maker, a use, or a wrong detail.
- **A named unique piece.** `T-TRE`.
- **A row that requires the ruleset to adjudicate its use.**
- **An instrument with no stated unit.** What it measures, and in what.

## Design questions

1. **What did the fallen power measure, and with what?**
2. **For each instrument: what does it read, and what would a party learn from
   reading it?**
3. **What breaks each of these, and how easily?**
4. **Which piece is one half of a pair, and where is the other half?**
5. **Which of these would a buyer in a lit place not believe was genuine?**
