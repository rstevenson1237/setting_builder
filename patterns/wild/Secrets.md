# Wild - Secrets

## Decides
Whether a WILD location hides something within itself, and the three parts that make it
findable.

## Read at
Step 4c, for every WILD location - consulted unconditionally to decide whether there is a
secret at all. Distinct from `wild/Secret.md`, which is a whole Secret-tier *location*
hanging off a parent; this is a detail concealed inside one location's features.

## Spec

```
SECRET
  1     Clue    - already visible through ordinary observation, not itself the secret
  1     Trigger - the specific action that acts on the clue
  1     Payload - what the trigger produces
```

Rate:

```
INCLUSION
  20%   of WILD locations carry a feature-level secret
```

Lower than DANGEROUS, because WILD's concealment budget is mostly spent on the Secret tier
itself. A region already hiding whole locations behind triggers does not need every
landmark hiding something too.

**A Secret without a stated Clue is not discoverable.** The clue has to be legible to a
player paying attention before they know there is anything to find. This is the most
common way the structure fails, and outdoors it fails more easily, because a party can
walk past a whole hillside.

## Patterns

**Clues that weather leaves.** Concealment outdoors is undone by time, and that is what
gives a party something to see: a hollow that has slumped; a covering that has rotted
through; a plant growing where the ground was disturbed; frost or dew that melts in a
shape; snow that lies differently; water that drains where it should pool; a stone that
has been moved and settled wrong; growth that is younger than the growth around it.

**Clues people leave** - a path worn to nowhere; a mark cut above standing height, from
when the ground was higher or the snow deeper; something tied and forgotten; a repair; a
cairn that is a marker rather than a grave; stones in a line under turf; an old cut end.

**Triggers, outdoors** - digging, at a stated spot; moving a stone that is clearly meant to
be moved; entering water; climbing to a stated vantage; waiting for a time, tide, or
weather; lighting or extinguishing; something left rather than taken.

**Payloads** - a cache; a way into a Secret-tier location; a piece of Lore; a Key; a route
that shortens a journey the party already knows is long; a sight of somewhere they have
not reached; a hazard understood before it is walked into.
