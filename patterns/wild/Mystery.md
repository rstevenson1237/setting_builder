# Wild - Mystery

## Provides
What is strange about a WILD location, how it is engaged, and what it costs to get wrong.
Distinct from a concealed detail, whose rate and triple the classification file draws: a
Mystery stands in plain sight; a Secret does not.

## Spec

```
MYSTERY
  1     The thing   {placed | grown | a property of the place itself}
                        (genre: mystery-placed, mystery-grown, mystery-properties)
  2     Physical details it can be reasoned from
  1     Trigger, stated explicitly                      (genre: mystery-triggers)
  1     What the correct trigger accomplishes
  1     What a genuinely wrong attempt costs
  1     Its price, per GENRE.md - power is never free and never routine
                                                        (genre: mystery-prices)
```

**Outdoors, a mystery has been exposed to weather and to other people.** Both leave marks,
and both are detail the party can reason from. Somebody has been here before: what did they
try, and did it work?

## Constraints

- **The price is stated, not implied.** A WILD mystery whose cost is left to the referee has
  made the extraordinary free, which is the drift `GENRE.md`'s Low Magic exists to stop.
