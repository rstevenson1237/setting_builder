# Wild - Mystery

## Decides
What is strange about a WILD location, how it is engaged, and what it costs to get wrong.

## Read at
Step 4c, when a spec line draws a mystery. Distinct from `wild/Secrets.md`: a Mystery is
content and stands in plain sight; a Secret is a discovery structure and does not.

## Spec

```
MYSTERY
  1     The thing - placed, grown, or a property of the place itself
  2     Physical details it can be reasoned from
  1     Trigger, stated explicitly
  1     What the correct trigger accomplishes
  1     What a genuinely wrong attempt costs
  1     Its price, per GENRE.md - power is never free and never routine
```

Left alone, a mystery is neutral. Looking, theorising, and a wrong guess that stops short
of a real attempt cost nothing. It fires only on a failed attempt to use it - which is
what separates it from the hazards in `wild/Trap.md`.

**Outdoors, a mystery has been exposed to weather and to other people.** Both leave marks,
and both are detail the party can reason from. Somebody has been here before. What did
they try, and did it work?

## Patterns

**Placed** - standing stones, singly or in a ring; a monolith with a worked face; a cairn
that is not a grave; an altar or offering stone in the open; a carved boundary marker
whose terms are still legible; a hand-print or figure cut into rock; something set into a
tree that the tree has grown around; a thing balanced in a way weather should have undone.

**Grown** - a tree of the wrong species, or the wrong age; a ring of fungus or stones that
returns; a plant that only grows where something happened; a hedge or thicket in a shape;
water that will not support life, or supports too much.

**Properties of the place** - a spring that changes what is put into it; ground that will
not hold what is buried; a direction that reads wrong; a silence with an edge to it; cold
with a boundary; a place where a sense fails; an echo that answers late or differently;
light that arrives from the wrong quarter.

**Triggers, outdoors** - something left rather than taken; a time of day, tide, or season;
a phrase in the tongue on the stone, per `setting/Language.md`; standing in a stated place;
water poured, fire lit, or blood given; walking a course; bringing something that came from
somewhere else in the setting.

**Prices** - it takes a thing carried, a memory, a name, time, or blood; it works once; it
works for one person; it draws attention from something that was not watching before; what
it grants is real and is not what was wanted.
