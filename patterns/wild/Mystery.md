# Wild - Mystery

## Provides
What is strange about a WILD location, how it is engaged, and what it costs to get wrong.
A Mystery is content and stands in plain sight; a concealed detail is a discovery
structure and does not.

## Spec

```
MYSTERY
  1     The thing                  {placed | grown | a property of the place itself}
  2     Physical details it can be reasoned from - including what weather and earlier
        visitors have left on it
  1     Trigger, stated explicitly - an act on the thing itself
                        {left rather than taken | timed to a tide or season | spoken |
                         walked as a course}
  1     What the correct trigger accomplishes
  1     What a genuinely wrong attempt costs
  1     Its price, at what GENRE.md's Magic level says power costs
```

## Constraints

- **Never cost anything short of a real attempt.** Looking, theorising, and a wrong guess
  that stops short of trying are free; that is what separates it from `wild/Hazard.md`.
