# Wild - Crossing

## Provides
What a Landmark defined by the cost of going around it guarantees, and what currently
controls passing through it. Drawn at the Landmark tier only, per that file's Constraint.

## Spec

```
KIND: CROSSING
  1     What must be crossed, and why the country will not simply be gone around
                                                        (genre: crossings)
  1     What controls or complicates the crossing today  (genre: crossing-controls)
  1     What waits on the far side, visible before the party commits to it
                                                        (genre: crossing-far-side)
  40%   Something else caught here, mid-crossing, when the party arrives
                                                        (genre: crossing-caught)
  30%   A toll, custom, or right-of-way someone here enforces
  30%   What controls it is one of the three factions           (wild/Faction.md)
```

## Constraints

- **The far side is named before the party commits.** A crossing whose other end is
  unstated is a coin flip, and the cost of going around is what the choice is supposed to
  be weighed against.
