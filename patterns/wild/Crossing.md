# Wild - Crossing

## Provides
What a Landmark defined by the cost of going around it guarantees, and what currently
controls passing through it.

Alone among the four kinds it is drawn at the Landmark tier only, per that file's own
Constraint. A Crossing is a Landmark whose function is that the region's own shape makes
going through it cheaper than going around it - built, natural, or held, and regardless
of who if anyone lives there.

## Spec

```
KIND: CROSSING
  1     What must be crossed, and why the country will not simply be gone around -
        a single fordable reach, one standing bridge or gate, one pass below the
        snow line, or open ground with nothing to hide behind
  1     What controls or complicates the crossing today - season, toll, hazard, guardian
  1     What waits on the far side, visible before the party commits to it - a
        territory's edge, a sign of who holds that ground, or nothing at all,
        which is its own kind of information
  40%   Something else caught here, mid-crossing, when the party arrives - waiting
        out a closed season, staffing a post, or wreckage from an earlier attempt
  30%   A toll, custom, or right-of-way someone here enforces
  30%   What controls it is one of the three factions           (wild/Faction.md)
```
