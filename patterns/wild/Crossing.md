# Wild - Crossing

## Decides
What a Landmark defined by the cost of going around it guarantees, and what currently
controls passing through it.

## Read at
Step 4c, when `wild/Landmark.md`'s spec draws Kind: Crossing - the only classification file
that does. That file's own KIND: CROSSING block states the contract (what must be crossed,
what controls it now, what waits on the far side, the percentage lines) - genre-neutral and
permanent. This file supplies what fills that contract, which is not: a Crossing is a
Landmark whose function is that the region's own shape makes going through it cheaper than
going around it - built, natural, or held, and regardless of who if anyone lives there.

## Examples

Compiled for this build, from this setting's chosen genre reference.

**What must be crossed** - a river at its one fordable reach; a gorge with one bridge or
none; a marsh with one causeway across it; a mountain's one pass below the snow line; a wall
with one gate still standing; a border a faction actually enforces; a stretch of open ground
with nothing to hide behind.

**What controls it now** - a toll collected by whoever holds the far bank; a season that
closes it (flood, snow, a tide); a guardian, human or otherwise, that has to be gotten past
rather than fought; a structural failure that makes the crossing itself the challenge; a
custom or right-of-way older than any single authority here.

**What waits on the far side** - visible and worth naming before the party commits: a
different territory's edge; a watch-fire; the first sign of who or what holds that ground;
nothing at all, which is its own kind of information.

**Caught mid-crossing** - a caravan or drover waiting out a closed season; someone who did
not make it across; a faction using the crossing as a checkpoint; a toll-taker's post,
staffed or not; wreckage or wear from an earlier, failed attempt.
