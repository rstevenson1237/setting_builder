# Wild - Lore

## Provides
What recorded thing a WILD location holds, and how it survived being outdoors. What earns
a Lore row is `patterns/setting/Lore.md`'s.

## Spec

```
LORE
  1     Form - made to outlast its makers, monumental, public, and meant to be read by
        strangers                            {stone | metal | bone | arrangement}
  1     Weathered - why weather has not destroyed it
  1     Voice - whose voice: each partial by nature, each with reason to overstate
                                                    {a claim | a boundary | a warning}
  1     Wrong about - what they were wrong about
  1     Names - something it names that the party has not found yet
  1     Does - the one thing it does
                        {names a place | fixes a date against setting/History.md |
                         establishes a claim | gives an older name}
  1     Lore row - a stub row in setting/Lore.md; its content is the registry entry's
  30%   Partial - the rest is missing, and where it went is knowable
```

## Constraints

- **Outdoors, lore is cut, not written.** Paper, ink and hide do not last in the open.
