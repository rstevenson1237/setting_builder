# Wild - Lore

## Provides
What recorded thing a WILD location holds, and how it survived being outdoors. What earns
a Lore row is `patterns/setting/Lore.md`'s.

## Spec

```
LORE
  1     Physical form, and why weather has not destroyed it - made to outlast its makers,
        monumental, public, and meant to be read by strangers
                                             {stone | metal | bone | arrangement}
  1     Whose voice, and what they were wrong about - each partial by nature, each with
        reason to overstate                         {a claim | a boundary | a warning}
  1     Something it names that the party has not found yet
  1     The one thing it does
                        {names a place | fixes a date against setting/History.md |
                         establishes a claim | gives an older name}
  1     A stub row in setting/Lore.md - its content is written at 4d
  30%   Partial - the rest is missing, and where it went is knowable
```

## Constraints

- **Outdoors, lore is cut, not written.** Paper, ink and hide do not last in the open.
