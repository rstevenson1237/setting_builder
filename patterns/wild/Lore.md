# Wild - Lore

## Provides
What written or recorded thing a WILD location holds, and how it survived being outdoors.
Lore is one of the things a WILD find can be, so it is reached through the reward block.
Criteria are in `patterns/setting/Lore.md`.

## Spec

```
LORE
  1     Physical form, and why weather has not destroyed it
                                                (genre: lore-forms-outdoors)
  1     Whose voice, and what they were wrong about     (genre: lore-voice)
  1     What it does to what the party already thinks   (genre: lore-effects)
  1     Something it names that the party has not found yet
  30%   Partial - the rest is missing, and where it went is knowable
```

## Constraints

- **Outdoors, lore is cut, not written.** Paper, ink and hide do not last in the open, so
  WILD lore is in stone, metal, bone, or arrangement - things made to outlast the people who
  made them. That is a different register from a dungeon's ledgers and letters: monumental,
  public, and meant to be read by strangers.

- **The people who cut it were making a claim, marking a boundary, or warning somebody.**
  All three overstate. A boundary stone is an argument, not a fact.
