# Wild - Lore

## Provides
What written or recorded thing a WILD location holds, and how it survived being outdoors.

Lore is one of the things a WILD find can be, so it is reached through the reward block
rather than as a hook of its own. Criteria are in `patterns/setting/Lore.md`.

## Spec

```
LORE
  1     Physical form, and why weather has not destroyed it - per this file's
        Constraints, cut or arranged rather than written
  1     Whose voice, and what they were wrong about - a claim, a boundary, or a
        warning, each partial by nature and each with reason to overstate
  1     Something it names that the party has not found yet
  30%   Partial - the rest is missing, and where it went is knowable
```

Record a stub row in `setting/Lore.md` now. Content is written at 4d.

**A useful find does one concrete thing.** It names a place, fixes a date against
`setting/History.md`, establishes that this country was claimed, worked, or fought over,
or gives the older name of somewhere the party knows by a newer one - never several at
once.

## Constraints

- **Outdoors, lore is cut, not written.** Paper, ink and hide do not last in the open,
  so WILD lore is in stone, metal, bone, or arrangement - things made to outlast the
  people who made them, which is why they were made that way. That is a different
  register from a dungeon's ledgers and letters: monumental, public, and meant to be
  read by strangers.
