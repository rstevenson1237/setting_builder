# Wild - Faction

## Provides
What it means for a WILD Landmark to be held, worked, or controlled by a faction, rather
than simply occupied by whatever the Kind block's own occupant already describes.

Whether a faction is even possible here depends on the Kind already drawn, which is why
the four rates live in the four kind files rather than one line up a level. Distinct
from `wild/Creature.md`, which covers what lives here on its own account; a faction's
presence implies purpose and consequence reaching beyond this one Landmark.

## Spec

```
FACTION PRESENCE
  1     Which faction, and what this Landmark is for - a position, a route, a resource, a
        watch
  1     Something visible that identifies them without naming them - repeated on
        gear, work, or markers, rather than stated
  1     How far its claim actually reaches from this Landmark, and how that reach
        is patrolled or enforced - a set path, a toll, a guard, or a signal back
        to a position held more securely elsewhere
  40%   A standing order or purpose the party can read or overhear
  30%   Friction - with another faction, with the country itself, or within the group
  20%   Someone here who would rather be somewhere else
```

**The difference from a Lair is deliberateness and range.** What lives in a Lair stays
because it must; a faction is here because it chose to be, for reasons that reach beyond
this one Landmark. A Lair's territory is simply where its occupant ranges to survive; a
faction's claim is enforced - patrolled, tolled, watched, or fought for - and that
enforcement is what a party actually meets before they meet the faction itself.

**Identity is visual and repeated**, the same as anywhere else a faction appears. A party
should recognise the same power two regions apart from its marks alone, before anyone
names it.
