# Wild - Faction

## Provides
What it means for a WILD Landmark to be held, worked, or controlled by a faction: here by
choice, for reasons reaching past this one place, with a claim that is enforced. What
lives here on its own account is `wild/Creature.md`'s.

## Spec

```
FACTION PRESENCE
  1     Which faction, and what this Landmark is for to them
                                       {a position | a route | a resource | a watch}
  1     Something visible that identifies them without naming them - its Identity from
        setting/Factions.md, repeated on gear, work or markers
  1     How far its claim reaches from this Landmark, and how that reach is enforced -
        what a party meets before it meets the faction
                        {a set path | a toll | a guard | a signal back to a surer post}
  40%   A standing order or purpose the party can read or overhear
  30%   Friction                    {with another faction | with the country | within}
  20%   Someone here who would rather be somewhere else
```
