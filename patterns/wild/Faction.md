# Wild - Faction

## Provides
What it means for a WILD Landmark to be held, worked, or controlled by a faction. Whether a
faction is possible here depends on the Kind already drawn, which is why the four rates live
in the four kind files. Distinct from `wild/Creature.md`, what lives here on its own account.

## Spec

```
FACTION PRESENCE
  1     Which faction, and what this Landmark is for   (genre: faction-positions)
  1     Something visible that identifies them without naming them
                                                       (genre: faction-signs)
  1     How far its claim reaches, and how that reach is patrolled or enforced
                                                       (genre: faction-enforcement)
  40%   A standing order or purpose the party can read or overhear
  30%   Friction - with another faction, with the country itself, or within the group
                                                       (genre: faction-friction)
  20%   Someone here who would rather be somewhere else
```

**The difference from a Lair is deliberateness and range.** What lives in a Lair stays
because it must; a faction chose to be here for reasons reaching beyond this one Landmark.
A Lair's territory is where its occupant ranges to survive; a faction's claim is enforced,
and that enforcement is what a party meets before they meet the faction.

## Constraints

- **A claim with no enforcement is a colour, not a presence.** What makes this a faction
  Landmark rather than a decorated one is that something happens to whoever crosses it.
