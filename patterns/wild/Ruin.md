# Wild - Ruin

## Provides
What a built-or-once-built Landmark is, and what its condition says about who left it:
anything made by hands and no longer used as made, including things still standing and
occupied by somebody other than their builders.

## Spec

```
KIND: RUIN
  1     What it was, and roughly when
                        {defensive | working | living | sacred | burial | passage}
  1     What state it is in now, and what did that to it - the storytelling is in the
        cause, not in the word ruined
  1     What holds it now, if anything   {nothing | beasts | a band sheltering |
                                          its builders, no longer living}
  40%   A second occupancy between the builders and now
  30%   Something the builders did that nobody does any more
  25%   What holds it is a faction from setting/Factions.md, using it as a position
                                                              (wild/Faction.md)
```
