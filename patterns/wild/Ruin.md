# Wild - Ruin

## Decides
What a built-or-once-built Landmark is, and what its condition says about who left it.

## Read at
Step 4c, when a classification file draws a kind and the kind is a ruin. A Ruin is
anything made by hands and no longer used as made - including things still standing and
still occupied by somebody other than their builders.

## Spec

```
RUIN
  1     What it was, and roughly when
  1     What state it is in now, and what did that to it
  1     Whether anything holds it now
  40%   A second occupancy between the builders and now
  30%   Something the builders did that nobody does any more
  20%   A name in an older tongue, per wild/Naming.md
```

**Condition is the storytelling.** A tower thrown down is a different story from a tower
abandoned, which is different from a tower still roofed and empty. State what happened to
it, not just that it is ruined.

## Patterns

**Defensive** - a watchtower on a height; a signal or beacon station; a wall running to
nowhere, and the gap in it; a gatehouse without its gate; a hillfort's ditch and bank; a
toll tower at a crossing; a bastion built against something that came from the wrong
direction.

**Working** - a mine head with its timber still in; a quarry face and its spoil; a kiln or
charcoal hearth; a mill and its race; a saltern; a smithy with its anvil bed; a
tannery, marked by what will not grow near it; a fishing weir.

**Living** - a farmstead burned or emptied; a longhouse fallen in on itself; a shepherd's
bothy still weathertight; a hamlet reduced to its foundations and its well; a hall with
its roof gone and its floor still swept by wind.

**Sacred** - a shrine at a crossing; a temple with its roof gone and its columns standing;
an altar in the open with nothing around it; a hermit's cell; a place of judgement or
oath-taking; something defaced deliberately and recently.

**Buried** - a barrow, opened or not; a grave field marked with stones; a crypt entrance
in a hillside; an ossuary; a single grave somewhere no one would bury anyone.

**Passage** - a bridge, whole or fallen; a ford paved by hands; a causeway across wet
ground; a road with its surface still under the turf; a stair cut into rock; a milestone
or boundary marker in a tongue nobody reads; an aqueduct's stony legs.

**What holds it now** - nothing, and that is worth noting; an animal; a small band using
it as shelter; a faction using it as a position, per `wild/Faction` guidance in
`patterns/dangerous/Faction.md`; the original occupants, in a form that no longer counts
as living; somebody keeping it up for reasons of their own.
