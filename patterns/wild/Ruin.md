# Wild - Ruin

## Provides
What a built-or-once-built Landmark is, and what its condition says about who left it.

A Ruin is anything made by hands and no longer used as made, including things still
standing and still occupied by somebody other than their builders.

## Spec

```
KIND: RUIN
  1     What it was, and roughly when - defensive, working, living, sacred,
        buried, or a passage
  1     What state it is in now, and what did that to it
  1     Whether anything holds it now - nothing, and that is worth noting; an
        animal; a small band sheltering; a faction using it as a position; or the
        original occupants, in a form that no longer counts as living
  40%   A second occupancy between the builders and now
  30%   Something the builders did that nobody does any more
  25%   What holds it is one of the three factions, using it as a position
                                                              (wild/Faction.md)
```

**Ruin condition is the storytelling.** A tower thrown down is a different story from a
tower abandoned, which is different from a tower still roofed and empty. State what
happened to it, not just that it is ruined.
