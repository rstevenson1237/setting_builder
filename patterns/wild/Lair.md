# Wild - Lair

## Provides
What an occupied Landmark is, and what its occupancy implies about the country around it. A
Lair is a place something currently lives, whether it built the place or moved in.

## Spec

```
KIND: LAIR
  1     What kind of holding it is                      (genre: lairs)
  1     What lives here, from setting/Bestiary.md
  1     The territory it claims, and how far that reaches
  1     A sign of it readable before the lair itself is reached  (genre: lair-signs)
  1     What it eats, and where that comes from         (genre: lair-diet)
  40%   Young, stores, or dependants - a reason it cannot simply leave
                                                        (genre: lair-anchors)
  30%   Absent when the party arrives, and elsewhere in the region
  30%   A child location: the den proper, the midden, the larder, the killing ground
  20%   What holds it is people, and they answer to one of the three factions
                                        (genre: lairs-peopled)  (wild/Faction.md)
```

**A Lair is the tier's natural place to carry depth.** A thing that lives somewhere lives
across several spaces, so complexity comes from attaching children rather than from writing
the parent heavier.

**What it eats is the leverage a party gets**, and territory is what makes a lair matter to
the region: a den nobody can find is a room; a den whose owner ranges three landmarks in
every direction is a fact about the whole region.

## Constraints

- **A lair with nothing keeping it here is a wandering monster with an address.** The
  anchor - young, a store, water, something it guards, something that put it here - is what
  makes the place worth returning to rather than a creature that has already left.
