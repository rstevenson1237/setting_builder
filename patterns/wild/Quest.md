# Wild - Quest

## Decides
How a WILD location participates in a Quest. WILD **carries**: it is where quests are
travelled through, obstructed, and occasionally offered by somebody living rough.

## Read at
Step 4c. Criteria are in `patterns/setting/Quests.md`; the registry is `setting/Quests.md`.

## Spec

```
QUEST - carrying end
  1     Its role   {waypoint | obstacle | supply | giver}
  1     A stub row or an addition to an existing row in setting/Quests.md
  30%   Evidence of somebody else on the same errand, ahead or behind
```

**Obstacle is the role that matters.** A quest with two ends is a delivery; what stands in
the way is what makes it an adventure, and in a point crawl the way is WILD country. A
DANGEROUS target states the obstacle at the target; a WILD location states the obstacle
*en route* - the crossing that is out, the country that must be gone around, the thing
that holds the only path.

**Givers out here are unusual and need a reason to be out here.** A hermit, a trapper, a
survey party, an outlaw, somebody hiding, somebody who cannot go home. Their ask is
smaller than a settlement's and more personal, and they rarely have coin.

## Patterns

**Waypoint** - the last shelter before the target; the place a guide will take a party to
and no further; where the trail is picked up or lost; where the giver said to turn; the
landmark named in a set of directions that is otherwise useless.

**Obstacle** - a crossing gone or guarded; country that cannot be carried through; a
territory whose owner will not be talked past; a season or tide that closes the route; a
toll, in coin or otherwise; the only person who knows the way, and what they want.

**Supply** - a WILD location can register supply the same way a dungeon does: a plant that
grows only here, an animal that must be taken alive, water from one spring, a stone from
one face, a sighting that must be made from one vantage.

**What a rough giver wants** - a body brought back or buried; a message carried to
somebody they cannot approach; an animal killed that is ruining them; something retrieved
that they left behind when they ran; company as far as somewhere; word of whether a place
still stands.

**Somebody else on the errand** - a camp a day old; a rope left fixed; a marker cut and
then cut again by a different hand; a body with the right gear; a party the group can
actually meet, and race, or join.
