# Wild - Key

## Provides
When a WILD location holds an object whose real function is opening something elsewhere. A
key found out here is a find, reached through the reward block; the lock belongs to
whichever location holds it. Criteria are in `patterns/setting/Keys.md`.

## Spec

```
KEY
  1     The object, and what it physically is           (genre: key-forms)
  1     What it opens, named by location code and feature
  1     How it came to be out here rather than with its lock
                                                (genre: key-provenance-outdoors)
  1     Where it sits                                   (genre: key-sites-outdoors)
  30%   A clue connecting object to lock                (genre: key-clues)
  20%   Somebody else knows it is here
```

**WILD is where a key gets separated from its lock**, and the distance between the two is
what makes a point crawl a network. A key found in open country points a party at a dungeon
they may not have heard of yet.

## Constraints

- **A key out here is not buried at random.** Somebody carried it and did not arrive, hid
  it at a distance on purpose, or was buried with it. A key with no account of how it got
  here is a dungeon cache dropped in a field.
