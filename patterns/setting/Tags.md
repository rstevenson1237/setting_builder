# Setting - Tags

## Provides
The flat, genre-derived thematic pool every `Setting.md` and Region Overview points to
with a single tag-line instead of embedding tags inline, and the two-tag draw (one
setting-level, one region-level) every location gazetteer stub uses instead of inventing
three tags fresh.

## Spec

```
TAGS
  25    Tags, each one or two words, each with a one-line gloss
  1     Every tag traces to the chosen reference's own concrete iconography, or a Q2
        answer that mattered - the same intentionality test patterns/setting/Genre.md's
        own tag bank already uses
  1     Flat - no rating split. Rating-specific texture is the genre pack's lists, not the
        tag pool's
```

**What makes a good tag** - it represents a theme, compressing something already true of the
chosen reference into a word or two rather than introducing a new idea; it acts as an index,
scannable and findable again by a step that does not know it is there; and it constrains the
material, ruling something out.

**Where the gloss earns its place** - specific and mechanically or texturally concrete
("half-flooded, walkable only at low water"), never decorative ("watery").

**A region's own corner of the pool** is what is true of this region specifically and not of
the setting generally: a local custom, a specific hazard, a texture of speech or trade.

## Constraints

- **A tag is pure seed, never structural.** It never selects which pattern file governs a
  location - that is Kind's job - and it never carries its own inclusion math. Rating-specific
  texture belongs to the genre pack's lists, not to the tag pool.

- **Region-level pools add, they do not restate.** A region's own 25 should feel like a
  corner of the setting-level 25, narrower and textured to this place. Read the
  setting-level pool first specifically to avoid this.
