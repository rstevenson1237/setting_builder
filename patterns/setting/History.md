# Setting - History

## Provides
One event in the setting's past: what happened, when, and the mark it left that a party
can find now. How many events and how they spread across time is `templates/History.md`'s.

## Spec

```
EVENT
  1     Kind                                                              {KIND}
  1     When - years before now, vaguer the older it is, unless setting/Truths.md fixes a
        calendar
  1     What happened, in one or two sentences - the event stated, never narrated
  1     Whether it is inside living memory, and if so, how the accounts of it differ
  1     Whether it is still resolving - what of it is arriving now
  1     Left - the physical mark findable now, and the Location Code(s) holding it
```

```
KIND - exactly one
  founding     - a place or power established, or re-established after the first ended
  withdrawal   - whoever held somewhere left it
  arrival      - a people, power or thing came
  discovery    - something found, and what finding it cost
  collapse     - something fell, by nature or by hand
  war          - powers fought, and how it settled
  blight       - plague, famine or flood
  betrayal     - a trust broken that is still cited
  boundary     - a line drawn that people still keep
  working      - a sorcery done, or used up
  sealing      - a thing shut away, by someone
```

## Constraints

- **An event is a fact of the past, not a clock aimed at the party.** It explains why the
  setting is shaped as it is; it schedules nothing, and no entry reads as act one of
  something the party is expected to finish.

- **Never narrate.** Every sentence past the event itself is context the referee spends
  and the players never see; an event that needs more than two sentences needs a
  location instead.
