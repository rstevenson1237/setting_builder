# Setting - History

## Provides
What events shaped the setting, how far back they run, and what physical marks they left
for a party to find.

## Spec

```
HISTORY
  3-7   Events, oldest to newest
  1     At least one event outside living memory, whose evidence is physical only
  1     At least one within living memory, with people who disagree about it
  1     At least one that is still resolving
  1     Each stated in one or two sentences - state the event, do not narrate it
  1     Each carries a Left line: the mark findable now, and the Location Code(s)
```

**Every event leaves a mark or it did not happen.** The players never meet the event; they
meet the wall. Left lines are completed at 5c and every event is re-tested then: one no
location cashed out is given a mark at a real location or removed from the file.

**Depth layering.** The strongest histories put three occupancies over one place - who built
it, who took it, who is squatting in it now. Every DANGEROUS region wants this, and it is
authored here rather than invented per region.

**Dating.** Count backward in years from now, unless `setting/Truths.md` establishes a
calendar. Older events get vaguer numbers, which is itself information.

**Disagreement.** For events inside living memory, state that accounts differ and roughly
how; `setting/Rumours.md` exploits the gap.

## Constraints

- **Length is the failure mode here.** Two sentences and a Left line. If the event needs
  more, what it actually needs is a location.

- **An event is a fact of the past, not a ticking clock aimed at the party.** It does not
  schedule anything, and no entry should read as act one of something the party is expected
  to finish.
