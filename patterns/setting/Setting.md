# Setting - Setting

## Provides
The setting's name, its tag line, and the referee-facing outline every other artifact
builds on.

## Spec

```
SETTING
  1     A name, per GENRE.md's Naming answer
  1     A tag line - what the setting is, one step past its name, in a phrase a
        referee could say aloud to start a campaign
  1     What the party is doing here, in a clause
  1     A pressure - something already going wrong, which is not aimed at the party
  1     Something the setting has that a generic setting of its genre does not
```

## Constraints

- **Never a tag line that fits another setting of its genre.** A line true of any
  frontier keep has defined nothing past the name.
