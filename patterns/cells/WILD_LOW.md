# WILD_LOW

> Dissolved into procedure rather than keyed.

A `WILD_LOW` location is a stretch of route, not a room. Its content is a rate,
a cue and a decision about the road, and most of what happens on it comes from
the region's Encounter table, its Weather table and the procedures in `T-PRC`.
The keyed entry exists so that the stretch has a name, a cost and a place on the
diagram. It is the connective ground between landmarks and it is written to be
crossed.

## What belongs here

- **A rate, on the route and off it.** A `WILD` region counts in yards and miles
  and in watches, six to a day. State what the route costs a watch, what leaving
  it costs a watch, and whether pace lost here can be made up by pushing. The
  rate is the location's primary content, not its footnote.

- **A non-visual approach cue.** Every location in a `WILD` region carries one,
  at a distance the party can still turn back from. Here it is usually the cue
  for the next thing rather than for this stretch: sound, smell, the ground
  underfoot, the wind changing. Say how far out it is, and say what turning back
  at that point costs, which on connective ground is usually only the watches
  already spent.

- **One decision, with both branches costed.** Cross here or walk round. Follow
  the road down or stay high. Neither branch is the mistake, and the text never
  says which is. Where one branch is cheaper in time, its cost is stated as what
  the party arrives without.

- **What the stretch does to the region's tables.** This is the cell's real
  lever. Name which Encounter rows are likelier here, which are impossible, and
  what the weather does to the rate. A stretch that shifts the procedure has
  earned its file without a single keyed feature.

- **One thing to look at, at most.** A bare frame, a milestone, a cut bank, a
  stand of something that has been kept cut. It is a landmark in the plain sense
  — it tells a party where they are — and it holds at most one hidden thing.

- **Where the route can be left.** State whether there is firm ground off the
  route and what happens when there is not. `None` is a legal answer with a
  reason after it, and on most connective ground it is the true one.

## Where the boundary falls

- **Up to `WILD_MEDIUM`** when something here changes how the route is walked
  rather than how fast: a ford that has to be read, a bend where the road is
  overlooked, a span that is under water at one hour and not another. A rate is
  `LOW`. A rate plus a thing to solve is `MEDIUM`.

- **Up to `WILD_HIGH`** when there is a reason to leave the route that is worth
  the watches. That is an arrival, and it is a landmark.

- **Not a location at all** when the stretch has no rate of its own, no cue and
  no decision. Then it is a line in the region's Fields and an edge in
  `connections.md`, and no file is written. `LOW` is thin, and thin is not
  failure, but a `LOW` location still costs a party something to cross.

## Form

- **Player Overview:** three to six sentences, and it is written for a party
  moving. What is ahead, what is underfoot, what is off to the sides. Bold one
  or two nouns at most.
- **Features:** one or two. Often one, and often it is the thing that names the
  stretch.
- **Referee Overview:** the rates first, in yards and miles a watch. Then the
  approach cue and its distance. Then the decision and both prices. Then the
  table effects. A referee should be able to run this location from the Referee
  Overview alone and never open the Features.
- **Exits:** two, usually, because a stretch of route runs between two things.
  Every cue is what a party notices from here, at a stated distance.
- **Tables it leans on:** `T-PRC` for the crossing or the walk itself, `T-HAZ`
  for what the ground does to a party that hurries, `T-ARC` for the empire's
  road under the mud, `T-BES` only through the region's Encounter table.

## Worked example

An illustration of the shape and the register. Nothing in it is canon, and the
codes are stand-ins.

**Player Overview.** For a mile and a half the road runs along the top of a
bank, and the bank is the road: cut stone under a foot of turf, with open ground
falling away on both sides. **A milestone** stands at the near end, face down in
the grass, and the ground has been trodden round it in a way the rest of the
bank has not. Further on, the turf is worn through to stone in a line down the
outward side, where something heavy has been dragged rather than carried.

**Referee Overview.** *Rate on the bank:* three miles a watch, walking, and it
holds in any weather. *Rate off it:* half a mile a watch through cut ground that
takes a boot to the ankle, and the pace does not come back by pushing. *Approach
cue:* a mile before the far end the sound of the party's own steps changes from
turf to stone underfoot, and stays stone. Turning back at that point costs the
two watches already spent and nothing else. *Decision:* the bank is the fast
road and it is visible from everything for two miles in both directions. The
drag line goes down the outward side to lower ground that is slow, wet and not
overlooked. *Table effects:* the Encounter row for anything working the open
ground cannot happen on the bank, because there is nowhere on it to be unseen.
Fog halves the rate on the bank and does not change the rate below it.

## Excluded patterns

- **A keyed room in a field.** This is a stretch. If it has an inside, it is a
  different cell.
- **A random encounter written into the entry.** Encounters are the region's
  table, rolled at the table. What belongs here is what this ground does to that
  table.
- **A secret that a party crossing at pace would have to stop to find.** The
  cell is written to be crossed. Put the secret where somebody has a reason to
  stand still.
- **Scenery with no cost.** Three sentences of landscape that change no rate and
  offer no decision is a line in the region's Overview.
- **Padding toward the neighbours.** `LOW` stretches are what make the landmarks
  read as landmarks. A region where every mile is interesting has no distance in
  it.
- **A conclusion stated for the party.** The road is exposed. Say what can see
  it and from how far. Never say that taking it is a risk.
