# Creature Patterns

Guidance for which `setting/Bestiary.md` entries fit a location, picked at generation time in `templates/Location.md` whenever the chosen location pattern calls for a creature (e.g. "a creature performing some task," "a major creature or boss-level threat"). This file guides *which entry*, not how it's cited - a creature is simply named directly in its Feature line, referencing its Bestiary entry.

- **SAFE landmarks** - Man only, and rarely armed for trouble; a creature here is going about routine business, not posing a threat.
- **WILD landmarks** - Man, Beast, or Humanoid roughly at or below the region's die size (AD close to or under the die); territorial, opportunistic, not a boss-tier threat.
- **DANGEROUS, low weight** - a creature is optional and, if present, passing or avoidable - AD well under the region's die. Most low-weight rooms have none.
- **DANGEROUS, medium weight** - one creature scaled near the region's die, performing a task or guarding something - this is the room's one reactive element, not one of several.
- **DANGEROUS, high weight** - the location's set-piece threat: AD scaled above the region's die, MA where the Bestiary entry has it, chosen for how directly it ties to the region's core theme.

If no existing Bestiary entry fits, describe the creature inline within the location's Feature instead of inventing a new reusable entry - the Bestiary holds only templates meant to recur.
