# Wild - Hidden Patterns

Thematic patterns for hidden-tier locations within WILD regions - roughly a third of a region's locations, existing to flesh out a Landmark rather than to stand alone. A Hidden location isn't found by roaming the region generally; it's found by actually investigating a specific Landmark and noticing the visible detail that connects onward to it (behind the waterfall, past the second bend in the trail, through the collapsed section of wall). Because it's visible rather than trigger-gated, the connection is a mundane Exit on the Landmark's own location file, not a Feature requiring a trigger - but it should read as something only found by being there and looking, not something obvious from a distance or listed among the region's other Landmarks.

- Extension of a Site's interior or grounds not visible from outside it - a second chamber, an inner court, a cellar.
- A pocket a Connection passes near but doesn't run through - a side channel, a hollow just off the trail, a ledge below a ridge line.
- The far or hidden side of a Natural Feature - behind a waterfall, inside a cliff face, within a dense copse.
- A resource or vantage the Landmark's own Feature already hinted at, once actually reached.
- A smaller camp, den, or cache belonging to whatever already occupies the parent Landmark.

Every Hidden location still follows `templates/Location.md` in full - its own Player Summary, Referee Notes, Features, and Exits - it's simply reached through, and thematically bound to, its parent Landmark rather than the open region.
