# Wild - Landmark Patterns

Thematic patterns for landmark-tier locations within WILD regions - the majority of a region's locations (roughly half or more), discoverable through open exploration anywhere in the region rather than by being led to them from elsewhere. Per `patterns/Dressing.md`'s Position guidance, every Landmark states roughly where within the region it sits - a cardinal direction, or a bearing/distance from another named Landmark or the region's entry point - so a party roaming the region can actually be placed relative to it.

A Landmark is one of three kinds - pick the one that fits before picking a bullet below:
- **Site** - a built or once-built place with a clear boundary: ruins, a lair, a fortification, a temple, a tomb.
- **Connection** - a route or channel that carries movement through the region: a road, a trail, a river, a ridge line.
- **Natural Feature** - an unbuilt feature of the land itself: a cliff, a waterfall, a mountain peak, a copse of trees - occasionally carrying a mysterious or subtly magical nature per GENRE.md's Low Magic.

## Site patterns
- Watched, held, or patrolled by a hostile faction.
- Bearing signs of a past event, ruin, or abandonment.
- Home to a beast, or marking the edge of its territory.
- Offering a vantage point, shelter, or defensible position.

## Connection patterns
- Serving as a natural waypoint or navigational reference.
- Presenting an environmental hazard or difficult terrain.
- Marking the transition toward a nearby DANGEROUS region.

## Natural Feature patterns
- Hiding a resource, foraging opportunity, or useful find.
- Carrying a mysterious or subtly magical quality - priced or dangerous to use, per GENRE.md.
- Home to a beast, or marking the edge of its territory.

Whichever kind and bullet is chosen, a Landmark may also carry a visible detail that leads onward to a Hidden-tier location (see `patterns/Wild_Hidden.md`) - state what's noticeable about it that a party actually investigating the Landmark, not just passing through the region, would find.
