# Wild - Secret Patterns

Thematic patterns for secret-tier locations within WILD regions - the smallest share of a region's locations (usually under a fifth), reached only through a trigger. Unlike a Hidden location's visible connection, a Secret location's connection is concealed until a Feature at its parent Landmark or Hidden location is investigated and acted on correctly - the same Clue/Trigger/Payload structure `patterns/Secrets.md` already formalizes, except the Payload here is always a secret Exit to this whole location, not a detail nested within the parent's own Features. State the Clue (an already-visible detail worth a second look), the Trigger (the specific action that acts on it), and the connection it reveals, within a Feature at the parent location; mark the connecting edge hidden (`-.-`) in the region's `Connections.mmd`.

A Secret location exists for one of these reasons - pick the one that fits:
- A reward area: a cache or hoard cited from a `patterns/Treasure.md` table, reached only this way.
- Special information: a piece of Lore, a Rumour confirmed or overturned, or a Truth made concrete.
- An alternate route: a way around a Landmark's danger, into a neighboring region, or toward a DANGEROUS region's entrance that bypasses its obvious approach.

Every Secret location still follows `templates/Location.md` in full - its own Player Summary, Referee Notes, Features, and Exits - it's simply unreachable until its parent's Trigger is found and acted on.
