# Wild Patterns

Thematic patterns for locations within WILD regions - major landmarks within untamed or lightly-traveled wilderness.

- Landmark serving as a natural waypoint or navigational reference.
- Landmark that is home to a beast or marks the edge of its territory.
- Landmark watched, held, or patrolled by a hostile faction.
- Landmark presenting an environmental hazard or difficult terrain.
- Landmark hiding a resource, foraging opportunity, or useful find.
- Landmark marking the transition toward a nearby DANGEROUS region.
- Landmark bearing signs of a past event, ruin, or abandonment.
- Landmark offering a vantage point, shelter, or defensible position.
