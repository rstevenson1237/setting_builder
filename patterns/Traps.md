# Trap Patterns

Reusable trap mechanisms, picked at generation time in `templates/Location.md` whenever the chosen location pattern calls for a trap or hazard. Traps aren't scaled by location weight the way creatures and treasure are - a low-weight room can carry a lethal trap precisely because nothing else is guarding it - but every trap picked should carry one of the three impact tiers below, and a single location shouldn't lean on the same tier twice.

## Resolution

- **Nuisance** - no lasting harm: an inconvenience, delay, or complication. Resolve narratively; no test required.
- **Damaging** - ordinary damage to whoever triggers it, resolved like any other hit; no test required.
- **Lethal** - severe enough to demand a test:
  - **TEST OF CONSTITUTION** - one character is exposed to lethal harm (they triggered it, or they're the one it's aimed at).
  - **TEST OF FATE** - the whole party is exposed at once (an area effect); roll to see who among those in the trigger area is actually caught, then resolve each of them.
  - Failure means a **WOUND** (a lasting injury) or a freeform **CONDITION** (poisoned, bloodied, blinded, and the like) - pick whichever fits the trap's fiction.

## Patterns

- Deadfall or collapsing section of floor/ceiling (Lethal, single target - TEST OF CONSTITUTION or WOUND).
- Poisoned dart, needle, or thorn (Lethal, single target - TEST OF CONSTITUTION or CONDITION: poisoned).
- Swinging blade, spear trap, or scythe (Lethal, single target - TEST OF CONSTITUTION or WOUND).
- Flooding chamber or rising water (Lethal, party-wide - TEST OF FATE).
- Choking gas, spores, or dust (Lethal, party-wide - TEST OF FATE, CONDITION: blinded or poisoned).
- Rockslide or cave-in (Lethal, party-wide - TEST OF FATE).
- Shallow pit or covered hole (Damaging - ordinary fall damage).
- Misfiring or long-rusted mechanism (Damaging or Nuisance, depending on what's left of it).
- Alarm - bell, chime, or released creature that alerts something elsewhere (Nuisance).
- Snare, net, or glue trap (Nuisance - restrained, no damage).
- Marking trap - dye, scent, or residue that draws unwanted attention later (Nuisance).
- Trap that destroys or fouls an item or resource rather than harming a person (Nuisance).
