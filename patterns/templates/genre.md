# Genre

<Premise in a paragraph. What the world is, and the one situation true
everywhere in it. No history, no factions, no named places: the setting is not
named here, and it takes its name at step 1.>

## Register

<How the prose sounds. What it states plainly and what it withholds. The units
it counts in. Two or three sentences and one worked line of each register that
carries across every region.>

## Play

<The kind of play this setting supports at the table: what a party spends its
sessions doing, what it is rewarded for, and what it is punished for. Written
as a statement of play, never as a plot.>

## Constants

<The least common denominators. Three to six of them, each one sentence, each
true in every region of the setting. Anything true of one region belongs in
that region and not here.>

## Absences

<What never appears anywhere in this setting, and one line on what stands in
its place. An absence a writer can check against is worth more than a
preference.>

## Naming

<How a name sounds and what a name is built from. Defer every actual root to
`T-LNG`, which is the root registry; name the shape here, not the vocabulary.>

## Influences

<Named outside works, as a statement of the kind of play this setting supports.
This is the only file permitted to name them. Each entry says what is taken:
one clause, naming a quality rather than a scene, a character or a plot. No
generated content imitates a named work.>
