# Wild - Ruin

## Provides
What a built-or-once-built Landmark is, and what its condition says about who left it:
anything made by hands and no longer used as made, including things still standing and
occupied by somebody other than their builders.

## Spec

```
KIND: RUIN
  1     Was - what it was     {defensive | working | living | sacred | burial | passage}
  1     When - roughly when it was built
  1     State - what state it is in now, and what did that to it: the storytelling is in
        the cause, not in the word ruined
  1     Holds - what holds it now, if anything   {nothing | beasts | a band sheltering |
                                                  its builders, no longer living}
  40%   Second occupancy - one between the builders and now
  30%   Lost practice - something the builders did that nobody does any more
  25%   Faction - what holds it is a faction from setting/Factions.md, using it as a
        position                                                  (wild/Faction.md)
```
