Quests of Fellward

The Missing Dispatch - given at A.1 The Granary, sought at A.3 The Barracks Block
- Wants: Corlan wants proof the original relief-column request ever left Fellward, before his skimming is traced back to him by name.
- Object: The original dispatch, filed among old company effects in footlockers in A.3's shuttered half - findable only by real searching time, not a roll.
- Obstacle: Sergeant Wrey won't let anyone through the shuttered half's locked door without a reason he personally approves.
- Terms: "Find it, and I'll owe you a season of looking the other way on what I skim."

The Trapper's Partner - given at B.6 The Trapper's Cache, evidence at B.8 The Scouted Barrow Mouth, resolved at C.18 The Rubble Choke
- Wants: The Trapper Clans want word of a partner who followed a stray trapline too far past the wallows and never came back - ground too far into Ingaraz for them to risk searching themselves.
- Object: The trapper's remains, buried under an old rockfall at C.18 The Rubble Choke - found only by real digging time, not a roll.
- Obstacle: The trail runs cold past B.8's barrow mouth, where the trapper's own half-buried knife is the only sign they came this far; past it lies the length of Ingaraz itself.
- Terms: "Bring me word of what happened to them - alive or dead - and I'll trade you everything we know about safe ground through here."
