Quests of Carrdun

The Gang's Last Contract - given at A.1, resolved at D.17
- Wants: Commander Cadel wants proof of what happened to the work-gang that vanished from
  a sealed section two winters ago - he can't risk the crown hearing a second vanishing
  went uninvestigated, and can't be seen going himself without questions he'd rather not
  answer.
- Object: the crew's own gear and tally page, still lying where they were dropped at the
  Sealed Gallery (D.17).
- Obstacle: two Coil-Marked tend the glyph-ring there and won't leave it undefended;
  reading the ring's Xoss glyphs through to the end draws worse than either of them.
- Terms: "Bring me back something of theirs and a straight account of what took them.
  Thirty cn now, seventy more if I believe you - and don't read anything out loud down
  there."

Fenna's Grandson - given at C.2, resolved at D.15
- Wants: Fenna's grandson followed dig-crew talk of a shortcut past the causeway three
  days ago and hasn't come back; she can't go herself - the platforms need an elder, and
  marsh-kin don't travel far over dry ground.
- Object: his trade-sack, dropped in the Rubble Chamber (D.15) where the Coil's hidden
  crawlspace took him and didn't let him back out.
- Obstacle: the crawlspace itself is easy to miss, and the Coil polices anyone who finds
  it - a second discovery in one season is exactly what they can't afford to ignore.
- Terms: "Bring back anything that was his, and I'll tell you where the current takes what
  it doesn't return. Iron if you want it, or the truth, whichever you'd rather carry."
