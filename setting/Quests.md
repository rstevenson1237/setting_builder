Quests of Telar

The Ford Kessin Won't Name - given at A.1, resolved at B.1
- Wants: Toll-master Kessin wants to know whether Brekar can be dealt with - bought off, bribed into easing the toll, anything that closes the gap in his ledger - without the crown ever hearing that its own toll-house treated with the band holding the ford. He cannot go himself: leaving his post to bargain with the people the crown expects him to be fighting is the kind of thing that ends with him losing the post rather than saving it.
- Object: at B.1 Sunk Bridge Ford, whether the Watch Rider's own superiors - not the rider himself, who only takes tolls - would take a deal to ease crown traffic through quietly.
- Obstacle: the Watch Rider deals in coin and passage only; reaching whoever actually decides for Brekar means either pushing past the toll camp's patience or finding another way in. Per setting/Factions.md, Brekar's answer to being crossed is to take everything from the next caravan belonging to whoever crossed them - so a clumsy approach costs somebody else, not the party, which is exactly the kind of consequence Kessin never mentions asking for.
- Terms: "Six crown-marks now, six more and a season's free crossing if you bring me anything I can use - and not one word to Captain Dessek about where it came from."

An Offer Velar Can't Send - given at C.2, resolved at B.1
- Wants: a remaining scion of one of Velar's two struggling trading families wants a private letter carried to Brekar's own camp, proposing to deal with them directly and cut the Nine Wagons out of the detour trade entirely. She cannot go herself: the Wagons' factor watches everyone leaving Warehouse Row who isn't obviously flying the company's colors, and being seen treating with Brekar at all would finish what little standing her family has left in Velar.
- Object: at B.1 Sunk Bridge Ford, getting the letter into Brekar's own hand rather than a rider's - the Watch Rider takes tolls and passage, not messages, and won't carry it further on his own initiative.
- Obstacle: per setting/Factions.md, Brekar's own tactics favor a fast, soft target over a negotiation, and its answer to being noticed is to raise the toll as a message rather than open a dialogue - meaning even a successful delivery may cost the family more than it gains, and there is no guarantee Brekar wants this deal at all.
- Terms: "Get this into Brekar's own hand, not a rider's, and however this shakes out, my family remembers who did us the one favor Velar wouldn't."
