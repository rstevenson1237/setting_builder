Tags of Greywatch

- **Bailey Line** - the keep's outer yard trades and lodges anyone who arrives; the inner yard past it answers to the garrison alone.
- **Frontier Levy** - the realm's law holds inside the keep's walls and along the one road back to it, and nowhere past either.
- **Tribal Warren** - each cave-dwelling people holds a stretch of the ravine as its own ground, and answers a stranger crossing it as a trespasser first.
- **Rival Chaos** - the tribes serve no single banner; two of them sharing a raid or a wall is a bargain struck, not a standing order.
- **Shrine Above** - a priesthood at the ravine's head calls the tribes toward one purpose, and keeps its own rites out of any tribe's sight.
- **Planted Agent** - someone already inside the keep answers to the shrine, and passes out what the garrison would rather keep in.
- **Watched Descent** - the one path down into the ravine is watched from both sides, by whoever holds the ground it passes and by whoever's hunting them.
- **Old Digging** - the ravine's tunnels were cut and shaped before any tribe now living moved in, and the shaping still outlines the rock the tribes just occupy.
- **Fen Barrow** - a mound raised in wet ground marks something buried before the keep stood, and whatever was set to watch it hasn't stopped.
- **Lone Squatter** - someone lives past the road's edge alone, in a shack with no other roof in sight, and trades what the wild gives up for what the keep can spare.
- **Road Raiders** - a band works the road itself instead of holding any ground, and is gone before the garrison can answer a rider's warning.
- **Formless Thing** - something in the dark takes no fixed shape a blade can find purchase on.
- **Tunnel Hunter** - something in the ravine reads the tunnels by feel or scent, not by sight, and closes on a stranger there faster than the stranger can map the way out.
- **Bound Service** - the ravine's dead don't rise on their own account; something at its head set each of them a task and hasn't released them from it.
- **Lone Beast** - something in the wild is dangerous on its own terms, answering to no tribe and no banner.
- **Spoken Side** - Law, Neutrality and Chaos are each a tongue as much as a loyalty, so what a stranger says before they're asked marks which they hold to.
- **No Market** - nothing sold in the keep outperforms a common blade; anything better than that is carried out of the wild or not had at all.
- **Short Reach** - a caster here holds one working a day at most, spent once and silent again until they've rested.
- **Killing Roll** - poison and a handful of other dangers end a character outright, with no wound track between the roll and the grave.
- **Carried Home** - what a find is worth is measured at the keep's gate, not in the room it was taken from; getting it there costs something too.
- **No Rescue** - the keep trades with anyone who walks back through its gate, and marches out for no one who doesn't.
- **New Keep** - the keep itself is freshly built and kept in repair; anything older standing nearby belongs to whoever's living in it now, not to the keep.
- **Bare Purse** - the adventurers who arrive here come with little money and nobody behind them but their own nerve.
- **Plain Naming** - almost everything a party meets here is called what it plainly is; a name that isn't plain marks something old, or something not of this keep's kind.
- **Retainer's Share** - anyone hired to carry a torch or a spear into the ravine is owed wages and a cut of what's found, the same as any other cost.
