Tags of Telar

- **Garrison** - a standing company holds one point by force, paid in coin or forage
  rights, not loyalty.
- **Tribute** - one power extracts wealth or labor from another on a fixed schedule
  somebody keeps a ledger of.
- **Slavers** - captives are a tradeable commodity; a raid's point is as often bodies as
  goods.
- **Usurers** - debt is a leash here; somebody's ledger says who owes what, and calling it
  in is a threat with teeth.
- **Caravans** - trade moves overland in armed convoys, because the road between
  settlements is not safe alone.
- **Walled City** - a settlement's wall is the edge of its law; past it, none of its writ
  reaches.
- **Decadent Court** - the people who hold power here inherited it rather than earned it,
  and everyone around them knows the difference.
- **Temple Cult** - a priesthood's authority rests on rites nobody outside it is permitted
  to witness.
- **Mercenary Guild** - loyalty here is a contract with a term and a price, renewable or
  not, never a bond.
- **Raiders** - a band takes what it wants by force and is gone before anyone can organize
  a response.
- **Nomads** - people who move with the seasons, owing no settlement their presence and
  owed nothing by it in turn.
- **Old Road** - a route built by hands better than any now living, still walkable, still
  watched by somebody.
- **Burial Mound** - the dead of a people no longer here lie under a mound that still marks
  the ground above them.
- **Standing Stones** - stones raised by unknown hands mark something - a boundary, a
  grave, a warning - that nobody now living placed there.
- **Trackless Waste** - ground with no path across it; whoever crosses makes their own way
  and answers for the choice.
- **Border Reavers** - raiding across a border is a way of life here, not a break from
  peace.
- **Serpent Cult** - a faith organized around a serpent, living or once-living, worshipped
  only where nobody is watching.
- **Black Ziggurat** - a stepped tower built for a rite too old for anyone now living to
  practice properly, but not too old to still work.
- **Drowned Temple** - a temple the water took keeps its rites underwater, dry only when
  something lets it be.
- **Elder Sorcery** - power drawn from something older than any kingdom now standing, and
  it remembers exactly what it is owed.
- **Man-Apes** - a people neither wholly human nor wholly beast lives where humans do not,
  in numbers nobody has counted.
- **Sunken City** - a city the sea or the sand took still holds, today, what it held the
  day it went under.
- **Undying Sorcerer** - somebody who should have died centuries ago still exercises
  authority through means nobody living understands.
- **Bloodstone Altar** - an altar that takes blood as payment, and grants nothing without
  it.
- **Pit of the Old Ones** - an opening into something that predates every people who has
  since built near it.
- **The Unlettered Dead** - the region's older tongue died with its last speakers, and
  whatever they wrote down is legible only as marks nobody now living can read.
