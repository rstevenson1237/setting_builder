## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Walking on it:* nothing, and there is nothing on the ground to trip over, which is the first thing here that does not sit right. *Striking a frame:* the note carries inward, down the stair, and does not carry out across the water. *Putting a light out:* nothing on the gate ground reacts, and the party is standing in ankle-deep water in the dark on level slab. *Shouting:* the sound goes out and does not come back. *Digging into the slab:* it is one course thick over made ground and it comes up, and under it is more standing water.

**Gate:** The face at the inward end, which is the region's own gate and is not this location's (R05-L02). What this ground gates is time: it is eight hundred feet of open, level, unbroken slab under standing water with nothing on it to stand behind, and it is crossed in a turn, and everything on it is visible from everything else on it.

*Job for the region.* This is the approach and the assembly ground. It is where a party decides what they are carrying down, because it is the last place they can put something down and expect to find it, and it is where every route into Bellgate meets.

*Cost in turns.* One turn to cross end to end. Two to work along the frames. None to leave, in any of four directions.

*What is true that the Overview withholds.* The ground is level because it was levelled, and it was levelled after the face was built: the slab runs up to the lip of the gate and stops, and the courses under it are cut to fit rather than laid. Somebody cleared this ground so that nothing could stand on it unseen, which is the same reasoning as the cut lanes, applied to stone.

## Features

### The Standing Water

Ankle-deep after rain, skinned over in still air with a tallow-coloured film that takes fire from a lamp and runs with the current (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-08) {TEST: Constitution} {WOUND: Fire}. The tell is the smell, a full chain upwind, and on this ground the wind comes off the fen.

It does not drain. The slab is level to within a hand across eight hundred feet, which is why.

### The Levelled Slab

Fitted, laid without mortar, each course a hand thicker than the one above (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-01). Here it is one course, over made ground, which is the only place on the Reach the Covenant laid a single course.

They were not building a road. They were building a floor to be seen across.

### The Ways Off It

The face stands at the inward end -> R05-L02. The frames stand out of the water -> R05-L03. The water deepens and stops draining to the north -> R05-L04. A cut runs east and goes down -> R05-L06. The kept lane leaves seaward -> R05-L07, the reed comes up at the seaward edge -> R05-L08, a driven post stands in the water -> R05-L09, and one stretch of slab has no water on it at all -> R05-L10.
