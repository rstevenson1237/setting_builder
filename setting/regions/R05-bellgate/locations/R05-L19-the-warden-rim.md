## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Approaching:* it speaks, once, to each person, and then never again to that person. *Speaking to it:* nothing, from then on. *Going down past it:* it acts, and it does not distinguish between going down and coming back up. *Going up past it:* it acts. *Putting the light out:* it does not need one and says so by not changing. *Drawing a peg anywhere on this rim:* it acts immediately and without the warning, because the warning is spent.

**Gate:** The warden itself, at a rim that is otherwise open. There is no door and no mechanism: the gate is a construct that gives one warning per person and then does not warn again (BESTIARY, T-BES-10). The answer that is not the warden is the pegged shaft to the north, which is sound, closed, and takes a bar and a turn to open, and which comes out at the same depth having cost the noise of opening it (R05-L15).

*Job for the region.* It is the region's one guarded crossing and it is the only thing in Bellgate that announces itself before it acts. Everything else here is construction.

*Cost in turns.* None to stand at the rim. One to work along it. Two to open the pegged shaft with a bar, loudly.

*What is true that the Overview withholds.* It is not guarding the shaft. It is standing at the rim of a shaft it was set at four generations ago, and the shaft it was set to keep people out of is the pegged one, which is thirty feet north and which nobody has opened. It has been giving its warning at the wrong hole for longer than anyone has been alive.

## Features

### The Warden

A Covenant construct, upright at the rim, of the same make as any other (BESTIARY, T-BES-10).

{AD: 4, +3} {TYPE: Construct} {WOUND: Crushing}

It gives one warning, in the Covenant's layer, in a voice pitched to carry down a shaft: *Stop at the sill.* Each person hears it once. Then it stops speaking to that person, permanently, and it does not repeat itself for a party that leaves and comes back.

Its feet are not on the rim it is guarding. They are thirty feet south of it, on stone, in two shallow depressions it has worn.

### The Rim

Cut square, pegged, the pegs seated, with the order cut round it in letters a hand deep and cut in haste (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-05), (LORE, T-LOR-05).

This is the copy: the last word is in a different hand from the rest, which is true of every rim in the Reach except one.

### The Slate

Lying in the lee of the rim where a watch-keeper would have kept it, face up: a slate copy of the order, carried by whoever was set to keep the watch (MAGICAL TOMES, T-TOM-03).

{VALUE: 6000 cn} {WT: 2} {QUALITY: Masterwork}

Read at a shaft mouth nothing comes up it for a watch. Read aloud anywhere else, everything within a chain that was standing still starts moving, and there is a construct within a chain.

### The Other Warden

At the deep shaft below this region, one of the same make has begun reciting the stop-digging order instead of giving the warning it was set to give, and it lets past anybody going up (NAMED CREATURES, T-CRE-08).

This one has not. A party who meets both, in that order, has the difference.

The wide shaft is west -> R05-L16, the pegged shaft north -> R05-L15, and a second rim with worse cutting lies east -> R05-L23.
