## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Walking inward:* the air warms with every hundred feet and the floor narrows from thirty feet to twelve. *Counting aloud:* the counting from the hall stops being audible at about the halfway point, and nothing replaces it. *Putting the light out:* the draught stops here, so there is no direction to navigate by, and this is the first place in Bellgate where that is true. *Going on to the end:* nobody has, and the region says so in its own Fields.

**Gate:** None, and that is the content. This cut has no door, no mechanism, no water and nothing standing in it, and it goes inward under the covenant ground for a quarter of a mile and has not been walked to its end.

*Job for the region.* It is the region's open edge. Bellgate is a built place with a bottom, and this is the one direction in it that does not have one, and it is how the Deep Sill gets into the region without being in it.

*Cost in turns.* Four to the point where the dressing stops. Unknown past that, and the region's Fields say unwalked rather than long.

*What is true that the Overview withholds.* The narrowing is not a taper. It is four separate reductions, each at a joint, each of a course, and each one is where the Covenant stopped for a season and started again narrower. They were running out of confidence rather than out of stone.

## Features

### The Narrowing

Thirty feet at the tier end, twelve at the far end, in four reductions at four joints. Each joint is clean and each reduction is exactly one course.

The dressing worsens with each: the first section is laid true, the second is true and rough, the third is cut and not dressed, and the fourth is cut in haste.

### The Warmth

A hand laid flat on the floor finds it warmer at each reduction than at the one before, and by the fourth it is warm through a boot standing still (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-09), (RUMOURS, T-RUM-03).

It is coming up rather than along. The warmth is under the floor and it is stronger the further inward the cut runs, which is what the whole Reach's warm stone is doing on a smaller scale.

### The Last Dressed Joint

Where the fourth reduction is, and past it the stone is only cut. There is nothing here: no mark, no order, no peg and no warning, which is the one place in the Covenant's work where they went past a boundary without cutting anything into it.

They cut orders at every rim on the Reach. They did not cut one here, and the reason is that this is not a shaft and they did not think of it as going down.

### Either End

The tier's open floor runs seaward -> R05-L24. The silted floor with the four openings is seaward too -> R05-L38. The stone stops being cut inward -> R05-L40.
