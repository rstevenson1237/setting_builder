## Player Overview

Twenty niches cut at shoulder height round the walls, sooted, with nothing burning in any of them and not a stub of tallow anywhere in the room. The spacing between them is exactly forty paces.

**The clean niche** has no soot in it, in a room where soot lies on the floor.

Something has been putting a hand in that one place.

## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Reading the niches:* every one is sooted and the spacing between them is exactly forty paces. *Putting a hand in the clean one:* it turns.

**Gate:** The pegged opening in the north wall, seated from inside the frame, which is a door in a level that has no doors (PUZZLES, T-PUZ-02). The answer that is not the pegs is the low doorway east, which is four feet high and comes out in the same corridor two rooms along.

*Cost:* one turn to cross. Two to read the niches. Three to work the pegs.

*What is true:* soot lies on every surface in this cell and one niche has none, and something has been putting a hand in that one place (PUZZLES, T-PUZ-07). It is not a niche: it is the handhold of a shaft plug.

*Nil:* no light and no fuel. Twenty niches cut for wicks, sooted from four generations of them, and not a stub of tallow anywhere in the room.

## Features

### The Clean Niche

Cut at shoulder height like the other nineteen, with no soot in it, in a room where soot lies on the floor (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-12), (PUZZLES, T-PUZ-07).

Turning it turns a plug, and the plug is the pegged opening's other end, and it opens from this side only.

A hank of tallow candles lies on the floor beneath it, sweating, which burn well and burn with a smell that carries a long way in still air (TREASURE TABLE I, T-TR1-08).
