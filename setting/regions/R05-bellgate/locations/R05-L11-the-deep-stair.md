## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Descending:* the count starts here and everything burning is burning against it. *Counting aloud:* nothing answers, which is worth establishing, because two hundred feet lower something does. *Putting the light out:* the niches are spaced exactly enough to pace by and the count comes out right. *Striking the wall:* the second turn rings hollow and nowhere else does.

**Gate:** None here. The stair is the way down and the only thing gating it is the water at the bottom, which is knee deep and cold and does not get deeper.

*Job for the region.* It is the descent and the clock. A party's turn count runs from the moment they go below the rim, and this is where they go below the rim, and the whole of Bellgate is played against the light they carried down these steps.

*Cost in turns.* Three turns down at a walk. Four up. Two more if anything is being carried between two people, because the stair is wide enough for two abreast only where it is not turning.

*What is true that the Overview withholds.* The niches below the last turn are cut, sooted and empty, and the spacing is exact to within a hand over two hundred feet. That is not decoration and it is not lighting: it is a measure. Pacing the niches gives the depth, and the Covenant cut them so that somebody in the dark would know how far down they were.

## Features

### The Turns

Four of them, each dropping about fifty feet, dressed the whole way and worn deepest along the outside edge (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-11).

At the second turn the wall rings hollow for six feet, and a squared opening comes out of that wall at that point with letters cut round it -> R05-L23.

### The Niches

Shoulder height, every forty paces, soot in every one and nothing burning in any of them (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-12). Below the last turn there are no lit niches anywhere in Bellgate and there never will be again.

The spacing is exact. A party pacing by them in the dark comes out right, and the arithmetic gives them the depth, and nobody has ever done it because everybody has carried a light.

### The Water

Rising a hand a turn from the third turn down, ankle then knee at the foot, and it does not go deeper than the foot. It is cold enough to close a chest on the first breath at the last turn (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-02) {TEST: Constitution} {WOUND: Frost}.

The tell is that the soot in the niches stops at a level, all the way round, and the level is where the water once stood and is a foot above where it stands now.

### Above and Below

The stair turns up toward a square of daylight -> R05-L05, and down onto silted floor at the foot -> R05-L12.
