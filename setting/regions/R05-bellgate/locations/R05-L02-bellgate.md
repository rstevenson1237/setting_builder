## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** The gate does not react. Nothing about it moves, answers, warms or opens to a hand, and that stays true for as long as a party works on it. *Standing in the opening:* nothing, and the stone is cold. *Putting a hand in the socket:* nothing, and the inside of the socket is clean and dry in standing rain. *Striking the face:* the note of a solid thing, and it carries inward down the stair rather than out across the water. *Speaking to it:* nothing. *Putting the light out:* nothing, and this is the one place on the gate ground where that is true.

**Gate:** The socket beside the opening, at the height a hand falls, cut to take a clapper (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-09), (KEYS, T-KEY-02). Seated, it lifts the whole face and holds it while the clapper is in. The clapper stays. The answer that is not the socket is the flooded east cut, chest deep, dark the whole way, which comes out below the gate rather than behind it and arrives with the light spent {TEST: Constitution} {WOUND: Frost}.

*Approaches.* Across the standing water from seaward, which shows the face first and the socket not at all, because the socket is on the inward side of the lip and is invisible until a party is against the stone. Up the kept lane from Stillhold, which comes at the face along the lip and shows the socket before the opening. Neither approach shows the stair.

*Why it is still shut.* There are ten clappers on the Reach that are not underwater and nine of them are hanging on frames in the fen with people camped in sight of them. The tenth is in a socket a mile up a road nobody has a reason to reach into. The Remnant has known that Bellgate takes a note for four generations and has never been able to spare one (RUMOURS, T-RUM-11).

*Departure.* A clapper seated in the socket holds the face up. Taken out, the face drops, and it drops whether or not anybody is under the stair. A party that goes in on the socket has left the only thing that opens the gate standing in the open on the gate ground, and anybody who comes along the lane can lift it out.

*Funding for the reversal.* The frames at (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-02) and the channel slabs at (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-09) are exactly what they appear to be everywhere else in this region. The gate is the one piece of Covenant work on the Reach that is doing what it was built to do and doing it against the people who built it.

## Features

### The Face

Dressed stone, forty feet across and twenty high, standing at the inward end of the gate ground with the water at its foot. The opening in it is eight feet by four and there is nothing in the opening: no ring, no bar, no hinge, no lock, and no jamb.

The stone round the opening is cut back to a lip a hand deep that runs the whole way round, top and both sides and across the threshold. The lip is what the face seats into when it is down, and the face is down.

The lane back across the standing water runs seaward -> R05-L01.

### The Socket

Cut into the lip on the inward side, at the height a hand falls when a person leans on the stone. Everything on this face is green with age. The inside of the socket is not, and it is dry in standing rain, and it is the only dry stone anywhere on the gate ground.

It is cut to a clapper: a hand and a half long, tapered, with a shoulder two thirds down. Nothing else on the Reach is that shape, which is the point of cutting a socket that shape.

### The Lip

Level all the way round to within the width of a knife, and worn along the threshold only, in two patches at the span of a pair of feet. Somebody stood in the opening long enough and often enough to polish the stone, and they stood facing out.

Below the wear, cut backwards into the underside of the lip and readable only in still water lying against the threshold, is a line about what answers when a bell is rung (LORE, T-LOR-10).

### The Reading in the Socket

Reaching into the socket, which no amount of looking substitutes for, finds the inner face of it cut with the same construction the region repeats: a channel, a groove, and a shoulder for a counterweight (PUZZLES, T-PUZ-03).

The construction is the one a party will meet again two hundred feet below this, working correctly, with its counterweight still in place. Anybody who has met that one already knows what this socket wants before they reach into it.

### Behind the Face

Past the opening the floor drops away and the first turn of a stair is visible from the lip, going down into standing water -> R05-L05.
