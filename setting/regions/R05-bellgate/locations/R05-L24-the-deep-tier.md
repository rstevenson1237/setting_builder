## Player Overview

**The floor** runs a quarter of a mile inward under the covenant ground, dressed slab, laid true, eleven courses deep and dry. Everything above it stands in water and none of it reaches here.

**The draught** comes down out of the openings at the seaward end and runs inward along the floor at ankle height, all day, reversing at dusk.

**The counting** is audible from the north at all times, from a room the light does not reach the far wall of. It does not stop and it does not approach.

**The doorways** leave in six directions, and the air gets warmer with every hundred feet a party goes inward.

## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Walking inward:* the air gets warmer with every hundred feet, on a level floor, and it does not stop. *Counting aloud:* something in the unlit half of the hall to the north answers by not stopping. *Putting the light out:* the draught from the shaft openings is the only thing that tells a party which way is out. *Standing still for a turn:* nothing comes. *Shouting:* it carries the whole length of the tier and comes back off the inward end, which means the tier ends.

**Gate:** None on this floor. What gates the tier is behind a party rather than in front: the channel slab and the flooded cut are the only two ways in, and both are worked from the shafts side.

*Job for the region.* It is the tier's spine and its map. Six ways leave it, it is dry, and it is the only place below the slab where a party can put a load down and know where it is.

*Cost in turns.* Two to cross seaward to inward at a walk. Six to work the doorways off it. None to leave by the way in.

*What is true that the Overview withholds.* The tier is dry and the level above it is not, which is the wrong way round and is the single most useful fact in Bellgate. Water stands in the shafts, on the stair and on the gate ground, and none of it reaches here. Something below is taking it, and the warmth that increases inward is coming from the same place.

A hand laid flat on the floor finds it warm and warmer inward. The water that is standing in the shafts, on the stair and on the gate ground is not getting down here, and the warmth that increases inward comes from the same place it is going.

## Features

### The Floor

Dressed slab, laid true, running a quarter of a mile inward under the covenant ground and not walked to its end (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-01). Each course a hand thicker than the one above, and here it is eleven courses, which is the deepest road-work anywhere in the setting.

A hand laid flat on it finds it warm, and warmer inward, and it is the same warmth as the fen's slab and much stronger (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-09).

### The Draught

Coming down out of the shaft openings at the silted end and running inward along the floor at ankle height, all day, reversing at dusk. It is the only moving air below the rim and it is what a party navigates by when the light is out.

A surveyor's rod in jointed sections lies against the wall where somebody left it, marked in the Covenant's units, which are not the units anybody now uses (TREASURE TABLE III, T-TR3-07).

{VALUE: 1000 cn} {WT: 2} {QUALITY: Fine}

### The Counting

Audible from this floor at all times, from the north, from a room the light does not reach the far wall of (BESTIARY, T-BES-08). It does not stop and it does not approach.

A party who counts their own turns aloud on this floor, which is the discipline the region is played with, is counting in the same room as something that is also counting, and the two counts are not the same count.

### The Doorways

The channel slab is at the silted end -> R05-L25. The hall the counting comes from is north -> R05-L26. Soot lies on the floor through a doorway west -> R05-L29. A cell with a pin socket in its ceiling is south -> R05-L33. Silt banked to the height of a man is at the seaward end -> R05-L38. The floor narrows and warms inward -> R05-L39.
