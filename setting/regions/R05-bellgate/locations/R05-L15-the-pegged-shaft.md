## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Striking the pegs:* the sound goes into the shaft and does not come back. *Drawing them:* they cannot be drawn, and the lip splits if they are forced, and the rim cannot be pegged again.

**Gate:** The pegs, seated from inside the frame (PUZZLES, T-PUZ-02), (LORE, T-LOR-05). The answer that is not the pegs is that this shaft is not the one anybody wants: the warden thirty feet south is standing at an open rim and this is the one it was set to keep.

*Cost:* one turn at the rim. Three to split the pegs, loudly, within a chain of a construct.

*What is true:* the pegs are sound and the shaft below them is dry and empty and goes down two hundred feet to the tier. It is the shortest route between the shafts and the tier and it does not need a clapper.

*Nil:* nothing to find. Lit and sounded, the shaft holds no gear, no rope and no mark, and the Covenant pegged it before it was used.

## Features

### The Pegged Rim

Squared, pegged all the way round, the pegs seated from within, no forcing anywhere on it (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-05), (PUZZLES, T-PUZ-02).

A door peg of Covenant make lies beside it, one that seats itself and cannot be drawn, sold as a lock and working as one on the side the user is standing on (TREASURE TABLE IV, T-TR4-06).
