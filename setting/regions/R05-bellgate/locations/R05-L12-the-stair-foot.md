## Player Overview

The stair ends in standing water and the floor past it is silted flat.

**The silt** is fine, ankle deep at the eastern end and a finger deep at the stair, and it holds a print for a turn. There are prints in it going inward that are not a party's own.

**The six openings** leave this floor in six directions: a cell with sooted niches, a squared shaft with cold coming out of it, a shaft wide enough to lower a bell, a doorway four feet high, a cut with water running down it, and a doorway onto uneven floor.

**The clean niche** is at shoulder height in a corridor where every surface has soot on it, and it has none.

**The sounding line** is coiled on a peg beside the wide shaft, knotted by fathom, the lead cut hollow.

## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Standing on the silt:* it holds a print for a turn and then closes, which makes it a clock and a record. *Lighting a wick:* something at the bottom of the wide shaft stops moving. *Putting it out:* it starts again, and it is closer when the light comes back (BESTIARY, T-BES-05). *Going into any of the six openings:* nothing here objects, and this floor does not hold territory. *Counting aloud:* nothing answers, and something two hundred feet inward is listening.

**Gate:** The channel slab on the inward side, seated in its groove with the socket beside it at hand height and nothing in the socket (R05-L25). The answer that is not the socket is the flooded cut, which runs from here down and inward and arrives in the tier wet (R05-L21).

*Job for the region.* It is the junction. Six ways leave this floor and it is the only room in the Bell Shafts that more than three routes reach, and a party who has not mapped it has not got a level.

*Cost in turns.* One to cross. Two to read the silt properly. Three to work the channel slab with a bar and fail.

*What is true that the Overview withholds.* The silt did not settle. It was carried in, on boots, over years, out of the flooded cut, and it is thickest at the cut's mouth and thinnest at the stair. Everybody who has used this floor came up the cut and went out by the stair, and nobody has ever done it the other way.

### The Clean Niche

One niche on this floor at shoulder height with no soot in it, in a corridor where every surface has soot on it (PUZZLES, T-PUZ-07), (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-12).

It is not a niche. It is the handhold of a shaft plug, and the plug turns, and something has been putting a hand in that one place for long enough to keep it clean.

### The Sounding Line

Coiled on a peg beside the wide shaft, knotted by fathom, the lead cut hollow to bring up a sample of whatever it lands in (TREASURE TABLE III, T-TR3-01).
