## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Reading the post:* it is driven, notched down one face and up the other, and the top notches are fresh. *Untying the rope:* nothing, then or later, and the rope is new the following day.

**Gate:** None. A driven post standing in ankle-deep water with rope on it.

*Cost:* one turn at the post. A watch to lie up beside it, which is a stated procedure and belongs to the fen's clock rather than the region's (PROCEDURES, T-PRC-04).

*What is true:* the notches are a watch count and they are current. Somebody is counting watches on the gate ground of Bellgate, which nobody in either neighbouring region admits to visiting, and the count agrees with the fen's posts to within one (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-08).

*Nil:* no frame. This is the only rope on the gate ground and there is nothing to hang from within eighty feet of it.

## Features

### The Post

Driven into a socket cut in the slab, notched by watch, the fresh notches at the top, with laid rope knotted round it in a bell knot (UNANSWERED QUESTIONS, T-UNQ-01).

{VALUE: 20 cn} {WT: 1} {QUALITY: Poor}

The knot is the one used for hanging a bell and it is tied on a post with nothing to hang. It is tied within the day, as every fresh rope in the Reach is, and nobody has seen it done anywhere (TREASURE TABLE I, T-TR1-01).
