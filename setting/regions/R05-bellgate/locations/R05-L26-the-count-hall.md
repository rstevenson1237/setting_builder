## Player Overview

A room large enough that the light does not reach its far wall, and something in it is counting.

**The seat** stands on the floor in the middle with nothing round it, bronze, its arms worn through the plating to the core on both sides. It faces the far wall rather than the door. The wear is on the outer faces of both arms.

**The master board** stands against the far wall out of the light, waxed bronze, four feet by three, ruled in two columns.

**The counting engine** is on its own plinth, bronze, and it is unwound and has been for a long time.

**The wax tally** lies on the plinth beside it, with a question already written on it and answered.

**The voice** is in the unlit half of the room and it is never seen. It will not stop mid-count.

## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Entering while the count is running:* nothing, and the count does not falter. *Interrupting the count:* it begins again from the beginning and it begins with whoever interrupted, by name, in the older layer. *Putting the light out:* the count goes on and the room does not change. *Sitting in the seat:* the count stops mid-number for the first and only time, and then resumes from the beginning. *Taking the master board:* the count runs to its end, whatever number that is, and then the room is silent and stays silent.

**Gate:** The hall itself is not gated. What is gated is the far door, pegged from this side with the board on its hook, and that is a hold door in a place that has no holds (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-06), (PUZZLES, T-PUZ-02). The board lifts off the hook and the hook is the peg head. The answer that is not the board is the thinning wall on the north side, which is one course thick above shoulder height and can be gone through, and which costs the noise of going through it in a room where something is listening to numbers.

*Approaches.* From the tier's open floor, which shows the seat first and the far wall not at all, because the light does not reach it. From the ranked cell, which comes in behind the seat and shows the master board before anything else. From the thinning wall, which comes in at the north corner and shows the whole room in section, including that there is nobody in it.

*Why it is still here.* Nothing here is portable by fewer than four, the counting is audible from two rooms away in every direction, and the two parties who reached it turned round in the doorway. That is a correct reading and it is not the whole of it: the counting thing has never left this room and has never had to.

*What comes out.* The count itself. The master board holds the tally of a whole stretch, still readable and still agreed, and it is the only surviving record that closes (TREASURE TABLE V, T-TR5-07). A party who takes it can put it against the boards hanging in Stillhold and find where the numbers stop closing.

*Departure.* The seat weighs what a bench weighs and cannot go up the stair. The board can. The engine can be carried by two and takes a watch to wind for every count it gives, which is a watch spent in a room where something else is counting.

Every column on the master board closes and the totals are correct, and the reason it closes is that it stops four rows before the roads do. The question on the wax tally asks how many went down, and the answer is one higher than the number of ranks standing two rooms inward.

### The Master Board

Waxed bronze, four feet by three, standing against the far wall out of the light, holding the count of a whole stretch of road ruled in two columns (TREASURE TABLE V, T-TR5-07).

{VALUE: 9000 cn} {WT: 15} {QUALITY: Masterwork}

It agrees with itself. Every column closes and the totals are correct, which makes it the only closed record on the Reach, and the reason it closes is that it stops four rows before the roads do.

### The Counting Engine

Bronze, on its own plinth, tells a tally aloud in the Covenant's layer, will not stop mid-count, and takes a watch to wind for every count it gives (TREASURE TABLE IV, T-TR4-04).

{VALUE: 5000 cn} {WT: 6} {QUALITY: Masterwork}

It is unwound and it has been unwound a long time. It is not what is counting.

### The Wax Tally

A board on the plinth beside the engine, of the kind that answers a question written as a count, in notches, by morning (MAGICAL TOMES, T-TOM-04).

{VALUE: 8000 cn} {WT: 2} {QUALITY: Cursed}

A question is already written on it, in the Covenant's layer, and it has been answered. The question asks how many went down. The answer is one higher than the number of ranks standing in the flooded cells two rooms inward.

### The Voice

Heard well before it is seen and it is never seen, telling a tally aloud somewhere in the unlit half of the hall (BESTIARY, T-BES-08).

{AD: 4, +2} {TYPE: Horror} {TEST: Sanity}

It will not stop mid-count. Interrupting it starts the count again from the beginning and it starts with whoever interrupted. It has been in this room since the water came up and it names everybody present, correctly, in the older layer, which is the one thing it has in common with what is standing in the cells (NAMED CREATURES, T-CRE-06).
