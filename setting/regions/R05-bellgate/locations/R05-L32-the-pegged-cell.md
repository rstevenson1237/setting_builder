## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Reading the board:* it is a hold count, on a door, four hundred feet below any hold. *Lifting it off the hook:* the peg draws and the door opens and the board cannot be rehung. *Prising the door:* a stated procedure, and it keeps the board (PROCEDURES, T-PRC-05).

**Gate:** The pegged door, with the board on the hook beside it and the hook the head of the peg (PUZZLES, T-PUZ-02). It is the same construction as the third door at Stillhold (KEYS, T-KEY-04). The answer that is not the board is the thinning wall in the room north, which is one course thick above shoulder height and can be gone through.

*Cost:* one turn to cross. One to take the board. Three to prise.

*What is true:* something is standing in the doorway on the far side, holding the peg it was driving, and it will not let the door be opened and it was somebody's neighbour (BESTIARY, T-BES-07).

*Nil:* nothing behind it but a wall. The door opens onto four feet of cut stone and the room it was cut for was never finished.

Every fitting on it is green. The hook is bright.
