## Overview

Standing water over slab, eight hundred feet of it, and at the inward end
a face of dressed stone with a gate in it. The frames on the approach carry
their pins and carry nothing else. The ground is level and stays level and there
is nothing on it to trip over, which is the first thing about this place that
does not sit right.

The gate does not open to a hand. There is no ring, no bar, no hinge and no
lock, and the stone around the opening is cut back to a lip that runs the whole
way round it. Beside it, at the height a hand falls, is a socket. The socket is
green with age everywhere except inside.

Behind the face the floor drops. A stair turns four times into standing water,
and the steps are worn deepest along the outside edge, where something wide was
carried down, repeatedly, by people going carefully. Below the last turn every
niche in the wall is cut, sooted, and empty. The spacing between them is exact
enough to walk by in the dark, and a party who tries it finds the count comes
out right.

Air moves in the shafts, downward all day and upward at dusk, and it carries wet
rope and cold bronze with no rope and no bronze anywhere in it. Somewhere below
the last light, at a distance that does not change however far down a party
goes, something is counting.

## Fields

**Extent:** Eight hundred feet of gate ground on the surface, then the
shafts, which drop about two hundred feet in four turns, then the tier, which
runs a quarter of a mile inward under the slab and has not been walked to its
end.

**Rate:** Sixty feet a turn on dry slab, half that in standing water, and a
quarter of that where the floor has to be read before it is trodden. On the
surface the gate ground is crossed in a turn.

**Time:** Turns, six to the hour. Bellgate is counted in turns from the moment a
party goes below the rim, and everything that is burning is burning against that
count.

**Light:** None below the rim. Every tallow niche in the shafts is cut and
sooted and every one of them is empty, and the spacing is exact enough that a
party can pace by them in the dark.

**Water:** Ankle-deep on the gate ground after rain, standing at the foot of the
stair, and chest-deep in the flooded east cut, which is the answer that is not
the socket.

**Reactions:** The gate does not react. Everything the Covenant left here reacts
to sound and to nothing else, and a party that works in silence gets a long way
in before anything addresses them.

**What the difficulty measures here:** The evil of the place and how readily its
traps and hazards find a party. Bellgate was built, it was pegged, and the
pegging held. It is dangerous by construction rather than by occupation, and it
is the more readable of the two dangerous regions for exactly that reason.

**Gates:** Two, and both are priced. The stair is a gate on light and on time.
The channel slab is a gate on the socket, and the socket takes a clapper, and
the Reach holds fewer clappers than it holds doors.

## Connections

The Gate Ground is the surface and the spine. It is the approach, the
frames, the standing water and the face of the gate itself, and it is the only
part of Bellgate a party reaches without going down. The Bell Shafts are the
Covenant's own vertical works cut through the slab beneath it, and the Deep Tier
is the level those shafts open into.

The three containers are a depth, not a map, and the region is crossed downward.
From the ground into the shafts is a stair turning four times into standing
water, worn deepest along the outside edge where something wide was carried down
(ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-11). From the shafts into the tier is a channel
slab that lifts rather than swings, with the socket beside it at hand height
(ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-09).

Both of those crossings have an answer that is not the crossing, and both
answers are longer and wetter. What the region will not give is a second route
between the shafts and the tier: everything below the slab is reached through
the socket or through the flooded east cut, and a party that can work neither is
a party going back up.

Inward and downward the Deep Tier opens onto the Deep Sill. Seaward the Gate
Ground opens onto the Ashen Fen, and the dry lane to Stillhold leaves from the
same ground.
