## Referee Overview

**Approach:** The sound changes. A kept lane carries a boot's noise; this one absorbs it, because the closing reed is packed to head height on both sides and the water gives nothing back. The change is at the lane's mouth and turning back costs nothing at all.

**Terrain:** In the lane, half a mile a watch, because the reed is closing over it from both sides and has to be pushed through. *Rate in the standing reed either side:* half a mile a watch, the same, which is what makes this lane worth nothing to anybody.

*Decision:* follow it, which costs a watch and arrives at the reed face where the cutting stopped; or leave it, which costs nothing. There is no reason to follow it except that it was cut, and that somebody stopped cutting it.

*Table effects:* nothing on the Encounter table that needs open ground reaches a party inside this lane. The reed-walker row is likelier here than anywhere in the region: it moves only while nobody is facing it and there is nowhere to face (BESTIARY, T-BES-11).

*Nil:* no post at either end. Every lane in the Reach has a post at its head with the standing order cut into it, and this one has a socket, cut, and nothing in it.

## Features

### The Closing Lane

Cut to the order's width and never kept, closing over at the base rather than at the top, so that it reads as a lane at head height and as reed at the ankle.

Counting the seasons of regrowth gives the watch the cutting stopped on this lane. It is the same watch the gap opens on every tally post in the Reach, and this is the only place a party can measure it in a growing thing rather than in a notch (PUZZLES, T-PUZ-06), (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-08).
