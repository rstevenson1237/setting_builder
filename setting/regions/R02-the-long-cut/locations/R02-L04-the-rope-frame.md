## Referee Overview

**Approach:** Wet rope, from half a mile, on a road where there is no rope in sight and no rope anywhere for nine miles. It is a specific smell rather than a general dampness and it is stronger downwind of the frame than up. A party turning back at the smell has spent a watch.

**Terrain:** Fitted slab at the road rate of two miles a watch, and the region's fourth and fifth frames stand two miles apart on this stretch. Off the road it is the cut at half a mile a watch.

*What has to be read.* The rope. It is new, it is tied at the pin, it is tied with a knot for hanging a bell rather than a knot for hanging anything else, and it was tied within the day (UNANSWERED QUESTIONS, T-UNQ-01). The tell that it was tied today rather than yesterday is the tar: a new rope sheds tar for one day and the slab under this pin is spotted.

*Both routes.* Lying up beside the frame through a full watch to see who ties it is a stated procedure and it costs the watch whatever comes of it (PROCEDURES, T-PRC-04). The answer that is not lying up is the cut: a party a hundred yards off in the reed can watch the frame without being on the road, and they cannot see the pin from there, and so they learn who came and not what they did.

*Table effects.* While a party is lying still beside the frame, everything on the Encounter table that closes distance while nobody is facing it closes it. Nothing that gives ground to struck metal comes near, because a party lying up is not striking anything.

## Features

### The Frame

Two uprights and a crossbeam, the pin seated, the socket clean (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-02). Nothing hangs from it and nothing has hung from it in living memory.

Sounding it here answers from inward rather than seaward, which is the wrong direction and is what the ringing order forbids (PUZZLES, T-PUZ-01), (LORE, T-LOR-01).

### The Rope

Laid, tarred, new, knotted at the pin, with the tail cut clean rather than parted. The knot is a bell knot and it is tied correctly, by somebody who has tied one before and expects to tie another.

Every frame in the Reach that a chapter reached is bare, and some of the bare ones have new rope. Nobody has ever seen it done and nobody has ever admitted to doing it.

### The Pin

Bronze, seated so long that it carries the shape of its socket down one side, and it will come out with work (TREASURE TABLE I, T-TR1-04).

{VALUE: 40 cn} {WT: 1} {QUALITY: Fine}

Waxed and folded into the socket behind it is a ledger page about why the reeds were cut back a chain: so that the tolling would carry, and so that nothing could stand close and not be seen standing (LORE, T-LOR-02).

### The Road Either Way

Back seaward to the bend and the branch lane -> R02-L03. Inward into smoke lying flat at knee height -> R02-L05. The cut is a step off the stone -> R02-L07.
