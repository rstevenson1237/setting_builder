## Referee Overview

**Approach:** The knocking. A rope end against a bare crossbeam carries a mile and a half up a straight span in still air, and it is the only sound on the road that is not the party's own. It is not rhythmic and it does not stop. There are two watches of open road behind a party at that distance and turning back costs only the watches.

**Terrain:** Fitted slab above the waterline, two abreast, at the road rate of two miles a watch. The lane branching inward is dry kept ground at the same rate and it is four days to a wall. Off both, reed and standing peat at half a mile a watch, and the pace does not come back by pushing.

*Why it is worth leaving the road for.* It is not: the road is the reason to be here. This is the fourth frame counted from the sea, it is the one junction on nine miles of span, and everything crossing the Reach between the Cut Water and the Covenant Ground passes it. What a party takes away from it is a route and a name, and both are the extraction.

*The gate and both prices.* The carrier stands between the road and the lane and it asks which road this one is (NAMED CREATURES, T-CRE-03). Answered correctly, it stands aside and will carry anything given to it as far as the next frame, set it down and wait. Answered wrongly it picks the bell up, and a party that wants past it while it is carrying goes the long way: back to the third frame and down into the cut, which costs a watch, arrives after dark, and arrives in the reed where the frames cannot be counted.

*What the correct answer is.* The road's name in the living layer is the Long Cut and it is not what the carrier is asking for. It wants the Covenant's count: which number this road is, counted from the sea inward. That count is on the tally post at the foot of this frame, and it is on every post in the region, and nobody has ever needed to read it before.

*Departure.* Whatever the party gave the carrier is at the next frame, set down, with the carrier standing beside it. It will not give it back and it will not stop it being taken. A party that gave it nothing walks past it both ways.

*Funding for the reversal.* The frames at (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-02) and the tally posts at (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-08) are exactly what they appear to be everywhere else in this region and do exactly what they were built to do. This is the one frame on the Long Cut where a party is asked a question by the thing standing at it.

## Features

### The Frame

Two uprights and a crossbeam, standing at the only bend in three miles, the pin seated and the socket clean. Nothing hangs from it. Cut into the underside of the crossbeam, upside down to anybody walking and right way up to anybody lying beneath it, is the ringing order (LORE, T-LOR-01).

Sounding it is a stated procedure and the note carries inward much further than it carries seaward (PROCEDURES, T-PRC-02). Sounded while the carrier is standing here, it sets the bell down and turns to face the frame, and does not ask its question until the note has gone.

### The Tally Post

Driven at the frame's foot, notched by watch down one face and up the other, the top notches fresh (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-08). Cut into the head of it, above the notches, in the Covenant's layer, is the road's number counted from the sea.

Nobody reads the head. The cutters read the notches, and they have read them so long that they no longer see the line above them. That number is the answer to the carrier's question.

### The Branch Lane

Dry kept ground leaving the stone inward, a chain wide, cut by the same standing order that keeps the span (LORE, T-LOR-11). It is the second route into the Covenant Ground and it is four days rather than one, and it is the route anybody carrying something heavy out of Bellgate actually takes -> R04-L06.

The reeds along it are cut to the root the whole way. Nobody in Reedhold will say who is keeping them.

### The Carrier

A drowned man carrying a bell too large for him, walking the road inward at a steady pace (BESTIARY, T-BES-03). It stops when it is watched, and then it goes on stopping. It sets the bell down, straightens, and asks.

Speaking to it is safe. Following it is not, and it does not object to being followed, which is what makes it a decision. It has been asking a long time and it does not expect an answer any more (RUMOURS, T-RUM-08).

Lying up beside the frame through a full watch to see who ties the rope is the other thing a party can do here, and it has its own outcomes (PROCEDURES, T-PRC-04), (UNANSWERED QUESTIONS, T-UNQ-01).

### The Span Either Way

The road runs back seaward toward the first frame and the light -> R02-L02, and inward toward a frame with new rope on the pin -> R02-L04. Trodden ground in the cut lies a hundred yards off the stone -> R02-L12.
