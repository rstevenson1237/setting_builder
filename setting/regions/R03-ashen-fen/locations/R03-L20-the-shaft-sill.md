## Player Overview

Air comes up out of the opening rather than down into it, and it carries wet rope and cold bronze with no rope and no bronze anywhere in the fen.

**The rim** is cut square, twelve feet across, pegged over, with an order cut into it in letters a hand deep. The strokes are uneven and they get worse across the line.

Standing at it is **the warden**, upright, and it says one thing to each person and then does not speak to that person again.

**The slate** lies in the lee of the rim where a watch-keeper would have kept it, carrying the same words.

**The ways from here** are three: a stair turning up toward green light, a dry passage running back to a wall with pegged doors, and the shaft.

## Referee Overview

**Approach:** Air, moving. It comes up out of the rim rather than down into it, and it carries wet rope and cold bronze with no rope and no bronze anywhere in the fen. It is felt at the last turn of the stair, dry, before the rim is visible.

**Terrain:** Dry dressed floor around a squared rim, the first dry ground below the waterline in the region. Feet rather than yards from here on: the rim is twelve feet across and the floor round it is forty by thirty.

*What has to be read.* The pegs. Every peg in this rim is seated and none has been drawn, which is what the order says to do and is what nine other rims in the Reach look like. The tell that this one is different is the order itself: it is cut here in the same letters as everywhere else, and the last word is cut by the same hand as the rest, and on every other rim it is not.

*Both routes.* Down the shaft, which is a stated procedure on rope, heaviest last (PROCEDURES, T-PRC-07). The answer that is not the shaft is the pegged passage back into the hold, which does not go down at all and which is the way out for anybody who reads the rim and decides against it.

*Table effects.* This is the boundary. Nothing on the Ashen Fen's Encounter table exists below this rim, and the Deep Sill's table starts at the pegs. A shaft-warden stands here and gives its one warning to each person once (BESTIARY, T-BES-10).

Every stroke of the order here is by one hand, including the last word. On every other rim in the Reach the last word is somebody else's, and a party who has read two rims knows which one is the copy.

## Features

### The Rim

Cut square, twelve feet across, pegged over, with the order cut into it in letters a hand deep and cut in haste (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-05).

*Stop at the sill. What lies under it was not made, and it is not ours to open. Peg the shaft and set a watch that will outlast us* (LORE, T-LOR-05).

Every stroke is by one hand and the last word is finished by that hand. On every other rim in the Reach the last word is somebody else's, and a party who has read two rims knows which one is the copy.

### The Warden

A Covenant construct set at the rim to keep people out of the shaft, still doing it, and it does not distinguish between going down and coming back up (BESTIARY, T-BES-10).

{AD: 4, +3} {TYPE: Construct} {WOUND: Crushing}

It gives one warning, in the older layer, in a voice pitched to carry down a shaft: *Stop at the sill.* It gives it to each person once, and then it stops speaking to that person permanently.

### The Slate

Lying in the lee of the rim where the watch-keeper kept it: a slate copy of the words, carried by whoever was set to keep the watch (MAGICAL TOMES, T-TOM-03).

{VALUE: 6000 cn} {WT: 2} {QUALITY: Masterwork}

Read at a shaft mouth, nothing comes up it for a watch. Read aloud anywhere else, everything within a chain that was standing still starts moving.

### The Ways From Here

The stair turns up toward green light -> R03-L19. The pegged passage runs back to the hold -> R03-L17. The shaft goes down -> R06-L01.
