## Player Overview

Within thirty yards the surface stops carrying the ripple of a party's own wading. It is not muffled. It is still.

**The lintel** stands square and whole a hand under the water, cut from one stone, undressed on its upper face where nobody was going to see it. Beyond it the floor runs on out of sight.

**The silt bank** lies against the near side of the threshold, a hand deep, fine, and undisturbed except in one place where something wide was dragged over it.

**The breath-bell** lies on its side on the floor slab six feet short of the threshold: a bronze hood taking two heads, straps out.

**Either side** of the lintel the water holds at a hand, which is true of nowhere else in the tier.

## Referee Overview

**Approach:** The water goes quiet. Not muffled: still. Within thirty yards of the doorway the surface stops carrying the ripple of a party's own wading, which is a thing a person notices as a change in what their legs are doing rather than as a sound. There is a quarter mile of open floor behind them at that point.

**Terrain:** Dressed floor under a hand of water on the near side. Past the lintel the floor holds level and the water holds at a hand for the whole length of the hold wall, which is the only place in the drowned tier where that is true.

*What has to be read.* Whether the doorway is a doorway. It is square, its lintel is a hand under the surface, and beyond it the floor runs on out of sight, which describes every drowned doorway in the region and most of them open onto water with no bottom (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-05). This one has a floor. The tell is the silt: a doorway with a floor behind it has silt banked against the near side of its threshold, because the water moved through it and stopped. A doorway with nothing behind it has none.

*Both routes.* Going through the doorway is a stated procedure, heaviest load first, and it costs a watch (PROCEDURES, T-PRC-01). The answer that is not the doorway is the hold wall itself, which is one course thick below the waterline and can be gone through with a bar, and which costs the noise and comes out inside the hold at a point the party cannot see before they commit.

*Table effects.* Nothing on the Encounter table can follow a party through the doorway, because everything on it works reed, peat or open water. Inside the wall the region's table stops applying and the hold's own contents are the content.

A doorway with a floor behind it has silt banked against its threshold, because the water moved through and stopped. A doorway with nothing behind it has none. The drag in the silt goes inward and nothing has come out.

## Features

### The Lintel

Square, whole, a hand under the surface, cut from one stone and undressed on its upper face where nobody was going to see it (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-04).

Ducking under it is the named action and there is no other way through. A party carrying anything above waist height puts it in the water to do it.

### The Silt Bank

Against the near side of the threshold, a hand deep, fine, and undisturbed except in one place where something wide was dragged over it. The drag is old and the silt has resettled in it, which takes years.

The drag goes inward. Nothing has come out.

### The Breath-Bell

Lying on the floor slab six feet short of the threshold, on its side, a bronze hood taking two heads (TREASURE TABLE IV, T-TR4-08).

{VALUE: 6000 cn} {WT: 8} {QUALITY: Masterwork}

It holds air for one crossing and draws the cold in through the metal as it empties. It is here because somebody set it down to go under the lintel and did not come back for it, and it is six feet short, which is the distance a person walks in the time it takes to change their mind.

### Either Side

The open floor of the tier runs back seaward -> R03-L13. Past the lintel the hold's wall stands with its doors along it -> R03-L17.
