## Referee Overview

**Approach:** The cold comes up rather than down. Everywhere else in the fen the water is a shade warmer than the air; here the cold arrives through the soles from below and the water over it is unchanged. It is met about thirty yards out and turning back at that point costs nothing.

**Terrain:** One mile a watch, half the road rate, because the cold takes the feeling out of the soles and the footing has to be read by eye on a floor that is under a hand of water.

*Decision:* cross it to the stair, which is a watch and is the shortest route from the tier's edge to the descent; or go back along the swept floor and the tier, which is two watches and is warm.

*Table effects:* nothing on the Encounter table works this floor. What it does instead is spend a party: numb feet do not feel a footing cue until it is stood on, and the next location is a stair (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-09) {TEST: Constitution} {WOUND: Frost} {CONDITION: Numb-footed / footing cues are not felt until they are stood on / until a fire}.

*Nil:* no fire possible. The floor is under water, there is nothing on it to burn, and the condition the cold sets does not lift until a party is somewhere they can light one, which is not in this region.

## Features

### The Cold Floor

Dressed slab under a hand of water, level, with the cold coming up through it out of the level below rather than out of the air (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-09), (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-04).

It is directly above the shaft the stair reaches, and the cold is strongest where the floor is over the shaft, which a party can map by walking it with a bare hand on the stone.

Scratched into a wall standing above the water at the floor's inward corner, with three marks beneath it, is a line about the water not having come up (LORE, T-LOR-08). The lowest mark is above the water now.
