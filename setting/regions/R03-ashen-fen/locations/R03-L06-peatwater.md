## Referee Overview

**Approach:** The water goes warm. It is a shade warmer than the air over it across the whole of this stretch, and it is the only warm water in the fen, and a party notices it inside three paces of entering. Turning back costs the paces.

**Terrain:** Half a mile a watch wading, water at the shin over peat that gives twice under every step, once for itself and once for whatever is under it.

*Decision:* wade it, which is half a mile a watch and connects the ash ground to the mere edge without going near the road; or go round by the causeway, which is the road rate and is a watch and a half longer and is overlooked from the bend.

*Table effects:* reed-hounds work these shallows in threes, giving ground to struck metal and coming back when the sound stops, which makes noise a resource here rather than a mistake (BESTIARY, T-BES-02). The water takes a sound and gives none of it back.

*Nil:* nothing built. No slab, no frame, no post and no cut, over the whole stretch. It is the one part of the Ashen Fen the Covenant did not touch, and there is no record of why.

## Features

### The Warm Shallows

Standing water a shade warmer than the air over it, the colour of strong tea, over cut peat that has been taken and has grown back (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-01) {TEST: Constitution}.

Under it, wherever a foot finds firm ground, the firm ground is dressed slab and it is not laid to any line: the pieces are broken and set down flat, in the way somebody fills a hole with what is nearest.

Somebody took a road apart here and used it to make a crossing, and the crossing is where the peat holds, and the cutters know the route and do not say how they know it.
