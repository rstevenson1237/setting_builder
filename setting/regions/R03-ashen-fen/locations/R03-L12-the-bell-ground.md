## Player Overview

Bronze on the air, cold and wet, from a mile out with nothing in sight that could be making it. The smell does not vary with the wind.

**The nine frames** stand in a line along the road at two hundred yard intervals, every pin seated. Eight of them carry a bell, and each bell reads as two colours with a waterline across it: green where it has been out of the water and clean where it has not.

**The ninth frame** is at the inward end past the last of the cleared ground. The bell on it has no waterline, no green, and the cord is uncut.

**The hauling gear** is stacked beside the fourth frame under a reed cover: three sets of blocks, laid rope, and a sledge on runners.

**The remnant's camp** stands four hundred yards off the road on the driest ground here. No wall, no ditch, a fire they let go out at dusk, and eleven of them.

Nailed to the inside of the fourth frame's upright, at the height a person kneels to, is **the order written out**: nine names, seaward to inward. Beside each, in a second hand and much smaller, is a number.

## Referee Overview

**Approach:** Bronze on the air, cold and wet, from a mile out with nothing in sight that could be making it. The smell arrives before the frames come over the horizon and it does not vary with the wind. A party turning back at the smell has spent a watch and nothing else.

**Terrain:** Fitted slab above the waterline, walked at the road rate of two miles a watch, with the cleared ground either side dry enough to stand on and not dry enough to sleep on. Inward the road goes under and does not come up.

*What is here.* Nine bells, hauled up out of the fen and hung again on nine frames along a mile of road, in what the Covenant Remnant believes is the old order (HOARDS, T-HRD-05). Each is a lift for four. Each is on a frame in open ground with nothing round it.

{VALUE: 40000 cn} {WT: 200} {QUALITY: Artifact}

*Why they are still here.* They weigh what they weigh, they are recognised on sight by every cutter between here and the sea, and the people who hung them are camped four hundred yards off and can see the whole mile. Taking one is not stealing from the dead. It is stealing in daylight from people who will still be here next season.

*The order is wrong.* The Remnant has them seaward to inward by size, which is not the order. The order is on a set of waxed bronze leaves nobody here holds the whole of (MAGICAL TOMES, T-TOM-01), and the fault is readable to anybody who has lain under a crossbeam and read the line cut into it (LORE, T-LOR-01). They know somebody is going to tell them. They have said so.

*The gate and both prices.* A clapper is what opens Bellgate, and the nine hanging here are the only clappers on the Reach that are not underwater (KEYS, T-KEY-02). The Remnant will give one, once, to a party who tells them the order and can show how they know. That costs the party the telling, and after it the Remnant re-hangs the whole mile in the right order, which takes a season and is a thing a party has caused. The answer that is not the asking is the ninth frame, which stands at the inward end past the camp's sightline, and taking that clapper costs a watch of dark, a rope, and the certainty that the Remnant will know by morning who was on this road.

*Departure.* A party who told them the order walks out with a clapper and an obligation. A party who took one walks out with a clapper and is known by name on every frame between here and the sea, because the Remnant tells the cutters and the cutters count.

The numbers beside the names are the road counts off the tally posts. Read as the order, they are correct, and the order the Remnant is keeping is not.

## Features

### The Nine Frames

Two uprights and a crossbeam each, standing in a line along the road at two hundred yard intervals, every pin seated (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-02). Eight of them carry a bell. The bronze is green everywhere it has been out of the water and clean everywhere it has not, so each bell reads as two colours with a waterline across it.

Struck out of order, a road answers from the wrong direction and something on it starts walking (PUZZLES, T-PUZ-01).

The road runs back seaward over warming slab -> R03-L10, and a cut lane leaves inland from the fourth frame -> R03-L09.

### The Ninth Frame

The inward end of the line, past the camp's sightline and past the last of the cleared ground. The bell on it is hung and it is the only one on this road that was never in the water: no waterline, no green, and the cord is uncut.

{VALUE: 12000 cn} {WT: 30} {QUALITY: Artifact}

It is not the tenth. The Remnant found it hanging where it had always hung, took it as proof that the recovery was sanctioned, and re-hung it in the line without saying that it had never been down.

### The Hauling Gear

Three sets of blocks, laid rope, and a sledge on runners, stacked beside the fourth frame under a reed cover (TREASURE TABLE II, T-TR2-05). The gear is Covenant work and it is in working order.

{VALUE: 250 cn} {WT: 2} {QUALITY: Masterwork}

A party can borrow it by asking. Nobody watches it. Using it to take a bell is a thing the Remnant will work out from the runner marks in the cleared ground, which do not weather for a season.

### The Remnant's Camp

Four hundred yards off the road on the driest ground here, no wall, no ditch, a fire they let go out at dusk. Eleven of them. They are not armed and they do not post a watch, because the mile of open ground is the watch.

They will trade safe conduct across the Covenant Ground, honoured at Stillhold, and the roots of names nobody else records, for news of any bell anywhere. They believe ringing the tenth finishes the debt, and they are certain, and their certainty is not evidence (RUMOURS, T-RUM-02).

### The Order Written Out

Nailed to the inside of the fourth frame's upright, at the height a person kneels to, is a board with the recovery order on it in the Remnant's own hand: nine names, seaward to inward, by size.

Beside each name, in a second hand and much smaller, is a number. The numbers are not in sequence with the list. They are the road counts off the tally posts, and read as the order they are correct.

Inward, past the ninth frame, the water stands at a hand over laid slab -> R03-L13.
