## Player Overview

The slab gets warmer course by course, and over about half a mile everybody in the party independently wants to sit down and several of them say so.

**The warm stretch** runs a mile and a half. A hand laid flat under any course finds the stone warm and the top of the same stone cold.

**The patches** are three places about twenty yards apart where the road is worn smooth in the middle and rough at the edges, each about the length of a person lying down.

Set into the road's edge at the second patch, flush with the slab and laid flat, are **the slate leaves**: nine of them in a bronze frame, face up.

**The road either way** cools going seaward and carries a smell of bronze going inward.

## Referee Overview

**Approach:** Heat through the boot. The slab gets warmer course by course going inward and the change is gradual and the effect is not: over about half a mile everybody in the party independently wants to sit down, and several of them say so, and that is the tell (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-06). It is warm enough to notice a full watch before it is warm enough to matter.

**Terrain:** Fitted slab above the waterline for a mile and a half, level, dry, and warm. The road rate of two miles a watch applies and nobody keeps it, because the walking is easy and the stopping is easier.

*What has to be read.* Whether to stop. The stone is a shade warmer than the air along the whole stretch and it is warmest in three places, each about twenty yards long, and those three are where everything that has ever stopped here stopped. The tell is physical: the slab in those places is worn smooth in body-length patches, and the patches are not where a road wears.

*Both routes.* Crossing at pace costs the watch and nothing else. Stopping costs a watch to a party who intended a quarter of one, and they wake a hand lower than they lay down {TEST: Fate} {CONDITION: Slab-slept / the party wakes a watch late and a hand lower than they lay down / until they are back on standing ground}. Neither is the mistake: the warm slab is the only dry ground on this stretch, and a party crossing at dusk with nowhere else to lie is not being foolish.

*Table effects.* Nothing on the Encounter table interferes with a party lying on the warm slab. That is the point. Everything on it is available to a party who has just woken a watch late with the light gone.

Roads do not wear in body-length patches. The reedcutters call the settling by another name, lie down on it anyway, and do not say so to strangers.

## Features

### The Warm Stretch

A mile and a half of road where a hand laid flat under any course finds the stone warm, and warmer the further inward, and the top of the same stone cold (RUMOURS, T-RUM-03), (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-01).

The warmth is not the stone's. It comes up from the level below and it is stronger where the road runs deepest, which on this stretch is where the courses are nine rather than five.

### The Patches

Three of them, twenty yards apart, each about the length of a person lying down, worn smooth in the middle and rough at the edges. Roads do not wear in body-length patches.

The reedcutters call it settling and lie down on it anyway, and they do not say so to strangers (RUMOURS, T-RUM-10). The land is going down a little each year, and always at night, and the people who measure it have a word for it that is not the true one.

### The Slate Leaves

Set into the road's edge at the second patch, flush with the slab and laid flat, are nine slate leaves in a bronze frame, and it is a set (MAGICAL TOMES, T-TOM-06).

{VALUE: 4500 cn} {WT: 4} {QUALITY: Cursed}

Read through the palm rather than the eye, they give the depth of the road under the reader. The reading takes a watch and the reader is a hand lower at the end of it.

Somebody laid the set into the road here on purpose, facing up, where anybody lying down would find them.

### The Road Either Way

Seaward the stone cools course by course -> R03-L11. Inward the bronze smell comes up and the frames stand in a row -> R03-L12. The road goes under and does not come up -> R03-L13.

A warming slab the size of a book, which holds a body's heat through a night and takes a watch of that body's sleep to charge, is lying at the third patch where somebody dropped it (TREASURE TABLE IV, T-TR4-05). {VALUE: 2000 cn} {WT: 2} {QUALITY: Masterwork}
