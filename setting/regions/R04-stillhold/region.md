## Overview

A wall two courses thick, standing on Covenant slab, with still water at
its foot on three sides. Inside it the ground does not give at all. It is the
one place on the Reach where a stake driven at dusk is where it was left in the
morning, and everybody who has been anywhere else notices within the first
hour.

Doors, and most of them are pegged. The peg is on the outside, driven home, and
a board hangs on a hook beside each one with the notches still in the wax. There
is no forcing anywhere on any of them, no broken frame, no splintering, nothing
taken off a hinge. Whatever is behind them was put there, counted, and shut in
by people who agreed the count on both sides of the door and then walked away.

The light is measured. It is given out, stated aloud with what remains, and it
comes up a four-day lane from seaward because nothing here renders any. Nobody
burns a wick for company. The dark inside the wall is not the dark outside it and
the people here know the difference and do not explain it.

A struck plate rings the day, on a plate because the bells are gone. When it
rings, everyone stops where they are until it has finished, including strangers,
including in the middle of a sentence. Then they go on. Inward from the gate the
lane runs one day to a place nobody here will walk to, and seaward the lane runs
four days to everything else.

## Fields

**Extent:** A walled hold of about two hundred yards across on the
covenant ground, with the Chapter House standing inside it against the inward
wall. The still water reaches the foot of the wall on three sides and there is
no landward approach that is not a lane.

**Rate:** The hold is crossed on foot in a fraction of a day. The dry lane
seaward to the Long Cut is four days on kept ground. The inward lane to Bellgate
is one day, and it is the reason the hold stands here.

**Time:** Days. Stillhold keeps the Covenant's own day and rings it, and the
ringing is done on a struck plate rather than a bell, because the bells are gone.

**Light:** Rationed, and the ration is stated to a guest on arrival. Tallow
comes up the four-day lane and nothing here renders any.

**Goods and services:** Shelter, kept water, a count of what is behind any door
in the hold, and a written safe conduct across the Covenant Ground that the
Remnant will honour. No tallow to spare, no rope, no boat.

**What the difficulty measures here:** The size of the settlement and what can
be got in it. Stillhold is small, it is supplied down a four-day lane, and it
gives out its stores against a reckoning it expects and cannot date. A party
that arrives needing to resupply has misread the map.

**Refusals:** The Closed Order will not open a pegged door, will not permit one
to be opened, and will not discuss the terms on which either might happen. They
will tell a party exactly what is behind it, item by item, in exchange for a
promise that it stays shut.

**Firm ground:** Covenant slab under the whole hold, and it is the only region
in the Reach where a party can put a stake in the ground and expect it to be
there in the morning.

## Connections

The Still Ground is the spine and the Chapter House sits inside it,
walled off. The ground is the working part of Stillhold: the landings, the
lanes, the rationed light and the people who live off the water. The Chapter
House is the Closed Order's own building, and a party crosses into it through
one door that is opened from inside or not at all.

Every hold wall here is two courses thick above the waterline and one course
below it, which is where the builders stopped expecting to be seen
(ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-10). The Order's doors are pegged from the
outside with the tally board still on the hook, and the count on the board is
accurate to the item (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-06),
(LORE, T-LOR-06).

Stillhold has two ways out and they are not alike. The inward lane to Bellgate
is short, direct and the reason the hold was put here. The seaward lane back to
The Long Cut is four days on dry ground and it is the route anybody carrying
something out of Bellgate actually takes.
