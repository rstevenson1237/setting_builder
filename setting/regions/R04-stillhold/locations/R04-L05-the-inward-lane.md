## Player Overview

**The lane** leaves the yard inward on Covenant slab, a chain wide, laid true, level, with the covenant ground open on both sides and the water standing off it at about a mile. It is the driest ground on the Reach.

**The cold underfoot** draws heat out of a boot rather than giving it, and it is worst in the middle third.

At about the halfway point are **the marks at the middle**: lettering cut backwards into the underside of a coping stone, readable only in the water that lies against it after rain.

**What is said about it** at Stillhold is accurate as far as the gate ground and stops there. Nobody in the hold has walked it, and they say so.

## Referee Overview

**Service:** The road to Bellgate, and it is the reason Stillhold stands where it does. One day on kept dry ground, and the hold will state the rate, the distance and the landmarks to anybody, accurately, without being asked twice.

**Cost:** Nothing to walk it. What it costs is that the yard writes down that a party went inward, with the day, and the Order does not forget and does not need to.

**Refusal:** Nobody here will walk it. Not as a guide, not for coin, not for a day's ration, and the refusal is flat. They will describe every yard of it and they will not set foot on it.

*The rule the lane keeps.* It is kept cut, and the hold does not keep it. The cutting is fresh, it is done to the standing order's width, and nobody in Stillhold does it or admits to knowing who does.

*Schedule.* Nothing uses the lane. In four years the book records eleven people going inward and four coming back, and the four are all the same person.

*Where a question goes.* Nowhere, because nobody asks. A party who asks the Order what is at the far end gets an accurate description of the gate ground and the face, and no description at all of anything past it, and they will say plainly that they have not been.

*Extent.* One day on dry kept slab, a chain wide, level, with the covenant ground open on both sides and the water standing off it at about a mile.

This is the shortest route between two regions anywhere in the setting and the only one nobody uses. In four years the yard's book records eleven people going inward and four coming back, and the four are all the same person.

## Features

### The Lane

Covenant slab, laid true, each course a hand thicker than the one above (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-01). It is the driest ground on the Reach and it is walked at the road rate the whole way with nothing on it to slow a party.

It is the shortest route between two regions anywhere in the setting, and it is the only one nobody uses.

The gate yard is at the seaward end -> R04-L02, and the standing water over slab at the inward end -> R05-L07.

### The Cold Underfoot

The slab draws heat out of a boot rather than giving it, and it is worse where the lane runs deepest, which is the middle third (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-09) {TEST: Constitution} {WOUND: Frost} {CONDITION: Numb-footed / footing cues are not felt until they are stood on / until a fire}.

This is the reverse of the fen, where the same slab is warm. Nobody in Stillhold has remarked on it because nobody in Stillhold has walked on the fen's roads.

### The Marks at the Middle

At about the halfway point, cut backwards into the underside of a coping stone and readable only in the standing water that lies against it after rain, is a line about what answers when a bell is rung (LORE, T-LOR-10).

Reading it takes lying down in water on the coldest ground in the Reach, which is why it has not been read.

### What Is Said About It

The hold will repeat that Bellgate opens to a note and not to a key, that this much is written and this much is certain, and that anyone who says which note has never stood in front of it (RUMOURS, T-RUM-11).

They are quoting the Remnant. They believe it because it is written down.
