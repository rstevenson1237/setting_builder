## Referee Overview

**Service:** A bed, a bare cell, and the door is not barred from either side. It is offered to anybody who asks and to several people who do not, and the Order writes down that it was offered.

**Cost:** Nothing at all, and that is stated at the point of offering so that it cannot be mistaken for a debt.

**Refusal:** No light. The cell has no niche and no wick and the ration does not extend to a guest burning one, and the Order will state the remaining count rather than argue about it.

*Hours:* always open. Somebody looks in at the middle ringing and again at dusk and does not knock.

*Extent:* one cell, ten feet by eight, dressed slab, a bed, a stool and nothing else. Feet indoors, days.

*Nil:* nothing in it. No fitting, no hook, no shelf and no mark on any wall, and the cell has been swept.

## Features

### The Cell

Dressed slab on all four walls and the ceiling, cut as one room and never altered (LANGUAGE AND ROOT VOCABULARY, T-LNG-51), (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-10).

The cold in it comes up through the floor rather than in from the walls, and it is the only room in Stillhold of which that is true, and the hold gives this cell to guests because it is the one nobody wants (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-09).

A pegged chest of ordinary household goods stands under the bed, closed from the outside, everything inside dry and in the order it was packed (TREASURE TABLE II, T-TR2-08).

{VALUE: 400 cn} {WT: 6} {QUALITY: Fine}

Nobody in the hold knows whose it is. It is in the register as a fixed thing.
