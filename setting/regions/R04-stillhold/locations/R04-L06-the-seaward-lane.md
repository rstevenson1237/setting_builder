## Referee Overview

**Service:** The road seaward, four days on dry kept ground to the Long Cut, and it is how everything in Stillhold arrives. The hold will state the rate, the distance and every landmark on it, accurately.

**Cost:** Nothing to walk it. The supply that comes up it is bought at the far end and the hold does not sell any of it onward.

**Refusal:** They will not send anybody down it out of turn. The supply goes when it goes, and a party who wants a message carried seaward waits for the run or carries it themselves.

*Hours:* the supply run leaves and arrives on a schedule the hold keeps in the book, and the lane is otherwise empty. Anybody on it who is not the run is written down when they come in.

*Extent:* four days on kept dry slab, a chain wide, from the gate yard to the fourth frame of the Long Cut. Miles on safe roads, days.

*Nil:* no water on it. Four days of dry lane with the fen a mile off on both sides and nothing drinkable between the two ends, which is why the run carries and a party must.

## Features

### The Kept Lane

Covenant slab, laid true, with the reed cut back a chain on both sides for its whole length and kept cut by nobody the hold can name (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-07), (LORE, T-LOR-11).

At the head of it stands a post with the standing order on it, copied letter for letter from somewhere else, fault and all.

Carrying an open light along it when the smoke is lying at knee height is a stated procedure (PROCEDURES, T-PRC-06), (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-04). Salt comes up it in sewn hide and is the one thing on the run that anybody steals (TREASURE TABLE II, T-TR2-04).
