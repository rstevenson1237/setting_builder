## Player Overview

One room, twelve feet by ten, one door, and the door is shut except after the middle ringing.

**The shelf** is stone, outside it at waist height, chalked with the day's issue and the count remaining. It is rewritten each morning and not wiped, so the chalk builds up in layers.

The remaining count is said aloud with every issue, to whoever is taking one, whether or not they asked.

## Referee Overview

**Service:** The ration. Tallow, kept water and a portion of what came up the four-day lane, issued once a day to anybody in the hold, strangers included, without being asked for.

**Cost:** Nothing, and the remaining count is stated aloud with every issue so that nobody can take a second without hearing what they have taken.

**Refusal:** No tallow to spare for a journey. The ration is for being in the hold and the Order will not issue against a party's road, and they will say what the remaining count is while refusing.

*Hours:* issued after the middle ringing, every day, and the door is shut the rest of the time with the shelf outside it still chalked.

*Extent:* one room, twelve feet by ten, one door, one shelf outside it. Feet indoors, days.

*Nil:* no keeper's want. Whoever is on the door that day is on it because it is their turn, and they want nothing from a party and will say so.

Read back through the layers, the remaining count falls every season at the same rate and has never once risen. Nobody in the hold has read it that way.
