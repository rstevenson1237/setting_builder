## Referee Overview

**Service:** Shelter, kept water, a written safe conduct across the Covenant Ground that the Remnant honours, and the count of what is behind any door in the hold, item by item. The Order recites; it does not hand anything over.

**Cost:** For shelter, nothing, and they write down that they gave it. For the safe conduct, a day and a statement of where the party is going, written and kept. For a count, an undertaking that the door stays shut, given aloud in front of whoever is in the room.

**Refusal:** They will not open a pegged door, will not permit one to be opened, and will not discuss the terms on which either might happen. Pressed, the answer gets shorter rather than longer, and the third time it is a number: the count of the door being asked about (RUMOURS, T-RUM-09).

*Standing.* A party opens as strangers who are fed and written down. Giving an undertaking and keeping it raises standing further than anything else available here. Being found near a pegged door without having said so first ends the shelter that evening, stated as a door: the chapter door is shut from inside and is not opened again.

*Why the hold is still keeping this.* Nobody rescinded the order. There is nobody who could. The Order expects a reckoning, cannot date it, and gives out its stores against it, which is why a party who arrives needing to resupply has misread the map.

*The refusal with an answer.* The count is the answer to the door, and it is a real one: the Warden knows what is behind hers to the item and will say so (NAMED CREATURES, T-CRE-02). A party that wants a thing that is behind a door can learn exactly what it is, where it stands and what it weighs, and then has to decide whether that is worth an undertaking they will have to break in front of somebody who wrote their name down.

*The second reading.* The counts are all still hung. Nobody has ever added them up. A party who has read boards in two other holds can put this hold's totals against them and see that the numbers do not close (UNANSWERED QUESTIONS, T-UNQ-03).

## Features

### The Ration Table

A plank on trestles inside the door, with the day's issue on it and a written column beside each portion showing what remains. The remaining figure is stated aloud to whoever takes a portion, including strangers, including in the middle of a sentence.

Nobody here burns a wick for company and the table is lit because it is being worked at.

The chapter door back to the yard is the only outer door -> R04-L02.

### The Written Count

A bound register of every door in the hold with its contents listed, cross-ruled against the boards on the doors themselves. The two agree everywhere. Where a board has faded the register is the copy of record, and where the register is unclear the board is.

There is a wax board in the same case that answers a question written as a count, in notches, by morning (MAGICAL TOMES, T-TOM-04). It is never wrong, it answers only what can be counted, and it has begun including whoever wrote the question in totals they did not mean to be in.

{VALUE: 8000 cn} {WT: 2} {QUALITY: Cursed}

### The Warden

Closed Order, at the far end of the passage, and she states the count of what is behind her door before a party has asked her anything at all (NAMED CREATURES, T-CRE-02). She wants the door kept shut. She will trade the count item by item and nothing else, and she is armed, and she says so before she is asked that too.

She is not hostile and there is no fight here. What she does about a party at her door is stand in front of it and keep stating the count, and the hold comes.

### The Safe Conduct

Written on waxed board, carrying the hold's mark and the day, and honoured by the Covenant Remnant anywhere on the Covenant Ground. It is written in the Covenant's own layer and a party who cannot read it cannot tell that it also states where they said they were going.

A counting frame in bronze stands beside the writing desk, strung with beads worn flat on one side only, so the direction of every count it ever kept is still readable (TREASURE TABLE III, T-TR3-06). {VALUE: 800 cn} {WT: 2} {QUALITY: Fine}

The inner passage runs back past the hanging boards -> R04-L10.
