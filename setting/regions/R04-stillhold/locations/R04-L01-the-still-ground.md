## Referee Overview

**Service:** Shelter inside the wall, kept water from roofs and cisterns, a place to stand a stake and find it where it was left, and the day's hour, rung on a struck plate and audible everywhere in the hold.

**Cost:** For shelter and water, nothing, and the Order writes down that it was given. For anything a party wants to carry out, a statement of where they are going, written and kept. There is nothing here that is sold.

**Refusal:** No tallow to spare, no rope, no boat. The hold is supplied down a four-day lane and it will not spend its ration on a party's journey, and it states the remaining count aloud while refusing so that nobody can think the refusal is personal.

*The rule the ground keeps.* When the plate rings, everybody stops where they are until it has finished, including strangers, including mid-sentence. Then they go on. Nobody explains it and nobody enforces it, and a party who walks through a ringing is not stopped and is noticed by everyone.

*Schedule.* The plate rings the Covenant's day at first light, at the middle, and at dusk. Rations are issued after the middle ringing. The gate yard is open from first light to dusk and the chapter door is opened from inside or not at all.

*Where a question goes.* To the Order, who answer accurately and briefly and write the question down. A question asked here is on a board within the day, which is the opposite of how Reedhold works and is worth a party knowing before they ask one.

*Extent.* Two hundred yards across, inside a wall, on Covenant slab. The hold is crossed on foot in a fraction of a day. Four days seaward to the Long Cut, one day inward to Bellgate.

## Features

### The Slab

Covenant work under the whole hold, laid true, and it is the only ground in the Reach where a stake driven at dusk is where it was left in the morning. Everybody who has been anywhere else notices within the first hour and most of them say so (NAMES, T-NAM-07).

The hold's name puts stone before water, which is the Covenant's own layer, and it names what the place keeps rather than where it stands (LANGUAGE AND ROOT VOCABULARY, T-LNG-20).

### The Cisterns

Cut into the slab at four points, roofed, with the run-off from every building led into them. Warm rain is caught on every roof and counted, and the count is chalked beside each mouth.

The cisterns are Covenant work and they are cut deeper than the hold uses. The lowest three feet of every one of them has never been drawn on.

### The Wall From Inside

Two courses thick above the waterline and one course thick below it, which is where the builders stopped expecting to be seen (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-10). The step in the wall face is at the waterline and it runs the whole way round.

The wall walk goes to the water's edge -> R04-L03, and the slipway runs down to the landing -> R04-L07.

### The Ground Itself

The gate yard is at the landward side -> R04-L02. The ration door is on the open ground -> R04-L04. The post with the struck plate stands alone with the ground walked flat round it -> R04-L12.

Nothing here reacts to a stranger with anything but a count. A party who tries to force a matter is refused and written down, and the writing is the whole of the consequence and it is enough (RUMOURS, T-RUM-09).
