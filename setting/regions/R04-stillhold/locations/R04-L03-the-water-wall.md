## Referee Overview

**Service:** The measurement. A tide plate is pegged to the wall here, graduated in hands, and anybody in the hold will read it to a party and say what it read last season and the season before.

**Cost:** Nothing. The reading is public and the Order regards giving it as part of keeping it.

**Refusal:** Nobody will agree that the water is rising. They will give the readings, which do not change, and they will not draw the conclusion, and pressed they say that a reading is a reading.

*Hours:* read at the same hour every day, at the middle ringing, by whoever is nearest.

*Extent:* three hundred yards of wall foot on the water sides, slab underfoot, the water still at the foot of it and not moving. Yards outdoors, days.

*Nil:* no way through. The wall has one opening and it is at the landward side, and this stretch has none at all.

## Features

### The Wall Foot

Two courses thick above the waterline and one course thick below it, and the step in the face where the thickness changes runs the whole way round at the waterline (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-10).

Scratched rather than cut into the face above the step, with three marks beneath it, is a line about setting a mark on the third course and looking again in the spring (LORE, T-LOR-08). The lowest of the three marks stands above the present water.

The tide plate is pegged beside it, graduated in hands, meant to be read at the same watch every day (TREASURE TABLE III, T-TR3-03).

{VALUE: 700 cn} {WT: 2} {QUALITY: Fine}

It reads what it read when it was pegged. The scratched marks and the plate disagree, and both are accurate, and the hold has not put them together (LANGUAGE AND ROOT VOCABULARY, T-LNG-49).
