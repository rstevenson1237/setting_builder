## Referee Overview

**Service:** Salt, in sewn hide, from the seaward end, by weight and divisible to any amount. It is the one thing in Reedhold that everybody buys and nobody talks about.

**Cost:** Coin, at the going rate, which is chalked on the door and changes with what the boats bring.

**Refusal:** She will not sell on credit to anybody, including people she has sold to for years, and she will say why: salt goes inland and does not come back, and neither do some of the people who carry it.

*Hours:* open whenever a boat is unloading and for a while after. Shut otherwise, and the door is barred rather than locked.

*Extent:* one room, twenty feet by twelve, grit underfoot, salt on the air from thirty feet outside. Days.

*Nil:* no keeper's want worth acting on. She is content, she is not owed anything, and she does not want anything a party can bring her.

## Features

### The Weighing Bench

A plank on stone with a set of hide-wrapped weights and a chalked rate on the door beside it, and everything is sold in front of the buyer (TREASURE TABLE II, T-TR2-04).

{VALUE: 160 cn} {WT: 3} {QUALITY: Fine}

Cut into the lintel above the door, in the older layer, is a name for this room that means a route the sea was said to reach along, and the sea has not come this far in living memory (NAMES, T-NAM-11), (LANGUAGE AND ROOT VOCABULARY, T-LNG-40).

She knows what it says. She thinks it is wrong and she is right, and she does not know that it was correct when it was cut.
