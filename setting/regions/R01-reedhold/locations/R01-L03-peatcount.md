## Referee Overview

**Service:** The count. Reedcount holds the whole watch count from the sea to the Long Cut and will give it to anybody who asks correctly, along with where the peat holds, the name of every frame between here and the water, and which crossings are walkable this season (NAMED CREATURES, T-CRE-01).

**Cost:** The answer to her question. She opens with *What watch,* and then waits, and she will wait out a whole conversation for it and say nothing else until she has it. The answer is on the post she is standing at, and she will not point at it.

**Refusal:** She will not say which face of her own post is wrong, and both faces are notched and they have not agreed for some years. She knows. She has not corrected it.

*The rule the post keeps.* The count is agreed aloud by whoever is standing there and corrected by whoever disagrees. A count nobody contradicts stands. This is why two posts a mile apart can disagree and both be correct: neither is a record, both are an agreement.

*Schedule.* She is at the post at dusk without exception, and at the post most of the day, and the hold reconciles the day count to the cutters' watch count in front of her.

*Where a question goes.* Everywhere. She repeats what she is asked to whoever asks her next, without judging it and without attributing it, and by the following dusk it has reached the landing and the houses.

*What she wants.* The count kept accurate after she stops keeping it. She has nobody to hand it to, she has not asked anybody, and she will not ask.

## Features

### The Post

Driven at the middle of the cleared ground, notched by watch down one face and up the other, the last notches fresh (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-08). It is the oldest continuously kept count in the Reach and it goes back further than any written record (UNIQUE TREASURES, T-TRE-08).

The two faces have not agreed for some years and the difference is not drift: it is a constant, and it is the same constant as the gap in the lane posts on the Long Cut (PUZZLES, T-PUZ-06).

### Reedcount

The oldest cutter on the seaward stretch, at the post, and she does not stop working while she talks (NAMED CREATURES, T-CRE-01).

Reading a lane's posts back to find where a count broke is a stated procedure and she will do it with a party, and it costs a watch whatever the result (PROCEDURES, T-PRC-03).

### The Reconciliation

At dusk the hold's day count and the cutters' watch count are put together at the post, out loud, by whoever is standing there. It takes a quarter of an hour and everybody within earshot stops.

Two counts that disagree are the most reliable sign in the Reach that something happened, and the reconciliation is how the hold finds out.

### The Doors Off the Ground

The nearest roof past the post is the factor's, with a scale outside it under its own lamp -> R01-L07. The counting house is fifteen yards the other way and its door stands open -> R01-L10. The cleared ground opens on every side -> R01-L01.
