## Referee Overview

**Service:** The boundary, stated. This is the last niche, it is lit at dusk with all the others, and standing inside its light is being in Reedhold. It is where the hold tells a party what the hold is.

**Cost:** Nothing. Nobody keeps it and nobody asks anything of anyone at it.

**Refusal:** The hold does not come past it after dark. A party standing outside the last niche is not in Reedhold any more and everybody here treats them as though they are not, including the person who lit the wick forty paces back.

*Hours:* lit at dusk by whoever is nearest, let burn out. Nobody relights it.

*Extent:* one niche, cut at shoulder height in dressed stone, throwing light about fifteen feet. The next one is forty paces back toward the ground. Days.

*Nil:* nothing is sold, kept or refused here by any person. The location is a line, and its content is which side of it a party is standing on.

## Features

### The Last Niche

Cut at shoulder height into a course of dressed slab that runs on landward under the peat, sooted, and the spacing to the next one is exactly forty paces as it is everywhere else (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-12).

The niches are Covenant work and the hold uses them. Counting them landward from here, into the dark, a party finds three more, unlit, before the stone goes under.

Tallow-moths come off the water and put the wick out by arriving, all at once, and they arrive here first because it is the last light (BESTIARY, T-BES-06).
