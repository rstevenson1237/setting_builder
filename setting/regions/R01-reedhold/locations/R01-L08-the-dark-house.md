## Player Overview

**The shuttered front** is the one house in the row with no wick in its niche at any hour. The boards across the window are seated in a rebate cut for them, and the rebate is older than the boards. The street door is unbarred and has been the whole time.

Inside is one room. **The lamp on the shelf** stands at eye height in the only part of it a person in the doorway can see, in a bronze sleeve, unlit.

By the door, still loaded, is **the kit by the door**: rope, a sounding line, cut peat, and a second lamp of ordinary make. The rope is short by a body's length at the top end and the cut is clean.

The man leaves the room when the door opens. He comes back when whoever came in has stopped standing, and then he asks **the question**.

## Referee Overview

**Service:** The route down through the sill, spoken once, in enough detail to walk. Sillwalker is the only person who has been through and come back (NAMED CREATURES, T-CRE-05).

**Cost:** Not coin. He wants a reason he judges good enough, and he will not say in advance what would qualify. What he has accepted before: somebody going down after a person rather than after a thing, and somebody who had already been as far as the last pegged rim and turned back.

**Refusal:** He will not go himself, will not draw a map, and will not sell anything he brought up. He leaves the room when a party comes in, the first time and every time, and comes back when they have stopped standing.

*Standing.* He opens as somebody who wants to be left alone and wants to be asked the right question, and he cannot have both. Asking about the sill lowers it. Asking about the lamp lowers it further. Asking about the person he went down for raises it, and nobody in Reedhold will tell a party there was one.

*Why the house is still shut.* Nothing here is for sale and everybody in the hold knows it, so nobody comes. The shutters have been down long enough that the hold has stopped noticing them.

*The refusal with an answer.* He will not give the route for coin and he will not withhold the lamp from somebody who has the route. A party who gets the route out of him and never mentions the lamp is offered it, unprompted, at the door, as they leave. A party who asks for the lamp first will not get either.

*The second reading.* A party who has been to the last pegged rim and read the order cut into it can name it back to him in the older layer (LORE, T-LOR-05). That is the one thing that makes him sit down.

He asks it once, standing, of a party who has stayed long enough to be worth asking. An answer in distance gets nothing. He is establishing whether they are coming back.

## Features

### The Shuttered Front

Boards across the window from the inside, seated in a rebate cut for them, and the rebate is older than the boards. One house in the row with no wick in its niche at any hour, and the hold lights every other niche at dusk without discussing it.

The street door is unbarred. It has been unbarred the whole time.

The lane side has its own door -> R01-L04, and the frontage picks up again toward the counting -> R01-L10.

### The Lamp on the Shelf

A pin-lamp in a bronze sleeve, set on a shelf at eye height in the one part of the room a person standing in the doorway can see (UNIQUE TREASURES, T-TRE-05). It is unlit. It has been unlit since it came up.

The plate riveted to it says it burns brighter with depth, and unlike every other plate on every other device on the Reach, that one is true (KEYS, T-KEY-06). Below the sill floor, nothing else a party can carry gives any light at all.

Pressed on why he stopped lighting it, he says it had been bright enough, and that is the whole of the answer.

### The Kit by the Door

A pack, still loaded, standing where somebody set it down and did not unpack it: rope, a sounding line, cut peat, and a second lamp of ordinary make that does not work below the rim (TREASURE TABLE IV, T-TR4-01).

The rope is short by a body's length at the top end, cut rather than worn, and the cut is clean.

### The Question

He asks it once, of a party who has stayed long enough to be worth asking, and he asks it standing: *how far in are you going.* An answer in distance gets nothing. An answer that names a person gets the route (UNANSWERED QUESTIONS, T-UNQ-04).

He is not testing anybody. He is establishing whether the party is going to come back, because he does not want to be the reason a second set of people goes down and stops.
