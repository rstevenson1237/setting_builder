## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Going down:* the pegs are exact and the spacing does not vary, and a party can descend in the dark by feel. *Counting the pegs:* the count is the depth and the Covenant meant it to be. *Putting the light out:* nothing changes, because the pegs are the navigation. *Reaching the last peg:* there is thirty feet of nothing below it and standing water at the bottom. *Shouting down:* the sound goes and does not come back, and this is the only shaft in the region of which that is true.

**Gate:** The last thirty feet. The pegs run out above standing water whose depth has nothing to do with the depth of the shaft above it, and there is no way down except rope or falling. The answer that is not the rope is the wide shaft to the seaward, which is guarded going down and which arrives at the sill floor rather than in the water.

*Job for the region.* It is the way into the deep water proper, and it is the only shaft that arrives in water rather than on stone. A party that comes down here is wet from the first moment and stays wet.

*Cost in turns.* Four down to the last peg. One more on rope. Eight up, and the last eight feet cannot be climbed at all without something to stand on.

*What is true that the Overview withholds.* This is the best-made shaft in the Deep Sill and the dressing on it worsens with depth exactly like the others, which means the Covenant cut it in the same season and to the same standard and got frightened at the same rate. The difference is that this one has no order cut round its rim. It is the only unlettered rim on the Reach, and it is the deepest.

## Features

### The Dressed Shaft

Four hundred feet, dressed the whole way, the pegs set exactly and spaced for a load (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-05). Going down is a stated procedure, heaviest last (PROCEDURES, T-PRC-07).

The dressing worsens with depth in four stages, each stage a season's work, and the last stage is only cut.

### The Unlettered Rim

Squared, pegged, and bare. No order, no count, no name and no mark of any kind, on a Reach where the Covenant cut a line into everything it stopped at (LANGUAGE AND ROOT VOCABULARY, T-LNG-19).

The rim is prepared for lettering: the face is dressed flat and the guide lines are scribed. Nobody cut the words.

### The Rope Still On It

Covenant rope, laid rather than twisted, hanging from the top peg, holding its knot wet and not shrunk (TREASURE TABLE II, T-TR2-05).

{VALUE: 250 cn} {WT: 2} {QUALITY: Masterwork}

It reaches to the last peg and no further. Whoever hung it measured the shaft and not the drop.

### The Watch Glass

Wedged in the seventh peg socket, in a padded case, running slow in cold and slower below the sill, and nobody has ever explained why (TREASURE TABLE III, T-TR3-05).

{VALUE: 1200 cn} {WT: 1} {QUALITY: Masterwork}

It is running. A party who carries it down and back up finds it has lost time against their own count of turns, and the loss is proportional to the depth.

### The Ways From Here

The lettered rim is seaward -> R06-L02, the wide guarded rim is seaward of that -> R06-L03, and a shaft that stops being dressed lies north -> R06-L06. The shaft goes down into standing water -> R06-L10.
