## Player Overview

**The rim** is cut square, pegged all the way round, with an order cut into the stone in letters a hand deep. The strokes are uneven and they get worse across the line, and the last word is in a different hand.

**The pegs** are oak, driven from outside the frame and seated from within. There is a door peg lying among them that is not one of theirs.

**The shaft** goes down thirty feet, then thirty more, on pegs set into the wall spaced for a load rather than for a body. The stone is dressed the whole way and it is dressed less carefully the further down it goes.

The air at the bottom is a shade warmer than at the lip.

**The ways from here** are four rims and a shaft, and the shaft comes up into green light and standing water.

## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Standing at the rim:* the air is a shade warmer at the bottom of the shaft than at its lip, on a descent where every other thing gets colder. *Counting aloud:* nothing answers here. Four hundred feet lower something does, and a party who establishes the silence now has the comparison. *Drawing a peg:* nothing at this rim, and one rim along, something that was pegged is not. *Putting the light out:* nothing changes, and nothing here has ever held a light. *Reading the order aloud:* nothing comes up this shaft for a watch.

**Gate:** This is the region's gate and it is not a door. Every peg in this rim is seated as the Covenant seated them, and a party can simply go down past them (LORE, T-LOR-05). The answer that is not going down is turning round at the letters, which costs the whole descent and is what nine parties have done. Neither is the mistake and the region's own Fields say what going down has cost everybody who did it.

*Job for the region.* It is the boundary between the fen and the sill and it is where the Deep Sill's discipline starts: the count begins at this rim and a party who stops counting does not notice that they have stopped.

*Cost in turns.* One to cross the rim floor. Three down on rope. Two to peg or unpeg a rim.

*What is true that the Overview withholds.* This is the ninth of the recorded shafts and the Covenant's own tally says eleven. Nine have been found. The two that have not are not lost: the tally gives their road counts, and the road counts are on the tally posts in the fen, and nobody has ever put the two lists side by side.

## Features

### The Rim

Cut square, pegged all the way round, every peg seated, with the order cut into the stone in letters a hand deep (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-05), (LORE, T-LOR-05).

*Stop at the sill. What lies under it was not made, and it is not ours to open. Peg the shaft and set a watch that will outlast us.*

The last word is in a different hand from the rest. It is in a different hand on every rim in the Reach except one.

### The Pegs

Oak, driven from outside the frame and seated from within so they cannot be drawn without splitting the rim's own lip (PUZZLES, T-PUZ-02).

They can be split. It takes a turn and it makes a noise that goes down the shaft rather than up it, and a rim once unpegged cannot be pegged again, which is the region's only irreversible act above the sill.

A door peg of Covenant make lies among them, one that seats itself and cannot be drawn, sold as a lock and working as one on the side the user is standing on (TREASURE TABLE IV, T-TR4-06).

### The Shaft

Thirty feet, then thirty more, on pegs set into the wall by people carrying loads and spaced for a load rather than for a body. The stone is dressed the whole way and it is dressed less carefully the further down it goes.

The air at the bottom is a shade warmer than at the rim.

### The Ways From Here

The shaft comes up into green light and standing water over dressed floor -> R03-L20. A rim with letters cut deeper lies thirty feet along -> R06-L02. The widest rim, pegged for loads, is inward -> R06-L03. Cold comes up out of a rim to the north -> R06-L04. A rim with nothing coming up out of it and no sound going down lies east -> R06-L09.
