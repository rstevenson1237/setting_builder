## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Standing on the last dressed course:* nothing. *Standing past it:* the air changes weight and everybody notices at once. *Lighting a wick:* everything in the region within reach of it turns toward it, together, the way a face turns. *Putting the wick out:* everything stops, exactly where it is. *Counting aloud:* the count comes back one number behind, in the party's own voices, from below and ahead. *Carrying nothing:* nothing at all happens, and that is the point.

**Gate:** The last course of cut stone. Past it nothing was made, nothing is square, and nothing runs parallel to anything else. There is no door and nothing to open. The gate is light: a party can go past the last course, and without a lamp that brightens with depth they cannot read a foot of it, and every ordinary light dims to nothing here (KEYS, T-KEY-06), (TREASURE TABLE IV, T-TR4-01). The answer that is not the lamp is to come down carrying nothing, which What Holds the Sill permits once, to anyone who does, and which arrives with no rope, no light and no way to bring anything up (NAMED CREATURES, T-CRE-07).

*Approaches.* Down the wide shaft, which arrives on the last dressed course and shows the angle of the floor before anything else. Across the unsquared floor from the shaft foot, which arrives below the course and shows the cut stone from underneath, as a lip. Out of the deep water, which arrives at the level of the floor and shows nothing until a light is lit.

*Why it is still here.* Because it is a floor. Nothing here is portable, nothing here is worth anything, and what a party takes away is a measurement and a fact. Both are worth more than anything anybody has carried up out of the Deep Sill.

*What comes out.* Where the Covenant's work stops and what is under it. The last course is the answer to a question the whole Reach is built on, and it can be stated in one sentence by anybody who has stood on it (UNANSWERED QUESTIONS, T-UNQ-04), (LORE, T-LOR-05).

*Departure.* The shaft up is findable from here because the party came down it. Nothing else in the Deep Water is findable from below. A party that goes on from here and turns back is relying on the pegging behind them, and a party that unpegged as they came has spent the region's only redundancy on speed.

## Features

### The Last Course

Dressed stone, laid true, running level and stopping. Not at a wall, not at a doorway: it stops mid-course, and the stone the course is laid against is not stone anybody cut.

The dressing worsens over the last thirty feet before it stops. Then it is only cut. Then it is not cut. All three of those states are visible at once from the shaft foot and they are visible in that order.

The wide shaft runs up -> R06-L03, and the shaft foot lies seaward across level floor -> R06-L10.

### The Angle

Where the last course ends, the floor and the sides stop meeting each other at an angle anybody chose. Nothing is square. Nothing is level. Water stands in it at a depth that has nothing to do with the depth of the shaft above it.

It is not rough and it is not broken. It is finished, and it is finished to a shape, and the shape is not one the Covenant would have made.

### The Lamp Reading

A pin-lamp that brightens with depth reads it (UNIQUE TREASURES, T-TRE-05), (KEYS, T-KEY-06). Nothing else does: an ordinary light dims to nothing here, and the plate on the ordinary one says the opposite and is wrong (TREASURE TABLE IV, T-TR4-01).

Under a lamp that works, the surface past the last course is not blank. It carries the same tooling all the way out, at the same interval, further than the light goes.

### The Warm Line

The air here is a shade warmer at the floor than at the rim of the shaft, on a descent where every other thing gets colder. The line is sharp: it can be found by holding a hand out and walking, and it runs along the last dressed course, and it is on the far side of it (RUMOURS, T-RUM-03).

The Covenant's work is on the cold side. Everything under the Reach that a hand finds warm is warm because of what is on this side of that line.

### The Way Back Up

The deep water lies inward and everything in it turns toward a wick -> R06-L13, and the floor where the counting starts runs on past the last course -> R06-L15.

Nothing in the Deep Water is findable from below. What stands in place of a second route out is the pegging: a shaft left pegged as the Covenant pegged it can be opened again from above.
