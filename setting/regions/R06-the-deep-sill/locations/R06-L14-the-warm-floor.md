## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Standing on it:* the floor draws a body down onto it and holds sleep well past the watch it was meant to last (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-06). *Saying so:* everybody has been about to. *Lighting a wick:* what turns toward it is further off here than anywhere else in the region, which reads as safety and is not. *Counting aloud:* the answering count starts here, faintly, and gets clearer inward. *Sleeping:* the party wakes a watch late and a hand lower than they lay down.

**Gate:** None. This is the only dry, warm, level ground below the sill, and the region's whole content here is that it is exactly what it appears to be and that is the problem.

*Job for the region.* It is the trap that is not a trap. Everything in the Deep Sill is cold, wet and lightless, and this is warm and dry and it is where a party will want to rest, and resting here costs them a watch and a hand of depth and is not otherwise punished.

*Cost in turns.* One to cross. A watch to sleep, whatever was intended.

*What is true that the Overview withholds.* The warmth is the same warmth that is under every cut stone on the Reach, and this is where it is coming from. It is not a hot spring and there is no source: the floor is warm across about two hundred feet and it is warmest in the middle and it has no edge that a hand can find, and it is the thing every warm slab in the Reach is a symptom of. A party who has put a hand under a road course anywhere seaward is standing on the reason.

## Features

### The Bare Floor

Rising out of the water, dry, level within a hand across two hundred feet, and the only level ground in the deep water. It is the same stone as everything else here and it is the one place it has been made level.

Nothing made it level. It is level the way a pond is level.

### The Warmth

Warm through a boot standing still, warmer in the middle than at the edges, and there is no edge a hand can find: it fades over about thirty feet at every side (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-06) {TEST: Fate} {CONDITION: Slab-slept / the party wakes a watch late and a hand lower than they lay down / until they are back on standing ground}.

Every cut stone on the Reach is warm underneath and warmer the further down the road runs, and the reedcutters lie down on it and do not say so (RUMOURS, T-RUM-03).

### The Slate Leaves

Nine of them, laid flat on the warm floor in a row, in a bronze frame, face up (MAGICAL TOMES, T-TOM-06).

{VALUE: 4500 cn} {WT: 4} {QUALITY: Cursed}

They are meant to be read through the palm rather than the eye and they give the depth of the road under the reader. Read here the reading takes a watch and the reader is a hand lower at the end of it, and the depth they give is a depth below this floor.

Somebody laid a set into the road four regions away, facing up, where anybody lying down would find them. This is the other set.

### The Warming Slab

Book-sized, on the floor at the edge of the warmth, holding a body's heat through a night and taking a watch of that body's sleep to charge (TREASURE TABLE IV, T-TR4-05).

{VALUE: 2000 cn} {WT: 2} {QUALITY: Masterwork}

It is charged. Nobody has slept down here for four generations.

### The Ways Off

The floor drops toward the shaft foot seaward -> R06-L10. The counting is inward -> R06-L15. The floor slopes away where the light stops reaching a wall -> R06-L19.
