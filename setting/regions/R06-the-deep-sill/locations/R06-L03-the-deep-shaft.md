## Player Overview

**The rim** is wider than the others, sixteen feet, with the order cut round it and the last word in a second hand.

Standing on it is **the warden**, and it does not give a warning. It recites, from the beginning, and it starts again if it is interrupted.

**The pack on the rim** sits where somebody set it down and did not pick it up again: instruments, a lamp, and a slate leaf. Soot has settled on it evenly and nothing has touched it.

**The shaft** goes down four hundred feet in stages on pegs spaced for a load, and the dressing worsens with depth until at one particular course it stops being dressed and is only cut, and then it stops being cut.

**The other rims** stand at intervals along the covenant ground in four directions.

## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Approaching the rim:* something at it recites the stop-digging order instead of giving a warning, in the Covenant's layer (NAMED CREATURES, T-CRE-08). *Going down past it:* it acts. *Going up past it:* it lets a party by, every time, without exception, and this is the whole difference between it and every other warden. *Speaking to it:* it recites again, from the beginning. *Putting the light out:* it recites in the dark. *Setting a load down at the rim:* nothing, and the load is where it was left.

**Gate:** The warden. Going down this shaft is refused and coming up it is not (NAMED CREATURES, T-CRE-08). The answer that is not this shaft is any of the other eight rims, all of which are pegged and none of which is guarded, and which cost a turn each to split and are the region's only irreversible act.

*Job for the region.* It is the widest shaft, it is the one the loads went down, and it is the only route to the sill floor that a party can carry anything down. Everything else is a body's width.

*Cost in turns.* One at the rim. Four down on rope, because the pegs are spaced for a load. Six up carrying anything.

*What is true that the Overview withholds.* It wants nothing, which is what makes it different from everything else in this table. It cannot be traded with and it can be satisfied: it lets anybody past who is going up. A party who goes down another shaft and comes back to this one is not stopped, and a party who works that out has the region's only reliable way home.

## Features

### The Rim

Wider than the others, sixteen feet, with pegs set into the shaft wall below it spaced for a load and not for a body (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-05).

The order is cut round it in letters a hand deep and the last word is in a second hand, which makes it a copy.

### The Warden

A shaft-warden of the same make as any other, except that this one has begun saying the stop-digging order aloud instead of giving the warning it was set to give (NAMED CREATURES, T-CRE-08), (BESTIARY, T-BES-10).

{AD: 4, +3} {TYPE: Construct} {WOUND: Crushing}

It opens by reciting. It does not stop mid-recitation and it starts again if it is interrupted, and it is standing on the rim rather than beside it.

### The Pack on the Rim

A single pack, dry, sitting on the rim where somebody set it down and did not pick it up again (HOARDS, T-HRD-06). Instruments, a lamp, and a slate leaf out of a set of nine.

{VALUE: 7000 cn} {WT: 5} {QUALITY: Masterwork}

Soot has settled on it evenly, which takes years, and nothing has touched it. The warden does not object to it being taken, then or later.

Whoever set it down was going up, which the warden would have permitted.

### The Shaft

Four hundred feet in stages, the pegs spaced for a load, the dressing worsening with depth until at one particular course it stops being dressed and is only cut, and then it stops being cut.

Going down it is a stated procedure on rope, heaviest last (PROCEDURES, T-PRC-07). It comes out on the last dressed course of the sill floor -> R06-L11.

### The Other Rims

The pegged rim is seaward -> R06-L01. The lettered rim is thirty feet along -> R06-L02. An unlit rim squared to the compass lies north -> R06-L05. The dressed shaft is inward -> R06-L08.
