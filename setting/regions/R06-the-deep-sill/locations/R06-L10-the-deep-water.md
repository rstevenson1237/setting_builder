## Player Overview

**The shaft foot** is thirty feet above the water and there is nothing between the last peg and the surface.

**The water** stands unbroken and does not move, and it is a shade warmer at the ankle than the air over it, which is the reverse of everything above.

**The floor under it** is not square, not level, and nothing on it runs parallel to anything else. It is not rough and it is not broken: under a hand it is smooth and continuous, and it has a grain.

**The ways from here** leave in five directions, and everything within reach of a lit wick turns toward it together.

## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Wading:* the water is a shade warmer at the ankle than the air over it, which is the reverse of everything above the sill. *Lighting a wick:* everything within reach of it turns toward it together, the way a face turns. *Putting it out:* everything stops, exactly where it is. *Counting aloud:* the count comes back one number behind from further in. *Trailing a rope:* what is in the water takes what is trailed rather than what is standing (BESTIARY, T-BES-09).

**Gate:** None here. This is open water on a floor nobody cut, and there is nothing in it to open. What gates it is the light: an ordinary wick dims with depth here and gives out entirely further in, and everything in the region responds to a lit wick as one (TREASURE TABLE IV, T-TR4-01).

*Job for the region.* It is the deep water's junction and the only part of it with a shaft foot in it. Five ways leave it and it is the only place below the sill a party can be sure of finding again.

*Cost in turns.* Two to cross. Ten feet a turn wading, and the rate is set by the light rather than by the footing.

*What is true that the Overview withholds.* The water stands at a depth that has nothing to do with the depth of the shaft above it. It is the same depth here as it is at the sill floor four hundred feet higher, and as it is at the far end of the deep water, and it does not vary. The water is not filling anything. It is at a level, and the level is not a water level.

## Features

### The Shaft Foot

The pegs run out thirty feet above the water and there is nothing between the last peg and the surface. A party coming down arrives on rope and a party going up cannot reach the first peg from the water.

Something has to be stood on. Whatever is used is left, because it cannot be carried up past the last eight feet.

### The Water

Standing, unbroken, a shade warmer at the ankle than the air, and it does not move (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-02) {TEST: Constitution} {WOUND: Frost}. The cold in it is not the water's: it comes up through the soles from below rather than out of the water.

An arm-thick thing works it, unhurried, taking what is trailed (BESTIARY, T-BES-09).

{AD: 2, +1} {TYPE: Beast} {WOUND: Piercing}

### The Floor Under It

Nothing is square. Nothing is level. Nothing runs parallel to anything else, and the sides do not meet the floor at any angle anybody chose.

It is not rough and it is not broken. Under a hand it is smooth and continuous and it has a grain, and the grain runs in one direction across the whole of the deep water.

### The Ways From Here

The shaft goes up -> R06-L08. The floor levels toward the last cut stone -> R06-L11. The water closes over the chest to the north -> R06-L12. The floor rises and warms inward -> R06-L14. The water drops to the knee over fine silt -> R06-L16. The floor slopes down and nothing on it runs parallel -> R06-L19.
