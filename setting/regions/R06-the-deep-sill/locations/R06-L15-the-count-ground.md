## Player Overview

**The count** comes back one number behind, in the party's own voices, from ahead of them and below. It does not decay, it does not change pitch, and it does not stop when the party stops. It has every voice, including the voices of people who have not spoken since the shaft.

**The weight of the air** changes, and everybody notices at the same moment.

**The terms** are stated once and are not repeated and are not explained.

**The set-down load** is whatever anybody puts down here, and it is where they left it when they come back, in the position it was put in.

A bronze engine stands on nothing, wound, telling nothing.

## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Counting aloud:* the count comes back one number behind, in the party's own voices, from ahead of them and below. *Stopping the count:* the answering count goes on for three more numbers and then stops. *Lighting a second wick:* the answer comes from two places. *Setting a load down:* nothing, then or later, and the load is where it was left. *Picking a load back up:* the air changes weight. *Going on inward carrying anything:* the air changes weight and does not change back.

**Gate:** The terms. What Holds the Sill will let a party go on, once, and only a party carrying nothing (NAMED CREATURES, T-CRE-07). It does not repeat the offer and it does not explain the terms, and it does not prevent anybody who declines from walking on. The answer that is not the terms is to walk on loaded, which works, and which is why nobody who has done it has come back up: a party inward of here with a load is a party the region has stopped counting with and started counting.

*Approaches.* From the sill floor, which arrives where the count starts and shows nothing at all. From the warm floor to the north, which arrives with the count already running and shows where it is coming from, which is a place with nothing in it. From the deep floor below, which arrives beneath the count and is the only approach from which the answering voices are above the party.

*Why it is still here.* Because there is nothing here. No stone, no work, no object, no body. This is the only landmark on the Reach that a party can walk into, stand in the middle of, and see nothing whatever, and it is the one that changes what the rest of the setting means.

*What comes out.* The terms, and the fact that they were offered. A party who is offered passage and declines it walks out knowing that something under the Reach negotiates, keeps to what it says, and asks for exactly one thing. Nothing anybody has carried up out of the Deep Sill is worth that (UNANSWERED QUESTIONS, T-UNQ-08).

*Departure.* The floor rises seaward and the count falls level again over about a hundred feet. A party who took the terms goes on. A party who declined goes back, and the way back is the way they came, and it is not interfered with.

There is no stone here, no work, no object and no body. Somebody carried the engine down, wound it, set it down, and took the terms. Its count and the answering count are not the same count.

## Features

### The Count

The party's own count, in the party's own voices, one number behind, from ahead of them and below. It is not an echo: it does not decay, it does not change pitch, and it does not stop when the party stops.

It has every voice. It has the voices of people who have not spoken since the shaft.

A party who has been counting turns since the rim, which is the region's whole discipline, has been giving it the numbers.

### The Weight of the Air

It arrives as a change in weight rather than as a sound or a smell, and everybody notices at the same moment, which is the only thing about it that can be tested (NAMED CREATURES, T-CRE-07).

{AD: 6, +6} {TYPE: Horror} {TEST: Sanity}

It uses the party's own count, one number behind, and it uses it to speak. It does not use words that are not already in the count.

### The Terms

Passage down, once, to anyone who comes carrying nothing. Nothing means nothing: no rope, no light, no tools, no weapon and no load. It is stated once and it is not repeated and it is not explained.

What it wants is the digging not resumed, and it does not say so here. That is on the rim, in the Covenant's hand, four hundred feet up (LORE, T-LOR-05).

The sill floor runs back seaward -> R06-L11, the warm floor lies north -> R06-L14, and the floor drops away inward -> R06-L19.

### The Set-Down Load

Whatever a party sets down here is where they left it when they come back, undisturbed, in the position it was put in. It is the one place in the Deep Sill where that is reliably true, and a party that has worked that out has worked out that the terms are being kept before they are taken.

A counting engine stands here, wound, on nothing, telling nothing (TREASURE TABLE IV, T-TR4-04). Somebody carried it down, wound it, set it down, and took the terms.
