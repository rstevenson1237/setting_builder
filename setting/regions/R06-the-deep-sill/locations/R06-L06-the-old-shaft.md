## Player Overview

**The narrowing shaft** is dressed for the first sixty feet and only cut below that, and from there it narrows at about a hand every thirty feet.

The pegs stop where the dressing does.

Below that there is nothing in the wall to take a hand, and the sides are too far apart to brace and too close to turn in.

The air at the bottom is warmer than at the rim, and it is the only shaft here where that can be felt with a hand from the top.

## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Going down:* the dressing stops at one course and the shaft is only cut below it, and it narrows. *Going back up:* the pegs are set for loads and are spaced for a load, and the narrowing means a body cannot turn. *Putting the light out:* nothing changes and the shaft is navigable by touch, because it is narrow enough to brace across. *Counting aloud:* nothing answers.

**Gate:** The narrowing. Past the point the dressing stops, this shaft goes down and does not come back up: there is nothing in the wall to take a hand and the sides are too far apart to brace and too close to turn in. It is one way in practice and in fact.

*Cost:* one turn at the rim, four down, and there is no coming up.

*What is true:* it is warm. The air at the bottom of this shaft is a shade warmer than at its rim, on a descent where every other thing gets colder, and it is the only shaft in the region where that is measurable from the top with a hand (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-07) {TEST: Fate} {WOUND: Crushing}.

*Nil:* no way back. This is the one edge in the setting that is typed one-way in the connections table, and the fiction is the shaft's own shape.

## Features

### The Narrowing Shaft

Dressed for the first sixty feet, then only cut, then narrowing at about a hand every thirty feet, with the pegs stopping where the dressing does (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-05), (LANGUAGE AND ROOT VOCABULARY, T-LNG-74).

Going down it is a stated procedure on rope, heaviest last, and the rope will not be recoverable (PROCEDURES, T-PRC-07).

It comes out in old water at the bottom, and the drop from the last handhold is more than a body's length -> R06-L18.
