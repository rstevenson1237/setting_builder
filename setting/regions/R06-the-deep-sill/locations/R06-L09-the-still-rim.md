## Referee Overview

**Reactions:** *Speaking at the rim:* the sound goes down and nothing comes back, and this is true of no other opening in the region. *Counting aloud:* the count does not answer and does not echo. *Lighting a wick:* nothing turns toward it, which is true nowhere else below the sill. *Dropping something down:* it is not heard landing.

**Gate:** None. The rim is pegged and sound and can be opened in a turn, and what makes this a decision is that a party who goes down it cannot be called back and cannot call.

*Cost:* one turn at the rim, one to split the pegs, three down on rope, and the descent is silent in both directions.

*What is true:* the silence is the shaft's shape rather than anything in it. It is cut on a slight curve rather than plumb, which no other shaft here is, and sound turns the corner and does not come back {TEST: Sanity}. A party split across it cannot work together (HAZARDS, T-HAZ-12).

*Nil:* no warden, no order and no watch-keeper's slate, on a rim cut and pegged exactly like the ones that have all three.

## Features

### The Silent Rim

Squared, pegged, sealed, and the order cut round it in letters a hand deep, with the last word in the same hand as the rest (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-05), (MAGICAL TOMES, T-TOM-03).

That makes two rims in the Reach whose order is by one hand: this and the one four hundred feet above the drowned tier. Both of them are the ones nobody set a watch at.

Something below is telling a tally aloud and it cannot be heard from the rim, and it can be heard from thirty feet down the shaft, and it stops when it is interrupted and starts again with whoever interrupted (BESTIARY, T-BES-08).
