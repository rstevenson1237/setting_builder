## Overview

A rim of cut stone, square, with pegs driven round it and an order cut
into the rim in letters a hand deep. The strokes are uneven and the last word is
finished by a different hand. Nine of these have been found. The Covenant's own
tally says eleven.

Down. Thirty feet, then thirty more, on pegs set into the shaft wall by people
carrying loads, spaced for a load and not for a body. The stone is dressed the
whole way and it is dressed less carefully the further down it goes, and then at
one particular course it stops being dressed and is only cut, and then it stops
being cut.

Past that the sides do not meet the floor at an angle anybody chose. Nothing is
square. Nothing is level. Nothing runs parallel to anything else, and water
stands in it at a depth that has nothing to do with the depth of the shaft above
it. The air here is a shade warmer at the bottom than at the top, on a descent
where every other thing gets colder.

No light has ever been in here that somebody did not bring. Everything in the
Deep Sill turns towards a lit wick the way a face turns, and everything in it
stops when the wick goes out. A party counting their turns aloud will hear the
count come back to them, in their own voices, one number behind, and it will not
be coming from above.

## Fields

**Extent:** Unmeasured. Eleven shafts are recorded on the Covenant's own
tally and nine of them have been found. The deep water beyond them has no
recorded extent, because nobody who has gone far enough into it to say has come
back and said it.

**Rate:** Thirty feet a turn in a shaft, going down, on pegs set for people
carrying loads. Ten feet a turn in the deep water, and the rate is set by the
light rather than by the footing.

**Time:** Turns, six to the hour, counted aloud, and the counting is the whole
discipline of the region. A party that stops counting in the Deep Sill does not
notice that it has stopped.

**Light:** None, and nothing here has ever held any. Light is the intrusion and
everything in the region responds to it as one.

**Reactions:** Everything below the last pegged rim reacts to light and to being
counted, in that order. Nothing here reacts to speech, and nothing here is
hostile in the sense of coming after a party who leaves.

**What the difficulty measures here:** The evil of the place and how readily it
finds a party. This is the hardest region in the setting. It is not built, it
was not made, and the Covenant stopped digging when it reached the sill and
pegged everything behind it, which is the most useful thing anybody has ever
recorded about it.

**Gates:** One, and it is not a door. The last pegged rim is the gate, the
answer is to go down carrying nothing, and the price is that a party who takes
the answer arrives with no light, no rope and no way to bring anything up.

**Weather:** None. The Deep Sill is under the slab from its first rim onward and
nothing here has weather. The air is a shade warmer at the bottom of a shaft
than at its rim, and that is the only thing about it that changes.

## Connections

The Covenant Shafts are the spine, and they are the only worked ground
in the Deep Sill. Every one of them is a vertical cut the Covenant made to reach
below the slab, rim cut square, pegged over, with an order cut into the rim in
letters a hand deep and cut in haste (ARCHITECTURE AND TERRAIN, T-ARC-05). The
order says to stop, and the shafts go down past the point it says to stop at
(LORE, T-LOR-05).

The Deep Water is what the shafts cut into. It was not made, it is not squared,
and nothing in it answers to the compass. A party crosses from the shafts into
it by leaving the last pegged rim behind, and that crossing is one way in
practice: the shafts are findable from above and nothing in the Deep Water is
findable from below.

There is no second route down and there is no second route back. What stands in
place of one is the pegging itself. A shaft left pegged as the Covenant pegged
it is a shaft that can be opened again, and a party that unpegs everything
behind them has spent the region's only redundancy on speed.

Above, the shafts open onto the Ashen Fen and onto the Deep Tier of Bellgate.
Nothing else reaches this region.
