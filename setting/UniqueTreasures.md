Unique Treasures of Telar

The Vaalsenn Tablet - found at D.13 Chamber of the Coiled Serpent
Description: A fist-sized basalt tablet, carved with the same binding-script that seals doors and traps names throughout Daghash. Held in both hands and spoken over correctly, it lets its bearer perform one Essath name-binding on any true name they already hold, per the same rule that spends a name spoken aloud (setting/Truths.md) - but the tablet itself is not spent, and can be used again later on a different name. What it costs is the same toll any Essath working exacts: a year off the caster's own life per use, rolled openly, the toll showing on the body exactly as setting/Truths.md describes - and a working scaled to more than a single person or room costs 1d6 years instead of one, per setting/Procedures.md. Sealed behind the coiled relief in the priest-kings' own inner chamber for as long as the Bound Dead have stood guard over it, the tablet has never left Daghash - and the Serpent Cult, who hoard an unspent Essath name of their own for exactly this purpose, would trade a great deal to learn it exists here at all.

The Kaash Collar - found at D.18 Nirguk's Hoard
Description: A collar of small worked stone links, each cut with a sliver of the same death-script that binds Nakhash's own gates, strung on a cord of braided hide long since replaced more than once. Worn against the skin, it lets its bearer see, faintly and at the edge of sight, whatever of the recently dead still lingers near a place - a use readable against setting/Truths.md's own rule that every Essath working is paid for, and this one is no different: the sight does not fade when the collar comes off, only when whatever it showed the bearer finally does, which can be hours or days depending on what was seen, and for as long as it lasts the bearer is exactly as visible to what lingers as it is to them. It is the third piece of Nakhash's own regalia, per setting/Keys.md's Membership Coil and Rod of the Coil - all three, not any two, are what its gates actually want. How it came to rest in Nirguk's own hoard rather than the tomb it was buried for is not answered anywhere in Daghash; what is certain is that the cell currently reopening Nakhash (setting/History.md) does not yet know where to look for it.
