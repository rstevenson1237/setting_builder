Unique Treasures of The Isle of the Serpent Crown

### The Serpent Crown
Found at: HC.7
Does: ward
Cost: sought, forbidden
Origin: grave - The Snake People Raise the Pyramid
Description: The last snake emperor's crown, gold coiled round a green stone the size of a fist, lying on the skull in its bier. Its wearer takes Tests of Constitution against Poison with Advantage, and a buyer on the mainland pays 5000 cn for it. The cult hunts whoever carries it, and no village will have it in a house.
