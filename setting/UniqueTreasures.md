Unique Treasures of Thornmarch

### The Founder's Reliquary
Found at: M.2 The Outer Shrine
Does: ward
Cost: sought
Origin: event - The Realm Raises the Keep
Description: A silver casket the length of a hand, the keep's white tower enamelled on blue on its lid, holding a knucklebone of the keep's founder. Its bearer rolls again the first failed Test against an Undead's blow each day. The chapel at the keep pays 300 cn and a blessing for its return, and the Black Altar's agents will look for whoever carries it.

### The Thalosae Lamp
Found at: M.11 The Sanctum
Does: knowledge
Cost: noticed, sought
Origin: reclaimed - The Shrine Reopens
Description: A bronze lamp cut with the makers' script, burning a black flame without oil. In its light the makers' script reads plain in the common tongue, and a sealed makers' door shows its seam. The flame is seen from far off in the dark, and the Black Altar sends the Zurnaz after whoever carries it away.
