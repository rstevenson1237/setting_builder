Unique Treasures of Telar

The Vaalsenn Tablet - found at D.13 Chamber of the Coiled Serpent
Description: A fist-sized basalt tablet, carved with the same binding-script that seals doors and traps names throughout Daghash. Held in both hands and spoken over correctly, it lets its bearer perform one Essath name-binding on any true name they already hold, per the same rule that spends a name spoken aloud (setting/Truths.md) - but the tablet itself is not spent, and can be used again later on a different name. What it costs is the same toll any Essath working exacts: a year off the caster's own life per use, rolled openly, the toll showing on the body exactly as setting/Truths.md describes - and a working scaled to more than a single person or room costs 1d6 years instead of one, per setting/Procedures.md. Sealed behind the coiled relief in the priest-kings' own inner chamber for as long as the Bound Dead have stood guard over it, the tablet has never left Daghash - and the Serpent Cult, who hoard an unspent Essath name of their own for exactly this purpose, would trade a great deal to learn it exists here at all.
