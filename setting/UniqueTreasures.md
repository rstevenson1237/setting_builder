Unique Treasures of The Buried City

### The King's Mask
Found at: G.7
Does: knowledge
Cost: forbidden
Origin: grave - The Last King Is Entombed
Description: A gold funerary mask with the last king's face, set with two black stones for eyes. A wearer who speaks to the dead in the cynidicean tongue is answered truthfully once a day; the Ruhiahe and the cult both count taking it a theft from the dead.
