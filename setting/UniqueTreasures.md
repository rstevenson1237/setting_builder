Unique Treasures of The Ashwrack Coast

The Vaelmourn Circlet - found at C.24 The Last Reliquary
Description: A slender circlet of black, corrosion-proof metal, its inner band engraved with the same bell-and-water motif found throughout the Cairn. Worn, it lets the wearer breathe and see clearly underwater and understand the speech of drowned things - the very working Vael's last court used to strike its forbidden pact with the tide. The cost is the same one that doomed Vael: wearing it, even briefly, marks the wearer as a debtor to the Drowned Court, in the same accounting the coast's tally-custom already fears - and a debt owed to the Court is not one any hold's Tally-Keeper can settle.
