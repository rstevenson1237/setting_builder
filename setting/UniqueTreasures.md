Unique Treasures of Carrdun

The Undrowned Idol - found at C.10 The Second District
Description: A fist-sized basalt figure the Nemhet called Weriru, "the great giving,"
carved in a shape no one at Carrdun recognises as animal or person. Held against anything
- cloth, flesh, wood - it stops that thing decaying for as long as contact is unbroken; a
wound held against it stops bleeding and stops healing in the same breath, and cloth
wrapped around it survives centuries in standing water untouched. It is the same principle
the Truths of Carrdun already describe in Nemhet stone, concentrated into something
portable - which means whatever it preserves stays exactly as it was the moment contact
began, injuries included, for as long as anyone is willing to keep touching it.

The Priest-King's Diadem - found at D.18 The Priest-King's Rest
Description: A wire-thin gold circlet the Nemhet called Wernekhu, "the great flame,"
resting untouched in a niche behind Werkamu's own sarcophagus - even the Tomb Warden has
never moved it. Worn, it grants fluent understanding and speech in Nemhet, as though the
wearer had always known it. What it costs is Werkamu himself: each time it is worn, a
fragment of his last hours intrudes unbidden - the sound of water finding the lower halls,
the certainty that someone he trusted had already left without him - and the fragments do
not fade with use. They accumulate.
