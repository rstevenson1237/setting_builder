Unique Treasures of Fellward

Nazirith - found at C.1 The Sealed Chamber
Description: A thin iron circlet, foundation-stone inlay the same grey as the vault's own walls - the king the old foundations' builders buried at C.1 was laid to rest without it on his brow, set instead on the shelf beside him (Named Creature: Nazirur). Worn, it reads to anything of the builders' own making as the seat's rightful authority: the Old-Ward Sentinel at C.2 The Warded Gatehouse will not wake for whoever wears it, seal or no seal. It costs exactly what taking a king's grave goods should - any Barrow-Kin who sees it worn treats the wearer as having already done the one thing that ends dealings with them for good, and nothing offered afterward buys that back.
