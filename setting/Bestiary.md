Bestiary of Kordath

Corsair Deckhand (Man) - AD: 1d6+1 [MA: 1]
Description: A sailor turned raider on whichever captain currently pays best, armed with a boarding axe or a short blade and whatever was looted last season.
Range: Found in crews of six to twenty aboard any corsair hull; eats whatever the ship's stores hold, and goes hungry with the rest of the crew when they run short.
Sign: Boot-prints in wet sand leading up from a beach with no boat left in sight, or a fresh knot tied wrong for any local trade.
Disposition: Will take a surrender and a ransom over a fight it can avoid; a crew that outnumbers a party fights without hesitation.
Special: none

Garrison Spear (Man) - AD: 3d6+2 [MA: 1]
Description: A drilled spearman holding a border post or a warlord's hall, disciplined where a corsair crew is not, and paid in coin or land rather than plunder.
Range: Found in squads of four to twelve at any garrison, keep, or toll post; fed and quartered by whoever commands the post.
Sign: A patrol's cadence carried on the wind before the patrol itself comes into view.
Disposition: Holds formation and challenges before striking; breaks off a chase at its post's boundary rather than pursue past it.
Special: none

Glass-back Crab Swarm (Beast) - AD: 2d6-1 [MA: 2]
Description: Hand-sized crabs whose shells have grown fused with shards of the drowned court's glass and tile, moving and feeding as one mass.
Range: Tide pools and wreck-strand within a day of the drowned court; eats carrion the tide brings in, and nothing that is still moving fast.
Sign: A dry clicking underfoot, and shell-shard cuts on anything left still too long.
Disposition: Scatters from anything larger than itself; swarms only what is already bleeding, drowned, or unable to move.
Special: counts and fights as one creature; clearing it from a doorway or a body costs one action per party member, per Searching in setting/Procedures.md.

Salt Wolf (Beast) - AD: 5d6+2 [MA: 3]
Description: A lean, pale-coated pack hunter out of the Salt Waste, its hide crusted stiff with dried salt that turns a glancing edge.
Range: Ranges the Salt Waste's margins and whatever borders it, in packs of four to nine, following herds and livestock rather than a fixed den.
Sign: Salt-crust rubbed off on a fence post or doorframe at wolf height, and tracks that vanish on hard salt pan.
Disposition: Shadows a party for a distance before committing, and breaks off if the first exchange goes badly for it.
Special: when two or more Salt Wolves flank one target, that target acts at disadvantage until it is no longer flanked.

Reef Strider (Beast) - AD: 7d6+4 [MA: 1]
Description: A wading, long-limbed thing that stands motionless in shallow harbor water for hours at a stretch before it moves.
Range: Solitary, one to a stretch of coastline; wades the shallows at night and takes whatever comes within reach - fish, gulls, an unwary swimmer.
Sign: A silhouette in the shallows that looks, and keeps looking, exactly like a piling.
Disposition: Does not move first; strikes only what comes within reach and otherwise holds still indefinitely.
Special: a party must succeed a Test before treating it as anything but harbor scenery, until it moves.

Cliff-Ram (Beast) - AD: 9d6+3 [MA: 2]
Description: A gargantuan, horned ram that climbs the ape-kin cliff terraces as easily as level ground, territorial rather than predatory.
Range: Solitary, holding one stretch of cliff terrace against all comers; grazes whatever grows on the terraces themselves.
Sign: Deep hoofmarks pressed into bare stone, and loose scree freshly knocked from a ledge above.
Disposition: Charges anything that climbs into its stretch of terrace; leaves anyone who retreats back down alone.
Special: a full charge knocks its target prone in addition to any damage dealt.

Khaggur Climber (Humanoid) - AD: 4d6+2 [MA: 1]
Description: A scattered descendant of the ape-kin who cut Kordath's oldest stonework, long-armed and quiet, still at home in the deepest ruins.
Range: Small bands of three to eight, living within the ape-kin ruins their ancestors cut; eat what the ruins' own crevices and pools support.
Sign: Hand- and foot-holds worn into stone no party's own climb would explain, spaced too wide for a human grip.
Disposition: Watches before it is seen, and will trade or talk where a party leaves its ruin-stone undamaged; treats any breaking of the old carvings as cause to fight.
Special: climbs sheer stone at full speed, without needing the Test a party would.

Horse-Clan Rider (Humanoid) - AD: 3d6+1 [MA: 2]
Description: A mounted rider of one of the inland horse clans, armed with a short bow and a curved blade, loyal to a chief chosen by combat rather than birth.
Range: Patrols in bands of five to fifteen across the clan's own stretch of open country, following the herds the clan lives on.
Sign: Hoofprints circling wide around a party's own camp rather than crossing it.
Disposition: Challenges strangers to state their business before anything else, and honors a clear answer; treats evasion as reason for suspicion.
Special: none

Tide-Kin (Humanoid) - AD: 6d6+2 [MA: 2]
Description: A secretive, amphibious people living in and around the drowned court, pale from a life spent as much underwater as above it.
Range: Small settlements of ten to thirty within reach of the drowned court and its shallows; eat what the court's flooded halls and the shallows around them provide.
Sign: Wet handprints on dry stone leading toward open water, and nothing leading back.
Disposition: Avoids contact where it can, and vanishes into open water rather than negotiate; fights only when a party is already inside water it considers its own.
Special: holds its breath indefinitely and moves through water at full speed; a fight that reaches open water favors it.

Drowned Thrall (Undead) - AD: 3d6-1 [MA: 1]
Description: A body pulled under and never properly buried, moving now with none of the person left in it, water still running from its mouth with every step.
Range: Found singly or in twos near any wreck, drowning-site, or stretch of water that has taken a body in living memory.
Sign: Waterlogged footprints leading out of the sea rather than into it, still wet an hour later.
Disposition: Walks toward the nearest living thing without hurry, and does not stop until destroyed or the living thing is gone.
Special: none

Ossuun Bound Dead (Undead) - AD: 6d6+2 [MA: 2]
Description: An old Ossuun corpse kept moving by a binding token buried with it, dry and cracked rather than rotted, dressed in what its office once wore.
Range: Found singly, wherever an Ossuun tomb or shrine still holds its binding token; does not range beyond the token's resting place.
Sign: Ossuun script scratched fresh into stone or dust nearby, though nothing living wrote it recently.
Disposition: Ignores anything that does not approach the token or its resting place; attacks without warning the moment either is touched.
Special: destroying the binding token ends the fight at once, whatever HP the creature has left.

Barrow Whisper (Undead) - AD: 9d6+3 [MA: 4]
Description: No body at all - a cold, dry voice that gathers wherever a king was buried angry, felt before it is heard.
Range: Bound to one barrow or sealed grave; never leaves the mound it rose from.
Sign: Breath fogging in a chamber that should not be cold, and a voice half-heard under one's own thoughts.
Disposition: Speaks before it acts, in half-sentences that are never quite an answer to what was asked.
Special: every round it is present, everyone within earshot must Test Sanity (per setting/Procedures.md) or take the hazard's forced Sanity loss, whether or not they are otherwise engaged with it.

Ward-Idol (Guardian) - AD: 4d6+3 [MA: 1]
Description: A carved stone figure - god, ancestor, or something with no name left - that moves only to hold its one threshold.
Range: Fixed at one shrine or doorway; does not range, forage, or want anything beyond what it guards.
Sign: Offerings left at its feet undisturbed for longer than any living cult would allow.
Disposition: Does nothing to anyone wearing the mark or token it was set to admit; refuses everyone else through a groan, then a struck stone, then a raised arm before it strikes.
Special: will not act against anyone carrying its shrine's token; escalates through three refusals before it strikes.

Khaggur Stone Warden (Guardian) - AD: 8d6+3 [MA: 2]
Description: A rough-hewn figure of the same stone as the ape-kin ruin it stands in, set to hold one threshold and nothing past it.
Range: Fixed at the entrance it was set to guard; does not range, forage, or leave that threshold under any circumstance.
Sign: Stone dust that never quite settles at the base of a doorway otherwise clear of debris.
Disposition: Ignores anyone who does not attempt to pass its threshold; blocks and fights anyone who does, without warning.
Special: cannot be moved from the threshold it guards, and regenerates 1d6 HP each round it is not struck by fire.

Ossuun Seal-Bound (Guardian) - AD: 12d6+5 [MA: 3]
Description: Ossuun's strongest binding, set over a sealed king's grave-goods and answering to the rite's own words rather than any living authority.
Range: Fixed at one sealed tomb; does not range, forage, or act until the seal itself is disturbed.
Sign: The seal's carved words, unweathered where everything around them has worn.
Disposition: Takes no action while the seal is unbroken and its rite-words are spoken correctly; once broken, fights without pause or bargaining.
Special: will not act against anyone who speaks the seal-rite's words correctly; once the seal is broken, it fights to destruction and cannot be bargained with again.

Riptide Shallows (Hazard) - AD: 3d6+0 [MA: 2]
Description: A stretch of shallow-looking water that hides a current strong enough to take a grown swimmer under without warning.
Range: Recurs anywhere the coastline meets old ruin or wreck debris that has changed how the water moves; does not move or spread on its own.
Sign: A patch of water that looks calmer than the water around it.
Disposition: none - forces its Test the moment anyone enters it, per setting/Procedures.md's Hazards.
Special: a failed Test of Fate here carries the character fifty yards out before the current releases them.

Ashfall Choke (Hazard) - AD: 5d6+1 [MA: 3]
Description: Ash from the Ash Road's burning, a century and a half settled but never fully bound into the soil, still capable of rising in a choking cloud.
Range: Recurs along any stretch of the Ash Road disturbed by digging, hard riding, or a fight; settles again within the hour.
Sign: Ground the color of old iron, and a paleness left on boots that crossed it.
Disposition: none - forces its Test on anyone who disturbs it, per setting/Procedures.md's Hazards.
Special: disturbing the ash forces the Test on everyone nearby, not only whoever caused it.

Salt-marsh Horror (Horror) - AD: 8d6+4 [MA: 3]
Description: Something that was once a person lost too long in the black lotus marsh, grown wrong in ways the marsh's own damp seems to feed.
Range: Solitary, confined to the marsh that made it; does not leave the wet ground it depends on.
Sign: A black lotus patch grown unnaturally thick around one spot, and a smell of rot the marsh itself does not otherwise carry.
Disposition: Watches from the reeds before it moves, and only attacks what has already found it rather than hunting.
Special: anyone it wounds carries the Poisoned condition (per setting/Procedures.md) until treated, whether or not the wound was otherwise serious.

Serpent Coil (Wyrm) - AD: 10d6+5 [MA: 2]
Description: A great serpent far larger than the Serpent Rite's own god-shaped idol, real where the idol is only carved.
Range: Solitary, holding one stretch of warm coastal water or ruin-cistern; eats whatever large prey that stretch supports.
Sign: A trail flattened through reeds or sand wider than any party member, and a shed skin longer than a boat.
Disposition: Avoids anything it cannot swallow whole; commits fully once it does attack.
Special: on a hit, it constricts instead of striking again - the target is Restrained until it breaks free or the coil is killed.

The Court's Deep (Horror) - AD: 17d6+6 [MA: 5]
Description: Whatever the drowned court's builders sealed their throne room against, still down there, still whole.
Range: Solitary, never leaving the drowned court's deepest flooded hall.
Sign: Every fish and crab within that hall gone, and a cold that reaches the surface on a still day.
Disposition: Does not surface for anything less than a direct assault on its hall; a party that does not go there does not meet it.
Special: does not chase past the drowned court's threshold, and nothing yet built in Kordath has damaged it enough to notice.
