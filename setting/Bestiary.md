Bestiary of The Isle of the Serpent Crown

### Caveman
Type: Men
AD: 1d6
Special: none
Sight: Shaggy, scarred hunters in hide wraps, with stone-headed clubs and spears of fire-hardened wood.
Range: Caves in the hill ridges, where they eat what they kill on the dinosaur trails. They raid anything smaller than a band of them.
Numbers: 8
Sign: a mark
Disposition: Hoots from the ridges to call the band, then rushes a party that has not gone round.
Description: A hand painted in red ochre on a rock or a cave mouth shows where they live. Shaggy, scarred hunters in hide wraps, with stone-headed clubs and spears of fire-hardened wood, they eat what they kill on the dinosaur trails and raid anything smaller than a band of them. They hoot from the ridges to call the band, then rush a party that has not gone round.
