Bestiary of Roadend

### Goblin
Type: Humanoid
AD: 1d6+0
Special: none
Description: Hip-high to a man, long-armed and grey-green, in scraps of stolen leather and a helm made from a cooking pot. Bare feet, yellow eyes that give back torchlight.
Range: Caves and rough ground away from daylight, in a tribe of dozens; eats whatever the road and the dark provide - mushrooms, rats, and what falls off wagons.
Sign: a sound - a dry clicking passed from one throat to the next ahead of a raid
Disposition: Fights in numbers and flees alone; a goblin outnumbered runs for the dark, and one that finds a lone traveller shouts for the rest.

### Wolf
Type: Beast
AD: 2d6+2
Special: none
MA: 2
Description: A lean grey wolf, ribs showing, low to the ground and watching sideways.
Range: Open and broken ground in packs of five or six; eats deer, strays, and the dead.
Sign: a track - splayed prints in soft ground, and a kill dragged under cover
Disposition: Circles before it closes, testing for the weak one; hunger makes it bold, a fire or a wounded pack-mate makes it back off.

### Soldier
Type: Men
AD: 2d6+1
Special: none
Description: A man in a padded jerkin and iron cap, carrying a spear and a round shield painted with the keep's grey tower.
Range: The keep and the road to it, in squads of six on patrol; draws rations and pay from the keep's stores.
Sign: a mark - the shield's grey tower, and a squad's boot-prints in the road dust
Disposition: Challenges strangers at the gate and on the road; polite to a traveller who answers plainly, hard on anyone carrying a weapon drawn.
