Bestiary of Sundfall

Party altitude: characters run 2-3 dice of d4-d6, apprentice panners and debt-runners, not
yet blooded - a d8 is already a notable edge, and nothing here starts with a d10 or d12.

Tideflat Eel-swarm (Beast) - AD: 1d6 MA: normal
Description: hundreds of finger-length eels that mass in the shallows at slack tide,
stripping flesh from anything that holds still too long.
Range: the tide-flats and flooded grain-cellars, in numbers the eel-grass alone could never
feed; they take what the tide brings dead as often as what's alive.
Sign: the water goes suddenly, unnaturally still, then ripples in a hundred places at once.
Disposition: indifferent to size or intent - they mass on stillness, not on threat, and
scatter the instant the water is disturbed hard enough.

Wader-heron, plague-carrier (Beast) - AD: 1d6
Description: tall grey herons that pick at the tide's drowned dead before anything else
reaches them, and carry a wasting sickness on their beaks and feet afterward.
Range: anywhere the tide leaves bodies exposed; a dozen or more work one stretch of flat.
Sign: herons standing over something in the shallows that isn't a fish.
Disposition: will not fight; flee any approach, but the sickness lingers where they fed.

Wrack-gull colony (Beast) - AD: 1d6
Description: gulls nesting thick in the ruined coping-stones, that mob anything that comes
near eggs or shiny salvage alike.
Range: the wall's broken spans and any exposed ruin above the tideline; a colony can run
into the hundreds.
Sign: wheeling, screaming birds visible long before the nest site itself.
Disposition: mob and steal small loose items rather than truly attack; disperse once
whatever was taken is out of reach.

Marsh Hound, feral (Beast) - AD: 2d6
Description: lean, wet-coated dogs gone wild in the drowned grainland, hunting in packs of
four to eight.
Range: the flooded grainland behind the breach; a pack needs the deer and drowned livestock
the ground still supports.
Sign: paw-prints crossing mud that should show no other tracks, and a rank wet-dog smell
that outpaces the pack itself.
Disposition: test a party at range before committing, and break off if the first exchange
goes badly for them.

Bonded panner-gang (Man) - AD: 1d6
Description: three to six desperate debt-panners, armed with gaffs and gutting-knives,
willing to rob at low tide to buy out a bond none of them will otherwise clear.
Range: work the flats nearest their own hold; won't be found far from water they know.
Sign: a boat pulled up somewhere it has no reason to be, and too many footprints for it.
Disposition: threaten first, hoping to be paid off rather than fought; genuinely frightened
of anything that doesn't frighten easily.

Bank-crab, giant (Beast) - AD: 3d6
Description: crabs the size of a hound that have moved into the old grain-cellars, shell
scarred from generations of scavenging metal debris.
Range: flooded cellars and undercrofts along the wall; solitary or in mated pairs.
Sign: fresh gouges in mud or timber at the height of a low table-top, and a low clicking.
Disposition: territorial over its cellar but otherwise uninterested; backs away from fire.

Silt-serpent (Beast) - AD: 4d6
Description: a heavy-bodied eel-like serpent, longer than a boat, that lies motionless in
flooded channels until something crosses directly above it.
Range: the deeper drowned channels; a channel supports at most one.
Sign: a channel with no eels, no herons, and no fish breaking the surface - the absence is
the sign.
Disposition: ambushes without warning; disengages once it has taken something and dragged
it under, rather than pursuing further.

Delta bull, feral (Beast) - AD: 3d6
Description: rangy cattle descended from the Old Rede's own grain-herds, gone wild in the
generations since, still marked with an old brand no one here can read.
Range: the drier hummocks above the flood line; small loose herds.
Sign: cropped grass in a dry line, and the old brand scarred into hide long since gone feral.
Disposition: avoids people; a cornered bull or one defending a calf charges without warning.

Ledger enforcer, pair (Man) - AD: 3d6
Description: two hired collectors who work the flats in matched pairs, carrying a branding
tool for marking defaulters' gear rather than weapons meant to kill.
Range: wherever the Tallyhouse holds paper; never alone, never far from a boat home.
Sign: the scale-pan brand, fresh, on a crate, a door, or a boat that wasn't there before.
Disposition: collect, threaten, and mark; will not risk the pair to kill outright, and
retreat to fetch more hands rather than press a losing fight.

Wall-digger crew (Man) - AD: 3d6
Description: three or four treasure-diggers tunneling under Durgath's foundations, armed
and territorial about a dig years in the making.
Range: wherever the wall is thick enough to tunnel; camps near their own working.
Sign: spoil-heaps of dressed stone dust that doesn't match the mud around it.
Disposition: hostile to anyone near the dig itself; otherwise avoid notice entirely.

Reed-folk raiders (Humanoid) - AD: 4d6
Description: a semi-nomadic people living in reed-built stilt camps upriver, who raid
low-tide camps for iron and salt rather than coin.
Range: the upper channels above the tideline proper; raiding parties of six to ten.
Sign: reed-woven markers tied to a camp's boundary the night before a raid.
Disposition: take iron and salt specifically and leave everything else, including
prisoners, untouched if unresisted.

Mudling (Humanoid) - AD: 2d6
Description: a small, amphibious people native to the delta before the Old Rede came,
wary of outsiders, who trade eel-oil and true knowledge of the tide for iron goods.
Range: the reed-beds and shallow margins the diking never fully drained; small family
groups.
Sign: eel-oil lamps left burning at the waterline, untended, as a standing invitation to
trade rather than a threat.
Disposition: will not approach first; trade fairly and warn honestly once approached in
turn, and vanish completely if threatened.

Old Rede thrall-remnant (Humanoid) - AD: 5d6
Description: descendants of a servile caste the Old Rede left behind, still keeping
half-understood duties - tending wardstones, walking rounds that lead nowhere now - in
isolated pockets of the delta.
Range: close by whatever they still tend; small isolated communities, rarely more than a
dozen.
Sign: a patrolled boundary with nothing behind it worth patrolling, walked at fixed hours.
Disposition: defend what they tend without explaining why, and are otherwise willing to
talk, in a mix of Kelder and words no living tongue quite claims.

Wrecker militia (Man) - AD: 5d6
Description: an armed fisher-hold band that lures boats onto the flats at night to strip
them, well-organized and locally protected.
Range: a stretch of coast their hold claims; ten to twenty strong when mustered in full.
Sign: a false channel-light burning where no marker should be.
Disposition: strip and rob rather than kill outright, but kill without hesitation anyone who
can identify them afterward.

Tide-risen (Undead) - AD: 2d6
Description: the delta's ordinary drowned dead, unsalted before the flood took them,
risen with the tide and dragging at the memory of whatever they were doing when they died.
Range: anywhere a body went under unsalted; solitary or in small clusters where several
died together.
Sign: waterlogged footprints leading up out of the tideline with no boat, no companion, and
no destination.
Disposition: repeats the last purposeful thing it was doing in life - hauling, rowing,
calling a name - until interrupted, then attacks whatever interrupted it.

Rede-warden (Undead) - AD: 6d6
Description: an Old Rede functionary bound to a specific vault or wardstone, articulate
enough to bargain in its own tongue, and unwilling to leave its post on any terms.
Range: fixed to a single site; never found away from what it guards.
Sign: the iron key motif recurs at its post, blade whole rather than broken.
Disposition: negotiates before it fights, and honors whatever bargain it strikes to the
letter, no further.

Flood-bear (Beast) - AD: 7d6
Description: an enormous brown bear that has adapted to hunting the tide flats at low
water, unafraid of open ground the way its forest kin would be.
Range: ranges the outer flats past the breach; solitary, and the ground supports at most
two or three in the whole delta.
Sign: a kill left half-buried in mud to cure, returned to across several days.
Disposition: avoids people when it isn't hungry; a fed bear moves off rather than press an
advantage, but a hungry one does not test twice before committing.

Salt-drowned matriarch (Undead) - AD: 8d6
Description: a powerful undead risen from one of the drowned grain-ports, once someone with
real authority in life, and dangerous enough that engaging her should be a considered
choice, not a default.
Range: fixed to the port she once ruled; does not leave it.
Sign: the tide runs backward, briefly, near her ground, before anything else gives her away.
Disposition: commands rather than attacks first, and expects to be obeyed; turns lethal
only once that expectation is refused.

Flood-horror (Horror) - AD: 9d6
Description: something that came up with the water the night Durgath's western span broke,
amorphous and chaos-touched, that answers to no motive a party could reason with.
Range: the breach itself and the drowned ground nearest it; never seen far from where it
arrived.
Sign: still water that will not reflect the sky correctly.
Disposition: gives no warning and wants nothing negotiable; the party is meant to recognize
the Sign and choose not to be there.

Wall-warden construct (Construct) - AD: 12d6 MA: slow
Description: an Old Rede stone guardian built into Durgath's own foundations, vast, slow,
and entirely beyond what this party can fight and win.
Range: fixed to the wall's foundations, specifically wherever the vault it protects lies.
Sign: seams in the wall's dressed stone that move, very slightly, when nothing else does.
Disposition: does not pursue past the boundary of what it guards, and gives ample warning
before it acts - the party is not meant to beat it, only to learn where that boundary is.
