Bestiary of Thornmarch

### Kobold
Type: Humanoid
AD: 1d6-2
Special: none
Description: Waist-high and dog-faced, rust-brown scales under rags, bone charms at the neck, a sling or a club in hand.
Range: Warrens and cellars. Eats rats, carrion and what the larger tribes leave.
Numbers: 30
Sign: a sound
Disposition: Flees anything that fights back and leads pursuers over its own traps; carries a message to the goblins for a coin.

### Goblin
Type: Humanoid
AD: 1d6-1
Special: Disadvantage on Strike Blows in full daylight.
Description: Child-high and long-armed, grey-green, yellow-eyed, in leather and scrap iron.
Range: Caves and ruins. Eats what it steals.
Numbers: 20-30
Sign: a mark
Disposition: Ambushes anyone it outnumbers and hides from anyone it does not; parleys only with a prisoner in hand, in the black speech and badly in the common tongue.

### Orc
Type: Humanoid
AD: 1d6+1
Special: A blow that costs a Wound leaves the target Bloodied.
Description: Man-high and broad, grey-brown and tusked, in boiled hide and iron caps, with heavy notched axes.
Range: Warrens and gutted halls. Eats meat of any kind, raw by choice.
Numbers: 20
Sign: a smell
Disposition: Attacks anything smaller than itself at once, quarrels with its own when there is nothing to fight, and is drunk by evening.

### Hobgoblin
Type: Humanoid
AD: 2d6+1
Special: Two standing side by side give Disadvantage on Strike Blows against either from the front.
Description: As tall as a man, copper-skinned, in mail and iron helms, spears and shields carried in step.
Range: Fortified holds, under a warlord. Eats stored grain and meat, and pays in goods for what it lacks.
Numbers: 12-15
Sign: a sound
Disposition: Holds a line and gives a challenge before it strikes; parleys in the black speech with anyone who comes unarmed.

### Gnoll
Type: Humanoid
AD: 2d6+2
Special: none
Description: Tall and hunched, hyena-headed, spotted and mangy, with barbed spears; the ones who lead wear iron collars on their captives.
Range: Dens and open ground. Eats carrion and anything it runs down.
Numbers: 12
Sign: a track
Disposition: Circles and hamstrings, takes prisoners to sell, and parleys with anyone who holds coin.

### Bugbear
Type: Humanoid
AD: 3d6+1
Special: If it strikes from hiding, its first blow costs a Wound with no Test.
Description: Seven feet tall and hairy, long-armed and soft-footed, in strips of hide, a spiked club in one fist.
Range: Dry forks and dens. Eats what it takes, and is paid in meat and tithe.
Numbers: 6-10
Sign: a mark
Disposition: Waits hidden and strikes the one last in line, then falls back; does not parley.

### Owlbear
Type: Beast
AD: 4d6+2
Special: A target struck by both claws in one Round is Held.
MA: 2
Description: Bear-bodied and feathered, hook-beaked and round-eyed, tall as a doorway when it rises.
Range: Rocky rims and caves, a mile of ground to each. Eats anything that moves.
Numbers: 1-2
Sign: a sound
Disposition: Attacks anything that enters its lair or comes between it and its kill; otherwise watches from the dark.

### Wolf
Type: Beast
AD: 2d6+0
Special: none
Description: Lean and grey, ribs showing in winter, running in a line with the leader a length ahead.
Range: Ravines and the edges of forest, in packs. Eats deer, sheep and carrion, and a straggler in a hard winter.
Numbers: 6-12
Sign: a sound
Disposition: Circles a party at dusk and tests it; takes the slowest and breaks off when two of the pack are killed.

### Soldier
Type: Men
AD: 1d6+1
Special: none
Description: A keep soldier in a blue tabard, chain shirt, shield and spear.
Range: The keep's walls and the road to the realm; patrols go out by day.
Numbers: 6
Sign: a track
Disposition: Challenges anyone near the road or the gate and holds a stranger who cannot answer.

### Lizard Man
Type: Humanoid
AD: 1d6+2
Special: Moves through water and mud at full pace; Disadvantage on Chase or Flee against it in a marsh.
Description: Man-sized and green-scaled, long-tailed and spiny-crested, in belts of woven reed, with spears tipped in fish-bone.
Range: Marshes and slow rivers, in villages. Eats fish, frogs and what drowns.
Numbers: 30
Sign: a track
Disposition: Watches strangers from the reeds and attacks one who leaves the causeway; parleys in the cant.

### Zombie
Type: Undead
AD: 1d6+1
Special: Does not flee, tire or sleep, and acts last in each Round.
Description: A dead man gone grey and swollen, in the rags he was buried in, arms held out.
Range: Crypts and graves; the dead of one place rise together. Eats nothing.
Numbers: 20
Sign: a smell
Disposition: Walks straight at the nearest living thing and does not stop.
