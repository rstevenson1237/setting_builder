Bestiary of The Ashwrack Coast

Wrecker (Man) - AD: 1d6
Description: A coastal scavenger who lures ships onto the rocks with false lantern-signals, then strips the wreck and its drowned crew before dawn. Armed with whatever the sea last gave up - a boat hook, a rusted cutlass, a stolen blade.

Coastal Bandit (Man) - AD: 1d6 +1
Description: A hardened raider who preys on the holds and trade caravans of the coast road, quick with a blade and quicker to vanish into the dunes. Fights for coin, grudge, or the simple fact that the wilderness enforces no law against it.

Drowned Folk (Humanoid) - AD: 2d6 +1 [MA: 1]
Description: A gill-slitted, web-fingered people said to be Vael's descendants remade by the deep, dwelling in flooded caverns beneath the reefs. They raid the shallows with trident and net, dragging the living down to trade to older, hungrier things.

Tideling (Humanoid) - AD: 1d6
Description: A small, wiry amphibious folk who trade salvage and secrets from the tidepools rather than fight for them, though they will bite and scratch when cornered. Curious and superstitious, they hoard trinkets they believe ward off the deep.

Coast Wolf (Beast) - AD: 2d6 +1
Description: A lean, salt-crusted wolf that hunts the dune grass and shoreline in packs, unafraid of torches and bold enough to test a camp's watch. Its howl carries strangely far across open water.

Giant Crab (Beast) - AD: 3d6 +1 [MA: 1]
Description: A cart-sized crab that buries itself in wet sand and erupts to seize prey in a crushing claw, dragging the struggling catch below the surf. Its shell is prized armor-stock when it can be pried free.

Giant Eel (Beast) - AD: 2d6 +1
Description: A serpentine hunter as thick as a man's waist, coiling around swimmers and small boats to drag them under before the victim can draw breath. It favors the flooded crypts and drowned streets where little light reaches.

Barnacle Horror (Fantasy Creature) - AD: 2d6 [MA: 1]
Description: A crusted mass of shell and half-formed limbs that grows slowly across a wreck's hull until it can pass for driftwood, then unfolds to grasp at anything that comes within reach. It does not hunt so much as wait, patient as the tide.

Bell-Wight (Undead) - AD: 3d6 +1
Description: A drowned sailor risen restless and cold, drawn irresistibly to the sound of bells - ship's bells, temple bells, the tolling that is said to come from beneath the water. Its touch withers warmth from flesh, and it will not stop following a ringing sound until it is destroyed.

Drowned Skeleton (Undead) - AD: 1d6
Description: Bleached bone knit loosely together by lingering malice, risen from a sunken grave to shamble after anything living. Slow and mindless, but tireless, and they rise from the silt in numbers.

Bloated Drowned (Undead) - AD: 2d6
Description: A corpse swollen and preserved by cold saltwater, animated by whatever chaos-tainted current stirred it from the deep. It moves with unnatural buoyancy, bursting with foul brine when finally destroyed.

Spire Wraith (Undead) - AD: 4d6 +2
Description: The incorporeal remnant of one of Vael's drowned nobility, still wearing the shape of its rank and still furious at its fall. It drifts through stone and water alike, and its touch drains warmth and will in equal measure.

Figurehead Golem (Construct) - AD: 4d6 +1
Description: A ship's carved figurehead - wood, bone, or brine-fused iron - animated by old and half-forgotten craft to guard a wreck or a hoard. It fights with the same relentless, unblinking purpose it was built for, centuries after its makers died.

Bone Ship (Construct) - AD: 6d6 +2 [MA: 2]
Description: A wrecked hull rebuilt from the bones of its own dead crew and driven by no visible hand, ramming and grappling anything that crosses its wake. Sailors swear the deck timbers still creak in rhythm with a heartbeat.

Deep Whisperer (Horror) - AD: 5d6 +2
Description: A presence more than a body, felt as pressure behind the eyes and a voice that speaks directly into thought, offering knowledge or bargains in exchange for small, escalating debts. Those who listen too long find their own thoughts no longer entirely their own.

Tide Serpent (Wyrm) - AD: 6d6 +2 [MA: 2]
Description: A vast draconic sea-dweller, older than Vael itself, that surfaces rarely and only when the tides run strange. Its coils can crush a longship's hull, and its passing is said to be what first called the sea down upon the old empire.

Salt Ogre (Giant) - AD: 4d6 +1
Description: A hulking brute of a creature, hide cracked and white with dried salt, that squats in coastal caves and demands tribute from any hold within reach of its club. Not clever, but strong enough that cleverness rarely matters.

Coast Troll (Giant) - AD: 5d6 +2 [MA: 1]
Description: A gaunt, rubbery-limbed horror that stalks the tidal flats and knits its wounds shut almost as fast as they are opened, driven back only by fire or a foe it cannot outlast. It smells of low tide and rot long before it is seen.

Fen Harpy (Fantasy Creature) - AD: 2d6 +1 [MA: 1]
Description: A carrion-bird-and-woman hybrid that nests in the drowned marsh reeds and lures the lost with a song that sounds like a loved one's voice. It strips its catch to bone long before the tide can claim the remains.

Court Herald (Fiend) - AD: 4d6 +2 [MA: 1]
Description: A rare and terrible emissary of the Drowned Court, wearing a shape assembled from coral, chain, and borrowed bone to speak its masters' will aloud. It does not fight readily, preferring to offer a bargain first - but its price is never small, and refusal rarely ends the conversation peacefully.
