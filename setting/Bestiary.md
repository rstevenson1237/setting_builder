Bestiary of Khirengaard

Fringe Wolf (Beast) - AD: 2d6
Description: A lean grey hunter that runs the treeline in packs of four to eight. Ordinary in every way except that it will not, under any provocation, cross a boundary stone - a fact trappers rely on more than they admit.

Wold-Marked Wolf (Beast) - AD: 3d6 +1, MA: 1
Description: A wolf that has spent time on the far side of a stone line and come back changed - patchy hide, eyes gone milk-pale, a second shadow that doesn't always move with it. Hunts alone, and hunts anything.

Bristleback Boar (Beast) - AD: 3d6 +2
Description: A tusked fringe boar bred tough by generations on poor forage. Charges anything that startles it, cutter or Concern crew alike.

Wold Elk (Beast) - AD: 2d6 +1
Description: An antlered elk grown unnaturally large on wold-side browse it shouldn't be able to reach. Placid unless cornered, but a single kick can break a leg.

Fen-Bear (Beast) - AD: 5d6 +2, MA: 1
Description: A shaggy brown bear that dens in the boggy hollows along the stone line. Territorial past reason near a den site, otherwise avoidant of torches and noise.

Concern Enforcer (Man) - AD: 2d6 +1
Description: Hired muscle carrying the Vansker Concern's brand, armed with a billhook and a company writ. Follows orders over conscience and reports every stranger it meets.

Fringe Bandit (Man) - AD: 1d6
Description: A displaced trapper or failed prospector turned to robbing the timber roads. Desperate rather than skilled, and quick to run from real resistance.

Boundary-Cutter (Man) - AD: 2d6 +2
Description: A guild surveyor trained to read which ground still belongs to the fringe. Carries a wold-iron blade and a set of blazing tools, and knows the stone lines better than anyone alive.

Marrgath Remnant (Man) - AD: 2d6 +1
Description: One of the vanished confederation's people, somehow still walking the fringe a century and a half after their kin disappeared. Speaks a dialect no living scholar can place and never explains where it's been.

Grey Charcoaler (Humanoid) - AD: 3d6
Description: A stooped, ash-skinned people who keep to the deep fringe and burn charcoal in kilns no one else tends. They trade wordlessly, leaving goods at agreed stones, and kill anyone who follows them home.

Thornling (Fantasy Creature) - AD: 2d6 +1
Description: A man-height tangle of blackthorn and old root that moves only when unwatched, closing the distance a few feet at a time. Motionless and indistinguishable from a deadfall thicket under direct observation.

Barrow-Moth (Fantasy Creature) - AD: 1d6 +2, MA: 2
Description: A pale, hand-sized moth that swarms in the dozens near the toppled Marrgath stone. Its wingbeats carry a faint keening that dulls pain - and judgment - in anyone who lingers.

Stone-Warden (Fantasy Creature) - AD: 4d6 +3
Description: A lichen-crusted, vaguely man-shaped thing that stands motionless for years at a stone line until the line itself is disturbed - a toppled marker, an unblessed crossing. Then it moves with purpose, and does not stop until the disturbance is corrected or it is destroyed.

Fringe Wight (Undead) - AD: 3d6 +2
Description: What's left of someone the wold took but didn't finish with - grey-skinned, cold to the touch, driven to walk the exact route of their death over and over. Anyone who interrupts that route draws its full attention.

Longhouse Shade (Undead) - AD: 2d6 +1
Description: A flickering, human-shaped absence that lingers in the empty Marrgath halls, mimicking half-remembered domestic motions - stirring a cold hearth, latching a door with no hinges left. Harmless unless its motions are mocked or interrupted.

Wold-Touched Stag (Fantasy Creature) - AD: 3d6 +1, MA: 1
Description: An antlered stag whose rack has grown through with pale fungal branches. Leads hunters on long, deliberate chases that always end at a stone line - and vanishes the instant it crosses one, whether or not the hunter follows.

Deep Crawler (Horror) - AD: 6d6 +4, MA: 2
Description: A many-limbed thing glimpsed only past the stone lines, moving low and fast through undergrowth that shouldn't hide something its size. No two witnesses describe the same number of limbs, and none who've gotten close have stayed sane enough to say more.

Marrgath Watch-Stone (Construct) - AD: 4d6 +2
Description: A humanoid figure of fused river-stone bearing traces of Marrgath tool-work, found only guarding what the Marrgath thought worth guarding. Inert until its charge is disturbed, then relentless and utterly silent.

Cutter's Blight (Undead) - AD: 2d6, MA: 3
Description: The scattered, still-twitching remains of a boundary-cutter who crossed a line without a proper blessing - fingers, an ear, a length of spine - each piece animate and hunting independently for what's left of the whole.

Hollow Antler (Fey) - AD: 3d6 +2
Description: A fey thing wearing a cast-off elk skull like a mask, bound by old bargain-rules it will state plainly if asked - and honor to the letter while breaking their spirit completely. Trades favors for names, never coin.
