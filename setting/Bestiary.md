Bestiary of Roadsend

### Goblin
Type: Humanoid
AD: 1d6-1
Special: Fights at -1 to hit in full daylight.
Description: Child-high and long-armed, grey-green, with yellow eyes; leather and scrap iron, and a red clay handprint daubed on the chest.
Range: Caves and ruins; a tribe numbers dozens. Eats what it takes from the road.
Sign: a mark
Disposition: Ambushes anyone it outnumbers and hides from anyone it does not; parleys only with a prisoner in hand, in the common tongue, badly.

### Wolf
Type: Beast
AD: 2d6+2
Special: none
Description: Lean and grey, ribs showing in winter, running in a line with the leader a length ahead.
Range: Ravines and the edges of forest; packs of six to twelve. Eats deer, sheep and carrion, and a straggler in a hard winter.
Sign: a sound
Disposition: Circles a party at dusk and tests it; takes the slowest and breaks off when two of the pack are killed.

### Soldier
Type: Men
AD: 1d6+1
Special: none
Description: A keep soldier in a blue tabard with a white tower, chain shirt, shield and spear.
Range: The keep's walls and the road to the realm; patrols go out in sixes by day as far as the ravine's mouth.
Sign: a track
Disposition: Challenges anyone near the road or the gate with "Who holds the road?" and holds a stranger who cannot answer.
