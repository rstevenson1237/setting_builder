Bestiary of the Borderlands

### Goblin
Type: Humanoid
AD: 1d6+0
MA: 1
Description: Child-sized, grey-skinned, in scavenged leather and road boots too big for them.
Range: Caves off the ravine, a score or more to a warren; eats what it steals.
Sign: Soot handprints on rock at shoulder height.
Disposition: Shrieks and falls back on its kin; fights only in numbers.
Special: none

### Wolf
Type: Beast
AD: 2d6+1
MA: 1
Description: Lean grey hunter, ribs showing, ears torn.
Range: Wilderness packs of four to eight; kept chained by goblins as guards.
Sign: Howling at dusk.
Disposition: Circles, then goes for the weakest.
Special: none
