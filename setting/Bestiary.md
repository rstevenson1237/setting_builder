Bestiary of Duen

Party altitude: 2-4 Action Dice, sized d4-d8 (d10 rare, d12 essentially unheard of), d6
average - Duen's garrison and the hangers-on camped against its walls, barely above the
commoners they're holding this crossing for.

Cliff Goat (Beast) - AD: 1d6-2
Description: A rangy, sure-footed goat that works the gorge's upper ledges in small herds,
more grey than white by midsummer.
Range: The gorge rim and upper slopes support several herds; grazes anything green,
including a farmer's second cutting.
Sign: Cropped stalks at an odd, upward angle, and scat on ledges no sheep could reach.
Disposition: Bolts uphill the instant it's noticed; never turns to fight anything larger
than itself.

Quarry Rat (Beast) - AD: 1d6
Description: A heavy-bodied rat gone fat and bold on quarry stores, moving in numbers
rather than alone.
Range: Every gallery and grain-shed in and under Duen holds a colony; eats grain, leather,
and anything else left unwatched.
Sign: Gnaw-marks on sacking, and a colony's dry, ammoniac smell before any rat is seen.
Disposition: Never fights - a lit torch or a slammed door scatters the whole colony, which
is exactly the problem: they're back within the week.

Gorge Lynx (Beast) - AD: 2d6
Description: A solitary, tufted-eared cat that hunts the broken ground below the rim,
seldom seen and rarely in the same place twice.
Range: The gorge slopes support perhaps half a dozen; takes goats, rock-hares, and
unwatched dogs.
Sign: A half-eaten kill wedged into a rock crevice, saved for later.
Disposition: Avoids anything upright and armed; only closes on something already wounded
or alone.

Marsh Hog (Beast) - AD: 3d6
Description: A low, broad-shouldered boar that roots through the river marsh below the
ford, tusked and short-tempered.
Range: The marsh supports a handful of sounders; eats roots, carrion, and anything smaller
that doesn't move fast enough.
Sign: Torn-up ground in a wide swath, and a rank, muddy wallow.
Disposition: Charges anything that startles it at close range; otherwise moves off
grumbling.

Grey Wolf (Beast) - AD: 3d6+1
Description: A lean, pale-coated wolf that runs in packs of four to eight beyond the
charter's patrolled ground.
Range: The high country past the last watch-cairn; takes anything from a stray goat to an
unescorted traveler in a bad winter.
Sign: A fresh kill stripped to bone in a single night, and tracks crossing the road in a
line.
Disposition: Tests a party at a distance before committing - a pack that finds an easier
kill elsewhere will take it and leave.

River Serpent (Beast) - AD: 5d6
Description: A grey-green, eel-bodied thing as long as two men, that lies still in a
plunge pool until something wades within reach.
Range: The gorge's deeper pools, one or two to a pool; eats fish, waterfowl, and anything
that fords at the wrong spot.
Sign: A dead-still stretch of water where the current should be breaking, and fish going
missing from a normally good pool.
Disposition: Strikes without warning from ambush; will not pursue onto dry ground.

Stone Bear (Beast) - AD: 8d6+2
Description: A massive, pale-furred bear that dens in abandoned quarry galleries rather
than caves, powerful enough to bring down a laden ox.
Range: One or two per generation of idle gallery; eats anything, including a season's
stores if it finds them.
Sign: Claw-gouges scored deep into a gallery's support timbers, man-height and higher.
Disposition: Ignores anything that stays clear of its den; a party that stumbles into the
den itself gets one warning bluff-charge before it's serious.

The Gorge-Worm (Beast) - AD: 12d6+4
Description: Nobody who has seen the whole of it has lived to compare notes - what's
agreed on is a pale, segmented bulk as wide as a wagon, surfacing from the gorge's flooded
lower caves perhaps once a season.
Range: The flooded caves beneath the gorge floor; what it eats is guessed at from what
stops showing up nearby afterward.
Sign: The Durad running unnaturally still and cold for a day beforehand, and every fish in
it gone quiet.
Disposition: Surfaces to feed, not to fight, and returns to the depths the instant it has
taken something - a party that stands and fights it has misunderstood what they're looking
at.

Quarry Convict (Man) - AD: 1d6
Description: A sentenced laborer working off a term in the Dutor galleries, underfed and
watched, more dangerous cornered than free.
Range: The quarry's work-gangs hold a dozen or more at a time; steals food, tools, or a
chance at the road.
Sign: A missing hand-tool, and a work-gang short a face at evening count.
Disposition: Surrenders or bargains rather than fights when caught in the open; fights
only when cornered with nothing left to lose.

Charter Regular (Man) - AD: 2d6
Description: A trained soldier from a neighboring holding or a passing column, armed and
disciplined but not looking for trouble on someone else's ground.
Range: Travels in squads of four to ten with supply trains and patrols; needs regular pay
and rations to stay disciplined.
Sign: Matched kit and a column's dust well before anyone's close enough to hail.
Disposition: Challenges and questions before drawing steel; stands down for anyone who can
produce papers or a plausible reason.

Highland Bandit (Man) - AD: 3d6+1
Description: An opportunist working the new quarry road since the ford went down, armed
with whatever was cheap and sharp.
Range: Bands of five to a dozen shift camp along the road; live off tolls taken at
knifepoint and whatever a wagon's carrying.
Sign: A felled tree or rockslide blocking the road at a blind bend.
Disposition: Wants the goods, not a fight - will let a wagon go if it's given up fast
enough, and gets ugly if it isn't.

Salir Runner (Man) - AD: 3d6+1
Description: A member of the rope-crossing band working the gorge downriver, lean and
sure-footed, marked by a length of red-dyed cord worn openly.
Range: The band holds perhaps thirty, camped wherever the current crossing point is; lives
off crossing fees and whatever a paying party doesn't watch closely.
Sign: A red cord snagged on brush near an unmarked trail down to the water.
Disposition: All business at first contact - names a price for the crossing before
anything else is discussed.

Marsh-Kin (Humanoid) - AD: 3d6
Description: A wading, long-fingered people living in the river marsh in reed-and-mud
steadings, wary of the keep but not hostile to it.
Range: The marsh supports a few dozen across several steadings; eats fish, reed-root, and
marsh fowl.
Sign: A woven reed marker staked at the edge of their claimed water, easy to miss and
deliberate.
Disposition: Withdraws and watches rather than approaches; will trade or talk from a safe
distance if approached slowly and without weapons drawn.

Deep Delver (Humanoid) - AD: 4d6
Description: A stocky, pale-eyed people who cut their own galleries far below where any
quarry crew has gone, and avoid the surface entirely.
Range: Unknown numbers in workings below the gorge; eats fungus, blind fish, and whatever
falls to them from above.
Sign: A tunnel wall scored with tool-marks no quarry chisel made.
Disposition: Retreats into unmapped passage rather than engage; only fights when a passage
is blocked behind it.

Ridge Nomad (Humanoid) - AD: 5d6
Description: A herding people who follow their flocks along the gorge rim by season,
camped in felt tents struck within the hour.
Range: Bands of twenty to fifty range the high ground above the gorge; their herds graze
wherever the grass is, charter boundary or not.
Sign: A cold fire-ring and cropped grass in a wide circle, days old.
Disposition: Indifferent to the keep's claims and unwilling to be told whose land they're
crossing; negotiates readily, fights hard if pressed on it.

Charter-Bound (Undead) - AD: 2d6
Description: The upright, translucent shape of a sentry in outdated kit, walking a patrol
route that hasn't been posted in years.
Range: One or two to a stretch of old rim road; takes nothing and eats nothing.
Sign: Footsteps on packed gravel with no one leaving them.
Disposition: Challenges anyone it meets exactly as it would have in life, and will not
stand down for anything except a formal discharge in the old form - it does not fight if
let alone.

Gallery Wight (Undead) - AD: 5d6+1
Description: A crew's worth of dead, still wearing quarry kit, moving with the shared,
wrong-timed motion of people who no longer need to breathe between swings.
Range: Bound to the sealed lower gallery where the nineteen-year-old cave-in trapped them;
does not leave it.
Sign: Pick-strikes echoing from behind a sealed door, evenly spaced, never stopping.
Disposition: Ignores anyone who doesn't open the sealed door; attacks without pause or
warning the moment it is.

The Unspoken Dead (Undead) - AD: 11d6+3
Description: Not one thing but a pressure along the old boundary line itself - the
accumulated dead the markers were raised to keep on one side, felt before anything is seen.
Range: The full length of the gorge-rim boundary; takes nothing, eats nothing, wants
nothing a party can name.
Sign: Every sound past the rim stones going flat and close, as if walled in.
Disposition: Does nothing to anyone who stays on the correct side of the boundary line; a
party that crosses it carrying what they shouldn't finds out what it does.

Boundary Warden (Construct) - AD: 6d6
Description: A man-shaped thing of fitted boundary-stone, seamless and cold, that rises
from a marker only when the line it guards is broken.
Range: One to a boundary stone, dormant otherwise; needs nothing, eats nothing.
Sign: Hairline seams in a boundary stone that weren't there on the last pass.
Disposition: Rises silently, gives no challenge, and returns to stone the instant the
breach is undone or the intruder is driven back across the line.

Xarask Broodling (Horror) - AD: 4d6+1
Description: A pale, many-jointed thing the length of an arm, moving low and fast, one of
the lesser numbers of whatever holds the flooded galleries below the Old Cut.
Range: The flooded lower workings; numbers unknown, but never found far from standing
water; eats anything that stops moving near it.
Sign: A foul, standing-water smell and a smeared spiral mark left on stone at shoulder
height.
Disposition: Hunts in numbers and flees in numbers - a lone Broodling separated from its
group breaks off rather than press an attack alone.
