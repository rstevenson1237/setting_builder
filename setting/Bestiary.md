Bestiary of Greywatch

Ravine Lynx (Beast) - AD: 4d6+3 [MA: 1]
Description: A big grey cat, low-slung, ears notched from old fights, moving without sound over broken rock.
Range: Solitary; the wilderness and the ravine's outer mouths both, wherever the game it eats can be run down. One holds a stretch of ground large enough that a party can cross paths with the same cat twice in a season.
Sign: Fresh gouges on bark at exactly the height of a stretching cat, always the same three claws.
Disposition: Watches from cover before it commits; breaks off a fight the moment it's taking more than it's giving.
Special: none

Herd-Biter (Beast) - AD: 2d6+0 [MA: 1]
Description: A tusked hog the size of a large dog, hide scarred pale where it's been driven off before.
Range: Roots and forages along the tree line past Greywatch's own fences; comes in at night for whatever's penned closest to the wood.
Sign: Fence rails rooted up from underneath, and a torn ear or a dead animal left where it fed.
Disposition: Runs from anything upright and armed unless it's already feeding, in which case it charges whoever's closest.
Special: none

Cave Wing (Beast) - AD: 1d6-1 [MA: 2]
Description: A ceiling's worth of bats, counted and fought as the one swarm they move as.
Range: Any cave mouth or old chamber deep enough to stay dark all day; the ravine holds more than one roost.
Sign: A smell of old droppings before the ceiling itself comes into torchlight.
Disposition: Ignores anything below it until disturbed, then pours out past whoever's in the doorway.
Special: none

Tusked Charger (Beast) - AD: 5d6+1 [MA: 1]
Description: A boar bred up past what wild stock ever grows to, girthed and saddled with cave-tribe harness.
Range: Kept, not hunted - stabled in whichever block can feed one, and ridden rather than met loose.
Sign: A harness-jingle and a floor shaking underfoot before it rounds a corner.
Disposition: Fights however its rider does; alone and unridden it charges anything that startles it and doesn't stop at one pass.
Special: none

Road Raider (Men) - AD: 1d6+2 [MA: 1]
Description: Leather and stolen mail, a face kept masked past the point of hiding that it's a face.
Range: Works the road itself in bands of four to eight, never holding ground long enough to be a lair a party could find twice.
Sign: Hoofprints crossing the road at an angle no trader's cart would take.
Disposition: Wants what's carried, not a fight over it; takes a surrender and leaves the survivors the road home.
Special: none

Greywatch Regular (Men) - AD: 2d6+2 [MA: 1]
Description: The realm's own mail and a Law-marked shield, drilled rather than desperate.
Range: The keep's own walls and the road it watches; never met past where the realm's writ runs.
Sign: A challenge called from a wall before anyone's close enough to answer it.
Disposition: Holds a line rather than chases one; stands down the moment a party's papers or errand check out.
Special: none

Khughik (Humanoid) - AD: 1d6-1 [MA: 1]
Description: Waist-high, grey-skinned, quick-fingered - Grozzgur for "little claws," which every other tribe simply calls them to their face. A warren's worth of them share one dug hole.
Range: Small warrens throughout the ravine, never more than a handful of chambers each; a warren this size can't hold ground alone and doesn't try to.
Sign: A chittering that stops all at once the moment torchlight reaches a bend.
Disposition: Swarms anything smaller than itself; scatters and doesn't return the same day against anything larger that fights back.
Special: none

Gorzgur (Humanoid) - AD: 3d6+2 [MA: 1]
Description: Lean, grey-hided, its warband marked in the matching red paint that gives it its Grozzgur name - "the red band" - and that no other tribe wears the same way.
Range: Organized bands holding one mid-sized cave complex as a household, with sentries posted at every entrance it fields.
Sign: A horn-call answered from deeper in, before the band itself is seen.
Disposition: Fights in formation and quits it in formation; will take a prisoner for ransom or hear a parley out rather than finish a fight it isn't sure of winning.
Special: none

Hukkgur (Humanoid) - AD: 6d6+4 [MA: 2]
Description: A head taller than a big man and twice the weight, knuckles scarred to the bone from bare-handed brawling - Grozzgur names it "the dread band," and every other tribe gives it room.
Range: Fewer of them per household than any other tribe, holding only the ground they can hold by force alone.
Sign: A floor that's cracked, not worn, wherever one's walked it often.
Disposition: Answers any challenge to its strength directly, and doesn't stop swinging once it's decided to.
Special: none

Barrow Ward (Fantasy) - AD: 4d6+1 [MA: 1]
Description: A shape the mound's own turned earth seems to stand up out of, mud-toned and only visible once it moves.
Range: Bound to the one mound it was set over.
Sign: Grass that lies flat in a ring, in dry weather, with no wind to explain it.
Disposition: Lets pass whatever carries or wears the mark it was set to answer; anything else that breaks the mound's own soil, it moves to stop.
Special: Acts only within sight of the mound - a threat carried more than a stone's throw from it stops mattering to the Ward entirely.

Weeping Growth (Fantasy) - AD: 2d6-2 [MA: 3]
Description: Pale, wet-looking growth furring a wall or a ceiling, weeping where it's thickest.
Range: Wherever the old digging stays damp and undisturbed long enough; a patch this size takes years to grow back once cleared.
Sign: A smell like a struck match gone out, before the growth itself is in torchlight.
Disposition: Does nothing on its own; it has no want and takes no action against anyone.
Special: Whatever brushes or breaks it forces a Test of Sanity, its spores costing what the local hazard tier allows rather than the Growth itself attacking.

Tunnel Hunter (Fantasy) - AD: 5d6+5 [MA: 1]
Description: Never fully seen; what's seen of it is always already turning away.
Range: Ranges the ravine's older cut tunnels only, never the tribes' own dug additions.
Sign: A draft that changes direction against the tunnel's own slope.
Disposition: Closes distance in the dark far faster than in torchlight, and breaks off the instant real light reaches it.
Special: Halves any distance a party has put between themselves and it once light is doused, once per encounter.

Bound Thrall (Undead) - AD: 1d6-1 [MA: 1]
Description: A corpse kept upright past any reason the body itself gives, dressed however it was found.
Range: Never far from the one post it was set; a Thrall met once is met at the same post again.
Sign: No smell of decay at all, where the room otherwise would carry one.
Disposition: Blocks or challenges exactly what it was set to and nothing else; ignores anyone not attempting that one thing.
Special: none

Ravine Restless (Undead) - AD: 3d6+0 [MA: 1]
Description: A dead tribesman or soldier, marked by whatever people or banner it served in life.
Range: Wanders a fixed circuit rather than a whole region; the same one is met twice by anyone who crosses its round.
Sign: Footsteps in step with nothing walking.
Disposition: Attacks anything living on its round without pause or challenge, and breaks off the instant it steps past the round's own edge.
Special: none

Grave-Ghast (Undead) - AD: 5d6+2 [MA: 3]
Description: Grey-fleshed and too fast for how it looks, nails grown out past any use a living hand has for them.
Range: Wherever bodies are left rather than burned or carried out; feeds where the dead already are.
Sign: Freshly disturbed earth or ash over a body that was whole the last time anyone looked.
Disposition: Feeds until driven off; doesn't defend ground otherwise and doesn't pursue past a body it's already claimed.
Special: A wound that doesn't close forces a Test of Constitution to use that limb at all until treated.

Shrine Wraith (Undead) - AD: 7d6+3 [MA: 2]
Description: A cowled shape a torch's light passes through rather than off; a voice comes from it before a face ever does.
Range: Never leaves the shrine's own halls; every Wraith met is met inside them.
Sign: A cold that reaches a party before the Wraith itself is in sight.
Disposition: Speaks first, in Grozzgur, to anything that hasn't already drawn a weapon; answers steel with steel the instant one is.
Special: A touch forces a Test of Sanity rather than Constitution; the Wraith carries no other attack.

Sealed Warden (Undead) - AD: 8d6+6 [MA: 1]
Description: Armored past recognizing the shape underneath, standing exactly where it was set with a weapon already drawn.
Range: One chamber, one door, or one seal; a Warden met anywhere else was moved there by someone, not by itself.
Sign: The seal or door it stands beside is always undamaged, whatever's happened around it.
Disposition: Lets pass whatever the seal's own mark permits, and stops - or kills - whatever tries the seal without it.
Special: Cannot be lured, bribed, or driven more than a few strides from what it guards; nothing forces it to leave.

The Bound Depth (Undead) - AD: 14d6+4 [MA: 4]
Description: Filling more of the chamber than torchlight ever reaches all of at once; what's seen of it doesn't agree between two people who saw it.
Range: The one deepest chamber the Old Tongue's builders set it in; it has not been found anywhere else in eight hundred years.
Sign: Every other bound thing in the ravine falls still at once, wherever it is, the moment the Depth itself stirs.
Disposition: Does not negotiate, does not chase, and has not yet been driven off by anything a party could plausibly field.
Special: What was set as its task eight hundred years ago is still running; ending the working, not the fight, is what would stop it - and nobody now living knows what that working was.

The Formless (Horror) - AD: 9d6+2 [MA: 3]
Description: No fixed outline a torch settles on twice; what a blade meets gives rather than cuts.
Range: The ravine's lowest-cut chambers, wherever standing water collects; met more than once only by whoever keeps going back down.
Sign: A slick, unbroken sheen on stone that should be dry.
Disposition: Rises against anything that disturbs the water it rests in; leaves anything that doesn't.
Special: A weapon's edge does not hold against it; every strike costs the blade a point of sharpness a smith must restore.

The Old Warden (Construct) - AD: 10d6+5 [MA: 1]
Description: Cut from the same stone as the tunnels around it, jointed at the shoulder and knee the way a person is.
Range: One post, set by whoever cut the ravine's oldest tunnels; it has not moved from it in nine hundred years.
Sign: Tool-marks on the floor before it, worn by a pivot no foot ever made.
Disposition: Motionless until whatever it was built to stop is attempted, then acts without pause or warning.
Special: Answers to the same mark the Sealed Warden does; without that mark shown to it first, nothing stops the Warden from acting.
