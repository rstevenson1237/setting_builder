Bestiary of The Buried City

### Skeleton
Type: Undead
AD: 1d6-2
Special: Disadvantage on Fire Missiles against it; it never tires or flees.
Numbers: 12
Description: A clatter of bone on stone comes before the thing itself: bones in rotted wrappings, a bronze blade or nothing in hand. It haunts the tombs, halls and stairs of the pyramid and eats nothing. It walks its post until something living comes within sight, then closes without a word.

### Zombie
Type: Undead
AD: 1d6
Special: Always strikes last in a Round; stops only when it takes a Wound.
Numbers: 8
Description: The smell of rot reaches a party first, then a swollen, grey-skinned corpse in the rags of a priest or servant. It lurks in the cisterns, crypts and lower halls and eats nothing. It shambles toward the nearest warm body and ignores anything else.

### Mummy
Type: Undead
AD: 3d6+1
Special: Its touch leaves Diseased unless a Test of Constitution is passed; a Wound of Fire is rolled twice.
MA: 2
Numbers: 1
Description: A dry, resinous smell hangs about its tomb, and the body itself is tall and wrapped in stiff linen, gold leaf flaking from its face. It keeps to sealed tombs and the king's halls and eats nothing. It waits until its seal is broken, then pursues the one who broke it.

### Ghoul
Type: Undead
AD: 2d6
Special: A hit Holds the target unless a Test of Constitution is passed.
MA: 2
Numbers: 6
Description: A charnel smell marks its crypts, and the thing is a gaunt grey man-shape on all fours with a split jaw and black nails. It lives among the crypts and ossuaries of the pyramid and eats the dead. It rushes whoever disturbs its feeding and drags the fallen back to its den.

### Gargoyle
Type: Construct
AD: 3d6
Special: Looks like carved stone until it moves; edged weapons strike it at Disadvantage.
MA: 2
Numbers: 2
Description: Grating stone betrays it a moment before it moves: a crouching winged figure of black stone with a lion's head and clawed hands. It sits on the ledges and doorways of the pyramid and eats nothing. It holds a doorway and strikes anyone who crosses it without the right word.

### Giant Scorpion
Type: Beast
AD: 2d6+1
Special: Its sting is a Lethal Poison: a first-level character who fails any Test of Constitution against it dies.
MA: 2
Numbers: 3
Description: Fresh tracks cross the sand, and the maker is a sand-pale scorpion the size of a dog, its tail raised over its back. It ranges the dunes, buried streets and dry cisterns, eating lizards and anything that stumbles into its burrow. It lies buried and strikes whatever steps within reach, then withdraws.

### Giant Centipede
Type: Beast
AD: 1d6
Special: Its bite is a Nuisance Poison: one failed Test of Constitution costs a Wound of Poison.
Numbers: 4
Description: Its legs click on stone from somewhere in the wall, and it is a reddish centipede as long as a man's leg. It nests in wall cracks, rubble and dark halls and eats rats and carrion. It strikes from a crack and retreats into it.

### Giant Spider
Type: Beast
AD: 2d6
Special: Its web Holds whoever touches it until a Test of Constitution frees them.
MA: 2
Numbers: 2
Description: Strands of web mark the way, and the spider hangs in the middle of one across a doorway: hairy and grey, its body as big as a man's torso. It haunts the dry stairwells and ceilings of the pyramid and eats what it webs. It waits in the web and drops on whoever is caught in it.

### Giant Rat
Type: Beast
AD: 1d6-2
Special: A bite may leave Diseased: a failed Test of Constitution.
Numbers: 20
Description: Scratching and squeals run ahead of it, and the rats are the size of dogs, mangy, with yellow teeth. They infest rubble, cisterns and the pyramid's upper halls and eat anything. They swarm what they outnumber and flee what they do not, back into their tunnels.

### Stirge
Type: Beast
AD: 1d6-2
Special: A hit clings and drains: a Wound each Round until it is killed.
Numbers: 6
Description: Wings flutter in the dark overhead before the shape is clear: a bat-winged thing with a long hollow beak and a rust-red body the size of a cat. It roosts in caves and on the ceilings of the lower halls and eats blood. It drops from the ceiling on the warmest target and flies off when full.

### Hyena
Type: Beast
AD: 1d6
Special: none
Numbers: 8
Description: Tracks circle the party's camp, and the animals are spotted, sloped-backed dogs with heavy jaws and a laugh in the throat. They range the dunes and the ruined city's edges, eating carrion and whatever tires. They trail a party at a distance and rush anything that lags behind.

### Soldier
Type: Men
AD: 1d6
Special: none
Numbers: 10
Description: A faction's colour shows at the wrist of a man in leather and bronze with a spear and a round shield. He holds the faction's halls and camps and eats what the faction gives him. He challenges a stranger at spear's length and calls for orders before he fights.

### Acolyte
Type: Men
AD: 1d6-2
Special: Binds a Wound in a Round: a Test of Skill and the Wound is not lost.
Numbers: 8
Description: A bell sounds as a shaven-headed man or woman in a pale robe comes into view, a bronze bell at the belt. The Ruhiahe keep them in their shrines and halls, and they eat what the Ruhiahe grow. An acolyte rings the bell to call help and fights only to cover the one who rings it.

### Adept
Type: Men
AD: 1d6
Special: Reads one scroll aloud, once, as a scroll of sleep.
Numbers: 6
Description: A smell of ink and old paper comes first, then a lean figure in an ink-stained robe with a satchel of scrolls and a pale stick. Adepts work in the Sihuahet libraries and workshops and eat what the Sihuahet store. An adept asks questions first and reads the scroll only when the answers are wrong.

### Cultist
Type: Men
AD: 1d6
Special: Chants: while two or more chant, a party within earshot rolls a Test of Sanity at 1d.
Numbers: 8
Description: Chanting carries ahead of a figure in black cloth with a bronze mask and ash on the hands. Cultists keep to the cult's halls below the sixth level and eat what is left at the altar. A cultist chants on sight of a stranger and drags captives toward the altar.

### Delver
Type: Men
AD: 1d6
Special: Fights Disadvantaged in the dark but reads a trap's trigger on a Test of Skill.
Numbers: 6
Description: A scuffed rope-mark on a wall shows an outsider has come through, and the outsider is sand-browned, in patched leather, with a rope coil and a crowbar. Delvers work the upper levels and ruined streets and eat what they steal. A delver trades with a party that waves a coin and ambushes one that waves a blade.

### Wraith
Type: Undead
AD: 4d6+1
Special: Passes through stone; only enchanted weapons harm it, and a failed Test of Constitution against its touch costs a Wound.
MA: 2
Numbers: 1
Description: A voice or the scrape of a crown on stone comes first, then a tall pale figure in a king's robe with no face under its crown. It keeps to thrones, tombs and the cult's cells and needs nothing. It drifts toward the nearest living thing and falls back to its tomb at the touch of daylight.

### Masked Dreamer
Type: Men
AD: 1d6
Special: none
Numbers: 12
Description: A low humming drifts down the quays before the figure appears: lean, in a painted animal mask, sleeves stained with spore and eyes unfocused. It wanders the lake city's quarters, quays and gardens and eats the fungus it grows. It parts for a wearer of a mask and follows one who goes bare-faced, humming, at a few paces.
