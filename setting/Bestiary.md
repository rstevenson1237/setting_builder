Bestiary of Carrdun

Salt-flat Adder (Beast) - AD: 1d6
Description: A pale, short-bodied viper that buries to its eyes in loose salt and dust,
striking anything that steps within reach.
Range: The dry flats south of the causeway, dozens to a mile; feeds on burrowing rodents
and ground-nesting birds.
Sign: A faint S-shaped drag in disturbed salt, gone within a day.
Disposition: Strikes only when stepped on or nearly so; otherwise holds still and lets a
party walk past unaware.

Carrion Kite (Beast) - AD: 1d6
Description: A lean, red-necked scavenger bird that circles in twos and threes wherever
something has recently died.
Range: The whole flood-plain, common; eats carrion almost exclusively, fresh kills only
when nothing else offers.
Sign: Slow circling specks overhead, visible for miles before whatever drew them is found.
Disposition: Never attacks anything living; a wheeling kite is itself the sign, not the
danger.

Steppe Wolf (Beast) - AD: 2d6, MA: 5
Description: A rangy grey wolf that hunts in packs of four to nine across open country,
faster than anything a party can outrun on foot.
Range: The steppe beyond the ford, in packs; brings down anything smaller than a horse and
scavenges the rest.
Sign: Fresh tracks in a loose line, a kill site picked to bone within a day.
Disposition: Tests a party from a distance before committing - circling, feinting closer -
and breaks off entirely if the first exchange goes badly for it.

Sand Cat (Beast) - AD: 2d6
Description: A tawny, oversized wildcat that hunts alone by night and dens in rock or
ruin by day.
Range: Broken ground and old stonework across the region, solitary; takes anything from
rodents up to a bound goat.
Sign: A single clawed scrape at a den mouth, and eyes catching lamplight before the shape
does.
Disposition: Avoids anything larger than itself unless cornered or guarding a den; a
sighting is far more common than an attack.

Tomb Jackal (Beast) - AD: 2d6, MA: 4
Description: A rust-coated jackal that dens in broken stonework and has learned to dig
where the ground has recently been turned.
Range: Anywhere within a day of the exposed ruins, in loose family packs of three to six;
scavenges graves as readily as carcasses.
Sign: Fresh digging at the base of a wall or mound, too neat to be erosion.
Disposition: Bold around fresh diggings, cowardly anywhere else - it will contest a
half-buried find but will not press a fight it did not start.

River Ox (Beast) - AD: 3d6
Description: A heavy-shouldered, black-horned bovine that wallows in the shallows and
grazes the reed banks in small herds.
Range: The riverbank and marsh edge, in herds of six to twenty; grazes reed and river-grass.
Sign: Flattened wallows in the mud and a heavy, sweetish smell carrying off the water.
Disposition: Placid and herd-bound; a bull will charge anything that startles a cow with a
calf, and nothing else provokes it.

Horse-lord's War Stallion (Beast) - AD: 3d6, MA: 6
Description: A branded, half-wild breeding stallion that has slipped its tribe's herd and
now runs alone or with a handful of mares.
Range: The open steppe, singly or in tiny bands; grazes wide territory and defends it
against anything on four legs.
Sign: A wide-churned patch of ground where it has fought off a rival, and hoofprints far
too large for a common horse.
Disposition: Ignores anything on foot that keeps its distance; charges any mounted rider
outright, tribe or stranger alike.

River Serpent (Beast) - AD: 6d6
Description: A genuinely enormous, non-venomous constrictor that lairs in undercut banks
and takes prey from the water's edge.
Range: The deep channel and its undercut banks, solitary, each holding a long stretch of
river to itself; takes anything that comes to drink.
Sign: A smooth-sided slide in the bank mud wider than a barrel, and a stretch of bank where
nothing else comes to drink.
Disposition: Ambushes from the water without warning; will not leave the water to pursue
anything that reaches dry ground more than a body-length back from the edge.

Border Raider (Man) - AD: 2d6
Description: A mounted skirmisher riding for coin or kinship rather than any crown, armed
light and ready to run from a fight that turns.
Range: The steppe and its trails, in bands of three to eight; lives off raid, toll, and
whatever a caravan will pay to be let alone.
Sign: A recent hoof-line crossing a trail at speed, and nothing else disturbed.
Disposition: Opens with a demand - toll, goods, or passage - and only presses to violence
if refused outright or already outnumbering the party badly.

Garrison Deserter (Man) - AD: 2d6
Description: A former Carrdun soldier gone to ground rather than face what deserting a
border post costs, armed with whatever he carried off.
Range: Broken ground within a few days of the fort, alone or in twos; steals food and tools
rather than hunts for them.
Sign: A cold camp abandoned in a hurry, and small thefts nobody in the garrison will
account for.
Disposition: Avoids contact with anyone who might recognise a uniform; fights only when
cornered, and fights to kill when he does.

Salvage Digger-bandit (Man) - AD: 3d6
Description: A dig-crew hand who has decided the split isn't worth reporting to, armed
with a looted blade and whatever tools double as weapons.
Range: The exposed diggings and the routes away from them, in twos and threes; lives on
what a hidden find can be traded for.
Sign: A concealed cache dug fresh into an unrelated slope, and tools gone missing from a
crew manifest.
Disposition: Bargains first if outnumbered, fights hard and dirty if not - a digger who has
already hidden one find has already decided what he'll do to keep it.

Cult Initiate (Man) - AD: 3d6
Description: A believer marked with a scratched coil-sign under the collar, calm in a way
that reads as wrong before anyone can say why.
Range: Anywhere near the sealed sections of the ruin, usually in threes; takes no wage and
answers to somebody unseen.
Sign: The coil-mark scratched fresh into stone or wood nearby, always pointing the same
direction.
Disposition: Will not be provoked into a fight it can avoid, and will not answer a direct
question about what it is doing - it simply keeps doing it, calmly, until stopped.

Coil-Marked (Humanoid) - AD: 4d6
Description: A lineage born into the cult's oldest families, skin patched with fine
overlapping scale along the throat and forearms, forked of tongue from birth.
Range: The sealed sections and whatever they connect to, in raiding bands of four to
seven; take what their kin do not provide.
Sign: A shed patch of fine scale caught on rough stone, and a taste like copper on the air
before they are seen.
Disposition: Wary rather than hostile on first contact - they gauge a stranger's reaction
to what they are before deciding whether to talk or to fight.

Marsh-kin (Humanoid) - AD: 2d6
Description: A quiet, web-fingered people living on stilted platforms in the reed marsh,
pale from a life spent mostly in shadow and water.
Range: The reed marsh downstream, in small kin-groups; fish, trap, and gather reed rather
than farm or raise stock.
Sign: A woven fish-weir strung between reeds, tended and clearly not abandoned.
Disposition: Watches from cover before showing itself, and trades readily for iron or
salt; violence is a last resort it will walk away from if given the room to.

Barrow-Folk (Humanoid) - AD: 3d6
Description: A small, stooped people who have lived in the margins of the tomb-city longer
than Carrdun has existed, dressed in whatever grave-cloth has gone soft enough to wear.
Range: Warrens dug into the unexcavated mounds, in extended family groups; live on what the
ruin sheds and what they can forage above it.
Sign: A hand-sized niche cut into a mound face, always empty, always facing the same star.
Disposition: Reclusive to the point of vanishing rather than being seen; will speak, from
hiding, to anyone who leaves an offering and asks rather than digs.

Husk (Undead) - AD: 5d6
Description: A preserved corpse still wearing the office it died in, moving with the slow,
unhurried certainty of something that has never needed to hurry.
Range: Sealed chambers within the tomb-city, alone or in the twos and threes they were
interred in; needs nothing and consumes nothing.
Sign: The smell of dry spice rather than decay, and dust that has not settled in a
draft-free room.
Disposition: Ignores anything that does not disturb what it was set to guard, and moves
without hesitation or mercy against anything that does.

Tomb Warden (Undead) - AD: 7d6
Description: A bound priest raised specifically to hold one threshold, articulate in
Nemhet and nothing else, wrapped in wire-thin gold that has not tarnished.
Range: One threshold each, never leaves it; requires nothing, guards absolutely.
Sign: Gold wire glinting in a doorway shadow, and a silence that swallows sound past it.
Disposition: Speaks first, in Nemhet, if approached without violence - it can be
reasoned with on its own narrow terms, but never moved from its post.

The Drowned (Undead) - AD: 8d6
Description: What the flood left of someone who died in it six hundred years ago, waterlogged
and patient, surfacing only where the old channel still runs beneath the ground.
Range: Anywhere water still reaches the drowned district, singly; does not eat, but pulls
the living toward the water given the chance.
Sign: A cold patch in still water, and footprints that end at the waterline in both
directions.
Disposition: Rises only for someone alone near deep water; will not follow onto dry ground
it cannot reach without surfacing first.

Ward-Statue (Construct) - AD: 9d6
Description: A stone guardian in the shape of no animal anyone alive can name, motionless
until its warding is broken and then relentless until it is remade or destroyed.
Range: One warded site each, immobile until triggered; needs nothing, cannot be
reasoned with once active.
Sign: Tool-marks on its base where generations of the curious have tried and failed to
move it, and a warding-mark, intact, somewhere on its surface.
Disposition: Inert and inert only - there is no partial state, no warning growl, nothing
between stillness and full violence the instant its warding is actually broken.

The Coiling (Horror) - AD: 14d6
Description: Not fully here. What surfaces is scale and coil and a hunger with no bottom
to it, and what does not surface is worse for being guessed at rather than seen.
Range: Wherever Xoss has been spoken in full and answered; does not hunt, because it has
never needed to.
Sign: A dropping temperature with no draft to explain it, and every animal in earshot
falling dead silent at once.
Disposition: This is not a fight. It does not negotiate, tire, or leave, and nothing in
Carrdun's Bestiary is built to end an encounter with it in the party's favour.
