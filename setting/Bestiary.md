Bestiary of The Buried City

### Skeleton
Type: Undead
AD: 1d6-2
Special: Disadvantage on Fire Missiles against it; it never tires or flees.
Sight: Bones in rotted wrappings, a bronze blade or nothing in hand.
Range: Tombs, halls and stairs of the pyramid; it eats nothing.
Numbers: 12
Sign: a sound
Disposition: Walks its post until something living comes within sight, then closes without a word.
Description: A sound reaches the party before the creature does, and then the bones in rotted wrappings come into view, a bronze blade in hand or nothing. It walks its post among the tombs, halls and stairs of the pyramid, eating nothing, and closes without a word on anything living that comes within sight.

### Zombie
Type: Undead
AD: 1d6
Special: Always strikes last in a Round; stops only when it takes a Wound.
Sight: A swollen, grey-skinned corpse in the rags of a priest or servant.
Range: Cisterns, crypts and the lower halls; it eats nothing.
Numbers: 8
Sign: a smell
Disposition: Shambles toward the nearest warm body and ignores anything else.
Description: A smell comes first, then a swollen, grey-skinned corpse in the rags of a priest or servant. It eats nothing and haunts the cisterns, crypts and lower halls, shambling toward the nearest warm body and ignoring anything else.

### Mummy
Type: Undead
AD: 3d6+1
Special: Its touch leaves Diseased unless a Test of Constitution is passed; a Wound of Fire is rolled twice.
MA: 2
Sight: A tall dry body wrapped in resin-stiff linen, gold leaf flaking from its face.
Range: Sealed tombs and the king's halls; it eats nothing.
Numbers: 1
Sign: a smell
Disposition: Waits in its tomb and rises only when its seal is broken, then pursues the one who broke it.
Description: A smell comes first, then a tall dry body wrapped in resin-stiff linen, gold leaf flaking from its face. It eats nothing and waits in its sealed tomb or the king's halls until the seal is broken, then pursues the one who broke it.

### Ghoul
Type: Undead
AD: 2d6
Special: A hit Holds the target unless a Test of Constitution is passed.
MA: 2
Sight: A gaunt grey man-shape on all fours with a split jaw and black nails.
Range: Crypts and ossuaries of the pyramid; it eats the dead.
Numbers: 6
Sign: a smell
Disposition: Rushes whoever disturbs its feeding and drags the fallen back to its den.
Description: A smell comes first, then a gaunt grey man-shape on all fours with a split jaw and black nails. It lives on the dead in the crypts and ossuaries of the pyramid, and rushes whoever disturbs its feeding, dragging the fallen back to its den.

### Gargoyle
Type: Construct
AD: 3d6
Special: Looks like carved stone until it moves; edged weapons strike it at Disadvantage.
MA: 2
Sight: A crouching winged figure of black stone with a lion's head and clawed hands.
Range: Ledges and doorways of the pyramid; it eats nothing.
Numbers: 2
Sign: a sound
Disposition: Holds a doorway and strikes anyone who crosses it without the right word.
Description: A sound comes first, then a crouching winged figure of black stone with a lion's head and clawed hands. It eats nothing and keeps to the ledges and doorways of the pyramid, holding a doorway and striking anyone who crosses it without the right word.

### Giant Scorpion
Type: Beast
AD: 2d6+1
Special: Its sting is a Lethal Poison: a first-level character who fails any Test of Constitution against it dies.
MA: 2
Sight: A sand-pale scorpion the size of a dog, its tail raised over its back.
Range: Dunes, buried streets and dry cisterns. It eats lizards and anything that stumbles into its burrow.
Numbers: 3
Sign: a track
Disposition: Lies buried and strikes whatever steps within reach, then withdraws.
Description: A track comes first, then a sand-pale scorpion the size of a dog, its tail raised over its back. It lives in the dunes, buried streets and dry cisterns, eating lizards and anything that stumbles into its burrow, and lies buried to strike whatever steps within reach before it withdraws.

### Giant Centipede
Type: Beast
AD: 1d6
Special: Its bite is a Nuisance Poison: one failed Test of Constitution costs a Wound of Poison.
Sight: A reddish centipede as long as a man's leg, its legs clicking on stone.
Range: Wall cracks, rubble and dark halls. It eats rats and carrion.
Numbers: 4
Sign: a sound
Disposition: Strikes from a crack in the wall and retreats into it.
Description: A sound comes first, then a reddish centipede as long as a man's leg, its legs clicking on stone. It lives in wall cracks, rubble and dark halls, eating rats and carrion, and strikes from a crack in the wall before retreating into it.

### Giant Spider
Type: Beast
AD: 2d6
Special: Its web Holds whoever touches it until a Test of Constitution frees them.
MA: 2
Sight: A hairy grey spider with a body as big as a man's torso, hanging in a web across a doorway.
Range: Dry stairwells and ceilings of the pyramid. It eats what it webs.
Numbers: 2
Sign: a mark
Disposition: Waits in its web and drops on whoever is caught in it.
Description: A mark comes first, then a hairy grey spider with a body as big as a man's torso, hanging in a web across a doorway. It eats what it webs in the dry stairwells and ceilings of the pyramid, waiting there to drop on whoever is caught in the web.

### Giant Rat
Type: Beast
AD: 1d6-2
Special: A bite may leave Diseased: a failed Test of Constitution.
Sight: A rat the size of a dog, mangy, with yellow teeth.
Range: Rubble, cisterns and the pyramid's upper halls. It eats anything.
Numbers: 20
Sign: a sound
Disposition: Swarms what it outnumbers and flees what it does not, back into its tunnels.
Description: A sound comes first, then rats the size of dogs, mangy, with yellow teeth. They eat anything in the rubble, cisterns and upper halls of the pyramid, swarming what they outnumber and fleeing back into their tunnels from what they do not.

### Stirge
Type: Beast
AD: 1d6-2
Special: A hit clings and drains: a Wound each Round until it is killed.
Sight: A bat-winged thing with a long hollow beak and a rust-red body the size of a cat.
Range: Caves and ceilings of the lower halls. It eats blood.
Numbers: 6
Sign: a sound
Disposition: Drops from the ceiling on the warmest target and flies off when full.
Description: A sound comes first, then a bat-winged thing with a long hollow beak and a rust-red body the size of a cat. It feeds on blood in the caves and on the ceilings of the lower halls, dropping on the warmest target and flying off when full.

### Acolyte

AD: 1d6-2

Range: Ruhiahe shrines and halls. It eats what the Ruhiahe grow.
Numbers: 8
Sign: a sound
Disposition: Rings a bell to call help and fights only to cover the one who rings it.
Description: A sound comes first, then a shaven-headed man or woman in a pale robe with a bronze bell at the belt. Acolytes live in the Ruhiahe shrines and halls on what the Ruhiahe grow, and ring the bell to call help, fighting only to cover the one who rings it.
