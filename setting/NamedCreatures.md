Named Creatures of The Buried City

### Krulox
Form: tongue
Type: Fiend
AD: 5d6+3
Special: A blow from its fist hurls its target back 10 feet and leaves a Wound of Crushing unless a Test of Constitution is passed.
MA: 3
Appears at: J.2
Reacts: Hostile to any who enter its hall unmasked, curious of one who wears a chosen's mask.
Wants: The demon's favour, and the first lieutenant's seat held against the other two.
Knows: Where the lost king hid the keys, as the cult has been told it.
Description: A broad figure in black basalt scale with a mask of bronze and a hand marked in ash, seated on a throne of bones.

### Xothox
Form: tongue
Type: Undead
AD: 5d6+1
Special: Its touch leaves Diseased unless a Test of Constitution is passed; a Wound of Fire is rolled twice.
MA: 3
Appears at: J.3
Reacts: Wary of any party that bargains, Hostile to one that threatens.
Wants: To be released from the demon's service, and to see the other two lieutenants fall.
Knows: What the demon feeds on, and the word the lieutenants use at the door.
Description: A tall dry figure in black silk and flaking gold, lying on a raised couch, its eyes two points of red.

### Zhurjul
Form: tongue
Type: Fiend
AD: 4d6+3
Special: Strikes twice in a Round, once with each hand.
MA: 3
Appears at: J.4
Reacts: Hostile to any who enter, delighted by a challenge.
Wants: The strongest in the cult dead and itself the last standing.
Knows: Where the cult's patrols are, and which cells are weakest.
Description: A small red-skinned figure in ash-stained scale with a drum at its belt and a blade in each hand.

### Ghulox
Form: tongue
Type: Fiend
AD: 11d6+4
Special: Its presence costs a Test of Sanity each turn; its touch rolls a Wound of Fire twice and a Wound of Crushing once.
MA: 6
Appears at: L.4
Reacts: Hostile to any who enter its hall; it addresses a party that carries a chosen's mask.
Wants: The cynidiceans' dead fed to it every day, and the lost king's keys.
Knows: What the pyramid was built to hold, and the cynidiceans' own names.
Description: A huge shape of black bone and dim flame seated on a throne, its shadow too large for the hall.
