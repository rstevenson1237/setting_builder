Named Creatures of Telar

Vray (Man) - AD: 4d6+2 [MA: 1] - appears at E.8 Cultist Dig Camp
Motivation: Reclaim Nakhash's own regalia and reopen its Sanctum in full. Vray was not born to the title - she took it, and completing the three-piece regalia her order buried with its own dead is what would make the cell she leads a claim nobody else in the Serpent Cult can dispute.
Remembers: Exactly which of her four cultists balked at going deeper than the First Hall, and which two went past it anyway without asking her - she has not yet decided what that costs them, but she has not forgotten it either.
Wants: The Membership Coil, the Rod, and the Kaash Collar, in that order of how close she believes she is to each. She will trade information, a truce, or a share of what the dig has already turned up for any one of the three - but not for less than the whole set, and not twice.
Description: Unremarkable in travelling clothes, addressed as Coil-Mother by everyone in her cell and by no title at all by anyone who doesn't know who she is. Gives no order she is not willing to have overheard, and has not yet picked up a digging tool herself in weeks of watching others do it.
Special: Knows Nakhash's layout better than her own cell does, including which mounds are decoys and which lead down - information she has not shared with all four of her own cultists, let alone anyone else.

Vaaluk (Humanoid) - AD: 7d6+2 [MA: 2] - appears at D.15 Nirguk Warren, D.18 Nirguk's Hoard
Motivation: Keep the enclave where it is. Daghash is the last place Nirguk holds that has a working Essath binding above it, and Vaaluk has spent a long tenancy making sure nothing gives anyone a reason to come down and test that.
Remembers: The exact wording of the Sealed Door's binding-line, which it has read a great many times and never once spoken - and that the crude copy scratched beneath it appeared during its own tenancy, cut by somebody who came down the stair and did not come back up it.
Wants: To know how the party got through the Sealed Door. Not to punish it - to find out whether the way in can be closed again, which is the only question in the ziggurat that matters to the enclave.
Description: A head longer than any other Nirguk in Daghash and greyer at the scale, moving at a deliberate pace the young ones do not keep to. It speaks to a party rather than about them, in accented but entirely fluent common, and it is the only thing in the ziggurat that will negotiate.
Special: Reads and speaks Essath binding-script fluently, so a seal, ward or threshold that stops the party stops nothing about Vaaluk - it can open the Sealed Door from either side, and has chosen for a long time not to.
Special: Will trade rather than fight, and the price is always information about the way in. It knows what the coiled relief wants and where the figurine was kept, and will say so to a party that gives it something better.
