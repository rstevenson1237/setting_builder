Named Creatures of Roadend

### Gronab
Type: Humanoid
AD: 3d6+2
Special: none
MA: 2
Appears at: C.5
Reacts: bellows for the warren the moment strangers show, and fights from his seat
Wants: the keep's grey-tower banner, nailed to the wall behind his seat
Knows: which wagons leave the keep
Description: A goblin half again as broad as the rest, grey-green gone to grey, wearing a wagon-wheel tyre as a collar and a kettle-helm with a horn riveted to it. Bare-armed, with a cleaver in each fist.
