Named Creatures of Khirengaard

Factor Aldric Vantor (Man) - AD: 2d6 - appears at A.1
Motivation: Survive his post long enough to matter - both of his predecessors died violently, and he means to be the first Concern factor who leaves Khirengaard on his own terms. He still pushes the Concern's claim deeper into the wold when it's safe to, but he's quicker than his predecessors to let a disputed crossing go unpushed rather than provoke the guild or the wold directly.
Description: A careful, watchful man in his middle years, more comfortable with a ledger than a blade, though he keeps a wold-iron dagger at his belt he's never had to use. Speaks in the Concern's language of contracts and charters even when the subject is a friend's death, and never signs anything in his own hand until a clerk has read it back to him twice.

Torvin Ashe (Undead) - AD: 2d6, MA: 3 - appears at C.5, C.11
Motivation: Reassemble itself and recover the wold-iron blade it was carrying when it died - each scattered piece is certain, in whatever passes for thought in a Cutter's Blight, that finding both will put a stop to what's happening to it. It has never once succeeded, and has no way of knowing it never will.
Description: The animate remains of Torvin Ashe, a boundary-cutter who crossed a line into the deep workings without a proper blessing years before the guild was formally chartered, and never came back out. Silent, fast for its size, and split across the upper and middle galleries as independently hunting pieces - a finger, an ear, a length of spine - each one searching for the rest of what it used to be.

Marek Coll (Undead) - AD: 3d6 +2 - appears at C.12
Motivation: Walk the same short, final route over and over, the way he died twenty-three years ago - not out of any purpose he could name, but because the wold never finished with what happened to him, and hasn't let him stop since. Interrupting his path is the only thing that draws his attention away from it.
Description: What's left of one of the seven prospectors who breached the vein and never came out, grey-skinned and cold to the touch, still carrying the rotted remains of the pack he died with. He scratched six names into a cloth before whatever caught him finished the job - his own name isn't among them, and neither is the fate that ends the list.

The Bound Sentinel (Construct) - AD: 4d6 +2 - appears at C.18
Motivation: Guard what the Marrgath left on the shelf behind it, exactly as instructed, for as long as it takes. It has no goal beyond this and no memory of anything else - correcting a disturbance and returning to dormancy is the whole of what it wants.
Description: A humanoid figure of fused river-stone bearing the same spiral chisel-work found throughout the Marrgath's older passages, motionless until its charge is disturbed. Silent even when active, and relentless - it does not tire, does not negotiate, and does not stop once the sequence that wakes it has been gotten wrong.

The Ninefold Hunger (Horror) - AD: 6d6 +4, MA: 2 - appears at C.24
Motivation: Hunt anything that lingers too long near the breached vein - not out of hunger in any ordinary sense, but as though the Hollow itself uses it to answer intrusion the way the wold above answers a crossing. It never leaves the deepest galleries and never stops noticing what enters them.
Description: A many-limbed thing that moves through tunnels far too narrow for its apparent size, glimpsed only in fragments and never described the same way by any two witnesses. Named for the nine prospectors who breached the vein above it - seven of whom it may or may not have had a hand in finishing.
