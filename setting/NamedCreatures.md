Named Creatures of Brackvaen

Gellen Torsling (Man) - AD: 2d6 - appears at A.2 Rendfast
Motivation: to avoid being the one who tables board eleven. He is not concealing the clause out of greed and not out of fear exactly; he is a careful man who has understood that the settlement he administers may grant the Assize a thing it cannot possibly hold, and that saying so aloud converts a manageable ambiguity into a decision. So he gathers, delays, and commissions - and would go on doing that if no party ever walked into his hall.
Description: An Assize head in his sixties, grey sash, ink to the second knuckle, who reads standing up because his back will not take a stool. He answers questions with questions as a matter of professional habit and it reads as evasion until a party notices he does it to everyone including his own clerks.
Remembers: which of the eleven copies were cut by which of the eleven men, because he has the itemised account and has read it too many times. Wants: somebody else's name on the finding.

Druva Halmling (Man) - AD: 1d6 - appears at A.7 Halmgaard
Motivation: to keep the long room exactly as it is. Twenty-two years of it, and she measures the health of Torsgaard by who is sitting where - which is why an empty bench end has become the thing she cannot let alone. She would be doing this if the stair had never opened.
Description: Keeps the holding's only lodging house, works the whole length of the room, and stops mid-sentence to listen to somebody else's table without apologising for it. She repeats perhaps a third of what she hears and is scrupulous about which third.
Remembers: every regular's seat, and every night any of them missed. Wants: Ferris back on his bench, or a reason good enough to give the seat to somebody else.

Vetha Naugling (Man) - AD: 1d6 - appears at A.9 Vethgaard
Motivation: to be found out by somebody she can deal with. Eleven years behind a speaking hatch holding boards that were never hers to hold, waiting for one person to do the arithmetic - because she cannot raise the subject herself and cannot go on indefinitely, and because the alternative to a party working it out is the Assize working it out. She has had eleven years to prepare the offer and it is a good one.
Description: Brined to the shoulder, which is well past the elbow and well past what anyone comes back from. A voice with nothing distinctive in it at all, deployed deliberately. The hand at the hatch is always wrapped, and half the pan crews wrap their hands.
Remembers: the exact weight of every board on the fourth tier and who each came off. Wants: a visible holder - a face that can stand at the weigh-house and be the creditor, while she keeps the arithmetic.

Ondtla Faelling (Man) - AD: 3d6 - appears at A.10 Ondtstad, C.1 Immorael
Motivation: to establish that the Brined can hold property, because the people who are the only ones able to reach the stair are the only ones who cannot legally own anything they bring up. She works the dawn window, takes a third share, and pushes the question with anyone who might carry it - and would be doing all three if the party never arrived.
Description: A Faelhond digger, forearms wrapped in oiled leather in all weathers, out before dawn and back before noon. Entirely open about her terms and entirely closed about what happens to somebody who works below too long. Marked six doorframes at Ondtstad herself.
Remembers: which courses flood on which tide, and the name of every person who has gone down her stair and not come up. Wants: the stair open, and the second course kept dry - and she knows the Faelhond made it so, and does not know that is why the eastern water dropped.

Zhakur-of-Nine (Humanoid) - AD: 4d6 +2 - MA: 2 - appears at B.3 Ghutrrun
Motivation: to have the founding arrangement with Torsgaard put in writing before the last elder who remembers what was actually agreed is dead - which is to say, before she is. Underneath that sits the older and heavier goal: keep Siwapelu drowned. She has held both positions since long before the stair opened and will hold them after.
Description: A Vingash Channel-Elder, bitter iron through both outer ears, nine rings, the knotted cord of nine reaches at her belt. She will talk at length, will trade what the Vingash know about Nessekire, and is not difficult to reach - she is difficult to satisfy, and the price is always a commitment about the stair.
Remembers: the sounding of every reach she holds, and eleven people who went west in the Gell Year - she keeps their rings laid out on the boards facing the water they went into. Wants: the eastern water back up. The four-foot drop frightened her more than anything since the founding and she does not know what caused it either.

Odaranu (Undead) - AD: 4d6 - appears at C.10 Wosaora
Motivation: to complete the errand, which is the only thing it has. It walks thirty-one paces from the east arch to the west and back, and has since before Torsgaard existed. It is not hunting, guarding, or waiting; per `setting/Procedures.md` a cured thing continues whatever it was doing, and this is what it was doing. It will continue whether or not anyone ever enters the room again.
Description: Odarath, nine hundred years dead and kept - skin like tallow-soaked leather, salt standing in the creases of the joints, carrying nothing. Named by the Faelhond rather than by anyone who knew it; *Odaranu* is their guess, pronounced wrong, and means only "of the king".
Remembers: nothing that can be reached. What it retains is the route, and the route is the entry's whole content: the flags down the room's centre are dished an inch below the flags at the walls, the wear stops dead at the west arch, and at the east end it runs six feet out into the gallery and stops. Wants: the six feet. Whatever ended there, ended.
