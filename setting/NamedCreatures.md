Named Creatures of Fellward

Nazirur (Undead) - AD: 9d6 - appears at C.1 The Sealed Chamber
Motivation: Holds the Sealed Chamber exactly as sealed as the day it was closed, a duty older than every living claim on Ingaraz and answerable to none of them.
Remembers: That the iron circlet was set on the shelf beside it rather than on its own brow - buried with real ceremony, but not with the crown it held in life.
Wants: The circlet back where it belongs, though nothing in the chamber suggests it can act on that want itself.
Description: What's left of a king the old foundations' builders buried with real ceremony and no peace to go with it - name and station stripped by the same "-ur" that marks anything former in the Old Tongue. It ignores anyone who doesn't breach the chamber; anyone who does gets no warning before it acts.
