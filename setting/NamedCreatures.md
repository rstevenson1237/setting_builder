Named Creatures of The Ashwrack Coast

Ossvane (Undead) - AD: 3d6 +1 - appears at C.21 The Bell-Wight's Chamber
Motivation: Ossvane was Vael's harbor-warden, whose duty was to ring a warning bell at the first sign of the tide's temper - the same night the sea rose without warning, silencing them before they could. In death, Ossvane still waits to fulfill that one unfinished duty: to answer, or sound, any bell before it's too late again.
Description: A drowned figure in warden's rags, endlessly turning in place as though scanning a shoreline only it can see - deceptively still until any ringing, chiming, or tolling sound reaches it, at which point it moves with sudden, single-minded urgency toward the source.

Corrow (Man) - AD: 2d6 +1 - appears at B.3 The Bandit Watch
Motivation: Corrow's own tally with the sea has run long - close enough to triple digits that he's stopped mentioning the exact count. Every toll taken on the road is meant to buy down that debt before it comes due, not to build any lasting stake in banditry.
Description: A lean, watchful man who leads more by nerve than force, always doing arithmetic under his breath. He'd rather take a toll than a life, but won't hesitate if refused outright - a bad debt is still better than a dead one.
