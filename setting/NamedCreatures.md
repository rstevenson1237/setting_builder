Named Creatures of Thornmarch

### Ansel the Hermit
Form: epithet
Type: Men
AD: 1d6-1
Special: Clears Poisoned or Diseased from anyone he nurses through a night.
Appears at: C.3
Reacts: Watches from his bean row and talks to anyone who shuts his thorn gate behind them, and nurses the sick for news.
Wants: News of the realm, and to be left to his stones.
Knows: Bugbears gather at the standing stones on dark nights, and the stones hum before a storm.
Description: A bent old man in patched wool, white-bearded, with a staff and a sling at his belt.

### Nib the Trapwright
Form: epithet
Type: Humanoid
AD: 1d6+0
Special: By wire from his den he can drop the Pit Run's boards or swing the Burrows' west door each Round.
Appears at: F.5
Reacts: Bargains for his life, then flees through a trap he has rigged.
Wants: The tribute kept from the goblins, and the strangers gone.
Knows: The goblins' collection days, and which of his traps spare the tribe's own.
Description: A hunched, greying kobold in a coat hung with wire and springs, a ring of tiny keys at his belt.

### Wen the Tar-Thumbed
Form: epithet
Type: Humanoid
AD: 2d6+0
Special: A bone whistle brings two Wolves from the kennel each Round.
Appears at: G.6
Reacts: Whistles for the wolves, then offers a bargain over the road's spoils.
Wants: The road's wagons taken, and the orcs broken.
Knows: The wagon days, copied from a carter's waybill, and how the orcs hate his tribe.
Description: A gaunt goblin in a wolfskin cloak, one thumb tar-black to the wrist, a bone whistle at his neck.

### Hurk the Brewmaster
Form: epithet
Type: Humanoid
AD: 3d6+1
Special: A bellow from him brings the feast hall's Orcs to his cave in two Rounds.
Appears at: H.6
Reacts: Bellows for the hall at the first threat, then fights to keep his seat and offers nothing.
Wants: His seat held against his captains, and the goblins broken.
Knows: Which captain schemes against him, and that the goblins loose their wolves at dusk.
Description: A broad grey orc gone to fat and muscle, one tusk snapped, a horn cup lashed to his belt and a notched axe in his fist.

### Varn the Red-Sashed
Form: epithet
Type: Humanoid
AD: 4d6+1
Special: A horn at his belt calls the barracks' Hobgoblins to his hall in one Round, in step.
Appears at: I.6
Reacts: Gives a challenge from his dais, then parleys in the black speech with anyone who comes unarmed.
Wants: The shrine's favour before the gnolls win it, and the Kurzun broken.
Knows: What the gnolls sell and to whom, the tithe days, and that his seal-key opens the sanctum door.
Description: A tall copper-skinned hobgoblin in blackened mail, a red sash across his chest knotted with a captain's tally, a long horn at his hip.

### Ruk the Collar-Keeper
Form: epithet
Type: Humanoid
AD: 3d6+2
Special: A target struck by his chain-whip is Held.
MA: 2
Appears at: J.5
Reacts: Names a price for any captive, and loosens his chain-whip and the pack on a party that will not pay.
Wants: A buyer's coin, and the hobgoblins broken.
Knows: The way through the maze, which buyers pay best, and who has gone missing from the road.
Description: A huge hunched gnoll, grey at the ruff, iron collars slung over one shoulder like a bandolier and a chain-whip coiled at his hip.

### Mort the Tithe-Taker
Form: epithet
Type: Humanoid
AD: 4d6+2
Special: A pull on the bell-cord by his bed rings a bell at the stair's top, and acolytes reach the back stair within a turn.
Appears at: K.5
Reacts: Waits hidden, strikes the last of a party, then falls back to the stair door; does not parley.
Wants: Every tribe's tithe carried up the stair, and no stranger on it.
Knows: Where each tribe keeps its tithe.
Description: A gaunt bugbear seven feet tall, hide-wrapped and soot-handed, one ear torn off, a short spiked club in each fist.

### Tanglehorn the Keeper
Form: epithet
Type: Horror
AD: 5d6+1
Special: Charging down a straight passage, it Holds the first target in its path.
MA: 2
Appears at: L.5
Reacts: Charges any light or sound in the passages and does not stop to parley.
Wants: Intruders found and driven to its nest, and the collection added to.
Description: A bull-headed giant nine feet tall, matted and hollow-flanked, one horn snapped, a makers' chain hung round its neck with a bronze token on it.

### Veck the Hollow
Form: epithet
Type: Men
AD: 4d6+1
Special: Raises one Zombie each Round from a body lying in the shrine.
MA: 2
Appears at: M.5
Reacts: Calls the dead and speaks to the party only while they rise.
Wants: Every tribe's tithe, the keep's wagon days, and the sanctum kept shut.
Knows: Who the Black Altar's agents inside the keep are, and what the sanctum was built to hold.
Description: A tall gaunt man in black robes, shaven and hollow-cheeked, a black candle in one hand and an iron inverted triangle on a chain at his chest.
