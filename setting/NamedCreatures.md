Named Creatures of The Isle of the Serpent Crown

### Kunotewa
Form: tongue
Type: Beast
AD: 10d6+2
Special: Its bite is Lethal (Test of Constitution, 3d, Crushing); its tread shakes loose earth and water.
MA: 3
Appears at: A.1, D.12
Reacts: Comes down out of the hills at dusk by the trail from the Wall Ridge, eats what is in the bowls at the gate's foot, and is back on the ridge by dawn; it takes whatever stands between it and the bowls.
Wants: The food the villages set out, and to be left to eat it.
Knows: none
Description: A forty-foot reptile on two legs with a notched tail and a head as long as a man, the southern villages' name for it is the bone-maker. Its three-toed prints cross the cleared ground below the wall every dawn.

### Thazsheek
Form: tongue
Type: Humanoid
AD: 4d6+1
Special: Its bite is Lethal Poison (Test of Constitution, 3d, Poison); its hissed word calls a Test of Sanity (Nuisance) from any who understand it.
MA: 3
Appears at: HB.11
Reacts: Parleys in a mocking hiss, offers its wares, and calls the hall's snake men the moment a blade is drawn.
Wants: Captives enough to feed the Sleeper until it wakes.
Asks: A living captive from outside the halls; it offers a vial of the sleeper's venom, and the watch's rota at the sanctum stair.
Knows: The sanctum's every way, what the god eats, and which of the bound men sit in the villages' councils.
Member: Tuuzesh
Description: A man-high serpent with a bronze-crested hood and a priest's green sash, it reads at its lectern by lamplight with its coil laid across the floor. It speaks the common tongue badly and the serpent speech beautifully.

### Zuuthtuuz
Form: tongue
Type: Wyrm
AD: 14d6
Special: Asleep it takes no action; woken, its bite is Lethal Poison (Test of Constitution, 3d, Poison) and its gaze is a Lethal Test of Sanity.
MA: 6
Appears at: HC.6
Reacts: Sleeps on its heap; its breathing quickens when a captive is killed, the crown is lifted or it is struck, and it wakes fully on a third such disturbance in a day.
Wants: To be fed until it wakes.
Knows: none
Member: Tuuzesh
Description: A black serpent thick as a tower, its coils heaped over a mound of old bones and gold, each slow breath moving the air in the cavern. Its eyes are shut, and the cult's priests call it the Sleeper.

### Nealvaol
Form: tongue
Type: Beast
AD: 6d6
Special: Its coils Hold a target: Held, then a Wound each Round (Test of Constitution, 2d, Suffocation); it has Advantage in water.
MA: 4
Appears at: I.4
Reacts: Lies in the flooded hold's black water and seizes whatever wades or swims, and does not take the mer people who feed it.
Wants: The food the mer people throw it, and the dark.
Knows: none
Member: Nealeu
Description: A blind white eel as long as a longboat and as thick as a man's waist, its mouth ringed with needle teeth. The mer people keep it in the hold and sing to it.

### Rank the Crested
Form: epithet
Type: Humanoid
AD: 3d6+1
Special: Its stench sickens (as a troglodyte's), and its roar calls every troglodyte within hearing to it within the turn.
MA: 3
Appears at: J.7
Reacts: Parleys in grunts from its bone throne, trades captives for iron, and roars for the band if refused.
Wants: Iron, and fresh captives for the pit.
Asks: Iron blades or tools, for which it gives back a captive or its loot.
Knows: The hill jungle's trails and what lies near the sinkhole.
Description: A grey-skinned troglodyte half as large again as the rest, its back crest scarred and torn, a necklace of cave-men's teeth on its chest. It is the oldest of the band and the only one that bargains.
