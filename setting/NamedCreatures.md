Named Creatures of Roadend

### Gronab
Type: Humanoid
AD: 3d6+2
MA: 2
Appears at: C.5
Role: leader
Motivation: expanding - taking the road a little farther from the keep each week, and the plunder that goes with it
Reaches by: a survivor - a carter who got away from a stripped wagon and heard the name shouted
Remembers: a name - the name of anyone who has hurt him, and he shouts it to the tribe
Wants: the keep's grey-tower banner, nailed to the wall behind his seat
Description: A goblin half again as broad as the rest, grey-green gone to grey, wearing a wagon-wheel tyre as a collar and a kettle-helm with a horn riveted to it. Bare-armed, with a cleaver in each fist.
Special: none
