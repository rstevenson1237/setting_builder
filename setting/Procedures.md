Procedures of the Setting

## Tests and Consequences
**Ability tests**: roll 1d20; the test succeeds if the result is at or under the relevant
ability score (3-18). No ability fits the situation cleanly → the referee sets a flat target
of 11 instead of forcing a fit.

**Saving throws**: one save target per character, starting at 15 and improving by 1 every
two levels (minimum 5 at name level and beyond). The same number covers poison, paralysis,
breath, device, and spell alike - the ruling is in when a save applies, not in which of five
categories it falls under.

**Consequences**: failure is never "nothing happens." Before the die is rolled, the referee
names what failure costs - time, a resource, position, noise that draws something closer,
an opportunity that closes. If nothing believable can go wrong, don't call for a test at all;
if nothing is actually uncertain, don't call for one either.

**Salt-packing.** Packing a wound, a corpse, or a find fully in worked salt takes one Turn
per Man-sized subject (less for something smaller) and costs one sack of salt per subject
packed. No roll is required - while packed, whatever the subject was in the middle of
(bleeding, rotting, rising) is held off entirely, per `setting/Truths.md`. Breaking or
drawing the packing resumes it at once, also without a roll; the only test is whatever it
takes to reach the packing in the first place.

**Bond buyout.** A panner's bond is a flat cn value set when the paper is first written,
typically 200-2,000 cn against the ordinary week's wage below. Only the current paper-holder
sets the buyout price, and may raise it at any time before it is paid - there is no roll
against this, only negotiation and the Reactions each faction states in
`setting/Factions.md`.

## Traps
Three impact tiers, chosen for what the trap is meant to do to the party, not rolled for:

- **Tier 1 - Nuisance.** No lasting harm. Costs time, noise, a resource, or an alerted guard.
  The default tier for anything that is a setback rather than a threat.
- **Tier 2 - Wound.** Real damage or a lasting condition - poison, disease, sundered gear, a
  wound that won't close on its own. A save (see above) prevents or halves the effect where
  the trap's fiction allows for one.
- **Tier 3 - Lethal.** Kills or maims outright, with no save or a save at heavy penalty.
  Reserved for what the fiction actually justifies as that dangerous - the Mythic
  Underworld's oldest and cruelest works, not a default for "the deep parts of the dungeon."

## Searching
Searching costs a Turn (10 minutes) of game time per area searched. What is described as
visible is noticed without a roll, the instant the players say they look.

A feature hidden well enough to need searching is found automatically once the players
describe searching the location that holds it - no roll, however small the feature. Dice are
for what happens *while* time is spent searching (a wandering check, a torch burning down),
never for whether the search itself succeeds.

## Time
Time is bound differently by region rating - see each rating's Region Overview Layout field
and `patterns/region/` for the reasoning:

- **SAFE** is not time-bound. Scenes resolve as long as they need to; the clock is not the
  pressure here.
- **WILD** runs on 4 hours per action. Travelling, searching a landmark, or resting each cost
  one such block, and the day divides into it.
- **DANGEROUS** runs on the Danger track's countdown instead of real time. Actions advance the
  countdown by however many steps the fiction earns; hours and turns don't apply once the
  track is running.

## Scaling
Four numbers in this framework look alike and are not - keeping them separate is what keeps
the math honest.

- **Players** carry 1-6 dice of d4 through d12, d6 average. This is the yardstick everything
  else below is pitched against.
- **Creatures** carry d6 only, count 1-18, with a bonus of -2 to +6. Reads roughly as classic
  Hit Dice, and is anchored to party altitude (fixed at `setting/Bestiary.md`, step 2e) - never
  to the region die below.
- **Factions** carry d6 only and **no bonus**. A faction's count means something only
  relative to the other factions' counts - it is not a power level read in isolation.
- **Wage.** An ordinary week's bonded labor in the delta pays 20 standard cn - the scale
  every find in `setting/Treasure1.md` through `Treasure5.md` should be read against.
- **A region's die is a difficulty die, not a power level.** Rolled when a region-scale
  action is at stake - a WILD traverse, a DANGEROUS countdown step - never for an individual
  character's action (that's Tests and Consequences, above). Read: 1 = failure, 2-3 =
  complication, 4+ = success, so a *smaller* die is *harder*. d8 is baseline; d6 is slightly
  tougher; d10 slightly easier; d4 and d12 are deliberate outliers reserved for regions the
  setting wants to stand out. The same die sets how many locations a region holds - see
  `patterns/region/` for the count math.
