Procedures of Fellward

## Tests and Consequences
A test is one roll against a stated die, read the same three-band way as a Region Die
(below): 1 = failure - the attempt does not work, and something is lost (time, position,
notice, a resource); 2-3 = complication - the attempt works, but not cleanly, and costs
something extra; 4+ = success - clean, no cost beyond the attempt itself. Default test die
is d6 unless a Truth or a specific situation sets another. Never call for a test with no
meaningful failure state, and never allow a re-roll of a failed test until the situation
itself has changed.

## Traps
Every trap carries one impact tier, stated plainly in its entry:
- **Nuisance** - no lasting effect: lost time, a dropped or damaged item, noise that draws
  attention, a scare.
- **Damaging** - harm survivable with ordinary care: Action Dice lost, a wound, an injury
  that heals on its own time.
- **Lethal** - can kill outright, without warning or a saving throw to fall back on.

The tier is decided when the trap is written, not rolled for, and is what lets a referee
judge the stakes before springing it. The same three tiers apply at every region rating;
what differs by rating is whether the trap carries a tell (see the rating's own `Trap.md`).

## Searching
A detail already stated in a location's text needs no roll to find: opening, moving,
looking behind, under, or into something the entry already described is enough - a stated
detail investigated is a detail found. Searching costs time instead (see Time, below), one
action's worth per thing searched; that time cost is the actual price of thoroughness.
Searching for something the entry does not describe finds nothing - there is nothing
hidden that was not written down somewhere.

## Time
Time defaults by region rating, restated in each Region Overview's Layout field:
- **SAFE** - not time-bound. Threat here is social and situational, not a countdown, so
  actions take as long as the fiction needs.
- **WILD** - 4 hours per action. Traveling a leg, searching a Landmark, resting, or
  investigating a Hidden detail each burn one 4-hour block; the region die (below) is
  rolled once per block moved through open ground.
- **DANGEROUS** - runs on the Danger track's countdown instead of real time. Entering or
  meaningfully searching a location advances the track at the same odds that govern Region
  Dice below, until a full clear has burned exactly 3 of the track's 6 steps.

## Scaling
Three Action Dice scales exist. They are never compared across each other - a creature's
count is not a player's, and neither is a faction's:
- **Players** carry 1-6 dice of d4-d12, d6 average.
- **Creatures** carry d6 only, count 1-18, bonus -2 to +6 - reading roughly as classic Hit
  Dice. Pitched against **party altitude** (what the party can actually survive, per
  `GENRE.md`'s lethality framing) - never against a region's die.
- **Factions** carry d6 only and **no bonus**; their count means something only relative to
  the other factions in play, never as an absolute power level.

## Region Dice
A region's die (d4-d12, d8 baseline) is a **difficulty die**, not a power level: rolled
1 = failure, 2-3 = complication, 4+ = success - so a smaller die is harder, not easier. A
WILD region holds about as many locations as its die has faces, so a full traverse expects
exactly one encounter at every die size (about a 65% chance of at least one). A DANGEROUS
region built as a collection holds about 3x its die in locations, so a full clear burns
exactly 3 of the Danger track's 6 steps at every die size. Location count is the pressure
mechanism; die size sets texture - short and spiky at d4, long and smooth at d12 - not
overall difficulty.

## Currency
Standard coin is the crown penny (cn), 100 to a standard pound of silver. An ordinary
hand's pay at Fellward, when it's actually paid, runs about 8 cn a week - the scale every
find on `setting/Treasure1.md` through `Treasure5.md` is measured against. Old-tongue coin
still turns up; it is real silver, but no merchant this side of the border takes it at face
value.
