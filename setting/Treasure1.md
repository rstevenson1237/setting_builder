Treasure Table I - Scavenged Loot

Values and weights throughout are in silver. The coins of the keep and the realm are
copper, silver and gold: ten coppers to a silver, ten silvers to a gold. A hundred coins
of any kind weigh 1 wt. A labourer's week at the keep earns 20 silver; a soldier's week,
30. Tables II to V state their values in silver and point back here for the scale.
