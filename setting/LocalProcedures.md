Local Procedures of The Isle of the Serpent Crown

### Passing the Great Gate
Kind: variant
Extends: Search
Found at: A.1
Roll: Difficulty - the warden's search of what a party carries through, on Puluni's d6
Stakes: Pay 50 cn to the chiefs for the word at the Chiefs' House, then a party's goods are searched at the gate; contraband such as the egg-stone is taken and the party shut outside for the night, where Kunotewa comes.
Results:
- FAILURE: the search finds what a party hid, or the warden turns the party back; it stays outside until dawn.
- COMPLICATION: the gate opens, but the warden keeps one item as a gift for the visitor's bowls until the party returns.
- SUCCESS: the gate opens for the party, its goods unsearched beyond a glance.
Tell: The warden stands at the gate with a lamp and a list of every party that went out; a bowl of fish sits at the tower's foot.

### Hiring a Guide and Canoes
Kind: contest
Extends: Recruitment
Found at: A.7
Roll: Skill - the party's, to bargain with the guides' hall master over fee and route
Stakes: A guide costs 30 cn a day and a canoe 20 cn a day, paid before leaving; a guide will not cross the serpent posts, and a party that haggles hard may lose its guide's goodwill.
Results:
- FAILURE: no guide is free for the route asked, or the fee doubles; try again at the next dawn.
- COMPLICATION: a guide is hired, but wants half the fee in advance and turns back at the first dinosaur sign.
- SUCCESS: a guide who knows the route asked is hired at the listed fee, with a canoe at the yard on the same terms.
Tell: Carved trail-staves hang on the walls of the guides' hall, one for each trail a guide has walked, and the hall master takes the fee in coin or iron.

### Diving the Wrecks and the Sea Caves
Kind: table
Extends: Search
Found at: C.5
Roll: d6 - one dive of a watch, the party's diver against the reef
Stakes: A diver risks drowning and the mer people's anger, and can win a wreck's loot; any take from a wreck unasked is answered by the mer people's song.
Results:
- 1: the hulk shifts; a Test of Constitution or a Wound of Suffocation.
- 2: nothing but weed and sodden boards; the breath is spent.
- 3: a mer sentry's song carries; the passages close behind the party's boat.
- 4: a scrap of loot; roll once on Treasure Table I.
- 5: a sea-chest of old coin, 100 to 300 cn.
- 6: a sealed cask in the hold; roll once on Treasure Table II.
Tell: Green weed streams from the wrecks' ribs, and a high song carries over the water whenever a swimmer goes down.
