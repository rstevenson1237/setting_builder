Local Procedures of Roadsend

### Knucklebones at the Rest
Kind: contest
Extends: Opposed roll
Found at: A.2 The Drover's Rest
Roll: The player and Edda Marsh each roll a d6 at once, an Opposed roll; the higher roll wins the hand.
Stakes: A hand is 1 to 10 cn a side, and Edda matches any stake up to 10 cn.
Results:
- SUCCESS (the player rolls higher): the player takes both stakes.
- COMPLICATION (a tie): both stakes stay on the table for the next hand.
- FAILURE (the player rolls lower): Edda takes both stakes.
Tell: Edda keeps a leather cup and six worn knucklebones behind the bar and rattles them when the taproom is quiet.

### Stream-Bank Forage
Kind: table
Extends: Forage or Hunt
Found at: B.1 Stream Path
Roll: The Difficulty die for Forage or Hunt; on a SUCCESS or a COMPLICATION the party rolls a d6 on the table below.
Stakes: A watch spent along the stream. A failed roll brings the region's Encounter.
Results:
- 1. Watercress and sorrel from the fords: a day's greens for the party, worth nothing sold.
- 2. Brook trout from the pools: a day's rations for the party.
- 3. Bilberries on the sunny bank: a day's rations for the party.
- 4. A roe deer at the water: meat for the party for two days, and a hide worth 20 cn at A.3.
- 5. Goldseal root among the fern: a healer's herb worth 40 cn at A.3.
- 6. Honeycomb in a split oak: a day's rations for the party, and wax worth 30 cn at A.3.
Tell: Deer slots in the mud at the fords, and trout holding in the pools.
