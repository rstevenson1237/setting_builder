Local Procedures of The Buried City

### Foraging for food and water among the ruins
Kind: table
Extends: Forage or Hunt
Found at: A.3
Roll: Difficulty - the party's, once a watch spent searching
Stakes: A watch of the party's time, and a Test of Constitution against thirst if it comes up empty. A find feeds or waters one character for a day.
Results:
- 1 - Nothing but sand and a scorpion's burrow; the watch is lost.
- 2 - A few dry dates in a jar: a day's rations for one.
- 3 - Brackish water seeping into a sump: a day's water for one, Poisoned unless boiled.
- 4 - A sealed jar of oil and dried figs: a day's rations for three.
- 5 - Clean water beading on the cistern's lowest course: a day's water for two.
- 6 - A buried larder of dates and a sealed water jar: a day of both for four, and the tomb robbers will want to know.
Tell: Damp stains and green streaks mark the walls where water still gathers.

### Making shelter and a place of respite
Kind: variant
Extends: Make Camp
Found at: A.2
Roll: Skill - the party's, once to shut the room and once to hold it
Stakes: A watch of work and what the party spends on it: spikes, rope, oil or a lamp. A made camp lets the party Rest out of the storm and keeps its stores through the night.
Results:
- Failure - The door will not hold; the night's Rest is lost to wind and sand.
- Complication - The room is made safe, but the work is seen from the tip, and the tomb robbers know where the party sleeps.
- Success - The room is shut, dry and shaded; the party Rests, and the camp holds its stores until it is left.
Tell: A dry roofed room with a hearth, a door that can be shut and a smoke hole to the sky.
