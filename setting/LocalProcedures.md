Local Procedures of The Buried City

### Foraging for food and water among the ruins
Kind: table
Extends: Forage or Hunt
Found at: A.3
Roll: Difficulty - the party's, once a watch spent searching
Stakes: A watch of the party's time, and a Test of Constitution against thirst if it comes up empty. A find feeds or waters one character for a day.
Results:
- 1 - Nothing but sand and a scorpion's burrow; the watch is lost.
- 2 - A few dry dates in a jar: a day's rations for one.
- 3 - Brackish water seeping into a sump: a day's water for one, Poisoned unless boiled.
- 4 - A sealed jar of oil and dried figs: a day's rations for three.
- 5 - Clean water beading on the cistern's lowest course: a day's water for two.
- 6 - A buried larder of dates and a sealed water jar: a day of both for four, and the tomb robbers will want to know.
Tell: Damp stains and green streaks mark the walls where water still gathers.

### Making shelter and a place of respite
Kind: variant
Extends: Make Camp
Found at: A.2
Roll: Skill - the party's, once to shut the room and once to hold it
Stakes: A watch of work and what the party spends on it: spikes, rope, oil or a lamp. A made camp lets the party Rest out of the storm and keeps its stores through the night.
Results:
- Failure - The door will not hold; the night's Rest is lost to wind and sand.
- Complication - The room is made safe, but the work is seen from the tip, and the tomb robbers know where the party sleeps.
- Success - The room is shut, dry and shaded; the party Rests, and the camp holds its stores until it is left.
Tell: A dry roofed room with a hearth, a door that can be shut and a smoke hole to the sky.

### Trading for the fungus and its drugs
Kind: variant
Extends: Commerce
Found at: B.3
Roll: Skill - the party's, once a visit
Stakes: Coin in cn paid for spore jars, 10 cn a dose, and the risk of what they do. A jar sells outside the city for nothing; the market sells only to the masked or to those who show a mask.
Results:
- Failure - The stall sells a cheap spore; a Test of Sanity at once, and no discount.
- Complication - The dose is sold true, but a Scholar at the next stall marks the buyer.
- Success - A good dose at its price, and the stallholder names what it does.
Tell: Jars of spore and carved masks on every stall, each priced on a slate.

### Joining the revels
Kind: table
Extends: Carousing
Found at: B.4
Roll: d6
Stakes: A night's time and 10 cn for the food and drink; a Test of Sanity for every night at the feast.
Results:
- 1 - Wakes in a mask not their own, and cannot remember taking it off.
- 2 - A dreamer shares a song that names a place under the city.
- 3 - Nothing but a full belly and a headache.
- 4 - Hears a rumour from a masked neighbour, true or false.
- 5 - A dreamer leads the party to a quiet landing on the quay.
- 6 - A masked stranger offers a warning about the stair below.
Tell: Drums and singing across the lake, and lamps burning all night.

### Joining a faction
Kind: contest
Extends: Recruitment
Found at: E.2
Roll: Skill - the party's, once to a branch
Stakes: A service done for the branch, and the enmity of the other two. A member is given the branch's guide, its stores and its hall to rest in.
Results:
- Failure - The branch refuses the party and asks it to leave the level.
- Complication - The branch accepts, but sets a service first: a message, a rescue, a sealed door.
- Success - The branch gives the party its word, its sign and one guide, and the other two send watchers within a day.
Tell: Three halls open off a turning room, each hung with its own colours and its own bell.
