Treasure Table V - Treasure Cache

*Average about 5000 cn - four hundred weeks of a pan-hand's wage, which is to say more than
a pan-hand earns in a working life. This is the only table where one result should change
what the party attempts next. Odarath coin is a find and a problem at once: it is good
metal, it is four hundred years out of any currency, and spending it in Torsgaard is a
public statement about where you have been.*
