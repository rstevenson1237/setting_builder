Treasure Table V - Treasure Cache

*Values against the wage anchor at the head of Treasure Table I. A result here is usually
more than a party carries out in one trip, and the wt column says so plainly: 1 wt is one
hand or about 100 coins, and a hoard is heavy because it is worth something. What to take
first, what to cache, what to come back for, and who is still there when they do, is the
whole of what this table hands the referee.*
