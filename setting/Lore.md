Lore of The Isle of the Serpent Crown

### The Shrine Frieze
Form: cut
Found at: HA.8
Voice: The snake emperors' carvers, proud and a thousand years old, who name the Sleeper's feeding and say nothing of how it was bound.
Does: purpose, hook
Text: Rows of carved figures lead bound captives up a stair to a coiled serpent, and the glyphs beneath read, in the serpent speech, "Feed the Sleeper and the Sleeper keeps the island; let it wake and the island is the Sleeper's." A later hand has scratched a tally of captives beside the last row, forty strokes, the last fresh, and an arrow pointing down.

### The Priests' Codex
Form: written
Found at: HB.11
Voice: The cult's tally-priest, exact about the feeding and silent on why, who counts the captives by their sellers.
Does: dating, contradiction
Text: A bark ledger in the serpent speech, in a neat hand: "Forty-two captives since the last rains; nineteen from the swamp-men, twenty-three from the red-sashed sea-men. The Sleeper turns twice in a night now, and at forty more it will lift its head." A margin note says the sea-men ask a higher price each time.

### The Captain's Log
Form: written
Found at: I.2
Voice: The galleon's captain, who blames the reef and the lights ashore for the wreck and says nothing of whose silver the ship carried.
Does: hook, dating
Text: A water-stained log in the common tongue: "Struck on the reef in the dark, a light ashore showing where no light should be. Lost twelve men to the sea-folk's nets by dawn. The captain's chest holds the silver we were to deliver, and it will not be given up." A later entry in another hand breaks off mid-line.
