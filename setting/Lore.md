Lore of Roadsend

### The Hall Tally
Form: cut
Found at: C.2 The Carved Hall
Voice: The makers' crew foreman, boasting of the work and counting what he was owed.
Does: purpose, hook
Text: Thirty hands cut this hall in three winters for the lord below. Nine died of damp, and their marks are cut at the foot of the pillars. The lord paid in full and sealed the pay in the strongroom east of this hall, which opens from the carving at its middle. He never came to see the work.
