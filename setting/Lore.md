Lore of Thornmarch

### The Carters' Waybill
Form: written
Found at: G.6 The Boss's Cave
Voice: A carter's clerk copying a manifest for his own guild, who never saw who would read it.
Does: hook, naming
Text: A waybill for the fortnightly wagon in a clerk's cramped hand: the day, the road, and the cargo, which is salt, grain, bolts of cloth, and a trader's strongbox sealed with a square knot. Beside the date, in a second hand, neater, are two initials and a word: "P.O. - sent." The ink is the same blue as the keep's gate ledger.

### The Buyer's Tally
Form: cut
Found at: J.4 The Bone Heap
Voice: A gnoll slaver counting his sales for himself, who marks the buyer and the price and never the captive.
Does: connection, hook
Text: A thighbone cut with a slaver's count: a notch for each captive sold, a stroke across it for the price in silver. The first thirty notches are bare. Every notch after carries an inverted triangle, cut deeper than the rest, and the stroke beside it is always a full purse. Whoever bought them paid on the day every time, and always the same buyer.

### The Tithe Sticks
Form: cut
Found at: K.4 The Back Stair
Voice: A bugbear tithe-keeper who cannot write, notching a stick for each payment so that no tribe can deny one.
Does: dating, connection
Text: Six bundles of sticks, each tied with a strip of one tribe's hide, a notch cut in them for every tithe carried up the stair. No notch is older than five winters. The Kurzun and Burgnaz sticks are cut deepest; the Gubak's have gaps, each followed by a stroke of soot. A seventh stick, bare, is tied with a blue thread and carries a single notch.

### The Keeper's Charge
Form: cut
Found at: L.5 The Minotaur's Lair
Voice: A makers' steward cutting a standing order for a keeper long dead, who expects to be read by the keeper's successor.
Does: purpose, naming
Text: A bronze plaque, the makers' script cut deep. "Keep the way to Thalosae, the place of light. Let none pass the white door unless the keeper has known them. The token goes with the keeper, and with the keeper alone, until the keeper is relieved." Below it, in a crude hand scratched much later, a bull's head and the words "who relieves?"

### The High Priest's Letter
Form: written
Found at: M.5 The High Priest's Chamber
Voice: Veck writing to his agents as to servants, unsigned, naming them only by their office.
Does: naming, connection
Text: A letter in black ink on fine paper, unsent, folded in a cabinet. "To the clerk of the gate: the wagon days you sent are received, and the next must come a day sooner. To the keeper of the sacristy keys: the bone is received and set where it is watched. Neither of you will be named to the keep until the tithe is whole." An inverted triangle is cut at the foot of the page.

### The Last Niche
Form: cut
Found at: M.8 The Makers' Niches
Voice: The last maker to die in the halls, cutting his own epitaph, who blames the flame and never says what it was.
Does: dating, contradiction
Text: Makers' script, cut round a niche made for one. "I am the last to lie in this hall. The sickness began when the flame in the sanctum was turned dark, and it did not end when we sealed the sanctum and the vault behind two seals. Let no hand hold both seals. Let the flame stay dark until one who knows the light lets it out."
