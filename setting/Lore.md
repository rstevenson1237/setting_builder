Lore of Telar

The Western Heir's Letter (*letter*) - found at A.3 Garrison Barracks
A single letter in the western heir's own hand, sealed and, so far as anyone at Tolkar knows, unread. It orders Captain Dessek to account for a courier who never reached the western court three months back, and warns that the western kingdom suspects a leak somewhere on the road. The heir places the courier's disappearance on the western stretch, before the boundary cairns - wrong: the courier was last seen crossing the Sunk Bridge itself, three days before Brekar took it, and nobody has mentioned him since. Opening the letter answers nothing about the toll shortfall Dessek actually needs solved; it hands him a second problem instead.

The Wagons' Own Ledger (*ledger*) - found at C.4 Nine Wagons Counting House
Bound in Wagons blue-grey, page after page tallying the detour markup against what the crown's own toll used to bring in before the ford closed - the company is earning, most months, close to double the old toll income. The factor's own dating is the interesting error: the entry recording the buyout of Velar's second-largest trading house is dated the day before the Sunk Bridge closed, not the week after, as the factor tells it aloud. Whether that is a clerical slip or something else, the ledger itself does not say.

The Old Seat Milestone (*milestone*) - found at B.6 Old Approach Road
A single squared stone at the road's one bend, cut in the old unified crown's own hand - the same hand that built the causeway itself - naming the distance remaining to the crown seat as a half day's easy travel. It was true once, when the road was kept and guarded; today the marsh has reclaimed enough of it that the same half day costs the better part of two, assuming nothing worse than mud slows the way. The milestone says nothing of the three armies now watching the seat from a distance, because none of them existed yet when it was cut.

The Last Tally (*scroll*) - found at D.12 Sealed Archive
A fragmentary Essath scroll, damaged but legible in patches - a running tally of offerings made at the coiled rite, counted and dated against the old calendar. The count is steady, unremarkable, for as far back as the surviving fragment goes - and it simply stops, mid-entry, with no sign the scribe who kept it expected anything to end. Whatever happened to the priest-kings within that single reign, this tally was being kept faithfully right up until it wasn't.

The Copied Seal (*inscription*) - found at D.17 Sealed Door
Scratched beneath the original binding-line in a cruder, later hand - somebody's attempt to copy the Essath seal-script word for word. The copy gets the shapes close enough to recognize but breaks the tongue's own rule that no word ends on an open vowel: whoever cut this did not actually speak Essath, only traced what they found, and traced it wrong. Somebody from outside the priest-kings' own tradition has stood at this exact door before, close enough to copy what they could not read - and left without whatever they came for.
