Lore of The Buried City

### The Story of the Panels
Form: cut
Found at: C.3
Voice: A cynidicean court scribe, writing for the living and leaving out the demon's name.
Does: purpose, hook
Text: Twelve panels, read right to left. The first shows the city raised; the second, a king at table with a veiled guest; the third, priests kneeling; the fourth, soldiers with masks. The fifth to the eleventh are too worn to read. The twelfth shows a stair going down, and a numeral beside each step.

### The Carver's Count
Form: written
Found at: D.5
Voice: A carver's apprentice, tallying faces cut for the royal hall and not the reason.
Does: dating, connection
Text: A tally on hide, in the cynidicean numerals: thirty-one kings cut in the Hall of Founders, then a gap, then a mark for each face scraped in the hall beyond. The last line says the guest was veiled on the king's order, and that the chisels stopped in the king's ninth year of the guest.

### The Gallery of the Fall
Form: drawn
Found at: D.6
Voice: A court painter, working from memory after the fall and softening what he saw.
Does: purpose, hook
Text: Nine paintings, west to east. A king at table with a veiled guest; the guest unveiled, its face blacked out; priests with candles; soldiers in masks; a queen leading children to a stair; a king alone with a bronze disc. The last panel shows a stair going down and an arrow pointing at the floor.

### The Three Claims
Form: written
Found at: E.5
Voice: A Scholar, writing for Sihuahet and sneering at the other two branches.
Does: connection, contradiction
Text: Each branch holds that it alone will free the people. The soldiers say strength, the priests say faith, we say knowledge, and all three admit the king's order was the same: to find the demon's name before the stair. The Guard keep the east hall, the Priesthood the north, the Scholars the west. None will walk through another's door.

### The Noble's Will
Form: written
Found at: F.5
Voice: A noble of the old court, writing for an heir, wrong about who the heir would be.
Does: dating, naming
Text: To my son, the house, the gold under the dais of the family crypt, and the key to my cell if the soldiers come. Do not trust the veiled guest of the last king, nor the priests who bless him. When the stair is shut, the order is: guards, priests, scholars, and no one else.

### The Captain's Last Orders
Form: written
Found at: F.11
Voice: The Royal Guard's missing captain, hurried and scared, scrawled on hide.
Does: hook, connection
Text: The stair is shut behind us. I go east to the cell, where the wall is bad. If I do not come back, send sixes, not threes. Tell the Guard the thing at the foot of the stair is awake and has been eating our scouts. Do not let the Scholars read this.

### The Priestesses' Rite
Form: woven
Found at: G.3
Voice: A priestess, writing in the Ruhiahe tradition and leaving out what only the high priestess was told.
Does: purpose, translation
Text: A tapestry strip in red and white. The sun is held up by three hands and a bell is rung to close each door; the rite of sealing is three words, said in the speech of the carved walls, then the bell. The fourth hand, the one beneath, is left unwoven.

### The Queen's Last Rite
Form: cut
Found at: G.8
Voice: The queen's own scribe, writing for the dead and hiding the reason.
Does: connection, hook
Text: Cut into the bier: the king went down to the veiled guest of his own will, the queen with the children went another way. The false tomb is for the soldiers who follow; the true king lies where the seal is unbroken. A ring is left with the bones to open the stair.

### The Cult's Ledger
Form: written
Found at: H.11
Voice: A cult scribe, listing captives and keys in the black speech's shorthand and fearing the lieutenants.
Does: hook, naming
Text: A tally of captives taken from the lake city, by mask and kin; the last six lines are crossed out and marked fed. Two keys are listed as found and brought down, two as hidden by the king in tombs above. The cult counts the false tomb among the places already searched.

### The Making of the Chosen
Form: written
Found at: I.8
Voice: A cult priest, recording the rite for the next priest and sparing no detail but its cost.
Does: purpose, connection
Text: A chosen is made at the warm altar and unmade at the black one; the blood from the unmaking runs down the channel to the demon's hall. Three nights in the cell, three masks, and the name given up. Those who do not return from the cell are counted fed.
