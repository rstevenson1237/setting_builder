Lore of Khirengaard

The Second Ledger (*ledger*) - found at A.3 The Cutters' Hall
Kept in cipher only the guild's wardens can read, the Second Ledger records every crossing the boundary-cutters' guild has ever blessed and what became of the one who made it - a private accounting far more honest than the public ledger every visitor sees on the Blessing Table above. Cross-referenced against what the guild already half-admits to itself, it would name at least one currently-practicing cutter taking Concern coin for false blessings, and it records, in full, the crossing that killed Khirengaard's founding factor three years ago - an entry the public ledger renders in careful, deniable vagueness. Whoever reads it holds enough to break the guild's authority over its own membership, or enough to get themselves quietly disappeared for asking the wrong questions of the wrong cutter.

The Last Survey (*note*) - found at B.4 The Toppled Stone
A waterproofed set of survey notes in a working cutter's hand, dated some years back, recording measurements and markings along this exact stretch of the stone line - then breaking off mid-entry, the final lines noting the stone already toppled and three fresh gouges scored through its mark, with no explanation offered and no follow-up ever filed. The guild's official records show this stretch as simply unsurveyed since, with no note of why. The notes identify their author only by a guild mark, not a name, and that mark matches no cutter currently practicing - suggesting whoever found the toppled stone didn't just stop surveying it. They stopped entirely.

The Ash-Tally (*knotted bark-cord tally*) - found at B.10 The Charcoalers' Kiln-Ring
Read correctly - and only a Charcoaler can truly read it - the Ash-Tally records the fringe's slow retreat: a knot for each stretch of ground the wold has quietly reclaimed, a notch for whether whoever stood there when it happened walked away or didn't. Most of the pattern concerns ordinary losses - a trapper's blind, a sawpit, ground the loggers gave up without ever knowing why. But the oldest knots, worked in a harder, different cord than the rest, trace a stretch matching no settlement anyone in Khirengaard has ever mapped - except that its shape lines up, knot for knot, with where the Marrgath's own territory is remembered to have run before they vanished a century and a half ago. The Charcoalers won't say outright that the wold reclaimed the Marrgath the same way it reclaims a sawpit. They don't have to. The tally already says it, for anyone patient enough to be shown how to read it.

The Last Entry (*journal page*) - found at C.1 The Shaft-Head
A single torn page from a prospector's journal, water-stained but legible, listing all nine names of the crew that went down to breach the vein twenty-three years ago - the same nine remembered in Khirengaard's own accounts, though this page predates any official record of them. The entry describes reaching a warmer-than-expected seam and disagreeing over whether to press on, then ends abruptly mid-sentence: "Aldous says we've gone far enough for one day's work, but Kell won't hear it, and I can't say I blame him with what's under that rock, so we're -" It offers no more than that - not which two of the nine survived, nor what happened next - but it fixes the crew's mood in the hours before the disaster, and confirms they knew something was wrong before it was too late to stop.

The Six Names (*cloth fragment*) - found at C.12 The Wight's Hollow
A scrap of oiled cloth, torn from a pack or cloak, with six names scratched into it in an unsteady hand - almost certainly by Marek Coll, whose own name doesn't appear among them. Each name is crossed through save the last, which trails into a dark stain gone to rust decades ago. Set against the crew list on the Last Entry found up at the Shaft-Head, these six account for six of the seven who never came out of the original breach - crossed through, it seems, as Marek watched each die or vanish in turn, alone in the dark with no one coming to help. The seventh name, and Marek's own fate, appear nowhere on the cloth. Whatever finished the list didn't leave him time to write it.

The Sealed Route (*map*) - found at C.20 Sealed Niche of the Nine
A hand-drawn, water-damaged map of the mine's lower workings, marked with a cutter's shorthand for hazard rather than distance - a wavy line through the flooded gallery near the ore cart siding, a broken-timber symbol over the narrow adit prone to collapse, and a spiral warning mark over the chamber holding the dressed-stone guardian. Whoever drew it had clearly been through these galleries more than once and survived each hazard by knowing it was there beforehand - a caution the nine who breached the vein twenty-three years ago plainly didn't have. It reveals no passage that can't already be found by exploration, but it turns three of the mine's worst surprises into known, avoidable dangers for anyone who can read a cutter's hand.
