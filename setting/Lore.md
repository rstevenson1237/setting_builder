Lore of Thornmarch

### The Carters' Waybill
Form: written
Found at: G.6 The Boss's Cave
Voice: A carter's clerk copying a manifest for his own guild, who never saw who would read it.
Does: hook, naming
Text: A waybill for the fortnightly wagon in a clerk's cramped hand: the day, the road, and the cargo, which is salt, grain, bolts of cloth, and a trader's strongbox sealed with a square knot. Beside the date, in a second hand, neater, are two initials and a word: "P.O. - sent." The ink is the same blue as the keep's gate ledger.

### The Buyer's Tally
Form: cut
Found at: J.4 The Bone Heap
Voice: A gnoll slaver counting his sales for himself, who marks the buyer and the price and never the captive.
Does: connection, hook
Text: A thighbone cut with a slaver's count: a notch for each captive sold, a stroke across it for the price in silver. The first thirty notches are bare. Every notch after carries an inverted triangle, cut deeper than the rest, and the stroke beside it is always a full purse. Whoever bought them paid on the day every time, and always the same buyer.

### The Tithe Sticks
Form: cut
Found at: K.4 The Back Stair
Voice: A bugbear tithe-keeper who cannot write, notching a stick for each payment so that no tribe can deny one.
Does: dating, connection
Text: Six bundles of sticks, each tied with a strip of one tribe's hide, a notch cut in them for every tithe carried up the stair. No notch is older than five winters. The Kurzun and Burgnaz sticks are cut deepest; the Gubak's have gaps, each followed by a stroke of soot. A seventh stick, bare, is tied with a blue thread and carries a single notch.
