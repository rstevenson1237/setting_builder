Lore of Roadend

### The Foreman's Cut
Form: cut
Found at: C.6 Thono
Voice: the foreman of the gang that cut the cave, who wanted the work to look finished - interested
Does: purpose, hook
Text: A long panel of tiny figures in rows, all facing one round shaft cut at its end, and under it a single line in the dead tongue of the cave's makers: "The seam runs down. The upper cuts are emptied and the gang is paid; the lower ones I leave for the next gang, and the next." The line is a boast, and a lie by omission: the upper cuts are the cave the goblins live in, and the seam it follows goes down the shaft in the floor.
