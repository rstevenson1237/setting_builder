The sensory register of the setting as a whole. Referee-facing. A location
draws on this through a T table, and states its own senses in its own words.

| ID | Entry | Sense |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| S-AMB-01 | Peat smoke that runs flat along the reed tops and never lifts. | Smell |
| S-AMB-02 | Water that takes a sound and gives none of it back. | Hearing |
| S-AMB-03 | Cut stone that is cold on top and warm underneath. | Touch |
| S-AMB-04 | Light the colour of weak tea, holding an hour longer than it should. | Sight |
