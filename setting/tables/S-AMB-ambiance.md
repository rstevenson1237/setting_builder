The sensory register of the setting as a whole: what it is like to stand in the
Reach with nothing happening. Referee-facing. A location draws on this through a
T table and states its own senses in its own words. Every row is true everywhere,
and the rows that contradict what the eye reports are the ones worth reaching
for first.
