The deliberate in-fiction open threads: what this setting does not answer, on
purpose. A row here is a decision to leave something open, not a note that it is
undecided. Each one is anchored in something a party can stand in front of, and
the second column prices the answer or says plainly that nothing in the Reach
settles it.

| ID | Entry | What an answer would cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| T-UNQ-01 | Who ties fresh rope to bare bell frames, within the day, on every road in the Reach? | A watch spent lying up beside a frame, and the willingness to be found doing it. The Reach does not settle this one and the referee may. |
| T-UNQ-02 | What was the ninth bell paid for? | The Drowned Chapter knows and no longer speaks. Answering it means going under with them and coming back. |
| T-UNQ-03 | Where did two hundred and six people out of the third hold go, in order, across four years? | Every hold's tally board read against every other. The counts are all still hung on their doors. Nobody has ever added them up. |
| T-UNQ-04 | What is under the sill, and was it ever anything other than what it is? | Going down. What Holds the Sill allows it once, to anyone who comes carrying nothing, and the allowance is not repeated. |
| T-UNQ-05 | Why is the cut stone warm? | S-TRU-07 answers it on the referee's page. The world does not, and the people who know lie down on it rather than say so. |
| T-UNQ-06 | Does the tenth bell still hang, and would ringing it finish the debt or reckon it? | The Covenant Remnant is certain of the answer. Certainty is not an answer, and the Reach does not settle this one either. |
| T-UNQ-07 | What is the Tallow House's list for? | Nothing yet. That is the honest answer and it should stay the answer until a party gives them a reason to decide. |
| T-UNQ-08 | If the cutting stopped, what would be reckoned differently? | Nothing in the Reach settles this. It is the one question a referee should feel free to answer themselves, and the answer will be the campaign. |
