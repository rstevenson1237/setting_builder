The root registry. Every proper name in the setting decomposes into roots
recorded below, and `validate.py` checks that it does (M18). A new root is
proposed in a step's opening question batch and written here before it is used.
It is never coined in passing.

**Phoneme set.** Flat vowels only: a, e, i, o, u, with no diphthong carried
across a root boundary. Consonant clusters are permitted at the head of a root
and not at its tail.

**Compounding.** Two roots join directly, with no linking vowel: `cause` plus
`way` gives `causeway`. A compound never runs to three roots. Plurals take a
trailing `s` and are not recorded separately. A participle that names a state is
recorded as its own root rather than derived, because the state is the meaning.

**Register.** Roots naming water are older than roots naming stone, and a name
that puts stone before water is a Covenant name. The living layer builds plain
compounds of ordinary words and decomposes on sight; the Covenant layer is often
wrong about what the place is now, and where a place carries a name in both
layers the two rarely agree.
