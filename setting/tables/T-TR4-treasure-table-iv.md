Working devices. Magic in the Reach is a found technology nobody now
understands, and this is the band it is found in. Every device half-works, works
differently than it is recorded to work, or works on the wrong thing, and every
one of them carries instructions that are wrong in exactly one particular. Every
device has a running cost. Nothing here is repairable.
