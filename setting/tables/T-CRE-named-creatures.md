The individuals a party can meet, recognise, and meet again. One of each. Every
entry states what it wants, what it will give, and what it does in the first ten
seconds, because that last is what a referee needs most and is most often
missing. Most of these are avoidable and several are negotiable.

| ID | Entry | Mechanics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| T-CRE-01 | Reedcount. The oldest cutter on the seaward stretch, and the only one who holds the whole count from the sea to the Long Cut. She opens with *What watch,* and then waits. She will wait out a whole conversation for the answer and will not say anything else until she has it. She wants the count kept accurate after she stops keeping it, and she has nobody to hand it to. She trades watch counts, where the peat will hold, and the name of every frame between here and the water. | {AD: 1, +0} {TYPE: Men} |
| T-CRE-02 | The Warden of the Third Door. Closed Order, at Stillhold, and she states the count of what is behind her door at a party before they have asked her anything at all: two hundred and six out, the fixed things left, the door pegged from without. She wants the door kept shut. She will trade the count item by item, and she will trade nothing else, and she is armed. | {AD: 3, +2} {TYPE: Men} {WOUND: Crushing} |
| T-CRE-03 | The carrier on the Long Cut. A bell-carrier that stops when it is watched, and then goes on stopping. It sets the bell down, straightens, and asks: *Which road is this one.* It is not a question it expects an answer to any more, and it has been asking it a long time. Answer it correctly and it will carry anything a party gives it as far as the next frame, and set it down there, and wait. Answer it wrongly and it picks the bell up. | {AD: 3, +2} {TYPE: Undead} {WOUND: Crushing} |
| T-CRE-04 | Tallowcount. The factor who prices Covenant work at the Tallow House, by the pound, without asking. He opens by weighing whatever is in a party's hands before anyone has offered it. He wants the list complete. He trades coin, light, and passage off the Reach, and he pays well, and every transaction goes into the list under a name. | {AD: 2, +1} {TYPE: Men} |
| T-CRE-05 | Sillwalker. The only person who has gone through the sill and come back up, living in Reedhold and selling none of what he brought with him. He opens by leaving the room. He wants to be left alone and he wants somebody to ask him the right question, and he cannot have both. He trades the route down, once, for a reason he judges good enough. | {AD: 2, +1} {TYPE: Men} {TEST: Sanity} |
| T-CRE-06 | Ashenpin. What is left of the officer who took the last chapter under, standing in the deepest flooded cell of Bellgate in the order it was standing in when the water closed. It opens by naming everyone present, correctly, in the older layer. It wants to be told the ringing is finished by somebody with the standing to say so. It will trade the whole ringing order, spoken, which nobody living holds. | {AD: 5, +4} {TYPE: Undead} {TEST: Sanity} |
| T-CRE-07 | What Holds the Sill, met. It has no name and does not take one. It arrives as a change in the weight of the air and a voice using the party's own count, one number behind. It wants the digging not resumed. It will trade passage down, once, to anyone who comes carrying nothing, and it does not repeat the offer or explain the terms. | {AD: 6, +6} {TYPE: Horror} {TEST: Sanity} |
| T-CRE-08 | The Warden at the Deep Shaft. A shaft-warden of the same make as any other, except that this one has begun saying the stop-digging order aloud instead of giving the warning it was set to give. It opens by reciting it. It wants nothing, which is what makes it different from everything else in this table, and it cannot be traded with, only satisfied: it lets anybody past who is going up. | {AD: 4, +3} {TYPE: Construct} {WOUND: Crushing} |
