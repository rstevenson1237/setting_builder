Rumours as spoken, in the words a player would hear. Each is in somebody's
mouth, and the `Source faction` column decides how the sentence is built: a
cutter counts, a remnant recites, a factor prices. The `True` column takes
`yes`, `partly` or `no`, and `partly` is where the work is.
