Who wants what, and what they will trade for it. Referee-facing. Nothing here
rules anything: a faction reaches as far as it can walk and no further, and the
`Trades` column is what a party can actually get out of it, which is usually not
coin. Six of them, because a faction is a standing pressure a referee holds in
their head across a campaign.

| ID | Faction | Wants | Trades |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| S-FAC-01 | The Covenant Remnant | Every bell recovered and hung again in the old order, and the tenth rung. They are certain that finishes it. | Safe conduct across the Covenant Ground, and the roots of names nobody else records. Seen first as fresh rope on a bare frame, tied within the day. |
| S-FAC-02 | The Reedcutters | The fen left exactly as it is, the lanes cut on schedule, and the roads walked at the stated rate. | Crossings, watch counts, where the peat will hold, and a bed inside the light. Seen first as a chain of cleared ground where reed has been taken to the root. |
| S-FAC-03 | The Drowned Chapter | To be rung under a second time, properly, and told the ringing is finished. | Nothing. It only asks, and it asks of whoever is nearest. Seen first as a bell-carrier walking a road inward at a steady pace. |
| S-FAC-04 | What Holds the Sill | The digging not resumed, and the shafts left pegged as the Covenant pegged them. | Passage down, once, to anyone who comes carrying nothing and takes nothing. Seen first as warmth in slab that has no business being warm. |
| S-FAC-05 | The Tallow House | Anything out of the Covenant's work, priced by what it weighs rather than by what it is, and no questions about which shaft it came out of. | Coin, light, and passage off the Reach. They are keeping a list of what came up and who brought it, and they have not yet decided what the list is for. |
| S-FAC-06 | The Closed Order | Every hold kept shut and every tally kept accurate, against a reckoning they expect and cannot date. | The count itself: what was in a hold before it was closed, item by item, in exchange for a promise the door stays shut. Seen first as a door pegged from the outside with the board still hung on it. |
