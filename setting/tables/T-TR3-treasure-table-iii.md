Fine work and instruments. The band where a find stops being worth its material
and starts being worth the hands that made it. Half of it measures something,
because the Covenant measured everything, and a party who works out what an
instrument reads has learned something about the world. An instrument reports
and does not act: anything that acts is the band above.
