Goods. Things made to be used by somebody, kept because they were worth keeping,
and left where their owner left them. This is the band where a party starts
finding possessions rather than material, and where a find can be used on the
way out. One or two rows are ordinary Covenant work that still functions, which
is the first hint of what the Reach is built on top of.
