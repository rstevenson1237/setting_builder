Environmental dangers a location places by reference: the ground, the water, the
air and the structure, where they can hurt somebody who has done nothing wrong.
Every row names what shows it before it is suffered. A parameter table: its rows
are data a referee reads, and they stop at Builder.

| ID | Entry | Mechanics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| T-HAZ-01 | Peat gives under the outermost stones and takes a leg to the knee, then holds it. The tell is colour: the give is a shade darker and always along the road's edge. | {TEST: Constitution} |
| T-HAZ-02 | Water at a depth the light does not reach, cold enough to close a chest on the first breath. The tell is that the reeds stop short of it. | {TEST: Constitution} {WOUND: Frost} |
| T-HAZ-03 | Slabs polished slick under shin-deep water, cambered a finger toward the open fen. | {TEST: Constitution} {CONDITION: Footsore / halves the stated rate / until a full rest} |
| T-HAZ-04 | Peat smoke lying at knee height in a still lane, thick enough that the road has to be read by foot. The tell is a mile of warning: it is visible long before it is stood in. | {CONDITION: Smoke-blind / the party moves at half the stated rate and reads no cue further off than arm's length / until wind or open ground} |
| T-HAZ-05 | A drowned tier's doorway, whole and square, with the lintel a hand under the surface and the floor beyond it out of sight. The tell is the square corner in water that has none anywhere else. | {TEST: Constitution} {WOUND: Crushing} |
| T-HAZ-06 | Warm slab that draws a body down onto it and holds sleep well past the watch it was meant to last. The tell is that everybody wants to sit down, and says so. | {TEST: Fate} {CONDITION: Slab-slept / the party wakes a watch late and a hand lower than they lay down / until they are back on standing ground} |
| T-HAZ-07 | A shaft rim eaten out from underneath, sound to look at and one course thick. The tell is the note it gives when it is struck, which is the note of a thing with nothing behind it. | {TEST: Fate} {WOUND: Crushing} |
| T-HAZ-08 | Standing water skinned over with a tallow-coloured film that takes fire from a lamp and runs with the current. The tell is the smell, a full chain upwind. | {TEST: Constitution} {WOUND: Fire} |
| T-HAZ-09 | Cold coming up through the soles on dry stone, out of the level below rather than out of the air. The tell is that it is worse where the road runs deepest. | {TEST: Constitution} {WOUND: Frost} {CONDITION: Numb-footed / footing cues are not felt until they are stood on / until a fire} |
| T-HAZ-10 | Reed cuts, fine and slow to close, taken through cloth on a bad crossing. The tell is that the cutters wear oiled reed-cloth and a party usually does not. | {WOUND: Piercing} {CONDITION: Fen-fever / one watch in six is lost to shaking / until a full rest inside the light} |
| T-HAZ-11 | A sunk span with its slabs lifted and reset by the water, one of them out of true and under a hand of silt. The tell is the seam, which runs crooked for a pace and then straightens. | {TEST: Constitution} {WOUND: Crushing} |
| T-HAZ-12 | Sound that will not carry: a shout dropped flat at a chain, so that a party strung out along a road cannot call each other back. The tell is that the water gives nothing back from the first step onto it. | {TEST: Sanity} |
