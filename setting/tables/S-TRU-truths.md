The facts of the world that no in-world source states plainly. Referee-facing.
A player reaches these by inference, never by being told.

| ID | Entry | Who could state it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| S-TRU-01 | The bells were never a warning. They were a payment, and the fen accepted them. | Nobody living. |
| S-TRU-02 | The water is not rising. The land is going down, a little each year, and always at night. | The reedcutters, who measure it and call it something else. |
| S-TRU-03 | What the fen takes, it keeps whole. Nothing in it rots. | Anyone who has pulled something out, which is nobody twice. |
| S-TRU-04 | The Covenant knew all three and wrote none of them down, because writing a debt down is a way of agreeing to it. | The Drowned Chapter, which no longer speaks. |
