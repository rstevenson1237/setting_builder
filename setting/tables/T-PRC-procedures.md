The one place in the corpus where a die roll may gate a result. Every entry
states all three outcomes, always in the same order, and the complication is
never failure with a smaller number: the thing is done and it costs something
the party will feel later. Nothing else in the setting writes a discovery as
roll-gated.

| ID | Procedure | Roll | Outcomes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| T-PRC-01 | Wading a sunk span with the water at shin height, heaviest load first. | One test per crosser. | {OUTCOME: Success} The party is across inside one watch and dry above the knee.<br>{OUTCOME: Complication} The crossing eats a second watch, and everything below the pack line is soaked through.<br>{OUTCOME: Failure} A pack goes under, and the fen closes over it before anyone can reach down. |
| T-PRC-02 | Sounding a bare bell frame with an improvised striker, to see what answers. | One test for the party. | {OUTCOME: Success} The note carries seaward, and for one watch nothing in the shallows will come within a chain.<br>{OUTCOME: Complication} The note carries inward instead, and something on the road ahead stops walking.<br>{OUTCOME: Failure} The frame answers in a voice that is not metal, and every crosser present takes a Test of Fate. |
| T-PRC-03 | Reading a lane's tally posts back to find where a count broke. | One test for the party, and one watch spent whatever the result. | {OUTCOME: Success} The gap is found, and its length gives the watch it opened and the watch it closed.<br>{OUTCOME: Complication} The gap is found and the party has read the posts from the road, standing still, for a watch, in the open.<br>{OUTCOME: Failure} Two posts disagree. Which of them is wrong is not recoverable, and the cutters will not be asked about it. |
| T-PRC-04 | Lying up beside a bell frame through a full watch to see who ties the rope. | One test for the party, per watch held. | {OUTCOME: Success} The rope is tied while they watch, and they see who does it and which way they go afterward.<br>{OUTCOME: Complication} The rope is tied and they do not see it happen, and the knot is one of theirs.<br>{OUTCOME: Failure} Nothing comes to the frame. Something comes to them, and it knows they have been lying still. |
| T-PRC-05 | Prising a pegged door without drawing the pegs. | One test per attempt, and each attempt is a turn. | {OUTCOME: Success} The door comes open, the pegs are intact, and it can be closed again exactly as it was.<br>{OUTCOME: Complication} It opens, and it will not close, and the tally board comes off its hook and cannot be rehung.<br>{OUTCOME: Failure} The frame splits, the noise carries, and what was inside is now inside an open doorway. |
| T-PRC-06 | Carrying an open light through a lane with the smoke lying at knee height. | One test per crosser, per lane. | {OUTCOME: Success} Across at the stated rate, with the light still burning at the far end.<br>{OUTCOME: Complication} Across, and the light is out, and it cannot be relit until the party is on open ground.<br>{OUTCOME: Failure} The smoke takes the light and gives it back along the surface of the water, and everything in the lane now knows exactly where they are. |
| T-PRC-07 | Going down a Covenant shaft on rope, heaviest last. | One test per descender. | {OUTCOME: Success} Everyone down, the rope sound, and the light still lit at the bottom.<br>{OUTCOME: Complication} Everyone down, and the rope is now short of the top by a body's length, which is a problem on the way back and not now.<br>{OUTCOME: Failure} The rim goes. What is at the bottom is reached faster than intended, and the rope goes with it. |
| T-PRC-08 | Selling Covenant work at the Tallow House without saying where it came from. | One test for the party, once per visit. | {OUTCOME: Success} Paid by weight, at a fair price, and nobody asks anything.<br>{OUTCOME: Complication} Paid well, and the piece is weighed twice, and the second weight is written down beside a name.<br>{OUTCOME: Failure} Paid, and the House knows exactly which shaft it came out of, and says so pleasantly, in front of whoever else is in the room. |
