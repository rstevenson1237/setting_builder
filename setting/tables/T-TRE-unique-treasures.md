The named singletons. One of each, never restocked, and every one of them wanted
back by somebody who is still here. Each carries a cost rather than a drawback:
it weighs something, it is recognised, it is owed, or it does not work the way
its own records say. Each is mentioned somewhere else in the corpus, so that it
can be sought rather than only stumbled on.

| T-TRE-01 | The Tenth. The bell that was hung and never rung, still on its frame, the pin seated and the clapper cord uncut. It is the only clean bronze in the Reach: nothing has grown on it, above the waterline or below. The Covenant Remnant wants it rung and the fen is understood to be waiting on it, and those two facts are not the same fact. | {VALUE: 20000 cn} {WT: 30} {QUALITY: Artifact} |
| T-TRE-02 | Ashenpin's clapper. Longer than a forearm, cut along its length with a road name that is not on any surviving list, and warm to the hand at any depth. Taking it is not theft from the dead; it is theft from somebody who is waiting. | {VALUE: 9000 cn} {WT: 4} {QUALITY: Artifact} |
| T-TRE-03 | The Long Count. A cutter's counting cord in three plies, knotted without a break from the sea to the inward end, and the only unbroken count in the Reach. | T-TRE-05 | Sillwalker's lamp. A pin-lamp that came back up. It is the only one anybody has seen that brightens with depth instead of dimming, which is what its plate always said it would do, and it has been unlit since it was carried out. The man who carried it out will not say why he stopped lighting it, and the one time he was pressed he said only that it had been bright enough. | {VALUE: 14000 cn} {WT: 1} {QUALITY: Artifact} |

| T-TRE-07 | The Face. The face cut carefully off the waist-deep relief, kept, and cut on its reverse to seat in a socket. It fits nowhere anybody has found.
