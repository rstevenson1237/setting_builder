Recurring construction and ground. Not split by region type: the value of this
table is that the same stonework appears in a safe region and a dangerous one,
because recognition is cheaper than explanation. The `Types` column marks which
region types an entry suits, and an entry may suit all three. A parameter table,
and the most cited in the corpus.
