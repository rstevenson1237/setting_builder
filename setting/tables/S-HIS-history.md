What happened, in order, and what it left behind. Referee-facing. No location
cites this table; its content reaches a player through a T table. The rows run
earliest first and stop short of the present, because what a party walks into is
the standing situation and the setting Overview carries that.

| ID | Entry | Left behind |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| S-HIS-01 | The Covenant raised its roads across standing water on courses of fitted slab, and sank shafts wherever the slab would not hold. | Raised road in every region of the Reach, and shaft mouths squared to the compass. |
| S-HIS-02 | Below the deepest shaft the diggers came out onto a level nobody had cut. It was already open, and it was already occupied. | The Deep Sill, and the order to stop digging, cut in haste into the shaft rim. |
| S-HIS-03 | The Covenant hung bells along the raised roads to time the tides, and rang them in a fixed order, seaward end inward. | A bell frame on every causeway, and a written ringing order nobody now holds the whole of. |
| S-HIS-04 | Reeds were cut back a chain either side of every road by standing order, and the cutting was let out to the people who lived in them. | The cut lanes, still cut, and the Reedcutters, who have kept the order longer than the Covenant lasted. |
| S-HIS-05 | The water rose in one season and did not go back down. | Drowned tiers under open water, still square, still doored, and nothing in them rotted. |
| S-HIS-06 | The Covenant read the rising as an unpaid debt and sent its last chapter out to ring the shrines under. | No bodies. No bells. One frame left bare on every road that chapter reached. |
| S-HIS-07 | The holds above the waterline were emptied by their own people, in order, across four years, and the tally was agreed at each door. | Doors closed and pegged from the outside, tally boards still hung on them, and nothing broken anywhere. |
| S-HIS-08 | Nothing came after. The roads were not taken, the holds were not occupied, and the reeds were cut on schedule through all of it. | A country of usable Covenant work with nobody in it, and one standing order still being kept. |
