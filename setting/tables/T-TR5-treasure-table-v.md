Regalia. What the Covenant made for its own offices, hung where everybody could
see it, or set up at a place that mattered. Everything in this band is
recognised on sight by anyone who knows the Reach, and most of it is a lift for
several people. A party who comes back with one of these has taken something
that was never meant to be portable, from somebody who may still be keeping it.
