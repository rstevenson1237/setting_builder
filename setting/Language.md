Tongues of Fellward

This setting's naming convention is sparse/utilitarian (`GENRE.md`): most people and place
names in Common are plain descriptive words, not coinages from the roots below. The Old
Tongue and the Wilds Tongue are the likelier sources of an actual coined name - a loan word
worn into a grave-marker, a creature-name a scout picked up from what it heard beyond the
wire. Roots exist below for all three regardless, for compounds, epithets, and the
coinages that do occur.

## Common (the Garrison Tongue)
Spoken by: everyone living at the keep and the borderland now - soldiers, conscripts, camp
followers, the last few settler families held over.
Consonants: p t k b d g m n s l r w h
Vowels: a e i o u
Syllable: (C)V(C) - open-leaning and easy to run together, plain rather than musical.
Affixes:
- **-ward** (suffix) - facing or toward; a direction of duty ("wall-ward")
- **-hold** (suffix) - a place kept or held; a holding, post, or station
- **-kin** (suffix) - a group bound by shared duty, not necessarily blood
- **un-** (prefix) - negation; undone, unmanned, unheld
- **-worn** (suffix) - used up, past its working life
- **-watch** (suffix) - an office of duty; a standing task owed by a person or place

Roots:
- dun - stone, wall
- mer - water, a running source
- fel - open ground, field
- kal - cold
- rud - rust, old blood-color
- han - hand
- mak - to make, to build
- brek - to break, to fail structurally
- tak - to take
- gev - to give, to issue (as supplies are issued)
- lir - light, a lit place
- dok - dark, unlit
- mor - dead, death
- pel - post, stake, marker
- sen - one who watches
- ren - road, a way traveled

Coined here:
- Fellward = fel + -ward - the keep and its holding; setting name (`setting/Setting.md`)
- Pelren = pel + ren - "the marked road"; the WILD region between Fellward and the treeline
  proper (`setting/region/Regions.md`)

## Old Tongue (for ruins and the dead)
Spoken by: whoever raised the older foundations under the keep, long gone; a handful of
trained clerks and grave-scribes are the only living readers left.
Consonants: p t k g d b v z m n r
Vowels: a i u
Syllable: CVC or CCVC - strict and closed, no open endings; clipped and heavy against
Common's open flow.
Affixes:
- **-ur** (suffix) - former; no longer what it names, a title stripped of function
- **gra-** (prefix) - beneath, buried, under
- **-ith** (suffix) - belonging to, of - possession or origin
- **-az** (suffix) - a place of the dead; a resting or burial marker
- **vel-** (prefix) - first, original, before

Roots:
- karth - stone, specifically a foundation-stone
- ustrum - water, still or standing - a cistern, not a running source
- brakt - to build, in the old permanent sense
- drukk - to fall, to collapse
- zulm - name, a true or recorded name
- ingar - grave, resting place
- vithan - watch, in the sense of guard-duty owed to the dead
- kathil - wall, specifically defensive
- ormuk - fire, kept or sacred
- nazir - king, or one who once held command
- tirak - road, in the old sense of a paved way
- ulvash - bone
- ghrast - iron, worked metal
- pallun - number, a counted or tallied thing
- essir - stone-marker, boundary
- vokrum - silence

Coined here:
- Ingaraz = ingar + -az - "grave-ground"; the DANGEROUS region of old foundations and
  burial mounds beneath Pelren (`setting/region/Regions.md`)
- Nazirur = nazir + -ur - "former king" / "one who was king no longer"; the Barrow Wight
  bound at C.1 The Sealed Chamber (`setting/NamedCreatures.md`)
- Nazirith = nazir + -ith - "of the king"; the iron circlet found at C.1 The Sealed
  Chamber (`setting/UniqueTreasures.md`)

## Wilds Tongue (non-human, beyond the border)
Spoken by: whatever moves in the treeline and answers back to it - not a people with
courts or writing, but the calls, marks, and short exchanges its creatures use to warn,
claim ground, and coordinate.
Consonants: k g q x(kh) gh ts s h n
Vowels: a u
Syllable: CVC, with heavy consonant clusters permitted at onset (CCVC); no true
multi-syllable compounding - names stack as short barks separated by a glottal stop
rather than fusing into one word.
Affixes:
- **reduplication** (repeat the root) - intensifies or pluralizes ("ktash-ktash" = many
  teeth, a swarm)
- **-q** (suffix, glottal stop) - a warning or claim-marker, appended to any root used as
  a boundary sign

Roots:
- ktash - tooth, fang
- grum - blood
- vux - smoke, or scent carried on air
- skra - claw
- hurn - dead flesh, carrion
- tuk - ground, packed earth
- ghash - fire, wild and uncontrolled
- nurg - night
- xul - pack, a hunting group
- ash - bone
- grat - taken, claimed
- vish - water, standing and foul
- karn - stone, rough and unworked
- thuk - strike, kill
- ulq - many, an uncounted quantity
- drun - path, a worn trail rather than a road
