Tongues of Greywatch

GENRE.md's three allegiances are also its three tongues: Common carries Law, and is Law's
own courteous register besides; the tongue below carries Chaos, shared by every tribe and
the priesthood over them regardless of what people they were born to. Neutrality keeps none
of its own - a beast has no tongue to speak of, and anyone who answers to no side just
speaks Common plain, without Law's own courtesies in it.

## Common
Spoken by: present - everyone born to the realm behind the keep and the road that reaches
it: the garrison, the traders who still make the trip, the priests of Law addressing their
own laity, and every farmstead between here and a throne that sent none of them. What a
party hears at the gate and in the market, and Law's own courteous register besides -
addressing a superior without it is heard as a slight, not just an omission.
Consonants: p b t d k g m n l r s w y h f
Vowels: a e i o u
Syllable: (C)V(C), leaning open - most words end clean on a vowel, closing only where a
root calls for it.
Affixes: -stead (place, a holding worked or settled); -mere (of standing water, or what
sits beside it); -ling (diminutive, smaller or younger); -folk (plural, a people together);
-'s (possession); un- (negation)

Roots:
- stan - stone, worked or raw
- brook - water, running
- mere - water, standing
- weald - a wild stand of trees, growing wood
- rime - frost, a cold edge on the air
- gray - the colour of old stone and old steel
- hand - the hand, and what it holds
- smith - to make, to shape by hand and fire
- coin - to trade, a payment made and counted
- torch - carried light, flame held and moved
- grave - the dead, and where they're put
- ane - one
- twa - two
- mony - many, several
- wend - a way, to turn toward
- ward - to guard, a place kept watched over
- trod - a trodden way, a road

Coined here:
- Wat - a man's name, the Greywatch drinker who died at the Old Cistern store's basin, C.41
- Wat's Drink - the keep's plain name for that store nook, after him, C.41
- Rat Door - the keep's plain name for the Old Cistern entrance, from a scratched rat mark, C.39

## The Old Tongue
Spoken by: departed - whoever cut and shaped the ravine's tunnels before any tribe now
living moved in. Nobody alive learned it from a living teacher, and nobody now living knows
what its own speakers called it - "the Old Tongue" is the keep's own label for what it
cannot otherwise name. What survives is cut into the ravine's oldest stone, and echoed
without understanding by whichever tribe or priest has learned one working by rote.
Consonants: k q t d s z gh th
Vowels: a i u
Syllable: (C)VC, strictly closed - no word in the Old Tongue ends on a bare vowel.
Affixes: aq- (old, first, before all others); -uz (place, a holding sealed or built);
-ith (possession, belonging to); qat- (direction, below, into the depth); -uth (a title,
one who holds a rite or office)

Roots:
- zut - stone, cut and dressed
- siq - held water, a cistern
- ziq - pale growth in the dark, moss or fungus
- tath - cold, a chill that never lifts
- duk - black, the colour of shadow
- qad - blood
- kut - to bind, to seal shut
- qaz - to give, an offering left and not reclaimed
- ghus - a flame kept for a rite, sacred fire
- saq - bone
- tik - one, the first, alone
- duq - two, a pair
- qath - below, downward, into the depth
- sut - to guard, to ward; a guardian set and left
- kuz - depth, what lies beneath
- kaz - silence

Coined here:
- Kutuz = kut (to bind, to seal shut) + -uz (place, a holding sealed or built) - the
  sealed rod set into the deepest chamber, setting/UniqueTreasures.md
- Qazuz = qaz (an offering left and not reclaimed) + -uz (place) - "the offering place," C.15
- Qatkut = qat- (below) + kut (to seal shut) - "sealed below," a warning cut at C.18
- Qathghus = qath (below) + ghus (a kept flame) - "fire below," cut on the leaf frame, C.32
- Kutsiq = kut (to seal shut) + siq (a cistern) - "keep the water sealed," on the cistern plug, C.33
- Sutuz = sut (to guard) + -uz (place) - "a guardian is set here," on the loose slab, C.34
- Ghusuz = ghus (a kept flame) + -uz (place) - "place of the kept flame," cut on the altar, C.40
- Tathsiq = tath (cold) + siq (a cistern) - "cold cistern," cut on the parapet, C.42
- Qathsiq = qath (below) + siq (a cistern) - "below the cistern," on the effigy's plinth, C.43
- Kutsut = kut (to seal shut) + sut (to guard) - "sealed, guarded," cut over the breach, C.39

## Grozzgur
Spoken by: side - every tribe holding a stretch of the ravine, whatever their own people
and whatever tongue they were born to, and the priesthood at its head besides. Chaos's own
tongue, per GENRE.md's Allegiance: taking this side means learning it, whatever people you
were before. The keep's garrison doesn't call it by its own name - they just call it
chaos-cant.
Consonants: g gh kh h k r rr z
Vowels: u o
Syllable: (C)(C)VC(C), closed throughout - a cluster is permitted at onset or coda, and
nothing ends bare.
Affixes: kra- (place, a den or lair claimed); -ik (diminutive, a runt or a whelp);
-gur (plural, a troop or warband together); -akh (possession, claimed by one); ugh-
(negation, forbidden)

Roots:
- grukk - ground: raw stone, broken rock
- horgu - water: standing water, foul with age
- zogh - growth: creeping growth, moss or mould
- khorr - weather: a cold that bites at night
- gorz - colour: red, the colour of a fresh kill
- khugh - body: a hand, a claw
- rukk - making: to break, to tear apart
- guk - exchange: a hoard, things taken and kept
- urgh - light: fire, a flame that burns wood or flesh
- hoz - dead: dead flesh, a kill left to rot
- gor - number: one
- khogur - number: many, a great host
- zurk - direction: down, into a hole or den
- grozz - to hunt, to run down prey
- hukk - fear, to make afraid
- rrogh - to bind, to hold fast; a leash or a chain

Coined here:
- Grozzgur = grozz (to hunt, run down prey) + -gur (a troop, together) - what this
  tongue's own speakers call it, "the hunting pack's speech," in this file's own heading
- Khughik = khugh (the body, a hand or claw) + -ik (diminutive) - the ravine's smallest
  and most numerous tribe, setting/Bestiary.md
- Gorzgur = gorz (colour, red) + -gur (a troop, together) - the ravine's organised,
  red-painted tribe, setting/Bestiary.md and setting/Factions.md
- Hukkgur = hukk (fear, to make afraid) + -gur (a troop, together) - the ravine's
  largest and fewest-numbered tribe, setting/Bestiary.md
- Zurkik = zurk (down, into a hole) + -ik (runt) - "little down-hole," the Silt Hollow
  lookout, setting/NamedCreatures.md, C.34
- Krazurkik = kra- (a den claimed) + Zurkik - "Zurkik's den," the Khughik's name for
  their entrance, C.30
- Zoghik = zogh (mould) + -ik (runt) - "mould-runt," the Old Cistern matriarch,
  setting/NamedCreatures.md, C.40
- Kraurgh = kra- (a den claimed) + urgh (fire) - "fire den," the Old Cistern nest, C.40
- Zoghikakh = Zoghik + -akh (claimed by one) - "Zoghik's," the Old Cistern store, C.41
- Rroghik = rrogh (to bind, a chain) + -ik (runt) - "chain-runt," the Gorzgur jailer,
  setting/NamedCreatures.md, C.16
- Hozakh = hoz (dead flesh) + -akh (claimed by one) - "the kill's own," the Grave-Ghast at
  the broken grave, setting/NamedCreatures.md, C.21
