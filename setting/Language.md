Tongues of The Isle of the Serpent Crown

## The Common Tongue
Spoken by: arrived - the party, the pirates and the treasure hunters, and a few traders in each village.
Consonants: b, c, d, f, g, h, k, l, m, n, p, r, s, t, th, v, w, y
Vowels: a, e, i, o, u
Syllable: (C)(C)V(C)(C), closed or open, English-shaped
Affixes: -ton - place; -mere - water; -er - belonging; -ling - smallness; un- - negation
Roots:
- stone - rock, and the ground it makes
- sand - loose ground by the sea
- mud - wet ground
- reef - rock under the sea
- creek - running water
- palm - a tree
- vine - a climbing growth
- rain - weather
- red - a colour
- black - a colour
- hand - body
- bone - body, and the dead
- forge - making
- coin - exchange
- fire - light
- three - a number
- south - a direction

## The Island Tongue
Spoken by: present - the southern and northern villages, one tongue in two accents and two words for each other.
Consonants: h, k, l, m, n, ng, p, t, v, w
Vowels: a, e, i, o, u
Syllable: CV(C), a consonant then a vowel, closed only by n
Affixes: -ni - place; -le - water; -ki - smallness; -ta - plural; ne- - negation
Roots:
- hato - ground
- pulu - rock
- lemu - a river
- vako - a swamp
- nalo - a tree
- kewi - a vine
- tuha - rain
- mano - a colour, red
- tiwa - a colour, white
- pawa - body, a hand
- kuno - body, a bone
- tewa - making
- loka - exchange
- huna - light, a fire
- nguha - the dead
- kali - a number, three
- hanu - a direction, north
Coined here:
- Puluni = pulu + -ni - the stone place
- Pulunita = pulu + -ni + -ta - the people of the stone place
- Lemuni = lemu + -ni - the river place
- Lemunita = lemu + -ni + -ta - the people of the river place

## The Serpent Speech
Spoken by: present - the snake people and their cult, the lizard men as an old soldiers' tongue, and cut into every stair of the pyramid.
Consonants: k, kh, r, s, sh, t, th, z, zh
Vowels: a, e, i, u, ee, uu
Syllable: CVC, always closed, ending in s, sh, th, k or z
Affixes: -ath - place; -zhus - water; -ik - smallness; -esh - belonging; ku- - negation
Roots:
- khus - rock
- tesh - water
- rak - a river
- zeth - a stair, a way up
- suk - light, the sun
- kheth - fire
- ruz - body, blood
- thesh - body, a scale
- zhak - body, a fang
- kuth - the dead
- thaz - exchange, gold
- sheek - body, a head
- tuuz - a snake
- reesh - an egg
- zuuth - a colour, black
- kis - a number, three
- tash - a direction, east
Coined here:
- Theshik = thesh + -ik - the little scales
- Tuuzesh = tuuz + -esh - the serpent's own

## The Mer Speech
Spoken by: present - the mer people, sung under water and spoken in air only as a broken trade-talk.
Consonants: f, h, l, m, n, r, v, w, y
Vowels: ae, ai, ao, ea, eu, ia, oa, oe, ua
Syllable: (C)V(C), a paired vowel, opened by a consonant or not, closed only by l
Affixes: -ia - place; -ual - belonging; -vae - smallness; -eu - plural; hoa- - negation
Roots:
- neal - water, the sea
- mua - a reef
- rael - a current
- wia - a wave
- foal - salt
- yeu - a weed
- hae - a shell
- loal - a pearl
- yoal - body, blood
- nao - body, a fin
- aol - making, a net
- vea - exchange, gold
- ial - light
- oel - the dead
- hoe - a number, three
- ael - a direction, north
- vaol - the deep
Coined here:
- Nealeu = neal + -eu - the sea folk
