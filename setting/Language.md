Tongues of Duen

## Isvren
Spoken by: the keep's garrison and the hangers-on camped against its walls, and by every
hamlet and holding within a hard day's ride - the living, whoever they currently answer to.

Consonants: p t k b d g m n s v r l

Vowels: a e i o u

Syllable: (C)V(C), open-leaning - most roots are a bare onset and vowel with no coda; a
closed, consonant-final root is the deliberate minority, marked below

Affixes:
- `-en` (suffix) - place, holding, or settlement: "the seat of [root]"
- `-ol` (suffix) - diminutive
- `-ir` (suffix) - plural or collective
- `av-` (prefix) - possession or belonging: "kept by," "sworn to"
- `nev-` (prefix) - former or old: what a thing was called before it was called what it's
  called now

Roots:
- `du` - stone, rock
- `ke` - still water: a pool, a cistern, a well
- `so` - running water: a stream, a channel
- `ta` - grain, cultivated growth
- `li` - leaf, green growth left to itself
- `ve` - cold
- `go` - storm, rough weather
- `mi` - pale, white
- `ra` - red, blood-colored
- `ba` - hand
- `do` - eye
- `ka` - to build, to raise
- `to` - to break, to fall down
- `sa` - to take
- `ge` - to give, to grant
- `lo` - light, a flame
- `nu` - dark, shadow
- `mo` - the dead, a corpse
- `pa` - one
- `kal` - many *(closed - the minority form)*
- `sen` - north, toward the cold *(closed)*
- `dol` - a holding, a garrison seat - distinct from `-en`, used where the settlement
  itself is the subject rather than just marked as a place *(closed)*
- `sal` - rope, cordage

Coined here:
- `Duen` = `du` + `-en` - "the seat of stone." The keep and the setting itself; every
  document in this framework uses it as the setting's name.
- `Dutor` = `du` + `tor` - "stonebreak." The quarry workings beneath Duen, opened at the
  keep's founding and still being worked.
- `Salir` = `sal` + `-ir` - "the corded ones." The rope-crossing band operating downriver
  of the fallen ford; see `setting/Factions.md`.

## Accalur
Spoken by: the throne that issued the charter this keep still holds by - dead now, or
scattered past finding, so spoken today only by what its dead left behind: lintels,
boundary stones, tomb doors, the deep courses of the ruins the Mythic Underworld is built
from.

Consonants: k t p g d b s r n m z

Vowels: a i u

Syllable: CVC, strictly closed - every root both opens and closes on a consonant, the
opposite shape from Isvren, so a party can hear which tongue a name came from before
anyone glosses it

Affixes:
- `-ad` (suffix) - place, or former settlement: names a seat that *was*, not necessarily
  one that still stands
- `gar-` (prefix) - first, oldest, or former: marks a thing as the original of its kind
- `-im` (suffix) - plural or collective
- `-uk` (suffix) - diminutive, but carrying the sense of a broken-off piece of something
  once whole, not merely something small
- `ta-` (prefix) - negation
- `-it` (suffix) - title or honorific: "the one who [root]s"

Roots:
- `kat` - stone, foundation-stone
- `zun` - still water: a cistern, a font
- `dur` - running water: a river, a course
- `bak` - a crop, a root vegetable, cultivated growth
- `pir` - frost, cold
- `maz` - black
- `sab` - grey, ash-colored
- `gan` - hand
- `rin` - eye
- `tuk` - to carve, to build in stone
- `bas` - to break, to shatter
- `nir` - to take, to seize
- `mut` - to give, to grant, to cede
- `zar` - light, flame
- `kun` - dark, shadow
- `dab` - the dead, a corpse
- `sib` - a tomb, a grave-seal
- `tiz` - one
- `gam` - many
- `dum` - below, underground - the root the setting's ruins and dungeons are named from

Coined here:
- `Durad` = `dur` + `-ad` - "the seat of the river." The gorge and the river running
  through it, named before Duen existed; glossed wherever it appears since nobody living
  speaks Accalur to translate it unprompted.

## Kreth
Spoken by: nothing that answers to being asked - the things that come up through the old
workings when the deep places under the ruins stir. Not a people's tongue; a noise the
things that use it can also make with a voice.

Consonants: k g x r s h

Vowels: a u

Syllable: (C)V(CC) - harsh consonant clusters permitted in the coda, the one shape of the
three that isn't clean; a Kreth name should read as slightly wrong to say out loud

Affixes:
- `-ux` (suffix) - place, but only a place below ground - Kreth has no word for a place
  under open sky
- `-ah` (suffix) - collective: not quite plural, more "the mass of," since Kreth speakers
  don't obviously count themselves as individuals
- `hu-` (prefix) - negation, doubling as "dead" or "unmade"
- `-akr` (suffix) - augmentative: bigger, older, worse than the root alone

Roots:
- `kar` - stone, but specifically stone that has been tunneled or hollowed
- `xus` - water, only ever standing and foul
- `gash` - rot, growth gone to spoil - Kreth has no root for growing things left healthy
- `har` - cold, but the specific cold of deep places, not weather
- `sur` - black, the only color-root Kreth has
- `rukk` - claw, limb - Kreth doesn't distinguish hand from claw
- `xar` - eye, or any organ used to sense in the dark
- `kask` - to tunnel, to unmake stone
- `gur` - to take, always by force
- `hax` - dark, absence of light - Kreth has no root for light itself
- `sak` - the dead, but carrying no grief - a resource, not a loss
- `ur` - one
- `ask` - many, specifically a swarm-many rather than a counted-many

Coined here:
- `Xarask` = `xar` + `-ask` - "elder-eye." The faction of things holding the flooded lower
  workings below the Old Cut; see `setting/Factions.md` and the Xarask Broodling entry in
  `setting/Bestiary.md`.
