Tongues of Roadsend

## The Common Tongue
Spoken by: arrived - the keep, the realm behind it, and every road traveller.
Consonants: b, d, f, g, h, l, m, n, p, r, s, t, w, y
Vowels: a, e, i, o, u
Syllable: (C)(C)V(C)(C), closed or open, English-shaped
Affixes: -ton - place; -mere - water; -er - belonging; -ling - smallness
Coined here:

Roots:
- stone - ground
- hill - rise
- dale - low ground
- marsh - wet ground
- brook - running water
- pool - standing water
- root - growth
- oak - tree
- frost - weather
- red - colour
- black - colour
- hand - body
- bone - the dead
- forge - making
- coin - exchange
- lamp - light
- grave - the dead
- north - direction
- three - number

## The Goblins' Tongue
Spoken by: non-people - the goblin tribe and whoever trades with it.
Consonants: g, k, kh, z, zh, r, m, n, b, dr
Vowels: u, a, o, short
Syllable: CVC or CVCC, always closed and clipped
Affixes: -uk - belonging; gu- - smallness; -zor - place; ko- - negation; -ar - plural
Coined here:
- Zhakuk = zhak + -uk - the goblin tribe of the cave; Factions

Roots:
- gur - stone
- zhak - hole
- drom - low ground
- khub - water
- bak - mud
- zug - tree
- mok - blood
- grub - red
- kul - black
- naz - hand
- rok - tooth
- zak - making
- khor - trade
- uz - dark
- gra - dead
- kon - one
- zun - many
- mud - north

## The Cave-Makers' Tongue
Spoken by: departed - the builders of the cave, met only cut into its walls.
Consonants: l, m, n, s, v, th, d, r, h
Vowels: a, e, i, o, ae, ei
Syllable: CV or CVV, always open and long
Affixes: -sae - place; -ve - belonging; ne- - negation; -thi - age; -na - plural
Coined here:

Roots:
- sora - stone
- aeda - ground
- vaela - water
- rei - rain
- hema - growth
- thalo - light
- dael - gold
- imo - dark
- vora - the dead
- lane - body
- sethi - making
- nam - hand
- eiro - exchange
- hae - one
- dai - three
- vone - east
- mela - white
- ora - deep
