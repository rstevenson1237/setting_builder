Tongues of The Buried City

## The Common Tongue
Spoken by: arrived - the party, the tomb robbers, and the cynidiceans only in a broken form.
Consonants: b, c, d, f, g, h, k, l, m, n, p, r, s, t, th, v, w, y
Vowels: a, e, i, o, u
Syllable: (C)(C)V(C)(C), closed or open, English-shaped
Affixes: -ton - place; -mere - water; -er - belonging; -ling - smallness; un- - negation

Roots:
- stone - rock, and the ground it makes
- sand - loose ground
- dune - a rise of sand
- well - water from the ground
- reed - a plant of wet ground
- wind - weather
- red - a colour
- black - a colour
- hand - body
- bone - body, and the dead
- forge - making
- coin - exchange
- lamp - light
- grave - the dead
- north - a direction
- three - a number

## The Cynidicean Speech
Spoken by: present - Kurumisa, Ruhiahe, Sihuahet and the lake city's dreamers, and cut into every wall of the pyramid.
Consonants: h, k, kh, m, n, r, s, sh, t, z
Vowels: a, e, i, u, aa
Syllable: (C)VCV(C), two syllables to a root, open but for a closing consonant, with a long vowel only in the first syllable
Affixes: -ra - place; -un - water; -it - smallness; -ahe - belonging; ne- - negation
Roots:
- mehe - sand
- kuru - rock
- zami - a well
- sihu - a reed
- hanu - wind
- sheru - red
- kheti - black
- misa - hand
- temu - bone
- kashu - making
- nazi - exchange
- ruhi - light
- ahet - the dead
- shena - three
- anuk - a direction, north

Coined here:
- Kurumisa = kuru + misa - rock hand, the city's shield-hand
- Ruhiahe = ruhi + -ahe - of the light
- Sihuahet = sihu + ahet - reed of the dead
- Zamiun = zami + -un - well-water

## The Black Speech
Spoken by: side - Voxkroth and the demon's servants.
Consonants: gh, dh, th, zh, d, k, kr, l, r, v, x, j
Vowels: o, u, ae
Syllable: CVC, closed, a root one syllable
Affixes: -ox - belonging; ur- - negation; -vod - place; -jul - smallness
Roots:
- ghoth - sand
- krul - rock
- dhov - a well
- xuv - a plant of the dark
- thoj - wind
- zhur - red
- vox - black
- kroth - hand
- ghul - bone
- dhaex - making
- jox - exchange
- thul - light
- xoth - the dead
- vur - three
- zhok - a direction, down

Coined here:
- Voxkroth = vox + kroth - black hand
- Krulox = krul + -ox - of the rock
- Xothox = xoth + -ox - of the dead
- Zhurjul = zhur + -jul - the red little one
- Ghulox = ghul + -ox - of the bone
