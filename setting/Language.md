## Marchspeech
Spoken by: the living, in settlements and on the roads between them. The tongue names are
coined in unless there is a reason otherwise.

Consonants: k kh g d t th b p m n r l s sh f v h w j
Vowels: a e i o u, plus the digraphs ae ei ou
Syllable: (C)V(C), commonly two syllables, occasionally three
Affixes:
- `-gaard` (place, holding) · `-marn` (water, a run of it) · `-stad` (a made place)
- `-ling` (lesser, child of) · `-fast` (held, defended) · `ur-` (old, first)

Roots:
- khir - stone, standing stone
- rend - split, cleft
- weld - wood, standing timber
- morn - dark, unlit
- brack - salt, brackish
- vaen - open ground, fen edge
- tors - a rise, a knuckle of rock
- gell - yellow, sick, souring
- druv - deep, sunken
- halm - shelter, a lee
- skeir - sharp, cut, a blade's edge
- ondt - want, hunger
- fael - pale, drained
- gorm - warm, close, breathless
- lind - soft, yielding, treacherous underfoot
- thrap - noise, hammering
- naug - none, empty, spent
- veth - white, bone

## Old Rede
Spoken by: nobody living. Found cut into stone, on grave goods, and in the mouths of
things that have not stopped speaking it. Names in Old Rede mark a place as older than the
people currently in it.

Consonants: t d n l r s z th m p k g w
Vowels: a e i o u, all of which may run long (aa ee ii oo uu)
Syllable: CV(CV), open and running, rarely closed
Affixes:
- `-anu` (of, belonging to) · `-ath` (great, greater) · `-el` (small, near)
- `si-` (under, beneath) · `ne-` (against, opposed) · `-ora` (the place where)

Roots:
- talu - year, turning
- meze - remember, keep
- dorai - road, the way through
- sela - light, kept light
- nuth - bind, oath
- kire - water, running
- azza - mother, source
- pelu - hand, work of hands
- zoreth - fear, the thing feared
- laani - long, enduring
- immo - gate, threshold
- teru - three, a triad
- wosa - flesh, the living part
- galu - fire, kept fire
- nesse - break, the breaking
- odar - king, the one who holds
- siwa - below, the under-part
- ranu - grain, what is grown

## Ghaunt
Spoken by: the non-human peoples of the wold and what they have taught to speak. Clipped,
consonant-heavy, and unfriendly to a human mouth.

Consonants: g gh k kk t tt ch sh zh r rr n ng v z
Vowels: a i u only, no diphthongs
Vowels never begin a word.
Syllable: CVC, strictly; compounds are built by simple juxtaposition
Affixes:
- `-ik` (belonging to a thing) · `-ur` (belonging to a person) · `-gash` (many, a mass of)
- `kk-` (not, unmade) - prefixed, doubling the following consonant where it can

Roots:
- gragh - eat, take in
- vin - see, watch
- zhak - cut, take apart
- rrik - stone, worked stone
- tugg - carry, bear
- ngash - blood
- chur - young, unfinished
- kith - cold
- zug - dig
- shak - stand, remain
- girn - want, reach for
- tazh - speak, make sound
- vugg - swell, rise, rot
- rrun - deep place, den
- kang - iron, bitter metal
- zhin - thin, worn away
- ghut - wet
- narr - hunt, follow
