Tongues of [Setting Name]

## Cael

Spoken by: the living - the frontier's clans, market towns, mercenary camps, and every
common tongue traded across a border. Everyone still breathing knows at least a little.

Consonants: p, b, t, d, k, g, m, n, r, l, s, f, w
Vowels: a, e, i, o, u
Syllable: (C)(r/l)V(C) - onsets may take a liquid cluster, syllables chain freely into
longer names; open syllables (no coda) are as common as closed ones.

Affixes:
- **-dun** (suffix) - place, holding, settlement: "the hold at"
- **-wy** (suffix) - water, river: "of the river"
- **-en** (suffix) - diminutive: "little, lesser"
- **-ath** (suffix) - plural, collective: "the many of"
- **mac-** (prefix) - possession, belonging: "of, son/daughter of"
- **sen-** (prefix) - age: old, first, former

Roots:
- carr - stone
- bryn - hill
- avon - river
- mor - sea
- coed - wood, forest
- glas - green-grey, growing, in leaf
- gwlyb - wet, rain
- oer - cold
- tan - fire, warmth
- gwen - white, fair
- du - black
- rhu - red
- pen - head, the topmost part
- llaw - hand
- gof - to forge, to make
- tor - to break
- cym - to take
- rho - to give
- gole - light
- nos - night, dark
- bal - death, the dead
- un - one
- dau - two
- de - toward, facing

Coined here:
- Carrdun = carr (stone) + -dun (place, holding) - the setting itself: a border garrison
  raised from salvaged tomb-stone.
- Oerath = oer (cold) + -ath (plural, collective) - Region B, the cold reaches: the open
  steppe between the ford and the horse-tribe camps.
- Glaswy = glas (green) + -wy (water) - Region C, the green water: the reed-choked
  backwater downstream of the ruins.
- Baldun = bal (death, the dead) + -dun (place, holding) - Region D, hold of the dead: the
  flood-exposed remnant of Nemhet's drowned capital.

## Nemhet

Spoken by: a river-kingdom that no longer rules anywhere - its priest-sorcerers built in
stone meant to outlast them, and did. Spoken now only in liturgy, on tomb-doors, and by
scholars who read the dead; a few of its native speakers have themselves outlasted their
kingdom and are not, strictly, alive.

Consonants: k, kh, q, t, d, s, sh, z, m, n, r, h, w
Vowels: a, i, u
Syllable: C V C, strict and closed - no syllable opens on a bare vowel and none ends on
one; consonant clusters are avoided rather than chained, giving the tongue its clipped,
carved-in-stone cadence against Cael's running one.

Affixes:
- **per-** (prefix) - place, holding: "the house of"
- **-senu** (suffix) - water, flood, river
- **-et** (suffix) - diminutive, a lesser form of the thing named
- **-im** (suffix) - plural, collective
- **sa-** (prefix) - possession, belonging: "of, belonging to"
- **wer-** (prefix) - age, rank: "great, first, elder"

Roots:
- khesu - stone
- senu - water, flood
- pethu - reed, marsh-growth
- shefu - heat, the dry season
- kharu - black
- hedu - pale, white
- rekhu - heart, chest
- kamu - shadow, a departed spirit
- sekhu - to cut, to carve
- wenu - to bind, to seal
- temu - to take
- iru - to give, to grant
- nekhu - flame, light
- mesu - dark, night
- usru - death, the dead
- wa - one
- senti - two
- ametu - toward, facing

Coined here:
- Senametu = senu (water) + ametu (toward, facing) - "the way over the water": the old
  Nemhet name for Oerath's Causeway (B.1), cut into one of its kerbstones.
- Perwenu = per- (place, holding) + wenu (to bind, to seal) - "the sealed place": the
  Nemhet warding-name cut into the Ward-Threshold's doors (D.7).
- Werkamu = wer- (great, elder) + kamu (shadow, a departed spirit) - "the great departed
  one": Nemhet's last priest-king, named on the epitaph in his own rest (D.18).

## Xoss

Spoken by: something that is not a people. Fragments of it are drilled into cultists who
serve what lives under the old places; whether its true speakers still draw breath, sleep,
or never did either, is not something Cael or Nemhet has a word for.

Consonants: s, z, sh, x (a dry, scraping fricative), k, g, h, th, '  (a glottal catch)
Vowels: a, i
Syllable: (S)CV(') - an optional extra sibilant (s-, z-, sh-, x-) stacks before the core
consonant, and a syllable may close on the glottal catch instead of a true consonant;
built to hiss and stop rather than flow.

Affixes:
- **ss-** (prefix) - place, holding: a nest or coil-place
- **-zha** (suffix) - water, the submerged or drowned
- **-'i** (suffix) - diminutive: lesser spawn
- **-ith** (suffix) - plural, collective: a swarm or brood
- **kha-** (prefix) - possession, belonging: "of the coil"
- **xa-** (prefix) - age: ancient, first, eldest

Roots:
- hass - stone, that which does not move
- zha - water, the drowned deep
- thik - scale, a shedding, a renewal
- xass - cold
- kiss - black, the dark between stars
- sha'i - pale, an underbelly colour
- va - coil, spine, body
- tikh - fang, tooth
- ka'ath - to strike, to rend
- hith - to swallow, to take
- gis - to yield, to give up
- thass - flame, surface-fire
- ki'a - dark, the deep
- ssen - death, an unmaking
- va'ith - sleep, the long dreaming
- ah - one
- zhi - toward, downward, into the deep

Coined here:
- Kissva = kiss (black) + va (coil, spine, body) - "the black coil": the Coil's own name
  for the Sealed Gallery (D.17), where their glyph-ring is scratched into the floor.
