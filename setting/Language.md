Tongues of the Setting

## Kelder
Spoken by: everyone living - farmers, traders, soldiers, the settled and the wandering alike.
The common tongue, and the one a location's own name is drawn from by default.
Consonants: p t k b d g m n r l s h w y
Vowels: a e i o u
Syllable: (C)V(C), open and running - syllables chain easily and names fall trippingly
Affixes:
- mar- : great, high (marhad - "high road")
- -oth : settlement, holding (Kelderoth - "the common hold")
- -en : plural, collective
- -lin : diminutive
- ka- : negation, un-
- -ar : place of, the [root] place

Roots:
- dun - hill
- mar - stone, standing stone
- val - water, river
- tor - tower, height
- had - road, way, path
- kel - hearth, home
- bren - fire, flame
- fal - fall, falling water
- grim - grey, dour
- lor - light, lantern
- sten - stone wall, rampart
- weld - forest, wood
- yar - grain, harvest
- brok - break, broken thing
- holt - shelter, roof
- sund - a sundering, a divide

## Old Rede
Spoken by: a vanished civilization - the builders of the ruins the party will dig through.
No one alive speaks it as a mother tongue; it survives in inscriptions, wards, and the
liturgy of those few orders that still read it.
Consonants: t d k g s z th f v r n m (closed clusters permitted: -rd, -st, -nk, -th)
Vowels: a i u (three only - narrower than Kelder, deliberately)
Syllable: CVC, strict and closed - no vowel stands alone, no syllable runs open
Affixes:
- ith- : old, first, elder (Ithdur - "the first stone")
- -ur : place of
- -ka : tomb, grave-marker
- an- : negation, un-, without
- -sal : guardian, keeper
- -gath : threshold, gate-marking

Roots:
- red - word, decree, law
- dur - stone, wall
- kaz - fire, forge
- vun - water, the deep water
- mith - shadow, the below
- gath - gate, threshold
- sil - blood
- narr - bone
- ith - first, old
- kur - king, crown
- zan - death, the dead
- tul - tomb, sealed place
- vash - ash, ruin
- rin - iron
- ost - east, rising, dawn
- fen - seal, ward, binding

## Grosk-Ur
Spoken by: nothing that still calls itself a person - the beastkin and old chaos-touched
things of the deep wild and the Mythic Underworld's lower reaches. Guttural, and meant to
sound like it costs the speaker something.
Consonants: k g q r rr kh gh (heavy clusters permitted: -grk, -thk, -sk-, -zg-)
Vowels: u a (two only - the narrowest of the three, and the harshest)
Syllable: CVCC or CCVC - clusters are the point, not an accident
Affixes:
- -grah : pack, many, a swarm of
- ur- : big, great, chief
- -esh : of the pit, of below
- vor- : hungry for, driven by
- -uk : small, weak, prey

Roots:
- grok - tooth, bite
- ur - big, great
- thak - meat, flesh
- skar - claw
- muk - mud, filth
- hrag - rock, cave-hollow
- yesh - pit, hole, the deep dark
- vor - hunger
- zug - stolen fire
- krath - night, moonless
- oss - bone
- ghul - death-thing, ghost
- rrag - pack-leader, alpha
- thurk - blood-debt
- gakh - warning cry, alarm
