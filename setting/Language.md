Tongues of Roadend

## Common
Spoken by: present - the keep, the road behind it, and the realm the road comes from.
Consonants: p b t d k g m n l r s f w h
Vowels: a e i o u
Syllable: (C)V(C)(C), closing on a consonant more often than not
Affixes: -stead (place, a holding or settlement); -ling (diminutive); -s (plural); old-
(age, former)

Roots:
- stone - stone, worked or raw
- clay - wet ground, earth that holds a print
- brook - running water
- thorn - growing things that catch and hold
- frost - cold, and the white it leaves
- grey - grey, the colour of stone and old iron
- hand - the hand, and what it holds
- smith - to make by hand and fire
- coin - to pay, a price counted out
- lamp - carried light
- barrow - the dead, and where they are put
- one - one
- twain - two
- yon - over there, the far side
- gate - a way through, kept or shut
- road - a way travelled

## Gob
Spoken by: present - the goblins of the cave, and anything that trades with them.
Consonants: g k r z sh n b
Vowels: a u o
Syllable: (C)CVC, every word closed, clusters only at the start
Affixes: -ak (plural, a crowd of); gro- (title, one who is bigger); -ush (possession);
nuk- (negation)

Roots:
- grak - rock, hard ground
- zug - water, any water
- skob - roots and fungus, what grows in the dark
- shak - wind, cold air moving
- rok - red, the colour of fire and blood
- gob - the mouth, and eating
- brak - to break
- nab - to take
- zaz - fire, its light
- kuz - dead, a dead thing
- ob - one
- shub - many, more than can be counted
- grub - down, deeper in
- snag - teeth, biting
- bruz - iron, anything taken from the road

Coined here:
- Gronab = gro- + nab - the goblin chief, C.5
- Bruzshub = bruz + shub - the chief's hoard, C.5

## Carved Speech
Spoken by: departed - whoever cut the cave the goblins live in. It survives only carved into
the cave's walls; nobody living reads it.
Consonants: t d m n l v th y
Vowels: e i o
Syllable: (C)V, every syllable open
Affixes: -no (place, a working or a cutting); ve- (age, first); -yi (possession); de-
(direction, below)

Roots:
- teli - stone, the rock face
- mi - water, seepage
- lo - growing things, the moss on wet stone
- thi - cold, the cold of deep ground
- yo - white, pale
- va - the hand, to hold
- toli - to cut stone
- vimo - a wage, what is owed for work
- ne - lamp-light, a flame kept lit
- mothe - the dead, a body
- eni - one
- dovi - a count, marks kept in a row
- de - down, below
- lithe - a seam, a vein of ore
- tho - a shaft, a way down

Coined here:
- Thono = tho + -no - the shaft-place, C.6
