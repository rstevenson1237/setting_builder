Tongues of Brackvaen

Seeded at step 1c with three working tongues, tailored at 2i. Appended to every time a
proper noun is coined, at every step thereafter. Never rewritten.

**Who speaks what, in Brackvaen.** Marchspeech is the holding's tongue and names everything
built in the last hundred and twenty years. Old Rede is the Odarath's, and a name in it
marks a place as older than the people currently standing in it - which is why the Assize
cut its boundary stones in a tongue none of its own heads can read. Ghaunt is the Vingash's
and is never spoken in front of a Marchman.

---

## Marchspeech
Spoken by: the living, in settlements and on the roads between them. The tongue names are
coined in unless there is a reason otherwise.

Consonants: k kh g d t th b p m n r l s sh f v h w j
Vowels: a e i o u, plus the digraphs ae ei ou
Syllable: (C)V(C), commonly two syllables, occasionally three
Affixes:
- `-gaard` (place, holding) · `-marn` (water, a run of it) · `-stad` (a made place)
- `-ling` (lesser, child of) · `-fast` (held, defended) · `ur-` (old, first)

Roots:
- khir - stone, standing stone
- rend - split, cleft
- weld - wood, standing timber
- morn - dark, unlit
- brack - salt, brackish
- vaen - open ground, fen edge
- tors - a rise, a knuckle of rock
- gell - yellow, sick, souring
- druv - deep, sunken
- halm - shelter, a lee
- skeir - sharp, cut, a blade's edge
- ondt - want, hunger
- fael - pale, drained
- gorm - warm, close, breathless
- lind - soft, yielding, treacherous underfoot
- thrap - noise, hammering
- naug - none, empty, spent
- veth - white, bone
- hond - hand, the working hand
- yald - year, a turning of the seasons

Coined here:
*(Appended as proper nouns are coined in this tongue. Format: `Name = root + root - where it appears`.)*

- Brackvaen = brack + vaen - the salt fen-edge; the setting entire
- Torsgaard = tors + -gaard - the rise-holding; region A, the only dry roof in Brackvaen
- Lindmarn = lind + -marn - the treacherous water; region B, the fen crossing
- Faelhond = fael + hond - the pale hands; the Brined who work the stair (faction)
- Gellyald = gell + yald - the souring summer of eighteen years ago; Torsgaard says "the Gell Year" in plain speech (History)
- Brackstad = brack + -stad - a made place of salt; any worked pan, in the common usage
- Mornhalm = morn + halm - the dark lee; a soured western pan nobody reworks
- Naugfast = naug + -fast - the emptied fastness; what the Marchfolk call the Odarath stonework they can see
- Urweld = ur- + weld - old timber; bog oak cut out of the pans, the holding's best material
- Skeirling = skeir + -ling - a lesser blade; the long knife a pan crew carries

**Marchfolk personal names**: a root plus `-a` or `-en`; family names a root plus `-ling`.
So Vetha Naugling is bone-and-empty, Gellen Torsling is souring-of-the-rise. The naming is
current and the namers are alive, which is why Torsgaard's names argue with each other.

- Thrapstad = thrap + -stad - the hammering place; A.1, the weigh-house and market ground
- Rendfast = rend + -fast - the division held; A.2, the Salt Assize's hall
- Gormhalm = gorm + halm - the close shelter; A.3, the salt stores
- Faelvaen = fael + vaen - the pale open ground; A.4, the drying floors
- Druvmarn = druv + -marn - the deep water; A.5, the landing
- Weldfast = weld + -fast - the timber held; A.6, the ropewalk and boatyard
- Halmgaard = halm + -gaard - the shelter-holding; A.7, the lodging house
- Gellhalm = gell + halm - the souring shelter; A.8, the tannery
- Vethgaard = veth + -gaard - the bone-holding; A.9, and the name was a joke before it was accurate
- Ondtstad = ondt + -stad - the want-place; A.10, the crew shanty below the rise
- Vetha Naugling = veth + -a, naug + -ling - the debt-holder at A.9
- Gellen Torsling = gell + -en, tors + -ling - an Assize head at A.2
- Druva Halmling = druv + -a, halm + -ling - keeps the lodging house at A.7
- Ondtla Faelling = ondt + -la, fael + -ling - a Faelhond digger at A.10
- Halmen Weldling = halm + -en, weld + -ling - the weighmaster at A.1
- Bracka Druvling = brack + -a, druv + -ling - the downriver factor at A.5
- Naugen Gelling = naug + -en, gell + -ling - the tanner and bone-setter at A.8

## Old Rede
Spoken by: nobody living. Found cut into stone, on grave goods, and in the mouths of
things that have not stopped speaking it. Names in Old Rede mark a place as older than the
people currently in it.

Consonants: t d n l r s z th m p k g w
Vowels: a e i o u, all of which may run long (aa ee ii oo uu)
Syllable: CV(CV), open and running, rarely closed
Affixes:
- `-anu` (of, belonging to) · `-ath` (great, greater) · `-el` (small, near)
- `si-` (under, beneath) · `ne-` (against, opposed) · `-ora` (the place where)

Roots:
- talu - year, turning
- meze - remember, keep
- dorai - road, the way through
- sela - light, kept light
- nuth - bind, oath
- kire - water, running
- azza - mother, source
- pelu - hand, work of hands
- zoreth - fear, the thing feared
- laani - long, enduring
- immo - gate, threshold
- teru - three, a triad
- wosa - flesh, the living part
- galu - fire, kept fire
- nesse - break, the breaking
- odar - king, the one who holds
- siwa - below, the under-part
- ranu - grain, what is grown

Coined here:
*(Appended as proper nouns are coined in this tongue. Format: `Name = root + root - where it appears`.)*

- Odarath = odar + -ath - the great holder; the drowned realm and its people
- Odaranu = odar + -anu - of the king; the Cured Man still walking its errand at C.10, named by the Faelhond rather than by anyone who knew it
- Siwapelu = siwa + pelu - the under-work; region C, the gallery beneath the pans
- Nessekire = nesse + kire - the breaking-water; the flood of four hundred years ago
- Nuthora = nuth + -ora - the place where oaths are made; a boundary stone, and by extension all eleven
- Zorethanu = zoreth + -anu - of the feared thing; what the lowest course is called on its own walls
- Meselaani = meze + laani - the long remembering; Old Rede cut to be read, rather than to bind
- Selora = sela + -ora - the place of kept light; an Odarath lamp-niche, found throughout Siwapelu
- Teruimmo = teru + immo - three-gate; C.13, a course that divides three ways
- Immora = immo + -ora - the place of the gate; B.2, the stair-head at the fen's edge
- Immorael = immo + -ora + -el - the small gate-place; C.1, the stair-head chamber below
- Doraiath = dorai + -ath - the great road; B.1, the causeway, on an Odarath pan wall

- Nuthorath = nuth + -ora + -ath - the great place of oaths; C.7, where the stones were cut

- Siwakire = siwa + kire - the under-water; C.11, the drainage hall

## Ghaunt
Spoken by: the non-human peoples of the wold and what they have taught to speak. Clipped,
consonant-heavy, and unfriendly to a human mouth.

Consonants: g gh k kk t tt ch sh zh r rr n ng v z
Vowels: a i u only, no diphthongs
Vowels never begin a word.
Syllable: CVC, strictly; compounds are built by simple juxtaposition
Affixes:
- `-ik` (belonging to a thing) · `-ur` (belonging to a person) · `-gash` (many, a mass of)
- `kk-` (not, unmade) - prefixed, doubling the following consonant where it can

Roots:
- gragh - eat, take in
- vin - see, watch
- zhak - cut, take apart
- rrik - stone, worked stone
- tugg - carry, bear
- ngash - blood
- chur - young, unfinished
- kith - cold
- zug - dig
- shak - stand, remain
- girn - want, reach for
- tazh - speak, make sound
- vugg - swell, rise, rot
- rrun - deep place, den
- kang - iron, bitter metal
- zhin - thin, worn away
- ghut - wet
- narr - hunt, follow

Coined here:
*(Appended as proper nouns are coined in this tongue. Format: `Name = root + root - where it appears`.)*

- Vingash = vin + -gash - the many who watch; the fen people's name for themselves (faction)
- Ghutrrun = ghut + rrun - the wet den; a Vingash deep-water fastness
- Kangzhin = kang + zhin - worn iron; a bitter-iron ear-ring passed down rather than earned
- Zhakur = zhak + -ur - the one who cuts; a Vingash watcher's office, held for a season
- Zhakur-of-Nine = zhakur + the count of her rings - the Channel-Elder at B.3; the Marchspeech half of the name is a translation the Vingash tolerate
- Tuggik = tugg + -ik - the carrying thing; the Odarath salt-hauler still walking its route
- Rrikgash = rrik + -gash - the mass of worked stone; Ghaunt for Siwapelu
- Kkithik = kk- + kith + -ik - the unmade-cold thing; what the Vingash call the western pans since the Gell Year
- Narrur = narr + -ur - the one who follows; a Sump-Kin, in the only Ghaunt word for them
