Keys of Brackvaen

Twelfth Socket Fitting (*fitted stone*) - found at B.4 Nuthora, fitted at C.7 Nuthorath
A dressed limestone plug about four inches across and nine long, tapered, cut to sit in a socket rather than to be held - it has no head, no grip, and nothing about it reads as a tool. It sits in the cap of the boundary stone standing in open water at Nuthora, five feet above a standing man's reach from a punt, and the weathering inside the socket is much fresher than the weathering around it, which is the only reason anyone would look twice.

Unlocks: the twelfth socket in the sunken salt floor at C.7 Nuthorath, at the northern point of the ring of eleven pillars. Setting the fitting into that socket is the one action in Siwapelu that acts on the whole region rather than on a room: the eleven pillars are the originals of the eleven Assize stones, and the twelfth position is the one the settlement's copies were never made from. Fitted, the ring is complete for the first time in nine hundred years, every Nuthora Oathkeeper in Brackvaen - the eleven above ground and the one in the dome - stands down at once, and the boundary stones stop being obeyed by anything but people. Withdrawn, they resume. The Assize's entire authority rests on stones nobody there can read, so a party holding this fitting is holding the enforcement of half of Torsgaard's arithmetic in a bag.

Why they are apart: they were never together. The stone at Nuthora is the original and the cap fitting was cut with it; the twelfth socket at Nuthorath is where the twelfth *pillar* should stand and does not. The Odarath left the ring one short deliberately, and put the missing piece six hundred yards out into open water where nobody would step on it - which worked for nine hundred years, and stopped working when the Assize sent men down to copy the stones and one of them looked up.

Peluora Fitting-Rod (*stone rod*) - found at C.5 Peluora, fitted at C.22 Nekire
A rod of dressed stone eighteen inches long, square-sectioned, with a four-inch head cut to a shallow taper, sitting in the third tool rack of the first course workshop among the iron drifts. It is the only stone object in a rack of forty iron ones, which is the whole of the clue, and it is not a tool - nothing about it will strike, cut, or lever.

Unlocks: the bearing sockets on the four screw pumps at C.22 Nekire, three courses down. It is the pump key. In the socket of the one screw still turning it declutches the flight, and the fourth course begins to fill further; in any of the three failed sockets it engages a bypass that puts flow through a sound section of housing, and a party that has read the maintenance record at C.6 Mezeora can work out which of the three is worth trying. The consequence is the largest lever in the setting and it points both ways: draining the lowest course is the first time in four hundred years anyone could reach what is down there, and *keeping the water over the salt* is precisely what the ninety feet of Old Rede at C.23 Zorethora exists to insist on.

Why they are apart: they are apart by three courses and four hundred years of ordinary institutional decay. The rod lived in the workshop because that is where the maintenance crews kept it between inspections, and the last inspection put it back on the rack. The sockets are unverdigrised because it was fitted and withdrawn so many times; the rack is undisturbed because the last man to use it expected to come back on schedule.
