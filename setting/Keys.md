Keys of Fellward

The Foundation Seal (*seal*) - found at B.7 The Old Watch Stump, opens at C.2 The Warded Gatehouse
Unlocks: The Sentinel-Niche at C.2 The Warded Gatehouse - fitted into the niche, it satisfies whatever rule the Old-Ward Sentinel still enforces, and crossing wakes nothing; without it, crossing wakes the construct in full. Old-tongue work, not keep-made, and nothing the commander who died defending this watchtower twelve years back should have been carrying - however he came by it, he never made it back to wherever it belonged before the tower fell under him, and it has sat buried with his sash ever since.
