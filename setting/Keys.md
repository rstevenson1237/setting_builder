Keys of Telar

A Brekar Passage-Mark (*token*) - found at A.3 Garrison Barracks (unlocks B.1 Sunk Bridge Ford)
A strip of red-dyed cloth knotted in Brekar's own three-loop hitch, cut from a captured rider caught trying to sell stolen goods under a false name rather than killed for it - the garrison kept the mark as a curiosity worth more intact than as evidence, and never thought to use it until now.
Unlocks: B.1 Sunk Bridge Ford - the Watch Rider feature. Presenting the mark waves the bearer through the toll once, unquestioned - Brekar's own three-loop hitch is honored on sight, whoever is carrying it. The rider reports it lost upstream the same evening, and it opens nothing at the ford a second time.

The Cairn Pilgrim-Token (*token*) - found at B.2 Boundary Cairns (unlocks D.17 Sealed Door)
A lead disc the size of a thumbnail, stamped on one face with a single line of Essath and worn smooth on the other by however long it was carried. The cairns were cut to teach the road's crossings what they demand, and a traveller who could not read took a stamped token away instead of the lesson. They are apart because nobody who left one at the cairn was going to the ziggurat - the tokens were for the waystones on the road, and the same line opens both. Whoever cut the copied script at the Sealed Door had seen a seal and not a token, and copied the wrong thing.
Unlocks: D.17 Sealed Door - the Essath Seal feature. The line on the token is the crossing-word the seal names and does not spell. Read aloud from the token at the threshold, the slab opens and stays open; crossing without it triggers the working instead. The token is not spent and opens any Essath crossing cut to the same line, which is most of the waystones on the road and none of the bindings deeper in Daghash.

The Coiled Figurine (*fitted object*) - found at D.10 Second Threshold (unlocks D.13 Chamber of the Coiled Serpent)
A fist-sized basalt figure cut in three coils, resting in a robing niche in the vestry where the priest-kings' procession took up what the rite needed. It is four times the size of the small fixed figure at the First Threshold and cut with the identical turn, which is the connection and the only one offered. They are apart because the rite carried it: the figure was fitted at the end of the procession and carried back to the vestry after, and the reign ended between one and the other.
Unlocks: D.13 Chamber of the Coiled Serpent - the Serpent Relief feature. Fitted into the empty socket where the relief's tail was never carved, it completes the coil and swings a floor panel open at the relief's base. Getting it there is the gate: D.10's own Bound Dead is bound against anything carried outward through the vestry's east archway, and the passage to D.13 runs through exactly that door. The south archway into the cellar is not that door.
