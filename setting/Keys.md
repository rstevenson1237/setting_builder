Keys of Telar

A Brekar Passage-Mark (*token*) - found at A.3 Garrison Barracks (unlocks B.1 Sunk Bridge Ford)
A strip of red-dyed cloth knotted in Brekar's own three-loop hitch, cut from a captured rider caught trying to sell stolen goods under a false name rather than killed for it - the garrison kept the mark as a curiosity worth more intact than as evidence, and never thought to use it until now.
Unlocks: B.1 Sunk Bridge Ford - the Watch Rider feature. Presenting the mark waves the bearer through the toll once, unquestioned - Brekar's own three-loop hitch is honored on sight, whoever is carrying it. The rider reports it lost upstream the same evening, and it opens nothing at the ford a second time.
