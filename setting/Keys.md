Keys of The Ashwrack Coast

The Silt-Buried Key (*key*) - found at C.14 The Silt Pool
A short iron key, teeth cut in an old, unfamiliar pattern, its bow worked into the shape of a coiling eel.
Unlocks: A sea-chest rumored to remain sealed aboard the wreck of the Sunken Spire (see `setting/History.md`, 40 years ago) - useless unless or until the Spire surfaces again, but distinctive enough that anyone familiar with Tallow Fleet salvage would recognize the eel-work as Spire-made.
