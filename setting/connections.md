# Region connections

Region to region. Binary and untyped: tiers 1 to 3 answer only whether two nodes
connect. Type is drawn at tier 4 and nowhere else.

The Reach reads seaward to inward, and the roads run that way, but the graph is
not a line. Two routes reach the Covenant Ground and two reach the Sunken Holds,
and the second of each pair is longer, colder or watched. No region is reachable
only by crossing the hardest region in the setting.
