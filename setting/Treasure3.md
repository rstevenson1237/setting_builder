Treasure Table III - Gems and Jewelry

*Average about 1000 cn and almost none of it weighs anything, which makes this the band
where a party stops having to decide what to leave behind. Several pieces carry a device.
A ring with three notches on it is worth more than its stones to the right person in
Torsgaard and is a very poor thing to be found holding by the wrong one.*
