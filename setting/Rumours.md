| 1 | The goblins hit the wagons at dusk from the wood's edge, and are gone before a patrol can reach them. | T | B.3 |
| 2 | The kobolds in the first cave number over a hundred, and pay tribute to the goblins above them. | P | E.2, F.3 - false half: count; the Gubak number about twenty, not a hundred |
| 3 | The goblins and the orcs share one chief and one cave. | F | G.6, H.6 - two chiefs, Wen and Hurk, in two caves |
