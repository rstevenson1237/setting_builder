Rumours of Brackvaen

| # | Rumour | T/P/F |
|---|--------|-------|
| 1 | The water in the eastern pans dropped four feet in one winter and has not come back up, and there is a cut stair at the bottom of it going down. | T |
| 2 | Siwapelu was a mine. The Odarath were down there taking something out. | P |
| 3 | Men who work below come back with the salt in them to the elbow, and after that they do not sicken and do not age the way other men do. | P |
| 4 | There are eleven Assize stones through the pans. There were twelve when they were cut. | P |
| 5 | The Gell Year was the sea shifting. It has shifted back, and the western pans would take salt again if anyone bothered to clear them. | F |
| 6 | The fen folk leave knotted cord on the channel stakes, and if you can read the knots you can cross Lindmarn dry-shod in any season. | T |
| 7 | Nobody in Torsgaard will cut or move a stone with the old letters on it, and none of them can tell you what the letters say. | T |
| 8 | A pan crew west of the causeway sank a man for stealing forty years ago and forgot to weight him properly. He is still down there and he is still where they put him. | P |
| 9 | The Assize sold something at the settlement sixty years ago that was not theirs to sell, and the families that lost pans that year know exactly what it was. | P |
| 10 | There is a thing in the deepest part of the gallery that has never come up and never will, and everything else down there is arranged around not waking it. | T |
| 11 | The fen folk hate the holding and have been picking off crews on the causeway since the founding. | F |
| 12 | An Odarath salt-hauler is still working somewhere under the pans - a made thing, stone and iron, walking a route to a wharf that has been underwater for four hundred years. | T |
| 13 | Salt cut from the eastern pans since the water dropped does not keep meat properly, and the traders downriver have started weighing it against the western stock. | F |
| 14 | Carry a bird down. When it stops singing you have already been down too long. | T |
| 15 | The Brined are organised. They know each other, they mark the doors of houses they have called at, and they are not going to let the stair be sealed. | T |
| 16 | Nessekire was not weather. Somebody opened the barrier, and the fen folk know who, because it was them. | P |
| 17 | There is a woman in Torsgaard who holds the salt-debt on half the holding and has never in her life left her own house. | P |
| 18 | A drowned man's boots are the cheapest boots in Brackvaen and the most expensive thing you will ever put on. | T |
| 19 | The Odarath left no graves anywhere in Brackvaen, in nine hundred years of holding it. | T |
| 20 | The stair was not uncovered. It was opened, from below, and the water went out through it. | P |
