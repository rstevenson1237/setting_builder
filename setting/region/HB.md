HB The Pyramid's Halls

Overview: Stone halls inside the pyramid's body, lit by sparse oil lamps, with captives' pens, a treasury and the snake people's living rooms. A party goes down the shaft for the treasury or the captives (Faction: Tuuzesh). The pens fill each week with captives from the lizard men and the pirates, and the bound men take the strongest down to the sanctum. If the pens are opened, captives pour into the halls and the watch turns out to drive them back; if the treasury is entered, a bell rings and every snake man comes.

Approach: The shaft, from the temple above, has a winch worked by bound men and a spiral stair beside it; a hundred feet down in the dark, past the cult's watch. The sanctum stair, from the god's sanctum, is a stair cut in the rock, lit by a brazier and watched by two snake men.

Prizes:
- HB.5 - Eight captives chained in barred pens; three bound men with whips and keys keep them.
- HB.8 - Four snake men coil in warm nest-beds; a hide bundle and a brood hoard lie in the bedding; a banded door opens on HB.9.
- HB.9 - A locked strongroom of heaped gold and chests (Hoard: The Cult Treasury); the Scale Seal opens its door.

Places:
- HB.1 - The shaft's foot; a chain hangs down from the winch.
- HB.2 - Four bound men turn the winch cranks in shifts.
- HB.3 - A lamp-lit guard room; a bell rope rings through the halls, and the Scale Seal hangs on a hook.
- HB.4 - A vaulted hall with carved serpents on its roof; a python coils above it.
- HB.6 - A bare cell with a drain, a hook-ring and stains.
- HB.7 - Six bound men and two snake men sleep in bunks.
- HB.10 - A cold cellar hung with sides of meat, some of it human.
- HB.11 - A priest in a green sash reads at a lectern and sells venom (Named Creature: Thazsheek).
- HB.12 - Racks of bronze blades; a chain behind the racks works a hammer.
- HB.13 - A steep stair mouth under a brazier; two snake men watch it.
- HB.14 - A dark cistern; the halls' water is drawn here.
