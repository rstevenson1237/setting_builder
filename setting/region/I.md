I The Drowned Galleon

Overview: A great ship's stern stands out of a reef gap, its hold half in air and half in water, its gilt rotted green. A party crosses the reef for the reef's best treasure (Faction: Nealeu). The mer people are stripping the hold's cargo to their caves, and a beast they keep guards what is left. If a party takes from the hold unasked, the mer people's song changes and the passages close behind its boat; when the tide turns, the hold floods to the second deck and the lower rooms are under water for a watch.

Approach: The reef gap, from the wreck coast, has the galleon's stern wedged across it and a mer guard in the water; it takes a boat with a pilot or a swim through the surf at low water, and a boat refused the gap is holed on the reef.

Entrances:
- I.1 - The stern deck, slanted and rotten with gilded rails, reached from C.9; a companionway goes down.

Prizes:
- I.2 - Four mer people strip the captain's cabin; a key hangs on a hook and a lead tube holds the captain's log.
- I.4 - A blind white eel as long as a longboat lies in the black water; a bilge door at its end takes the Captain's Key (Named Creature: Nealvaol).

Places:
- I.3 - A low gun deck of rotted planks that break underfoot.
- I.5 - Eight mer people load nets with cargo; bales of silk lie stacked.
- I.6 - A blackened galley; a cold stove and shelves of dishes.
- I.7 - A foul bilge; four crabs lie in the muck, and a bolted strongroom holds the strongbox (Hoard: The Galleon's Strongbox).
