I Pyramid Level 7 - The Altars of Making

Overview: A smaller level of altars and cells where the cult's chosen are made and unmade. A party comes for the keys the cult is hunting and to learn what is fed. The cult unmakes its chosen faster than it can make them. Freeing a chosen marks the party and opens every cell at once; fouling an altar brings the lieutenants' word within the hour.

Approach: The stair from the cult's halls (H.12) lands at I.1, beside a black hand on the wall and a pair of guards (Faction: Voxkroth). A party must show a mask or a mark; none is refused.

Prizes:
- I.3 - A black altar over a drain; six priests chant, zombies lie on the benches and a bowl holds coin (Treasure III, d20).
- I.9 - An iron-bound gate carved with a black hand; four guards and a chest (Treasure IV, d20); the gate takes a mask (Keys: The Chosen's Mask).

Places:
- I.1 - A narrow landing, a black hand on the wall.
- I.2 - Barred cells packed along a long hall; four guards walk it.
- I.4 - A warm black altar with runes cut round it; the runes wake on touch.
- I.5 - Six locked cells with muttering behind each door; wraiths.
- I.6 - Bones stacked to the roof; a crate of grave goods.
- I.7 - Masks, robes and paints; a gas trap on the paint shelf and a worn mask (Keys: The Chosen's Mask).
- I.8 - A channel of slow red water; ghouls wade it and a bottle holds a priest's notes (Lore: The Making of the Chosen).
- I.10 - Stone tubs of black water, frost on the rims; warm jugs by the door.
- I.11 - A bare room, its floor scored at the walls, a black hand by the iron door.
- I.12 - A railed pit of warm ash, a rake on the rail (Treasure II, d20).
- I.13 - Four black-curtained cells; three to five priests and a coffer (Treasure III, d20).
- I.14 - A narrow black passage with pocked walls; its middle flag sits proud.
