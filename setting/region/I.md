I Hobgoblin Hold

Kind: single holding
Overview: The Burgnaz's hold on the east wall: Hobgoblins behind a built wall, with barracks, an armoury and a warlord's hall. The warlord is a Named Creature, and the hold keeps one of the two keys to the shrine. A party comes for the key and the warlord's hoard. The Burgnaz want the shrine's favour. If the gnolls strike first, the whole hold marches out in step. If a party lays a grievance of the gnolls before the warlord, he may hear it.
Approach: One mouth opens on the ravine's east wall behind a timber and stone wall with a gate; a pair stands guard by day and night, relieved on the watch. A makers' passage runs from the barracks toward the gnolls' den, closed with an iron grate.
Conditions: Dressed makers' stone with timber braces, ceilings 10 feet and passages wide enough for a shield line. Light is oil lamps in niches. Sound carries along the straight halls to the gate. Footing is swept and dry. The air is clean, and a lit fire is smothered at once by a watch trained to do it.
