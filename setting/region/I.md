I Hobgoblin Hold

Overview: The Burgnaz's hold on the east wall, behind a built wall, with barracks, an armoury and a warlord's hall; it keeps one of the two keys to the shrine. A party comes for the key and the warlord's hoard. The Burgnaz want the shrine's favour. If the gnolls strike first, the whole hold marches out in step; if a party lays a grievance against the gnolls before the warlord, he may hear it.

Approach: One mouth behind the hobgoblin ledge (E.5): a timber and stone wall with a gate, spears planted in ranks before it, and a pair on guard day and night, relieved on the watch. A makers' passage runs from the barracks toward the gnolls' den, closed with an iron grate.

Conditions: Dressed makers' stone with timber braces, ceilings 10 feet and passages wide enough for a shield line. Light is oil lamps in niches. Sound carries along the straight halls to the gate. Footing is swept and dry. The air is clean, and a lit fire is smothered at once by a watch trained to do it.

Entrances:
- I.1 - A passage behind the wall, arrow slits in both its walls, barred from within.

Prizes:
- I.6 - Through a wooden door beyond the drill court: red banners marked with a white hand, a dais at the far end, and a table of flat stones chalked with the ravine's ledges and the gnoll den's mouths.

Places:
- I.2 - Ranked spear racks, lamps in wall niches and a red hand stencilled on the far wall.
- I.3 - Sixteen cots in two rows, a horn by the door, and at the far end an iron grate across a low passage, a gnoll stink blowing through it and a heap of stones beyond.
- I.4 - Behind a banded door barred from the court side: mail on frames floor to roof, and a chest with a heavy hasp at the back.
- I.5 - Beyond the armoury, sacks stencilled with a red hand in rows, swept aisles between.

Tables:
