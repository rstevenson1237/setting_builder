C The Wreck Coast

Overview: Beaches, sea cliffs and caves along a reef awash with broken hulls, a cove with a ship at anchor, and a tent camp on the sand. A party comes for the reef's wrecks, the mer people's trade or the pirates' anchorage (Faction: Nealeu; Faction: The Cove Pirates; Faction: The Treasure Hunters). The pirates and the mer people are at war over the cove's water, and the hunters survey the beach for a way to the pyramid. If the anchorage is cleared, the mer people open the reef's passages and name a wreck to dive; a boat that crosses the reef without a pilot is holed.

Approach: By the great gate at Puluni, a warden and a watch, the chiefs' word and a search to pass. By the western ridge trail from the hill jungle, red hand-prints at its head and a cave men's watch on the ridge at dusk. By the reef gap from the drowned galleon, a mer guard in the water. By the surf landing from the sea, a pirate lookout on the cliff and a hunters' sentry on the beach.

Terrain: Hard sand, scrub, sea cliffs and caves. On the beach at low tide a party walks 4 miles a watch on the hard sand; at high tide the beach is under water and the cliff path takes 2. Wading the reef at low water is the worst ground, with a Test of Constitution each watch against the coral's cuts, a Wound of Piercing on a failure. In a storm the surf breaks over the whole reef, no boat puts out and the wrecks are under water.

Ways:
- C.1 - The shore path runs from the gate A.1, 3.6 miles end to end: the first hard sand, the wave-cut arch with a path to C.8, the hunters' beach track to C.3 and the lookout cliff path to C.10. The pirates' cove C.2 lies beyond the dunes.

Landmarks:
- C.2 - A sheltered cove with a black ship at anchor and plank huts; twelve pirates drink round a beach fire.
- C.3 - Ordered canvas tents above a chalked beach; eight hunters and their leader survey with a chain.
- C.4 - Sea caves breathing surf, weed-hung mouths, a high wavering song; twelve mer people and an elder.
- C.5 - A listing merchantman stripped to its ribs on a sandbar, crabs buried beside it.
- C.6 - A burned brig beached with its mast down; the tide floods its hull at the turn.
- C.7 - A split hull weeded green on the reef shelf; a weed belt on a rib marks it as the mer people's.
- C.8 - A towering sea arch booming with surf; rubble heaps below it and loose stone falls.
- C.9 - A narrow surging gap in the reef, a faint song under the water, the galleon's stern across it.
- C.10 - A sheer cliff above the cove, a goat path to a lookout ledge.
