C The Ravine

Overview: A collection - ten households in the cut-stone halls and dug warrens under the
ravine, from a tribe's sentry gate by the ford down to a shrine of bound dead at the head.
A party comes for what the households have taken and hoarded, and for what the shrine
keeps below its deepest stair.

Approach: Two ways in from outside. The Sentry Gate (C.11), reached from the Old Ford
(B.5), is Gorzgur's front door: a sentry and a horn, answered from the Muster Yard. The
fissure under Hermit's Croft (B.8) comes out at the Cistern Approach (C.57), in the
Formless's reach, watched by nothing. Every other household is entered through another's
ground: two Khughik warrens open off the Sentry Gate (C.30, C.39), two off Hukkgur's
Second Entrance (C.29), and two only by crawls from their neighbours' bolt-holes (C.34 to
C.48, C.43 to C.53). The Shrine is entered from the Broken Stair warren's bolt-hole (C.52)
or the Drip Gallery warren's store nook (C.56), both into the Tribute Hall (C.2).

Conditions: The old halls are dressed stone squared to one measure, a bind-rune - a closed
loop around a single stroke - cut at their thresholds; ceilings about seven feet, passages
single file, no room to swing a spear. A household's own digging is rougher and a stride
wider, and the line between the two is plain underfoot. Dark everywhere but a household's
own lit halls. Sound carries the length of a dressed hall and dies within a few strides in
dug earth; at the Cistern Approach (C.57) and the Prisoner Hold (C.16) it does not carry at
all. Footing is broken in the old cutting and smooth in the new. Cold and wet throughout;
in some old passages the air moves uphill. An open flame in the Bat Reach warren (C.44 to
C.47) brings the roost out.

Inhabitants:
- Gorzgur, about thirty, and Vraz Red-Hand - C.11 to C.22; drilling, guarding, counting
  the take; want Hukkgur's ground and to give the shrine as little as they can; parley -
  take a prisoner for ransom, hear out an offer, and fight in formation when they are sure.
- Hukkgur, six, and Ghorn Bare-Knuckle - C.23 to C.29; brawling in the pit or sleeping it
  off; want a challenge worth answering; fight, bare-handed, and don't stop.
- Khughik, six warrens of five to eleven - C.30 to C.56; picking over scraps; want what a
  larger household leaves; flee, and don't come back the same day.
- Bound Thralls, two - the Tribute Hall (C.2); standing over the tribute; ignore anyone not
  taking tribute away without the Tribute Rod's mark.
- Shrine Wraith, one - the Rite Chamber (C.3); behind the altar; parley, in Grozzgur, with
  anyone who hasn't drawn a weapon.
- Ravine Restless, one - the Grave Niche of the Faithful (C.7); walking its round; fight,
  anyone living on the round.
- Sealed Warden, one - Warden's Threshold (C.5); standing in the arch; fight, anyone
  trying the shaft without the mark.
- The Bound Depth - the Deepest Chamber (C.10); still; ignore, until Kutuz is touched.
- Grave-Ghast, one - Gorzgur's Grave Niche (C.21); feeding; ignore, unless driven from the
  body.
- The Formless - the Flooded Hall (C.58); resting in the water; ignore, unless the water is
  disturbed.
- Cave Wing, one roost - Roost Overlook (C.46); roosting; ignore until light or noise
  reaches them, then pour out past the doorway.

Alarm: A fight or a forced door in dressed stone is heard by the nearest household. In
Gorzgur's halls a sentry's horn brings the six from the Muster Yard (C.14) in one action,
the dozen from the Warband Hall (C.12) with Vraz in two, and the sleepers from the
Sleeping Hall (C.18) in three; the Rival Watch Point (C.22) falls back to the Muster Yard.
Hukkgur posts no watch: a noise draws whoever is in the pit (C.24), and nobody else. A
Khughik warren goes silent, then scatters to its bolt-hole. The shrine's dead never leave
their posts for an alarm; when the Depth stirs, every bound thing in the ravine falls
still.

Places:
- C.1 to C.10 The Shrine - the dead; in from C.52 or C.56 to the Tribute Hall (C.2);
  holds the stacked tribute (C.2), the shrine's stores (C.8) and, below the Sealed Warden,
  Kutuz (C.10); every household owes it tribute.
- C.11 to C.22 Gorzgur Household - Vraz and his warband; in by the Sentry Gate (C.11);
  holds the household hoard (C.17), Vraz's own chest (C.13) and a captive (C.16); at odds
  with Hukkgur across C.22 and C.23.
- C.23 to C.29 Hukkgur Household - Ghorn and five more; in by the Broken Gate (C.23) from
  Gorzgur's watch point, or the Second Entrance (C.29); holds the pit's wagers (C.24) and a
  worked trophy (C.25); at odds with Gorzgur.
- C.30 to C.34 Khughik of the Silt Hollow - about ten; in from the Sentry Gate (C.11); a
  heap of scraps; a crawl behind its bolt-hole to the Broken Stair.
- C.35 to C.38 Khughik of the Cracked Vault - about six; in from C.29; scraps.
- C.39 to C.43 Khughik of the Old Cistern - about nine; in from C.11; its own water; a
  crawl behind its bolt-hole to the Drip Gallery.
- C.44 to C.47 Khughik of the Bat Reach - about six; in from C.29; scraps, under the roost.
- C.48 to C.52 Khughik of the Broken Stair - about eleven; in only by the crawl from C.34;
  its bolt-hole opens into the Tribute Hall (C.2) and its water nook drops to C.57.
- C.53 to C.56 Khughik of the Drip Gallery - about five; in only by the crawl from C.43;
  its store nook opens into the Tribute Hall (C.2).
- C.57 to C.60 The Weeping Cistern - the Formless; in by the fissure from B.8 or down from
  C.51; a cache under the water (C.58).

Situation: Every household's tribute goes up to the Tribute Hall, and Gorzgur, paying
least, is pressing Hukkgur for the crossing at C.22. Next: when Gorzgur takes a loss it
cannot hide - Vraz hurt, or its hoard (C.17) emptied - it calls a truce with Hukkgur, and
the two hold the crossing together against whoever did it. After that: if the stacked
tribute leaves the Tribute Hall (C.2), the Thralls leave their post for the first time and
walk down the Broken Stair (C.48 to C.52) after it.

Loot: The largest prize is Kutuz (C.10), in its niche behind the Bound Depth, below the
Sealed Warden, who passes only the mark on the Tribute Rod (C.2). The stacked tribute (C.2,
Treasure V) stands under two Thralls; Gorzgur's hoard (C.17, Treasure V) under one guard;
a cache under the Formless's water (C.58, Treasure V). An ordinary find is Treasure III.
The Toll House (A.14) buys all of it at the ledger's price and old-tongue coin at a tenth.
Bosk (A.13) pays double weight for raw stone from the outer cutting at C.30. Vraz wants
his chest (C.13) back and will trade the captive (C.16) for it.

Secrets:
- The Tribute Rod (C.2) carries the three-line mark the Sealed Warden (C.5) and the Bound
  Thralls answer to; shown first, it passes the threshold and the tribute both - nobody
  living knows it; brought out by showing it.
- Kutuz is what holds the Bound Depth to its task: touched, the Depth acts until Kutuz
  leaves the chamber and not after; carried out of the ravine, every bound thing in it
  falls still for good - nobody living knows it; brought out by the Glyph Primer's missing
  lower half, or by trying.
- The crawls behind the Silt Hollow and Old Cistern bolt-holes (C.34, C.43) reach the
  Broken Stair and Drip Gallery warrens, and through them the Tribute Hall - the Khughik
  know; brought out by the draught through the loose slab.
- The Shrine Wraith (C.3) was the priest Ellard (B.3) served; told his apology in
  Grozzgur, it lets the teller take from the altar basin unharmed - Ellard knows who it
  was; brought out by carrying his word.
