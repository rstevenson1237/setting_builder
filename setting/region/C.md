C The Hollow Cairn

Overview: The Cairn is the module's first true dungeon - a compact, escalating DANGEROUS site meant to be run as a countdown: each failed roll ticks the Danger table down, and the barrow grows more aware and more hostile the longer the party lingers. It exists to deliver Vael-era history, an undead threat, and a tangible link back to Saltmere's missing bell.

Ambiance: Cold, still air heavy with salt-mineral dust; carved stone passages half-collapsed and packed with silt from old flooding; faint phosphorescence clinging to damp walls; and an intermittent, directionless tolling that seems to come from somewhere deeper in.

Layout: A compact barrow of interconnected burial chambers radiating from a central sunken hall, roughly a dozen rooms across two descending levels. It is reachable from the Salt-Scrub Hills (B) either through its exposed entrance or the unmapped sinkhole passage.

Features: The central sunken hall with its dry, cracked-open basin (once flooded); sealed burial niches lining the passages; a collapsed stair to a lower level that floods at certain tides; and old iron fittings rusted to dust wherever a debt, per the coast's custom, was left unpaid.

Dangers: The Cairn is restless, not mindless. Disturbance - noise, and especially anything resembling a bell - draws its dead toward the source, and the longer intruders linger the more of the barrow wakes. It does not forget an unpaid debt among the party any more than the sea does.

Creatures: Drowned Skeletons (Bestiary) rise from the burial niches in twos and threes. A single Bell-Wight (Bestiary) anchors the lower level, drawn utterly to any bell, chime, or ringing sound the party carries or makes.

Secrets: A sealed niche in the sunken hall holds the missing bronze diving-bell itself, placed there generations ago as tribute rather than stolen recently - Saltmere's current theft rumor is a coincidence layered atop an older truth. A passage beneath the cracked basin connects to the sinkhole from the Hills (B), offering a second way in or out.

Treasure: Grave goods in the sealed niches - worked coral jewelry, a scattering of old coin, and two intact widow-glass bottles - alongside the diving-bell itself as the barrow's centerpiece prize.

Tables: DANGEROUS - d6 Danger Table, counting down from 6 with each failed Difficulty roll.
6. A distant tolling echoes through the passages - something has noticed.
5. Dust and grit sift down from the ceiling; the ground trembles faintly.
4. A pair of Drowned Skeletons rise from a nearby niche.
3. The air turns bitterly cold, and breath mists in unnatural silence.
2. The Bell-Wight begins to move toward the party's position.
1. The barrow's dead rise in force from every niche within reach.
