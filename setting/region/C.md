C The Ninefold Hollow

Overview: The collapsed workings of the ore vein that swallowed seven of the nine prospectors who breached it twenty-three years ago, reached by a half-collapsed mineshaft within the Yrsk Wold Fringe. Older, Marrgath-cut passages underlie the Concern's crude tunnels, and the referee should run the dungeon as a place actively unsettled by its own history - what's down there has had over two decades to become something, and hasn't finished.

Ambiance: Cold, still air thick with wet stone and a faint mineral tang that sharpens the deeper one goes. Water drips constantly somewhere out of sight, and sound carries strangely - a voice at one end of a passage can seem to answer from the other. The cold deepens unnaturally near the older, Marrgath-cut sections, well past what the stone alone accounts for.

Architecture: The upper workings are rough-hewn rock shored with rotting Concern timber, hasty and poorly maintained. Below them, older passages are dressed stone cut with a precision no fringe mason matches, every archway carved with the same recurring spiral chisel-mark found on the standing stones above. Concern tunnels run about 7 feet high and shoulder-width; the Marrgath-cut passages open wider, closer to 9 feet, with room enough for two abreast.

Layout: A vertical mineshaft descends into a branching network of galleries - the shallow Concern workings above, the older Marrgath excavation below, and the breached ore vein itself at the deepest point reached. No real time applies; the region runs entirely on the Danger table's countdown, triggered by failed Difficulty rolls rather than clock time.

Features: The collapsed shaft-head where the original crew's gear still lies scattered; flooded lower galleries that block certain routes without care; the breached ore vein, still faintly warm to the touch for reasons no one has explained.

Dangers: Every failed Difficulty roll ticks the Danger table down from 6 - this is the hollow's actual clock, not any real-world hour. Sections of the upper workings remain genuinely unstable and can collapse under careless weight or noise.

Creatures: A Deep Crawler is known to move through the lowest galleries near the vein. A Marrgath Watch-Stone stands dormant somewhere in the older passages, guarding whatever the Marrgath thought worth guarding down here. Cutter's Blight and a Fringe Wight - remnants of past breaches gone wrong - haunt the middle galleries.

Secrets: What actually happened to the seven prospectors who didn't come out is discoverable here, in fragments, rather than stated outright. The connection between the Marrgath's original excavation and their disappearance a century and a half earlier is buried in the lowest, oldest galleries.

Treasure: Scavenged gear and coin off Treasure Tables I and II near the shaft-head and old crew's remains; the ore vein itself and any Marrgath-worked goods off Tables III and V in the deeper galleries.

Tables: d6 Danger Table (counts down from 6 on each failed Difficulty roll)
6. A support beam groans; dust and grit sift down, but the way stays open.
5. A section of ceiling gives way behind the party, cutting off the route they came by.
4. Something in the dark answers a voice raised too loud, and begins moving toward it.
3. Flooded lower galleries rise unexpectedly, forcing an unplanned route or a cold, blind swim.
2. The Marrgath Watch-Stone stirs, having judged the party's presence a disturbance worth answering.
1. The Deep Crawler is now actively hunting whoever triggered this countdown, and knows exactly where they are.
