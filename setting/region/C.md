C The Dark Wood

Overview: Old forest north of the keep, thick enough to hide a camp from a hundred yards, with the ravine at its far end. A party comes to reach the ravine, to hunt, or to find what the outlaws hide. The Wood Outlaws' trade has failed with the convoys, and they now stop any party on the trail for a toll: a party that pays goes on untouched, and one that refuses meets arrows from the trees at the next bend. The Zhakuk's Wolves run the wood's edge, and the Zurnaz walk the trail by night to collect the tithe and take the last of any party camped on it. Ansel the Hermit lives off the trail.

Approach: Entered at the keep's postern on the south, at the mere's north shore on the west, and at the ravine's mouth on the north. The forest trail runs 12 miles from the postern to the ravine: three watches.

Terrain: forest - On the trail a watch covers 4 miles; off it, 2. Sight carries 30 yards, and a FAILURE on Navigate off the trail loses the party for a watch; fog or a storm adds Disadvantage. Forage or Hunt succeeds on a 4 or more within a mile of the trail, at Disadvantage deeper in. At night nothing is seen beyond a lit torch; a fire is seen at 200 yards, but the canopy hides its smoke.

Ways:
- C.1 - North from the postern (A.2) to the ravine's mouth (E.1), single file between old pines and oaks. Its stops in order: the mere path, west to C.6; the standing stones, C.5; the hare's foot post, where a track runs west to C.2; the hermit's path, east to C.3; the old watchtower, C.4; and the ravine mouth.

Landmarks:
- C.2 - Lean-tos round a cookfire under a lip of rock, a hare's foot nailed to each post; only woodsmoke on the wind gives it away, and a second trail comes up to it from the mere path.
- C.3 - A turf hut, bean rows and a well inside a thorn fence, a bell on the gate.
- C.4 - A roofless tower of makers' stone broken at 15 feet, ivy-choked, its stair ending in rubble after the third turn and a cellar hole at its foot.
- C.5 - Seven grey pillars twice a man's height round a waist-high altar-stone, soot-smeared sticks pushed into the earth to the south.
- C.6 - A rotting jetty in the reeds with a pole boat, and firm ground through the bog to the causeway (D.1).

Places:
- C.7 - Beyond the camp, up a climbing game trail: turf cut over a pit under an uprooted oak, thorn cuttings laid between, a hare's foot nailed to the trunk at head height.

Tables:
d6 Encounter
1. Four outlaws with bows step from the trees and ask 10 cn a head.
2. A hunter's bell sounds, and a deer crashes through the undergrowth.
3. Wolves howl at dusk; five circle the party's fire.
4. A bugbear's soot handprint on a tree at head height, still wet.
5. The hermit's smoke rises to the north-east; Ansel is at a snare.
6. A storm breaks; paths turn to mud and visibility falls to a few paces.
