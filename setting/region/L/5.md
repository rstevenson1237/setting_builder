L.5 **The Minotaur's Lair** (High) - *high, bare, stinking*
A high bare cave round a stinking **nest** of rags and bone, deep gouges in the walls, a stone **stair** climbing from the far end. **Tanglehorn** lies on the nest, listening.
*Cave 50 ft by 40 ft, ceiling 25 ft. Tanglehorn is on the nest half the time and in the passages the rest.*
**Tanglehorn the Keeper:** Charges any light or sound and does not stop to parley (Named Creature: Tanglehorn the Keeper).
**Token:** Bronze, stamped with the makers' sign, hung on a chain round its neck (Keys: The Vault Key).
**Plaque:** Bronze, set in the floor beside the nest, cut deep with the makers' script (Lore: The Keeper's Charge).
**Nest:** Rags, bones and stripped sacking, heaped waist-high.
**Stair:** Makers' stone, wide and worn, climbing north from the cave's end.
**Gouges:** Horn-marks in the walls at the height of a man's shoulders.
**Exits:** archway, south end of the cave -> L.3 The Statue Hall, open, south-west end of the cave -> L.4 The Collection, archway, north end of the cave, up a stair -> M.13 The Passage Landing
