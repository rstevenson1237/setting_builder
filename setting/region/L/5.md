L.5 **The Bone Gallery** (Medium) - *whispering, stacked, narrow*

Skulls are stacked to the roof on either side of a path, their jaws moving. Whispers fill the gallery in the black speech.

*A gallery 30 feet square, the skulls of those the demon has fed on. A party that stops to listen to one hears its death (Test of Sanity, 1d).*

**The Skulls:** Thousands, their jaws shifting as a party passes.
**The Path:** Bare stone between them, worn by the cult's feet.

**Exits:** an open passage -> L.1 The Threshold, an open passage to the floor -> L.2 The Feeding Floor
