L.3 **The Statue Hall** (Medium) - *round, carved, silent*
A round hall fifty feet across, a ring of stone **statues** standing round its edge, six archways leading off. The **lamp-bearer** at the centre holds an unlit lamp in one raised arm.
*Hall 50 ft across, ceiling 20 ft. Silence lies unbroken; no echo comes back.*
**Statues:** Ten makers in long robes, faces worn blank, all alike but for the lamp-bearer.
**Lamp-Bearer:** Stands at the hall's centre with a stone lamp in its raised arm and a scuffed ring on the floor round its base; turned by the arm, it lights a line along the floor that marks the maze's true way, through the archway to the lair.
**Archways:** Six, identical, none marked.
**Exits:** open, south-west archway, 300 feet -> L.2 The Winding Passages, archway, north archway, 80 feet -> L.5 The Minotaur's Lair
