L.3 **The Offering Pit** (High) - *deep, red, steaming*

A deep red pit steams in a round room, bubbling. A ledge runs round its rim.

*A round room 40 feet across and 30 high. The cynidiceans' dead are poured here; the demon is fed every day.*

**The Ledge:** Slick and cracked; stepping on it drops a party into the pit (Test of Constitution, 3d, Crushing).
**The Dead:** Stir in the red (Hostile, 1, Bestiary : Mummy). The bubbling thickens when anyone nears the rim.
**The Chest:** A drowned chest holds a book (Magical Tome: The Book of the Binding).

**Exits:** an open passage -> L.2 The Feeding Floor, a stone door -> L.4 The Demon's Seat
