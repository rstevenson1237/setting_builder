L.2 **The Winding Passages** (Medium) - *identical, dark, turning*
Narrow **passages** of smooth stone, cut to one pattern, turn left and right, each turn the twin of the last, the floor worn glassy. Chalk **scratches** mark the first turn.
*Passages 6 ft wide, ceiling 10 ft. A bellow here seems to come from every side.*
**Passages:** Miles of them, no landmark but the turns; a party chalking or cording its way has an easier time of it (Local Procedure: Finding the Way Through the Maze).
**Scratches:** Chalk marks and a frayed cord end at the first turn, left by those who did not come back.
**False Flag:** One flag in a passage floor sits loose over a pit; its edges are clean of dust, and a tread on it drops the walker onto stakes below (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing).
**Exits:** archway, south end -> L.1 The Barred Door, open, north-east, 300 feet -> L.3 The Statue Hall, open, north-west, 400 feet -> L.4 The Collection
