L.2 **The Feeding Floor** (Medium) - *stained, wide, slick*

A wide stained floor slopes west, slick with offerings. Dripping and then breathing carry in the dark.

*A hall 50 feet by 60 and 30 high (The Demon Is Invited). The cult kneels here to feed the floor; every cultist on the level comes at once if the demon is wounded. The lip of the offering ledge crumbles into steam under whoever steps on it (Test of Constitution, 2d, Fire).*

**The Cultists:** Kneel and feed the floor (Hostile, 8, Bestiary : Cultist), tipping what the altars above send down toward the pit. They want the party fed to the demon.
**The Floor:** Slick, sloping west to the pit.

**Exits:** an open passage -> L.1 The Threshold, an open passage -> L.3 The Offering Pit, an archway -> L.4 The Demon's Seat, an open passage between skulls -> L.5 The Bone Gallery, an open passage onto the ledge -> L.6 The Offering Ledge
