L.2 **The Feeding Floor** (Medium) - *stained, wide, slick*

A wide stained floor slopes west, slick with offerings. Dripping and then breathing carry in the dark.

*A hall 60 feet by 80 and 30 high (The Demon Is Invited). The cult kneels here to feed the floor; every cultist on the level comes at once if the demon is wounded.*

**The Cultists:** Kneel and feed the floor (Hostile, 8, Faction : Voxkroth). They want the party fed to the demon.
**The Floor:** Slick, sloping west to the pit.

**Exits:** an open passage, south, 80 feet -> L.1 The Threshold, an open passage, west, 80 feet -> L.3 The Offering Pit, an archway, north, 90 feet -> L.4 The Demon's Seat
