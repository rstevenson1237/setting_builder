L.1 **The Threshold** (Low) - *warm, thick, glowing*

A warm threshold glows faintly, two wraiths standing motionless on either side. A pulse can be felt through the floor.

*A landing 20 feet square and 30 high. The wraiths do not move until a party has crossed the threshold, which it must do with no light.*

**The Wraiths:** Stand at the threshold (Hostile, 1, Bestiary : Wraith). They bar the way to a party with a light.
**The Mark:** A black hand on the wall (Faction : Voxkroth).

**Exits:** an open passage, north, 80 feet -> L.2 The Feeding Floor, up a stair, south, 40 feet -> K.5 The Last Threshold
