L.1 **The Barred Door** (Low) - *heavy, barred, cold*
Behind a heavy **door** of iron-bound oak lies a bare cold vestibule of cut stone, a **bar** resting in iron brackets across the door. An archway opens north into dark passages.
*Vestibule 15 ft square, ceiling 10 ft. The door is locked on both sides.*
**Door:** Locked on this side as well as the other; the lock's slot is cut with notches, and the bar will not lift until the lock is opened (Keys: The Notched Bone).
**Bar:** Oak, thick as a thigh, held in brackets of black iron.
**Exits:** banded, locked, south end of the vestibule -> E.8 The Maze Door, archway, north end of the vestibule -> L.2 The Winding Passages
