Layout: The door (L.1) opens in the cliff's foot at the ravine's head, entered from the maze door (E.8), with the winding passages (L.2) 30 feet north. The statue hall (L.3) is 300 feet north-east along the passages and the collection (L.4) 400 feet north-west; the lair (L.5) is 80 feet north of the hall and 90 feet north-east of the collection, with a stair 120 feet north to the shrine's passage landing (M.13).

Places:
- L.1 The Barred Door - a locked oak door; the lock's slot is cut with notches.
- L.2 The Winding Passages - identical turns, a false flag over a pit.
- L.3 The Statue Hall - ten statues and a lamp-bearer.
- L.4 The Collection - picked-over packs and gear.
- L.5 The Minotaur's Lair - Tanglehorn the Keeper, a plaque, and the stair up.

Inhabitants: Tanglehorn the Keeper, one Minotaur, in the lair half the time and in the passages the rest.

Alarm: Forcing the door at L.1 raises it. Tanglehorn hears at once from wherever it is and charges any light in the passages; the Zurnaz at the dry fork are told, and Mort sends two bugbears to the door in six turns.

Loot: The largest prize is the shrine's vault key, a bronze token on Tanglehorn's neck chain at L.5; Treasure II lies in the collection at L.4. The key is not sold.

Secrets:
- A false flag in a passage floor drops the walker onto stakes - the Minotaur; the clean edges of the flag (L.2) bring it out.
- Turning the lamp-bearer's arm lights the maze's true way along the floor - the makers; the scuffed ring round its base (L.3) brings it out.

Leads:
- Rumour 11 - L.5 Token.
