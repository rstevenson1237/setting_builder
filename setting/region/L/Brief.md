Layout: The maze has one door, the maze door (E.8) in the cliff's foot at the ravine's head, 1,760 yards up from the ravine's mouth and 20 yards west of the shrine stair: a door of iron-bound oak 7 feet high in a frame carved with script, locked, no light showing at its edges. The barred door (L.1) lies 10 feet north of it, a vestibule 15 feet square with a ceiling of 10 feet, the door barred on this side by an oak bar in iron brackets and locked on both sides, and an archway opening north into the dark passages. The maze is makers' cut stone, passages 6 feet wide and ceilings 10 feet, every turn alike; no light shows but what the party carries, sound loses its direction so that a bellow seems to come from all sides, the footing is dry and worn smooth, the air is cold and dry, and a lit fire gives light but no smoke.

The Minotaur's lair (L.5), the one High location, lies 80 feet north of the statue hall (L.3) through an archway, about 210 feet east and 320 north of the barred door (L.1). It is a high bare cave 50 feet by 40 with a ceiling of 25 feet round a stinking nest of rags and bone heaped waist-high, deep horn-marks in the walls at the height of a man's shoulders, a bronze plaque set in the floor beside the nest and a wide stone stair climbing north from the far end 120 feet to the shrine's passage landing (M.13); Tanglehorn the Keeper (Named Creature: Tanglehorn the Keeper) lies on the nest half the time, a bronze token on a chain round its neck (Keys: The Vault Key).

The winding passages (L.2) lie 30 feet north of the barred door (L.1): narrow passages of smooth stone, the floor worn glassy, chalk scratches and a frayed cord end at the first turn, and a party can notice a flag in one floor whose edges are clean of dust (Local Procedure: Finding the Way Through the Maze). The statue hall (L.3) lies 300 feet north-east of the passages (L.2): a round hall 50 feet across with a ceiling of 20 feet, ten statues in long robes ringing its edge, six identical archways and, at the centre, a lamp-bearer holding an unlit lamp in a raised arm with a scuffed ring on the floor round its base. The collection (L.4) lies 410 feet north-west of the passages (L.2): a chamber 60 feet by 20 with a ceiling of 12 feet, heaps of packs, bones and bright scraps along both walls and a broad trampled path through the dust that leads 500 feet east to the lair (L.5).

Inhabitants: Tanglehorn the Keeper (Named Creature: Tanglehorn the Keeper), one Minotaur, lies on the nest at L.5 listening half the time and wanders the passages the rest, hostile, charging any light or sound and never stopping to parley, wanting intruders found and driven to the nest.

Alarm: Forcing the door at L.1 raises it. Tanglehorn hears at once from wherever it is and charges any light in the passages; the Zurnaz at the dry fork are told, and Mort sends two bugbears to the door in six turns.

Loot: The maze holds one key and what the dead left. The largest prize is the shrine's vault key (Keys: The Vault Key), a bronze token on the chain round Tanglehorn's neck at L.5, with Tanglehorn over it; Treasure II lies in the heaps of the collection at L.4, and the plaque in the lair's floor holds the Keeper's charge (Lore: The Keeper's Charge). The key is not sold, and the Moneychanger (A.7) buys the rest at 80% of value.

Secrets:
- A false flag in a passage floor drops the walker onto stakes - the Minotaur; the clean edges of the flag (L.2) bring it out.
- Turning the lamp-bearer's arm lights the maze's true way along the floor - the makers; the scuffed ring round its base (L.3) brings it out.
