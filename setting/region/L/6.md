L.6 **The Offering Ledge** (Low) - *narrow, hot, crumbling*

A crumbling ledge hangs over the red pit, steam hissing up through its cracks. A stair goes down its side.

*A ledge 20 feet square from which the dead are poured into the pit. Its lip gives way from the feeding floor's side (L.2).*

**The Lip:** Cracked, steaming, edged with old stains.
**The Stair:** Narrow steps cut down to the pit's edge.

**Exits:** an open passage onto the floor -> L.2 The Feeding Floor, a stair down -> L.3 The Offering Pit
