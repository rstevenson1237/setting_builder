L.4 **The Collection** (Low) - *heaped, dry, ransacked*
**Heaps** of packs, bones and bright scraps lie along both walls of a long stone chamber, every pack torn open and picked over. Dust lies thick on everything but a broad trampled path through the middle.
*Chamber 60 ft by 20 ft, ceiling 12 ft. The trampled path leads 500 ft east to the lair.*
**Heaps:** Hundreds of things taken from the dead, helms and scabbards and shattered lanterns, sorted by shine (Treasure II, d20).
**Bones:** Human mostly, picked clean and set in piles by size.
**Exits:** open, east end of the chamber, 500 feet -> L.5 The Minotaur's Lair, open, south-east end of the chamber, 410 feet -> L.2 The Winding Passages
