L.4 **The Demon's Seat** (High) - *raised, black, humming*

A black raised seat shaped like a throne stands on a dais, a deep voice almost forming words. The hall is vast.

*A hall 40 feet square and 30 high (The Demon Is Invited). The end of the descent.*

**Ghulox:** Sits the seat (Named Creature: Ghulox). It speaks to one who carries a chosen's mask, and its presence costs a Test of Sanity each turn.
**The Chain:** Under the seat lies a chain of white bone (Unique Treasure: The Binding Chain).

**Exits:** an archway, south, 90 feet -> L.2 The Feeding Floor, a stone door, south-west, 120 feet -> L.3 The Offering Pit
