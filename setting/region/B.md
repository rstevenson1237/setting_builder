B Marfen

Overview: Marfen is the marsh and river country the old road crosses between Tolkar and
Velar, dry ground threaded between standing water, and it has never been settled because
nobody has ever held the ford long enough to build on it. Crossing costs either the
week-long detour or Brekar's own toll, in coin or cargo, and there is no third way over the
river within a day's travel either direction. Marfen is the only ground in Telar where all
three kingdoms' claims still physically overlap, marked by cairns none of them has moved in
a generation.

Ambiance: Reeds and standing water under a flat grey sky, mosquito whine rising at dusk, mud
that holds a print for days after it is made. Old road paving shows through in patches where
the silt hasn't swallowed it yet, and what little is built here - the boundary cairns, the
old causeway's kerbstones - is fieldstone rather than anything worked fine.

Terrain: Marsh and slow river country. The old causeway is the only guaranteed dry footing;
leaving it means wading, and the mud is deep enough in places to lose a boot or a wheel.
Fording anywhere but the causeway's own crossing or a known ford costs an extra action and
risks what waits in the deep water.

Foraging: Reed-root is bland but always findable, raw or cooked. Waterfowl can be taken
along the marsh's open pools. Silver dace run in the slower stretches of the river and are
easy to net. Bog iron can be drawn from the mud in places - low-grade, worth more to a
tanner than a smith. None of it carries any healing value beyond what it plainly is.

Layout: The old road runs east-west along a raised causeway through the marsh, with the Sunk
Bridge ford at roughly its center. The boundary cairns sit about half a mile north of the
causeway, where the three kingdoms' claims meet. The burial mounds lie in drier ground some
two miles south of the causeway. Daghash's hills rise about four miles north, past the
cairns. An action here costs four hours - a move between neighbouring landmarks, a search, a
forage, a tracking attempt, or making camp.

Features: The causeway is the only dry crossing besides the ford itself. At low water, the
drowned temple downriver of the ford bares its upper stones above the surface - the two
trading companies that went in after them fifteen years ago never came back out. The old
approach road to the crown seat has gone back to weed north of the cairns and carries no
marker anymore.

Dangers: The marsh is only as dangerous as carelessness makes it - the mud holds a wagon
wheel as readily as a boot. Brekar keeps a rider on the causeway in sight of the ford at all
hours; anyone crossing without paying is seen before they are a hundred yards out. Man-Ape
ground begins somewhere past the cairns and carries no border marker of its own - it is
found by finding them.

Creatures: A Ford-Eel holds the deep water at the ford and at any crossing besides the
causeway. A Grey Wolf pack ranges the whole of Marfen and has been seen shadowing travelers
on the causeway itself. Man-Apes hold the ground past the cairns and answer the iron-truce
the same as anywhere else in Telar. Barrow-Kin tend the mounds south of the causeway and
have not approached anyone in living memory. At least one Drowned Thing has never left the
flooded temple downriver. (setting/Bestiary.md)

Factions: Brekar holds the Sunk Bridge ford and the causeway approaching it from both sides
- the one piece of ground in Marfen anyone actually controls. Neither the Nine Wagons nor
the Serpent Cult holds ground here, though the Wagons watch closely whether Brekar's grip on
the ford loosens.

Secrets: The old approach road north of the cairns still reaches the crown seat that three
armies are now said to watch from a distance - nobody currently uses it. What is actually
inside the one burial mound nobody has opened is Marfen's own unanswered question. The
drowned temple keeps whatever took the two trading companies fifteen years ago.

Treasure: What the marsh and the ruins give up leans on setting/Treasure1.md (scavenged
finds) and setting/Treasure5.md (what the mounds and the drowned temple may cache).

Tables: d6 Encounter, rolled on each failed Difficulty roll.
1. A Brekar rider intercepts the party on the causeway to collect the toll before they've
   reached the ford.
2. A Ford-Eel takes something left too near the water's edge, seen only as a ripple closing
   over it.
3. A Grey Wolf pack shadows the party's line of travel for a stretch before peeling off.
4. Knuckle-prints beside a footprint, and bark stripped from a tree well above head height -
   Man-Apes have passed this way recently.
5. A carrion vulture flock lifts off ahead of the party, circling over something already
   dead.
6. A boundary patrol from one of the three kingdoms stops the party to ask which crown's
   ground they think they're standing on.
