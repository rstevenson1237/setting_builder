B The Borderland

Overview: Cleared verge beside the road nearest the keep, true wood past it, fen off the
road's east side and broken high rock to the southwest. Traders too poor to wait for an
escort, a raiding band, a hermit and scouts from the ravine all cross it, and nobody holds
it.

Approach: From Greywatch's Outer Gate (A.1) the road runs to the Old Ford (B.5), then on to
the ravine's Sentry Gate (C.11). Gate to ford is one move on the road, ford to ravine one
more; a party leaving at first light can be at the ravine by noon and back by dusk if
nothing stops it.

Terrain: Forest and swamp. On the road, a move to the next Landmark along it is one action.
Off the road through the wood, a move is two actions, each with its own Difficulty roll.
The fen bears weight only on the Mire Path, which runs from the road to the Fen Mound
(B.4); off it, every action is a Test of Fate, failed as Held. A failed Difficulty roll
off the road with neither the road nor the high rock in sight also loses the party: its
next action goes to finding the road again, or to climbing the high rock above the Lynx's
Den (B.1), from which the whole region can be seen. After dusk the wood falls silent all
at once, and every night move rolls the Difficulty roll twice. A lit fire in camp at night
fails its Difficulty roll on a 1 or a 2. After rain the Old Ford is too deep to wade, and
crossing means the long way upstream: two actions.

Inhabitants:
- Road Raiders, four to eight - work the road between the gate and the ford, camp at
  Raiders' Hollow (B.2) with two left minding it; hunting; state a price, take a
  surrender, and leave the survivors the road home.
- Ravine Lynx, one - ranges the broken high rock and the ravine's outer mouths, denned at
  B.1; watching; breaks off the moment a fight costs it more than it takes.
- Herd-Biter, one to three - root along the keep's fences and the tree line at night;
  avoiding; charge whoever is closest if already feeding.
- Gorzgur, two to four scouts - cross to the far bank of the Old Ford (B.5) before dawn,
  most often the morning after a market day at the keep; watching; withdraw once seen.
- Ellard, alone - at Hermit's Croft (B.3); trading; hears out only a buyer.
- Barrow Ward, one - the Fen Mound's ring (B.4) and nowhere else; watching; rises against
  anyone digging or cutting the mound's soil, and ignores everyone else.

Places:
- B.1 Ravine Lynx's Den - an overhang in the broken rock a mile southwest of the gate;
  bones below it, and the lynx.
- B.2 Raiders' Hollow - a cut-bank screened by a brush blind, half a mile north of the
  road; the band's camp, and what it took off the road.
- B.3 Hermit's Croft - forge smoke a mile past the last farm; Ellard's blades.
- B.4 The Fen Mound - a mound in the fen east of the road, its grass flattened in a ring;
  a collapsed opening on its downhill side.
- B.5 The Old Ford - the road's crossing, a bell on a dead tree; the way to the ravine.
- B.6 Waystone Cairn - a cairn where two faint paths cross between the ford and the fen.
  Unstocked: the referee's to fill.

Situation: The raiders have seen the Gorzgur scouts at the ford and have started leaving
the ford alone on market mornings. Next: when the band loses two more of its number - to
the garrison or to a party - it strikes a bargain with Gorzgur and the two share the
road, raiding in larger numbers with red paint among the masks. After that: once the
bargain has held a month, Gorzgur camps at the Old Ford (B.5) and rings its bell for
anyone crossing, at a price.

Loot: Fen-reed, 1 cp a bundle at Trade Row. Numbing moss from the high rock, 2 sp a bundle
to Anse at the Healer's Tent (A.11), who uses it untested. A lynx pelt, 5 gp at the Toll
House (A.14); a Herd-Biter's hide, 5 sp. Ellard sells a ravine-stone blade for 60 gp, or
gives one for an errand. Finds out here draw Treasure I and Treasure IV; the raiders'
cache at B.2 is Treasure IV and whatever carts they stopped; old-tongue coin dropped along
the road is worth a tenth of its weight at the gate.

Secrets:
- Ellard went down to the shrine with its priesthood six years ago and walked out when
  they began binding the dead; the priest he served is the Shrine Wraith at the Rite
  Chamber (C.3), and his apology is for it - Ellard knows; brought out by trust bought at
  his forge, or by the cubby in the Shrine Approach (C.1), which holds his carved mark.
- A fissure under Ellard's root cellar (B.8) runs down to the Cistern Approach (C.57),
  past every household's watch - Ellard knows; brought out by the steady cold draught
  through the cellar hatch, or by his trust.
- The Barrow Ward rises only against digging or cutting the mound's soil; walking in
  through the collapse (B.4) breaks nothing, and it lets the party pass to the Sunken
  Vault (B.7) - nobody living knows; brought out by entering without a spade.
- The Gorzgur scouts at the ford take word from Pell (A.4) - Pell and the scouts know;
  brought out by being on the far bank at dawn after a market day.

Tables:
d6 Encounter, rolled on each failed Difficulty roll
1. A Road Raider band has the way blocked ahead and states its price.
2. A Ravine Lynx watches from cover at range, and breaks off if challenged directly.
3. A Herd-Biter is already feeding nearby and charges if approached.
4. Fresh raider sign crosses the path - hoofprints at an angle no trader's cart would
   take.
5. The wood goes silent all at once, well before dusk.
6. Something on the high rock breaks line of sight the moment it is noticed.
d6 Between Landmarks, anywhere off the keyed map
1. A snare line, freshly reset, with a hare still kicking in it.
2. A dead raider face down off the road, one ear cut away.
3. A boundary stone knocked flat, old-tongue letters worn past reading, fresh earth under
   it.
4. A cold camp: a ring of fire-stones, a fallen lean-to, a mess tin left behind.
5. A broken axle in the ruts, the load gone, drag marks toward the wood.
6. A charcoal burner's stack smouldering, with nobody tending it.
