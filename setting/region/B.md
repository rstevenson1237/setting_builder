B The Wagon Road

Overview: Open grass country on either side of the realm's road, which runs west from the keep's main gate, 12 miles to the map's edge and on to the realm. The Carters' Guild runs a convoy over it on the keep's wagon days. The Keep's Soldiers patrol its first stretch in sixes, and the Wood Outlaws and the Zhakuk raid the miles where the wood's edge and the mere come close to the road. A party comes to ride with the wagon, to see where the last ones were lost, or to turn off toward the mere. Wagons are taken in the last miles before the keep by raiders working from the wood's edge. If the next convoy arrives whole, the Carters' Guild adds a second wagon. If it is lost, the Guild stops running and posts a bounty at the keep's market.
Approach: Three ways on: the keep's main gate at the east end, the realm at the west end off the map, and the mere's causeway, which leaves the road 5 miles out. The road is two watches end to end; off it, three.
Terrain: plains - On the road a watch covers 8 miles; off it, through tall grass, 4. Sight carries 3 miles by day, so a party on the road is seen from the wood's edge long before it sees anyone there. Off the road a FAILURE on Navigate loses the party for a watch, a COMPLICATION for half of one. At night a fire shows 5 miles, and travel off the road is at Disadvantage. Rain turns the verge to mud and halves the pace off the road.
