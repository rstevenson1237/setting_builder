B The Ravine

Overview: A long cleft in broken ground, with a stream in the bottom and steep slopes of scree and thorn on each side, running from the road beyond the keep to a cave mouth in the far wall. Wolves den on its scree, goblins watch it from the ridge, and the keep's soldiers patrol its mouth.

Approach: Entered from the road a quarter-hour beyond the keep's gate, where a path leaves it and drops into the cleft. The crossing by the path takes half a day, three actions of four hours; off the path, along the ridge, it takes a full day.

Terrain: Hills. A path along the stream costs one action for a stretch of the ravine between neighbouring Landmarks; off the path, through thorn and scree, it costs two. A party that leaves the stream is lost until it finds the stream, the ridge-line or a Landmark again. Night in the ravine without a fire draws wolves; a lit fire keeps them off, and shows for a mile from the ridge. Rain turns the path to mud and the scree to a slide, and doubles the cost of leaving the path.

Situation: Goblins are working further up the road each week and have begun leaving a chevron scratched on the ravine's path-stones. Next, they will watch the path itself and take parties that come out, which follows a party killing goblins on the ridge. After that, they come down from the cave in force to take the keep's patrols, which follows the squad's first loss in the ravine.
