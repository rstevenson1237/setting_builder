B The Wagon Road

Overview: Open grass either side of the realm's road, which runs west from the keep's main gate 12 miles to the map's edge and on to the realm. A party comes to ride with the wagon, see where the last ones were lost, or turn off toward the mere. The Carters' Guild runs a convoy on the keep's wagon days, and the Keep's Soldiers patrol the first stretch in sixes, but wagons are taken in the last miles before the keep by the Wood Outlaws and the Zhakuk working from the wood's edge. If the next convoy arrives whole, the Guild adds a second wagon; if it is lost, the Guild stops running and posts a bounty at the keep's market.

Approach: Three ways on: the keep's main gate at the east end, the realm at the west end off the map, and the mere's causeway, which leaves the road 5 miles out. The road is two watches end to end; off it, three.

Terrain: plains - On the road a watch covers 8 miles; off it, through tall grass, 4. Sight carries 3 miles by day, so a party on the road is seen from the wood's edge long before it sees anyone there. Off the road a FAILURE on Navigate loses the party for a watch, a COMPLICATION for half of one. At night a fire shows 5 miles, and travel off the road is at Disadvantage. Rain turns the verge to mud and halves the pace off the road.

Ways:
- B.1 - West from the keep gate (A.1), 24 feet wide, a milestone at every mile numbered from the keep. Its stops in order: the watch rise, showing three miles of road and the wood's edge; the burned frames, by B.3; the causeway turning, a reedy track north to B.5; the ford stones over B.2; the wayside shrine, B.4; and the last milestone, where the map ends. The grass beside it hides a man lying down at 30 yards.

Landmarks:
- B.2 - 30 yards of knee-deep stream on the road itself, crossed by flat stones a yard apart, the banks churned by hooves and wheels.
- B.3 - Charred frames and burst casks along 60 feet of ditch, a step off the road; smoke rises from the wood beyond.
- B.4 - A hooded grey stone box 3 feet high beside the road, a tower carved on its face and a bowl at its foot.
- B.5 - A muddy landing in the reeds at the causeway's south end (D.1), a hare's foot on a post at head height and red thread on the posts.

Tables:
d6 Encounter
1. A convoy rolls by from the realm with a green wheel on the lead wagon; its drivers trade news for a round.
2. Six goblins break from the grass at dusk with spears and slings.
3. The patrol, a man short, escorts two carts toward the keep.
4. Outlaws watch from a rise and withdraw when seen.
5. A rider with a message for the commander asks the party to ride on with him.
6. A squall rolls in; the verge turns to mud and the pace off the road is halved.
