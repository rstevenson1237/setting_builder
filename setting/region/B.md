B Zamiun

Overview: A cavern city round a still lake, lit by glowing growths on the ceiling, its people in animal masks feasting at long tables. A party comes down from the funerary level for food, water, trade and a way further down. Dreamers (Bestiary: Masked Dreamer) hold the quarters, market and revel house; giant spiders, rats, centipedes and stirges hold the edges, and three Scholars (Faction: Sihuahet) buy spores at B.3. A party can trade for the fungus (Local Procedure: Trading for the fungus and its drugs) or join the revels (Local Procedure: Joining the revels). The fungus beds spread into the quarters, untended.

Approach: By the path from the funerary level (G.14), two masked guards at the gate (B.1) step aside for anyone in a mask and follow anyone without. A bronze door at the head of the black stair (B.10) leads down to the sixth level (H.1), unbarred from below and barred from above. Crossing the city takes a watch; leaving it takes two.

Terrain: A cavern of quays, stone streets, fungus terraces and a black lake. A watch covers 4 miles on the quays and streets, where the dreamers part for a mask; 1 mile through the fungus gardens, with a Test of Sanity at each bed breathed over. A boat crosses the lake in a turn; a swimmer takes a Test of Constitution in the cold. Torches are seen from every quay and the masked ask for them put out.

Ways:
- B.8 - The Quay Walk curves round the north shore from the gate: the first lamp-post (30 yards), the boat steps (60), the singing rail (90, to B.7) and the far landing (120, to B.6). Stirges hang under the lamps.

Landmarks:
- B.1 - An arch of glowing growths where the path meets the city; two masked guards, spare masks in the posts.
- B.2 - Painted doors in three tiers round a lit stair; twelve dreamers sing and paint behind them.
- B.3 - Two aisles of stalls selling caps, spore jars and masks; three Scholars buy at the east stalls.
- B.4 - Long tables under lamps round a raised hearth; twenty dreamers feast, drum and dance.
- B.5 - A vaulted granary, empty bins; two giant spiders in the vault and a carved face in the west wall.
- B.6 - Terraced beds of glowing fungus under a haze that costs a Test of Sanity.
- B.7 - A black lake with floating lamps; reed boats on the stone shore, cold water.

Places:
- B.9 - A loft of unfinished masks above the granary; five giant rats nest in the stock.
- B.10 - A narrow black stair behind a bronze door, opened by pressing the carved face; two giant centipedes.

Tables:
d6 Dressing and Challenges
1. Drums across the lake, and singing from behind painted doors.
2. A hum from the quays, lamps floating out from the shore.
3. Glowing haze drifting from the beds toward the quarters.
4. A thin beating of wings under the quay lamps.
5. Water dripping in the granary vault, and a spider's web across it.
6. A mask-seller's shelves, rats gnawing through the stock.
