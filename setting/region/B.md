B Yrsk Wold Fringe

Overview: The worked edge of a forest old enough to predate humankind, marked by standing stones no one living raised. Loggers, trappers, and prospectors work this fringe for timber, pelts, and ore, and the referee should run it as a place where the party can operate freely and profitably right up until they choose - or are forced - to cross a stone line, at which point the region's rules change underfoot. The wold does not chase; it answers.

Ambiance: Old growth closes overhead within a few dozen yards of any trail, cutting daylight to a green half-dusk even at noon. The air is cold and mineral-damp, thick with wet bark and leaf-rot; birdsong stops abruptly near any stone line, and the silence that replaces it is the fringe's most reliable warning. Standing stones are grey and lichen-crusted, unweathered in a way their age doesn't explain, and the roofless Marrgath longhouses sag with a century and a half of moss and collapse.

Terrain: Old-growth forest, dense enough off-trail that travel without a cut path or a guide who knows it runs at half the usual pace. Established logging roads and trapper trails move quickly; anything past them does not.

Foraging: Fringe mushrooms and medicinal barks are common and freely gathered along the trail edges. Wold-marrow moss, gathered only within sight of a stone line, is locally reputed to slow bleeding when packed into a wound - rare, genuinely effective, and always worth more than its weight suggests. Nothing gathered from past a stone line is trusted, whatever its apparent value.

Layout: A rough band of forest surrounding Khirengaard, a few miles deep before the true, unworked wold begins, threaded by logging roads and trapper trails that run tens to a few hundred yards between landmarks. The Ninefold Hollow lies within this region, its entrance reachable by trail. Time-bound at 4 hours per action - travel, tracking, and foraging each cost a slot at that scale.

Features: The standing stones themselves, spaced along the fringe's inner edge and each subtly different in shape and marking; the roofless ruins of the old Marrgath longhouses; scattered sawpits and trapper's blinds still in active use; the toppled stone near the longhouses that no cutter has dared reset.

Dangers: A stone line is a real jurisdictional boundary, not a wall - nothing stops a crossing, but an unblessed one draws consequence the wold delivers on its own schedule, not the party's. Wolves den aggressively near several stretches of the line. The wold notices sustained intrusion and answers through its creatures rather than through any single dramatic event.

Creatures: Fringe Wolves run the treeline in packs but will not cross a stone line either direction; a Wold-Marked Wolf hunts alone near the toppled stone. Fen-Bears den in the boggy hollows and a Wold Elk or Wold-Touched Stag can be encountered leading a hunt toward - and always ending at - a stone line. Grey Charcoalers keep to the deep fringe, trading wordlessly and vanishing if followed. A Stone-Warden stands dormant near any undisturbed line and activates only if that line is disturbed.

Secrets: The Grey Charcoalers' kiln sites and their knowledge of the Marrgath's true fate are hidden throughout the deep fringe, findable only through patient, respectful contact rather than pursuit. The toppled stone marks a section of boundary the guild has quietly stopped surveying.

Treasure: Pelts and wold-iron off Treasure Tables I and II; scavenged Marrgath goods off Table I and III found among the longhouse ruins; the rare wold-marrow moss and other foraged goods, valued but never listed on a standard table.

Tables: d6 Encounter Table (rolled on each failed Difficulty roll)
1. A Fringe Wolf pack shadows the party at a distance, breaking off the instant anyone nears a stone line.
2. A Grey Charcoaler is glimpsed at a drop-stone, gone before the party can close the distance.
3. A sudden cold rain reduces visibility and muffles sound for the next several hours.
4. A Wold-Touched Stag leads whoever gives chase on a long, deliberate route toward the nearest stone line.
5. A disturbed Stone-Warden is found already in motion, converging on whatever disturbed its line.
6. Concern enforcers are found pushing a survey stake past an unblessed line, and don't welcome witnesses.
