B The Salt-Scrub Hills

Overview: The Hills are the connective wilderness between Saltmere (A) and the Hollow Cairn (C) - a WILD region meant to be crossed with risk, not lingered in. Run it as a sequence of Difficulty rolls for travel, foraging, and tracking, punctuated by encounters; its purpose is to make the short trip to the dungeon feel earned.

Ambiance: Salt-bleached grass and thorny scrub bent permanently by onshore wind, chalky exposed rock underfoot, the smell of dry earth undercut by brine carried inland, and distant wolf calls at dusk.

Layout: Rolling hill country roughly a day's travel across, cut by dry streambeds and the recently exposed stretch of Vael's old inland road. There are no fixed paths beyond the road, which runs generally from Saltmere (A) toward the barrow (C). Neighboring landmarks sit anywhere from a few dozen to a few hundred yards apart; the crossing to Saltmere or the Cairn is measured in miles. As a WILD region, run travel, tracking, and foraging here at 4 hours per action.

Features: The swept-clear stretch of buried road itself, unnaturally clean for something exposed overnight; a ruined shepherd's cairn used as a local landmark; and sinkholes where the old road has collapsed into hollow ground below.

Dangers: The Hills are indifferent, not hostile. Most dangers are environmental - exposure, disorientation, a collapsing sinkhole - or opportunistic, like wolves or bandits, rather than anything actively hunting intruders. A bandit patrol, however, will treat trespassers as prey.

Creatures: Coast Wolf (Bestiary) packs roam the hills and test unguarded camps after dark. A Coastal Bandit (Bestiary) band uses the high ground to watch the road between Saltmere and the barrow, taking a toll or a fight from anyone who passes.

Secrets: The swept-clear road, if followed, leads directly and unnaturally straight to the Hollow Cairn (C) - too straight to be wind or erosion, as though something wanted it found. A sinkhole near the road's midpoint drops into an unmapped side-passage of the Cairn, offering a second way in.

Treasure: Little beyond what the bandits carry and what can be foraged; the Hills' true reward is safe passage and information rather than loot.

Tables: WILD - d6 Encounter Table, rolled on each failed Difficulty roll.
1. A Coast Wolf pack shadows the party at a distance, testing resolve before dark.
2. A Coastal Bandit patrol demands a toll or a fight.
3. The ground gives way underfoot near an old sinkhole.
4. A sudden squall off the sea cuts visibility to a few yards.
5. The party stumbles on bones - animal or otherwise - scattered along the road.
6. A distant, out-of-place tolling sound carries on the wind for a few seconds, then stops.
