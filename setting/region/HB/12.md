HB.12 **The Armoury** (Medium) - *racked, dry, locked*
Racks of bronze blades and spears fill a dry room, oiled metal gleaming, shields hung on chains.
*30 by 20 feet. The cult's weapons are kept here.*
**The racks:** curved bronze blades and spears racked on rails, a chain behind each; lifting a spear works a hammer on a chain (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**The oil jars:** clay vessels of oil for the blades.
**The locked racks:** a single shelf with an iron lid (Treasure II, d20).
**Exits:** a wooden door -> HB.4 The Long Hall
