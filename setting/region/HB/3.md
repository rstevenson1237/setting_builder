HB.3 **The Guard Room** (Medium) - *lamp-lit, armed, cramped*
A cramped lamp-lit room racks spears by its door, a table with lots drawn on it, a bell rope hanging from the ceiling and a key board on the wall.
*30 by 20 feet. The bell here brings every snake man in the halls.*
**The guards:** two snake men and four bound men drawing lots by the lamp (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Snake Man); they answer an alarm in one turn.
**The bell rope:** hemp running to a bronze bell in the roof, which rings through the halls.
**The key board:** hooks by the door, the Scale Seal hanging on one (Keys: The Scale Seal).
**Exits:** a wooden door -> HB.4 The Long Hall
