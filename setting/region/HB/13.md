HB.13 **The Sanctum Stair Head** (Medium) - *steep, braziered, watched*
A steep stair mouth burns under a bronze brazier, a slow breathing rising from below and a hiss from the guards.
*20 by 20 feet. The stair goes down sixty feet to the god's sanctum.*
**The stair guards:** two snake men watching the stair under the brazier (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Snake Man); they answer an alarm in one turn.
**The brazier:** bronze, on a tripod, lighting the stair.
**Exits:** an open passage -> HB.8 The Snake People's Quarters, down the cut stair -> HC.1 The Sanctum Stair Foot
