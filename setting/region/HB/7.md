HB.7 **The Cult Barracks** (Medium) - *bunked, stinking, armed*
A bunked room reeks of musk and sweat, bunks creaking, weapons on pegs, snoring and a hiss.
*40 by 30 feet. The cult's off-duty watch sleeps here.*
**The off-duty watch:** six bound men and two snake men resting in the bunks, armed (Hostile, 8, Bestiary : Snake Man); they answer an alarm in one turn.
**The weapon pegs:** curved bronze blades and spears hung on pegs by the door.
**Exits:** a wooden door -> HB.4 The Long Hall
