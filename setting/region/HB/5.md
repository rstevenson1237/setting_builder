HB.5 **The Captives' Pens** (High) - *barred, fouled, crowded*
Barred pens pack the room with captives on straw, weeping and chains sounding, flies thick and a keeper's bench by the door.
*40 by 40 feet. The pens fill each week with captives from the lizard men and the pirates; the strongest are taken down to the sanctum.*
**The captives:** eight tribespeople of the villages, chained in the straw, begging for rescue or a quick death, who know who was taken from where (Fearful, 8, Bestiary : Tribesman).
**The keepers:** three bound men with whips and keys, each with a green scale-mark inked over the heart under their clothes (Hostile, 3, Bestiary : Tribesman); they answer an alarm in one turn.
**The bundles:** the dead captives' belongings piled by the trough (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** a wooden door -> HB.4 The Long Hall
