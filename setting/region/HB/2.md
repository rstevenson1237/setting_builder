HB.2 **The Winch Room** (Medium) - *geared, dim, watched*
A geared room grinds under a dim lamp, chains rattling, bound men at the cranks.
*30 by 20 feet. The winch lowers captives to the halls; the crew works in shifts.*
**The crew:** four bound tribesmen turning the cranks in shifts, each with a green scale-mark inked over the heart under their clothes (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Tribesman); they answer an alarm in two turns.
**The gear frame:** stout timber holding the chain drum and cogs.
**Exits:** a wooden door -> HB.1 The Shaft Foot, an open passage -> HB.4 The Long Hall
