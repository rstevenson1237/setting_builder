HB.10 **The Meat Larder** (Low) - *cold, hung, fly-ridden*
A cold cellar hangs with sides of meat on hooks, flies loud and dripping from a drain, salt barrels by the wall.
*20 by 20 feet. What the cult eats is hung here; the meat is a sign of the pens' purpose.*
**The hooks:** iron barbs in rows from the roof, hung with pale sides of meat, some of it human.
**The salt barrels:** cooperage along the wall packed with salted meat.
**Exits:** a wooden door -> HB.4 The Long Hall
