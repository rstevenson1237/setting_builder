HB.8 **The Snake People's Quarters** (High) - *warm, musky, coiled*
Warm musky halls hold nest-beds in alcoves, a slow hissing and the rustle of scales, a banded door at the far side and a stair going down.
*60 by 40 feet. The floor is heated stone. The treasury behind is locked; the Scale Seal opens its banded door. A bell at the guard room calls every snake man here.*
**The snake men:** four coiled in warm nest-beds (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Snake Man); a long hiss comes first, and they answer an alarm in one turn.
**The nest-beds:** disturbing them lets out a sour musk (Test of Constitution, 1d, Poison).
**The brood hoard:** the nest-beds' bedding hides gold and gems (Treasure IV, d20).
**The hide bundle:** wrapped in skins under one bed (Magic, d12).
**The banded door:** iron-banded, to the treasury, locked; it takes the Scale Seal (Keys: The Scale Seal).
**Exits:** an open arch -> HB.4 The Long Hall, an open passage -> HB.13 The Sanctum Stair Head, an open passage -> HB.14 The Cistern, a locked banded door -> HB.9 The Treasury
