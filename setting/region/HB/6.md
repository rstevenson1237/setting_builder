HB.6 **The Feeding Cell** (Low) - *bare, stained, small*
A small bare cell has a drain in a stained floor, a hook-ring in the wall, drips and a chill.
*20 by 20 feet. Captives are held here before they go down to the sanctum; the stains and the drain are a sign of the pens' purpose.*
**The drain:** a round grate at the floor's centre, rust-brown.
**The hook-ring:** an iron ring set in the wall at head height, worn bright.
**Exits:** a wooden door -> HB.4 The Long Hall
