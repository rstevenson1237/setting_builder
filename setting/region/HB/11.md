HB.11 **The Priests' Study** (Medium) - *lamp-lit, carved, cluttered*
A cluttered lamp-lit study crowds with scrolls and carved stone, lamp-hiss and rustling bark, a lectern with a codex open.
*30 by 30 feet. The priest here reads by lamplight; the stair watch's rota is written on the desk.*
**Thazsheek:** a man-high serpent priest in a green sash, reading at the lectern (Named Creature: Thazsheek); it parleys in a hiss, wants a living captive for the Sleeper, and sells a vial of the sleeper's venom for 300 cn, three vials, and knows the sanctum's every way.
**The codex:** a bark ledger of captives counted by their sellers, the lizard men and the red-sashed pirates, with a note that the lizard men are paid in gold, open on the lectern (Lore: The Priests' Codex).
**The carved stone chair:** serpents cut into its arms, set by the desk.
**Exits:** a wooden door -> HB.4 The Long Hall
