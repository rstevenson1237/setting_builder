HB.4 **The Long Hall** (Medium) - *vaulted, lamp-lit, hissing*
A vaulted hall runs a hundred feet, lamps fluttering in brackets and a hiss carrying down its length, doors to either side.
*10 by 100 feet, a hall. It is the spine of the halls, with carvings of the snake people's rule along its vault.*
**The vault:** carved serpents run the length of the roof, a thousand years old, left by the empire that raised the pyramid; a sloughed skin lies on the floor below.
**The python:** a thirty-foot snake coiled in the vault above the hall (Hostile, 1, Bestiary : Giant Python); it drops on what passes beneath.
**The lamp brackets:** bronze arms down both walls, burning oil.
**Exits:** an open passage -> HB.2 The Winch Room, a wooden door -> HB.3 The Guard Room, a wooden door -> HB.10 The Meat Larder, a wooden door -> HB.11 The Priests' Study, a wooden door -> HB.12 The Armoury, a wooden door -> HB.5 The Captives' Pens, a wooden door -> HB.6 The Feeding Cell, a wooden door -> HB.7 The Cult Barracks, an open arch -> HB.8 The Snake People's Quarters
