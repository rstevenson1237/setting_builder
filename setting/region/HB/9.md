HB.9 **The Treasury** (High) - *vaulted, locked, glittering*
A vaulted strongroom glitters with heaped coin and banded chests, cold air and a metallic clinking, a bell hanging above the door.
*30 by 30 feet. The door is locked and takes the Scale Seal. A bell rings when it opens.*
**The treasure:** banded chests of gold plate, temple coin, green stones and captives' bracelets (Hoard: The Cult Treasury).
**The spike trap:** a chest lid with bronze points under its rim; lifting it drives them in (Test of Constitution, 2d, Piercing).
**The coffer:** iron-bound, at the back (Treasure V, d20).
**The centipedes:** four coiled among the coin chests, a dry rustle in the coin (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Giant Centipede).
**Exits:** a locked banded door -> HB.8 The Snake People's Quarters
