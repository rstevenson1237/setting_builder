HB.1 **The Shaft Foot** (Low) - *cold, echoing, dark*
A cold stone chamber lies below the shaft's foot, dripping and echoing, a winch chain hanging down from the dark above.
*20 by 20 feet. The shaft's foot; a spiral stair climbs beside the chain to the temple above.*
**The hanging chain:** iron links coming down the shaft from the winch overhead, the end on the floor.
**The bunk:** a rough cot against the wall where a winch hand sleeps.
**Exits:** a wooden door -> HB.2 The Winch Room, up the shaft stair -> HA.10 The Shaft Room
