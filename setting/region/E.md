E The Ravine

Overview: A stream cut a quarter mile into the hills, 60 to 100 yards wide between walls 80 feet high, the tribes' caves in its walls and the maze and the shrine stair at its head. A party comes for the tribes' hoards, and for the shrine. Six tribes hold its walls, the Black Altar its head and an Owlbear its east rim. The tribes have paid the shrine five years without open war, though each wall holds a pair that hates its neighbour. If a fight rings out at one mouth, its neighbours send watchers to their ledges within a turn. If the tithe is stopped on the path, the Zurnaz search the ravine ledge by ledge.

Approach: Entered at the mouth, on the south, from the forest trail. The stream path runs north along the stream a quarter mile to the head, and a goat track along the east rim above the caves is reached by a climb at the mouth.

Terrain: hills - On the stream path a watch covers 4 miles; on the rim, through scree and broken rock, 2. The walls need a climb (Maneuver) of one Round per 20 feet. A shout is heard at every mouth within 200 yards, a fire on the path at night shows from every ledge, and a party on the path is in bowshot of the ledges on both walls. Storm floods the path to the knee.

Ways:
- E.1 - North from the forest trail (C.1), ten yards wide between the walls. Its stops in order: the crack, worn handholds climbing 80 feet to the rim track (E.11); the stepping stones across the stream; the dry bed mouth, where the dry fork (E.7) enters from the east; and at the head, the maze door (E.8) and the shrine stair (E.9) either side of the path's end.

Landmarks:
- E.2 - First on the west wall, a six-foot shelf a few paces above the path, grinning faces chalked over a crawlhole at its back and a tin rattle faintly heard; it opens on the kobold warren (F.1).
- E.3 - 30 feet up the west wall, a wolf skull on a pole by an iron-barred kennel door and a slingers' notch above it, wolves baying behind; it opens on the goblin hold (G.1).
- E.4 - Last on the west wall, 50 feet up, a wide shelf littered with bone before a waist-high cairn of skulls, drunken roaring heard from the path; it opens on the orc warren (H.1).
- E.5 - First on the east wall, 40 feet up, a built wall 10 feet high with an iron-bound gate and a red hand stencilled beside it, a horn call carrying from it; it opens on the hobgoblin hold (I.1).
- E.6 - 60 feet up the east wall, a narrow shelf with iron collars on pegs along its edge and laughing barks heard from below; it opens on the gnoll den (J.1).
- E.7 - A dry stream bed 15 feet wide running east under overhanging walls to the bugbears' den (K.1), soot handprints on the rock higher than a man can reach and no sound in it.
- E.8 - A door of iron-bound oak 7 feet high in a frame carved with script, set in the cliff's foot; no light shows at its edges. It opens on the maze (L.1).
- E.9 - Fifty steps of black-stained makers' stone, 6 feet wide, climbing to a landing with a gong and on to the shrine (M.1); a trodden path from each cave mouth meets its lowest tread.
- E.10 - A shallow cave on the east rim, 80 feet above the path, a nest of bones at its back and torn feathers on its floor; its hooting carries down the ravine at night.

Places:
- E.11 - A goat track 2 feet wide along the brink of the east wall, from the crack at the mouth to E.10; loose stone gives under a heavy load, and a hollow trodden flat above E.6 shows every east-wall ledge.
- E.12 - A niche 6 feet deep behind a fallen slab leaning at the stair's foot, fresh scrape marks at the slab's foot.

Tables:
d6 Encounter

3. Orcs roar from the cairn, and two come down the slope to the path.
4. A hobgoblin horn sounds twice from the east wall.
5. A bugbear's soot handprint on the rock beside the path, still wet.
6. Chanting drifts down the stair from the shrine.
