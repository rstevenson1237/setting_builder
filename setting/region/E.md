E The Ravine

Overview: A stream cut a mile through the hills, 60 to 100 yards wide between walls 80 feet high. On the west wall from the mouth up are the Gubak, the Zhakuk and the Rokmok. On the east wall are the Burgnaz and the Kurzun, then a dry fork holding the Zurnaz. At the head stand the maze in the cliff's foot and the stair up to the Black Altar's shrine. An Owlbear lairs on the east rim. A party comes for the tribes' hoards, and for the shrine. The tribes have been paying the shrine for five years and have not been fighting one another in the open, though each wall holds a pair that hates its neighbour. If a fight rings out at one mouth, its neighbours send watchers to their ledges within a turn. If the tithe is stopped on the path, the Zurnaz search the ravine ledge by ledge.
Approach: Entered at the mouth, on the south, from the forest trail. The stream path runs north along the stream to the head, a mile. A goat track runs the east rim above the caves, reached by a climb at the mouth. Each mouth is a ledge its tribe watches.
Terrain: hills - On the stream path a watch covers 4 miles; on the rim, through scree and broken rock, 2. The walls need a climb (Maneuver) of one Round per 20 feet. Sound carries along the walls: a shout is heard at every mouth within 200 yards, and a fire on the path at night shows from every ledge. A party on the path is in bowshot of the ledges on both walls. Storm floods the path to the knee.
