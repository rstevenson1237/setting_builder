E The Eastern Jungle

Overview: Low jungle under a closed canopy, the vine-choked stone of the snake people's towns, a slow brown river, and a stepped pyramid standing over the trees. A party comes past the serpent posts for the pyramid's gold, or is driven here (Faction: Tuuzesh). Patrols and bound men walk the serpent roads each night leading captives to the pyramid, and the boundary posts at the jungle's edge are being re-carved. If a party passes the posts, patrols come onto the road behind it within the watch and any village that learns of it withdraws its standing; if a captive is led into the pyramid, a tremor runs through the stair and the birds go silent for a watch.

Approach: By the serpent posts from the hill jungle, carved serpents at the jungle's edge and no guide past them; crossing takes going unguided, and a villager who goes is cast out. By the pyramid's foot from the upper temple, two bound men and a snake man on the first terrace by day and a patrol by night; a hundred feet of open stair.

Terrain: Low jungle under a closed canopy. On the serpent roads worn stone is a road, 4 miles a watch, and every patrol uses it. Under the canopy light is dim at noon and dark by the second watch, a fire is seen through the leaves, and a party off the roads is Lost on a failed Test of Fate. A raft goes down the slow river at 2 miles a watch, in sight of both banks and every crocodile.

Ways:
- E.1 - The serpent road runs east from the posts D.11 to the pyramid, 7 miles end to end: the posts at its start, patrol fires leading to E.5, the burned market E.3, the statue court E.4 and the pyramid's foot E.9.

Landmarks:
- E.2 - A leaning carved gate-tower hung with moss; grit falls from its stones.
- E.3 - Scorched stalls and fallen arcades round a square; centipedes nest in the burned wood.
- E.4 - Fallen serpent statues in a hushed court; a faint hissing in the air whispers in the head.
- E.5 - A trampled patrol camp round a fire, sentries on stakes; eight bound men and a snake man rest here by day.
- E.6 - A low shuttered hut with scale-marks scratched on the door; six bound men sit quiet inside.
- E.7 - A domed temple hissing in the vine-dark; a python coils in its dome.
- E.8 - A slow brown river under hanging branches, snagged logs hidden under the water.
- E.9 - A vast stepped pyramid climbing out of the canopy, a snake man and two bound men on its first terrace, the south stair rising to HA.1.

Tables:
