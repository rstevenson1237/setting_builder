E Nakhash

Overview: Nakhash is the Serpent Cult's own burial ground, a field of low mounds two miles
south of the Sunk Bridge ford, sealed since whatever reign filled it and reopened only in
the last season or two by a cell of the cult itself, digging back in for what was buried
with their dead. Getting past its three inner chambers costs the same three things carried
together - a token, a rod, and a necklace - plus the name of whoever answered for that
chamber while they lived, and the cell doing the digging wants exactly the same three
things for exactly the same reason. What is true here and nowhere else in Telar: Nakhash's
own defenses test what a party is carrying rather than merely how hard they can fight, and
the same regalia that opens every gate on the way in is the one sure way to wake what
guards the hoard at the end of it.

Ambiance: Turned earth and old grave-silt up top, giving way below to dry stone and a
colder, thinner smell with nothing rotting in it. The Sanctum carries sound the wrong way -
a voice pitched normally in the First Chamber reaches the branch chambers off it more
clearly than a whisper would two rooms over on the surface. No standing water anywhere
below the mounds; whatever channeled it away in the original cutting still works.

Architecture: The mounds are earth banked over dry-laid stone lining, rough and low, cut
through rather than built to be entered twice. The Sanctum beneath them is properly cut
stone, corbelled rather than arched wherever a chamber needs to bear weight, in the same
building tradition that raised Daghash - the same three-coil serpent turn already seen
there repeats here too, cut into the floor at every chamber's own threshold rather than at
a lintel. Passages branching off the three main chambers run narrow, barely three feet,
built for one person at a time; the chambers themselves run wide and tall by comparison,
cut for a procession to stand in rather than pass through.

Layout: A collection - a worked complex of twenty-four locations across two quarters, the
Barrow Field on the surface and the Sanctum beneath it. Two entrances, both mounds whose
floor has given way, one by digging and one on its own; a third, formal entrance on the
mound field's northern edge is sealed by stone no party can move and is not a way in at
all. The Sanctum runs in a straight line beneath the mounds - First Chamber, Second
Chamber, Third Chamber, each gated from the last and each with its own burial sites and
side chambers branching off it - ending at the cult's own hoard past the Third Chamber. The
cult's current dig camp sits among the mounds nearest the true entrances. Time here runs on
the Danger countdown below, not the clock.

Features: The mounds hold their own chill even at midday, air trapped under packed earth
that never warms the way open ground does. Grave-silt, fine and pale, coats the floor of
every opened mound and nowhere else in the field - a boot print in it lasts for days.
Below, air moves reluctantly through the Sanctum's cut vents; a struck flame burns steady
rather than guttering, but a room sealed since the last chamber's gate closed sits noticeably
staler than one still open to the passage air.

Dangers: What is old here sleeps until disturbed - a mound's own dead answer only to that
mound being opened, and the Sanctum's oldest guards act only against whoever is carrying
what they were set to recognise. What is new here does not sleep at all: the cult cell
digging the mounds keeps a rotating watch and reacts to noise, light, and any mound opened
that they did not open themselves.

Creatures: Barrow Wights hold at least one still-sealed mound each, waking only when that
mound is breached (setting/Bestiary.md). A second Barrow-Kin kin-group, distinct from the
one two miles north, tends the field and will say which mounds are safe to whoever asks
before digging. The Coil-Mother's own cell - a handful of Serpent Cult members under her
direct order - works the true entrances and camps among the nearest mounds. Six Stone
Wards, carved as coiled serpents, stand dormant over the hoard past the Third Chamber and
have not moved since the last reign sealed it.

Factions: The Serpent Cult holds Nakhash outright - their own ancestral ground, worked
currently by the Coil-Mother's cell at the Barrow Field's true entrances and dig camp.
Neither Brekar nor the Nine Wagons has ever had reason to come this far from the ford.

Secrets: Nakhash's dead are not an anonymous older people - they are the Serpent Cult's own
reign, three chamber-masters and whoever they buried with, and nothing else in Telar says
so plainly. The two true entrances and the one false one are not obvious from the surface
alone; which mounds actually lead down is a fact the cult already has and the party does
not.

Treasure: setting/Treasure3.md (gems and jewelry) is most of what the mounds themselves
give up - grave-goods left with the ordinary dead. setting/Treasure5.md (treasure cache) is
what waits past the Third Chamber, kept exactly as the last reign left it.

Tables: d6 Danger, counting down from 6 with each failed Difficulty roll.
6. A mound settles somewhere out of sight - dirt sifting, nothing more, and no way yet to
   tell which one.
5. The cell's dig-ropes creak under a weight that isn't the party's.
4. A Barrow-Kin is watching from the field's edge, and was not a moment ago.
3. Voices carry from a mound the party hasn't reached yet - the cell, working another
   entrance.
2. Grave-silt has been disturbed somewhere the party has already been.
1. Whatever is nearest - a mound's own dead, or the cell itself - has noticed exactly where
   the party is.
