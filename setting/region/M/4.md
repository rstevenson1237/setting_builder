M.4 **The Refectory** (Medium) - *long, bare, cold*
Long bare **tables** run the length of a vaulted hall, scrubbed pale and set with wooden bowls. A **pantry door** stands shut at the far end, **shelves** of stores beyond it.
*Hall 50 ft by 25 ft, ceiling 15 ft.*
**Tables:** Scrubbed deal, benches tucked under, a lamp hook above each.
**Pantry Door:** Plain oak with a green crust on its hinges; opened without care it vents a gas from a jar behind (Test of Constitution, 1d, Poison).
**Shelves:** Plank shelves floor to roof, stocked for the acolytes (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** archway, west end of the hall -> M.2 The Outer Shrine, wooden, north end of the hall, north-west -> M.5 The High Priest's Chamber
