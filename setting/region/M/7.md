M.7 **The Ossuary** (Medium) - *stacked, dry, silent*
Bones are stacked in ranked **niches** to the roof of a long hall, skulls in rows along the top. **Zombies** stand in the lowest niches, grey and swollen, their arms at their sides.
*Hall 60 ft by 20 ft, ceiling 12 ft. A zombie stirs at the first light or sound; the dead of the crypts rise together.*
**Zombies:** Six step out of their niches together and close in (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Zombie).
**Skull Niches:** The upper rows, each skull with something wedged in its jaw (Treasure III, d20).
**Shrouds:** Rotted scraps hang from the lowest shelves.
**Exits:** archway, south end of the hall -> M.6 The Crypt Stair, open, west end of the hall -> M.8 The Makers' Niches, stone, north end of the hall -> M.9 The Priests' Tomb
