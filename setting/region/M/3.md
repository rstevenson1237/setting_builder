M.3 **The Acolytes' Cells** (Medium) - *narrow, sparse, quiet*
Eight bare **cells** open off a sixty-foot passage, a pallet and a tallow stub in each. **Acolytes** in black robes kneel at their prayers, knives at their belts.
*Passage 60 ft long and 5 ft wide. A shout carries to the outer shrine.*
**Acolytes:** Four kneel at prayer between watches; at a stranger they seize their knives and call the shrine. They know the high priest's hours.
**Cells:** Pallets and rushlights, a shelf of tallow; cut hair is swept into the corners.
**Passage:** Worn bare in the middle, blackened by tallow drips.
**Exits:** wooden, east end of the passage -> M.2 The Outer Shrine, wooden, north end of the passage, north-east -> M.5 The High Priest's Chamber
