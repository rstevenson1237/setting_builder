M.2 **The Outer Shrine** (Medium) - *black-stained, candlelit, vaulted*
A vaulted hall eighty feet long, black stains pooled before a stone **altar** at the north end, **candles** burning in iron stands down both walls. Chanting carries along the vault.
*Vault 80 ft by 40 ft, ceiling 24 ft. The chanting hides small noises.*
**Altar:** An old black slab stained fresh, with a stone bowl brimming with blood; whoever touches the bowl tells the high priest a stranger is within.
**Shelf:** Cut into the rock behind the altar, holding a silver casket with a white tower on its lid (Unique Treasure: The Founder's Reliquary).
**Candles:** Black, guttering, set in iron stands every ten feet.
**Chanting:** Low, from the cells beyond; it stops and starts without a leader.
**Exits:** banded, south end of the vault -> M.1 The Stair-Hall, wooden, west end of the vault -> M.3 The Acolytes' Cells, archway, east end of the vault -> M.4 The Refectory, archway, east end of the vault, down forty feet -> M.6 The Crypt Stair
