M.9 **The Priests' Tomb** (Medium) - *sealed, ornate, cold*
A sealed tomb round a black stone **sarcophagus**, its lid carved with a priest in robes, one hand raised. Burnt incense lies cold in a dish at its foot.
*Tomb 30 ft across, ceiling 14 ft. The door at the south is stone and stands ajar.*
**Sarcophagus:** Black stone, its lid a single slab; the carved priest's raised hand is worn bright where fingers have pressed it, and pressing it slides the lid aside.
**Book:** A black-bound psalter lies under the lid beside the priest's bones (Magical Tome: The Black Psalter).
**Incense Dish:** Brass, with a pinch of cold ash.
**Exits:** stone, south end of the tomb -> M.7 The Ossuary, stone, north end of the tomb, up thirty feet -> M.10 The Antechamber
