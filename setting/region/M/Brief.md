Layout: The shrine is cut into the cliff above the maze at the ravine's head and has three ways in. The stair from the ravine (E.9), 1,760 yards up from the ravine's mouth, climbs fifty steps of black-stained makers' stone, 6 feet wide, to a landing where a gong hangs and acolytes watch, and ends at the stair-hall (M.1): a cold hall 40 feet square with a ceiling of 18 feet, black stains spreading from a banded inner door that is barred from within at dusk. The makers' passage from the maze rises 120 feet by a wide stair from the Minotaur's lair (L.5) to the passage landing (M.13), a landing 10 feet square with a ceiling of 8 feet, dust thick on the floor with one long trail across it, and a makers' door of stone at its east side. The bugbears' back stair climbs 120 feet north-east from their den (K.4) to the back stair door (M.14), a landing 8 feet by 6 with a bell hung on a rope beside a plastered door, soot handprints thick round the door's edge, 40 feet east of the high priest's chamber (M.5). The shrine is makers' cut stone, ceilings 14 to 20 feet and halls wide; light is black candles and a fire in the sanctum, chanting carries along the halls and hides small noises, the footing is smooth stone stained black before the altar, and the air is thick with incense, a lit fire changing nothing.

The high priest's chamber (M.5) lies 130 feet north of the stair-hall (M.1), 99 feet north-east of the cells (M.3) and 99 north-west of the refectory (M.4): a chamber 30 feet square with a ceiling of 15 feet, black hangings on every wall, censers smoking and a brazier glowing at its centre; Veck the Hollow (Named Creature: Veck the Hollow) burns incense there nine nights in ten with two zombies at the doors, a black wood cabinet beside the brazier holds a folded letter (Lore: The High Priest's Letter) and an iron coffer by it holds tribute goods, and a party can notice the east hanging soot-smeared. The sanctum (M.11) lies 60 feet north of the antechamber (M.10) behind a locked iron door (Keys: The Sanctum Key), 230 feet north of the stair-hall (M.1): a vast black hall 80 feet across with a ceiling of 40 feet, a fire of black flame burning on a stone altar at its centre, three acolytes tending it, a bronze lamp burning black flame on the altar's edge, and in the north wall a slab of makers' stone with a round recess at its centre; whispers fill the air, and a party can notice the cold of the black fire. The vault (M.12) lies 70 feet north of the sanctum (M.11) behind that slab (Keys: The Vault Key), 300 feet north of the stair-hall (M.1): a sealed room 30 feet square with a ceiling of 15 feet, eight iron-bound chests stacked two high against the walls with gold gleaming through their lids, rime on every edge, breath hanging white and the cold deepening near the gold, and a niche sealed in the wall with black wax.

The outer shrine (M.2) lies 60 feet north of the stair-hall (M.1) through the banded door: a vaulted hall 80 feet by 40 with a ceiling of 24 feet, an old black altar at its north end with a stone bowl brimming with fresh blood, a shelf cut in the rock behind it, and black candles in iron stands every ten feet down both walls. The cells (M.3) lie 70 feet west of the outer shrine (M.2): a passage 60 feet long and 5 wide with eight bare cells off it. The refectory (M.4) lies 70 feet east of the outer shrine (M.2): a vaulted hall 50 feet by 25 with a ceiling of 15 feet, long scrubbed tables and, at the far end, a pantry door with a green crust on its hinges. The crypt stair (M.6) leads 40 feet east of the outer shrine (M.2) and 40 feet down: a stair 40 feet long and 4 wide with a ceiling of 8 feet, turning once. The ossuary (M.7) lies 60 feet north of its foot, a hall 60 feet by 20 with a ceiling of 12 feet and bones stacked in ranked niches to the roof. The makers' niches (M.8) lie 50 feet west of the ossuary (M.7): a gallery 70 feet by 20 with a ceiling of 10 feet and four rows of empty burial niches, one cut deeper than the rest, and dust-free streaks running to the last niche in the south-west row, 60 feet from the passage landing (M.13). The priests' tomb (M.9) lies 50 feet north of the ossuary (M.7): a sealed tomb 30 feet across with a ceiling of 14 feet round a black stone sarcophagus whose carved priest's raised hand is worn bright, a stone door standing ajar in its south side and a stair climbing 40 feet west to the antechamber (M.10). The antechamber (M.10) lies 40 feet north of the high priest's chamber (M.5) through an archway: a chamber 20 feet by 10 with a ceiling of 14 feet, runes warm to the touch carved in a ring round the iron door at its north end, and the tomb stair rising into its east wall.

Inhabitants: The Black Altar (Faction: The Black Altar) holds the shrine. Three acolytes keep the landing of the ravine stair (E.9) with knives and a gong, hostile; two stand the barred door at M.1, wary, challenging and then barring the inner door, wanting no stranger past it and knowing who climbed at dusk; four rest at their prayers in the cells (M.3), wary, seizing knives and calling the shrine, and know the high priest's hours; three tend the black fire in the sanctum (M.11), hostile, striking, raising the dead and sounding the gong, and know the keep's wagon days. Veck the Hollow (Named Creature: Veck the Hollow) burns incense at his brazier in M.5 nine nights in ten, hostile, calling the dead at the first sound and speaking to a party only while they rise, wanting the tithe whole and the sanctum kept; he knows who the agents at the keep are. Two Zombies stand at the doors of M.5 and six in their niches in the ossuary (M.7), hostile, walking at the nearest living thing and never stopping, the six stepping out together. Bugbears of The Zurnaz (Faction: The Zurnaz) come up the back stair (K.4) to collect the tithe.

Alarm: The gong on the landing (E.9) or a hand in the altar's bowl (M.2) raises it. The acolytes at M.1 hear first; the four at M.3 reach the outer shrine (M.2) in a turn, Veck at M.5 hears a turn later and raises a zombie each Round from the dead, and the six in the ossuary (M.7) climb the crypt stair in two turns. A pull on Mort's bell-cord (K.5) rings the bell at M.14, and acolytes reach it within a turn. If the sanctum is entered, Veck knows by the time the party leaves.

Loot: The shrine's wealth is every tribe's tithe, kept in its vault. The largest prize is the Altar's Hoard (Hoard: The Altar's Hoard) in the eight chests at M.12, behind the vault door (Keys: The Vault Key) and a ward of cold, with Treasure V in the niche in the vault wall; the makers' lamp (Unique Treasure: The Thalosae Lamp) burns on the altar at M.11 under three acolytes, the founder's reliquary (Unique Treasure: The Founder's Reliquary) lies on the shelf behind the altar at M.2, the black psalter (Magical Tome: The Black Psalter) under the sarcophagus lid at M.9, Treasure I on the pantry shelves at M.4, Treasure III in the skull niches at M.7 and Treasure IV in the coffer at M.5. The Moneychanger (A.7) buys the hoard back at 80% of value, and the Chapel of Law (A.9) pays 300 cn for the reliquary.

Secrets:
- A black hanging in the high priest's chamber hides the door to the bugbears' back stair - Veck and the Zurnaz know; the soot on the cloth (M.5) brings it out.
- The back of the emptiest niche opens on the maze's passage landing - nobody knows; the dust-free streaks on the floor (M.8) bring it out.
- Pressing the carved hand slides the priest's sarcophagus lid aside - the dead priests; the worn fingers (M.9) bring it out.
- A hand in the altar's bowl tells Veck a stranger is within - the acolytes; the fresh blood in the bowl (M.2) brings it out.
- A voice raised by the antechamber door blinds the speaker - the runes (M.10) bring it out.
- Lifting coin from the vault's chests drains the lifter - the rime on the gold (M.12) brings it out.
