Layout: The shrine is cut into the cliff above the maze at the ravine's head. A stair of fifty steps from the ravine (E.9) climbs north to the stair-hall (M.1), with the outer shrine (M.2) 60 feet north, the cells (M.3) 70 feet west and the refectory (M.4) 70 feet east. The high priest's chamber (M.5) lies 90 feet north of the cells and refectory, with the back stair door (M.14) 40 feet east of it; the antechamber (M.10) is 50 feet north of the chamber, the sanctum (M.11) 60 feet beyond it and the vault (M.12) 70 feet further. The crypts lie below: the crypt stair (M.6) is 40 feet down from the shrine's east end, the ossuary (M.7) 60 feet north of it, the niches (M.8) 50 feet west and the priests' tomb (M.9) 50 feet north, which climbs 30 feet to the antechamber. The passage landing (M.13) is 60 feet south-west of the niches, 120 feet above the maze's lair (L.5), and the back stair runs 120 feet down south-west from M.14 to the bugbears' den (K.4).

Places:
- M.1 The Stair-Hall - the way in from the ravine; two acolytes and a barred inner door.
- M.2 The Outer Shrine - the altar and its bowl, and the reliquary on a shelf behind it.
- M.3 The Acolytes' Cells - four acolytes resting.
- M.4 The Refectory - long tables and a pantry door over a gas jar.
- M.5 The High Priest's Chamber - Veck the Hollow, two zombies, a letter and a coffer.
- M.6 The Crypt Stair - the way down.
- M.7 The Ossuary - six zombies in their niches.
- M.8 The Makers' Niches - empty niches and the last maker's epitaph.
- M.9 The Priests' Tomb - a sarcophagus and a psalter.
- M.10 The Antechamber - runes round an iron door.
- M.11 The Sanctum - the black fire, three acolytes, a lamp.
- M.12 The Vault - eight chests under rime.
- M.13 The Passage Landing - the stair down to the maze.
- M.14 The Back Stair Door - a bell and a plastered door.

Inhabitants: Three of The Black Altar on the ravine stair, two at M.1, four at M.3 and three at M.11, Veck the Hollow at M.5, two Zombies at M.5 and six Zombies at M.7.

Alarm: The gong on the landing (E.9) or a hand in the altar's bowl (M.2) raises it. The acolytes at M.1 hear first; the four at M.3 reach the outer shrine (M.2) in a turn, Veck at M.5 hears a turn later and raises a zombie each Round from the dead, and the six in the ossuary (M.7) climb the crypt stair in two turns. A pull on Mort's bell-cord (K.5) rings the bell at M.14, and acolytes reach it within a turn. If the sanctum is entered, Veck knows by the time the party leaves.

Loot: The largest prize is the Altar's Hoard in the chests at M.12, behind a ward of cold, with Treasure V in the vault wall; the makers' lamp lies at M.11, the reliquary at M.2, the psalter at M.9, and Treasure I in the pantry at M.4, Treasure III at M.7 and Treasure IV at M.5. A.7 buys the hoard at 80%, and A.9 pays 300 cn for the reliquary.

Secrets:
- A black hanging in the high priest's chamber hides the door to the bugbears' back stair - Veck and the Zurnaz know; the soot on the cloth (M.5) brings it out.
- The back of the emptiest niche opens on the maze's passage landing - nobody knows; the dust-free streaks on the floor (M.8) bring it out.
- Pressing the carved hand slides the priest's sarcophagus lid aside - the dead priests; the worn fingers (M.9) bring it out.
- A hand in the altar's bowl tells Veck a stranger is within - the acolytes; the fresh blood in the bowl (M.2) brings it out.
- A voice raised by the antechamber door blinds the speaker - the runes (M.10) bring it out.
- Lifting coin from the vault's chests drains the lifter - the rime on the gold (M.12) brings it out.
