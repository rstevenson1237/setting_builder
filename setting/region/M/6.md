M.6 **The Crypt Stair** (Low) - *dark, worn, cold*
A narrow **stair** of worn steps goes down into the dark, turning once, the air colder at each tread. Grave **dust** is scuffed by many feet.
*Stair 40 ft long, 4 ft wide, ceiling 8 ft. No light but the party's.*
**Stair:** Makers' cut stone, worn hollow in the middle of each step.
**Dust:** Grey grave dust, scuffed from the stair's foot to the shrine above.
**Exits:** archway, west, up 40 feet -> M.2 The Outer Shrine, archway, north end of the stair, 60 feet -> M.7 The Ossuary
