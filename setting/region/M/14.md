M.14 **The Back Stair Door** (Low) - *plastered, narrow, listening*
A narrow landing at the stair's head, a plastered **door** beside a **bell** hung on a rope. Soot **handprints** cover the wall at head height.
*Landing 8 ft by 6 ft, ceiling 8 ft. The bell's rope runs down the stair; it rings when pulled from the den below.*
**Door:** Plaster over wood, soot-smeared; it opens into the high priest's chamber behind a black hanging.
**Bell:** Bronze, on a rope that drops down the stair to the bugbears' den.
**Handprints:** Soot, thick round the door's edge and the bell.
**Exits:** wooden, west side of the landing, 40 feet -> M.5 The High Priest's Chamber, stone, south-west, down 120 feet -> K.4 The Back Stair
