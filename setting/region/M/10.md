M.10 **The Antechamber** (Medium) - *narrow, runed, silent*
A narrow chamber, silent as a held breath, with **runes** carved in a ring round an **iron door** at its north end. No sound echoes back.
*Chamber 20 ft by 10 ft, ceiling 14 ft. The tomb stair rises into its east wall.*
**Runes:** Makers' script, warm to the touch; a voice raised near them flashes white and blinds (Test of Fate, Blinded).
**Iron Door:** Black iron, no handle, with a flat keyhole at its centre; the sanctum lies beyond (Keys: The Sanctum Key).
**Exits:** archway, south end of the chamber, 40 feet -> M.5 The High Priest's Chamber, stone, east, down 40 feet -> M.9 The Priests' Tomb, iron, locked, north end of the chamber, 60 feet -> M.11 The Sanctum
