M.13 **The Passage Landing** (Low) - *dust-thick, narrow, quiet*
A narrow landing at the head of a **stair**, dust lying thick and unmarked on the floor. A makers' **door** of stone stands at the east side.
*Landing 10 ft square, ceiling 8 ft. The stair runs down to the minotaur's lair.*
**Stair:** Makers' stone, wide, cut square to one pattern, going down into the dark.
**Door:** Stone, carved with a line of script, closing on the niches beyond.
**Dust:** Undisturbed except for one long trail from the stair to the door.
**Exits:** archway, south end of the landing, down a hundred and twenty feet -> L.5 The Minotaur's Lair, stone, east side of the landing -> M.8 The Makers' Niches
