M.8 **The Makers' Niches** (Low) - *carved, empty, dusty*
Four rows of empty burial **niches** run the length of a long gallery, each cut with a line of the makers' script. One **niche** is cut deeper than the rest.
*Gallery 70 ft by 20 ft, ceiling 10 ft. The makers' dead were taken away centuries ago.*
**Niches:** A hundred or more, long enough for a body, dust-filled and bare, the rows' ends shut by sealed doors of makers' stone.
**Inscribed Niche:** The deepest, with script cut round its mouth in a single hand (Lore: The Last Niche).
**Emptiest Niche:** The last in the south-west row; dust-free streaks run to it, and its back swings in when pressed onto the passage landing (M.13 The Passage Landing), 60 feet south-west.
**Exits:** open, east end of the gallery, 50 feet -> M.7 The Ossuary
