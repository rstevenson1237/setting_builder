M.1 **The Stair-Hall** (Medium) - *cold, stained, watched*
A cold hall at the stair's head, its floor stained black before a barred **inner door**. Two **acolytes** in black robes stand beside a **candle** stub burning on the flags.
*Hall 40 ft square, ceiling 18 ft. The inner door is barred from within at night.*
**Acolytes:** Two with knives, who challenge anyone who climbs, then bar the inner door; they know who climbed at dusk.
**Inner Door:** Banded oak in a stone frame, barred on the shrine side from dusk.
**Candle:** A black stub on the floor, burned to a pool of wax.
**Stains:** Black, spreading from the door across the flags.
**Exits:** archway, south end of the hall, down fifty steps -> E.9 The Shrine Stair, banded, north side of the hall, 60 feet -> M.2 The Outer Shrine
