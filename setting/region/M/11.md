M.11 **The Sanctum** (High) - *black, firelit, vast*
A vast black hall eighty feet across, a **fire** of black flame burning on a stone **altar** at its centre, whispers filling the air. **Acolytes** tend the fire in black robes.
*Hall 80 ft by 80 ft, ceiling 40 ft.*
**Altar:** A black stone block, the fire burning on its top; all who pass the doorway are asked a Test of Sanity (Test of Sanity, 1d).
**Acolytes:** Three feed the fire in black robes, and at a stranger they strike, raise the dead and sound the gong. They know the keep's wagon days.
**Lamp:** Bronze, on the altar's edge, burning a black flame without oil (Unique Treasure: The Thalosae Lamp).
**Vault Door:** A slab of makers' stone in the north wall, a round recess at its centre; the lamp's light shows its seam (Keys: The Vault Key).
**Exits:** iron, south end of the hall, 60 feet -> M.10 The Antechamber, stone, locked, north wall, 70 feet -> M.12 The Vault
