M.5 **The High Priest's Chamber** (High) - *hung, incensed, guarded*
A chamber with black **hangings** on every wall, censers smoking, a **brazier** glowing at its centre. **Veck** burns incense there, two **Zombies** standing at the doors.
*Chamber 30 ft square, ceiling 15 ft. Veck is here nine nights in ten; the hangings hide a door.*
**Veck the Hollow:** Calls the dead at the first sound and speaks to the party only while they rise. He knows who the agents at the keep are (Named Creature: Veck the Hollow).
**Zombies:** Two stand at the doors and walk at the nearest living thing without stopping (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Zombie).
**Cabinet:** Black wood, beside the brazier, a folded letter inside (Lore: The High Priest's Letter).
**Coffer:** Iron, warm from the brazier, packed with tribute goods (Treasure IV, d20).
**Hangings:** Black cloth on every wall; one, on the east side, is soot-smeared, and pushing it aside shows a plastered door.
**Exits:** wooden, south-west of the chamber -> M.3 The Acolytes' Cells, wooden, south-east of the chamber -> M.4 The Refectory, archway, north side of the chamber -> M.10 The Antechamber, hidden, behind the hanging, east -> M.14 The Back Stair Door
