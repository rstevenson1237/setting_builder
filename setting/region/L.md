L The Maze

Overview: A maze of makers' passages in the cliff's foot at the ravine's head, kept by one Minotaur, Tanglehorn the Keeper, with the way up to the shrine running out of its far end. A party comes for the key the Minotaur holds and the way through. The Minotaur wanders the passages and does not leave them. If a party is lost in the maze for six turns, the Minotaur finds it; if the door is forced, the Zurnaz at the dry fork are told.

Approach: One door at the foot of the cliff off the ravine's head (E.8), barred with a lock and watched by the Minotaur. A makers' passage runs from the maze's far end up to the shrine.

Conditions: Makers' cut stone, passages 6 feet wide and ceilings 10 feet, every turn alike. No light but what the party carries. Sound loses its direction: a bellow seems to come from all sides. Footing is dry and worn smooth. The air is cold and dry, and a lit fire gives light but no smoke.

Entrances:
- L.1 - A vestibule behind the door, barred on this side by an oak bar in iron brackets and locked on both, an archway opening into the dark passages.

Prizes:
- L.5 - Through an archway beyond the statue hall: a high bare cave round a stinking nest of rags and bone, deep horn-marks at shoulder height, a bronze plaque in the floor by the nest and a wide stone stair climbing 120 feet to the shrine (M.13).

Places:
- L.2 - Narrow passages of smooth stone worn glassy, chalk scratches and a frayed cord end at the first turn, and one floor flag clean of dust at its edges.
- L.3 - A round hall of ten robed statues and six identical archways round a lamp-bearer holding an unlit lamp aloft, a scuffed ring on the floor round its base.
- L.4 - Packs, bones and bright scraps heaped along both walls, a broad trampled path through the dust leading to the lair.

Tables:
Danger, 6 noticing to 1 acting

5. Fresh gouges at shoulder height scar the wall.
4. The floor sounds hollow in the passage ahead (L.2).
3. Hoofbeats sound close by and fade; the party may hide or move.
