L The Maze

Kind: single holding
Overview: A maze of makers' passages in the cliff's foot at the ravine's head, kept by one Minotaur, a Named Creature, and what it has collected. The way up to the shrine runs out of its far end. A party comes for the key the Minotaur holds and the way through.
Approach: One door opens at the foot of the cliff off the ravine's head, barred with a lock. The Minotaur watches it. A makers' passage runs from the maze's far end up to the shrine.
Conditions: Makers' cut stone, passages 6 feet wide and ceilings 10 feet, every turn alike. No light but what the party carries. Sound loses its direction: a bellow seems to come from all sides. Footing is dry and worn smooth. The air is cold and dry, and a lit fire gives light but no smoke.
Situation: The Minotaur wanders the passages and does not leave them. If a party is lost in the maze for a watch, the Minotaur finds it. If the door is forced, the Zurnaz at the dry fork are told.
