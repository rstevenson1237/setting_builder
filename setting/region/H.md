H Orc Warren

Overview: The Rokmok's warren of gutted makers' halls at the head of the west wall, with pens, a feast hall and a still. A party comes for the chief's hoard, or to carry the goblins' stores through to the orcs. The chief's captains want his seat. If the chief is killed or leaves, they fight one another before they turn on any party; if the goblins attack, the whole tribe comes out to the ledge.

Approach: One mouth behind the orc ledge (E.4), a cairn of skulls before it and two Orcs on watch day and night. A makers' passage runs from the stores toward the goblins' hold; the Rokmok have walled their end and think it ends there.

Conditions: Gutted makers' halls of cut stone, ceilings 12 feet and rooms wide and square. Light is torches in iron brackets. Sound is lost in the big halls. Footing is slick with spilled brew and offal. The air reeks of smoke and meat, and a lit fire is no alarm here.

Entrances:
- H.1 - Skulls heaped to a man's height beside its door, rib bones across the floor and a notched tusk nailed over the arch at its back.

Prizes:
- H.6 - Through a banded door beyond the feast hall: skulls and axes hung round a fire pit, a bed heaped against the far wall with an iron-bound chest under it, and a rack of notched trophy axes.

Places:
- H.2 - Long tables down its length, spits turning at both ends, spoils heaped behind the high table and a notched tusk over each of its five doors.
- H.3 - A stone passage of four plank pens, boar squealing in three and a trough in the last.
- H.4 - Through an archway: four copper vats on brick hearths fired from below, a coil of tubing, and a sharp reek and scorched stone round the largest vat's tap.
- H.5 - Through a broken door: sacks along both walls, a makers' door smashed from its frame at the near end and square-cut stone at the far end, a cold draught under the sacks.
