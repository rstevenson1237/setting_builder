H Orc Warren

Kind: single holding
Overview: The Rokmok's warren at the head of the west wall: Orcs in gutted halls, with pens, a feast hall and a still. Its chief is a Named Creature. A party comes for the chief's hoard, or to carry the goblins' stores through to the orcs. The chief's captains want his seat. If the chief is killed or leaves, the captains fight one another before they turn on any party. If the goblins attack, the whole tribe comes out to the ledge.
Approach: One mouth opens on the ravine's west wall, a cairn of skulls before it and two Orcs on watch day and night. A makers' passage runs from the stores toward the goblins' hold; the Rokmok have walled their end and think it ends there.
Conditions: Gutted makers' halls of cut stone, ceilings 12 feet and rooms wide and square. Light is torches in iron brackets. Sound is lost in the big halls, and carries up the stairs. Footing is slick with spilled brew and offal. The air reeks of smoke and meat, and a lit fire is no alarm here.
