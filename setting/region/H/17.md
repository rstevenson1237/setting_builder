H.17 **The Drain** (Low) - *narrow, slick, reeking*

A sloping channel of fouled water runs down a narrow passage to a grating. Water trickles and rats squeak.

*A passage 10 feet wide and 40 long, 8 high, from the cistern to the captives' pen.*

**The Channel:** Ankle-deep, warm, stinking of blood from the cistern.
**The Grating:** Iron, at the low end, an iron door beside it to the pen.

**Exits:** a broken door -> H.6 The Offering Cistern, an iron door -> H.19 The Captives' Pen
