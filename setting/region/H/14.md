H.14 **The Smoke Room** (Medium) - *sealed, sooted, choking*

Censers hang on chains round a central drain, the walls black to the waist. Smoke hisses slowly from them.

*A room 20 feet square where the cult breathes its incense before the rites. When the door swings shut, the censers pour smoke until the sealed room chokes (Test of Constitution, 3d, Poison). The cult props the door with a wedge kept by it.*

**The Censers:** Bronze, on chains, a black resin smouldering in each.
**The Soot Line:** Waist high round the walls, where the smoke settles.
**The Wedge:** Wooden, by the door, worn by use; set under the door, it keeps it open.

**Exits:** a wooden door -> H.3 The Sacrifice Kitchens
