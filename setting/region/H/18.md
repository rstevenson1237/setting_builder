H.18 **The Hall of Hands** (Medium) - *narrow, grasping, black*

Black hands carved from both walls reach into a narrow hall. Stone creaks like knuckles.

*A hall 10 feet wide and 40 long. The hands close on whoever walks it without showing the cult's sign, and hold (Test of Fate, Held). A black hand shown, painted or worn, stills them. A lidded ash bin at the far end hides darts in its lid.*

**The Hands:** Polished at the wrist by use; they flex as a party passes.
**The Ash Door:** Wooden, at the end, a lidded bin beside it. Lifting the lid fires darts (Test of Constitution, 1d, Piercing). The cult knows it.

**Exits:** a crude opening -> H.13 The Weeping Gallery, a wooden door -> H.20 The Ash Store
