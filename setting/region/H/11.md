H.11 **The Scriptorium** (High) - *inked, locked, dim*

Inked scrolls and slates lie on tables in rows, a locked cabinet at the back. No one is writing.

*A hall 40 feet by 30. The cult's records; skeletons stand among the tables.*

**The Skeletons:** Stand among the tables (Hostile, 4-8, Bestiary : Skeleton).
**The Cabinet:** Locked and ticking. Opening it, a reader meets a page of the black speech that will not leave the eye (Test of Sanity, 2d). It holds the cult's ledger (Lore: The Cult's Ledger), and a slate chalked with one word, *vox*, "black", the word the lieutenants' gargoyles answer to (Keys: The Lieutenants' Word).

**Exits:** an iron door -> H.8 The Pit of Captives, a stone door -> H.9 The Hall of Masks
