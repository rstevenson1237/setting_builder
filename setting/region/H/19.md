H.19 **The Captives' Pen** (High) - *packed, barred, pleading*

Bars cross the middle of a packed room, masked captives pressed against them. They plead in broken speech.

*A room 30 feet square where the cult keeps the captives taken from the lake city (The Living Go Down to Dream). Two are taken to the altar each day.*

**The Captives:** Masked, dazed, hungry (Fearful, 6-10, Bestiary : Masked Dreamer). They want freedom and the lake, and know the cult's rounds and when the guards change.
**The Gate:** Iron, barred from this side, a black hand painted on it (Faction : Voxkroth).
**The Trinkets:** Hidden in the straw, what the captives kept back (Treasure II, d20).

**Exits:** an iron door to the drain -> H.17 The Drain, an iron door to the pit -> H.8 The Pit of Captives
