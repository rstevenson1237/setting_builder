H.7 **The Altar of Bones** (High) - *stacked, black, loud*

Bones are stacked round a black altar on a dais, a drum and a chant filling the hall. The altar is wet.

*A hall 50 feet by 40 and 24 high, the old feast hall (The Demon Is Invited). The cult feeds captives to the demon here; the chant rises if a cultist is killed.*

**The Cultists:** Eight chant round the altar (Hostile, 8, Faction : Voxkroth). They want a captive fed and answer an alarm within a turn.
**The Altar:** Black stone, wet with stains. Shedding blood on it makes the demon's thirst fill the hall.
**The Offerings:** The altar is hollow and holds the cult's gifts (Hoard: The Altar's Offerings).
**The Bowl:** A bronze bowl on the dais holds coin (Treasure III, d20).

**Exits:** an open passage, west, 70 feet -> H.4 The Cells, a crude door, north, 70 feet -> H.8 The Pit of Captives
