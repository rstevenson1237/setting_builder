H.1 **The Stair Landing** (Low) - *black, close, smoking*

A black landing, smoke clinging to the roof. A black hand is painted on the wall, and a chant comes faintly from the east.

*A landing 10 feet by 20 feet. The stair from G.19 comes down 24 feet; its arch at the top locked behind the party, and the queen's ring opens it again. A second stair climbs 64 feet north-west to the lake city (B.10), where a bronze-faced door is barred on the far side.*

**The Black Hand:** Painted on the wall to claim it (Faction : Voxkroth).
**The Stairs:** Two rise from here. The one to the tombs ends at a bone lock-plate cut with a sunburst (Keys: The Queen's Ring). The black one ends at a door that only a dreamer on the far side unbars; a party can knock, or break it in a turn and every dreamer hears.

**Exits:** an open passage -> H.2 The Black Corridor, a wooden door -> H.10 The Cult Guard Room, up a stair -> G.19 The Stair Down, up a stair -> B.10 The Black Stair
