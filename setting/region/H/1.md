H.1 **The Cairn Mouth** (Medium) - *skull-strewn, stinking, watched*
A square hall door opens under a **cairn** of skulls, a notched **tusk** nailed above the arch inside. Two **Orcs** drink on the watch, axes across their knees.
*Hall mouth 30 ft square and 40 ft deep; roars here carry to the feast hall.*
**Cairn:** Skulls heaped to a man's height beside the door, human and tribal alike.
**Tusk:** Nailed above the arch at the hall's back.
**Orcs:** Two keep the watch day and night, drinking, and roar for the hall before they charge (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Orc). They know which captain relieves them.
**Rib Bones:** Gnawed and scattered across the floor.
**Exits:** open, east, 20 feet -> E.4 The Orc Ledge, archway, west end of the mouth, 60 feet -> H.2 The Feast Hall
