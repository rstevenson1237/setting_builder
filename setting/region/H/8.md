H.8 **The Pit of Captives** (Medium) - *deep, fouled, moaning*

A deep pit in the floor is covered by an iron grating, moaning rising from below. The stink is foul.

*A room 30 feet square. The pit holds the captives the altar has finished with, for the ghouls.*

**The Grating:** Iron, bolted down; it lifts with a bar.
**The Ghouls:** Feed on the captives below (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Ghoul). The moaning stops when they look up.

**Exits:** a crude door -> H.7 The Altar of Bones, an iron door -> H.11 The Scriptorium, an iron door to the pen -> H.19 The Captives' Pen
