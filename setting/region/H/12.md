H.12 **The Descent Landing** (Low) - *steep, cold, dark*

A steep cold stair goes down from a landing, warm air rising. A black hand is painted on the wall.

*A landing 10 feet by 20 feet. The stair goes down 20 feet to I.1.*

**The Stair:** Steep and dark; the warm air smells of smoke.

**Exits:** an open passage -> H.4 The Cells, down a stair -> I.1 The Stair Landing
