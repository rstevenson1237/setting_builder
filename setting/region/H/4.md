H.4 **The Still** (Medium) - *hot, reeking, dripping*
Copper **vats** stand along one wall of a low hall, a coil of **tubing** dripping into a trough, the air hot and sweet. A **sump** lies under the largest vat.
*Hall 40 ft by 30 ft, ceiling 10 ft. No torch burns here; the orcs fire the vats from below with coals.*
**Vats:** Four coppers on brick hearths, the largest filled with brew strong enough to take fire; open it near a flame and it bursts into one (Test of Constitution, 2d, Fire). A sharp reek and scorched stone round its tap give it away.
**Tubing:** Copper, coiled through a barrel of cold water, dripping spirit.
**Sump:** A stone pit under the largest vat, thick with dregs, a bag sunk in the bottom (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** archway, north side of the hall, 40 feet -> H.2 The Feast Hall, broken, west end of the hall, 50 feet -> H.5 The Walled Stores
