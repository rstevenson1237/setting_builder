H.4 **The Cells** (Low) - *barred, wet, packed*

Barred cells line a passage, wet straw on every floor. Weeping and coughing carry from within.

*A passage 30 feet by 40 with eight cells. The cult holds captives from the lake city here for the altar (Faction : Voxkroth).*

**The Captives:** Masked people of the lake city, bound and dazed; they beg to be freed. Freeing one brings the cult in pursuit.
**The Bars:** Iron, set in wet stone; one breaks under a crowbar in a turn.

**Exits:** a crude door -> H.2 The Black Corridor, a wooden door -> H.5 The Chosen's Quarters, an open passage -> H.7 The Altar of Bones, an open passage to the stair -> H.12 The Descent Landing, a crude door -> H.15 The Shackle Room
