H.6 **The Chief's Cave** (High) - *bone-hung, firelit, guarded*
A cave hall hung with **skulls** and **axes**, a fire pit at its centre and a heaped **bed** against the far wall. **Hurk** drinks at the fire with four **Orcs** sprawled round him.
*Cave 40 ft by 40 ft, ceiling 14 ft. Hurk is here three nights in four.*
**Hurk the Brewmaster:** Bellows for the hall at the first threat, then fights to hold his seat and offers nothing. He knows which captain schemes against him (Named Creature: Hurk the Brewmaster).
**Orcs:** Four round the fire pit, who guard him until he falls and then bolt (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Orc).
**Chest:** Iron-bound, under the bed (Hoard: Hurk's Hoard).
**Axe Rack:** Notched trophy axes on a rack of bone; one is not like the rest (Magic, d12).
**Skulls:** Hung from the roof by thongs, each with a tusk notch cut in the brow.
**Exits:** banded, east end of the cave -> H.2 The Feast Hall
