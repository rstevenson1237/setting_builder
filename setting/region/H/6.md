H.6 **The Offering Cistern** (Medium) - *dark, still, red*

A square basin of red water sits in a stepped floor, water lapping softly. The air smells of iron.

**The Dead:** Rise from the water (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Zombie). They move first as a ripple.
