H.2 **The Black Corridor** (Low) - *straight, sooty, watched*

A straight sooty corridor runs on, black hands painted on its walls. A footfall is lost in the chant.

*A corridor 10 feet wide and 50 long.*

**The Hands:** Painted black every ten paces, palm out, the cult's claim (Faction : Voxkroth).
**The Soot:** Thick on the roof; a torch leaves a trail on it.

**Exits:** an open passage -> H.1 The Stair Landing, an open passage -> H.3 The Sacrifice Kitchens, a crude door -> H.4 The Cells
