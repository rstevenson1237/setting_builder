H.2 **The Feast Hall** (Medium) - *huge, greasy, roaring*
Long **tables** run down a vast hall lit by iron-bracketed torches, **spits** turning at both ends. **Orcs** quarrel over the meat, and a notched **tusk** is nailed over each of the five doors.
*Hall 80 ft by 40 ft, ceiling 12 ft. Noise here hides small sounds, and a lit fire is nothing new.*
**Orcs:** Ten at the tables with axes to hand. They brawl one another first and rush an intruder as one, and they know when the goblins loose their wolves (Hostile, 10, Bestiary : Orc).
**Tables:** Axe-notched planks on trestles, wet with ale.
**Spits:** Boar and mutton turning over pits, tended by whoever lost the last quarrel.
**Tusks:** Notched, one over each door.
**Spoils:** Heaped behind the high table, loot from the ledge path (Treasure II, d20).
**Exits:** archway, east end of the hall -> H.1 The Cairn Mouth, wooden, north side of the hall -> H.3 The Pens, archway, south side of the hall -> H.4 The Still, banded, west end of the hall -> H.6 The Chief's Cave
