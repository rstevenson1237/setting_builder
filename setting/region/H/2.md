H.2 **The Black Corridor** (Low) - *straight, sooty, watched*

A straight sooty corridor runs east, black hands painted on its walls. A footfall is lost in the chant.

*A corridor 8 feet wide and 70 long.*

**The Hands:** A black hand every ten paces (Faction : Voxkroth).
**The Soot:** Thick on the roof; a torch leaves a trail on it.

**Exits:** an open passage, west, 70 feet -> H.1 The Stair Landing, an open passage, north, 70 feet -> H.3 The Sacrifice Kitchens, a crude door, east, 70 feet -> H.4 The Cells
