H.16 **The Cult Dormitory** (Medium) - *crowded, snoring, black*

Pallets lie in four rows, black robes on pegs above them. Snoring and a muttered prayer fill the room.

*A room 30 feet by 20 where the cult sleeps between rites. An alarm brings them out within a turn.*

**The Robes:** Black, a hand stitched on each breast (Faction : Voxkroth); one worn passes at a glance.
