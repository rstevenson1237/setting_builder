H.3 **The Pens** (Low) - *foul, barred, loud*
Four plank **pens** line a stone passage, **boar** squealing in the first three, straw and dung ankle-deep. A **trough** stands in the last pen, empty of swill.
*Passage 60 ft long. The squealing hides small noises.*
**Pens:** Plank, bar-latched; the boar crash against the boards at a stranger and break out if the latches are lifted.
**Trough:** Cut stone, caked with swill; a bag lies under the crust (Treasure I, d20).
**Straw:** Wet and rank, deep in the corners.
**Exits:** wooden, south end of the passage, 50 feet -> H.2 The Feast Hall
