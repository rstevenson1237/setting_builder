H.3 **The Sacrifice Kitchens** (Medium) - *greasy, bloodied, hot*

Greasy slabs and cauldrons stand under a smoke-black roof, blood on the floor. Fat spits somewhere.

*A room 40 feet by 30. The cult cooks its offerings here.*

**The Cauldrons:** Five, of iron, on fires that never go out. Stirring one throws burning fat (Test of Constitution, 1d, Fire).
**The Slabs:** Greasy and stained; blood in the grooves.

**Exits:** an open passage -> H.2 The Black Corridor, an archway -> H.5 The Chosen's Quarters, an open passage to the cistern -> H.6 The Offering Cistern, an open passage to the guards -> H.10 The Cult Guard Room, a wooden door -> H.14 The Smoke Room
