H.3 **The Sacrifice Kitchens** (Medium) - *greasy, bloodied, hot*

Greasy slabs and cauldrons stand under a smoke-black roof, blood on the floor. Fat spits somewhere.

*A room 40 feet by 30. The cult cooks its offerings here.*

**The Cauldrons:** Five iron cauldrons on fires that never go out. Stirring one throws burning fat (Test of Constitution, 1d, Fire).
**The Slabs:** Greasy and stained; blood in the grooves.
