H.13 **The Weeping Gallery** (Medium) - *narrow, wailing, damp*

A narrow gallery is carved with weeping faces, damp running down them. A thin wailing carries along it.

*A gallery 8 feet by 70.*

**The Faces:** Carved, tear-streaked, set at head height.
**The Stirges:** Cling to the carved faces (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Stirge). Their wings tick against the wet stone.

**Exits:** a crude door -> H.10 The Cult Guard Room
