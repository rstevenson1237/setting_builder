Layout: The warren runs west into the rock from its mouth on the ravine's west wall. The cairn mouth (H.1) lies at the mouth, with the feast hall (H.2) 40 feet west, the pens (H.3) 50 feet north of the hall, the still (H.4) 40 feet south, the walled stores (H.5) 50 feet west of the still, and the chief's cave (H.6) 70 feet west of the hall.

Places:
- H.1 The Cairn Mouth - the way in; two orcs drinking on the watch.
- H.2 The Feast Hall - ten orcs at the long tables, spoils behind the high table.
- H.3 The Pens - boar in plank pens.
- H.4 The Still - copper vats, one of them liable to burst into flame.
- H.5 The Walled Stores - sacks, and a wall of plain stones over a makers' passage.
- H.6 The Chief's Cave - Hurk the Brewmaster, four orcs, a chest and an axe rack.

Inhabitants: Three of The Rokmok on the ledge below, two Orcs at H.1, ten of The Rokmok at H.2, and Hurk the Brewmaster with four Orcs at H.6.

Alarm: A roar at H.1 raises it. The feast hall (H.2) hears at once and brawls for a Round before it rushes the mouth; Hurk in H.6 hears a Round later, and his bellow brings the hall's orcs to his cave in two Rounds. The stores (H.5) hear nothing.

Loot: The largest prize is Hurk's Hoard in the chest under his bed at H.6, with the trophy axes beside it (Magic); Treasure II lies at H.2, Treasure I at H.3, Treasure III under the vat at H.4 and Treasure IV at H.5. A.7 buys it at 80%.

Secrets:
- The largest vat holds spirit that bursts into flame near a fire - the Rokmok know; the sharp reek and scorched stone round it (H.4) bring it out.
- The lowest stones at the end of the stores lift out onto a makers' passage to the goblins' stores - neither tribe knows; the draught under the sacks (H.5) brings it out.

Leads:
- Rumour 3 - H.6 Hurk the Brewmaster.
- Rumour 4 - H.5 End Wall.
