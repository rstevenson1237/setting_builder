H.5 **The Chosen's Quarters** (Medium) - *dim, masked, whispering*

Narrow beds stand in three dim rooms, masks hung on pegs and a whisper in the dark.

*Three rooms 40 feet by 40 in all. The cult's chosen sleep here and answer an alarm within a turn.*

**The Masks:** Black and bronze, hung on pegs above each bed.
**The Chosen:** Whisper in the dark (Hostile, 4, Faction : Voxkroth). They want the keys found and strike a stranger without a word.

**Exits:** an archway, west, 70 feet -> H.3 The Sacrifice Kitchens, a wooden door, south, 70 feet -> H.4 The Cells, an archway, north, 70 feet -> H.9 The Hall of Masks
