H.5 **The Walled Stores** (Low) - *dusty, walled, undisturbed*
**Sacks** of grain and salt are stacked along a long narrow store, cold stone at its end. A makers' door, smashed from its frame, stands open at the near end.
*Room 50 ft by 10 ft. The makers' passage once ran on; the orcs walled their end and think it stops here.*
**Sacks:** Stacked to the roof along both walls; one stack hides a bag among the grain (Treasure IV, d20).
**End Wall:** Plain square-cut stone with a cold draught under the sacks; the lowest stones lift out, and a passage runs on behind, south to the goblins' stores (G.5 The Goblin Stores).
**Door:** Makers' stone, hanging off one hinge, carved with their script.
**Exits:** broken, east end of the room -> H.4 The Still
