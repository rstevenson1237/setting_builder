H.9 **The Hall of Masks** (High) - *hung, tall, watched*

Bronze masks hang to the roof in rows, their faces turned to the door. Faint whispers rise from them.

*A hall 60 feet by 50 and 24 high (The Demon Is Invited). The masks watch; the cult searches the hall for the keys.*

**The Masks:** Hundreds of bronze faces. Meeting their eyes costs the mind (Test of Sanity, 2d).
**The Dais:** A hollow behind the masks holds the cult's store (Treasure IV, d20).

**Exits:** an archway -> H.5 The Chosen's Quarters, a stone door -> H.11 The Scriptorium, an open passage -> H.6 The Offering Cistern
