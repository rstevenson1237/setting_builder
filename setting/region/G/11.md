G.11 **The Priests' Vestry** (Low) - *robed, cold, cramped*

Robes hang on pegs round a basin, benches in the middle. A sun-disc is painted over the door.

*A room 20 feet by 30, the clergy's robing room (Faction : Ruhiahe).*

**The Robes:** Pale linen, moth-eaten; a priest's robe lets a wearer pass the Ruhiahe halls.
**The Basin:** Dry, with a bronze bell at its edge.

**Exits:** an archway -> G.3 The Priestesses' Hall, a wooden door -> G.18 The Clerics' Cells
