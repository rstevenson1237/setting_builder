G.28 **The Hall of Masks** (Medium) - *hung, powdered, stifling*

Death masks hang in rows on three walls, white powder heaped on the floor beneath. Powder sifts down with every step.

*A room 30 feet by 20, where the masks of the dead were cast. Taking down a mask brings down a cloud of embalming powder (Test of Fate, Blinded). The Priesthood know to wet a cloth first.*

**The Masks:** Plaster, each a face of the dead; none is gold.
**The Powder:** Fine and white, heaped knee-deep at the walls.

**Exits:** an archway -> G.4 The Embalmers' Room, an archway hung with masks -> G.20 The Hall of the Lake Doors
