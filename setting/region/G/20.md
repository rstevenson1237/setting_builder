G.20 **The Hall of the Lake Doors** (High) - *vast, blocked, echoing*

Bronze doors two storeys high fill the far wall of a great hall, fallen stone heaped against them to the lintel. Water drips somewhere beyond them, and armoured dead stand in ranks before the rubble.

*A hall 40 feet by 50 and 24 high, once the way down to the lake city (The Living Go Down to Dream). The doors are blocked shut from both sides and do not open; a side door (G.21) is now the way those coming up from the city take. A shout here echoes for a long time.*

**The Great Doors:** Bronze, a lake and masked figures in relief; the rubble against them is roof-fall, a turn's work for a strong party to shift and still the far side holds.
**The Skeletons:** Stand in ranks before the doors in old bronze scale (Hostile, 4-8, Bestiary : Skeleton). They turn on anything that touches the rubble.
**The Toll Chest:** Bronze-bound, by the doors, where the dead once paid to go down (Treasure III, d20).
**The Tracks:** Damp footprints and smears of glowing growth run from the side door across the floor.

**Exits:** an archway -> G.15 The Rites Hall, an archway hung with masks -> G.28 The Hall of Masks, an open passage -> G.21 The Side Door
