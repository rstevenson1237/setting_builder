G.14 **The Lake Path** (Medium) - *sloping, damp, glowing*

A sloping path leads down, its walls damp and glowing with growths. A distant hum rises from below.

*A path 10 feet wide and 60 long (The Living Go Down to Dream). It descends 10 feet to the lake city (B.1). At its top, the great doors of the lake hall (G.20) are shut and heaped with fallen stone on this side too; those coming up from the city use the side door, a stone that swings on a pin (G.21).*

**The Path:** Wet stone with a channel at its side; stepping off it onto the glowing growths soaks a party (Test of Fate, Exposed).
**The Great Doors:** Bronze, two storeys high, buried to the lintel in fallen stone; they do not move.
**The Side Door:** A worn stone in the wall beside them, a smear of glowing growth where hands push it.
**The Hum:** Voices and drums from the city below.

**Exits:** the side door -> G.21 The Side Door, down a stair -> B.1 The Mask Gate
