G.14 **The Lake Path** (Medium) - *sloping, damp, glowing*

A sloping path leads east and down, its walls damp and glowing with growths. A distant hum rises from below.

*A path 10 feet wide and 90 long (The Living Go Down to Dream). It descends 30 feet to the lake city (B.1).*

**The Path:** Wet stone with a channel at its side; stepping off it onto the glowing growths soaks a party (Test of Fate, Exposed).
**The Hum:** Voices and drums from the city below.

**Exits:** an open passage, west, 90 feet -> G.16 The Mourners' Gallery, down a stair, east, 60 feet -> B.1 The Mask Gate
