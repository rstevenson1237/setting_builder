G.15 **The Rites Hall** (Medium) - *pillared, echoing, stained*

Pillars stand in a ring round a stained altar. A voice raised here comes back changed.

*A hall 50 feet square and 24 high, where the rites for the dead were done.*

**The Altar:** Brown stains on the top. Spilling blood on it makes the dead stir through the level.

**Exits:** an archway -> G.4 The Embalmers' Room, an open passage -> G.16 The Mourners' Gallery
