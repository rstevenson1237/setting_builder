G.13 **The Guardian Hall** (Medium) - *carved, tall, watched*

Carved guardians stand along both sides of a tall hall, stone cracking faintly. An arch lies beyond.

*A hall 30 feet by 80 and 24 high. Gargoyles on the plinths watch the way to the royal tombs.*

**The Guardians:** Twelve carved plinths, six a side. Gargoyles perch on two (Hostile, 1-2, Bestiary : Gargoyle).

**Exits:** a stone door -> G.2 The Hall of Niches, an archway -> G.5 The Chamber of Jars, an archway -> G.12 The Wells of Offering, a stone door -> G.6 The Royal Antechamber
