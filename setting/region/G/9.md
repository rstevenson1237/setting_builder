G.9 **The False Tomb** (High) - *gilded, opened, hollow*

An opened sarcophagus sits on a dais, its lid askew and its seal cut. A dull click sounds when anything moves.

*A tomb 40 feet square and 24 high, a decoy for the demon's servants (The Last King Is Entombed). Its seal was cut from the inside; the sarcophagus is a trap.*

**The Dais:** Scratches round the lid mark where blades drop. Lifting the lid drops spikes (Test of Constitution, 3d, Piercing).
**The Sarcophagus:** Empty but for a bronze seal ring (Keys: The King's Seal).

**Exits:** a stone door, north, 85 feet -> G.6 The Royal Antechamber
