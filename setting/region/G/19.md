G.19 **The Stair Down** (Medium) - *steep, dark, warm*

A steep dark stair leads down in the north wall, warm air rising. A low hum comes from far below.

*A chamber 10 feet by 30 (The Demon Is Invited). Skeletons guard the arch, which is locked.*

**The Skeletons:** Stand at the stair (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Skeleton).
**The Lock:** The arch to the stair has a bone lock-plate cut with a sunburst (Keys: The Queen's Ring).

**Exits:** an open passage -> G.17 The Ossuary, an archway -> G.8 The Queen's Tomb, down a stair -> H.1 The Stair Landing
