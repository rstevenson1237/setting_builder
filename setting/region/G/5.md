G.5 **The Goblin Stores** (Medium) - *walled, damp, cold*
A narrow store of sacks and tally sticks ends in a **wall** of newer stones. **Goblins** count the stock by a lamp.
*Room 25 ft by 12 ft. The makers' passage once ran on; the goblins walled their end and think it stops here.*
**Goblins:** Four goblins count the stores and sound the alarm at any intruder (Wary, 4, Bestiary : Goblin).
**Tally Sticks:** Notched sticks in racks, the notches marking sacks and casks.
**New Wall:** Newer stones in fresh mortar, set across the end of the room; loose stones can be pulled out, and a cold draught blows from the makers' passage behind, whose square-cut stone shows a line of the makers' script at the wall's edge; the passage runs west to the orcs' walled stores (H.5 The Walled Stores).
**Exits:** wooden, east end of the room, 60 feet -> G.2 The Main Cavern
