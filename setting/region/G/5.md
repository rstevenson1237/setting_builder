G.5 **The Cave Men's Camp** (Lair) - *smoky, skin-hung, noisy*
A smoky camp of skin tents stands among egg-shells, hoots carrying from a lookout rock, red hand-prints on the stones.
*400 by 300 feet. A cave men's band raids the cliffs for eggs.*
**The band:** eight shaggy, scarred hunters in hide wraps with stone clubs (Hostile, 8, Bestiary : Caveman).
**The egg heaps:** shell and cracked yolk piled beside the fire pits.
**The lookout rock:** high stone above the camp, where a hunter watches.
