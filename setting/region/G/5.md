G.5 **The Chamber of Jars** (Low) - *stacked, sealed, dry*

Sealed jars stand stacked in aisles to the roof. The air is dry and silent.

*A chamber 20 feet square off the chapel, the organs of the dead in jars.*

**The Jars:** Hundreds, sealed with wax and labelled in the cynidicean script; the topmost hold small offerings (Treasure I, d20).

**Exits:** an archway -> G.13 The Guardian Hall, an archway -> G.18 The Clerics' Cells, an archway to the dome -> G.22 The Chapel of the Sun
