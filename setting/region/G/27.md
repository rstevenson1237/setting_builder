G.27 **The Bier Room** (Low) - *bare, cold, waiting*

A bare stone bier stands empty between two arches. The room is cold and still.

*A room 20 feet square where the dead waited for burial, between the antechamber and the drowning tomb.*

**The Bier:** Stone, empty, worn smooth by linen.

**Exits:** a stone door to the antechamber -> G.6 The Royal Antechamber, a stone door to the tomb -> G.26 The Drowning Tomb
