G.6 **The Royal Antechamber** (Medium) - *gilded, painted, vast*

Gilded walls are painted with the royal line, four tomb doors in the floor of a vast hall. Gold ticks in the dry air.

*A hall 60 feet square and 24 high (The Last King Is Entombed). Statues of kings stand in a ring before the tomb doors.*

**The Kings' Statues:** Four statues in a ring, scuffed in arcs where they have been turned. Touching the first turns them all toward the true tomb.
**The Doors:** West to the king's (G.7), east to the queen's (G.8), south to the false tomb (G.9) and to the guardian hall (G.13).

**Exits:** a stone door, south, 75 feet -> G.13 The Guardian Hall, a stone door, south, 55 feet -> G.9 The False Tomb, a stone door, west, 70 feet -> G.7 The King's Tomb, a stone door, east, 65 feet -> G.8 The Queen's Tomb
