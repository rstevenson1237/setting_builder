G.6 **The Crater Floor** (Natural) - *green, sunken, warm*
A green warm crater floor lies sunken inside the rim, steam over pools, hiss and trickling water in the still air.
*1500 by 1200 feet. A stream crosses it.*
**The warm pools:** sulphurous water under steam; a swimmer falls asleep for the whole watch.
**The stream:** a trickle across the floor between scree walls.
