G.6 **The Boss's Cave** (High) - *high, firelit, guarded*
A high cave hung with **wolfskins**, a fire pit at its centre and a stone **seat** beside a **chest**. **Wen** sorts loot by the fire.
*Cave 40 ft by 40 ft, ceiling 25 ft. Wen whistles for the wolves from here; a shout from the cavern brings him to the seat.*
**Wen the Tar-Thumbed:** Whistles for the wolves at the first threat, then offers a bargain over the road's spoils. He wants wagons taken and the orcs broken (Named Creature: Wen the Tar-Thumbed).
**Guards:** Four goblins sharpen spears at his seat and guard him to the death (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Goblin).
**Chest:** By the stone seat, holding the boss's hoard (Hoard: Wen's Hoard).
**Satchel:** Wolfskin, on the seat's arm, with a clerk's waybill folded inside (Lore: The Carters' Waybill).
**Wolfskins:** Hung from the roof, tar thumbprints daubed on each.
**Exits:** banded, north-east side of the cave, 85 feet -> G.2 The Main Cavern
