G.6 **The Royal Antechamber** (Medium) - *gilded, painted, vast*

Gilded walls are painted with the royal line, five carved doors round a vast hall. Gold ticks in the dry air.

*A hall 40 feet square and 24 high (The Last King Is Entombed). Statues of kings stand in a ring before the tomb doors.*

**The Kings' Statues:** Four, in a ring, scuffed in arcs where they have been turned. Touching the first turns them all toward the true tomb.
**The Doors:** Five, each carved: a crowned king for the king's tomb (G.7), a crowned queen for the queen's (G.8), a crowned king for the false tomb (G.9), guardians for the guardian hall (G.13) and an empty bier for the bier room (G.27).

**Exits:** a stone door -> G.13 The Guardian Hall, a stone door -> G.9 The False Tomb, a stone door -> G.7 The King's Tomb, a stone door -> G.8 The Queen's Tomb, a stone door carved with a bier -> G.27 The Bier Room
