G.17 **The Ossuary** (High) - *stacked, sickly, cold*

Bones lie stacked in sickly heaps to the roof along both walls. Gnawing stops as you enter.

*A hall 50 feet by 40, where the bones of the common dead were piled. Ghouls feed here.*

**The Ghouls:** Feast among the bones (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Ghoul).
**The Heap:** A skull among the stacks holds a bone ring (Keys: The Queen's Ring).

**Exits:** an open passage, east, 65 feet -> G.16 The Mourners' Gallery, an open passage, north, 55 feet -> G.19 The Stair Down
