G.8 **The Cliff Cave** (Hidden) - *narrow, hidden, dripping*
A narrow cave drips behind a cleft, a faint warmth in the air and an echo.
*80 by 60 feet. The way is a cleft down from the shore track.*
**The dry ledge:** shelf of rock above the drip, dry and warm, where a hunter could sleep.
**The cleft:** the narrow entrance, hidden from the track.
**Exits:** up the cleft -> G.1 The Shore Track
