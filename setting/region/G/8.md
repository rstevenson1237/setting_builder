G.8 **The Queen's Tomb** (High) - *gilded, sealed, sweet*

A gilded sarcophagus stands among jars of sweet oil, a faint perfume in the air. A pale shape hangs over the bier.

*An eight-sided tomb 30 feet across and 24 high (The Last King Is Entombed). The queen's wraith keeps it; an archway leads to the stair down (G.19).*

**The Wraith:** Hangs over the bier in the queen's rotted gown and a thin gold crown (Hostile, 1, Bestiary : Wraith). It drains what it touches.
**The Bier:** Cut with a story in the cynidicean script (Lore: The Queen's Last Rite).
**The Oil:** Sweet, in sealed jars along the walls; it burns bright.

**Exits:** a stone door -> G.6 The Royal Antechamber, an archway -> G.19 The Stair Down
