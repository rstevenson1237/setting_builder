G.8 **The Queen's Tomb** (High) - *gilded, sealed, sweet*

A gilded sarcophagus stands among jars of sweet oil, a faint perfume in the air. A pale shape hangs over the bier.

*A tomb 40 feet square and 24 high (The Last King Is Entombed). The queen's wraith keeps it; the way east leads to the stair down (G.19).*

**The Wraith:** Hangs over the bier (Hostile, 1, Bestiary : Wraith). It drains what it touches.
**The Bier:** Cut with a story in the cynidicean script (Lore: The Queen's Last Rite).
**The Oil:** Jars of sweet oil, sealed; they burn bright.

**Exits:** a stone door, south-west, 125 feet -> G.6 The Royal Antechamber, an archway, east, 90 feet -> G.19 The Stair Down
