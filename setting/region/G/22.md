G.22 **The Chapel of the Sun** (High) - *domed, gilded, central*

An eight-sided chapel rises to a gilded dome, a bronze sun-bowl on a stand at its heart. Four bronze mirrors stand round the bowl, and a dry font sits by the door. A breath echoes back from the dome.

*A chapel 40 feet across and 24 high at the level's centre, where the dead were blessed before burial (The Last King Is Entombed). Its rites still work. The Priesthood know them and will teach them to a party that serves them.*

**The Sun-Bowl:** Bronze, sooty, oil channels running to it from the floor. Lit, the mirrors throw its light round the dome and the chapel fills with daylight; while it burns the dead will not enter, and any already inside flee.
**The Font:** Dry, carved with suns. While the bowl burns it fills with clear water, and a drink heals one Wound, once a day for each drinker.
**The Mirrors:** Bronze, on swivels; turned away from the bowl, the daylight dies to lamplight.
**The Censer:** Gilded, hanging from the dome on a chain, a priestess's gift still in it (Magic, d12).
**The Mark:** A sun-disc over every door (Faction : Ruhiahe).

**Exits:** an archway -> G.13 The Guardian Hall, an archway to the jars -> G.5 The Chamber of Jars, a stone door -> G.23 The Hall of Scales
