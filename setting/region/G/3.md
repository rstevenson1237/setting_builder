G.3 **The Priestesses' Hall** (Medium) - *painted, incensed, quiet*

Painted priestesses line the walls, incense burning before a dais. A soft chant rises and falls.

*A hall 50 feet by 40. The former clergy's hall, held by the Priesthood. They send help in 2 turns.*

**The Painted Women:** Rows of priestesses in blue, each holding a bell; the sun-disc is painted over the door (Faction : Ruhiahe).
**The Priestesses:** Six chant before the dais (Wary, 6, Faction : Ruhiahe). They want the stair sealed and know the rites for the dead; they ask a stranger to wash.
**The Niche:** A sealed prayer niche holds a tapestry strip (Lore: The Priestesses' Rite).

**Exits:** an archway, east, 100 feet -> G.2 The Hall of Niches, an archway, north, 70 feet -> G.11 The Priests' Vestry
