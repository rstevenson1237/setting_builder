G.3 **The Crater Rim** (Natural) - *bare, wide, windy*
A bare windy rim curves round an old crater, scrub and a goat path along its lip, a sea-bird cry and a long silence.
*800 by 300 feet. A sentry watches from the lip by day. The crater trail leads from here south.*
**The sentries:** two treasure hunters watching the trail from the lip, a blazed trunk behind them, grey canvas coats with brass buttons (Neutral, 2, Bestiary : Treasure Hunter).
**The goat path:** narrow ground along the lip above the drop inside.
**Exits:** the crater trail down -> D.5 The Pterodactyl Rock
