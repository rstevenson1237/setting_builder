G.3 **The Loot Hall** (Low) - *heaped, cluttered, fouled*
A long hall of **wagon wheels**, casks and bolts of cloth, sorted into stacks by kind. A **strongbox** stands at the end, a square knot cut into its lid.
*Hall 60 ft by 20 ft. The goods are the road's; the goblins keep a tally of what they hold.*
**Wagon Wheels:** Stacked by size, iron tyres rusted at the rim.
**Casks and Cloth:** Salt, grain and tallow casks with sacks slit and half-empty, their contents crusted and gnawed.
**Strongbox:** Iron-banded and sealed with a square knot; it holds a trader's savings (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** crude, north end of the hall -> G.2 The Main Cavern
