G.3 **The Priestesses' Hall** (Medium) - *painted, incensed, quiet*

Painted priestesses line the walls, incense burning before a dais. A soft chant rises and falls.

*A hall 30 feet by 40. The former clergy's hall, held by the Priesthood. They send help in 2 turns.*

**The Painted Women:** Rows of priestesses in blue, each holding a bell; the sun-disc is painted over the door (Faction : Ruhiahe).
**The Priestesses:** Six of the Priesthood chant before the dais (Wary, 6, Bestiary : Acolyte). They want the stair sealed and know the rites for the dead; they ask a stranger to wash.
**The Niche:** Sealed, for prayers; a tapestry strip lies inside (Lore: The Priestesses' Rite).

**Exits:** an archway -> G.2 The Hall of Niches, an archway -> G.11 The Priests' Vestry
