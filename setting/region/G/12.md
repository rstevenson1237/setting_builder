G.12 **The Wells of Offering** (Medium) - *dry, deep, echoing*

Four dry wells stand in a ring round a central stone, offerings scattered by the rims. A hollow echo answers every sound.

*A round room 40 feet across. The wells were fed with gifts for the dead.*

**The Wells:** Deep and dry. Leaning over a rim cracks the lip (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing).
**The Offerings:** Coin and small figurines lie at the bottom of the wells (Treasure III, d20).

**Exits:** an archway, west, 70 feet -> G.13 The Guardian Hall, an open passage, east, 50 feet -> G.16 The Mourners' Gallery
