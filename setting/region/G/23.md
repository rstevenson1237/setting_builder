G.23 **The Hall of Scales** (Medium) - *split, balanced, bronze*

A floor split down the middle into two halves, a bronze pan hung over each from the roof. The floor creaks as weight shifts.

*A room 30 feet by 20, where the dead were weighed against their deeds. The two halves pivot: weight on one half alone drops it like a trapdoor into a pit (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing). Equal weight on each half holds it level. Nobody living knows.*

**The Floor:** Two slabs meeting at a central seam, scraped where they have dropped before.
**The Pans:** Bronze, empty, swinging; a weight in one tips its half of the floor.

**Exits:** a stone door -> G.22 The Chapel of the Sun, an open passage -> G.24 The Painted Passage
