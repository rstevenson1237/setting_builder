G.25 **The Lamp Store** (Low) - *oily, cramped, dark*

Jars of lamp oil stand on shelves to the roof, wicks in bundles below. The air is close and smells of oil.

*A room 20 feet square, the clergy's lamp store. The oil burns hot and bright.*

**The Oil:** Enough for every lamp on the level, and for the chapel's sun-bowl.
**The False Jar:** One jar rings hollow; its false bottom hides a purse (Treasure I, d20).

**Exits:** a wooden door -> G.24 The Painted Passage
