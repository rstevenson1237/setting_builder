G.16 **The Mourners' Gallery** (Low) - *narrow, carved, quiet*

A narrow gallery is carved with kneeling mourners. Soft footfalls carry along it.

*A gallery 10 feet by 80, joining four halls.*

**The Mourners:** Kneeling figures, each with a hand to its face.

**Exits:** an open passage -> G.12 The Wells of Offering, an open passage at its south end -> G.15 The Rites Hall, an open passage at its north end -> G.17 The Ossuary, an open passage -> G.14 The Lake Path
