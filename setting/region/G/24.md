G.24 **The Painted Passage** (Low) - *narrow, painted, pricked*

A narrow passage is painted with mourners on both walls. Something ticks softly in the walls.

*A passage 10 feet wide and 40 long between the hall of scales and the vestry. Touching a painted mourner at the vestry end sets off darts from the palms (Test of Constitution, 1d, Piercing). The Priesthood know it.*

**The Mourners:** Painted women with hands raised; pinholes in their palms.
**The Lamp Store:** A wooden door off the passage (G.25).

**Exits:** an open passage -> G.23 The Hall of Scales, an open passage to the robes -> G.11 The Priests' Vestry, a wooden door -> G.25 The Lamp Store
