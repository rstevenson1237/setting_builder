G.29 **The Dreamers' Watch** (Medium) - *damp, lamplit, masked*

Lamps, mats and a pot of fungus stand round a door. Soft laughter and the jingle of mask bells come from masked figures on the mats.

*A room 20 feet square beside the side door, where those coming up from the lake city rest before going on.*

**The Dreamers:** Masked as animals, resting before the climb (Neutral, 2-4, Bestiary : Masked Dreamer). They want fungus, lamp oil and quiet, and know the side door and the lake path; they show either for a gift.
**The Fungus Pot:** Pale caps in damp earth, the dreamers' food.

**Exits:** a wooden door -> G.21 The Side Door
