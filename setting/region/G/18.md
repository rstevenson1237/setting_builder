G.18 **The Clerics' Cells** (Low) - *small, plain, empty*

Small plain cells hold beds of stone. A sun-disc is painted over each door.

*A passage 30 feet by 40 with six cells off it (Faction : Ruhiahe).*

**The Cells:** Stone beds, a peg and a bowl; each has a name scratched inside.

**Exits:** a wooden door, south, 80 feet -> G.11 The Priests' Vestry, an archway, east, 90 feet -> G.5 The Chamber of Jars
