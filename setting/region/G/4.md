G.4 **The Bird Cliffs** (Lair) - *white, screaming, sheer*
White cliffs stream with screaming sea-birds, ledges crowded with nests, a fishy stench and wingbeats overhead.
*300 by 250 feet. Pterodactyls roost on the ledges and stoop at fires; cave men climb for the eggs.*
**The pterodactyls:** six leathery-winged reptiles roosting on the ledges (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Pterodactyl).
**The ledges:** crowded nests and shell, white with guano, and cave men's ropes hanging from some.
