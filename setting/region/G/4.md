G.4 **The Tribute Pens** (Low) - *barred, musty, stacked*
Three stone-walled **pens** open off a short passage, each stacked with sacks and crates. A goblin tally in tar is drawn on the wall above them.
*Pens 10 ft by 10 ft, each barred with a plank.*
**Pens:** Three, one for each tribe that pays: kobold grain, orc hides, road salt; the sacks are fewer than the tally says (Treasure IV, d20).
**Tally:** A column of sacks owed drawn in tar on the wall above, the last lines crossed.
**Exits:** wooden, south end of the passage -> G.2 The Main Cavern
