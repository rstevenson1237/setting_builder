G.4 **The Embalmers' Room** (Medium) - *stained, resinous, cold*

Stained slabs stand in two rows with jars of resin, hooks and blades between. Resin drips somewhere.

*A room 40 feet by 30 (The Demon Is Invited). The embalmers worked here; the dead on the slabs are not all still.*

**The Slabs:** Stained black, with drain grooves; hooks and blades hang from a rail.
**The Dead:** Shamble between the slabs (Hostile, 4-8, Bestiary : Zombie).
**The Jar:** A resin-sealed jar holds the embalmers' fee (Treasure II, d20).

**Exits:** an archway, west, 45 feet -> G.2 The Hall of Niches, an archway, east, 45 feet -> G.15 The Rites Hall
