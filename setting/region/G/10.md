G.10 **The Sealed Treasury** (Medium) - *locked, dark, dry*

Chests stand along three walls behind a locked iron door. The air is dry and lifeless.

*A room 30 feet square, locked from G.7. Every chest lid is pricked with pinholes.*

**The Chests:** The lids carry pinholes. Opening one fires a poisoned dart (Test of Constitution, 3d, Poison).
**The Largest Chest:** Holds the king's last reserve (Treasure V, d20).

**Exits:** an iron door, south, 35 feet -> G.7 The King's Tomb
