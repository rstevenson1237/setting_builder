G.2 **The Hall of Niches** (Medium) - *long, hushed, wrapped*

A long hall of wall niches, each holding a body wrapped in linen. Linen rustles somewhere far along.

*A hall 10 feet wide and 80 long (The Last King Is Entombed). A party must walk it in silence; turned back, it walks it again.*

**The Niches:** Hundreds of wrapped bodies, resin-dark; some with a bead or coin set in the wrapping.
**The Skeletons:** Stand in the niches (Hostile, 6-12, Bestiary : Skeleton). They step down when the silence breaks.

**Exits:** an open passage -> G.1 The Stair Landing, an archway -> G.3 The Priestesses' Hall, an archway -> G.4 The Embalmers' Room, a stone door -> G.13 The Guardian Hall
