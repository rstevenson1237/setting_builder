G.2 **The Hunters' Landing** (Lair) - *shingled, tented, guarded*
A tented camp stands above a shingle landing, a new palisade rising, hammering and whistles, a boat drawn up and crossbows at the posts.
*400 by 300 feet. The treasure hunters' second landing; they are fortifying it and watch for a party with a map. Hulls on the reef in sight of the camp.*
**The garrison:** ten treasure hunters in grey canvas coats with brass buttons and a copper compass on a chain, surveyors and sentries with crossbows (Wary, 10, Bestiary : Treasure Hunter); they want news of the party's map.
**The palisade:** a new wall of stakes round the tents, half built.
**The landing craft:** a ship's boat drawn up on the shingle.
