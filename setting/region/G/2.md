G.2 **The Main Cavern** (Medium) - *wide, smoky, crowded*
A wide cavern of **cookfires** and heaped **furs**, thirty paces across, five passages leading off it. **Goblins** gamble and quarrel round the fires, tar thumbprints daubed on every post.
*Cavern 90 ft across, ceiling 20 ft. A shout here carries to every passage; a bone whistle calls the boss.*
**Cookfires:** Four of them, burning fat and bones, the smoke blackening the roof.
**Goblins:** Twenty swarm intruders and shout for the boss (Hostile, 20, Bestiary : Goblin); they know the ledge watch's changes.
**Furs:** Heaped in the warm corners, wolf and deer, with trinkets in the folds (Treasure I, d20).
**Thumbprints:** Black tar thumbprints on every post, fresh over old.
**Exits:** crude, east side of the cavern, 70 feet -> G.1 The Kennel Mouth, crude, south side of the cavern, 80 feet -> G.3 The Loot Hall, wooden, north side of the cavern, 60 feet -> G.4 The Tribute Pens, wooden, west side of the cavern, 60 feet -> G.5 The Goblin Stores, banded, south-west side of the cavern, 85 feet -> G.6 The Boss's Cave
