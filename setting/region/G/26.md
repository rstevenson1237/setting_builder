G.26 **The Drowning Tomb** (Medium) - *tide-marked, sealed, wet*

A sealed bier stands under a dark line drawn round the walls at the height of the roof's edge. Water gurgles under the floor.

*A tomb 30 feet square, a burial drowned by design. Lifting the bier's lid opens the drains backward and floods the room to the roof in five Rounds (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing). Nobody living knows; the door does not open against the water.*

**The Bier:** Sealed with wax, a carved swimmer on the lid.
**The Tide Line:** Dark, at the roof's edge, with a crust of lake salt.
**The Drains:** Bronze grates in the corners, wet and gurgling.

**Exits:** a stone door -> G.27 The Bier Room
