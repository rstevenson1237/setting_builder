G.7 **The King's Tomb** (High) - *gilded, sealed, silent*

A gilded sarcophagus lies under an unbroken seal, chests along the walls. No sound reaches here.

*A tomb 40 feet square and 24 high (The Last King Is Entombed). Breaking the seal wakes the mummy, which follows whoever broke it.*

**The Seal:** Unbroken, with the royal sun cut in it.
**The Mummy:** Lies in the sarcophagus (Hostile, 1, Bestiary : Mummy). It rises when the seal breaks and follows the one who broke it.
**The Hoard:** The sarcophagus holds the king's gold (Hoard: The King's Hoard).
**The Mask:** A gold funerary mask lies on the king's face (Unique Treasure: The King's Mask).
**The Iron Door:** North, locked, its lock a hollow the shape of the royal seal (Keys: The King's Seal).

**Exits:** a stone door, east, 65 feet -> G.6 The Royal Antechamber, an iron door, north, 55 feet -> G.10 The Sealed Treasury
