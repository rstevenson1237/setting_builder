G.7 **The Windward Point** (Natural) - *bare, wind-scoured, high*
A wind-scoured point of bare rock and grass juts to the sea, a cairn on it, spray flying and a gale roaring along the edge.
*300 by 200 feet. A gale makes the cliff edge a danger.*
**The cairn:** a stack of stones at the point's tip.
**The cliff edge:** wind-torn rock above spray rocks; a gale takes a step off it (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing).
