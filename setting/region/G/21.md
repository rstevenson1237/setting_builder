G.21 **The Side Door** (Low) - *narrow, damp, trodden*

A narrow niche runs off the lake hall, its floor worn by many feet to a blank wall. Damp air comes through a crack.

*A niche 10 feet wide and 20 long, a doorkeeper's niche beside the blocked doors. Its end wall is a stone that swings on a pin onto the lake path (G.14); pressing the worn stone at shoulder height opens it. The dreamers know it and use it.*

**The Worn Stone:** Smoother than the rest, a smear of glowing growth on it.
**The Tracks:** Feet worn into the floor, ending at the blank wall.
**The Dreamers' Door:** A wooden door to the room where those coming up rest (G.29).

**Exits:** an open passage -> G.20 The Hall of the Lake Doors, a wooden door -> G.29 The Dreamers' Watch
