G.1 **The Stair Landing** (Low) - *still, cold, dim*

A cold landing opens on a long hall of wrapped dead. Your breath is the only sound.

*A landing 10 feet by 20 feet. The stair from F.13 comes down 20 feet (The Last King Is Entombed).*

**The Landing:** Bare basalt, a sun-disc cut over the arch to the hall.

**Exits:** an open passage, north, 60 feet -> G.2 The Hall of Niches, up a stair, south, 40 feet -> F.13 The Stair Gallery
