G.1 **The Shore Track** (Route) - *narrow, cliff-edged, windy*
A narrow track runs along a windy cliff top, the grass torn by the gale, surf far below and shrill cries from above.
*Two miles a watch on the scrub; every fire and lantern is seen from the sea. The track runs from the northern villages to the headlands.*
**The track's start:** where the route begins, a narrow path along a windy cliff top, pterodactyls circling above it (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Pterodactyl).
**The windward point:** 0.9 miles along, bare rock, a cairn and flying spray; a path leads to G.7.
**The hunters' landing:** 2.8 miles along, tents above shingle with a boat drawn up; a path leads to G.2.
**The crater trail:** 7.2 miles along, a trail climbs to a bare rim; it leads to G.3.
**The bird cliffs:** 11 miles along, white cliffs and wheeling birds; a path leads to G.4.
**Exits:** a cleft down to a cave -> G.8 The Cliff Cave, the track along the shore -> F.2 The Headland Track End
