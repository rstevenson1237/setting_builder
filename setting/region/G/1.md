G.1 **The Kennel Mouth** (Medium) - *stinking, barking, ledge-watched*
Rows of iron-barred **kennels** line both walls of the cave mouth, gnawed bones heaped before them. **Wolves** strain at their chains, and tar thumbprints are daubed on every cage door.
*Cave mouth 20 ft wide and 40 ft deep; a goblin on the ledge above watches the path by day.*
**Kennels:** Iron-barred cages cut into the rock, the doors daubed with tar thumbprints.
**Wolves:** Six chained at their cages by day and loosed at dusk, they bay and rush (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Wolf).
**Bones:** Gnawed deer, horse and man bones in heaps, hair still on some.
**Exits:** open, east, 20 feet -> E.3 The Goblin Ledge, crude, west end of the mouth -> G.2 The Main Cavern
