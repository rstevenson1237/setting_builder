Layout: The hold has one mouth on the ravine's west wall, the goblin ledge (E.3), 700 yards up from the ravine's mouth and 30 feet above the path, where a wolf's skull stands on a pole by an iron-barred kennel door and a goblin watches from a notch by day; the wolves are loosed at dusk. The kennel mouth (G.1) opens 20 feet west of the ledge, a cave mouth 20 feet wide and 40 deep with iron-barred kennels cut into the rock along both walls, gnawed bones heaped before them and tar thumbprints daubed on every cage door. The hold is natural cave widened with picks, ceilings 8 to 12 feet and passages wide enough for two abreast; light is tallow lamps and cookfires, sound carries along the main passage and dies in side caves, the footing is bone, scraps and filth, the air stinks of wolf, and a lit fire sends its smoke out through the mouth and sends the wolves bolting for the kennel.

The boss's cave (G.6), the one High location, lies 85 feet south-west of the main cavern (G.2) through a banded door, about 130 feet west and 60 south of the kennel mouth (G.1). It is a cave 40 feet by 40 with a ceiling of 25 feet, hung with wolfskins tar-daubed, a fire pit at its centre, a stone seat beside a chest holding Wen's Hoard (Hoard: Wen's Hoard) and a wolfskin satchel on the seat's arm; Wen the Tar-Thumbed (Named Creature: Wen the Tar-Thumbed) sits here with four goblin guards who hold it to the death, and a bone whistle calls the wolves from the kennel.

The main cavern (G.2) lies 70 feet west of the kennel mouth (G.1): a cavern 90 feet across with a ceiling of 20 feet, four cookfires blackening the roof, furs heaped in the warm corners, tar thumbprints on every post, and five passages leading off it. The loot hall (G.3), 60 feet by 20, lies 80 feet south of the cavern (G.2): wagon wheels, casks and bolts of cloth stacked by kind, and a strongbox at its end with a square knot cut into the lid. The tribute pens (G.4) lie 60 feet north of the cavern (G.2) off a short passage: three stone-walled pens, 10 feet by 10 and each barred with a plank, with a goblin tally in tar on the wall above. The goblin stores (G.5), a room 25 feet by 12, lie 60 feet west of the cavern (G.2): sacks and tally sticks, and at the room's end a wall of newer stones in fresh mortar, a cold draught blowing at its foot and a line of the makers' script showing at its edge.

Inhabitants: Six Wolves are chained at their kennels in G.1 by day and loosed at dusk, hostile, baying and rushing, wanting feeding. Twenty of The Zhakuk (Faction: The Zhakuk) gamble over knucklebones at the cookfires of the main cavern (G.2), hostile, swarming intruders and calling the boss, wanting wagon days and loot; they know the ledge watch's changes. Four Goblins count the stores by a lamp at G.5, wary, sounding the alarm and falling back, wanting the stores kept from the orcs. Wen the Tar-Thumbed (Named Creature: Wen the Tar-Thumbed) sorts loot by his fire at G.6 five days in six, hostile, whistling for the wolves and then bargaining, wanting wagons taken and the orcs broken; he knows the wagon days. Four Goblins sharpen spears at his seat and guard him to the death.

Alarm: A stranger on the ledge or at G.1 sets the wolves baying. The main cavern (G.2) hears at once and twenty goblins swarm the mouth in a Round; Wen in G.6 hears a Round later and his whistle brings two wolves out of the kennel each Round. The stores (G.5) bar their door and fall back.

Loot: The hold's wealth is what the road carries, and with the convoys short it is thin. The largest prize is Wen's Hoard (Hoard: Wen's Hoard) in the chest by the stone seat at G.6, with Wen and four guards over it, and the carters' waybill (Lore: The Carters' Waybill) in the wolfskin satchel on the seat's arm; Treasure I lies in the furs at G.2, Treasure III in the trader's strongbox at G.3 and Treasure IV in the tribute sacks at G.4. The Moneychanger (A.7) buys it back at 80% of value.

Secrets:
- A makers' passage runs west from the stores to the orcs' stores - the goblins and orcs each think it ends at their own wall; the fresh mortar and the draught at the new wall (G.5) bring it out.
- The boss keeps the carters' waybill with the wagon days - Wen knows; the satchel at his seat (G.6) brings it out.
