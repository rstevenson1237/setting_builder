Layout: The hold runs west into the rock from its mouth on the ravine's west wall. The kennel mouth (G.1) lies at the mouth, with the main cavern (G.2) 40 feet west, the loot hall (G.3) 50 feet south of the cavern, the tribute pens (G.4) 30 feet north, the stores (G.5) 60 feet west, and the boss's cave (G.6) 70 feet south-west.

Places:
- G.1 The Kennel Mouth - the way in; six wolves in cages.
- G.2 The Main Cavern - twenty goblins at their fires, five passages.
- G.3 The Loot Hall - wagon goods and a trader's strongbox.
- G.4 The Tribute Pens - three pens of sacks, one for each tribe that pays.
- G.5 The Goblin Stores - four goblins and a wall of new stones, makers' script at its edge.
- G.6 The Boss's Cave - Wen the Tar-Thumbed, four guards, a chest and a satchel.

Inhabitants: Six Wolves chained at G.1, twenty of The Zhakuk at G.2, four Goblins at G.5, and Wen the Tar-Thumbed with four Goblins at G.6.

Alarm: A stranger on the ledge or at G.1 sets the wolves baying. The main cavern (G.2) hears at once and twenty goblins swarm the mouth in a Round; Wen in G.6 hears a Round later and his whistle brings two wolves out of the kennel each Round. The stores (G.5) bar their door and fall back.

Loot: The largest prize is Wen's Hoard in the chest at G.6; Treasure I lies at G.2, Treasure III in the strongbox at G.3 and Treasure IV in the pens at G.4. A.7 buys it at 80%.

Secrets:
- A makers' passage runs west from the stores to the orcs' stores - the goblins and orcs each think it ends at their own wall; the fresh mortar and the draught at the new wall (G.5) bring it out.
- The boss keeps the carters' waybill with the wagon days - Wen knows; the satchel at his seat (G.6) brings it out.

Leads:
- Rumour 3 - G.6 Wen the Tar-Thumbed.
- Rumour 4 - G.5 New Wall.
- The Road Bounty - G.2 Goblins.
- The Lost Strongbox - G.3 Strongbox.
