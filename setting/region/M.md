M Thalosae

Kind: collection
Overview: The makers' first shrine, cut into the cliff above the maze and reopened by the Black Altar five years ago. The Black Altar's high priest, a Named Creature, rules acolytes, guards and the Zombies they raise; the Zurnaz visit to collect the tithe. It has four blocks: the stair-hall and outer shrine, the priests' cells and refectory, the crypts, and the sanctum and vault. A party comes for the sanctum's secrets and the vault. The Black Altar needs every tribe's tithe and every wagon day. If the tithe stops, the Zurnaz are sent into the ravine. If the sanctum is entered, the high priest knows by the time the party leaves it.
Approach: Three ways in: a stair from the ravine's head, watched by acolytes; a makers' passage from the maze; and the bugbears' back stair from the dry fork. The stair-hall holds a door barred at night.
Conditions: Makers' cut stone, ceilings 14 to 20 feet and halls wide. Light is black candles and a fire in the sanctum. Chanting carries along the halls and hides small noises. Footing is smooth stone, stained black before the altar. The air is thick with incense, and a lit fire changes nothing. In the sanctum the Black Altar itself asks a Test of Sanity of everyone who enters.
