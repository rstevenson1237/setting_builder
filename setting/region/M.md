M Thalosae

Overview: The makers' first shrine, cut into the cliff above the maze and reopened by the Black Altar five years ago, in four blocks: the stair-hall and outer shrine, the priests' cells and refectory, the crypts, and the sanctum and vault. A party comes for the sanctum's secrets and the vault. The Black Altar needs every tribe's tithe and every wagon day, and the Zurnaz visit to collect the tithe. If the tithe stops, the Zurnaz are sent into the ravine; if the sanctum is entered, the high priest knows by the time the party leaves it.

Approach: Three ways in: the shrine stair from the ravine's head (E.9), watched by acolytes at its gong; a makers' passage climbing from the Minotaur's lair (L.5); and the bugbears' back stair from their den (K.4). The stair-hall's inner door is barred at night.

Conditions: Makers' cut stone, ceilings 14 to 20 feet and halls wide. Light is black candles and a fire in the sanctum. Chanting carries along the halls and hides small noises. Footing is smooth stone, stained black before the altar. The air is thick with incense, and a lit fire changes nothing.

Entrances:
- M.1 - A cold hall at the stair's head, black stains spreading from a banded inner door barred from within at dusk.

Prizes:
- M.5 - Between the cells and the refectory: black hangings on every wall, the east one soot-smeared, smoking censers and a brazier at the centre, a black wood cabinet and an iron coffer beside it.
- M.11 - Behind a locked iron door beyond the antechamber: a vast black hall, a fire of black flame on a stone altar, a bronze lamp burning black at its edge, and in the north wall a makers' slab with a round recess; whispers fill the air, and the fire is cold.
- M.12 - Behind that slab: eight iron-bound chests stacked two high, gold gleaming through their lids, rime on every edge and breath hanging white, the cold deepening near the gold, and a niche sealed with black wax.

Places:
- M.2 - Through the banded door: a vaulted hall, an old black altar at its north end with a stone bowl brimming with fresh blood, and black candles in iron stands down both walls.
- M.3 - A passage 5 feet wide off the outer shrine, eight bare cells along it.
- M.4 - Long scrubbed tables, and at the far end a pantry door with a green crust on its hinges.
- M.6 - A stair 4 feet wide leading 40 feet down from the outer shrine, turning once.
- M.7 - Bones stacked in ranked niches to the roof, at the crypt stair's foot.
- M.8 - Four rows of empty burial niches, one cut deeper than the rest, and dust-free streaks running to the last in the south-west row.
- M.9 - A black sarcophagus whose carved priest's raised hand is worn bright, a stone door ajar in its south side and a stair climbing 40 feet to the antechamber.
- M.10 - Through an archway from the high priest's chamber: runes warm to the touch in a ring round the iron door at its north end, the tomb stair rising into its east wall.
- M.13 - Thick dust with one long trail across it, at the head of the stair from the maze, a makers' door of stone at its east side.
- M.14 - At the head of the bugbears' stair: a bell on a rope beside a plastered door, soot handprints thick round its edge.
