C.21 **Taluora** (low) - *years, layers, deposit*
A narrow room off the stepped channel where the wall on one side has been **cut back in benches**, and the exposed face shows the ground behind it in **fine banded layers** running level for the room's whole length, hundreds of them, each no thicker than a fingernail.
*A room 30 by 12 feet, ceiling 9 feet, standing in about a foot of running water, with the eastern wall cut away to expose a face of banded deposit 7 feet high. It was cut deliberately as an inspection face and it has no other purpose. The water running through it is loud for so small a room, and it is the sound that tells a party where they are when they come back round.*
**Banded Layers:** The exposed face shows the salt pan deposit in fine bands, dark and pale alternating, hundreds of them stacked level. It is a year count, which is why the Odarath cut this face and why they built a room round it. Anyone can count them; the count runs to something over nine hundred bands before the face is lost in the floor, and the top four inches are a different colour entirely.
**Top Four Inches:** The uppermost bands are coarse and yellow rather than fine and grey, and there are about eighteen of them. That is the Gell Year and every year since, recorded here forty feet below the pans, in a room nobody in Torsgaard has ever seen. Whatever soured the western pans eighteen years ago reached this far down.
**Bench Cuts:** The stepped benches left by the cutting make a rough stair up the face to within two feet of the ceiling, and there is a ledge at the top wide enough to sit on. Somebody has sat there - the deposit is rubbed smooth in a patch about two feet across, and there is a stub of tallow candle wedged in a crack beside it that is nothing like nine hundred years old.
**Exits:** opening at the room's south end onto a stepped channel with a walkway beside it -> C.19 Azzakire, low corbelled arch at the north end, water running out through it -> C.22 Nekire
