C.21 **Grave Niche** (medium) - *Red Seals, Ghast, Broken Grave*
A narrow tomb-passage is cut on both sides with body-niches closed by slabs and **red clay seals**, and two red-daubed **Gorzgur keepers** with a torch are shoving a body into the one niche near the far end that gapes broken open. Inside it a gaunt grey **ghast** crouches, chewing, and below it on the floor stands a squat **grave jar** stuck fast to the stone.
*A dressed passage-tomb 30 feet long and 8 wide, six niches in each wall in two rows of three, where the Gorzgur put their dead and still do, the clay of the newest seal still soft, and where they now keep the ghast fed on their dead so that it stays in its niche. The sweet rot smell and the chewing that reach the Stores come from the broken niche, and the keepers' torchlight shows through the gaps of the plank door.*
**Gorzgur Keepers:** Two warriors with red-daubed faces and a red hand painted on each shield are pushing a Gorzgur corpse into the broken niche with spear butts, the elder repeating in Grozzgur feed it and keep it in, the younger shaking and edging toward the door, and if either gets out he wedges the plank door shut from the Stores side and runs shouting for the Warband Hall (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Ghast:** In the broken niche nearest the west end a gaunt grey thing with a jaw that hangs too wide crouches over the bodies it is given, pulling them apart and eating, paying no heed to anyone who leaves it to its meal and coming out of the niche at whoever pulls a body away or strikes it (Absorbed, 1, Bestiary : Grave-Ghast).
**Red Clay Seals:** Eleven niches are closed each with a slab sealed round in red clay pressed with a clawed handprint, and prying a seal loose takes a knife and a minute and cracks loud enough in the narrow passage to lift the ghast's head from its meal (Quest: Red Seal Clay).
**Grave Jar:** Below the broken niche a squat bronze-banded jar stands where that grave's goods were set, its base fused to the floor slab by red clay spilled from the burst seal and dried hard, and chipping it free takes time within arm's reach of the ghast (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** door of lashed planks hung on rope hinges inside a rough breach, sagging so that its foot drags a groove in the floor, east wall -> C.20 Stores
