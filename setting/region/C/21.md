C.21 **The Bell-Wight's Chamber** (high) - *restless dead, bell-drawn, central threat*
A wide, colder-still chamber holds a single drowned figure that turns slowly in place, as if listening for something just out of reach.
*A wide chamber roughly 30 by 30 feet, originally the barrow's central shrine, now given over entirely to the Bell-Wight's restless waiting; colder than anywhere else in the barrow, the chill deepening the nearer one approaches its center. The Bell-Wight does not hunt so much as wait for a sound to follow; carrying anything that rings, chimes, or tolls draws its full attention immediately.*
**Bell-Wight:** (Bestiary) Anchored to this chamber and the passages immediately around it; a silent, careful party may bypass it entirely.
**Exits:** wide stone archway -> C.20 Cold Vestibule, open threshold, iron long dissolved -> C.22 Rusted Vault, low stone passage -> C.24 Last Reliquary
