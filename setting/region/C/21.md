C.21 **The Deep Gallery Fork** (low) - *branching, dark, connective*
Three passages split off from this junction, each leading deeper into the older excavation. A faint warmth drifts from one of them, out of place against the cold everywhere else down here.

**Three Passages:** the junction's three branches, each visibly different in age and construction - one carrying warmth, one leading toward old camp-smoke, one heading toward standing water.
