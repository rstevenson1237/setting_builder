C.21 **Grave Niche** (medium) - *Red Seals, Ghast, Broken Grave*
A narrow chamber lined with burial niches where a grey **Ghast** tears at a dead Gorzgur below a smashed **grave**, five other niches stand shut behind red **seals**, and a trough of red **clay** sits against the wall. Goods lie loose on the **slab** under the broken niche.
*The chamber is 30 feet by 12 under a 7-foot ceiling, six niches cut in its long walls, the household's burial room, in use. Red clay and rot smell hang together over the grave, and tearing sounds come from the Ghast.*
**Ghast:** Hozakh crouches on the grave's ledge tearing at a shrouded dead Gorzgur, nails grown long past any use, grey flesh streaked red (Feeding, 1, Bestiary : Grave-Ghast) (Named Creature: Hozakh).
**Grave:** The fourth niche's clay seal lies smashed on the floor, its shroud torn out and grave earth fanned across the flags.
**Seals:** Five niches are shut with red clay stamped with the red band and a tally of notches -> breaking a seal shows a shrouded Gorzgur missing an ear.
**Clay:** A stone trough of red clay stands against the east wall, its surface smoothed and printed with one wet palm in each row -> scooping clay breaks a row (Quest: Red Seal Clay).
**Slab:** A stone bier-slab fused to the floor under the broken niche carries goods left lying in the open (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** hole knocked through the dressed wall with broken edges chipped rough and the course-ends showing, rubble cleared, north wall -> C.20 Stores
