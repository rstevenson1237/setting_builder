C.13 **The Flooded Landing** (low) - *tide-filled floor, shallow water, connective space*
A low landing at the stair's foot sits ankle-deep in cold, tide-fed **water** that never fully drains, its faint lapping the only sound down here.
*A roughly circular landing, about 10 feet across, standing water throughout; once a dry stair-foot, now permanently reached by the tide. Its faint tidal rise and fall is a passive reminder the sea still reaches this deep.*
**Standing Water:** Cold and tide-fed; nothing hidden within it, just a fact of the lower level.
