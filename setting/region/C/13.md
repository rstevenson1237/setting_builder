C.13 **Chieftain's Chamber** (high) - *Ears, Parley, Chute*
Vraz Red-Hand holds this chamber alone but for whoever's brought before him, a wall of cut ears and trophies kept on plain display behind his own seat.
*Rough-cut chamber fifteen yards across, active, a single carved seat facing the door, the trophy wall lit by its own dedicated torch where the rest of the room is dim.*
**Vraz Red-Hand:** Vraz hears out anyone brought before him rather than fighting on sight, and takes a prisoner for ransom or a parley rather than finishing a fight he isn't sure of winning (Hearing, 1, Named Creature : Vraz Red-Hand).
**Trophy Wall:** Cut ears and other trophies taken from outsiders hang in rows behind the seat, sorted by raid rather than by age, the newest darker rather than dried pale.
**Vraz's Own Take:** What Vraz has kept back from the household's own common hoard sits in a chest beside the seat, locked and answering to no key any of his own warband carries (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** fitted door, west wall -> C.12 Warband Hall, sloped chute, floor -> C.16 Prisoner Hold, fitted door, east wall -> C.17 Household Hoard Vault
