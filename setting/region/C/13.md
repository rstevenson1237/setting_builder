C.13 **Chieftain's Chamber** (high) - *Ears, Parley, Chute*
Under a ribbed dome furred with grey mould, rotted **ear strings** hang over a heap of mouldering bedding and a flat-topped **Parley Stone**, and an iron-strapped **chest** stands alone in a ring of clean floor. Torchlight comes and goes behind a hide curtain in the east wall, where a **Gorzgur patrol** walks its round.
*A round chamber 30 feet across under a dome 12 feet high at the crown, set a few feet below the Warband Hall, once the chieftain's sleeping and parley room and left to rot since, its hides and bedding soft with mould and its air thick with the smell of them. The only sound when the patrol is gone is a slow drip from the dome's crown into a cup it has worn in the floor beside the Parley Stone.*
**Gorzgur Patrol:** Two warriors with a torch walk a round from the Hoard Vault curtain, past the Parley Stone and the chest, to the head of the steps and back, their torchlight showing through the curtain and the hide flap before they step through -> each touches two fingers to the forehead and murmurs the word Vraz as they pass the ring around the chest (Vigilant, 2, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Chest in the Ring:** An iron-strapped chest the size of a crouching man stands in a ring of flagstones two strides across that is clean of the mould furring every other stone, the rats' droppings strewn over the floor stop at the ring's edge, and a Khughik's rag bundle lies at the edge where it was dropped -> speaking the word Vraz at the ring lets the speaker step in and lift the chest, while stepping in or reaching across without it turns the reacher's nerve so they cannot close with the chest again (Test of Fate, Condition).
**Parley Stone:** In the centre of the floor a waist-high block with its top worn smooth by elbows holds an iron key set flat into a key-shaped recess under a poured band of lead, the lead gouged around its edge and a snapped knife-tip still standing in it, and holding a torch to the band until the lead softens lets the key be worked free (Keys: Fetter Key).
**Ear Strings:** From the dome's ribs hang rotted cords of cut ears, many fallen to the floor, and anyone sorting them finds three kinds kept on separate cords, small pointed Khughik ears, broad torn Hukkgur ears and human ears with iron rings in some.
**Exits:** five rubble-and-cut steps climbing to a ragged breach high in the wall, the back of a stiff hide flap hanging across its top, west wall -> C.12 Warband Hall, square stone hatch set in the floor on a bronze pivot pin over a chute worn too smooth and steep to climb back up, north side of the floor -> C.16 Prisoner Hold, heavy curtain of stitched hides on a pole wedged across a cut doorway, its lower edge black with torch-soot, east wall -> C.17 Household Hoard Vault
