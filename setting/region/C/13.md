C.13 **Chieftain's Chamber** (high) - *Ears, Parley, Chute*
A domed chamber of dressed stone, its rim pegged with hundreds of dried **ear-strings** on an iron **peg rail**, a ring of swept floor circling an iron-bound **chest**. A round shaft opens in the floor at the southwest corner.
*The chamber is 25 feet across under a cracked dome 9 feet at the crown, the chieftain's own room where he takes parley and keeps what is his, its cords rotting. Cured-hide reek comes off the ear-strings, and their cords creak in the draught from the shaft.*
**Ear-Strings:** Forty strings of dried ears hang from the pegs, some the size of a thumbnail and some the size of a palm, mould greening the lowest.
**Ring:** Swept floor rings the chest, dust and ear-strings stopping at its edge, a dead rat on the line -> speaking Gorzgur at the edge lets one cross, crossing without it (Test of Fate, Held).
**Chest:** An iron-bound oak chest stands inside the ring, its latch pinned with a peg, a red hand painted on the lid, two men to lift.
**Peg Rail:** One peg on the rail is bored with a slot holding a fist-long iron key snug, chisel marks chipped round it -> lifting the key out frees it (Keys: Fetter Key).
**Pot:** A pot of red pitch-paint hangs over the key peg, a dead Khughik red to the elbows below -> lifting the key tips it, staining hands and pack.
**Exits:** iron-strapped oak hatch on greased hinges in the ceiling, reached by iron staples set in the wall beneath, north wall -> C.12 Warband Hall, round shaft with iron rungs riveted to its wall and a rim worn smooth, southwest corner -> C.16 Prisoner Hold, natural gap in the rock with chipped-rough edges and a floor worn to a hollow, south wall -> C.17 Household Hoard Vault
