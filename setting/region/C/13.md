C.13 **Silent Gallery** (medium) - *acoustic, deceptive, hushed*
Every sound in this gallery arrives from the wrong direction, echoes doubling back and overlapping until it's impossible to tell where anything actually came from. A whisper here can return as something that sounds almost, but not quite, like an answer.
*An elongated gallery roughly 40 by 10 feet, walls oddly smooth and canted at angles that don't match the passage's overall shape. The acoustics are unnatural even by the wold's standards, and the air is unnervingly still despite a faint draft somewhere in it.*
**Echo Trap:** the gallery's peculiar acoustics carry any raised voice far beyond its natural range and scramble its point of origin - speaking or calling out above a whisper here risks being heard by whatever else moves in the dark, which begins moving toward the sound.
**Exits:** passage, back toward the threshold archway -> C.9 Threshold Archway, passage, toward a quieter side chamber -> C.14 The Miner's Rest, passage, toward a rope-and-ladder descent -> C.15 Broken Ladder Shaft
