C.13 **The Old Foundation Course** (low) - *older-cut stone, matches the keep's own cellar, three centuries predates the charter*
A course of heavier, differently-cut stone shows through where the passage floor has settled, older by its style than the rest of Ingaraz's own foundation-work.
*A short connecting gallery roughly 15 feet long, floor uneven where it's settled onto the older course beneath; smells of cold, dry stone, and radiates a chill even where the passage elsewhere runs merely cool.*
**Exposed Course:** The older stone's joints are cut in a style none of Ingaraz's other builders' work matches - evidence something stood on this ground before even the barrow-builders did.
**Exits:** narrow cut, loose spoil, west -> C.3 The Digger's Cut, low mound mouth, claw-scored stone, south -> C.5 The Bear's Den Mound
