C.17 **Household Hoard Vault** (high) - *Chests, Lone Guard, Held Back*
A low-arched vault of dressed stone where eight iron-strapped **chests** stand on plinths in the dark, one **guard** sits on a stool among them, and a rotten-egg **stink** hangs in the air. One chest lid shows bright new **nails**.
*The vault is 25 feet by 20 under a 7-foot arch, the household's hoard room, in daily use, kept dark on purpose. The stink is thickest behind the chests where the wall cracks.*
**Chests:** Eight iron-strapped chests stand on dressed plinths cut with the bind-rune, the largest at the back wall, lid unlocked -> lifting it (Treasure V, d20).
**Guard:** One Gorzgur sits on a stool between the chests with a spear across his knees and an unlit lamp at his boot (Alert, 1, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Stink:** The vault air stinks of struck matches and thickens each turn, a dead bat on the floor, the guard's lamp unlit -> a struck light ignites it (Test of Constitution, 1d, Blast).
**Nails:** One chest lid is re-nailed bright over rusted old holes -> prying it lifts a false floor over a rolled hide painted red with a ring of nine dots (Lore: Painted Hide).
**Bell Cord:** A greased cord runs from the padlock's hasp up through a ceiling ring toward the south -> opening the lock rings bone bells in C.20, costing the quiet.
**Exits:** natural gap in a broken course of the dressed wall, edges chipped rough and the floor worn to a hollow, north wall -> C.13 Chieftain's Chamber, stitched ox-hide curtain slung on an iron bar padlocked to a wall ring with a fist-sized keyed lock, south wall -> C.20 Stores
