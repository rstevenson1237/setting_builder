C.17 **The Empty Antechamber** (low) - *bare old-tongue stonework, low ceiling, shoulder-width passage*
A bare waiting-room of worked stone, once a checkpoint by the look of the worn threshold-groove, sits just past the outer entrance with passages breaking off in two more directions.
*A plain room roughly 12 by 10 feet, ceiling low and shoulder-width throughout like the rest of Ingaraz; smells of undisturbed dust, and nothing here has been touched in a very long time.*
**Scratched Glyph:** A single closed-ring mark, cruder and shallower than the deep-cut version further in, is scratched beside the passage heading east - a first, rougher sign of the grander threshold waiting ahead.
**Exits:** low passage, worked stone, north -> C.10 The Outer Mound Path, narrow squeeze, rubble-choked, south -> C.18 The Rubble Choke, wide archway, worn threshold, east -> C.2 The Warded Gatehouse
