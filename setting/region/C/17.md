C.17 **The Spiral Archway Hall** (medium) - *dressed-stone, patterned, guarded*
Every archway in this hall is carved with the same recurring spiral chisel-mark, dressed stone fitted with a precision no fringe mason matches. A robed figure stands motionless in the far archway's shadow, retreating without a word the instant it's noticed.
*A long hall roughly 40 by 15 feet, dressed stone walls and three archways all bearing the same spiral motif, ceiling a full 9 feet up throughout. The cold here is markedly sharper than the galleries above, and the air carries the faint, mineral smell of very old, undisturbed stone.*
**Spiral Archways:** three archways lead from this hall, each carved with the same recurring spiral chisel-mark found on the standing stones above the fringe - proof, if anyone still needed it, that the wold's boundary markers and the Marrgath's own work share a single hand. A Marrgath Remnant is sometimes glimpsed in one of the archways, silent and retreating the instant it's noticed, never confirmed hostile and never successfully followed.
**Exits:** passage, back up toward the echoing vault -> C.16 The Echoing Vault, archway, spiral-carved, leading to the sentinel's chamber -> C.18 The Watch-Stone Chamber, archway, spiral-carved, leading toward standing water -> C.19 The Lower Cistern
