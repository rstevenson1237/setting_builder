C.17 **Household Hoard Vault** (high) - *Rival Chaos, Household Hoard*
What Gorzgur's taken in raid and not yet traded up the ravine is kept whole in one guarded chamber, a single Gorzgur posted at the door day and night.
*Rough-cut chamber fifteen yards across, active, the floor here swept clean of the cracks that mark the rest of the household's halls, the one room besides the chieftain's own that's never left unwatched.*
**Vault Guard:** A single Gorzgur posted here answers only to Vraz, and fights rather than calls for help the instant the hoard itself is touched by anyone not already known to carry it out (Standing, 1, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Hoard:** What the household's taken and not yet traded stands stacked rather than scattered, in chests too heavy to carry out whole without help (Treasure V, d20).
**Second Take:** Beneath the chests, wrapped separate from the rest, sits whatever Vraz doesn't trust even his own guard to see counted (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** fitted door, west wall -> C.13 Chieftain's Chamber, fitted door, south wall -> C.20 Stores
