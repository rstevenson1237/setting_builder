C.17 **Household Hoard Vault** (high) - *Chests, Lone Guard, Held Back*
A narrow vault under a steep-pitched ridge of dressed stone lies dark and reeking of marsh-stink, and a **lone guard** sits on a **chest stack** against the east wall. A faint hiss comes from a clay-packed **plugged seam** in the north wall.
*A vault 30 feet long and 12 wide under a ceiling that rises steeply to a ridge 15 feet up, cut to hold the household's take and kept dark by the Gorzgur, who carry no light past its door. The hiss and the stink reach anyone standing at the door before they step through with a light.*
**Plugged Seam:** A seam in the north wall is packed with clay and bound over with a strap of iron, the hiss and marsh-stink from around the clay growing louder and ranker while anyone stays, and there is no soot on the ceiling and no torch ring on any wall -> a struck light or open flame in the vault fires the gas pooled under the ridge in a thunderclap that throws everyone in the room off their feet (Test of Constitution, 1d, Blast).
**Lone Guard:** One Gorzgur sits cross-legged on top of the chest stack in the dark with an axe across his knees and an iron key on a thong round his neck, passing coins from one hand to the other and counting them under his breath in Grozzgur (Watchful, 1, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Chest Stack:** Against the east wall six ironbound chests stand stacked two high, their lids nailed down and daubed with red handprints, and prying a lid takes a bar and makes a shriek of nails (Treasure V, d20).
**Reset Slab:** Beneath the bottom chest at the south end a floor slab has been lifted and set back in fresh grey clay that shows round its edge once the chest is dragged aside, and under it a hollow bone tube stopped with wax holds a rolled strip of hide painted with picture-marks for passages, a ford, and three short lines beside one blank place (Lore: Painted Hide).
**Exits:** iron-strapped plank door on leather hinges, swollen at its foot so that it scrapes an arc into the floor, the stitched hide curtain hanging on its far side, west wall -> C.13 Chieftain's Chamber, grille of iron bars set in a dressed arch and dropped to the floor, a locked wicket of bars in it, raised only by two hauling together on a rope wound over a wooden drum in the arch's side, a line of copper bells tied along its bottom bar that ring the whole time it rises, south wall -> C.20 Stores
