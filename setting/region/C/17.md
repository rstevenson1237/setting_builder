C.17 **Kireath** (medium) - *flooded, wading, depth*
A wide hall standing in **water to the knee** across its whole floor, still and clear and cold, with the **drowned arches** of three ways out showing black at the waterline. The **gorm** sits on this room like a lid - lanterns halo, flame will not hold, and the air tastes faintly sweet.
*A hall 70 by 50 feet, ceiling 9 feet, floored in dressed flags now under 18 to 22 inches of water that rises and falls about four feet with the tide above. This is the head of the third course and the point below which nothing in Siwapelu is dry. The water is completely still and completely clear, and a party can see the flagged floor and everything on it - which is what makes the one thing they cannot see worth writing.*
**Drowned Arches:** Three corbelled arches at the waterline, north, south and east, each with its cap character visible above the surface. The water level tells the party what the tide is doing before they commit to a course: at the low of the tide the sills are clear and passable, at the high the north arch is under and impassable, and the swing takes about five hours.
**Gorm Lid:** The heavy air sits at chest height across the whole hall and does not disperse. Open flame gutters and dies here without exception - this is the room where a party using torches finds out, and the Gorm track runs at one step per action in it per `setting/Procedures.md`, plus one for any general search. A caged finch brought this far will have stopped singing before the party notices anything.
**Brine Eel:** One, lying along the base of the west wall where the flags have scoured out into a trough three hands wide - the trough is visible through the clear water and reads as a construction detail until a party works out that nothing else in Siwapelu is worn like that. 5d6-1. It takes the person at the back of a wading line without warning. The tell is in the water: there are no leeches, no weed, and nothing living anywhere in this hall, and the room the party waded through to get here was thick with all three.
**Exits:** drowned corbelled arch in the north wall, sill just at the waterline at low tide and under at high -> C.18 Sipelu, drowned corbelled arch in the east wall, its cap carrying the threshold character, water moving through it -> C.19 Azzakire, low arch in the south-west corner, half-blocked with fallen bench stone, dry ground rising beyond it -> C.15 Peluath
