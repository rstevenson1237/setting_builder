C.34 **Bolt-Hole** (medium) - *False Wall, Lookout, Escape*
A dug room fallen in to knee-high **rubble** under its own roof-stone, a runt Khughik (**Zurkik**) crouched at a slit facing the shaft and a **loose slab** leaning in the north wall.
*Dug room about 10 feet by 8, once the Silt Hollow Khughik's lookout post over the shaft and bolt-hole for the warren, its roof since fallen to knee height. Cold air moves at the north slab's edge and carries the smell of wet stone.*
**Zurkik:** A runt Khughik crouches at a slit between two roof-slabs facing the shaft, gnawing a bone, and a party's arrival -> he squeals and bolts (Named Creature: Zurkik).
**Loose Slab:** A slab cut with Sutuz leans in the north wall, cold air at its edge, and drawing it aside -> an arch onto C.48 Broken Stair Entrance.
**Rubble:** Fallen roof-stone and earth fill the room to knee height, leaving a strip of floor worn bare from the shaft to the north slab.
**Sealed Bundle:** A knife-long bundle of hide sealed with pitch lies in a niche behind the loose slab, and cutting it open -> what the hide holds (Unique Treasure: The Grey Knife).
**Exits:** climb - wall-cut footholds and iron staples up a shaft, its plank hatch locked with a padlock on the underside hasp opened by (Keys: Bolt-Hole Key), roof, northwest corner -> C.31 The Chittering Nest
