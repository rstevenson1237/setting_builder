C.34 **Bolt-Hole** (medium) - *False Wall, Lookout, Escape*
A crawlspace barely three feet high under a roof fall, where a Khughik on his knees scrapes at a **squint** cut through the rubble. The far end is a **silt-daubed wall**, and a rubble block by the floor hatch is smeared with red.
*What is left of the Silt Hollow's guard post, once 20 feet by 15 and now a strip 3 feet high between a roof fall and the ceiling of the old cutting, dug out by the Khughik into a lookout and a way out. Keep delvers who have mapped this far call it the Second Squint. The Old Tongue on the leaning slab reads Sutuz, "a guardian is set here". The scrape of a bone spoon on stone comes from the squint, and the footing is loose rubble throughout.*
**Zurkik:** A Khughik with a wrist-thick braid of cut ears at his belt kneels at the squint scraping rubble loose with a bone spoon, the silt he dislodges sifting down through the hatch seam into the incline below, and at the first light or voice he goes for the silt-daubed wall unless someone already stands between him and it (Named Creature: Zurkik).
**Silt-Daubed Wall:** The west end is a hide stretched on a pole frame and daubed with silt to match the dug walls, its edges lifting in a draught beside a loose slab of dressed stone cut with Old Tongue letters, and pulling the hide aside opens a crawl running down and away, which a pole dropped across the frame from the far side bars against anyone left on this side -> C.48 Broken Stair Entrance.
**Squint:** A slot a hand high cut through the fallen rubble at the east end looks down the incline onto the nest's pivot slab, its lintel a single wedged stone.
**Pitched Hide Roll:** A hide roll sealed with pitch is wedged in the rubble up behind the squint's lintel stone, out of sight from below, and an arm reached up behind that stone finds it (Unique Treasure: The Grey Knife).
**Painted Block:** A rubble block beside the floor hatch carries a smear of red paint on its upturned face, and turning it over shows rows of notches scratched in fives on its underside beside a red thumbprint in the same paint.
**Exits:** hatch of mortared flagstones on a timber frame set flush in the floor at the east end, too heavy for one back and heaved up by two together, over a short incline down to the nest's pivot slab -> C.31 The Chittering Nest
