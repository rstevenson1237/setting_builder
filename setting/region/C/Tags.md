Tags of C The Ravine

- **Cut Threshold** - every doorway in the ravine's oldest halls is dressed stone squared to the same measure, whoever's since moved in around it.
- **Sealing Glyph** - a bind-rune cut into the lintel of certain passages, unreadable to anyone who hasn't learned the Old Tongue by rote.
- **Tribe Line** - the point in any tunnel where dressed Old Tongue stone gives way to a tribe's own rough-cut digging, plain to see underfoot.
- **Sentry Horn** - every household posts a horn-call at its own entrance, answered from deeper in before the sentry itself is seen.
- **Household Hoard** - what a tribe's taken in raid and not yet traded up the ravine is kept together in one guarded chamber, never scattered through its halls.
- **Old Water** - cisterns cut by the ravine's first builders still hold water in the oldest chambers, fouled or clean depending who's since fouled it.
- **Rite Smoke** - a particular smoke, sweet and heavy, marks wherever a Grozzgur rite has been spoken recently.
- **Bound Post** - a fixed point - a door, a chamber, a chokepoint - that something set centuries ago still answers to, whatever's since moved in around it.
- **Trophy Wall** - cut ears and other trophies taken from outsiders are kept on display somewhere in a household's own hall, not hidden.
- **Rival Marker** - a scratched-out sign at a boundary between two households, redone by each side faster than the other can erase it.
- **Shrine Road** - the one route every household's tribute travels upward, watched by whichever household currently holds the stretch it crosses.
- **Dripping Vault** - a chamber where standing water and old stone together grow the pale, weeping fungus no household will clear itself.
- **Bat Roost** - a chamber deep enough to stay dark all day, shared by no household because nothing living wants to clear it out.
- **Cracked Floor** - stone floor cracked rather than worn, wherever the largest and fewest tribe has walked it often enough.
- **Harness Rack** - tack and saddle for a household's mounts, kept at whichever chamber can feed the beast stabled there.
- **Grave Niche** - a cut recess holding a household's own dead, sealed differently by every household that keeps one.
- **Draft Reversal** - a passage where the air moves against its own slope, felt before it's understood.
- **Warband Paint** - the particular colour and pattern a household marks its own fighters with, worn nowhere else in the ravine the same way.
- **Old Ledger** - tally-marks cut into a wall by whoever first counted what this chamber held, in a hand no household's since matched.
- **Second Entrance** - a household's own back way, known to its own people and kept from every rival's notice.
- **Silent Reach** - a stretch of tunnel where no sound carries at all, past which two people standing an arm's length apart can't hear each other.
- **Cold Chamber** - a chamber colder than anything around it, for no reason any household's ever explained.
- **Guarded Crossing** - a passage two households both claim a right to, held by whichever posted a sentry there most recently.
- **Broken Ward** - a Grozzgur working half-undone, still doing part of what it was set to and no longer all of it.
- **Deep Stair** - a stair cut by the ravine's first builders, running lower than any household's since bothered to dig on their own.
