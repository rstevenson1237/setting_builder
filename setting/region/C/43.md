C.43 **Bolt-Hole** (medium) - *Slab, Timed Gap, Watcher*
A rubble-choked dug pocket split by a fallen **slab** and a broken **plinth**, a cold **draught** furring the stone with frost. A small grey **watcher** crouches behind the slab with a red-banded horn at its neck.
*Twelve feet east to west by eight north to south, once a warren's escape run and now a ceiling fall over dug earth, its way onward buried and only the gap under the slab still open. The cold comes off the gap, and the pocket behind the slab lies out of the draught.*
**Draught:** Frost-furred stone at the lip of the gap under the slab, cold air pouring through, a full load dragged under it -> gear frosts stiff and a torch is spent.
**Slab:** Fallen ceiling slab across the west half, a padlocked hide hung behind its lower edge and tugged by the draught, opened with the Bolt-Hole Key -> C.53 Drip Gallery Entrance (Keys: Bolt-Hole Key).
**Watcher:** Khughik crouched in the still pocket behind the slab, gnawing a bone, scuttling toward the hide when a light reaches it (Twitchy, 1, Bestiary : Khughik).
**Horn:** Ram-horn with a scuffed red-painted band, hung on a rope cord round the watcher's neck (Quest: The Lost Horn).
**Plinth:** Squat dressed-stone plinth against the north wall, Qathsiq cut in its face, its effigy broken to gravel around it.
**Spear Shaft:** Red-daubed spear shaft snapped short in the mouth of the gap, a leather wrist-strap still knotted round it.
**Exits:** Portcullis - iron bars dropped from a ceiling slot, rust-fused and hanging a forearm above the floor, east wall -> C.40 The Chittering Nest
