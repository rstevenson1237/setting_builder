C.43 **Bolt-Hole** (medium) - *Slab, Timed Gap, Watcher*
A cold crawlspace of fallen vault slabs, the middle of it a **rimed squeeze** furred white with frost, with a **Khughik watcher** crouched in a hollow beside it and a brass horn on a thong round its neck. Beyond, a half-buried **kneeling effigy** faces a **loose slab** whose edges whistle with cold air.
*A cavity about 15 feet by 10 and rarely more than four feet high, all that is left of an old builders' guardroom beside a sealed arch after its vault came down, and now the Khughik of the Old Cistern's watch over their escape. Its old name, Qathsiq, is cut on the effigy's plinth. The whistle from the loose slab's edges carries to the hatch, the frost reaches only as far as the draught blows, and the rubble underfoot shifts at every step.*
**Rimed Squeeze:** Between two tilted vault slabs in the middle of the cavity a squeeze a shoulder wide is furred with hoarfrost where the draught from the loose slab blows through it, anyone pushing through with a full pack, a carried body or any load that fills it -> the load freezes fast to the rime and is torn free only with a ripping noise heard in the nest and the torn gear left behind, and the hollow under the lower slab lies out of the draught and bare of rime, loads can be passed through it one piece at a time at the cost of a long crawl back and forth.
**Khughik Watcher:** Crouched in the hollow under the lower slab and watching the hatch with a stoppered gourd at its feet, a lone Khughik wears a brass-bound garrison horn on a thong, blows it and flees for the loose slab the moment it sees anyone it does not know, and the horn goes with it (Watchful, 1, Bestiary : Khughik) (Quest: The Lost Horn).
**Frozen Spear:** A garrison spear snapped a hand below the head is frozen by its haft into the rime of the squeeze, with a scrap of blue cloak torn and frozen beside it.
**Kneeling Effigy:** Half-buried in rubble at the back of the cavity, a stone figure kneels with its cupped hands held out at its waist and its gaze bent down into them, a hole bored through each wrist and its palms worn smooth, a dry channel stained with tide lines runs across the floor from its knees to the foot of the loose slab, and a second stoppered gourd sits in a scooped hollow beside its knee, water poured into the cupped hands -> it runs out through the wrists and along the channel and the arch behind the loose slab stands open for as long as the channel runs wet, anything else left in the hands -> the draught through the slab gusts to a freezing blast that snuffs every flame in the cavity.
**Loose Slab:** Leaning against the rear wall beside the effigy, a slab taller than a person whistles with cold air round every edge and walks aside on its lower corner when two push it, baring the dressed stone arch behind.
**Exits:** hinged plank hatch on leather straps, swollen tight in its frame and opened only by a shoulder driven hard into it, front of the cavity -> C.40 The Chittering Nest, dressed stone arch behind the loose slab with the bind-rune on its keystone and a black crawl beyond breathing cold air, warded so that it refuses anyone while the effigy's channel is dry, anyone forcing it dry -> thrown back frost-burned (Test of Constitution, 1d, Frost), rear wall -> C.53 Drip Gallery Entrance
