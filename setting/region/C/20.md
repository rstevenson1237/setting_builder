C.20 **Nessekirel** (low) - *breach, entrance, swimming*
A dead-end chamber on the third course with its **ceiling broken through** where a drainage run came down from above and brought the vault with it, so that a shaft of rubble and open water goes up into the dark. The room stands **thigh-deep** and the water in it is moving, which it should not be, and there are **bones** caught in the rubble at the waterline.
*A room 25 by 25 feet, ceiling 9 feet where it survives and open where it does not, under about 30 inches of moving water. It was a plain cistern annexe. It is now the complex's second entrance, and it is a dead end in every direction but up. It smells of the fen rather than of Siwapelu - reed rot and open water - which is the first warning a party gets that the way out here goes somewhere different.*
**Broken Ceiling:** A ragged opening about 4 feet across in the vault, with the drainage run above it choked with fallen stone and the fen's silt. It is a swimmable ascent of some twenty feet on a held breath, coming up inside the drowned pan-house at Naugfast. Going up is harder than coming down and neither is possible carrying a load.
**Bones:** Caught in the rubble at the waterline, the ribs and long bones of at least four large fish and one thing that is not a fish - a human forearm, still in an oiled leather wrapping, the leather Torsgaard-made and no more than three years old. It was not carried here; it was brought here, which is a different thing, and the eels came in this way.
**Moving Water:** The water in this dead-end room runs steadily north, out through the wall culvert, which means something is driving it - it is the inflow at Azzakire, three rooms away, and the current here is the far end of the same system. A party that has been in this room understands, when they later reach Azzakire, that the two are connected.
**Exits:** flooded corbelled arch in the north wall, sill under water at all tides -> C.18 Sipelu, broken opening in the vaulted ceiling, four feet across, a held-breath swim straight up some twenty feet -> B.5 Naugfast
