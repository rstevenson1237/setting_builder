C.20 **Stores** (medium) - *Tally Lamp, Traded Up, Marked Goods*
Head-high stacks of **rotted sacks** stand in three aisles under a fur of grey powder, with **branded crates** along the south wall and a lamp niche by the steps holding a notched **tally board**. By a breach in the west wall a stack of black-corded **tribute bundles** stands apart on a clean patch of floor.
*A dressed storeroom 45 feet by 30, the household's stores gone to rot, its air thick with dust and the sour smell of spoiled meal. From the breach in the west wall comes a sweet smell of rot and, now and then, a wet sound of something chewing.*
**Rotted Sacks:** The grain and meal in the three aisles have rotted to a grey powder that lies ankle-deep between the stacks -> anyone moving quickly in the aisles raises a fog of it that shuts sight to arm's length and chokes torchlight to a glow until it settles, costing the time to grope out, and it never reaches the lamp niche by the east steps, whose floor is swept bare.
**Tally Board:** In a niche by the east steps a clay lamp with a crusted wick sits on a swept ledge above a board of split planks notched in rows under painted picture-signs, a red handprint heading the top row and the lowest rows cut short at a scorch mark (Lore: Gorzgur Tally Board).
**Wicket Lock:** The iron box lock on the wicket in the north grille is stained black and tacky round its keyhole, a dead rat curled on the floor beneath, and a light held close shows a needle's point just inside the keyhole -> anything but its own key turned in the lock drives the needle into the hand that turns it (Test of Constitution, 2d, Poison).
**Branded Crates:** Along the south wall stand crates and casks burnt with the realm's toll brand, some with the brand scraped half away and a red hand daubed over it, holding road goods gone to rot, salt fish, bolts of mildewed cloth, and one sound cask of lamp oil.
**Tribute Bundles:** By the west breach a stack of bundles tied in black cord stands on a patch of floor clean of dust, each tagged with a bone tab scratched with two faceless standing figures.
**Exits:** four cut steps rising to a doorway hung with a beaded cord screen, east wall -> C.19 Mess Hall, iron grille in a dressed arch with a wicket of bars set into it, the wicket shut by an iron box lock and the rope drum and bells on the grille's far side, north wall -> C.17 Household Hoard Vault, low breach knocked through the dressed wall with its broken edges scraped smooth by passing shoulders, west wall -> C.21 Grave Niche
