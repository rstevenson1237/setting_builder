C.20 **The Cold Vestibule** (low) - *unnatural chill, bare stone, warning sign*
A bare antechamber here is noticeably colder than the passages leading into it, breath misting even without exertion.

**Chill:** A recognizable warning sign to anyone who's faced its like before.
