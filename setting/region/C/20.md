C.20 **Sealed Niche of the Nine** (medium) - *sealed, fragmentary, grim*
A shallow niche cut into the cistern-side wall here has been deliberately sealed with mortared rubble, hasty compared to the precision stonework around it. Scratched into the mortar, barely legible, are nine names.
*A small alcove roughly 6 by 4 feet, set into an otherwise smooth dressed-stone wall, sealed with a rough mortar patch that doesn't match the surrounding masonry's quality. The air here smells faintly of old candle-tallow, and everything is dead quiet.*
**Sealed Mortar:** the rough seal itself, scratched over with nine names in a shaking, hurried hand - breaking through the mortar, which gives way easily to a few solid blows, reveals a cramped hollow behind it holding a single guttered-out lantern and a folded, water-damaged map marking a route through the lower galleries that avoids two of the region's known dangers entirely (Lore: The Sealed Route).
**Exits:** broken mortar gap, now open into the cistern -> C.19 The Lower Cistern
