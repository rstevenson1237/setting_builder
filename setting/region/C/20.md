C.20 **Stores** (medium) - *Tally Lamp, Traded Up, Marked Goods*
A dark, close storeroom of dressed stone stacked with branded **crates**, a row of chalked **bundles** apart by the wall, a notched **board** hung beside a dead **lamp**. Two gated ways lead north and a broken wall opens south.
*The room is 30 feet by 20 under a 7-foot ceiling, the household's store for goods it keeps and trades on, badly kept and still filled. Stale air holds the lamp's oil stink close, and soot marks streak the wall above the lamp.*
**Lamp:** Stale still air holds torches low, the lamp's wick dead -> moving quickly snuffs a torch, costing it and a turn in the dark, the breach end stands clear.
**Board:** A notched wooden board hangs by the lamp, four columns of notches each headed by a tribe's mark, the column headed by a fist left bare (Lore: Gorzgur Tally Board).
**Crates:** Crates of trade goods stand stacked to the ceiling, foreign brands scratched out and the red band burned over them.
**Bundles:** Six hide bundles corded shut and chalked with three short strokes stand apart along the east wall.
**Grille Stone:** A stone block hangs on a cord inside the grille, a flattened Khughik skeleton on the floor under it -> swinging the gate cuts the cord (Test of Constitution, 3d, Crushing).
**Lock Needle:** A black-crusted needle stands in the padlock's keyhole, a dead rat with blackened paws beneath -> forcing the lock drives it into the hand (Test of Constitution, 2d, Poison).
**Exits:** iron grille of flat bars riveted in a lattice with a gate hung on pins and a keyed lock, north wall west end -> C.19 Mess Hall, stitched ox-hide curtain slung on an iron bar padlocked to a wall ring with a fist-sized keyed lock, north wall east end -> C.17 Household Hoard Vault, hole knocked through the dressed wall with broken edges chipped rough and the course-ends showing, rubble cleared, south wall -> C.21 Grave Niche
