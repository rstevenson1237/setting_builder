C.24 **The Last Reliquary** (high) - *final chamber, oldest grave-goods, deepest secret*
The barrow's deepest and oldest chamber holds grave-goods and inscriptions predating Vael's fall itself, its stonework untouched by the flooding that marked the rest of the Cairn.
*A finished, finely-worked chamber roughly 30 feet across, square, built as Vael's original reliquary and never repurposed; the air here is unnervingly dry and still compared to the rest of the flooded barrow. Studying the inscriptions carefully reveals something no living hold, faction, or ledger currently remembers about why the sea first turned on Vael.*
**Inscriptions:** Predate Vael's fall; careful study reveals a piece of history unknown to anyone else in the setting.
**Grave-Goods:** The oldest and finest in the barrow, centered on a single object unlike anything else found here (Unique Treasure: The Vaelmourn Circlet).
**Exits:** wide stone archway -> C.21 Bell-Wight's Chamber, hand-widened passage -> C.23 Basin Depths
