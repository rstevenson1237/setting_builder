C.24 **The Brawling Pit** (high) - *Killing Roll, Warband Paint*
A wide pit is where Hukkgur settles anything by strength alone, the floor cracked deep enough to catch a foot from all the bare-handed brawling done here.
*Rough cavern floor thirty yards across, sunk a yard below the halls around it, active, cracked floor throughout rather than at any one spot, no weapon-rack or banner anywhere in it.*
**Pit Floor:** The floor here is cracked and pitted a yard down from the surrounding halls, worn that way by bare-handed brawling rather than by any tool.
**Hukkgur Band:** A handful of Hukkgur answer any challenge to their strength directly here rather than posting a real watch, and don't stop swinging once they've decided to (Answering, 4, Bestiary : Hukkgur).
**Ghorn Bare-Knuckle:** Among them, one's knuckles are scarred past any other's, put down more than once and never for long, the rest giving room without being told to (Named Creature: Ghorn Bare-Knuckle).
**Wagered Take:** What's wagered and lost here by whoever's challenged and beaten sits piled at the pit's own edge, nobody's claimed rather than everybody's (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** natural gap, west wall -> C.23 Broken Gate, archway, north wall -> C.25 Bone Trophy Hall, archway, east wall -> C.26 Sleeping Den, natural gap, south wall -> C.29 Second Entrance
