C.24 **Sizoreth** (medium) - *threshold, unpassed, lid*
Past the heaped salt the passage opens into a chamber that is not finished. The **walls stop** at about eight feet and above that is raw cut rock; the **floor** is dressed flags for twenty feet and then bare stone; and at the far side there is a **shaft** going down, ten feet across, perfectly circular, with cut hand-holds descending into it and no bottom that a light will find.
*A chamber about 35 by 35 feet, ceiling irregular between 8 and 14 feet, half-built and abandoned mid-construction. It is the head of the fourth course and it is as far as anyone has been and come back. It is silent - not quiet, silent; the constant carrying sound of the rest of Siwapelu stops dead at the salt threshold behind, and a party's own voices sound close and flat.*
**Walls Stop:** The dressed courses run up eight feet and end in a level line all round the room, with raw rock above. The Odarath were building this chamber and stopped, and they stopped everywhere at once - there is no half-laid course, no stone left on a bank, no tools. They finished a day's work, went up, and did not come back down, and then somebody laid a foot of salt across the threshold behind them.
**Shaft:** A circular shaft 10 feet across in the chamber's southern side, cut true, with hand-holds cut down its wall at 18-inch intervals. A dropped stone is not heard landing. The air out of it is not sour - it is clean and very cold, and the Gorm track does not advance in this room, which is the most alarming single fact in Siwapelu given everything above it. This is the way to the fourth course proper and this framework stops here: what is below is Zorethanu at 14d6+6, it has never come up, and the party's decision is whether that remains true.
**Cap Stone:** The chamber's one finished arch, the one the party came through, carries a blank cap - the third of the three thresholds in Siwapelu without the character. The other two are the dome at Nuthorath and the threshold at Zorethora, and a party that has found all three has the shape of the whole complex: the character marks a gate that is meant to be passed, and these three were never meant to be (Unique Treasure: Immo Cap of the Third Threshold).
**Exits:** corbelled arch in the north wall, blank capped, heaped salt across its sill -> C.23 Zorethora
