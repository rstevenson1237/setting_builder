C.24 **The Crawler's Deep** (high) - *deepest, tunneled, hunting*
Countless narrow tunnels converge on this cavern, far too many and far too smoothly bored for anything human to have made them. Something large moves in the dark beyond the torchlight's reach, never quite showing the same shape twice.
*An irregular cavern roughly 40 by 30 feet, its walls pocked with dozens of smooth-bored tunnels too narrow for a person, dropping away into total dark. The air is close and stale, and every sound - a footstep, a whisper - seems to come from more than one direction at once.*
**Tunnel Mouths:** dozens of narrow borings pocking the cavern walls at every height, none large enough for a person to follow far - home to the Ninefold Hunger, a Deep Crawler that moves through them low and fast (Named Creature: The Ninefold Hunger); no two who've glimpsed it agree on what it actually looks like, and it is already aware of anything that lingers here.
**Old Remains:** bones and rusted gear from past victims wedged into several of the wider tunnel mouths, out of easy reach but visible - recovering what's salvageable is possible only while the Crawler's attention is elsewhere (Treasure V, d20).
**Exits:** passage, back up toward the breached vein -> C.23 The Breached Vein
