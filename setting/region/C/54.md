C.54 **The Chittering Nest** (high) - *Rot Smell, Thinning, Count*
The chittering here competes with a steady drip from the gallery next door, this claw's own numbers thinned by whatever grows in the damp beside them.
*Dug chamber thirteen yards across, active, a sweet, faint rot smell reaching in from the weeping growth next door.*
**Warren:** A half-dozen Khughik share this chamber, swarming anything smaller and scattering against anything larger that fights back, fewer of them than most claws for however long the growth next door's been spreading (Swarming, 5, Bestiary : Khughik).
**Steady Drip:** A drip from the gallery next door sounds through the wall at an even count, loud enough in the otherwise-quiet chamber to cover a footstep timed against it.
**Claw's Take:** What's been carried back sits furthest from the shared wall, moved there rather than risk it against whatever the drip's own growth might do to it (Treasure III, d20).
