Places:
- C.1 The Watch Passage - the way in; the chevrons and spoke heap show what the tribe is taking.
- C.2 The Warren - the tribe's sleeping hall, 10-14 goblins; the way on to C.4 and C.5.
- C.3 The Mushroom Pits - the tribe's food; the Chest Key lies here in a pouch.
- C.4 The Spoil Hall - Treasure IV in the low crates; a roped stack over them; a hidden gap to C.5.
- C.5 The Chief's Hall - Gronab, the bone seat, the locked chest holding Bruzshub, and a pouch of Magic.
- C.6 Thono - the makers' carvings, The Foreman's Cut, and a shaft cover.

Alarm: Raised by the watchers' clicking at C.1 or by Gronab's bellow in C.5; the sleepers in C.2 hear it first. The 10-14 goblins of C.2 come, one turn to C.1, C.4 or C.5, two to C.3, three to C.6, and Gronab fights from his seat.

Loot: The largest prize is Bruzshub in C.5, 5900 silver and 16 wt in a locked chest under Gronab, its key in C.3. Ordinary finds draw on Treasure IV and Magic. Pell, A.3, buys finds at half value and pays less for anything chevron-marked; the commander, A.4, wants the signet ring with the grey tower.

Secrets:
- The shaft in C.6 goes down past anything the goblins have cut, and its cover lifts to a ring - nobody living; Mystery C.6.
- The makers cut the cave for ore, and the lower cuts were left unfinished - nobody living; The Foreman's Cut, C.6.
- Goblins hold back from anyone seated in the bone seat - the goblins; Mystery C.5.
- Pulling a low crate in C.4 brings the roped stack down - the goblins; Hazard C.4.
- C.4 and C.5 join behind the heaped crates - the goblins; Connection C.4 to C.5.
- The Chest Key in C.3 opens the chest in C.5 - nobody now; Lock on Reward C.5.

- Rumour 2 - the cave predates the goblins; settled at C.6 and C.2.
- Rumour 3 - wolves strip the wagons; settled at C.4.
- Rumour 4 - goblins carry whole wagons in; settled at C.4 and C.1.
- Rumour 5 - something went below; the referee's, tried at C.6.
- Rumour 6 - a bandit leads them; settled at C.5.
