C.39 **Old Cistern Entrance** (low) - *Damp, Seep, Squeeze*
A small rubble-floored room whose west wall is broken through into a one-person squeeze, water seeping down the broken face, a **warning cut** in the block above it and a **rat mark** scratched on the jamb below.
*Ten feet square, once the Khughik's watch post over the way in and now a fallen ceiling over the west half, the seat and sill it kept gone to rubble. Seep water runs down the breach face and pools on the floor below the rat mark.*
**Warning Cut:** Kutsut cut across the lintel block above the breach, water tracing its grooves, the block hanging a hand's width out of true.
**Rat Mark:** Rat scratched with a knife point on the breach jamb at knee height, its tail running toward the breach.
**Exits:** Breach - hole knocked through the dressed wall, broken block edges, rubble sill hip-high and one person wide, west wall -> C.11 Sentry Gate, Leaf - oak planks on strap hinges, swollen at the foot, barred from the far side by an iron bar, north wall west end -> C.40 The Chittering Nest, Arch - dressed-stone voussoirs, bind-rune worn smooth at the keystone, sound, north wall east end -> C.41 Store Nook
