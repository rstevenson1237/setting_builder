C.39 **Old Cistern Entrance** (low) - *Tribal Warren, Second Entrance*
A dug opening barely shoulder-width marks this claw's own way out, damp reaching it from the cistern chamber further in.
*Cramped dug passage six yards long, active, walls damp rather than dry, silt and standing damp tracked in equally.*
**Damp Passage:** The passage here stays damp regardless of season, water finding its way out from the cistern chamber further in rather than from anything outside.
