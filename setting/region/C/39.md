C.39 **Old Cistern Entrance** (low) - *Damp, Seep, Squeeze*
A rubble-choked hollow of fallen dressed stone opens off a tight **breach squeeze**, where a **bronze bar** juts from the wall inside the gap. A **seep** trickles across a floor strewn with the warren's **eating heap**, and a sharp crack of splitting bone comes again and again from beyond a slab in the north wall.
*A hollow about 20 feet across and ragged-sided, all that is left of an old builders' sluice house after its vault came down into it, now where the Khughik of the Old Cistern eat. The Old Tongue letters Kutsut are cut over the breach on its inner face, and the escaped captive's scratched rat beside it is why Greywatch calls this the Rat Door. The bone-cracking is Zoghik's, in the nest (C.40). Cold and wet, the floor slick with seep mud between the rubble.*
**Bronze Bar:** A stride inside the breach on the hollow's side of the old wall face, a bronze bar juts from the stone at waist height in a slot a forearm long, its top rubbed bright, the slot scored deepest at its bottom and a chain running from the bar up into a hole that clanks when it moves, the mud floor of the squeeze is printed with small bare footprints that halt short of the old wall face and go on past it only from a smear where a body stood by the bar, grimy handprints sit on the wall above the slot at a Khughik's reach, and pressing the bar hard to the bottom of its slot and letting go -> the chain clanks up and the breach stands open until the bar creeps back to the top on its counterweight, about a count of twenty, pressing it only partway -> it jams at the midpoint and holds the breach shut until it is hammered free, a noise heard at the Sentry Gate.
**Breach Marks:** Cut over the breach's inner face are the Old Tongue letters Kutsut, and scratched into the jamb below them at shoulder height is the outline of a rat with a line drawn under it.
**Eating Heap:** Across the middle of the floor lie gnawed rat bones, fungus stalks and a ring of hearth stones black with old char, and the largest bones are split lengthwise down their whole length as if with a single heavy blow.
**Seep:** Water beads out of a crack high in the east wall and runs in a sheet down the rubble and across the floor into the sluice mouth, leaving the rubble below the crack too slick to climb.
**Exits:** breach, a squeeze a shoulder wide knocked through a thick dressed-stone wall, warded so that it refuses anyone crossing while the bronze bar stands at the top of its slot, anyone forcing it then -> thrown back across the squeeze onto the rubble (Test of Fate, Condition), west wall -> C.11 Sentry Gate, pivot slab, a salvaged facing block hung on an iron pin in a hacked hole that swings to a single push, north wall -> C.40 The Chittering Nest, sluice, a square stone water mouth at floor level with its bronze sluice plate seized half down in scaled grooves, wide enough for a Khughik to wriggle under and opened for a person only by levering the plate up with a bar, east wall -> C.41 Store Nook
