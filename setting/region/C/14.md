C.14 **Muster Yard** (high) - *Drill, Cracks, Three Ways*
A wide cavern floor cracked from years of drilling boots is where Gorzgur forms up before any raid, a sentry posted at every one of its own three ways out.
*Natural cavern forty yards across, active, floor cracked in long lines radiating from its own centre, ceiling high enough to lose in torchlight.*
**Cracked Floor:** The cavern's own floor is cracked rather than worn in long lines radiating outward, each crack widest where boots have crossed it most.
**Drilling Band:** A half-dozen Gorzgur drill here at any hour, forming ranks the instant a horn call answers whatever's approaching rather than scattering (Forming, 6, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Sentry Post:** A raised ledge overlooking all three of the yard's own exits holds a single sentry whose horn call is answered from deeper in before the sentry itself is seen (Watching, 1, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Exits:** archway, south wall -> C.12 Warband Hall, natural gap, east wall -> C.15 Harness Rack Stable, fitted door, north wall -> C.22 Rival Watch Point
