C.14 **The Silt Pool** (medium) - *still water, hidden drop-off, murky depth*
A still, silt-clouded **pool** spans the passage here, its surface unbroken and giving no hint of how deep it runs.
*A silt-clouded pool spanning the passage, roughly 15 feet across, in what was once a dry corridor. The pool looks shallow but hides a sudden drop-off near its far edge - a genuine irregularity in an otherwise dry barrow; testing depth with a pole or rope before crossing avoids a sudden submersion.*
**Pool:** Hides a drop-off near its far edge; wading in carelessly risks a submersion, testing first avoids it.
**Something Underfoot:** Something can be felt near the drop-off, recoverable only by searching the deeper water directly (Treasure I, d20).
