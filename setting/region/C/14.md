C.14 **Muster Yard** (high) - *Drill, Cracks, Three Ways*
Under a vault split end to end by one long crack, its west third fallen into a slope of rubble half-burying an **iron-banded coffer**, six **Gorzgur spearmen** stand in rank behind a red-painted line with spears levelled at the crack's mouth in the north wall. In that mouth a huge, scarred **Hukkgur brawler** beats his bare fists bloody on the rock and bellows across the yard.
*An old-cut hall 50 feet by 40, its ceiling 10 feet where it still stands, the west third brought down into a rubble slope, used by Gorzgur as their drill ground, with a row of hide targets stuffed with straw along the east wall and the floor between scuffed bare in two lanes. The crack runs across the vault and down the north wall into the gap to the Rival Watch Point, and grit sifts from it wherever it is struck, so the brawler's pounding can be heard as a steady patter of falling stone from the Warband Hall steps.*
**Hukkgur Brawler:** Ghorn stands in the crack's mouth in the north wall, pounding the rock until his knuckles bleed and roaring a challenge in Grozzgur for any one of them to come and meet him bare-handed, and nobody in the yard answers it (Named Creature: Ghorn Bare-Knuckle).
**Gorzgur Spearmen:** Six warriors hold a double rank four strides back from the crack behind a line of red paint across the floor, spears levelled and not crossing the line, and the rearmost of them keeps turning to look toward the Warband Hall steps (Wary, 6, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Iron-Banded Coffer:** At the foot of the rubble slope by the west wall, the lid and one corner of an iron-banded coffer show from under a fallen roof slab, fused to the floor by a skin of flowstone, and chiselling it free takes the better part of an hour (Treasure III, d20).
**Pot String:** A cord of cracked pots and knucklebones hangs from a peg driven into the slab above the coffer, newly knotted, and every blow of a chisel or pick on the flowstone sets it clattering loud enough to turn every head in the yard.
**Stuffed Crack:** Low in the east wall behind the hide targets a crack a hand wide runs up from the floor, its mouth stuffed with a wad of greasy hide, and behind the wad sits a clay jar broken open at the shoulder with a cache still inside (Treasure III, d20).
**Crack Snare:** Behind the jar a gut noose on a bent-bone spring still holds a dried rat by the neck -> a hand reaching past the wad for the jar is caught at the wrist and held until the gut is cut, and the jerk of it rattles a hide target against the wall.
**Exits:** natural cleft in the south end of the floor where the old cutting cracked open, the back of a warped plank gate swollen in its frame across its top and cut steps dropping beneath, south end -> C.12 Warband Hall, iron portcullis dropped from a slot in a dressed lintel and rusted fast a foot above the floor, heaved up only by several backs together or crawled under on the belly, east wall -> C.15 Harness Rack Stable, the crack's own mouth where it widens into a raw gap in the rock just wide enough for one to squeeze through sideways, north wall -> C.22 Rival Watch Point
