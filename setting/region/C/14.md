C.14 **The Miner's Rest** (low) - *hollowed, sparse, forgotten*
A shallow, hollowed-out alcove offers a rare stretch of quiet away from the gallery's stranger sounds. A few old bedrolls, stiff with age, are still stacked against the back wall.
*A small alcove roughly 10 by 8 feet, walls smoothed by regular use rather than any natural process. It's noticeably quieter here than the gallery outside, and the air smells only of dust.*
**Old Bedrolls:** a stack of stiff, decades-old bedrolls against the back wall - this was a rest stop for the original crew, and it's held its relative safety ever since.
