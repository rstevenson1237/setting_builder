C.14 **Muster Yard** (high) - *Drill, Cracks, Three Ways*
A wrecked stone yard under a flat roof carried on one central **pillar**, where a giant **Hukkgur** hammers the pillar's foot and six **Gorzgur** stand at the west end. Split flags run to all three ways, and a wet **purse** lies in a stone **trough**.
*The yard is 45 feet by 40 under an 8-foot slab, the household's drill ground, its posts and racks smashed and its floor split. Knuckle thuds and falling chips point at the pillar, and a drip runs in the trough.*
**Hukkgur:** Ghorn Bare-Knuckle, a head taller than a big man, hammers a bare fist into the pillar's foot, chips dropping, knuckles scarred to the bone (Truculent, 1, Bestiary : Hukkgur) (Named Creature: Ghorn Bare-Knuckle).
**Gorzgur:** Six Gorzgur stand in two ranks at the west end, spears levelled at the Hukkgur, not advancing, red paint fresh (Disciplined, 6, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Pillar:** A dressed-stone pillar cut with the bind-rune holds the roof at the yard's centre, split flags running from its foot to every wall.
**Purse:** A wet leather purse lies in a dressed-stone trough fused to the floor, its strap tied to a cord across the yard -> lifting it (Treasure III, d20).
**Rack Cord:** The purse strap's cord runs to a smashed spear-rack propped on its last post -> lifting the purse topples the rack, clattering across the yard.
**Breach:** A niche in the south wall was broken open, its bricked face in chunks and a dark gap behind under a propped lintel -> reaching in finds (Treasure III, d20).
**Prop:** A splintered prop holds the niche's lintel, a dead Khughik pinned under its fallen half -> pulling stones drops the lintel, costing an hour of digging and a spoiled pack.
**Sluice Chain:** A chain from the sluice-board runs to a wall hook, a dead ford-rat hung on it by the neck -> hauling the board rattles it loose, costing the quiet.
**Exits:** wide dug sluice-channel with an oak sluice-board swollen fast in its groove and freed only by force, west wall -> C.12 Warband Hall, stiff dung-crusted ox-hide hung on a rail from a stone counterweight and lifted only when two haul the cord down, south wall -> C.15 Harness Rack Stable, natural gap in the rock with chipped-rough edges, east wall -> C.22 Rival Watch Point
