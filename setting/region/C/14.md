C.14 **The Barrow-Kin Grave-Row** (low) - *tended graves, recent offerings left, undisturbed if left alone*
Neat graves line both sides of **The Barrow-Kin Grave-Row**, markers upright and swept clean, small offerings of cloth and dried flowers left at several of them.
*A long, narrow chamber roughly 30 by 8 feet, floor swept clear of the rubble that litters most of Ingaraz; smells of old tallow and dried flowers rather than dust, tended rather than merely quiet.*
**Tended Markers:** Reading the markers without disturbing them costs only time and turns up real names and dates - generations of the same family holds, recorded nowhere the keep would ever think to look.
**Exits:** open passage, swept floor, west -> C.8 The Barrow-Kin Threshold, low archway, cold draft, north -> C.7 The Wrong-Facing Grave
