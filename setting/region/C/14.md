C.14 **Laanidorai** (low) - *corridor, distance, sound*
A long featureless corridor running south off the second course, wholly empty, ending at a **broken lip** where the floor has fallen away into a drop, with **cold wet air** coming up out of it.
*A corridor 90 by 6 feet, ceiling 9 feet, ending in an 18-foot vertical drop into water. It was a plain passage down to the third course and the stair that served it has collapsed entirely. The corridor's length and bare walls make it the most acoustically active space in Siwapelu - a party's own footsteps come back at them from ahead, which is unnerving for ninety feet and then stops mattering.*
**Broken Lip:** The corridor's south end simply ends: the floor and the stair beyond it have gone, leaving a clean broken edge and an 18-foot drop into standing water. Going down is possible with a rope, or without one at a cost - it is a fall into 22 inches of water over dressed flags, Damaging, no test. Coming back up without a rope fixed at the top is not possible, which makes this a one-way route into the third course and a party that takes it casually has committed itself.
**Cold Wet Air:** A steady flow of cold air up the corridor from the drop, carrying the smell of the flooded course - silt and the sour edge of the gorm. It gets stronger as the party goes south, which is the only information the corridor offers and it is enough: a party that reads it knows there is water ahead before they reach the lip.
**Exits:** corbelled arch at the north end -> C.13 Teruimmo, broken floor lip at the south end, an eighteen-foot drop into standing water, no way back up unaided -> C.17 Kireath
