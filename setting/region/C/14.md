C.14 **The Barrow-Kin Grave-Row** (low) - *tended graves, recent offerings left, undisturbed if left alone*
Neat graves line both sides of **The Barrow-Kin Grave-Row**, markers upright and swept clean, small offerings of cloth and dried flowers left at several of them.
*A long, narrow chamber roughly 30 by 8 feet, swept clear of Ingaraz's usual rubble; old tallow and dried flowers mark the Tended Markers' recent offerings.*
**Tended Markers:** Reading markers undisturbed costs time, reveals real family names.
