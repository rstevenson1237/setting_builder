C.57 **Cistern Approach** (low) - *Dead Sound, Slope, Standing Water*
A passage slopes down toward standing water somewhere ahead, no sound carrying back up it at all.
*Sloping passage fifteen yards long, active, footing dry until the last few strides, no echo returning however loud a call.*
**Dead Sound:** No sound carries at all past a stated point partway down the passage, and anyone testing it finds their own voice simply stops rather than fades.
**Exits:** natural gap, up -> C.51 Water Nook, natural gap, down -> C.58 The Flooded Hall, natural gap, east -> C.59 Damp Gallery, natural gap, west -> C.60 Old Cistern Nook
