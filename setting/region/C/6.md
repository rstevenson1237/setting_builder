C.6 **Old Quarter** - *Serpent Cult, Unopened Door*
Velar's original streets, narrower and older than the trade district around them, predate the Wagons. **Three doors** here have not opened, to a Wagons factor, in three months.
*A tangle of older stone houses, forty yards across, streets too narrow for a loaded wagon. Smells of the river and old smoke, and quieter after dark than the square, a street away.*
**Three Doors:** Three households of long standing, each turning away every Wagons factor sent, since the ford closed, each answering anyone else promptly.
**Old Debt:** The phrase the old debt, ordinary in Velar conversation, spoken at any of the three doors -> that door opens without question (Faction: The Serpent Cult).
**Exits:** open street - narrow, uneven paving, north side -> C.3 Market Square
