C.6 **Mezeora** (low) - *inscription, record, reading*
A plain square room whose four walls are **cut over with old letters** from floor to ceiling in neat ruled bands, and which contains nothing else at all - no fitting, no furniture, no niche.
*A room 25 by 25 feet, ceiling 9 feet, built for exactly one purpose: to be read. The letters are cut in the same careful unhurried hand as the pillars at Nuthorath. The room is acoustically dead - the ruled banding breaks up the sound - and it is the one place on this course where a party can talk without being heard elsewhere, which the Faelhond know and use.*
**Ruled Bands:** Nine horizontal bands of Old Rede running round all four walls, each band ruled top and bottom with a scored line, read left to right and top to bottom. This is a record rather than a binding - the same tongue as the pillars but a completely different purpose, and a party that has seen both can tell the difference by the ruling alone. It is the longest continuous text in Brackvaen (Lore: The Mezeora Course).
**Blank Band:** The ninth and lowest band, at knee height, is ruled and empty for its whole length round all four walls. The ruling was cut and the text never was. Whatever this room was recording, it was still being kept up to date when it stopped.
**Exits:** corbelled arch in the east wall, its jambs rubbed smooth at shoulder height -> C.5 Peluora, corbelled arch in the north wall, wider than the standard section, with a large dark space beyond -> C.7 Nuthorath
