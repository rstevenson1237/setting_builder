C.6 **Second Niche Row** (medium) - *burial alcoves, disturbed dust, rising dead*
A second row of burial alcoves lines this passage, several sealed with cracked **mortar** rather than left open like those encountered earlier.
*A rectangular passage roughly 25 by 10 feet, lined with burial alcoves; several are sealed with mortar rather than left open like those encountered earlier - a departure from the barrow's usual practice. Prying a sealed niche open carelessly triggers its occupant to rise immediately; a careful search for the freshest mortar seams gives warning first.*
**Sealed Niches:** Prying one open carelessly wakes a Drowned Skeleton (Bestiary); one sealed niche holds something among its occupant's remains (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** low stone passage -> C.4 Cracked Antechamber, collapsed doorway, rubble-choked -> C.7 Fallen Lintel
