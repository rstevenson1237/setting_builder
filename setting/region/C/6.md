C.6 **Second Niche Row** (medium) - *burial alcoves, disturbed dust, rising dead*
A second row of burial alcoves lines this passage, several sealed with cracked **mortar** rather than left open like those encountered earlier.
*Prying a sealed niche open carelessly triggers its occupant to rise immediately; a careful search for the freshest mortar seams gives warning first.*
**Sealed Niches:** Prying one open carelessly wakes a Drowned Skeleton (Bestiary); one sealed niche holds a small coral pendant among its occupant's remains.
**Exits:** passage -> C.4 Cracked Antechamber, passage -> C.7 Fallen Lintel
