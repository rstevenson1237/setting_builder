C.6 **Old Quarter** - *Serpent Cult, Unopened Door*
Velar's original streets, narrower and older than the rebuilt trade district around them, predate the Wagons entirely. Three respected households here have not opened their doors to a Wagons factor once in three months - long enough that people have started to notice.
*A tangle of older stone houses forty yards across, streets too narrow for a loaded wagon to enter. Smells of the river and old smoke; noticeably quieter after dark than the market square a street away.*
**Three Doors:** Three households, each of long standing in Velar, that have turned away every Wagons factor sent to call on them since the ford closed - each door otherwise ordinary, and each answered promptly to anyone else who knocks.
**Old Debt:** A phrase - "the old debt" - used in ordinary conversation among certain people in Velar to mean something else entirely to the right ear. Spoken plainly at any of the three doors, one opens without question; who answers, and what they say next, is not something Velar itself has ever learned (Faction: The Serpent Cult).
**Exits:** open street - narrow, uneven paving, north side -> C.3 Market Square
