C.6 **Thono** (Medium) - *carved walls, draught, cold*
A small round room at the foot of a worn stair, its walls covered in **carved figures** in rows, and a stone **shaft cover** with an **iron ring** set in the middle of the floor. A cold draught rises past the cover's edge.
*Thono is the makers' word for "shaft-place". The shaft is the end of the makers' work, and the goblins do not come here willingly. Nobody living reads the makers' tongue.*
**Carved Figures:** Rows of small figures are cut into the walls, all facing the shaft, with a single line of the makers' script beneath them (Lore: The Foreman's Cut).
**Shaft Cover:** A round stone cover lies flush with the floor over the shaft, scraped at the rim where it has been moved.
**Iron Ring:** An iron ring set in the cover has been worn bright by hands; hauling it lifts the cover onto a shaft whose bottom no light reaches.
**Exits:** archway, up the worn stair -> C.3 The Mushroom Pits
