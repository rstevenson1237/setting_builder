C.6 **The Chief's Hall** (High) - *high-roofed, firelit, guarded*
A high cavern black with smoke, a **fire pit** at its centre and a carved stone **seat** facing the south door. An iron-banded **chest** stands by the seat, **bales** are stacked against the wall, and an **arch** has fallen in the east wall.
*Cavern 40 ft by 40 ft, roof 25 ft. The chief and five guards feast at the fire; a shout from here wakes the warren. The chest is locked.*
**Fire Pit:** The chief and five guards eat and drink around the flames (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Goblin).
**Stone Seat:** Grey stone cut with marks, where the chief sits.
**Chest:** Locked. Inside lie the strongbox of the first wagon and the tribe's silver (Hoard: The Chief's Hoard), opened by a rusted iron key (Keys: Chief's Chest Key).
**Bales:** Stolen bales stacked against the wall, each marked with a red handprint (Treasure IV, d20).
**Fallen Arch:** A rubble slope in the east wall, passable on hands and knees.
**Exits:** wooden, south door, plank barricade -> C.3 The Warren, broken, east wall, collapsed arch -> C.5 The Old Vault
