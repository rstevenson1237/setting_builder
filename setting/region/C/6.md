C.6 **Old Ledger Vault** (medium) - *Spoken Side, Old Ledger*
Tally-marks cut into the far wall count out what this chamber once held, in a hand no household since has matched.
*Cut stone vault twelve yards across, abandoned, shelving long since rotted from the walls, the tally-marks the only thing here that hasn't decayed.*
**Old Ledger:** Tally-marks cut floor to ceiling on the far wall count out what the vault once held, in a single hand throughout despite a count that must have taken a lifetime to cut (Lore: Old Ledger).
**Trip-Cord:** A cord strung ankle-height between two fallen shelves is easy to see once it's pointed out, tied to a stack of loose stone rigged to slide and clatter down rather than to hurt whoever trips it (Test of Fate, Prone).
**Exits:** fitted door, south wall -> C.2 Tribute Hall, natural gap, east wall -> C.8 Stores of the Shrine
