C.6 **The Mere Landing** (Natural) - *wet, rotting, reedy*
A **jetty** of rotting planks reaches into reeds, a pole **boat** beside it, and a **path** of firm ground runs on toward the causeway.
*Jetty 20 ft long, 5 miles west of the trail by the mere path; half a mile of bog to the causeway.*
**Jetty:** Red-threaded reed bundles hang from its posts, and fish-bone points stand upright in the mud.
**Boat:** Beached and sound, its pole laid across the thwarts.
**Path:** A firm strip through bubbling mud; a step off it sinks a crosser to the thigh (Test of Fate, Held).
**Exits:** open, west end of the path -> D.1 The Causeway
