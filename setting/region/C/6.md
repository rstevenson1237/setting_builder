C.6 **The Offering Niche** (Low) - *cramped, dusty, pricked*

A low niche, too small to stand in, a ledge of clay offering bowls at its back. The ledge is pocked with tiny holes.

*A niche 10 feet square and 8 feet high off the end of the mural wall. Its dart trap is set off by a hand reaching in from the mural wall (C.3).*

**The Bowls:** Clay, dusty, a few old coins and beads in them (Treasure I, d20).
**The Pinholes:** Rows of them in the ledge's face, a dart's tip showing in one. Nobody living knows of them.

**Exits:** an open passage -> C.3 The Mural Wall
