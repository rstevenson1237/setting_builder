C.6 **The Drowned Cistern** (medium) - *standing old water, uneven submerged footing, echo behaves oddly*
Black, still water fills the floor of **The Drowned Cistern** from wall to wall, its true depth impossible to judge by looking. A length of old rope, tied off at both ends, still spans the middle of it.
*A square chamber roughly 15 feet on a side, floor entirely submerged under still, standing water; smells of old wet stone rather than rot, and a dropped stone here takes noticeably longer to sound than the drop should allow.*
**Submerged Drop:** The floor shelves away sharply a few feet past the rope's near end, deep enough to fully submerge someone carrying real weight. Impact: damaging - it won't drown a careful party, but a loaded pack and armor make it a real fight to surface and climb out. A stiller, darker patch of water marks the drop for anyone looking before stepping; the old rope traces the one safe line across.
**Lost Gear:** Whatever the rope's original crew dropped when they lost their footing still sits on the shelf's edge, recoverable by anyone willing to go in after it (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** low archway, still water, west -> C.5 The Bear's Den Mound, narrow gap, dry ledge, east -> C.9 The Collapsed Vault
