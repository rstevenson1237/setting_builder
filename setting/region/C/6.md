C.6 **The Burned Brig** (Ruin) - *blackened, beached, scattered*
A blackened brig lies beached with its mast down, char on the wind and scattered cargo along the tide line, a red sash snagged on a spar.
*150 by 50 feet. A pirate prize, burned after it was looted. The tide fills the hull at the turn.*
**The burned hull:** charred planks ticking in the sun, a red sash and a black cloth caught on the broken spar.
**The flooding hold:** a tide-line on the hull shows how high the water rises; a hold below deck floods quickly when the tide turns (Test of Constitution, 2d, Suffocation).
**The scattered cargo:** spilled barrels and charred bales along the tide line.
