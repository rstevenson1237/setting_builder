C.6 **The Sawn Vein Adit** (medium) - *unstable, dust-choked, narrow*
A narrow side-tunnel here is shored with cracked, buckling timber, dust hanging thick enough to taste. A section of ceiling ahead sags visibly under its own weight, ready to come down at the wrong disturbance.
*A cramped adit roughly 30 feet long and barely 4 feet wide, ceiling dropping to under 5 feet at its narrowest point. Rock dust coats every surface and hangs in the air, and each footstep sounds louder than it should in the enclosed space.*
**Sagging Ceiling:** a long stretch of visibly bowed support timber partway down the adit - any loud noise or careless passage through this stretch brings the ceiling down behind whoever's already past it (TEST OF FATE, WOUND to anyone still beneath it when it goes), sealing the way back to the Timbered Gallery for good.
**Exits:** passage, back toward the timbered gallery -> C.4 Timbered Gallery, narrow gap, squeezing past the sagging timber -> C.7 Collapsed Stope
