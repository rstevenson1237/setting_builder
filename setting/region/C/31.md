C.31 **The Chittering Nest** (high) - *Rival Chaos, Tribe Line*
A packed warren-chamber holds most of the Silt Hollow claw's own numbers at once, chittering that stops all at once the moment torchlight reaches the bend outside.
*Dug chamber fifteen yards across, active, ceiling low enough to duck in places, floor packed hard by generations of small bare feet.*
**Warren:** Ten or so Khughik share this dug chamber, swarming anything smaller than themselves and scattering rather than returning the same day against anything larger that fights back (Swarming, 10, Bestiary : Khughik).
**Chittering Hush:** A constant low chittering fills the chamber and stops all at once the instant torchlight reaches the bend outside it, replaced by a silence that's its own warning.
**Claw's Take:** What the claw's carried back from whatever a larger household won't finish sits in a heap rather than sorted, worth going through by hand rather than at a glance (Treasure III, d20).
