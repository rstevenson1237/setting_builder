C.31 **The Chittering Nest** (high) - *Crowded, Hush, Low Ceiling*
A low dug hollow ringed with **sleeping ledges** heaped with rags, every bed crowded against the walls and away from a **grey film** spread across the middle of the floor. A **reed cage** hangs from the ceiling over the film's edge, and something inside it beats against the reeds at the light.
*A Khughik sleeping hollow, 30 feet by 20 and 5 feet to the ceiling, dug outward from a single old-cut grave slab at its centre and left when the adipocere welling through the slab's crack crossed the lines the Khughik scratched against it. The sweet, rancid tallow smell is strongest over the slab.*
**Grey Film:** Adipocere seeps from a crack across the dressed grave slab at the room's centre and spreads a slick grey film about 12 feet across, its edge a hand's width past the newest of five lines scratched into the floor around it, and anyone crossing it faster than a creep goes down with a clatter heard in the Store Nook and the Water Nook, and a torch that touches it gutters out.
**Sleeping Ledges:** Thirty-odd body-length scoops are dug three tiers high into the silt of the east and west walls, each packed with rags and gnawed bones deep enough that a person lying in one cannot be seen from any doorway.
**Reed Cage:** A cage of plaited reed hangs from a bone peg in the ceiling over the film's east edge, holding a thin grey pigeon with a brass tube stamped with a tower mark bound to its leg, alive and beating at the reeds when anyone comes near, and opening the cage without a hand on the bird sends it flapping for the grille at the south doorway (Quest: The Caged Pigeon).
**Pitched Jug:** A squat clay jug with a wooden bung sealed in pitch lies in the open on the lowest east ledge nearest the film, and cutting the pitch away and drawing the bung opens it (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** raised grille of lashed poles hung on a hide rope, the rope frayed where it runs over its lintel peg, south wall -> C.30 Silt Hollow Entrance, plank hatch in the floor propped open on a rawhide hinge over a three-step ramp down, northeast corner -> C.32 Store Nook, natural crack in the silt wall choked at knee height by a slumped boulder that two people's weight together rolls aside, a Khughik-wide gap above it, west wall -> C.33 Water Nook, dressed stone slab turning on an iron pin through its top and foot, north wall -> C.34 Bolt-Hole
