C.31 **The Chittering Nest** (high) - *Crowded, Hush, Low Ceiling*
A dug warren four feet high sits silent, its west wall crowded with **sleeping niches** and grey **mould** furring the floor from a **heap of dead** in the far corner. A **wicker cage** hangs over the middle and a **sealed crock** stands against the west wall.
*Dug warren about 30 feet by 20, ceiling 4 feet held by bone-and-timber props, once the Silt Hollow Khughik's sleeping hall and now abandoned. Sound dies within a few strides in the dug earth, so the only noise is wings shuffling in the cage over the center, and the only smell is the mould's, sour and strongest at the northeast corner.*
**Rot-Mould:** Grey mould, thumb-thick at its edge, creeps from the northeast corner toward the exits, and wading it -> boots clog, costing an action per ten feet to scrape clean.
**Dead Heap:** Nine Khughik lie curled together in the northeast corner under the mould, and searching the heap shows every ear cut off.
**Sleeping Niches:** Twenty niches scooped in three tiers up the west wall, each lipped with thumb-pressed red clay and floored with a pad of rotted rag, all empty.
**Wicker Cage:** A split-willow cage hangs by a sinew cord over the center of the room, a live grey pigeon inside and its door tied shut with a slip-knot (Quest: The Caged Pigeon).
**Sealed Crock:** A bucket-sized clay crock left against the west wall, its lid sealed down with black pitch, and cracking the seal -> what was left inside (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** pivot slab - slate on an iron pin, sill worn to a groove, swings only under two people's weight, south wall -> C.30 Silt Hollow Entrance, grille - iron bars set in packed earth, one bar-wide gap bent open at the bottom, rust flaking, east wall -> C.32 Store Nook, natural gap - crack between two rock ribs, chained across with a rusted padlock opened by (Keys: Water Nook Key), north wall -> C.33 Water Nook, climb - wall-cut footholds and iron staples down a shaft, a plank hatch over its mouth locked with a hasp and padlock opened by (Keys: Bolt-Hole Key), northwest corner -> C.34 Bolt-Hole
