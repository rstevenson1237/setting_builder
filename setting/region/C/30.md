C.30 **Silt Hollow Entrance** (low) - *Silt, Squeeze, Worn Smooth*
A dug squeeze with shoulder-polished walls, its floor drifted with **silt tracks** and a raw **outer cutting** open in the west wall.
*Dug squeeze about 25 feet by 5, ceiling 5 feet, the Silt Hollow Khughik's front passage from the Sentry Gate to the warren and abandoned as a post, nothing standing guard in it. The Khughik call it Krazurkik, and sound dies within a few strides in the dug earth.*
**Outer Cutting:** A face of raw ravine stone shows old tool-cuts in the west wall, and fist-sized blocks prised loose lie in the silt below it (Quest: Ravine-Cut Stone).
**Silt Tracks:** A drift of fine silt floors the passage, printed with small bare feet running from the grille to the slab in both directions.
**Exits:** grille - iron bars in a slate frame set into rock, a hinged gate section closed with a padlock opened by (Keys: Silt Hollow Grille Key), rust flaking, west wall -> C.11 Sentry Gate, pivot slab - slate on an iron pin, sill worn to a groove, swings only under two people's weight, north wall -> C.31 The Chittering Nest
