C.30 **Silt Hollow Entrance** (low) - *Silt, Squeeze, Worn Smooth*
A dug hollow floored ankle-deep in soft grey silt, a cold **ring hearth** in the middle under a soot-black patch of ceiling and gnawed bones trodden in all round it. In the east wall a seam of **grey cut-stone** shows through the digging, fist-sized chunks lying broken off beneath it.
*The Silt Hollow's old feeding floor, 25 feet by 15 and 6 feet to the ceiling, left when the warren stopped eating here, its hearth long cold and the bones gone grey. The Khughik call it Krazurkik. It smells of old ash and nothing moves in it, the air is still and cold, and the silt holds every footprint pressed into it.*
**Grey Cut-Stone:** A seam of grey stone a yard wide runs through the east wall where the digging broke into the outer face of the old cutting, chipped raw and bright in a dozen places, fist-sized chunks lying loose in the silt below, and boot prints far larger than a Khughik's run to it from the squeeze and back (Quest: Ravine-Cut Stone).
**Ring Hearth:** A ring of blackened stones 4 feet across sits in the middle of the floor, its ash crusted solid and a green-wood spit lying snapped across it, and the ceiling above is sooted black.
**Exits:** natural fissure in rock and silt, its sides worn smooth and greasy at shoulder height by passing bodies, a squeeze one person wide taken sideways, south wall -> C.11 Sentry Gate, fence of sharpened stakes driven floor to ceiling with a gap two stakes wide worn pale by hands, north wall -> C.31 The Chittering Nest
