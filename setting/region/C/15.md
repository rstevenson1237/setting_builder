C.15 **Broken Ladder Shaft** (medium) - *vertical, rope-frayed, treacherous*
A narrow vertical shaft drops here, its old wooden ladder broken through in several rungs and its accompanying rope frayed down to a few strands. The climb down looks far less safe than whoever built it originally intended.
*A vertical shaft roughly 15 feet deep and 4 feet across, walls rough-cut and slick with condensation. A broken ladder and fraying rope offer two unreliable ways down, and the sound of dripping water echoes up from below.*
**Broken Ladder:** missing or cracked rungs through most of its length - climbing it without testing each rung risks a fall partway down (TEST OF CONSTITUTION, WOUND); the accompanying rope, frayed to a few strands, offers a slower but more reliable alternative for anyone willing to take the extra time.
**Exits:** passage, back toward the silent gallery -> C.13 Silent Gallery, shaft bottom, climbing down through the dark -> C.16 The Echoing Vault
