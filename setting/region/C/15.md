C.15 **The Bone Midden** (low) - *old refuse, scattered bone, minor detail*
A heap of old **bone** and broken grave-goods fills a side alcove, refuse pushed aside by whoever last cleared these passages.
*A shallow alcove roughly ten feet across, originally a minor storage niche, now a dumping ground for refuse cleared from elsewhere in the barrow; it carries a dry, dusty-bone smell. Sifting the midden takes time but isn't dangerous.*
**Midden:** Sifting patiently turns up one intact item overlooked among the debris - an unbroken widow-glass bottle.
**Exits:** low stone passage -> C.14 Silt Pool, narrow fissure passage -> C.16 Whispering Crack
