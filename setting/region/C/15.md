C.15 **Harness Rack Stable** (high) - *Boar, Inlaid Saddle, Dung*
A wrecked old-cut stable where a stone **manger** runs the east wall under heaps of **dung**, splintered stall boards litter the floor, and a boar-saddle hangs rusted to an iron **harness rack**. A **boar** stands in the far stall, and a roof **slab** sags over the aisle.
*The stable is 30 feet by 20 under a 7-foot ceiling, a household's boar-stable whose stalls were smashed to kindling and whose Charger is gone. Dung reek is strongest at the far stall, and dust sifts down the aisle from the slab.*
**Manger:** A dressed-stone trough with the bind-rune cut at its end and its lip gnawed round by tusks runs the east wall, dung heaped along its whole length.
**Slab:** A roof slab sags over the aisle on two split stall posts, dust sifting -> lingering beneath drops it, costing a turn of clearing, the doorway end stands clear.
**Harness Rack:** An iron rack on the west wall has a bone-and-copper inlaid saddle rusted fast to it, its pannier flap buckled shut and sagging heavy -> unbuckling the flap opens it (Treasure III, d20).
**Trap:** Behind the rack a bent ash pole holds a boar-spear cocked on a cord run under the pannier buckle -> unbuckling the flap looses the spear (Test of Constitution, 1d, Piercing).
**Boar:** A boar in the far stall, no dung under it, a bone dropped through it -> food left at the rail turns it away, crossing bare sets it squealing.
**Musk Crocks:** Nine wax-sealed crocks stamped with the red band ring the ledge behind the far stall, dust clean under each -> a crock taken leaves a bare ring (Quest: Tusker Musk).
**Exits:** hanging of stiff dung-crusted ox-hide on a rail hung from a stone counterweight, lifted only when two haul the counterweight cord down, north wall -> C.14 Muster Yard
