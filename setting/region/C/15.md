C.15 **Harness Rack Stable** (high) - *Boar, Inlaid Saddle, Dung*
Most of this long stable lies under its own fallen roof, the upright slabs of its stalls snapped off at the rubble line, while grit ticks steadily down from the **cracked roof** still standing over the middle. Rotted harness hangs from **harness pegs** along the one clear wall beside a **rubbing post** black with grease, and the far end is a solid-looking **rubble heap** with a cup-hollow cut at its foot.
*A dressed stable 40 feet north to south and 20 wide, six stalls parted by single upright slabs along its east side, built for war-boars and wrecked when the roof came down over the middle four stalls, the floor still trodden with old dung under the fall. The musk reek of boar in the room comes from the rubbing post, and the ticking of grit comes from the cracked roof and quickens the longer anyone stays.*
**Cracked Roof:** Over the middle of the stable the surviving roof is spalling along a crack from wall to wall above a fallen slab that pins a dead war-boar by its hindquarters, and the grit falling from it ticks faster the longer anyone stands beneath -> anyone staying on the open floor longer than a short search brings down a shower of spall that puts out every torch beneath it and buries whatever was set down, everywhere but inside the last stall at the north end, whose upright slabs still carry the roof.
**Rubble Heap:** The north end looks like a solid fall of rubble to the ceiling, yet a draught blows out of it, the grit that settles on everything else leaves no dust on it, and at its foot a cup-hollow is cut into the floor under a lintel incised with the old angular letters QAZUZ, dried meat scraps scattered just outside the hollow -> an object left in the hollow thins the heap to a grey haze for as long as it lies there and taking it back restores the heap, while anyone who walks into the heap without leaving one wanders in grey and comes out where they went in, the time spent under the roof.
**Inlaid Saddle:** Behind the heap in the last whole stall, a boar's war-saddle with its cantle inlaid in bone and red stone sits on an iron rack bolted into the wall, its iron fittings rusted fast to the rack, and its two saddlebags hold a cache under buckled flaps (Treasure III, d20).
**Rack Bow:** Under the saddle rack a horn bow lies lashed and drawn with a bone-headed bolt, a new gut line running from its catch up into the nearer saddlebag where anyone looking under the rack finds it -> opening that bag's flap looses the bolt into whoever stands before the saddle (Test of Constitution, 1d, Piercing).
**Rubbing Post:** Beside the exit a squared stone post is rubbed smooth and black to shoulder height with boar grease and musk, and scraping the musk off into any vessel takes a few minutes and leaves its reek on the scraper's hands and clothes, strong enough for any boar or Gorzgur to smell across a room (Quest: Tusker Musk).
**Harness Pegs:** Along the clear west wall iron pegs hold rotted boar harness, and among it hangs one bridle whose iron bit is worn bright at both rings.
**Exits:** natural gap through raw rock where the dressed wall has fallen away, the rusted portcullis grille showing at its far end a foot off the floor, a bronze cradle set in the gap's side at chest height with its sides rubbed bright and a bit-shaped rust stain in its trough, warded so that nothing carrying the reek of boar can pass until an iron bit lies in the cradle and anyone forcing through with the reek on them is held fast in the gap (Test of Fate, Condition), west wall -> C.14 Muster Yard
