C.15 **The Root-Broken Gallery** (low) - *root-broken floor, slow going, connective passage*
Roots have broken up through the floor of **The Root-Broken Gallery** from somewhere above, splitting the passage into two rough ways forward around a fallen root-mass nobody's bothered to clear.
*A wide, low gallery roughly 25 feet long, floor heaved and cracked by root growth throughout; smells of damp earth rather than stone, and root-fiber hangs from the broken ceiling in places.*
**Split:** Fresh timber props mark the way toward one dig, worn rope-guides the way toward the other - the two crews working Ingaraz clearly haven't chosen to share a route, and neither leads back to the other once taken.
