C.15 **The Root-Broken Gallery** (low) - *root-broken floor, slow going, connective passage*
Roots have broken up through the floor of **The Root-Broken Gallery** from somewhere above, splitting the passage into two rough ways forward around a fallen root-mass nobody's bothered to clear.
*A wide, low gallery roughly 25 feet long, floor heaved and cracked by root growth throughout, root-fiber hanging from the broken ceiling.*
**Split:** Timber props mark one dig's route, rope-guides the other.
**Exits:** narrow cutting, old lintel, north -> C.11 The Second Mound Mouth, braced fissure, timber props, east -> C.3 The Digger's Cut, rope-guided cleft, worn footholds, west -> C.4 The Grave-Robbers' Shoring
