Locations of C Ingaraz

C.1 The Sealed Chamber (high) - *the Diggers' true target, warded threshold, barrow wight bound within*
C.2 The Warded Gatehouse (high) - *closed-ring glyph carved deep, old-ward sentinel's post, crossing it wakes it*
C.3 The Digger's Cut (medium) - *fresh timber shoring, defended dig, fenced-buyer relics*
C.4 The Grave-Robbers' Shoring (medium) - *rival crew at work, coordinated defense, old-tongue fragments discarded*
C.5 The Bear's Den Mound (medium) - *claw-scored stonework, old bedding and bone, ignored unless entered*
C.6 The Drowned Cistern (medium) - *standing old water, uneven submerged footing, echo behaves oddly*
C.7 The Wrong-Facing Grave (medium) - *buried facing the keep, wall-bound dead, custom broken*
C.8 The Barrow-Kin Threshold (medium) - *closed-ring boundary mark, warning before action, ends at the first disturbed grave*
C.9 The Collapsed Vault (medium) - *caved roof, broken descent, old-tongue carving still visible below*
C.10 The Outer Mound Path (low) - *first entrance, cleared cart-ruts, nearest the boundary stones*
C.11 The Second Mound Mouth (low) - *second entrance, narrower approach, less traveled*
C.12 The Silent Passage (low) - *sound stops carrying, no echo past this point, unnerving quiet*
C.13 The Old Foundation Course (low) - *older-cut stone, matches the keep's own cellar, three centuries predates the charter*
C.14 The Barrow-Kin Grave-Row (low) - *tended graves, recent offerings left, undisturbed if left alone*
C.15 The Root-Broken Gallery (low) - *root-broken floor, slow going, connective passage*
C.16 The Cold Draft Passage (low) - *cold air moving the wrong way, sealed passage nearby, unexplained draft*
C.17 The Empty Antechamber (low) - *bare old-tongue stonework, low ceiling, shoulder-width passage*
C.18 The Rubble Choke (low) - *partial collapse, narrow squeeze, dead end*
