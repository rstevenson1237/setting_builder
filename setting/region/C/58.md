C.58 **The Flooded Hall** (high) - *Sheen, Sunken Glint, Cold*
Standing water fills the lowest-cut chamber in the ravine, its surface holding an unbroken sheen that shouldn't be there on stone this old.
*Cut chamber twenty yards across, ruined, the floor sunk a foot below the passage in, water standing throughout rather than merely pooled, colder here than anywhere else in the ravine.*
**Formless:** No fixed outline holds twice against a torch's light in the standing water, rising against anything that disturbs it and leaving anything that doesn't (Rising, 1, Bestiary : The Formless).
**Weeping Growth:** Pale, wet growth furs the chamber's far wall above the waterline, doing nothing on its own account until brushed or broken (Test of Sanity, 1d).
**Sunken Cache:** Something pale glints beneath the standing water at the chamber's own centre, reachable only by wading through water deep enough to lose a footing in (Treasure V, d20).
**Exits:** natural gap, south wall -> C.57 Cistern Approach, natural gap, east wall -> C.59 Damp Gallery, natural gap, north wall -> C.60 Old Cistern Nook
