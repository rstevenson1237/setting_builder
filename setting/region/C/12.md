C.12 **Galuora** (low) - *hearth, cold, ash*
A room with a great stone **hearth** built into its western wall, a **flue** rising through the masonry above it, and a bed of **ash** in the hearth that has been cold for nine hundred years and has never been swept out.
*A room 35 by 25 feet, ceiling 11 feet - two feet above the standard course, to take the flue. It was the second course's fire room, for cooking and for warmth. It is markedly the coldest room in Siwapelu, which is the joke the building is playing, and the flue draws hard enough that a party can hear the fen wind in it.*
**Hearth:** A dressed stone fire-place 9 feet wide and 5 deep set into the west wall, with a raised hearthstone and iron fire-dogs still in place. Building a fire here is the only place in Siwapelu where that is possible without taking the air, because the flue works - which makes this room the obvious camp, and the Faelhond do not use it and will tell a party not to and will not say why.
**Ash Bed:** Four inches of fine grey ash across the whole hearth floor, undisturbed. Raked through, it holds nothing but ash and small bone - ordinary kitchen bone, fish and fowl. It has not been swept because nobody came back to sweep it, and it is the most domestic thing in Siwapelu.
**Flue:** Rising through the wall above the hearth, about 20 inches square, going up and out somewhere into the fen. Air moves down it steadily, cold and clean, which is why this room is the coldest and also why it is the one room on the second course where the Gorm track does not advance.
**Exits:** corbelled arch in the east wall, cap carrying the threshold character, cold air spilling out of it -> C.10 Wosaora, corbelled arch in the south wall -> C.13 Teruimmo
