C.12 **The Collapsed Stair** (medium) - *broken steps, unstable descent, fall hazard*
A stairway down to the barrow's lower level drops away in places, several **steps** broken clean off, the gap between them falling into darkness.
*The stair descends roughly twenty feet; careful descent is safe, but haste or carelessness risks a fall.*
**Broken Steps:** Descending carefully avoids the gap; descending in haste or under pressure risks a fall.
**Rope-and-Hook:** Left at the base of the stair by an earlier visitor who didn't need it going back out; a careful eye spots it.
