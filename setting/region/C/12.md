C.12 **Warband Hall** (high) - *Banners, Muster, Dark Paint*
A wide hall hung with red-painted banners is where Gorzgur's warband gathers between raids, red paint fresh on more than one face turned toward the door.
*Rough-cut hall twenty-five yards across, active, torches kept lit at all hours, floor cracked in a path worn by boots crossing the same line for years.*
**Painted Banners:** Strips of hide dyed the same red as the warband's own paint hang from the ceiling beams, each cut rather than dyed with the same horn-call notation.
**Warband:** A dozen Gorzgur gather here between musters, forming up rather than scattering the moment anyone not already known to them crosses the threshold, led by whoever's paint runs darkest (Forming, 12, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Vraz Red-Hand:** Among them stands the one whose paint is applied by someone else's hand rather than his own, a mark the rest give room to without being told, gone as often as not to whichever entrance is currently pressed hardest (Named Creature: Vraz Red-Hand).
**Raid Take:** What the warband's brought back and not yet moved to the household's own vault sits piled along the hall's own north wall, worth more to carry than to fight fairly for (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** natural gap, west wall -> C.11 Sentry Gate, fitted door, east wall -> C.13 Chieftain's Chamber, archway, north wall -> C.14 Muster Yard, fitted door, south wall -> C.18 Sleeping Hall
