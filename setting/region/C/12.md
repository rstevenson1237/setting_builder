C.12 **The Silent Passage** (low) - *sound stops carrying, no echo past this point, unnerving quiet*
Past a clean line on the floor, sound simply stops carrying at all - a footstep, a whisper, a dropped stone, all swallowed the same way.
*A plain cut passage roughly 20 feet long and barrow-scale throughout; smells of the same cold mineral dust as the chamber it leads to, and the silence itself is the only sensory fact worth noting here.*
**Dead Point:** A testable line, not a gradual fade - voices and footsteps carry normally up to it and not at all past it. Whatever holds that quiet so absolutely this far out is a fair warning of what waits at the passage's far end.
**Exits:** low passage, cold draft, west -> C.16 The Cold Draft Passage, narrow passage, absolute silence, east -> C.1 The Sealed Chamber
