C.12 **The Wight's Hollow** (high) - *deathly-cold, echoing, haunted*
A bone-deep cold radiates from this hollow chamber, where a grey, frost-rimed figure paces the same short route again and again without ever varying its steps. Deep gouges scored into the floor mark where it's worn the same path for twenty-three years.
*A roughly 25-foot circular chamber with a domed, naturally-hollowed ceiling, the cold here sharper than anywhere else in the workings. Every footstep and breath echoes back distorted, and the air carries no smell at all - not even stone.*
**Worn Path:** a shallow groove worn into the floor, tracing a tight loop around the chamber - walked over and over by Marek Coll, one of the seven prospectors who never came out of the original breach, now a Fringe Wight driven to walk the exact route of his death (Named Creature: Marek Coll); interrupting his path draws his full attention.
**Frost-Rimed Alcove:** a shallow niche in the wall where Marek's path always turns back, his rotted pack still wedged inside it; searching it, once he's been dealt with or drawn away, turns up a scrap of oiled cloth with six names scratched into it, the last trailing into a smear that was once blood (Lore: The Six Names).
**Exits:** passage, back through the cold -> C.10 The Cold Passage
