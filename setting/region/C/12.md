C.12 **Warband Hall** (high) - *Banners, Muster, Dark Paint*
A long dressed-stone hall hung with red **banners**, a dozen Gorzgur mustered at two **trestles** under a smoking **hearth**, and their chief on a **dais** at the north end. A **foot-plate** sits below the dais step.
*The hall is 40 feet by 30 with a 7-foot ceiling, the household's muster hall, where Vraz hears his warband, in daily use. Hearth smoke rolls off the fire-back, and a chain ticks in the draught from the sluice.*
**Dais:** Vraz Red-Hand sits on a stone chair on the north wall's raised step, mail on, a red hand painted on his chest, spear across his knees (Alert, 1, Bestiary : Gorzgur) (Named Creature: Vraz Red-Hand).
**Trestles:** Twelve Gorzgur sit at two long trestles down the hall's middle whetting spears, faces streaked dark over the red (Restless, 12, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Banners:** Twelve red hide banners hang from an iron rail along the north wall either side of the dais, each a six-foot stiff painted hide that lifts down from its socket.
**Hearth:** West wall hearth, iron fire-back fused into the stone, soot rubbed from its foot in a crescent -> prying the foot up lifts a plate on a cavity (Treasure III, d20).
**Strongbox:** An iron-banded oak chest in the southeast corner, its lid sealed with red pitch and gouged in threes, two men to carry -> cutting the pitch opens it (Treasure III, d20).
**Lynx:** A Ravine Lynx on a chain riveted to the chest's ring tears a haunch beside it, ears notched, crouching lower as the party nears (Watchful, 1, Bestiary : Ravine Lynx).
**Foot-Plate:** Below the dais step, one heel-sized dip worn bright, a chain over one pulley to a gong -> one step brings the six from C.14, a second sleepers from C.18.
**Ash Pit:** Seven Khughik doze in the warm ash under the hearth grate, small grey tracks wearing a path in from the sluice and none to the fire-back's foot (Skittish, 7, Bestiary : Khughik).
**Sluice Cord:** Red-daubed weight stones stacked by the C.14 sluice, a slab propped over it on a shin-high hide cord -> snagging the cord drops the slab (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Exits:** squared sluice-channel of dressed stone at stooping height, cold runoff a hand deep flowing in over a worn sill, south wall -> C.11 Sentry Gate, flush oak hatch iron-strapped on greased hinges with no lock, dais floor behind the chair -> C.13 Chieftain's Chamber, wide dug sluice-channel with an oak sluice-board swollen fast in its groove and freed only by force, east wall -> C.14 Muster Yard, fixed iron grille of square-cut rust-scaled bars with one gap wide enough to turn through sideways, northwest corner -> C.18 Sleeping Hall
