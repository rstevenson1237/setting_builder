C.12 **The Collapsed Stair** (medium) - *broken steps, unstable descent, fall hazard*
A stairway down to the barrow's lower level drops away in places, several **steps** broken clean off, the gap between them falling into darkness.
*A straight stone stairway descending roughly twenty feet, several steps broken clean off partway down - a hazard beyond ordinary wear. Careful descent is safe, but haste or carelessness risks a fall.*
**Broken Steps:** Descending carefully avoids the gap; descending in haste or under pressure risks a fall (TEST OF CONSTITUTION or a WOUND).
**Left Behind:** Something left at the base of the stair by an earlier visitor who didn't need it going back out; a careful eye spots it (Treasure II, d20).
**Exits:** worn stone stair -> C.9 Sunken Hall, broken stone stair -> C.13 Flooded Landing
