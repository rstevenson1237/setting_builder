C.12 **Warband Hall** (high) - *Banners, Muster, Dark Paint*
A barrel vault twice the height of any passage covers two rows of squared pillars hung with red-daubed hide banners, and against them squat twelve soot-blackened warriors of the **Gorzgur Warband**, chanting, beside a **soot-paint trough** that reeks of scorched tallow along the west wall. Above the banners at the north end, a lead-lidded **stone urn** sits on a narrow ledge nine feet up the wall.
*An old-cut hall 60 feet north to south and 25 wide under a 14-foot barrel vault, twelve squared pillars in two rows of six, each with a fist-sized boss on its east face at shoulder height, now Gorzgur's eating and muster hall, lit by torches in iron rings on the pillars. The warband sits here until a horn from the Sentry Gate sends it out, and the hall stands empty for as long as the alarm runs. The scorched-tallow smell that reaches the Sentry Gate comes from the trough.*
**Gorzgur Warband:** Twelve warriors squat motionless in two rows against the pillars, bodies smeared black so that in torchlight they read as part of the banners until they rise, chanting a muster count in Grozzgur that carries down the dressed passage as far as the Sentry Gate, while Vraz walks the rows daubing each forehead red from a clay bowl -> a shout from any one of them brings all twelve to their feet in formation (Disciplined, 12, Bestiary : Gorzgur) (Named Creature: Vraz Red-Hand).
**Stone Urn:** Nine feet up the north wall above the banners, a dressed-stone urn the height of a forearm sits on a ledge barely wider than its base, its lid sealed with a band of lead crusted over by grey cave-snails so still they read as rough stone until touched, and cutting the lead band opens it (Treasure III, d20).
**Tipping Ledge:** The urn's ledge is a stone tongue on a pivot, and a hand run along its back finds a slot in the wall where a rust-dry chain runs down to a counterweight cut by the hall's builders -> lifting the urn off the ledge swings the tongue down and throws whoever is reaching from shoulders or a ladder onto the flagstones below (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Fourth Pillar Boss:** On the fourth pillar of the east row the boss stands a finger's width proud where every other sits flush, a hairline seam runs from it down the pillar's face to the floor, and a quarter-circle is scraped into the flagstones at the pillar's foot -> pressing the boss flush swings a slab of the pillar's base out along the scrape, while prying at the seam jams the slab for good with a grinding that carries the length of the hall.
**Pillar-Base Coffer:** Behind the swung slab, a coffer hollowed from the pillar's own stone and not separable from it holds a nest of pale blind rats that scatter squealing into cracks at the first light, and under their droppings, a hand deep, lies what was left there (Treasure III, d20).
**Sentry Passage Tripwire:** A stride into the gap toward the Sentry Gate, a dead Khughik hangs skewered on a rack of three fire-hardened stakes lashed to an ash pole bent back against the roof, and a gut cord, new and taut, runs from the pole's catch across the gap at shin height -> breaking the cord whips the rack down across the gap at chest height (Test of Constitution, 1d, Piercing).
**Soot-Paint Trough:** Along the west wall south of the breach runs a stone trough of soot worked into rendered fat, its rim printed with black handprints, and a torch dropped into it lights the fat into a thick black smoke that fills the vault.
**Exits:** natural gap in the rock where the dressed south wall gives way, a clapperless bronze bell hung in its roof on a chain that runs up into the rock and the floor beneath it worn into a dip where feet stop, warded so the bell rings loud enough to carry to the Muster Yard when anyone passes under it without first speaking a word of Grozzgur up at it, south wall -> C.11 Sentry Gate, ragged breach knocked through the dressed wall behind a stiff hide flap, a slab beside it printed over and over with red palms and a pot of red paint on its sill, warded so the flap closes and holds fast on anyone stepping through without first pressing a red-painted palm to the slab (Test of Fate, Condition), north end of the east wall -> C.13 Chieftain's Chamber, warped plank gate swollen tight in its frame at the head of a flight of cut steps climbing up, shouldered open only with a bang, north wall -> C.14 Muster Yard, rough breach hacked through the dressed wall into packed earth, its unshored edges still dropping grit, west wall -> C.18 Sleeping Hall
