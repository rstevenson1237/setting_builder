C.48 **Broken Stair Entrance** (low) - *Rubble, Draft Gap, Crawl*
A crude opening at the rubble-choked base of an old stair serves as this claw's own way out, a draft curling from a gap in the rubble that shouldn't carry air at all.
*Rubble-choked landing eight yards across, active, the old stair's own steps buried rather than climbed from here, draft steady rather than seasonal.*
**Draft Gap:** A gap low in the rubble carries a steady draft no dead-end should produce, and clearing the loose stone from it by hand opens a crawl through to another claw's ground entirely.
**Exits:** natural gap, up -> C.49 The Chittering Nest, cleared gap, low in the rubble -.- -> C.34 Bolt-Hole
