C.33 **Water Nook** (medium) - *Cracked Jars, Slick, Spillage*
A cramped, dripping nook, its floor slick with grey slurry from a row of cracked **water jars**, and a bulging **clay dam** beading water across the north wall. A waist-high **weighted jar** stands apart in the corner, its lid pitched shut.
*The Silt Hollow's water nook, 10 feet by 8 and 5 feet to the ceiling, dug up against an old sealed cistern whose dressed-stone plug the Khughik chipped through for a spout, and gone to decay as the plug seeps and the jars crack. The plug's face is cut with the Old Tongue Kutsiq, "keep the water sealed". The drip off the dam is the loudest thing in the nook, and the floor is cold slurry underfoot.*
**Clay Dam:** A bulging face of puddled clay packed around an old dressed-stone plug fills the lower half of the north wall 6 feet across, beading water round a dry reed spout jammed into a chipped hole in the plug, soft to a pressed thumb with hairline cracks spreading from the spout, and its drip quickens to a trickle the longer anyone stays until running feet anywhere on the floor burst it in a jet of water and clay clods across the nook (Test of Constitution, 1d, Blast).
**Water Jars:** A dozen knee-high clay jars stand along the south wall, all but two cracked through and leaking into the slurry, and those two still hold clean water under wooden lids.
**Weighted Jar:** A waist-high clay jar stands in the open in the northwest corner, its lid pitched shut and pressed with a three-line mark scratched in by knife point, claw gouges round the seam and a snapped bone lever still wedged in it, too heavy for one person to tip and grating stone on stone when rocked, and breaking the pitch seal reaches an oilcloth bundle lying on top of the stones (Lore: The Stoppered Journal).
