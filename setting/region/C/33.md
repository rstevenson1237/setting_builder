C.33 **Water Nook** (medium) - *Cracked Jars, Slick, Spillage*
A slick dug nook with six **cracked jars** lying spilled along the west wall and a man-high **stoppered jar** at the foot of a back wall that bellies round a **weeping plug**. **Pitted shards** lie in the spillage.
*Dug nook about 12 feet by 10, ceiling 5 feet, once the Silt Hollow Khughik's water store and now decayed to spilled jars and a seep. The air is cold and smells of wet clay, and the only sound is a drip from the plug's rim, with the weep lengthening the longer the party stays.*
**Weeping Plug:** A stone plug cut with Kutsiq weeps at its rim in the bellied back wall, and moving fast across the floor -> the clay skin bursts (Test of Constitution, 1d, Blast).
**Stoppered Jar:** A man-high glazed jar stands at the back wall's foot, wax-sealed, too heavy to lift, a frayed rope round its neck, and breaking the stopper -> a hand-bound book (Lore: The Stoppered Journal).
**Cracked Jars:** Six glazed water jars lie cracked and empty along the west wall, the floor slicked wet beneath them.
**Pitted Shards:** Among the spilled jars lie shards eaten thin as paper along their edges, the water that wets them clear and cold.
**Exits:** natural gap - crack between two rock ribs, chained across with a rusted padlock opened by (Keys: Water Nook Key), south wall -> C.31 The Chittering Nest
