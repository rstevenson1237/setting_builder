C.2 **The Carved Hall** (Low) - *ancient, dusty, inscribed*
A pillared **hall** lies under thick dust, its walls cut with rows of marks. A **carving** of one spiral stands taller than the rest at the hall's middle, and **tracks** cross the floor in a straight line.
*Hall 60 ft by 30 ft, two rows of six pillars. Goblin feet have worn one path through the dust, door to door. The flush stone door in the east wall is closed.*
**Pillars:** Twelve pillars, each cut at its foot with a single mark. Nine of the marks are scored through.
**Wall Carving:** A long panel of marks cut at eye level. A reader of the dead tongue learns what the makers boasted of (Lore: The Hall Tally).
**Central Spiral:** Carved deeper than the rest and worn smooth by hands. Pressed, it swings the flush stone door in the east wall inward onto C.5.
**Goblin Path:** Tracks run straight from the south door to the west wall and never touch the east wall.
**Exits:** archway, south door -> C.1 The Cleft Passage, crude gap, west wall behind pillars -> C.3 The Warren
