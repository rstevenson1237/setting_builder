C.2 **Selora** (low) - *niches, lamps, order*
A long gallery lined on both walls with shallow **lamp niches** at regular intervals, most of them empty, and **one of them lit** - a steady small flame that has been burning since before Torsgaard was founded and does not appear to be consuming anything.
*A gallery 60 by 12 feet, ceiling 9 feet, running west off the stair-head chamber. It was the lamp gallery for the first course and it still is, technically. The room is warmer than the rest of Siwapelu by a noticeable degree and the air in it is clean - a party's lamps burn brighter here, and nobody has ever explained that either.*
**Lamp Niches:** Forty-two shallow niches cut at chest height at 3-foot intervals down both walls, each about 8 inches square with a shallow dish cut in its floor and a soot-shadow above it. Forty-one are cold and empty. This is where the Faelhond cache spare lanterns, and there are usually two or three in the niches nearest the east arch, which they will not object to a party borrowing and will notice.
**Lit Niche:** The twenty-first niche on the north wall, exactly halfway down the gallery, holds a small flame the size of a thumbnail standing over a dish of clear liquid that has not gone down. It gives no smoke and no soot - the stone above it is clean while every other niche is shadowed - and it is warm to a hand held near it. **Clue**: it is the only clean niche in a gallery of forty-two soot-marked ones, and it is exactly halfway along, which is placement rather than accident. **Trigger**: lifting the dish out of the niche, which nobody has done because the Faelhond regard it as luck and will ask a party not to. **Payload**: the dish comes out, still burning, and goes on burning in a party's hand and in the gorm, where nothing else will (Unique Treasure: Selora Lamp).
**Soot Shadows:** Above the forty-one cold niches, the soot has stained the stone in tall tapers, and on the north wall the shadows are markedly heavier than on the south. Lamps were lit down this side habitually and not down the other, which means the traffic through this gallery went one way far more than the other, and it went west.
**Exits:** corbelled arch in the east wall, air drawing through it toward the stair -> C.1 Immorael, corbelled arch in the west wall, cap carrying the threshold character -> C.3 Doraiel, low corbelled arch in the south wall, its sill dished by water -> C.8 Kirel
