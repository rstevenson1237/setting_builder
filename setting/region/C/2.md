C.2 **Tribute Hall** (high) - *Rival Chaos, Sentry Horn*
A wide dressed-stone hall holds a stone tally-slab sunk into its floor, where two Bound Thralls stand motionless beside the tribute already stacked against the far wall.
*Cut stone hall thirty yards across, ceiling higher than anywhere else in the old halls, active - swept and kept clear rather than left to gather dust, sound carrying its full length undamped.*
**Tally Slab:** A stone slab sunk into the floor's own centre is worn smooth in a ring where a bronze rod's been set and lifted for longer than anyone posted here now remembers.
**Bound Thralls:** Two corpses kept upright at the tribute stack answer only to whoever approaches carrying the mark kept on the bronze rod, and ignore anyone not attempting to take tribute away unmarked (Blocking, 2, Bestiary : Bound Thrall).
**Stacked Tribute:** What every household's owed the shrine sits stacked against the far wall under the Thralls' own watch, worth carrying out whole rather than piecemeal (Treasure V, d20).
**Tribute Rod:** A bronze rod racked beside the tally slab is the one mark the shrine's own bound and sealed things answer to, and it's never once left this hall in living memory (Keys: Tribute Rod).
