C.2 **The Warren** (Medium) - *straw, smoke, crowded*
A low, wide hall thick with smoke, where a fire of broken wagon-board burns at the centre and **goblins** sleep in **straw heaps** all around it. **Pots** hang from pegs along the walls.
*Sixty feet by thirty, the makers' bunk-hall, with the bed-niches they cut still showing in the walls. Snoring and quarrelling carry up the tunnel to the Watch Passage.*
**Goblins:** Ten to fourteen sleep sprawled in the straw, quarrelling in their sleep, and wake shrieking and snatching knives when the curtain moves (Hostile, 10-14, Bestiary : Goblin).
**Straw Heaps:** Straw is heaped in a ring about the fire, each with gnawed bones in it.
**Pots:** Dented pots and kettles hang from pegs driven into cracks in the walls, black with smoke.
**Exits:** crude hide curtain, entrance -> C.1 The Watch Passage, wide arch, north side -> C.4 The Spoil Hall, wooden plank door, east wall -> C.5 The Chief's Hall
