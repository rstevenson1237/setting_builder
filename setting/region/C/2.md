C.2 **The Warded Gatehouse** (high) - *closed-ring glyph carved deep, old-ward sentinel's post, crossing it wakes it*
The widest passage in Ingaraz opens into the **Warded Gatehouse**, a threshold room built to be noticed rather than passed through unremarked. A jointed shape of foundation-stone stands motionless against the far wall, easy to mistake for a statue until it isn't.
*A vaulted crossing-chamber roughly 25 by 15 feet, taller than any passage feeding it, floor worn in a shallow semicircle before the far wall; a faint grinding carries from the Sentinel's joints even at rest.*
**Old-Ward Sentinel:** Inert until crossed; worn floor semicircle marks its reach (see setting/Bestiary.md, AD: 6d6).
**Sentinel-Niche:** Fits a foundation-cut seal; without one, crossing wakes it fully (Keys: The Foundation Seal).
**Threshold Warning:** Old-tongue script promises the marked seal's carrier passes unchallenged (Lore: The Threshold Warning).
**Exits:** wide archway, worn threshold, south wall -> C.17 The Empty Antechamber, sealed stone door, hidden ward, north wall -> C.1 The Sealed Chamber, low doorway, closed-ring mark, east wall -> C.8 The Barrow-Kin Threshold
