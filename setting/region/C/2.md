C.2 **The Warded Gatehouse** (high) - *closed-ring glyph carved deep, old-ward sentinel's post, crossing it wakes it*
The widest passage in Ingaraz opens into the **Warded Gatehouse**, a threshold room built to be noticed rather than passed through unremarked. A jointed shape of foundation-stone stands motionless against the far wall, easy to mistake for a statue until it isn't.
*A vaulted crossing-chamber roughly 25 by 15 feet, taller than any passage feeding it, floor worn in a shallow semicircle before the far wall; smells of cold stone, and carries a low grinding sound with no visible source even when nothing here is moving.*
**Old-Ward Sentinel:** A jointed foundation-stone construct fixed to this one post (see setting/Bestiary.md, AD: 6d6), inert until whatever rule it still enforces is crossed, at which point it acts without hesitation and without malice. The worn semicircle in the floor traces exactly how far it can reach.
**Sentinel-Niche:** A shallow depression at the construct's feet, shaped to hold something small and disc-like, sits inside the closed-ring glyph carved deep into the threshold stone. Fitting a matching foundation-cut seal into the niche stops the construct from waking at all - crossing without one wakes it in full (Keys: The Foundation Seal).
**Threshold Warning:** A line of old-tongue script beside the niche, weathered but legible, reads roughly as a promise that whoever carries the marked seal passes unchallenged - written, evidently, by someone who never imagined the seal leaving the barrow field at all (Lore: The Threshold Warning).
**Exits:** wide archway, worn threshold, south wall -> C.17 The Empty Antechamber, sealed stone door, hidden ward, north wall -> C.1 The Sealed Chamber, low doorway, closed-ring mark, east wall -> C.8 The Barrow-Kin Threshold
