C.2 **The Pirates' Cove** (Lair) - *sheltered, anchored, rum-stinking*
A sheltered cove holds a ship at anchor, plank huts on the beach and a rum store, and a captain's voice rises above shanties round a beach fire.
*1200 by 900 feet. The cove is the pirates' anchorage; the mer people want it cleared. The pirates trade captives to the pyramid and boats to anyone.*
**The crew:** twelve pirates in red sashes with a black cloth tied on the left arm, hailing visitors and offering a drink, who toast "Share and share", answered "And the cove keeps the rest" (Wary, 12, Bestiary : Pirate).
**Jory Scarr:** the pirate captain, jovial and ruthless, wants a map, a boat and a buyer's price; Scarr knows the coast's landings and the pyramid buyers' rates.
**The ship:** black hulled and at anchor, a prize crew below deck and a boat ready for the reef.
**The wrecks' leavings:** hulls and spars on the reef beyond the cove, where the pirates take what the reef gives up.
