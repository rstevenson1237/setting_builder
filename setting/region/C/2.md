C.2 **Warehouse Row** - *Caravans, Blue-Grey Wagon*
Velar's river frontage answers to the Nine Wagons now, and their **factor** settles every dispute on it. A blue-grey wagon stands half-unloaded at the door, its brake-bell still swinging.
*A run of six stone warehouses along the river frontage, each sixty feet deep, built for a dozen trading houses, now held by one. Smells of tar, river mud, and stored grain, and brake-bells carry down the row ahead of the carts.*
**Nine Wagons Factor:** Settles every dispute touching the row himself, in company blue-grey, and charges any caravan without Wagons colours, for river access (Faction: The Nine Wagons).
**Bought-Out House:** One warehouse carries a half-scraped sign, the second-largest trading house in Velar, until the week the ford closed.
**Struggling Family's Offer:** A selling-out family's scion waits most mornings, at the row's far end, watched by the factor's people, wanting a letter carried to Brekar's camp (Quest: An Offer Velar Can't Send).
**Exits:** open yard, river side -> C.8 River Docks, open door, south side -> C.3 Market Square
