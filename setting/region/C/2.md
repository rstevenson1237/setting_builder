C.2 **The Silted Passage** (low) - *collapsed rubble, tight squeeze, packed silt*
A narrow corridor is packed with old **flood-silt**, low enough in places to force a stoop, faint tool-marks scored beneath the grime.
*A low, narrow passage roughly 4 by 60 feet, originally a service corridor; purely connective, and a loud or careless party moving through carries sound further into the barrow than they might expect.*
**Silt and Tool-Marks:** Nothing hidden here, just old craftsmanship beneath the grime.
