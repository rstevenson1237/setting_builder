C.2 **The Outlaws' Camp** (Lair) - *hidden, smoky, armed*
**Lean-tos** of bark and hide stand under a lip of rock round a **cookfire**, a **hare's foot** nailed to each post.
*Two paths reach the camp, a track west from the trail and a trail up from the mere path; a third leaves it north-west for the cache.*
**Lean-Tos:** A bedroll under each, a bow strung at each door.
**Cookfire:** Twelve outlaws mend arrows round it, and talk before they fight (Wary, 12, Bestiary : Soldier).
**Hare's Foot:** Nailed to each post at head height.
**Exits:** open, 1 mile north-west of the camp -> C.7 The Outlaws' Cache
