C.2 **Warehouse Row** - *Caravans, Blue-Grey Wagon*
The only stretch of river frontage in Velar worth having, and all of it now answers to the Nine Wagons. A **blue-grey wagon** stands half-unloaded at the nearest door, its brass brake-bell still swinging.
*A run of six stone warehouses along the river frontage, each sixty feet deep, built for a dozen trading houses and now held by one. Smells of tar, river mud, and stored grain; the brake-bells of arriving carts carry the length of the row before the carts themselves are seen.*
**Nine Wagons Factor:** Settles every dispute that touches the row himself rather than referring it anywhere - wears the company blue-grey openly and answers questions about rates plainly. Any caravan not flying Wagons colors pays the row's own fee to use the river access (Faction: The Nine Wagons).
**Bought-Out House:** One warehouse still carries a half-scraped sign from the second-largest trading house in Velar before the Wagons bought it out the week the ford closed - the new paint over it hasn't quite taken in the damp.
**Struggling Family's Offer:** A remaining scion of one of the two trading families now close to selling out waits at the row's far end most mornings, watched by the factor's own people, hoping to catch anyone not obviously Wagons-connected. She wants a private letter carried to Brekar's own camp at the Sunk Bridge Ford, proposing to deal with them directly and cut the Wagons out entirely - a proposal nobody has yet dared answer (Quest: An Offer Velar Can't Send).
**Exits:** open yard, river side -> C.8 River Docks, open door, south side -> C.3 Market Square
