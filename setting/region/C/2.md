C.2 **The Weir Platforms** (landmark) - *Stilted, Wary, Traded*
The **Weir Platforms** are a cluster of stilted huts and fish-weirs where the marsh-kin gather to trade. **Fenna**, an elder, watches strangers from her doorway before deciding whether to come down.
*A dozen reed-thatched platforms on pilings, connected by narrow plank walks over black water; smells of drying fish and reed-smoke, quiet except for water lapping the piles.*
**Fenna:** Marsh-kin elder who trades iron and salt for fever-moss and fish; wary of anyone armed.
**Fish-Weirs:** Woven traps strung between reeds on every side, tended daily - a stranger wading through one is noticed instantly.
**Missing Grandson:** Fenna's grandson left three days ago to trade at Carrdun and hasn't returned; she fears the worst (Quest: Fenna's Grandson).
**Deeper Platform:** A further platform sits apart, reachable only if Fenna trusts you enough to lead the way.
**Exits:** the plank walk, north -> A.5 The Postern Watch, open water, east -> C.1 The Serpent's Reach, open water, south -> C.3 The Silt Bar, open water, southwest -> C.4 The Drowned Stair, open water, west -> C.5 The Still-Fruit Thicket, a hidden plank walk, only if led -> C.6 The Elder's Platform
