C.2 **The Tile Floor** (Medium) - *coloured, trapped, echoing*

A square floor of red, blue and white tiles lies under a carved roof, a faint click sounding underfoot. An arch or a door opens in every wall.

*A hall 40 feet square and 20 feet high, the hub every other room of the level opens from. A safe path of red tiles leads to each opening; the blue and white tiles shift. Centipedes nest in the gaps. One of the three hidden ways down lies under the middle of the floor.*

**The Tiles:** The red tiles are firm and lead to the openings; blue and white ones tilt under weight. A party that crosses on them wakes the centipedes.
**The Centipedes:** Brown, as long as an arm, hiding in the gaps between tiles (Hostile, 2-4, Bestiary : Giant Centipede). They bite what crosses.
**The Numbered Tiles:** Twelve tiles in the middle of the floor carry cynidicean numerals, one each, the edges of the first scuffed (The Cynidiceans Raise the City). Stepped on in order, one to twelve, they fold down into a stair to the robbers' end of the level below (D.1). Stepped out of order, spikes stab up through the twelfth (Test of Constitution, 2d, Piercing). The tomb robbers know the order and sell it.

**Exits:** an open passage to the sill -> C.1 The Threshold, an archway -> C.3 The Mural Wall, an open passage to the plinth -> C.4 The Plinth, a wooden door -> C.5 The Sand Clock, down the folding stair -> D.1 The Stair Landing
