C.2 **Windlass Landing** (low) - *rope-scarred, drafty, connective*
Old rope ends and iron rings are bolted to the rock here, the first solid footing after the drop down from the Shaft-Head above. A steady draft threads through unseen from somewhere deeper in the workings.
*A roughly 15 by 15 foot landing carved from bare rock, the shaft opening overhead and two passages leading off at floor level. A constant draft moves through, carrying the mineral smell of deep stone.*
**Mooring Rings:** iron rings bolted into the rock beside the shaft opening, still sound, where the original crew's descent rope was once tied off.
**Exits:** broken windlass shaft, climbing back up roughly 20 feet -> C.1 The Shaft-Head, low passage, north wall -> C.3 Overseer's Cubby, wider passage, east wall -> C.4 Timbered Gallery
