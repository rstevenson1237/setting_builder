C.2 **The Tile Floor** (Medium) - *coloured, trapped, echoing*

A wide floor of red, blue and white tiles stretches away under a carved roof, a faint click sounding underfoot. Arches open east and north.

*A room 50 feet square and 20 feet high, tiled wall to wall. A safe path of red tiles leads to each arch; the blue and white tiles shift. Centipedes nest in the gaps.*

**The Tiles:** The red tiles are firm and lead to the arches; blue and white ones tilt under weight. A party that crosses on them wakes the centipedes.
**The Centipedes:** Brown, as long as an arm, hiding in the gaps between tiles (Hostile, 2-4, Bestiary : Giant Centipede). They bite what crosses.
**The Sign:** Twelve tiles in the east arch carry numerals, one each (The Cynidiceans Raise the City).

**Exits:** an open passage, south, 50 feet -> C.1 The Threshold, an archway, east, 60 feet -> C.3 The Mural Wall, an open passage, north, 50 feet -> C.4 The Plinth
