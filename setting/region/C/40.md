C.40 **The Chittering Nest** (high) - *Slick Floor, Own Water, Crowded*
A low dressed hall with a soot-blackened **altar** on the north wall, a **bedding heap** of rags and bone banked in the middle, and green-glazed flags that make a **slick floor**. A cask-sized **stone jar** sits in the northeast corner, its pitch lid scored with three-claw gouges.
*Thirty feet east to west by twenty north to south, dressed stone squared to one measure under a seven-foot ceiling, the west third dug rougher and a stride wider where sleepers widened it. It was a chamber where a flame was kept at the altar and is now the Old Cistern Khughik's sleeping hall, which they call Kraurgh, its rites gone to soot and wear. Chittering rises from the bedding heap, and water drips from the ceiling seam onto the flags below the altar.*
**Altar:** North-wall slab, Ghusuz cut in its face, soot-black channels from its kerbed bowl to a lead-plugged plinth seam, torch brought near the bowl -> channels burn, plug runs.
**Slick Floor:** Green slime glazes the flags from the west wall inward, skid scars ending at the heap, a running step -> feet go out and the fall lands on stone (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing).
**Bedding Heap:** Rags and gnawed bone banked against the jar, Zoghik on top chewing a hide strip, a lump of fresh tallow held out to her -> she takes it (Named Creature: Zoghik).
**Stone Jar:** Northeast corner, cask-sized and too heavy to lift, black pitch sealing its lid, chipped free where it stands (Treasure III, d20).
**Ravine Lynx:** Three-claw gouges score the jar's pitch and the dust around it holds no hand-print, a lynx ranging the hall between sleeps (Watchful, 1, Bestiary : Ravine Lynx).
**Plinth Drawer:** Iron-lipped drawer behind the lead plug, a knife tip snapped in the seam, a torch held to the seam not the bowl -> lead slags and fuses the drawer shut (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** Leaf - oak planks on strap hinges, swollen at the foot, barred from this side by an iron bar in two wall brackets, south wall -> C.39 Old Cistern Entrance, Grille - iron bars leaded into the stone, bent apart at the base into a gap a crawling body wide, east wall -> C.41 Store Nook, Climb - iron rungs leaded into a shaft, lowest two rusted thin, capped by a floor hatch that turns only to a fitted crank, southeast corner -> C.42 Cistern Chamber, Portcullis - iron bars dropped from a ceiling slot, rust-fused and hanging a forearm above the floor, west wall -> C.43 Bolt-Hole
