C.40 **The Chittering Nest** (high) - *Slick Floor, Own Water, Crowded*
Under a **corbelled vault**, rag-and-straw sleeping hollows are packed wall to wall, broken by a **weeping floor** that shines wet in a band running up to a soot-black **altar** against the far wall. On a **pitch-sealed jar** in the middle of the bedding squats **Zoghik**, splitting bones and chittering at every sound.
*An oval chamber 35 feet by 25, cut by the old builders in dressed stone and gone to decay: facing blocks have dropped from the lower courses in places, and the Khughik of the Old Cistern have heaped nine sleeping hollows into every dry foot of it, the whole warren sleeping here in shifts. The Khughik call it Kraurgh, after one of their own was scalded at the altar. A steady drip from the altar's drain hole can be heard from the doorway. Cold, and footing is slick only within the wet band.*
**Zoghik:** Squatting on the pitch-sealed jar at the center of the bedding and cracking marrow bones on its lid, the warren's bloated matriarch chitters a Grozzgur challenge at whoever enters, turns her back and lets a party pass for a hand of meat or rations laid on the floor before her, and fights to the last anyone who lays a hand on the jar (Named Creature: Zoghik).
**Pitch-Sealed Jar:** A waist-high clay jar in the room's center, its wooden bung sealed over with pitch, is a two-person carry and holds, once the pitch is cut away, (Treasure III, d20).
**Altar:** Against the north wall a hip-high block of dressed stone carries Old Tongue letters reading Ghusuz across its front, a spiral channel cut into its top that winds down to a hand-wide drain hole at the center, and a bronze socket the width of a torch haft at its head, channel and socket both crusted black with old soot, a lit flame set upright in the socket -> the water on the weeping floor sinks back into the stone for as long as it burns, a flame thrust down the drain hole instead -> a gout of steam snuffs it and scalds the hand that held it (Test of Constitution, 1d, Fire).
**Knelt Hollows:** Two knee-worn hollows sit in the dry floor at the altar's head facing the socket, and the ceiling directly above the socket is burned black in a ring the width of a shield.
**Weeping Floor:** A film of water wells up through the dressed floor in a band two strides wide from the middle of the room to the altar's foot and, to anyone who crouches and watches it, creeps uphill into the altar's channel and down its drain toward the cistern below, and anyone crossing the band faster than a walk skids on the polished stone into the bedding with a clatter heard from the doorway and a dropped torch hissing out.
**Loose Floor Slab:** In the middle of the wet band the snapped end of a bone lever sticks up out of the film from a seam scarred with pry marks, the seam and the slab it outlines show plainly once the floor is dried, and the slab lifts out onto a soldered lead box the size of a loaf with a blind white cave newt coiled on its lid, holding (Treasure III, d20).
**Corbelled Vault:** The ceiling steps inward in corbelled courses to a capstone twelve feet up, room enough to swing a spear or stand a pole upright.
