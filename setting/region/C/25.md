C.25 **Bone Trophy Hall** (high) - *Hooks, Worked Trophy, Display*
Cut ears and other trophies taken from outsiders hang on display the length of one wall, kept rather than hidden the way every other household keeps its own.
*Rough hall twenty yards across, active, the trophy wall the only worked surface in the room, everything else left as bare cut rock.*
**Trophy Wall:** Cut ears and larger trophies both hang from hooks driven straight into the rock, sorted by size rather than by raid, the largest kept at eye height for anyone brought through.
**Unstable Hooks:** A hook pulled rather than lifted brings down everything hung from the same run of rock, worth nothing beyond the noise it makes and whoever it brings (Test of Fate, Impact).
**Kept Trophy:** One trophy among the rest is worked rather than simply cut, set with a stone that doesn't match anything Hukkgur's ever traded for on its own account (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** archway, south wall -> C.24 The Brawling Pit, natural gap, east wall -> C.27 Stores
