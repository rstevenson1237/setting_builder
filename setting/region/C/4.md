C.4 **Nine Wagons Counting House** - *Decadent Court, Factor's Office*
The company's office in Velar, where its factor settles disputes behind a **barred counter** rather than under any crown's writ. The **Wagons' own ledger** tracks the ford's closure, to the coin.
*A single stone-built room thirty feet across behind a barred counter, ledgers shelved floor to ceiling behind it. Smells of ink and tallow, and a coin-scale clicks, whenever a dispute is weighed rather than argued.*
**Barred Counter:** The factor works behind it, and rarely comes around, a dispute heard through the bars, his decision final on stating it.
**Wagons' Own Ledger:** Bound in company blue-grey, tallying the detour's earnings against the old toll, read aloud for a fee or a favour, never handed over (Lore: The Wagons' Own Ledger).
**Exits:** open door - barred, iron-strapped, west wall -> C.3 Market Square
