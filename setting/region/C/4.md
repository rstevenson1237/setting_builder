C.4 **Nine Wagons Counting House** - *Decadent Court, Factor's Office*
The company's own office in Velar, where its factor settles disputes his own way rather than taking them to any crown's writ. A ledger on the counting table tracks exactly what the ford's closure is worth to the company, to the coin.
*A single stone-built room thirty feet across behind a barred counter, ledgers shelved floor to ceiling on the wall behind it. Smells of ink and tallow; a coin-scale clicks whenever a dispute is being weighed rather than argued.*
**Barred Counter:** The factor works from behind it and rarely comes around; a dispute is heard through the bars, and a decision is final the moment he states it.
**Wagons' Own Ledger:** Bound in the company's blue-grey, tallying the detour's earnings against what the ford's toll used to bring the three crowns - a fee, a favor, or simply being vouched for by a caravan already flying the colors gets it read aloud, never handed over (Lore: The Wagons' Own Ledger).
**Exits:** open door - barred, iron-strapped, west wall -> C.3 Market Square
