C.4 **Timbered Gallery** (low) - *shored, rot-timbered, connective*
Rotting support timbers shore up this long gallery at regular intervals, several visibly bowed under the weight above. It's the main thoroughfare between the shaft-head and the workings beyond.
*A long gallery roughly 50 by 8 feet, timber shoring every ten feet or so, several posts cracked but still holding. The smell of old resin from the shoring timbers lingers under the general damp.*
**Shoring Line:** the timber supports running the gallery's length, original Concern work, hasty but so far holding despite years of neglect.
