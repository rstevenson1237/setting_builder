C.4 **The Spoil Hall** (Medium) - *stripped crates, chevrons, heaped*
A square hall with **heaps** of stripped wagon-boards, crates and barrels along every wall, each board scratched with a chevron. A **roped stack** of crates leans over the **low crates** beneath it, and the air smells of stale wine and spilled grain.
*Forty feet square, the makers' ore store. Everything the road has lost to the tribe ends here before it is carried on. Nothing in it is guarded by anyone.*
**Heaps:** Boards, crates, barrels and torn canvas lie in piles to the height of a goblin's head, every board chevron-marked and stripped of its fittings.
**Roped Stack:** The top crates are roped to one another and lean out over the low ones; pulling a low crate brings the stack down on whoever stands under it (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Low Crates:** Crates under the stack have not yet been broken open (Treasure IV, d20).
**Draught Behind the Crates:** A cold draught blows through the heap at the back wall, and shifting the heaped crates uncovers a crude gap through to the Chief's Hall.
**Exits:** archway, south side -> C.2 The Warren
