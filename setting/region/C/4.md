C.4 **The Drowned Stair** (landmark) - *Submerged, Old-Stone, Foreboding*
The **Drowned Stair** descends in worn stone steps from the bank straight into black water, the lower half lost from sight. Marsh-kin give it a wide berth without saying why.
*Twenty-some steps cut into a stone facing southwest of the Weir Platforms, the top dry, the rest disappearing under the waterline; slick with algae throughout, and colder here than the open water nearby.*
**Worn Steps:** Wear runs only downward, never up - whoever used this stair mostly didn't come back this way.
**Corroded Ring:** An iron ring, rusted through, is set into the stone at the waterline - once held something moored.
**Slick Footing:** Algae coats every step below the waterline; a careless descent risks a fall into black water (Damaging).
**Old Piton:** A rusted piton and frayed rope-end are driven into the top step - somebody tried to go down before, and stopped.
**Exits:** open water, northeast -> C.2 The Weir Platforms, the submerged steps, continuing down -> C.7 The Sunken Antechamber
