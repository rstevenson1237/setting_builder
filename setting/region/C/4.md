C.4 **Ranuora** (low) - *store, empty, shelving*
A small dead-end room with **stone shelving** cut into three of its walls in four tiers, entirely empty, and a **draught** coming from somewhere that a lantern flame picks up plainly.
*A room 20 by 15 feet, ceiling 8 feet - a foot lower than the standard course. It was a dry store for the first course, emptied deliberately rather than looted. It is the driest room in Siwapelu and the air in it moves, which no other dead end here does.*
**Stone Shelving:** Four tiers of cut stone shelf running round the north, east and west walls, 18 inches deep, swept clean. Not a crumb, not a sherd, not a scrap of matting. Whoever left took the contents and then tidied, which is a fact about the Odarath's departure that no other room states this plainly.
**Draught:** A steady movement of air across the room from the north-west corner, enough to lean a flame. **Clue**: this is a dead end with no opening in it and the air is coming from somewhere; and the fourth-tier shelf in the north-west corner is worn along its front edge while every other shelf in the room is not. **Trigger**: clearing the corner shelf and pushing on the back of the recess behind it, or simply following the draught with a flame to its source. **Payload**: the back slab of the corner recess is a fitted stone, not bonded, and swings inward on its own weight into a crawl of about fifteen feet that comes out behind the cistern - a way between C.4 and C.8 that is not on any route the Faelhond use, and the reason a draught crosses a sealed room.
**Exits:** narrow corbelled arch in the south wall, lower than the standard section -> C.3 Doraiel
