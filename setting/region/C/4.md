C.4 **The Midden and Stores** (Medium) - *foul, littered, pilfered*
A dead-end chamber, a **midden** heaped at the back and a stack of looted **crates** against one wall, stinking of rot and sour beer. A crude **drawing** is scratched above the heap.
*Chamber 20 ft by 30 ft. The crust over the midden is 4 ft deep and hides a pit.*
**Midden:** Crusted refuse over a sinkhole; flies hang over one patch. A character who steps on the crust drops into the pit (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing). A rusted iron key lies buried in the heap (Keys: Chief's Chest Key).
**Looted Crates:** Stamped with a white tower, split open, and emptied but for weevily oats.
**Drawing:** A crude chest, a key and an arrow pointing down, scratched into the wall above the midden.
**Exits:** open, north end, low gap -> C.3 The Warren
