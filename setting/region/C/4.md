C.4 **The Mer Caves** (Lair) - *wet, singing, echoing*
Sea caves breathe surf, their weed-hung mouths ringed by a high wavering song, and silver-green forms rise and fall in the tidal pools.
*800 by 600 feet. The mer people hold the caves and the reef's wrecks and trade for iron and fire-worked things.*
**The singers:** silver-green folk with fish tails and webbed hands in shell armour, twelve in the water, singing three falling notes before any approach (Wary, 12, Bestiary : Merman); they trade before they fight.
**Loalvae:** the mer elder, calm and unhurried, wears a belt and collar of green weed and shell, wants the pirates' anchorage cleared, and knows every wreck on the reef and its passages.
**The weed galleries:** passages hung with weed leading into the caves, awash at high tide.
