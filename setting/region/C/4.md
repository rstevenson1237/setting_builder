C.4 **The Cowled Hall** (medium) - *Glyphs, Vestry, Broken Tablet*
A narrow vestry adjoins the rite chamber, sealing glyphs cut into every lintel here rather than just at the thresholds further out.
*Cut stone hall ten yards across, active, walls lined with hooks for vestments long since rotted away, air that tastes faintly of old iron near the glyphs themselves.*
**Sealing Glyphs:** The glyphs cut into every lintel still carry whatever charge the working set into them eight hundred years ago, and tracing or defacing one rather than simply passing beneath it costs the hand that tries (Test of Sanity, 1d).
**Glyph Primer:** A cut tablet propped against the far wall explains, in the Old Tongue, part of what the glyphs were set to hold, though the tablet's own lower half has broken away and gone missing (Lore: Glyph Primer).
**Exits:** archway, north wall -> C.3 Rite Chamber, fitted door, east wall -> C.5 Warden's Threshold
