C.4 **The Grave-Robbers' Shoring** (medium) - *rival crew at work, coordinated defense, old-tongue fragments discarded*
Rope-guides and worn footholds mark a second, rival dig at **The Grave-Robbers' Shoring**, better equipped and more disciplined than the Diggers' own cut. Whoever's working it knows exactly what they're doing.
*A narrow cleft driven deliberately into older stonework, roughly 12 by 8 feet, rope-guided along both walls; smells of lamp-oil and old dust, and the air here sits noticeably stiller than the passage leading to it.*
**Old Tongue Grave-Robbers:** A disciplined crew working this dig alone until it's exhausted (see setting/Bestiary.md), avoiding a fight it doesn't need but defending an active haul with real, coordinated force.
**Discarded Fragment:** A broken shard of old-tongue script lies tossed on the spoil-heap, judged worthless by a crew after grander things. Read closely, it names a direction and a threshold-glyph rather than a grave - a clue the crew never thought to follow toward whatever the closed-ring marks actually guard.
**Crated Relics:** What the crew has already pulled free sits half-wrapped and ready to move (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** rope-guided cleft, worn footholds, east -> C.15 The Root-Broken Gallery
