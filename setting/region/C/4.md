C.4 **The Plinth** (High) - *raised, carved, central*

A round room, its middle taken by a carved stone plinth scuffed at the base. A gargoyle crouches on top of it; along the walls stand rows of bronze-green armour.

*A round room 40 feet across and 20 feet high, the heart of the first level. The plinth turns on its base. A scraped arc shows in the floor round its foot.*

**The Plinth:** Carved with the faces of kings, its base scraped in an arc. Turned on its base, it opens a stair down to the second level (D.1).
**The Gargoyle:** Perched on the plinth's top (Hostile, 1-2, Bestiary : Gargoyle); it lets nobody touch the stone.
**The Skeletons:** Six armoured figures stand in rows along the wall (Hostile, 6-12, Bestiary : Skeleton). They move when the plinth is touched or an alarm sounds.
**The Hollow:** Inside the plinth lies a bronze disc on a cord, the sun symbol of the old kings (Keys: The Sun-Disc Key).
**The Mark:** A rope coil and notches cut near the plinth's foot show that the tomb robbers have been here (The Tomb Robbers).

**Exits:** an open passage, south, 50 feet -> C.2 The Tile Floor, a stone passage, south-east, 80 feet -> C.3 The Mural Wall
