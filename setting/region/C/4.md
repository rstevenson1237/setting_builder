C.4 **The Old Watchtower** (Ruin) - *fallen, overgrown, cold*
A roofless **tower** of makers' stone stands ivy-choked, its **stair** collapsed and a **cellar hole** gaping at its foot.
*Walls broken at 15 ft, 20 ft across. The wolves hunt at dusk.*
**Tower:** Square-cut blocks laid without mortar, the lowest courses carved with script no one reads now.
**Stair:** A spiral of stone steps that ends after the third turn in rubble.
**Cellar Hole:** Gnawed bones lie round its black mouth; five wolves den below (Hostile, 5, Bestiary : Wolf).
