C.23 **Zorethora** (high) - *approach, warning, salt*
A descending passage far wider than any other in Siwapelu, its walls, floor and ceiling **cut over every inch** with old letters, and at its foot a **threshold** with grey salt heaped a foot deep across it and standing untouched. The letters begin careful and become **worse and more hurried** the further down they go, and past the threshold the passage continues into a dark the lanterns do not reach.
*A ramped passage 15 feet wide and 90 feet long, dropping 30 feet over its length to the threshold at its foot - three times the width of a standard course and the only place in the complex where the builders spent volume on an approach rather than a room. This is the way down to the fourth course. It is markedly colder than the rest of the third course and the air is completely still: whatever the gorm does elsewhere, it does not come up this passage, and a lamp that guttered two rooms back burns clean here.*
**Cut Over Every Inch:** Old Rede on every surface, floor and ceiling included, thousands of characters. At the head of the passage the hand is the same careful unhurried cutting as the pillars at Nuthorath. By halfway down the spacing has gone irregular. In the last twenty feet the characters are shallow, crowded, cut across each other and in one place cut over an earlier line, and the final six feet before the threshold are gouged rather than cut. It says one thing, repeated, for ninety feet (Lore: Hand of the Last Warden).
**Threshold:** A corbelled arch at the passage foot, and grey salt heaped a foot deep across its full width, laid deliberately and undisturbed for four hundred years - not spilled, not stored, not mined, but placed across a doorway by people who were doing nothing else at the time. Its cap stone is blank. It is the second of the three thresholds in Siwapelu that carry no character, and by this point a party that has been checking caps knows exactly what that means.
**Wall Niches:** Cut at intervals of ten feet down both walls at chest height, eighteen shallow niches, each holding the stub of a tallow lamp burned to nothing. They were lit from the top down and they went out in order, and the lowest four were never lit at all. Nothing has been in this passage to light them since.
**What Is Below:** Nothing comes up. Zorethanu has never left the fourth course and there is no reason to think it can - the whole of the Odarath's under-work, the drainage that keeps the lowest course flooded, the eleven stones, the salt on this threshold, is an arrangement for keeping it there, and this passage is where the arrangement is legible in one view. Crossing the salt is possible and entirely unopposed - nothing in this passage stops anyone, and nothing has for four hundred years.
**Exits:** wide ramped passage climbing north out of the cut letters toward the sound of moving water -> C.19 Azzakire, corbelled arch at the passage foot beyond the heaped salt, cap stone blank, opening south into the fourth course -> C.24 Sizoreth
