C.23 **The Breached Vein** (high) - *warm, riven, central*
The ore vein itself lies breached and open here, a jagged wound in the rock still faintly warm to the touch for reasons no one has ever explained. Fine, dark veins run through the surrounding stone in patterns too regular to be entirely natural.
*An irregular chamber roughly 35 by 25 feet, dominated by the breached vein's exposed rock face along one long wall. The warmth here has no visible source and doesn't fade with distance from the vein, and the air smells faintly of hot metal even though nothing here is burning.*
**Breached Face:** the vein's exposed rock, warm under a bare hand even this deep from any surface heat, its surface threaded with hairline seams of a duller ore in a pattern that reads as worked rather than natural. Pressing a flat palm against the warmest patch of the seam and holding it there causes a fist-sized section of rock to give way, uncovering a raw ore-cache wedged inside (Treasure V, d20) - though whoever does it walks away with the same faint, unplaceable warmth in their hand for days afterward.
**Old Tool Marks:** pick-scars and drill-holes from the original breach twenty-three years ago, still visible along the vein's edges - whatever caused the initial collapse started here, though nothing about the marks explains why the crew kept digging once the wold-warmth became obvious.
**Exits:** passage, back toward the gallery fork -> C.21 The Deep Gallery Fork, passage, deeper into the dark -> C.24 The Crawler's Deep
