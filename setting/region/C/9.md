C.9 **The Collapsed Vault** (medium) - *caved roof, broken descent, old-tongue carving still visible below*
Half of the ceiling at **The Collapsed Vault** has come down entirely, leaving a ragged gap into darkness below. What's left of the floor slopes visibly toward the break.
*A once-square chamber roughly 15 feet across, now half rubble and half open pit where the floor gave way; smells of raw stone dust even now, and a dropped pebble takes a long moment to land somewhere below.*
**Failing Floor:** The floor's far half is cracked clean through and holds weight only until it doesn't. Impact: damaging - a real fall, but a survivable one, dropping straight through to C.16 The Cold Draft Passage below with no way back up unaided. The tell: a visible sag and old crack-lines radiating from the break, plus fine dust still sifting down whenever weight shifts nearby.
**What Brought It Down:** Old-tongue carving is still visible on a slab tipped down into the gap - a fragment of the same closed-ring motif, suggesting this vault mattered enough to mark before whatever collapsed it did its work.
**Exits:** low archway, dry stone, west -> C.6 The Drowned Cistern, ragged floor gap, straight down -> C.16 The Cold Draft Passage
