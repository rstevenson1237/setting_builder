C.9 **Cistern Reach** (low) - *Old Digging, Old Water*
An old cistern cut by the ravine's first builders still holds water, clear and cold, in a chamber otherwise as bare as the day it was cut.
*Cut stone chamber ten yards across, active, the cistern itself sunk into the centre of the floor, water perfectly still but for the drip that feeds it.*
**Old Cistern:** The cistern's own water is fouled by nothing that's touched it since the shrine took the halls over, kept clean rather than fouled the way most of the ravine's old cisterns have gone.
**Exits:** natural gap, north wall -> C.8 Stores of the Shrine
