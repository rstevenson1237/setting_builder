C.9 **Threshold Archway** (low) - *spiral-carved, transitional, chilled*
A single stone archway marks a clear transition here, the rough Concern tunnel giving way on the far side to smoother, older stonework. A faint spiral chisel-mark is carved into the archway's keystone, the first sign of anything not Concern-built.
*A short passage roughly 10 by 8 feet, centered on a freestanding stone archway unlike anything else in the upper workings. The temperature drops noticeably passing through it, and the air beyond smells faintly of old, undisturbed stone.*
**Carved Archway:** a stone archway spanning the passage, its keystone carved with a single spiral chisel-mark matching the standing stones above the fringe - the first clear sign that something older than the Concern's mine runs beneath it.
**Exits:** passage, back toward the timbered gallery -> C.4 Timbered Gallery, passage, through the archway -> C.10 The Cold Passage, passage, branching south before the archway -> C.13 Silent Gallery
