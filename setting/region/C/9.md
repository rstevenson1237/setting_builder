C.9 **The Reef Gap** (Natural) - *narrow, surging, wrecked*
A narrow gap in the reef surges and hisses between coral walls, a faint three-note song under the water and a galleon's stern wedged across it.
*60 by 200 feet. A boat needs a pilot to cross; one refused the gap is holed on the reef. The way into the drowned galleon is here.*
**The gap:** a narrow cut through the coral with a strong current, four mer people guarding it, who sing three falling notes and want a pilot's token or iron (Wary, 4, Bestiary : Merman).
**The galleon's stern:** wedged across the gap, a great ship's broken after-end, reached by its stern deck.
**Exits:** out to the stern -> I.1 The Stern Deck
