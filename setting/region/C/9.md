C.9 **Doraianu** (low) - *gallery, length, drainage*
A long straight gallery, the spine of the second course, with **arches down both sides** and a **drainage channel** running its whole length that carries a thin stream of water at some hours and nothing at others.
*A gallery 140 by 8 feet, ceiling 9 feet, running east to west, the longest single space in Siwapelu. It was and is the second course's main circulation. Its length is the sensory fact: a lantern does not reach either end from the middle, and a party in it can see two pools of their own light and nothing between.*
**Arches Down Both Sides:** Four openings off the gallery, two north and two south, all of standard section and all with the threshold character on their caps. There is nothing to choose between them by looking, which is what makes this a divide rather than a branch - the party has to go and find out, and the gallery is long enough that going and finding out costs real time.
**Drainage Channel:** The standard 6-inch channel down the centre, which on this course actually runs. Which way it runs is the tide above: south on a making tide, north on a falling one, turning over about ten minutes. A party that has been to the drainage hall understands what they are reading; a party that has not can still time it and use it.
**Worn Ends:** The gallery's flags are dished with wear at the east end and hardly at all at the west, and the wear at the east end continues six feet out of the room it serves and then stops dead. Something walked that six feet a very great number of times and then did not walk it again.
**Exits:** wide corbelled arch in the east wall with a stepped ramp climbing to the first course -> C.7 Nuthorath, corbelled arch in the north wall, first of the two on that side -> C.10 Wosaora, wide corbelled arch in the south wall, running water audible through it -> C.11 Siwakire
