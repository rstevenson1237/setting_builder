C.9 **The Serpent's Midden** (hidden) - *Bone-Strewn, Reeking, Marked*
The **Serpent's Midden** is a foul clearing in the reeds, heaped with bones and the indigestible leavings of years of kills. Nothing has cleared it out, because nothing wants to.
*A trampled clearing ten yards across in the reeds behind the Serpent's Reach, thick with the reek of old kills; bones of every size are heaped and half-sunk in black mud.*
**Bone Heap:** Years of kills, heaped and rotting; mostly fish and river-game, some larger.
**Indigestible Leavings:** Buckles, blades, and a boot sole are mixed into the heap, swallowed and brought back up (Treasure II, d20).
**Fresh Sign:** A kill dragged in within the last day lies half-eaten near the heap's edge - the serpent was here recently.
**Exits:** the reeking trail, back -> C.1 The Serpent's Reach
