C.9 **The Sunken Hall** (high) - *central basin, cracked stone, barrow's heart*
The barrow's heart opens into a wide chamber dominated by a cracked, long-dry **basin**, stone steps descending into it from every side. Carvings ring the rim, repeating the same image of a **bell** lowered into water.
*A circular chamber sixty feet across with a ten-foot-deep basin at its center; sound carries unusually far from here through the rest of the barrow, so anything loud done in this room risks being heard elsewhere.*
**Basin:** Cracked open at its base; descending into it and examining the crack closely reveals it as more than damage (see Cracked Basin Floor).
**Cracked Basin Floor:** The crack matches the repeating bell-and-water carving pattern from the rim; tracing that shape by hand or torchlight against the crack widens the gap enough to pass through, opening a hidden route down to C.23 Basin Depths.
**Rim Carvings:** Depict a bell being lowered into water, again and again; studying them ties the imagery directly to Vael's fall.
**Sealed Niche of Tribute:** A mortared-shut niche off the hall, its mortar unmistakably older than any other sealed surface in the barrow; breaking the seal reveals Saltmere's missing bronze diving-bell, resting on a bed of coral offerings, opening access to C.10.
**Exits:** passage -> C.8 Coral-Choked Hall, passage -> C.11 Grave-Goods Row, passage -> C.12 Collapsed Stair
