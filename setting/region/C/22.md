C.22 **Rival Watch Point** (medium) - *Ledge, Border, Fall Back*
A wrecked stone watch-post whose broken **ledge** looks east through a loophole at a dented iron door, with a **stack** of rubble piled over the west gap and a hide **cord** run along the wall to a post. Fist-sized **dents** pock the iron.
*The post is 30 feet by 15 under a 7-foot ceiling, a Gorzgur watch position on the border with Hukkgur, its post smashed and abandoned. Grit trickles from the stack whenever the floor shakes.*
**Ledge:** A waist-high stone ledge runs the east wall with its outer half broken away, red tally marks scratched on its lip and a loophole cut above it.
**Dents:** Fist-sized dents pock the iron plates of the east door's leaf, the oak splintered round its bar-socket.
**Stack:** Rubble sits on a plank over the west gap's lintel, an ankle-high cord across the gap -> snagging the cord drops it across the gap, costing an hour's clearing.
**Cord:** A second cord runs from the plank along the wall to a hook on the ledge's end post -> unhooking it lets the plank down so the stack spills aside.
**Exits:** natural gap in the rock with chipped-rough edges and a floor worn to a hollow, west wall -> C.14 Muster Yard, iron-plated oak leaf hanging off one hinge and knuckle-dented, splintered round its bar-socket, east wall -> C.23 Broken Gate
