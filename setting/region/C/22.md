C.22 **Rival Watch Point** (medium) - *Ledge, Border, Fall Back*
A rock ledge runs along the side of a raw cut past a **wrecked watch hut** smashed to kindling, and at its east end a black, glistening **greased lip** slopes down to a breach in old dressed stone. Against the back wall lie a **maul and wedges**.
*A natural ledge 40 feet long and 10 wide above a 20-foot drop into a rubble-choked cut, the Gorzgur watch over the crossing to the Broken Gate, now wrecked and empty since the watch fell back to the Muster Yard. The smell of rendered fat on the ledge comes from the greased lip.*
**Greased Lip:** At the ledge's east end a slab slopes ten feet down to the breach, black and slick with fresh fat worked over old into the stone -> anyone stepping onto it or climbing it from below slides to the bottom with a clatter of whatever they carry and has to go back up on hands or a rope.
**Wrecked Watch Hut:** A timber hut that stood at the ledge's edge is smashed to kindling, a split Gorzgur horn lies in the wreck beside a smear of dried blood, and snagged in the splinters hangs a bone tab scratched with two faceless standing figures.
**Maul and Wedges:** Against the back wall lie a stone maul and a stack of oak wedges, and wedges of the same oak show under the top edge of the greased lip where it meets the ledge, their heads flattened by the maul -> knocking those wedges out drops the slab away into the cut, leaving a sheer ten-foot face between the ledge and the breach.
**Exits:** fixed iron bars mortared across the end of the crack, two of them wrenched apart into a gap one can squeeze through, south side at the west end -> C.14 Muster Yard, hole smashed through the old dressed wall at the foot of the greased lip, its edges crumbling, east end below the ledge -> C.23 Broken Gate
