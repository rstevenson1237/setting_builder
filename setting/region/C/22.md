C.22 **The Rusted Vault** (medium) - *dissolved iron, old debt, trapped door*
A small vault's **iron door** has rusted to little more than orange dust, its former contents scattered across the threshold in a wide, telling ring.
*A small vault roughly 10 by 10 feet, its iron door long dissolved to orange dust - an irregularity, since nowhere else in the barrow used iron fittings this heavy. The threshold itself is trapped - a plate beneath the dust triggers a fall of loose stone from above if stepped on directly; skirting the edges avoids it.*
**Threshold:** Trapped beneath the dust; stepping directly on it triggers a fall of loose stone from above (TEST OF CONSTITUTION or a WOUND). Skirting the edges avoids the trap.
**Scattered Remains:** Whatever survived the iron's dissolution, too worn to identify without careful cleaning - among it, a single legible fragment (Lore: The Vault Ledger).
**Exits:** open threshold, iron long dissolved -> C.21 Bell-Wight's Chamber
