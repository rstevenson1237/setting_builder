C.22 **Prospectors' Last Camp** (medium) - *scattered, decayed, disturbed*
A ring of scattered, rotted supplies marks where the original crew made a last camp before whatever happened here happened. A cold, long-dead fire pit sits at the center, ringed by packs no one ever came back to claim.
*An open gallery roughly 30 by 20 feet, floor littered with decades-rotted canvas, rope, and scattered tools around a long-cold fire pit. The smell of old ash lingers under the general damp, and every surface here is thick with undisturbed dust.*
**Last Camp:** packs, bedrolls, and tools rotted nearly to nothing, arranged in a rough circle around the dead fire pit - clearly a deliberate camp, not a scattering from panic; searching through what's left turns up whatever of the original crew's gear survived intact (Treasure II, d20).
**Cold Fire Pit:** at the circle's center, ringed with stones - the ash is long dead, but a few half-burned scraps of paper are still legible among the embers, mentioning a decision to press on past where the boundary-cutters' warning said to stop.
