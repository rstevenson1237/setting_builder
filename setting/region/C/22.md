C.22 **The Rusted Vault** (medium) - *dissolved iron, old debt, trapped door*
A small vault's **iron door** has rusted to little more than orange dust, its former contents scattered across the threshold in a wide, telling ring.
*The threshold itself is trapped - a plate beneath the dust triggers a fall of loose stone from above if stepped on directly; skirting the edges avoids it.*
**Threshold:** Trapped beneath the dust; stepping directly on it triggers a fall of loose stone from above. Skirting the edges avoids the trap.
**Scattered Remains:** Whatever survived the iron's dissolution - non-metal grave goods too worn to identify without careful cleaning.
**Exits:** passage -> C.21 Bell-Wight's Chamber
