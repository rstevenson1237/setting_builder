C.5 **The Ore Cart Siding** (medium) - *rusted, rail-worn, lurking*
Three rusted ore carts sit dead on a warped rail siding here, one tipped half off its track where something last disturbed it. A single **Severed Finger**, pale and still twitching, drags itself along the nearest rail with slow, deliberate purpose.
*A roughly 20 by 15 foot alcove branching off the main gallery, rail track sunk into a warped wooden bed underfoot, rust flaking from every metal surface at a touch. The carts smell of old iron and stagnant oil, and a faint, wet dragging sound never quite stops.*
**Ore Carts:** three rusted carts on the siding, one overturned - a single detached finger, part of Torvin Ashe's scattered remains, drags itself between and beneath them without pause, endlessly searching for what's left of the whole (Named Creature: Torvin Ashe).
**Warped Rail:** the siding's track bed, rotted and rust-fused in several places - anyone moving quickly along it risks a twisted ankle on the uneven ties, though nothing here is severe enough to warrant more than caution.
**Exits:** passage, back toward the timbered gallery -> C.4 Timbered Gallery, passage, following the rail bed down -> C.8 The Flooded Cut
