C.5 **Moneylender's House** - *Usurers, Moneylender's Ledger*
The moneylender who finances Velar's overextended houses works a narrow house off the square. Her **ledger** is the real record of who still owns a warehouse here.
*A narrow three-story house, twenty feet across, the ground floor given over to counting. Smells of dust and old paper, and a strongbox drags across the floor above, when a large sum changes hands.*
**Moneylender:** Lends against a warehouse, a caravan, or a season's trade, terms steeper since the ford closed.
**Moneylender's Ledger:** Names who in Velar cannot cover, what they owe her, and she will not sell it for coin.
**Exits:** open door - narrow, iron-hinged, north wall -> C.3 Market Square
