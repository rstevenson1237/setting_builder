C.5 **Peluora** (medium) - *tools, benches, abandonment*
A workroom with **stone benches** running down both long walls and **tool racks** cut into the masonry above them, most of the racks still holding what was put in them nine hundred years ago. A **hoist frame** of iron stands over a shaft in the floor at the far end, and the shaft is not deep and does not go anywhere.
*A rectangle 45 by 20 feet, ceiling 9 feet, with a 6-foot shaft sunk in the floor at the west end. It was the working shop for the first course - hafting, sharpening, repair - and it was left in good order rather than abandoned in a hurry. The room smells of iron and old tallow, and it is dead quiet: the benches and racks break up the sound that carries everywhere else in Siwapelu, and it is the one room where a party can hear each other properly.*
**Tool Racks:** Cut as slots into the dressed masonry above both benches, some forty of them, about two-thirds still holding tools - stone-headed hammers, bronze chisels, iron feathers and wedges, drifts, a set of nine graded punches. Four hundred years of salt air has left the iron unmarked. They are Odarath work, they are the oldest usable tools anybody in Brackvaen has ever handled, and they are worth a great deal to the wrong person in Torsgaard (Treasure II, d20).
**Hoist Frame:** An iron A-frame 7 feet high standing over the floor shaft at the room's west end, with its sheave still turning freely and a length of chain hanging in it. The shaft below is 6 feet deep, flagged at the bottom, and empty - it is not a well and not a passage. It is a fitting pit, cut so that something could be stood in it and worked on from bench height, and the flag at its bottom is scored in a ring 4 feet across by something that was turned in place there repeatedly.
**Fitting Rod:** In the third rack from the door, among the drifts, is a rod of dressed stone rather than iron - 18 inches, square-sectioned, with a four-inch head cut to a shallow taper. It does not belong in a tool rack and it is not a tool. It is a fitting, and it matches sockets found elsewhere in the complex (Keys: Peluora Fitting-Rod).
**Exits:** corbelled arch in the east wall, cap carrying the threshold character -> C.3 Doraiel, corbelled arch in the west wall, its jambs rubbed smooth at shoulder height by long use -> C.6 Mezeora
