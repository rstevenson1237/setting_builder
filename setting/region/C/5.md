C.5 **Warden's Threshold** (medium) - *Old Digging, Cut Threshold*
A cut stone archway stands squared to the same old measure as everywhere else in the ravine's oldest halls, an armored shape standing motionless within it, weapon already drawn.
*Cut stone passage eight yards across, active, the threshold itself worn only at its edges, dead centre untouched by any foot in nine hundred years.*
**Sealed Warden:** Armored past recognizing the shape underneath, it lets pass whoever shows the same three-line mark the bronze tribute rod carries, and stops or kills whoever tries the threshold without it (Warded, 1, Bestiary : Sealed Warden).
**Exits:** fitted door, west wall -> C.4 The Cowled Hall, sealed shaft, floor, gated by the mark shown first -> C.10 The Deepest Chamber
