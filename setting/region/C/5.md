C.5 **Moneylender's House** - *Usurers, Moneylender's Ledger*
The moneylender who finances Velar's overextended trading houses works from a narrow house just off the square, her own ledger the real record of who in Velar actually still owns their warehouse.
*A narrow three-story house, twenty feet across, the ground floor given over entirely to a counting room. Smells of dust and old paper; a strongbox is dragged across the floor above whenever a large sum changes hands.*
**Moneylender:** Lends against a warehouse, a caravan, or a season's expected trade - terms run steep since the ford closed, and steeper still for anyone she has not dealt with before.
**Moneylender's Ledger:** Names, so far unspoken elsewhere, of exactly who in Velar currently owes her more than they can cover - information she will not part with for coin alone, only for something she cannot buy.
**Exits:** open door - narrow, iron-hinged, north wall -> C.3 Market Square
