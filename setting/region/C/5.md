C.5 **The Standing Stones** (Natural) - *tall, moss-grown, humming*
Seven tall **stones** ring a low rise round a flat **altar-stone**, moss on their north faces. A **row of sticks** is pushed into the earth to the south.
*Ring 30 ft across. The place is empty by day.*
**Stones:** Seven grey pillars, each twice a man's height; a hum rises from them when a hand is laid on one, and soot handprints show on the nearest.
**Altar-Stone:** Waist-high, its top worn smooth by hands.
**Row of Sticks:** Their tops smeared dark with soot.
**Watchers:** At night three bugbears wait among the pillars, strike from hiding and do not parley (Hostile, 3, Bestiary : Bugbear).
