C.5 **The Merchantman's Wreck** (Ruin) - *listing, green, stripped*
A listing merchantman lies stripped to its ribs on a sandbar, its hold open to the gulls and water slapping the hull.
*200 by 60 feet. A diver can search wrecks like this (Local Procedure: Diving the Wrecks and the Sea Caves). Anything taken unasked is answered by the mer people.*
**The split hull:** ribs and planking green with weed, the hold open to the tide.
**The sand claws:** four shield-sized crabs half buried in the sand beside the hull (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Giant Crab).
**The hull's cargo:** sodden crates and a broken mast on the sandbar, picked over by the sea.
