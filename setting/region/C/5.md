C.5 **The Bear's Den Mound** (medium) - *claw-scored stonework, old bedding and bone, ignored unless entered*
Claw-marks score the old stonework at head height all along the approach to **The Bear's Den Mound**, and the smell of rot and fur only gets stronger going in.
*A wide, low mound-chamber roughly 20 feet across, ceiling scraped and rubbed smooth in patches; smells strongly of old fur and worse, and stays noticeably warmer than the passages around it.*
**Barrow Bear:** An old, scarred bear that has denned in this ruin rather than a natural cave (see setting/Bestiary.md), ignoring anything that stays outside its den but treating anyone who enters it as an intruder in its home, not prey to be chased down elsewhere.
**Dragged Kit:** A pack and a broken digging tool lie half-buried in the old bedding, dragged in with some meal the bear didn't finish (Treasure II, d20).
**Exits:** low mound mouth, claw-scored stone, north -> C.13 The Old Foundation Course, narrow gap, warm air, south -> C.6 The Drowned Cistern
