C.5 **The Sand Clock** (Medium) - *sealed, sanded, ticking*

A bare cell, sand drifted in its corners and a bronze plug set in the middle of the floor. Sand trickles somewhere overhead.

*A room 20 feet square and 20 feet high. The door swings shut on a counterweight once a party is inside. Then the floor sinks a hand's breadth a Round and sand pours from slots in the roof; in ten Rounds the room is full.*

**The Door:** Wooden, on a counterweight; it shuts by itself and does not open from inside until the sand stops.
**The Roof Slots:** A row of narrow slots, sand trickling from them. Once the door shuts they pour, and each Round in the sand is a Test (Test of Constitution, 3d, Crushing). The tomb robbers know the room and wedge the door.
**The Bronze Plug:** Set in the floor, a ring on top. Pulled, it drains the sand into the level below and the floor rises back.

**Exits:** a wooden door -> C.2 The Tile Floor
