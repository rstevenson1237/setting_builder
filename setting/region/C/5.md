C.5 **The Chief's Hall** (High) - *bone seat, furs, kettle-helms*
A high round hall greasy with roast fat, a **bone seat** of stacked bone and hide opposite the door and **furs** heaped on the floor before it, with **kettle-helms** hung on pegs around the wall. **Gronab** gnaws ribs on the seat, and a torn **banner** is nailed behind him.
*Gronab, called in Gob "the bigger taker", is here nine times in ten. The hall was the makers' foreman's office, forty feet across. A plank door opens to the Warren, and shouting here is heard there. The goblins call the chief's heap Bruzshub, Gob for "many taken things".*
**Gronab:** A goblin half again as broad as the rest, who bellows for the warren the moment strangers show and fights from his seat with a cleaver in each fist (Named Creature: Gronab).
**Bone Seat:** Stacked bone and hide, higher than a man's knee; no goblin touches it, and any who see someone seated in it hold back.
**Banner:** A torn banner with a grey tower on a white field is nailed to the wall behind the seat.
**Kettle-Helms:** Kettles and pots cut into helms hang from pegs around the wall, some of them with horns riveted on.
**Iron-Bound Chest:** An iron-bound chest lies beneath the furs, its lid heaped with bones (Hoard: Bruzshub).
**Pouch:** A leather pouch is tied beneath the bone seat (Magic, d6).
**Exits:** wooden plank door, near wall -> C.2 The Warren, crude gap behind seat -> C.4 The Spoil Hall
