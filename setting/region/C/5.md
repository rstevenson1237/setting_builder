C.5 **The Listening Alcove** (medium) - *echoing stone, whispered names, unnerving trick*
A curved alcove of unusually smooth **stone** catches and warps sound, throwing back fragments of whatever was just said - including, unnervingly, names no one in the party spoke aloud.
*A small curved alcove roughly 10 feet across, its walls polished unnaturally smooth - unlike anywhere else in the barrow, with no clear original purpose. A trick of acoustics, not a listening intelligence; speaking a specific question toward the curve echoes it back distorted but answerable.*
**Curved Wall:** Speaking a question toward it yields a fragment of the barrow's history or layout in reply.
**Exits:** low stone passage -> C.3 First Niche Row
