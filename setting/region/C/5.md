C.5 **The Old Vault** (Low) - *sealed, silent, avoided*
A low, bare **chamber**, clean of goblin litter, dry and faintly warm. A **niche** in the far wall holds a small bronze **chest**, and rubble slopes up through a gap in the north wall.
*Chamber 15 ft square, 8 ft high. Two ways in: the flush stone door west and the rubble slope north. Goblins do not come in.*
**Chamber:** Swept stone floor, undisturbed by feet. Scuff marks run to the foot of the rubble slope and turn back.
**Niche:** A recess in the east wall holding a bronze chest. Its contents are the makers' pay (Magic, d6).
**Flush Stone Door:** The west wall swings outward at a push, onto C.2.
**Rubble Slope:** A collapsed arch in the north wall, passable on hands and knees.
**Exits:** stone, west wall, flush stone -> C.2 The Carved Hall, broken, north wall, rubble slope -> C.6 The Chief's Hall
