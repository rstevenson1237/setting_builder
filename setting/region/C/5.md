C.5 **The Still-Fruit Thicket** (landmark) - *Abundant, Reed-Choked, Foraged*
The **Still-Fruit Thicket** is a reed island thick with dark, sweet berries the marsh-kin prize, growing wild and abundant. A **River Ox** cow grazes here most days, calm unless startled.
*A raised reed island thirty yards across west of the Weir Platforms, drier underfoot than the surrounding marsh; smells sweet and fermented where fallen fruit rots, loud with insects.*
**Still-Fruit:** Dark berries grow thick and abundant here - easy foraging, prized by the marsh-kin for trade.
**Reed-Root:** Edible root grows in the shallows ringing the island, plentiful for anyone willing to wade.
**Grazing Cow:** A River Ox cow grazes here most days, calm unless startled or crowded (Territorial, 1, Bestiary : River Ox).
**Exits:** open water, east -> C.2 The Weir Platforms
