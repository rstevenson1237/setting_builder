C.60 **Old Cistern Nook** (medium) - *Old Digging, Old Water*
A smaller cistern sits apart from the flooded hall proper, its own water untouched by whatever rests in the larger chamber.
*Cut nook eight yards across, ruined, cistern here shallow rather than deep, cold enough to numb an unprotected hand quickly.*
**Cold Water:** The cistern's own water sits colder than the air around it, numbing enough that reaching in past a few moments rather than briefly costs the reaching hand (Test of Constitution, 1d, Frost).
**Exits:** natural gap, only way -> C.58 The Flooded Hall
