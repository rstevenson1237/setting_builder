C.1 **Immorael** (medium) - *stair-head, entrance, dawn*
The stair comes down forty feet into a square landing chamber of dressed limestone, dry underfoot, with **lantern hooks** driven into the wall at head height and a **kit line** strung between them holding coils, weights and a wicker cage. Three or four of the **Faelhond** are usually here at any hour before noon, coming or going, and they will want to know what you are doing on their stair.
*A square chamber 30 by 30 feet, ceiling 9 feet, at the foot of the entry stair, with three arches out of it. It was the Odarath's own entrance hall and it is one again. It is the only room in Siwapelu that smells of people - tallow, wet wool, eel - and the draw of air down the stair is strong enough here to lean a candle flame over.*
**Faelhond:** Three or four at any hour before noon, working the drawing hours and out before the air reverses; twelve or so are below at any given time. 3d6 each and markedly hard to put down. They ask a party's business and do not accept a vague answer. They will guide for a third share, they will not take anyone past the second course, and the word 'seal' ends the conversation. Ondtla Faelling runs the head more often than not (Named Creature: Ondtla Faelling).
**Kit Line:** A tarred line strung between two lantern hooks along the east wall at head height, hung with shuttered lanterns, spare weights, dry wrappings, and a wicker cage holding a live pan-finch. The finch is the Gorm track made visible - it stops singing at two steps, which is well before anything is wrong with a person, and the Faelhond will lend one to a party that has been civil.
**Wall Datum:** Cut into the north wall at 4 feet above the floor, a horizontal groove running the room's full width, with Old Rede characters at each end. It is a water datum, and the floor of this room is well below it. The whole of the first course should be under four feet of water and has not been for three years, and the Faelhond point this out to newcomers as a joke about their good fortune.
**Exits:** dressed stone stair climbing north out of the chamber, twenty-two double treads, forty feet up to daylight -> B.2 Immora, corbelled arch in the west wall, cap carrying the threshold character, a lit lamp visible some way beyond it -> C.2 Selora
