C.1 **The Shaft-Head** (high) - *collapsed, gear-strewn, entrance*
Splintered support beams and a collapsed windlass frame ring the mouth of the old mineshaft here, the original crew's **Gear** still scattered where it was dropped twenty-three years ago. A jagged crack in the shaft wall marks where the first, hasty collapse sealed most of them in for good.
*A roughly 25 by 20 foot chamber with a caved ceiling raised just high enough to stand in one corner, centered on the vertical shaft dropping into darkness. Old rope and rusted chain hang from the broken windlass overhead, and the air smells of wet rock undercut by something faintly like scorched hair.*
**Scattered Gear:** packs, tools, and a jumble of rope lie half-buried in rockfall along the chamber's west side, untouched since the crew that dropped them never came back for it twenty-three years ago; sifting through it carefully turns up whatever's still usable (Treasure I, d20), and a torn, mud-stiffened journal page wedged beneath a fallen pack names the nine who went down and ends mid-sentence (Lore: The Last Entry).
**Cracked Ceiling:** a jagged fracture running the length of the chamber's ceiling above the east wall, visibly unstable - any loud noise or careless weight beneath it brings down a shower of rock and rubble on whoever's caught there (TEST OF FATE, WOUND).
**Broken Windlass:** the collapsed hoist frame above the shaft mouth at the chamber's center - its rope is frayed through, but its chain is still sound enough to lower someone down carefully.
**Exits:** half-collapsed mineshaft entrance, sagging timber frame -> B.3 Tannaskar Sawpits, broken windlass shaft, a rope-and-chain descent roughly 20 feet down -> C.2 Windlass Landing
