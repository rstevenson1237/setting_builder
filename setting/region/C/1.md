C.1 **West Gate** - *Tribute, Crown Bribe*
Velar's one gate opens onto the causeway road, its toll-house built into the same wall. Which crown the **toll-house clerk** collects for today turns on whose bribe landed largest.
*A stone gatehouse, wider than Tolkar's timber one, built for the wagon traffic the causeway sends. Smells of river damp off the road, and a hired watchman's boots, the only sound at the toll window after dark.*
**Toll-House Clerk:** Charges the standing toll in crown-mark, and waves certain wagons through unweighed, which ones turning on whose bribe landed, this season.
**Crown Bribe Ledger:** A second tally, of who has paid him personally, hidden in a false-bottomed toll box.
**Watch Post:** The hired watch keeps one man here, around the clock, paid by the merchant guild, answering to no crown.
**Exits:** open gate - stone, wagon-width, west wall -> B.1 Sunk Bridge Ford, open gate - matching stone, east wall -> C.3 Market Square
