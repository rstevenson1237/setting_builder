C.1 **The Serpent's Reach** (landmark) - *Deep-Channel, Ambush, Undercut*
The **Serpent's Reach** is a long, deep bend in the channel where the bank has been slid smooth by something far larger than anything else in these waters. Nothing else comes to drink along this whole stretch.
*A channel bend forty yards long, a short pole downstream of the Weir Platforms; water black and still even at midday, reeds on the near bank undisturbed and overgrown.*
**Slide:** A smooth-sided slide in the mud, wider than a barrel - whatever made it is far larger than a person.
**Serpent:** The River Serpent that holds this reach is not visible now, submerged or elsewhere along its stretch (Ancient, 1, Bestiary : River Serpent).
**Dead Water:** No fish rise and no birds wade along this whole bend - nothing here competes with it.
**Midden Trail:** A packed, reeking trail runs from the slide back into the reeds, worn by something dragging its kills.
**Exits:** open water, upstream -> C.2 The Weir Platforms, the reeking trail, into the reeds -> C.9 The Serpent's Midden
