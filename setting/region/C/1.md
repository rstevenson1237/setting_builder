C.1 **The Watch Passage** (Low) - *wet stone, clicking, narrow*
A narrow tunnel of dripping wet stone bends left, then left again, its walls scratched with **chevrons** at head height. A **sentry's stool** stands at the first bend, a **spoke heap** of snapped wagon wheels lies against the wall, and a **hide curtain** hangs across the far end with firelight under its hem.
*Thirty feet of tunnel the cave's makers cut as their way in, wide enough for two abreast. The clicking of the goblins' watch comes down it from the Warren.*
**Chevrons:** The goblins' mark, a chevron cut with a knife into the wall every few feet at a goblin's reach.
**Sentry's Stool:** A three-legged stool and a cooking-pot helm sit at the first bend, where a goblin watches the way in.
**Spoke Heap:** Spokes, hub-caps and wheel-rims, snapped and still pale at the break, are piled against the wall, and the pile grows each week.
**Hide Curtain:** A stitched hide hangs from pegs across the end of the tunnel, and anyone pushing through is seen from the hall beyond.
**Exits:** open way out, outer end, daylight -> B.3 The Cave Mouth, crude hide curtain, left -> C.2 The Warren, open fork, right, slopes down -> C.3 The Mushroom Pits
