C.1 **The Threshold** (Low) - *trapped, sunlit, narrow*

A bronze door stands half open on a tiled sill, a shaft of sunlight lying along the floor. The room runs north into shadow, its floor a field of coloured squares.

*A passage 10 feet wide and 20 feet long, 15 feet below the statues of A.1. Sand has drifted in at the door. The stair up is 20 feet and ends at the tip.*

**The Sill Tiles:** Red, blue and white squares laid in a pattern; blue tiles sit a finger lower than the rest. A party that steps on one drops a pit's worth of floor (Test of Constitution, 2d, Piercing).
**The Door:** Bronze, green with age, swinging on a pivot; it can be shut and wedged from inside.
**The Light:** A single shaft falls through the door and lights the first tile; beyond it the room is dark.

**Exits:** an open passage, north, 50 feet -> C.2 The Tile Floor, up a stair, south, 20 feet -> A.1 The Three Statues
