C.1 **The Sealed Chamber** (high) - *the Diggers' true target, warded threshold, barrow wight bound within*
Every passage in Ingaraz that goes anywhere eventually reaches the **Sealed Chamber**'s threshold, carved with a crowned closed-ring glyph unlike anything else in the barrow field. The silence here has no edge to it at all - it simply is, all the way to the walls.
*A single vaulted room roughly 20 by 20 feet, higher-ceilinged than any passage leading to it, cut foundation-stone throughout; carries no sound past its threshold, the same silence that gives Nazirur no warning.*
**Threshold Glyph:** Closed-ring mark doubled, crowned, unique to this room.
**Nazirur:** Never at peace; ignores until breached, then no warning (see setting/Bestiary.md, Barrow Wight - AD: 9d6) (Named Creature: Nazirur).
**Nazirith:** Thin iron circlet, foundation-stone inlay, rests on the center shelf (Unique Treasure: Nazirith).
**Exits:** sealed stone door, hidden ward, west wall -> C.2 The Warded Gatehouse, low archway, cold draft, north wall -> C.7 The Wrong-Facing Grave, narrow passage, absolute silence, east wall -> C.12 The Silent Passage
