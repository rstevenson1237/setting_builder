C.1 **Shrine Approach** (low) - *Watched Descent, Shrine Road*
A plain cut corridor is the shrine's own way out to the surface, footing worn smoother here than anywhere else in the old halls.
*Cut stone corridor fifteen yards long, active, footing smoother here than anywhere else in the old halls from centuries of the same tribute-bearers' steps.*
**Worn Niche:** A stretch of wall at knee height is worn smoother than the corridor around it, and pressing it slides free a shallow cubby holding a single carved mark matching one a wilderness hermit still carves absently into wood, proof he once passed this way and never came back (Quest: The Hermit's Word).
**Exits:** archway, east wall -> C.2 Tribute Hall
