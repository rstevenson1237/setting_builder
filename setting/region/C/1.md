C.1 **The Forest Trail** (Route) - *narrow, shaded, trodden*
A single-file trail climbs north between old pines and oaks, laced with roots. **Bark rings** are slashed round the trunks at the bends, and **toll-takers** wait at the next.
*Trail 12 miles from the postern to the ravine, three watches. Side paths leave it at 2.5, 3.5 and 7 miles.*
**Bark Rings:** Slashed at head height, fresh where the outlaws wait.
**Toll-Takers:** Four outlaws with bows ask 10 cn a head and shoot those who refuse (Wary, 4, Bestiary : Soldier).
**Forage:** Snares and drying herbs hang by the trail where a hunter can work (Local Procedure: Forage and Hunt in the Wood).
**Mere Path:** Two and a half miles out, a worn path runs five miles west through the trees to the mere landing (C.6).
**Standing Stones:** Three miles out, seven stones ring a rise beside the trail (C.5).
**Hare's Foot Post:** Three and a half miles out, a hare's foot nailed to a post marks a track west to the outlaws' camp (C.2).
**Hermit's Path:** Seven miles out, a path runs a mile east to a thorn fence and a gate with a bell, the hermit's clearing (C.3).
**Old Watchtower:** Eight miles out, an ivy-choked tower rises beside the trail (C.4).
**Ravine Mouth:** Twelve miles out, the trail ends where the ravine's stream path begins (E.1).
**Exits:** barred, south end of the trail -> A.2 The Postern, open, north end of the trail -> E.1 The Stream Path
