C.1 **The Cleft Passage** (Medium) - *cramped, echoing, watched*
A narrow cleft slopes into the dark, **thorn** hanging over its mouth. Fifty paces in the walls turn to cut stone, and a **niche** in one wall holds the goblins' watch.
*Passage 5 ft wide and 8 ft high, running 60 ft north. One goblin watches here by day and two by night; any alarm carries to the warren in two rounds.*
**Thorn:** Thorn hangs thick over the mouth. A party that cuts it makes noise the watch hears.
**Cut Stone:** The rock gives way to fitted blocks, 6 ft wide, 8 ft high, running straight as a rule.
**Niche:** Two goblins sit in a side niche and shout a warning before they fight (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Goblin).
**Exits:** open, south, mouth of cleft -> B.4 Broken Arch, archway, north end of passage -> C.2 The Carved Hall
