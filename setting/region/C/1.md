C.1 **The Sealed Chamber** (high) - *the Diggers' true target, warded threshold, barrow wight bound within*
Every passage in Ingaraz that goes anywhere eventually reaches the **Sealed Chamber**'s threshold, carved with a crowned closed-ring glyph unlike anything else in the barrow field. The silence here has no edge to it at all - it simply is, all the way to the walls.
*A single vaulted room roughly 20 by 20 feet and higher-ceilinged than any passage leading to it, cut foundation-stone throughout; smells of cold, mineral dust rather than earth, and carries no sound whatsoever past its own threshold - not footsteps, not breath, nothing.*
**Threshold Glyph:** The closed-ring mark repeated throughout Ingaraz appears here doubled, a crown shape worked into the stone above it - architecture found nowhere else in the barrow field, unique to this one room.
**Nazirur:** What's bound here was buried with real ceremony and never given peace to go with it (see setting/Bestiary.md, Barrow Wight - AD: 9d6). It ignores anyone who does not breach the chamber; anyone who does gets no warning before it acts (Named Creature: Nazirur). It remembers nothing of the world above and wants only that the chamber stay sealed - it is not persuadable, and it does not negotiate.
**Nazirith:** A thin iron circlet rests on the stone shelf at the chamber's center, inlaid with foundation-stone the same grey as the walls themselves (Unique Treasure: Nazirith).
**Exits:** sealed stone door, hidden ward, west wall -> C.2 The Warded Gatehouse, low archway, cold draft, north wall -> C.7 The Wrong-Facing Grave, narrow passage, absolute silence, east wall -> C.12 The Silent Passage
