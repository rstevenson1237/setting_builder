C.1 **The Cairn Mouth** (low) - *wind-worn arch, entrance, old carving*
A wind-worn **stone archway** half-swallowed by scrub marks the barrow's only above-ground entrance, its lintel carved with weathered figures no longer clearly human.
*A narrow stone threshold roughly 8 feet across, arched overhead - a simple entrance, unchanged from its original purpose. Nothing here demands a roll, though the carving rewards a close look.*
**Carved Lintel:** The figures wear robes and ornaments recognizable, on reflection, as pre-dating any coastal hold by centuries.
**Exits:** weathered stone road -> B.1 Buried Road, low stone passage -> C.2 Silted Passage
