C.1 **West Gate** - *Tribute, Crown Bribe*
Velar's one gate opens onto the causeway road, its toll-house built into the same wall. Which of the three crowns the toll-house clerk collects for today depends on whose bribe landed largest this month.
*A stone gatehouse, wider than Tolkar's timber one, built for the wagon traffic the causeway sends it. Smells of river damp off the causeway road; a hired watchman's boots are the only regular sound at the toll window after dark.*
**Toll-House Clerk:** Charges the standing toll in crown-mark, same as anywhere in Telar, but is known to wave certain wagons through unweighed and uncounted - which ones changes with whichever crown paid his own factor best this season.
**Crown Bribe Ledger:** A second, unofficial tally the clerk keeps of who has paid him personally, hidden inside a false-bottomed toll box - not the crown's own ledger, and not for crown eyes.
**Watch Post:** The hired watch keeps one man at the gate around the clock, paid by the merchant guild rather than any crown, and answering to neither.
**Exits:** open gate - stone, wagon-width, west wall -> B.1 Sunk Bridge Ford, open gate - matching stone, east wall -> C.3 Market Square
