C.1 **The Shore Path** (Route) - *hard-packed, salt-crusted, open*
A hard-packed path of sand and scrub runs along the shore under salt wind, surf on one hand and dunes on the other, tide lines drawn across the sand.
*Four miles a watch on the hard sand at low tide; at high tide the beach is under water and the cliff path takes two. A boat can land at the surf, with a pilot through the reef.*
**The first hard sand:** where the route begins, the hard sand leads on, with tide lines and the ash of an old cook-fire.
**The wave-cut arch:** 1.1 miles along, a sea arch booms with surf and rubble heaps below it; a path leads to C.8.
**The hunters' beach track:** 3.1 miles along, chalk arrows on rocks and boot-prints in the sand mark a track to C.3.
**The lookout cliff path:** 3.6 miles along, a goat path climbs a gull-hung cliff to C.10.
**Exits:** the gate's road, shoreward and uphill -> A.1 The Great Gate
