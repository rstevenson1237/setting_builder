C.42 **Cistern Chamber** (medium) - *Old Digging, Old Water*
An old cistern cut by the ravine's first builders serves this claw for water, deep enough in the dark to lose a footing in.
*Cut stone chamber ten yards across, active, cistern sunk below the surrounding floor, water fouled by whatever's washed into it rather than kept clean.*
**Deep Water:** The cistern itself runs deeper than it looks from the rim, and stepping in without testing the drop first rather than easing down costs whoever's careless (Test of Fate, Held).
**Exits:** natural gap, only way -> C.40 The Chittering Nest
