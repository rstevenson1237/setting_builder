C.42 **Cistern Chamber** (medium) - *Deep Drop, Fouled, Old Cut*
Gut cords hung with split bones, the **bone rattles**, are strung at shin height across a half-fallen dressed-stone hall toward the broken parapet of a **cistern shaft** that reeks of rot. On the shaft's far side a narrow **ledge slab** runs under a wall niche where a green **bronze tablet** is set.
*A hall 30 feet by 15, cut by the old builders around a cistern and ruined since, the eastern half of its vault fallen into a rubble slope that climbs to the fissure out, and its surviving courses cracked. The Khughik of the Old Cistern use it to put away their dead, dropping them down the shaft. Every rattle cord runs along the foot of the south wall into a crack. The rot smell rises from the shaft, and a drip falls into it from a fist-wide hole in the vault above the west end.*
**Bone Rattles:** A dozen gut cords strung between pegs at shin height fill the floor between the rubble slope and the parapet, each hung with split bones that clatter loud enough to be heard in the nest above at any foot or pole that catches one, and the Khughik restring any cord that snaps.
**Cistern Shaft:** At the west end a round shaft ten feet across drops forty feet behind a broken parapet cut with the Old Tongue letters Tathsiq, open black water on its south side and a silted stone floor under a hand of water on its north, where the warren's dead float face down with an ear cut from each.
**Ledge Slab:** A slab a stride wide overhangs the north side of the shaft beneath the tablet's niche, a clean gap showing all round its edges where the parapet is missing, and any weight on its outer half tips it on a hidden pivot and throws whoever stands there onto the silted stone forty feet below (Test of Constitution, 3d, Crushing).
**Bronze Tablet:** Leaded into the niche above the ledge, a bronze tablet a forearm square is cut with Old Tongue lines worn soft with green corrosion below a tide mark and sharp above it (Lore: Cistern Tablet).
**Needle Lip:** A pinhole sits in the niche's lower lip beneath the tablet's leading, and prying the tablet from its lead -> a bronze needle springs from the pinhole into the prying hand (Test of Constitution, 1d, Piercing).
**Tie-Off Peg:** Where the rattle cords end in the south wall's crack, every one is knotted round a single wooden peg jammed into it, and pulling the peg -> the whole rattle line drops slack and silent to the floor.
**Exits:** natural fissure in the rock above the top of the rubble slope, climbing up, its mouth jammed by a fallen facing block with a Khughik-wide gap beside it, passable by a person only once the block is prised or heaved clear by three backs together, east end -> C.40 The Chittering Nest
