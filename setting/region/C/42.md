C.42 **Cistern Chamber** (medium) - *Deep Drop, Fouled, Old Cut*
A ruined cistern hall where a narrow ledge rings a black drop, a **trip-cord** strung across the ledge with a row of worn **handholds** in the wall above it. A slate **tablet** lies at the far end of the ledge.
*Thirty feet square under a seven-foot ceiling, a four-foot ledge running the north and east sides around a twenty-foot pit that drops thirty feet to foul water, the south and west ledge fallen into it. It held the hall's water and is now where the Old Cistern Khughik drop their dead, the rot rising off the pit to the ledge. The climb arrives in the northwest corner and the tablet lies at the northeast.*
**Trip-Cord:** Hemp cord at ankle height across the north ledge, tin sheep-bells hung above it, a foot through it -> the bells clang up the shaft.
**Handholds:** Row of hand-sized pockets worn into the north wall above the cord, a climber working along them -> passes over the cord and the bells stay still.
**Tipping Flag:** Flagstone in the ledge before the tablet, a hair-line seam on three sides, a step onto it -> it tips and the stepper goes into the drop (Test of Constitution, 3d, Crushing).
**Tablet:** Slate tablet lime-fused to the parapet ledge at the northeast corner, Tathsiq cut along the rim beside it, scratched lines across its face, scraped free with a blade (Lore: Cistern Tablet).
**Tablet Seat:** Iron-framed seat under the lime crust, a sprung lath beneath the frame, the tablet pried up -> the lath snaps a spike across the prying hand (Test of Constitution, 1d, Piercing).
**Exits:** Climb - iron rungs leaded into the shaft wall, lowest two rusted thin, capped by a ceiling hatch that turns only to a fitted crank, northwest corner -> C.40 The Chittering Nest
