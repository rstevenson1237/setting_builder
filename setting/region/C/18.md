C.18 **The Rubble Choke** (low) - *partial collapse, narrow squeeze, dead end*
Old rockfall narrows this passage to a crawling squeeze before it stops dead against a wall of collapsed stone entirely. Nothing about it looks worth the effort of getting through.
*A choked passage roughly 10 feet deep, narrowing from shoulder-width to barely a crawl before the final blockage; smells of undisturbed dust and, faintly, of something long dead, and stays dead silent even by Ingaraz's usual standard.*
**Blockage:** A boot sole, cracked and old, just visible where the rubble meets the floor is easy to miss without a light held low and close. Clearing the stone around it (real digging time, not a roll) turns up a trapper's remains, gear, and the rest of the trapline crew's missing partner - dead of the collapse itself, not of anything that lives here (Quest: The Trapper's Partner).
**Exits:** narrow squeeze, rubble-choked, north -> C.17 The Empty Antechamber
