C.18 **Sipelu** (low) - *lower work, silt, remains*
A wide low room standing **thigh-deep** in water with a floor of soft black **silt** underfoot rather than flags, and the shapes of **fallen stone** breaking the surface in a rough line across the middle.
*A room 50 by 35 feet, ceiling 9 feet, under about 30 inches of water over two feet of silt - so the wading is genuinely hard, and every step comes up black. It was a lower workroom and its floor has not been seen in four hundred years. It stinks, properly, of rotted organic silt, and it is the only room in Siwapelu that smells of decay rather than salt.*
**Silt:** Two feet of soft black silt over the flags, which makes crossing this room cost twice what it looks like it should and makes anything dropped in it gone. It is also a record: four hundred years of what the fen has washed in through the breach, laid down in order, and anything that came in has ended up in here.
**Fallen Stone:** A line of collapsed vault blocks breaking the surface across the room's middle, which a party will naturally use as stepping stones and which are the only footing here that is not silt. They are stable. Standing on them is also standing in the open in a room with two flooded arches, which is worth a thought.
**Turned Silt:** Along the north wall the silt has been turned over and dug through in a stretch about fifteen feet long, recently enough that the disturbed black has not settled level. Somebody has been working this room over, thoroughly, and they were not looking for treasure - the digging is in a line rather than in patches, the way you search for an edge or an opening rather than for a thing.
**Exits:** flooded corbelled arch in the south wall, sill under water at all tides -> C.17 Kireath, flooded corbelled arch in the north wall, water moving out through it -> C.20 Nessekirel
