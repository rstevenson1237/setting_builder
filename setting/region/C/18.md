C.18 **Sleeping Hall** (medium) - *Furs, Off-Watch, Tribe Line*
A long hall of dressed stone runs on past an **old lintel** into a lower, rougher **sagging dug end** propped with split timbers, and along both walls of the dressed half five **Gorzgur sleepers** lie snoring in heaps of mangy furs. Dirt trickles steadily from between the props at the far end.
*A hall 60 feet long, the east 40 feet dressed stone 15 feet wide under a sound cut roof, the west 20 feet raw digging where the Gorzgur ran it on past the old cutting, the furs mouldering and the props splitting. The smell of old fur and sweat comes from the sleepers, and the trickle of dirt comes from the dug end.*
**Sagging Dug End:** Past the old lintel the earth roof sags between split timber props, the nearest prop bowed and dark with damp and more dirt falling the longer anyone moves beneath it -> anyone moving quickly under it shakes down a sheet of earth that buries whatever lies on the floor there and cuts the dug end, with its ladder, off from the dressed half until it is dug through, while the dressed half, whose cut stone carries its own roof, takes none of it.
**Gorzgur Sleepers:** Five off-watch warriors lie snoring in heaps of furs along both walls of the dressed half with weapons laid by their heads, and a shout, a fall of earth or a horn from the Warband Hall brings them up (Drowsy, 5, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Old Lintel:** The last dressed lintel before the digging is cut with the bind-rune and beneath it the old angular letters QATKUT, the last three letters cut deeper and cleaner than the first three.
**Exits:** screen of stitched furs hung from pegs across a rough breach in the dressed wall, east wall -> C.12 Warband Hall, notched log ladder standing in the dug end's far corner up through a hole to a flat stone lid that one person cannot shift and two pushing from the ladder barely can, west end -> C.19 Mess Hall
