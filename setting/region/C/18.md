C.18 **Sleeping Hall** (medium) - *Tribal Warren, Tribe Line*
Rows of furs and bedding fill a long dug hall, a handful of the warband sleeping off the last watch in whatever corner they claimed first.
*Dug hall thirty yards long, active, furs and bedding in loose rows rather than ranks, the tribe line plain where dressed stone gives way to this rougher digging.*
**Off-Duty Warband:** A half-dozen Gorzgur sleep here between watches, waking to a fight rather than a question the instant anyone not already known crosses the tribe line into the hall (Waking, 6, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Kept Furs:** Bedding and personal effects piled beside each sleeper include a few things worth more than the rest, easy to take from someone still sleeping and considerably harder from someone awake (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** fitted door, west wall -> C.12 Warband Hall, natural gap, east wall -> C.19 Mess Hall
