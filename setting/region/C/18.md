C.18 **The Watch-Stone Chamber** (high) - *dormant, sentinel, ancient*
A humanoid figure of fused river-stone stands motionless at the center of this chamber, spiral chisel-marks running the length of its arms. Whatever it was built to guard rests untouched on a low stone shelf behind it, exactly as the Marrgath left it.
*A round chamber roughly 30 feet across, dressed stone walls carved with the same spiral motif found throughout the older passages, ceiling raised to a shallow dome nine feet up. The cold here is Marrgath-deep, and the silence is total enough that a held breath feels loud.*
**Bound Sentinel:** the fused-stone guardian standing at the chamber's center (Named Creature: The Bound Sentinel), inert and motionless - close study shows a shallow handprint worn smooth into the stone shelf behind it, matched by a corresponding hollow in the sentinel's own upraised palm, with a single unbroken groove scored into the floor connecting the two. Pressing a hand into the sentinel's palm hollow first, before touching anything on the shelf, keeps it dormant while the shelf's contents are taken; touching the shelf without doing so, or getting the sequence backward, wakes the sentinel, which attacks with full force and does not stop until the disturbance is corrected or it is destroyed.
**Guarded Shelf:** a low stone shelf against the chamber's far wall, holding whatever the Marrgath thought worth guarding down here, undisturbed for longer than anyone left alive can account for (Treasure V, d20).
**Exits:** archway, back into the spiral-carved hall -> C.17 The Spiral Archway Hall
