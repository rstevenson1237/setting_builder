C.18 **Sleeping Hall** (medium) - *Furs, Off-Watch, Tribe Line*
A long hall of dressed stone and rough digging where mouldering **furs** lie in rows with **sleepers** among them, a cracked **lintel** sags over the aisle, and a **seam** crosses the floor beneath a cut **word**.
*The hall is 45 feet by 20 under a 7-foot ceiling in its dressed half and 8 in its dug half, the household's sleeping room, long past its best and still used. Damp stinks from the furs, and stones tick as they shift under the lintel.*
**Sleepers:** Six off-watch Gorzgur sleep on the pallets nearest the arch, spears racked at their heads, paint smeared on the furs (Drowsy, 6, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Furs:** Some twenty pallets of matted fur lie in rows, green with mould in the wet corners and bare where the sleepers lie.
**Lintel:** A cracked lintel sags over the aisle on a stall-post prop -> moving quickly beneath drops rubble, costing noise and a turn of clearing, the dug half stands clear.
**Seam:** Dressed floor stops at a straight seam and rough digging begins, a rival's mark scratched out on both sides of it.
**Word:** Qatkut is cut deep in old letters over the arch, gone soft with damp, a Gorzgur tally scratched beneath.
**Exits:** fixed iron grille of square-cut bars scaled with rust, one gap wide enough to turn through sideways, southeast corner -> C.12 Warband Hall, open arch of dressed stone with a chipped keystone and a sill worn hollow, west wall -> C.19 Mess Hall
