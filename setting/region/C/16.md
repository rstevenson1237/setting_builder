C.16 **Prisoner Hold** (high) - *Captive, Silence, Ransom*
A cramped old-cut cell hall where a **captive** sits chained on a stone cot and a **jailer** gnaws a bone by the shaft foot. Tally marks cover the wall slab behind the cot, and a pale **mat** of growth furs the aisle to it.
*The hold is 25 feet by 15 under a 6-foot ceiling, an old-cut cell the Gorzgur once filled with ransom prisoners and no longer keep, and no sound carries out of it. A chain clinks whenever the captive shifts.*
**Captive:** A man in torn Greywatch mail sits on the cot's stone slab, lips cracked, his water bowl dry and a bare heel scabbed under an ankle iron.
**Fetter:** The ankle iron is chained to a wall ring by a fist-sized keyed lock, its keyhole worn bright -> the fetter key opens it (Keys: Fetter Key).
**Jailer:** Rroghik, a runt Gorzgur in a stained red band, sits on a stool at the shaft foot gnawing a bone, gnawed bones piled at his feet (Wary, 1, Bestiary : Gorzgur) (Named Creature: Rroghik).
**Scratchings:** Tally lines and a crude stair are gouged into the wall slab fused to the living rock behind the cot, a place-name cut at the stair's foot (Lore: Prisoner's Scratchings).
**Sentries:** Five Khughik perch along the slab's ledge chattering, the dust around them unmarked by any other feet (Twitchy, 5, Bestiary : Khughik).
**Mat:** A pale fungal mat furs the aisle wall to the slab and narrows it to shoulder width -> squeezing past with a full pack tears it and looses spores (Test of Constitution, 1d, Poison).
**Exits:** none out, the fixed climb from C.13 Chieftain's Chamber comes down a round ceiling shaft on riveted iron rungs, the lowest four sawn off flush, southwest corner
