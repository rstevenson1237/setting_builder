C.16 **Nesseora** (medium) - *collapse, subsidence, footing*
Half a room. The northern side stands as dressed courses under a sound vault; the southern side has **dropped** some six feet where the ground beneath it gave way, taking the floor, the wall and part of the ceiling with it into a **slumped mass** of broken stone. The break is old, and the **fresh cracks** in the standing half are not.
*A room that was 40 by 30 feet and is now about 40 by 16 with a rubble slope where the rest was, ceiling 9 feet over the standing half and open broken vault over the slope. It was a plain storeroom off the second course. The sound here is wrong even for Siwapelu: the broken vault swallows noise instead of carrying it, so a party crossing this room cannot hear the rest of the complex and the rest of the complex cannot hear them.*
**Slumped Mass:** A slope of broken vault stone and floor flags dropping six feet to the south, loose, with voids under it. It is crossable and it moves when crossed. Anything a party sends down it dislodges more, and the whole southern half of the room is a Damaging fall for anyone who takes it at a run - ordinary damage, no test, and a real chance of being under something afterwards.
**Fresh Cracks:** In the standing north wall, three vertical cracks running from the springing of the vault down to the floor, with clean stone faces and dust still lying at their feet. They are recent, they are widening, and they are the region's subsidence made visible - the same subsidence that dropped the eastern water three winters ago is still going on, and this is where a party can see it happening rather than be told about it.
**Wedged Block:** Near the top of the slope, a single dressed block the size of a chest sits jammed between two others with a gap beneath it, and something has been packed into the gap and left. A party that shifts the block - one action, and it brings a good deal of the slope down behind it - finds what was cached (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** corbelled arch in the east wall, sound, with running water audible beyond -> C.11 Siwakire, corbelled arch in the north wall, its cap cracked through but standing -> C.13 Teruimmo
