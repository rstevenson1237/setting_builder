C.16 **The Echoing Vault** (low) - *vast, hollow, connective*
This natural cavern opens up far larger than anything else in the workings, its true ceiling lost somewhere overhead in the dark. Every small sound returns as a long, hollow echo.
*A large natural chamber roughly 40 by 35 feet, ceiling height indeterminate beyond the reach of torchlight. The air is markedly colder and stiller than the tunnels leading in, and even quiet footsteps echo noticeably.*
**Echoing Dark:** the vault's sheer scale swallows light and amplifies sound in equal measure - a reminder that the Concern's crude tunnels are a small, recent addition to something far older and far larger.
**Exits:** shaft bottom, climbing up -> C.15 Broken Ladder Shaft, passage, opposite side of the vault -> C.17 The Spiral Archway Hall
