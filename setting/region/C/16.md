C.16 **Prisoner Hold** (high) - *Captive, Silence, Ransom*
A bottle-shaped cell of dressed stone widens below a sagging **rope net** hung under the chute's mouth, its floor strewn with rotted straw around a pale **flowstone crust** in the middle. A ragged **captive** in fetters sits against the far wall, and a low **niche** beside him is choked with a grey, furred **spore mat** where a dead Khughik lies half grown over.
*An oubliette cut from the old stone, 25 feet across at the floor and 14 feet to a neck five feet wide where the chute enters, left to itself but for whoever is dropped in, its straw rotted to a black mulch that feeds the mat and gives the room its mushroom smell. No other way out is cut, and the chute is climbed only on a rope made fast above.*
**Captive:** A Greywatch soldier in a torn tabard sits fettered by one ankle to an iron ring in the west wall, hoarse, scratching at the floor with a bone, and the fetter is closed by a heavy iron barrel lock with a square keyhole that the bone has scored but not opened (Desperate, 1, Bestiary : Greywatch Regular) (Keys: Fetter Key).
**Spore Mat:** A grey fungus mat as thick as a fist fills the mouth of the low niche in the east wall and creeps a stride onto the floor, the dead Khughik lying face-down in it, and it puffs and spreads a little further each time it is disturbed -> moving quickly anywhere within a stride of it bursts a cloud of spores from the mat that fills the throat (Test of Constitution, 2d, Poison).
**Scratched Niche:** Behind the spore mat the niche's back wall is scratched over in Common letters and lines around a crude plan, a single pale cave spider sitting at the centre of an unbroken web strung across it, and copying it means standing in the niche for as long as that takes (Lore: Prisoner's Scratchings).
**Flowstone Crust:** In the middle of the floor a ring of pale flowstone two strides across rings hollow underfoot, a hairline crack running round its edge where it meets the dressed floor -> it bears a person alone but breaks under anyone carrying a full pack or another person, dropping them ten feet down the blind drain shaft it has grown over onto broken stone (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Rope Net:** Under the chute mouth a net of knotted rope rotted grey hangs sagging from four iron rings in the neck of the ceiling -> whoever drops down the chute lands in it and hangs tangled until cut free or until it tears and drops them onto the straw, and whatever they held loose falls through the mesh first.
**Exits:** none - the chute from the Chieftain's Chamber only drops into this cell, fourteen feet up and too smooth and steep to climb, and no other way out is cut
