C.16 **The Whispering Crack** (medium) - *fissure in stone, cold air, unsettling voice*
A narrow fissure breathes a thin stream of cold air, and faintly, what sounds like a single **voice** speaking just below understanding.
*The fissure is too narrow to pass through; the voice is a remnant, not a presence that can act, and answers only what's asked aloud nearby.*
**Fissure:** Cold air and a faint voice; asking a question aloud near the crack yields a true but fragmentary answer about Vael's fall or the barrow's layout, offered freely.
