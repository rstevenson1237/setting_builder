C.16 **The Cold Draft Passage** (low) - *cold air moving the wrong way, sealed passage nearby, unexplained draft*
Cold air moves steadily against what should be the natural flow here, coming from somewhere overhead rather than ahead. Broken stone and fresh dust litter the floor beneath a ragged gap in the ceiling.
*A plain passage roughly 15 feet long, ceiling broken open on one end where something above gave way; smells of raw stone dust freshly settled, and the draft is cold enough to notice against skin even briefly exposed.*
**Break Above:** Whatever fell through the ceiling gap did so recently and from some height - there's no climbing back up it without a rope from above, and nothing here to anchor one.
**Exits:** low passage, cold draft, east -> C.12 The Silent Passage
