C.16 **Prisoner Hold** (high) - *Killing Roll, Silent Reach*
A bare cell reached only by the chute above holds whoever Vraz hasn't yet decided a price for, no sound at all carrying up out of it.
*Bare cut cell twelve yards across, active, no sound carrying up the chute at all, cold enough that anyone held here without a cloak is worse for it by the day.*
**Chute Guard:** A single Gorzgur posted at the chute's base answers to Vraz alone, keeping whoever's held here alive rather than harmed unless a rescue's actually attempted (Watching, 1, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Captive:** Whoever's currently held here is a scout caught scouting the household's own hoard, worth more ransomed than killed if word can reach whoever sent them.
**Confiscated Effects:** What little the captive carried when taken sits in a heap by the guard's own stool, unclaimed and unsorted (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** none - the chute above admits no way back up unaided
