C.16 **The Cold Draft Passage** (low) - *cold air moving the wrong way, sealed passage nearby, unexplained draft*
Cold air moves steadily against what should be the natural flow here, coming from somewhere overhead rather than ahead. Broken stone and fresh dust litter the floor beneath a ragged gap in the ceiling.
*A plain passage roughly 15 feet long, ceiling broken open at one end; cold draft flows from the Break Above, moving against the passage's natural pull.*
**Break Above:** Recent, high fall; unclimbable without a rope anchored from above.
