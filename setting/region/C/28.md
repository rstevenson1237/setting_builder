C.28 **Grave Pit** (medium) - *Bound Service, Grave Niche*
Hukkgur's own dead go into a shared pit rather than a sealed recess, covered over rather than closed properly.
*Rough pit fifteen yards across, active, bodies covered rather than buried deep, air here thicker and worse than anywhere else in the household's own halls.*
**Foul Air:** Air over the pit itself sits heavier than it should, thick enough that lingering over it rather than passing through costs whoever breathes it too long (Test of Constitution, 1d, Poison).
**Exits:** natural gap, east wall -> C.27 Stores
