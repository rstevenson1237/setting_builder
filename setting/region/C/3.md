C.3 **Market Square** - *Walled City, Market Bell*
Velar's market runs busy for its size, every stall posting the detour's markup openly. The **market bell** rings dawn trade open, a different note from any cart's brake-bell.
*A stone-paved square eighty feet across, ringed by shuttered stalls that unfold each morning, painted shutters marking whichever household can afford them. Smells of hot food and river fish, and both bells carry across it at once, most mornings.*
**Market Bell:** Rung at dawn, from a post at the square's centre, to open trade.
**Posted Prices:** Every stall posts its detour markup, on a board, a fifth over the standard rate (Treasure IV, d20).
**River Fish Stalls:** The one stock not marked up, never having crossed the ford, fresh and cheap.
