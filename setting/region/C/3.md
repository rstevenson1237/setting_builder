C.3 **Doraiel** (low) - *passage, groove, echo*
A plain connecting passage of dressed limestone with nothing in it, running between three arches, its floor carrying a **shallow drainage channel** down the centre and a **second groove** beside it that is not drainage and is polished.
*A passage 40 by 6 feet, ceiling 9 feet, the standard section for the first course. Its purpose is circulation and nothing else, then and now. The drainage channel down the centre catches a boot and it is the first place most parties trip; the sound of that carries the length of the complex and arrives somewhere else entirely.*
**Drainage Channel:** The standard 6-inch channel cut down the centre of every passage in Siwapelu, dry on this course, and a genuine trip hazard for anyone moving quickly. Which way the channel falls is worth reading - here it falls west, toward the cistern.
**Second Groove:** Running beside the drainage channel on its north side, a band about eighteen inches wide where the flags are polished smooth and dished a little. It is faint here and a party may take it for wear. It is the same groove that is six inches deep at Peluath two courses down, and a party that notices it here and recognises it there has been rewarded for looking at a room that offered them nothing.
**Exits:** corbelled arch in the east wall -> C.2 Selora, corbelled arch in the west wall, cap carrying the threshold character -> C.5 Peluora, narrow corbelled arch in the north wall, noticeably lower than the standard section -> C.4 Ranuora
