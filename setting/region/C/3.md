C.3 **First Niche Row** (medium) - *burial alcoves, disturbed dust, rising dead*
A row of shallow **burial alcoves** lines both walls here, thick with undisturbed dust - until it isn't.
*A rectangular chamber roughly 20 by 15 feet, lined with burial alcoves, unchanged from its original purpose; thick, undisturbed dust covers everything, and the alcoves here run deeper than elsewhere in the barrow, wide enough for more than one occupant apiece - an irregularity worth noticing. Disturbing two or more niches (searching, looting, or careless passage) causes their occupants to rise; they bar the way rather than pursue far beyond this room.*
**Niche Row:** Disturbing it wakes two Drowned Skeletons (Bestiary); a deliberate searcher turns up something buried in the dust (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** low stone passage -> C.2 Silted Passage, low stone passage -> C.4 Cracked Antechamber, low stone passage -> C.5 Listening Alcove
