C.3 **The Warren** (Medium) - *crowded, filthy, noisy*
A wide cavern ringed with **alcoves** of rags and straw, smoke hanging under the roof. Goblins sleep in **heaps**; a **barricade** of planks closes the north end, and a **tally** is cut into a post.
*Cavern 50 ft across, 12 alcoves. Ten goblins sleep and gamble here; the rest of the tribe is out raiding or hiding. A shout carries to the chief's hall in a round.*
**Alcoves:** Straw and rag nests, two goblins to an alcove. Scavenged goods lie under the straw (Treasure I, d20).
**Sleepers:** Ten goblins in heaps, a few awake gambling with bones (Hostile, 10, Bestiary : Goblin).
**Barricade:** A plank door lashed shut at the north end, barred from this side.
**Tally Post:** Scored with fourteen red notches, twelve cut through. Empty sacks stamped with a white tower lie stacked beside it.
**Exits:** crude, east wall, wide gap -> C.2 The Carved Hall, open, south end, low gap -> C.4 The Midden and Stores, wooden, north end, plank barricade -> C.6 The Chief's Hall
