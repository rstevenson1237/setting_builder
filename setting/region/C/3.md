C.3 **First Niche Row** (medium) - *burial alcoves, disturbed dust, rising dead*
A row of shallow **burial alcoves** lines both walls here, thick with undisturbed dust - until it isn't.
*Disturbing two or more niches (searching, looting, or careless passage) causes their occupants to rise; they bar the way rather than pursue far beyond this room.*
**Niche Row:** Disturbing it wakes two Drowned Skeletons (Bestiary); a handful of worn coin lies among the dust for a deliberate searcher.
**Exits:** passage -> C.2 Silted Passage, passage -> C.4 Cracked Antechamber, passage -> C.5 Listening Alcove
