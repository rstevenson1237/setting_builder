C.3 **Market Square** - *Walled City, Market Bell*
Velar's market runs busier than its size explains, every stall marked with the detour's stated markup rather than hiding it. The **market bell** rings dawn trade open, distinct from any Wagons cart's own brake-bell.
*A stone-paved square eighty feet across, ringed by shuttered stalls that unfold each morning, painted shutters marking whichever household can afford them. Smells of hot food and river fish; both bells - market and cart - carry across it at once most mornings.*
**Market Bell:** Rung at dawn from a post at the square's center to open trade; nobody has ever tried ringing it any other time.
**Posted Prices:** Every stall states its detour markup openly on a board rather than in the asking - roughly a fifth over the standard rate on ordinary goods (Treasure IV, d20), and nobody haggles it down.
**River Fish Stalls:** The one stock not marked up, since it never crossed the ford to begin with - fresh, cheap, and the actual reason locals still shop here daily.
