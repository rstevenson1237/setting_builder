C.3 **Overseer's Cubby** (low) - *cramped, papered, abandoned*
A small alcove off the landing holds a collapsed desk and a scatter of water-ruined papers, the old overseer's post long abandoned. Nothing here has been touched in years.
*A cramped room roughly 8 by 6 feet, ceiling low enough to duck under in places, walls lined with rotted shelving. The smell of mildewed paper is the strongest thing here, undercut by damp stone.*
**Ruined Desk:** a collapsed writing desk against the back wall, its papers long since turned to illegible pulp - whatever records the overseer once kept here are unrecoverable.
**Exits:** low passage, back to the landing -> C.2 Windlass Landing
