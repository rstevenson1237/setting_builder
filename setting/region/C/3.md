C.3 **The Mushroom Pits** (Low) - *seep, fungus, damp*
A long cavern whose wet ledges step down toward the far end, every one of them lined with **mushroom beds** of pale caps. Water drips constantly from the roof, and a **sack** lies half hidden on the lowest ledge.
*The makers' drainage cut, where the seep from the rock collects. The goblins' food grows here, and at the far end a worn stair leads down to Thono, the makers' word for the shaft-place.*
**Mushroom Beds:** Pale caps crowd every ledge in wet black soil, the ones nearest the path trodden flat.
**Seep:** Water beads on the roof and runs down the ledges to a channel at the cavern's centre.
**Sack:** A sack lies among the fungus on the lowest ledge, where the ground is driest (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open slope, up the slope -> C.1 The Watch Passage, archway, deep end, worn stair -> C.6 Thono
