C.3 **Rite Chamber** (high) - *Altar, Smoke, Speaking Dead*
Sweet, heavy smoke hangs thick around a stone altar at the chamber's own centre, and a cowled shape rises from behind it the instant torchlight crosses the threshold.
*Cut stone chamber twenty-five yards across, active, channels cut into the floor running from the altar to a drain at the north wall, smoke thick enough to sting eyes unused to it.*
**Altar:** Channels cut into the floor run from the altar's own basin to a drain in the north wall, stained darker at the basin's edge than anywhere further out, and an offering left rather than taken from the basin is answered with silence while taking from it instead costs the taker (Test of Sanity, 1d).
**Shrine Wraith:** A cowled shape a torch's light passes through rather than off rises from behind the altar, and speaks first in Grozzgur to whoever hasn't already drawn a blade (Speaking, 1, Bestiary : Shrine Wraith).
**Rite Ledger:** A ledger of cut bone tallies rite-nights kept at the altar going back further than any household now holds ground, in the same hand throughout despite the years it must have taken (Lore: Rite Ledger).
**Exits:** archway, west wall -> C.2 Tribute Hall, fitted door, east wall -> C.4 The Cowled Hall, natural gap, south wall -> C.7 Grave Niche of the Faithful
