C.3 **The Digger's Cut** (medium) - *fresh timber shoring, defended dig, fenced-buyer relics*
Fresh-cut timber shores up a working excavation at **The Digger's Cut**, tools laid out in neat rows for a crew that means to be back any moment. A spiral scratched into the stone at knee height marks it as theirs.
*A widened natural fissure roughly 15 by 10 feet, braced with new-sawn props along both walls, floor churned to loose spoil; smells of raw timber and turned earth over the barrow field's usual cold stone.*
**Digger's Crew:** A work party of the Diggers holds this cut, fast and thorough and gone before anyone can organize a response to interference (see setting/Factions.md). Noticed, they simply relocate; injured, they abandon subtlety and take what they can carry.
**Crated Relics:** Half-crated grave goods sit ready to move, foundation-stone carvings and worked metal bound for a fenced buyer off the border (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** braced fissure, timber props, west -> C.15 The Root-Broken Gallery, narrow cut, loose spoil, east -> C.13 The Old Foundation Course
