C.3 **The Silt Bar** (landmark) - *Shifting, Exposed, Treacherous*
The **Silt Bar** is a wide, firm sandbar breaking the channel's surface, littered with whatever the current has carried down and dropped. Its shape is never quite the same from one season to the next.
*A bar sixty yards long southwest of the Weir Platforms, firm sand and packed silt above the waterline; gulls work it after every flood, and a low reek of rot follows the wash-up line.*
**Wash-Up Line:** Driftwood, bone, and river-junk collect along the bar's downstream edge after every flood (Treasure I, d20).
**Soft Patches:** Firm sand hides soft silt in patches; a careless step sinks to the knee (Nuisance).
**Timber Ribs:** Old, silt-crusted ribs of something wooden break the surface at the bar's far end, mostly buried.
**Exits:** open water, northeast -> C.2 The Weir Platforms, the buried timber ribs, far end -> C.8 The Wreck
