C.3 **The Hermit's Clearing** (Lair) - *quiet, tended, strange*
A **turf hut** stands inside a thorn fence, bean rows ranged before it, a well at its side. A bell on the gate sounds when it opens.
*Clearing 60 ft across. Ansel keeps the thorn gate shut.*
**Turf Hut:** Herbs hang drying from the rafters; a party that brings news may sleep under the roof.
**Ansel the Hermit:** Tends his beans until the gate bell sounds, then asks for news of the realm before he will answer a question. He nurses Poisoned or Diseased through a night for news (Named Creature: Ansel the Hermit).
