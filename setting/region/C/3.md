C.3 **The Mural Wall** (Medium) - *carved, faded, climbing*

Twelve carved panels run along a wall, their paint worn thin and a row of niches below. Stone ticks as it cools.

*A hall 60 feet long and 20 feet wide, the panels set 5 feet up. The story they tell is the cynidiceans' own, ending at a stair.*

**The Panels:** Twelve carvings numbered with cynidicean numerals, the figures' faces scraped away on the last six (The Demon Is Invited). Read in order they show a city raised, a veiled guest, kneeling priests and masked soldiers. A party that reads them in order sees a hidden stair swing open in the wall behind.
**The Niche:** Under the seventh panel, a hollow holds a rolled hide inscribed in the cynidicean script (Lore: The Story of the Panels).
**The Wall:** Carved sandstone with a hand-and-footholds pattern up to the roof, where an old lamp hangs.

**Exits:** an archway -> C.2 The Tile Floor, a stone passage -> C.4 The Plinth
