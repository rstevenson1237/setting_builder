C.3 **The Hunters' Beach Camp** (Lair) - *tented, ordered, watched*
Ordered rows of canvas tents stand above a chalked beach, sentries watching at posts, a ship's boat drawn up and a leader surveying with a chain.
*500 by 400 feet. The treasure hunters survey the beach for a way to the pyramid and trade news for news.*
**The surveyors:** eight treasure hunters in grey canvas coats with brass buttons and a copper compass on a chain, with crossbows (Neutral, 8, Bestiary : Treasure Hunter); they greet a party as rivals and trade news.
**Aldous Crane:** the leader, clipped and driven, wants the deserter and the half-map he took. Crane knows a surveyed route to the eastern jungle's edge.
**The tents and stores:** canvas rows with tinned stores from home, and a ship's boat drawn up on the sand.
