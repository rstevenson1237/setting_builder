C.11 **The Second Mound Mouth** (low) - *second entrance, narrower approach, less traveled*
A second, tighter mound-mouth breaks the surface some distance from the first, its approach overgrown enough that finding it at all takes knowing to look.
*A narrow cutting barely 4 feet wide, roofed with old lintel-stone rather than open sky; smells muskier and more sealed than the outer path, and the air inside stays notably stiller.*
**Overgrown Approach:** Bramble and old growth have closed in over the cutting's mouth, undisturbed enough to say this entrance sees far less traffic than the other.
**Exits:** narrow cutting, old lintel, down -> C.15 The Root-Broken Gallery
