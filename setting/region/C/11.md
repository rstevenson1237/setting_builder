C.11 **Sentry Gate** (low) - *Rival Chaos, Sentry Horn*
A horn hangs ready beside the gate proper, answered from deeper in before whoever's arriving is even seen.
*Cut-and-dug junction fifteen yards across, active, the transition from old dressed stone to Gorzgur's own rougher digging plain underfoot at the threshold itself.*
**Sentry Horn:** A horn hangs from a peg beside the gate, sounded by whoever's posted here and answered from deeper in before the sentry's even placed the caller.
**Loose Seam:** A seam low in the rock wall beside the horn-peg sits slightly proud of the stone around it, and working it free with a blade's point opens a shallow gap holding whatever one sentry's skimmed and never reported to the household's own hoard (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** natural gap, west wall -> C.30 Silt Hollow Entrance, natural gap, east wall -> C.39 Old Cistern Entrance, archway, south wall -> C.12 Warband Hall
