C.11 **The Grave-Goods Row** (medium) - *worked coral, old coin, guarded wealth*
A short row of burial niches here holds worked **coral jewelry** and old coin, richer than anything found in the barrow's outer chambers.
*Three niches along one wall; searching them thoroughly enough to find the wealth risks waking whatever rests in the fullest one.*
**Grave-Goods:** Worked coral jewelry and old coin; a careful, quiet search takes it without incident, but a prolonged or careless one risks waking a Drowned Skeleton (Bestiary) in the fullest niche.
**Exits:** passage -> C.9 Sunken Hall
