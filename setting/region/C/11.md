C.11 **Sentry Gate** (low) - *Horn, Skim, Junction*
A square chamber of dressed stone where four ways meet, its floor coated with a **silt skim** tracked in from the ford, and a lone **sentry** stands on a block beside a **great horn** hung in a wooden stand. Daylight and the noise of the ford come through a gap in the south wall.
*A junction 30 feet square, the Gorzgur's front door, where the way from the Old Ford meets the household's own sluice-channel and the two slab doors into the Khughik warrens, cold with the ford's draught through the south gap.*
**Sentry:** One Gorzgur stands on a stone block by the north wall facing the ford gap with his spear grounded, calls out Gorzgur in challenge at anyone coming through, and blows the horn when the answer is wrong or slow (Alert, 1, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Great Horn:** A war-horn as long as a man's arm hangs in a wooden stand beside the sentry's block, its mouthpiece black with grease, and one blast of it carries up the sluice-channel to the rest of the household.
**Silt Skim:** A skim of grey ford-silt coats the floor and holds every print, broad clawed Gorzgur feet running between the ford gap and the sluice-channel, small bare Khughik feet going to both slab doors and none of them toward the sluice-channel.
**Exits:** squared sluice-channel cut through the dressed wall at stooping height, a hand's depth of cold runoff flowing out of it over worn stone, north wall -> C.12 Warband Hall, slab of dressed stone turning on an iron pin in a low doorway, scraped pale where small hands push it, east wall -> C.30 Silt Hollow Entrance, rough-hewn slab turning on a wooden pin in a narrow dug doorway, green with damp and leaving a wet arc on the floor, west wall -> C.39 Old Cistern Entrance, natural gap in the rock open to the ford, a band of floor two strides deep across it clean of the silt that skims the rest of the floor, the silt stopping at its edge in a straight line and a dead ford-rat lying just short of it, warded so that anyone crossing the band without speaking the word Gorzgur drops as if their legs were cut from under them (Test of Fate, Condition), south wall -> B.5 The Old Ford
