C.11 **Sentry Gate** (low) - *Horn, Skim, Junction*
A square chamber of dressed stone where four ways meet, a lone **sentry** on a stone block by the south arch and a war-**horn** hung on the wall beside him. Daylight and the ford's noise come through the arch.
*The junction is 30 feet square under a 7-foot ceiling, the Gorzgur's front door and guard post, in daily use, cold with the ford's draught. The ford's noise points at the arch.*
**Sentry:** One Gorzgur stands on the block with his spear grounded, facing the arch and calling a challenge in Grozzgur at anyone coming through (Alert, 1, Bestiary : Gorzgur).
**Horn:** A war-horn hangs on the wall beside the block, its mouthpiece black with grease -> one blast brings the six from C.14 in one action.
**Exits:** squared sluice-channel of dressed stone at stooping height, cold runoff a hand deep flowing out over a worn sill, north wall -> C.12 Warband Hall, iron grille of flat bars set in a dug doorway with a gate in it locked by a keyed padlock, east wall -> C.30 Silt Hollow Entrance, hole knocked through the dressed wall with broken edges chipped rough and rubble cleared, west wall -> C.39 Old Cistern Entrance, open arch of dressed stone with an iron-barred gate hung in it and a keyed drop-bolt, its lock plate worn bright on the ford side, south wall -> B.5 The Old Ford
