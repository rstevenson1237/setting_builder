C.11 **The Grave-Goods Row** (medium) - *old grave-goods, hidden wealth, guarded row*
A short row of burial **niches** here holds grave-goods richer than anything found in the barrow's outer chambers.
*A narrow chamber roughly 15 by 10 feet, three burial niches along one wall, richer in grave-goods than anything found in the barrow's outer chambers - an irregularity worth the risk. Searching them thoroughly enough to find the wealth risks waking whatever rests in the fullest one.*
**Grave-Goods:** A careful, quiet search of the niches takes what's here without incident, but a prolonged or careless one risks waking a Drowned Skeleton (Bestiary) in the fullest niche (Treasure III, d20 and Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** wide stone archway -> C.9 Sunken Hall
