C.11 **The Blighted Seam** (high) - *fungal, rot-sweet, lurking*
A thick mat of pale fungus creeps across the walls and floor of this gallery, feeding on something long dead beneath it. Detached **Fingers** twitch faintly among the fungal mat, still hunting long after the rest of what they belonged to stopped moving.
*An irregular gallery roughly 30 by 20 feet, walls and floor thick with pale, faintly luminous fungus growing over old rockfall. The air is warm and cloyingly sweet with rot, thick enough to taste, and a faint clicking sound comes from somewhere within the fungal mat itself.*
**Fungal Mat:** fungus spread across most of the floor and both walls, feeding on the still-scattered remains of Torvin Ashe, a boundary-cutter who crossed a line without a proper blessing years ago - his remains now move independently as a Cutter's Blight, a finger, an ear, and a length of spine each hunting separately for what's left of the whole (Named Creature: Torvin Ashe).
**Old Marker:** a wold-iron cutter's blade against the north wall, corroded but still recognizable, half-swallowed by fungus - the last thing Torvin carried, and the reason his remains still hunt for it specifically.
**Exits:** passage, back the way in through the fungal growth -> C.10 The Cold Passage
