C.8 **The Wave-Cut Arch** (Natural) - *towering, dripping, booming*
A towering sea arch booms with surf, spray falling and grit raining from its roof, rubble heaped on the floor below.
*100 by 200 feet. The arch is on the way along the shore path.*
**The arch roof:** loose stone above the passage, where fresh rubble lies below; a party passing beneath is in its fall (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing).
**The rubble heap:** broken rock piled on the wave-cut floor.
