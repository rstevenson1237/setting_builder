C.8 **The Barrow-Kin Threshold** (medium) - *closed-ring boundary mark, warning before action, ends at the first disturbed grave*
A closed circle cut around a single old-tongue mark is scored into the stone at the mouth of **The Barrow-Kin Threshold** - the same sign the Barrow-Kin use wherever they hold ground beyond the treeline.
*A wide, low passage-chamber roughly 15 feet across, swept noticeably cleaner than the rooms around it; smells faintly of tallow and old flowers rather than dust, and stays quiet in a tended, deliberate way rather than an empty one.*
**Barrow-Kin Watch:** A family hold of the Barrow-Kin wards this stretch of the barrow field against the Diggers and the Grave-Robbers both (see setting/Factions.md). They avoid contact by default and will parley if approached openly and without weapons drawn - but treat any disturbance of a grave-site, here or anywhere in Ingaraz, as an act that ends the conversation for good.
**Moved Marker:** The boundary stone just inside the threshold sits closer to the passage mouth than the worn groove beneath it suggests it once did - the mark of a warning already given once, to somebody who ignored it.
**Exits:** low doorway, closed-ring mark, west -> C.2 The Warded Gatehouse, open passage, swept floor, east -> C.14 The Barrow-Kin Grave-Row
