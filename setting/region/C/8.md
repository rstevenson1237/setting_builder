C.8 **River Docks** - *Old Road, River Ferry*
The one water crossing Velar sees, a ferry working the river below the walls - the only traffic here that never has to think about the Sunk Bridge at all.
*A timber jetty forty feet along the river frontage, downstream of Warehouse Row proper. Smells of river mud and tar; the ferry rope creaks against its post whenever the current takes the barge.*
**Ferryman:** Runs the ferry across and along the river for 1 cn a head, 3 cn a cart - slower than the causeway road but the only way to move goods that never has to cross Brekar's ground at all.
**River Ferry:** A flat barge worked by a single guide-rope strung between two posts; it carries six on foot or one loaded cart at a time.
**Exits:** open yard, south side -> C.2 Warehouse Row, open path, riverside, west side -> beyond, following the river out of Telar's mapped country
