C.8 **The Flooded Cut** (medium) - *submerged, black-watered, cold*
Black water fills the lower half of this gallery, submerging whatever floor and passage continued beyond it. A cold current pulls faintly toward a submerged gap in the far wall, the only way through without swimming blind.
*A flooded gallery roughly 25 by 20 feet, water standing chest-deep at the near end and rising to submerge the ceiling entirely at the far end. The cold is immediate and bone-deep on entry, and the water carries no smell at all, disturbingly still despite the current beneath its surface.*
**Submerged Passage:** the flooded gallery's far end, where the ceiling dips fully underwater - crossing means committing to a cold, blind swim through a narrow submerged gap, with no way to see or breathe until reaching the far side; anyone who panics or lingers too long underwater risks running out of air (TEST OF CONSTITUTION, CONDITION: exhausted) before surfacing.
**Exits:** flooded passage, a cold blind swim through the submerged gap -> C.5 The Ore Cart Siding
