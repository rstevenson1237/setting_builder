C.8 **The Wreck** (hidden) - *Silted, Forgotten, Salvageable*
The **Wreck** is a half-buried boat's ribs and shattered planking, cargo scattered and silted into the bar around it. Whatever it was carrying went down with it, unclaimed since.
*A wrecked hull twenty feet long, broken-backed and silted in place at the Silt Bar's far end; smells of rot and old pitch, timbers soft enough to crumble underfoot.*
**Broken Hull:** Ribs and planking, silted in place; whatever sank it broke its back across the bar.
**Scattered Cargo:** Silted crates and bundles, mostly ruined, some still worth digging out (Treasure IV, d20).
**Claim:** A faded, painted mark on the sternpost matches nothing known at Carrdun - somebody else's boat, from somewhere else.
**Exits:** the silt bar, back -> C.3 The Silt Bar
