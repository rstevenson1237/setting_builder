C.8 **River Docks** - *Old Road, River Ferry*
A ferry works the river below the walls, the one crossing here that never touches the Sunk Bridge. The **ferryman** takes 1 cn a head.
*A timber jetty forty feet long, on the river frontage, downstream of Warehouse Row. Smells of river mud and tar, and the ferry rope creaks against its post, when the current takes the barge.*
**Ferryman:** Runs the ferry across and along the river, 1 cn a head, 3 cn a cart.
**River Ferry:** A flat barge worked by one guide-rope, between two posts, carrying six on foot or one loaded cart.
**Exits:** open yard, south side -> C.2 Warehouse Row, open path, riverside, west side -> beyond, following the river out of Telar's mapped country
