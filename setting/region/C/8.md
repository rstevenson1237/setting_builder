C.8 **Kirel** (low) - *cistern, still, cold*
A round chamber holding a **cistern** of dressed stone sunk into its floor, brim-full of water so still and clear that the bottom eight feet down looks like it is a hand's depth away. A **fill channel** enters at the ceiling and has been dry for centuries.
*A circle 30 feet across, ceiling 9 feet, with the cistern 14 feet across and 8 deep sunk in the middle of it, leaving a walkway of about 8 feet all round. It was the first course's water supply. It is the coldest room on this course and the stillness is the sensory fact - a party's own noise comes back off the water flat and immediate, quite unlike everywhere else here.*
**Cistern:** 14 feet across, 8 deep, full to the brim with water that is clear, cold and entirely clean - drinkable, which makes this the only reliable water in Siwapelu and a genuinely valuable room. The bottom is visible: dressed flags, a drain plug, and nothing else. Refraction makes the depth read as about a foot, and somebody stepping in casually is going straight to the bottom with whatever they are carrying.
**Fill Channel:** A cut channel entering at the ceiling on the north side, dry, its mouth crusted with old mineral deposit. It fed from the surface once. The cistern is nevertheless full, and has stayed full, which means it is being fed from below - and the drain plug at the bottom is seated the wrong way up.
**Blind Corner:** On the room's north-west arc the walkway widens by about three feet into a shallow recess with no apparent purpose, and the wall at its back is dressed stone laid in a slightly different bond from the rest of the chamber. There is fifteen feet of crawl behind it to a store room on the other side, if a party has come the other way and knows.
**Exits:** low corbelled arch in the north wall, its sill dished by water -> C.2 Selora, corbelled arch in the east wall, its sill worn into a shallow dish -> C.7 Nuthorath
