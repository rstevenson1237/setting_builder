C.8 **Stores of the Shrine** (medium) - *Tribal Warren, Household Hoard*
Sacks and sealed jars line the walls of a long storeroom, floor slabs uneven enough underfoot to catch a careless step.
*Cut stone storeroom twenty-two yards long, active, sacks and jars stacked to shoulder height along both walls, the smell of dry grain over older stone dust.*
**Loose Slab:** One floor slab rocks rather than sits flat, rigged beneath to slide and drop whoever's weight lands wrong into the narrow space below rather than to kill them outright (Test of Fate, Impact).
**Shrine Stores:** What the shrine keeps against its own needs sits sacked and jarred rather than scattered, worth carrying out a portion of without anyone here noticing for a season (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** fitted door, north wall -> C.2 Tribute Hall, natural gap, west wall -> C.6 Old Ledger Vault, natural gap, south wall -> C.9 Cistern Reach
