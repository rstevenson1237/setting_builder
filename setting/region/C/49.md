C.49 **The Chittering Nest** (high) - *Stair, Spread Out, Easy Take*
This claw has taken to an old stair rather than a single dug hollow, spread up its own broken length rather than packed into one chamber.
*Dug chamber at the head of an old broken stair, sixteen yards across, active, the stair itself worn to rubble underfoot below the chamber's own floor.*
**Warren:** A dozen or so Khughik share this chamber and the broken stair below it, swarming anything smaller and scattering against anything larger that fights back (Swarming, 11, Bestiary : Khughik).
**Broken Stair:** The stair below the chamber is worn to rubble rather than clean steps, footing uneven enough that anyone hurried on it risks turning an ankle rather than a fall outright (Test of Fate, Impact).
**Claw's Take:** What's been carried up from below sits at the stair's own head rather than carried further in, easiest of any claw's hoard in the region to reach and leave with (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** natural gap, south wall -> C.48 Broken Stair Entrance, natural gap, east wall -> C.50 Store Nook, natural gap, west wall -> C.51 Water Nook, natural gap, north wall -> C.52 Bolt-Hole
