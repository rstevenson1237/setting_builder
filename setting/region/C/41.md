C.41 **Store Nook** (medium) - *Damp Stash, Keeper, Hoard*
Grey mould furs every wet stone of this cramped cellar except the rim of a clear **seep basin** in the corner, ringed on the floor by a line of rat skulls, with a **crusted coffer** showing through its water. Against the far wall the warren's **hoard** is heaped high, and a **peg bundle** pokes out of it.
*A cellar 12 feet by 10, cut square by the old builders as a store and decayed since, its south-east corner caved into loose rubble and its facing blocks furred and sweating. The Khughik of the Old Cistern keep their stores here and Zoghik (C.40) keeps the stores, counting the hoard whenever she wakes and coming through from the nest when she finds it short. The Khughik call the nook Zoghikakh after her, and it is known at Greywatch as Wat's Drink, after a captive who got out through the Old Cistern warren and told of a man who drank here and died. The reek of strung rat carcasses leads straight to the hoard, and a slow drip sounds into the basin.*
**Seep Basin:** A knee-high dressed-stone basin in the north corner catches a seep running down the wall, its water clear to the bottom while the grey mould stops a hand short of its rim on every side, a searcher finds the bind-rune cut on its inner lip facing the water, and any bare skin that touches the water or the wet inside of the basin -> (Test of Constitution, 2d, Poison).
**Crusted Coffer:** Sunk on the basin's floor under the water, a bronze-strapped coffer the size of a saddlebag is fused to the stone by a skin of white limescale, unbroken except where a snapped Khughik hook-spear lies across the rim with its iron hook bedded in the crust, a file of blind white beetles tracks round and round its lid, and the Khughik count it worthless since it can be neither pried loose nor eaten, holding (Treasure III, d20).
**Hoard:** Heaped against the south wall well clear of the basin, the warren's store of strung rat carcasses, dried cave fungus, three pots of Gorzgur red paint and a magpie pile of buckles, nails and a stamped tin garrison badge is Zoghik's, and anything taken from it is missed the next time she counts.
**Peg Bundle:** Among the dried fungus lies a bundle of twelve hardwood pegs tied with gut, each as long as a forearm and worn shiny at one end.
