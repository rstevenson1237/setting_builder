C.41 **Store Nook** (medium) - *Tribal Warren, Household Hoard*
A dug nook off the nest holds whatever the claw hasn't eaten yet, one Khughik posted here more out of habit than any real order.
*Dug nook eight yards across, active, damper than most of the claw's own halls, odds heaped rather than shelved.*
**Nook Keeper:** One Khughik crouches over the stores, scattering rather than fighting the instant anything larger than itself presses the point (Scattering, 1, Bestiary : Khughik).
**Claw's Stash:** What the claw's carried back from whatever a larger household won't finish sits heaped rather than sorted, worth going through by hand rather than at a glance (Treasure III, d20).
