C.41 **Store Nook** (medium) - *Damp Stash, Keeper, Hoard*
A narrow dressed-stone nook with a still-watered **basin** at the back and shelves of gnawed scraps stacked along both walls, a greened **coin lump** on the basin lip. A file of small grey figures shuffles between the shelves.
*Fifteen feet north to south by ten east to west under a seven-foot ceiling, a cistern overflow basin the hall's makers once drew from and now the Old Cistern Khughik's water and pantry, which they call Zoghikakh and the keep's drinkers call Wat's Drink. The basin water carries a working its makers cut and left, and a sweet rot rises off it where it stands.*
**Basin:** Green-black water at the back wall over a man's bones and a Greywatch drinking horn, a hand dipped or a mouthful drunk -> the throat burns and the drinker drops (Test of Constitution, 3d, Poison).
**Coin Lump:** Greened coins fused into one lump on the basin lip, chisel nicks scoring its edge, no gap worked loose (Treasure III, d20).
**Khughik:** Four Khughik file between the shelves on their round from the nest to the entrance, sniffing each shelf as they pass (Furtive, 4, Bestiary : Khughik).
**Exits:** Arch - dressed-stone voussoirs, bind-rune worn smooth at the keystone, sound, south wall -> C.39 Old Cistern Entrance, Grille - iron bars leaded into the stone, bent apart at the base into a gap a crawling body wide, west wall -> C.40 The Chittering Nest
