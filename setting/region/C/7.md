C.7 **The Wrong-Facing Grave** (medium) - *buried facing the keep, wall-bound dead, custom broken*
A single marker among the tended Barrow-Kin graves faces the wrong way entirely - toward open ground rather than the deep treeline - and the cold around it has an edge nothing else here quite matches.
*A narrow grave-row chamber roughly 10 by 8 feet, one grave set apart from the others by its orientation alone; smells of turned earth same as any grave here, but the air over the wrong-facing plot runs noticeably colder than the rest of the room.*
**Wall-Bound Dead:** Whoever lies here was buried facing the wrong way, and is bound close to the grave for it (see setting/Bestiary.md). It does not attack and mostly cannot - but nothing will rest, store, or linger comfortably within its reach, and the frost-killed grass over the plot never quite recovers, season to season.
**Marker Itself:** No name is cut into the stone, only the same closed-ring glyph used throughout Ingaraz, scratched crudely rather than carved - whoever buried this person wrong did not have the builders' own skill, or did not want the grave found easily.
**Exits:** low archway, cold draft, south -> C.14 The Barrow-Kin Grave-Row, narrow passage, absolute silence, north -> C.1 The Sealed Chamber
