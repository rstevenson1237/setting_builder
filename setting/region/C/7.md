C.7 **Watch House** - *Mercenary Guild, Hired Watch*
Velar keeps no garrison of any crown - its watch is a hired mercenary company, paid by the merchant guild and answering to it alone.
*A stone barracks thirty feet across behind the watch house's own yard, plainer than the Counting House next door. Smells of oiled leather and horse; a duty roster chalked on the door names whoever stands the gate tonight.*
**Hired Watch:** A company of sellswords keeps Velar's peace on a Nine Wagons contract, renewable each season at the company's own price (setting/Factions.md). Will talk before it fights, and takes no side in a dispute that doesn't touch the guild's own interests.
**Duty Roster:** Chalked on the yard door, naming who stands the gate, the square, and the row each night - useful to anyone who wants to know exactly when nobody in particular is watching.
**Exits:** open gate - timber, guild mark burned into the post, north side -> C.3 Market Square
