C.7 **The Outlaws' Cache** (Hidden) - *dry, buried, trapped*
Turf is cut over a **pit** beneath an uprooted oak, and a **hare's foot** is nailed to the trunk beside it.
*Pit 6 ft across and 5 ft deep, a mile north-west of the camp; the outlaws keep their share here.*
**Pit:** Turf lies cut over it, with thorn cuttings laid between; a hand reaching under is raked (Test of Constitution, 1d, Piercing).
**Hare's Foot:** Nailed to the trunk at head height.
**Exits:** open, 1 mile south-east, along the game trail -> C.2 The Outlaws' Camp
