C.7 **The Cracked Hull** (Ruin) - *split, weeded, hollow*
A split hull weeded green lies on the reef shelf, its hold open, drips echoing and a belt of green weed and shell hung on a rib.
*180 by 60 feet. The mer people keep it as their own wreck.*
**The hold:** a hollow, weed-hung space open to the water, dripping.
**The weed belt:** green weed and shell woven into a collar and hung on a rib, a mer people's mark of a claimed wreck.
