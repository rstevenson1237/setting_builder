C.7 **The Fallen Lintel** (low) - *collapsed doorway, loose stone, minor hazard*
A doorway here has partly collapsed under its own **lintel stone**, the gap still passable but ringed with loose rubble.
*A narrow, roughly 6-foot gap where a formal threshold once stood, rubble-ringed and half-collapsed. Passing through carelessly can shed a small rockfall, easily avoided with caution.*
**Rubble:** Sifting it carefully turns up something lost when the lintel first fell (Treasure I, d20).
