C.7 **Grave Niche of the Faithful** (medium) - *Bound Service, Grave Niche*
Cut recesses line both walls, each sealed by the shrine's own hand rather than any household's, one recess standing open with nothing resting in it.
*Cut stone niche-hall twenty yards long, active, cold enough that breath shows even this close to the rite chamber, every recess but one sealed flush with the wall.*
**Ravine Restless:** A dead priest marked by the shrine's own vestments walks a fixed round the length of the niche-hall and back, attacking anything living on its round without pause and breaking off the instant it steps past the round's own edge (Walking, 1, Bestiary : Ravine Restless).
**Sealed Recesses:** Every recess but one is sealed flush with worked stone, the seals differing subtly from each other in a way no two households would explain the same.
**Empty Recess:** The one open recess holds grave goods left for whoever was meant to fill it, undisturbed since it was cut and never used (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** natural gap, north wall -> C.3 Rite Chamber
