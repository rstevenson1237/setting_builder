C.7 **The Wrong-Facing Grave** (medium) - *buried facing the keep, wall-bound dead, custom broken*
A single marker among the tended Barrow-Kin graves faces the wrong way entirely - toward open ground rather than the deep treeline - and the cold around it has an edge nothing else here quite matches.
*A narrow grave-row chamber roughly 10 by 8 feet, one grave set apart by orientation alone; the air over that plot runs noticeably colder, the Wall-Bound Dead's cold.*
**Wall-Bound Dead:** Bound to its grave; nothing lingers comfortably in reach (see setting/Bestiary.md).
**Marker Itself:** No name, only a crude, scratched closed-ring glyph.
**Exits:** low archway, cold draft, south -> C.14 The Barrow-Kin Grave-Row, narrow passage, absolute silence, north -> C.1 The Sealed Chamber
