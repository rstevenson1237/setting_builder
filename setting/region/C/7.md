C.7 **Nuthorath** (high) - *eleven, binding, copies*
A round chamber far larger than anything else on this course, roofed by a corbelled dome that steps inward twenty-two times to a closed cap, with **eleven pillars** of dressed limestone standing in a ring around a **sunken floor**. Every pillar is cut from waterline to cap with **old letters**, and the floor between them is laid with **grey salt** a foot deep that has been walked through recently.
*A circle 60 feet across with the floor sunk 3 feet below the entry courses, under a corbelled dome rising 34 feet at the crown - by far the largest volume in Siwapelu. It was where the Odarath cut and consecrated the stones, and it is the room the whole complex is arranged around. The dome makes it the one place here where sound goes where it should: a voice comes back off the cap clean and centred, and after eleven rooms of arriving wrong that is genuinely disorienting.*
**Eleven Pillars:** Standing in a ring 40 feet across, each 3 feet square and 12 feet high, each cut on all four faces in Old Rede in the same careful unhurried hand. They are the originals. The eleven stones set through the eastern pans sixty years ago are copies of these, cut letter for letter by Marchmen who could not read a word of them - which is why the Assize's boundary stones are obeyed by people who have no idea why, and why nobody in Torsgaard will touch one. A party that has seen the boundary chart at Rendfast can match them off one by one (Quest: Count the Stones).
**Sunken Floor:** The floor within the pillar ring is dropped three feet and filled level with coarse grey salt, a foot deep, laid rather than spilled. It has been crossed recently - a single line of footprints goes in to the twelfth position and comes back out, made by someone who was not carrying anything. The Faelhond have been in here and have not mentioned it.
**Twelfth Position:** There are eleven pillars and twelve sockets. The twelfth socket is cut into the sunken floor at the ring's northern point, four inches across, matching the empty socket in the cap of the stone standing in open water at Nuthora. It is empty, and the salt has been scraped back from it by hand. Setting the fitting from Nuthora's cap into this socket is the one action in Siwapelu that does something to the whole region rather than to a room (Keys: Twelfth Socket Fitting).
**Dome Cap:** The corbelled dome closes with a single cut cap stone at the crown, 34 feet up, and unlike every other threshold in Siwapelu it carries no character on its underside - it is dressed smooth and blank. It is one of the three. From directly beneath it, a party looking up in lantern light can see the blank face plainly, and a party that has been checking cap stones since the stair will understand that they have found one.
**Nuthora Oathkeeper:** One, standing between the fourth and fifth pillars, a dry shape of salt and wrapped cloth that has not moved in nine hundred years and does not need to. It has the room and nothing beyond it. It does nothing whatever to a party that reads the pillars, crosses the salt, and leaves - and a party can take everything this room has to offer without ever establishing that it is not a statue. Cut a pillar, prise the letters, or take the salt out of the floor, and there is no negotiation: 6d6+2, implacable within these walls, and gone the moment a party is out of them.
**Exits:** corbelled arch in the south wall, cap carrying the threshold character, opening onto a passage of dressed limestone -> C.6 Mezeora, corbelled arch in the west wall, its sill worn into a shallow dish by water -> C.8 Kirel, wide corbelled arch in the north wall with a stepped ramp beyond it descending to the second course -> C.9 Doraianu
