C.7 **The Fallen Lintel** (low) - *collapsed doorway, loose stone, minor hazard*
A doorway here has partly collapsed under its own **lintel stone**, the gap still passable but ringed with loose rubble.
*Passing through carelessly can shed a small rockfall, easily avoided with caution.*
**Rubble:** Sifting it carefully turns up a few coins lost when the lintel first fell.
**Exits:** passage -> C.6 Second Niche Row, passage -> C.8 Coral-Choked Hall
