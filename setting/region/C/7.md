C.7 **Watch House** - *Mercenary Guild, Hired Watch*
Velar keeps no crown's garrison. Its **hired watch** is a mercenary company, paid by the merchant guild and answering to it alone.
*A stone barracks thirty feet across behind its own yard, plainer than the Counting House next door. Smells of oiled leather and horse, and a duty roster chalked on the door, names tonight's gate watch.*
**Hired Watch:** A company of sellswords, on a Nine Wagons contract, renewable each season at the company's price, talking before it fights (setting/Factions.md).
**Duty Roster:** Chalked on the yard door, naming who stands the gate, the square, and the row each night.
**Exits:** open gate - timber, guild mark burned into the post, north side -> C.3 Market Square
