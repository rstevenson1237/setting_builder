C.7 **The Sunken Antechamber** (hidden) - *Flooded, Old-Stone, Quiet*
Past the last dry step, the **Sunken Antechamber** opens into a low, flooded stone room, air trapped in pockets against the ceiling. Old brackets on the walls once held something now long gone.
*A stone room fifteen feet across below the Drowned Stair, ceiling just above the waterline in pockets, walls dressed in the same cut stone as Baldun's own ruins; silent below the surface and cold enough to numb bare skin quickly.*
**Air Pockets:** Trapped air against the ceiling gives a few breaths' rest without surfacing fully.
**Empty Brackets:** Corroded metal brackets line one wall, sized for urns or idols, all missing.
**Buried Cache:** Silt on the floor has half-buried a scatter of grave goods (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** the submerged steps, up -> C.4 The Drowned Stair
