C.19 **The Lower Cistern** (low) - *water-filled, still, connective*
Still, black water fills a carved stone basin at the center of this chamber, undisturbed long enough to have gone perfectly flat. The dressed stone here matches the precision of the halls above it.
*A round chamber roughly 20 feet across, centered on a sunken stone cistern basin filled with dark, unmoving water. The air is cold and mineral-smelling, and the only sound is an occasional, distant drip.*
**Cistern Basin:** a sunken stone basin roughly 12 feet across at the chamber's center, filled with still water clear enough to see the bottom - a section of the basin's inner wall, just below the waterline, has mortar noticeably rougher than the surrounding stonework. Reaching underwater and pressing against the rough patch reveals it isn't structural at all, and it swings inward on old hinges to open a passage beyond (Secret path -> C.20 Sealed Niche of the Nine).
**Exits:** archway, spiral-carved -> C.17 The Spiral Archway Hall, passage, opposite the archway -> C.21 The Deep Gallery Fork
