C.19 **The Sinkhole Junction** (medium) - *unstable ceiling, hidden entrance, loose earth*
Sunlight and loose earth spill down through a ragged opening overhead, where the Hills' **sinkhole** breaks through into the barrow.
*An irregular junction roughly 20 feet across, ceiling breached by the Hills' sinkhole above - not part of the barrow's original design, an unstable intrusion from the surface. Passing beneath it carelessly, or after any loud disturbance elsewhere in the barrow, risks a partial collapse.*
**Unstable Ceiling:** Loud disturbance nearby risks bringing part of it down on everyone in the junction (TEST OF FATE for who's caught; a WOUND for any who are).
**Exits:** ragged sinkhole opening, loose earth and roots -> B.6 Sinkhole, low stone passage -> C.18 Drowned Passage, low stone passage -> C.20 Cold Vestibule
