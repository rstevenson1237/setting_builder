C.19 **Mess Hall** (medium) - *Firepit, Water Jugs, Smoke Crack*
Collapsed trestles and frozen scraps litter a long hall around a cold firepit, above which an **ice-choked smoke crack** in the ceiling hangs with icicles as long as an arm. A row of tall clay **water jugs** stands along the north wall.
*A dressed hall 40 feet by 25, the household's old mess, its trestles and benches rotted through and fallen, bones and scraps frozen into a skin of ice across the floor that makes the footing slick. The steady drip in the room is meltwater from the icicles over the hearth.*
**Ice-Choked Smoke Crack:** Above the firepit in the middle of the hall a crack in the ceiling that once drew its smoke is hung with icicles, the ash in the pit frozen to a grey crust by their drip, and a light held beneath shows a plug of grey ice as big as a barrel wedged in the crack above -> a fire lit in the pit thaws the plug loose within minutes and drops it with its icicles onto the hearth (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Water Jugs:** Along the north wall stand tall clay jugs with a dipper on a rope in the nearest, the rope worn wet for three times the jug's depth, and a trail of frozen spills runs from them across the floor to the iron back-plate of the hearth -> lifting the back-plate from the hearth's rear shows a well shaft under the firepit with a rope and bucket hooked inside and cold water glinting twenty feet down.
**Exits:** flat stone lid set in the floor over a hole with a notched log ladder going down beneath, too heavy for one to shift and dragged aside by two, south-east corner -> C.18 Sleeping Hall, hanging screen of knotted cord strung with bone beads that clack when anyone pushes through, in a cut doorway, west wall -> C.20 Stores
