C.19 **Mess Hall** (medium) - *Tribal Warren, Old Water*
A firepit at the hall's own centre serves whoever's eating in shifts, water carried up from the old cistern behind Gorzgur's own claim on it.
*Dug hall twenty yards across, active, smoke finding its own way out through a crack in the ceiling rather than any built vent, firepit ringed in stones scavenged from older cutting.*
**Firepit:** The firepit's ring of stone runs hotter at its own edge than the coals inside would explain, close enough that reaching past it carelessly costs the reaching hand (Test of Constitution, 1d, Fire).
**Water Store:** Jugs carried up from the old cistern sit racked along the wall nearest the fire, Gorzgur's own claim on water the rest of the ravine has to dig or beg for.
**Exits:** natural gap, west wall -> C.18 Sleeping Hall, natural gap, east wall -> C.20 Stores
