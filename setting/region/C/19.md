C.19 **Mess Hall** (medium) - *Firepit, Water Jugs, Smoke Crack*
A dressed hall gone to disrepair where a **firepit** of blackened stones sits in the middle, a rime-crusted **sump** fills the low end, a soot-lipped **crack** runs across the ceiling, and an iron **ring** is set in the floor by the pit.
*The hall is 35 feet by 25 under a 7-foot ceiling, where the household eats, its long tables gone to splinters and only the firepit still used. Smoke stink and a drip sound point at the crack and the sump.*
**Firepit:** A ring of blackened stones holds cold ash and a bent spit-iron at the hall's middle, bones and split marrow-shells heaped around it.
**Sump:** A rime-ice skin roofs a drip-fed sump across the low end, jugs frozen in -> striking a light over it drops whoever stands on the crust into the water (Test of Constitution, 1d, Frost).
**Crack:** A hand-wide crack across the ceiling over the firepit draws the smoke up, its lips furred black with soot.
**Ring:** An iron ring beside the firepit, its sill worn to a dish, a chain running under the low end -> hauling it drains the sump through a slot.
**Pot:** A clay pot of damp ash balances on the grille's lintel bar, grey dust trailing below -> swinging the gate tips it, dousing torches held under it.
**Exits:** open arch of dressed stone with a chipped keystone and a sill worn hollow, east wall -> C.18 Sleeping Hall, iron grille of flat bars riveted in a lattice with a gate hung on pins and a keyed lock, its plate polished by hands, south wall -> C.20 Stores
