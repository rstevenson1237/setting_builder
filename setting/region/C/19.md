C.19 **The Sinkhole Junction** (medium) - *unstable ceiling, hidden entrance, loose earth*
Sunlight and loose earth spill down through a ragged opening overhead, where the Hills' **sinkhole** breaks through into the barrow.
*The ceiling here is unstable; passing beneath it carelessly, or after any loud disturbance elsewhere in the barrow, risks a partial collapse.*
**Unstable Ceiling:** Loud disturbance nearby risks bringing part of it down.
**Exits:** climb up through the opening -> B.6 Sinkhole, passage -> C.18 Drowned Passage, passage -> C.20 Cold Vestibule
