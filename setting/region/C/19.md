C.19 **Azzakire** (medium) - *inflow, source, current*
A round chamber where **water comes in** through a cut mouth in the eastern wall with real force, filling the room to the thigh and running out again through two ways that go in opposite directions. To the north the water leaves **quietly** down a stepped channel; to the south it leaves **cold and silent** down a wide ramp cut over with letters, and a party has to pick one.
*A circle 45 feet across, ceiling 11 feet, floored in dressed flags under about 30 inches of moving water. It is the inflow chamber - the point where Lindmarn comes into Siwapelu and the reason the lowest course has stayed flooded for four hundred years. It is the noisiest room in the complex, and the noise is directional: standing in the centre a party can hear that the two exits sound completely different.*
**Cut Mouth:** An opening 5 feet across in the east wall at chest height, dressed and splayed, with water pouring through it steadily. It runs from Lindmarn above and it has never stopped. The flow is heaviest on a falling tide. It cannot be blocked with anything a party is carrying and it is not a way out - the bore narrows to a hand's width within twenty feet.
**Divide:** This is the choice that does not come back. Both ways out are legible and a party is meant to choose on evidence rather than guess. **North** sounds busy and smells of silt and rot; the water goes down a stepped channel and the steps are heaped with four hundred years of what the fen has washed in. **South** is quieter than it should be, markedly colder, the air completely still, and the ramp beyond is cut over every inch with old letters that a lantern picks up twenty feet in. Neither route rejoins the other, and getting back means coming back through this room.
**Stepped Channel:** In the north wall, a channel 4 feet wide dropping in eight cut steps, with the water running down it and a walkway beside it. Caught in the steps, packed down by four centuries of current, is the accumulated wash of the whole complex (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** stepped channel and walkway leading north out of the chamber, water running down it, silt smell -> C.21 Taluora, wide ramped passage leading south, cut over every inch with old letters, still and cold -> C.23 Zorethora
