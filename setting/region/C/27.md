C.27 **Stores** (medium) - *Teetering, Unsorted, Plunder*
Sacks and crates sit stacked without much care for order, piled high enough in places to threaten whoever pulls the wrong one first.
*Rough-cut store twenty yards long, active, stacks piled higher than a person in places, nothing sorted by kind or by age.*
**Unstable Stack:** One stack piled higher than the rest shifts rather than holds if pulled from low down, coming down on whoever's careless enough to try it (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Raided Goods:** What Hukkgur's taken by force rather than traded for sits mixed in with the rest, worth sorting out by hand from whatever else is piled around it (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** natural gap, west wall -> C.26 Sleeping Den, natural gap, east wall -> C.28 Grave Pit, archway, south wall -> C.25 Bone Trophy Hall
