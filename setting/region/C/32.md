C.32 **Store Nook** (medium) - *Scraps, Habit, Crouched*
A warm, smoky nook too low to stand in, its back half floored with a cracked **black crust** threading smoke, and an iron-strapped **strongbox** heaved up at the crust's edge with rotten scraps heaped against its sides. A gut **rattle cord** hung with knucklebones runs ankle-high across the foot of the ramp you came down.
*The Silt Hollow's store, 12 feet by 10 and 4 feet to the ceiling, dug through an old-cut niche into a coal seam that smoulders beneath the back half, and gone to decay since the shelves collapsed into the scraps the Khughik still toss down the ramp. The old dressed-stone frame of the leaf carries the Old Tongue cutting Qathghus, "fire below", and a Grozzgur-laid ward holds the leaf. Coal smoke and rotted meat thicken over the crust, the air is warm and dry, and the floor crumbles underfoot toward the back.*
**Black Crust:** The back half of the floor is a crust of coal ash over the burning seam, too hot to hold a palm on and threaded with smoke from cracks that stop at a line of charred scraps along the dressed floor's edge, small clawed footprints cross it unbroken, and anyone crossing it under a full load breaks through into the seam (Test of Constitution, 2d, Fire).
**Strongbox:** An iron-strapped box sits in the open at the crust's edge with its lower third fused into the ash and heaved up in a lump, rotten scraps heaped against it as if it were a wall, and chiselling the fused crust away from its foot frees it to be carried off (Treasure III, d20).
**Rattle Cord:** A gut cord strung ankle-high across the foot of the ramp just inside the leaf, retied at one end with fresh knots, carries a bunch of knucklebones and a dead ravine rat tangled in it, and stepping into it sets the bones clattering loud enough to carry up into the nest.
**Exits:** hinged plank leaf in an old dressed-stone frame at the foot of a three-step ramp up to the nest's floor hatch, which from the ramp side shows as an open doorway onto an empty earth cell while the ramp's draught and any sound stop dead at the frame, swings once a cut Khughik ear is brought near it and otherwise holds against an hour's hacking at planks no one can see (Test of Fate, Impact), north wall -> C.31 The Chittering Nest
