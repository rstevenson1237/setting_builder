C.32 **Store Nook** (medium) - *Scraps, Habit, Crouched*
A crouched dug nook four feet high, its middle taken by a waist-high **scrap heap** steaming at the cracks with a glassy **clinker lump** in its crown, and the grille's frame is cut with a word (**Frame Cut**).
*Dug nook about 15 feet by 10, once the Silt Hollow Khughik's scrap store and now decayed to a heap of rotted stock heating from within. The air is dry and sour-warm, and the only sound is a slow crackle from the heap's crust.*
**Scrap Heap:** A waist-high heap of rotted rag fills the middle, its grey crust warm to the hand, and crossing it under a full load -> it breaks over a glowing core (Test of Constitution, 2d, Fire).
**Clinker Lump:** A glassy lump fused into the heap's crown holds something metal, and prying it loose -> whatever was fused in (Treasure III, d20).
**Frame Cut:** The grille's frame is cut in Old Tongue letters with the word Qathghus, and searching the heap's edge shows a sack-shaped hollow slumped and blackened.
**Exits:** grille - iron bars set in packed earth, one bar-wide gap bent open at the bottom, rust flaking, west wall -> C.31 The Chittering Nest
