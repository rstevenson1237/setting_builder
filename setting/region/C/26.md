C.26 **Sleeping Den** (medium) - *Ledges, Winnings, Bruised*
Bare stone ledges serve Hukkgur for beds, a couple of the household sleeping off whatever the pit's last bout cost them.
*Rough-cut den twenty yards across, active, ledges cut rather than furnished, the tribe line plain at the doorway where Hukkgur's own crude digging meets the pit's older cut.*
**Resting Hukkgur:** Two Hukkgur sleep here between challenges, answering anything that wakes them with a swing rather than a question (Waking, 2, Bestiary : Hukkgur).
**Kept Winnings:** What's been won rather than earned sits under one sleeper's own ledge, unguarded beyond whoever's sleeping on top of it (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** archway, west wall -> C.24 The Brawling Pit, natural gap, east wall -> C.27 Stores
