C.10 **The Lookout Cliff** (Natural) - *sheer, windy, gull-hung*
A sheer cliff hung with gulls overlooks the cove, a goat path climbing to a ledge where a lookout stands with a horn.
*200 by 150 feet. The pirates and the treasure hunters both watch from here.*
**The lookout:** a pirate on the cliff-top ledge, a red sash and a black cloth tied on the left arm, a horn at hand (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Pirate).
**The goat path:** narrow steps of rock up from the beach.
**Exits:** the ridge trail up from the cliff -> D.2 The Ridge Caves
