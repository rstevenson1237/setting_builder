C.10 **The Outer Mound Path** (low) - *first entrance, cleared cart-ruts, nearest the boundary stones*
Worn cart-ruts run straight into a mound-mouth here, the most obviously used way into Ingaraz from the treeline above.
*A short cutting roughly 10 feet wide sloping down into the hillside, open to the sky at its mouth; smells of cold air rising to meet the warmer air above ground, strongest on still mornings.*
**Cart-Ruts:** Wheel-ruts, old and fresh both, run the length of the cutting - proof this mouth sees regular traffic, Diggers' and otherwise.
**Exits:** open cutting, cart-ruts, up -> B.8 The Scouted Barrow Mouth, low passage, worked stone, down -> C.17 The Empty Antechamber
