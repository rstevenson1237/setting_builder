C.10 **The Cold Passage** (low) - *unnaturally-cold, still, connective*
The cold here runs sharper than the stone alone accounts for, deepening steadily the further one goes. Nothing moves, and nothing but footsteps breaks the stillness.
*A straight passage roughly 30 by 6 feet, walls smooth and undecorated. The temperature drops noticeably along its length, and the silence is heavier than the passage's size would suggest.*
**Deepening Cold:** the passage's temperature falls steadily from one end to the other, colder than anything the stone or depth alone explains - a warning, to anyone who knows to read it, of what waits further in.
**Exits:** passage, back toward the threshold archway -> C.9 Threshold Archway, opening, fungus-fringed, at the passage's far end -> C.11 The Blighted Seam, archway, frost-rimed, at the passage's midpoint -> C.12 The Wight's Hollow
