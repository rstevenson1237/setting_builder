C.10 **The Sealed Niche of Tribute** (high) - *bronze bell, generations-old seal, hidden truth*
Behind the broken seal, a shallow niche holds the missing bronze **diving-bell** resting amid a scatter of old offerings, undisturbed for generations.
*A shallow, arched niche roughly six feet deep and four feet wide, built specifically to hold the bell and its offerings; once broken, the mortar seal cannot easily be restored.*
**Diving-Bell:** Saltmere's missing bell, placed here as tribute generations ago rather than stolen recently - removing it is possible, but likely draws the attention of anything in the barrow alert to bell-sounds.
**Ritual Offerings:** Arranged around the bell's base, undisturbed since they were placed (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** shattered mortar seal -> C.9 Sunken Hall
