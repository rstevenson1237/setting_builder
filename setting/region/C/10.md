C.10 **The Second District** (secret) - *Hidden, Drowned, Untouched*
The **Second District** rises just above the waterline, a cluster of intact Nemhet rooftops and columns nobody from Carrdun has ever touched. The marsh-kin have kept its location to themselves for longer than anyone can say.
*A cluster of half-drowned stone buildings forty yards across, roofs and upper stories intact where Baldun's own ruins are all rubble and cellar; ivy and reed have grown through everything, silent even by the marsh's standards.*
**Intact Rooftops:** Whole buildings stand here, roofed and walled, unlike anything at Baldun - untouched since the flood.
**Unbroken Shrine:** A small shrine room, sealed and undisturbed, holds a single untouched idol (Unique Treasure: The Undrowned Idol).
**Wall Record:** Carved panels record names and a direction - a fragment of Nemhet's history nobody at Carrdun has read (Lore: The Second District Panels).
**Marsh-kin's Watch:** A single carved marker, recently maintained, shows the marsh-kin still tend this place quietly.
**Exits:** the plank walk, back -> C.6 The Elder's Platform
