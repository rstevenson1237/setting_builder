C.10 **Wosaora** (medium) - *cured, continuing, errand*
A long low room off the second course's gallery, its floor **worn into a path** down the centre from one arch to the other, and a **figure** walking that path - dry, leather-coloured, salt standing in the creases of its joints, going from one end to the other and back without pause and without any interest in you at all.
*A rectangle 50 by 18 feet, ceiling 9 feet, a plain connecting room that was never anything more. Its purpose now is entirely accidental. The sound is the thing's feet - a dry regular scuff, thirty-one paces one way and thirty-one back, which a party hears from two rooms off and consistently mislocates.*
**Cured Errand:** A Cured Man, 4d6, Odarath, nine hundred years dead and not finished. It walks the path from the east arch to the west arch and back, carrying nothing, and it has been doing so since before Torsgaard existed. Per `setting/Procedures.md` a cured thing continues whatever it was doing: a party that stays off the centre line of the room is not attacked and can pass within arm's reach, and a party standing in the path is obstruction. This is findable by watching for one circuit, which costs nothing but the nerve to stand still (Named Creature: Odaranu).
**Worn Path:** The flags down the room's centre are dished a full inch below the flags at the walls, in a band about two feet wide, and the wear is even from end to end. Nine hundred years of the same thirty-one paces. At the west end the wear stops dead at the arch; at the east end it continues out into the gallery and then stops after six feet, which means whatever the errand was, it ended six feet outside this room and nobody has done it since.
**Ferris's Coat:** Folded on the floor against the north wall, out of the path, an oiled leather coat of recent Torsgaard make with the wrappings still in the pockets. It has been placed rather than dropped. Ferris came out of Siwapelu three months ago Brined to the elbow, and he came out of this room, and he left his coat behind on purpose (Quest: What Came Up With Ferris).
**Exits:** corbelled arch in the east wall opening onto the long gallery -> C.9 Doraianu, corbelled arch in the west wall, its cap carrying the threshold character, cold air coming through it -> C.12 Galuora
