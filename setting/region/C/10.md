C.10 **The Deepest Chamber** (high) - *Bound Thing, Sealed Rod, Stillness*
A chamber too large for any torch to light all at once holds something that fills more of it than the eye agrees with itself about, still until whatever it's bound to do stirs it.
*A chamber forty yards across at least, cut lower and older than anything else in the ravine, cold enough to see breath, every other bound thing in the ravine falling still the instant this one moves.*
**Bound Depth:** What fills the chamber does not negotiate, does not chase, and has not yet been driven off by anything a party could plausibly field, and has not moved from this chamber in eight hundred years except at whatever set it its task (Still, 1, Bestiary : The Bound Depth).
**Kutuz:** Set into a niche at the chamber's own far wall, untouched by the cold that reaches everywhere else in the room, a single sealed rod carries the same three-line mark the whole ravine answers to, cut nine hundred years before any household now living moved in (Unique Treasure: Kutuz).
**Exits:** sealed shaft, ceiling -> C.5 Warden's Threshold
