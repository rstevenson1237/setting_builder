C.29 **Second Entrance** (medium) - *Scree, Loose Ceiling, Back Way*
A back way out sees less traffic than the broken gate, loose rock underfoot the only thing standing watch over it.
*Rough-cut passage twelve yards across, active, loose scree under a ceiling that's shed more than one chunk of itself in disuse, no torch bracket fitted anywhere along it.*
