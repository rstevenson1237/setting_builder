K Pyramid Level 9 - The Antechamber

Overview: The demon's antechamber, where the air itself costs Sanity. A party comes to prepare for the last hall. The demon's breath thickens the air and the cult wears its masks tighter each day. A party that lingers pays a Test of Sanity every hour; a mask torn brings a scream that the whole level hears.

Approach: The stair from the lieutenants' halls (J.6) lands at K.1, where a wraith in a king's robe stands. A party must pass without drawing steel; none is refused.

Prizes:
- K.3 - A low hall of choking air over bone dust, a gate at the east end; gargoyles and a cache in a niche (Treasure V, d20).
- K.5 - A carved iron door, warm to the hand; a mirror that shows the demon's hall and a cache behind it (Treasure IV, d20).
- K.8 - Skeletons kneeling in rows toward a door; they rise behind whoever turns a back, and offerings lie at the door (Treasure III, d20).

Places:
- K.1 - A dim landing with a wraith standing at its head.
- K.2 - A long cold hall of thick air; ghouls in the dark.
- K.4 - Faceless masks on four walls; four cultists and a mask cut with writing (Lore: The Demon's Breath).
- K.6 - Ribbed walls that swell and sink, vents sucking overhead.
- K.7 - Censers smoking on a ledge, sweet smoke under the door (Treasure II, d20).

Tables:

4. Wailing from the far end of a hall.
3. Dust sifting from the roof.
