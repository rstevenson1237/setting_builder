K Bugbear Den

Overview: The Zurnaz's den in the dry fork off the east wall, paid by the Black Altar to keep the fork and collect the tithe. A party comes for the tithe store, and for the back stair. The Zurnaz hold every tribe's tithe until it is carried up the stair. If a tithe is lost, they search the ravine; if a party finds the stair, the shrine is told within a turn.

Approach: Up the dry fork (E.7) from the ravine's east wall, 50 yards along a dry stream bed under an overhang, with Bugbears hidden above the bed day and night. A back stair runs from the den up to the shrine, behind a plastered door.

Conditions: Dry stream bed and natural cave, ceilings 7 to 20 feet. No light but what the party carries. Sound dies at once in the soft dust. Footing is dry silt that holds tracks. The air is still, and a lit fire shows from the ravine.

Entrances:
- K.1 - A low cave mouth behind a sooty hide screen, soot handprints round it and a bugbear dozing on a stool behind the screen.

Prizes:
- K.5 - By a crude passage beyond the sleeping cave: bones strewn round a heaped bed, a bone-lined pit beside it, a bell-cord hanging through a hole in the rock, and a skull cup on a ledge.

Places:
- K.2 - Shelves cut in the rock, sacks, chests and bundles stacked by tribe, each tied with a strip of its hide, behind a door sealed with pitch.
- K.3 - Just inside the mouth: a round cave of six beds of matted hide, one low door at its west end.
- K.4 - Behind a plaster wall in the leader's cave, cracked at a seam that rings hollow: ninety narrow steps turning twice, climbing 120 feet to the shrine (M.14), with a niche at the first turn.

Tables:
Danger, 6 noticing to 1 acting
6. The hide screen at the den mouth (K.1) stirs and falls still.
5. Fresh drag marks cross the silt toward the tithe store (K.2).
4. The silt shifts underfoot; the party's tracks show to whoever follows.
3. A bugbear drops silently behind the last in line, then is gone.
2. Soot handprints appear fresh on the walls ahead and behind.
1. The bell rings overhead; bugbears close from the overhang and the back stair at once.
