K Bugbear Den

Kind: single holding
Overview: The Zurnaz's den in the dry fork off the east wall: Bugbears paid by the Black Altar to keep the fork and collect the tithe. Their leader is a Named Creature. A party comes for the tithe store, and for the back stair. The Zurnaz hold every tribe's tithe until it is carried up the stair. If a tithe is lost, they search the ravine. If the stair is found by a party, the shrine is told within a turn.
Approach: The fork's mouth opens on the ravine's east wall; the den lies 200 yards up a dry stream bed under an overhang, with Bugbears hidden above the bed day and night. A back stair runs from the den to the shrine, behind a plastered door.
Conditions: Dry stream bed and natural cave, ceilings 7 to 20 feet. No light but what the party carries. Sound dies at once in the soft dust. Footing is dry silt that holds tracks. The air is still, and a lit fire shows from the ravine.
