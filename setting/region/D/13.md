D.13 **The Fallen Colonnade** (low) - *Collapsed, Dusty, Quiet*
Toppled columns litter the **Fallen Colonnade** floor to ceiling, banquet-hall grand once, rubble now. One heap of fallen stone looks stacked rather than collapsed.
*A long hall forty feet by twenty, columns down in a jumble across the whole floor, ceiling partially open to a black void above; smells of dust and settling grit, echoing even more than the flooded hall nearby.*
**Toppled Columns:** Broken pillars cover the floor, dressed stone still bearing the pyramid-arch motif.
**Stacked Rubble:** One heap of stone looks deliberately piled rather than fallen; cleared by hand, it hides a cache beneath (Treasure III, d20).
**Open Ceiling:** A gap above shows only darkness - whatever was up there collapsed long before anyone now living.
**Exits:** a collapsed gap in the near wall, east -> D.4 The Scribes' Hall
