D.13 **Chamber of the Coiled Serpent** (high) - *Serpent Cult, Serpent-Coil Lintel*
A single unbroken **serpent relief** runs the full circuit of this chamber, coiling three times before meeting its own tail. Two **Bound Dead** stand posted at the chamber's inner face, motionless.
*A round chamber forty feet across, cut into the ziggurat's core rather than built up from it, the one room in Daghash with no straight wall. Smells of old ash, and the floor carries a faint thrum, felt through the boots, stopping when a hand is set flat against the relief.*
**Serpent Relief:** Three unbroken coils meeting at a gap, where the tail was never carved, the gap holding a fist-sized socket, the figurine fitted in -> a floor panel swings open, at the relief's base (Keys: The Coiled Figurine).
**Under-Floor Recess:** Behind the swung panel, a recess cut into the core stone, beneath the tail (Unique Treasure: The Vaalsenn Tablet).
**Bound Dead:** Two stand posted facing the relief, rather than any door, acting only if the socket is forced (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Bound Dead).
**Priest-King Niche:** A shallow niche behind the relief, where the tail would meet the head, left as the priest-kings' reign ended (Treasure III, d20).
**Essath Name-Cut:** Above the socket, one line of Essath names the chamber, Nirgash, the Serpent's Temple.
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, serpent-coil lintel, single entrance -> D.7 First Threshold
