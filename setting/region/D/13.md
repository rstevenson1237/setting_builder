D.13 **Chamber of the Coiled Serpent** (high) - *Serpent Cult, Serpent-Coil Lintel*
A single unbroken **serpent relief** runs the full circuit of this chamber, floor to ceiling, coiling three times around the room before meeting its own tail - except at one point, where the coil breaks around an empty, fitted socket the size of a closed fist. Two **Bound Dead** stand posted at the chamber's inner face, motionless.
*A round chamber forty feet across, cut into the ziggurat's core rather than built up from it - the one room in Daghash with no straight wall. Smells of old ash, stronger here than anywhere else on the Middle tier; the floor carries a faint thrum, felt through the boots rather than heard, that stops the moment a hand is set flat against the relief.*
**Serpent Relief:** The coiled-serpent motif repeated at every lintel elsewhere in the ziggurat, here completed - three unbroken coils cut floor to ceiling around the whole room, meeting at a single gap where the last few inches of tail were never carved. The gap holds a fist-sized socket, empty. Fitting a carved serpent figurine into it completes the coil and swings a floor panel at the relief's base open on a hidden hinge (Unique Treasure: The Vaalsenn Tablet); forcing the socket, or fitting anything that is not the missing figure, does nothing but wake both Bound Dead at once.
**Bound Dead:** Two stand posted facing the relief rather than any door, bound against whatever the missing figure was carved to complete - not against the party, unless the socket is forced (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Bound Dead).
**Priest-King Regalia:** Set into a shallow niche behind where the coil's tail would meet its head, a scatter of grave-goods left exactly as the priest-kings' own reign ended (Treasure III, d20) - untouched, since nothing here has ever needed them.
**Essath Name-Cut:** Above the socket, a single line of Essath script names the chamber - Nirgash, the Serpent's Temple - in a hand older than the lintels outside it.
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, serpent-coil lintel, single entrance -> D.7 First Threshold
