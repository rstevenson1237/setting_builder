D.13 **Chamber of the Coiled Serpent** (high) - *Serpent Cult, Serpent-Coil Lintel*
A single unbroken **serpent relief** runs the full circuit of this chamber, floor to ceiling, coiling three times around the room before meeting its own tail - except at one point, where the coil breaks around an empty, fitted socket the size of a closed fist. Two **Bound Dead** stand posted at the chamber's inner face, motionless.
*A round chamber forty feet across, cut into the ziggurat's core rather than built up from it - the one room in Daghash with no straight wall, and the only one whose floor was laid after its walls rather than before. Smells of old ash, stronger here than anywhere else on the Middle tier; the floor carries a faint thrum, felt through the boots rather than heard, that stops the moment a hand is set flat against the relief.*
**Serpent Relief:** The coiled-serpent motif repeated at every lintel elsewhere in the ziggurat, here completed - three unbroken coils cut floor to ceiling around the whole room, meeting at a single gap where the last few inches of tail were never carved. The gap holds a fist-sized socket, empty. Fitting the coiled figurine from the vestry into it completes the coil and swings a floor panel at the relief's base open on a hidden hinge (Keys: The Coiled Figurine); forcing the socket, or fitting anything that is not the missing figure, does nothing but wake both Bound Dead at once.
**Under-Floor Recess:** Behind the swung panel, a recess cut into the core stone beneath the relief's own tail - the priest-kings' inner chamber within the inner chamber, and never opened since their reign ended (Unique Treasure: The Vaalsenn Tablet).
**Bound Dead:** Two stand posted facing the relief rather than any door, bound against whatever the missing figure was carved to complete - not against the party, unless the socket is forced (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Bound Dead).
**Priest-King Niche:** A shallow niche behind where the coil's tail would meet its head, left exactly as the priest-kings' own reign ended and never disturbed since, because nothing that has lived here afterward has needed what is in it (Treasure III, d20).
**Essath Name-Cut:** Above the socket, a single line of Essath script names the chamber - Nirgash, the Serpent's Temple - in a hand older than the lintels outside it.
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, serpent-coil lintel, single entrance -> D.7 First Threshold
