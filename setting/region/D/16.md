D.16 **Enclave Nursery** (medium) - *Pit of the Old Ones, Vented Floor*
Cut originally as a nursery for the priest-kings' own household, this chamber now holds **Nirguk's young** instead - the one continuity between the two tenancies that nobody planned, and the whole reason the enclave has never simply left Daghash for somewhere less contested.
*A nursery twenty feet across, its floor channels - cut to carry water away, and still working after five centuries - repurposed by Nirguk to keep the chamber dry for its own young. Smells of cold stone; the vented floor keeps this room free of the damp that reaches every chamber near it.*
**Nirguk Young:** A handful of them, too small yet to leave the chamber and entirely unable to be reasoned with, coiling toward anything that enters and calling loudly enough to be heard in the warren (Territorial, 5, Bestiary : Nirguk).
**Vented Floor Channels:** Cut shallow into the floor throughout, still carrying what little moisture reaches this deep away from the room entirely - priest-king work, unmaintained and still working.
**Scale-Polished Patch:** Low on the chamber's outer wall, the same hand's-width polish as marks Nirguk's other work here. Pressing and pushing it swings the wall inward on a hidden pivot, opening a low crawlway that reaches the north service tunnel directly, well clear of the Sealed Door and every bound threshold above.
**Exits:** open doorway - scale-polished stone, west side -> D.15 Nirguk Warren
