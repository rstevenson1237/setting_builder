D.16 **Enclave Nursery** (low) - *Pit of the Old Ones, Vented Floor*
Cut originally as a nursery for the priest-kings' own household, this chamber now holds Nirguk's young instead - the one continuity between the two tenancies that nobody planned.
*A nursery twenty feet across, its floor channels - cut to carry water away, and still working after five centuries - repurposed by Nirguk to keep the chamber dry for its own young. Smells of cold stone; the vented floor keeps this room free of the damp that reaches every chamber near it.*
**Nirguk Young:** A handful of Nirguk's young keep to this chamber, too small yet to leave it - the whole reason the enclave has never simply abandoned Daghash for somewhere less contested (Territorial, 5, Bestiary : Nirguk, the Serpent-kin).
**Vented Floor Channels:** Cut shallow into the floor throughout, still carrying what little moisture reaches this deep away from the room entirely - priest-king work, unmaintained and still working.
**Scale-Polished Patch:** Low on the chamber's outer wall, the same hand's-width polish as marks Nirguk's other work here. Pressing and pushing it swings the wall inward on a hidden pivot, opening a low crawlway that reaches D.14's culvert directly, well clear of the Sealed Door and every bound threshold above.
**Exits:** open doorway - scale-polished stone, west side -> D.15 Nirguk Warren
