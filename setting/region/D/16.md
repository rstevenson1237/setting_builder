D.16 **The Coil's Vestibule** (medium) - *Marked, Guarded, Coiled*
Three calm-eyed figures wait in the **Coil's Vestibule**, a coil scratched into the floor at the doorway to either side. They won't fight unless blocked from moving past.
*A square antechamber fifteen feet across between three archways, swept clean unlike anywhere else nearby; smells of tallow candles, currently lit, and utterly silent.*
**Cult Initiates:** Three wait here calmly, unmoved by threats, and answer to somebody unseen (Wary, 3, Bestiary : Cult Initiate).
**Coil-Marks:** A coil is scratched into the floor before each archway, always pointing the same direction.
**Standing Order:** Overheard, one repeats the same instruction quietly - let no one pass to the rest without the elder's word.
**Nervous One:** One initiate flinches at loud noise and won't meet anyone's eyes, clearly less certain than the others.
**Meager Stores:** A small cache of food and candles is tucked behind the door, nothing valuable (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** a carved arch, west -> D.7 The Ward-Threshold, a low arch, north -> D.17 The Sealed Gallery, a carved arch, east -> D.18 The Priest-King's Rest
