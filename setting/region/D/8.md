D.8 **Procession Passage** (low) - *The Unlettered Dead, Procession Width*
Cut single-file, exactly as wide as the priest-kings' own procession rite required and no wider, this passage runs between the gallery and the two bound thresholds beyond it.
*A passage sixty feet long and barely three feet wide, cut for one person at a time rather than a crowd. Smells of old ash, carried down from the Sky Well above; footsteps here carry two tiers in either direction, exactly as they do everywhere else on this tier.*
**Procession Grooves:** Two shallow grooves run the passage floor its full length, worn by generations of feet that all walked the same line rather than spreading to fill the width available. They stop six feet short of the western threshold, all at once, as though the walking did.
**Trodden Groove:** Something small was dropped into one of the grooves and trodden flat there by everyone who came after, until it became part of the floor (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open passage - basalt, single-file, north end -> D.9 Inscribed Gallery, open archway - basalt, worn threshold, west end -> D.7 First Threshold, open archway - basalt, worn threshold, east end -> D.10 Second Threshold
