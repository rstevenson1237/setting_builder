D.8 **Procession Passage** (low) - *The Unlettered Dead, Procession Width*
Cut single-file, exactly as wide as the procession rite required, and no wider. **Procession grooves** run the floor its full length.
*A passage sixty feet long and barely three feet wide, cut for one person at a time. Smells of old ash, carried down from the Sky Well above, and footsteps carry two tiers, in either direction.*
**Procession Grooves:** Two shallow grooves run the full length, worn by feet that all walked one line, stopping six feet short of the western threshold.
**Trodden Groove:** Something small was dropped into one groove, and trodden flat, until it became part of the floor (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open passage - basalt, single-file, north end -> D.9 Inscribed Gallery, open archway - basalt, worn threshold, west end -> D.7 First Threshold, open archway - basalt, worn threshold, east end -> D.10 Second Threshold
