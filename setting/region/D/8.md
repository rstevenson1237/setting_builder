D.8 **The Ravine Falls** (Natural) - *roaring, slick, hidden*
Hidden falls roar into a slick ravine, spray hanging and echoes filling the narrow floor under rock ledges.
*200 by 500 feet. The ravine floods within a watch of rain.*
**The ravine floor:** narrow, slick and under the falls; a rising roar means the water is coming, and a party caught in it takes a Wound (Test of Constitution, 2d, Suffocation).
**The rock ledges:** shelves on the ravine walls above the water line, wet but clear of the flood.
