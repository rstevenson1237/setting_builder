D.8 **The Kings' Stair** (Low) - *steep, carved, narrow*

A steep stair comes down between the carved feet of two kings onto a stone sill. Grit trickles from above.

*A stair 10 feet wide and 20 long, where the hidden stair from the mural wall (C.3) lands in the founders' halls. The doorjambs at the sill hide darts.*

**The Kings' Feet:** Carved sandals as tall as a man, a name on each toe-ring.
**The Sill:** Smooth stone, the jambs either side pricked with pinholes. Touching the sill or jambs sets off darts (Test of Constitution, 1d, Piercing). The tomb robbers step over it.

**Exits:** an open passage -> D.2 The Hall of Founders, up the hidden stair -> C.3 The Mural Wall
