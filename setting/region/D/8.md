D.8 **The Granary Vaults** (medium) - *Sealed, Unspoiling, Wrong*
Grain sacks line the **Granary Vaults**, centuries old and somehow still whole. Something moves in the deepest bin - too many legs, and it doesn't die easy.
*A vaulted storeroom twenty feet deep past the Ward-Threshold, grain sacks stacked floor to ceiling on iron racks; smells of dry grain rather than rot, unnervingly fresh for its age.*
**Wrong Vermin:** A nest of rat-things fills the deepest bin, too many legs, slow to die and quick to bite.
**Preserved Grain:** Sacks stacked to the ceiling, whole and dry despite the centuries - the same stillness that hangs over the priest-king's own rest deeper in.
**Sealed Bin:** One bin, sealed rather than stacked, holds coin set aside with the grain (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** a plain arch, the only way in or out -> D.7 The Ward-Threshold
