D.8 **Procession Passage** (low) - *The Unlettered Dead, Procession Width*
Cut single-file, exactly as wide as the priest-kings' own procession rite required and no wider, this passage runs between the Inscribed Gallery and the two bound thresholds beyond it.
*A passage sixty feet long and barely three feet wide, cut for one person at a time rather than a crowd. Smells of old ash, carried down from the Sky Well above; footsteps here carry two tiers in either direction, exactly as they do everywhere else on this tier.*
**Procession Grooves:** Two shallow grooves run the passage floor its full length, worn by generations of feet that all walked the same line rather than spreading to fill the width available.
**Wedged Coin:** A single old coin, dropped and trodden flat into one procession groove, still legible enough to date it to well before Telar's own crown existed (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open passage - basalt, single-file, north end -> D.9 Inscribed Gallery, open archway - basalt, worn threshold, west end -> D.7 First Threshold, open archway - basalt, worn threshold, east end -> D.10 Second Threshold
