D.11 **Ash-Choked Cellar** (medium) - *Sunken City, Old Ash*
A charnel pit from the priest-kings' reign, its floor banked knee-deep in cremation-ash. The **ash-choke** is loose under the crust, lifts at a footfall, and does not settle while anyone moves.
*A charnel pit thirty-five feet across, ash banked knee-deep in drifts, against the walls. Smells of old ash before anything else, thick enough to taste.*
**Ash-Choke:** The drifts are crusted on top, and loose beneath, a step through the crust -> the whole room goes into the air, light and tracks and each other with it (Choking, 1, Bestiary : Ash-Choke).
**Fresh Debris:** Two piles of newer rubble stand out, against the old drifts, one under the hall's breach, one under the gallery's chute.
**Undisturbed Corner:** One corner's ash lies level and unbroken, untouched by either collapse, digging into it -> the layer the original rite left (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, intact, north side -> D.10 Second Threshold, open archway - basalt, intact, west side -> D.14 North Service Tunnel
