D.11 **Ash-Choked Cellar** (medium) - *Sunken City, Old Ash*
A charnel pit from the priest-kings' own reign, its floor banked knee-deep in centuries of cremation-ash. The **ash itself** is the danger here: it is still loose under the crust, it lifts at a footfall, and it does not settle again while anyone in the room is moving.
*A charnel pit thirty-five feet across, ash banked knee-deep in drifts against the walls. Smells of old ash before anything else, thick enough here to taste; nothing above the Middle tier smells quite like this room.*
**Ash-Choke:** The drifts are crusted on top and loose beneath, and a single step through the crust puts the whole room in the air within moments - light, tracks and each other all go together, and the way out is whichever direction someone was facing when it lifted (Choking, 1, Bestiary : Ash-Choke).
**Fresh Debris:** Two piles of newer rubble and grit stand out against the old drifts - one under the Roofless Hall's breach, one under the gallery's chute - each marking exactly where something arriving from above lands.
**Undisturbed Corner:** One corner's ash lies untouched by either collapse, its surface still level and unbroken, and digging into it reaches the layer the original rite left rather than anything since (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, intact, north side -> D.10 Second Threshold, open archway - basalt, intact, west side -> D.14 North Service Tunnel
