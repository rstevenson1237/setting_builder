D.11 **The Serpent Posts** (Ruin) - *carved, weathered, coiled*
A line of posts carved with coiled serpents stands at the jungle's edge above a bare track, vines curtaining the gap, no birds calling.
*300 by 60 feet. Guides turn back here; a villager who crosses is cast out. The posts are being re-carved, and the cult's patrols use the track beyond them.*
**The carved line:** weathered stone carved with coiled serpents, some re-cut with fresh edges; no guide passes it.
**The bare track:** packed earth running into the eastern jungle.
**Exits:** the track east past the posts -> E.1 The Serpent Road
