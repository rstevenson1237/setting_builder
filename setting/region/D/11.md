D.11 **The Tilting Hall** (Medium) - *long, balanced, creaking*

A long narrow hall, its floor a single slab balanced on a stone ridge. It creaks as weight moves along it.

*A hall 40 feet long and 10 wide. The floor pivots at its middle: one walker crossing the middle alone tips it, and the walker slides into the stone at the low end (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing). Two crossing together keep it level. The tomb robbers know the trick.*

**The Floor:** One slab, a ridge underfoot at the middle, scrape marks at both ends.
**The Ends:** A step down at each door, worn by the slab's knocking.

**Exits:** a stone door -> D.4 The Scraped Hall, an open passage -> D.12 The Hall of Hammers
