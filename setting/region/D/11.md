D.11 **The Undercroft Passage** (low) - *Looping, Low-Ceiling, Damp*
Old drainage channels run along both walls of the **Undercroft Passage**, long dry, the ceiling low enough to brush a raised hand.
*A cramped corridor thirty feet long, ceiling under six feet throughout, drainage channels cut into the floor on both sides; smells faintly of old waste long since dried to nothing.*
**Drainage Channels:** Cut into the floor on both sides, bone-dry, leading toward the Weeping Wall.
**Low Ceiling:** Barely six feet throughout - anyone tall enough to mind stoops the whole way.
**Exits:** the low passage, south -> D.10 The Watcher's Alcove, the low passage, north -> D.12 The Weeping Wall
