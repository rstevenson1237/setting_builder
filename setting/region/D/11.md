D.11 **Ash-Choked Cellar** (low) - *Sunken City, Old Ash*
A charnel pit from the priest-kings' own reign, its floor already banked in centuries of cremation-ash before the Roofless Hall's collapse and the Inscribed Gallery's own chute added fresh rubble and grit on top.
*A charnel pit thirty-five feet across, ash banked knee-deep in drifts against the walls. Smells of old ash before anything else, thick enough here to taste; nothing above the Middle tier smells quite like this room.*
**Ash Drifts:** Centuries of banked cremation-ash, disturbed only where the Roofless Hall's rubble and the Inscribed Gallery's chute have each dumped fresh debris on top of the old.
**Undisturbed Corner:** One corner's ash lies untouched by either collapse, its surface still level and unbroken. Digging into it turns up old bone-fragments and a handful of grave-goods the original rite left behind, never scattered by anything since (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, intact, north side -> D.10 Second Threshold, open archway - basalt, intact, west side -> D.14 North Service Tunnel
