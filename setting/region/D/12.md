D.12 **Sealed Archive** (medium) - *The Unlettered Dead, Confining Passage*
Essath scroll-cases line this narrow room's shelves, still sealed against a working that has outlasted every reader who might once have known better than to disturb it. A pale residue rings the shelf nearest the door.
*A single-file room, six feet wide, cut deep enough to hold three ranks of shelving to the ceiling. Smells of old dust and something faintly bitter, worse near the marked shelf; nothing here has moved in longer than anyone left to move it.*
**Warded Shelving:** A faint pale residue rings the nearest shelf's cases - a warning to anyone who has seen what that residue means elsewhere. Drawing a case from a warded shelf without first clearing the ring releases a fine choking spore, an Essath preservative ward five centuries stale and still working (Damaging; drawing a warded case without clearing the residue first -> a coughing fit and a lungful of old spores).
**Cleared Shelf:** One shelf's ring has already been swept clear, at some point, by somebody who knew to do it - its case sits removed and never returned, resting open on the floor before it.
**Sealed Scroll-Case:** Behind a loose panel at the third shelf's base, one case the residue never reached - a fragmentary Essath tally, damaged but legible in places (Lore: The Last Tally).
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, sealed panel ajar, south side -> D.10 Second Threshold
