D.12 **The Wall Ridge** (Natural) - *high, trampled, open*
A high bare ridge lies trampled on the hills' southern side, wind rolling over it, drums far off and huge three-toed prints stamped in the dung.
*600 by 200 feet. Far to the south, thirteen miles off, the great wall's gate-tower shows on the peninsula's neck on a clear day. The trail to the gate starts here.*
**The trampled slope:** bare ground beaten flat by a huge tread, the trail to the gate running south off it.
**Kunotewa:** a forty-foot reptile on two legs stands here at dawn, back from the gate (Named Creature: Kunotewa).
**The dung heaps:** droppings in piles among huge prints.
**Exits:** the trail south, down out of the hills to the wall -> A.1 The Great Gate
