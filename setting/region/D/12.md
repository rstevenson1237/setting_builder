D.12 **The Wall Ridge** (Natural) - *high, cleared, open*
A high cleared ridge lies bare above the great wall, wind rolling over it, drums far off and huge three-toed prints stamped in the dung.
*600 by 200 feet. The ridge drops to the great gate, and the villages watch it.*
**The cleared slope:** bare ground cleared by the villages, with a view of the wall's gate-tower.
**Kunotewa:** a forty-foot reptile on two legs stands here at dawn (Named Creature: Kunotewa).
**The dung heaps:** droppings in piles among huge prints.
**Exits:** a slope down to the wall -> A.1 The Great Gate
