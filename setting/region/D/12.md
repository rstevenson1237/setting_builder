D.12 **The Weeping Wall** (low) - *Seeping, Mineral-Streaked, Cold*
Mineral-streaked water seeps constantly down one wall in the **Weeping Wall**, pooling into a worn stone basin below. The water is cold enough to numb fingers quickly.
*A small chamber twelve feet across, one wall stained white and rust-orange from centuries of seepage into a carved basin; smells mineral and faintly sweet, cold enough to see breath.*
**Weeping Wall:** Water seeps constantly from cracks, mineral-streaked, feeding the basin below.
**Old Basin:** Carved to catch the seepage, still holds clean, ice-cold water fit to drink.
**Exits:** the low passage, south -> D.11 The Undercroft Passage, a low passage, west -> D.5 The Flooded Colonnade
