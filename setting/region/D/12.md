D.12 **The Hall of Hammers** (Medium) - *narrow, carved, swinging*

Carved carvers line a narrow hall, six a side, each with a stone mallet raised overhead. Chips of carved stone lie scattered on the floor.

*A hall 30 feet by 10, the back way into the gallery. Each mallet swings down on a carved chip trodden beneath it (Test of Constitution, 3d, Crushing). The bare stones between the chips are safe; the Scholars know it.*

**The Carvers:** Twelve figures, their mallets on stone arms that grind in the walls.
**The Chips:** Small carved stones scattered under every mallet, scars in the floor round them.

**Exits:** an open passage -> D.11 The Tilting Hall, a stone door -> D.6 The Gallery of the Fall
