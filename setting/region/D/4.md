D.4 **The Scraped Hall** (Medium) - *defaced, cold, silent*

Murals gouged to bare stone cover every wall, drifts of pale dust lying below. Water drips from the ceiling.

*A hall 30 feet across, its corners cut back, at the heart of the scraped halls, where the kings were painted. Every face and name has been scraped off. The dead that walk here are this area's challenge.*

**The Murals:** Scraped to the plaster, a veiled figure still visible at a king's elbow (The Demon Is Invited). The Scholars copy what is left; a grey eye scratched by the door is theirs (Faction : Sihuahet).
**The Dust:** Thick and pale; footprints show clearly in it, going in and out.
**The Dead:** Several zombies shamble the hall (Hostile, 4-8, Bestiary : Zombie). A wet dragging sound comes before them.

**Exits:** a winding passage -> D.3 The Robbers' Watch, an open passage -> D.10 The Dust Room, a stone door -> D.11 The Tilting Hall
