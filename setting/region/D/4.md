D.4 **Wind-Worn Colonnade** (low) - *Standing Stones, Basalt Cut*
A processional colonnade runs between the Collapsed Portico and the Sky Well, its columns wind-scoured smoother on their northern faces than anywhere else in the ziggurat.
*A colonnade sixty feet long and ten wide, roofless like the rest of the Upper tier, a dozen basalt columns still standing in an even row. Smells of clean, wind-scoured stone; the wind that funnels up the South Stair crosses this room on its way to the Sky Well.*
**Wind-Scoured Columns:** Each column's northern face has been sanded smoother than its other three sides by the same wind, over more time than the priest-kings' own reign lasted.
**Column Base Cache:** One column's base has cracked along an old seam, wide enough to have collected windblown grit, old coin, and small debris over centuries (Treasure I, d20) - nothing hidden here, only accumulated.
**Exits:** open colonnade - basalt, west end -> D.5 Collapsed Portico, open colonnade - basalt, east end -> D.6 Sky Well
