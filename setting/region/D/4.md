D.4 **The Python Tree** (Lair) - *vast, hung, coiled*
A vast fig tree stands hung with coils of vine, its buttress roots wide, shed skins in the leaf-litter and creaking from the canopy.
*200 by 200 feet. The python drops on what passes beneath.*
**The python:** a thirty-foot mottled snake hanging in the fig's branches (Hostile, 1, Bestiary : Giant Python); a sloughed skin lies on the trunk.
**The buttress roots:** walls of wood wet with drip, shielding hollows in the trunk.
**The shed skins:** drifted deep in the leaf-litter, a dozen sloughs of different ages.
