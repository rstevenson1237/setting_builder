D.4 **Wind-Worn Colonnade** (low) - *Standing Stones, Basalt Cut*
A processional colonnade runs east off the Collapsed Portico and stops at a blank wall. Its **wind-scoured columns** are sanded smooth on their northern faces.
*A colonnade sixty feet long and ten wide, roofless like the rest of the Upper tier, a dozen basalt columns still standing in an even row. Smells of clean, wind-scoured stone, and the wind funnelling up the South Stair crosses this room, and does not come back out.*
**Wind-Scoured Columns:** Each column's northern face is sanded smoother, than its other three sides, by the same wind.
**Twelfth Column:** The twelfth column stands against the blank wall, too close to bear anything, its base turning on a floor socket, walking it a quarter-turn -> a man-wide gap opens, onto the Sky Well's rim.
**Column Base Cache:** Another column's base has cracked, along an old seam, wide enough to have collected windblown grit (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open colonnade - basalt, twelve columns standing, west end -> D.5 Collapsed Portico, open archway - basalt, plain and unmoulded, southwest corner -> D.1 South Stair, vertical stair - basalt, eight steps up between two columns, north side -> D.2 Upper Terrace
