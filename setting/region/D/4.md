D.4 **Wind-Worn Colonnade** (low) - *Standing Stones, Basalt Cut*
A processional colonnade runs east off the Collapsed Portico and stops at a blank wall - the Upper tier's one real junction, with the south stair's top landing at one corner and eight steps up to the terrace at the other. Its columns are wind-scoured smoother on their northern faces than anywhere else in the ziggurat.
*A colonnade sixty feet long and ten wide, roofless like the rest of the Upper tier, a dozen basalt columns still standing in an even row. Smells of clean, wind-scoured stone; the wind that funnels up the South Stair crosses this room and does not come back out of it.*
**Wind-Scoured Columns:** Each column's northern face has been sanded smoother than its other three sides by the same wind, over more time than the priest-kings' own reign lasted.
**Twelfth Column:** The colonnade ends at a blank east wall, and the twelfth column stands close enough to it to be pointless as a support - the wind that crosses this room goes past it and is not heard returning. Its base turns on a socket cut into the floor, and walking the column a quarter-turn opens a man-wide gap in the wall behind it, onto the Sky Well's rim. Nothing marks the socket; what marks the column is that the wind stops where it stands.
**Column Base Cache:** Another column's base has cracked along an old seam, wide enough to have collected windblown grit and small debris over centuries (Treasure I, d20).
