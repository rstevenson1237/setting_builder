D.4 **The Sinking Flat** (Natural) - *grey, soft, treacherous*
A level grey **flat** of mud and algae stretches east, with **tussocks** of sedge scattered across it and gas bubbling in the shallows.
*Half a mile across.*
**Flat:** A skin of grey algae covers soft mud, and gas rises from it; a crosser off the tussocks sinks and may drown (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing).
**Tussocks:** Firm clumps of sedge a stride apart across the flat.
