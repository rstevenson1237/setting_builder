D.4 **The Scraped Hall** (Medium) - *defaced, cold, silent*

Murals gouged to bare stone cover every wall, drifts of pale dust lying below. Water drips from the ceiling.

*A hall 40 feet square where the kings were painted. Every face and name has been scraped off; the Scholars copy what is left.*

**The Murals:** Scraped to the plaster, a veiled figure still visible at a king's elbow (The Demon Is Invited). An adept of the Scholars copies it (Faction : Sihuahet).
**The Dust:** Thick and pale; footprints show clearly in it, going in and out.
**The Dead:** Several zombies shamble the hall (Hostile, 4-8, Bestiary : Zombie). A wet dragging sound comes before them.

**Exits:** an open passage, west, 60 feet -> D.2 The Hall of Founders, a stone door, south, 60 feet -> D.6 The Gallery of the Fall
