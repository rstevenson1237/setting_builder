D.4 **The Scribes' Hall** (medium) - *Old-Tongue, Ledgered, Warded*
Collapsed shelving and scattered scroll-cases fill the **Scribes' Hall**, one cabinet still sealed and marked with a scratched warding. A stamped case lies apart from the rubble, untouched by the collapse.
*A long, narrow room forty feet by twelve, shelving collapsed along both walls; smells of old parchment gone to dust, the floor gritty with decayed scroll-cases underfoot.*
**Warded Shelf:** A scratched ward-mark on one cabinet door; defaced first it opens safe, forced outright it bursts preserving dust that chokes the room (Damaging).
**Collapsed Shelving:** Rotted shelving and broken cases line both walls, mostly illegible.
**Stamped Case:** One case, untouched by the collapse, bears the same seal as a sarcophagus somewhere deeper.
**Intact Roster:** Inside the warded cabinet, a legible work-roster survives (Lore: The Temple Roster).
**Exits:** a wide arch, east -> D.2 The Antechamber of Masks, a collapsed gap in the far wall, west -> D.13 The Fallen Colonnade
