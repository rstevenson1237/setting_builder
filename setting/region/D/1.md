D.1 **South Stair** (low) - *Old Road, Broken Stair*
The ziggurat's formal entrance climbs the south face in broken, uneven flights, still passable in pieces where the stone hasn't sheared away entirely. From the top step, Marfen's boundary cairns show small and distant to the south.
*A stair hall open to the sky along its outer edge, forty feet of climb across three broken flights, cut into the south face rather than built against it. Smells of open air and cold stone; wind funnels up the stairwell hard enough to be heard before it's felt.*
**Broken Flights:** The lowest flight has sheared away in two places, forcing a climb over loose rubble rather than a walk up cut steps - slow, but not dangerous to anyone paying attention to their footing.
**Sight Line South:** From partway up the stair, the boundary cairns and the causeway beyond them are visible on a clear day - the only point in the whole ziggurat with a sightline out rather than in.
**Counted Risers:** The third flight's risers are cut in groups, a deep-scored line every seventh step, all the way up - and the scoring continues past where the stone has sheared away, so the count the priest-kings walked can still be read off the surviving stretches. Anyone who has stood at a boundary cairn and read its own scored count will recognise the hand.
