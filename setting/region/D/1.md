D.1 **The Causeway** (Route) - *raised, slick, narrow*
A causeway of planks a yard wide runs north on black **piles**, with **passing bays** every hundred yards and no rails. **Watchers** stand in the reeds.
*Three miles long; the planks nearest the road are the newest.*
**Piles:** Driven deep and black with weed; reed bundles tied with red thread hang from every tenth one.
**Passing Bays:** Wider spurs every hundred yards where two parties pass or a camp stops.
**Watchers:** Four lizard-folk with fish-bone spears let those who keep to the planks go by (Wary, 4, Bestiary : Lizard Man); webbed footprints mark the mud at the edges.
**Crossing:** A party can walk the planks in a watch, or hire a pole boat from the landings for 30 cn and a spear's escort; a plank under a heavy load gives way and drops the crosser into the water (Local Procedure: Crossing by Causeway or Pole Boat).
**Exits:** planked, south end of the causeway -> B.5 The Causeway Head, open, north end of the causeway -> C.6 The Mere Landing
