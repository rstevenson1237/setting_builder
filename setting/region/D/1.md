D.1 **The Footing-Shaft Landing** (low) - *Reopened, Boarded, Watched*
Rough-cut wooden bracing shores up the **Footing-Shaft Landing**, a hole punched down through Carrdun's own foundations into older stone below. A rope ladder and a windlass basket are the only way down.
*A cramped landing ten feet across at the shaft's bottom, timber-braced where the diggers cut through, opening onto finished Nemhet stone beyond; smells of fresh-cut wood over older damp, voices carrying faintly down from the surface.*
**Shaft Mouth:** A rope ladder and windlass basket connect up to Carrdun's own foundations, the crews' only way in or out.
**Timber Bracing:** Raw, recently cut lumber shores the shaft's upper reach - everything below it is centuries older.
**Crew Marks:** Chalk tallies count days worked; the count stopped abruptly two winters back, then restarted under a new hand.
**Exits:** the windlass shaft, straight up -> A.6 The Quartermaster's Stores, a low passage, deeper in -> D.2 The Antechamber of Masks
