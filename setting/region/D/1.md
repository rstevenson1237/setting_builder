D.1 **The Stair Landing** (Low) - *dusty, watched, narrow*

A narrow landing lies under a carved lintel, torchlight and low voices coming from the archway east. A red hawk is painted on the doorpost.

*A landing 10 feet by 20 feet, where the stair from C.4 comes down 20 feet. Scouts of the Royal Guard hold the east arch; the robbers' watch is south.*

**The Doorpost:** A red hawk painted at head height, the mark of Kurumisa (Faction : Kurumisa). The Royal Guard challenge "Who holds the gate?"; the answer is "The gate holds."
**The Scouts:** Two soldiers in bronze scale patrol the east archway (Wary, 2, Faction : Kurumisa). They want the halls cleared of robbers and will trade a guide for supplies.
**The Stair:** Worn smooth in the middle of each step, leading up 20 feet to C.4.

**Exits:** an archway, east, 60 feet -> D.2 The Hall of Founders, a wooden door, south, 60 feet -> D.3 The Robbers' Watch, up a stair, south, 40 feet -> C.4 The Plinth
