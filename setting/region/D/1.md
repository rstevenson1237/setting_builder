D.1 **The Hill Trail** (Route) - *worn, rutted, muddy*
A muddy game trail climbs from the swamp's edge into jungle, ferns and ravines on both sides, insects loud and distant hoots rolling from the ridges.
*Two miles a watch on a game trail, leaving a track a cave men's band can follow; a party without a guide takes a Test of Fate for Lost each day. The crossing is two days.*
**The trail head:** at the way in, a rutted game trail climbs into the jungle.
**The trail watchers:** short-bowed pair with a dog tracking a pig and watching the party, blue-black net patterns on their forearms (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Hill Hunter); they want word of the lost party and know where the cave men's fires burn.
**The waterhole track:** 0.8 miles along, trampled mud and torn ferns lead to D.7.
**The clearing's edge:** 1.4 miles along, huge bones and dung lie in the open; the track leads to D.3.
**The hunters' cairn:** 2 miles along, a stacked cairn and a snapped bow beyond lead to D.10.
**Exits:** down into the swamp's edge -> B.8 The Last Dry Rise
