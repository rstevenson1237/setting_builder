D.1 **The Stair Landing** (Low) - *dusty, watched, narrow*

A narrow landing lies under a trapdoor in the roof, torchlight and low voices coming from an archway. A red hawk is painted on the doorpost.

*A landing 10 feet by 20 feet in the robbers' halls, where the folding stair from the tile floor (C.2) comes down 20 feet. Scouts of the Royal Guard hold the archway to the founders' halls; the robbers' watch is behind the wooden door.*

**The Trapdoor:** Twelve tiles' undersides form the roof; a cord hangs from it, and pulled it folds the stair down or up.
**The Doorpost:** A red hawk painted at head height, the Royal Guard's mark (Faction : Kurumisa). The Royal Guard challenge "Who holds the gate?"; the answer is "The gate holds."
**The Scouts:** Two soldiers in bronze scale patrol the archway (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Soldier). They want the halls cleared of robbers and will trade a guide for supplies.

**Exits:** a wooden door -> D.3 The Robbers' Watch, an archway -> D.9 The Hall of Names, up the folding stair -> C.2 The Tile Floor
