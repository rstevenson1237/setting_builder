D.1 **South Stair** (low) - *Old Road, Broken Stair*
The ziggurat's formal entrance climbs the south face in **broken flights**, passable where the stone has not sheared away. From partway up, Marfen's boundary cairns show small to the south.
*A stair hall open to the sky along its outer edge, forty feet of climb across three broken flights, cut into the south face rather than built against it. Smells of open air and cold stone, and wind funnels up the stairwell, heard before it is felt.*
**Broken Flights:** The lowest flight has sheared away, in two places, forcing a climb over loose rubble.
**Sight Line South:** From partway up the stair, the boundary cairns and the causeway show, on a clear day.
**Counted Risers:** The third flight's risers are cut in groups, a deep-scored line every seventh step, the scoring continuing past the sheared stone.
**Exits:** open stair - basalt, broken in two flights, south face -> D.5 Collapsed Portico, open archway - basalt, plain and unmoulded, top landing east side -> D.4 Wind-Worn Colonnade, open ground, south, down the hillside -> B.2 Boundary Cairns
