D.6 **Sky Well** (high) - *Elder Sorcery, Struck Light*
A shaft cut straight down through all three tiers opens here to the sky above and the Middle tier's gallery below, its rim polished to a gloss no tool made. Light struck at this rim shows from the causeway in Marfen, four miles off - which is how anyone outside Daghash knows the ziggurat is not empty.
*A circular shaft-head fifteen feet across, the floor around it laid in a single unbroken ring of basalt with no joint anywhere in it - the only such cut in the ziggurat, and beyond the masonry that built everything else here. Smells of open air, out of place this deep in; the shaft carries sound both up and down far past where it should fade.*
**Unjointed Rim:** The ring of floor around the shaft is one piece of stone, fifteen feet across and cut without a seam. Every other surface in Daghash is jointed work, well made and plainly assembled; this was not assembled.
**Shaft Edge:** No rail or kerb marks where the floor gives way. A careless step at the rim, or one taken in poor light -> a thirty-foot fall down the shaft to D.9 (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Struck Light:** The rim's inner face is polished to a gloss, all the way round, and throws back any light brought near it hard enough to be seen from the causeway. The polish is not wear - it has no direction, and it stops dead at the joint where the unjointed ring ends. The party can make the ziggurat visible for four miles by standing here with a torch, and nothing here says who else has.
**Worn Hauling-Point:** One stretch of rim is grooved where a rope ran, for longer than any single tenancy here explains - old work, from before either the priest-kings' fall or Nirguk's arrival, and the grooves run inward rather than outward. Whatever the rope carried was being brought up.
**Wedged Below:** Something small went down the shaft and never came out, caught in a crack a third of the way down - reachable by climbing partway rather than falling the whole distance (Treasure I, d20).
**Rim-Cut Socket:** Set flush into the unjointed ring on the colonnade side, a shallow square socket holds a basalt plug worked to the same gloss as the rim. Levered out, the plug leaves a gap a person can turn sideways through, onto the colonnade's blind east end (Treasure III, d20 - the plug itself, which is not basalt all the way through).
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, intact, southwest side -> D.5 Collapsed Portico, vertical shaft - basalt, worn smooth on one side, center -> D.9 Inscribed Gallery
