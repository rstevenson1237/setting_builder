D.6 **The Gallery of the Fall** (High) - *painted, vast, ominous*

A vast gallery is hung with paintings in order, west to east, each king's face blacked out. A low hum comes from overhead, and armoured figures stand before the last panel.

*A hall 60 feet by 50 and 20 high, the end of the second level. The last panel hides a stair down (E.1). The skeletons keep the stair.*

**The Paintings:** Nine panels, from a king at table with a veiled guest to a stair going down (The Demon Is Invited).
**The Guard:** Skeletons stand in bronze-green armour (Hostile, 6-12, Bestiary : Skeleton). They move together if a panel is touched or an alarm sounds.
**The Last Panel:** Touching the veiled guest's worn patch slides the panel aside, showing a stair down (E.1).
**The Niche:** Behind the paint, a hollow holds a scroll (Lore: The Gallery of the Fall).

**Exits:** a stone door -> D.4 The Scraped Hall, an archway -> D.5 The Carvers' Cells, down a stair -> E.1 The Stair Landing
