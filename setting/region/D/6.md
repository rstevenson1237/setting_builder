D.6 **The Sinkhole** (Lair) - *rooted, rank, deep*
A sinkhole falls into rank darkness, its sides hung with roots, a rope-ladder lookout on its rim and a rotten smell rising from the dark.
*150 by 150 feet. The drop is fifty feet to the warren's floor. The troglodytes hold the caves below.*
**The lookouts:** three grey-skinned lizard-faced troglodytes on the rope-ladder lookout at night, none by day (Hostile, 3, Bestiary : Troglodyte).
**The root ladder:** roots and rope hang down the shaft, the way to the caves.
**Exits:** down the roots -> J.1 The Sinkhole Floor
