D.6 **The Silt-Choked Stair** (low) - *Descending, Silted, Slick*
Silt has choked half the steps on this stair, slick and treacherous underfoot. A collapsed section on one side drops straight through into darkness below.
*A stair fifteen feet long descending a full story, silt-slicked steps throughout; smells of wet stone, a gap in the outer wall opening onto a straight drop.*
**Silted Steps:** Every step is slick with fine silt; careless footing here is how most falls happen.
**Collapsed Gap:** A breach in the outer wall drops straight down into a cistern below - one-way, and a long way down.
**Exits:** the stair, up -> D.3 The Cracked Passage, the stair, down -> D.7 The Ward-Threshold, the collapsed gap in the outer wall, dropping through -> D.14 The Empty Cistern
