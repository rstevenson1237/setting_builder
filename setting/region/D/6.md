D.6 **Sky Well** (high) - *Elder Sorcery, Struck Light*
A shaft cut straight down through all three tiers opens to the sky above and the gallery below. Its **rim** is polished to a gloss, and light struck here shows from the causeway, four miles off.
*A circular shaft-head fifteen feet across, the floor around it laid in one unbroken ring of basalt, with no joint in it. Smells of open air, out of place this deep in, and the shaft carries sound both up, and down.*
**Unjointed Rim:** The ring of floor around the shaft, is one piece, fifteen feet across, cut without a seam, where every other surface here is jointed work.
**Struck Light:** The rim's inner face is polished round, throwing back any light brought near it, hard enough to be seen from the causeway.
**Shaft Edge:** No rail or kerb marks the edge, where the floor gives way, a careless step at the rim -> a thirty-foot fall to D.9 (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Worn Hauling-Point:** One stretch of rim is grooved, where a rope ran, the grooves running inward rather than outward.
**Rim-Cut Socket:** Set flush into the ring, on the colonnade side, a shallow square socket holds a basalt plug, levered out -> a gap onto the colonnade's blind east end (Treasure III, d20).
**Wedged Below:** Something small went down the shaft, and caught in a crack, a third of the way down (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, intact, southwest side -> D.5 Collapsed Portico, vertical shaft - basalt, worn smooth on one side, center -> D.9 Inscribed Gallery
