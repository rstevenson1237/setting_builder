D.6 **Sky Well** (medium) - *Elder Sorcery, Struck Light*
A shaft cut straight down through all three tiers opens here to the sky above and the Middle tier's Inscribed Gallery below - the one place in Daghash open enough that a struck light shows all the way to the causeway in Marfen at night.
*A circular shaft-head fifteen feet across, its rim worn smooth on one side where something has been hauled up or lowered many times. Smells of open air, out of place this deep in; the shaft carries sound both up and down far past where it should fade.*
**Shaft Edge:** No rail or kerb marks where the floor gives way to the shaft itself - a party moving carelessly in poor light risks the same thirty-foot drop the shaft offers on purpose (Damaging; a careless step at the rim -> a fall down the shaft to D.9).
**Worn Hauling-Point:** The rim's smoothed edge marks where a rope has run for longer than any single tenancy here explains - old work, from before either the priest-kings' fall or Nirguk's arrival.
**Dropped Below:** Something small, dropped down the shaft and never retrieved, wedged in a crack a third of the way down - reachable only by climbing partway rather than falling the whole distance (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, southwest side -> D.5 Collapsed Portico, open archway - basalt, south side -> D.4 Wind-Worn Colonnade, vertical shaft - basalt, worn smooth on one side, center -> D.9 Inscribed Gallery
