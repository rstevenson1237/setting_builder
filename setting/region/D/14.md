D.14 **The Empty Cistern** (low) - *Dry, Echoing, Deep*
The **Empty Cistern** is a deep, dry stone pit, walls sheer except for a rope-scarred groove where something has climbed in and out before. The only way up is a hard climb.
*A round cistern fifteen feet across and twelve deep, dry and long unused, walls scored where past climbers have gone hand over hand; smells of dust, every sound echoing flatly off the curved walls.*
**Sheer Walls:** Smooth stone on every side but one groove scored by generations of climbers.
**Climbing Groove:** Worn hand and footholds lead back up to the stair above - slow, but climbable.
**Dry Floor:** Bare stone, swept clean by whatever falls through - nothing valuable has survived being dropped in.
**Exits:** the scored climbing groove, a hard climb up -> D.6 The Silt-Choked Stair
