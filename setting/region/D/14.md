D.14 **North Service Tunnel** (low) - *Man-Apes, Hillside Approach*
Cut into the hillside rather than the ziggurat's own face, this half-collapsed culvert was never part of the priest-kings' formal procession - a service way, built plain, opening onto the same slope Marfen's Man-Ape ground borders.
*A culvert six feet wide and half-collapsed along its outer third, forty feet from the hillside opening to the cellar within. Smells of wet earth rather than old ash, this close to the outside; the collapse muffles sound coming from either direction.*
**Half-Collapsed Roof:** The tunnel's outer third has come down in slabs, leaving a crouching gap rather than a clean passage - slow going, but not blocked.
**Scale-Polished Patch:** Low on the tunnel's inner wall, past the collapse, a hand's-width patch of stone is worn smooth by scale rather than tool or weather - out of place on rough-cut culvert stone, and the only worked-looking thing in a tunnel that was never worked. Pressing flat against it and pushing swings a section of wall inward on a hidden pivot, low enough to require crawling, onto a crude connecting passage that reaches the enclave's nursery without passing anywhere near either bound threshold above.
**Exits:** open culvert - rough-cut, half-collapsed, east end -> D.11 Ash-Choked Cellar, open ground, hillside opening, west end -> beyond, out onto the same slope Marfen's Man-Ape ground borders
