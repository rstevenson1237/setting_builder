D.14 **North Service Tunnel** (low) - *Man-Apes, Hillside Approach*
Cut into the hillside rather than the ziggurat's face, this **half-collapsed culvert** was never part of the formal procession. It opens onto the slope Marfen's Man-Ape ground borders.
*A culvert six feet wide and half-collapsed along its outer third, forty feet from the hillside opening to the cellar. Smells of wet earth rather than old ash, and the collapse muffles sound, from either direction.*
**Half-Collapsed Roof:** The outer third has come down in slabs, leaving a crouching gap, rather than a clean passage.
**Scale-Polished Patch:** Low on the inner wall past the collapse, a hand's-width patch worn smooth by scale, pressed and pushed -> a wall section pivots inward, onto a crawlway to the nursery.
**Exits:** open culvert - rough-cut, half-collapsed, east end -> D.11 Ash-Choked Cellar, open ground, hillside opening, west end -> beyond, out onto the same slope Marfen's Man-Ape ground borders
