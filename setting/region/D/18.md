D.18 **Nirguk's Hoard** (high) - *Serpent Cult, Unbroken Ward*
Where worked stone gives way to walls polished by scale, Nirguk's kept wealth sits in the open, under the **Stone Ward's** gaze. It stands dust-free in a room thick with it.
*A rectangular reliquary twenty-five feet across, cut to hold the priest-kings' sacred vessels, its walls still showing tool-marks, under the polish, its ceiling corbelled rather than vaulted. Smells of cold stone, and a draft with no source moves, the dust everywhere but across the Ward's feet.*
**Stone Ward:** Carved in Nirguk's own likeness, standing dust-free between the hoard and the door, inert until it acts, and stilled again by the hoard being returned (Ancient, 1, Bestiary : Stone Ward).
**Nirguk Guard:** Two or three keep the hoard chamber itself, coiled rather than standing, watching the door (Territorial, 3, Bestiary : Nirguk).
**Corbelled Ceiling:** Corbelled, and carrying something heavy directly above, where the ziggurat's own reckoning shows nothing.
**Kept Wealth:** Stacked rather than displayed, along the chamber's inner wall, taken in tribute and trade (Treasure V, d20).
**Near-Corner Chest:** A shallow chest pushed into the near corner, left where it was, rather than added to the stack (Treasure III, d20).
**Strung Collar:** Coiled loose atop the stacked wealth, small worked-stone links on a braided cord, cut with a script the enclave cannot read (Unique Treasure: The Kaash Collar).
**Exits:** open doorway - polished stone, single entrance -> D.15 Nirguk Warren
