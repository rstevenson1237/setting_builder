D.18 **Nirguk's Hoard** (high) - *Serpent Cult, Unbroken Ward*
Where the priest-kings' worked stone gives way to walls polished smooth by scale rather than tool, Nirguk's own kept wealth sits in the open under a **Stone Ward's** gaze - dust-free in a room thick with it everywhere else.
*A rectangular reliquary twenty-five feet across, cut to hold the priest-kings' own sacred vessels - its walls still show their tool-marks under the polish, and its ceiling is corbelled rather than vaulted, the only room in Daghash built to bear weight from directly above. Smells of cold stone that has never seen sun, sharper here than anywhere else in the enclave; a draft with no visible source moves the dust everywhere except across the Ward's own feet.*
**Stone Ward:** Carved in Nirguk's own likeness, standing dust-free between the hoard and the door - inert until whatever last named its trigger, giving no warning between inert and acting, and put back to stillness by the hoard being put back rather than by anything done to it (Ancient, 1, Bestiary : Stone Ward).
**Nirguk Guard:** Two or three of the enclave keep the hoard chamber itself rather than the warren beyond it, coiled rather than standing, watching the door before the Ward ever needs to (Territorial, 3, Bestiary : Nirguk).
**Corbelled Ceiling:** The ceiling steps inward in courses rather than arching, carrying something heavy directly above it - and there is nothing above this room on any reckoning of the ziggurat the party has been given.
**Kept Wealth:** Stacked rather than displayed, along the chamber's inner wall, and taken in tribute and trade over longer than anyone outside the enclave has kept count (Treasure V, d20).
**Near-Corner Chest:** A shallow chest pushed into the room's near corner and plainly never opened by whoever lives here, left where it was rather than added to the stack (Treasure III, d20).
**Strung Collar:** Coiled loose atop the stacked wealth rather than worn or displayed - small worked-stone links on a braided cord, cut with a script none of the enclave reads as their own, kept for what it plainly is rather than what it does (Unique Treasure: The Kaash Collar).
**Exits:** open doorway - polished stone, single entrance -> D.15 Nirguk Warren
