D.18 **The Priest-King's Rest** (high) - *Gilded, Guarded, Final*
Faded murals of a river god arch overhead in the **Priest-King's Rest**, incense-must still faint beneath the damp. A wrapped, gold-wired figure stands motionless before a sealed sarcophagus.
*A vaulted chamber twenty-five feet across, ceiling painted rather than carved - a river god's procession, faded but legible; smells faintly of old incense beneath the general damp, unlike anywhere else in Baldun.*
**Tomb Warden:** A bound priest-guardian in wire-thin gold stands before the sarcophagus, speaking only Nemhet - reasoned with on its own terms, never moved (Relentless, 1, Bestiary : Tomb Warden).
**River-God Mural:** Painted overhead, a procession leading to a throne - Nemhet cosmology, faded but whole.
**Sealed Sarcophagus:** Carved with the same lettering as the Ward-Threshold; prying it open without the Warden's leave triggers a dart set into its rim (Damaging).
**Grave Goods:** Scattered about the chamber's edges, plate and coin from the burial itself (Treasure V, d20).
**Priest-King's Diadem:** Resting in a niche behind the sarcophagus, untouched even by the Warden (Unique Treasure: The Priest-King's Diadem).
**Wall Epitaph:** A carved plaque names the priest-king - Werkamu, "the great departed one" - and states plainly how his line ended (Lore: The Priest-King's Epitaph).
**Exits:** a carved arch, the only way in or out -> D.16 The Coil's Vestibule
