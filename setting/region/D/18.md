D.18 **Nirguk's Hoard** (high) - *Serpent Cult, Unbroken Ward*
Where the priest-kings' worked stone gives way to walls polished smooth by scale rather than tool, Nirguk's own kept wealth sits in the open under a **Stone Ward's** gaze - dust-free in a room thick with it everywhere else.
*A rectangular reliquary twenty-five feet across, originally cut to hold the priest-kings' own sacred vessels - its walls still show their tool-marks under the polish. Smells of cold stone that has never seen sun, sharper here than anywhere else in the enclave; a draft with no visible source moves the dust everywhere except across the Ward's own feet.*
**Stone Ward:** Carved in Nirguk's own likeness, standing dust-free between the hoard and the door - inert until whatever last named its trigger, and giving no warning between inert and acting (Ancient, 1, Bestiary : Stone Ward).
**Nirguk Guard:** Two or three of the enclave keep the hoard chamber itself rather than the warren beyond it, coiled rather than standing, watching the door before the Ward ever needs to (Territorial, 3, Bestiary : Nirguk, the Serpent-kin).
**Kept Wealth:** Stacked rather than displayed, along the chamber's inner wall (Treasure V, d20) - Nirguk's own hoard, taken in tribute and trade over longer than anyone outside the enclave has kept count.
**Grave-Silver:** A shallow chest of old Essath coin, pushed into the room's near corner and plainly never touched since - worth its metal alone, and worthless to whoever actually lives here (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** open doorway - polished stone, single entrance -> D.15 Nirguk Warren
