D.9 **Inscribed Gallery** (medium) - *Pit of the Old Ones, Essath Inscription*
Essath script covers this scriptorium's walls floor to ceiling, ash-weathered by five centuries down the Sky Well. One **unworn panel**, low on the shaft-side wall, is as sharp as the day it was cut.
*A scriptorium fifty feet long, its walls cut in shallow panels, each holding its own inscribed passage. Smells of old ash settling down through the open shaft, and a dropped word is audible at the far end.*
**Unworn Panel:** One low panel's lettering stands sharp, where the panels around it have gone soft, reading it aloud or clearing the ash -> the working takes the reader's years, rather than forcing a Test, and the toll shows before the line ends (setting/Procedures.md).
**Inscribed Walls:** Essath lettering fills every other panel, plainly administrative, apart from one long passage describing a rite, in a round chamber coiled three times.
**Struck-Through Roster:** A panel low on the shaft-side wall, lists names in Essath, most struck through with the same tool, the final several left unstruck.
**Ash Chute:** A narrow opening low in the far corner, angled too steeply to climb, taking it -> a one-way drop into D.11's ash, landing prone in the drift (Test of Fate, Impact).
**Exits:** vertical shaft - basalt, worn smooth on one side, center -> D.6 Sky Well, open passage - basalt, single-file, south end -> D.8 Procession Passage, ash chute - basalt, steep, floor corner, one-way down -> D.11 Ash-Choked Cellar
