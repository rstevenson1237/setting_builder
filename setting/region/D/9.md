D.9 **The Broken Causeway Stair** (medium) - *Collapsed, Precarious, Old-Stone*
The **Broken Causeway Stair** crosses a deep shaft on a stretch of crumbled steps, half the span simply gone. Something dropped mid-crossing glints in the dark below.
*A stone stair spanning a twenty-foot shaft, roughly half its steps sheared away entirely; cold air rises steadily from below, and the drop is not visible to the bottom from the top.*
**Crumbled Span:** Half the steps are gone outright; the remaining ledge is a hand's width in places (Damaging).
**Shaft:** Cold air rises from below; the bottom isn't visible from the top, and nothing thrown in is heard landing.
**Dropped Gear:** Something glints on a ledge partway down, dropped by whoever crossed before and lost their footing (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** the crumbled span, south -> D.5 The Flooded Colonnade, the crumbled span, north -> D.10 The Watcher's Alcove
