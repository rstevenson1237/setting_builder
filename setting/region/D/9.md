D.9 **The Fern Glade** (Natural) - *still, green, dim*
A still glade of ferns lies round a steaming spring, dim and quiet, drips falling from rotting logs.
*400 by 300 feet. Dinosaurs come here to drink.*
**The warm spring:** steaming water in a stone-edged hollow; its water cures a Diseased drinker, and nothing more.
**The rotting logs:** fallen timber on the glade's edge, home to what lives in rot.
