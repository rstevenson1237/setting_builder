D.9 **Inscribed Gallery** (medium) - *Pit of the Old Ones, Essath Inscription*
Essath script covers this scriptorium's walls floor to ceiling, line after line of ritual record. Every panel is weathered by five centuries of ash sifting down the Sky Well - **except one**, low on the shaft-side wall, which is as sharp as the day it was cut.
*A scriptorium fifty feet long, its walls cut in shallow panels each holding its own inscribed passage. Smells of old ash settling down through the open shaft above; a dropped word here is audible at the passage's far end.*
**Unworn Panel:** One low panel's lettering stands sharp where every panel around it has gone soft with ash-wear - a working still sitting in the cut, five centuries after the hand that made it. Reading it aloud, tracing it, or clearing the ash off it finishes what the panel was cut to do -> the working takes the reader's own years rather than forcing a Test, per `setting/Procedures.md`'s cost of sorcery, and the toll shows on the body before anyone has finished the line.
**Inscribed Walls:** Essath lettering fills every other panel, most of it administrative - tallies, rosters, offerings recorded and struck through. One long passage near the shaft-side wall describes a rite completed in a round chamber, coiled three times, that the party may not have found yet.
**Struck-Through Roster:** A panel low on the shaft-side wall lists names in Essath, most struck through with the same tool - a roster of some kind, its final several names left unstruck.
**Ash Chute:** A narrow opening low in the floor's far corner, angled steeply down - too tight to climb back up, and too steep to descend except by sliding. Taking it -> a rough drop into D.11's ash below, one-way (Test of Fate, Impact) - the chute delivers whoever slid it prone in the drift, and getting up out of it is a turn nobody else can spend for them.
**Exits:** vertical shaft - basalt, worn smooth on one side, center -> D.6 Sky Well, open passage - basalt, single-file, south end -> D.8 Procession Passage, ash chute - basalt, steep, floor corner, one-way down -> D.11 Ash-Choked Cellar
