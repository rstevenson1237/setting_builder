D.9 **Inscribed Gallery** (low) - *Pit of the Old Ones, Essath Inscription*
Essath script covers this scriptorium's walls floor to ceiling, line after line of ritual record - and one long passage of it describes, in outline, the same coiled rite completed in full two rooms deeper.
*A scriptorium fifty feet long, its walls cut in shallow panels each holding its own inscribed passage. Smells of old ash settling down through the open Sky Well above; a dropped word here is audible at the passage's far end.*
**Inscribed Walls:** Essath lettering fills every panel, most of it administrative - tallies, rosters, offerings recorded and struck through. One long passage near the shaft-side wall describes a rite completed in a round chamber, coiled three times, that the party may not have found yet.
**Struck-Through Roster:** A panel low on the shaft-side wall lists names in Essath, most struck through with the same tool - a roster of some kind, its final several names left unstruck.
**Ash Chute:** A narrow opening low in the floor's far corner, angled steeply down - too tight to climb back up, and too steep to descend except by sliding (one-way; sliding the chute -> a rough, bruising drop into D.11's ash below, with no way back up the same route).
**Exits:** vertical shaft - basalt, worn smooth on one side, center -> D.6 Sky Well, open passage - basalt, single-file, south end -> D.8 Procession Passage, ash chute - basalt, steep, floor corner, one-way down -> D.11 Ash-Choked Cellar
