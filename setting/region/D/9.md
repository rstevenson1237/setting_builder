D.9 **The Hall of Names** (Medium) - *ringing, lettered, trapped*

Bronze plates are set in rows across the floor, a name cut in each, and a note rings underfoot at every step. The last plate is blank.

*A hall 20 feet by 30, the founders' halls' challenge. The thirty-one plates carry the kings' names out of order. Trodden oldest to youngest, as the kings stand in D.2, they stay cold; a plate trodden out of turn flares with runes (Test of Constitution, 2d, Fire). Scorched plates show where others failed. (The Cynidiceans Raise the City)*

**The Plates:** Thirty-one bronze plates, a name on each in the cynidicean script; the Priesthood know the order.
**The Blank Plate:** At the far end; a party that reaches it in order can lift it (Treasure I, d20).
**The Adept:** A Scholar copies the plates in the doorway to the carvers' cells (Wary, 1, Faction : Sihuahet). He knows the order of the kings and trades it for news.

**Exits:** an archway -> D.1 The Stair Landing, a stone door to the founders -> D.2 The Hall of Founders, a stone door to the cells -> D.5 The Carvers' Cells
