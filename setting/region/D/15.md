D.15 **Nirguk Warren** (high) - *Elder Sorcery, Shed Skin*
Nirguk's enclave keeps this chamber as its own, scale-polish replacing tool-marks on every wall within easy reach and the priest-kings' petition-benches cut out of the floor entirely rather than worked around. A single **shed skin**, unnaturally long, lies coiled in the driest corner, and **Vaaluk** is somewhere in the room before anyone notices it was.
*A wide audience chamber thirty feet across, cut for the priest-kings to receive petitioners before they were let further in - its floor still shows the sockets where the benches stood, cut off flush and rubbed smooth rather than pulled. Smells of cold stone that has never seen sun; nothing here echoes the way the Middle tier does, and the warren swallows sound instead.*
**Vaaluk:** The enclave's eldest, a head longer than any other Nirguk here and the only one that speaks to a party rather than about them. It will talk before it fights, and what it wants from the conversation is to find out how the party got past the Sealed Door (Named Creature: Vaaluk).
**Nirguk Enclave:** Three or four of the dozen-strong enclave keep this chamber at any time, coiled rather than standing, watching the stair down from the Sealed Door rather than anything further in - and taking their lead from Vaaluk rather than from the intrusion (Territorial, 4, Bestiary : Nirguk).
**Cut-Off Benches:** The petition-benches were removed at the floor and the stumps rubbed smooth, which is more work than breaking them out would have been. Nothing in the enclave sits.
**Shed Skin:** Left deliberately in the driest corner - the only sign Nirguk leaves on purpose, and proof enough of what lives here for anyone who knows to read it.
**Dust-Free Likeness:** Set into the inner wall toward the hoard beyond, a carved likeness of Nirguk's own shape stands utterly free of dust in a room that holds it everywhere else - stiller, somehow, than anything merely carved should be, and the twin of the thing standing in the hoard chamber.
**Tribute Shelf:** A ledge along the inner wall holds what the enclave has been given or has taken over a long tenancy, set out in the open rather than kept - the enclave does not guard this and does not expect anyone to reach it (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** vertical stair - basalt, narrow, northwest side -> D.17 Sealed Door, open doorway - scale-polished stone, east side -> D.16 Enclave Nursery, open doorway - polished stone, north side -> D.18 Nirguk's Hoard
