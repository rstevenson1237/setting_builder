D.15 **Nirguk Warren** (medium) - *Elder Sorcery, Shed Skin*
Nirguk's enclave keeps this chamber as its own, scale-polish replacing tool-marks on every wall within easy reach. A single **shed skin**, unnaturally long, lies coiled in the driest corner.
*A wide audience chamber thirty feet across, cut for the priest-kings to receive petitioners before they were let any further in, now given over entirely to Nirguk's own use. Smells of cold stone that has never seen sun; nothing here echoes the way the Middle tier does; the warren swallows sound instead.*
**Nirguk Enclave:** Three or four of the dozen-strong enclave keep this chamber at any time, coiled rather than standing, watchful of the stair down from the Sealed Door above rather than anything further in (Territorial, 4, Bestiary : Nirguk).
**Shed Skin:** Left deliberately in the driest corner - the only sign Nirguk leaves on purpose, and proof enough of what lives here for anyone who knows to read it.
**Dust-Free Likeness:** Set into the inner wall toward the hoard beyond, a carved likeness of Nirguk's own shape stands utterly free of dust in a room that holds it everywhere else - stiller, somehow, than anything merely carved should be.
**Personal Effects:** Odds and ends taken in tribute over a long tenancy, kept rather than hoarded - worth little (Treasure I, d20), and left in the open because the enclave does not consider it worth guarding.
**Exits:** vertical stair - basalt, narrow, northwest side -> D.17 Sealed Door, open doorway - scale-polished stone, east side -> D.16 Enclave Nursery, open doorway - polished stone, north side -> D.18 Nirguk's Hoard
