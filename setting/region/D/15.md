D.15 **The Rubble Chamber** (low) - *Choked, Deceptive, Hidden-Route*
Loose rubble chokes most of the **Rubble Chamber**, a dead-end mine face by every visible sign. A faint draft moves through it that dust and debris don't explain.
*A collapsed working fifteen feet deep, rubble piled shoulder-high in most of it, one old mine face visible at the back; smells of dry rock dust, a thin draft moving steadily despite no visible source.*
**Rubble Pile:** Loose stone fills most of the chamber; digging reveals nothing but more rubble at a glance.
**Draft:** A steady draft moves through the rubble from a specific low gap - air doesn't move like this without an opening somewhere.
**Cleared Crawlspace:** Following the draft and clearing the low gap opens a tight crawlspace climbing toward daylight -> B.1 The Causeway; only the Coil use it, and they'd rather it stayed that way.
**Exits:** the collapsed working, back -> D.5 The Flooded Colonnade
