D.15 **Nirguk Warren** (high) - *Elder Sorcery, Shed Skin*
Nirguk's enclave keeps this chamber as its own, scale-polish replacing tool-marks, on every wall within reach. A single **shed skin** lies coiled in the driest corner, and **Vaaluk** is in the room before anyone notices.
*A wide audience chamber thirty feet across, cut for the priest-kings to receive petitioners, its floor still showing the bench sockets, cut off flush and rubbed smooth. Smells of cold stone, never having seen sun, and the warren swallows sound.*
**Vaaluk:** The enclave's eldest, talking before it fights, and wanting to know how the party passed, the Sealed Door (Named Creature: Vaaluk).
**Nirguk Enclave:** Three or four keep this chamber, coiled rather than standing, watching the stair from the Sealed Door (Territorial, 4, Bestiary : Nirguk).
**Cut-Off Benches:** The petition-benches were removed at the floor, the stumps rubbed smooth, and nothing in the enclave sits.
**Shed Skin:** Left in the driest corner, the one sign Nirguk leaves on purpose.
**Dust-Free Likeness:** Set into the inner wall toward the hoard, a carved likeness of Nirguk's own shape, dust-free in a room that holds it everywhere.
**Tribute Shelf:** A ledge along the inner wall holds, what the enclave has been given or taken, set out in the open (Treasure I, d20).
