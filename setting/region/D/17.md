D.17 **The Sealed Gallery** (high) - *Sealed, Silent, Watched*
Scorched, radiating cracks spread from a ring of scratched glyphs at the center of the **Sealed Gallery**, where a whole work-gang vanished two winters ago. Two robed figures kneel at its edge, tending it in silence.
*A wide chamber thirty feet across, walls blackened in a spreading pattern from the center outward; a continuous faint hiss seems to come from everywhere and nowhere, and the air is noticeably colder here than the passage outside.*
**Coil-Marked Tenders:** Two kneel at the glyph-ring's edge, tending it and armed; they will not leave it undefended (Cunning, 2, Bestiary : Coil-Marked).
**Glyph-Ring:** Xoss glyphs scratched into the floor, still faintly warm - the Coil call this Kissva, the black coil.
**Radiating Scorch:** Cracks and black scoring spread outward from the ring's center, violent and sudden.
**Listening:** Reading the glyphs aloud from the center fixes three Xoss words in the listener unasked; finishing the whole working draws something worse (Truths of Carrdun).
**Crew's Gear:** Scattered, half-looted equipment from the vanished work-gang lies against the far wall (Treasure II, d20).
**Torn Manifest Page:** A page from the crew's own tally, describing what they heard before they stopped writing (Lore: The Last Tally Page).
**Exits:** a low arch, the only way in or out -> D.16 The Coil's Vestibule
