D.17 **Sealed Door** (medium) - *Black Ziggurat, Sealed Door*
The priest-kings' working still holds this stair shut, to anyone crossing without the word the **seal** wants. Nirguk has never tested it.
*A narrow landing at the stair's foot, ten feet across, the door a slab of fitted basalt, rather than anything hinged. Smells of old ash, and a line of Essath script runs the seal's face, worn but mostly legible.*
**Essath Seal:** The slab's face carries a binding-line, in Essath naming a word that opens it, the word written nowhere here, crossing without speaking it -> the working discharges into whoever crossed (Test of Constitution, 1d, Blast) (Keys: The Cairn Pilgrim-Token).
**Copied Script:** Scratched crudely beneath the binding-line, in a clumsier hand, an attempt to copy the seal's wording (Lore: The Copied Seal).
**Exits:** vertical stair - basalt, narrow, northwest side -> D.7 First Threshold, vertical stair - basalt, narrow, southeast side -> D.15 Nirguk Warren
