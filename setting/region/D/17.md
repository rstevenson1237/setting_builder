D.17 **Sealed Door** (medium) - *Black Ziggurat, Sealed Door*
The priest-kings' own working still holds this stair shut to anyone who crosses it without the word the **seal** wants - a working Nirguk itself has never once tested, by the enclave's own choice rather than any fear.
*A narrow landing at the stair's foot, ten feet across, the door itself a slab of fitted basalt rather than anything hinged. Smells of old ash, thickest here of anywhere in the ziggurat; a line of Essath script runs the seal's face, worn but mostly legible.*
**Essath Seal:** The slab's face carries a binding-line in Essath naming a word that opens it safely. The word is not written here and never was - the same crossing-word the waystones on the road were cut to teach, which is how a traveller who never came near Daghash could still know it. Crossing the threshold without speaking it triggers the working instead (Damaging; crossing unspoken -> a binding-shock that leaves the crosser marked for a day, or worse for anyone already hurt) (Keys: The Cairn Pilgrim-Token).
**Copied Script:** Scratched crudely beneath the original binding-line, in a second, clumsier hand, an attempt to copy the seal's own wording - by somebody who could not actually read what they were copying (Lore: The Copied Seal).
**Exits:** vertical stair - basalt, narrow, northwest side -> D.7 First Threshold, vertical stair - basalt, narrow, southeast side -> D.15 Nirguk Warren
