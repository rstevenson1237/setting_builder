D.2 **The Stilt Village** (Lair) - *rickety, fish-reeking, watchful*
Huts on **stilts** stand round a central **platform**, joined by rope bridges, with fish drying on **poles**. A pole **boat** lies at the platform's north-west edge.
*Reached by pole boat, a quarter-mile east of the causeway at 2 miles. Twelve huts.*
**Stilts:** Fish-bone points stand upright in the mud round the foot of every post.
**Platform:** Planks and rope, wide enough for a council; spears lean at every hut door.
**Poles:** Smoked fish hangs from racks of reed, enough to feed the village through the winter.
**Boat:** Tied at the north-west edge, its wake running into the reeds toward a hidden island.
**Villagers:** Twelve lizard-folk with spears and nets spear anyone who lands unasked (Hostile, 12, Bestiary : Lizard Man).
**Exits:** pole boat, north-west, 0.7 miles from the platform's edge -> D.5 The Hidden Island
