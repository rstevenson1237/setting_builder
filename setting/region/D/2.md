D.2 **The Hall of Founders** (Medium) - *carved, tall, painted*

Life-size kings stand carved along both long walls of a high hall, their robes still blue and gold in places. A bell sounds far off, and a sun-disc is painted over every door.

*A hall 40 feet by 20 and 15 high, the heart of the founders' halls. The kings run oldest to youngest along the walls; each plinth carries a name in the cynidicean script, and their order is the key to the hall of names (D.9).*

**The Kings:** Thirty-one, carved life-size, each with a name beneath; the youngest has no face (The Cynidiceans Raise the City).
**The Acolytes:** Two priests of the Priesthood pray before the first king (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Acolyte). They want the rites kept and ask a stranger to wash and state a purpose; the sun-disc over the doorway is theirs (Faction : Ruhiahe). Asked, they recite the kings oldest first.

**Exits:** an open passage -> D.8 The Kings' Stair, a stone door -> D.9 The Hall of Names
