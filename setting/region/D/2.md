D.2 **The Hall of Founders** (Medium) - *carved, tall, painted*

Life-size kings stand carved along both long walls of a high hall, their robes still blue and gold in places. A bell sounds far off, and a sun-disc is painted over every door.

*A hall 40 feet by 30 and 15 high. The kings run oldest to youngest from west to east; each plinth carries a name in the cynidicean script.*

**The Kings:** Thirty-one carved kings, each a name beneath; the youngest at the east end has no face (The Cynidiceans Raise the City).
**The Acolytes:** Two priests pray before the first king (Wary, 2, Faction : Ruhiahe). They want the rites kept and ask a stranger to wash and state a purpose; the sun-disc over the doorway is theirs.
**The Adept:** An adept of the Scholars copies glyphs at the east arch (Wary, 1, Faction : Sihuahet). He knows what the murals showed and trades it for what a party knows.

**Exits:** an archway -> D.1 The Stair Landing, an open passage -> D.4 The Scraped Hall, a stone door -> D.5 The Carvers' Cells
