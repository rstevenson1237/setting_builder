D.2 **Upper Terrace** (medium) - *Black Ziggurat, Open Roof*
Open to the sky along its whole width, this terrace was the priest-kings' open-air oratory. Its **parapet** is the only thing between a standing figure and a thirty-foot drop.
*An open-air oratory thirty feet across, its low parapet worn round rather than square. Smells of clean air, out of place this high, in a place thick with old ash, and wind is the only sound.*
**Undercut Parapet:** The waist-high basalt rail looks sound, and is hollow behind, the coping course sitting on its own weight, leaning or bracing on the outer stretch -> the coping goes over, and takes whoever used it (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Worn Rest:** One stretch of the rail's top course, is worn smooth, and it is the one stretch still bedded, visible from directly above.
**Loose Coping Stone:** Within the worn stretch, a single coping stone sits loose, rather than mortared, lifting it clear -> a shallow cavity cut into the rail, packed and closed (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** vertical stair - basalt, narrow and open to the drop on one side, south side -> D.5 Collapsed Portico, vertical stair - basalt, eight steps down between two columns, west side -> D.4 Wind-Worn Colonnade
