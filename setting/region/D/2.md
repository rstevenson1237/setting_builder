D.2 **The Ridge Caves** (Lair) - *painted, smoky, watched*
Red hand-prints mark smoky cave mouths in a ridge face, hoots carrying from a ledge, bone heaps outside and a watch of shaggy figures above.
*500 by 300 feet. A cave men's band holds the caves; it hoots from the ridge to call the band, then rushes a party that has not gone round. The ridge trail runs down to the wreck coast.*
**The red hand-prints:** hands in red ochre on the rock and each cave mouth.
**The band:** eight shaggy, scarred hunters in hide wraps with stone-headed clubs and fire-hardened spears (Hostile, 8, Bestiary : Caveman).
**The bone heaps:** gnawed remains before the caves, a dinosaur's among them.
**Exits:** the ridge trail down to the shore -> C.10 The Lookout Cliff
