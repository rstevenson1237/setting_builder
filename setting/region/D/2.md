D.2 **The Antechamber of Masks** (medium) - *Preserved, Many-Doored, Still*
Dozens of niches line the **Antechamber of Masks**, most holding a funerary mask staring blankly out; several are broken, and something in a burial wrap stirs at the sound of footsteps.
*A wide junction chamber twenty feet across, niches cut floor to ceiling on three walls; smells of dry dust and old cloth, and the room's four archways carry sound oddly, doubling footsteps.*
**Husks:** Two preserved dead in burial wraps stir at the disturbance, slow but relentless once moving (Relentless, 2, Bestiary : Husk).
**Mask Niches:** Dozens of funerary masks, mostly intact; one wall's niches are all broken and empty.
**Pyramid Niche:** One niche's arch is stepped like a tiny pyramid, matching a warded door somewhere deeper in.
**Grave Wrappings:** A disturbed wrap has spilled old coin and a broken blade (Treasure II, d20).
**Exits:** a boarded shaft-mouth, south -> D.1 The Footing-Shaft Landing, a low arch, west -> D.3 The Cracked Passage, a wide arch, north -> D.4 The Scribes' Hall, a wide arch, east -> D.5 The Flooded Colonnade
