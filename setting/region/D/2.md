D.2 **Upper Terrace** (low) - *Black Ziggurat, Open Roof*
Open to the sky along its whole width, this terrace once served as the priest-kings' own open-air oratory - the roofless tier's silence is closest to whatever it once was here, where the roof was never meant to stand.
*An open-air oratory thirty feet across, its low parapet worn round rather than square. Smells of clean air, out of place this high in a place otherwise thick with old ash; wind is the only sound.*
**Worn Parapet:** A waist-high basalt rail runs the terrace's open edge, its top course worn smooth in one stretch where hands have rested on it far more than the rest.
**Loose Coping Stone:** The worn stretch of parapet hides a single coping stone set loose rather than mortared - lifting it clear reveals a shallow cavity cut into the rail itself (Treasure I, d20), sealed there by whoever last stood at this rail and never came back for it.
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, south side -> D.5 Collapsed Portico
