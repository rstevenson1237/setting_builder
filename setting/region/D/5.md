D.5 **The Hidden Island** (Hidden) - *dry, reed-walled, secret*
A dry **knoll** stands ringed by tall reeds, a **cairn** of white bones at its top and a **landing** on its south-east side.
*Knoll 30 ft across, 0.7 miles north-west of the village. It cannot be seen from the causeway.*
**Knoll:** Dry turf under the reeds, trodden in a ring round the cairn.
**Cairn:** White bones heaped within a ring of stones bound with red thread; disturbing them brings boats out of the reeds within a turn, spears levelled.
**Landing:** Stakes and planks on the south-east side, where a pole boat can ground.
**Exits:** pole boat, south-east, 0.7 miles from the landing -> D.2 The Stilt Village
