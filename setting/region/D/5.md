D.5 **The Pterodactyl Rock** (Lair) - *bare, guano-stained, screaming*
A bare guano-stained rock stack screams with wings, leathery shapes wheeling above and a sour stench on the wind.
*300 by 250 feet. Pterodactyls roost on the ledges and stoop on the smallest of a party or on anyone alone. The crater trail leads from here north.*
**The pterodactyls:** six ten-foot-spanned leathery-winged reptiles circling and stooping (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Pterodactyl).
**The ledges:** crowded with nests and white with guano.
**The scree:** loose stone below the stack, scattered with fish bones.
**Exits:** the crater trail -> G.3 The Crater Rim
