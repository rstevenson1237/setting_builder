D.5 **Collapsed Portico** (medium) - *Undying Sorcerer, False Walk*
Where the Upper tier's roofline came down, four ways still meet across the rubble. The **rubble slope** underfoot shifts audibly with every step.
*A forty-foot chamber, originally a columned portico, now half-filled with its own fallen roof. Smells of old dust kicked loose with every step, and sound does not carry, swallowed by the broken stone.*
**Rubble Slope:** Loose fallen masonry banked across the chamber's centre, shifting underfoot without warning, crossed at any pace but a careful one -> the bank goes and takes whoever is, on it (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Fallen Lintel:** One collapsed lintel block, face-up in the rubble, carries a single coil of the serpent motif, broken off mid-turn.
**Buried Archway:** The slide banked hardest against the west wall, an arch-top showing above it, the rubble loose rather than fused, clearing a crawl-height gap -> an hour's work by hand, opening into the Roofless Hall.
**Buried Fixture:** Further along the same slope, a length of tarnished fitting, set into a fallen column base (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, broken lintel, south side -> D.1 South Stair, vertical stair - basalt, narrow and open to the drop on one side, east side -> D.2 Upper Terrace, open colonnade - basalt, twelve columns standing, north side -> D.4 Wind-Worn Colonnade, open archway - basalt, intact, northeast side -> D.6 Sky Well
