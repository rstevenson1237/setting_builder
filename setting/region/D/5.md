D.5 **Collapsed Portico** (medium) - *Undying Sorcerer, False Walk*
Where the Upper tier's roofline came down, four ways still meet across the rubble - the one room up here a party cannot avoid passing through more than once. The **rubble slope** underfoot shifts audibly with every step.
*A forty-foot chamber, originally a columned portico, now half-filled with its own fallen roof. Smells of old dust kicked loose with every step; sound doesn't carry far here, swallowed by the broken stone.*
**Rubble Slope:** Loose fallen masonry banked across the chamber's center, shifting underfoot without warning - a careless crossing risks a further slide (Damaging; disturbed footing -> a fall and a rolled ankle, or worse if already hurt).
**Fallen Lintel:** One collapsed lintel block, face-up in the rubble, carries a single coil of the serpent motif repeated at every doorway here - broken off mid-turn, as though continuing somewhere the party has not yet found.
**Buried Archway:** The slide banked hardest against the west wall, and the top of an arch shows above it - the portico had a fifth way out and the roof closed it. The rubble is loose, not fused; clearing a crawl-height gap is an hour's work by hand and opens into the Roofless Hall, which has no other way in since the collapse.
**Buried Fixture:** Further along the same slope, a length of tarnished fitting is still set into a fallen column base, exposed by the slide rather than hidden by it (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, broken lintel, south side -> D.1 South Stair, vertical stair - basalt, narrow and open to the drop on one side, east side -> D.2 Upper Terrace, open colonnade - basalt, twelve columns standing, north side -> D.4 Wind-Worn Colonnade, open archway - basalt, intact, northeast side -> D.6 Sky Well
