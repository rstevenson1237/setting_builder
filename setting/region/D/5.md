D.5 **The Flooded Colonnade** (medium) - *Ankle-Deep, Echoing, Pillared*
Water stands ankle-deep between rows of pillars in the **Flooded Colonnade**, its echoes doubling every footstep. One flagstone underwater looks recently cracked.
*A colonnaded hall thirty feet wide and much longer than it is wide, water pooling ankle-deep across the whole floor; every sound doubles and lingers among the pillars, air thick with standing water and old stone.*
**Cracked Flagstone:** Hairline cracks radiate from one submerged flagstone; stepped on, it gives way into a drop below (Damaging).
**Standing Pillars:** Rows of stepped-pyramid pillars, one row still whole, the rest broken off at the waterline.
**Sunken Debris:** Silt and rubble collect against the pillars nearest the loop passage, half-burying old debris (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** a wide arch, west -> D.2 The Antechamber of Masks, a broken archway, north -> D.9 The Broken Causeway Stair, a low passage, east -> D.12 The Weeping Wall, a narrow gap, south -> D.15 The Rubble Chamber
