D.5 **The Carvers' Cells** (Low) - *small, chiselled, dusty*

Six tiny cells open on a single passage, chisels and mallets lying on the floors. The air is dry and silent.

*A passage 20 feet by 30 with six cells off it, at the door of the gallery. The carvers lived and slept here; their tools lie where they were dropped. The archway to the gallery is shut by a stone keyed to a sun-disc. (The Cynidiceans Raise the City)*

**The Cells:** Each holds a straw pallet, a clay lamp and a chisel; one carries a scratched tally on the wall.
**The Chest:** A tool chest at the passage's end holds a hide scroll (Lore: The Carver's Count).
**The Archway:** A sun-disc cut in its keystone. A bronze disc on a cord fits it and slides the stone (Keys: The Sun-Disc Key).

**Exits:** a stone door -> D.9 The Hall of Names, an archway -> D.6 The Gallery of the Fall
