D.7 **First Threshold** (medium) - *Temple Cult, Bound Threshold*
The Middle tier's outer door stands bound by an Essath working older than any hand now living, a single **Bound Dead** posted before it, unmoving until the threshold itself is crossed uninvited.
*A narrow chamber fifteen feet across, built as an antechamber before the ziggurat's own inner rites - built for the procession to pause before continuing rather than to be lingered in. Smells of old ash; a line of Essath script runs the lintel, one word short of complete.*
**Bound Dead:** Stands posted before the inner archway, facing it rather than any other exit, and acts only against whoever crosses that specific threshold without first being announced - a fact nobody now living knows how to satisfy (Wary, 1, Bestiary : Bound Dead).
**Worn Lintel Script:** Essath lettering runs the inner archway's lintel, its final word worn past reading - whatever the threshold was bound to require, the missing word is it.
**Small Stone Figure:** Set into the frame beside the Bound Dead's post, a carved figure no taller than a hand, coiled and dust-free despite everything around it holding centuries of grime - smaller than, but unmistakably kin to, something else in this ziggurat.
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, worn threshold, west side -> D.8 Procession Passage, open archway - basalt, serpent-coil lintel, north side -> D.13 Chamber of the Coiled Serpent, vertical stair - basalt, narrow, southeast side -> D.17 Sealed Door
