D.7 **First Threshold** (high) - *Temple Cult, Bound Threshold*
The Middle tier's outer door stands bound by an Essath working older than any hand now living - the **script along its lintel** is the working itself, unworn in a building worn everywhere else, and it is still holding. A single **Bound Dead** is posted before it, and is the lesser of the two things in this room.
*A narrow chamber fifteen feet across, built as an antechamber for the procession to pause in before continuing rather than to be lingered in - and cut so the inner archway is visible from every point in it, which nothing else on this tier bothers to do. Smells of old ash; the chamber is quieter than the passage outside it by a margin a party notices before it knows why.*
**Bound Threshold:** The inner archway's lintel carries a line of Essath binding-script, its final word worn past reading. Crossing without speaking what the binding wants spends the working on everyone across the line at once - a party that files through singly loses the one who went first (Damaging; crossing the inner archway unspoken -> the working strikes every person across the threshold in the same instant) (Undying, 1, Bestiary : Threshold-Bane).
**Bound Dead:** Stands posted before the inner archway, facing it rather than any other exit, and acts only against whoever crosses that specific threshold without first being announced - a fact nobody now living knows how to satisfy (Wary, 1, Bestiary : Bound Dead).
**Worn Lintel Script:** The binding's final word is worn past reading, and it is the only worn thing on the lintel - whatever the threshold requires, the missing word is it. The rest of the line is legible to anyone who reads Essath, and says only what it will do, never what opens it.
**Small Stone Figure:** Set into the frame beside the Bound Dead's post, a carved figure no taller than a hand, coiled and dust-free despite everything around it holding centuries of grime. It is fixed in place and does not come out. It is also unmistakably kin to something larger elsewhere in this ziggurat, and the same three-coil turn is cut into both.
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, worn threshold, west side -> D.8 Procession Passage, open archway - basalt, serpent-coil lintel, north side -> D.13 Chamber of the Coiled Serpent, vertical stair - basalt, narrow, southeast side -> D.17 Sealed Door
