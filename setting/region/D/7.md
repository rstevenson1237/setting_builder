D.7 **First Threshold** (high) - *Temple Cult, Bound Threshold*
The Middle tier's outer door stands bound by an Essath working, the **script along its lintel** unworn and still holding. A **Bound Dead** stands posted before it.
*A narrow chamber fifteen feet across, built for the procession to pause in, cut so the inner archway is visible from every point in it. Smells of old ash, and the chamber is quieter than the passage outside it.*
**Bound Threshold:** The inner archway's lintel carries a line, of Essath binding-script, crossing without speaking what it wants -> the working strikes everyone across the line, at once (Undying, 1, Bestiary : Threshold-Bane).
**Bound Dead:** Stands posted before the inner archway, facing it rather than any other exit, acting against whoever crosses unannounced (Wary, 1, Bestiary : Bound Dead).
**Worn Lintel Script:** The binding's final word is worn past reading, the only worn thing on the lintel, the rest legible to anyone who reads Essath.
**Small Stone Figure:** Set into the frame beside the Bound Dead, a carved figure a hand tall, coiled and dust-free, fixed in place and not coming out.
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, worn threshold, west side -> D.8 Procession Passage, open archway - basalt, serpent-coil lintel, north side -> D.13 Chamber of the Coiled Serpent, vertical stair - basalt, narrow, southeast side -> D.17 Sealed Door
