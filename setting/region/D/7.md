D.7 **The Robbers' Camp** (High) - *crowded, smoky, barricaded*

Crates, a cookfire and a barricade of upended coffins fill a low hall, laughter and the rattle of dice coming over it. Smoke hangs under the roof.

*A hall 30 feet square, the tomb robbers' upper camp and this area's challenge: a party gets past by trade or by a fight with the whole camp.*

**The Barricade:** Coffins stood on end across the door, a gap a body wide.
**The Robbers:** Six to eight delvers dice, cook and sort loot (Wary, 6-8, Bestiary : Delver). They want a fair split of anything a party finds and know two of the three ways down from the level above, and sell either for a share.
**The Strongbox:** Iron-bound, under the cook's bench (Treasure II, d20).
**The Mark:** Notches and rope coils over the door (The Tomb Robbers).

**Exits:** an open passage -> D.3 The Robbers' Watch
