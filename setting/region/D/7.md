D.7 **The Waterhole** (Natural) - *trampled, shallow, muddy*
A trampled shallow waterhole lies in the clearing's shade, its mud printed with tracks, grunts and lapping carrying over the water.
*300 by 250 feet. Cave men and dinosaurs fight over the waterholes.*
**The triceratops:** three beaked reptiles the size of wagons drinking at the water's edge (Neutral, 3, Bestiary : Triceratops); they graze undisturbed until a party nears their young.
**The game trails:** worn to the water from every side.
