D.7 **The Ward-Threshold** (high) - *Warded, Dividing, Marked*
Twin door-leaves carved with a stepped-pyramid arch and old-tongue lettering divide the passage here - Carrdun's diggers call it the **Ward-Threshold** and won't work past it alone. A silent stone **Guardian** flanks the door, motionless, its base worn where something has scraped at the warding and stopped.
*A ceremonial passage ten feet wide and twenty deep, floor and walls dressed in the same finely cut stone as the rest of Baldun, built as a ritual crossing between two wings; abandoned, though the warding cut into the doors still holds.*
**Guardian:** A stone Ward-Statue flanks the door, inert until the warding breaks - then relentless (Relentless, 1, Bestiary : Ward-Statue).
**Ward-Marks:** Old-tongue lettering - "Perwenu," the sealed place - cut into both door-leaves, unbroken.
**Scraped Warding:** One mark is scratched, not fully defaced; whoever tried lost their nerve, or worse.
**Faint Undermark:** Beneath the scratch, an older carving shows the correct undoing - traced right, the door opens quiet; defaced instead, it wakes the Guardian (Truths of Carrdun).
**Abandoned Satchel:** A rotted satchel lies just short of the door, dropped and never retrieved (Treasure II, d20).
**Warning Note:** A scrap of hide pinned under a loose flagstone warns not to touch the marks at all (Lore: The Failed Warding Note).
**Exits:** carved double doors, the way in from the south -> D.6 The Silt-Choked Stair, a plain arch, west wall -> D.8 The Granary Vaults, a carved arch matching the pyramid motif, east wall -> D.16 The Coil's Vestibule
