Layout: The causeway (D.1) runs 3 miles north from the road. From its south end, the stilt village (D.2) lies 2 miles north by pole boat, the drowned shrine (D.3) 2 miles north-east, the sinking flat (D.4) 1 mile east, and the hidden island (D.5) a mile north-west.

Places:
- D.2 The Stilt Village - the Sellaca's huts and their dried fish; twelve spears.
- D.3 The Drowned Shrine - the makers' bath-shrine, with eight dead men standing in the water.
- D.4 The Sinking Flat - a mile of mud crossed only by tussocks.

Inhabitants: Four of The Sellaca watch D.1 and twelve live in D.2, and eight Zombies stand in the water at D.3.

Loot: A pole boat costs 30 cn and an escort. No treasure lies on the mere; the dead at D.3 carry nothing a party can sell.

Secrets:
- The cairn on the hidden island is bound with red thread, and disturbing the bones brings boats from the reeds - the Sellaca know; the red-bound stones (D.5) bring it out.

Leads:
- Rumour 6 - D.1 Watchers, D.2 Villagers.
- Rumour 12 - D.3 Dead Men, D.5 Cairn.

Tables:
d6 Encounter, per failed Difficulty roll
1. A pole boat slides from the reeds with two spears in it, silent, and falls in behind the party.
2. A plank gives under the lead crosser and drops them to the waist.
3. Fog closes over the water, and the causeway's edges vanish.
4. A pale shape stands motionless in the water, then walks toward the party.
5. Marsh gas bubbles up beside the planks, and a spark would set it alight.
6. A heron lifts from the reeds and the watchers ahead go still.
