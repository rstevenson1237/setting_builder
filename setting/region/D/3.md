D.3 **Roofless Hall** (medium) - *Trackless Waste, No Nesting Birds*
A wide chantry standing open to weather that has scoured it bare of anything that might otherwise have nested or rooted here. The reason nothing nests is standing in it: four of the **Unlettered Dead** came up through the floor breach out of the charnel pit below and have not managed to get back down.
*A chantry forty feet across, its roof reduced to a scatter of collapsed vault-stone along one wall. Smells only of open air and wet stone after rain; nothing here carries the sound the tiers below hold onto.*
**Unlettered Dead:** Four of them, ash-grey to the shoulder from the pit they climbed out of, moving without pattern across the open floor and closing on anything warm that enters (Aimless, 4, Bestiary : The Unlettered Dead).
**Collapsed Vault-Stone:** A scatter of fallen roof-stone banked against the west wall, the source of the slide that opened the floor through to the cellar below - and the reason this hall no longer opens onto the portico the way it was built to.
**Open Floor Breach:** Where the vault-stone came down, the floor gave way with it - a straight drop through to the cellar beneath, wide enough to fall through but not to climb back up, and the way the dead came in. A step near the lip, or a push -> a fall through to D.11, one-way (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Exits:** open breach - collapsed floor, center, one-way down -> D.11 Ash-Choked Cellar
