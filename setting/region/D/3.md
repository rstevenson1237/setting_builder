D.3 **Roofless Hall** (medium) - *Trackless Waste, No Nesting Birds*
A wide chantry open to weather, scoured bare of anything that might have nested or rooted. Four of the **Unlettered Dead** came up through the **floor breach** and have not got back down.
*A chantry forty feet across, its roof reduced to a scatter of collapsed vault-stone along one wall. Smells only of open air and wet stone after rain, and nothing here holds sound, the way the tiers below do.*
**Unlettered Dead:** Four of them, ash-grey to the shoulder, moving without pattern across the open floor, closing on anything warm (Aimless, 4, Bestiary : The Unlettered Dead).
**Collapsed Vault-Stone:** A scatter of fallen roof-stone banked, against the west wall, the source of the slide, that opened the floor.
**Open Floor Breach:** Where the vault-stone came down, a straight drop through to the cellar, wide enough to fall through, and not to climb, a step near the lip -> a one-way fall to D.11 (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Exits:** open breach - collapsed floor, center, one-way down -> D.11 Ash-Choked Cellar
