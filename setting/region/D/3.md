D.3 **The Tyrant's Clearing** (Lair) - *trampled, open, bone-strewn*
A trampled clearing lies strewn with huge bones, the jungle silent round it and flies thick over a rank carcass, a bowl of flattened ferns at its centre.
*600 by 500 feet. A forty-foot reptile hunts the clearing and its trails; a party caught in the open cannot hide.*
**The tyrannosaur:** a forty-foot reptile on two legs with a head as long as a man, stalking along a trail from the trees (Hostile, 1, Bestiary : Tyrannosaurus); its bite is Lethal.
**The bone heaps:** the bones of other dinosaurs, some picked clean, some fresh.
**The nest hollow:** a bowl of flattened ferns at the clearing's centre, ringed with dung.
