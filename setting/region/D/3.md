D.3 **The Drowned Shrine** (Ruin) - *sunken, green, silent*
Green stone **pillars** rise from black water in what was the makers' bath-shrine, and **steps** lead down into the dark. **Dead men** stand among the pillars.
*Hall 40 ft by 40 ft, 1.4 miles east of the causeway at 1.4 miles; the water is knee-deep at the pillars and deeper beyond them.*
**Pillars:** Square-cut and green with weed, carved with script wherever the water leaves them bare.
**Steps:** Descend into water over a party's head; the lowest steps cannot be seen.
**Dead Men:** Eight stand among the pillars and walk toward the living, never stopping; their bootprints are waterlogged and sink at once (Hostile, 8, Bestiary : Zombie).
