D.3 **The Cracked Passage** (low) - *Narrow, Settling, Damp*
Water once ran through the **Cracked Passage**, its floor still grooved from a channel long dry. Cracks run wall to ceiling in a few spots, and one section requires a stoop.
*A low, narrow corridor twenty feet long, floor grooved down its center from a drained runoff channel; smells of dry dust and old damp, quiet except for settling stone.*
**Dry Channel:** A shallow groove runs the passage's length - water was drained deliberately, long before Carrdun existed.
**Cracked Ceiling:** Stress cracks run wall to ceiling in two places; noisy but structurally sound.
**Worn Relief:** A shallow relief carving, worn past reading, runs along one wall - the same hand as the finer work deeper in.
**Exits:** a low arch, east -> D.2 The Antechamber of Masks, a narrow gap, west -> D.6 The Silt-Choked Stair
