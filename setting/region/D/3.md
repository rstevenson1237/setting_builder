D.3 **The Robbers' Watch** (Medium) - *lamplit, littered, tense*

Bedrolls, rope and a lit lamp fill a doorway, the room smelling of oil and sweat. A notch is cut in the frame, and coughing carries from within.

*A guard room 20 feet square at the front of the robbers' halls. Their watchers sit here, the camp a shout away (D.7). A winding passage leads off to the scraped halls.*

**The Robbers:** Two to four delvers lounge and watch the trapdoor (Wary, 2-4, Bestiary : Delver). They want coin and a way past; a killed robber brings six from the camp within the hour.
**The Frame:** A notch is cut for each haul, a rope coil beside it (The Tomb Robbers). The hail is "Fair split?", answered "Fair split."
**The Gnawing:** In the winding passage's mouth, rats chew the frame (Hostile, 4-8, Bestiary : Giant Rat). They come out when the lamp is shuttered.

**Exits:** a wooden door -> D.1 The Stair Landing, an open passage to the camp -> D.7 The Robbers' Camp, a winding passage -> D.4 The Scraped Hall
