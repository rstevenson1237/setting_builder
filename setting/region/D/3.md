D.3 **The Robbers' Watch** (Medium) - *lamplit, littered, tense*

Bedrolls, rope and a lit lamp fill a doorway, the room smelling of oil and sweat. A notch is cut in the frame, and coughing carries from within.

*A guard room 30 feet square. The tomb robbers' watchers sit here, the camp's six more a shout away.*

**The Robbers:** Six to eight delvers lounge and watch the stair (Wary, 6-8, Bestiary : Delver). They want coin and a way past; a killed robber brings six more within the hour.
**The Frame:** A notch is cut for each haul, a rope coil beside it (The Tomb Robbers). The hail is "Fair split?", answered "Fair split."
**The Gnawing:** In the east doorway, rats chew the frame (Hostile, 4-8, Bestiary : Giant Rat). They come out when the lamp is shuttered.

**Exits:** a wooden door, north, 60 feet -> D.1 The Stair Landing, an open passage, east, 60 feet -> D.5 The Carvers' Cells
