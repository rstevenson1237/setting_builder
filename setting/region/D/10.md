D.10 **Second Threshold** (medium) - *Temple Cult, Untested Threshold*
A second **Bound Dead** stands this vestry's outward doorway, bound not against crossing it but against carrying anything back out through it - a different working than the first, and no less absolute. Everything the rite needed was robed and taken up from here, which is why there is still something here worth taking.
*A vestry where the priest-kings' own procession robed before going deeper, fifteen feet across, its lintel carrying the same unfinished Essath line as the threshold above. Smells of old ash; the chamber holds an odd, expectant quiet even by the ziggurat's own standard.*
**Bound Dead:** Stands facing the passage back toward the Procession Passage rather than either inner doorway, and acts only against anything carried across it outward - empty hands pass both ways without incident, and it does not care what route a thing leaves by as long as it is not this one (Wary, 1, Bestiary : Bound Dead).
**Coiled Figurine:** In a robing niche at shoulder height, a fist-sized basalt figure cut in three coils, the same turn as the small fixed figure at the First Threshold and four times its size. It lifts straight out of the niche; carrying it back out through the east archway is the one thing the Bound Dead exists to stop (Keys: The Coiled Figurine).
**Abandoned Pack:** A travel pack, decades old, sits just inside the threshold on the inner side - set down by somebody who worked out what the binding stopped and left everything rather than test it (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, worn threshold, east side -> D.8 Procession Passage, open archway - basalt, intact, south side -> D.11 Ash-Choked Cellar, open archway - basalt, sealed panel ajar, north side -> D.12 Sealed Archive
