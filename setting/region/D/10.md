D.10 **The Lost Hunters' Camp** (Ruin) - *abandoned, tattered, ash-grey*
Three collapsed tents lie round a cold ash ring, a snapped bow in the dust and flies over rotting hide, the camp abandoned and still.
*200 by 150 feet. A northern hunting party camped here and has not come back. The river trail runs north to the northern villages.*
**The tattered tents:** collapsed skin shelters, a net pattern in blue-black pigment on a hide wall; the hunters' gear lies scattered among them.
**The centipedes:** four reddish many-legged worms nesting in the rot, a dry rustle in the litter (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Giant Centipede); a bite is Poison.
**The ash ring:** a cold circle of fire-blackened stones with a snapped bow beside it.
**Exits:** the river trail north -> F.1 The River Trail Head
