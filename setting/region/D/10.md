D.10 **The Watcher's Alcove** (medium) - *Alcove, Watching, Still*
A single wrapped figure stands motionless in a carved alcove overlooking the passage - the **Watcher's Alcove** - until something disturbs it.
*A narrow alcove ten feet deep set into the passage wall, raised half a step above the floor; dry and undisturbed, smelling faintly of preserving spice.*
**Posted Husk:** A single preserved guard stands in the alcove, motionless until disturbed, then relentless (Relentless, 1, Bestiary : Husk).
**Old Wrappings:** Its wrappings bear old-tongue lettering matching a warded door somewhere close.
**Guard's Kit:** A spear and a corroded badge of office lie at its feet, still wearable (Treasure II, d20).
**Exits:** the passage, south -> D.9 The Broken Causeway Stair, the passage, north -> D.11 The Undercroft Passage
