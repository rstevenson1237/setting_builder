D.10 **The Dust Room** (Low) - *choked, pale, silent*

Pale dust lies knee-deep, a stair hole open in the roof. Nothing moves; the dust settles.

*A room 20 feet square where the plinth stair (C.4) comes down 20 feet into the scraped halls. A pit under the deepest drift drops a wader into the dust (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).*

**The Drift:** Knee-deep, sunk in a hollow by the way out; the pit lies under it. Nobody knows of it.
**The Stair Hole:** Square, overhead, the plinth room's stair folded down through it.

**Exits:** an open passage -> D.4 The Scraped Hall, up the plinth stair -> C.4 The Plinth
