D.10 **Second Threshold** (medium) - *Temple Cult, Untested Threshold*
A second Bound Dead stands this inner doorway, bound not against crossing it but against carrying anything back out through it - a different working than the first, and no less absolute.
*A vestry where the priest-kings' own procession once robed before going deeper, fifteen feet across, its lintel carrying the same unfinished Essath line as the threshold above. Smells of old ash; the chamber holds an odd, expectant quiet even by the ziggurat's own standard.*
**Bound Dead:** Stands facing the passage back toward the Procession Passage rather than either inner doorway, and acts only against anything carried across it outward - empty hands pass both ways without incident (Wary, 1, Bestiary : Bound Dead).
**Abandoned Pack:** A travel pack, decades old, sits just inside the threshold on the inner side - dropped, or set down deliberately, by somebody who did not carry it back out (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - basalt, worn threshold, east side -> D.8 Procession Passage, open archway - basalt, intact, south side -> D.11 Ash-Choked Cellar, open archway - basalt, sealed panel ajar, north side -> D.12 Sealed Archive
