HA.5 **The Offering Terrace** (Medium) - *stained, sloped, channelled*
A sloped terrace of stained stone runs with channels, a stone block at its head, dripping and flies, chanting carrying along it.
*40 by 30 feet. Captives are first laid on the block; their blood runs down the channels toward the shaft.*
**The bound men:** six tribesmen chanting over the channel, each with a green scale-mark inked over the heart under their clothes (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Tribesman); they answer an alarm in two turns.
**The stone block:** waist-high and stained black, with channels cut from its foot.
**The channels:** grooves that run east across the slope, dark and sticky (a sign of the shrine above).
**Exits:** an open passage -> HA.4 The Bell Court, an archway -> HA.7 The Blood Channel
