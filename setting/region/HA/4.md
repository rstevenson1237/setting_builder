HA.4 **The Bell Court** (Medium) - *paved, ringing, walled*
A paved court stands round a great bronze bell on a frame, the metal humming faintly and echoes ringing off the walls.
*40 by 30 feet. The bell is rung by anything stepping on the flagstones.*
**The bell:** bronze as tall as a man on a stone frame, humming in the wind.
**The flagstones:** worn polished slabs in a path across the court; any tread rings the bell and calls the watch (Mystery: Flooring, a tell of polish on the stones, known to the guards).
**Exits:** an archway -> HA.3 The Second Terrace, an open passage -> HA.5 The Offering Terrace, an archway -> HA.8 The Shrine on the Top
