HA.3 **The Second Terrace** (Low) - *narrow, windy, bare*
A narrow terrace of bare stone lies whipped by wind, a low parapet along its edge and a cold draught from an arch.
*60 by 20 feet. A way off the stair to the guard room and the bell court.*
**The terrace wall:** a low parapet with serpents carved along it.
**The draught:** cold air from the arch on the east side, smelling of blood.
**Exits:** an archway down -> HA.2 The Great Stair, an archway -> HA.4 The Bell Court, an open passage -> HA.6 The Guard Room
