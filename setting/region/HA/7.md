HA.7 **The Blood Channel** (Low) - *grooved, dark, sticky*
A deep groove runs down in the dark, flies thick above it, the floor sticky and a slow drip falling.
*10 by 40 feet, a hall. The channel carries blood from the offering terrace down to the shaft room; the stains are a sign of the shrine's work.*
**The groove:** a hand-wide trough along the middle, dark with old blood and fresh.
**The side ledges:** narrow shelves either side where a person can walk clear of the flow.
**Exits:** an archway -> HA.5 The Offering Terrace, an open passage -> HA.10 The Shaft Room
