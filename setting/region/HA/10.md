HA.10 **The Shaft Room** (High) - *round, echoing, dark*
A round room echoes round a gaping shaft, rope creaking from a winch frame and a cold updraught rising from the dark.
*30 by 30 feet, round. The shaft drops a hundred feet to the halls, and the winch rope is the only way down it. Captives are lowered here at dusk.*
**The winch crew:** four bound tribesmen working the winch ropes, each with a green scale-mark inked over the heart under their clothes (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Tribesman); they answer an alarm in two turns.
**The shaft:** a gaping round hole with no rail; a step to the edge drops a person a hundred feet (Test of Constitution, 3d, Crushing).
**The crew's box:** iron-bound beside the winch frame (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** an open passage -> HA.7 The Blood Channel, an open passage -> HA.8 The Shrine on the Top, the winch rope, a hundred feet down -> HB.1 The Shaft Foot
