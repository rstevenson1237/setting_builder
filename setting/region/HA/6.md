HA.6 **The Guard Room** (Medium) - *cramped, armed, smoky*
A cramped room of bunks, spear racks and a smoking brazier, snoring and spear-butts, a dice game on a table.
*30 by 20 feet. The watch that answers an alarm in one turn.*
**The watch:** two snake men and four bound men drawing lots by the brazier, spears racked beside them (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Snake Man); they answer an alarm in one turn.
**The guards' purse:** coin and dice on the table (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** an open passage -> HA.3 The Second Terrace, an open passage -> HA.9 The Priests' Vestry
