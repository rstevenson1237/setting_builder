HA.9 **The Priests' Vestry** (Medium) - *lamp-lit, dry, robed*
A dry vestry glows by lamp, robes hung on pegs and rustling, chests along one wall.
*20 by 30 feet. The priests robe here before the altar.*
**The robes:** green-black cloth on pegs, cut for a hooded neck, as the snake men wear.
**The robe chest:** locked, with a pinprick hole in its lid; opening it fires a dart (Test of Constitution, 3d, Poison) and holds (Treasure II, d20).
**Exits:** an open passage -> HA.6 The Guard Room, an open passage -> HA.8 The Shrine on the Top
