HA.8 **The Shrine on the Top** (High) - *roofless, carved, burning*
A roofless shrine round a burning altar roars under the open sky, wind and hissing in the flames, a carved frieze along one wall.
*50 by 30 feet. The first emperor's throne room; the cult's watch climbs the stair within the turn if the fire is put out.*
**The priests:** three snake men feeding the altar's flames (Hostile, 3, Bestiary : Snake Man); they answer an alarm in one turn.
**The altar:** burning stone where the flames lean toward a speaker; a name spoken over it brings a vision (Test of Sanity, 2d).
**The offering bowl:** gold, on the altar's rim (Treasure III, d20).
**The frieze:** carved captives led up a stair to a coiled serpent, with a later tally scratched beside it (Lore: The Shrine Frieze).
**Exits:** an archway -> HA.4 The Bell Court, an open passage -> HA.10 The Shaft Room, an open passage -> HA.9 The Priests' Vestry
