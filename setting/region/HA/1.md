HA.1 **The First Terrace** (Low) - *open, worn, watched*
A wide worn terrace of mossy limestone lies open to the sky above the jungle canopy, wind and far drums on the air, a stair mouth at its north side.
*60 by 20 feet, 15 feet to the sky. The pyramid's first ledge; the south stair from the jungle comes up to it.*
**The parapet:** a low carved wall along the south edge, serpents coiling along it, the mark of the snake people's empire, where the canopy shows beyond.
**The stair mouth:** on the north side a worn opening climbs into the pyramid's face.
**Exits:** the great stair, an archway -> HA.2 The Great Stair, down the south stair into the jungle -> E.9 The Pyramid's Foot
