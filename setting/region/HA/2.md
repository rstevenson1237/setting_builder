HA.2 **The Great Stair** (Medium) - *steep, wide, watched*
A steep wide stair climbs the pyramid's face between torch niches, chanting rolling from above and the wind carrying every voice down it.
*10 by 40 feet, a hall. Bound men carry a captive up it at dusk; a patrol climbs by night.*
**The carvers' risers:** each step cut with a coiled serpent, worn by feet.
**The bearers:** four bound tribesmen carrying a captive up the stair, with a snake man leading, each with a green scale-mark inked over the heart under their clothes (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Snake Man); they answer an alarm in two turns.
**The torch niches:** one every ten steps, each holding a torch that burns lower after each captive.
**Exits:** an archway down -> HA.1 The First Terrace, an archway up -> HA.3 The Second Terrace
