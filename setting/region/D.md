D The Hill Jungle

Overview: Jungle-covered ridges and ravines, grassy clearings where dinosaurs graze, and caves in the ridges with red hand-prints at their mouths. No village claims it; a party crossing between the shores crosses it. Cave men and dinosaurs fight over the clearings' waterholes, and a northern hunting party has not come back. If a fire is lit in a clearing, hoots call a band of cave men within the watch and every beast within a mile comes to look; if a party turns east out of the hills, its guide stops at the serpent posts and goes no further.

Approach: By the great gate at Puluni: a warden, the chiefs' word, a search. By the hill-foot hummocks from the swamps: a lizard men's lookout by day, a canoe patrol by night. By the western ridge trail: red hand-prints at its head. By the serpent posts: no guide past them. By the river trail: a hunters' camp at the last bend. By the crater trail: a sentry at the lip. By the sinkhole: troglodyte lookouts at night.

Terrain: Jungle hills and ravines. On a game trail a party covers 2 miles a watch and leaves a track a cave men's band can follow. In a clearing a party sees a dinosaur a bowshot off and is seen by it, and cannot hide. A ravine floods within a watch of rain, and a party in it takes a Test of Constitution against the water, a Wound of Suffocation on a failure.

Ways:
- D.1 - The hill trail climbs from the swamp's edge into the hills, 2 days end to end: the trail head, a track to the waterhole D.7, the clearing's edge D.3 and a cairn leading to the lost hunters' camp D.10.

Landmarks:
- D.2 - Red hand-prints round smoky cave mouths in a ridge face; eight cave men hoot to call the band.
- D.3 - A trampled clearing strewn with huge bones; a forty-foot reptile stalks the trails from the trees.
- D.4 - A vast fig tree hung with vine coils; a thirty-foot python hangs in it.
- D.5 - A bare guano-stained rock stack screaming with wings; six pterodactyls stoop on the smallest of a party.
- D.6 - A root-hung sinkhole falling into rank darkness; the way down to the troglodytes' warren.
- D.7 - A trampled shallow waterhole; three triceratops drink at its edge.
- D.8 - Hidden falls roaring into a slick ravine; the ravine floods within a watch of rain.
- D.9 - A still glade of ferns round a steaming spring whose water cures a Diseased drinker.
- D.10 - Tattered tents round a cold ash ring; four centipedes nest in the rot.
- D.11 - Weathered serpent posts at the jungle's edge, some freshly re-cut; a guide turns back here.
- D.12 - A high cleared ridge above the great wall, huge three-toed prints in the dung at dawn.
