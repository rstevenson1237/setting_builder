D Daghash

Overview: Daghash is the black ziggurat the priest-kings of Essath cut into Marfen's
northern hills more than five hundred years ago, roofless since whatever ended their reign,
and unclaimed by any of the three living kingdoms. Getting in costs more than the climb -
every threshold the priest-kings bound still holds exactly what it was told to hold, and
nothing since has troubled to unbind it. Unlike anywhere else in Telar, what is true here was
decided over five hundred years ago, and nobody currently living has the authority to change
it.

Ambiance: Cold stone even at noon. A smell of old ash pools in the lower levels with nowhere
left to vent. The Upper tier is silent but for wind through the open roof.

Architecture: Cut black basalt, dressed and jointed by a masonry tradition better than any
now practiced. A carved serpent-coil repeats at every lintel and door frame, tying the whole
work together as one build rather than three. Ceilings run tall on the Upper tier, about
twelve feet; passages throughout are cut narrow, barely three feet, built for a single-file
procession rather than a crowd.

Layout: A collection - a worked complex of roughly eighteen chambers across three stepped
tiers. Two entrances: a broken formal stair up the south face, and a half-collapsed service
tunnel opening into the hillside from the north. The Upper tier is roofless and open to sky;
the Middle tier carries the priest-kings' bound thresholds and their Essath inscriptions; the
Lower tier holds Nirguk's own sealed enclave, cut off from the rest of the complex behind its
own door. Time here runs on the Danger countdown below, not the clock.

Features: Sound carries strangely down through the stepped tiers - a footstep two levels
below is as audible as one in the same room, and nothing muffles it. No standing water
anywhere in the complex; whatever channeled it away still works after five centuries. A
struck light on the Upper tier is visible from the causeway in Marfen at night - the
roofless tier hides nothing from outside.

Dangers: The Bound Dead hold the Middle tier's thresholds and act only against whatever
their specific binding names - an intruder, a crossed threshold, an object removed. Nirguk
on the Lower tier is territorial only around whatever their enclave guards. The Upper tier is
otherwise genuinely abandoned rather than watched.

Creatures: Bound Dead stand posted at the Middle tier's thresholds (setting/Bestiary.md). A
Nirguk enclave of a dozen or fewer holds the Lower tier behind its sealed door and never
leaves it undefended. A Stone Ward stands at whatever that door actually guards, inert until
its stated trigger.

Factions: None of Telar's three named factions hold any part of Daghash as a position -
Nirguk answers to nobody outside their own enclave.

Secrets: What actually ended the priest-kings' reign within a single generation goes
unanswered anywhere else in Telar - if the ziggurat knows, it hasn't said. Nirguk's own way
to the surface avoids every bound threshold on the Middle tier entirely, which is the only
reason their enclave has never had to test one.

Treasure: setting/Treasure3.md (gems and jewelry) for the priest-kings' own regalia, and
setting/Treasure5.md (treasure cache) for what the Lower tier has kept sealed.

Tables: d6 Danger, counting down from 6 with each failed Difficulty roll.
6. Something on the Lower tier notices a threshold has been disturbed - nothing visible
   happens yet.
5. Dust sifts down from the Upper tier's open roof - wind, or something moving up there.
4. A Bound Dead unit already posted turns to face the party's direction, and does not turn
   back.
3. A sound carries down from two tiers away that has no business being there at this hour.
2. Something is on the Middle tier that was not there when the party passed through the
   first time.
1. Whatever the nearest disturbed threshold was bound to hold no longer considers itself
   bound.
