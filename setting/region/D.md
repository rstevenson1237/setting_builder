D The Black Mere

Overview: Reed marsh north of the road and west of the wood, three miles across by the causeway. The Sellaca live on it in a stilt village. The makers' drowned shrine lies under its eastern flats, and the dead in it do not stay down. A party comes to cross to the wood, to look for what the drowned shrine holds, or to turn the Sellaca against the outlaws. The Sellaca are closing the mere to every stranger. If a party keeps to the causeway, they let it pass; if it leaves the causeway, they close in boats within a watch. The outlaws are testing the causeway's edge, and a clash between the two would close it to everyone.
Approach: Entered from the road by the causeway, 5 miles from the keep gate, and from the wood by a side path to the mere's north shore. The causeway crosses the mere in one watch; the Sellaca watch it from the reeds and allow those who keep to it.
Terrain: swamp - On the causeway a watch covers 8 miles, by pole boat 4, and wading through the worst ground, 2. Off the causeway a FAILURE on Maneuver sinks a party to the waist and holds it until freed, a Test of Constitution each Round. Fog hides the causeway's edges, and a party that leaves it by night is lost until dawn. A fire on the flats draws the drowned dead.
