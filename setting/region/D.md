D Sihura

Overview: Halls and cells of the second level where murals, carvings and the rooms between them tell the cynidiceans' history in order. A party comes down to read it and to find the way on. The tomb robbers hold the watch (D.3); Kurumisa, Ruhiahe and Sihuahet scouts hold the halls. Killing a robber sends six within the hour; defacing a mural brings a scout from one branch to see who did it.

Approach: The stair from the first room (C.4) lands at D.1: two soldiers of Kurumisa patrol the arch, and the tomb robbers' watchers sit at D.3 with a rope and a torch. A coin or a name buys a way past; refuse and the watchers raise the camp.

Conditions: Carved sandstone, 15 feet high and 8 wide; torch only. Stone carries a shout down every hall; dust over smooth stone underfoot; the air dry and oil-scented. Smoke from a fire drifts down the stair.

Entrances:

Prizes:
- D.6 - A painted gallery guarded by skeletons; a niche behind the paint (Lore: The Gallery of the Fall) and a stair behind the last panel.

Places:
- D.1 - A narrow landing under a carved lintel, a red hawk on the doorpost and torchlight from the east arch.
- D.2 - Thirty-one carved kings, a sun-disc over every door; two acolytes and an adept.
- D.3 - Bedrolls and a lamp in a doorway; six to eight delvers and rats at the east door.
- D.4 - Murals scraped to plaster, pale dust and zombies.
- D.5 - Six tiny cells, a tool chest (Lore: The Carver's Count) and an archway locked by a sun-disc stone.
