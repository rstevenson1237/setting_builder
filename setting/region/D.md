D Pyramid Level 2 - The Halls of History

Overview: Four areas of halls and cells on the second level, joined by winding passages, where murals, carvings and the rooms between tell the cynidiceans' history in order: the robbers' halls (D.1, D.3, D.7), the founders' halls (D.2, D.8, D.9), the scraped halls (D.4, D.10, D.11) and the gallery (D.5, D.6, D.12). Each of the three stairs from above lands in a different area. Killing a robber sends six from the camp within the hour; defacing a mural brings a scout from one branch to see who did it.

Approach: The folding stair from the tile floor (C.2) lands at D.1, where two Royal Guard soldiers patrol the archway and the robbers' watch sits behind a door: a coin or a name passes, or the watchers raise the camp. The hidden stair from the mural wall (C.3) lands at D.8 on a darted sill. The plinth stair (C.4) lands at D.10 in knee-deep dust over a pit.

Prizes:
- D.6 - A painted gallery guarded by skeletons, the gallery area's challenge; a niche behind the paint (Lore: The Gallery of the Fall), a word cut under the last panel's frame (Keys: The Door Word) and a stair behind the panel.
- D.7 - The robbers' camp behind a coffin barricade, the robbers' area's challenge; six to eight delvers sell the ways down above, and a strongbox (Treasure II, d20).

Places:
- D.1 - A narrow landing under a trapdoor, a red hawk on the doorpost; two Royal Guard soldiers at the archway.
- D.2 - Thirty-one carved kings in order, a sun-disc over every door; two acolytes recite them.
- D.3 - Bedrolls and a lamp in a doorway; two to four delvers, and rats in the winding passage.
- D.4 - Murals scraped to plaster, pale dust and zombies, the scraped area's challenge.
- D.5 - Six tiny cells, a tool chest (Lore: The Carver's Count) and an archway locked by a sun-disc stone.
- D.8 - A steep stair between carved kings' feet onto a sill with pinholed jambs.
- D.9 - Thirty-one bronze name plates, scorched in places, a blank one last (Treasure I, d20), the founders' challenge; an adept copies them.
- D.10 - Knee-deep pale dust under a stair hole, sunk in a hollow by the way out.
- D.11 - A long hall whose floor creaks on a ridge underfoot.
- D.12 - Carved carvers with stone mallets raised over chipped floor.
