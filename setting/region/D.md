D The Black Mere

Overview: Reed marsh north of the road and west of the wood, three miles across by the causeway. A party comes to cross to the wood, to look for what the drowned shrine holds, or to turn the Sellaca against the outlaws. The Sellaca, from their stilt village, are closing the mere to every stranger; the makers' drowned shrine lies under its eastern flats, and the dead in it do not stay down. If a party leaves the causeway, the Sellaca close in boats within a watch. The Wood Outlaws are testing the causeway's edge, and a clash between the two would close it to everyone.

Approach: Entered from the road by the causeway, 5 miles from the keep gate, and from the wood by a side path to the mere's north shore. The causeway crosses in one watch; the Sellaca watch it from the reeds and let pass those who keep to it.

Terrain: swamp - On the causeway a watch covers 8 miles, by pole boat 4, and wading through the worst ground 2. Off the causeway a FAILURE on Maneuver sinks a party to the waist and holds it until freed, a Test of Constitution each Round. Fog hides the causeway's edges, and a party that leaves it by night is lost until dawn. A fire on the flats draws the drowned dead.

Ways:
- D.1 - North on black piles from the causeway head (B.5), a yard wide, with passing bays every hundred yards and no rails. Its stops in order: the flat edge, where tussocks lead east onto D.4; the shrine channel, a reed channel east to D.3; the boat landing, where pole boats wait and D.2 lies east; and the far shore, where the planks end and firm ground runs on to the mere landing (C.6).

Landmarks:
- D.2 - Twelve huts on stilts round a central platform, joined by rope bridges, smoked fish on reed racks and fish-bone points standing in the mud at every post; its smoke shows over the reeds. Reached by pole boat from the landing.
- D.3 - Green stone pillars standing out of black water, knee-deep at the pillars and over a man's head beyond, steps leading down and eight motionless figures among them.
- D.4 - A level grey flat of mud and algae, gas bubbling in the shallows, sedge tussocks a stride apart.

Places:
- D.5 - Beyond the village by pole boat, unseen from the causeway: a dry knoll ringed by tall reeds, a cairn of white bones bound with red thread at its top. The village boat's wake runs to it from the platform's north-west.

Tables:
d6 Encounter
1. A pole boat slides from the reeds with two spears in it, silent, and falls in behind the party.
2. A plank gives under the lead crosser and drops them to the waist.
3. Fog closes over the water, and the causeway's edges vanish.
4. A pale shape stands motionless in the water, then walks toward the party.
5. Marsh gas bubbles up beside the planks, and a spark would set it alight.
6. A heron lifts from the reeds and the watchers ahead go still.
