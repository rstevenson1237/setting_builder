I.11 **The Narrowing Room** (Medium) - *scored, bare, closing*

A bare room, its floor scored deep where it meets the walls. A black hand is painted by the door, and something grinds deep down.

*A room 20 feet square. When its iron door shuts, the walls grind inward and meet in ten Rounds (Test of Constitution, 3d, Crushing). Pressing the black hand stops them. The cult uses it on the chosen who fail.*

**The Walls:** Bare basalt, their bases scored into the floor.
**The Black Hand:** Painted by the door at shoulder height (Faction : Voxkroth); the palm gives a little when pressed.

**Exits:** an iron door to the cells -> I.5 The Chosen's Cells, an iron door to the corridor -> I.14 The Black Corridor
