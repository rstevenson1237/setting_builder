I.3 **The Altar of Unmaking** (High) - *black, wet, loud*

A black altar stands in a hall, a drain round its base, benches ranked behind it. A chant and a rhythmic knock fill the air.

*A hall 50 feet by 40 and 16 high (The Demon Is Invited). The chosen are unmade here; the lieutenants hear within the hour if the altar is fouled.*

**The Priests:** Six chant over the unmade (Hostile, 6, Faction : Voxkroth). They want a captive unmade and answer an alarm within a turn.
**The Unmade:** Lie on the benches (Hostile, 4-8, Bestiary : Zombie).
**The Bowl:** An altar bowl holds coin (Treasure III, d20).

**Exits:** an archway -> I.2 The Hall of Cells, an open passage -> I.6 The Bone Store, an iron door -> I.9 The Chosen's Gate
