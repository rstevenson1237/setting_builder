I.3 **The Gun Deck** (Medium) - *low, long, rotting*
A low long deck rots between gun-ports, water slapping and timbers groaning, a ladder and a hatch.
*30 by 30 feet. The planks are rotten.*
**The gun-ports:** square openings along the sides, cannon rusted fast in them.
**The soft planks:** weeping timber underfoot; stepping on it breaks through (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Exits:** a wooden door -> I.2 The Captain's Cabin, a wooden door -> I.5 The Cargo Hold, a wooden door -> I.6 The Galley
