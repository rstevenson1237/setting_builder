I.3 **The Barracks** (Medium) - *bare, rowed, cold*
Sixteen **cots** stand in two rows down a long hall, every blanket squared. **Hobgoblins** rest between watches, oiling their mail. An iron **grate** closes a low passage at the far end.
*Hall 70 ft by 20 ft, ceiling 10 ft. A horn by the door calls the whole hold.*
**Hobgoblins:** Four off the watch, who arm, form a line and sound the horn (Wary, 4, Bestiary : Hobgoblin). The oldest keeps the grate.
**Cots:** Straw pallets and a chest to each, nothing in them but kit.
**Grate:** Iron bars set in the makers' stone across a passage that runs on north-east; a gnoll stink blows through it, and a heap of stones lies beyond it.
**Exits:** open, south end of the hall -> I.2 The Drill Court, iron, north-east end of the hall behind the grate -> J.4 The Bone Heap
