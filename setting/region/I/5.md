I.5 **The Chosen's Cells** (Medium) - *small, locked, muttering*

Six small cells open off a passage, each door locked and muttering behind it. The sound never stops.

*A passage 30 feet by 40 with six cells (Faction : Voxkroth).*

**The Unmade:** Mutter behind the doors (Hostile, 1-2, Bestiary : Wraith). The muttering breaks off when a light shows.
