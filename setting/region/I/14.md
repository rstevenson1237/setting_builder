I.14 **The Black Corridor** (Low) - *narrow, pocked, dark*

A narrow black passage, its walls pocked with small holes. Something clicks in the wall.

*A corridor 6 feet wide and 40 long between the stair landing and the narrowing room. Its dart trap is set off from the landing end (I.1).*

**The Pocked Walls:** Holes at chest height, a dart's tip in one.
**The Middle Flag:** It sits a finger proud of the rest.

**Exits:** a narrow opening -> I.1 The Stair Landing, an iron door -> I.11 The Narrowing Room
