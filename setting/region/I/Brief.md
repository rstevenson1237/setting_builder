Layout: The hold runs east into the rock from its gate on the ravine's east wall. The gate (I.1) lies at the mouth, with the drill court (I.2) 30 feet east, the barracks (I.3) 40 feet north of the court, the armoury (I.4) 30 feet south, the grain stores (I.5) 30 feet east of the armoury, and the warlord's hall (I.6) 60 feet east of the court. A grate at the barracks (I.3) opens 90 feet north-east to the gnolls' bone heap (J.4).

Places:
- I.1 The Hobgoblin Gate - the way in; two hobgoblins behind iron-banded bars.
- I.2 The Drill Court - four hobgoblins drilling in ranks.
- I.3 The Barracks - four hobgoblins off the watch, a horn, an iron grate.
- I.4 The Armoury - arms on frames and a chest.
- I.5 The Grain Stores - hundreds of stencilled sacks.
- I.6 The Warlord's Hall - Varn the Red-Sashed, two hobgoblins, a belt pouch and a strongbox.

Inhabitants: Two of The Burgnaz on the ledge, two Hobgoblins at I.1, four of The Burgnaz at I.2, four Hobgoblins at I.3, and Varn the Red-Sashed with two Hobgoblins at I.6.

Alarm: An unanswered challenge at I.1 raises it. The court (I.2) hears at once and closes ranks; the barracks (I.3) sound the horn by the door in a Round; Varn at I.6 hears it and his own horn brings the barracks' hobgoblins into his hall, in step, in one Round. A lit flame is smothered by the watch before it spreads.

Loot: The largest prize is Varn's Hoard in the strongbox under the dais at I.6, with the iron sanctum key in his belt pouch; Treasure II lies in the arms chest at I.4 and Treasure III under the grain at I.5. A.7 buys it at 80%.

Secrets:
- An iron grate in the barracks closes a makers' passage to the gnolls' den - the Burgnaz keep watch on it, the Kurzun have heaped bones over their end; the grate and the gnoll stink (I.3) bring it out.
- The iron key in Varn's belt pouch opens the shrine's sanctum door - the Burgnaz; the pouch beside his horn (I.6) brings it out.

Leads:
- Rumour 10 - I.6 Varn the Red-Sashed.
- Rumour 11 - I.6 Belt Pouch.
