Layout: The hold has one mouth on the ravine's east wall, the hobgoblin ledge (E.5), 40 feet above the path, behind a built wall of stone and timber 10 feet high with an iron-banded gate and a red hand stencilled beside it, spears planted upright in ranks before it; a pair stands guard day and night, relieved on the watch. The gate (I.1) lies behind the wall: a passage with arrow slits in both walls, barred from within. The hold is dressed makers' stone with timber braces, ceilings 10 feet and passages wide enough for a shield line; light is oil lamps in niches, sound carries along the straight halls to the gate, the footing is swept and dry, the air is clean, and a lit fire is smothered at once by a watch trained to do it.

The warlord's hall (I.6), the one High location, lies beyond the drill court (I.2) through a wooden door. It is a hall with a ceiling of 14 feet, hung with red banners marked with a white hand, a dais at its far end and a table of flat stones chalked with the ravine's ledges and the gnoll den's mouths; Varn the Red-Sashed (Named Creature: Varn the Red-Sashed) reads the stones with two hobgoblins on the dais steps and a horn at his hip, and a belt pouch beside the horn and a strongbox under the dais hold his key and his hoard.

The drill court (I.2) lies beyond the gate (I.1): a court with a ceiling of 10 feet, ranked spear racks, lamps in wall niches and a red hand stencilled on the far wall. The barracks (I.3) open off the court (I.2): a hall with sixteen cots in two rows, a horn by the door and, at the far end, an iron grate across a low passage that runs on to the gnolls' bone heap (J.4); a party can notice a gnoll stink blowing through the grate and a heap of stones beyond it. The armoury (I.4) opens off the court (I.2) behind a banded door barred from the court side: a narrow room with mail on frames from floor to roof and a chest with a heavy hasp at the back. The grain stores (I.5) lie beyond the armoury (I.4): a long store with sacks stencilled with a red hand stacked in rows and swept aisles between them.

Inhabitants: Two of The Burgnaz (Faction: The Burgnaz) stand at the gate on the ledge below, at E.5. Two Hobgoblins stand the watch behind the bars of I.1, wary, challenging and barring the inner gate if the challenge is not answered; they know the relief hours. Four of The Burgnaz drill in ranks in the court (I.2), wary, closing ranks at a stranger and wanting the hold ready to march; they know the signal for the barracks. Four Hobgoblins rest between watches in the barracks (I.3), wary, arming, forming a line and sounding the horn; the oldest keeps the grate. Varn the Red-Sashed (Named Creature: Varn the Red-Sashed) reads his stones in I.6 five days in six, wary, challenging from the dais and parleying with the unarmed in the black speech, wanting the shrine's favour and the gnolls broken; he knows what the gnolls sell and to whom. Two Hobgoblins hold the line at his dais and guard him.

Alarm: An unanswered challenge at I.1 raises it. The court (I.2) hears at once and closes ranks; the barracks (I.3) sound the horn by the door in a Round; Varn at I.6 hears it and his own horn brings the barracks' hobgoblins into his hall, in step, in one Round. A lit flame is smothered by the watch before it spreads.

Loot: The hold keeps the shrine's favour in iron and grain. The largest prize is Varn's Hoard (Hoard: Varn's Hoard) in the iron strongbox under the dais at I.6, with Varn and two hobgoblins over it, and in his belt pouch the flat iron key to the shrine's sanctum door (Keys: The Sanctum Key); Treasure II lies in the locked arms chest at I.4 and Treasure III under the sacks at I.5. The Moneychanger (A.7) buys it back at 80% of value.

Secrets:
- An iron grate in the barracks closes a makers' passage to the gnolls' den - the Burgnaz keep watch on it, the Kurzun have heaped bones over their end; the grate and the gnoll stink (I.3) bring it out.
- The iron key in Varn's belt pouch opens the shrine's sanctum door - the Burgnaz; the pouch beside his horn (I.6) brings it out.
