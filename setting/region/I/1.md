I.1 **The Hobgoblin Gate** (Medium) - *walled, drilled, barred*
A timber **gate** stands in a built stone **wall** across a swept passage, a red hand stencilled beside it. Two **Hobgoblins** in mail stand the watch behind the bars.
*Gate passage 20 ft deep and 10 ft wide, arrow slits in both walls.*
**Gate:** Iron-banded timber, barred from within; the guard lifts the bar for a party that answers the challenge, and the slits cover the whole passage.
**Wall:** Ten feet of stone and timber, spears planted upright in ranks before it.
**Red Hand:** Stencilled beside the gate in red dye.
**Hobgoblins:** Two stand inside with spears grounded; they call a challenge, and bar the inner gate if it is not answered (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Hobgoblin). They know the relief hours.
**Exits:** banded, west, through the gate, 20 feet -> E.5 The Hobgoblin Ledge, archway, east end of the passage, 40 feet -> I.2 The Drill Court
