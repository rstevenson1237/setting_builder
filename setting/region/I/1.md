I.1 **The Stern Deck** (Low) - *slanted, rotten, gilded*
A slanted rotten stern deck keeps its gilded rails, surf slapping and timbers creaking, a companionway going down at its centre.
*30 by 20 feet. The galleon's way in from the reef gap; a mer guard waits in the water. A captain's quarterdeck once.*
**The gilded rails:** green-rotted gilt on a rail still standing along the deck's edge, a belt and collar of green weed and shell hung on one post.
**The companionway:** a steep wooden stair down to the cabin below.
**Exits:** a wooden door -> I.2 The Captain's Cabin, out to the reef -> C.9 The Reef Gap
