I.12 **The Ash Pit** (Low) - *grey, deep, warm*

A railed pit of warm grey ash fills the floor, ticking as it cools.

*A room 20 feet square where the altars' ash is raked. The pit is 10 feet deep in soft ash.*

**The Pit:** Railed in bronze, the ash too soft to stand on; a rake hangs on the rail.
**The Ash:** Warm and soft; raked through, it gives up rings and buckles the fire did not take (Treasure II, d20).

**Exits:** an open passage to the bones -> I.6 The Bone Store
