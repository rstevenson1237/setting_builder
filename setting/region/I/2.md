I.2 **The Hall of Cells** (Medium) - *barred, packed, whispering*

Barred cells pack both sides of a long hall, whispering and chains rattling. A black hand marks each door.

*A hall 50 feet by 30. Captives and the chosen wait here; every cell opens at once if a chosen is freed.*

**The Cells:** Iron-barred, a dozen a side, each with a straw bed and a mask on a hook (Faction : Voxkroth).
**The Guards:** Walk the hall (Wary, 4, Faction : Voxkroth). They want a mask or a mark shown and answer an alarm within a turn.

**Exits:** an open passage, west, 70 feet -> I.1 The Stair Landing, an archway, north, 70 feet -> I.4 The Altar of Making, an archway, east, 70 feet -> I.3 The Altar of Unmaking, a wooden door, south, 70 feet -> I.7 The Dressing Room
