I.2 **The Drill Court** (Medium) - *swept, ordered, echoing*
A swept court of ranked **spear racks** lies under **lamps** in wall niches, a red hand stencilled on the far wall. **Hobgoblins** drill in ranks, stamping in step.
*Court 60 ft square, ceiling 10 ft. Stamping and shouted orders carry to the gate and the barracks.*
**Hobgoblins:** Four drill in mail and close ranks at a stranger's first step (Wary, 4, Bestiary : Hobgoblin); one carries the signal that calls the barracks.
**Spear Racks:** Rank on rank of ash spears, all the same length.
**Lamps:** Oil, in niches at head height; an overturned lamp is smothered with a sand scoop by the nearest guard.
**Red Hand:** Stencilled on the far wall, the paint fresh.
**Exits:** archway, west side of the court, 40 feet -> I.1 The Hobgoblin Gate, open, north side of the court, 65 feet -> I.3 The Barracks, banded, south side of the court, 40 feet -> I.4 The Armoury, wooden, east side of the court, 70 feet -> I.6 The Warlord's Hall
