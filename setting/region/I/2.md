I.2 **The Captain's Cabin** (High) - *panelled, flooded, locked*
A panelled cabin lies knee-deep in water, lapping and groaning wood, a table and a sea-chest and a key on a hook.
*30 by 20 feet. The mer people strip the cabin's silver; the strongbox lies in the bilge, behind a lock the captain's key opens.*
**The mer people:** four stripping the cabin's silver, wanting iron and fire-worked things, and knowing the beast's feeding hours (Wary, 4, Bestiary : Merman); a high wavering song comes first, and they answer an alarm in two turns.
**The cabin door:** opening it lets water seep in (Test of Constitution, 2d, Suffocation).
**The key-hook:** a brass key on a rotted lanyard (Keys: The Captain's Key).
**The log tube:** lead, holding the captain's log, which names a light ashore at the western cove (Lore: The Captain's Log).
**Exits:** a wooden door -> I.1 The Stern Deck, a wooden door -> I.3 The Gun Deck
