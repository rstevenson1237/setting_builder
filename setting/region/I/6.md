I.6 **The Warlord's Hall** (High) - *high, banner-hung, guarded*
A long hall hung with red **banners**, a **dais** at the far end and a **table** of marked stones before it. **Varn** reads the stones, two **Hobgoblins** at his shoulder.
*Hall 70 ft by 25 ft, ceiling 14 ft. A horn at Varn's hip brings the barracks in one Round.*
**Varn the Red-Sashed:** Gives a challenge from the dais, then parleys in the black speech with anyone who comes unarmed. He knows what the gnolls sell and to whom (Named Creature: Varn the Red-Sashed).
**Hobgoblins:** Two on the dais steps, who hold the line and guard him (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Hobgoblin).
**Belt Pouch:** At Varn's hip beside the horn, holding the iron key to the shrine's sanctum door (Keys: The Sanctum Key).
**Table:** Flat stones on a plank, chalked with the ravine's ledges and the gnoll den's mouths.
**Strongbox:** Iron, under the dais, shut with a plain latch (Hoard: Varn's Hoard).
**Banners:** Red, each marked with a hand in white.
**Exits:** wooden, west end of the hall -> I.2 The Drill Court
