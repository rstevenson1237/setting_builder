I.4 **The Armoury** (Low) - *racked, oiled, locked*
Spears, helms and mail shirts stand on wooden **frames** from floor to roof, a barred **door** behind you. A **chest** with a heavy hasp stands against the back wall.
*Room 30 ft by 10 ft. The bar is on the outside of the door.*
**Frames:** Mail on shaped wood, each shirt oiled and numbered in red chalk; a few frames stand empty.
**Chest:** Locked with a bar and hasp, holding spare blades and fittings (Treasure II, d20).
**Door:** Iron-banded, barred from the court side.
**Exits:** banded, north side of the room -> I.2 The Drill Court, wooden, east end of the room -> I.5 The Grain Stores
