I.4 **The Flooded Hold** (High) - *black, deep, echoing*
Black water fills a vast echoing hold, slow ripples with no wind, ribs rising out of it and crates sunk below.
*30 by 30 feet. The tide floods it. The mer people keep a beast here and feed it.*
**The beast:** a blind white eel as long as a longboat, lying in the black water (Named Creature: Nealvaol).
**The tide:** water climbs the walls as the tide turns (Test of Constitution, 2d, Suffocation).
**The sunk cargo:** crates under the water (Treasure IV, d20).
**The far door:** wood at the hold's end, locked; it takes the Captain's Key (Keys: The Captain's Key).
**Exits:** a wooden door -> I.5 The Cargo Hold, the locked bilge door -> I.7 The Bilge
