I.4 **The Altar of Making** (Medium) - *black, humming, warm*

A black altar is warm to the touch, runes cut round it. A low hum fills the room.

*A room 40 feet by 30 (The Demon Is Invited). The chosen are made here.*

**The Altar:** Warm stone with runes cut round its base. Touching it wakes the runes (Test of Sanity, 2d).
**The Coffer:** A runed coffer by the altar (Treasure IV, d20).

**Exits:** an archway -> I.2 The Hall of Cells, a wooden door -> I.5 The Chosen's Cells, a stone door -> I.6 The Bone Store
