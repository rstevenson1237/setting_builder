I.9 **The Chosen's Gate** (High) - *carved, guarded, black*

An iron-bound gate carved with a black hand fills the north end of the hall, guards on either side. The gate hums.

*A hall 20 feet by 40 and 16 high. The gate leads down to the lieutenants' level; it opens only to a worn mask.*

**The Guards:** Stand either side (Wary, 4, Faction : Voxkroth). They want a mask shown and answer an alarm within a turn.
**The Gate:** Iron and black stone, with a plate shaped to a mask's face (Keys: The Chosen's Mask).
**The Chest:** A guards' chest (Treasure IV, d20).

**Exits:** an iron door, west, 70 feet -> I.3 The Altar of Unmaking, down a stair, north, 40 feet -> J.1 The Stair Landing
