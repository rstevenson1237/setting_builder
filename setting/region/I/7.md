I.7 **The Dressing Room** (Medium) - *masked, cluttered, dim*

Masks, robes and paints stand on shelves along three walls, a mirror at the back. The room is dim and still.

*A room 30 feet square, where the chosen are dressed (Faction : Voxkroth).*

**The Shelves:** Masks of bronze and black, paint pots, robes. The paint shelf hides a gas trap (Test of Constitution, 2d, Poison), shown by a sweet smell.
**The Mask:** Black bronze, ash-rimmed at the eyeholes, on the top shelf (Keys: The Chosen's Mask).

**Exits:** a wooden door -> I.2 The Hall of Cells, an open passage -> I.8 The Blood Channel, a wooden door -> I.10 The Washing Room
