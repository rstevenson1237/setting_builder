I.7 **The Bilge** (Medium) - *foul, low, sloshing*
A foul low bilge sloshes with dark water under ribs, creaking and a rot-stench, an iron strongbox sunk in the muck at its end.
*20 by 10 feet, a hall. The strongbox is here; the only way in is the locked door from the flooded hold, which the captain's key opens.*
**The bilge claws:** four shield-sized shells half buried in the muck (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Giant Crab); prints cross the wet planks.
**The strongbox:** a bolted iron box sunk in the muck at the bilge's end (Hoard: The Galleon's Strongbox).
**Exits:** a wooden door -> I.4 The Flooded Hold
