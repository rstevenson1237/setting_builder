I.8 **The Blood Channel** (Medium) - *sunk, red, slick*

A sunk channel of slow red water runs the length of the room. The floor is slick.

*A room 60 feet by 10 (The Demon Is Invited). The channel carries blood from the altar toward the demon.*

**The Channel:** Slick stone, red water to the knee.
**The Ghouls:** Wade the channel (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Ghoul). The water breaks in slow rings round them.
**The Bottle:** A sealed bottle in the channel holds a priest's notes (Lore: The Making of the Chosen).

**Exits:** an open passage -> I.7 The Dressing Room
