I.10 **The Washing Room** (Medium) - *cold, wet, echoing*

Stone tubs of black water line two walls, frost on their rims. Water drips and echoes.

*A room 20 feet square where the chosen are washed before they are dressed. The water is bitterly cold; touching it numbs to the bone (Test of Fate, Exposed). The cult knows to use the warm jugs by the door.*

**The Tubs:** Black water, frost on the stone, a scum of ash on top.
**The Jugs:** Clay, by the door, still faintly warm from the altars; poured into a tub, one takes the cold off for a turn.

**Exits:** a wooden door -> I.7 The Dressing Room
