A Fellward

Overview: Fellward is the only place within days that can resupply, pay, shelter, or heal
anyone who reaches it, so every road a party takes eventually bends back to its gate. Stay
past a season and a name goes on the duty roster whether it was offered or not - the keep
presses whoever it can reach. It is the one region in this setting where the treeline is
not the nearest danger; the grain count is.

Ambiance: Grey fieldstone curtain wall in three visibly different mortars, patched by three
different generations who didn't match the last one's work. Mud year-round from more people
and animals than the ground was built to drain. Cookfire smoke, wet wool, and the sound of
a bell that rings watch changes on a schedule the roster can't actually keep.

People: Soldiers carried on the roster past their term because nobody's struck them off,
conscripted farmhands serving out a press they didn't choose, camp followers, and the last
few settler families who never left when the paved road did. No smith of standing - the
forge is worked part-time by whoever's turn it is, and blades go unsharpened longer than
they should. No dedicated healer, only a field medic stretched thin. The Quartermaster's
Ring holds the real say over who gets fed and who gets issued what; the Diggers and the
Barrow-Kin both have dealings that pass through Fellward's gate without the garrison
knowing which is which.

Situation: The relief column requisitioned three seasons ago hasn't come, and the granary
is already being rationed against the assumption it never will. The Quartermaster's Ring
decides who actually gets fed regardless of what the ledger claims; soldiers, conscripts,
and camp followers alike eat what's issued to them. The rung it's on now: the night watch
already runs two names short because rations don't stretch to feeding people who aren't
also working. The next rung: if the count drops further, someone gets struck from the
roster outright - and losing the token that proves service erases it, no back pay, no
record they were ever here.

Layout: Not time-bound; nothing here runs on a clock the referee needs to track. A single
gate faces the supply road, flanked by the curtain wall's two remaining corner towers
(the original four are down to these two - the other pair fell twelve years back, past
the current watch-line, not here). Granary, barracks, forge, and stable sit within sixty
yards of the gate; the barracks block itself was built for three times the garrison it
now holds, half of it shuttered and stripped for its own timber.

Features: Gate stands open dawn to dusk and closed after, no exceptions made even for
Charter Agents on schedule. Prices run to what the Quartermaster's Ring allows through
official channels - anything better moves off the books. Trades for coin, labor owed, or
goods the Ring wants moved quietly.

Dangers: Nothing here comes out of the treeline. What threatens a party at Fellward is
debt, the roster, and being noticed by the wrong faction - crossing the Ring costs access
first, then standing; crossing the Barrow-Kin's dealings here costs a great deal more than
either side says out loud.

Creatures: Man, working or penned, and one recurring problem: a Bramble-Back Boar has
rooted through the kitchen garden and grain stores by night at least once a season for as
long as anyone remembers - killing it solves nothing, since another always takes the same
wallow. An Ashmoth Swarm nests somewhere in the granary's older thatch and rises the
instant its nest is disturbed.

Secrets: The granary's real count against what the official ledger claims - known only to
the Quartermaster's Ring. Which names still drawing pay are ghost entries the Ring is
carrying for a cut.

Treasure: What's in stock rather than found - keep-issue gear, service tokens (real and
forged), and old-tongue coin the Ring will take at a steep discount from anyone who
doesn't ask why. Draws on `setting/Treasure1.md` (Scavenged Loot) and `setting/Treasure2.md`
(Equipment and Armaments).

Tables: d6 Events table, rolled on entry and each week thereafter
1. A Charter Agent pair arrives unannounced, asking why the numbers don't add up.
2. The Ashmoth Swarm gets loose in the granary overnight; stores go through it before
   anyone notices.
3. A name turns up missing from evening muster with no one able to say when they left.
4. The Bramble-Back Boar roots up the kitchen garden again; the Ring wants it dealt with
   quietly, not reported.
5. A forged service token surfaces for sale, close enough to the real pattern to pass a
   careless check.
6. Word reaches the gate that the relief column was never actually sent past Fellward's
   own request.
