A Tolkar

Overview: Tolkar is the western heir's garrison town, the last walled bed on the old road
before Marfen's ford country begins, and every crossing here still pays a toll the Sunk
Bridge no longer collects for anyone. Its gates close at dusk regardless of who is still
outside them, and a late arrival pays double the next morning's toll for the privilege of
having knocked. Unlike Velar to the east, Tolkar answers to soldiers before merchants - its
garrison commander outranks its own toll-master in every dispute that matters, though the
ledger says otherwise.

Ambiance: Squat timber-and-fieldstone construction under shingle roofs, built plain the way
a border garrison outlasts fashion. Tallow-smoke hangs over the toll queue at dawn, a drum
sounds the gate open and shut at the same two hours every day, and horse and hot metal from
the farrier's yard carry over the whole west quarter.

People: Captain Dessek commands a company of some twenty Garrison Soldiers in the western
kingdom's colors, and answers for the toll shortfall whether or not the crown ever sends him
a writ. Toll-master Kessin, a crown appointee, keeps the toll ledger and is owed deference on
paper that the company does not give him in practice. A farrier reworks captured Brekar tack
and gear into garrison-standard kit rather than melting it down. The Waystone Cup is the
town's only inn, and doubles as the one room where garrison officers and passing merchant
factors sit at the same table. Tolkar has no resident priest of standing - what faith is
practiced here is carried privately, not preached - and no healer beyond the company's own
regimental medic, who treats soldiers first and everyone else only after. Wary of anyone who
hasn't yet paid the toll and shown papers; open enough once they have, since trade is the
whole reason the town exists.

Situation: Toll income has run half of what the western crown's own ledgers expect since
Brekar took the Sunk Bridge three months ago, and the shortfall lands on Captain Dessek, who
has taken no writ to retake the ford - doing so means crossing the boundary cairns into the
middle kingdom's claimed stretch first, uninvited. Toll-master Kessin has begun quietly
asking after the Nine Wagons' own hired sellswords about doing the job the garrison won't;
nothing has been contracted yet.

Layout: A town, walled in timber and fieldstone, with one gate west onto the coast road and
one east onto the road into Marfen. The garrison barracks and the toll-house both stand just
inside the east gate, so nothing crosses east without passing both. The farrier's yard and
the Waystone Cup sit along the single market street between the two gates, about two hundred
yards apart. SAFE, and not time-bound - nothing here is tracked in hours.

Features: The gates open at dawn and shut at dusk without exception, even for a caravan
already in sight of the wall. The toll is 1 cn a head and 1 cn a pack animal, paid at the
east gate in crown-mark only - Essath coin and grave-silver are turned away at the ledger the
same as everywhere else in Telar. A late arrival camps outside until dawn and pays double at
the gate for it.

Dangers: A ledger dispute with the toll-house follows a traveler to every other crossing in
the western kingdom's writ. A soldier who takes badly to being laughed at over the ford has a
company standing behind him.

Creatures: Man, overwhelmingly - Garrison Soldiers (setting/Bestiary.md) at the gates and
barracks, and the garrison's own remounts and the farrier's stock penned in the yard.

Factions: The Nine Wagons keep a single factor watching toll traffic and garrison temper
from a table at the Waystone Cup - no warehouse here, that trade belongs to Velar. Neither
Brekar nor the crowns hold acknowledged ground beyond the garrison's own writ. Whether the
Serpent Cult holds any unacknowledged ground in Tolkar is this region's Secret, below.

Secrets: One of the tanning-yard hands answers to the coiled-serpent mark
(setting/Bestiary.md's Serpent Cult Initiate) and reports what he overhears at the toll-house
eastward, to a cell nobody at Tolkar has ever found.

Treasure: Ordinary road goods, tack, and remounts change hands at the farrier's and the
market street stalls - setting/Treasure1.md and setting/Treasure2.md are what Tolkar's trade
leans on.

Tables: d6 Events, rolled on entry and again each week.
1. A caravan from Velar arrives a day early, its guards already arguing over the detour
   toll.
2. A Brekar rider is caught trying to sell captured goods at the market under a false name.
3. Toll-master Kessin's ledger comes up short a full day's count; he blames the last shift
   at the gate.
4. A company of Mercenary Sellswords passes through looking for work, asking loudly about
   the ford.
5. A rider from the middle kingdom reaches the garrison demanding Tolkar's patrols stay off
   contested ground near the cairns.
6. Captain Dessek receives a sealed letter from the western heir - what it says, he has not
   told the toll-house.
