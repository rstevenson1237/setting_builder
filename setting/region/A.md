A The Drifted Streets

Overview: Sand lies drifted to the rooftops of a gridded city, a stepped pyramid's tip standing from its centre ringed by three wind-worn statues. A party comes in out of the storm for shelter, then food and water. Tomb robbers (Bestiary: Delver) work the rubble, six at A.7 and two at the statues by day (A.1), their water running out. A day with no water costs each character a Test of Constitution at dawn; once the tip's door opens (A.1) the scouts reach the statues within the watch. Hyenas, scorpions, centipedes and rats hold the rest. A party can make camp (Local Procedure: Making shelter and a place of respite) or forage (Local Procedure: Foraging for food and water among the ruins).

Approach: Out of the open dunes, in the storm, a party crosses the storm wall: a watch of blind travel, the sand taking any track left behind, and nobody to meet it. The city shows only when a party is in its streets. The way out is the tip's door at the statues (A.1), which leads to the pyramid (C.1). Crossing the region end to end takes a watch; leaving it by the dunes takes three.

Terrain: Desert, sand, drifted streets and half-buried roofs. A watch covers 4 miles on a cleared street, 2 over drifted sand and 1 over deep dune, with a Test of Constitution against thirst each watch there. At night and in the storm sight is a few paces, and a lit fire is seen from the tip.

Ways:
- A.8 - The Dry Canal runs west from the ruins, a mile and a half of sunken channel: the canal head (20 yards), the sunken bridge (40 yards), the drowned shrine (75 yards) and the scorched camp (110 yards). A storm climbs from the west and costs a Test of Fate against Exposed.

Landmarks:
- A.1 - Three wind-worn statues round the tip, a bronze door half-open south; two delvers watch it by day.
- A.2 - A roofed house, the one roof still standing, hearth on the north wall; a stair behind it leads down to A.9.
- A.3 - A round cistern, dry; rats nest in the floor and brackish water gathers in the sump.
- A.4 - A collapsed market under drifts; a cracked stone awning hangs over a heap on the north side.
- A.5 - A broken barracks wall with a strip of shade; four hyenas rest at its foot.
- A.6 - Two rows of scoured columns lead to a sealed door; scorpions burrow under the bases.
- A.7 - A camp of six hide tents among tumbled walls, six delvers; smoke shows at dusk.

Places:
- A.9 - A cool cellar under the roofed house, shelves of old jars and two giant centipedes on the ceiling.

Tables:
d6 Dressing and Challenges
1. Wind in hollow faces: the statues' door sings low.
2. Sand sifting over stone; awnings sag and a heap of fallen stone lies beneath.
3. Smoke at dusk over the south ruins; tomb robbers are in camp.
4. Laughter over the dunes, a hyena pack trailing a party that tires.
5. Brown dust climbing the western sky; a storm will break on the canal.
6. A rustle of rats in the cistern floor, six of them, and they rush a light.
