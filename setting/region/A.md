A Roadsend Keep

Kind: hold
Overview: The Keep's Soldiers hold Roadsend Keep. The Carters' Guild keeps a hall in its market yard, and the Black Altar has two agents living among them unknown. A party comes to outfit, hire, sell what it carries home and hear what is said of the ravine. Wagons have been lost near the wood's edge for two months, and the Keep's stores last the winter only if the next wagons arrive. If the Carters' Guild stops running convoys, prices at the market double within the month. If the commander is shown the Black Altar's hand, he sends a patrol into the ravine; none has gone yet.
View: First seen from the road three miles west: a grey stone hold on a bare hill some 60 feet above the plain, the road climbing its west slope to the gate and the wood dark beyond it to the north. Its curtain wall, 20 feet high with a walk along the top, runs about 160 yards east to west and 120 north to south; twin towers of 30 feet stand over the gate in the west wall, and a lower inner wall, 12 feet high, cuts off the eastern third. The slate roofs of the hall and chapel show over the wall, the chapel's bell-cote the highest point, and thatch and shingle crowd the outer yard. A blue pennant with a white tower flies from each gate tower, and at dusk a lantern is lit at the gate.
Approach: The main gate stands open from dawn to dusk. A Soldier asks a stranger's name and business and holds one who cannot answer the challenge; the stranger's blade stays peace-strapped inside the walls. The postern opens on a Soldier's word, for patrols and for any party carrying the commander's pass. Both are barred at dusk, and whoever is outside camps under the wall.
Law: Drawn steel inside the walls costs 50 cn, or a night in the cells for a party that cannot pay. Theft costs the goods returned and 100 cn, or the lash. A killing is held for the commander's judgement. Soldiers enforce; a stranger found in the outer yard after dusk is held until morning.
