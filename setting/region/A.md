A Roadend Keep

Overview: Roadend is a hold: a square grey keep with a tower at each corner, built where the realm's road ends, with a straggle of houses, stalls and pens inside its curtain wall. A party arrives down the road on foot or by wagon with little money, and comes to buy gear, hear what the ravine holds, and sell what they bring back.

Approach: One gate, on the road, shut at dusk. A soldier at it asks name and business; wagons pay a toll of 5 silver, people on foot nothing. A party that gives a false name or will not answer is held at the gate until the commander's sergeant has heard it; anyone turned away may camp outside the wall and gets no protection there.

People:
- Commander Edda Rook, the commander - wants the road open and the realm not troubled; gives writs to travel, pays a bounty for proof of goblins killed, and sends no one out to be rescued.
- Sergeant Tobin Marsh, the gate-sergeant - wants quiet at the gate; tells a party what the patrols have seen, and what they have lost, for a drink.
- Wynn Haller, the innkeeper - wants coin and no trouble in the common room; sells beds, meals and talk, and knows which wagons were late.
- Osric Pell, the trader - wants silver and finds from the cave; sells gear and buys what a party carries back, and pays less for anything with a chevron on it.

Services:
- Bed in the inn's common room 2 silver a night, a meal 1, a mug of ale 2 copper.
- The trader sells ordinary gear at the values on Treasure Table II and buys finds of any kind at half their value in silver; he buys no magic items.
- The commander's clerk writes a writ to travel the road for 5 silver.
- The garrison's smith mends gear at 1 silver a day.
- Not to be had: a healer, a priest, a hireling who will go into the cave, or credit.

Law: A drawn weapon inside the wall costs a fine of 10 silver, or a night in the gatehouse cell if unpaid. Theft is paid back double, and a second theft means expulsion down the road. The garrison enforces it, and the commander's word is final inside the wall and worth nothing past the road.

Situation: Wagons leaving the keep are being stripped on the road, and the toll-money that pays the garrison's rations is falling with them. Next, the commander stops sending wagons out without an escort, which cuts the keep's trade in half and raises the trader's prices by half; this follows a third wagon lost. After that, she writes to the realm for more men, which will bring a larger force and an end to the keep's frontier freedoms; this follows the escort itself being taken.
