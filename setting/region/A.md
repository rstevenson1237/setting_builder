A Greywatch

Overview: Greywatch stands at the one point the realm's road actually ends, holding no
ford or crossing that would justify a garrison here on its own account. It lives on what
that road still carries from the realm behind it and what a party hands over at its own
gate for what's carried out of the ravine, paying for both out of the same granary and the
same purse. Being this far past the realm's settled country costs it any answer faster
than a rider can carry one - what happens at Greywatch is settled at Greywatch, or not in
time.

Ambiance: Grey dressed stone throughout, cut low and thick rather than tall - built for
holding, not display. Woodsmoke and wet stone in the outer bailey, where trade fires burn
day and night; incense and old wax past the second gate, where Law's own chapel keeps its
rites. A bell in the gate tower marks every change of watch, on a schedule the roster
can't always keep to strength.

People: The garrison's own soldiery, drilled rather than desperate, holds every gate and
wall. Traders working the outer bailey's own row of stalls pay a season's bond for the
pitch they work, and answer to no guild of their own. A part-time smith works the one
forge on credit against whatever a party brings back, and calls it in before cutting
anything new. No dedicated healer past a single field medic, stretched between the wall
and whoever a party carries in hurt. Newcomers are traded with, lodged, and heard out at
the gate; nobody past the second gate answers to anyone who hasn't already proved
themselves to the garrison.

Situation: Who's responsible hasn't narrowed past "someone past the second gate"; who's
affected is every patrol whose route gets reassigned because of it. The rung it's on now:
routes are reshuffled two and three times in a week rather than held to any fixed
schedule, on the chance that alone makes the next warning harder to carry. The next rung:
if a raid still beats the garrison's own answer to it even once more, the outer gate
itself closes to anyone whose name isn't already on the roster.

Layout: Hold - an outer bailey open to anyone who can pay the gate's toll, and an inner
bailey sealed behind a second gate to everyone the garrison hasn't vouched for. The outer
gate faces the road, flanked by two watch towers; the trade stalls, the stable block, and
the chapel all sit within it, in sight of the wall. The second gate is the only crossing
into the inner bailey, where the granary, the armoury, the commander's tower, and a barred
cell sit behind it.

Features: Law holds inside the walls and along the road back to the realm, and nowhere
past either - past the gate, a party answers to nothing but itself. Prices at the gate run
against what a find is worth carried out of the ravine, not what it cost to get; the gate's
own ledger sets that price, not the finder. The outer gate stands open from first light to
last and closed after, with no exception made for anyone still on the road. Outsiders are
welcome anywhere in the outer bailey and nowhere past the second gate without an escort.

Dangers: Nothing here comes from the wilderness; the wall keeps that out. What threatens a
stranger at Greywatch is the garrison's own suspicion - a name asked for twice, a story
that doesn't match the last patrol's report, or being seen coming in from the ravine
without whatever the gate's own ledger says should have come with it.

Creatures: Man, working or under guard, and the garrison's own stabled mounts besides. A
Herd-Biter (Bestiary) roots up the stable block's own feed store some nights - a recurring
problem, driven off before it's fed rather than hunted down, since another only takes its
place.

Factions: Greywatch Garrison holds every gate, wall, and office in the hold outright; no
other faction in setting/Factions.md holds any open influence here.

Secrets: Whoever inside the second gate is passing the garrison's own patrol schedule out
to the ravine has done it long enough that suspicion now falls on everyone with a name on
the roster rather than one. The bonded traders working the outer bailey's stalls are the
one class of person who leaves the gate on a schedule the toll ledger tracks and nobody
else checks.

Treasure: What's for sale is keep-issue gear and whatever a trader's brought up the road
this season, priced against what the ravine's own finds are worth rather than what either
cost to make. Draws on `setting/Treasure2.md` (Equipment and Armaments) and
`setting/Treasure4.md` (Luxury and Trade Goods). Coin taken at face value here is minted
realm currency only; old-tongue coin from the ravine is taken at the gate for a fraction of
its weight.

Tables: d6 Events table, rolled on entry and each week after
1. A relief detail from the realm arrives at last, asking why the last three requisitions
   went unanswered.
2. The Herd-Biter gets into the stable block's own feed store overnight; the stock goes
   hungry until it's restocked.
3. A patrol comes back short a name, with no one able to say when or where it happened.
4. A trader's toll payment doesn't match what's actually in their cart, and the ledger
   says so before they do.
5. Word reaches the gate of a raid on the road before any patrol's reported seeing one
   coming.
6. The second gate's watch is doubled for a week, with no reason given to anyone posted at
   the first.
