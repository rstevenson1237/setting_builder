A Greywatch

Overview: A hold - a stone keep at the end of the realm's road, its outer bailey open to any
stranger who pays, its inner bailey behind a second gate for the garrison alone. A party
comes here to sell what it carried out of the ravine, to buy gear and hire hands, and to
sleep behind a wall.

Approach: One way in, the Outer Gate (A.1), on the road from the Old Ford (B.5). Open
first light to last; a name is taken against the morning roster, and anyone still outside
at dusk sleeps against the wall, whatever they are carrying. Traders pass on their bond,
everyone else on a copper. The walls are walked day and night (A.15, A.27) and a climber is
challenged once, then shot at. The Second Gate (A.20) passes no outsider without an escort
from someone on the inner roster.

People:
- Commander Reyne, holds the keep - A.21; wants every raid on the road answered and the
  leak inside the gate found; hears a petitioner only on an inner-roster voucher, and pays
  a contract to anyone who brings proof of who raided.
- Priest Ordell, keeps Law's rites - A.9; wants a Grozzgur speaker, since his rites cannot
  lift a Grozzgur ward; lays a wound's curse or a restless dead for a donation, and hears
  out only those who greet him in Law's courteous register.
- Bosk, the smith - A.13; wants ravine-cut stone straight from the outer cutting and his
  debts settled; repairs and forges common arms, never a ravine-stone blade.
- Yarrow, keeps the bonded traders' lodging - A.5; wants her missing cart back before her
  bond is forfeit; beds and meals, and the goods' worth in coin for the cart.
- Dott, a camp follower - A.6; wants custom; mends armour and clothing, and passes on what
  is said about raids before the garrison does, to anyone who has bought from her.
- Pell, a peddler on Trade Row - A.4; wants to leave the gate on market days unsearched;
  sells odds and ends, and asks questions about patrols he has no reason to.
- Garrow, the quartermaster - A.26; wants every issued item returned or paid for; sells
  nothing to outsiders, but lets a party pick over the unclaimed effects bin for a price.
- Anse, the field medic - A.11; wants coin and linen; treats a wound for what a party can
  pay then and there, and turns away poison she cannot treat.

Services: Bed at the lodging (A.5) 3 cp a night, meal 1 cp. Mount hire (A.7) 1 sp a day,
paid up front, only to names on the roster. Common arms and armour at Trade Row (A.4) at
the book price, a copper more to a stranger; repairs at the forge (A.13) at half the
item's price. Torchbearer 1 sp a day and half a share; spearman 2 sp a day and a full
share, hired at the Retainer's Board (A.10). Healing (A.11) 5 sp a wound. Water free at
the well (A.12), one skin a head. The Toll House (A.14) buys anything carried out of the
ravine at the ledger's price, and takes old-tongue coin at a tenth of its weight in
realm silver. Nothing better than a common blade is sold here, and no magic at any price.

Law: Brawling in the bailey costs 5 sp or a night in the Prisoner's Cage (A.24). Steel
drawn on a soldier puts the drawer in the cage until Reyne hears it, then out of the gate.
A stranger past the Second Gate unescorted is taken to the cage. A trader short-changing a
soldier forfeits the season's bond. Shed rent unpaid past one market day forfeits what is
inside (A.19). The Gate Watch and the wall watch enforce all of it; Reyne alone lifts it.

Places:
- A.1 Outer Gate - getting in, and news of who came in before.
- A.2, A.3 Gate Towers - the watch bell, and the signal fire that calls the garrison.
- A.4 Trade Row - common gear every third day; Pell's stall.
- A.5 Bonded Traders' Lodging - a bed, and Yarrow's missing cart.
- A.6 Camp Followers' Row - mending, and raid talk from Dott.
- A.7, A.8 Stable Block and Feed Store - hired mounts; whatever roots the feed at night.
- A.9 Chapel of Law - Ordell, his register, and what his rites cannot do.
- A.10 Retainer's Board - hired hands, and posted jobs.
- A.11 Healer's Tent - Anse.
- A.12 Riverside Well, A.18 Well Square - water, and everything said in the bailey.
- A.13 The Forge - Bosk, his ledger, and his standing order for ravine stone.
- A.14 Toll House - selling finds; the ledger's missing middle pages.
- A.15, A.16 Outer Wall Walk and Gate Watch Post - the roster, rewritten again.
- A.17 Outer Bailey Midden, A.19 Storage Sheds - refuse, and rented sheds.
- A.20 Second Gate - the way into the inner bailey, escorted.
- A.21 Commander's Tower - Reyne's hearing, and his locked dispatches.
- A.22 Granary - the grain count, and the coin store beside it.
- A.23 Armoury, A.26 Quartermaster's Store - issue gear; Garrow's unclaimed bin.
- A.24 Prisoner's Cage - a captured raider with something to sell.
- A.25 Garrison Barracks, A.30 Training Yard - the old timers; what a hired hand is owed.
- A.27 Inner Wall Walk, A.29 Inner Cistern - the garrison's own watch and water.
- A.28 Watch Log Archive - every patrol's report, and corrections nobody asked for.

Situation: Word of the garrison's patrols is reaching the ravine before the patrols do,
and every raid on the road since has met no watch. Reyne answers by rewriting patrol
routes two and three times a week; the soldiers walking them are worn thin and the
sergeants grumble. Next: if one more raid beats the garrison's answer, the Outer Gate
closes to every name not on the roster, and every trader on Trade Row stands to lose a
season's bond. After that: if anyone reports the Granary's coin short (A.22), Reyne has
Garrow arrested for it and the search for the leak is declared over.

Secrets:
- Commander Reyne is the shrine's agent; the patrol routes he reshuffles reach the ravine
  in his own words - he and Pell know it; brought out by the mark worn under his table
  (A.21), by laying the watch log's corrections (A.28) beside the hand in his dispatches
  (A.21), or by following Pell.
- Pell carries Reyne's word out on market days and hands it to a Gorzgur scout at the Old
  Ford (B.5) - Pell and the scout know it; brought out by the three-line mark under his
  scale's weights (A.4), or by being at the ford at dawn after a market day.
- The Granary's coin store is short because Reyne pays Pell from it - Reyne knows it, and
  Garrow suspects a shortfall without knowing whose; brought out by counting the sacks and
  the coin by hand rather than by the ledger (A.22).
- The Tally Chit in the Granary's ledger box (A.22) opens the Prisoner's Cage (A.24) -
  nobody living at the keep remembers it; brought out by trying it.

Tables:
d6 Events, rolled on arrival and each week after
1. A relief detail from the realm arrives at last, asking why the last three requisitions
   went unanswered.
2. The Herd-Biter gets into the Feed Store (A.8) overnight; the stock goes hungry until it
   is restocked, and the Retainer's Board posting doubles its pay.
3. A patrol comes back short a name, with no one able to say when or where it happened.
4. A trader's toll payment doesn't match what is in their cart, and the ledger says so
   before they do.
5. Word reaches the gate of a raid on the road before any patrol has reported seeing one
   coming.
6. The Second Gate's watch is doubled for a week, with no reason given to anyone posted at
   the first.
d6 People and trouble, anywhere in the bailey no location keys
1. An off-duty regular, drunk, picks a quarrel with whoever looks newest to the keep.
2. A camp follower offers a map of the caves for 5 sp; it shows the ford and nothing past.
3. A trader's cart has lost a wheel in the gate mud; she pays a copper a hand to lift it.
4. Two old timers argue about why the keep was raised here, and ask a stranger to settle
   it.
5. A torchbearer back from the ravine alone wants his dead master's share paid to him.
6. Pell is seen leaving by the gate with a pack too light for a trader going to market.
