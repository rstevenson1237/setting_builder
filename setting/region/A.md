A Khirengaard

Overview: The only permanent settlement on the fringe, built around the Vansker Concern's original stockade. It exists to provision and reward: the referee should run it as the party's safe harbor between wold excursions - a place to sell, resupply, hire, and hear rumour, not a site of adventure in its own right. Every faction keeps a visible presence here, and their frictions (Concern coin against guild authority, guild discipline against the town's patience) are more useful played out in overheard conversation than staged as an encounter.

Ambiance: Woodsmoke and pine sap hang over the streets, cut by the sawmill's resin-sweet cut-lumber smell and the tannery's sourer bite. The rasp of the mill saw and axe-fall carries most of the day; evenings bring fiddle and argument from the tavern. Buildings are rough-hewn timber and fieldstone, the stockade's original logs silvered with age, newer construction raw and pale beside it - the town reads as a place still being built, not a finished one.

People: A mix of loggers, trappers, prospectors, and the tradesfolk who supply them - plainspoken, hard-handed, and slow to trust anyone who hasn't put in a season's work. No formal government beyond a Concern-appointed factor (a post that's changed hands twice in violent circumstances) and an informal council of the town's oldest families, who hold more real authority than the factor does. Known locally for wold-iron tools and cured lumber, traded honestly if warily with anyone who's proven they'll come back.

Layout: A tight cluster around the old stockade and sawmill, perhaps 300 yards across at its widest, with muddy streets radiating out past the sawpits and tannery to the treeline. Not time-bound - the referee need not track hours here except where a specific event demands it.

Features: The market square, busiest on market day; the boundary-cutters' guildhall, where crossings are surveyed and blessed; the sawmill, the town's economic engine and loudest single feature; the smithy, one of few places on the fringe willing to work wold-iron.

Dangers: Mostly what any frontier town risks - drink-fueled brawls, a sawmill fire if care lapses, Concern enforcers throwing their charter's weight around a debtor or rival claim. Nothing in Khirengaard itself answers intrusion the way the wold does.

Creatures: Concern Enforcers patrol the stockade and warehouses on the Concern's orders; Fringe Bandits occasionally test the town's edges after dark, more opportunist than organized; otherwise, ordinary townsfolk going about ordinary work.

Secrets: The true circumstances of the founding factor's death three years ago are known only to a handful of guild cutters. The guild's unwritten second ledger, recording every crossing it has ever blessed and what became of the crosser, is kept somewhere in the guildhall.

Treasure: Nothing exotic - coin from trade, gear off Treasure Tables I and II, and the occasional Concern payroll shipment (Treasure Table V) moving through town under guard.

Tables: d6 Events Table (rolled on entry and each week thereafter)
1. Market day - prices favor sellers, and half the town turns out to trade.
2. A Concern crew rides in demanding labor or supply, and pays late or short.
3. A trapper returns early and badly shaken, refusing to say what she saw past the stones.
4. The guild and a Concern factor argue publicly over a disputed crossing.
5. A tavern brawl spills into the street, drawing enforcers and onlookers alike.
6. A stranger passes through paying well above market rate for ordinary fringe goods, gone before anyone thinks to ask why.
