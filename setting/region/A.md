A Saltmere Hold

Overview: Saltmere is the party's home base, market, and information hub - a low-friction SAFE location for restocking, gossip, and hooks. Run it as a service and social space; keep on-screen violence rare here to preserve its SAFE rating, and let its politics (the missing diving-bell, the debt tallies) drive the party outward rather than settle here.

Ambiance: Salt air and the wet-rope smell of drying nets, gray weathered plank buildings raised on stilts above the tidal flats, gull cries and the distant clank of bell buoys. Roofs are patched, walls built in part from salvaged ship timber, and everything carries a faint permanent dampness from the sea air.

Layout: A cluster of stilt-houses and a single stone hall (the Bell Hall, once home to the diving-bell) arranged around a natural harbor, connected by plank walkways above the mudflats. The hold can be crossed on foot in under an hour; a single road leads out into the Salt-Scrub Hills (B). As a SAFE region, Saltmere isn't time-bound at all - don't track hours here unless something specific demands it.

Features: The Bell Hall, its dais now empty since the bell's disappearance; the salvage market where wreck-goods and rumors trade hands equally; the smokehouse; and the tally-keeper's shed, which holds the hold's debt ledgers.

Dangers: Saltmere does not attack; its danger is social and economic. Crossing the hold-elders, defaulting on a tally, or being caught prying into the bell's disappearance can turn a welcome into suspicion or exile, but nothing here strikes first.

Creatures: Ordinary hold-folk go about their business and are not a combat threat. If tempers or debts run out among the dockside drifters, treat the aggressor as a Coastal Bandit (Bestiary).

Secrets: The tally-keeper's ledgers, read carefully, show which hold members' debts are old enough to have drawn the sea's attention. A loose plank behind the Bell Hall's dais hides a private letter suggesting the hold's own council quietly gave the bell away rather than having it stolen - the popular blame laid on the Tallow Fleet may be convenient, not accurate.

Treasure: Ordinary salvage goods sellable at the market, a handful of intact widow-glass bottles kept by the tally-keeper, and, if the hidden letter is found, a lead toward the bell's true resting place.

Tables: SAFE - d6 Events Table, rolled on entry and each week thereafter.
1. A fishing boat returns short a crew member, and rumor spreads quickly.
2. The tally-keeper posts a new name on the debt-board, drawing stares.
3. A trader arrives from another hold with news and a handful of foreign coin.
4. Someone claims to have heard the bell tolling louder than usual last night.
5. A hold-elder publicly accuses a rival of the diving-bell's theft.
6. A widow-glass bottle washes ashore in the harbor, unbroken.
