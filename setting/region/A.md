A The Smoke Hole

Overview: A single holding: one goblin tribe living in a mine cut by earlier diggers, its mouth venting cook smoke into the ravine. A party comes for what the tribe has taken off the road.

Approach:
- The one mouth, A.1, opens on the ravine floor behind a smouldering brush fire; four to six goblins tend it day and night.

Conditions: Cut rock, timbered where the diggers shored it; passages five feet wide, ceilings a man can just stand under. Unlit but for the mouth fire and the hall hearth. Smoke hangs at head height from A.1 to A.4; below it the air is clear. Sound carries along the timbers - a shout in any room is heard in A.4.

Inhabitants:
- Goblin, about twenty - A.1 and A.4; tending the fire, dicing over plunder; want grain and iron from the road; on meeting a party, shriek and fall back on the hall.
- Wolf, three - A.3, chained; fed by the tribe; want meat; lunge to the chain's end and howl.

Situation: Raiders bring a captive or a cartload back each dusk. Next: the chief, unable to open the pay chest, sets captives to break it; then, with iron enough, the tribe forges and raids the keep's road by day.
