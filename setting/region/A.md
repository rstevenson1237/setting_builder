A Roadsend Keep

Kind: hold
Overview: A walled keep at the end of the realm's road, garrisoned by The Keep's forty Soldiers under a commander. A party comes to buy gear, sleep inside walls, and sell what it carries back from the cave.
Approach: One gate faces the road and stands open by day. A sergeant of Soldiers challenges every stranger with "Who holds the road?"; the answer is "The road holds." Passing costs nothing by day. A stranger who cannot answer is held until the commander has seen them; at dusk the gate shuts and whoever is outside stays on the road.
People:
- Commander Mara Vell, commander of the keep - wants the road's raiders dealt with and her garrison kept inside the walls; gives a gate pass and 500 cp for proof of a dead goblin chief.
- Sergeant Tobin Hask, sergeant of the gate - wants the challenge answered and steel kept sheathed; gives word of the last two lost wagons.
- Edda Marsh, innkeeper - wants coin before the bed; sells a bed, meals and ale, and repeats what travellers say.
- Garrick Dunn, trader - wants silver and salvage; sells gear and buys finds.
Services: A bed is 10 cp a night and a meal 5 cp. The trader sells any normal Treasure II item at 100 cp and buys a party's finds at half their value; he will not buy a cursed item knowingly. Healing, spells and magic items cannot be had.
Law: Drawn steel inside the walls costs 50 cp or a night in the cells. Theft costs the goods back and 200 cp. A killing within the walls is hanged by the commander's order. The Soldiers enforce; the commander's writ ends at the walls and the road back to the realm.
Situation: The Zhakuk, out of the cave, are taking wagons near the ravine's mouth, and the keep is hurt by it: two have been lost in two months and the commander will not march out. If the next fortnightly wagon is lost, the gate shuts to trade and the commander posts a reward. If a second wagon is lost after that, she sends twenty Soldiers up the ravine and leaves the walls thin.
