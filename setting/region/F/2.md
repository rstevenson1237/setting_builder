F.2 **The Headland Track End** (Gate) - *windy, worn, staked*
A staked track ends above a windy headland, a **watch post** and a **boat rack** beside it, while shrill cries carry from the cliffs.
*25 by 25 feet. The track runs west along the north shore below cliffs and above a surf-beaten beach, to the headlands.*
**Nalokewi:** the track-end watcher wears the blue-black net pattern on the forearms, talks readily and wants news of the southern wall. Nalokewi knows where the pterodactyls roost on the cliffs.
**The watch post:** a staked platform with a horn, where a fisher watches the track and the sea.
**The cliffs' cries:** a pterodactyl's shrill cry from the headland cliffs, carried on the wind.
**Exits:** a staked track along the shore -> G.1 The Shore Track
