F.2 **The Long Corridor** (Low) - *straight, low, cobwebbed*

A low straight corridor runs north, hung with cobweb, cloth rustling in a draught. Every door along it stands open.

*A corridor 6 feet wide and 70 feet long, ceiling 12 feet. Doors were never shut behind the people who left (The Living Go Down to Dream).*

**The Cobweb:** Thick and old across the passage; brushing it aside raises dust and a rustle.
**The Doors:** Open, hung with moth-eaten cloth; each shows a room abandoned in haste.

**Exits:** an open passage -> F.1 The Stair Landing, an open passage -> F.3 The Kitchens, a broken door -> F.4 The Servants' Cells
