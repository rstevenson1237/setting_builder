F.2 **The Pit Run** (Low) - *narrow, pitted, dark*
A narrow run of loose earth, crumbling underfoot, with **boards** laid over the floor in patches. Cold air smells of mould.
*Run 40 ft long.*
**Boards:** Laid over pits and patched with mud where kobolds have stepped; a thin one gives way over spikes below (Test of Constitution, 2d, Piercing).
**Exits:** crude, east, 40 feet -> F.1 The Rattle Passage, crude, west, 60 feet -> F.3 The Burrows
