F.14 **The Steam Baths** (Medium) - *sunken, scaled, hissing*

A sunken bath of blue tile, dry, its vents crusted white with scale. Something hisses behind the walls, and a bronze valve stands by the door.

*A room 30 feet by 20, the nobles' baths. Turning the valve floods the sunken floor waist-deep with scalding water from the vents (Test of Constitution, 2d, Fire); nobody living knows it. Turned back, the water drains in a turn.*

**The Bath:** Sunken three steps, blue tile, a drain in the middle.
**The Vents:** Bronze lion mouths round the rim, crusted with scale and warm to the hand.
**The Valve:** Bronze, a wheel on a post by the door; it turns stiffly, and the hiss behind the walls rises as it turns.

**Exits:** a wooden door -> F.5 The Nobles' Rooms, an open passage -> F.17 The Hall of Chimes
