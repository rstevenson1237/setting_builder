F.16 **The Laundry** (Medium) - *dusty, white, stinging*

Stone troughs stand along one wall, slumped sacks along the other, white dust over everything. The dust stings the eyes.

*A room 20 feet square, the quarters' laundry. The sacks hold lye; disturbing them throws up a cloud (Test of Fate, Blinded).*

**The Troughs:** Dry, a crust of old soap in the bottoms; water from a skin wets the dust down for a turn.
**The Sacks:** Lye, rotted through; the dust on the floor is from them.

**Exits:** an open passage -> F.3 The Kitchens, a wooden door -> F.20 The Wine Cellar
