F.3 **The Burrows** (Medium) - *crowded, stifling, noisy*
**Alcoves** of straw and rags ring a round cavern thick with kobold musk and smoke, a grinning **face** chalked above each. **Kobolds** huddle in them over fires of rat.
*Doors lead south and west; the west door is wired.*
**Alcoves:** Straw and rags in heaps, rat bones scattered, a few holding loot from the road (Treasure I, d20).
**Kobolds:** Twenty huddle in the alcoves and scatter when pressed, then fight cornered (Desperate, 20, Bestiary : Kobold).
**Chalk Faces:** The same grin above every alcove, drawn by a different hand each time.
**Tally Post:** Scratched with a column of days, the last crossed off and the next pegged with a bone.
**West Door:** Wired at the jamb; swung without first unhooking the wire, it drops a weighted log (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Exits:** crude, east, 60 feet -> F.2 The Pit Run, wooden, south, 30 feet -> F.4 The Tribute Store, wooden, west, 50 feet -> F.5 The Chief's Den
