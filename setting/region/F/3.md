F.3 **The River Mouth Beach** (Gate) - *shingled, netted, drum-watched*
A shingle beach hangs with nets and drawn-up boats, the **boat watch** beside a **horn post**.
*70 by 30 feet. A boat that dries its nets here is trading; nothing is asked for landing.*
**Lemuhuna:** the boat-watch fisher is cheerful and sharp-eyed, wears the blue-black net pattern on the forearms, wants a horn blown for any strange sail, and knows what boats passed the river mouth.
**The boats:** hulls on racks at the tide line, hung with nets drying on poles.
**The horn post:** a conch horn on a staked pole, sounded for strange sails.
