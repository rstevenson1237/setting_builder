F.3 **The Kitchens** (Medium) - *sooty, greasy, rotten*

Stone **ovens** as tall as a man line the north wall and greasy spits hang over cold hearths, a sour smell rising from the rotted **stores**.

*A room 40 feet by 30. The old kitchens, left as the cooks left them (The Living Go Down to Dream).*

**The Ovens:** Four, of stone, their flues black with soot. Lighting one fills the flue with fire back into the room (Test of Constitution, 2d, Fire).
**The Stores:** Jars and sacks rotted to slime; a few sealed jars of oil, still sound.

**Exits:** an open passage -> F.2 The Long Corridor, an archway -> F.5 The Nobles' Rooms, an open passage past the bins -> F.6 The Granary, an open passage to the guards -> F.12 The Guard Room, an open passage to the troughs -> F.16 The Laundry
