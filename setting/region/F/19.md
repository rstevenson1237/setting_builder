F.19 **The Hyena Den** (High) - *reeking, gnawed, loud*

Gnawed bones and torn packs lie in a reeking heap. Yipping and the crunch of bone come from the dark.

*A room 30 feet square, an old guard room where hyenas that came down from the sand have denned. They range the quarters hungry; their tracks show in the scorpion burrow and the children's way.*

**The Hyenas:** Crunch bones in the heap (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Hyena). They laugh before they rush.
**The Packs:** Torn open, what the hyenas dragged in (Treasure II, d20).

**Exits:** a crude opening -> F.18 The Children's Way, a broken door -> F.8 The Scorpion Burrow
