Layout: The warren runs west into the rock from its mouth on the ravine's west wall. The rattle passage (F.1) lies at the mouth, with the pit run (F.2) 40 feet west, the burrows (F.3) 60 feet beyond that, the tribute store (F.4) 30 feet south of the burrows, and the chief's den (F.5) 50 feet west of them.

Places:
- F.1 The Rattle Passage - the way in; two sentries and a line of rattles.
- F.2 The Pit Run - boards over spiked pits.
- F.3 The Burrows - twenty kobolds, a wired west door and a tally post.
- F.4 The Tribute Store - half-empty crates of trade goods.
- F.5 The Chief's Den - Nib the Trapwright, a chest, a bed.

Inhabitants: Two Kobolds sentry F.1, twenty of The Gubak live in F.3, and Nib the Trapwright keeps F.5.

Alarm: The rattles at F.1 raise it. The burrows (F.3) hear first, at once; Nib in F.5 hears a Round later and wires the pit boards or the west door. The goblins' notch above E.3 hears only a faint rattling and looks down in two turns; none come.

Loot: The largest prize is Nib's Hoard in the iron chest at F.5, behind a Lethal dart; Treasure I lies at F.3, Treasure IV at F.4, and Treasure II at F.5. A.7 buys it at 80%.

Secrets:
- Thin boards in the pit run drop a crosser onto spikes - Nib and the kobolds know; mud-patched boards (F.2) bring it out.
- The west door of the burrows drops a weighted log when swung unhooked - the kobolds know; the wire at its jamb (F.3) brings it out.
- The chief's chest is wired to a poisoned dart - Nib knows; the wire at its hasp (F.5) brings it out.

Leads:
- Rumour 2 - F.3 Kobolds.

Tables:
