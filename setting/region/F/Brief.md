Layout: The warren has one mouth, a crawlhole at the back of the kobold ledge (E.2) on the ravine's west wall, 300 yards up from the ravine's mouth and a few paces above the path; grinning faces are chalked over one another on the rock above it, and two kobolds watch it by day and one by night. The rattle passage (F.1), 40 feet long, opens 20 feet west of the ledge and runs west into the earth, hung with tin rattles and dripping from the roof, with tripwires at knee height round two bends. All of the warren is dug earth and soft rock, passages 4 feet high and 3 wide, so a man goes crouched and a weapon longer than a spear cannot swing; no light shows but tallow lamps and the party's own, sound carries straight along the passages and dies at bends, the footing is loose damp earth over pits, the air is close, and a lit fire fills the passages with smoke that drives the Gubak into their deepest rooms.

The chief's den (F.5), the one High location, lies 150 feet west of the rattle passage (F.1) and 50 feet west of the burrows (F.3), through a wooden door. It is a den 15 feet by 12 with a bed of stolen cloth heaped with bones and wires running across the roof, and an iron-banded chest at the bed's foot with a wire running from its hasp; Nib the Trapwright (Named Creature: Nib the Trapwright) holds it four nights in five, and the chest holds Nib's Hoard (Hoard: Nib's Hoard).

The pit run (F.2) lies 40 feet west of the rattle passage (F.1), a narrow run of loose earth 40 feet long with boards laid over the floor in patches, some patched with mud. The burrows (F.3) lie 60 feet west of the pit run (F.2): a cavern 30 feet across ringed by five alcoves of straw and rags, a grin chalked above each, a tally post scratched with a column of days, and doors south and west, a wire hooked at the jamb of the west door. The tribute store (F.4), a dead-end chamber 20 feet by 15 with crates stacked to the roof and shelves cut in the earth, lies 30 feet south of the burrows (F.3) through a wooden door.

Inhabitants: Two Kobolds sentry the rattle passage (F.1) with slings, fearful, rattling the alarm and bolting for the burrows; they know which bends are trapped. Twenty of The Gubak (Faction: The Gubak) huddle in the alcoves of the burrows (F.3) over fires of rat, desperate, scattering when pressed and fighting cornered, wanting tribute paid and strangers gone; they know where the chief's door is rigged. Nib the Trapwright (Named Creature: Nib the Trapwright) tinkers at his trigger-line in the den (F.5) four nights in five, cunning, bargaining for his life first and then springing a trap and running; he wants the tribute kept from the goblins.

Alarm: The rattles at F.1 raise it. The burrows (F.3) hear first, at once; Nib in F.5 hears a Round later and wires the pit boards or the west door. The goblins' notch above E.3 hears only a faint rattling and looks down in two turns; none come.

Loot: The warren's wealth is its chief's hoard and what the kobolds have taken from the road. The largest prize is Nib's Hoard (Hoard: Nib's Hoard) in the iron-banded chest at F.5, which Nib stands over and a Lethal poisoned dart guards; Treasure I lies in the alcoves of F.3, Treasure IV in the crates of F.4 and Treasure II in the bed at F.5. The Moneychanger (A.7) buys it back at 80% of value.

Secrets:
- Thin boards in the pit run drop a crosser onto spikes - Nib and the kobolds know; mud-patched boards (F.2) bring it out.
- The west door of the burrows drops a weighted log when swung unhooked - the kobolds know; the wire at its jamb (F.3) brings it out.
- The chief's chest is wired to a poisoned dart - Nib knows; the wire at its hasp (F.5) brings it out.
