F.7 **The Hunters' Camp** (Merchant) - *bluffed, skin-hung, wary*
A skin-hung camp on a bluff over the river has dog pens and a fire ring, hunters with short bows, and a **leader** watching the hill trails.
*50 by 40 feet. A hill guide costs a third more to a southerner.*
**Hanukali:** the hunters' leader is gruff and grieving, wants proof of what took the lost hunters, and knows the river trail and the hunters' last camp.
**The camp's bowmen:** six of the hunters, with dogs and short bows, blue-black net patterns on the forearms (Wary, 6, Bestiary : Hill Hunter).
**The trading skins:** a hill guide is 30 cn a day, plus 10 cn for a southerner; a short bow is 100 cn, and twenty arrows 20 cn, fire-hardened and flint-tipped.
