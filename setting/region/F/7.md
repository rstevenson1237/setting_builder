F.7 **The Spider Hall** (High) - *webbed, tall, silent*

Webs thick as sails hang across a tall hall, creaking when something shifts. The silence is complete.

*A hall 50 feet by 40 and 24 high, the old feast hall (The Demon Is Invited). The spiders guard the way to the gallery stair.*

**The Webs:** Hung from the roof to the floor, they hold bundles of the dead.
**The Spiders:** Wait in the web (Hostile, 1-2, Bestiary : Giant Spider). They drop on whoever tears a web.
**The Bundle:** A web-wrapped bundle holds a dead delver's gear (Treasure II, d20).

**Exits:** an open passage -> F.4 The Servants' Cells, a broken door -> F.8 The Scorpion Burrow
