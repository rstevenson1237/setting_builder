F.9 **The Council House** (Authority) - *round, raised, closed*
A round raised house with a closed door stands at the villages' centre, a **watch** at its stair, and low voices and a fish-skin drum inside.
*40 by 40 feet. The council sits behind a closed door; a southern token is refused. Its watch is turned out by a horn within two Rounds.*
**Tiwahato:** the council elder is grave and tired, wants proof before the council sends spears, and knows what the council has heard from village to village.
**Vakomano:** the council's speaker is reasonable and unhurried, keeps the shirt laced to the throat, argues for no war party and no trust in southerners. Vakomano knows what each elder fears.
**The watch:** ten bowmen with dogs, blue-black net patterns on the forearms (Neutral, 10, Bestiary : Hill Hunter).
**The seal:** the council's mark on a guide's hire costs 30 cn a hire.
**Nets on the back wall:** hung drying; pulled aside, a smoke-black seam in the wall opens on a way down.
**Exits:** nets pulled aside on a smoke-black seam -> F.10 The Smokehouse Cellar
