F.9 **The Ghoul Den** (High) - *bone-strewn, foul, dark*

Bones lie heaped round a rotting dais, the air foul and thick. Gnawing and low muttering come from the dark.

*A rough-walled crypt some 30 feet across (The Demon Is Invited). The ghouls have begun to hunt the quarters from here; the Royal Guard captain's trail of scraped scale runs through it to the cell beyond (F.11).*

**The Bones:** Heaped round the dais, gnawed clean; a few still wear scraps of scale.
**The Ghouls:** Feast on the dead (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Ghoul).
**The Dais:** A hollow beneath holds the ghouls' gleanings (Hoard: The Ghoul's Hoard).

**Exits:** an archway -> F.5 The Nobles' Rooms, a stone door -> F.11 The Captain's Cell, an open passage -> F.6 The Granary, an archway hung with chimes -> F.17 The Hall of Chimes
