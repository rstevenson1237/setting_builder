F.8 **The Boat Shed** (Merchant) - *long, dripping, stacked*
A long shed stacked with boats drips on a slip to the river, and a **boatwright** works an adze at a bench.
*50 by 25 feet. Boats are hired at the villages for the coast.*
**Nalole:** the boatwright is easygoing and curious, wants iron nails, worked metal and tales of ships, and knows the coast's landings east to the headland.
**The hire racks:** a dugout canoe is 20 cn a day, a paddler 15 cn a day, and a fishing boat with two fishers 60 cn a day, under sail along the coast.
**The slip:** stone steps down to the river, wet with weed.
