F.8 **The Scorpion Burrow** (Medium) - *sandy, low, hot*

A low burrow under cracked tile is floored in sand, hot and dry. A click sounds in the dark.

*A burrow 30 feet square and 8 high, the old store (The Living Go Down to Dream).*

**The Sand:** Drifted deep, hot to the hand; a track runs to the north wall.
**The Scorpions:** Lie buried (Hostile, 1-3, Bestiary : Giant Scorpion). A sting is a Lethal Poison.
**The Jar:** Split, half-buried in the sand, old coin spilling from it (Treasure I, d20).

**Exits:** a broken door -> F.7 The Spider Hall, a crude door -> F.11 The Captain's Cell, a broken door into the reek -> F.19 The Hyena Den
