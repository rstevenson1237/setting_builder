F.15 **The Nursery** (Low) - *small, painted, still*

Six small beds stand round a painted box, animals painted on every wall. A draught stirs dust and nothing else.

*A room 20 feet square, the nobles' nursery.*

**The Walls:** Hawks, jackals and fish painted in a row, a child's handprint among them.
**The Toy Box:** Painted, its lid stiff; inside, wooden toys and a purse of old coin (Treasure I, d20).

**Exits:** a painted wooden door -> F.5 The Nobles' Rooms, an open passage -> F.6 The Granary
