F.1 **The Stair Landing** (Low) - *dusty, narrow, quiet*

A dusty landing ends at the stair, two soldiers in bronze scale standing at its head. A red hawk is painted on the wall behind them.

*A landing 10 feet by 20 feet. The Royal Guard stop everyone who goes down; a party must name its business or find the stair shut.*

**The Soldiers:** Two stand watch; they challenge with "Who holds the gate?" and answer "The gate holds." (Faction : Kurumisa)
**The Stair:** Steps worn smooth in the middle, 20 feet from E.9.

**Exits:** an open passage, east, 70 feet -> F.2 The Long Corridor, a wooden door, north, 70 feet -> F.12 The Guard Room, up a stair, south, 40 feet -> E.9 The Stair Chamber
