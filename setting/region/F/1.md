F.1 **The River Trail Head** (Gate) - *muddy, rutted, dog-watched*
A muddy trail ends at a post under a drying rack, with a **watcher** at it, two **hunters** and a dog, and the jungle closing dark beyond.
*25 by 25 feet. The trail runs up the river to the hunters' last bend and the hills; no road crosses the hills between the two shores.*
**Hanukuno:** the river-trail watcher wears a blue-black net pattern on the forearms, says little, and wants to know every stranger's errand. Hanukuno knows which hunters went up the river, and when.
**The post guard:** two hill hunters and a dog, who watch from the post (Wary, 3, Bestiary : Hill Hunter).
**The trail's end:** a rutted track that narrows into jungle after a bowshot, with no road beyond it toward the south.
**Exits:** a muddy trail climbing into jungle -> D.10 The Lost Hunters' Camp
