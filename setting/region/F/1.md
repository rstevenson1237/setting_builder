F.1 **The Rattle Passage** (Medium) - *low, rattling, watched*
A passage so low a man goes crouched hangs with tin **rattles**, water dripping from the roof, and runs west into the earth. Kobolds squat at the first bend.
*Tripwires run to the rattles at both bends.*
**Rattles:** Tin cans and bone strung from the roof; a pull on a knee-high wire sets them clattering through the warren.
**Kobold Sentries:** Two squat at the bend with slings and sound the rattles before they bolt for the burrows (Fearful, 2, Bestiary : Kobold).
**Exits:** open, east, 20 feet -> E.2 The Kobold Ledge, crude, west, 40 feet -> F.2 The Pit Run
