F.4 **The Fishers' Guest House** (Inn) - *stilted, smoky, cramped*
A cramped stilt house under smoky thatch, the **keeper** at its hearth, with **fishers** off the boats murmuring round a plank where knucklebones clatter.
*40 by 25 feet. A fish is laid on the sill of each guest's door; a southerner's token brought into the house withdraws its hospitality.*
**Kewituha:** the keeper is shy and exact, wants a fish laid on every guest's door-sill, and knows who has asked after the lost hunters. A bed is 10 cn a night, a third more to a southerner; smoked fish and boiled taro are a meal for pennies; word of the hills at the fireside is 10 cn a night's talk.
**The fishers:** six off the boats, wary of southerners, blue-black net patterns on their forearms (Wary, 6, Bestiary : Hill Hunter).
**The knucklebones:** a wooden cup of knuckle-bones clacks on a plank at the hearth, and a fisher keeps an eye on the door.
