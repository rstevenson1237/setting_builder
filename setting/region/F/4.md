F.4 **The Servants' Cells** (Low) - *small, cramped, stale*

Tiny cells open off a short passage, each holding straw and a clay lamp. The air is stale.

*A passage 30 feet by 40 with eight cells. The servants' quarters, as they were left (The Living Go Down to Dream).*

**The Cells:** Straw pallets, clay lamps, a peg for a cloak; nothing worth taking.
**The Passage:** Leads east to the spider hall and south to the gallery stair.

**Exits:** a broken door -> F.2 The Long Corridor, a wooden door -> F.5 The Nobles' Rooms, an open passage -> F.7 The Spider Hall, an open passage -> F.13 The Stair Gallery
