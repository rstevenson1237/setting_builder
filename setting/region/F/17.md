F.17 **The Hall of Chimes** (Medium) - *hung, humming, close*

Rows of bronze chimes hang from the roof over a long bench. A hum sits just under hearing.

*A room 20 feet square, the nobles' music room. Striking any chime starts every chime humming; the hum swells until it presses everyone in the room to the floor (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing). Chimes muffled with cloth stay silent. The Scholars know of it.*

**The Chimes:** Bronze tubes in rows, from finger-long to man-high.
**The Bench:** Stone, a rotted cushion and a padded mallet on it.

**Exits:** an open passage -> F.14 The Steam Baths, an archway hung with chimes -> F.9 The Ghoul Den
