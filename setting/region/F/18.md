F.18 **The Children's Way** (Low) - *winding, low, cracked*

A low passage winds back on itself, grit ticking down from the roof. A grown man must stoop.

*A passage 10 feet wide, 40 long and 8 high, the back way the household's children used, from the spider hall to what is now the hyenas' den.*

**The Roof:** Low and cracked; grit falls at every step.
**The Scratches:** Children's marks at knee height, and fresh claw marks over them where the hyenas pass to and from their den.

**Exits:** a low passage -> F.7 The Spider Hall, a crude opening -> F.19 The Hyena Den
