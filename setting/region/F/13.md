F.13 **The Stair Gallery** (Low) - *long, draughty, steep*

A long gallery of steps runs down, wind rising from below. The steps are worn smooth in the middle.

*A gallery 10 feet by 70 feet (The Living Go Down to Dream). It descends 20 feet to G.1.*

**The Steps:** Worn and uneven; a draught blows a torch sideways.

**Exits:** an open passage, north, 70 feet -> F.4 The Servants' Cells, down a stair, north, 40 feet -> G.1 The Stair Landing
