F.12 **The Guard Room** (Medium) - *bare, benched, barred*

Benches and spear racks stand round a barred inner door, chains rattling. A red hawk is painted over it.

*A room 30 feet square, held by four of the Royal Guard.*

**The Soldiers:** Hold the inner door (Wary, 4, Faction : Kurumisa). They want the captain found and know where he went; they trade the hint for a hand searching.
**The Door:** Barred, with a heavy beam; it leads to the robbers' camp (F.10).

**Exits:** a wooden door, south, 70 feet -> F.1 The Stair Landing, a crude door, west, 70 feet -> F.10 The Robbers' Deep Camp, an open passage, east, 70 feet -> F.3 The Kitchens
