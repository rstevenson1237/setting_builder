F.6 **The Net-Makers** (Merchant) - *tangled, busy, tarred*
Nets lie heaped in coils under a tar smell, and a **net-maker** hums at a spinning rack among clacking shuttles.
*40 by 25 feet. The net-makers trade goods as readily as coin.*
**Pawakewi:** the net-maker is fussy and proud, wants hemp and iron needles in trade, and knows who buys rope for the hills.
**The coils:** hemp rope, 50 ft, is 15 cn each; a woven backpack, not leather, 10 cn; a cast net 30 cn.
**The tar pot:** tar kept warm over embers for tarring twine.
