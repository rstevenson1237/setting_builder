F.6 **The Granary** (Medium) - *vaulted, dry, rat-ridden*

Stone bins stand in rows under a vaulted roof, split sacks and scattered husks on the floor. Something scurries in the dark.

*A vault 40 feet by 30. The grain store, picked clean (The Living Go Down to Dream).*

**The Bins:** Stone and empty, husks and rat droppings in the bottoms.
**The Floor:** Cracked tile in the middle; crossing it drops a party into the cellar below (Test of Constitution, 3d, Crushing).

**Exits:** an open passage, south, 70 feet -> F.3 The Kitchens, an open passage, east, 70 feet -> F.9 The Ghoul Den
