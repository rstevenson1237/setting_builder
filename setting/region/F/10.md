F.10 **The Robbers' Deep Camp** (High) - *lit, crowded, barricaded*

Lamps burn among barricades of furniture, bedrolls and gear piled between. Talk, dice and a fiddle carry across the room, and a man in a stork mask sings by the fire.

*A wing 40 feet square, the tomb robbers' deepest camp. They hunt any thief within a turn.*

**The Camp:** Eight delvers gamble and mend gear (Wary, 8, Bestiary : Delver), notches cut in the doorframe for each haul (Faction : The Tomb Robbers). They want a way out of the pyramid and know which rooms are trapped; they trade with a party that waves a coin.
**The Barricades:** Furniture piled across the one door; they can be climbed or burned.
**The Singer:** A cynidicean in a stork mask who wandered up from the lake city, fed for his songs (Neutral, 1, Bestiary : Masked Dreamer). He wants to go back down to the lake; his songs of the tombs name the door the dead king truly lies behind.
**The Strongbox:** Chained under a bedroll (Treasure IV, d20).

**Exits:** a crude door -> F.12 The Guard Room
