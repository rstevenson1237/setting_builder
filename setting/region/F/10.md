F.10 **The Smokehouse Cellar** (Secret) - *dark, dug, scale-marked*
A dug cellar holds smoke racks hung with fish, cold smoke and no sound, with a **scale** carved in a post.
*15 by 15 feet. The way in is behind the nets in the Council House; the bound men know it.*
**The carved post:** a scale shape cut deep in a post and inked green, the mark a bound man bears over the heart.
**The smoke racks:** fish hung to cure, and under them a bench worn by sitters.
**Exits:** nets that pull aside from below -> F.9 The Council House
