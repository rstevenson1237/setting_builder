F.10 **The Robbers' Deep Camp** (High) - *lit, crowded, barricaded*

Lamps burn among barricades of furniture, bedrolls and gear piled between. Talk, dice and a fiddle carry across the room. A rope coil and notches are cut into the doorframes.

*A wing 50 feet by 40, the tomb robbers' deepest camp. They hunt any thief within a turn.*

**The Camp:** Eight delvers gamble and mend gear (Wary, 8, Faction : The Tomb Robbers). They want a way out of the pyramid and know which rooms are trapped; they trade with a party that waves a coin.
**The Barricades:** Furniture piled across two doors; they can be climbed or burned.
**The Strongbox:** Chained under a bedroll (Treasure IV, d20).

**Exits:** a crude door, east, 70 feet -> F.12 The Guard Room
