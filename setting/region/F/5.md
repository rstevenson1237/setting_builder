F.5 **The Fish Market** (Market) - *wet, loud, silvered*
A wet plank floor under racks of silver fish rings with knives and gulls, **fishers** bargaining and a **market warden** at the weighing beams.
*60 by 40 feet. The market is loud and quarrels are settled at the beams.*
**Lemumano:** the market warden is calm and blunt, wants a southerner's token never shown, and knows what the fish bring and who underbids.
**The fishers and gutters:** thirty bargaining at the racks, blue-black net patterns on the forearms (Neutral, 30, Bestiary : Hill Hunter).
**The stalls:** fresh fish and boiled taro by the portion for pennies; a day's rations of dried fish and hill pig are 5 cn, and a waterskin 5 cn.
