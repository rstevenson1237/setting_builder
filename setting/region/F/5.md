F.5 **The Nobles' Rooms** (Medium) - *faded, plastered, grand*

Three plastered rooms are linked by arches, their gold faded and couches rotted. Cloth hangings muffle every sound, and shapes hang in the ceiling folds.

*Three rooms 30 feet by 30 in all. A noble's apartments, once grand, with the baths and the nursery off them (The Cynidiceans Raise the City).*

**The Hangings:** Cloth heavy with dust; they burn, and smoke fills the quarters.
**The Stirges:** Hang from the ceiling folds (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Stirge). Wings beat faintly in the folds.
**The Chest:** Lacquered, under a rotted couch, a hide scroll inside (Lore: The Noble's Will).

**Exits:** an archway -> F.3 The Kitchens, an archway -> F.9 The Ghoul Den, a wooden door to the baths -> F.14 The Steam Baths, a painted wooden door -> F.15 The Nursery
