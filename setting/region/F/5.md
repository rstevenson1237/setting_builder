F.5 **The Nobles' Rooms** (Medium) - *faded, plastered, grand*

Three plastered rooms are linked by arches, their gold faded and couches rotted. Cloth hangings muffle every sound, and shapes hang in the ceiling folds.

*Three rooms 40 feet by 40 in all. A noble's apartments, once grand (The Cynidiceans Raise the City).*

**The Hangings:** Cloth heavy with dust; they burn, and smoke fills the quarters.
**The Stirges:** Hang from the ceiling folds (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Stirge). A thin beating of wings warns of them.
**The Chest:** A lacquered chest holds a hide scroll (Lore: The Noble's Will).

**Exits:** an archway, west, 70 feet -> F.3 The Kitchens, a wooden door, south, 70 feet -> F.4 The Servants' Cells, an archway, north, 70 feet -> F.9 The Ghoul Den
