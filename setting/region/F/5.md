F.5 **The Chief's Den** (High) - *cramped, hoarded, guarded*
A low den with a **bed** of stolen cloth heaped with bones, an iron-banded **chest** at its foot, and wires running across the roof. A grin is chalked above the chest. **Nib** tinkers at a trigger-line.
*Den 15 ft by 12 ft. Nib is present four nights in five.*
**Nib the Trapwright:** Bargains for his life at the first threat, then pulls a wire to drop the Pit Run's boards or swing the west door behind the party, and runs. He says where the goblins collect, but not which traps spare his own (Named Creature: Nib the Trapwright).
**Chest:** Iron-banded and shut; a wire runs from the hasp, and lifting the lid without unhooking it fires a poisoned dart (Test of Constitution, 3d, Poison). Inside lies the chief's hoard (Hoard: Nib's Hoard).
**Bed:** Stolen cloth, bones and a few weapons heaped together (Treasure II, d20).
**Exits:** wooden, east, 50 feet -> F.3 The Burrows
