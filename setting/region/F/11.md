F.11 **The Captain's Cell** (Medium) - *cold, barred, small*

A small barred cell holds a pallet and a broken lamp, water dripping and then nothing. A red hawk is painted over the bars.

*A cell 10 feet square. The Royal Guard's captain was held here; he is gone, and his men moved after him.*

**The Bars:** Bronze, cut through at the base; a man could crawl out.
**The Dead:** Shuffle in the dark in soldiers' scale (Hostile, 2-4, Bestiary : Zombie).
**The Pouch:** A hide pouch holds the captain's note (Lore: The Captain's Last Orders).

**Exits:** a crude door -> F.8 The Scorpion Burrow, a stone door -> F.9 The Ghoul Den
