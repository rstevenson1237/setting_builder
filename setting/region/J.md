J Pyramid Level 8 - The Lieutenants' Halls

Overview: A short level of halls where the cult's lieutenants keep the way down. A party comes for the lieutenants' hoard and the way past them. The lieutenants fear the demon more than they hate each other and have begun to talk. Killing a lieutenant makes the others close ranks and bar the stair; bargaining with one is learned by the others within the day.

Approach: The stair from the cells (I.9) lands at J.1, a gargoyle on each side of the door. They still only for the lieutenants' word, *vox*, chalked on a slate in H.11 (Keys: The Lieutenants' Word); a party without it is struck, or slips by the servants' passage (J.8) past its darts.

Prizes:
- J.2 - A grand black hall hung with trophies; Krulox on a throne of bones and a hoard in its base (Hoard: The Lieutenants' Hoard).
- J.6 - A vast iron gate with the demon's mark; wraiths before it and a cache beside (Treasure V, d20).
- J.9 - Racks of black spears round a sanded floor; four to six bodyguards spar and a pay chest lies under the last rack (Treasure III, d20).

Places:
- J.1 - A narrow landing with two gargoyles flanking the door.
- J.3 - Black silk hangings; Xothox on a raised couch and a silk-wrapped box (Keys: The Council Seal).
- J.4 - Stained flags over a fighting floor; Zhurjul drums and drills and a red ring hangs on the rack (Unique Treasure: The Red Ward).
- J.5 - A long black table with three chairs; mummies sit the empty ones and an iron door north is locked (Keys: The Council Seal).
- J.7 - Upside-down trophies over a floor of bronze rings; the outer ring is worn smooth.
- J.8 - A narrow passage behind the halls' hangings, voices from the halls; pinholes in the landing door's jamb.
