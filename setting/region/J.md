J Gnoll Den

Kind: single holding
Overview: The Kurzun's den on the east wall above the hobgoblins: Gnolls with pens of captives, some of them hirelings or kin of a party's. The pack-leader is a Named Creature, and the den holds the key to the maze. A party comes for the captives, the key and the leader's hoard. The Kurzun sell to any buyer, and a buyer is due. If a captive is taken from the pens, the pack hunts the party down the ravine. If the hobgoblins fall, the den takes their mouth.
Approach: One mouth opens on the ravine's east wall, iron collars hung at its edge and Gnolls lounging on the ledge by day. A makers' passage runs from the pens toward the hobgoblins' hold, and the Kurzun have sealed their end with a heap of stone.
Conditions: Natural cave, rough floor and ceilings 6 to 15 feet. Light is cookfires. Sound is a constant laughing and howling that hides small noises. Footing is trampled dung and bone. The air is foul, and a lit fire makes the captives scream and the Gnolls laugh.
