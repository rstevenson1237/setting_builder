J Gnoll Den

Overview: The Kurzun's den on the east wall above the hobgoblins, with pens of captives, some of them hirelings or kin of a party's, and the key to the maze. A party comes for the captives, the key and the leader's hoard. The Kurzun sell to any buyer, and a buyer is due. If a captive is taken from the pens, the pack hunts the party down the ravine; if the hobgoblins fall, the den takes their mouth.

Approach: One mouth behind the gnoll ledge (E.6), iron collars hung at its edge and Gnolls lounging on the ledge by day. A makers' passage runs from the den toward the hobgoblins' hold, and the Kurzun have sealed their end with a heap of stone.

Conditions: Natural cave, rough floor and ceilings 6 to 15 feet. Light is cookfires. Sound is a constant laughing and howling that hides small noises. Footing is trampled dung and bone. The air is foul, and a lit fire makes the captives scream and the Gnolls laugh.

Entrances:
- J.1 - Iron collars hung from hooks round the cave mouth, a strip of spotted hide nailed over the lintel and knucklebones across the floor.

Prizes:
- J.5 - Through a wooden door beyond the den floor: collars and dung across the floor, a raised bed of bones with a hollow dug beneath it, and a rack of iron collars on the wall in size order.

Places:
- J.2 - Three pens of rough iron bars off the collar mouth, a strip of spotted hide tied to each gate, nine captives chained inside and their gear piled in the aisle corner.
- J.3 - Floor trampled flat, tallies scratched at head height, four passages off it and a bundle of goods lashed against one wall.
- J.4 - The cave's end filled to the roof with splintered bones and broken stone, a notched thighbone on its crest and a cold draught through the heap.

Tables:
Danger, 6 noticing to 1 acting

4. Dung and bone litter the floor; the next Move Silently is at Disadvantage.
3. Two gnolls prowl the passage from J.1 to J.2, spears down.
2. The pack circles the party in the den floor's dark, laughing.
1. Ruk's order sounds; the pack hamstrings the slowest and drags off the fallen.
