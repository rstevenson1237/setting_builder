J The Troglodytes' Warren

Overview: A sinkhole in the jungle floor, its sides hung with roots, and caves opening off it into the dark. A party comes for the band's loot, or after something the band took. The band raids cave men and travellers from the hills and drags the captives down to feed on. If a light is seen at the sinkhole, the watch calls the band down the caves and the lower galleries are manned within the turn; if a captive is freed, the band follows the party up the sinkhole and through the jungle for a day.

Approach: The sinkhole, from the hill jungle, has troglodytes on a rope-ladder lookout at night and none by day, their stench over the rim; the way is a fifty-foot climb down roots or rope into the dark, and a party driven back up is pelted from below.

Entrances:
- J.1 - The sinkhole's floor, root-hung and littered with bone; the roots lead up to D.6 and caves open off it.

Prizes:
- J.4 - Three troglodytes feed round the cook pits; fire sickens in the foul air.
- J.7 - The chief on a throne of bones, guards beside it; a pressed stone in the back wall leads to the loot (Named Creature: Rank the Crested).

Places:
- J.2 - A low cave strung with snares; a pit under the strewn leaves.
- J.3 - A rank gallery; three troglodytes wait in the niches.
- J.5 - A deep pit barred with logs; cave men captives huddle in it.
- J.6 - Heaped bones and refuse; trinkets among them.
- J.8 - A narrow hidden cave of stolen goods; four centipedes nest in the heaps.
