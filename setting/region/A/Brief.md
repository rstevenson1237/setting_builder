Layout: From the road (B.1) the keep shows as a wall around a hill, twin towers over its main gate (A.1), a pennant of a white tower on blue on each, and at dusk a lantern lit at the gate's mouth for patrols still out. The main gate on the west wall is the way in from the road, open from dawn to dusk: Sergeant Ada Brandt challenges all comers, the clerk Pell Orrin writes each stranger's name and business in a ledger, a stranger who cannot give them is held, and a blade goes in peace-strapped. The postern (A.2), on the north wall, is the way to the wood (C.1); Corporal Dag Venn opens it for patrols and for a party carrying the commander's pass. Both are barred at dusk, and whoever is outside camps under the wall.

The hold is about 150 yards across: an outer yard inside the west wall, and an inner wall 130 yards east of the main gate. From the main gate, the Market Yard (A.8) is 40 yards east, the Traveller's Inn (A.3) 40 yards north, the Broken Wheel (A.4) 50 yards south, the General Store (A.5) 60 yards north-east, the Armourer's Forge (A.6) 70 yards south-east and the Moneychanger (A.7) 80 yards east; the postern (A.2) is 120 yards north-east. Inside the inner wall the Chapel (A.9) is at 130 yards, the Barracks (A.11) south-east at 130 and the Commander's Hall (A.10) at 150. The cellar (A.12) lies under the Broken Wheel; the sally tunnel (A.13) runs 150 yards east under the yard from the hall to a rusted door.

At the gate, Pell Orrin turns his ledger's wagon page for anyone who asks which wagons run when (A.1). Travellers sleep at the Traveller's Inn (A.3) for 10 cn a night, kept by Edda Marsh and Jory Fenn. At the Broken Wheel (A.4) Marta Voss pours strong drink, four soldiers play knucklebones by the hearth, Lysa Torren asks after a lost party, and the floor by the casks behind the bar is scraped. Garrick Dunn's General Store (A.5) sells kit at the realm's prices, its shelves thinning before a wagon day; Hild Ironhand's forge (A.6) sells blades, shields and leather; Selwyn Crake at the Moneychanger (A.7) changes coin for 3% and buys a hoard. In the Market Yard (A.8) Brenn Calloway and four drivers wait by their wagons, and hirelings' wages are chalked on the notice board. Inside the inner wall, Mother Hesper Lund and Wenna Pryce heal at the Chapel of Law (A.9), where Old Gil mutters of those who kneel at odd hours. Commander Roderic Hale receives any party with a name and a purpose at his hall (A.10), Lieutenant Cass Ferrow, his adjutant, beside him, six guards at the door, the road mapped on his table and a ring on the dais step polished by hands. At the Barracks (A.11) twenty soldiers sleep within reach of their spears under Sergeant-Major Bram Tolley, and the alarm drum by the door brings them out within two turns.

Secrets:
- Pell Orrin sells the wagon days to the Black Altar - he knows; the wagon page of his ledger (A.1) and the torn ledger page under the table in A.12 bring it out.
- The Black Altar's other agent is Brother Anselm, who meets Orrin in a cellar - Anselm knows; scraped boards behind the third barrel (A.4) bring it out.
- A stair under the dais leads to a tunnel and a rusted door to the wood - the commander and his adjutant know; the polished ring on the dais step (A.10) brings it out.
