A.21 **Commander's Tower** - *No Rescue, Commander's Tower*
The tallest standing structure in Greywatch holds **Commander Reyne**'s own quarters, the watch schedule, and whoever's currently under confinement, and getting a hearing here means already having a name the garrison trusts.
*Four stories of the same dressed grey stone, narrow windows facing the road and the ravine both, a bell-rope from the roof brazier running down through every floor.*
**Commander Reyne's Hearing:** Reyne hears a petitioner only once vouched for by someone already on the inner roster, and settles boundary disputes and disputed finds both from behind the same plain table.
**Reshuffled Roster:** Patrol routes are rewritten two and three times a week rather than held to any fixed schedule, and the sergeant who used to walk the river path by night has been reassigned to the wall instead, worse off for it by every account but Reyne's own.
**Next Rung:** If a raid beats the garrison's own answer to it even once more, Reyne closes the outer gate to anyone whose name isn't already on the roster, and every trader on Trade Row stands to lose a season's bond the day that happens.
**Scratched Mark:** A faint three-line mark cut through a circle scores the underside of Reyne's own report table, worn smooth by a thumb that returns to it often enough to matter.
**Commander's Dispatches:** A locked case holds Reyne's own unsent dispatches to the realm, admitting a shortfall in men the keep's public roster doesn't show (Lore: Commander's Dispatches).
