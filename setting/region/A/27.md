A.27 **Inner Wall Walk** - *Frontier Levy, Inner Bailey*
A shorter stretch of parapet rings the inner bailey, watched by whoever's posted here against nothing more than an unannounced guest.
*Thirty feet of parapet, narrower than the outer wall's own walk, overlooking the granary roof and the commander's tower both.*
**Inner Watch:** Whoever's posted here watches the inner bailey's own yard rather than the road, and answers to Reyne alone rather than the outer gate's own rota.
**Exits:** stair door, south end -> A.20 Second Gate
