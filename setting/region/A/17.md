A.17 **Outer Bailey Midden** - *Refuse, Stench, Nothing*
The bailey's own refuse goes here, a pit dug against the outer wall and called exactly what it is by everyone at the gate.
*A ten-foot pit walled in loose stone, the smell carrying further on a wet day than anyone posted nearby would like.*
**Midden Pit:** Whatever the bailey throws out ends up here, and anyone digging through it for something specific finds only what a midden actually holds.
**Exits:** open path, north side -> A.19 Storage Sheds
