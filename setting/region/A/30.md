A.30 **Training Yard** - *Retainer's Share, Old Timers*
A bare patch of packed earth behind the barracks is where the garrison drills, and where any retainer hired on for the ravine gets a morning's look at the wages and the cut they're actually owed before they sign.
*Forty feet of open ground, a rack of blunted practice weapons along one wall, the earth beaten hard by drilling boots.*
**Drill Yard:** An old timer runs anyone new to the roster through a morning's drill, and states plainly what a hired spear or torch is owed in wages and in a cut before they're sent out.
**Exits:** side door, north side -> A.25 Garrison Barracks
