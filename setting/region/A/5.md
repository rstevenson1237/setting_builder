A.5 **The Kitchen Garden** - *rooted up nightly, bramble-back wallow, standing grievance*
Rows in the **Kitchen Garden** are staked, netted, and still get rooted up most nights the moon's dark. **Bettan** replants what's ruined each morning without much hope it'll hold.
*A fenced quarter-acre plot behind the granary, furrows running east-west, netting strung low over the nearer rows; smells of turned earth and, some mornings, boar musk.*
**Bettan:** One of the last settler-family holdovers, tending what the garden actually produces rather than what the roster assumes it does. She'll trade fresh greens for actual coin or a favor worth having - the one thing had here and nowhere else at Fellward - but can't promise a harvest on any given week, and says so before taking anyone's coin.
**Boar Sign:** A wallow of packed earth and old bramble sits just outside the fence's weakest stretch, refreshed most weeks regardless of what's done about the boar that made it. Killing one solves nothing; another always takes the same wallow within a season.
**Exits:** fence gate, rope latch, north side -> the drill yard and the rest of the keep
