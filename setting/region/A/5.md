A.5 **Druvmarn** - *landing, outsiders, downriver*
A timber **jetty** on piles reaching sixty feet out into the only water deep enough to float a laden hull, with a **crane** at its head and a shuttered counting-house at its root. Eight or nine times a year the shallow-draught boats come up from downriver and for four days Torsgaard has iron, wine, glass and strangers.
*A jetty about 60 by 12 feet standing on tarred oak piles, with a hard of rammed shell and rubble some 40 yards across at its landward end, at the foot of the rise's north face. Built for the downriver trade and idle most of the year. It smells of tar and river mud rather than brine, which makes it the one place in the holding that does not smell like the pans, and it is quiet enough that the water against the piles is the loudest thing.*
**Crane:** A timber jib crane on a pivot post at the jetty head, twelve feet of arm with a hand-winch and a hook, rated by nobody and trusted by everybody. It is the only mechanical advantage in Torsgaard and the only way anything over about eight slots comes off a hull or goes onto one. A party trying to move something genuinely heavy out of the holding is coming here or it is not going.
**Counting-House:** A shuttered hut at the jetty's root, about 15 feet square, unmanned except when boats are in. When it is open, Bracka Druvling factors for a downriver house, buys at a third under Torsgaard prices and pays in standard silver on the spot. She is the only buyer in Brackvaen who will take Odarath copper at anything near its metal value, and the only person here who does not care where a thing came from. What she wants is a reliable supply of prime pelts and will pay over the odds to anyone who can promise one twice.
**Tide Board:** A painted board nailed to the jetty's second pile, marked in feet, showing the depth at the jetty head. It is the only public record of the water in Brackvaen, kept for four generations, and the last three winters of readings sit four feet below every year before them. Nobody at the landing has any explanation and one of the boatmen has started making a joke of it that is not funny.
