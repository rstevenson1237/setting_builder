A.5 **The Postern Watch** - *Gate, River, Curfew*
A narrow river-gate manned round the clock, its stone lintel carved with old-tongue lettering nobody here can read. **Sergeant Hywel** checks every face against a chalked tally by lamplight after dark.
*A gate-passage ten feet deep through the wall's thickest stretch; the lower blocks are unmistakably older and finer-cut than the wall above them, mismatched by half a hand's width at the seam.*
**Hywel:** Holds the gate by the commander's own appointment; will not open it after dark for anyone, not even Cadel.
**Old Lintel:** Carved with old-tongue lettering, worn but intact; guards touch it for luck on the way through (Truths of Carrdun).
**Seam:** Where salvaged tomb-stone meets later quarried block, plain to anyone who looks for it.
**Exits:** arched gate, inner side -> A.3 The Market Row, river-worn stone steps, outer side -> C.2 The Weir Platforms
