A.5 **The Waystone Cup** - *Walled City, Off-Duty Soldier*
Tolkar's only inn is also the one room where garrison officers and passing merchant factors share a table without incident - tonight that table holds a **Nine Wagons factor**, watching the room over a cup he has not finished. An **off-duty soldier** argues good-naturedly with the innkeeper about the price of the last barrel.
*A single common room forty feet across, low-beamed, with a loft of shared cots above for travelers who can pay for a bed rather than the floor. Smells of tallow and spilled ale; argument and dice carry over the fire most evenings.*
**Innkeeper:** Runs the Cup alone but for a serving girl. A bed in the loft runs 3 cn a night, floor space free with a drink bought; a hot meal is 1 cn and, by local agreement, the only decent one in town.
**Nine Wagons Factor:** Sits alone at the officers' table most evenings, watching toll traffic and garrison temper rather than drinking much. Wears the company's blue-grey openly and answers questions about trade freely - about anything else, he deflects (Faction: The Nine Wagons).
**Standing Table:** The one table in the room where an off-duty officer and a merchant factor sit without anyone remarking on it - a fact the room takes for granted and a stranger will not, once it is pointed out.
**Talk Tonight:** Tonight's argument is over whether Toll-master Kessin's ledger is actually short by design rather than by Brekar's toll - nobody at the table will say so plainly, and everyone at it believes it a little.
**Exits:** open door - timber, iron-strapped, east wall -> A.6 Market Street
