A.5 **The Harbor Docks** - *fishing fleet, tide schedule, missing crew*
**Stilted piers** crowded with fishing boats creak against the tide, gulls fighting over gutted fish. One crew returned shorthanded this morning and won't say why.
*A long open pier structure roughly 5 by 35 yards, built and still used for mooring the fishing fleet, exposed to weather and loud with gulls and creaking rope; boat traffic runs on the tide schedule, not the clock.*
**Fishing Boats:** Can be hired for passage, tide-dependent.
**Shorthanded Crew:** Pressing them for answers, with some rapport or coin, reveals more than they meant to share about what happened out on the water.
