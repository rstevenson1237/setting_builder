A.5 **The Beach Market** (Market) - *noisy, bartering, open*
An open square of sand lined with mats and drying racks fills with **bartering villagers** round a central weighing-stone, and a **market warden** with a pair of scales.
*70 by 50 feet. Fights are stopped by the warden; a blade drawn inside the stockade is an offence.*
**The market crowd:** gardeners, fishers and potters bartering fish for taro and cloth, white clay stripes on the brow and cheeks (Neutral, 40, Bestiary : Tribesman).
**Pulutuha:** the market warden wants honest weights and no blades drawn, and knows every price and every trader's name.
**The mat stalls:** a machete is 30 cn, a spear 50 cn (stone-tipped; iron at double the price), and cooked fish and palm wine are a portion for pennies.
**The weighing-stone:** a flat stone with brass weights in a leather pouch, where the warden settles quarrels over a measure.
