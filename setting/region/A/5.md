A.5 **The Waystone Cup** - *Walled City, Off-Duty Soldier*
Tolkar's only inn is the one room where garrison officers and merchant factors share a table without incident. Tonight that table holds a **Nine Wagons factor**, watching the room over a cup he has not finished.
*A single common room forty feet across, low-beamed, a loft of shared cots above. Smells of tallow and spilled ale, with argument and dice over the fire most evenings.*
**Innkeeper:** Runs the Cup with one serving girl, a loft bed at 3 cn a night, floor space free with a drink, a hot meal 1 cn.
**Nine Wagons Factor:** Sits alone at the officers' table most evenings, watching toll traffic more than drinking, answering freely on trade, deflecting anything else (Faction: The Nine Wagons).
**Standing Table:** The one table in the room, where an officer sits with a merchant factor, without remark.
**Talk Tonight:** Tonight's argument is whether Kessin's ledger runs short, by design rather than by Brekar, and nobody says so plainly.
**Exits:** open door - timber, iron-strapped, east wall -> A.6 Market Street
