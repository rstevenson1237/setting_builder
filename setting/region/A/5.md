A.5 **Ashenhearth Forge** - *smoke-wreathed, wold-iron, hard-won*
Heat rolls off Smith Ivar Renn's hearth as he works a piece of dull grey wold-iron, one of the few smiths on the fringe willing to handle the stuff. A **Tool Rack** of finished work stands ready for sale near the door.
*A single soot-blackened room roughly 25 by 20 feet, walls hung with tongs, hammers, and half-finished blanks. The hearth's heat is constant even with the door open, and the air tastes of coal smoke and scorched metal.*
**Hearth & Anvil:** at the room's center, where Smith Ivar Renn works wold-iron - a service most fringe smiths refuse, since the metal blunts ordinary tools and takes a toll on whoever handles it; he charges roughly double the going rate for anything wold-iron and won't say why he still takes the work.
**Tool Rack:** against the wall beside the door - finished stock available for purchase; a buyer willing to pay full price without haggling can walk away with something better than the rack usually holds (Treasure II, d20).
**Exits:** plank door, north wall -> A.4 The Long Saw
