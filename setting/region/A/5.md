A.5 **The Kitchen Garden** - *rooted up nightly, bramble-back wallow, standing grievance*
Rows in the **Kitchen Garden** are staked, netted, and still get rooted up most nights the moon's dark. **Bettan** replants what's ruined each morning without much hope it'll hold.
*A fenced quarter-acre plot behind the granary, furrows running east-west, netting strung low over the nearer rows; some mornings carries the Boar Sign's musk.*
**Bettan:** Trades fresh greens for coin or favor; harvest never guaranteed.
**Boar Sign:** Wallow at the fence's weak stretch; another boar always returns.
**Exits:** fence gate, rope latch, north side -> the drill yard and the rest of the keep
