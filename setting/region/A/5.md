A.5 **The Barracks Wall** (Ruin) - *tall, broken, shaded*

A long wall of fitted blocks, twice a man's height and broken at both ends, throws a strip of shade across a roofless yard. Gnawed bones lie in the dust at its foot, and old dung dots the sand.

*The wall is 50 feet high at its tallest point and the yard behind it about 80 feet by 40. It shades the coolest ground in the north of the ruins, and a pack of hyenas has taken it.*

**The Wall:** A face of fitted bronze-tooled blocks; a handful of cut steps climb to a broken guard walk with a view of the pyramid's tip and the dunes beyond.
**The Yard:** A roofless parade ground with the bases of rack posts and a dry trough along one side.
**The Den:** Four hyenas rest in the shade at the foot of the wall, laughing and growling in turn (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Hyena). Their laughter carries far over the dunes; they trail a party that tires and rush one that lags.
