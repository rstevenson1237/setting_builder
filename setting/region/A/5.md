A.5 **Bonded Traders' Lodging** - *Bond, Missing Cart, Tallow*
A long timber lodging house holds a cot and a locked chest for every trader who's posted the season's bond, and **Yarrow**, who keeps the house, wants a missing cart brought back before she loses the bond herself.
*Forty feet of shared sleeping loft over a common room, the smell of tallow and wet wool thick enough to taste, busiest the night before a market day.*
**Yarrow's Terms:** Yarrow charges three coppers a night and a copper more for the meal, and turns away anyone who can't show a name already on the gate's own roster.
**Missing Cart:** Yarrow posted bond against a cart of goods due back off the Raiders' Hollow road and it hasn't come, and she'll pay what the goods are worth to whoever brings back the cart or the coin it cost her (Quest: The Bonded Cart).
**Exits:** canvas gap, east end -> A.4 Trade Row
