A.5 **The General Store** (Merchant) - *cluttered, cramped, shrewd*
Barrels, coils of rope and jars crowd a narrow shop, and a counter stands at the back where the trader waits among the **shelves**.
*Shop 30 ft by 12 ft. Stock is what the wagons bring, so the shelves thin before a wagon day.*
**Shelves:** Rope, torches, rations and kit at the listed prices of the realm.
**Oil:** Lamp oil by the flask at 6 cn.
**Odds and Ends:** Spikes, a crowbar and sacks at 5 to 10 cn each.
**Garrick Dunn:** Counts every coin twice, and tallies the lost wagons' cargoes aloud to anyone who stops: salt, grain, and his own strongbox; his wagon was found stripped, a black thumbprint on its tailboard (Quest: The Lost Strongbox).
