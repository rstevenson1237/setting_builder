A.26 **Quartermaster's Store** - *Ledger, Unclaimed, Labels*
Keep-issue gear is drawn from here against a name on the roster, and nothing moves through the quartermaster's own **ledger** unrecorded.
*Thirty feet of shelved stores from boots to bowstrings, everything labeled plainly by what it is rather than what it's for.*
**Quartermaster's Ledger:** Every item drawn is logged against the name that took it, and the quartermaster won't issue a second of anything until the first's returned or paid for.
**Unclaimed Bin:** A bin of unclaimed effects, taken off men who never came back to reclaim them, holds a single strongbox key that matches no lock in the keep's own stores (Keys: Unclaimed Strongbox Key).
**Exits:** side door, west side -> A.25 Garrison Barracks
