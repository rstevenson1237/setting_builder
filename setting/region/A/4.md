A.4 **The Long Saw** - *resinous, deafening, industrious*
The mill's great **Saw Carriage** shrieks through logs from dawn to dusk, feeding stacked lumber onto carts bound for the yard outside. Sawmaster Dunstan Holt works the carriage himself, too loud over the blade to speak without shouting.
*A single open-timbered shed roughly 50 by 30 feet, sawdust ankle-deep in the corners and every beam resin-dark from decades of pitch smoke. The blade's shriek is constant during working hours, underlaid by the sweet-sharp smell of fresh-cut pine.*
**Saw Carriage:** an 8-foot log-bed feeding a water-driven blade at the shed's center, tended personally by Sawmaster Dunstan Holt, who's missing two fingers from an old mistake he won't discuss and knows which loggers bring in wood taken from past a stone line without asking questions.
**Lumber Stacks:** drying stock awaiting cart pickup, stacked along the east wall - sap-sticky underfoot even well clear of the saw itself.
**Exits:** wide cart-track, east side of the shed -> A.2 Sootrow Market, plank door, south wall -> A.5 Ashenhearth Forge
