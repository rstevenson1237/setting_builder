A.4 **Farrier's Yard** - *Caravans, Busiest Yard*
The farrier's forge runs hot from before dawn, shoeing and re-shoeing every mount the garrison and every passing caravan brings through. A rack of **reworked gear** near the pens shows Brekar tack turned to garrison standard rather than melted down.
*An open-sided yard forty feet across behind a low fieldstone wall, forge and pens under one shared roof at the back. Smells of hot metal and horse; the ring of the hammer carries the length of Market Street from dawn.*
**Farrier:** Works the forge without looking up until a job is stated plainly. Shoeing runs 1 cn a horse; tack repair 2-5 cn depending on the damage.
**Reworked Gear:** Brekar tack and weapons, stripped of the red dye and refitted to garrison pattern. A serviceable reworked shortsword can be had for 15 cn - unusual stock for a farrier's yard, and cheaper than anything at Velar.
**Remount Pen:** Garrison horses and the farrier's own stock, penned along the back wall. No fine warhorse is kept here - for anything better than a garrison remount, the farrier says plainly to try Velar.
**Exits:** open gate - low fieldstone wall, north side -> A.6 Market Street
