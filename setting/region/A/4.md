A.4 **The Digger's Rest** - *Tavern, Rumour, Off-book*
Cheap beer and cheaper stew keep the **Digger's Rest** loud most nights, dig-crews trading news and grievances over both. **Tomos** waters the beer for strangers, and waters it less the longer you've been coming.
*A long, low timber hall built from ship-salvage, one big room under a loft of cots; smells of tallow smoke, spilled beer, and crews back from a shift.*
**Tomos:** Keeper and rumour-clearing-house; talks freely to anyone buying, more freely to anyone who's bought before.
**Crew Argument:** Two dig-crews loudly dispute whose contract got the richer section - ask either side, hear a different map.
**Buttoned Man:** One quiet hand keeps his collar buttoned to the throat even in the heat; nobody comments on it (Wary, 1, Bestiary : Cult Initiate).
**Rumour:** Diggers repeat that hiding a find from the commander brings bad luck fast - too many say it to be only superstition.
**Exits:** open timber porch, north wall -> A.3 The Market Row
