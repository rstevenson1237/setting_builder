A.4 **The Smokehouse** - *fish-smoke, gossip, off-duty hands*
A low, smoke-blackened building is strung with **curing fish**, warm and close, where off-duty hold-folk sit and talk. Buying a round loosens tongues quickly.
*One smoky room dominated by hanging racks of fish; visibility is poor and the smell clings to clothes long after leaving.*
**Curing Racks:** Hung thick with smoking fish; mostly atmosphere, though a hungry party can buy a meal here.
**Off-Duty Hold-Folk:** Buying them food or drink yields a rumour (see setting/Rumours.md).
**Exits:** walkway -> A.2 Salvage Market, walkway -> A.5 Harbor Docks
