A.4 **The Commander's Hall** (Authority) - *stone-floored, drafty, orderly*
A long stone hall hung with blue pennants, swept bare. Commander Mara Vell works at a **map table**, and her **office** door stands closed behind it.
*Hall 50 ft by 25 ft. Two Soldiers at the door, one runner at the table. The commander receives any party that comes through the gate with a name and a purpose.*
**Map Table:** The road is drawn out to the ravine's mouth, eight miles on. Past it the ravine is inked in as far as the cave, and beside the cave is written "number unknown".
**Commander Mara Vell:** Takes a party's measure before she speaks, and will not send her garrison out. She offers a party the strongbox lost on the first wagon, if it will go and look (Quest: The Lost Strongbox).
**Office:** Locked, with the garrison roll and the wagon schedule inside. Nothing from it leaves the room.
