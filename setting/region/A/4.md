A.4 **Bell Hall** (high) - *bone seat, dice, captives*
A long smoky hall where a dozen goblins crouch dicing along a plank table, a **bronze bell** hanging beside the bone seat at its far end. Under the seat sits an **iron-banded chest**.
*Sixty feet by twenty, the diggers' old mess hall and now the tribe's hearth. Each dusk raiders return here with a captive or a cartload. The chest is the diggers' pay chest, and no goblin can open it.*
**Goblins:** Nine to twelve goblins dice for a captive's boots at the table, want grain and iron from the road, and say the chest's key is somewhere old for a share (Rowdy, 9-12, Bestiary : Goblin).
**Bronze Bell:** A dented bronze bell hangs on a chain beside the bone seat, and the chief strikes it the moment strangers appear.
**Iron-Banded Chest:** Under the bone seat an iron-banded chest stamped with a digger's pick mark has turned every goblin blade that tried its lock (Keys: Tally Key) (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** smoky archway, behind the table -> A.1 The Smoke Hole, wooden door, left of the seat -> A.5 Old Shaft, iron-banded door, right of the seat -> A.6 Tally Room
