A.4 **Trade Row** - *No Market, Trade Row*
A row of a dozen stalls fills the yard nearest the gate on market days, and nothing sold here beats the price and edge of a common blade already carried in.
*Sixty feet of open stalls and awnings between Well Square and the outer wall, canvas patched more often than replaced, busiest at midday and bare by dusk.*
**Market Day:** Trade Row runs every third day, open to any trader who's posted the season's bond, and a stranger pays a copper over whatever a local's charged for the same goods.
**Bent-Nail Barrow:** One stall trades in odds a party won't find twice, a coil of good rope, a jar of lamp oil, a folding stool, priced fair because the trader doesn't know what half of it's actually worth (Treasure IV, d20).
**Listening Ear:** One of the Row's own peddlers passes whatever he overhears to a buyer past the gate he never names, and the same three-line mark cut through a circle turns up scratched into the underside of his scale's own weights.
