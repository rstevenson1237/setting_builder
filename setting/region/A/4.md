A.4 **The Collapsed Market** (Ruin) - *strewn, drifted, open*

A square the size of a field lies under a low sea of sand, stall frames and fallen awnings sticking out in rows. Broken pots, bronze weights and a few withered trunks of date palms lie round its edges.

*An open square about 120 feet across ringed by stalls, the awnings propped on stone frames that have not all held. It holds nothing worth carrying and a little that can be sold; the roofs are what can hurt.*

**The Stalls:** Rows of stone frames with rotted awnings. Pots and weights lie under the sand, worth a coin or two to anyone who will dig.
**The Heap:** A stone awning on the north side sags, its frame cracked through, over a heap of fallen stone and dry bones. Anyone who walks under it or pulls at the frame (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing) is buried when it comes down.
**The Wells:** A stone-lined shaft in the middle is choked with sand to the brim.
