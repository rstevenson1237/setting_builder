A.4 **Farrier's Yard** - *Caravans, Busiest Yard*
The forge runs hot from before dawn, shoeing every mount the garrison and the caravans bring through. A rack of **reworked gear** by the pens holds Brekar tack refitted to garrison pattern.
*An open-sided yard forty feet across, behind a low fieldstone wall, forge and pens under one roof at the back. Smells of hot metal and horse, and the hammer carries down Market Street from dawn.*
**Farrier:** Works the forge without looking up, until a job is stated, shoeing at 1 cn a horse, tack repair 2-5 cn by damage.
**Reworked Gear:** Brekar tack and weapons, stripped of the red dye, refitted to garrison pattern, a reworked shortsword at 15 cn.
**Remount Pen:** Garrison horses and the farrier's own stock, penned along the back wall, no warhorse among them, and the farrier says try Velar.
**Exits:** open gate - low fieldstone wall, north side -> A.6 Market Street
