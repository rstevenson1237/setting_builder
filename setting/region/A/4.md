A.4 **The Forge** - *unsharpened blades, worked in turns, no smith of standing*
The **Forge** stands cold more hours than it burns, worked in turns by whoever's rotation lands on it that week. **Old Petra** has the longest turn of anyone, and the least patience for it.
*A single-room stone forge roughly 20 by 20 feet, one chimney, floor scorched black around the hearth and clean everywhere else; the Cold Hearth smells of ash more often than hot iron.*
**Old Petra:** Sharpens or mends for the next in line, never forges.
**Cold Hearth:** Unlit most days; anvil worn smooth from repeated boot-nail repairs.
**Exits:** plank door, leather hinge, west wall -> the drill yard and the rest of the keep
