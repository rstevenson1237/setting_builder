A.4 **The Commander's Hall** (Authority) - *map table, clerks, banners*
A long hall hung with **grey-tower banners**, **clerks' desks** down one side, and a **map table** before the commander's chair at its head.
*Sixty feet long, inside the keep itself; two soldiers stand at its door.*
**Map Table:** The road and the ravine inked on hide and weighted with stones, red pins marking where wagons were lost.
**Clerks' Desks:** Three clerks copy writs and keep the bounty roll, which lists each goblin killed and who claimed it.
**Grey-Tower Banners:** Six banners of a grey tower on a white field, the keep's device, hung the length of the hall.
