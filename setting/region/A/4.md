A.4 **The Broken Wheel** (Inn) - *dim, rowdy, cellared*
Low black beams hang over one smoky room, with a **dice table** by the hearth and a **bar** along the east wall. A row of **barrels** stands behind it.
*Room 30 ft by 20 ft, beams at 7 ft. The cellar below is the Black Altar's meeting place.*
**Dice Table:** Knucklebones for stakes of 5 to 100 cn; a seat costs 5 cn (Local Procedure: Knucklebones at the Broken Wheel).
**Bar:** Ale and a hot pie for pennies a round, and a skin of strong spirits at 12 cn.
**Soldiers:** Four soldiers off duty brag over their mugs of the last patrol (Wary, 4, Bestiary : Soldier).
**Marta Voss:** Knocks a mug on the bar whenever a blade clears a scabbard, and tells whoever buys a round what the soldiers let slip when drunk.
**Lysa Torren:** Stops each party at the door with the same words: her brother Kerrin went up the ravine with four others, and she will pay anything to know where. She remembers the road he took (Quest: The Missing Party).
**Barrels:** The third barrel from the east wall rolls aside on a pull; the boards behind it are scraped bare, and a hatch with a ladder lies beneath.
**Exits:** hidden, behind the third barrel, south -> A.12 The Cellar Under the Wheel
