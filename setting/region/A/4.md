A.4 **The Guest House** (Inn) - *thatched, smoky, hospitable*
A long thatched house on posts glows with an always-lit fire, its **keeper** at the hearth, and in the corner a **stranger** in a grey coat sits with their back to the wall.
*40 by 30 feet. The guest house holds twelve beds. The keeper hears everything said at the fire.*
**Kewimano:** the keeper, warm and nosy, wants trade goods and news of the ship's land. A bed is 10 cn a night, fish, taro and palm wine are a meal for pennies, and a night of talk about the raids from the fireside is 5 cn. Kewimano knows who lodges here and who hired which guide.
**The sleeping platforms:** twelve beds on raised platforms along the walls, under thatch; the fire never goes out.
**Ellis Marr:** the stranger wears a grey canvas coat with brass buttons and a copper compass on a chain, and keeps a hand near the door. Marr deserted the treasure hunters with half their map, wants passage home on the party's ship unnoticed, and knows the hunters' camps.
