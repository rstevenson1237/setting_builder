A.4 **The Forge** - *unsharpened blades, worked in turns, no smith of standing*
The **Forge** stands cold more hours than it burns, worked in turns by whoever's rotation lands on it that week. **Old Petra** has the longest turn of anyone, and the least patience for it.
*A single-room stone forge roughly 20 by 20 feet, one chimney, floor scorched black around the hearth and clean everywhere else; smells of cold ash more often than hot iron.*
**Old Petra:** A conscript who's held this turn longer than anyone else has wanted it, competent enough to keep tools serviceable and no more. She'll sharpen a blade or mend a buckle for whoever's next in line - the one thing had here and nowhere else - but won't forge anything new; there's no steel to spare for it, and she'll say to try the Quartermaster's Stores if someone insists there must be some.
**Cold Hearth:** Ash banked but unlit more days than not; the anvil's face is worn smoothest in one spot from the same repair done over and over - a boot-nail, by the shape of it.
**Exits:** plank door, leather hinge, west wall -> the drill yard and the rest of the keep
