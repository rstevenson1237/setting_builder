A.4 **Faelvaen** - *drying, labour, birds*
A wide shelf of terraced boards stepping down the rise's western flank, where **drying floors** lie white and blinding under any sun at all and crews work them barefoot with wooden rakes. **Saltcrows** stand along every ridge-line in numbers, and the crews have stopped bothering to drive them off.
*About 300 by 150 yards of terracing in six stepped ranks, roughly rectangular, each rank floored with pitched and tarred boards. Built for drying and turning salt off the pans below and still used for exactly that. It is the brightest place in Brackvaen and the sound is rakes on board, dozens of them, slightly out of time with each other.*
**Drying Floors:** Six ranks of tarred boarding, each rank about 300 by 25 yards, pitched a hand's width across their width so brine runs off. Salt is barrowed up from the pans, spread, turned, and barrowed on to Gormhalm. Barefoot is not a hardship here but a requirement - boots carry grit that spoils the grade, and a party that walks across a working floor shod will be shouted off it by everyone in sight, which is the fastest way to be known in Torsgaard.
**Saltcrows:** Forty or more standing along the ridge poles and the terrace edges, dropping shellfish onto the boards to crack them, which is why the crews tolerate them and why the boards are pitted. They follow. A party crossing Faelvaen and then leaving the holding westward will find some of the same birds over them on the causeway, which is the single most useful thing about a Saltcrow: they are already circling somewhere before anyone has been there.
**Barrow Run:** A plank runway down the terrace's south end, four boards wide with a rope handrail, running from the lowest rank down to the pans proper. Loaded barrows go up it and empty ones come down, all day, and standing on it is not possible for long. It is also the quickest way onto the eastern flats on foot, and the crews use it as such.
**Sallow's Rank:** The fourth rank has been short-crewed for eleven days and its salt is going grey at the edges for want of turning. The hand who worked it drowned in a cut out past the flats and his kit has not been claimed, because the weight recorded against it is more than the kit is worth and everyone here can do that arithmetic. Two of the crew have taken his rakes anyway and are being quietly frozen out for it (Quest: The Weight on Sallow's Boots).
**Exits:** rutted cart track climbing east between the upper terraces -> A.1 Thrapstad
