A.8 **Feed Store** - *Carried Home, Grain Count*
Sacks of feed stacked against the Stable Block's rear wall show the same rooted-up disorder most mornings, torn open from underneath rather than the top.
*Fifteen feet of low storeroom, sacks stacked shoulder-high, the smell of spilled grain and animal underneath the straw stronger than the stable itself.*
**Rooted Sacks:** Something gets into the feed most nights and roots the sacks up from underneath before anyone's awake to stop it, and scatters the moment a lit torch reaches the doorway unless it's already feeding (Skittish, 1, Bestiary : Herd-Biter).
**Exits:** low doorway, front wall -> A.7 Stable Block
