A.8 **The Market Yard** (Market) - *open, muddy, busy*
Stalls and carts fill an open yard, and a **notice board** stands by the door of the carters' **guildhall**. Drivers wait by their wagons.
*Yard 80 ft across, the carters' hall on its north side. Busy at dawn and on a wagon day.*
**Notice Board:** Chalked names and wages: a porter at 10 cn a day, a torchbearer at 15, a light mercenary at 30; six hirelings in all and two mercenaries at a time (Local Procedure: Hiring at the Market Yard).
**Guildhall:** A green wheel is painted on its door and on every tailboard in the yard.
**Brenn Calloway:** Walks the yard with a ledger of drivers, stopping at each empty stall to say who vanished and where the wagon was found, and asks any party passing for a guard on the next convoy (Quest: The Road Bounty).
**Carters:** Four drivers lounge by their wagons and talk of the road for the price of a round.
