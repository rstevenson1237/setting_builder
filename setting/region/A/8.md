A.8 **The Dry Canal** (Route) - *sunken, straight, sand-filled*

A straight channel cuts across the ruined city from east to west, its floor deep in sand and heat shimmering along its length. Stone banks, broken in places, rise on both sides.

*A water canal about 20 feet wide and 8 deep, running a mile and a half out of the ruins to the west. Walking it costs a watch for four stops; the storm wall climbs from the west.*

**The Canal Head:** A dry stone basin at the east end, its sluice rusted shut; damp sand lies beneath the lip. 20 yards east of the way in.
**The Sunken Bridge:** A stone arch half-buried across the cut, 40 yards west; its shade covers a party from sun and wind.
**The Drowned Shrine:** A niche in the south bank, 75 yards west, holds a clay bowl with a sun-disc scratched on it and a few old coin.
**The Scorched Camp:** A black ring on the north bank, 110 yards west, with ash, a snapped spear and tracks leading west only.
**The Storm:** Brown dust climbs the western sky; anyone caught in the cut when it arrives (Test of Fate, Exposed) is battered and blinded until it passes.
