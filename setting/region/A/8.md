A.8 **The Fish Stores** (Merchant) - *salty, stacked, fly-ridden*
Racks of smoked fish crowd three low sheds under a cloud of flies, with salt pits dug between them and a **keeper** at a tally-post.
*40 by 30 feet. The stores are the village's larder; goods are traded for goods as readily as for coin.*
**Lokapawa:** the keeper, gossipy and penny-wise, wants iron or cloth in place of coin and knows which huts burned and who is missing.
**The smoked fish:** a day's rations are 5 cn, a waterskin 5 cn, a torch of tarred reed 3 cn, and a flask of lamp oil (fish oil, smoky) 6 cn.
**The salt pits:** shallow basins of seawater evaporating in the sun, edged with white crust.
**The tally-post:** a post cut with a notch for every string of fish sold.
