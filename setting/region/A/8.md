A.8 **Regimental Infirmary** - *Tribute, Regimental Medic*
Tolkar's only healer works out of a single room off the barracks, and treats the company first whatever else is waiting. A civilian with coin and patience gets seen after.
*A single whitewashed room twenty feet across, cots along one wall and a dosing table along the other. Smells of vinegar and dried herbs; a groan from behind the dosing screen is as often a soldier's hangover as a real wound.*
**Regimental Medic:** Sets a bone for 5 cn, doses a fever for 3 cn, stitches a wound for 4 cn - soldiers first, always, and a civilian waits behind any of them regardless of how long that takes.
**Dosing Table:** Herbs, vinegar, and clean linen laid out by use; nothing here is for sale on its own, only as part of a treatment.
**No Second Healer:** Tolkar has no priest of standing and no other healer. In a true emergency, the medic says plainly there is nowhere else in town to go.
**Rod on the Shelf:** A short, ugly length of black stone carved in a single spiralling coil, kept among jars and bandage-rolls as a curiosity rather than anything treated with care - tribute from a patient who couldn't pay in coin, years back, and never worth enough to the medic to bother selling. Hers to give once the debt she's owed is actually settled (Quest: The Debt on the Dosing Table) (Keys: The Rod of the Coil).
**Exits:** open door - plain timber, north wall -> A.6 Market Street
