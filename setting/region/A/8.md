A.8 **Regimental Infirmary** - *Tribute, Regimental Medic*
The **regimental medic** works a single room off the barracks, and treats the company first. A civilian with coin and patience is seen after.
*A single whitewashed room twenty feet across, cots along one wall, a dosing table along the other. Smells of vinegar and dried herbs, and a groan behind the screen, as often a hangover as a wound.*
**Regimental Medic:** Sets a bone for 5 cn, doses a fever for 3 cn, stitches a wound for 4 cn, soldiers first.
**Dosing Table:** Herbs, vinegar, and clean linen laid out by use, nothing sold on its own, only as treatment.
**No Second Healer:** Tolkar has no priest of standing, and no other healer, and the medic says so plainly.
**Rod on the Shelf:** A short length of black stone, carved in one spiralling coil, kept among jars and bandage-rolls, hers to give once her debt is settled (Quest: The Debt on the Dosing Table) (Keys: The Rod of the Coil).
**Exits:** open door - plain timber, north wall -> A.6 Market Street
