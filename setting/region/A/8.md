A.8 **Gellhalm** - *hides, bone-setting, stink*
A low shed and a yard of sunken **tan pits** downwind of everything, where hides come out of the fen and go downriver as leather. The same man who works the pits **sets bones**, on the same bench, and does not always wash first.
*A shed about 25 by 18 feet with a walled yard some 40 by 30 yards behind it holding fourteen sunken pits. Built for tanning and pressed into service as the holding's only medicine. The smell is the worst in Brackvaen - dog dung, urine, and bark liquor - and it can be found from the far side of the rise by anyone who is lost.*
**Tan Pits:** Fourteen pits sunk in three rows across the yard, each about six feet square and five deep, at various stages from lime to bark liquor. Hides go in raw off the fen and come out in nine to fourteen months. Prime marten and otter are worth more than everything else here together and are kept in the shed rather than the yard. A finished oxhide is 45 cn, a marten skin 60, and he will buy raw skins off a party at a third of that.
**Setting Bench:** Against the shed's north wall, a scrubbed oak bench with a strap at each end and a box of splints beneath it. Naugen Gelling is the closest thing Torsgaard has to a healer and is entirely frank that he is a tanner - he sets bones, stitches, draws teeth, and will not attempt anything internal. He charges 10 cn and takes it in kind. What he wants is for somebody else to learn it, and he has asked four people this year.
**Exits:** narrow way climbing north between two houses, downwind and unmistakable -> A.1 Thrapstad
