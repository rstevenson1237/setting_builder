A.7 **The Guides' Hall** (Merchant) - *long, hung, quiet*
A long, quiet hall hangs with carved **trail-staves** racked on the walls, and the **hall master** at its end.
*40 by 25 feet. A guide will not cross the serpent posts, and a guide is paid before leaving.*
**Hanuhato:** the hall master, dry and honest, wants the fee paid before leaving and no party sent past the serpent posts; Hanuhato knows the trails north, the camps and the cave men's ridges. A guide is 30 cn a day, a porter 10 cn a day, and a coast chart on bark cloth 50 cn (the coast only). Hiring is bargained here (Local Procedure: Hiring a Guide and Canoes).
**The trail-staves:** one carved stave for each trail a guide has walked, racked on the walls.
**The guides:** five waiting for hire, white clay stripes on the brow and cheeks, none for the east (Neutral, 5, Bestiary : Tribesman).
