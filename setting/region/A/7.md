A.7 **Stable Block** - *New Keep, Stable Block*
New-built stalls hold the garrison's few mounts and whatever's hired out to a party that can pay, the straw turned fresh enough to still smell of the cart it came in on.
*Six stalls under a single low roof, built alongside the keep itself rather than added after, the far end opening onto the Feed Store.*
**Hired Mounts:** The stablehand rents a mount for a silver a day, payable up front, and won't let one out past the gate to anyone who can't already show a name on the roster.
**Exits:** open yard, north side -> A.18 Well Square, low doorway, rear wall -> A.8 Feed Store
