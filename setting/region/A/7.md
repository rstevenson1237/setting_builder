A.7 **The Scouts' Camp** (Lair) - *trampled, tented, watched*

Six hide tents huddle among tumbled walls round a dead fire pit, smoke rising from one of them. Waterskins hang from the tent poles, nearly flat, and a lookout watches the three statues.

*A former courtyard about 50 feet across, the tomb robbers' camp in the ruins, on rising ground south of the statues. They want water and a way down; they will trade what they know of the first room for either.*

**The Tents:** Six stretched hides, one for each pair of scouts, with bedrolls, rope and iron spikes inside.
**The Fire Pit:** Cold ash and a blackened pot. Smoke at dusk is the one sign of the camp from afar.
**The Scouts:** Six delvers mend gear and watch the statues (Wary, 6, Bestiary : Delver). They trade what they know of the first room for water, and ambush a party that has robbed them.
**The Mark:** A coil of rope and a row of notches are cut in every doorframe and tent pole.
**The Skins:** Eight waterskins, all but one nearly empty; the camp will be dry in two days.
