A.7 **Tanning-Yard** - *Serpent Cult, Camp Follower Trade*
Kept to the town's edge for the smell, the yard turns raw hides into leather. The **tanner** works it with two **yard hands**, and packs up whenever the garrison moves.
*An open yard thirty feet across at Tolkar's southern edge, vats sunk into the ground, hides stretched on frames along the fence. Smells of lime and rot before anything else.*
**Tanner:** Runs the yard and its two hired hands, hides priced by weight and condition, 2 cn a poor scrape, 20 cn a worked aurochs hide.
**Yard Hand:** Works the lime vats with sleeves rolled high, showing a coiled-serpent scar on the forearm, asked about it -> he denies everything and reports the asking eastward (Faction: The Serpent Cult).
**Exits:** open gate - low fieldstone wall, north side -> A.6 Market Street
