A.7 **Halmgaard** - *lodging, drink, talk*
A two-storey house of black timber with a **long room** on the ground floor where most of Torsgaard ends its day, and a **loft stair** at the back going up to beds nobody has paid for yet. There is a **weighted door** at the far end that leads out to the yard, and a **grey smear** at hand height on its frame that nobody wipes off.
*About 45 by 30 feet on the ground floor with a loft above, an L in plan where a byre was taken into the house forty years ago. Built as a household and now the holding's only lodging worth the name. It smells of wet wool, tallow smoke and eel stew, and it is the loudest room in Brackvaen after the weigh-house.*
**Long Room:** Benches and four trestle tables filling the ground floor, with the hearth on the north wall and Druva Halmling working the length of it. A night on the floor is 3 cn, a bed in the loft 8, a bowl of eel and barley 2, and the beer is thin and endless. Druva has kept this room for twenty-two years, misses nothing said in it, and repeats perhaps a third of it - she has a habit of stopping mid-sentence to listen to somebody else's table, and does not apologise for it (Named Creature: Druva Halmling).
**Pan-Crew Corner:** The two tables nearest the hearth belong, by custom nobody states, to working crews, and a stranger who sits there is tolerated exactly once. This is where a party will hear what is circulating: at present, that the water in the eastern pans did not drop but was let out from below, and that a man who had no business being under the ground at all came up Brined last month. Both are repeated with certainty and neither is sourced to anybody.
**Weighted Door:** At the room's south end, a plank door hung on a rope-and-stone weight so it swings shut on its own, opening onto the yard and the privy. The frame carries a smear of coarse grey salt at hand height, left deliberately. Druva has not wiped it off and will not discuss it, and there are eleven more like it on doorframes down the lower holding - the Faelhond have called at all of them.
**Loft Stair:** A boxed stair in the room's north-east corner, steep, with a rope for a handrail, going up to a loft of eight straw beds under the thatch. Guests only; Druva stops anyone else on the third step without appearing to move. The loft's gable end has a shutter that opens onto the roof of the byre and from there to the lane, which is worth knowing and which several of her long-term lodgers already know.
**Ferris's Bench:** The end of the second table, kept clear. A hand called Ferris drank here every night for nine years and came up out of Siwapelu three months ago with the salt to his elbows and has not been back in the room since, though he is still in the holding. Nobody has taken the seat and nobody can explain why not (Quest: What Came Up With Ferris).
**Exits:** packed track running north-west up the rise, the busiest in the holding -> A.1 Thrapstad
