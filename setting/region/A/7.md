A.7 **Tanning-Yard** - *Serpent Cult, Camp Follower Trade*
Kept to the town's edge for the smell, the tanning-yard turns raw hides from the farrier's yard and passing caravans into leather - one of the trades that packs up and moves whenever the garrison does, and stays only because the garrison has stayed three months longer than planned.
*An open yard thirty feet across at Tolkar's southern edge, vats sunk into the ground and hides stretched on frames along the fence. Smells of lime and rot before anything else; the smell alone explains the location.*
**Tanner:** Runs the yard and its two hired hands. Hides run by weight and condition, from 2 cn for a poor scrape to 20 cn for a full aurochs hide worked soft.
**Yard Hand:** Works the vats with his sleeves rolled to the elbow more often than the smell requires. A coiled-serpent scar on his forearm shows plainly when he reaches into the lime - covered again the moment he notices being seen. Asked about it directly, or caught alone at closing when he assumes nobody is watching, he denies everything and reports the asking eastward that same night, to a cell nobody at Tolkar has ever found (Faction: The Serpent Cult).
**Exits:** open gate - low fieldstone wall, north side -> A.6 Market Street
