A.7 **The Moneychanger** (Bank) - *tidy, barred, quiet*
A barred **counter** and a chained **scale** divide a small room, with a **strongroom** door at the back.
*Room 15 ft by 12 ft. A clerk at the counter; the strongroom is iron and two keys.*
**Counter:** Coin of any issue changed for 3% of the sum.
**Strongroom:** A locked box holds a party's coin for 5 cn a week, up to 500 cn.
**Scale:** A hoard is weighed and bought here (Local Procedure: Selling Loot to the Moneychanger).
**Selwyn Crake:** Weighs each coin without a word, and sets any of an old strange issue aside to ask where it was found and by whom.
