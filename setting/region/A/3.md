A.3 **Pell's Trading Post** (Merchant) - *scales, crates, wagon-boards*
A shopfront crammed with **stacked crates**, and **hanging scales** over a **wagon-board counter**. A back door opens on a yard with room for one wagon.
*Goods bought off the road wait in the yard before they are shelved.*
**Hanging Scales:** Brass scales on a chain, with a box of iron weights; a find is weighed here before a price is named.
**Stacked Crates:** Rope, oil, nails and rations, the crates stencilled with merchants' marks from the realm.
**Wagon-Board Counter:** Built from wagon boards, one of them scratched with a chevron and planed half smooth.
