A.3 **Garrison Barracks** - *Mercenary Guild, Empty Barracks Wing*
Captain Dessek holds this command from a room barely large enough for the desk in it, in a barracks built for twice the company now billeted here. The **empty wing** behind him has stood unused since before he took command.
*A long timber-and-fieldstone hall, sixty feet by twenty, built to sleep forty and currently holding twenty-two. Smells of oiled leather and banked fire; boots and drilled orders carry from the yard outside at all hours.*
**Captain Dessek:** Commands from a desk stacked with unanswered requisitions. States his business plainly and expects the same back - he has no patience left this season for anyone wasting it.
**Empty Barracks Wing:** The unused half of the hall, cots stacked and canvas-shrouded, built for a company twice this one's size in a kingdom that used to need it. Nobody has re-tasked the space; it simply stands.
**Toll Shortfall:** Dessek's own copy of the toll returns sits on his desk, matching Kessin's - three months short since Brekar took the ford, and the western crown's writ has not once acknowledged the gap. He has taken no order to retake it, since doing so means crossing the boundary cairns into the middle kingdom's claimed ground first, uninvited.
**Sealed Letter:** A letter from the western heir, seal unbroken, sits beneath the requisitions on Dessek's desk. He has not told the toll-house what it says, and has not yet decided whether to open it in front of anyone (Lore: The Western Heir's Letter).
**Confiscated Red Knot:** A strip of red-dyed cloth, knotted in Brekar's own three-loop hitch, taken off a captured rider and kept in the quartermaster's stores rather than burned (Keys: A Brekar Passage-Mark).
