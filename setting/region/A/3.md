A.3 **Gormhalm** - *stores, stock, restriction*
Three long sheds banked against the south side of the rise behind **barred doors**, holding the holding's salt against the weather. The air inside is close enough to make your eyes water and the **white drifts** heaped along the walls come waist-high in a good season.
*Three parallel sheds, each about 60 by 20 feet, roofed in weighted reed thatch and floored with raised boards over a drainage void. Built for exactly this and nothing else. It smells overwhelmingly of ammonia and brine, and the only sound is the trickle of drainage under the boards and the constant scratching of rats in the thatch.*
**White Drifts:** Salt heaped along both long walls of each shed to a height of three or four feet, coarse western stock in the first shed and fine eastern grade in the second and third, shovelled and turned by storemen twice a week. Outsiders are not admitted without a storeman, which is a rule about theft rather than about secrecy. A party that gets inside alone will find the third shed's stock is fine eastern grade stacked in barrels stamped for the western pans, which is either a clerical convenience or the whole of the Assize's problem.
**Saltrats:** In the thatch of all three sheds, in numbers - hundreds to a shed, which the storemen have stopped pretending to control and now merely audit. Two cats are kept and are entirely inadequate. A party spending any time here in the dark will meet a dozen of them at once, and a pack left unattended on the boards will be stripped of leather, tallow and anything glued.
**Exits:** low double doors, banded and barred on the outside, at the north end of the middle shed -> A.1 Thrapstad
