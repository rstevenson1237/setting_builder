A.3 **The Dry Cistern** (Ruin) - *cool, hollow, echoing*

A wide round pit opens in the sand, its floor cracked clay and a ramp winding down the wall. The air at its lip is cool, and somewhere below water drips.

*A round pit about 60 feet across and 30 deep. The cistern stored the quarter's water; the last of it seeps into a sump at the bottom. A party can search the walls for it (Local Procedure: Foraging for food and water among the ruins). (The Cynidiceans Raise the City)*

**The Ramp:** Winds down the wall; the lower turns are cracked and loose underfoot.
**The Sump:** A hollow at the bottom holds a hand's depth of brackish water, green at the edges, enough for a few skins if it is boiled.
**The Walls:** Damp stains and green streaks mark the lowest courses, where water still gathers.
**The Nest:** A mass of straw, bone and rag packs the cracks round the sump, home to a swarm of rats (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Giant Rat). They rush anyone who comes down.
