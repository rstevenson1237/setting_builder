A.3 **The Market Row** - *Trade, Overpriced, Crowded*
Stalls crowd the **Market Row** three deep some mornings, selling everything from rope to river-fish at a third over what it costs anywhere else. **Enid** has elbowed her way into arbitrating whose stall goes where, and keeps a running tally of who owes whom.
*An open gravel yard sixty yards long between the granary and the hall, awninged stalls down both sides, busiest at midday and empty by dusk.*
**Enid:** Arbitrates stall placement and debts between traders; will vouch for a stranger's credit, for a cut.
**Stalls:** Rope (45 cn), dried fish (8 cn), a whetstone (30 cn) - a third over river-town prices, same as everything here.
**What's Missing:** No independent smith trades here; the garrison's own smith works only under the commander's contract.
