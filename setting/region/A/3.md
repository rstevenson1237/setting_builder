A.3 **Gate Tower South** - *Signal, Soot, First Word*
An unlit **Signal Fire** sits ready in an iron cage atop this twin to the north tower, and the watch posted here is the first to hear whatever a stranger says before they're asked anything at all.
*Same climb and same platform as the north tower, the brazier's iron scorched black under a coat of soot nobody's scrubbed off.*
**Signal Fire:** An unlit brazier stands ready in an iron cage, kept laid and dry to call the garrison to the wall the moment it's struck.
**Exits:** stair door, north wall -> A.1 Outer Gate, wall-walk, west side -> A.16 Gate Watch Post
