A.3 **The Barracks Block** - *half stripped for timber, roster two names short, built for thrice the garrison*
Half the **Barracks Block** still houses the watch in neat, close-packed bunks; the other half stands shuttered, stripped down to its bones for the timber. **Sergeant Wrey** keeps both halves in his head down to the name.
*A long timber-and-stone hall roughly 90 by 20 feet, built for three companies and holding less than one; the occupied half smells of wool, oil, and too many bodies in too little air, the shuttered half of dust and standing damp.*
**Sergeant Wrey:** Carried on the roster years past when he should have mustered out, and the only one who can recite the current watch rotation without checking the board. He corrects anyone who gets it wrong, on duty or off, and wants badly not to be one of the names struck when the granary's count comes up short again. He can't grant leave, discharge, or extra rations for any reason - that's the Ring's business at the Quartermaster's Stores, not his.
**Watch Board:** A slate by the door lists the current rotation; two names are crossed through rather than erased, the gaps unfilled since whoever held them stopped being carried. Reading it tells a party exactly who stands where and when - the one thing had here and nowhere else at Fellward.
**Bunks:** Packed two to a wall's width closer than regulation allows, each claimed by whoever's actually on the current roster; a few stand permanently empty, blankets still folded, kept that way out of habit more than expectation.
**Shuttered Half:** Stripped to the frame for its own timber, floor open to what's left of the roof in two places. Wrey won't let anyone through the locked inner door without a reason he approves of himself - he's the whole obstacle, not a lock worth forcing. Past it, old company effects sit in footlockers nobody's sorted in a decade; finding one particular paper among the rot costs real searching time, not a roll (Quest: The Missing Dispatch).
**Exits:** plank door, iron strap hinges, north gable -> the drill yard and the rest of the keep
