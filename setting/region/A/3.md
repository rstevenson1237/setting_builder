A.3 **The Barracks Block** - *half stripped for timber, roster two names short, built for thrice the garrison*
Half the **Barracks Block** still houses the watch in neat, close-packed bunks; the other half stands shuttered, stripped down to its bones for the timber. **Sergeant Wrey** keeps both halves in his head down to the name.
*A long timber-and-stone hall roughly 90 by 20 feet, built for three companies and holding less than one; the Shuttered Half smells of dust and standing damp.*
**Sergeant Wrey:** Knows the watch rotation cold; grants no leave or rations.
**Watch Board:** Lists the current watch rotation; two names crossed through, unfilled.
**Bunks:** Overcrowded, claimed bunks; a few stand permanently empty, blankets folded.
**Shuttered Half:** Wrey gates the locked door; searching inside finds the dispatch (Quest: The Missing Dispatch).
**Exits:** plank door, iron strap hinges, north gable -> the drill yard and the rest of the keep
