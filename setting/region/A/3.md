A.3 **The Tally-Keeper's Shed** - *debt ledgers, sea's reckoning, private records*
A cramped shed smells of tallow and old paper, its **shelves** stacked floor to ceiling with ledgers. The **tally-keeper** watches visitors closely over a pair of cracked spectacles.
*A single small room, barely large enough for two people and the tally-keeper's desk; the ledgers are organized by hold-family name, and nothing leaves this room without the tally-keeper's say-so.*
**Ledger Shelves:** Reading a specific name's tally, with permission or a bribe, reveals whether that debt has run long enough to have drawn the sea's attention.
**Tally-Keeper:** Can be asked about hold history or specific names, for a price.
**Exits:** door -> A.1 Bell Hall
