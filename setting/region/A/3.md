A.3 **The East Wall End** (Gate) - *stone-built, watched, sea-lapped*
The wall ends in a stone tower standing in the channel shallows with burned huts beside it, **watch canoes** slung from the tower, and a raider's body laid out on the mud.
*30 by 30 feet, the tower 25 feet high. Two watch canoes keep each wall end by day and one by night; a canoe caught slipping by is turned back. The channels beyond lead to the southern swamps.*
**The tower:** square stone, scarred by fire, with a log boom across the channel below it and a slip for the watch canoes. The wall runs from its foot across the neck.
**The watch canoes:** paddlers and spearmen of four canoes, with white clay stripes on the brow and cheeks, slung beside the tower (Neutral, 8, Bestiary : Tribesman).
**Vakopawa:** the watch-canoe captain, grim and careful, wants spears for the night canoes and pays in guide-lore. Vakopawa knows the channels the raiders use, and when.
**The raider's body:** a lizard man laid out under a cloth for the burial rite, an ochre band painted across the throat.
**The burned huts:** black, fallen roofs along the shore beside the tower, where the last raid landed.
**Exits:** a canoe out through the boom and the channel -> B.1 The Black Channel
