A.3 **The Traveller's Inn** (Inn) - *crowded, smoky, noisy*
A long **common room** under granary beams, trestle tables down its length and a fire at one end. A stair climbs to the **private rooms** above, and a door at the back opens on the **stable**.
*Common room 40 ft by 20 ft; eight rooms above, a stable for twelve mounts. It fills on a wagon day and is half empty between.*
**Common Room:** Straw pallets by the fire; a bed in the room costs 10 cn a night.
**Private Rooms:** Two upstairs with bolted doors, 25 cn a night.
**Stable:** Stabling and fodder for a mount, 6 cn a night.
**Edda Marsh:** Names the price before the party has finished asking, and between prices repeats what travellers have said of the ravine, true or not.
**Jory Fenn:** Hangs about the stable door and offers to carry a pack up the ravine for a share. He mentions, unasked, which mounts came back lame from the wood.
