A.3 **Garrison Barracks** - *Mercenary Guild, Empty Barracks Wing*
Captain Dessek commands from a room barely larger than its desk, in a barracks built for twice the company billeted here. The **empty wing** behind him stands cot-stacked and shrouded.
*A long timber-and-fieldstone hall, sixty feet by twenty, built to sleep forty and currently holding twenty-two. Smells of oiled leather and banked fire, with boots and drilled orders carrying from the yard at all hours.*
**Captain Dessek:** Commands from a desk stacked with unanswered requisitions, stating his business plainly, and expecting the same back.
**Empty Barracks Wing:** The unused half of the hall, cots stacked and canvas-shrouded, standing open and put to nothing.
**Toll Shortfall:** Dessek's copy of the toll returns matches Kessin's, three months short since Brekar took the ford, and no order to retake it has come.
**Sealed Letter:** A letter from the western heir, seal unbroken, beneath the requisitions on Dessek's desk (Lore: The Western Heir's Letter).
**Confiscated Red Knot:** A strip of red-dyed cloth, knotted in Brekar's three-loop hitch, kept in the quartermaster's stores (Keys: A Brekar Passage-Mark).
**Exits:** open door - timber, iron-banded, south wall -> A.6 Market Street
