A.3 **Dunn's Stores** (Merchant) - *cluttered, cramped, shrewd*
A shuttered stall-front stacked with **crates** and coils of rope opens onto the yard. Garrick Dunn watches from behind the **counter**, and a **tally** is pinned to the wall.
*Shop 15 ft by 12 ft, a stockroom behind. Dunn sleeps over the shop with a crossbow under the counter.*
**Counter:** Garrick Dunn, wary and tight-fisted, wants silver and salvage and knows what both lost wagons carried. He weighs a party's finds before he names a price, and turns over anything stamped without saying what the stamp means.
**Crates:** Rope, spikes, torches, iron-rimmed shields and plain steel stacked to the beams. Hemp rope, 50 ft, is 15 cp, and any normal Treasure II item is 100 cp; he sells no cursed one.
**Loss Tally:** A sheet pinned by the door, in Dunn's hand, listing the two lost wagons: salt, grain and a bale of cloth of his own on the second, and on the first a strongbox of gold sealed with the keep's tower.
