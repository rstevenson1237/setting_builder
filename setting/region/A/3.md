A.3 **The Cutters' Hall** - *ledgered, wary, official*
Inside the guildhall, a long **Blessing Table** scarred with old blade-marks holds the guild's public ledger of every crossing it has sanctioned. Warden-Cutter Ysolt Kray oversees the hall, wary of any stranger asking too many questions about a crossing gone wrong.
*A single rectangular room roughly 30 by 20 feet, dressed stone footing beneath timber walls, smelling of the linseed oil used to dress the guild's wold-iron blades. A tally-board of notched sticks lines one wall, one notch per blessed crossing this season.*
**Blessing Table:** a scarred worktable roughly 8 feet long at the room's center, where a cutter touches a fresh-cut chip of boundary stone to a client's blade before blessing a crossing; its open ledger has pages initialed and countersigned, but the binding looks decades newer than the guild's founding and several early years are missing outright. Prying up the loose floor-plank beneath the table's near leg reveals a second, unwritten ledger hidden below (Lore: The Second Ledger).
**Warden-Cutter's Desk:** in the room's far corner, where Warden-Cutter Ysolt Kray, a Boundary-Cutter by training, reviews petitions for new crossings - curt with outsiders and unwilling to discuss the founding factor's death beyond calling it "wold's business."
**Exits:** door, iron-strapped oak set into the west wall -> A.2 Sootrow Market
