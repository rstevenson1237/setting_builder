A.13 **The Sally Tunnel** (Secret) - *low, dry, forgotten*
A low **tunnel** runs east under the yard, with a barred **door** at its far end and **steps** at this one.
*Tunnel 150 ft long and 4 ft high. The builders cut it as a way out; the door at the far end is rusted shut.*
**Tunnel:** A strip down the middle of the flagstones is worn smooth and free of dust.
**Far Door:** The bar is rusted into its brackets; cold air smelling of pine comes from under the door.
**Steps:** Climb west to the step under the dais in the Commander's Hall.
**Exits:** hidden, west, up steps -> A.10 The Commander's Hall, rusted shut, east end of the tunnel -> the wood outside the wall, off the map
