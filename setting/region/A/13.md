A.13 **The Forge** - *Debt, Refusal, Ringing Stone*
**Bosk** works the keep's one forge on credit against whatever a party brings back, and calls in what's owed before he'll shape anything new.
*Twenty-five feet of soot-blackened stone around a single hearth, the ring of the hammer audible clear across Well Square whenever the bellows are pumped.*
**Bosk's Ledger:** Bosk keeps a running tally of what's owed against him and won't start new work for anyone who hasn't settled the last debt first.
**Refused Commission:** Bosk buys raw ravine-cut stone at a fair weight but won't forge a blade from it himself, and sends the buyer back out to whoever will risk the ringing edge.
**Standing Order:** Bosk will pay double weight for a load of the same stone brought straight from the ravine's own outer cutting rather than traded up through a household first (Quest: Ravine-Cut Stone).
**Exits:** open door, west side -> A.18 Well Square
