A.18 **Well Square** - *No Market, Outer Bailey*
Every path in the outer bailey opens onto this square around the yard's own **well**, where nothing sold beats the price of a common blade already carried in.
*An open yard sixty feet across, packed earth worn to mud at the well's own edge, busiest at midday and empty but for the watch after dark.*
**Well:** Riders, traders, and off-duty soldiers all cross the square on their way to somewhere else, and word of anything that's happened at the gate reaches here before it reaches anywhere past the second gate.
