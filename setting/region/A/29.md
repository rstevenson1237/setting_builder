A.29 **Inner Cistern** - *New Keep, Inner Bailey*
A newer cistern than the outer well serves the inner bailey alone, its water kept back from anyone without a name past the second gate.
*A stone-ringed shaft ten feet across, capped with a lid nobody but the garrison's own bothers to lift.*
**Inner Cistern:** Water here is drawn only by those who sleep past the second gate, and rationed the same moment the granary's own count comes up short.
**Exits:** side path, east side -> A.20 Second Gate
