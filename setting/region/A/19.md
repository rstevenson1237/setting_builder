A.19 **Storage Sheds** - *Padlocks, Forfeit, Rent*
A row of plank sheds behind Trade Row holds whatever a trader can't fit on their own stall, rented by the season and locked with whatever the trader brought themselves.
*Four sheds of unpainted plank, each barely ten feet square, padlocks of every make hanging from hasps that don't all match.*
**Rented Sheds:** A trader pays a season's rent for shed space and loses it the moment the rent goes unpaid past a single market day, the keep taking whatever's left inside to cover the debt.
**Exits:** plank walk, north end -> A.4 Trade Row, open path, south side -> A.17 Outer Bailey Midden
