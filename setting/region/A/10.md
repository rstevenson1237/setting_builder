A.10 **The Chiefs' House** (Authority) - *raised, carved, guarded*
A raised hall on carved posts stands at the village's centre, a **guard** at the head of its stair, and inside, a **council** sits round a hearth.
*50 by 40 feet, the hall 20 feet high. The chiefs hear petitions at dawn; entering the house unasked is an offence, and the party then gets no guide or canoe until the chiefs have heard it. The egg-stone is kept in a niche behind the hearth.*
**The chiefs' guard:** twelve spearmen with white clay stripes on the brow and cheeks and hide shields, who call out from the stair (Neutral, 12, Bestiary : Tribesman).
**Pulumano:** the senior chief, slow and proud, wants the raids ended and the captives home, and pays in canoes. Pulumano asks any party that can handle a canoe to bring home the captives of the last swamp raid, for canoes and a guide (Quest: The Raid's Captives), and knows how the war began and the story of the egg-stone.
**Kunotiwa:** an elder of the council, mild and sorrowful, wants the egg-stone carried back to the lizard men in secret, and says nothing of it to the chiefs (Quest: The Egg-Stone Returned); Kunotiwa knows the niche behind the hearth.
**Hatokali:** the council's speaker, smooth and patient, wants the egg-stone kept and no deal made with the lizard men. Hatokali keeps the shirt laced to the throat in the heat. Hatokali knows what each chief fears.
**The hearth:** a great flat hearthstone, scraped against its neighbour with a trail of soot; lifted, it opens a way down to the niche behind it.
**The chiefs' word:** the chiefs will say the word that opens the great gate for a party for 50 cn, and refuse it to known thieves.
