A.10 **Ondtstad** - *shanty, crews, exclusion*
Thirty-odd huts of reed and salvaged board on the flat ground at the foot of the rise, below the drying floors, where the crews who cannot get a bed in the holding sleep. Half a dozen of the doorframes carry a **grey smear** at hand height, and the **cook fires** here burn low and long because there is nothing to burn but dried reed.
*About 200 by 120 yards of flat ground just above the spring tide line, an irregular sprawl with no plan to it, the huts each some 10 by 8 feet. It was a seasonal camp for pan crews and has been permanent for three years. It smells of reed smoke and wet salt, and there is no quiet hour - crews come off the pans and go onto them at every hour the tide allows.*
**Cook Fires:** A dozen shared fires in scooped hollows between the huts, burning bundled reed that gives more smoke than heat, each with three or four people round it at any time. This is where a party can be talked to without standing a round first, and where nobody will ask their business. What circulates here is not what circulates at Halmgaard: down here the accepted account is that the Assize will seal the stair before the year is out, and that the people who go down are the only ones getting anything out of the eastern flats.
**Marked Doors:** Six huts at the shanty's eastern edge carry the coarse grey salt smear on the frame, and their occupants keep to themselves without being excluded - the difference between Ondtstad and the holding proper is that nobody down here pretends not to see it. Ondtla Faelling lodges in the furthest of them, goes out before dawn and comes back before noon, and wraps her forearms in oiled leather in all weathers. She will take a party down the stair for a third share and is entirely open about the terms; what she will not do is discuss what happens to someone who works below too long (Named Creature: Ondtla Faelling).
**Salvage Heap:** A long spoil heap of pan-scrap, broken board, rotted line and drowned gear at the shanty's north end, picked over daily and added to weekly - anything that comes off the flats and is not worth carrying up the rise ends here. A party willing to spend an hour in it finds what a hundred people have already rejected, which is occasionally not nothing (Treasure I, d20).
**Ferris's Hut:** Second from the marked doors, and its occupant sits outside it most of the day rather than in it. Ferris came up out of Siwapelu three months ago Brined to the elbow, has not gone back down and has not gone back to Halmgaard either, and answers direct questions about what he saw with complete willingness and no useful detail - he does not appear to be concealing anything, which is what unsettles the people who knew him (Quest: What Came Up With Ferris).
**Exits:** cinder path climbing north up the rise's flank toward the open ground -> A.1 Thrapstad
