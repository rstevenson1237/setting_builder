A.10 **Retainer's Board** - *Retainer's Share, Retainer's Board*
A weathered board at the yard's edge carries a scatter of **postings** for anyone willing to carry a torch or a spear, and whoever answers one is owed wages and a cut the same as any other hire.
*Just a plank wall and a few nailed notices, standing room only, read by anyone passing through Well Square.*
**Postings:** Among the postings, one asks plainly for whoever will clear whatever's rooting up the stable block's own feed store, wages and a fair cut both promised (Quest: Feed Store Watch).
**Exits:** open yard, all sides -> A.18 Well Square
