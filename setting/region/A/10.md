A.10 **The Commander's Hall** (Authority) - *stone-floored, drafty, orderly*
A long stone hall hung with blue pennants, each carrying a white tower, swept bare. A **map table** stands before a **dais**, and an office door stands closed behind it.
*Hall 50 ft by 25 ft. The commander receives any party that comes in with a name and a purpose.*
**Map Table:** The road is drawn out to the ravine's mouth, twelve miles on. Past it the ravine is inked in, and beside it is written "number unknown".
**Guard:** Six soldiers in mail stand along the walls; a bell rope by the dais brings six more within a turn (Wary, 6, Bestiary : Soldier).
**Commander Roderic Hale:** Looks up from the map only when a party gives a name and a purpose, and says plainly that he cannot march out and will pay 500 cn for a letter or a name he can take to the realm (Quest: Proof of the Hand).
**Lieutenant Cass Ferrow:** Stands at the commander's elbow and relays what the patrols report from the wood, trimmed to what matters.
**Dais Ring:** Iron, set in the step and polished by hands; turned, it lowers the step and shows a stair beneath.
**Exits:** hidden, under the dais step, east -> A.13 The Sally Tunnel
