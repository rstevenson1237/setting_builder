Tags of A Tolkar

- **Toll Ledger** - the daily tally of who paid what, kept religiously and used as leverage
  when it's needed.
- **Gate Drum** - the gates open and shut to a drummed signal, not a clock, and nobody
  argues with it.
- **Remount Yard** - garrison horses bred and reissued here; it's the whole reason cavalry
  patrols exist at all.
- **Captured Gear** - Brekar tack and weapons, reworked and resold instead of destroyed.
- **Sealed Writ** - an order from the crown that hasn't arrived yet, and everyone is
  waiting on it.
- **Boundary Patrol** - riders who never cross the cairns, whatever the provocation.
- **Double Toll** - a late arrival's penalty, paid at the gate before anyone is let past it.
- **Regimental Medic** - the only healer in town, and only for the garrison.
- **Hedge Shrine** - small and unaffiliated; no temple of real standing stands here.
- **Crown Colors** - the western kingdom's own dye and cut, worn to be told apart from the
  other two.
- **Distrusted Clerk** - the toll-house job everyone suspects and nobody wants to lose.
- **Quartermaster's Word** - garrison supply runs on his say alone, crown writ or not.
- **Old Habit Fire** - a watch-fire kept lit past dawn out of habit rather than current need.
- **Busiest Yard** - the farrier's, shoeing and tack repair for every company that passes
  through.
- **Contract Rider** - a private courier paid to outrun the crown's own post.
- **Camp Follower Trade** - trade that packs up and moves whenever the garrison does.
- **Checked Papers** - travel papers inspected at the gate; forging them badly is worse
  than carrying none.
- **Underpaid Company** - soldiers who haven't seen full pay in a season, and it shows in
  who they'll talk to.
- **Ledger Dispute** - a toll disagreement that follows a traveler to the next crossing in
  the same crown's writ.
- **Wall Watch** - sentries who know every regular face and notice every new one.
- **Market Street** - the single street between the gates where all of the town's trade
  actually happens.
- **Off-Duty Soldier** - a different conversation than the same soldier standing the wall.
- **Toll Queue** - the line at the east gate, longer some mornings than others, for reasons
  worth asking about.
- **Ford Refugee** - a trader stuck in Tolkar since the ford closed, waiting out the detour.
- **Empty Barracks Wing** - built for a company twice this one's size, in a kingdom that
  used to need it.
