A.1 **East Gate** - *Garrison, Toll Queue*
The east gate stands open between dawn and dusk, and this morning's **toll queue** already backs up past the barracks wall. **Toll-master Kessin** works the shuttered window built into the gate's inner face, checking papers and weighing coin against his ledger.
*A timber-and-fieldstone gatehouse built into the town wall, twenty feet across, doubling as Tolkar's toll-house since the garrison barracks stands just inside it. Smells of tallow smoke from the queue and horse from the barracks yard beyond; the gate drum sounds from the tower above at dawn and dusk exactly.*
**Toll-master Kessin:** Works the toll-house window; takes 1 cn a head and 1 cn a pack animal, crown-mark only, and checks travel papers against his ledger before waving anyone through. A late arrival camps outside until dawn and pays double.
**Toll Ledger:** Open on the counter inside the window. The tally for the past three months runs visibly short of what the western crown's own writ expects, and Kessin does not like it read over his shoulder.
**Certified Papers:** For 2 cn, Kessin stamps a set of travel papers as checked at Tolkar - good for passing back through this gate without requeueing, though it buys nothing at any other crossing in Telar.
**Kessin's Question:** Once papers are settled and no one else is in earshot, Kessin asks quietly whether the party has dealt with hired swords before - he has not yet found the nerve to put the question to the Nine Wagons' own factor (Quest: The Ford Kessin Won't Name).
**Exits:** open gate - timber and iron strapping, worn smooth by traffic, east wall -> B.1 Sunk Bridge Ford, open gate - matching timber, west wall -> A.6 Market Street
