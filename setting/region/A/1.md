A.1 **The Main Gate** (Gate) - *iron-bound, watched, dusk-barred*
Twin **towers** flank a tunnel through the wall, a **founding stone** cut with the realm's arms above its iron-bound doors and a swinging **lantern** lit at its mouth. Slots in the ceiling look down on the line below, and a **ledger desk** stands against the guardroom wall.
*Tunnel 40 ft long and 12 ft wide. A sergeant at the guardroom door and a clerk at the desk. Pell Orrin, the clerk, is an agent of the Black Altar.*
**Founding Stone:** Set over the gate, cut with the realm's arms and the year the keep was raised.
**Towers:** A pennant on each tower shows a white tower on blue, and the same device is stamped on the doors.
**Lantern:** Lit at dusk on an iron arm at the tunnel's mouth, and watched for by patrols still out on the road.
**Sergeant Ada Brandt:** "Who holds the road?" she asks every stranger, and waits for "The road holds." before a hand goes near a blade. She has the week's faces by heart and will say who came through.
**Murder-Holes:** Slots in the ceiling, spears angled down through them.
**Ledger Desk:** Pell Orrin writes each name and business without looking up, and asks for it twice if the answer changes. He turns the ledger's wagon page to show which wagons run when, and what each carries.
**Exits:** open, west end of the tunnel -> B.1 The Realm's Road
