A.1 **The Bell Hall** - *empty dais, council seat, hidden letter*
The hold's stone council hall centers on a **bare dais** where the bronze diving-bell once sat, now empty and argued over daily. A handful of **hold-elders** cluster near the hearth, their voices low but clearly heated.
*A single low-ceilinged chamber, roughly square, forty feet across; built as the hold's council seat and unchanged in purpose. Warmed by a central hearth, it smells faintly of woodsmoke and damp stone, and voices carry easily off the bare walls, so any argument here is audible from the doorway.*
**Dais:** Empty since the bell's disappearance; a loose plank hidden behind its base - sitting slightly proud of the others, worth a careful eye - can be pried open to reveal a private letter (Lore: The Elders' Letter) suggesting the council quietly gave the bell away rather than had it stolen.
**Hearth:** Keeps the hall warm and is the natural gathering point for conversation.
**Hold-Elders:** A handful gathered in argument; can be approached for news, favors, or paid work.
**Exits:** weathered plank door -> A.2 Salvage Market, low side door, salt-swollen -> A.3 Tally-Keeper's Shed
