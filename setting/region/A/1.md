A.1 **The Granary** - *rationed grain, ledger vs. count, ashmoth-infested thatch*
The **Granary**'s doors stand open under guard through the day, sacks stacked to the rafters while the scoop-line outside moves slower than it used to. **Corlan** measures out what's owed against a chalk tally only he seems able to read.
*A long, low stone-and-timber building roughly 40 by 20 feet, raftered in old ship-salvage timber, floor swept but for a fine grain-dust that never fully clears.*
**Grain Stores:** Ring-marked sacks count a fifth short against the posted ledger.
**Corlan:** Skims grain; trades quiet skimming for the missing relief-column dispatch (Quest: The Missing Dispatch).
**Scoop Line:** Trade labor for an extra half-scoop here, off the books.
**Old Thatch:** Disturbing soft thatch looses a blinding Ashmoth Swarm, ruining grain.
**Counting Slate:** Pressing the shortfall publicly gets Tam Ruley struck from roster.
**Exits:** timber double doors, worn threshold, south wall -> the drill yard and the rest of the keep
