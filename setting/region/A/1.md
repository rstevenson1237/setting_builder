A.1 **The Granary** - *rationed grain, ledger vs. count, ashmoth-infested thatch*
The **Granary**'s doors stand open under guard through the day, sacks stacked to the rafters while the scoop-line outside moves slower than it used to. **Corlan** measures out what's owed against a chalk tally only he seems able to read.
*A long, low stone-and-timber building roughly 40 by 20 feet, raftered in old ship-salvage timber, floor swept but for a fine grain-dust that never fully clears; smells of dry chaff and the tallow used to seal its door hinges against damp.*
**Grain Stores:** Sacks and bins line both long walls, each marked in chalk with a tally-stroke crossed twice - the Quartermaster's Ring's mark, on every sack that's passed through their hands. Counting the sacks against the posted ledger comes up a fifth short, if anyone actually does it.
**Corlan:** One of the Ring's men, doling grain by scoop rather than the sack the roster still claims. He wants off this duty before the shortfall traces back to him by name, and will trade a season of quiet skimming for a copy of the original relief-column dispatch - proof the request left Fellward at all - last seen filed in the shuttered half of the Barracks Block (Quest: The Missing Dispatch).
**Scoop Line:** A worn dooryard where rations are doled out twice daily; a soldier or conscript with a free afternoon can trade labor here for an extra half-scoop, off the books - the one thing had here and nowhere else at Fellward. Corlan won't issue more than a day's allotment for any price; anyone wanting more is sent to the Quartermaster's Stores to ask higher up.
**Old Thatch:** Older thatch above the third bin has gone soft and pale. Disturbing it or the crates beneath looses a blinding, choking Ashmoth Swarm that does no lasting harm but ruins whatever grain it settles back onto.
**Counting Slate:** A slate by the door totals what's issued against what the ledger claims remains; it hasn't balanced since Corlan took this duty, and asking him straight out gets a careful non-answer. Pressing the point in front of witnesses gets it "fixed" within the hour - Tam Ruley, a conscript already two names short of full pay, struck from the roster to make the count true instead.
**Exits:** timber double doors, worn threshold, south wall -> the drill yard and the rest of the keep
