A.1 **Roadsend Gate** (Authority) - *iron-bound, watched, dusk-barred*
A squat **gatehouse** of grey stone holds the road's end, its oak **gate** bound in iron and newly tarred. A **sergeant's post** stands in the passage, and a **board** nailed beside it lists the wagon days.
*Gate passage 10 ft wide and 20 ft deep, with a murder-hole overhead. Six Soldiers stand the day watch and two the night watch once the gate is shut. The cobbled yard beyond opens onto A.2, A.3 and A.4.*
**Gatehouse:** Two storeys of grey stone over the passage; the barracks behind it lodge the garrison. A Soldier on the roof sees the road for a mile (Wary, 6, Bestiary : Soldier).
**Gate:** Open from first light to dusk. Shut at dusk, it stays shut to anyone still on the road.
**Sergeant's Post:** Sergeant Tobin Hask sits on a stool in the passage and asks every stranger "Who holds the road?". Gruff and by the book, he wants steel kept sheathed and the challenge answered, and holds a stranger who cannot say "The road holds" until A.4 has seen them. He will say how the last two wagons were lost.
**Wagon Board:** A plank nailed to the gatehouse wall, chalked with the day of each fortnightly wagon. Any carter, drover or passer-by reads it as easily as the garrison does.
