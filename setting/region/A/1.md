A.1 **The Bell Hall** - *empty dais, council seat, hidden letter*
The hold's stone council hall centers on a **bare dais** where the bronze diving-bell once sat, now empty and argued over daily. A handful of **hold-elders** cluster near the hearth, their voices low but clearly heated.
*A single low-ceilinged chamber roughly forty feet across, warmed by a central hearth; voices carry easily off the bare stone walls, so any argument here is audible from the doorway.*
**Dais:** Empty since the bell's disappearance; a loose plank hidden behind its base can be pried open to reveal a private letter suggesting the council quietly gave the bell away rather than had it stolen.
**Hearth:** Keeps the hall warm and is the natural gathering point for conversation.
**Hold-Elders:** A handful gathered in argument; can be approached for news, favors, or paid work.
**Exits:** door -> A.2 Salvage Market, side door -> A.3 Tally-Keeper's Shed
