A.1 **The Three Statues** (Ruin) - *wind-worn, towering, watched*

Three statues of robed kings stand round the stone tip of the pyramid, their faces scoured smooth and their eyes hollow. Between them a **bronze door** hangs half-open on a dark room, and the wind makes a low note in the empty faces.

*The way in to the region, in the middle of the ruined city. The statues stand about 40 feet apart; the door faces south and opens on C.1. The tomb robbers keep a watch here by day and none by night. (The Cynidiceans Raise the City)*

**The Statues:** Three kings, each twice a man's height, carved from one block with the cynidicean script cut round their bases. Anyone standing at the door is looked down on by all three faces.
**The Bronze Door:** Green with age and a hand's breadth open, it can be pushed wide or shut from the sill; the sand has packed hard round its foot.
**The Scouts:** Two delvers sit in the shade of the south statue, ropes coiled beside them and a notch cut in the plinth for each day they have waited (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Delver). By night the plinth is empty. They know the first room is trapped, and trade the little else they know for water.
**The Mark:** A rope coil and a row of notches scratched on the statue's plinth show that the tomb robbers hold this ground.
