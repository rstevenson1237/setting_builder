A.1 **The Gate** (Authority) - *toll post, spears, iron-banded*
An arched passage runs under the gate-tower to an **iron-banded gate**, with a **toll table** against one wall and a **spear rack** against the other. Carters haggle at the table over the toll.
*Twenty feet of passage, one wagon wide; a wagon stopped at the table blocks it to everyone behind.*
**Iron-Banded Gate:** Two oak leaves banded with iron, barred from inside by a beam it takes two soldiers to lift.
**Toll Table:** A trestle with a strongbox chained to it and a ledger of every wagon out and back, its last pages short of returns.
**Spear Rack:** Six spears and six shields, one of each for the gate squad.
