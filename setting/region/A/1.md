A.1 **The Vansker Stockade** - *timbered, weathered, guarded*
The old stockade's palisade walls ring a muddy compound where the **Factor's Hall** stands watch over the town's business, a Concern banner hanging limp above its door. A **Concern Enforcer** paces the gate, billhook in hand, logging every stranger who passes beneath it.
*A roughly 40 by 30 yard fenced compound, the original silvered-log palisade squaring off around a single timber hall and a scatter of outbuildings - once the whole of Khirengaard, it now serves only as the Concern's seat and the town's one defensible point. The creak of the gate's iron hinge carries over the mill's distant sawing, and the compound smells faintly of tallow from the hall's lamps.*
**Factor's Hall:** a single-room timber hall at the compound's center, roughly 30 by 20 feet, where Factor Aldric Vantor (Named Creature: Factor Aldric Vantor) hears petitions and disputes from a plank desk bearing the Concern's charter-seal; a clerk in the outer doorway takes a visitor's name and business before anyone is let past. Decisions on surveyed claims, unpaid debts, and Concern contracts get settled at his word, though the town's older families quietly out-rule him on anything not written on Concern paper.
**Payroll Strongroom:** a locked side room off the hall, roughly 10 by 10 feet behind an iron-banded door - when a Concern payroll shipment is in town it's kept here under a second Concern Enforcer's watch, the only key held by the Factor himself; forcing or picking the lock without him noticing turns up whatever's currently inside (Treasure V, d20).
**Palisade Gate:** the compound's north opening, flanked by the original silvered stockade logs - a Concern Enforcer stands post here through the day, more interested in who's coming than in stopping anyone.
