A.1 **Thrapstad** - *scales, record, obligation*
The top of the rise is a beaten oval of trodden shell and salt-crust with the **weigh-house** standing open on its north side, and the knock of its **great scales** runs from before dawn until the light goes. Every crew in Torsgaard passes over this ground twice a day, and the **tally boards** racked along the weigh-house wall carry the name of everyone who owes anything to anyone.
*About 120 by 80 yards of open ground, an irregular oval worn flat by six generations of feet, with the weigh-house itself some 40 by 25 feet of bog oak on staddle stones at the north edge. It has always been the market ground and the weighing ground both, and it is the hub of the holding - every other location in Torsgaard opens onto it directly. It smells of brine and hot tallow, and under the scales' knock is the constant grit of shell underfoot.*
**Great Scales:** A beam scale on a timber gantry in the centre of the weigh-house floor, the beam eleven feet across, counterweighted with sealed lead blocks that are themselves checked against a master weight nobody is allowed to touch. It is the only scale in Brackvaen anybody trusts, and using it is not optional for a party that wants silver rather than barter - which means the Assize sees every haul that becomes money. Weighing a haul takes as long as the queue, and the queue is where a party hears what everyone else brought up.
**Tally Boards:** Five tiers along the weigh-house's west wall, four hundred boards of split oak, each cut with the salt-weight one party owes another and the goods it attaches to. Public, and read all day. Name anybody in the holding to a clerk → he finds their board in a minute or two → what they owe, on what, and who holds it. A fifth of the boards are pale-cut and recent. Anything carried up out of Lindmarn or Siwapelu already has a board → put it on the scales and the weight transfers with it (Lore: Vethgaard Tally, Fourth Board).
**Weighmaster's Desk:** Set against the east wall under the only glazed window in Torsgaard, four panes of green downriver glass that cost more than the desk. Halmen Weldling has weighed here for nineteen years, writes with the stub of his left hand curled round the pen, and will tell any stranger their haul's worth before he records it - a courtesy he extends because he would rather not be argued with afterward. He wants his son off the pans and onto a downriver boat, and says so to anyone who will listen (Quest: The Weight on Sallow's Boots).
**Standing Market:** Boards and trestles set up daily along the ground's southern arc, locals only - an outsider may buy but may not sell here without the Assize's word. What is actually on the boards this season: eel, fowl, reed, tallow, coarse salt at 2 cn the barrel, cured hides, and bog oak by the length. Rope, oil and iron are sold from Weldfast and Gormhalm rather than here, and are half again what they would cost downriver.
**Fresh Lead:** Nailed to the gantry post at the weigh-house door, a lead seal with three notches, stamped this month. Four more posts around the ground carry older seals, and the dates on them are all within the last three years - the Assize has begun re-measuring things it had not measured since the settlement, and everyone crossing this ground has noticed.
