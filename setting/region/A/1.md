A.1 **Outer Gate** - *Frontier Levy, Gate Hours*
Two iron-bound oak doors stand open in the grey wall where the realm's road finally ends, and a **Gate Watch** checks every arriving name against the morning roster before waving anyone through.
*Thirty feet of dressed-stone gatehouse, built to hold rather than impress, the doors barred the instant the sun clears the wall.*
**Gate Watch:** Two Greywatch Regulars check every name against the roster, wave through anyone already listed, and turn back after dusk anyone still on the road with no exception made (Disciplined, 2, Bestiary : Greywatch Regular).
