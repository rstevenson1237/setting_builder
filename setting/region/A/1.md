A.1 **East Gate** - *Garrison, Toll Queue*
The east gate stands open between dawn and dusk, this morning's toll queue already backed up past the barracks wall. **Toll-master Kessin** works a shuttered window in the gate's inner face, checking papers and weighing coin.
*A timber-and-fieldstone gatehouse built into the town wall, twenty feet across, doubling as Tolkar's toll-house, with the garrison barracks just inside. Smells of tallow smoke off the queue, and horse from the barracks yard beyond.*
**Toll-master Kessin:** Takes 1 cn a head, 1 cn a pack animal, crown-mark only, and checks travel papers before waving anyone through.
**Toll Ledger:** Open on the counter inside the window, the three-month tally running short, and Kessin dislikes it read over his shoulder.
**Certified Papers:** For 2 cn, Kessin stamps travel papers as checked at Tolkar, good for passing back through this gate, and nothing at any other crossing.
**Kessin's Question:** Papers settled and no one in earshot -> Kessin asks, quietly, whether the party has dealt with hired swords (Quest: The Ford Kessin Won't Name).
**Exits:** open gate - timber and iron strapping, worn smooth by traffic, east wall -> B.1 Sunk Bridge Ford, open gate - matching timber, west wall -> A.6 Market Street
