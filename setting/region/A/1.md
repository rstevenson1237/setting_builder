A.1 **The Commander's Hall** - *Authority, Ledger, Watched*
Commander **Cadel** signs dig-contracts at a broad **Ledger-Table**, two diggers in stamped tin tags waiting by the side door. Every blade is bound at the entrance in a peace-cord, enforced tonight by an officer who looks like he'd rather be anywhere else.
*A converted long-hall, forty feet by twenty, timber-roofed over old stone footings; benches down both walls, the ledger-table on a low dais at the far end.*
**Cadel:** Commands Carrdun by an old captaincy grant; answers to no one closer than three weeks' ride.
**Ledger-Table:** Where digging contracts into Baldun are signed - draw gear after, from the Quartermaster's Stores.
**Salvage Tags:** Two diggers wait by the side door in tin tags stamped with a stepped pyramid, saying nothing.
**Rhys:** The officer who asked too many questions now enforces the door's peace-cord, demoted rather than silenced.
**Posted Board:** Calls for diggers at 30 cn a week, more for anyone bringing their own tools.
**Sealed Gallery Contract:** Cadel pays well for confirmation of what happened to the crew that vanished there, two winters gone (Quest: The Gang's Last Contract).
**Exits:** timber double doors, center of the near wall -> A.3 The Market Row
