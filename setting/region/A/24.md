A.24 **Prisoner's Cage** - *Bargain, Old Lock, Tally Marks*
A barred cell holds whoever the garrison hasn't yet decided what to do with, currently a single captured **raider** who'll talk if the price is right.
*Ten feet of bare stone and iron bars, a single high slit window too narrow to squeeze through, the lock old enough to want a specific key rather than any picked substitute.*
**Iron Lock:** The cage's own lock is old enough to have lost its match among the garrison's current keys, and only a specific spare chit still opens it (Keys: Tally Chit).
**Captured Raider:** The raider offers up where the Raiders' Hollow band caches what it takes off the road, in trade for a word put in for him before the commander decides his fate (Quest: A Raider's Bargain).
**Scratched Count:** The raider's scratched a tally of days into the wall beside the cot, one mark short of what the garrison's own watch log says he's actually been held.
**Exits:** stair door, east side -> A.21 Commander's Tower
