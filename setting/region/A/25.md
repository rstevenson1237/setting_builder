A.25 **Garrison Barracks** - *Frontier Levy, Old Timers*
Rows of cots fill the barracks where the garrison sleeps in shifts, a handful of **Old Timers** who've served since the founding taking the beds nearest the door out of long habit.
*Fifty feet of open sleeping hall, cots and footlockers in tight rows, quieter at midday than any hour the watch actually changes.*
**Old Timers:** The old timers disagree with each other about why the keep was ever raised on this exact stretch of road, and one of them swears the routes have been reshuffled more this month than any month he can remember.
**Exits:** open yard, south side -> A.20 Second Gate, side door, east side -> A.26 Quartermaster's Store, side door, north side -> A.30 Training Yard
