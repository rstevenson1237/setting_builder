A.11 **The Barracks** (Authority) - *bare, noisy, damp*
Rows of **bunks** run down a long hall, mail hung on pegs above each, and a shared table stands at the centre. A **wall stair** climbs from the far end.
*Hall 60 ft by 25 ft, forty bunks. The alarm drum hangs by the door.*
**Bunks:** Soldiers off watch sleep within reach of their spears; the alarm drum by the door brings twenty out within two turns (Wary, 20, Bestiary : Soldier).
**Sergeant-Major Bram Tolley:** Eyes every party that comes in, then tells of the last patrol that came back short, and says it would be a kindness not to join the next.
**Wall Stair:** Climbs to the wall walk and the gate towers; a soldier on the landing asks who sent the party up.
