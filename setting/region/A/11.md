A.11 **The Watch House** (Authority) - *stockaded, armed, loud*
A stockaded yard rings with spear-butts, a **drum tower** at its corner, a **captain** barking orders, and in a cell a **captive** with an ochre band painted across the throat.
*40 by 30 feet. The watch turns out to three drum-beats within two Rounds; a blade drawn inside the stockade is held for the chiefs' hearing and a fine of 100 cn or a month's labour on the wall.*
**The watch:** forty spearmen and shield-bearers with white clay stripes on the brow and cheeks (Neutral, 40, Bestiary : Tribesman).
**Tuhahuna:** the watch captain, blunt and loud, wants strong spears for the night watch and knows where the raiders landed last and the rota.
**Khethsheek:** a captive lizard man, proud and hungry, with the ochre band across the throat, who asks every stranger "Where is the egg?" and offers the swamp's channels and hummocks for freedom. Khethsheek knows the enclaves and the captives' sale-road.
**The drum tower:** the watch's drum, struck three times to turn out the watch.
