A.11 **Healer's Tent** - *Vinegar, Stretched, Poison*
A canvas tent stands pitched rather than built, where the keep's one **field medic** works stretched between the wall and whoever a party carries in hurt.
*Twenty feet of canvas over cots and a single trestle table, the smell of vinegar and boiled linen covering whatever's under it.*
**Field Medic:** The medic treats a wound for what a party can pay in the moment, and says plainly that anything past a clean cut or a broken bone is past what she can do for a poison that kills outright.
**Exits:** tent flap, north side -> A.18 Well Square
