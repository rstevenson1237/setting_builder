A.22 **Granary** - *Bare Purse, Grain Count*
Sacked grain and the keep's own toll-coin reserve share the same locked store, tallied weekly against what the garrison actually holds.
*Thirty feet of raised stone floor keeping the grain off the damp, the strongroom door barred separate from the sacks themselves.*
**Grain Count:** The weekly tally never quite matches what's actually stored, and the difference is never announced to anyone below the quartermaster.
**Strongroom:** The tally's own mismatch is the only sign the coin store's been quietly skimmed, and whoever counts the sacks by hand instead of trusting the ledger is the one who finds it first, with the quartermaster hearing about it before the day's out.
**Bent Tally-Chit:** A single brass tally-chit, unaccounted for in the grain count and matching no lock anyone in the granary can name, sits forgotten at the bottom of the ledger box (Keys: Tally Chit).
**Exits:** side door, southwest -> A.20 Second Gate
