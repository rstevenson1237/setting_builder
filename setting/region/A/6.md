A.6 **The Quartermaster's Stores** - *Ledger, Missing, Locked*
Quartermaster **Idris**'s stores are neat to the point of strange - issued, counted, and locked, though nobody has seen him behind the counter in months. **Corporal Wren** signs for gear in his place, and answers every question about Idris the same way: reassigned.
*A long stone storeroom built into the wall's old footings, racked gear down both sides and a small back office with a locked strongbox nobody has opened since Idris left.*
**Wren:** Signs out gear in Idris's place, uneasy at his name, and reports any tampering to the commander within the hour.
**Idris's Desk:** Untouched, papers still stacked mid-task; one ledger entry lists contract numbers with nothing matching in stock.
**Strongbox:** Locked in the back office; forced or picked open, a second ledger taped under its bottom drawer records every find the commander has actually taken.
**Exits:** plank door, north wall -> A.3 The Market Row
