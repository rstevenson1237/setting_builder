A.6 **The Temple Colonnade** (Ruin) - *pillared, scoured, watched*

Two rows of fluted columns run toward a sealed stone door, their paint scoured to bare rock and the wind singing between them. Faces have been scraped from the figures cut round the door.

*A front of two rows of ten columns, 100 feet from the street to the sealed door. The door is a plain slab that cannot be opened from outside; behind it is solid rock. Scorpions burrow under the column bases.*

**The Columns:** Each is carved in bands with the cynidicean script, and every band's last figure has had its face scraped away (The Demon Is Invited). The wind sets a different note in each pair.
**The Door:** A single slab of grey stone with a sun-disc cut in its middle. The cut has been chiselled out, and the slab cannot be moved.
**The Burrows:** Two giant scorpions lie buried in the drifts between the column bases, tails up (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Giant Scorpion). A step among the bases draws a strike.
