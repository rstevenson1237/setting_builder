A.6 **Camp Followers' Row** - *Carried Home, Camp Followers*
A run of lean-to stalls behind the trade row houses whoever came up the road with the garrison but answers to no roster, mending, cooking, and trading whatever the bailey will bear.
*Twenty feet of patched canvas and cookfire smoke, the ground packed hard by traffic that never quite stops.*
**Mender's Corner:** A camp follower called Dott will patch armor or clothing for a fair price and passes along whatever's being said about a raid before the garrison's own report reaches the gate, so long as a party's bought something from her first.
**Exits:** open lean-to, south side -> A.18 Well Square
