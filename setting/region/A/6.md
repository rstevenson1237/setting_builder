A.6 **Tally Room** (low) - *tally marks, chalk, dry*
A small dry room whose walls are scored floor to ceiling with **pay tallies**, chalk stubs scattered across the floor. Dust lies unbroken on every surface.
*Ten feet square, the diggers' tally office, where each shift's pay was scored against the chest in A.4.*
**Pay Tallies:** Rows of tally marks run around the walls, each block of five headed by the same digger's pick mark stamped on the chest in A.4.
**Chalked Block:** One wall block carries chalk brighter than the rest, and pulling it free opens a cavity holding a heavy iron key (Keys: Tally Key).
**Exits:** low iron-banded door, east -> A.4 Bell Hall, rubble-choked gap, west -> A.5 Old Shaft
