A.6 **The Armourer's Forge** (Merchant) - *hot, ringing, sooty*
An open forge glows at the back of a shop with no front wall, **anvils** stand in the yard, and a **rack** wall holds blades and shields.
*Forge 25 ft by 20 ft, open to the yard. One smith and two apprentices; the shop is quiet at night.*
**Rack Wall:** Spears, daggers and swords at 30 to 100 cn each; shields and leather armour at 50 to 100 cn.
**Anvils:** Mending armour or sharpening a blade, 5 cn a piece.
**Hild Ironhand:** Hands over a notched spear without a word and asks a party to try it on a goblin hide, and to bring back what the hide did to it.
