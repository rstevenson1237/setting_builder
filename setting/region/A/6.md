A.6 **Market Street** - *Usurers, Ford Refugee*
The single street between Tolkar's two gates carries all its trade, a **stall row**, the farrier's hammer, and the Cup's noise, inside two hundred yards. One **stranded trader** still has not unpacked, three months stuck since the ford closed under him.
*A packed-earth street sixty yards long, between the east and west gates, lined with a dozen stalls that reset daily. Smells of dust, hot bread, and forge-smoke, and the gate drum carries its length twice a day.*
**Stall Row:** A dozen stalls selling road goods, tack, cloth, and food, restocked from whatever caravan last passed (Treasure I, d20; Treasure II, d20).
**Stranded Trader:** Meant to reach Velar three months ago, unpacking and repacking the same wagon since, selling at a discount to stop carrying it.
**Moneylender's Table:** Works a dead stall-keeper's pitch on market mornings, financing traders caught by the ford's closure, at rates nobody says in front of Kessin.
