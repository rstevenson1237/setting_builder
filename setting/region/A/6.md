A.6 **The Canoe Yard** (Merchant) - *tarred, stacked, busy*
Dugouts rest on their stocks under a hot tar smell, with a **canoe-builder** at work on a hull.
*60 by 40 feet, Naloni's yard, on the lagoon two miles south of Puluni. Three canoes are free to hire at any time; none is sold to a party that means to pass the wall's ends without the chiefs' word.*
**Nalotewa:** the canoe-builder, slow and proud, wants iron blades and adzes and knows every canoe's faults and how raiders slip past the watch.
**The hire stocks:** a dugout canoe hired is 20 cn a day, and a paddler 15 cn a day; a canoe bought outright is 400 cn.
**The paddlers:** six hireable boat-hands and apprentices, white clay stripes on the brow and cheeks (Neutral, 6, Bestiary : Tribesman).
**The tar pit:** boiling tar in a stone-lined hole by the launching ramp.
