A.6 **The Groaning Auger** - *fiddle-loud, quarrelsome, smoky*
Fiddle music and raised voices spill from the Groaning Auger most evenings, its **Common Room** thick with pipe smoke and old arguments revived. Tavernkeeper Senna Brakk keeps peace with a heavy hand and an ear for everything said within her walls.
*A single low-beamed room roughly 30 by 25 feet, smoke-stained rafters hung with drying herbs and one cracked hunting horn no one remembers the story behind. Fiddle music, argument, and woodsmoke are constant once the sun's down.*
**Common Room:** the main floor, where Tavernkeeper Senna Brakk hears everything said over a drink; currently circulating is a half-serious wager over who'll be the next factor to die in the post, and a standing grudge between two logging crews over a poached claim near the toppled stone.
**Back Table:** in the corner farthest from the hearth, where the town's older families are known to meet quietly - asking too many questions here draws Senna's attention fast.
**Exits:** door, north wall -> A.2 Sootrow Market
