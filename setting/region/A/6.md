A.6 **Weldfast** - *rope, hulls, timber*
Two long open-sided sheds under one roof-line, where a **ropewalk** runs the full length of the building and a punt sits half-planked on **trestles** at the far end. Bog oak lengths are stacked to the purlins and the whole place smells of tar.
*About 200 by 30 feet, a single long rectangle open on its south side, with the ropewalk occupying the length of it and the boatyard the eastern third. Built as a ropewalk and extended for hulls when the causeway trade grew. The sound is the ropewalk's crank turning and the smell is pitch, hemp and cut oak.*
**Ropewalk:** A hemp walk running 180 feet down the shed's spine, with a crank frame at the west end and a travelling top at the east, laying three-strand line. This is where every fathom of rope in Brackvaen comes from and it is not a fast process. Tarred line runs 4 cn the fathom, plain 2 cn, and a full coil of 20 fathoms is 70 cn and weighs 3 slots. What Weldfast cannot supply is chain, wire, or any iron fitting whatever - those come up the causeway on the barges, the last barge was thin, and the boatwright will send a party to Druvmarn to wait for the next one.
**Trestles:** At the shed's eastern end, a flat-bottomed punt about 16 feet long sits half-planked on two oak trestles, and two more finished hulls lean against the wall behind it. A new punt is 300 cn and takes eleven days; a used one, of which there are usually two or three, runs 180 to 220 and will need caulking. Nothing else in the holding will get a party across Lindmarn with a load on board.
**Exits:** gravel path running north-east up the rise past the sheds' open side -> A.1 Thrapstad
