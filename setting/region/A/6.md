A.6 **The Old Graveyard** - *buried facing the treeline, wrong-way invitation, wall-bound dead*
Every marker in the **Old Graveyard** faces the treeline rather than the keep, by a custom nobody living can explain. **Ness** digs each new grave to match, and keeps his own private count of the ones that don't.
*An unwalled plot of turned earth and wooden markers roughly sixty yards past the gate, uneven ground pocked with older, sunken rows; quiet in a way the rest of Fellward never is, and cold underfoot even at midday.*
**Ness:** A conscript rotated onto grave duty longer than most rotations last, and the one who actually enforces the facing-custom, grave by grave. He'll dig - or re-dig - a grave facing whichever way is asked, for the right favor, though he'll want to know why first - the one thing had here and nowhere else at Fellward. He can't dig up an existing grave without cause the keep will back him on, and sends anyone insisting to take it up with whoever's actually in charge that week - which, this being Fellward, is nobody in particular.
**Burial Register:** A cracked ledger kept in Ness's tool shed records who's buried where and which way, in a hand that isn't his. It's missing its earliest pages entirely (Lore: The Burial Register) - Ness suspects whoever kept it before him took them, or that someone beyond the treeline wanted them, and won't say which he believes on a given day.
**Turned Row:** A short stretch of markers near the fence line faces the keep instead of the treeline - old work, done wrong or done on purpose, nobody now living remembers which. Ness won't go near it after dark and won't say why.
**Exits:** broken rail fence, low gap, south side -> the treeline and the open ground beyond
