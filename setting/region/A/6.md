A.6 **The Watch Cairn** - *lookout point, signal fire, storm warning*
A stone cairn atop the hold's highest point is ringed with **signal-fire** fuel and commands a clear view over the harbor and the hills beyond. The current watcher seems uneasy.
*A small open platform roughly seven yards across, roughly circular, reached by a steep switchback path; built and still used as the hold's lookout point. Wind is constant up here, and conversation has to be raised to be heard over it.*
**Signal Fire:** Lighting it summons aid or warns the hold of danger.
**Watcher:** Can be relieved for a shift, or pressed about what they heard - they swear a bell tolled from inland last night, not from the sea.
**Exits:** steep switchback path -> A.5 Harbor Docks, weathered stone road -> B.2 Shepherd's Cairn
