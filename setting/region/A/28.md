A.28 **Watch Log Archive** - *Planted Agent, Watch Log*
A small records room holds every patrol's own written report, kept by the commander and read, officially, by no one else.
*Fifteen feet of shelved ledgers behind a single locked door, the newest reports still damp with the ink that wrote them.*
**Watch Log:** The log records every patrol's route and report going back years, and one shelf of entries shows a hand that isn't the current clerk's making corrections nobody's asked about (Lore: Watch Log).
**Ear-Tally Ledger:** A second ledger catalogs which tribe's cut-ear pattern marks every raid reported, tallied against a chart the garrison won't admit is wrong as often as it's right.
**Second Key:** The archive's own lock has been rekeyed twice this year, and the reason given each time doesn't match what the watch log itself says happened either time.
**Exits:** stair door, west side -> A.21 Commander's Tower
