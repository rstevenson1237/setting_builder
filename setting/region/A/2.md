A.2 **The Granary Tower** - *Sealed, Old-Stone, Unspoiling*
The **Granary Tower**'s grain sacks are stacked to the rafters and untouched by weevil or rot, no matter how old. **Nesta** keeps the stores and a few pens of livestock below - though nothing born down there in years has hatched right.
*A round tower forty feet across, its interior stepped inward like a hollowed pyramid, utterly unlike the plain outer wall built around it later; cool, dry, and smelling of grain dust.*
**Nesta:** Keeps the grain stores and the garrison's ration chits; wants to know why nothing here breeds true.
**Grain Stores:** Sacks kept here never spoil - nobody who works here will explain why (Truths of Carrdun).
**Stepped Interior:** The tower's inner walls step inward in courses far finer-cut than Carrdun's own wall.
**Lower Pens:** Chickens and a milk-goat, penned here for the garrison; three broods hatched here in as many years came out wrong.
**Ration Chits:** Grain and hardtack are drawn here against a signed chit; fresh meat is the Market Row's business.
**Tally Shard:** A broken stone tablet under the lowest grain-bin, old-tongue rows of marks nobody at Carrdun can read (Lore: The Grain-Tower Tally).
**Exits:** iron-banded door, ground level facing the row -> A.3 The Market Row
