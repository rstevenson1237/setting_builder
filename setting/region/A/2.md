A.2 **Sootrow Market** - *muddy, bustling, provisioning*
Rows of open-air stalls and awnings fill Sootrow Market, thickest on market day when half the town turns out to trade. A weathered **Notice Board** nailed to the well-post carries the week's postings, jobs, and warnings alike.
*A roughly 50 by 50 yard open square, packed earth churned to mud in wet weather, ringed by permanent stalls and a central town well. Voices, haggling, and livestock noise dominate by day, and the smell of sawdust and cured hides drifts over from the streets nearby.*
**Vendor Row:** stalls lining the square's perimeter, selling trail goods, pelts, and tools; a patient buyer willing to dig through one trader's odds-and-ends bin turns up a genuinely worthwhile find (Treasure I, d20).
**Notice Board:** nailed to the well-post at the square's center - currently pinned with a Concern notice offering coin for reports of unblessed crossings, and a hand-scrawled note from a trapper seeking company back into the fringe.
**Town Well:** stone-lined, roughly 4 feet across, at the square's center beside the Notice Board - the town's main gathering point outside of market day itself.
**Exits:** packed dirt road, north side of the square -> A.1 The Vansker Stockade, narrow lane, east side of the square -> A.3 The Cutters' Hall, wide cart-track, west side of the square -> A.4 The Long Saw, muddy footpath, south side of the square -> A.6 The Groaning Auger
