A.2 **The Roofed House** (Ruin) - *dark, dry, sheltered*

A square room stands whole under its flat roof, sand drifted to the sills of its narrow windows. Inside the air is still and cool, and a **hearth** of fitted stone fills the north wall.

*One room about 20 feet square, the one roof in sight still standing. The party can make a camp here (Local Procedure: Making shelter and a place of respite). A merchant's house, its doors never shut behind its people.*

**The Door:** Cedar, hung on one hinge; spikes or a wedge will hold it shut.
**The Windows:** Two slits face the dunes, shuttered on the east side. A light inside shows through them.
**The Hearth:** A chimney opens to the sky, so a fire can burn without smoking out the room. Tomb robbers and anyone with eyes on the tip will see the smoke. Behind the hearth's back plate a seam in the stone runs to the floor; the plate can be pulled out to show a stair going down (A.9).
**The Floor:** A low bench and a clay oven lie under the dust, and a stack of broken pots in a corner.

**Exits:** a stair down behind the hearth, south-south-west, 30 yards -> A.9 The Sealed Cellar
