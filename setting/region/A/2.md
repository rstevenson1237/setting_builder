A.2 **West Gate** - *Old Road, Gate Drum*
The west gate opens onto the coast road, out of Telar entirely, and shuts to the **gate drum** at dusk. No toll is taken here, and papers are checked all the same.
*A plainer timber gate than the east, twenty feet across, with no toll-house beside it, and thinner, mostly local traffic. The drum tower above stands free of the wall, built for the one job.*
**Wall Watch:** A Garrison Soldier posted here checks papers, already stamped at the east gate, and issues none of his own.
**Gate Drum:** A cracked hide drum in the tower above, beaten at dawn to open the gate, at dusk to shut it.
**Coast Word:** Asked, the soldier passes on the day's word, off the coast road, weather, a wash-out, a raiding sighting.
**Exits:** open gate - plain timber, west wall -> A.6 Market Street
