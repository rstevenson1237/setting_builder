A.2 **The Roadend Inn** (Inn) - *common room, smoke, drovers*
One long, smoky room, with a **long hearth** down one side, **drovers' benches** crowded at the tables, and **ale casks** racked at the far end. A ladder climbs to a sleeping loft.
*Forty feet long and seating forty; the drovers fill it by dusk, and the dice run until the casks are shut.*
**Long Hearth:** A fire the length of a wagon, with a spit and a kettle; wet cloaks steam on a line above it.
**Drovers' Benches:** Drovers dice for copper at the benches nearest the fire, and make room for anyone who stakes.
**Ale Casks:** Four casks on a rack, the day's count chalked on each; the innkeeper draws every mug.
