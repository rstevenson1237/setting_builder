A.2 **The Midden** (low) - *bones, shells, draught*
A reeking slope of gnawed **bones** and river shells falls away into the dark. A cold draught rises from somewhere underfoot.
*A thirty-foot slope, once the diggers' spoil heap, now where the tribe dumps its scraps. The draught comes up through a crack at the heap's foot.*
**Bones:** Bones and shells shift underfoot, the draught whistles loudest at the foot of the heap, and digging there opens a crude crack down -> A.5 Old Shaft.
**Exits:** open slope, up -> A.1 The Smoke Hole
