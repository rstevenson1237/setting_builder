A.2 **The Quartermaster's Stores** - *forged requisition, tally-mark chalk, moved without questions*
Crates and barrels fill the **Quartermaster's Stores** floor to the rafters, each stamped with the same crossed tally-mark chalked onto everything the Ring's hands have touched. **Weyland Cross** keeps the books here, and keeps them exactly as tidy as he's told to.
*A converted warehouse roughly 50 by 25 feet, shelved along both long walls and stacked three crates deep down the center aisle.*
**Weyland Cross:** Requisitions tools or weapons properly asked for; never food.
**Marked Crates:** A few crates bear a scratched spiral - Diggers' relics.
**Ledger:** Naming a wrong date to Weyland reveals a hidden ledger (Lore: The Ring's True Ledger).
**Loading Bay:** Deep cart-ruts, used daily; empty barrels await an unscheduled delivery.
**Exits:** iron-bound double doors, loading bay, east wall -> the drill yard and the rest of the keep
