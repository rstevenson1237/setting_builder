A.2 **The Quartermaster's Stores** - *forged requisition, tally-mark chalk, moved without questions*
Crates and barrels fill the **Quartermaster's Stores** floor to the rafters, each stamped with the same crossed tally-mark chalked onto everything the Ring's hands have touched. **Weyland Cross** keeps the books here, and keeps them exactly as tidy as he's told to.
*A converted warehouse roughly 50 by 25 feet, shelved along both long walls and stacked three crates deep down the center aisle; smells of tar, oiled canvas, and the mildew working through the crates nearest the north wall.*
**Weyland Cross:** The Ring's clerk of record, and theirs in every way that matters though the garrison only suspects it. He'll requisition tools, rope, or a spare weapon for anyone who asks properly - the one thing had here and nowhere else at Fellward - but won't touch food; that's the Granary's business. Nothing he issues goes past what the Ring allows, and there's no one above him at Fellward left to appeal to when he says no.
**Marked Crates:** Every crate here carries the same crossed-tally chalk mark that turns up on sacks at the Granary and wherever else the Ring's hands have been. A handful near the door carry a second, unfamiliar mark instead - a spiral, scratched rather than chalked - on relics the Diggers move through here for a cut Weyland doesn't report upward.
**Ledger:** The official requisition ledger sits open on a standing desk, entries neat and current. Counting what's actually on the shelves against what it lists comes up wrong on at least four lines. Naming one of the four to Weyland by date gets him to glance, just once, at a loose flagstone behind the desk - lift it and a second ledger turns up, in his own hand, admitting a truer count than he's ever shown anyone (Lore: The Ring's True Ledger). He notices it's missing within the day, and the Ring hears of it before he does.
**Loading Bay:** Cart-ruts worn deep into the floor from the east doors, still used almost daily despite how little arrives by cart anymore; a stack of empty barrels waits by the wall for a delivery nobody's scheduled.
**Exits:** iron-bound double doors, loading bay, east wall -> the drill yard and the rest of the keep
