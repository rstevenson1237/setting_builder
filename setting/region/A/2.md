A.2 **The Lagoon Beach** (Gate) - *sandy, crowded, drummed*
A curved sand beach crowds with drawn-up canoes, and a **signal drum** stands on a platform above them while the party's **ship's boats** rest on the sand with their master beside them.
*40 by 80 feet, below Puluni's stockade. This is where strangers land from the sea and the party's ship lies at anchor; landing is free to those who trade, and a beach watch of three tribesmen and a drum keeps it.*
**The beach watch:** three tribesmen with white clay stripes on the brow and cheeks, hailing every boat; they take nothing for landing and ask what a party carries (Neutral, 3, Bestiary : Tribesman).
**Tuhaki:** the beach-watch drummer, young and boastful, beats the drum for any strange boat. Tuhaki wants tales of the ship's home country, and knows which boats crossed the lagoon last night.
**Dagget Orme:** the master of the party's ship, blunt and impatient, wants every soul aboard on the sailing day set at landing. Orme can say what the wreck coast looks like from the sea.
**The ship's boats:** the party's own landing craft, drawn up on the sand above the tide line.
