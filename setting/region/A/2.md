A.2 **Gate Tower North** - *Bell, Short-Handed, Sharp Stone*
A stair of new-cut stone climbs to a narrow platform where a **Watch Bell** hangs ready to mark every change of the guard.
*Twenty feet up a spiral stair barely wide enough for one in mail, the stonework still sharp-edged and unweathered next to anything older nearby.*
**Watch Bell:** A bronze bell hung from an iron bracket is struck at every change of watch, on a schedule the roster can't always keep to strength.
**Exits:** stair door, south wall -> A.1 Outer Gate, wall-walk, east side -> A.15 Outer Wall Walk
