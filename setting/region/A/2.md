A.2 **The Drover's Rest** (Inn) - *smoky, crowded, loose-lipped*
A low timber inn under a cartwheel **sign** lies off the yard, full of drovers and off-duty Soldiers. A fire burns in the **taproom**, and a ladder climbs to the **loft rooms**.
*Taproom 30 ft by 20 ft, kitchen behind, six loft rooms. Forty drinkers on a wagon-day evening, a dozen otherwise.*
**Taproom:** Edda Marsh pulls ale at the bar, shrewd and talkative, and wants coin before she gives a bed. Ale is 1 cp a mug and a meal 5 cp. She repeats whatever travellers have said to whoever asks next.
**Carters' Bench:** Two carters from the second lost wagon drink on credit by the fire. They saw goblins break from the scree at the ravine's mouth and run up the stream path. Their wagon carried grain and salt; the garrison's pay was in the first.
**Loft Rooms:** Six rooms under the roof at 10 cp a night, each with a straw bed and a bar for the door. The window of the end room looks down on the gate.
**Sign:** A cartwheel painted red, hung from an iron bracket over the door.
