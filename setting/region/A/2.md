A.2 **The Postern** (Gate) - *narrow, low, quiet*
A narrow iron **door** in the north wall opens on a short passage, with a **spy-hole** at eye height and two heavy **bars** across it. A warden's bench stands against the wall.
*Passage 8 ft long and 4 ft wide, the door opening outward onto the wood. One warden by day, two by night.*
**Door:** Opens outward on a key the warden wears on his belt.
**Spy-Hole:** Shows the first yards of the forest trail, climbing away north under the pines.
**Bars:** Each as thick as a man's arm; the warden lifts them alone.
**Corporal Dag Venn:** Dozes on his bench until a step sounds, then asks for a pass without opening his eyes. Pressed, he mutters of who left by the postern after dark, though never by name.
**Exits:** barred, north end of the passage -> C.1 The Forest Trail
