A.2 **The Salvage Market** - *wreck-goods, barter stalls, rumor trade*
**Plank stalls** line the harbor walkway, hung with drying nets and salvaged fittings, loud with haggling over the day's finds. Traders here will buy, sell, or trade nearly anything pulled from a wreck.
*A dozen open-air stalls stretching the length of the walkway; gulls and haggling voices make this the loudest place in Saltmere, and news travels through it fast.*
**Stalls:** Buy or sell salvage and ordinary goods at market rates.
**Trader's Private Stock:** Paying extra to see it sometimes turns up an intact widow-glass bottle.
**Chatter:** Lingering here is the easiest way to overhear a rumour (see setting/Rumours.md).
**Exits:** walkway -> A.1 Bell Hall, walkway -> A.4 Smokehouse, walkway -> A.5 Harbor Docks
