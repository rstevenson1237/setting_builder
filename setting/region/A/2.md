A.2 **Rendfast** - *settlement, boundary, authority*
A long hall of black bog oak on the east side of the rise, its **door-beam** carved with the three-notch device and its shutters open in all weathers because the room is always full. Inside, clerks work at a **standing desk** the length of the north wall, and the **lead press** at the far end stamps the seals that decide which pans belong to whom.
*About 70 by 30 feet, a single aisled hall with a partitioned back room, built as a barn a hundred years ago and converted when the settlement was written. It is the Salt Assize's hall and the nearest thing Torsgaard has to a government. It smells of ink, wet wool and lead, and the room is loud - a dozen conversations and the press coming down every minute or so.*
**Standing Desk:** Running the length of the north wall, thirty feet of scarred oak at chest height, with six clerks working it and the holding's current business spread across it in loose boards and papers. Anyone may approach it. What a stranger must do to get a hearing is wait until a clerk is free and state a matter that concerns salt, ground, or debt - the Assize will settle nothing else, and says so without embarrassment.
**Lead Press:** A screw press bolted to a stone block in the hall's east corner, four feet high, with the three-notch die fitted in its head and a box of blanks beside it. It stamps the seals nailed to measured pans. The die is never left in the press overnight and the storeman who carries it away does so openly, which tells a party exactly how much a blank seal is worth to somebody.
**Boundary Chart:** Pinned to the west wall on four battens, a chart of the eastern pans some eight feet across, inked and re-inked over sixty years, showing eleven numbered stones and every pan they divide. The eleventh stone sits well outside the worked ground, in open water, and no pan is drawn against it. A clerk asked about this will say it marks the settlement's edge; Gellen Torsling, if the party has any standing at all, will say that is what he was told too (Named Creature: Gellen Torsling) (Quest: Count the Stones).
**Back Room:** Behind a plank partition at the hall's east end, a room perhaps 12 feet square holding the Assize's strongbox, the master weight, and a lead case on the top shelf. The case holds the full text of the settlement on oak boards - the only complete copy - and the two heads who have read it all the way through do not discuss the closing clauses (Lore: The Settlement of the Salt Assize). The partition door has a lock better than the hall's front door deserves, which is the sort of thing a party notices.
**Notice Post:** A squared post beside the door outside, the wood furred with old nail holes. Posted now: a summons for two crews to answer why they worked pans the Assize has established are not theirs; a standing offer of 40 cn for information on salt sold outside the weigh-house; and a death, nailed up eleven days ago, for a pan-hand called Sallow whose kit has not been claimed.
