A.2 **West Gate** - *Old Road, Gate Drum*
The west gate opens onto the coast road, out of Telar entirely, and closes to the same drummed signal as the east - a soldier on the tower above beats it at dawn and dusk without exception. No toll is taken here; papers are checked all the same.
*A plainer timber gate than the east, twenty feet across, unpaired with any toll-house - traffic this way is thinner and mostly local. The drum tower above is shared structurally with nothing else, built for the one job.*
**Wall Watch:** A Garrison Soldier posted here knows every regular face on the coast road and checks papers already stamped at the east gate rather than issuing new ones.
**Gate Drum:** A cracked hide drum in the tower above, beaten at dawn to open the gate and at dusk to shut it; nothing short of a company at the wall changes the timing.
**Coast Word:** The soldier on duty will pass on the last word from anyone who came down the coast road that day - weather, a wash-out, a raiding sighting - free, for the asking.
**Exits:** open gate - plain timber, west wall -> A.6 Market Street
