A.15 **Outer Wall Walk** - *Treeline, Challenge, Parapet*
A walkway of the same grey stone runs between the two outer towers, wide enough for two abreast and watched at all hours.
*A stretch of parapet forty feet long, waist-high on the outward face and open on the yard side.*
**Watch Rota:** Whoever's posted here watches the road and the treeline both, and calls a challenge the moment anyone's close enough to hear it answered.
**Exits:** stair door, west end -> A.2 Gate Tower North, stair door, east end -> A.16 Gate Watch Post
