A.14 **Toll House** - *Weighing, Missing Pages, Old Coin*
A narrow booth beside Trade Row holds the **Toll Ledger** that prices everything carried out of the ravine, weighed against what it's worth at the gate rather than what it cost to get.
*Ten feet of counter and a locked strongbox behind it, the ledger chained to the counter itself.*
**Toll Clerk:** The clerk weighs and prices whatever's carried out of the ravine against the ledger's own figures, and takes old-tongue coin at only a fraction of a common coin's weight.
**Toll Ledger:** The ledger's own tallies run back years further than the clerk currently posted has held the job, and pages from its middle years are missing rather than simply worn through (Lore: Toll Ledger).
**Exits:** counter window, east side -> A.4 Trade Row
