A.20 **Second Gate** - *Frontier Levy, Second Gate*
A barred gate manned day and night is the only crossing into the inner bailey, and nobody passes it without an escort or a name the garrison's already vouched for.
*A single iron-barred gateway ten feet wide, set into a stretch of wall thicker than anywhere else in the outer bailey.*
**Gate Watch:** Two Regulars posted here turn back anyone without an escort or a standing name on the inner roster, whatever excuse is offered (Disciplined, 2, Bestiary : Greywatch Regular).
**Exits:** open yard, south side -> A.18 Well Square, barred passage, north side -> A.21 Commander's Tower, side door, northeast -> A.22 Granary, side door, east -> A.23 Armoury, open yard, north side -> A.25 Garrison Barracks, stair door, northwest -> A.27 Inner Wall Walk, side path, west -> A.29 Inner Cistern
