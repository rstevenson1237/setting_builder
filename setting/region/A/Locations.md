Locations of A Fellward

A.1 The Granary - *rationed grain, ledger vs. count, ashmoth-infested thatch*
A.2 The Quartermaster's Stores - *forged requisition, tally-mark chalk, moved without questions*
A.3 The Barracks Block - *half stripped for timber, roster two names short, built for thrice the garrison*
A.4 The Forge - *unsharpened blades, worked in turns, no smith of standing*
A.5 The Kitchen Garden - *rooted up nightly, bramble-back wallow, standing grievance*
A.6 The Old Graveyard - *buried facing the treeline, wrong-way invitation, wall-bound dead*
