A.16 **Gate Watch Post** - *Reshuffled, Struck Names, Bench*
A **Roster Board** posted here each morning names whoever's on duty, names struck off the moment they're not there to answer it.
*A covered stretch of parapet with a bench and a lean-board for the roster, the names rewritten more often than the board's edges suggest.*
**Roster Board:** The roster's been rewritten three times this week alone, routes reshuffled rather than held to any fixed schedule on the chance that alone makes the next warning harder to carry.
