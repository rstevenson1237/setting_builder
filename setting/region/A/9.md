A.9 **The Coin-Changers' Hut** (Bank) - *shuttered, small, careful*
A shuttered hut holds a table with **scales** and a **strongbox** chained to a post, the **coin-changer** silent behind them.
*20 by 20 feet. The changer weighs honestly and pays only in cn.*
**Lokatiwa:** the coin-changer, exact and wary, wants old coin weighed honestly and the fee paid, and knows whose coin is stolen and the chiefs' debts. Old and foreign coin is changed to cn for 3% of the sum, and goods taken to resale (fish, hides, tools) fetch 80%, a fee of 20% of the sum.
**The scales:** brass pans on a bronze beam, with pebbles for weights.
**The strongbox:** an iron-banded chest chained to a post set in the floor.
