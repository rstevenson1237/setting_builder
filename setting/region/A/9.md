A.9 **Chapel of Law** - *Spoken Side, Law's Register*
Incense and old wax hang thick past the chapel door, where **Priest Ordell** keeps Law's own rites and hears out anyone who greets him properly before asking for anything else.
*Thirty feet of narrow nave and a plain stone altar, the only room past the outer gate where Law's rites are spoken rather than just enforced, quiet enough to hear a page turn.*
**Ordell's Hearing:** Ordell hears anyone out who greets him in Law's own courteous register first, and turns away without a word anyone who doesn't know to.
**Law's Register:** A written register behind the altar logs every rite performed and every ward laid, kept separate from the garrison's own rolls and readable to anyone Ordell vouches for (Lore: Law's Register).
**Retained Warding Duty:** Ordell keeps a standing retainer from the garrison to put down anything bound that won't stay where it's set, paid whether or not he's called on in a given month.
**Uncomfortable Admission:** Ordell admits plainly that no rite of Law he knows touches a ward laid in Grozzgur, and the garrison keeps coin aside for whoever can speak that tongue instead.
**Exits:** chapel door, east wall -> A.18 Well Square
