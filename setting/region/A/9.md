A.9 **The Ancestor House** (Temple) - *smoky, carved, hushed*
A smoke-dark hut of carved **ancestor posts** keeps a low fire, a **skull shelf** above it and the **keeper** chanting softly beside the embers.
*30 by 20 feet. Food is set before the posts at dusk; a stranger enters only when the keeper calls them in.*
**Nguhahuna:** the keeper, hushed and old, wants the dead fed at dusk and strangers kept out, and knows which villagers no longer feed the dead. For 5 cn the dead are asked to watch a party's journey.
**The ancestor posts:** carved figures of the founders, smoke-blackened, their mouths smeared with the day's food.
**The skull shelf:** the old chiefs' skulls in a row above the fire pit, each in a collar of shells.
