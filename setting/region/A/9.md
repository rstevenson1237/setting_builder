A.9 **The Chapel of Law** (Authority) - *cold, candlelit, hushed*
A plain stone nave with an **altar** at its end and a lamp hanging above it. A **sacristy** door stands beside the altar.
*Nave 40 ft by 20 ft. The priest, a sexton and an acolyte keep it; it is open from dawn to dusk.*
**Altar:** A tower, the old mark of Law, is cut into its face; the bowl before it holds the poor's coins.
**Mother Hesper Lund:** Meets a party at the porch and asks, low, whether it has seen a silver reliquary. She can say that Brother Anselm held the sacristy keys, and little else (Quest: The Stolen Reliquary).
**Wenna Pryce:** Hurries to bind any wounded who come in and asks each what the ravine did to them. She clears Bloodied, Exhausted or Poisoned for 15 cn a Condition, and Diseased or a Madness for 100 cn a rite.
**Old Gil:** Rings the bell at dusk and mutters of those who kneel here at odd hours.
**Sacristy:** Locked, the key on Mother Hesper's belt; she will open it for a party that offers to look, and the reliquary's niche inside is empty.
