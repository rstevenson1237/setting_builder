A.9 **Vethgaard** - *debt, seclusion, inheritance*
A tall narrow house at the end of a walled lane, shuttered on every window, with a **speaking hatch** cut into the door at chest height and a **delivery shelf** below it worn smooth as soap. The woman inside holds the salt-debt on something near half the holding and has not been seen outside this door in eleven years.
*About 20 by 35 feet over two storeys, a narrow rectangle wedged between two older houses, built as an ordinary household and now used as an office nobody enters. It is the quietest place in Torsgaard - no hearth smoke, no voices - and what a party notices standing at the door is the smell of brine coming out of the house, which is stronger inside than it is on the pans.*
**Speaking Hatch:** A hinged shutter about eight inches square set into the door at chest height, opened from within. Vetha Naugling conducts all her business through it: debts taken up, weights recorded, boards issued and redeemed. Her voice is unremarkable. She never opens the hatch fully, and a caller sees a hand at most - the hand is bound in oiled leather to past the wrist, which everybody in Torsgaard has seen and nobody has thought about, because half the pan crews wrap their hands (Named Creature: Vetha Naugling).
**Delivery Shelf:** A plank shelf below the hatch, worn concave by sixty years of boards being pushed across it, with a lidded box beneath for anything left when she does not answer. **Clue**: the shelf is worn deepest on the outer edge and barely at all on the inner - things are put down here and drawn in, but nothing is ever set down from inside; and the house takes in no firewood, no water, and no food, in eleven years. **Trigger**: leaving a tally board on the shelf and waiting out of sight rather than leaving the lane, or asking her directly whether a debt can attach to her. **Payload**: she is Brined to the shoulder and has been since before she inherited. A debt cannot attach to her and she cannot inherit - so the boards she holds were never hers to hold, and something near half the obligation in Torsgaard is unenforceable and has been for eleven years. **Consequence**: she knows the moment the party knows, because she has been waiting eleven years for somebody to do the arithmetic. Within a week she will offer them the whole of it - the boards, the house, the standing - to be the visible holder, and she will be entirely reasonable about what happens if they decline and talk.
**Fourth Board Rack:** Visible through the hatch when it is open, a rack of tally boards on the wall behind her, four tiers deep. The fourth tier is boards she has taken up from the dead rather than issued to the living, and it is the longest of the four (Lore: Vethgaard Tally, Fourth Board).
**Exits:** plank door, iron-strapped, at the head of a walled lane running west, no through way -> A.1 Thrapstad
