A.9 **The Sealed Cellar** (Hidden) - *cool, shut, dry*

A flight of steps leads under a lifted flagstone to a low vault, cool and silent, shelves running along both walls. The air has no wind in it.

*A vault 30 feet long and 12 wide under A.2, reached only by the stair behind the hearth. It holds a merchant's store, mostly spoiled, and a nest of centipedes, untouched since the city emptied. (The Living Go Down to Dream)*

**The Stair:** Twelve worn steps, smooth in the middle, down from A.2.
**The Shelves:** Rotted baskets, sealed jars, bronze scales and lengths of rope; a few jars are still sealed and hold oil and dried figs.
**The Ceiling:** Two giant centipedes hang from the vault, brown against brown stone (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Giant Centipede); they drop on the first light that crosses the shelves.

**Exits:** a stair up behind the hearth, north-north-east, 30 yards -> A.2 The Roofed House
