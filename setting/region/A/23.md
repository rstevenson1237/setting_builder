A.23 **Armoury** - *New Keep, Inner Bailey*
Racked spears and stacked shields fill the armoury in neat rows, issued against a name on the roster and returned the same way.
*Twenty feet of raised racking along both walls, the iron kept oiled against the damp that gets into everything else in the inner bailey.*
**Weapons Rack:** The quartermaster's own second issues arms against a name on the roster, and won't hand anything out that isn't logged back in by the same watch's end.
**Exits:** side door, east side -> A.20 Second Gate
