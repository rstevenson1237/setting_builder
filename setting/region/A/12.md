A.12 **Riverside Well** - *Bare Purse, Riverside Well*
The keep's one working well stands at the yard's low point, its **rope** and bucket worn smooth by hands with little else to spare.
*A stone-ringed shaft ten feet across, the water rationed the moment the grain count comes up short.*
**Well Rope:** Anyone may draw water here for free, though the rope's kept short enough that filling more than a single skin draws a look from whoever's waiting behind them.
**Exits:** open yard, south side -> A.18 Well Square
