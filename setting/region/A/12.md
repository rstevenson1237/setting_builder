A.12 **The Cellar Under the Wheel** (Secret) - *damp, black-candled, hidden*
Black candle stubs crowd an old **cask** at the centre of a low vault, with a bare **table** beside it and rows of old barrels against the walls. A **ladder** climbs to the hatch above.
*Vault 30 ft by 15 ft, ceiling 6 ft. Brother Anselm, the chapel's sacristan, is an agent of the Black Altar and is here by night.*
**Cask:** Candle stubs burned to wax pools on its head, and a triangle point-down cut into the wood.
**Table:** Bare but for wax drippings; a ledger page torn from a clerk's book lies under it.
**Brother Anselm:** Starts at every sound on the ladder and begs a party to say nothing of the cellar. Pressed, he admits there is a second who meets him here.
**Ladder:** Climbs to the hatch under the tavern's third barrel.
**Exits:** hidden, north, up a ladder -> A.4 The Broken Wheel
