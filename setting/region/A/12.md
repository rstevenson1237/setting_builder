A.12 **The Egg-Stone Niche** (Secret) - *cool, dark, guarded*
A stone cell behind a hearth holds a **niche** with a round grey stone, warm to the touch, and an old **keeper** sitting beside it.
*15 by 15 feet. The way in is behind the Chiefs' House hearth; the chiefs alone know it.*
**Kewikali:** the keeper, old and patient, sings low beside the niche, wants the stone left alone and warm, and knows it warms when lizard men come.
**The egg-stone:** a round grey stone the size of a man's head in a niche cut in a snake emperor's altar, warm to the touch; the lizard men's war is for it, and the tribes took it from its altar in the rising.
**The way out:** a hearthstone that lifts from below leads back to the Chiefs' House.
**Exits:** a hearthstone that lifts from below -> A.10 The Chiefs' House
