F Kobold Warren

Overview: The Gubak's warren of dug passages on the ravine's west wall, nearest the mouth. A party comes for the tribute hoard and to get past the first door. The Gubak are behind on their tribute to the Zhakuk. If it is not paid, the Zhakuk come to the warren in force; if the Gubak are broken, the Zhakuk take the first mouth on the wall.

Approach: One mouth, a crawlhole at the back of the kobold ledge (E.2), grinning faces chalked over it. Two Kobolds watch it by day and one by night, and rattles hang across the first passage.

Conditions: Dug earth and soft rock, passages 4 feet high and 3 wide, so a man goes crouched and a weapon longer than a spear cannot swing. No light but tallow lamps and the party's own. Sound carries straight along passages and dies at bends. Footing is loose, damp earth over pits. The air is close; a lit fire fills the passages with smoke that drives the Gubak deep.

Entrances:
- F.1 - Runs west into the earth behind the crawlhole, dripping from the roof, tin rattles hung along it and tripwires at knee height round two bends.

Prizes:
- F.5 - Through a wooden door beyond the burrows: a bed of stolen cloth heaped with bones, wires across the roof, and an iron-banded chest at the bed's foot with a wire from its hasp.

Places:
- F.2 - A narrow run of loose earth, boards laid over the floor in patches, some patched with mud.
- F.3 - Five alcoves of straw and rags, a grin chalked over each, a tally post scratched with days, and doors south and west, a wire hooked at the west door's jamb.
- F.4 - A dead end through the burrows' south door, crates stacked to the roof and shelves cut in the earth.

Tables:
