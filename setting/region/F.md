F Zamira

Overview: The fourth level's living quarters, cells and kitchens, left as their cynidicean owners left them, with lairs of stronger monsters that came down from the sand and the tomb robbers' deepest camp (F.10). A party comes for the quarters' stores and the lairs' hoards. Robbing the camp sends it hunting the thief through the quarters; following the captain's trail brings out the monsters nearest the stair.

Approach: The stair from the three halls (E.9) lands at F.1, where two Kurumisa soldiers stop all who go down; a party must name its business or find the stair shut behind it.

Conditions: Plastered sandstone, 12 feet high and 6 wide; torch only. Cloth hangings muffle sound; dust lies over worn tile; the air is stale and faintly rotten. Hangings burn and smoke fills the quarters.

Entrances:

Prizes:
- F.7 - A hall hung with web; giant spiders in the web and a bundle of a dead delver's gear (Treasure II, d20).
- F.9 - A crypt of heaped bones; ghouls feed on the dead and a hoard lies beneath the dais (Hoard: The Ghoul's Hoard).
- F.10 - Lamps and barricades; eight delvers and a strongbox (Treasure IV, d20).

Places:
- F.1 - A dusty landing; two soldiers of Kurumisa at the head of the stair.
- F.2 - A low corridor hung with cobweb; every door open.
- F.3 - Sooty ovens and rotten stores; an oven flue that fires back.
- F.4 - Eight tiny cells of straw and clay lamps.
- F.5 - Three plastered rooms; stirges in the hangings and a chest with a scroll (Lore: The Noble's Will).
- F.6 - Stone bins under a vault; a cracked floor in the middle.
- F.8 - A hot sand burrow; giant scorpions buried and a split jar of coin.
- F.11 - A barred cell, bars cut through; zombies in soldiers' scale and a note (Lore: The Captain's Last Orders).
- F.12 - A barred guard room; four soldiers of Kurumisa hold the inner door.
- F.13 - A long gallery of steps going down, wind from below.

Tables:
d6 Danger
1. Cloth rustling in the corridor.
2. A sour smell from the kitchens.
3. A thin beating of wings among the hangings.
4. Dice and a fiddle through a barricade.
5. Gnawing and low muttering from the east.
6. The camp's sentries rise to hunt the thief.
