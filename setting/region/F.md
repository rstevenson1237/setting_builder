F Lemuni

Overview: Two villages of stilt-houses and terraced gardens at a river's mouth on the north shore, a hunters' camp up the river, and fish drying on racks along the beach. A party comes down from the hills or by boat for fish, guides and word of the hills. The hunters report a northern party lost in the hill jungle, and the council wants proof of what is taking its people before it sends a war party. If a southern party asks for a guide the price is a third higher and a southern token is refused; when the council sits, a bound man argues against a war party until proof comes.

View: From the river's mouth, 200 yards off, two villages of stilt-houses stand on either bank with a hunters' camp on a bluff up the river: drying racks, nets hung on poles, hunters' dogs, and the hills dark behind the river's bend.

Approach: The river trail, from the hill jungle, has a hunters' camp at the last bend, two bowmen and a dog; it takes a day's walk under their eye, and a party turned back is followed to the hills' edge. The shore track, from the northern headlands, has fish racks and a boat watch at each end; a party turned back is not followed. The river mouth beach, from the sea, has a boat watch of two fishers and a horn; a boat that dries its nets here is trading.

Law: Bringing a southern token into a house withdraws the house's hospitality, and word goes round (the village elders). Taking a guide's pay and not going is exile from both villages and the pay repaid threefold (the hunters' leader).

Arrival:
- F.1 - Hill trail's end; two hunters and a dog watch every stranger, and the trail leads up the river toward the lost hunters' camp D.10.
- F.2 - Track end above the headland; pterodactyl cries carry from the cliffs, and the track leads on to the treasure hunters' landing country at G.1.
- F.3 - Shingle beach with a boat watch and a horn blown for any strange sail; nothing is asked for landing.

Plan: F.9, the council house, stands on the nearer bank at the centre. A quay runs from the beach F.3 past the fish market F.5 and F.9 to the river trail's head F.1; a lane runs west to the guest house F.4, the net-makers F.6 and the headland track F.2; a lane runs to the boat shed F.8; a path climbs the bluff to the hunters' camp F.7.

Places:
- F.4 - Beds, meals and talk of the hills; a southern token brought in withdraws hospitality.
- F.5 - Fresh fish, rations and waterskins; the warden notes any southerner's token.
- F.6 - Rope, packs and cast nets; the net-maker wants iron needles.
- F.7 - Hill guides at a southerner's surcharge, bows and arrows; the hunters' leader wants proof of the lost party (Quest: The Lost Hunters).
- F.8 - Canoes, paddlers and a fishing boat with two fishers for the coast.
- F.9 - The council sits behind a closed door; its seal on a guide's hire costs 30 cn, and a speaker keeps the shirt laced to the throat.
- F.10 - A scale carved and inked green on a post marks a bound men's cellar behind the nets.
