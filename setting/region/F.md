F Kobold Warren

Kind: single holding
Overview: The Gubak's warren on the west wall, nearest the ravine's mouth: Kobolds in dug passages. Its chief is a Named Creature. A party comes for the tribute hoard and to get past the first door. The Gubak are behind on tribute. If it is not paid, the Zhakuk come to the warren in force. If the Gubak are broken, the Zhakuk take the first mouth on the wall.
Approach: One mouth opens on the ravine's west wall; two Kobolds watch it by day and one by night, and rattles hang across the first passage.
Conditions: Dug earth and soft rock, passages 4 feet high and 3 wide, so a man goes crouched and a weapon longer than a spear cannot swing. No light but tallow lamps and the party's own. Sound carries straight along passages and dies at bends. Footing is loose, damp earth over pits. The air is close, and a lit fire fills the passages with smoke that drives the Gubak into their deepest rooms.
