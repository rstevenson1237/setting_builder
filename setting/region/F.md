F Pyramid Level 4 - The Living Quarters

Overview: The fourth level's living quarters, cells and kitchens, left as their cynidicean owners left them, with lairs of stronger monsters that came down from the sand and the tomb robbers' deepest camp (F.10). A party comes for the quarters' stores and the lairs' hoards. The ghouls of the old family crypt (F.9) have begun to hunt the quarters, and the Royal Guard captain who went after them has not come back. Robbing the camp sends it hunting the thief through the quarters; following the captain's trail brings the ghouls out of the crypt to meet whoever follows it.

Approach: The stair from the three halls (E.9) lands at F.1, where two Royal Guard soldiers (Faction: Kurumisa) stop all who go on; a party must name its business or be walked back up the stair.

Prizes:
- F.7 - A hall hung with web; giant spiders in the web and a bundle of a dead delver's gear (Treasure II, d20).
- F.9 - A crypt of heaped bones; ghouls feed on the dead and a hoard lies beneath the dais (Hoard: The Ghoul's Hoard).
- F.10 - Lamps and barricades; eight delvers, a masked singer from the lake city, and a strongbox (Treasure IV, d20).
- F.19 - A reeking heap of bones in an old guard room; hyenas from the sand and their torn packs (Treasure II, d20).

Places:
- F.1 - A dusty landing; two Royal Guard soldiers at the foot of the stair.
- F.2 - A low corridor hung with cobweb; every door open.
- F.3 - Sooty ovens and rotten stores; an oven flue that fires back.
- F.4 - Eight tiny cells of straw and clay lamps.
- F.5 - Three plastered rooms; stirges in the hangings and a chest with a scroll (Lore: The Noble's Will).
- F.6 - Stone bins under a vault; a cracked floor in the middle.
- F.8 - A hot sand burrow; giant scorpions buried and a split jar of coin.
- F.11 - A barred cell, bars cut through; zombies in soldiers' scale, one in the captain's plumed helm, and a note (Lore: The Captain's Last Orders).
- F.12 - A barred guard room; four soldiers of Kurumisa hold the inner door.
- F.13 - A long gallery of steps going down, wind from below.
- F.14 - A dry sunken bath, scale-crusted vents and a bronze valve; a hiss behind the walls.
- F.15 - Six small beds round a painted toy box (Treasure I, d20).
- F.16 - Stone troughs and slumped sacks; white dust that stings the eyes.
- F.17 - Rows of bronze chimes over a bench; a hum just under hearing.
- F.18 - A low winding passage, a cracked flag at its mouth, claw marks over children's scratches.
- F.20 - Racks of broken amphorae; giant rats and one sealed jar (Treasure I, d20).
