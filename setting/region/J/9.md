J.9 **The Bodyguards' Hall** (High) - *armed, black, watchful*

Racks of black spears stand round a sanded floor, spear butts knocking on stone. A black hand is painted over each rack.

*A hall 40 feet square where the lieutenants' sworn guard spar and sleep. They answer any alarm on the level first.*

**The Bodyguards:** Spar on the sand in the cult's black, bronze masks pushed up (Hostile, 4-6, Bestiary : Cultist).
**The Racks:** Black spears and shields marked with a hand (Faction : Voxkroth).
**The Pay Chest:** Under the last rack (Treasure III, d20).

**Exits:** a stone door -> J.5 The Council Table, a stone door to the drum -> J.4 The Third Lieutenant's Hall
