J.1 **The Collar Mouth** (Medium) - *foul, loud, lounging*
Iron **collars** hang from hooks round the edge of a wide cave mouth, a strip of spotted **hide** over its lintel. **Gnolls** lounge on the ledge outside, laughing.
*Mouth 40 ft wide and 30 ft deep. Howling and laughing hide small noises; the ledge outside is the gnolls' own.*
**Collars:** Iron, rusted, each too small for a gnoll's neck and hung in a row.
**Hide:** Spotted, nailed over the lintel.
**Gnolls:** Three on the ledge, who laugh, circle and hamstring the slow (Hostile, 3, Bestiary : Gnoll). They know when the buyer is due.
**Knucklebones:** Gnawed, scattered across the floor.
**Exits:** open, west, 20 feet -> E.6 The Gnoll Ledge, crude, north side of the mouth, 50 feet -> J.2 The Slave Pens, crude, east end of the mouth, 90 feet -> J.3 The Den Floor
