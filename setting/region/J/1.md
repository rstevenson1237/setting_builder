J.1 **The Stair Landing** (Low) - *smoke-stained, narrow, quiet*

A narrow smoke-stained landing, a gargoyle on each side of the door. Incense and a far chant drift through.

*A landing 10 feet by 20 feet. The gargoyles flank the arch to the first hall; they still for the lieutenants' word, *vox*, and strike anyone without it. Parting the hanging over the servants' door sets off darts from its jamb (Test of Constitution, 1d, Piercing).*

**The Gargoyles:** Carved on each side of the door, flanking the way in (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Gargoyle). Stone grates when they stir.
**The Hand:** Painted black over the arch, the cult's claim (Faction : Voxkroth).

**Exits:** an archway -> J.2 The First Lieutenant's Hall, a wooden door behind a hanging -> J.8 The Servants' Passage, up a stair -> I.9 The Chosen's Gate
