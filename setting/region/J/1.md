J.1 **The Sinkhole Floor** (Low) - *rooted, littered, dim*
A root-hung floor of cave and clay lies littered with bones and dung under a dim shaft of light, drips and a scratching in the dark, cave mouths opening off it.
*30 by 30 feet, a cave. The sinkhole's foot, fifty feet below the jungle; the roots and a rope reach the rim.*
**The roots:** a curtain of tendrils hanging down the shaft, the way up.
**The bone litter:** gnawed bones and dung in a drift across the clay, with cave men's teeth among them.
**Exits:** a low cave mouth hung with rope -> J.2 The Rope Cave, a narrow rank mouth -> J.3 The Guard Gallery, a long natural passage -> J.4 The Warren Hall, up the roots -> D.6 The Sinkhole
