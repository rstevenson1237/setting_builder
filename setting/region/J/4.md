J.4 **The Warren Hall** (High) - *broad, fouled, crowded*
A broad fouled cavern crowds with hide beds, grunting and gnawing, cook pits smoking and side tunnels opening off it.
*50 by 40 feet, a cave. The band eats and sleeps here. Fire smokes toward the sinkhole.*
**The troglodytes:** three feeding and gnawing round the cook pits (Hostile, 3, Bestiary : Troglodyte); they answer an alarm in one turn.
**The cook pits:** stone-lined hollows of ash and gnawed bone; lighting a flame in the foul air here sickens (Test of Constitution, 1d, Suffocation).
**The cook-pit stores:** meat and tools laid by the pits (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** a long natural passage -> J.1 The Sinkhole Floor, a cave mouth -> J.5 The Captives' Pit, a cave mouth -> J.6 The Bone Midden, a cave mouth -> J.7 The Chief's Cave
