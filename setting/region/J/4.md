J.4 **The Bone Heap** (Low) - *piled, splintered, sealed*
A **heap** of splintered bones and broken stone fills the end of a cave to the roof. A thighbone lies on top, cut with notches.
*Cave 30 ft by 20 ft. The makers' passage once ran on south-west; the gnolls filled it and no longer look at it.*
**Heap:** Bones and stone packed to the roof; a cold draught blows through it, and pulling it apart opens the passage beyond, south-west to the hobgoblins' barracks (I.3 The Barracks).
**Tally Bone:** A thighbone on the heap's crest, cut with a count of sales (Lore: The Buyer's Tally).
**Exits:** crude, north end of the cave, 80 feet -> J.3 The Den Floor
