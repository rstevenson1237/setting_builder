J.8 **The Loot Cave** (Medium) - *narrow, heaped, hidden*
A narrow cave heaps with stolen goods, a faint draught and silence, a stone at its end.
*20 by 20 feet, a cave. The band's loot; the way in is the chief's.*
**The centipedes:** four reddish many-legged worms nesting in the heaps (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Giant Centipede); their bite is Poison.
**The stolen goods:** heaped packs and bundles (Treasure V, d20).
**Exits:** a pressed stone, a crude passage -> J.7 The Chief's Cave
