J.8 **The Servants' Passage** (Low) - *narrow, hidden, pricked*

A narrow passage runs behind the halls' hangings, voices from the halls coming through muffled.

*A passage 10 feet wide and 50 long, the servants' back way from the landing to the second lieutenant's hall. Its dart trap is set off from the landing (J.1).*

**The Hangings:** Black silk on the halls' side; a party here hears what is said in the halls.
**The Jamb:** Pinholes in the landing door's frame.

**Exits:** a wooden door -> J.1 The Stair Landing, a wooden door to the silks -> J.3 The Second Lieutenant's Hall
