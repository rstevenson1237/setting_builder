J.6 **The Bone Midden** (Low) - *heaped, stinking, dry*
Heaped bones and refuse fill a dry cave, a dry rattle and flies, a slope of refuse under a low roof.
*20 by 20 feet, a cave. Each raid's leavings end here; the heaps are a sign of the band's raids.*
**The bone heaps:** cave men's and travellers' bones, some picked clean, some fresh.
**The trinkets:** small bright things among the bones (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** a cave mouth -> J.4 The Warren Hall
