J.6 **The Gate of the Way Down** (High) - *carved, barred, huge*

A vast iron gate fills the east wall, the demon's mark carved on it. The gate hums.

*A hall 30 feet square and 20 high (The Demon Is Invited). The stair to K.1 lies beyond; the wraiths bar it.*

**The Wraiths:** Hover before the gate (Hostile, 1-2, Bestiary : Wraith). They drain what they touch.
**The Cache:** A barred cache beside the gate (Treasure V, d20).

**Exits:** an iron door, west, 80 feet -> J.5 The Council Table, down a stair, north, 40 feet -> K.1 The Stair Landing
