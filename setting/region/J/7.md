J.7 **The Trophy Gallery** (Medium) - *hung, bronze-plated, sinking*

Trophies of the cult's wars hang over a floor of bronze plates laid in rings. The plates tick underfoot.

*A room 40 feet square. Weight on the centre plate sinks the rings one by one toward the middle, sliding all on them into a pit (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing). The lieutenants walk the outer ring only.*

**The Trophies:** Shields, masks and banners of the three branches, hung upside down.
**The Plates:** Bronze rings round a centre plate, the outer ring worn smooth.

**Exits:** a stone door hung with trophies -> J.2 The First Lieutenant's Hall, an archway -> J.4 The Third Lieutenant's Hall
