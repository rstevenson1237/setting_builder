J.7 **The Chief's Cave** (High) - *raised, hide-hung, rank*
A raised cave hangs with hides, a throne of bones at its back, heavy breathing and a stench.
*30 by 30 feet, a cave. The chief eats on its throne and roars for the band if refused; a stone at the back wall presses in.*
**Rank the Crested:** the chief, a grey-skinned troglodyte half as large again as the rest with a torn crest and a necklace of cave men's teeth (Named Creature: Rank the Crested); it grunts for iron and fresh captives, and sells a captive's ransom for 50 cn, each.
**The guards:** two troglodytes beside the throne (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Troglodyte); they answer an alarm in one turn.
**The chief's bed:** hides piled behind the throne (Treasure III, d20).
**The leather bundle:** stained leather under the bed (Magic, d12).
**The back wall:** a stone in the wall that presses in, a faint draught around it.
**Exits:** a cave mouth -> J.4 The Warren Hall, a pressed stone, a crude passage -> J.8 The Loot Cave
