J.2 **The Rope Cave** (Medium) - *low, damp, strung*
A low damp cave strings with rope snares, dripping and a creak of rope, the floor strewn with leaves.
*20 by 20 feet, a cave. A band's snares.*
**The snare ropes:** hemp and hide strung across the cave at ankle height.
**The leaf floor:** leaves strewn thick over wet clay; stepping on them drops a person into a pit (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing), rope ends showing under them.
**Exits:** a cave mouth -> J.1 The Sinkhole Floor
