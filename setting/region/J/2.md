J.2 **The Slave Pens** (Medium) - *barred, crowded, wailing*
Three **pens** of rough iron bars line a passage, each crowded with chained **captives**, a strip of spotted hide tied to each gate. **Gnolls** with spears pace the aisle.
*Passage 50 ft long and 10 ft wide; wailing hides small noises.*
**Pens:** Iron bars set in the rock, chained shut with a gnoll's collar-lock for each gate.
**Captives:** Nine in all - five who went up the ravine together, among them Kerrin Torren, and four taken from the road - who will say what they know of the den and ask to be taken home.
**Gnolls:** Two with spears, who jab through the bars and call the den (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Gnoll). They know which captives have kin at the keep.
**Gear:** Packs, torn cloaks and kit piled in the aisle corner (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** crude, south end of the passage -> J.1 The Collar Mouth
