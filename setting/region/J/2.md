J.2 **The First Lieutenant's Hall** (High) - *grand, black, watched*

A grand black hall hung with trophies, lamps hissing, a throne of bones at the east end. The room is watched.

*A hall 60 feet by 50 and 20 high (The Demon Is Invited). The first lieutenant holds it; the others bar the stair if he is killed.*

**Krulox:** Sits the throne (Named Creature: Krulox). He answers an alarm in a turn.
**The Trophies:** Masks and weapons of the cult's victims, hung from the walls.
**The Throne's Base:** Hollow and full (Hoard: The Lieutenants' Hoard).

**Exits:** an archway, west, 80 feet -> J.1 The Stair Landing, a stone door, north, 80 feet -> J.3 The Second Lieutenant's Hall, a stone door, south, 80 feet -> J.4 The Third Lieutenant's Hall, an archway, east, 80 feet -> J.5 The Council Table
