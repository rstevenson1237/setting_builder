J.5 **The Captives' Pit** (Medium) - *deep, wet, barred*
A deep wet pit lies barred with root-logs, weeping and the clink of chains, a pool of drips at the bottom.
*20 by 20 feet, a cave. The band's raids fill it.*
**The captives:** four to eight cave men huddled in the pit, begging for rescue, who know the raids' victims (Fearful, 4-8, Bestiary : Caveman).
**The root-log bars:** logs lashed across the mouth, a rope lift beside them.
**Exits:** a cave mouth -> J.4 The Warren Hall
