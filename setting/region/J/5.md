J.5 **The Pack-Leader's Den** (High) - *high, dung-strewn, guarded*
A high cave strewn with collars and dung, a raised **bed** of bones at one end, a **hollow** beneath it and a **rack** of iron collars on the wall. **Ruk** prices a captive with a stick, the **pack** crouched round him.
*Cave 40 ft by 40 ft, ceiling 25 ft. A bellowed order from Ruk brings the pens' guards in two Rounds.*
**Ruk the Collar-Keeper:** Names a price for any captive he holds, and loosens his chain-whip and the pack on a party that will not pay. He knows the way through the maze (Named Creature: Ruk the Collar-Keeper).
**Pack:** Six crouch round Ruk with barbed spears and drag off whoever falls (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Gnoll).
**Collar Chain:** Among the iron collars on his chain hangs a thighbone cut with a lock's pattern (Keys: The Notched Bone).
**Hollow:** Dug out under the bone bed and packed with cloth-wrapped goods (Hoard: Ruk's Hoard).
**Rack:** Iron collars hung in size order, the newest bright.
**Exits:** wooden, west end of the cave -> J.3 The Den Floor
