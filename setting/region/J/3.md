J.3 **The Second Lieutenant's Hall** (Medium) - *grand, hung, cold*

Black silk hangs on bone hooks round a raised couch, stirring in the cold. The hall is silent.

*A hall 50 feet by 40 and 20 high.*

**Xothox:** Lies on the couch (Named Creature: Xothox). He wants release from the demon and will bargain.
**The Box:** A silk-wrapped box holds a black stone seal (Keys: The Council Seal).

**Exits:** a stone door, south, 80 feet -> J.2 The First Lieutenant's Hall, a stone door, south-east, 115 feet -> J.5 The Council Table
