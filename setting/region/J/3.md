J.3 **The Den Floor** (Low) - *trampled, reeking, scrawled*
A cavern forty paces across, its floor trampled flat, its walls **scratched** with tallies and handprints. Four passages lead off it, and a **bundle** of goods lies against one wall.
*Cavern 120 ft across, ceiling 15 ft. Dung and rot mask any scent.*
**Tallies:** Scratched in rows at head height; a captive's handprint is pressed in blood at the end of each row, and the newest rows are unfinished.
**Bundle:** Cloth, hides and a carter's harness lashed together for the buyer (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** crude, west side of the cavern -> J.1 The Collar Mouth, crude, south side of the cavern -> J.4 The Bone Heap, wooden, east side of the cavern -> J.5 The Pack-Leader's Den
