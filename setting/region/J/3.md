J.3 **The Guard Gallery** (Medium) - *narrow, rank, watched*
A narrow rank gallery has watch-niches along its wall, a stench and a scrape of claws, a stone ledge at its end.
*20 by 30 feet, a cave. The troglodytes' watch calls the band when a light is seen at the sinkhole.*
**The troglodytes:** three grey-skinned lizard-faced cave dwellers waiting in the niches with stone knives (Hostile, 3, Bestiary : Troglodyte); a rank rotten smell comes first, and they answer an alarm in one turn.
**The watch-niches:** hollows in the gallery wall, each with a knife-worn sill.
**Exits:** a cave mouth -> J.1 The Sinkhole Floor
