Layout: The den runs east into the rock from its mouth on the ravine's east wall. The collar mouth (J.1) lies at the mouth, with the slave pens (J.2) 40 feet north, the den floor (J.3) 50 feet east, the bone heap (J.4) 40 feet south of the floor, and the pack-leader's den (J.5) 60 feet east of the floor. The heap (J.4) hides a passage 90 feet south-west to the hobgoblins' barracks (I.3).

Places:
- J.1 The Collar Mouth - the way in; three gnolls laughing on the ledge.
- J.2 The Slave Pens - nine captives in three pens, two gnoll guards.
- J.3 The Den Floor - a bundle of sold goods and scratched tallies.
- J.4 The Bone Heap - a sealed passage and a tally bone.
- J.5 The Pack-Leader's Den - Ruk the Collar-Keeper, six gnolls, a chest and a collar chain.

Inhabitants: Three of The Kurzun on the ledge, three Gnolls at J.1, two Gnolls at J.2, and Ruk the Collar-Keeper with six of The Kurzun at J.5.

Alarm: Laughing and howling hides small noises. A fight at J.1 or J.2 raises it; the den floor (J.3) hears at once, and a bellowed order from Ruk at J.5 brings the pens' guards in two Rounds. If a captive is taken, the pack hunts the party down the ravine.

Loot: The largest prize is Ruk's Hoard in the hollow under his bed at J.5, with the maze door's bone key on his collar chain; Treasure I lies in the captives' gear at J.2 and Treasure III in the bundle at J.3. A.7 buys it at 80%.

Secrets:
- The bone heap hides a makers' passage to the hobgoblins' barracks - the Kurzun; the draught through the bones (J.4) brings it out.
- The notched thighbone on Ruk's collar chain is the key to the maze door - Ruk knows; the chain (J.5) brings it out.
- A single buyer, marked with an inverted triangle, has bought the captives - the tally bone (J.4) brings it out.

Leads:
- Rumour 9 - J.2 Captives.
- The Missing Party - J.2 Captives.
