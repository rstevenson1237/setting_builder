Layout: The den has one mouth on the ravine's east wall, the gnoll ledge (E.6), 1,000 yards up from the ravine's mouth and 60 feet above the path, where iron collars hang on pegs along the edge and gnolls lounge, laughing. The collar mouth (J.1) opens 20 feet east of the ledge: a cave mouth 40 feet wide and 30 deep with iron collars hung from hooks round its edge, a strip of spotted hide nailed over the lintel and knucklebones scattered across the floor. The den is natural cave with a rough floor and ceilings 6 to 15 feet; light is cookfires, a constant laughing and howling hides small noises, the footing is trampled dung and bone, the air is foul, and a lit fire makes the captives scream and the gnolls laugh.

The pack-leader's den (J.5), the one High location, lies 90 feet east of the den floor (J.3) through a wooden door, 180 feet east of the collar mouth (J.1). It is a cave 40 feet by 40 with a ceiling of 25 feet, strewn with collars and dung, a raised bed of bones at one end with a hollow dug out beneath it and a rack of iron collars on the wall in size order; Ruk the Collar-Keeper (Named Creature: Ruk the Collar-Keeper) prices a captive with a stick, six gnolls crouched round him, a thighbone cut with a lock's pattern hanging among the collars on his chain (Keys: The Notched Bone), and the hollow packed with cloth-wrapped goods (Hoard: Ruk's Hoard).

The slave pens (J.2) lie 50 feet north of the collar mouth (J.1): a passage 50 feet long and 10 wide with three pens of rough iron bars, a strip of spotted hide tied to each gate, nine captives chained inside and gear piled in the aisle corner. The den floor (J.3) lies 90 feet east of the collar mouth (J.1): a cavern 120 feet across with a ceiling of 15 feet, its floor trampled flat, its walls scratched with tallies at head height, four passages leading off it and a bundle of goods lashed against one wall. The bone heap (J.4) lies 80 feet south of the den floor (J.3): a cave 30 feet by 20 whose end is filled to the roof with splintered bones and broken stone, a notched thighbone on its crest; a party can notice a cold draught blowing through the heap.

Inhabitants: Three of The Kurzun (Faction: The Kurzun) lounge on the ledge below, at E.6. Three Gnolls lounge on the ledge outside J.1, laughing, hostile, circling and hamstringing the slow, wanting a buyer's coin; they know when the buyer is due. Two Gnolls with spears pace the aisle of the pens (J.2), hostile, jabbing through the bars and calling the den, wanting the captives kept unharmed until sold; they know which captives have kin at the keep. Nine captives are chained there, five who went up the ravine together, among them Kerrin Torren, and four taken from the road. Ruk the Collar-Keeper (Named Creature: Ruk the Collar-Keeper) is at J.5 four days in five, hostile, naming a price for any captive and then loosing his chain-whip and the pack, wanting a buyer's coin and the hobgoblins broken; he knows the way through the maze. Six of The Kurzun crouch round him with barbed spears, hamstringing and dragging off whoever falls.

Alarm: Laughing and howling hides small noises. A fight at J.1 or J.2 raises it; the den floor (J.3) hears at once, and a bellowed order from Ruk at J.5 brings the pens' guards in two Rounds. If a captive is taken, the pack hunts the party down the ravine.

Loot: The den's wealth is its captives and what it has sold. The largest prize is Ruk's Hoard (Hoard: Ruk's Hoard) in the hollow under the bone bed at J.5, with Ruk and six gnolls over it, and on his collar chain the notched thighbone that opens the maze door (Keys: The Notched Bone); Treasure I lies in the captives' gear at J.2, Treasure III in the bundle at J.3, and the tally bone on the heap at J.4 is a record of sales (Lore: The Buyer's Tally). The Moneychanger (A.7) buys it back at 80% of value.

Secrets:
- The bone heap hides a makers' passage to the hobgoblins' barracks - the Kurzun; the draught through the bones (J.4) brings it out.
- The notched thighbone on Ruk's collar chain is the key to the maze door - Ruk knows; the chain (J.5) brings it out.
- A single buyer, marked with an inverted triangle, has bought the captives - the tally bone (J.4) brings it out.
