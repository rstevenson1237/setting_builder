HC.4 **The Bone Passage** (Low) - *narrow, bone-lined, dry*
A narrow passage lined with stacked bones runs down in dry air, the breathing louder at its end.
*10 by 40 feet, a hall. The passage leads to the god's cavern; the bone stacks and the breathing are signs of the god.*
**The bone stacks:** skulls and long bones piled to the roof along both walls.
**The low roof:** pressing at head height all the way.
**Exits:** an open passage -> HC.2 The Sacrifice Hall, an open passage -> HC.6 The God's Cavern
