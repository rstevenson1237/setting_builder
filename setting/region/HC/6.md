HC.6 **The God's Cavern** (High) - *vast, warm, breathing*
A vast cavern breathes warm wind, a black serpent thick as a tower asleep on a mound of old bones and gold, its slow breath moving the air.
*70 by 50 feet, a cave 30 feet high. The god is asleep; it quickens when a captive is killed, the crown is lifted or it is struck.*
**The god:** coiled asleep over the bones (Named Creature: Zuuthtuuz); the priests call it the Sleeper.
**The bone heap:** old bones piled under the coils; the heap shifts underfoot (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing).
**The gold in the bones:** old gold scattered among the bones (Treasure V, d20).
**The bier gate:** iron bars across the way to the bier, locked; it takes the Serpent Ring (Keys: The Serpent Ring).
**Exits:** an open passage -> HC.4 The Bone Passage, the locked iron bier gate -> HC.7 The Emperor's Bier, an open way -> HC.8 The Offering Pit
