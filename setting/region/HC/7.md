HC.7 **The Emperor's Bier** (High) - *raised, golden, bone-strewn*
A raised round bier of gold lies under drifts of bone, warm and dry, a skull at its centre wearing a golden crown.
*30 by 30 feet, round. The last snake emperor's tomb. The crown is the serpent crown.*
**The skull:** the last emperor's, on a gold plate, bone-strewn round it.
**The crown:** gold coiled round a green stone the size of a fist; lifting it from the skull stirs the god (Test of Sanity, 3d) (Unique Treasure: The Serpent Crown).
**Exits:** an open way -> HC.6 The God's Cavern
