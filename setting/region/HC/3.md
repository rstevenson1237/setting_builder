HC.3 **The Altar Gallery** (Medium) - *ringed, carved, watched*
A ringed gallery of carved niches overlooks the hall, chains clinking and weeping.
*30 by 30 feet. Captives wait here for the altar.*
**The captives:** four to ten tribespeople chained in the niches, awaiting the altar, who beg for rescue and know who is taken next (Fearful, 4-10, Bestiary : Tribesman).
**The chain rings:** iron loops set in the niches' backs.
**The reliefs:** carved panels behind the niches, a crowned serpent-king going down to a gold bier and a priest's ring closing an iron gate (Lore: The Gallery Reliefs).
**Exits:** an archway -> HC.2 The Sacrifice Hall
