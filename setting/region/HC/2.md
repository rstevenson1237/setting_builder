HC.2 **The Sacrifice Hall** (High) - *vaulted, stained, golden*
A vaulted hall glints with gold leaf, a stained altar at its centre, drips and an echo of slow breathing, a gallery arch above.
*60 by 40 feet. An emperor's audience hall; each captive is killed on the altar and the god nears its waking with each.*
**The priests:** three to six snake men in green sashes chanting at the altar (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Snake Man); they answer an alarm in one turn.
**The altar:** a stone slab stained black; a hand on its stain hears the names of the sacrificed whispered (Test of Sanity, 1d).
**The gold leaf:** gold plates peeled from the walls and heaped by the altar (Treasure III, d20).
**The carved vault:** serpents run along the vault, left by the empire that raised the pyramid.
**Exits:** an archway -> HC.1 The Sanctum Stair Foot, an archway -> HC.3 The Altar Gallery, an open passage -> HC.4 The Bone Passage, an archway -> HC.5 The Priests' Cell
