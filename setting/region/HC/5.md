HC.5 **The Priests' Cell** (Medium) - *small, dim, cold*
A small dim cell holds a cot and a carved stone box on a shelf, the air cold and silent.
*20 by 20 feet. A priest's cell; the box is warded.*
**The cot:** bare, under a cold blanket.
**The stone box:** carved with runes ringing its lid; opening it brings a test (Test of Sanity, 1d) and holds a book (Magical Tome: The Hissing Litany).
**Exits:** an archway -> HC.2 The Sacrifice Hall
