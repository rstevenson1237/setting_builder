HC.8 **The Offering Pit** (Medium) - *round, deep, dark*
A round pit rimmed with chains and bones drops into the dark, an echo and a deep breath rising from it.
*30 by 30 feet, round. Offerings are dropped here for the god.*
**The pit:** a deep round drop; a step past the rim falls into the dark (Test of Constitution, 3d, Crushing).
**The chain rim:** links hanging from the rim into the dark.
**Exits:** an open way -> HC.6 The God's Cavern
