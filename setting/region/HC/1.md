HC.1 **The Sanctum Stair Foot** (Low) - *cut, braziered, steep*
A stair cut in raw rock ends at a braziered landing, a slow breathing coming through the stone and crackling from the brazier.
*20 by 20 feet. The stair climbs sixty feet to the halls.*
**The landing brazier:** bronze, lighting the stair's last steps, soot-black above it.
**The arch:** carved with coiled serpents, leading on into the sacrifice hall.
**Exits:** an archway -> HC.2 The Sacrifice Hall, up the cut stair -> HB.13 The Sanctum Stair Head
