K.8 **The Hall of the Kneeling** (High) - *long, bowed, silent*

Skeletons kneel in rows, every skull bowed toward a door. Nothing moves; bone settles.

*A hall 40 feet square, its dead kneeling toward the demon's hall below (The Demon Is Invited). They rise behind whoever passes and turns a back.*

**The Kneeling:** Rows of the dead in rotted robes (Hostile, 6-12, Bestiary : Skeleton).
**The Offerings:** At the door they face, coin and bronze left by the cult (Treasure III, d20).

**Exits:** an archway to the ribs -> K.6 The Breathing Room, an archway -> K.3 The Choking Hall
