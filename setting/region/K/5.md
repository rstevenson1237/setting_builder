K.5 **The Leader's Cave** (High) - *high, bone-strewn, quiet*
A high cave strewn with bones, a heaped **bed** against the far wall, a **pit** beside it and a **bell-cord** hanging by the bed. **Mort** sits in the dark, counting tithes.
*Cave 35 ft by 30 ft, ceiling 20 ft. No light but the party's.*
**Mort the Tithe-Taker:** Strikes from hiding and never parleys, then falls back toward the stair. He knows where each tribe keeps its tithe (Named Creature: Mort the Tithe-Taker).
**Bell-Cord:** Hung by the bed, running through a hole in the rock; a pull rings a bell at the top of the stair.
**Pit:** Dug beside the bed, lined with bones, a leather bag at the bottom (Hoard: Mort's Hoard).
**Skull Cup:** Set on a ledge of the rock, grey with soot (Treasure III, d20).
**Soot:** Handprints across the rock beside the stair.
**Exits:** crude, west end of the cave -> K.3 The Sleeping Cave, hidden, behind the plaster, south side of the cave -> K.4 The Back Stair
