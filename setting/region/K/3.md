K.3 **The Choking Hall** (High) - *wide, choking, dim*

A wide, low hall of choking air, bone dust underfoot and a heartbeat loud in every ear. A **gate** stands at the east end.

*A hall 40 feet square and 10 high (The Demon Is Invited). Each hour spent here costs a Test of Sanity.*

**The Air:** Thick and hard to breathe, the low roof holding it in; flames gutter and smoke. Breathing deep draws the demon's breath into the lungs (Test of Sanity, 2d).
**The Gargoyles:** Stand at the walls (Hostile, 1-2, Bestiary : Gargoyle).
**The Niche:** Cut in the gate's frame, holding a cache (Treasure V, d20).
**The Mark:** A black hand painted over the gate (Faction : Voxkroth).

**Exits:** an archway -> K.2 The Wailing Hall, an iron door -> K.5 The Last Threshold, a stone door -> K.4 The Mask Store, an archway -> K.8 The Hall of the Kneeling
