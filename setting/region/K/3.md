K.3 **The Hall of Thin Air** (High) - *vast, airless, dim*

A vast airless hall, bone dust underfoot and only your own heartbeat in the silence. A gate stands at the east end.

*A hall 60 feet square and 10 high (The Demon Is Invited). Each hour spent here costs a Test of Sanity.*

**The Air:** Thick and hard to breathe; flames gutter and smoke. Breathing deep draws the demon's breath into the lungs (Test of Sanity, 2d).
**The Gargoyles:** Stand at the walls (Hostile, 1-2, Bestiary : Gargoyle).
**The Niche:** A niche in the gate holds a cache (Treasure V, d20).
**The Mark:** A black hand painted over the gate (Faction : Voxkroth).

**Exits:** an archway, west, 70 feet -> K.2 The Wailing Hall, an iron door, east, 70 feet -> K.5 The Last Threshold, a stone door, north-west, 100 feet -> K.4 The Mask Store
