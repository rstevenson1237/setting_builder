K.3 **The Sleeping Cave** (Medium) - *dark, matted, quiet*
Six **beds** of matted hide lie round a dark round cave, no light and no sound. **Bugbears** sleep on them by day.
*Cave 30 ft across, ceiling 8 ft. The one low door is at the west end.*
**Bugbears:** Three asleep on the hides; roused, they come for the rearmost without a sound (Hostile, 3, Bestiary : Bugbear).
**Beds:** Matted hair and bone splinters in the hide; bags and bundles are stuffed under them (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open, west end of the cave -> K.1 The Dry Bed, wooden, north side of the cave -> K.2 The Tithe Store, crude, east side of the cave -> K.5 The Leader's Cave
