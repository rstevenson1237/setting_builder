Layout: The dry bed (K.1) climbs east 200 yards from the ravine's east wall, entered from the dry fork (E.7), to the den under its overhang. The sleeping cave (K.3) lies at its head, with the tithe store (K.2) 40 feet north, the leader's cave (K.5) 60 feet east, and the back stair (K.4) 30 feet south of the leader's cave, climbing 120 feet north-east to the shrine's back stair door (M.14).

Places:
- K.1 The Dry Bed - the way in; two bugbears on the overhang.
- K.2 The Tithe Store - a stack for each tribe, sealed with pitch.
- K.3 The Sleeping Cave - three bugbears asleep on matted hides.
- K.4 The Back Stair - a plaster wall, ninety steps and a niche of tithe sticks.
- K.5 The Leader's Cave - Mort the Tithe-Taker, a chest and a bell-cord.

Inhabitants: Two of The Zurnaz on the dry fork, two Bugbears at K.1, three Bugbears at K.3, and Mort the Tithe-Taker at K.5.

Alarm: Nothing is shouted. Bugbears at K.1 drop on the last in line and fall back to K.3, which wakes without a sound; Mort in K.5 hears a Round later and pulls the bell-cord, ringing the bell at M.14, and acolytes reach the back stair within a turn. A tithe lost sends the Zurnaz searching the ravine.

Loot: The largest prize is the tithe in the stacks at K.2 (Treasure V), and Mort's Hoard in the pit by his bed at K.5; Treasure I lies in the beds at K.3 and Treasure III in the skull cup at K.5. A.7 buys it at 80%.

Secrets:
- The plaster behind Mort's bed hides a stair to the shrine - the Zurnaz; the hollow sound and cracked seam (K.5) bring it out.
- The tithe sticks in the stair niche record every tribe's tithe, and one for the keep - Mort knows; the niche (K.4) brings it out.

Leads:
- Rumour 7 - K.2 Stacks.
