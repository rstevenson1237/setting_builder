Layout: The den's mouth is the dry fork (E.7) on the ravine's east wall, 1,400 yards up from the ravine's mouth: a dry stream bed 15 feet wide, level with the path, with soot handprints on the rock higher than a man can reach. The dry bed (K.1) begins 20 yards east of the fork's mouth and climbs east 200 yards in all between rock walls, under a long overhang that shelves out 12 feet above the bed, soot handprints at head height on both walls and a row of sticks with smeared tops up its middle; bugbears crouch along the overhang's edge day and night, hidden above the bed. The den is dry stream bed and natural cave, ceilings 7 to 20 feet; no light shows but what the party carries, sound dies at once in the soft dust, the footing is dry silt that holds tracks, the air is still, and a lit fire shows from the ravine.

The leader's cave (K.5), the one High location, lies 60 feet east of the sleeping cave (K.3) by a crude passage, 200 yards east of the dry bed's start (K.1). It is a cave 35 feet by 30 with a ceiling of 20 feet, strewn with bones, a heaped bed against the far wall, a pit beside it lined with bones and a bell-cord hanging by the bed through a hole in the rock, a skull cup on a ledge; Mort the Tithe-Taker (Named Creature: Mort the Tithe-Taker) sits in the dark counting tithes, and a leather bag lies at the bottom of the pit (Hoard: Mort's Hoard).

The sleeping cave (K.3), at the bed's head 180 yards east of the dry bed's start (K.1), is a round cave 30 feet across with a ceiling of 8 feet, six beds of matted hide and one low door at its west end. The tithe store (K.2) lies 40 feet north of the sleeping cave (K.3): a square store 25 feet on a side with shelves cut in the rock, stacks of sacks, chests and bundles by tribe, each tied with a strip of its hide, and a door sealed with pitch. The back stair (K.4) lies 30 feet south of the leader's cave (K.5) behind a plaster wall cracked at a seam that rings hollow, soot handprints on the plaster at head height; it is 4 feet wide with a ceiling of 8 feet, ninety narrow steps turning twice and climbing 120 feet north-east to the shrine's back stair door (M.14), with a niche cut in the wall at the first turn.

Inhabitants: Two of The Zurnaz (Faction: The Zurnaz) lie along the overhang at the fork's mouth (E.7), hostile, dropping on the last in line and wanting the tithe collected. Two Bugbears crouch at the overhang's edge in K.1, hostile, wanting no one up the bed unmarked; they know which tribe's tithe is late. Three Bugbears sleep on the matted hides of K.3 by day, hostile, waking without a sound and striking the last in line. Mort the Tithe-Taker (Named Creature: Mort the Tithe-Taker) sits in the dark of K.5 four days in five, hostile, striking from hiding and never parleying, wanting every tithe carried up the stair; he knows where each tribe keeps its tithe.

Alarm: Nothing is shouted. Bugbears at K.1 drop on the last in line and fall back to K.3, which wakes without a sound; Mort in K.5 hears a Round later and pulls the bell-cord, ringing the bell at M.14, and acolytes reach the back stair within a turn. A tithe lost sends the Zurnaz searching the ravine.

Loot: The den's wealth is every tribe's tithe, held until it goes up the stair. The largest prize is the tithe in the stacks at K.2 (Treasure V), behind a pitch-sealed door with the bugbears of K.3 asleep beside it, and Mort's Hoard (Hoard: Mort's Hoard) in the pit by his bed at K.5 under Mort himself; Treasure I lies in the beds at K.3 and Treasure III in the skull cup at K.5. The Moneychanger (A.7) buys it back at 80% of value.

Secrets:
- The plaster behind Mort's bed hides a stair to the shrine - the Zurnaz; the hollow sound and cracked seam (K.5) bring it out.
- The tithe sticks in the stair niche record every tribe's tithe, and one for the keep - Mort knows; the niche (K.4) brings it out.
