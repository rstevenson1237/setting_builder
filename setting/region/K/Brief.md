Layout: The den is reached up the dry fork (E.7) on the ravine's east wall: a dry stream bed 15 feet wide, level with the path, under a long overhang, with soot handprints on the rock higher than a man can reach and bugbears hidden along the overhang day and night. The den mouth (K.1) at the bed's head is a low cave mouth behind a sooty hide screen, soot handprints round it and a bugbear dozing on a stool behind the screen. The den is dry stream bed and natural cave, ceilings 7 to 20 feet; no light shows but what the party carries, sound dies at once in the soft dust, the footing is dry silt that holds tracks, the air is still, and a lit fire shows from the ravine.

The leader's cave (K.5), the one High location, lies beyond the sleeping cave (K.3) by a crude passage. It is a cave with a ceiling of 20 feet, strewn with bones, a heaped bed against the far wall, a pit beside it lined with bones and a bell-cord hanging by the bed through a hole in the rock, a skull cup on a ledge; Mort the Tithe-Taker (Named Creature: Mort the Tithe-Taker) sits in the dark counting tithes, and a leather bag lies at the bottom of the pit (Hoard: Mort's Hoard).

The sleeping cave (K.3), just inside the den mouth (K.1), is a round cave with a ceiling of 8 feet, six beds of matted hide and one low door at its west end. The tithe store (K.2) opens off the sleeping cave (K.3): a square store with shelves cut in the rock, stacks of sacks, chests and bundles by tribe, each tied with a strip of its hide, and a door sealed with pitch. The back stair (K.4) opens off the leader's cave (K.5) behind a plaster wall cracked at a seam that rings hollow, soot handprints on the plaster at head height; it is 4 feet wide with a ceiling of 8 feet, ninety narrow steps turning twice and climbing 120 feet to the shrine's back stair door (M.14), with a niche cut in the wall at the first turn.

Inhabitants: Two of The Zurnaz (Faction: The Zurnaz) lie along the overhang at the fork's mouth (E.7), hostile, dropping on the last in line and wanting the tithe collected. One Bugbear dozes on a stool behind the hide screen of K.1, hostile, bellowing and then fighting in the mouth, wanting no one past unmarked; it knows which tribe's tithe is late. Three Bugbears sleep on the matted hides of K.3 by day, hostile, waking without a sound and striking the last in line. Mort the Tithe-Taker (Named Creature: Mort the Tithe-Taker) sits in the dark of K.5 four days in five, hostile, striking from hiding and never parleying, wanting every tithe carried up the stair; he knows where each tribe keeps its tithe.

Alarm: Nothing is shouted. The bugbear at K.1 bellows once and falls back to K.3, which wakes without a sound; Mort in K.5 hears a Round later and pulls the bell-cord, ringing the bell at M.14, and acolytes reach the back stair within a turn. A tithe lost sends the Zurnaz searching the ravine.

Loot: The den's wealth is every tribe's tithe, held until it goes up the stair. The largest prize is the tithe in the stacks at K.2 (Treasure V), behind a pitch-sealed door with the bugbears of K.3 asleep beside it, and Mort's Hoard (Hoard: Mort's Hoard) in the pit by his bed at K.5 under Mort himself; Treasure I lies in the beds at K.3 and Treasure III in the skull cup at K.5. The Moneychanger (A.7) buys it back at 80% of value.

Secrets:
- The plaster behind Mort's bed hides a stair to the shrine - the Zurnaz; the hollow sound and cracked seam (K.5) bring it out.
- The tithe sticks in the stair niche record every tribe's tithe, and one for the keep - Mort knows; the niche (K.4) brings it out.
