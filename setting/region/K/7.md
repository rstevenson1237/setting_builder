K.7 **The Censer Room** (Low) - *smoky, small, sweet*

Censers smoke on a ledge over a stone bench, the smoke sweet and thick.

*A room 20 feet square where the cult's masks are smoked. Its smoke is let out by the door from the mask store (K.4).*

**The Censers:** Bronze, still smoking; in one bowl lies an offering of coin and rings (Treasure II, d20).
**The Bench:** Stone, worn by kneeling.

**Exits:** a wooden door -> K.4 The Mask Store
