K.6 **The Breathing Room** (Medium) - *heaving, ribbed, close*

Ribbed walls swell and sink, slow as breath. Air moves in and out of vents overhead.

*A round room 20 feet across. Every few Rounds the room breathes in, pulling the air out of every chest in it (Test of Constitution, 2d, Suffocation). A party that holds its breath through it is unharmed; the cult knows to.*

**The Ribs:** Stone, cold, flexing like a chest.
**The Vents:** Slits overhead that suck and blow.

**Exits:** an open passage between ribs -> K.2 The Wailing Hall, an archway -> K.8 The Hall of the Kneeling
