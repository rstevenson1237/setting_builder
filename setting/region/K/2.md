K.2 **The Wailing Hall** (Medium) - *echoing, smoky, cold*

A long hall of thick air and echoes, a faint wailing coming from the dark. The cold bites.

*A hall 50 feet by 30 (The Demon Is Invited). The air is thick with the demon's breath; a flame gutters and smokes.*

**The Wailing:** Faint, from the far end of the hall; it stops when a light shows.
**The Ghouls:** Crouch in the dark (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Ghoul).
**The Mark:** A black hand over each arch (Faction : Voxkroth).

**Exits:** an open passage -> K.1 The Stair Landing, an archway -> K.3 The Choking Hall, an open passage -> K.4 The Mask Store, an open passage between ribs -> K.6 The Breathing Room
