K.2 **The Tithe Store** (Low) - *stacked, dry, sealed*
**Stacks** of sacks, chests and bundles stand by tribe on shelves cut in the rock of a square store, a strip of hide tied to each. The door is sealed with **pitch**.
*Store 25 ft square. Nothing here is hidden; no bugbear keeps watch inside.*
**Stacks:** One for each tribe, each tied with a strip of its hide (Treasure V, d20).
**Pitch:** Black, thumb-thick, broken at the door's edge by every visit.
**Exits:** wooden, south end of the store -> K.3 The Sleeping Cave
