K.2 **The Wailing Hall** (Medium) - *echoing, thin-aired, cold*

A long hall of thin air and echoes, a faint wailing coming from the dark. The cold bites.

*A hall 50 feet by 30 (The Demon Is Invited). The air is thinner here; a flame gutters.*

**The Wailing:** Faint, from the far end; it stops when a light shows.
**The Ghouls:** Crouch in the dark (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Ghoul).
**The Mark:** A black hand over each arch (Faction : Voxkroth).

**Exits:** an open passage -> K.1 The Stair Landing, an archway -> K.3 The Hall of Thin Air, an open passage -> K.4 The Mask Store
