K.4 **The Mask Store** (Medium) - *hung, dusty, faceless*

Faceless masks hang dusty on hooks on four walls. Dust sifts down from the roof.

*A room 30 feet square, where the cult's masks are stored. A torn mask is a scream the whole level hears. Sweet smoke seeps under the censer room's door; opening it lets the smoke out (Test of Fate, Poisoned).*

**The Masks:** Bronze and black, faceless; a cultist wears one tighter each day (Faction : Voxkroth).
**The Cultists:** Tighten their masks (Hostile, 4, Faction : Voxkroth). They answer an alarm within a turn.
**The Mask's Hollow:** One mask is cut inside with writing (Lore: The Demon's Breath).

**Exits:** an open passage -> K.2 The Wailing Hall, a stone door -> K.3 The Hall of Thin Air, a wooden door -> K.7 The Censer Room
