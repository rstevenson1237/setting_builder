K.4 **The Back Stair** (Low) - *steep, plastered, hidden*
A plaster **wall** hides a steep **stair** of ninety narrow steps that turns twice and climbs north-east. A **niche** is cut into the wall at the first turn.
*Stair 4 ft wide, ceiling 8 ft. Sound carries up it to the shrine.*
**Wall:** Plaster over stone, cracked at a seam; it breaks inward with a shove.
**Stair:** Makers' cut stone, worn in the middle of each tread.
**Handprint:** Soot, at head height, on the plaster.
**Niche:** Cut into the wall at the first turn, with bundled sticks inside (Lore: The Tithe Sticks).
**Exits:** hidden, behind the plaster, south end of the stair -> K.5 The Leader's Cave, stone, north-east, up the stair -> M.14 The Back Stair Door
