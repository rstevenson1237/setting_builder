K.1 **The Den Mouth** (Medium) - *low, screened, sooty*
At the head of the dry bed a low cave mouth is hung with a sooty **hide screen**, soot **handprints** on the rock round it. Behind the screen a **bugbear** dozes on a stool.
*Cave 20 ft across, ceiling 7 ft. Soft dust deadens every footfall; a lit fire here shows from the ravine.*
**Hide Screen:** A bear hide hung from a pole across the mouth, black with soot where hands push it aside; it hides the cave from the bed.
**Handprints:** Soot, at head height, on the rock round the mouth.
**Bugbear:** One dozes on a stool behind the screen, and bellows before it fights in the mouth (Hostile, 1, Bestiary : Bugbear). It knows which tribe's tithe is late.
**Exits:** open, west, 50 yards down the dry bed -> E.7 The Dry Fork, open, east, 40 feet -> K.3 The Sleeping Cave
