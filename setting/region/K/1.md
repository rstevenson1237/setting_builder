K.1 **The Dry Bed** (Medium) - *dusty, silent, overhung*
A dry **stream bed** fifteen feet wide climbs between rock walls under a long **overhang**, soot handprints on the stone at head height. **Bugbears** crouch along the overhang's edge.
*Bed 200 yards long. Soft dust deadens every footfall; a lit fire shows from the ravine.*
**Stream Bed:** Fine silt that holds every track; a row of sticks, tops smeared dark, runs up its middle.
**Overhang:** Shelves out twelve feet above the bed, the rock dark with soot where hands have gripped it.
**Handprints:** Soot, at head height, on both walls.
**Bugbears:** Two on the overhang, who drop on the last in line (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Bugbear). They know which tribe's tithe is late.
**Exits:** open, west, 20 yards -> E.7 The Dry Fork, open, east end of the bed, 180 yards -> K.3 The Sleeping Cave
