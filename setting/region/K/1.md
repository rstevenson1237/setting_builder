K.1 **The Stair Landing** (Low) - *thick, dim, silent*

A dim landing with a wraith in a king's robe standing at the head of it. Sound is swallowed whole.

*A landing 10 feet by 20 feet. The stair from J.6 comes down 20 feet; the wraith bars the way to a party that draws steel.*

**The Wraith:** Stands in a king's robe (Hostile, 1, Bestiary : Wraith). It wants no steel drawn; a party that passes without drawing it is let go.
**The Mark:** A black hand painted on the wall (Faction : Voxkroth).

**Exits:** an open passage, east, 70 feet -> K.2 The Wailing Hall, up a stair, south, 40 feet -> J.6 The Gate of the Way Down
