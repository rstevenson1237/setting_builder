G Goblin Hold

Overview: The Zhakuk's hold on the west wall above the kobolds, with the Wolves they keep in a kennel. A party comes for the road's lost cargo and the boss's hoard. The Zhakuk eat what the road carries, and with the convoys short they are short. If the wagons stop, they raid the Gubak's tribute stores; if the Rokmok move against them, they fall back from the mouth to the stores.

Approach: One mouth behind the goblin ledge (E.3), a Goblin in a notch above it by day and the Wolves loosed at dusk. A makers' passage runs from the stores toward the Rokmok's warren; the Zhakuk have walled their end and think it ends there.

Conditions: Natural cave widened with picks, ceilings 8 to 12 feet and passages wide enough for two abreast. Light is tallow lamps and cookfires. Sound carries along the main passage and dies in side caves. Footing is bone, scraps and filth, and the air stinks of wolf. A lit fire sends its smoke out through the mouth, and the Wolves bolt for the kennel.

Entrances:
- G.1 - Iron-barred kennels cut into both walls, gnawed bones heaped before them and tar thumbprints on every cage door.

Prizes:
- G.6 - Through a banded door off the main cavern: wolfskins tar-daubed on the walls round a fire pit, a stone seat with a chest beside it and a wolfskin satchel on its arm. A bone whistle there calls the wolves.

Places:
- G.2 - Four cookfires blackening the roof, furs in the warm corners, tar thumbprints on every post, five passages off it.
- G.3 - Wagon wheels, casks and bolts of cloth stacked by kind, a strongbox at the end with a square knot cut into its lid.
- G.4 - Down a short passage: three stone pens, each barred with a plank, a goblin tally in tar above them.
- G.5 - Sacks and tally sticks, and at the end a wall of newer stones in fresh mortar, a cold draught at its foot and a line of makers' script at its edge.
