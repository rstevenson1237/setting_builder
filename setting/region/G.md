G Goblin Hold

Kind: single holding
Overview: The Zhakuk's hold on the west wall above the kobolds: Goblins and the Wolves they keep in a kennel. Its boss is a Named Creature. A party comes for the road's lost cargo and the boss's hoard. The Zhakuk eat what the road carries, and with the convoys short they are short. If the wagons stop, they raid the Gubak's tribute stores. If the Rokmok move against them, they fall back from the mouth to the stores.
Approach: One mouth opens on the ravine's west wall, a Goblin on a ledge above it by day and the Wolves loosed at night. A makers' passage runs from the stores toward the Rokmok's warren; the Zhakuk have walled their end and think it ends there.
Conditions: Natural cave widened with picks, ceilings 8 to 12 feet and passages wide enough for two abreast. Light is tallow lamps and cookfires. Sound carries along the main passage and dies in side caves. Footing is bone, scraps and filth, and the air stinks of wolf. A lit fire sends its smoke out through the mouth, and the Wolves bolt for the kennel.
