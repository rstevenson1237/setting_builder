G The Northern Headlands

Overview: Windward headlands of scrub and grass above sea cliffs, the rim of an old volcanic crater, and a tented camp above a shingle landing. A party comes for the treasure hunters' second camp, the bird cliffs or the crater (Faction: The Treasure Hunters). The hunters are fortifying their landing, and a band of cave men is raiding the cliffs for eggs. If a party camps on the crater rim, pterodactyls roost on it and stoop on any fire; if the hunters learn that a party holds a map, their sentries follow its boat from the landing.

Approach: By the crater trail from the hill jungle, a hunters' sentry at the lip by day and none at night; a day's climb in the sentry's sight, and a party turned back is followed down. By the shore track from the northern villages, fish racks and a boat watch at each end; a day's walk below cliffs. By the crater landing from the sea, a sentry on the cliff and a boat on the shingle; in a storm none lands.

Terrain: Windward scrub and grass above cliffs. At the cliff edge in a gale a Test of Constitution each watch, a Wound of Crushing on a failure at the foot. On the crater rim the open scrub is 2 miles a watch, and every fire and lantern is seen from the sea.

Ways:
- G.1 - The shore track runs along the cliff top from the northern villages F.2, 3 watches end to end: the windward point G.7, the hunters' landing G.2, the crater trail to G.3 and the bird cliffs G.4; a cleft leads down to the cliff cave G.8.

Landmarks:
- G.2 - A tented camp above a shingle landing, a new palisade half built; ten hunters hammer and watch.
- G.3 - A bare windy rim round an old crater, a goat path along its lip and a sentry watching the trail.
- G.4 - White cliffs streaming with screaming sea-birds; six pterodactyls roost on the ledges.
- G.5 - A smoky camp of skin tents among egg-shells; eight cave men raid the cliffs for eggs.
- G.6 - A green warm crater floor with steaming pools; the water puts a swimmer to sleep for the watch.
- G.7 - A wind-scoured point of bare rock and grass, a cairn at its tip; the gale takes a step off the edge.

Places:
- G.8 - A narrow dripping cave behind a cleft, a dry ledge above the drip; reached by a cleft down from G.1.

Tables:
d6 Encounter
1. Pterodactyls circle over the cliff track and stoop on a lone traveller.
2. A hunters' sentry hails the party from the crater's lip.
3. Cave men's hoots carry from the cliffs as they come down for eggs.
4. A gale rises; the cliff edge becomes a danger.
5. Treasure hunters' crossbows track the party's boat along the coast.
6. Steam over the crater floor drifts across the rim.
