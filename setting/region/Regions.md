Regional Gazetteer of Brackvaen

A Torsgaard - SAFE, d10, *Rise, Ledger, Salt*
The only dry roof and the only honest scales in eighty square miles of fen, holding the eastern pans on the strength of a written settlement that half the families it bound have never forgiven.

B Lindmarn - WILD, d6, *Channel, Cord, Drowning*
Eighty square miles of standing brackish water over the drowned grid of a dead realm's pans, crossed by one causeway the Marchfolk built and a hundred channels the Vingash hold.

C Siwapelu - DANGEROUS, d8, *Gallery, Gorm, Arrangement*
The Odarath's under-work, sealed by four hundred years of water until the eastern water dropped three winters ago and left a cut stair standing open - it sits within B, directly beneath Lindmarn's eastern reach, so a party reaches it by crossing the fen and recrosses the fen carrying whatever it brings up.
