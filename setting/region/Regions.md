Regional Gazetteer of Fellward

A Fellward (facing the open ground) - SAFE, d6, *half-collapsed outer wall, night watch short two names, well running low*
Fellward is the last walled ground before the treeline, and everyone inside its wall answers to whichever runs out first: the duty roster or the grain count.

B Pelren (the marked road) - WILD, d8, *empty guard towers, supply road washed out, unburied dead past the wire*
Pelren is the cleared verge and washed-out supply road running from Fellward's gate into the true treeline, where the only fixed things left are the boundary stones nothing can be tracked across.

C Ingaraz (grave-ground) - DANGEROUS, d6, *buried foundation stones, older watch-points, borrowed coin from a dead man's pay*
Ingaraz is the field of older foundations and burial mounds sunk beneath Pelren's far treeline, raised three centuries before Fellward had a charter and still watched by whatever its builders left on guard.
