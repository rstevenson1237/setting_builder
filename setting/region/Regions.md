Regional Gazetteer of Greywatch

A Greywatch - SAFE, d8
The garrison's hold at the road's end, where everything carried out of the ravine is weighed.

B The Borderland - WILD, d6
Cleared verge, true wood and fen between the hold's gate and the ravine's mouth.

C The Ravine - DANGEROUS, d4
Cut-stone caves where rival tribes, and a shrine over them, gather Chaos.
