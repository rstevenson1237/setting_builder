Regional Gazetteer of Telar

A Tolkar (toll-place) - SAFE, d8
Tags: see setting/region/A/Tags.md
Tolkar's garrison holds the western toll-house on the old road, still collecting the toll the waystones demand though the crown that once paid its wages split into three twenty-eight years ago.

B Marfen (river-marsh) - WILD, d6
Tags: see setting/region/B/Tags.md
Marfen is the marsh and river country the old road crosses at the Sunk Bridge, where the Brekar reaver band has held the ford three months and taxes anyone unwilling to take the week-long detour around them.

C Velar (coin-place) - SAFE, d8
Tags: see setting/region/C/Tags.md
Velar is the eastern market town where the Nine Wagons trading company has based its detour operation, growing richer for exactly as long as the Sunk Bridge stays blocked.

D Daghash (pit-temple) - DANGEROUS, d6
Tags: see setting/region/D/Tags.md
Daghash is the roofless black ziggurat the priest-kings of Essath cut into Marfen's hills more than five hundred years ago, unclaimed since their kingdom ended within a single reign for reasons no one now living knows; it sits within Marfen's own borders.
