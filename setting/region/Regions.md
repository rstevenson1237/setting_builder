Regional Gazetteer of The Ashwrack Coast

A Saltmere Hold - SAFE, d6, *fishing hold, diving-bell, salvage market*
A fortified fishing hold built around a tidal harbor, home to the bronze diving-bell that grants its keeper authority along this stretch of coast.

B The Salt-Scrub Hills - WILD, d10, *wind-scoured hills, wolf packs, buried road*
Wind-scoured hills and salt-scrub thicket rising behind Saltmere, home to coast wolves and coastal bandits, and cut through by the remnants of Vael's old inland road.

C The Hollow Cairn - DANGEROUS, d8, *buried barrow, restless dead, bell-drawn*
A half-collapsed barrow within the Salt-Scrub Hills (B) where Vael's coastal dead were once interred, now stirred to unrest by drowned skeletons and a bell-wight drawn to any sound of ringing.
