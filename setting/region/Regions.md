Regional Gazetteer of Carrdun

A Carrdun (a native name, not a loan) - SAFE, d6, *Salvaged, Border, Waking*
A border garrison raised from salvaged tomb-stone, holding the crossing where the frontier crown's writ finally runs out.

B Oerath (the cold reaches) - WILD, d8, *Horse Lords, Border Reavers, Trackless Waste*
Open, wind-scoured grassland between the ford and the horse-tribe camps, crossed by traders and raided by the same riders who patrol it.

C Glaswy (the green water) - WILD, d10, *Reed Marsh, Marsh-kin, Drowned Edge*
A slow, reed-choked backwater downstream of the ruins, home to a wary marsh-people and whatever the current has carried down to them.

D Baldun (hold of the dead) - DANGEROUS, d6, *Drowned Temple, Elder Sorcery, Undying Sorcerer*
The flood-exposed remnant of Nemhet's drowned capital, sitting within Oerath's borderland across the causeway, worked open a room at a time by the commander's diggers and guarded by what still answers for it.
