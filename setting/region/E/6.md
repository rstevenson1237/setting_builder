E.6 **The Bound Men's Hut** (Lair) - *low, shuttered, quiet*
A low hut with a shuttered window and a locked door, scale-marks scratched on the door, no voices and the sound of breathing.
*80 by 60 feet. The bound men rest here between errands to the villages.*
**The door:** scale-marks scratched deep in the wood, locked from outside.
**The bound men:** six tribesmen sitting quiet in the hut, each with a green scale-mark inked over the heart, wanting a stranger's silence and knowing the roads and the pyramid's stair (Neutral, 6, Bestiary : Tribesman).
**The bunks:** six cots round a cold hearth.
