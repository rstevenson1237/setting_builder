E.6 **The Sworn Mound** (medium) - *Burial Mound, Membership Coil*
A modest catacomb, three burial niches cut into its walls, one of them holding a skeleton laid out with both hands closed around something small rather than folded at rest. Grave-silt lies undisturbed across the floor except in a single trail of footprints leading straight to that niche and back out again - somebody has already been here, and left what they came for behind anyway.
*A catacomb fifteen feet across, three shallow burial niches cut into the walls at chest height, drystone lining throughout. Quiet even by the field's own standard - nothing here carries sound the way the mounds nearer the camp do.*
**Closed Hand:** The niche skeleton's hands are folded around a small carved token rather than crossed at rest, an unusual enough posture that whoever laid this burial out meant the token held rather than buried loose - the Membership Coil (Keys: The Membership Coil), taken by prising the fingers open rather than breaking them.
**Warning Marking:** A single scored line runs the width of the niche's lip, matching nothing in Nakhash's own coiled-relief motif - a plain do-not-touch mark, cut after the burial rather than as part of it.
**Needle Catch:** the niche's lip sits a finger's width proud where the scoring runs; lifting the token without first pressing the lip flat -> a needle set into the stone catches whoever's hand is closest, a puncture and a foul ache lasting a day.
**Broken-Through Wall:** A low gap behind the third, empty niche, stone cleared away by hand rather than fallen on its own - a collapsed grave-tunnel, just wide enough to crawl, leading to a mound the field's own dig hasn't touched.
**Exits:** open path - worn ground, east side -> E.7 The Cut-Stone Mound, open path - worn ground, south side -> E.8 Cultist Dig Camp, hidden passage - the broken-through wall behind the third niche, cleared by hand -> E.5 The Waking Mound
