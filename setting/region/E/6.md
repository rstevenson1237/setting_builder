E.6 **The Armoury** (Medium) - *racked, oiled, locked*

Racks of bronze arms line three walls behind an iron door, a table at the middle. Armoured shapes stand among them, not moving.

*A room 20 feet square, locked from the Guard hall (E.3) by an iron door with a hawk-head keyhole.*

**The Racks:** Spears, swords and shields, oiled and ready, black with age at the tips (The Demon Is Invited).
**The Guard:** Skeletons stand along the racks (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Skeleton); they move at the first sound from anyone without the hawk.
**The Chest:** For weapons, under the table (Treasure II, d20).

**Exits:** an iron door -> E.3 The Guard Hall
