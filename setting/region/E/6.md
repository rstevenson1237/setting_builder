E.6 **The Sworn Mound** (medium) - *Burial Mound, Membership Coil*
A modest catacomb, three burial niches, one holding a skeleton with both hands **closed** around something small. Grave-silt lies undisturbed but for one trail, of footprints to that niche and back.
*A catacomb fifteen feet across, three shallow burial niches cut into the walls, at chest height, drystone lining throughout. Quiet even by the field's own standard.*
**Closed Hand:** The niche skeleton's hands are folded around, a small carved token, rather than crossed at rest, taken by prising the fingers open (Keys: The Membership Coil).
**Warning Marking:** A single scored line runs the width, of the niche's lip, the lip sitting a finger's width proud, the token lifted without pressing it flat -> a needle takes the nearest hand (Test of Constitution, 1d, Poison).
**Broken-Through Wall:** A low gap behind the third, empty niche, stone cleared by hand rather than fallen, just wide enough to crawl.
**Exits:** open path - worn ground, east side -> E.7 The Cut-Stone Mound, open path - worn ground, south side -> E.8 Cultist Dig Camp, hidden passage - the broken-through wall behind the third niche, cleared by hand -> E.5 The Waking Mound
