E.6 **The Armoury** (Medium) - *racked, oiled, locked*

Racks of bronze arms line three walls behind an iron door, a table at the middle. Armoured shapes stand among them, not moving.

*A room 30 feet square, locked from E.2 by an iron door; a second, open way leads in from E.3.*

**The Racks:** Spears, swords and shields, oiled and ready, black with age at the tips (The Demon Is Invited).
**The Guard:** Skeletons stand along the racks (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Skeleton); they move at the first sound.
**The Chest:** A weapon chest under the table (Treasure II, d20).

**Exits:** an iron door, west, 130 feet -> E.2 The Turning Room, an open passage, west, 60 feet -> E.3 The Guard Hall
