E.6 **The Gnoll Ledge** (Natural) - *foul, strewn, loud*
Iron **collars** hang on pegs along the edge of a narrow shelf, a cave mouth beyond. **Gnolls** lounge on the shelf, laughing.
*On the east wall above the hobgoblins, 250 yards up and sixty feet above the path.*
**Collars:** Hung on pegs like trophies, some sized for children.
**Gnolls:** Three with barbed spears hamstring the slow and drag off the fallen (Hostile, 3, Bestiary : Gnoll).
**Exits:** open, east, 20 feet into the cave -> J.1 The Collar Mouth
