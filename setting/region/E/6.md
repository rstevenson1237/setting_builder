E.6 **The Gnoll Ledge** (Natural) - *foul, strewn, loud*
A foul ledge hangs on the east wall, iron **collars** hung along its edge and a **cave** beyond. **Gnolls** lounge on it, laughing.
*Ledge ten feet wide, in the east wall above the hobgoblins.*
**Collars:** Iron slave collars, rusted and bright, hung on pegs along the edge.
**Gnolls:** Three lookouts with barbed spears lounge and laugh, hamstring the slow and drag off prey (Hostile, 3, Bestiary : Gnoll).
**Cave:** Dung and bones lie heaped across the floor of the mouth beyond the ledge.
**Exits:** open, east of the ledge -> J.1 The Collar Mouth
