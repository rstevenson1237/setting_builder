E.12 **The Empty Mound** (low) - *Burial Mound, Scattered Mound*
Larger and better built than any mound around it - dressed stone lining rather than dry-laid fieldstone, a proper corbelled roof - and entirely empty. Whoever this was meant for was never put here, or was and is long since gone without a trace.
*A storeroom-sized chamber twenty feet across, finer construction than the field's other mounds by a wide margin. Nothing in it - no niche, no bones, no goods, no sign it was ever used for what it was built to hold.*
**Fine Construction:** Dressed stone rather than fieldstone, cut and fitted with real care - whoever built this expected it to matter. Nothing about the chamber itself explains why it doesn't.
