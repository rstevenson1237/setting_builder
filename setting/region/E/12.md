E.12 **The Empty Mound** (low) - *Burial Mound, Scattered Mound*
Larger and better built than any mound around it, its **fine construction** dressed stone rather than fieldstone, under a corbelled roof, and entirely empty. Nothing was ever put here.
*A storeroom-sized chamber twenty feet across, finer construction than the field's other mounds. No niche, no bones, no goods, and no sign it was ever used.*
**Fine Construction:** Dressed stone rather than fieldstone, cut and fitted with care, and nothing in the chamber says why.
**Exits:** open gap - broken drystone, south side -> E.11 The Old Procession Track, open path - worn ground, west side -> E.9 Barrow-Kin Watch, open path - worn ground, south side -> E.10 The Ward-Marked Mound
