E.16 **The Sprung Floor** (medium) - *Elder Sorcery, Death-Script*
A bare guard chamber, empty but for a wide, discoloured patch of flagstone at its centre - and a scatter of old bones pressed flat at its edges, exactly where anyone stepping around the patch would have to stand. This room was built to catch somebody, and already has.
*A guard chamber fifteen feet across, walls otherwise bare of niche or marker - unlike every other side chamber off the First Hall, this one was never meant to hold the dead, only to stop the living.* 
**Discoloured Floor:** A section of flagstone noticeably paler than the rest, its mortar joints slightly wider - not original work, and not settled the way the rest of the floor has. The old, flattened bones ringing its edge are the clearest warning this Sanctum offers anywhere.
**Deadfall:** stepping onto the discoloured section anywhere -> the whole panel drops on a hinge into a shaft cut beneath it, a fall onto broken stone deep enough to kill outright.
**Exits:** hidden passage - a seam behind a fixed wall-hanging in the First Hall, opened by lifting it aside -> E.13 The First Hall
