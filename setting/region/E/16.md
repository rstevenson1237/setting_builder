E.16 **The Sprung Floor** (medium) - *Elder Sorcery, Death-Script*
A bare guard chamber, empty but for a wide **discoloured floor** patch at its centre, and old bones pressed flat at its edges. This room was built to catch somebody.
*A guard chamber fifteen feet across, walls bare of niche or marker, never meant to hold the dead, only to stop the living.*
**Discoloured Floor:** A section of flagstone at the centre, paler than the rest, its mortar joints wider, stepped onto anywhere -> the panel drops on a hinge, onto broken stone (Test of Constitution, 3d, Crushing).
**Exits:** hidden passage - a seam behind a fixed wall-hanging in the First Hall, opened by lifting it aside -> E.13 The First Hall
