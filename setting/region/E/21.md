E.21 **The Second Master's Niche** (medium) - *Temple Cult, Leader's Name*
A small chapel holding a single carved seat facing the wall's own niche, and a written record set into that niche behind a stone lid too well-fitted to simply lift by hand.
*A chapel chamber, twelve feet across, a plain stone seat facing the niche rather than a door. Dust here is thinner than elsewhere in the Sanctum - this room has had less time sealed than the First Master's own.*
**Fitted Lid:** Set flush into the niche, no gap to grip; pressing at its near edge rather than the far one swings it open cleanly, but pressing at the far edge first -> a hidden reservoir behind it lets go, a wash of old, dark dye across whoever is reaching (Test of Fate, Marked).
**Skaruk's Own Record:** Behind the lid once properly opened - a written tally naming Skaruk as the Second Hall's own master, and noting, in passing, that Gholuk came after him (Lore: Skaruk's Tally).
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, entrance side -> E.18 The Second Hall
