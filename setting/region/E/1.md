E.1 **The Stair Landing** (Low) - *dim, watched, short*

A short room at the foot of a stair ends at a rune-marked door, a bell hung over it. A hawk is painted on one doorpost and a sun-disc on the other.

*A room 20 feet square, the short way in from the level above. Its arch onto the turning hall shows a door rune. The Royal Guard hold the door by day and the Priesthood by night; both ask for a word in the cynidicean speech.*

**The Door:** Rune-marked, with a bell that rings when it opens. A party that gives the word of the cynidicean speech passes; one that does not is followed to the stair.
**The Marks:** A red hawk on one post and a sun-disc on the other (Faction : Kurumisa, Faction : Ruhiahe).
**The Arch Rune:** A door shape cut in the keystone of the arch beyond: this passage's rune.

**Exits:** an archway -> E.2 The Turning Hall, up a stair -> D.6 The Gallery of the Fall
