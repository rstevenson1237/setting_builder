E.1 **The Stream Path** (Route) - *wet, echoing, exposed*
The gravel **path** hugs a loud, shallow stream. **Wolves** trot it at dusk, and a **crack** in the east wall near the mouth gives a climb to the rim.
*Path 1 mile long, ten yards wide beside the stream, between walls 80 yards apart. A shout carries to every mouth.*
**Path:** Gravel and wet slabs beside the stream; footfalls ring off the rock.
**Wolves:** Six lean grey wolves circle a party and rush the slowest (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Wolf); tracks and droppings mark the gravel.
**Crack:** Ten yards in from the mouth, a fissure in the east wall has handholds worn smooth in it, climbing 80 feet to the cliff top (E.11).
**Stepping Stones:** Four hundred and fifty yards up, flat stones cross the stream from one bank to the other.
**Dry Bed Mouth:** Fourteen hundred yards up, a dry stream bed enters from the east, the dry fork (E.7).
**Maze Door:** At the head, 1,760 yards up and 20 yards west of the path's end, an iron-bound door stands in the cliff's foot (E.8).
**Shrine Stair:** At the head, 20 yards east, black-stained steps climb the cliff (E.9).
**Exits:** open, south end of the path -> C.1 The Forest Trail, up the crack, east wall near the mouth -> E.11 The Rim Track
