E.1 **The Sealed Approach** (high) - *Elder Sorcery, Unmovable Door*
Two slabs of fitted stone, taller than a person and cut flush to the hillside, block what was once Nakhash's only formal way in - a line of unworn Essath script runs the width of the sill, kept clean where the dust and grave-silt everywhere else lies undisturbed. Old bones sit stacked a careful few feet short of the line, arranged there rather than fallen.
*A stepped approach twenty feet wide narrowing to the sealed slabs themselves, open to the sky, cut into the hillside's own rock rather than built up from it. Nothing here has moved in longer than anyone digging the mounds behind it has been alive.*
**Sealed Slabs:** Fitted stone, no hinge or seam a lever finds purchase on, and no combination of strength Table II equipment could bring counts for anything against it - it is not locked, it is not barred, it simply does not move. Whatever opened it once did not open it by force.
**Death-Script:** A line of Essath cut into the sill and kept unnaturally clean - not swept, simply never gathering what settles on everything else here. Read cold (a reasoned detail: the same hand that cut Daghash's own bound thresholds cut this), its shape is the same shape setting/Truths.md's spent-name workings take. Approaching, reading, or taking a rubbing from a stated arm's length back costs nothing; touching it, scraping at it, or forcing the slabs it is cut into wakes the working bound into the sill (Alert, 1, Bestiary : Threshold-Bane), which strikes everyone across the line in the same instant and is spent for good the moment it does.
**Stacked Remains:** Bones from at least four people, arranged in a low cairn a few feet short of the sill - not a burial, a marker. Whoever stacked them meant it as a warning rather than a grave.
**Struck-Short Tool Marks:** Pick and lever scars scattered across the stepped approach's lower risers, none within six feet of the slabs themselves - the cult's own current dig has tried this route and given it up rather than tested it.
**Fallen Kit:** A pack, rotted through, wedged between two risers below the marks - a whetstone, a corroded belt-buckle, and a coil of rope too rotten to trust (Treasure I, d20).
