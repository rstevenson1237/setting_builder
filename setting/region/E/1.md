E.1 **The Stair Landing** (Low) - *dim, watched, narrow*

A narrow landing ends at a rune-marked door, a bell hung over it. A hawk is painted on one doorpost and a sun-disc on the other.

*A landing 10 feet by 20 feet. The Royal Guard hold the door by day and the Priesthood by night; both ask for a word in the cynidicean speech.*

**The Door:** Rune-marked, with a bell that rings when it opens. A party that gives the word of the cynidicean speech passes; one that does not is followed to the stair.
**The Marks:** A red hawk on one post and a sun-disc on the other (Faction : Kurumisa, Faction : Ruhiahe).

**Exits:** a stone passage, north-north-east, 95 feet -> E.2 The Turning Room, up a stair, south, 40 feet -> D.6 The Gallery of the Fall
