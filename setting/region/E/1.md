E.1 **The Serpent Road** (Route) - *paved, vine-choked, straight*
A paved road runs straight under a canopy of vines, carved kerbs on either side, cicadas shrilling and the light dim even at noon.
*Four miles a watch on the worn stone, seen by every patrol that uses it; off the roads the canopy is dark by the second watch and a party is Lost on a failed Test of Fate. A fire is seen through the leaves.*
**The serpent posts:** at the way in, carved serpent posts mark the jungle's edge, and a guide turns back here.
**The patrol fires:** 0.3 miles along, a fire shows through the leaves and patrol voices carry; a trail leads to E.5.
**The burned market:** 1.5 miles along, scorched stalls lie under creepers; a track leads to E.3.
**The statue court:** 1.6 miles along, fallen stone serpents lie in a hushed court; a track leads to E.4.
**The pyramid's foot:** 1.8 miles along, a stair climbs out of the canopy; the road ends at E.9.
