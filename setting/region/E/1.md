E.1 **The Sealed Approach** (high) - *Elder Sorcery, Unmovable Door*
Two slabs of fitted stone, taller than a person, block Nakhash's only formal way in. A line of unworn **death-script** runs the sill, and old bones sit **stacked** a few feet short of it.
*A stepped approach twenty feet wide, narrowing to the sealed slabs, open to the sky, cut into the hillside's own rock rather than built up from it. Smells of cold rock and dry grave-silt, and no sound carries up, from behind the slabs.*
**Sealed Slabs:** Fitted stone, no hinge or seam a lever finds, and no equipment on Table II moves it.
**Death-Script:** A line of Essath cut into the sill, never gathering what settles on everything else, touching or scraping it, or forcing the slabs -> the working bound into the sill wakes, striking everyone across the line (Alert, 1, Bestiary : Threshold-Bane).
**Stacked Remains:** Bones from at least four people, arranged in a low cairn, a few feet short of the sill.
**Struck-Short Tool Marks:** Pick and lever scars across the lower risers, none within six feet of the slabs.
**Fallen Kit:** A pack rotted through, wedged between two risers, below the tool marks (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open path - worn stone steps, south end -> E.11 The Old Procession Track
