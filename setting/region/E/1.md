E.1 **The Stream Path** (Route) - *wet, echoing, exposed*
A gravel **path** runs beside the stream between sheer walls, in bowshot of the ledges on both sides. **Wolves** trot it at dusk, and a **crack** in the east wall near the mouth offers a climb.
*Path ten yards wide, a mile from the mouth to the head.*
**Path:** Gravel and wet slabs, the stream beside it; every voice carries up the walls.
**Wolves:** Six lean grey wolves trot the path at dusk, circle a party and rush the slowest (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Wolf); their tracks and droppings mark the gravel.
**Crack:** A fissure in the east wall, a few yards in from the mouth, with handholds worn in it; it climbs to the rim.
**Exits:** open, south end of the path -> C.1 The Forest Trail, up the crack, east wall near the mouth -> E.11 The Rim Track
