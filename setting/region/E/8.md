E.8 **Cultist Dig Camp** (high) - *Serpent Cult, Cult Cell*
A windlass and two dig-ropes stand rigged over the nearest mounds, a low canvas tent pitched between them, coiled-serpent marks worn openly, on more than one sleeve. A woman the others call **Coil-Mother** directs the work from a camp stool.
*A shallow cleared hollow forty feet across between three mounds, trampled bare of turf, ringed by the camp's gear, a fire pit, a rope store, a scatter of packs. Recently occupied and meant to look it.*
**Vray, the Coil-Mother:** Directs four cultists working the dig, giving no order she is unwilling, to have overheard, and knows which two mounds lead down (Named Creature: Vray).
**Cultist Dig-Crew:** Four working the ropes and the spoil, a coiled-serpent mark under a collar, or cuff (Watchful, 4, Man - AD 2d6+1 [MA 1]).
**Standing Order:** Overheard by anyone who watches a while, nobody touches the Sealed Approach, and nobody works once the light goes.
**Younger Cultist:** One of the four flinches at every sound, barely past novice, and says quietly she did not expect, the dead.
**If This Camp Is Lost:** Nothing comes looking on a schedule, and the cell answers a crossing quietly, later, out of sight (setting/Factions.md).
**Rope Store:** A canvas roll of spare cordage and pitons, with an iron-banded strongbox, kept under whoever is not on the ropes (Treasure IV, d20).
**Second Coil of Rope:** Slung behind the tent, rather than with the store, showing the same wear as the windlass rope, more cordage than one shaft needs.
**Exits:** open path - worn ground between mounds, west side -> E.6 The Sworn Mound, open shaft - rigged with the camp's own dig-rope and windlass, north side -> E.2 The Dug Mound, hidden route - a second rigged shaft the cell has not marked or spoken of openly, kept just out of casual sight beyond the tent -> E.3 The Broken Crown
