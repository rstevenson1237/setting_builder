E.8 **The Maze Door** (Natural) - *barred, cold, carved*
A heavy **door** is cut in the cliff's foot at the ravine's head, set in a **frame** carved with script, an iron lock at its centre.
*Door seven feet high and set deep; no light shows at its edges.*
**Door:** Iron-bound oak, locked; the lock takes a heavy key.
**Frame:** Script in the makers' hand runs round it, cut into the stone and worn at the edges.
**Exits:** locked, north of the door -> L.1 The Barred Door
