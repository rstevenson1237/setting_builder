E.8 **The Slow River** (Natural) - *brown, still, logged*
A slow brown river runs under hanging branches, still water with snagged logs and mud banks, insects loud.
*100 by 600 feet. A raft goes down it at 2 miles a watch in sight of both banks.*
**The ford:** shallows where logs hide under brown water; wading it risks a snag and a fall (Test of Constitution, 1d, Suffocation).
**The logs:** snagged trunks along the banks.
