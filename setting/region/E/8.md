E.8 **Cultist Dig Camp** (high) - *Serpent Cult, Cult Cell*
A windlass and two fixed dig-ropes stand rigged over the mounds nearest here, and a low tent of undyed canvas sits pitched between them - whoever is working this field is still here, and the coiled-serpent mark worn openly on more than one sleeve says who. A woman in unremarkable travelling clothes, addressed by the others as **Coil-Mother**, directs the work from a camp stool without once picking up a tool herself.
*A shallow, cleared hollow forty feet across between three of the field's mounds, trampled bare of turf, ringed by the camp's own gear - a fire pit, a rope store, a scatter of packs. Recently occupied and meant to look it; nothing here is hidden.*
**Vray, the Coil-Mother:** Directs four cultists working the dig, gives no order herself she is not willing to have overheard, and is the only one present who already knows which two mounds actually lead down (Named Creature: Vray). Her cultists work under her at AD 2d6+1 [MA 1] each, unremarkable but for the mark most wear under a collar or cuff.
**Standing Order:** Overheard plainly by anyone who watches a while: nobody touches the Sealed Approach - "that one isn't ours to open twice" - and nobody keeps working once the light goes; whoever is on the ropes when dusk comes ties off and comes up, no exceptions.
**Younger Cultist:** One of the four, barely past being a novice, flinches at every sound from the mounds and has said more than once, quietly, that she did not expect the dead to still be down there - she would rather be almost anywhere else and it shows.
**If This Camp Is Lost:** Vray does not report to anyone waiting nearby, so nothing comes looking on a fixed schedule - but per setting/Factions.md, a cell that is crossed rather than merely noticed spends its effort finding out what whoever crossed it most wants hidden, and a cell that loses Vray herself answers that loss the same way the wider cult always does: quietly, and later, somewhere the party will not see it coming.
**Rope Store:** A canvas roll holding spare cordage, pitons, and a strongbox banded in iron, the camp's shared coin and what little of value the dig has already turned up, kept under whoever is not on the ropes (Treasure IV, d20).
**Second Coil of Rope:** Slung behind the tent rather than with the rest of the store, showing the same wear as the windlass rope proper - more cordage than one shaft's worth of digging needs, for a camp that has only ever spoken of one.
**Exits:** open path - worn ground between mounds, west side -> E.6 The Sworn Mound, open shaft - rigged with the camp's own dig-rope and windlass, north side -> E.2 The Dug Mound, hidden route - a second rigged shaft the cell has not marked or spoken of openly, kept just out of casual sight beyond the tent -> E.3 The Broken Crown
