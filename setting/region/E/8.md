E.8 **The Maze Door** (Natural) - *barred, cold, carved*
A deep-set **door** of iron-bound oak stands in a **frame** carved with script, an iron lock at its centre.
*Door seven feet high; no light shows at its edges.*
**Door:** Locked; the lock takes a heavy key.
**Frame:** Script in the makers' hand runs round it, worn at the edges.
**Exits:** locked, north, through the door -> L.1 The Barred Door
