E.18 **The Second Hall** (high) - *Temple Cult, Three Chambers*
Wider than the First Hall and cut a full step down from it, this second rectangular chamber carries **Skaruk's** name across its own far gate - and two living cultists crouch at that gate now, working at its socket by lamplight, plainly stuck. Ash-dark stone streaks the walls here in long vertical runs, older than anything the cultists have brought with them.
*Sixty feet by thirty, floor sunk a full three feet below the First Hall's own level, reached by a short worn stair. The ash-streaked walls run floor to corbelled ceiling on both long sides - whatever burned here once burned high and hot.*
**Skaruk's Cultists:** Two of Vray's cell, having found the hidden crawl-route through E.15 and E.19 on their own, are trying to force this gate without the Rod - Vray does not know they came this far, and they know it (Furtive, 2, Man - AD 2d6+1 [MA 1] each). They want past the gate more than they want a fight, and will deal, delay, or run before they commit to one.
**Skaruk's Name:** Cut across the gate's lintel, smaller than Vornuk's own cut at the First Hall but struck deeper - a different mason's hand.
**Ash-Streaked Walls:** Long vertical scorch runs on both long walls, floor to ceiling - whatever rite burned here left a mark the coiled-relief motif elsewhere in the Sanctum never needed to carry.
**Dart Niches:** Three narrow niches cut into the wall flanking the gate's own approach, each still holding a dart on a cocked spring; a raised flagstone a pace short of the gate is the trigger - stepping on it without first easing it down by hand -> looses all three at once, a puncture and a foul ache lasting a day for whoever is standing in line with them.
**Skaruk's Own Offering:** A small sealed alcove beside the gate, plugged with a single stone the cultists have not yet found - incense-burners and woven grave-cloth, undisturbed (Treasure IV, d20).
**Sealed Gate:** Matches the First Hall's own gate in build, its coiled-ring carving cut around Skaruk's name instead of Vornuk's. It wants the same three regalia pieces presented together, and Skaruk's own name spoken at the threshold - Vornuk's name spoken here, or Skaruk's spoken at the First Hall's gate, opens nothing.
**Exits:** worn stair - open stone, already clear from this side, west side -> E.13 The First Hall, open archway - worn stone, entrance side -> E.19 The Filled-In Grave, open archway - worn stone, south side -> E.20 The Barred Recess, open archway - worn stone, north side -> E.21 The Second Master's Niche, gated doorway - wanting the Membership Coil, the Rod of the Coil, and the Kaash Collar together, and Skaruk's name spoken at the threshold, far wall -> E.22 The Third Hall
