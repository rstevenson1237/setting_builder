E.18 **The Second Hall** (high) - *Temple Cult, Three Chambers*
Wider than the First Hall and cut a step down, this chamber carries **Skaruk's** name across its far gate. Two **cultists** crouch at that gate now, working its socket by lamplight.
*Sixty feet by thirty, floor sunk a full three feet below the First Hall, reached by a short worn stair. Ash-streaked walls run floor to corbelled ceiling, on both long sides.*
**Skaruk's Cultists:** Two of Vray's cell, working this gate without the Rod, Vray not knowing they came this far, dealing or running before they fight (Furtive, 2, Man - AD 2d6+1 [MA 1] each).
**Skaruk's Name:** Cut across the gate's lintel, smaller than Vornuk's cut but struck deeper, a different mason's hand.
**Ash-Streaked Walls:** Long vertical scorch runs on both long walls, floor to ceiling.
**Dart Niches:** Three narrow niches flanking the gate's approach, each holding a dart on a cocked spring, a raised flagstone a pace short the trigger, stepped on -> all three loose at once (Test of Constitution, 1d, Poison).
**Skaruk's Own Offering:** A small sealed alcove beside the gate, plugged with a single stone (Treasure IV, d20).
**Sealed Gate:** Matches the First Hall's gate in build, its coiled-ring carving cut around Skaruk's name, the same three regalia and Skaruk's name spoken -> the gate opens.
**Exits:** worn stair - open stone, already clear from this side, west side -> E.13 The First Hall, open archway - worn stone, entrance side -> E.19 The Filled-In Grave, open archway - worn stone, south side -> E.20 The Barred Recess, open archway - worn stone, north side -> E.21 The Second Master's Niche, gated doorway - wanting the Membership Coil, the Rod of the Coil, and the Kaash Collar together, and Skaruk's name spoken at the threshold, far wall -> E.22 The Third Hall
