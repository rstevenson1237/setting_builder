E.5 **The Hobgoblin Ledge** (Natural) - *swept, ordered, watched*
A swept ledge lies before a built stone **wall** with a **gate** in it, spears planted upright in ranks beside the door. Armoured **sentries** stand at the gate.
*Ledge twenty feet wide, forty feet above the path.*
**Wall:** Stone and timber, ten feet high, a red hand stencilled beside the gate.
**Gate:** Iron-bound and shut; it opens to a challenge in the black speech.
**Sentries:** Two hobgoblins in mail challenge anyone who climbs, and bar the gate on any who come armed (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Hobgoblin).
**Exits:** barred, east of the ledge -> I.1 The Hobgoblin Gate
