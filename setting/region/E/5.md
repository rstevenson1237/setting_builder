E.5 **The Waking Mound** (high) - *Burial Mound, Waking Mound*
The turf roof over this mound is unbroken and the drystone lining beneath it is intact - nothing has opened this one yet, and the low lintel stone over its entrance is scored with the same worn warning-mark the Barrow-Kin cut into every mound they will not enter. Cold air moves out of the entrance even at midday.
*A single mortuary chamber twelve feet across, drystone walls corbelled inward to a capped roof, the entrance a crawl-height gap under the lintel stone rather than a doorway proper. Undisturbed since it was sealed - no tool marks, no spoil, no sign anyone has been inside since the burial itself.*
**Sealed Lintel:** Cut with a warning-mark distinct from Nakhash's own coiled-relief motif - older, cruder, and matching nothing else in the field. Whoever set it meant this mound kept shut.
**Barrow Wight:** Lies still in a stone alcove at the chamber's rear, exactly as still as the rest of the mound, until the chamber is actually entered - the crawl-height gap itself does not wake it, but a person standing upright inside does (Ancient, 1, Bestiary : Barrow Wight). Once woken it does not stop short of the mound being sealed again or the wight destroyed, and its grip leaves a cold no warmth, food or rest lifts.
**Grave Niche:** Cut into the chamber's rear wall behind the alcove, plugged with a single dressed stone - grave-goods left with whoever was buried here, kept exactly as placed (Treasure III, d20).
**Low Fall in the Rear Wall:** A patch of rubble at the chamber's own rear, low to the floor, where the drystone lining has come away from the earth behind it rather than sitting flush like every other course - clearing it by hand opens a crawl-space through to another mound.
**Needle Trap:** the plug stone sits proud of its course by a finger's width; prising at it without lifting it square trips a needle set into the frame -> a puncture wound and a foul ache that does not ease for a day.
**Exits:** stuck opening - crawl-height gap under a fitted lintel, south end -> E.10 The Ward-Marked Mound, hidden passage - a collapsed grave-tunnel, low in the rear wall, opened only by clearing the fall by hand -> E.6 The Sworn Mound
