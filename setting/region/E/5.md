E.5 **The Waking Mound** (high) - *Burial Mound, Waking Mound*
The turf roof is unbroken and the drystone lining intact, and the **sealed lintel** carries the warning-mark the Barrow-Kin cut into every mound they will not enter. Cold air moves out of the entrance, at midday.
*A single mortuary chamber twelve feet across, drystone walls corbelled inward to a capped roof, the entrance a crawl-height gap, under the lintel stone. No tool marks, no spoil, and no sign anyone has been inside.*
**Sealed Lintel:** Cut with a warning-mark older and cruder, than Nakhash's coiled-relief motif.
**Barrow Wight:** Lies still in a rear alcove, the crawl-height gap not waking it, standing upright inside -> it wakes, stopping only at its own destruction, or the mound resealed (Ancient, 1, Bestiary : Barrow Wight).
**Grave Niche:** Cut into the rear wall behind the alcove, plugged with a dressed stone proud, by a finger's width, prised without lifting square -> a needle set into the frame catches, the hand (Treasure III, d20) (Test of Constitution, 1d, Poison).
**Low Fall in the Rear Wall:** A patch of rubble low at the rear, where the drystone lining has come away, from the earth, cleared by hand -> a crawl-space through to another mound.
**Exits:** stuck opening - crawl-height gap under a fitted lintel, south end -> E.10 The Ward-Marked Mound, hidden passage - a collapsed grave-tunnel, low in the rear wall, opened only by clearing the fall by hand -> E.6 The Sworn Mound
