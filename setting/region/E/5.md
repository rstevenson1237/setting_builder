E.5 **The Patrol Camp** (Lair) - *fire-lit, trampled, watched*
A trampled camp circles a fire, sentries on stakes, lean-tos and a road barrier, orders murmured among men with a snake man in command.
*200 by 150 feet. The pyramid's patrol rests here by day.*
**The patrol:** bound tribesmen with a green scale-mark inked over the heart under their clothes, led by a snake man, resting round the fire and wanting captives for the pyramid (Hostile, 8, Bestiary : Snake Man).
**The road barrier:** poles lashed across the road.
**The lean-tos:** shelters of palm and hide behind the fire.
