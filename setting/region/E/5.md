E.5 **The Library** (Medium) - *shelved, dusty, dry*

Racks of scrolls climb to the ceiling, and pens scratch somewhere among the aisles. A grey eye is drawn over the door.

*A hall 40 feet by 30. The Scholars' library, a hundred scrolls long.*

**The Racks:** Scrolls in the cynidicean script, ordered by age; a party can read what it can read.
**The Adepts:** Four copy and compare (Neutral, 4, Faction : Sihuahet). They want the demon's name; they know the cult's rites and the carved script.
**The Case:** A locked scroll case on the long table (Lore: The Three Claims).

**Exits:** an archway, east, 70 feet -> E.2 The Turning Room, an open passage, west, 70 feet -> E.8 The Workshop
