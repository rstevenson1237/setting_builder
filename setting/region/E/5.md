E.5 **The Hobgoblin Ledge** (Natural) - *swept, ordered, watched*
A built **wall** with a **gate** bars the swept ledge, spears planted in ranks before it. Two hobgoblins stand at the gate in mail.
*Forty feet above the path on the east wall.*
**Wall:** Ten feet of stone and timber, a red hand stencilled beside the gate.
**Gate:** Iron-bound and shut; the two hobgoblins call a challenge to anyone who climbs (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Hobgoblin).
**Exits:** barred, east, 20 feet through the gate -> I.1 The Hobgoblin Gate
