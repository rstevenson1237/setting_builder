Layout: The stream path (E.1) runs a mile north from the mouth, where the forest trail enters, to the head. On the west wall, going up, the kobold ledge (E.2) is 300 yards north-west, the goblin ledge (E.3) 700 and the orc ledge (E.4) 1,200. On the east wall the hobgoblin ledge (E.5) is 600 yards north-east, the gnoll ledge (E.6) 1,000 and the dry fork (E.7) 1,400. The owlbear's cave (E.10) is 900 yards north-east on the rim, and the rim track (E.11) 800 yards north-east, climbed from the crack at the mouth. At the head, the maze door (E.8) is 1,700 yards north, the shrine stair (E.9) 1,740, and the tithe niche (E.12) behind a slab at 1,720.

Places:
- E.2 The Kobold Ledge - the first mouth; three sentries with slings.
- E.3 The Goblin Ledge - the kennels and a wolf skull; two lookouts.
- E.4 The Orc Ledge - a cairn of skulls; three drunk orcs.
- E.5 The Hobgoblin Ledge - a built wall and gate; two sentries in mail.
- E.6 The Gnoll Ledge - iron collars and three lookouts.
- E.7 The Dry Fork - the way to the bugbears; two lookouts above.
- E.8 The Maze Door - a locked door at the ravine head.
- E.9 The Shrine Stair - fifty steps to the shrine; three acolytes.
- E.10 The Owlbear's Rim - an owlbear's cave on the east rim.

Inhabitants: Six Wolves hunt E.1, an Owlbear lairs at E.10, and the ledges are watched by three of The Gubak, two of The Zhakuk, three of The Rokmok, two of The Burgnaz, three of The Kurzun, two of The Zurnaz and three of The Black Altar.

Loot: No game or forage lies in the ravine that is not an Encounter. Every prize lies inside a holding, and A.7 pays 80% of its value.

Secrets:
- A fallen slab at the stair's foot hides a crevice where the tribes leave their tithe - the Zurnaz know; the scrape marks round the slab (E.9) bring it out.
- The rim track along the east wall looks down on every ledge on it - no tribe watches it; the crack near the mouth (E.1) leads up to it.

Leads:
- Rumour 2 - E.2 Sentries.
- Rumour 7 - E.12 Sacks, E.7 Handprints.
- Rumour 9 - E.6 Gnolls.
- Rumour 10 - E.5 Gate.
- Rumour 11 - E.8 Door.

Tables:
d6 Encounter, per failed Difficulty roll
1. Six wolves trot the path at dusk and circle the party.
2. A tin rattle sounds from the kobold ledge, and a sling-stone clips the rock.
3. Orcs roar from the cairn, and two come down the slope to the path.
4. A hobgoblin horn sounds twice from the east wall.
5. A bugbear's soot handprint on the rock beside the path, still wet.
6. Chanting drifts down the stair from the shrine.
