E.19 **The Filled-In Grave** (low) - *Burial Mound, Corbel Vault*
Once a proper vestry, its robing-niches now packed with **hurried burials**, rather than vestments. The fill runs **loose** on the far wall.
*A vestry chamber, fifteen feet across, its original fittings long since built over with close-packed grave-fill. Cramped compared to the room it was meant to be.*
**Hurried Burials:** Packed in without the care given, the masters' niches nearby.
**Loose Fill:** One patch of grave-fill low on the, far wall sits looser than the rest, a thin trickle of pale silt sifting, from it, cleared by hand -> a crawl-space through.
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, entrance side -> E.18 The Second Hall, hidden passage - loose grave-fill on the far wall, cleared by hand -> E.15 The Charnel Cut
