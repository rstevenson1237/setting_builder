E.19 **The Filled-In Grave** (low) - *Burial Mound, Corbel Vault*
Once a proper vestry, its robing-niches now packed with hurried burials rather than vestments - whoever ran out of room elsewhere in the Sanctum started using this one instead, without much ceremony about it.
*A vestry chamber, fifteen feet across, its original fittings long since built over with close-packed grave-fill. Cramped compared to the room it was clearly meant to be.*
**Hurried Burials:** Packed in without the care given the masters' own niches nearby - whoever did this was in a hurry, or had stopped believing anyone would come back to check the work.
**Loose Fill:** One patch of grave-fill low on the far wall sits noticeably looser than the rest, a thin trickle of pale silt sifting from it even undisturbed; clearing it by hand -> opens onto a crawl-space through to a pit somewhere on the other side, cleared already from the far end.
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, entrance side -> E.18 The Second Hall, hidden passage - loose grave-fill on the far wall, cleared by hand -> E.15 The Charnel Cut
