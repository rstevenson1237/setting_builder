E.22 **The Third Hall** (high) - *Temple Cult, Three Chambers*
The grandest of the three chambers, cut a further step down and wider still than the Second Hall, ends in a raised dais bearing **Gholuk's** name in the deepest-cut lettering of all three masters. The air here sits noticeably heavier than in the halls above - sealed since Gholuk's own reign ended, and never once reopened until now.
*Seventy feet by thirty-five, the dais at the far end raised three steps above the main floor. Corbelled ceiling higher here than either hall above it - built, unmistakably, to be the procession's actual destination rather than a stop on the way to one.*
**Gholuk's Name:** Cut into the dais itself rather than a lintel, deeper than either of the other two masters' names - the third and last chamber-master, and by the cut of it, the one whose reign this Sanctum most wanted remembered.
**Sealed Air:** Nothing has moved this air since the gate above last closed on it - thin and heavy in a way that costs real effort. Working hard here - fighting, forcing, hauling - counts double against the Danger track's countdown unless it is done from the dais steps themselves, where a vent cut into the stone still draws a thread of fresher air up from below.
**Gholuk's Own Offering:** Set into a shallow recess at the dais's base, unmarked and unguarded but for the room's own sealed air - grave-goods left for the third master, richer than either of the halls above (Treasure III, d20).
