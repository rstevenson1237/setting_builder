E.22 **The Third Hall** (high) - *Temple Cult, Three Chambers*
The grandest of the three chambers, cut a further step down and wider still, ends in a raised dais bearing **Gholuk's** name. The **air** here sits heavier than in the halls above.
*Seventy feet by thirty-five, the dais at the far end raised, three steps above the main floor. Corbelled ceiling higher here than either hall above, built to be the procession's destination.*
**Gholuk's Name:** Cut into the dais itself, rather than a lintel, deeper than either other master's name.
**Sealed Air:** Thin and heavy, costing real effort, working hard here counting double against the, Danger track, except from the dais steps.
**Dais Vent:** Cut into the stone at the dais steps, still drawing a thread of fresher air, up from below.
**Gholuk's Own Offering:** Set into a shallow recess, at the dais's base, unmarked and unguarded (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** open stair - worn stone, already clear from this side, west side -> E.18 The Second Hall, open archway - worn stone, east side -> E.23 The Third Master's Niche, open archway - worn stone, far end past the dais -> E.24 Nakhash's Hoard
