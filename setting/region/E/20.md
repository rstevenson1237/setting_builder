E.20 **The Barred Recess** (medium) - *Elder Sorcery, Death-Script*
A guardroom off the Second Hall, its one inner recess braced shut from this side with a timber bar gone hard as stone with age. **Skaruk's** name is cut small above the recess's own frame, the same hand that cut it larger at the gate beyond.
*A guardroom twelve feet across, otherwise bare - a bench cut into the wall, long since gone to dust, and the one barred recess set into the far wall.*
**Skaruk's Mark:** A small cut of the same name seen at the Second Hall's gate, here above a door rather than a threshold - this room answered to him specifically, whatever it was for.
**Barred Recess:** Braced shut with a timber bar seated in stone sockets on this side - meant to keep something in rather than out. A weighted brace is rigged above the bar itself; lifting the bar without first easing the brace down by hand -> the weight swings free and drops on whoever is lifting (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**What Was Kept:** Behind the recess, once the bar is cleared - a guard's own kit, old but sound, left exactly where it was racked (Treasure II, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, entrance side -> E.18 The Second Hall
