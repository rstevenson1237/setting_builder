E.20 **The Barred Recess** (medium) - *Elder Sorcery, Death-Script*
A guardroom off the Second Hall, its one **barred recess** braced shut, from this side with a timber bar, gone hard with age. **Skaruk's** name is cut small above the frame.
*A guardroom twelve feet across, otherwise bare, a bench cut into the wall long since gone to dust, and the one barred recess in the, far wall.*
**Skaruk's Mark:** The same name as the Second Hall's gate, here above a door, rather than a threshold.
**Barred Recess:** Braced shut with a timber bar seated, in stone sockets on this side, a weighted brace rigged above it, the bar lifted without easing the brace -> the weight swings free onto whoever lifts (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**What Was Kept:** Behind the recess once the bar is cleared, left exactly where it was racked (Treasure II, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, entrance side -> E.18 The Second Hall
