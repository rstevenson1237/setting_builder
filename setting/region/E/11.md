E.11 **The Rim Track** (Hidden) - *narrow, windy, unseen*
A goat **track** runs the cliff top, two feet wide above a long drop. A **hollow** is trodden flat above the ledges.
*Runs the length of the east wall, ending at the owlbear's cave.*
**Track:** Loose stone at the edge gives under a heavy load (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing).
**Hollow:** Flat and trodden; lying in it for a watch gives a view of every east-wall ledge (Local Procedure: Watching the Cave Mouths).
**Exits:** down, by the crack at the mouth -> E.1 The Stream Path
