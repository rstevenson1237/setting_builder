E.11 **The Priestesses' Cells** (Low) - *whitewashed, hushed, bare*

Six whitewashed cells open round a washing basin. A prayer is whispered behind one curtain.

*A room 20 feet square where the Priesthood sleep.*

**The Basin:** Stone, kept full; a stranger is asked to wash here before the temple.
**The Cells:** A mat and a lamp in each, a sun-disc painted over every curtain (Faction : Ruhiahe).

**Exits:** a wooden door -> E.4 The Temple of the Bell
