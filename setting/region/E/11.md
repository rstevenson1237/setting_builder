E.11 **The Rim Track** (Hidden) - *narrow, windy, unseen*
A goat **track** runs the cliff top above the east wall, two feet wide, the edge **crumbling** on one side. Every ledge on the east wall lies below it.
*Track runs the length of the east wall; the owlbear's rim is its far end.*
**Track:** Two feet wide, the cliff edge beside it; the ledges lie in view below, and a hollow is trodden flat above them.
**Crumbling Edge:** Loose stone at the edge gives under a heavy load (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing).
**Watch:** A party lying in the hollow can learn the ledges' habits (Local Procedure: Watching the Cave Mouths).
**Exits:** down, by the crack at the mouth -> E.1 The Stream Path
