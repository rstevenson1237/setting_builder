E.11 **The Old Procession Track** (low) - *Old Road, Procession Track*
A shallow, worn ramp rises out of the open marsh two miles south of the causeway and runs straight into the mound field proper - older than any single mound beside it, cut long before the field grew up around it. This is where the ground stops being open marsh and starts being Nakhash.
*A packed-earth track twenty feet wide, worn a foot below the surrounding ground by however long it has carried traffic. Runs dead straight north to south, the mound field opening out on both sides as it climbs.*
**Worn Ramp:** Packed hard enough that nothing has grown on it in longer than the mounds have stood - a processional road, built before whatever it served needed defending.
**Traveler's Loss:** A boot-buckle and a few coin, trodden into the verge by someone who passed through and didn't notice dropping them (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open track - open marsh, south end -> the Marfen causeway, two miles north (B.3 Burial Mounds), open path - worn ground, north end -> E.12 The Empty Mound, open path - worn ground, west side -> E.1 The Sealed Approach, open path - worn ground, east side -> E.7 The Cut-Stone Mound, open gap - broken drystone, north side -> E.4 The Robbed Mound
