E.11 **The Old Procession Track** (low) - *Old Road, Procession Track*
A shallow **worn ramp** rises out of the open marsh and runs straight into the mound field. This is where open marsh stops and Nakhash starts.
*A packed-earth track twenty feet wide, worn a foot below the surrounding ground. Runs dead straight north to south, the mound field opening out on both sides, as it climbs.*
**Worn Ramp:** Packed hard and bare of growth, a processional road, cut before whatever it served needed defending.
**Traveler's Loss:** Trodden into the verge by someone passing through (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open track - open marsh, south end -> the Marfen causeway, two miles north (B.3 Burial Mounds), open path - worn ground, north end -> E.12 The Empty Mound, open path - worn ground, west side -> E.1 The Sealed Approach, open path - worn ground, east side -> E.7 The Cut-Stone Mound, open gap - broken drystone, north side -> E.4 The Robbed Mound
