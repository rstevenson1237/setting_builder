E.4 **The Robbed Mound** (low) - *Burial Mound, Robbed Mound*
Picked clean long before anyone alive was digging - the niches stand open and empty, bones scattered rather than resting, and whoever did this left in a hurry or without much care for what they disturbed.
*An ossuary twelve feet across, three burial niches all standing open, bones pulled out and left where they fell. Old spoil, long settled, covers the floor - this happened decades ago at least, not recently.*
**Scattered Bones:** Pulled from every niche and left in a rough heap rather than restacked - whoever robbed this mound was after grave-goods, not the dead themselves.
**Filled Floor-Niche:** One low niche cut into the floor itself rather than the walls sits packed with settled earth, unlike the wall niches, which stand open - the robbers missed it, or never knew to look down; clearing the packed earth by hand reaches what they left behind (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open gap - broken drystone, south side -> E.11 The Old Procession Track, open path - worn ground, west side -> E.9 Barrow-Kin Watch
