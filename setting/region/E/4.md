E.4 **The Statue Court** (Ruin) - *fallen, carved, hushed*
Fallen serpent statues lie in a hushed court, broken colonnades round it and a dry fountain at its centre, a faint hissing in the air.
*200 by 200 feet. The forecourt of a temple, burned in the rising.*
**The statues:** carved serpents toppled across the court, their mouths hollow; a party that enters hears the hissing speech whispered in the head (Test of Sanity, 1d).
**The dry fountain:** a basin choked with leaves at the court's centre.
