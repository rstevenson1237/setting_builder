E.4 **The Temple of the Bell** (Medium) - *incensed, quiet, painted*

Incense smoke hangs over a painted bell on a dais, pews facing it. A bell is rung once and its note fades.

*A temple 30 feet by 20, the Priesthood's seat, reached by the bell-rune arch. The walls show the demon's face scraped from the murals.*

**The Bell:** Bronze, on a dais; ringing it brings every priest on the level.
**The Acolytes:** Four ring and pray (Wary, 4, Faction : Ruhiahe). They want the stair sealed and know the rites that seal a door; they ask a stranger to wash and state its purpose.
**The Murals:** Painted with a sun and a stair; a face scraped away in each (The Demon Is Invited).

**Exits:** a bell-rune archway -> E.2 The Turning Hall, an open passage -> E.7 The Temple Cistern, a wooden door -> E.11 The Priestesses' Cells
