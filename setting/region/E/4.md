E.4 **The Orc Ledge** (Natural) - *wide, stinking, skull-strewn*
A **cairn** of skulls stands before a cave at the head of the west wall, bone littering the whole shelf. **Orcs** lie drunk across it among broken bottles.
*The last cave on the west wall, 300 yards up; the ledge lies fifty feet above the path.*
**Cairn:** Skulls heaped waist-high at the mouth, a notched tusk nailed over the cave.
**Drunk Orcs:** Three orcs with axes roar at the path and charge smaller parties (Hostile, 3, Bestiary : Orc).
**Exits:** open, west, 20 feet past the cairn -> H.1 The Cairn Mouth
