E.4 **The Robbed Mound** (low) - *Burial Mound, Robbed Mound*
Picked clean long before anyone alive was digging, the niches standing open and empty, bones **scattered** rather than resting. Old spoil covers the floor.
*An ossuary twelve feet across, three burial niches all standing open, bones pulled out and left where they fell. Old spoil, long settled, covers the floor.*
**Scattered Bones:** Pulled from every niche, and left in a rough heap, rather than restacked.
**Filled Floor-Niche:** One low niche cut into the floor, sits packed with settled earth, where the wall niches stand open, cleared by hand -> what the robbers left (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open gap - broken drystone, south side -> E.11 The Old Procession Track, open path - worn ground, west side -> E.9 Barrow-Kin Watch
