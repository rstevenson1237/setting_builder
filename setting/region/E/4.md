E.4 **The Orc Ledge** (Natural) - *wide, stinking, skull-strewn*
A wide ledge lies strewn with bones and skulls, a **cairn** of them at the mouth of a cave. Orcs sit **drunk** on the cairn.
*Ledge twenty feet wide, at the head of the west wall.*
**Skulls and Bones:** Hundreds of them, from deer to horses and men, a notched tusk nailed above the cave mouth.
**Cairn:** Skulls heaped waist-high at the cave mouth.
**Drunk Orcs:** Three orcs with axes sit on the cairn among broken bottles and roar at the path; they charge smaller parties (Hostile, 3, Bestiary : Orc).
**Exits:** open, west of the cairn -> H.1 The Cairn Mouth
