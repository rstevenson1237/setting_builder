E.15 **The Charnel Cut** (medium) - *The Unlettered Dead, Charnel Silence*
A deep **pit** cut into the chamber floor holds bones in number rather than order, dozens packed in rather than laid out. Something in the pit shifts at footsteps.
*A charnel pit ten feet deep and twenty across, cut into the floor of an otherwise plain side chamber. No niches and no markers, and whatever went in went without the rite.*
**Unlettered Dead:** Two dozen or so, raised without a binding or a purpose, closing on whatever is warm and nearest, once disturbed (Aimless, 2 dozen, Bestiary : The Unlettered Dead).
**Pit's Far Wall:** Rough-cut and unfinished, a seam running low along it, where the stone does not match, worked open by hand from the pit floor -> a crawl-space through.
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, west side -> E.13 The First Hall, hidden passage - a worked seam at the pit's far wall, cleared by hand from the pit floor -> E.19 The Filled-In Grave
