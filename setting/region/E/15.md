E.15 **The Charnel Cut** (medium) - *The Unlettered Dead, Charnel Silence*
A deep pit cut into the chamber floor holds bones in number rather than order - dozens buried without ceremony, packed in rather than laid out. Something in the pit shifts at the sound of footsteps, and does not stop once it starts.
*A charnel pit ten feet deep and twenty across, cut into the floor of an otherwise plain side chamber. No niches, no markers - whatever went into this pit went in without the rite the rest of the Sanctum's dead received.*
**Unlettered Dead:** Two dozen or so, raised without a binding and without a purpose, closing on whatever is warm and nearest once disturbed - counted in dozens rather than as individuals, and not to be fought so much as gotten past or sealed off (Aimless, 2 dozen, Bestiary : The Unlettered Dead).
**Pit's Far Wall:** Rough-cut and unfinished compared to the rest of the chamber, a seam running low along it where the stone doesn't quite match its neighbours in colour; working the seam open by hand, from the pit floor itself -> clears a crawl-space through to a room the pit's own dead have never reached.
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, west side -> E.13 The First Hall, hidden passage - a worked seam at the pit's far wall, cleared by hand from the pit floor -> E.19 The Filled-In Grave
