E.15 **The Stirge Roost** (High) - *high, fouled, fluttering*

A light well choked with droppings, ledges climbing its walls and bones heaped below. Wings flutter overhead.

*A round room 30 feet across, an old light well reached by the wing-rune arch. Stirges roost on the ledges and hunt down the passages at dusk; their droppings show in the long store.*

**The Stirges:** They hang from the ledges until something warm comes in (Hostile, 4-8, Bestiary : Stirge).
**The Bones:** Of those the stirges fed on, with their packs (Treasure II, d20).

**Exits:** a wing-rune archway -> E.2 The Turning Hall
