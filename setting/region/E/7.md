E.7 **The Temple Cistern** (Medium) - *cool, still, echoing*

A square basin of still water sits in a stepped floor, water dripping into it. A grey stone shape crouches on the rim.

*A room 30 feet square, the Priesthood's clean water. A gargoyle guards it.*

**The Basin:** Clean water, enough for a day's skins; drinking brings the gargoyle.
**The Gargoyle:** Crouched on the rim (Hostile, 1-2, Bestiary : Gargoyle); it strikes anyone who draws water without a priest's word.
**The Mark:** A sun-disc painted on the wall (Faction : Ruhiahe).

**Exits:** a wooden door -> E.2 The Turning Room, an open passage -> E.4 The Temple of the Bell
