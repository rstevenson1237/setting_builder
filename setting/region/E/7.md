E.7 **The Cut-Stone Mound** (medium) - *Standing Stones, Leader's Name*
A small shrine-mound, its single chamber centred on a standing stone cut with a name in Essath rather than any image - **Vornuk**, worn shallow by weather at the surface but still legible. A low growl answers anyone who steps in before they see what made it.
*A shrine chamber ten feet across, drystone walls corbelled to a low capped roof, the standing stone set upright at its centre rather than against a wall. Colder and danker than the mounds either side of it - this one vents poorly.*
**Standing Stone:** Cut with Vornuk's name in worn Essath lettering, the same name the First Hall's own gate carries far below - the first place a party is likely to read it, and the first of at least two more places it repeats.
**Grey Wolves:** A pair, scouting the field's freshly turned ground rather than denned here - drawn by the scent of newly opened earth, tense rather than settled, and quick to peel off if given room to (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Grey Wolf).
**Old Offering Niche:** A shallow cut below the standing stone, holding what little was left here for Vornuk once - most of it long since taken (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open path - worn ground, south side -> E.11 The Old Procession Track, open path - worn ground, east side -> E.6 The Sworn Mound
