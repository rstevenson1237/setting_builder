E.7 **The Cut-Stone Mound** (medium) - *Standing Stones, Leader's Name*
A small shrine-mound, its chamber centred on a **standing stone** cut with a name in Essath, rather than an image, **Vornuk**, weathered but legible. A low growl answers anyone who steps in.
*A shrine chamber ten feet across, drystone walls corbelled to a low capped roof, the standing stone upright at its centre. Colder and danker than the mounds either side, and this one vents poorly.*
**Standing Stone:** Cut with Vornuk's name, in worn Essath lettering, the same name the First Hall's gate carries.
**Grey Wolves:** A pair scouting the freshly turned ground, drawn by the scent of opened earth, quick to peel off (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Grey Wolf).
**Old Offering Niche:** A shallow cut below the standing stone, holding what was left here for Vornuk (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open path - worn ground, south side -> E.11 The Old Procession Track, open path - worn ground, east side -> E.6 The Sworn Mound
