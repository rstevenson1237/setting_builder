E.7 **The Python Temple** (Lair) - *domed, dark, hissing*
A domed temple hisses in the vine-dark, sour musk in the air, a cracked altar under the dome and snake pits round an inner court.
*150 by 150 feet. A priests' hall of the old empire.*
**The python:** a thirty-foot mottled snake coiled in the dome's dark (Hostile, 1, Bestiary : Giant Python); a long hiss comes before it is seen.
**The cracked altar:** a carved slab split across, stained dark.
**The snake pits:** empty hollows round the inner court, floored with shed skins.
