E.7 **The Dry Fork** (Natural) - *dusty, silent, overhung*
A dry stream **bed** runs east under overhanging walls, soot **handprints** at head height on the rock. Nothing makes a sound in it.
*Two hundred yards of bed before the den.*
**Bed:** Fifteen feet of dust that keeps every track.
**Handprints:** Soot, on both walls, higher than a man can reach.
**Lookouts:** Two bugbears lie along the overhang and drop on the last in line (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Bugbear).
**Exits:** open, east, 20 yards up the dry bed -> K.1 The Dry Bed
