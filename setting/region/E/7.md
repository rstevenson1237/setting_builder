E.7 **The Dry Fork** (Natural) - *dusty, silent, overhung*
A dry **stream bed** runs east from the ravine under overhanging walls, soot **handprints** at head height on the rock and sticks smeared dark at its mouth. No sound comes from it.
*Bed fifteen feet wide and running two hundred yards before it narrows to the den.*
**Stream Bed:** Dust a hand deep that holds every track.
**Handprints:** Soot handprints at head height, one for each bugbear that has passed.
**Lookouts:** Two bugbears lie along the overhang and drop on the last in line (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Bugbear).
**Exits:** open, east, up the dry bed -> K.1 The Dry Bed
