E.14 **The Hidden Strongroom** (Low) - *secret, sealed, dry*

A sealed room of chests, the dust on the floor unmarked. The air is dead and still.

*A room 20 feet square and 10 high between the long store and the turning hall, its two ways in hidden: a sliding shelf at the store's end and a plastered arch on the hall's key rune.*

**The Chests:** Iron-bound, against three walls, the old level's stores of coin (Treasure IV, d20).
**The Plastered Arch:** From inside, a door of plaster over the key rune; it breaks out with a blow.

**Exits:** a sliding shelf -> E.13 The Long Store, the plastered arch -> E.2 The Turning Hall
