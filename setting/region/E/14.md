E.14 **The Unmarked Grave** (low) - *Burial Mound, Corbel Vault*
A modest reliquary, one low shelf holding a scatter of bone fragments rather than a full burial - somebody's remains, kept rather than interred whole. A single line of Essath under the shelf names no one but "a servant of **Vornuk**."
*A reliquary chamber, eight feet across, corbelled roof low enough to duck under at its edges. Plainer than either master's own niche nearby - built for someone who served rather than led.*
**Servant's Shelf:** A stone shelf holding bone fragments and little else, the inscription beneath it worn but legible - unnamed, but plainly loyal enough to be kept close to the First Hall.
**Loose Shelf-Stone:** One stone at the shelf's near edge sits slightly higher than its neighbours; pressing it flush by hand -> a shallow cavity beneath opens, holding what little this servant was buried with (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, entrance side -> E.13 The First Hall
