E.14 **The Unmarked Grave** (low) - *Burial Mound, Corbel Vault*
A modest reliquary, one low shelf holding bone fragments rather than a full burial. A line of Essath beneath names no one but a servant of **Vornuk**.
*A reliquary chamber eight feet across, corbelled roof low enough to duck under, at its edges. Plainer than either master's niche nearby, built for someone who served.*
**Servant's Shelf:** A stone shelf holding bone fragments, the inscription beneath worn but legible, naming a servant of Vornuk.
**Loose Shelf-Stone:** One stone at the shelf's near edge sits, higher than its neighbours, pressed flush by hand -> a shallow cavity beneath opens (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, entrance side -> E.13 The First Hall
