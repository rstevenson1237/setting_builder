E.24 **Nakhash's Hoard** (high) - *Elder Sorcery, Coiled Statue*
Past the dais, the Sanctum ends in a vault where six **coiled wards** stand around the walls, dust-free in a sealed room. **Gholuk's** name is cut a final time above the threshold, the **kept hoard** stacked beneath it.
*A rounded chamber forty feet across, domed rather than corbelled, the only room in the Sanctum, built to that shape. Cold, faintly reptilian, and utterly still.*
**Six Coiled Wards:** Carved serpent-shapes set around the curve, inert until the regalia cross the threshold -> all six wake and take the carrier, the regalia set down -> all six still again (Dormant, 6, Bestiary : Stone Ward).
**Gholuk's Final Name:** Cut above the vault's threshold, the same cut as at the Third Hall, and its niche.
**Kept Hoard:** Stacked along the curved wall, in worked chests and open trays, three chamber-masters' worth kept together (Treasure V, d20).
**Discarded Corner:** Pushed into the chamber's one flat recess, left out of the rest of the hoard (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, single entrance -> E.22 The Third Hall
