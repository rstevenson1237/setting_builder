E.24 **Nakhash's Hoard** (high) - *Elder Sorcery, Coiled Statue*
Past the dais, the Sanctum ends in a vault where six statues stand coiled around the walls in the shape of serpents rearing to strike, dust-free in a room that has otherwise sat sealed and undisturbed since the last reign. **Gholuk's** name is cut a final time above the vault's own threshold, and beneath it the cult's kept wealth sits stacked exactly as it was left.
*A rounded chamber forty feet across, domed rather than corbelled - the only room in the Sanctum built to that shape. Cold, faintly reptilian, and utterly still; nothing here has stirred in longer than the mounds above have been mounds.*
**Six Coiled Wards:** Carved serpent-shapes set around the chamber's own curve, inert until the moment a party carrying the Membership Coil, the Rod of the Coil, or the Kaash Collar crosses the threshold - all six wake together, give no warning between inert and acting, and take hold of whoever is carrying the regalia first (Dormant, 6, Bestiary : Stone Ward). Nothing living has ever reported taking one down; only the binding-script cut into each one's own base stops it, and reaching that means getting behind it. Per the same entry, setting down what a Ward woke for - here, at the threshold, or back at the dais above - returns all six to stillness at once, whatever has happened in the meantime.
**Gholuk's Final Name:** Cut above the vault's threshold, matching the two cuts already seen at the Third Hall and its niche - the same name, meant to be recognised on sight by now rather than read fresh.
**Kept Hoard:** Stacked along the chamber's curved wall in worked chests and open trays exactly as the last reign left it, three chamber-masters' worth kept together rather than divided (Treasure V, d20).
**Discarded Corner:** Pushed into the chamber's one flat recess, plain enough that nobody who packed the rest of the hoard bothered to add it in (Treasure III, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, single entrance -> E.22 The Third Hall
