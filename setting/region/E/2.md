E.2 **The Turning Hall** (High) - *round, runed, turning*

A round drum of stone with a passage straight through it, eight arches round its rim, each with a rune cut in its keystone. Inside the drum the runes are carved again, in pairs. Stone grinds underfoot when it turns.

*A drum 40 feet across and 16 feet high, set in a ring of eight arches. Its passage always joins two arches on opposite sides; the other six face blank drum wall. Each arch shows its own rune, so a party can learn which rune is which passage. At rest the drum joins the door rune (E.1) and the jar rune (E.13). A party can join a faction here (Local Procedure: Joining a faction). (The Cynidiceans Raise the City)*

**The Paired Runes:** Four pairs carved inside the passage, each pair the runes of two opposite arches: door and jar (E.1, E.13); hawk and eye (E.3, E.5); bell and falling stair (E.4, E.9); wing and key (E.15, E.14). Pressing both runes of a pair together turns the drum until it joins those two arches. All three branches know the system.
**The Rim:** Grooves at the drum's edge. Whoever stands across the gap as the drum turns is caught between drum and arch (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing).
**The Arch Runes:** A door, a hawk, a bell, an eye, a falling stair, a jar and a wing, each over its arch; the eighth arch is a blank plastered wall.
**The Key Rune:** Its pair is carved inside the drum but no arch shows it. Pressed with the wing, the drum turns to the plastered wall, and knocking the plaster out opens a passage to the hidden strongroom (E.14). The Scholars know it.
**The Reliquary:** Set in the drum's wall, a casket of offerings (Treasure III, d20).

**Exits:** a door-rune archway -> E.1 The Stair Landing, a hawk-rune archway -> E.3 The Guard Hall, a bell-rune archway -> E.4 The Temple of the Bell, an eye-rune archway -> E.5 The Library, a falling-stair archway -> E.9 The Corridor of Darts, a jar-rune archway -> E.13 The Long Store, a wing-rune archway -> E.15 The Stirge Roost
