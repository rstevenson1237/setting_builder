E.2 **The Kobold Ledge** (Natural) - *narrow, rattling, chalked*
A narrow **ledge** hangs on the west wall, grinning faces chalked on the rock above a low **hole**. **Sentries** crouch in the rubble with slings.
*Ledge six feet wide, a few paces above the path.*
**Chalk Faces:** Dog-faced grins, a dozen of them, chalked over each other on the rock.
**Sentries:** Three kobolds with slings watch the path, and shake a rattle if anyone climbs (Wary, 3, Bestiary : Kobold); tin scraps and scrawls litter the stone.
**Low Hole:** A burrow mouth at the back of the ledge, tall enough to crawl through.
**Exits:** low, west of the ledge -> F.1 The Rattle Passage
