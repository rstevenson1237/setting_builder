E.2 **The Dug Mound** (medium) - *Burial Mound, Fresh Spoil*
Its turf roof is stripped back and its drystone lining broken through, a **windlass** rigged over the gap, a rope running down into the dark. A **rope-watch** sits at the edge, watching the rope rather than the mound.
