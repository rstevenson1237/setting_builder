E.2 **The Dug Mound** (medium) - *Burial Mound, Fresh Spoil*
Its turf roof has been stripped back and its drystone lining broken through deliberately, a windlass rigged over the gap and a rope running down into the dark - somebody has been digging here for weeks, and a single cultist sits at the rope's edge now, watching it rather than the mound around him.
*A barrow chamber gutted from above, its original drystone lining broken outward into a wide gap rather than entered through the mound's own low door. Fresh spoil is heaped to one side, still dark and loose.*
**Rope-Watch:** One of Vray's cell, posted here to mind the windlass and warn of anything coming up it that shouldn't - he is expecting his own people, not a party, and says so before he does anything else (Watchful, 1, Man - AD 2d6+1 [MA 1]).
**Windlass:** Fixed over the broken gap, rope run down and out of sight - the rigged way down to E.13 The First Hall below, built by the cell rather than found this way.
**Old Grave-Goods:** Turned up with the spoil and set aside rather than kept - the cell wants what is below, not what this mound's own dead were buried with (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open path - worn ground, south side -> E.8 Cultist Dig Camp, open shaft - rigged with windlass and rope, straight down -> E.13 The First Hall
