E.2 **The Gate Tower** (Ruin) - *leaning, carved, moss-hung*
A leaning carved gate-tower hangs with moss, creaking, grit falling from its stones, the gate arch beside it tumbled.
*60 by 60 feet. The east gate of a snake people's town, burned in the rising.*
**The leaning tower:** carved stone hung with moss and the burn-marks of the tribes' rising; grit falls from it (Test of Constitution, 2d, Crushing).
**The gate arch:** tumbled stone with a stair behind it.
