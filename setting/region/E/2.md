E.2 **The Turning Room** (High) - *round, runed, turning*

A round floor of raised runes, eight arches round its rim and a single rune at the centre. Stone grinds underfoot as the room turns.

*A round room 60 feet across and 16 feet high, with a reliquary on a central dais. The room turns to face the rune pressed, so each exit is chosen and the halls move. A party can join a faction here (Local Procedure: Joining a faction). (The Cynidiceans Raise the City)*

**The Runes:** Raised, one for each exit; pressing one turns the room until the matching arch faces the party. The central rune turns it back.
**The Arches:** Open arches north to the Guard Hall (E.3), north-west to the temple (E.4) and south to the library (E.5); an iron door north-east to the armoury (E.6, locked by the Armoury Key); a wooden door west to the cistern (E.7), another south-west to the workshop (E.8); stone passages east to the landing (E.1) and south-east to the stair (E.9).
**The Reliquary:** On the dais, a casket of offerings (Treasure III, d20).
**The Lock:** The iron door north-east is locked, its keyhole shaped like a hawk's head (Keys: The Armoury Key).

**Exits:** a stone passage -> E.1 The Stair Landing, a stone passage -> E.9 The Stair Chamber, an archway -> E.3 The Guard Hall, an archway -> E.4 The Temple of the Bell, an archway -> E.5 The Library, an iron door -> E.6 The Armoury, a wooden door -> E.7 The Temple Cistern, a wooden door -> E.8 The Workshop
