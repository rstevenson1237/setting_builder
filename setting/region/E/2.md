E.2 **The Kobold Ledge** (Natural) - *narrow, rattling, chalked*
Grinning faces are chalked on the rock above a crawlhole at the back of a six-foot shelf. **Kobolds** squat in the rubble with slings.
*Ledge a few paces above the path, the first on the west wall.*
**Sentries:** Three kobolds in the rubble sling stones at the path, and shake a rattle if anyone climbs (Wary, 3, Bestiary : Kobold).
**Chalk Grins:** Painted over each other on the rock, the newest still wet.
**Crawlhole:** A burrow mouth tall enough only to crawl through; a tin rattle hangs just inside.
**Exits:** open, east, 20 feet -> F.1 The Rattle Passage
