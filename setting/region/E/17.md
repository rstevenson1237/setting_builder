E.17 **The First Master's Niche** (medium) - *Temple Cult, Leader's Name*
A small chantry sealed since Vornuk's day, its walls furred grey-green with **grave-mould** that has taken to the worked stone. A written record sits propped in a niche, behind the growth.
*A chantry chamber, cramped at ten feet across, sealed tightly enough that the growth has had five centuries to spread. Cold to the touch, everywhere the growth has taken.*
**Grave-Mould:** A soft grey-green growth, over most of the walls, carried by whatever touches it, into cloth, leather and rope (Spreading, 1 patch, Bestiary : Grave-Mould).
**Vornuk's Own Record:** Propped in the niche behind the mould-covered wall, reached by brushing past the growth (Lore: Vornuk's Tally).
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, entrance side -> E.13 The First Hall
