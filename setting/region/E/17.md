E.17 **The First Master's Niche** (medium) - *Temple Cult, Leader's Name*
A small chantry sealed since Vornuk's own day, its walls furred grey-green with a growth that has taken to the worked stone and nothing else in the room. A written record sits propped in a niche behind the growth, legible if it can be reached without touching what's grown over everything around it.
*A chantry chamber, cramped at ten feet across, sealed tightly enough that the growth on its walls has had five centuries undisturbed to spread. Cold to the touch everywhere the growth has taken - the one room in the Sanctum where that's true.*
**Grave-Mould:** A soft, cold, grey-green growth covering most of the chamber's walls and the niche's own edge, dying within a season of open air but thriving in a room shut this long; what touches it carries it, into cloth, leather, and rope alike (Spreading, 1 patch, Bestiary : Grave-Mould).
**Vornuk's Own Record:** Propped in the niche behind the mould-covered wall, reachable by anyone willing to brush past the growth to reach it - a written tally naming Vornuk as the First Hall's own master (Lore: Vornuk's Tally).
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, entrance side -> E.13 The First Hall
