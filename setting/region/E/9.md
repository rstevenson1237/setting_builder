E.9 **The Shrine Stair** (Ruin) - *worn, black-stained, climbing*
A stair of fifty worn **steps** climbs the cliff, black stains on every tread. **Acolytes** wait on the landing at its head, and a fallen **slab** leans against the rock beside the lowest step.
*Stair six feet wide, cut by the makers as a pilgrim way. A gong hangs on the landing.*
**Steps:** Fifty worn steps, black-stained, a black candle stub burned on the lowest.
**Acolytes:** Three acolytes in black robes with knives guard the landing and sound a gong at any climber.
**Slab:** Leaned against the cliff beside the lowest step; fresh scrape marks lie round its foot, and it moves with a shove.
**Exits:** worn, north end of the stair -> M.1 The Stair-Hall, hidden, behind the slab, west -> E.12 The Tithe Niche
