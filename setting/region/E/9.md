E.9 **The Shrine Stair** (Ruin) - *worn, black-stained, climbing*
**Steps** stained black climb fifty treads to a landing, where **acolytes** in black robes wait with knives. A **slab** leans against the cliff at the foot of the stair.
*A gong hangs on the landing.*
**Steps:** Cut square to one pattern, the makers' script worn on the risers; a trodden path from each cave mouth meets the lowest tread, where a black candle stub is burned.
**Acolytes:** Three stand at the landing and sound the gong at any climber.
**Slab:** Scrape marks show at its foot; it shifts with a shove, and a crevice lies behind it.
**Exits:** worn, north end of the stair, fifty steps up -> M.1 The Stair-Hall, hidden, behind the slab, west -> E.12 The Tithe Niche
