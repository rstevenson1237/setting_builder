E.9 **The Corridor of Darts** (Medium) - *long, pocked, sloping*

A long corridor slopes away from the falling-stair arch, its walls pocked with small holes. Wind moans from a stair at its end.

*A corridor 10 feet wide and 50 long, the trapped way to the level below. Raised flags along it set off darts from the walls (Test of Constitution, 1d, Piercing). The stair at its end runs 24 feet down to F.1; its top step drops into a deep spiked pit (Test of Constitution, 3d, Piercing). The Royal Guard know both.*

**The Flags:** Every few paces one sits a finger proud; the darts fly at whoever treads it.
**The Pocked Walls:** Holes at waist height the length of the corridor, a broken dart in one.
**The Stair:** Narrow and steep, a hairline seam across the top step.

**Exits:** a falling-stair archway -> E.2 The Turning Hall, down a stair -> F.1 The Stair Landing
