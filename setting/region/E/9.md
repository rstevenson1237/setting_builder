E.9 **Barrow-Kin Watch** (medium) - *Burial Mound, Barrow-Kin*
A ring of flat stones marks a clear patch where three **Barrow-Kin** sit watching the field. They do not speak first, and do not move to stop anyone walking past.
*An open court between mounds, thirty feet across, the flat stones arranged in a rough circle, rather than built up as anything. No roof and no walls, the one non-burial space in the field.*
**Barrow-Kin:** Three of them, watching rather than working, silent unless spoken to, and asked before any digging starts -> one names the sealed mounds from the robbed (Wary, 3, Bestiary : Barrow-Kin).
**Kept Path:** A cleared track through the ring, brushed free of the scrub, the Barrow-Kin's own route between mounds.
**Exits:** open path - worn ground, east side -> E.12 The Empty Mound, open path - worn ground, north side -> E.4 The Robbed Mound
