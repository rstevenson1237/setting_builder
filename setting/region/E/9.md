E.9 **The Pyramid's Foot** (Ruin) - *vast, stepped, carved*
A vast stepped pyramid climbs above the trees, its first terrace carved with serpents, a south stair rising into the open sky and drains running down its face.
*500 by 500 feet. The way into the pyramid opens from here. Two bound men and a snake man stand on the first terrace by day, a patrol by night.*
**The first terrace:** carved serpents run along its wall, the mark of the snake people's empire.
**The watch:** a snake man and two bound men standing watch on the terrace, a hiss on the stair (Hostile, 3, Bestiary : Snake Man).
**The south stair:** a hundred feet of open stair in sight of the jungle.
**Exits:** up the south stair -> HA.1 The First Terrace
