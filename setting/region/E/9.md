E.9 **Barrow-Kin Watch** (medium) - *Burial Mound, Barrow-Kin*
A ring of flat stones marks a clear patch of ground where three pale, silent figures sit watching the field without approaching it - Barrow-Kin, tending mounds nobody asked them to tend. They do not speak first, and do not move to stop anyone walking past.
*An open court between mounds, thirty feet across, the flat stones arranged in a rough circle rather than built up as anything. No roof, no walls - the one clearly non-burial space in the whole field.*
**Barrow-Kin:** Three of them, watching rather than working, silent unless spoken to first (Wary, 3, Bestiary : Barrow-Kin). Asked plainly which mounds are safe to open, one of them answers - once, and only if asked before any digging starts. They know the sealed mounds from the robbed ones and say so without being pressed further.
**Kept Path:** A cleared track through the ring, brushed free of the scrub reclaiming the ground elsewhere - the Barrow-Kin's own route between the mounds they watch.
**Exits:** open path - worn ground, east side -> E.12 The Empty Mound, open path - worn ground, north side -> E.4 The Robbed Mound
