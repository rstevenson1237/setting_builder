E.9 **The Stair Chamber** (Low) - *bare, steep, draughty*

A bare chamber with a stair going steeply down in the south wall, wind moaning below.

*A chamber 20 feet square at the level's end. The stair runs 20 feet down to F.1.*

**The Stair:** Narrow and steep, steps worn smooth in the middle.

**Exits:** a stone passage, north-north-west, 95 feet -> E.2 The Turning Room, down a stair, north, 40 feet -> F.1 The Stair Landing
