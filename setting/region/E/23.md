E.23 **The Third Master's Niche** (medium) - *Temple Cult, Leader's Name*
A small scriptorium, shelves cut directly into the stone still holding a handful of sealed document-cases - most crumbled to nothing, one still whole. **Gholuk's** name is worked into the shelf itself above the one intact case, small and functional rather than monumental.
*A scriptorium eight feet across, shelving cut straight into the walls on three sides. Driest room in the Sanctum by feel alone - whatever kept the damp out here worked better than anywhere else.*
**Intact Case:** A sealed document-case, bone and hide gone brittle but whole, wedged tightly enough on its shelf that pulling it straight out risks it - working it free by tilting rather than pulling -> comes loose whole; pulled straight -> crumbles to nothing in the hand, the record inside lost with it.
**Gholuk's Record:** If the case is freed whole - a written tally naming Gholuk as the Third Hall's own master, and the last of the Sanctum's three (Lore: Gholuk's Tally).
**Crumbled Shelves:** Everything else here went to dust long before the current dig ever reached this deep - nothing left to find in them.
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, entrance side -> E.22 The Third Hall
