E.10 **The Ward-Marked Mound** (medium) - *Elder Sorcery, Grave-Silt*
A small oratory cut into the mound, its niche wall scored with the **coiled relief** carried throughout the Sanctum below. A string of small **bones** hangs across the entrance, dry enough to rattle.
*An oratory chamber eight feet across, walls close in on a single kneeling-niche facing the coiled-relief carving. Grave-silt coats the floor thickly, undisturbed but for a narrow footpath worn, straight to the niche.*
**Bone String:** Strung chest-height across the entrance, brittle and dry, pushed through rather than ducked -> the bones rattle loud enough to carry, across the field (Test of Fate, Impact).
**Coiled Relief:** The same motif seen throughout the Sanctum below, carved here on the surface.
**Worn Footpath:** A single trail through the grave-silt, straight to the niche and back.
**Exits:** open path - worn ground, north side -> E.12 The Empty Mound, open path - worn ground, east side -> E.5 The Waking Mound, open shaft - the crypt's own broken roof, straight down -> E.3 The Broken Crown
