E.10 **The Owlbear's Rim** (Lair) - *feathered, bone-littered, high*
A shallow **cave** opens on the east rim, a **nest** of bones and torn feathers at its back. An **owlbear** sleeps on it by day.
*Cave 15 ft deep, reached from the rim track; the owlbear hunts at night and its hooting carries down the ravine.*
**Nest:** Bones, torn feathers and scraps of cloth, a collar among them.
**Owlbear:** Sleeps by day and attacks anything that enters its cave (Hostile, 1, Bestiary : Owlbear).
