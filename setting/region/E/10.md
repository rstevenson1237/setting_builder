E.10 **The Barracks** (Low) - *crowded, sweaty, bunked*

Stone bunks three high line the walls, kit hung on pegs. Somebody snores and a dice cup rattles.

*A room 30 feet by 20 where the Royal Guard's sixes sleep in turns.*

**The Bunks:** Thirty-six berths, a red hawk scratched on each headboard (Faction : Kurumisa).
**The Kit:** Spare scale, waterskins and spear heads on pegs.

**Exits:** an open passage -> E.3 The Guard Hall
