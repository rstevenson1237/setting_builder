E.10 **The Owlbear's Rim** (Lair) - *feathered, bone-littered, high*
Torn feathers cover the floor of a shallow cave on the east rim, a **nest** of bones at its back.
*On the east rim 400 yards up, 80 feet above the path; reached along the rim track from the mouth.*
**Nest:** A collar and cloth scraps lie among the bones.
**Owlbear:** It sleeps on the nest by day and attacks whatever enters (Hostile, 1, Bestiary : Owlbear); its hooting at night carries down the ravine.
