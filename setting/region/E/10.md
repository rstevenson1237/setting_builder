E.10 **The Ward-Marked Mound** (medium) - *Elder Sorcery, Grave-Silt*
A small oratory cut into the mound, its single niche wall scored with the same coiled-relief motif carried throughout the Sanctum below - the first sign on the surface that whatever is under these mounds connects to something larger. A string of small bones hangs strung across the entrance, dry enough to rattle at the lightest touch.
*An oratory chamber eight feet across, walls close in on a single kneeling-niche facing the coiled-relief carving. Grave-silt coats the floor thickly here, undisturbed but for a single narrow footpath worn straight to the niche.*
**Bone String:** Strung chest-height across the entrance, brittle and dry; ducking under it carefully disturbs nothing, but pushing through -> the bones rattle loud enough to carry across the field, a warning meant for ears that already know what it means, and whoever set it off has the turn it takes them to come to be somewhere else (Test of Fate, Impact).
**Coiled Relief:** The same motif seen throughout the Sanctum below, carved here on the surface first - whoever built this oratory knew what was under the field before any of the current mounds were opened.
**Worn Footpath:** A single trail through the grave-silt, straight to the niche and back - somebody visits this mound regularly, and does not linger.
**Exits:** open path - worn ground, north side -> E.12 The Empty Mound, open path - worn ground, east side -> E.5 The Waking Mound, open shaft - the crypt's own broken roof, straight down -> E.3 The Broken Crown
