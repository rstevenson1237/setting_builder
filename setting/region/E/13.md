E.13 **The Long Store** (Medium) - *rotten, collapsed, long*

Rotted shelves sag the length of a long hall, jars broken beneath them. The wood creaks and settles.

*A hall 20 feet by 50, the level's old storehouse, left when the living went down to dream (The Living Go Down to Dream). Leaning on or climbing a shelf brings it down (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing). The end shelf runs on hidden runners and slides aside on a passage to the hidden strongroom (E.14); nobody knows it.*

**The Shelves:** Split uprights, a few sound jars of oil and grain among the wreck.
**The End Shelf:** Its feet sit on worn grooves, not on the floor.

**Exits:** a jar-rune archway -> E.2 The Turning Hall
