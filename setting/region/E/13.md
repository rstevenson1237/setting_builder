E.13 **The First Hall** (high) - *Temple Cult, Three Chambers*
A great rectangular hall opens beneath the mounds, its far wall a fitted **sealed gate**, carved with a coiled ring around a name cut deep, **Vornuk**. A **coil-floor path** of inlaid stone leads toward the gate.
*Fifty feet by twenty-five, ceiling corbelled up to nearly fifteen feet, at the centre, built to hold a procession standing. A voice pitched normally here carries, into every chamber branching off it.*
**Vornuk's Name:** Cut in Essath a hand's width deep, across the gate's lintel, the first of the Sanctum's three master-names.
**Coil-Floor Path:** An inlaid ring set into the plain flagstone, the pattern breaking short of the threshold, the flagstone beyond sitting proud and unworked, stepping past its edge -> the panel gives, into a shallow pit (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Vornuk's Own Offering:** Set in a shallow floor-medallion, at the coil pattern's centre, reached by walking the marked path (Treasure III, d20).
**Sealed Gate:** A single slab of fitted stone, the Membership Coil, the Rod and the Kaash Collar, presented together at it, and Vornuk's name spoken -> the gate opens.
**Wall-Hanging:** Heavy cloth hung against the south wall, unworn under a thick layer of dust, lifted aside -> a seam in the dressed stone behind.
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, entrance side -> E.14 The Unmarked Grave, open archway - worn stone, east side -> E.15 The Charnel Cut, hidden passage - a seam in the dressed stone wall, concealed behind a fixed wall-hanging until it is lifted aside -> E.16 The Sprung Floor, open archway - worn stone, west side -> E.17 The First Master's Niche, gated doorway - wanting the Membership Coil, the Rod of the Coil, and the Kaash Collar together, and Vornuk's name spoken at the threshold, far wall -> E.18 The Second Hall
