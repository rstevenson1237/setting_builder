E.13 **The First Hall** (high) - *Temple Cult, Three Chambers*
A great rectangular hall opens beneath the mounds, its far wall dominated by a fitted stone gate carved with a coiled ring around a name cut too deep to have ever been meant for casual reading - **Vornuk**, the first chamber's own master, dead longer than anyone digging above has been alive. A path of inlaid coil-pattern stone leads toward the gate across an otherwise plain floor.
*Fifty feet by twenty-five, ceiling corbelled up to nearly fifteen feet at the centre - built to hold a procession standing, not to be walked through quickly. A voice pitched normally here carries clearly into every chamber branching off it; nothing said here stays private.*
**Vornuk's Name:** Cut in Essath a hand's width deep across the gate's own lintel, the first of the Sanctum's three master-names and the largest cut of the three - whoever made this gate wanted it read, not hidden.
**Coil-Floor Path:** An inlaid ring of worked stone set into the plain flagstone, winding from the hall's entrance to the gate - the only marked-safe way to approach it. Where the pattern breaks a hand's width short of the threshold, the flagstone beyond sits slightly proud of its neighbours, unworked; stepping past the pattern's own edge onto that stone -> the panel gives, dropping whoever's weight found it into a shallow pit beneath, a fall hard enough to cost a limb's use for the rest of the day.
**Vornuk's Own Offering:** Set in a shallow floor-medallion at the coil pattern's centre, reachable only by walking the marked path correctly - grave-goods left for the first chamber's master, undisturbed since (Treasure III, d20).
**Sealed Gate:** A single slab of fitted stone, its own coiled-ring carving matching the doorway's motif exactly. It does not answer to force, a spoken word alone, or any one of the three regalia pieces alone - the Membership Coil, the Rod, and the Kaash Collar have to be presented together, at the gate, while Vornuk's own name is spoken aloud.
**Wall-Hanging:** A single length of heavy, rot-resistant cloth hung against the south wall, the only soft furnishing anywhere in the Sanctum and dusty enough that nobody has moved it in years - lifting it aside shows a seam in the dressed stone behind it.
**Exits:** open archway - worn stone, entrance side -> E.14 The Unmarked Grave, open archway - worn stone, east side -> E.15 The Charnel Cut, hidden passage - a seam in the dressed stone wall, concealed behind a fixed wall-hanging until it is lifted aside -> E.16 The Sprung Floor, open archway - worn stone, west side -> E.17 The First Master's Niche, gated doorway - wanting the Membership Coil, the Rod of the Coil, and the Kaash Collar together, and Vornuk's name spoken at the threshold, far wall -> E.18 The Second Hall
