E.3 **The Broken Crown** (medium) - *Burial Mound, Broken Crown*
This mound's turf roof gave way on its own, no tool marks near the break, a ragged hole opening onto a **rubble slope**, down into a crypt. Nobody has finished clearing it.
*A crypt whose ceiling has partly come down into it, rubble sloping from the break down to the original floor, eight feet below. The drystone walls beneath the collapse are otherwise intact.*
**Rubble Slope:** Loose stone and packed earth, a cracked support course visible, on the north wall, crossed quickly or under load -> a section gives underfoot, and the noise carries (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**What the Break Exposed:** A wall-niche the collapse cracked open, half-buried in the rubble, found by searching the slope by hand (Treasure I, d20).
**Fresh Tool-Marks:** Pick-scars in the south wall, newer than the collapse, running straight rather than following any weakness.
