E.3 **The Broken Crown** (medium) - *Burial Mound, Broken Crown*
This mound's turf roof gave way on its own, no tool marks anywhere near the break - a ragged hole opens straight down into a crypt below, rubble still loose around its edge. Nobody has finished clearing it, cult or otherwise.
*A crypt whose ceiling has partly come down into it, rubble sloping from the break down to the original floor eight feet below. The drystone walls beneath the collapse are otherwise intact.*
**Rubble Slope:** Loose stone and packed earth, stable enough to descend carefully; a cracked support course is visible on the crypt's north wall, plainly still settling. Moving quickly or carrying a full load across the slope -> a section gives underfoot, costing a fall, noise enough to carry, and whatever wasn't strapped down.
**What the Break Exposed:** A wall-niche the collapse cracked open, half-buried in the rubble and easily missed unless the slope is searched by hand (Treasure I, d20).
**Fresh Tool-Marks:** Pick-scars in the crypt's south wall, newer than the collapse itself and running in a straight line rather than following any natural weakness - somebody has already been widening a gap here on purpose, quietly enough that nothing on this side marks where it comes out.
