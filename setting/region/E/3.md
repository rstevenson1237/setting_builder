E.3 **The Goblin Ledge** (Natural) - *watched, fouled, wolf-haunted*
A **skull** on a pole stands by a **kennel** door cut into the rock, a **notch** above where goblins peer down. Wolf hair is caught on the stone.
*Ledge thirty feet above the path.*
**Skull:** A wolf's, on a pole, tar thumbprints round its jaw.
**Kennel Door:** Iron-barred and cut into the rock; wolves bay behind it at a stranger on the ledge.
**Notch:** Two goblins loose slings from it at small parties, and wait for word of the next wagon (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Goblin).
**Exits:** open, west, 20 feet -> G.1 The Kennel Mouth
