E.3 **The Burned Market** (Ruin) - *scorched, tumbled, stalled*
Scorched stalls and fallen arcades surround a square, fire-black walls leaning, flies loud and a dry rustle in the litter.
*400 by 300 feet. The market of a snake people's town, burned in the rising.*
**The stalls:** fire-black stone counters under creepers, nests of reddish many-legged worms in the scorched wood (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Giant Centipede); a bite is Poison.
**The square:** flagged stone rubbed bare by patrols' boots.
