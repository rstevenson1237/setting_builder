E.3 **The Goblin Ledge** (Natural) - *watched, fouled, wolf-haunted*
A broad ledge on the west wall, a wolf **skull** raised on a pole at its edge and a **kennel** hole in the rock behind. A **lookout** peers from a notch above.
*Ledge twelve feet wide, thirty feet above the path.*
**Skull:** Raised at the ledge's edge, tar thumbprints daubed round its jaw.
**Kennel Hole:** A dark mouth in the rock, rank with wolf musk.
**Lookouts:** Two goblins in a notch loose slings at small parties, and signal with a sling's whir (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Goblin).
**Exits:** open, west of the ledge -> G.1 The Kennel Mouth
