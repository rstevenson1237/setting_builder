E.3 **The Guard Hall** (High) - *ordered, bronze-hung, loud*

Bronze scale and spears hang in ranks, a red hawk banner at one end. Boots and orders ring across the floor.

*A hall 30 feet by 20 and 16 high, the Royal Guard's seat, reached by the hawk-rune arch. The Guard's forty soldiers sleep in the barracks and drill here a six at a time; the old Guard's dead stand at the banner. The Guard send help in 2 turns.*

**The Soldiers:** A six drills in ranks (Wary, 6, Bestiary : Soldier). They want the way to level 6 cleared; they know the stair and the patrols below. They trade a guide for supplies.
**The Banner:** A red hawk, bronze-edged, guarded by skeletons in old scale (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Skeleton); they strike any who come at it without the hawk on them.
**The Chest:** The captain's, iron-bound, under the banner (Treasure II, d20).
**The Iron Door:** To the armoury, locked, its keyhole shaped like a hawk's head (Keys: The Armoury Key).

**Exits:** a hawk-rune archway -> E.2 The Turning Hall, an iron door -> E.6 The Armoury, an open passage -> E.10 The Barracks
