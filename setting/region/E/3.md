E.3 **The Guard Hall** (High) - *ordered, bronze-hung, loud*

Bronze scale and spears hang in ranks, a red hawk banner at the east end. Boots and orders ring across the floor.

*A hall 50 feet by 40 and 16 high, the Royal Guard's seat. Forty soldiers in sixes drill here; skeletons stand at the banner. The Guard send help in 2 turns.*

**The Soldiers:** Six sixes drill in ranks (Wary, 6, Faction : Kurumisa). They want the way to level 6 cleared; they know the stair and the patrols below. They trade a guide for supplies.
**The Banner:** A red hawk, bronze-edged, guarded by skeletons in old scale (Hostile, 3-6, Bestiary : Skeleton).
**The Chest:** The captain's chest, under the banner (Treasure II, d20).

**Exits:** an archway -> E.2 The Turning Room, an open passage -> E.6 The Armoury
