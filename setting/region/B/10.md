B.10 **The Scrub Thicket Maze** - *dense thorn, disorienting paths, easy to lose the way*
A tangle of overgrown **thorn-scrub** taller than a person swallows the trail here, its narrow gaps twisting unpredictably. Sound and sightlines both die within a few steps.
*A dense patch roughly two hundred feet across; cutting through saves distance, but a party without a means of tracking direction may need a Difficulty roll to find the far side rather than double back to where they started.*
**Thorn Tangle:** Pushing through blind risks disorientation; a marked trail or careful tracking avoids it.
**Cover:** Good cover for anything that doesn't want to be seen approaching.
**Exits:** path north -> B.4 Dry Streambed, path north -> B.9 Old Boundary Stones
