B.10 **The Black Stair** (Secret) - *black, narrow, barred*

Behind the granary wall a narrow passage climbs through the rock to a **bronze-faced door**, and beyond it a black stair turns twice going down, cold air rising. The steps are worn smooth in the middle.

*A secret way behind the carved face in the granary wall (B.5): a passage of some 80 yards climbs 50 feet to the stair's head, and the stair runs 64 feet down to the sixth level (H.1).*

**The Door:** Iron faced with bronze, with a bar on this side. Going down, a party lifts the bar; the dreamers drop it again behind. From below, it stays barred: a party knocks until a dreamer answers, or breaks it in a turn and every dreamer hears.
**The Walls:** Scraped smooth where murals were, a demon's face still visible in one corner (The Demon Is Invited).
**The Centipedes:** Two wait on the turn of the stair (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Giant Centipede), where it narrows.

**Exits:** into the granary -> B.5 The Old Granary, down the black stair -> H.1 The Stair Landing
