B.10 **The Charcoalers' Kiln-Ring** (secret) - *smoke-scented, wordless, hidden*
Low earthen kiln-mounds smolder quietly in a hidden clearing here, tended by stooped, ash-skinned figures who don't look up from their work. A woven cord hung with **notched bark strips** dangles from the nearest kiln, swaying though there's no wind to move it.
*A clearing roughly 30 by 30 yards, ringed by a dozen low earthen kiln-mounds venting thin smoke through cuts in their tops. Everything here smells of banked charcoal smoke, and the Grey Charcoalers themselves communicate only in gesture, never breaking their silence for a visitor who's arrived respectfully.*
**Kiln-Mounds:** a dozen low earthen mounds, each roughly 6 feet across, banked and vented to smolder wood into charcoal over days - tended in shifts by Grey Charcoalers who acknowledge a respectful visitor with a nod and nothing more.
**Ash-Tally:** a woven cord hung with knotted bark strips from the nearest kiln, each knot and notch recording, in the Charcoalers' own silent notation, a stretch of fringe the wold has reclaimed and what became of whoever was there when it did - including, near the cord's oldest end, a pattern of knots that matches no known settlement but sits exactly where the Marrgath's territory once ran (Lore: The Ash-Tally). A Charcoaler will let it be studied, but never let it leave the clearing.
**Drying Racks:** racks of split wood staged for the next kiln-load along the clearing's far edge - unremarkable except for a few branches too strange-grained to be local timber.
