B.10 **The Black Stair** (Secret) - *black, narrow, barred*

A narrow black stair turns twice behind a bronze door, its walls scraped and cold air rising from below. The steps are worn smooth in the middle.

*A secret stair behind the granary wall (B.5), pressed open from the mask in the wall; the way is barred from the lake city's side. It runs 30 feet down to the sixth level (H.1).*

**The Door:** Bronze, unbarred from below, cold to the touch; the dreamers bar it from above.
**The Walls:** Scraped smooth where murals were, a demon's face still visible in one corner (The Demon Is Invited).
**The Centipedes:** Two wait on the turn of the stair (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Giant Centipede), where it narrows.

**Exits:** into the granary, east-north-east, 60 yards -> B.5 The Old Granary, down the black stair, east, 30 feet -> H.1 The Stair Landing
