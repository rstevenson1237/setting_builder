Seed B terrain="swamp" comes="Black channels and hummocks under mangrove and drowned trees, reed beds loud with insects, and lizard men's enclaves smoking on the higher mud. Rung B if="a raid is beaten off at the wall" then="The lizard men carry their dead back to the enclaves, and the next raid comes by another channel within the day."

Ruling B on="channels by canoe" buys="A canoe covers 4 miles a watch in the open channels; a party without a paddler who knows them takes a Test of Fate for Lost each watch."
Ruling B on="the hummocks on foot" buys="Each hummock crossed is a watch of the worst ground, and every day a Test of Fate against swamp fever, Diseased on a failure."
Ruling B on="night" buys="Raiding canoes work the channels in the dark, and a lit lantern or fire is seen from every hummock for a mile."
