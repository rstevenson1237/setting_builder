B.9 **The Old Boundary Stones** - *worn markers, forgotten claim, faded carving*
A line of weathered **standing stones** runs across a low ridge, their carved faces worn to shadows of whatever symbols once marked them. They predate every hold on this coast.
*A row of six weathered stones, evenly spaced across a low ridge roughly 50 yards end to end, standing since before any hold on this coast was founded. Nothing here is hostile, but a rubbing of the worn carvings (charcoal or chalk against cloth) can recover a symbol.*
**Standing Stones:** A rubbing recovers a symbol recognizable, on reflection, as pre-dating Vael's fall (Lore: The Boundary Symbol) - a clue worth carrying to someone who'd know its meaning.
