B.9 **The Rider's Cache** (secret) - *Sealed, Tribute, Unmarked*
A narrow shaft drops from the marked cairn above into a dry, stone-lined cache packed with tribute-silver and a scatter of jewelry taken as toll.
*A cramped stone-lined chamber six feet down, barely wide enough to turn around in, dry and undisturbed; smells of old leather from the wrapped bundles stacked along one wall.*
**Silver Hoard:** Wrapped bundles of tribute-silver, roughly 400 cn worth, taken in toll rather than raid.
**Toll Jewelry:** A handful of rings and clasps taken alongside the silver (Treasure III, d20).
**Reason for Secrecy:** Only a handful of Ashward Riders know this cache exists; discovery here is a blood matter to them, not a theft.
**Exits:** the narrow shaft, up -> B.3 The Old Cairn Road
