B.9 **The Long Trapline** (hidden) - *snared, threadbare, quiet*
A line of weathered **Snares** strung along a narrow game trail marks a trapper's working line, clearly not checked this week. A battered lean-to shelter offers the only real cover along its length.
*A winding trail roughly 200 yards long through dense underbrush, threading between snares set at ankle height every twenty or so paces. Wet leaf-mold and old blood are the trail's constant smell, and the underbrush presses close enough on both sides to slow anyone not already walking single-file.*
**Snare Line:** a dozen or so wire and cord snares strung along the trail, most empty, one or two holding small game gone still days ago - whoever set these hasn't checked them in some time.
**Trapper's Lean-to:** a sagging shelter of branches and hide roughly halfway along the trail, sheltering a cold fire-pit and a few stashed tools; searching the stashed gear turns up whatever the trapper left behind (Treasure I, d20).
**Ash-Marked Stone:** a flat stone at the trail's far end past the lean-to, bearing a smear of charcoal dust and a few scattered dried herbs that don't belong to any trapper's usual line - a drop-stone for silent trade. Leaving something of genuine value here and withdrawing to wait quietly, rather than searching further or trying to follow whoever comes, reveals a narrow path branching off through the brush once whatever's left has been taken (Secret path -> B.10 The Charcoalers' Kiln-Ring).
**Exits:** narrow trapper's path, back south to the clearing -> B.3 Tannaskar Sawpits
