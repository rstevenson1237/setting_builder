B.9 **The Mask-Seller's Loft** (Hidden) - *cluttered, painted, quiet*

Shelves of unfinished masks line a low loft, brushes and pots of paint laid between them. The air is quiet and smells of pigment.

*A loft about 25 feet square above the granary, reached by a ladder (B.5). The mask-sellers know the carved face in the granary wall opens a way. Rats nest in the mask stock.*

**The Shelves:** Clay and reed masks of owls, wolves, hares and herons, some half-painted, some with a name inked inside.
**The Paints:** Pots of ochre, white and black, and a cracked bowl of water to mix them.
**The Rats:** Five giant rats nest in the stock (Hostile, 5, Bestiary : Giant Rat). They rush a light and go for the face.
**The Stair Mask:** A mask on the north wall is carved like the one in the granary (B.10); the sellers say pressing it opens the way.

**Exits:** down the ladder -> B.5 The Old Granary
