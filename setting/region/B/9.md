B.9 **The Mask-Seller's Loft** (Hidden) - *cluttered, painted, quiet*

Shelves of unfinished masks line a low loft, brushes and pots of paint laid between them. A woman in a wren mask paints by a single lamp. The air smells of pigment.

*A loft about 25 feet square above the granary, reached by a ladder (B.5). Rats nest in the mask stock.*

**Wren:** The mask-seller, quick-handed and grieving behind her painted bird's face. Her brother walked down the black stair in his dream and the cult took him; she asks a party to find him, a heron mask on his face, and in return shows the carved face in the granary wall that opens the way down (Quest: The Kinsman in the Lake).
**The Shelves:** Clay and reed masks of owls, wolves, hares and herons, some half-painted, some with a name inked inside.
**The Paints:** Pots of ochre, white and black, and a cracked bowl of water to mix them.
**The Rats:** Five nest in the stock (Hostile, 5, Bestiary : Giant Rat). They rush a light and go for the face.

**Exits:** down the ladder -> B.5 The Old Granary
