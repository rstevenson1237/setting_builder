B.9 **The Sale Hummock** (Hidden) - *staked, hidden, crowded*
A reed-screened hummock crowds with staked pens, weeping and chains sounding, and an ochre band on the throat of every guard.
*250 by 200 feet. Captives taken in raids are held here for sale; a buyer comes by the water road to the pyramid.*
**The captives:** villagers of the southern villages in pens of stakes, chained and weeping, a woman with a scale-mark under her shirt at the back.
**The guards:** six lizard men with nets, spears and ropes, an ochre band across the throat of each, who guard the sale (Wary, 6, Bestiary : Lizard Man).
**The hidden landing:** a mud beach screened from the channel by reeds, with drying racks beside it.
**Exits:** a way through the reeds -> B.1 The Black Channel
