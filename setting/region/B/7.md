B.7 **The Sunken Ring** (hidden) - *Buried, Older, Unmarked*
Stone steps lead down into a **Sunken Ring** of smaller stones, buried to their shoulders, circling a single flat stone worn smooth by countless feet. A groove across its face lines up with a gap in the ring, facing sunrise.
*A circular pit twenty feet across and eight deep, reached by the worn steps at the Standing Stones above; the air is still and colder than the ring above it, and the center stone rings faintly underfoot when struck.*
**Flat Center Stone:** Worn smooth, a groove across its face aligned to the ring's one gap.
**Wear Pattern:** Ground scuffed sunwise around the stone - generations circled it the same way, the same direction.
**Working:** Touched at sunrise from the gap, the stone shifts aside to a shallow cist (Treasure III, d20); forced any other way, it cracks and ruins what was under it.
**Exits:** the worn stone steps, up -> B.2 The Standing Stones
