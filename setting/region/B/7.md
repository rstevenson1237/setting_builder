B.7 **The Windbreak Hollow** - *sheltered bowl, old firepit, common campsite*
A shallow bowl in the hillside sits sheltered from the onshore wind, its center marked by a long-used stone-ringed **firepit**. Flattened grass shows it's a popular place to camp.
*A defensible, bowl-shaped hollow roughly ten yards across, sheltered from the onshore wind by design of the terrain rather than construction; the old firepit leaves a faint char smell that lingers even when cold. A party resting here may find the site already claimed for the night by someone else, bandit or otherwise.*
**Firepit:** Long used and stone-ringed.
**Camping Ground:** Flattened grass shows frequent use; the site may already be claimed by someone else.
**Previous Camper's Leavings:** A search of the flattened grass turns up something small, overlooked when they broke camp (Treasure I, d20).
