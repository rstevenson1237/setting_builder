B.7 **The Old Watch Stump** (hidden) - *collapsed twelve years back, broken shell, abandoned watch-line*
What's left of a watchtower stands broken off at knee height past the boundary stones, the rest of it scattered downhill as rubble. Whatever took it did the job twelve years ago and never came back to finish the ground around it.
*A collapsed round tower base roughly 8 yards across, walls broken down to a single course in most places, rubble spread downhill in a rough fan; smells of old char under the moss, the Collapsed Base's fire.*
**Collapsed Base:** Scorched foundation stone; sifting rubble turns up worthless keep-issue fittings.
**Commander's Marker:** Freeing the buried sash reveals an unrecognized old-tongue stone seal (Key: The Foundation Seal).
**Fresh Cart-Ruts:** Recent wheel-ruts cross the rubble-fan, leading to a shored opening.
**Exits:** worn footpath, south -> back to the boundary stones
