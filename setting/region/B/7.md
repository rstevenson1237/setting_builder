B.7 **The Old Watch Stump** (hidden) - *collapsed twelve years back, broken shell, abandoned watch-line*
What's left of a watchtower stands broken off at knee height past the boundary stones, the rest of it scattered downhill as rubble. Whatever took it did the job twelve years ago and never came back to finish the ground around it.
*A collapsed round tower base roughly 8 yards across, walls broken down to a single course in most places, rubble spread downhill in a rough fan; smells of old char under the moss, and the stone stays colder to the touch than the air around it.*
**Collapsed Base:** Scorched stone at the foundation confirms fire played some part in what finally brought the walls down twelve years back. Sifting the rubble costs real time but turns up small keep-issue fittings - buckles, a bent sconce, nothing worth carrying.
**Commander's Marker:** A weathered strip of an officer's sash lies caught under a fallen block near the old doorway. Working it free from the mud beneath turns up a small foundation-cut stone seal - old-tongue work, not keep-made, and nothing the commander who died here should have been carrying. Nobody at Fellward knows what it opens; it doesn't fit anything on record here (Key: The Foundation Seal).
**Fresh Cart-Ruts:** Wheel-ruts far too recent to be twelve years old cut across the old rubble-fan and away into the trees, easy to miss among the tumbled stone unless a party is actually reading the ground rather than the tower. Following them leads to a shored opening cut into the hillside beyond, well past where the tower's own shadow falls.
**Exits:** worn footpath, south -> back to the boundary stones
