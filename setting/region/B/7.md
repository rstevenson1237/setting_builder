B.7 **The Sunken Vault** (hidden) - *Bound Service, Dead Ground*
Past the collapsed entrance, a low sunken chamber holds a single stone-lidded grave, the earth around it undisturbed by anything that's come through since the roof gave way.
*Ten feet below the Fen Mound's own collapsed entrance, a chamber six yards across, the air close and cold, roof timbers long since given way to packed earth.*
**Stone Lid:** A single grave, lidded in fitted stone rather than the fen's own loose fieldstone, carries a line of cut marks around its rim that match nothing else in the region (Lore: Barrow Marks).
**Grave Goods:** Whatever was left at the lid's own foot when the grave was sealed sits undisturbed there still, exactly as it was placed (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** collapsed entrance, up -> B.4 The Fen Mound
