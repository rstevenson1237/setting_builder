B.7 **The Reed Maze** (Natural) - *tall, humming, blind*
Head-high reeds hum with insects over a mud floor, tangled paths ending in walls of reed, no horizon in any direction.
*600 by 500 feet. A party without a guide is easily Lost in here.*
**The reed walls:** the reeds close over every path within a few yards, and the hum drowns calls.
**The mud claws:** four shield-sized red-brown shells lie half buried in the mud (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Giant Crab); one claw closes round a leg and holds.
