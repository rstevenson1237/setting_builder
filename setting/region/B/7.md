B.7 **The Longhouse Undercroft** (hidden) - *buried, cold, Marrgath-cut*
Roots and packed earth close in below the collapsed hatch, opening into a low **Storage Cellar** that survived the longhouse above it collapsing. Rows of long-empty ceramic jars still line one wall, undisturbed since the Marrgath vanished.
*A single earthen-walled chamber roughly 15 by 10 feet, ceiling barely 5 feet high and shored with half-rotted timber posts. The air is close and cold, and everything smells of wet clay and old root.*
**Storage Cellar:** the room itself, lined along its north wall with a dozen ceramic jars, most cracked and long empty; a handful still sealed with old wax hold whatever survived a century and a half underground (Treasure III, d20).
**Support Posts:** the rotted timber shoring holding up the low ceiling - one post, visibly more recent than the others, was propped into place long after the Marrgath were gone, though nothing else down here explains who did it or why.
**Exits:** root-cellar stair, climbing back up through the hatch -> B.2 The Marrgath Longhouses
