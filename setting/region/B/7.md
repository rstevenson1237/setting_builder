B.7 **The Lake Shore** (Natural) - *still, wide, cool*

A wide black lake lies still under the cavern roof, lamps floating on it like stars. A shore of flat stone runs to the water, boats drawn up on it.

*The shore is about 150 yards long. A boat crosses the lake in a turn; a swimmer takes a Test of Constitution in the cold.*

**The Boats:** Reed boats with a paddle each, left on the stone; any visitor may take one.
**The Water:** Cold and very deep. Mist rises off it, and a swimmer without shelter afterwards (Test of Fate, Exposed) is chilled.
**The Lamps:** Floating wicks in clay saucers drift out from the shore and are replaced each night.
