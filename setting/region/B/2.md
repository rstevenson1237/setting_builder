B.2 **Boundary Cairns** (landmark) - *Standing Stones, Overlapping Claim*
Three stone cairns stand close together where the three kingdoms' claims still physically overlap, thrown up twenty-eight years ago and unmoved since. A **boundary patrol** from the western kingdom sits its horses nearby, watching the ground rather than crossing it.
*Half a mile north of the causeway, on drier ground than the marsh around it. The three cairns stand within twenty yards of each other, each built in a different hand. Smells of horse and cold stone; wind carries further here than anywhere else in Marfen, with nothing standing to break it.*
**Three Cairns:** Each built by a different kingdom's masons in the weeks after Telar's crown split, in a different style, and each naming the same disputed ground in its own tongue. None has been moved, added to, or defaced since.
**Boundary Patrol:** A mounted patrol from one of the three kingdoms - which one varies by the day - holds position near the cairns without crossing to the others' side of them. Stops anyone found here to ask plainly which crown's ground they think they're standing on; answering wrong costs nothing but the delay.
**Man-Ape Sign:** Past the northernmost cairn, a knuckle-print beside a boot-print, and bark stripped from a stunted tree at a height no person could reach - Man-Ape ground begins somewhere past here, found only by following this.
**Old Ground:** Behind the middle cairn, a line of ground holds firmer and less overgrown than the marsh to either side of it, running straight north - the kind of straightness that is not an accident of the land.
**Offering Heap:** At the foot of the middle cairn, under the newer coins and rags travellers leave for luck, a layer of small lead tokens nobody has added to in living memory - each stamped on one face with a line of Essath, all of them the same line. The cairns stand on an Essath crossing and were cut to teach what a crossing wants; the tokens are what a traveller carried away so they would not have to remember it (Keys: The Cairn Pilgrim-Token).
