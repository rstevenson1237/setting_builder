B.2 **Raiders' Hollow** (landmark) - *Road Raiders, Watcher's Blind*
A cut-bank hollow screened by a brush blind overlooks the road below, horse-lines strung between two trees showing the **Road Raiders** made camp here recently, though most of the band's away working the road today.
*A cut-bank hollow forty yards across, a half-mile north of the road, screened from below by the blind; a fresh grave dug just outside it with one ear missing from the body before burial, the same mark any raid elsewhere leaves.*
**Watcher's Blind:** A screen of cut brush overlooking the road below has been rebuilt more than once, gaps in it worn to the exact height of somebody kneeling to watch through.
**Horse-Lines:** Two picket lines strung between trees still hold the smell of horse, though whatever was tied here rode out with the rest of the band.
**Watch:** Two Road Raiders left to mind the camp watch the road below from the blind, wanting whatever's carried rather than a fight over it, and taking a surrender rather than pressing one (Wary, 2, Bestiary : Road Raider).
**Cached Goods:** A stack of goods taken off the road sits under an oiled hide behind the horse-lines, Yarrow's own missing cart-goods still bound the way they were loaded (Quest: The Bonded Cart).
**Second Stash:** Wrapped separate from the rest, a smaller bundle holds whatever the band's own caged member back at Greywatch could still claim a share of, if he's the one who tells someone where to look (Quest: A Raider's Bargain).
**Exits:** open slope, every direction -> the wood beyond
