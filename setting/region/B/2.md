B.2 **The Marrgath Longhouses** (landmark) - *roofless, moss-swallowed, ancient*
Three roofless **Longhouses** sag under a century and a half of moss and collapse, their timber frames still standing despite everything that's fallen around them. A flicker of movement in the **Middle Longhouse**'s empty doorway resolves into nothing when looked at directly.
*A cluster of three longhouse ruins roughly 40 by 15 yards each, set about 300 yards west of the stone line along a footpath the wold hasn't yet reclaimed. Wet moss and old ash hang over the whole clearing, and wind through the empty roof-beams makes a sound uncomfortably close to breathing.*
**Longhouses:** three roofless timber-and-stone frames, the largest still holding a standing central hearth; picking through the collapsed hearth-stones of the smallest house turns up whatever the Marrgath left behind when they vanished (Treasure I, d20).
**Middle Longhouse:** the best-preserved of the three, home to a Longhouse Shade that mimics half-remembered domestic motions - stirring a cold hearth, latching a door with no hinges left; harmless unless its motions are mocked or interrupted, at which point it grows agitated until whoever provoked it leaves.
**Exits:** footpath, east back to the stone line -> B.1 The Yrsk Stone-Line, trail, south through wet ground -> B.5 Hollmyr Fen, half-buried root-cellar hatch, sagging on old rope hinges in the largest longhouse's floor -> B.7 The Longhouse Undercroft
