B.2 **The Ford** (Natural) - *shallow, slick, muddy*
Flat **stones** a yard apart cross thirty yards of **stream**, the banks churned by hooves and wheels.
*Ford 30 yards across, 6 miles out. A wagon crosses on the stones with a driver at each wheel.*
**Stones:** Weed-green and slick; a crosser under a heavy load slips off one and falls into the stream (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Stream:** Knee-deep and slow, carrying leaves out of the wood to the east; a party can wade it, off the stones, if it keeps its pack high.
