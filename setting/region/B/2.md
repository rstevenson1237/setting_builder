B.2 **The Shepherd's Cairn** (landmark) - *ruined landmark, old bones, waypoint*
A collapsed ring of stones marks an old **sheep pen**, tumbled and half-buried in scrub grass, old bone fragments scattered among the rubble. It's the most reliable landmark for miles.
*A low, roughly circular mound five yards across, once a shepherd's pen and now just a ruin marking the spot; the scrub grass here carries a faint, dry animal smell even after however many years of disuse. Nothing here demands a roll, but it's a useful place for a lost party to reorient.*
**Tumbled Stones:** All that remains of an old sheep pen.
**Scattered Bones:** A careful look confirms they're animal - a shepherd's long-abandoned trade, nothing sinister.
