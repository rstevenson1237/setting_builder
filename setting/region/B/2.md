B.2 **The Quarters** (Lair) - *crowded, painted, humming*

Painted doors climb the cavern wall in three tiers round a lit stair, each door a different animal's face. A low song comes from behind them, never the same tune twice.

*The lake city's homes, about 200 people on three tiers 20 feet apart. Nobody here wants to be woken, and nobody leaves a door shut behind them.*

**The Doors:** Painted with a fox, an owl, a stag, a heron; none are locked or barred. Inside, a mat and a mask on a peg, a bowl of cap broth.
**The Stair:** A lit spiral with a lamp on every landing; it is wide enough for two to pass.
**The Dreamers:** Twelve sing and paint behind their doors (Friendly, 12, Bestiary : Masked Dreamer). They want nobody woken and will share a bowl with a stranger in a mask.
**The Stain:** Spore dust lies on every sill; a thin glow of new growth creeps up the lowest tier.
