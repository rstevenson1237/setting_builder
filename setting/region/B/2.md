B.2 **The Boundary Stones** (landmark) - *old-tongue carved, scent runs cold here, weathered past reading*
Waist-high stones stand at intervals along an invisible line where the cleared verge gives way to true treeline, each cut with the same worn old-tongue word. Past them the woods close in at once, and everything about the ground changes.
*A loose line of standing stones spaced roughly forty yards apart across the road and the cleared ground either side of it, each squared by hand to about waist height; smells of wet stone and moss, and the wind seems to drop the moment the line is crossed.*
**Carved Stones:** Each stone bears the same old-tongue word cut deep enough to still read - essir, the old word for a boundary-marker - repeated rather than varied, as though whoever set them wanted no argument about what they meant. Frost sits in the carved lines longest of anywhere near Fellward, most winters past thaw everywhere else (Lore: The Boundary Line).
**Dead Line:** No scent carries more than a few hundred yards past this line, for hound, wolf, or person, without exception - a fact easily tested and never once found false. Attempting to track something across it costs the rest of the day's light before the trail's accepted as truly gone; pressing on regardless, following a cold trail on hope alone, is how a party ends up benighted in the deep treeline with nothing to show for it.
**Watchtower Shell:** A broken stone shell shows through the trees to the north - what's left of one of the two watchtowers lost twelve years back. Getting a proper look means leaving the stones and walking to it directly.
**Exits:** worn footpath, north through the brush -> B.7 The Old Watch Stump, open road, south -> back toward Fellward, open ground past the line -> onward into the deep treeline
