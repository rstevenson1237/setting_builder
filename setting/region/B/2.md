B.2 **Wolf Scree** (Landmark) - *scree, bones, tracks*
A slope of **loose scree** rises from the stream to an overhang, littered with **scattered bones** and crossed by **splayed tracks**.
*Two hundred feet of scree from the stream to the overhang; climbing it costs an action.*
**Loose Scree:** Stones the size of fists that slide at a step, and a slide here is heard under the overhang.
**Scattered Bones:** Deer and sheep bones cracked for marrow, and a man's boot with the foot still in it.
**Splayed Tracks:** A pack's prints, running up to the overhang and down to the stream.
**Exits:** down the scree -> B.4 The Stream Path
