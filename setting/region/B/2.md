B.2 **Boundary Cairns** (landmark) - *Standing Stones, Overlapping Claim*
Three stone cairns stand close together, where three kingdoms' claims overlap on the ground. A **boundary patrol** sits its horses nearby, watching rather than crossing.
*Half a mile north of the causeway, on drier ground than the marsh around it. The three cairns stand within twenty yards of each other. Smells of horse and cold stone, with nothing standing to break the wind.*
**Three Cairns:** Each built in a different hand and style, each naming this ground in its own tongue.
**Boundary Patrol:** A mounted patrol holds near the cairns, from one of the three kingdoms, which one varying by the day, stopping anyone to ask whose ground this is.
**Man-Ape Sign:** Past the northernmost cairn, a knuckle-print beside a boot-print, bark stripped from a stunted tree, above head height.
**Old Ground:** Behind the middle cairn, a line of ground runs arrow-straight north, firmer and less overgrown than the marsh.
**Offering Heap:** At the foot of the middle cairn, under newer coins and rags, a layer of small lead tokens, each stamped with the same line of Essath (Keys: The Cairn Pilgrim-Token).
