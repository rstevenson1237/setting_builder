B.2 **The Ford** (Natural) - *shallow, slick, muddy*
Flat **stones** a yard apart cross thirty yards of brown **stream**, green with weed, and a worn **bank** of churned mud on either side.
*Stream knee-deep and slow; a wagon crosses on the stones with a driver at each wheel.*
**Stones:** A slick one tips a crosser into the water, and a party carrying a heavy load risks a fall (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Stream:** Brown and cold, knee-deep, carrying leaves from the wood to the east.
**Bank:** Churned by hooves and wheels; the freshest prints are of a patrol's boots.
