B.2 **The Standing Stones** (landmark) - *Old-Tongue, Weathered, Watched*
A ring of weathered **Standing Stones**, several fallen, the rest still precisely spaced; smaller cairns ring its outer edge, clearly raised by different hands, later. The center stone's base looks worn like steps, half-buried in turf.
*A stone ring forty yards across on a low rise, half a day's walk north of the Causeway; the wind never seems to fully stop here, and the grass inside the ring grows shorter than outside it.*
**Ring Stones:** Nine still upright, three fallen; spaced too evenly for the ground to have done it, aligned to something.
**Outer Cairns:** Smaller stone piles ring the outside, clearly a later people's addition, not the ring-builders'.
**Worn Center Stone:** Its base looks stepped, half-buried - something is worn smooth just below the turf line here.
**Loose Socket:** One stone rocks underfoot if climbed on, and can tip (Nuisance).
**Offerings:** Small bundles of horsehair and grain are left at the easternmost stone, replaced every season.
**Exits:** the worn side-path, south -> B.1 The Causeway, the stepped base of the center stone -> B.7 The Sunken Ring
