B.2 **The Leech Pool** (Lair) - *still, black, crawling*
A still black pool lies under drowned mangroves, bubbles rising and a buzzing over its surface, and the legs of a heron-picked **corpse** hang in the roots.
*150 by 100 feet. The leeches wait in the water and fasten on whatever wades.*
**The leeches:** black ribbed slugs as long as a man's arm, six waiting in the black water (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Giant Leech); they bleed a target, and a fever follows from the swamp water.
**The drowned trunks:** dead mangroves standing in the pool with a mud shelf along one edge, the only footing across.
**The corpse:** a blood-streaked wader's body caught in the roots, with a machete at its belt.
