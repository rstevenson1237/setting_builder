B.2 **Ravine Mouth** (Natural) - *scree-strewn, exposed, ambush-ground*
The road narrows between two steep **scree banks** where the ravine opens off it. Red **handprints** mark the rocks, and the **wreck** of a wagon lies in the ditch.
*Gap 30 ft wide at its narrowest. Scree on both banks gives the goblins cover to the edge of the road. A patrol of Soldiers turns back here by day.*
**Scree Banks:** Six goblin raiders lie behind the scree with spears and slings, waiting for the next wagon or a small party (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Goblin).
**Handprints:** Red clay handprints daubed on the rocks at head height, fresh ones over cracked old ones.
**Wagon Wreck:** Wheels hacked, sacks of grain and salt split open; this was the second wagon. No strongbox lies in it.
**Stream Mouth:** The trail up the ravine starts at the water's edge.
**Exits:** open, the road south, eight miles -> A.1 Roadsend Gate
