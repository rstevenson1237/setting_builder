B.5 **Naugfast** (hidden) - *stonework, drowned, salvage*
The upper courses of an Odarath building standing out of the reed at a slight lean, roofless, with its **doorway** half full of water and its **interior** silted to within three feet of the wall-heads. A **punt** is moored against the north wall, swamped and abandoned, and there is **gear** caught in the reed all round the building that did not come off it.
*The standing stonework is about 20 by 14 yards and stands 9 feet above the water at its highest, in a reed channel nine hundred yards north of Immora. It was a pan-house - the Odarath equivalent of Gormhalm, a store for finished salt - and it is the thing Immora's dragged tide-line leads to. It smells strongly of rot rather than brine, because the silt inside it is full of drowned reed, and the water inside the walls is dead still while the channel outside it moves.*
**Doorway:** A corbelled false arch in the south wall, its cap stone carrying the Old Rede character for threshold, standing half submerged so that a punt can just be poled through it at low water. Inside, the building is silted almost to the wall-heads and the water is chest-deep over the silt. Anything dropped in here is not recoverable without dredging, which is why what has accumulated in it has accumulated.
**Swamped Punt:** Moored on a short line to a stone at the north-east corner, sunk to its gunwales and full of silt, with the line still sound. It is Torsgaard-built, eleven days in the water, and the kit still in the bow under the silt is a pan-hand's: rakes, a coat, and boots in reasonable order. This is Sallow's, and the weight recorded against it at Thrapstad is more than the kit is worth, which is why nobody from the holding has come nine hundred yards to collect it (Quest: The Weight on Sallow's Boots).
**Silted Interior:** The floor of the building is under three to four feet of black silt beneath the water, and four hundred years of the channel has washed things in and not washed them out again. A party willing to work it over with a boat-hook and an hour finds what the silt has kept (Treasure I, d20). What the silt has also kept is a Brine Eel, one, which lies in the deep corner against the north wall where the silt has scoured out, and which takes the person at the back of a wading line without any warning at all - the tell is that there are no leeches anywhere inside these walls and the whole of the rest of the channel is thick with them.
**Scoured Corner:** In the building's north-west corner the silt has been scoured away entirely by a current that should not exist inside a still building, exposing the original flagged floor and a rectangular opening in it about four feet across where two flags have dropped into a void beneath. The current runs down into it. This is a drainage run into Siwapelu's third course, it is swimmable by someone willing to go down in the dark on a held breath, and it is where the eels come in.
**Exits:** flooded corbelled doorway in the south wall, poled through at low water, into a reed channel running south nine hundred yards -> B.2 Immora, flagged opening in the floor of the north-west corner, four feet across, a held-breath swim straight down -> C.20 Nessekirel
