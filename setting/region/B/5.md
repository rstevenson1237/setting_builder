B.5 **The Wolf Den** - *scrub-hidden burrow, gnawed bone, pack territory*
A **burrow** is dug into a scrub-covered slope, its entrance ringed with gnawed bone and churned earth. Fresh tracks cross in every direction.
*A shallow den large enough for a wolf pack to shelter in; approaching within earshot, especially near dusk, risks drawing the pack's attention.*
**Burrow Entrance:** Ringed with gnawed bone and churned earth.
**Tracks:** A careful look estimates the pack at four to six animals.
**Wolf Pack:** A Coast Wolf (Bestiary) pack that dens here and hunts the surrounding scrub.
**Exits:** path east -> B.4 Dry Streambed, path north -> B.6 Sinkhole
