B.5 **Man-Ape Grounds** (hidden) - *Man-Apes, Iron Truce*
Past the boundary cairns' knuckle-prints and stripped bark, the ground itself changes - the marsh gives way to firmer, wooded rises Man-Apes hold and nobody has troubled to survey.
*Rising ground beginning perhaps three hundred yards past the northernmost cairn, wooded and drier than the marsh behind it. Smells of turned earth and green wood, freshly broken; nothing here is quiet the way the cairns behind it are.*
**Man-Ape Band:** A band of a dozen or so holds this stretch, staying close to it for the young it is currently raising rather than ranging further. Will not strike first at anyone wearing worked iron above the elbow, honouring the truce nobody now remembers the terms of - and answers its absence exactly as fast as anything else this size would (Watchful, 12, Bestiary : Man-Ape).
**Stripped Trees:** Bark stripped at head height and well above it, marking the band's own range rather than any deliberate boundary - the clearest sign of how far this ground actually reaches.
