B.5 **Man-Ape Grounds** (hidden) - *Man-Apes, Iron Truce*
Past the cairns' knuckle-prints and stripped bark, the marsh gives way to firmer wooded rises. A **Man-Ape band** holds this ground.
*Rising ground beginning three hundred yards past the northernmost cairn, wooded and drier than the marsh behind it. Smells of turned earth and green wood, freshly broken, and noise carries from cover on all sides.*
**Man-Ape Band:** A dozen or so hold this stretch, ranging no further while the young are raised, and will not strike first at worked iron, above the elbow (Territorial, 12, Bestiary : Man-Ape).
**Stripped Trees:** Bark stripped at head height and above, marking the band's range, not a boundary.
**Exits:** open ground, south, back past the stripped trees -> B.2 Boundary Cairns
