B.5 **The Old Granary** (Ruin) - *vaulted, empty, damp*

A vaulted hall stands empty, rows of stone grain bins set in a damp floor and water dripping from the roof. A carved face is worn smooth in the west wall.

*A hall about 70 feet by 30 west of the city, abandoned by the dreamers. Giant spiders hang in the vault. A carved face in the west wall hides a secret way (B.10); a ladder in the north corner climbs to B.9. (The Cynidiceans Raise the City)*

**The Bins:** Thirty stone bins, empty, their lids cracked; fungus grows in the bottoms.
**The Vault:** Two giant spiders hang webbed in the roof (Hostile, 2, Bestiary : Giant Spider). They drop on whatever walks under them.
**The Carved Face:** A mask carved into the west wall, its nose and brow worn by many hands. Pressing it opens a section of wall to the stair beyond (B.10).
**The Ladder:** A wooden ladder in the north corner climbs to a trapdoor (B.9).

**Exits:** up a ladder, north-north-west, 40 yards -> B.9 The Mask-Seller's Loft
