B.5 **Hollmyr Fen** (landmark) - *boggy, dank, denned*
Black, standing water pools between mossy hummocks in Hollmyr Fen, thick reeds hiding a **Fen-Bear's Den** dug into the highest of the mounds. The ground here swallows a footstep whole in places, and the smell of stagnant water and rot hangs low.
*A boggy hollow roughly 60 by 60 yards, standing water and reed-choked hummocks slowing movement to a crawl off the packed trails, set roughly 300 yards south between the longhouses and the sawpits. Insects are a constant, low drone here, and the smell of stagnant water and rot doesn't lift even in wind.*
**Fen-Bear's Den:** dug into the highest hummock at the fen's center, roughly 8 feet across at its mouth - home to a Fen-Bear, territorial past reason near the den itself but otherwise avoidant of torches and noise; scattered around the den's mouth are the remains of whatever it last dragged back, including gear worth recovering if the bear can be avoided or driven off (Treasure II, d20).
**Standing Water:** pools of black water between the hummocks, some more than waist-deep and hiding uneven footing beneath.
**Exits:** trail, north toward the longhouses -> B.2 The Marrgath Longhouses, muddy track, east toward the sawpits -> B.3 Tannaskar Sawpits, half-hidden animal trail, disappearing into reeds at the fen's south edge -> B.8 The Elk Wallow
