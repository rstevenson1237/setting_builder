B.5 **The Lynx Ledge** (hidden) - *overlooks the toll stretch, claw-scored bark, patient stalker*
Climbing the brush-covered rise above the Toll Stretch puts a party on a shelf of broken rock with a clear view down onto the road, and onto whoever's holding it. Claw-marks score the bark at head height all along the approach.
*A rock shelf roughly 15 by 10 yards, reached by a scramble up loose scree from the rise above the Toll Stretch; smells faintly of old kill-sites, and stays dry even when the cutting below turns to mud.*
**Quarry Lynx:** Holds this stretch of high ground as its own hunting range (see setting/Bestiary.md), stalking anything that lingers rather than passing through - which makes the ledge a poor place to sit and watch for long. It lets anyone who doesn't linger pass unseen.
**View Down:** From here the whole barricade and cookfire below are visible, along with anyone posted as a lookout - real advance warning of exactly how many are holding the road today.
**Exits:** loose scree, downhill -> back to the Toll Stretch and the road
