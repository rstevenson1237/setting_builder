B.5 **The Old Ford** (landmark) - *Spoken Side, Old Ford*
The road fords the region's one river here, shallow enough to wade at low water, and a **bell** hung from a dead tree on the near bank waits to be rung by whoever's crossing so the far bank knows to expect them.
*The river runs forty yards wide at this crossing, on the road itself about two miles past the cleared verge, waist-deep at low water and impassable at high.*
**Ford Itself:** The river runs waist-deep and slow at low water, forcing a long way round upstream whenever rain's put it higher, with the road picking up plainly on the far bank.
**Fording Bell:** A bell hung from a dead tree at the near bank is rung by anyone crossing, a custom kept by everyone who uses the ford whether or not they know why it started.
**Watching Ground:** Fresh red-painted sign turns up on the far bank more mornings than not, the same mark Gorzgur's own warband wears, gone again before anyone crosses to look closer.
**Exits:** wagon track, northeast -> A.1 Outer Gate, forded water, southwest -> C.11 Sentry Gate
