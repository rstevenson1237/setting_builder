B.5 **The Causeway Head** (Natural) - *reedy, trampled, watched*
Reeds crowd a **landing** of trampled mud where a planked **causeway** runs north into the mere. A **hare's foot** hangs from a post, and **toll-takers** wait in the reeds.
*Landing 30 ft across, 9 miles from the keep; the causeway is a yard wide on piles.*
**Landing:** Trampled mud and wheel marks; the road verge ends in a stand of reeds.
**Causeway:** Planked and slick, a yard wide, running north into the mere; bundles of reed tied with red thread hang on its posts, and fish-bone points stand upright in the mud beside it.
**Toll-Takers:** Five outlaws with bows wait in the reeds and ask 10 cn from each traveller; they let payers pass and shoot at those who run (Wary, 5, Bestiary : Soldier).
**Hare's Foot:** Tied to a post at head height, the outlaws' sign.
**Exits:** planked, north end of the landing -> D.1 The Causeway
