B.5 **The Salt Flat** (landmark) - *Barren, Sun-Cracked, Adder*
The **Salt Flat** is a stark white break in the grassland, ground cracked into a honeycomb of dry plates underfoot. Nothing grows here, and nothing has for longer than anyone can say.
*A barren flat some three hundred yards across, southeast of the Causeway, blindingly bright at midday; silent except for the crunch of crossing it.*
**Salt Crust:** Cracked into dry plates a hand deep; harvestable and worth carrying out, for a party with the means.
**Buried Adders:** Salt-flat Adders bury to the eyes in the loose crust and strike anything that steps too close (Venomous, several, Bestiary : Salt-flat Adder).
**Dead Ground:** Nothing has grown here in living memory; old-timers say it was river silt once, before something changed it.
**Exits:** open grassland, northwest -> B.1 The Causeway
