B.5 **The Causeway Head** (Natural) - *reedy, trampled, watched*
Reeds crowd a muddy **landing** where a planked **causeway** runs north into the mere. A **hare's foot** hangs from a post, and **toll-takers** wait among the reeds.
*Landing 30 ft across, where a reedy track from the road ends, 9 miles from the keep.*
**Landing:** Wheel marks end at the water; the road verge gives way to a stand of reeds.
**Causeway:** A yard wide on piles; red-threaded reed bundles hang from its posts, and fish-bone points stand upright in the mud beside it.
**Toll-Takers:** Five outlaws with bows ask 10 cn from anyone taking the causeway, let payers pass, and shoot those who run (Wary, 5, Bestiary : Soldier).
**Hare's Foot:** Hung at head height on a post, where the toll is asked.
**Exits:** planked, north end of the landing -> D.1 The Causeway
