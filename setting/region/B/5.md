B.5 **The Egg Hummock** (Lair) - *high, staked, bare*
A bare high hummock stands ringed with stakes, a serpent-carved **altar** empty at its centre, and the reeds hush round it.
*350 by 300 feet. The altar is the lizard men's god's, where the egg-stone sat before the tribes took it. The lizard men here are elders and guards.*
**The altar:** a carved stone slab with serpents coiled across it, a thousand years old, a hollow worn in its top where the egg lay; the empty hollow is the lizard men's quarrel with the southern villages.
**The stake ring:** ochre handprints on every stake, a ring of them round the hummock.
**The elders:** ten lizard men guarding the altar, ochre bands painted across their throats, wary and wanting the egg-stone returned (Wary, 10, Bestiary : Lizard Man); they know every channel and hummock of the swamp.
