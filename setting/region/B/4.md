B.4 **The Wayside Shrine** (Ruin) - *weathered, leaning, tended*
A waist-high **shrine** of stone stands under a hood beside the road, a **bowl** at its foot holding wilting flowers.
*Shrine 3 ft high, beside the road 14 miles out; drivers leave coins here.*
**Shrine:** A grey stone box under a hood, a tower carved on its face so worn it can only be felt.
**Bowl:** A few pennies lie among the flowers; a driver who stops here says what he has seen on the road.
