B.4 **The Wayside Shrine** (Ruin) - *weathered, leaning, tended*
A waist-high **shrine** of stone stands under a stone hood by the road, a white **tower** carved on its face, with flowers wilting in a **bowl**.
*Shrine 3 ft high, 14 miles out. Drivers leave coins here; no one keeps it but the road.*
**Shrine:** A leaning box of grey stone under a hood, lichen on its north side.
**Carved Tower:** A white tower cut into the face, the mark of Law, worn nearly smooth.
**Bowl:** Holds a few pennies and fresh flowers left by drivers on their way past.
