B.4 **Broken Arch** (Ruin) - *carved, ancient, thorn-grown*
A carved stone **arch**, half fallen and choked with thorn, spans the stream path at the ravine's head. A **cleft** in the rock opens beyond it, and **marks** cut in the arch's stones are worn smooth at hand height.
*Arch 12 ft high, one pier standing and one fallen. The cleft beyond is the cave's only entrance.*
**Arch:** One pier stands to its full height, the other lies across the path in three pieces. Thorn grows through both.
**Marks:** Rows of marks cut in the standing pier, worn bright at hand height. A character who traces them and knows the dead tongue reads: Here the works begin.
**Wolf Tracks:** Paw prints follow the stream path up to the arch's shadow and stop. None cross.
**Cleft:** A split in the rock beyond the arch, hung with thorn, wide enough for one man to enter.
**Exits:** open, north, through the thorn -> C.1 The Cleft Passage
