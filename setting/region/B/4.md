B.4 **The Revel House** (Lair) - *lit, loud, feasting*

Three long tables are laid under strings of lamps round a raised hearth, drums beating and laughter rolling out to the quay. Masked figures dance between the tables and eat from shared bowls.

*A feast hall about 90 feet by 50 holding some twenty dreamers at any hour. A stranger in a mask can sit down and join the revels (Local Procedure: Joining the revels). The dreamers gather here if a mask or a bed is broken.*

**The Tables:** Laid with cap stew, roasted lake fish and a fungus wine; every bowl is shared.
**The Hearth:** A stone dais raised a step, fed with dried reeds. A drummer sits at its edge, and the beat quickens as the night goes on.
**The Dreamers:** Twenty feast, drum and dance (Friendly, 20, Bestiary : Masked Dreamer). They are slow to anger, quick to share, and press a bowl into a stranger's hands.
**The Left:** Every wall is painted with a stair going down and masked figures going down it (The Living Go Down to Dream).
