B.4 **Drowned Temple** (hidden) - *Drowned Temple, Bared Stones*
Following the current downriver from the ford, the temple's **bared upper stones** break the surface at low water. Two trading companies went in after its stones fifteen years ago, and did not come back out.
*A single sunken chamber and its collapsed portico, forty feet across, standing in six feet of slow water. Smells of standing water and old stone, and the water swallows sound.*
**Bared Upper Stones:** Roofline and the tops of two columns, breaking the surface at low water only.
**Drowned Thing:** Waits below the surface, slow to notice, striking anything that enters the water, and will not climb onto the bared stones (Skulking, 1, Bestiary : Drowned Thing).
**Lost Company's Gear:** Wedged in the collapsed portico, below the waterline, one lost company's equipment (Treasure V, d20).
**Exits:** open water, upriver, against the current -> B.1 Sunk Bridge Ford
