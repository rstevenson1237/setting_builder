B.4 **The Raiders' Hummock** (Lair) - *stockaded, smoking, watched*
A stockaded hummock smokes in the mangroves, war canoes drawn up beneath it, hide drums sounding, and an ochre band painted across the throat of every guard.
*400 by 300 feet. Raiders load canoes here for the night's raid on the southern villages; the post was a snake soldiers' fort before.*
**The raiders:** ten lizard men in war canoes with nets and spears, loading for the night, each with an ochre band painted across the throat, who ask "Where is the egg?" (Hostile, 10, Bestiary : Lizard Man).
**The stockade:** a stake wall round three hide huts and fire pits, with a canoe landing on the water side.
**The war canoes:** long dugouts with nets and bone clubs piled in them, drawn up on the mud.
