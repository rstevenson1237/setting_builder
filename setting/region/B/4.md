B.4 **Wolf Hollow** (landmark) - *Denning, Territorial, Bone-Strewn*
**Wolf Hollow** is a shallow bowl of broken ground, gnawed bones scattered near a half-collapsed rock shelter. Fresh tracks cross it in every direction, and the pack that made them denned here well before anyone can remember otherwise.
*A wind-scoured hollow eighty yards across, a morning's walk northeast of the Causeway, broken by low rock outcrops; smells faintly of carrion, and what birds there are elsewhere go quiet approaching it.*
**Kill Site:** Bones from a recent kill, picked clean within a day - whatever did this is still close.
**Rock Shelter:** A half-collapsed outcrop where the pack rests by day; a narrow gap leads deeper in.
**Pack:** Seven Steppe Wolves range this hollow and the ground for miles around it (Territorial, 7, Bestiary : Steppe Wolf).
**Pups:** Newly whelped pups are denned somewhere close; the pack will not abandon this ground while they're small.
**Exits:** open grassland, southwest -> B.1 The Causeway, the narrow gap in the rock shelter -> B.8 The Denning Cave
