B.4 **The Fen Wallows** (landmark) - *fen-reed stands, auroch calving ground, wet footing*
Standing reed and soft ground spread wide at the **Fen Wallows**, cut through by firmer paths only a careful eye picks out. A small herd of **Fen Aurochs** grazes the wetter ground beyond, placid at a distance.
*A wetland roughly 400 yards across, reed stands taller than a person in patches, threaded by firmer raised ground barely a yard wide in places.*
**Fen-Reed Stands:** Tall, cuttable reed, free here for thatching and cordage.
**Fen Aurochs:** Ignores distance; the bull charges anything crowding a calf (see setting/Bestiary.md).
**Pelt Cache:** Cured hides stretched on frames, left by an absent trapper (Treasure IV, d20).
**Exits:** trapline markers, cut bark, east -> B.6 The Trapper's Cache, raised path west -> back toward the road and Fellward
