B.4 **Nuthora** (hidden) - *stone, letters, untouched*
A single squared pillar of dressed limestone standing eight feet out of open water with no ground around it at all, cut on all four faces with **old letters** running from the waterline to the cap. For thirty yards in every direction the water is **clear and empty** - no reed, no weed, no leeches, no fowl - in a fen where everything is choked with all four.
*The stone is about 3 feet square and stands 8 feet above the waterline in water 5 feet deep, six hundred yards north-north-west of the Doraiath milestone across open water. It is the eleventh Assize stone, or rather the original of which the eleven above ground are copies. The silence around it is the sensory fact - a party poling in loses the reed noise entirely about thirty yards out, and notices.*
**Old Letters:** Old Rede cut deep on all four faces of the pillar, from the waterline to the cap, in a hand that is careful and unhurried and nine hundred years older than the Assize. This is the stone the Assize's boundary chart marks well outside the worked ground with no pan drawn against it, and it is the stone its copies were copied from - a party that has seen the chart at Rendfast can recognise it on sight (Quest: Count the Stones).
**Clear Water:** For thirty yards on every side, the water holds nothing living. No reed roots, no weed, no leeches, nothing nesting on the stone, no fowl overhead. Beyond that ring the fen resumes exactly as it is everywhere else. The ring is the Oathkeeper's ground and its edge is precisely where the thing's reach ends. Nothing happens to a party that poles in, reads the stone, and poles out again - a Nuthora Oathkeeper does nothing whatever to anyone who leaves the stone alone, and it is entirely possible to visit this location, take everything it offers, and never learn that anything was here.
**Cap Stone:** The pillar's top, five feet above a standing man's reach from a punt, is dressed flat and carries a shallow socket cut into its centre about four inches across, empty, with the weathering inside it much fresher than the weathering outside. Something sat in that socket for a long time and has not been in it for a long time either. Prising at the socket, cutting the letters, or taking the stone's facing is the one thing in Lindmarn that has no negotiation attached: per `setting/Procedures.md`, whatever the letters were holding is no longer held, and what stands up out of the water inside the thirty-yard ring is a Nuthora Oathkeeper at 6d6+2, implacable within its ground and non-existent outside it (Keys: Twelfth Socket Fitting).
**Exits:** open water south-south-east back across the flat toward a leaning milestone on the causeway bank, six hundred yards -> B.1 Doraiath
