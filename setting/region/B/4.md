B.4 **Drowned Temple** (hidden) - *Drowned Temple, Bared Stones*
Reached by following the current downriver from the ford until the temple's bared upper stones break the surface, this Essath-sacred ruin has stood flooded since long before anyone living can date. Two trading companies went in after its stones fifteen years ago and did not come back out.
*A single sunken chamber and its collapsed portico, forty feet across, standing in six feet of slow water at the ford's own water level. Smells of standing water and old stone; sound doesn't carry here the way it does on the open causeway - the water swallows it.*
**Bared Upper Stones:** The temple's roofline and the tops of two columns break the surface at low water only - the whole reason anyone knows this place is here at all.
**Drowned Thing:** At least one waits below the surface, patient and slow to notice, striking anything that enters the water rather than pursuing onto the bared stones. It has never once left the water it drowned in (Patient, 1, Bestiary : Drowned Thing).
**Lost Company's Gear:** Wedged in the collapsed portico below the waterline, the equipment of one of the two trading companies lost here fifteen years ago - reaching it means going into the water the Drowned Thing holds (Treasure V, d20).
**Exits:** open water, upriver, against the current -> B.1 Sunk Bridge Ford
