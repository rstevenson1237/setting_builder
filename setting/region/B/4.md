B.4 **The Fen Wallows** (landmark) - *fen-reed stands, auroch calving ground, wet footing*
Standing reed and soft ground spread wide at the **Fen Wallows**, cut through by firmer paths only a careful eye picks out. A small herd of **Fen Aurochs** grazes the wetter ground beyond, placid at a distance.
*A wetland roughly 400 yards across, reed stands taller than a person in patches, threaded by firmer raised ground barely a yard wide in places; smells of standing water and reed-rot, and the footing gets measurably worse for a full day after any real rain.*
**Fen-Reed Stands:** Tall, cuttable reed used at Fellward for thatching and cordage, abundant here and free for the cutting - the one thing had here and nowhere else nearby.
**Fen Aurochs:** A small family herd follows this wet ground in a loose seasonal circuit (see setting/Bestiary.md), grazing and ignoring anything that keeps its distance; a lone bull charges anything that crowds a calf or the herd's path. Going wide around the herd costs time on already bad footing.
**Pelt Cache:** A few cured hides are stretched on frames tucked into the reeds, left by whichever trapper last worked this stretch and hasn't come back for them yet (Treasure IV, d20).
**Exits:** trapline markers, cut bark, east -> B.6 The Trapper's Cache, raised path west -> back toward the road and Fellward
