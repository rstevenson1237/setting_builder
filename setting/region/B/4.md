B.4 **The Toppled Stone** (landmark) - *fallen, unsurveyed, ominous*
A single boundary stone lies **Toppled** on its side here, half-sunk into the leaf litter, and no cutter has come to reset it. A haze of pale, hand-sized moths drifts around the fallen stone even in daylight, their wingbeats carrying a faint, unsettling keening.
*A small clearing roughly 20 by 20 yards, centered on the fallen stone, a further 200 yards north along the line from where it first crosses the logging road. The air here runs colder than the surrounding wood for no reason the season accounts for, and the moths' keening is audible before the clearing itself comes into view.*
**Toppled Stone:** the stone itself, roughly 6 feet long, fallen face-down years ago at the clearing's center - its carved marking, unlike every other stone in the line, is scratched through with three deliberate parallel gouges, as if someone tried to strike it from the record rather than simply note it fallen. Levering the stone up to look beneath it turns up a sealed waxed tube wedged into the impression it left in the ground, holding a cutter's last survey notes on this stretch of line before the guild quietly stopped tracking it (Lore: The Last Survey).
**Moth Swarm:** a dozen or more Barrow-Moths circle the clearing at all hours, their keening dulling pain - and judgment - in anyone who lingers too long nearby.
**Wolf Sign:** scratched bark and a half-buried kill a short way past the stone mark where a Wold-Marked Wolf has claimed this stretch as its own, hunting alone and hunting anything that lingers.
**Exits:** faint track, south back toward where the line crosses the logging road -> B.1 The Yrsk Stone-Line, trampled game-trail, disappearing into brush at the clearing's north edge -> B.6 The Wold-Marked Den
