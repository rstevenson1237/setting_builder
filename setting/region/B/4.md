B.4 **The Dry Streambed** (landmark) - *cracked clay, seasonal flood, easy walking*
A wide bed of sun-cracked **clay** winds between the hills, bone-dry and easy walking underfoot. It floods only in storm season, when it becomes impassable.
*A flat, open route roughly three yards wide, winding between the hills - a natural streambed, dry outside of storm season - the fastest way through this stretch of the Hills, and also the most predictable; anyone watching the hills knows travelers favor it. The clay underfoot carries a faint, dusty mineral smell.*
**Clay Bed:** Cracked and bone-dry, offering the fastest walking through this stretch of the Hills.
**Root Systems:** Exposed along the banks; searching them turns up nothing but a place to shelter from wind.
**Exits:** trampled dirt path -> B.2 Shepherd's Cairn, trampled dirt path -> B.5 Wolf Den, trampled dirt path -> B.10 Scrub Thicket Maze
