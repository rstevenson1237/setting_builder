B.4 **The Fen Mound** (landmark) - *Flattened Ring, Warded, Collapse*
A mound of raised earth stands clear of the surrounding fen, its **grass** lying flat in a ring around the base in dry weather with no wind to explain it, and a half-collapsed opening on its downhill side shows where the ground's given way.
*A raised mound twenty yards across in the low fen ground east of the road, the fen's own wet ground giving way to firmer footing only at the mound's own base.*
**Flattened Ring:** Grass around the mound's base lies flat in a perfect ring on even the stillest day, never trampled from outside it, only ever from within.
**Barrow Ward:** A shape the turned earth itself seems to stand up out of lets pass anyone who carries or wears its own bound mark, and moves only to stop whoever else breaks the mound's soil, never following past the ring the flattened grass itself marks (Still, 1, Bestiary : Barrow Ward).
**Warded Ground:** Nothing planted takes root anywhere inside the flattened ring, and no rite of Law tried against the ground here has ever been reported to take either.
**Collapsed Entrance:** A sunken gap on the mound's downhill side shows where old timbers gave way, low enough to duck through and opening onto a passage angled down into the dark below.
**Exits:** sunken entrance, downhill side -> B.7 The Sunken Vault, open fen, all sides -> the fen ground beyond
