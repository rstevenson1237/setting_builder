B.4 **The Stream Path** (Route) - *path-stones, chevrons, willows*
**Path-stones** run along the stream's bank under **willows**, and some of the stones have **chevrons** scratched on them.
*Every chevron points downstream, toward the cave.*
**Path-Stones:** Flat stones set a stride apart in the bank, worn hollow by feet.
**Chevrons:** Knife-scratches on every tenth stone or so, fresh and pale against the moss.
**Willows:** Leaning over the water, thick enough to hide a party from the ridge above.
