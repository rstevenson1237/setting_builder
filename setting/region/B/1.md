B.1 **The Path-Head** (Landmark) - *stream, thorn, mud*
Off the road, a **path** drops steeply into head-high **thorn**, mud underfoot, and the stream can be heard below.
*Fifty yards of cut; a party coming up it is seen from the road before it reaches the top.*
**Path:** Stepped with roots and slick with mud, its boot-prints going down outnumbering those coming up.
**Thorn:** Head-high on both sides, with a scrap of wagon canvas caught on it at shoulder height.
**Exits:** up to the road -> A.1 The Gate
