B.1 **The Causeway** (landmark) - *Ancient, Crossing, Exposed*
The **Causeway** runs arrow-straight across the flood-plain on a raised bed of dressed stone, walkable only when the river runs low. Old lettering is cut into one kerbstone in a tongue nobody at Carrdun reads, and an Ashward rider is usually visible somewhere along its length.
*A raised roadbed twelve yards wide and better than a mile long, Nemhet-built and never rebuilt since; flagstones settled unevenly, several stretches underwater when the river runs high.*
**Roadbed:** Dressed stone laid true across the flood-plain, trailing off east into unexcavated ruin nobody has mapped; no crown living could quarry or lay it now.
**Kerbstone Marker:** Old-tongue lettering reading roughly "the way over the water" - nobody at Carrdun can read it (Lore: The Senametu Marker).
**Undercut Span:** One stretch sounds hollow underfoot; a cracked flagstone gives way if trod dead-center (Damaging).
**Ford-stone:** A waystone breaks the surface only when the river runs low, half a mile out along the span.
**Riders:** A mounted Ashward patrol, red tassels on the bridle, is visible along the causeway on all but the worst weather.
**Exits:** the settlement's main gate, west end -> A.3 The Market Row, a worn side-path, north of the road -> B.2 The Standing Stones, a worn side-path, south of the road -> B.3 The Old Cairn Road, open grassland, northeast -> B.4 Wolf Hollow, open grassland, southeast -> B.5 The Salt Flat, the ford-stone breaking the surface, half a mile out -> B.6 The Ford-Marker, the roadbed, continuing east -> unexcavated ruin, unmapped
