B.1 **The Buried Road** - *swept-clear stone, unnatural line, old empire*
A stretch of foreign-cut **paving** runs arrow-straight through the scrub, swept unnervingly clean of the sand that should still bury it. No wind or rain accounts for the clearing.
*A road roughly twenty feet wide, exposed for perhaps a quarter mile, easy and fast to walk; anyone who studies its line notices it runs unnaturally straight rather than following the terrain.*
**Swept Paving:** Tracing its line, by map or from high ground, shows it points directly toward the Hollow Cairn.
**Tool-Marks:** Worked into the stone; recognizing them as pre-dating any coastal hold takes a moment's careful study.
**Exits:** path north -> A.6 Watch Cairn, road west -> B.2 Shepherd's Cairn, path south -> B.3 Bandit Watch, collapse -> B.6 Sinkhole, road (continuing) -> C.1 Cairn Mouth
