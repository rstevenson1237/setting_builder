B.1 **The Washed-Out Ford** (landmark) - *supply road gone under, exposed crossing, half-day detour*
The old supply road drops straight into brown, moving water where the **Washed-Out Ford** has eaten the roadbed whole. Older paving-stones show as darker shapes under the current for anyone who knows to look.
*A crossing roughly 30 yards wide where the road meets a swollen streambed, the original paved verge crumbling into the bank on both sides.*
**Ford Stones:** Follow the shadow-line of buried stones for safe footing.
**Silt Channel:** Invisible gap downstream; bleached wagon wood on the bank warns (Impact: damaging).
**Detour Trail:** Leads upstream to a shallower crossing, a half-day round trip.
**Exits:** open ford, worn bank, south -> back along the road toward Fellward; broken roadbed, north bank -> onward along the supply road, deeper into the treeline; footpath, upstream bank, west -> the detour crossing and open country
