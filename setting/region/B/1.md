B.1 **The Mask Gate** (Lair) - *arched, glowing, guarded*

An arch of glowing growths spans the path where it meets the city, a guard post on each side and a low humming from the quays beyond. Two figures in animal masks stand under it, one in a wolf's face and one in a hare's.

*The way in to Zamiun, at the foot of the lake path. The arch is 15 feet high. The guards do not fight; they step aside for a mask and follow anyone who goes bare-faced, humming. (The Living Go Down to Dream)*

**The Arch:** Glowing caps grow thick along its curve, throwing a green light that makes every face look masked.
**The Guards:** Two dreamers in a wolf's and a hare's mask hold the gate (Neutral, 2, Bestiary : Masked Dreamer). They want every visitor masked and know who has come down the path. A party that puts on masks is waved through.
**The Posts:** Two stone booths hold a stack of spare masks, plain clay ones, free to any visitor.

**Exits:** up the path, west, 60 feet -> G.14 The Lake Path
