B.1 **Sunk Bridge Ford** (landmark) - *Border Reavers, Watch Rider*
Where the old road's causeway meets the river, the bridge itself sunk below the current and the ford beside it now held by a **Brekar watch rider** who has seen the party coming since they were a hundred yards out. A stack of **confiscated cargo** sits openly at the toll camp on the far bank.
*The causeway's center point, sixty yards of packed earth narrowing to the ford itself, roughly midway between Tolkar and Velar. The old bridge's stones show as a broken line beneath the current at any water height. Smells of river mud and woodsmoke from the toll camp's fire; the water itself runs louder here than anywhere else on the causeway, breaking over the sunk stones.*
**Watch Rider:** A mounted Brekar sentry holds the near bank in plain sight, posted here at all hours. Demands 1 cn a head and 2 cn a pack animal before waving anyone across, or a look at anything carried worth more than that. A party showing a captured red-dyed passage-token in Brekar's own three-loop hitch is waved through without question, once, before the rider reports the token lost upstream (Keys: A Brekar Passage-Mark).
**Toll Camp:** Brekar's barrier and fire-pit stand on the causeway's far side, in full view from the near bank - never abandoned, never more than three riders at once.
**Confiscated Cargo:** A stack of goods taken from travelers who didn't pay, piled openly at the camp and not yet sold on - claimed, and watched, but not yet moved.
**Ford-Eel:** Holds the deep water beside the ford proper, striking anything that wades rather than crosses the causeway itself; it will not follow onto dry ground, and a party that knows this can cross the long way for free (Territorial, 1, Bestiary : Ford-Eel).
**Bared Stones:** At low water, looking downriver from the ford, the drowned temple's upper courses break the surface - visible from here and nowhere else on the causeway.
**Exits:** open causeway, packed earth, west end -> A.1 East Gate, open causeway, packed earth, east end -> C.1 West Gate, open water, downriver, following the current -> B.4 Drowned Temple
