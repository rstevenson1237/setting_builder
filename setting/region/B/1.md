B.1 **Sunk Bridge Ford** (landmark) - *Border Reavers, Watch Rider*
The causeway meets the river here, the old bridge sunk below the current, **Brekar's watch rider** holding the ford, having marked the party a hundred yards out. **Confiscated cargo** sits stacked at the toll camp, on the far bank.
*The causeway's centre point, sixty yards of packed earth, narrowing to the ford, midway between Tolkar and Velar. The old bridge's stones show beneath the current, a broken line at any water height. Smells of river mud and woodsmoke, the water breaking loud over the sunk stones.*
**Watch Rider:** A mounted Brekar sentry holds the near bank, demanding 1 cn a head, 2 cn a pack animal, or a look at anything carried.
**Passage-Mark:** Brekar's own three-loop hitch in red-dyed cloth, shown at the ford -> the rider waves the party through once, then reports the token lost upstream (Keys: A Brekar Passage-Mark).
**Toll Camp:** Brekar's barrier and fire-pit stand opposite, in full view from the near bank, manned at all hours, three riders at most.
**Confiscated Cargo:** Goods taken from travellers who did not pay, stacked open at the camp, watched but not yet moved.
**Ford-Eel:** Holds the deep water beside the ford, striking anything that wades into it, and will not leave the water (Territorial, 1, Bestiary : Ford-Eel).
**Bared Stones:** At low water, looking downriver from the ford, the drowned temple's upper courses break the surface.
**Exits:** open causeway, packed earth, west end -> A.1 East Gate, open causeway, packed earth, east end -> C.1 West Gate, open water, downriver, following the current -> B.4 Drowned Temple
