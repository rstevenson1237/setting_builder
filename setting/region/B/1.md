B.1 **The Realm's Road** (Route) - *rutted, open, wind-swept*
The road runs west over open grass, **milestones** at every mile and a **patrol** walking it in column. Tall **verges** crowd both sides.
*Road 24 ft wide, 24 miles from the keep to the map's edge. The convoy the guild runs every fortnight uses its full length.*
**Milestones:** A white tower on blue is cut into each post, numbered from the keep.
**Verges:** Tall grass hides a man lying down at 30 yards.
**Patrol:** Six soldiers walk in column and escort a wagon as far as the ford before turning back (Wary, 6, Bestiary : Soldier).
**Convoy:** Joining the fortnightly wagon train changes who watches the road (Local Procedure: Travelling with the Fortnightly Wagon).
**Watch Rise:** One mile out, a rise in the grass shows three miles of road in both directions and the wood's edge to the north.
**Burned Frames:** Three miles out, charred wagon frames lie in the ditch beside the road; the burned wagons are there (B.3).
**Ford Stones:** Six miles out, flat stones cross a stream where the road dips to the water; this is the ford (B.2).
**Causeway Turning:** Nine miles out, a reedy track leaves the road north to the causeway head (B.5).
**Wayside Shrine:** Fourteen miles out, coins lie in a bowl beside the road at the shrine (B.4).
**Last Milestone:** Twenty-four miles out, milestone 24 stands where the map ends and the road runs on toward the realm.
**Exits:** open, east end of the road -> A.1 The Main Gate, open, west end of the road -> the realm, off the map
