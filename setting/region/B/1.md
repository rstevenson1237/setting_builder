B.1 **Stream Path** (Route) - *wet, narrow, overlooked*
A narrow **trail** follows a fast, shallow **stream** between steep banks, loud with spray. The trail crosses the water at three **fords** over green rock.
*Trail 3 ft wide, four miles from the ravine's mouth to its head. Banks rise 40 ft on both sides and cannot be climbed quickly.*
**Trail:** Packed earth and gravel beside the stream, firm enough for a loaded mule.
**Fords:** Three crossings over slick green rock; a character who steps wrong falls on the stones (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Tracks:** Boot-less prints and paw prints cross in the mud at each ford. The prints run up the ravine; none run down.
