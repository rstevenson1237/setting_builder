B.1 **Ravine Lynx's Den** (landmark) - *Bone Litter, Claw Marks, Overhang*
A scatter of gnawed bone and old fur marks a shallow overhang below the region's own broken high ground, fresh gouges on the rock face nearby showing where something big comes to sharpen itself.
*A shallow rock overhang twelve yards deep, roughly a mile southwest of Greywatch's own gate, the ground beneath it packed with old bone and dry.*
**Den Overhang:** The overhang is visible from below as a dark seam in the broken rock, dry underfoot and littered with the picked bones of whatever's been brought back to it.
**Claw-Marks:** Three parallel gouges score the rock face at head height beside the overhang's mouth, always the same three claws, cut fresh enough to still show pale stone beneath the weather-grey.
**Lynx:** A big grey cat watches from the rocks above the overhang before committing to anything, breaking off the moment a fight costs it more than it's getting (Watching, 1, Bestiary : Ravine Lynx).
**Prey Trail:** A trodden path of torn fur and small bones leads downhill from the overhang toward the tree line, wide enough to walk but bearing no footprint, only churned dirt at uneven strides.
**Exits:** broken slope, downhill -> the true wood beyond
