B.1 **The Yrsk Stone-Line** (landmark) - *warded, lichen-crusted, silent*
A line of grey, lichen-crusted **Boundary Stones** marches into the trees here, each subtly different in shape and marking despite bearing no tool-mark any mason recognizes. Birdsong stops dead within a dozen yards of the nearest stone, and nothing about the silence that replaces it feels natural.
*A roughly 60-yard stretch of standing stones spaced ten to fifteen yards apart, sitting about 400 yards north of Khirengaard's stockade gate where the logging road first meets the tree line. The stones are cold to the touch even at midday, and the wold beyond them smells of wet bark and old iron.*
**Boundary Stones:** each stone stands roughly waist-to-head high, individually carved with a different, unrepeated mark - crossing here without a cutter's blessing is the entire reason the boundary-cutters' guild exists, and the wold answers an unblessed crossing on its own schedule rather than the crosser's.
**Dormant Warden:** half-buried in leaf litter a few yards past the northernmost stone stands what looks like an unusually man-shaped boulder, lichen-crusted like the stones themselves - a Stone-Warden, inert unless this stretch of line is disturbed, after which it moves with purpose and does not stop until the disturbance is corrected or it is destroyed.
**Exits:** packed trail, south along the logging road -> A.1 The Vansker Stockade, footpath, west along the stone line -> B.2 The Marrgath Longhouses, trail, east along the stone line -> B.3 Tannaskar Sawpits, faint track, north past the dormant warden -> B.4 The Toppled Stone
