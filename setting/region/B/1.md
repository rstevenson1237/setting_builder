B.1 **Doraiath** (landmark) - *causeway, sightline, disrepair*
A raised bank of stone and rubble twelve feet wide running dead straight across the fen as far as can be seen in both directions, with the reed standing higher than a man to either side of it. About halfway along, the bank has **slumped** into a forty-yard dip where the water crosses it shin-deep, and an **Assize milestone** leans at the dip's western end with letters on it nobody here can read.
*The causeway runs twelve miles east to west; this landmark is its midpoint, some six miles west of Torsgaard and the only high ground in Lindmarn. The bank is 12 yards wide at the base and stands about 4 feet above the water, with the slumped section running 40 yards. It was built on an Odarath pan wall a hundred and twenty years ago and is the only made road in the region. Underfoot it is loose rubble over dressed stone and it sounds like it - a party on the causeway can be heard a long way, and in the reed nothing can be heard at all.*
**Slumped Section:** A forty-yard dip where the underlying pan wall has failed and the bank has settled into the cut beneath it, so the water crosses shin to knee deep with a soft rubble bottom. It is passable at any season and is where every crossing slows down, which is exactly why the Wreckers work it. In winter, with the reed flat, a party can see the dip from two miles off and see anybody standing in it; in summer they are on top of it before they know. Drowned-road Wreckers, six to ten of them, use the reed within thirty yards of the dip's north side, and what they do is rearrange rather than ambush - a stake moved, a marker shifted - so they are met after the disaster rather than before it.
**Assize Milestone:** A squared post of dressed limestone about four feet high, leaning west at the dip's near end, cut with Old Rede characters on three faces and the three-notch device on the fourth. It is not an Assize stone and the Assize did not cut it; the notches were added later and badly. Nobody in Torsgaard will touch it, which is why it is still standing in a stretch of causeway that has otherwise been robbed for rubble. From the milestone, looking north-north-west across the open water in any season, a single squared shape stands out of the fen about six hundred yards off, too regular to be anything but cut stone - that is the way to Nuthora, and a punt or a chest-deep wade on the pan walls will reach it.
**Robbed Facing:** Along the causeway's south flank for a quarter mile either side of the dip, the dressed facing stones have been prised out and carried off, leaving the rubble core open and slumping. This is where Torsgaard's older walls came from and everyone knows it. The exposed core is loose enough to give way under a laden party and is the reason nobody drives a cart along the south edge.
**Sallow's Marker:** A channel stake driven into the rubble at the dip's east end with a strip of grey wool knotted round it, put there eleven days ago by a crew who could not recover a body. The wool is Assize serjeant's cloth, which is not what a pan crew would use, and the knot is Vingash work, which is not what a Marchman could tie (Quest: The Weight on Sallow's Boots).
**Exits:** the causeway proper continuing dead east, stone and rubble, twelve feet wide, six miles -> A.1 Thrapstad, a wade or a punt north-north-west from the milestone across open water toward a squared shape on the horizon, six hundred yards -> B.4 Nuthora, the causeway continuing west out of the region into open water and the old sea, its far end unmaintained and under water for the last two miles
