B.1 **The Black Channel** (Route) - *winding, dark, reed-walled*
A black channel winds between walls of mangrove and reed, insects loud over slow water, with ochre handprints on the roots where lizard men have passed.
*Four miles a watch by canoe in the open channels; a party without a paddler who knows them risks being Lost each watch. The channel runs from the wall's end to the hummocks.*
**The channel mouth:** 0.8 miles in from the wall's end, two watch canoes from the southern villages turn back all but those who pass; the way runs on by water to the wall's end tower, A.3.
**The fish weir:** 0.3 miles along, stakes of a lizard men's weir, ochre handprints on each; a party's canoe passes through its gap.
**The fork:** at the way in, two black channels part, and a lookout's fire burns on one bank.
**The jammed log:** 1 mile along, a log jammed across the channel where giant centipedes nest in the rot (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Giant Centipede); a dry rustle comes from it before it is seen.
**The hummock landing:** 1.4 miles along, staked mud with canoes drawn up and smoke over the reeds; a path through the reeds leads to B.9.
**Exits:** a way through the reeds -> B.9 The Sale Hummock, by water in -> A.3 The East Wall End
