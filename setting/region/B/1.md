B.1 **The Realm's Road** (Route) - *rutted, open, wind-swept*
A road of packed earth runs west across open grass, **milestones** at every mile and tall-grass **verges** on both sides. A **patrol** walks it in column.
*Road 24 ft wide, 24 miles from the keep's main gate to the map's edge: three watches on the road, six off it. The fortnightly convoy runs its length.*
**Milestones:** Stone posts at every mile, a white tower on blue cut into each, numbered from the keep.
**Verges:** Tall grass hides a man lying down at 30 yards, though sight from a rise carries three miles.
**Patrol:** Six soldiers walk in column and escort a wagon as far as the ford, then turn back (Wary, 6, Bestiary : Soldier).
**Convoy:** A wagon train leaves the keep each fortnight on the guild's chalked day (Local Procedure: Travelling with the Fortnightly Wagon).
**Exits:** open, east end of the road -> A.1 The Main Gate, open, west end of the road -> the realm, off the map
