Layout: The realm's road (B.1) is entered at the keep's main gate (A.1) at its east end, at the realm off the map at its west end, and at the causeway head (B.5), where the mere's causeway (D.1) leaves it north. It runs west over open grass, 24 feet wide, with a milestone at every mile numbered from the keep: two watches end to end, three off it. Its stops in order: the watch rise, which shows three miles of road and the wood's edge to the north; the burned frames, beside the burned wagons (B.3); the causeway turning, a reedy track north to the causeway head (B.5); the ford stones, crossing the ford (B.2); the wayside shrine, beside the shrine (B.4); and the last milestone, where the map ends.

The burned wagons (B.3) are charred frames and burst casks in a line 60 feet long along the ditch beside the road, smoke rising from the wood beyond them; a party reaches them by stepping off the road at the burned frames. The ford (B.2) is 30 yards of knee-deep stream crossed by flat stones a yard apart, the banks churned by hooves and wheels; the road itself runs over it. The causeway head (B.5) is a muddy landing 30 feet across in a stand of reeds, with a planked causeway a yard wide on piles running north into the mere, a hare's foot hung on a post at head height and red-threaded reed bundles on the posts; the causeway turning leads to it. The wayside shrine (B.4) is a grey stone box 3 feet high under a hood beside the road, a tower carved on its face and a bowl of flowers at its foot; the road passes it.

Nothing on the road is hidden or secret. Off it the grass stands tall and open on both sides to the horizon and hides a man lying down at 30 yards; the wood's edge lies to the north, and the mere north of the causeway head.

Inhabitants: The Keep (Faction: The Keep) walks the road in a column of six soldiers, wary, who escort a wagon as far as the ford (B.2), challenge strangers and want the road clear to it; they know where the last convoy was struck (B.1). Six goblins of The Zhakuk (Faction: The Zhakuk) lie in the grass at the burned wagons (B.3) with spears and slings, hostile to small parties and waiting for the next wagon. Five of The Wood Outlaws (Faction: The Wood Outlaws) take the toll at the causeway head (B.5), 10 cn a head, shoot those who run, and know which carters use the causeway. The fortnightly wagon (B.1) is run by the Carters' Guild (Faction: The Carters' Guild) (Local Procedure: Travelling with the Fortnightly Wagon).

Loot: The road pays a wage, not goods; the game on the open grass is all Encounter. A place on the fortnightly wagon costs 20 cn a head, and the Carters' Guild pays 50 cn at the Market Yard (A.8) to each party whose wagon arrives whole. No treasure lies on the road; the casks at B.3 are emptied.
