Places:
- B.1 The Path-Head - where the path leaves the road and drops to the stream; thorn and mud show first.
- B.2 Wolf Scree - a slope of scree and bones, with tracks; the wolves' ground.
- B.3 The Cave Mouth - a ledge above the stream with chevrons scratched at it; the only way into C.

Loot:
Thorn-berries by the stream at B.1 sell at the inn, A.2, for 2 copper a pint. Finds here draw on Treasure I, from wagon wreckage.

Secrets:
- Goblins, not wolves, strip the wagons and carry the boards into the cave - the goblin tribe; C.4's heaps.

Leads:
- Rumour 3 - wolves strip the wagons; settled at B.2 and C.4.

Tables:
d6 Encounter, rolled on each failed Difficulty roll
1. The wolf pack, six, circles at dusk and takes whoever falls behind.
2. Goblin watchers, a dozen, click from the ridge and send for the rest.
3. The soldier squad, six, passes on the rim and questions the party.
4. Goblins see the party, click, and withdraw toward B.3.
5. A wolf kill is dragged under cover; the pack is near.
6. A goblin raiding party comes down toward the road.
d6 Met between Landmarks, each a thing to stop for
1. A broken wagon, stripped to its frame, chevrons on its boards; Treasure I.
2. A milestone post with the grey tower painted on it, a chevron cut beneath.
3. A pool in the stream where the wolves drink, tracks all around.
4. A scree slide across the path, fresh, rain-loosened.
5. A soldier's round shield with the grey tower, face down in the mud.
6. A goblin's cooking-pot helm, dropped on the path.
