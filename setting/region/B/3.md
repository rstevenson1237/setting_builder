B.3 **The Toll Stretch** (landmark) - *felled tree roadblock, price stated first, held by numbers*
A felled tree blocks the road dead across a narrow stretch between two low rises, and the **Road Toll Gang** holding it wants payment before anyone hears anything else out of them.
*A hundred-yard stretch where the road runs through a shallow cutting between two brush-covered rises, the felled trunk laid deliberately rather than fallen; smells of woodsmoke from a cookfire kept just out of sight, and the ground stays churned and muddy longer than open stretches after rain.*
**Barricade:** A single large trunk, notched and dropped rather than felled by weather, blocks the road at its narrowest point. Going around means climbing the brush on either rise in full view of whoever's watching from it.
**Road Toll Gang:** Four to a dozen, working this stretch until it stops being worth the trouble (see setting/Bestiary.md). They state a price - coin, goods, or passage information - before anything else happens, and fight only once it's refused and they've committed to holding the road. Avoiding them costs the climb over the rises and the risk of being seen doing it.
**Toll Box:** A lockbox kept near the barricade holds whatever's been collected this stretch so far - not a fortune, but real coin, worth more to whoever holds it than to whoever it was taken from (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** goat-track, up the eastern rise -> B.5 The Lynx Ledge, open road south -> back toward Fellward, open road north -> onward toward the boundary stones
