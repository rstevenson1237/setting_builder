B.3 **Hermit's Croft** (landmark) - *Lone Squatter, Hermit's Trade*
A one-room shack with no other roof in sight stands past the last farm, smoke rising from a forge-vent though nobody answers a first knock, and **Ellard**, who lives here alone, trades worked goods for coin but never comes as far as the gate to spend it.
*A one-room shack of mismatched timber a mile past the last farm, no other roof in sight, smoke from the forge-vent visible before the shack itself is.*
**Ellard's Forge:** A small forge out back rings when Ellard works a bar of ravine-cut stone into a blade, trading the finished edge for coin or goods and never asking where the raw stone came from.
**Numbing Moss:** A bundle of grey moss hangs drying from the rafters, packed into a wound on Ellard's own say-so to dull the pain, though he admits plainly nobody's tested the claim against a wound that mattered.
**Closed Door:** Ellard hears out anyone who's bought something from him first, and shuts the door without a word on anyone who comes only asking questions.
**Old Habit:** Ellard asks, of anyone he's decided to trust, that they carry word to whoever still keeps the deepest rite that he's sorry, nothing more, and offers a finished blade for the errand (Quest: The Hermit's Word).
**Exits:** root cellar hatch, behind the forge -> B.8 The Second Descent, footpath, north -> the wood beyond
