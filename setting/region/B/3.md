B.3 **Burial Mounds** (landmark) - *Burial Mound, Mound-Tender*
A field of grave-mounds on drier ground south of the causeway, tended by a silent people who do not explain themselves and do not approach first. One mound among them has never been opened - and everyone who has tried has turned back the same night.
*Two miles south of the causeway, a low rise holding perhaps a dozen mounds across sixty yards of dry ground, grass kept unnaturally short between them. Smells of turned earth and old smoke from small, cold fire-rings; otherwise silent in a way the surrounding marsh is not.*
**Barrow-Kin:** A kin-group of pale, silent tenders keeps this ground, offering nothing and taking nothing from the graves themselves. They will not approach first, and dig into any mound they tend is the one act that reliably ends their silence (Wary, 4, Bestiary : Barrow-Kin).
**Tended Mounds:** Most of the field, kept clear of brush and marked with small, fresh offerings nobody living placed there. Opening one of these finds old grave-goods, undisturbed (Treasure III, d20) - and Barrow-Kin attention, the moment the first spadeful is turned.
**Untried Mound:** Set apart from the others at the field's northern edge, unmarked by any offering and unmentioned by the Barrow-Kin at all. Digging into it costs nothing but time and effort - and whatever answers from inside has never yet let anyone who started digging finish the job.
**Exits:** open ground, no marked path -> reached and left by roaming the drier ground south of the causeway, with no fixed trail connecting it to anywhere else in Marfen
