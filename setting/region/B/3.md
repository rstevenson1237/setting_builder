B.3 **The Burned Wagons** (Ruin) - *charred, scattered, picked-over*
The charred **frames** of wagons lie along the ditch, their **casks** burst, and black prints mark the timbers. Smoke rises from the wood beyond the grass.
*Wreckage in a line 60 ft long. The raiders watch the road from the grass and hide from large parties.*
**Frames:** Axles, tyres and a burnt tailboard lie where they fell, with black thumbprints on the timbers, fresh over cracked old ones.
**Casks:** Burst and emptied; salt and grain are crusted in the mud.
**Raiders:** Six goblins lie in the grass with spears and slings, waiting for the next wagon or a small party (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Goblin).
