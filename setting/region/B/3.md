B.3 **Wolf Rocks** (Lair) - *bone-littered, sheltered, foul-smelling*
A heap of tumbled **boulders** stands off the stream path, **bones** scattered between them. Something has torn a scrap of blue **cloth** to rags in the entrance of a hollow.
*Boulder heap 40 ft across with hollows under the largest rock. Four wolves lie up here by day; the pack's rest is deep sleep until disturbed.*
**Boulders:** The largest rock leans over a hollow big enough for a man to crawl into. Narrow gaps between the rocks let a wolf through but not a man in armour.
**Bones:** Deer, sheep and a goblin's skull, cracked open and licked clean.
**Blue Cloth:** A keep soldier's tabard, chewed through, with no body in it.
**Wolf Den:** The pack lies in the hollows, four of them, grey and lean (Hostile, 4, Bestiary : Wolf).
