B.3 **The Cave Mouth** (Landmark) - *ledge, chevrons, clicking*
High on the far wall, a **ledge** runs before a dark **cave mouth**, and clicking sounds from inside.
*The ledge is a dozen feet wide; the whole bend of the ravine can be seen from it, and anyone on it can be seen from the path.*
**Ledge:** Bare rock trodden smooth, with splinters of wagon-board scattered at its edge.
**Cave Mouth:** A rough opening twice a man's height, chevrons scratched around it at a goblin's reach.
**Exits:** down off the ledge -> B.4 The Stream Path, into the cave mouth -> C.1 The Watch Passage
