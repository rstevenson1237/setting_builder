B.3 **The Fungus Market** (Lair) - *stalled, sweet, bustling*

Two aisles of stalls under one low roof sell caps, spore jars and carved masks. The air is sweet and thick, and haggling rises over the hum.

*A covered market about 80 feet by 40. Trade here is the fungus and its drugs (Local Procedure: Trading for the fungus and its drugs); a grey eye is chalked above the east door.*

**The Stalls:** Dreamers sell caps by the basket and spore by the jar, each jar a dose, with the price on a slate; masks hang from every rafter.
**The Scholars:** Three adepts in ink-stained grey haggle at the east stalls (Wary, 3, Faction : Sihuahet). They buy spores for their experiments and know what the fungus does to the mind; they trade that knowledge for knowledge.
**The Eye:** A grey eye is chalked over the east door and another on the nearest stall.
