B.3 **The Fungus Market** (Lair) - *stalled, sweet, bustling*

Two aisles of stalls under one low roof sell caps, spore jars and carved masks. The air is sweet and thick, and haggling rises over the hum.

*A covered market about 80 feet by 40. Trade here is the fungus and its drugs (Local Procedure: Trading for the fungus and its drugs); a grey eye is chalked above the east door.*

**The Stalls:** Dreamers sell grey caps, red spore and blue jelly by the jar, a dose a jar, the price on a slate; masks hang from every rafter.
**The Scholars:** Three in ink-stained grey haggle at the east stalls (Wary, 3, Bestiary : Adept). They buy spores for their experiments and know what the fungus does to the mind; they trade that knowledge for knowledge.
**The Eye:** Chalked grey over the east door and again on the nearest stall, the Scholars' mark (Faction : Sihuahet).
