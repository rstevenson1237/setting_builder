B.3 **The Bandit Watch** - *lookout ridge, campfire smoke, toll demanded*
A ridge-top camp of **lean-tos** overlooks the road below, its banked campfire trailing only the thinnest smoke. A handful of watchful figures track anyone who crosses into view.
*A cleared patch on high ground with clear sightlines over B.1 and much of the surrounding scrub; the watchers demand a toll from anyone they spot, escalating to violence if refused.*
**Lean-Tos:** Simple shelter for the watch.
**Campfire:** Banked low to hide the smoke.
**Bandits:** A band of Coastal Bandits (Bestiary) who demand coin, goods, or information as a toll from anyone they spot.
**Hidden Cache:** Beneath one lean-to's floorboards, holding their hoard; finding it requires searching that specific lean-to's floor.
**Exits:** path down -> B.1 Buried Road, path south -> B.8 Salt Flat Overlook
