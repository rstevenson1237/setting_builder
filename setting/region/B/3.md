B.3 **The Crocodile Bank** (Lair) - *muddy, sunning, scored*
A sun-baked mud bank lies scored with claw-furrows to the water, flies thick over the nest mounds and a hiss carrying from the channel's edge.
*300 by 120 feet. Three crocodiles sun here and strike a wader from the water's edge.*
**The crocodiles:** twelve-foot green-brown reptiles lying with eyes above water, three of them (Hostile, 3, Bestiary : Crocodile); a bite holds a target and drags it under.
**The nest mounds:** heaped mud and reeds scattered along the bank, each guarded.
**The channel edge:** the deep water beside the bank, the only way past it.
