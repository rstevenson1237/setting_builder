B.3 **The Old Cairn Road** (landmark) - *Buried, Sacred, Overgrown*
A buried road runs beneath the turf here, flanked by dozens of burial cairns older than anyone now living. One cairn stands apart, freshly re-stacked, marked with the same spiral brand the Ashward burn into their horses.
*A grassed-over roadbed a mile long, southeast of the Causeway, edged by low cairns in loose rows; quiet even for open country, and something has dug into at least three of the older cairns.*
**Buried Road:** Paved stone still runs true under the turf in places, older than the Causeway and never rebuilt.
**Cairn Rows:** Dozens of low burial cairns line the road, most intact, three visibly dug into.
**Tomb Jackals:** A pack dens among the dug cairns, bold around fresh digging, cowardly elsewhere (Skulking, 4, Bestiary : Tomb Jackal).
**Marked Cairn:** One cairn stands apart, freshly re-stacked, branded with the Ashward's own spiral; shifting its capstone reveals a narrow shaft down -> B.9 The Rider's Cache.
**Exits:** the worn side-path, north -> B.1 The Causeway
