B.3 **The Toll Stretch** (landmark) - *felled tree roadblock, price stated first, held by numbers*
A felled tree blocks the road dead across a narrow stretch between two low rises, and the **Road Toll Gang** holding it wants payment before anyone hears anything else out of them.
*A hundred-yard stretch where the road runs through a shallow cutting between two brush-covered rises, the felled trunk laid deliberately rather than fallen; woodsmoke from the Gang's cookfire drifts from just out of sight.*
**Barricade:** Trunk blocks the road; skirting means climbing rises in view.
**Road Toll Gang:** States a price first; fights only once it's refused (see setting/Bestiary.md).
**Toll Box:** Lockbox by the barricade holds real coin collected so far (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** goat-track, up the eastern rise -> B.5 The Lynx Ledge, open road south -> back toward Fellward, open road north -> onward toward the boundary stones
