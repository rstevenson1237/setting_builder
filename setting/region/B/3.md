B.3 **The Bandit Watch** - *lookout ridge, campfire smoke, toll demanded*
A ridge-top camp of **lean-tos** overlooks the road below, its banked campfire trailing only the thinnest smoke. A handful of watchful figures track anyone who crosses into view.
*A cleared patch on high ground roughly 15 yards across, built and still used as a lookout camp, with clear sightlines over B.1 and much of the surrounding scrub. It smells of banked woodsmoke, and the watchers demand a toll from anyone they spot, escalating to violence if refused.*
**Lean-Tos:** Simple shelter for the watch.
**Campfire:** Banked low to hide the smoke.
**Bandits:** A band of Coastal Bandits (Bestiary), led by Corrow (Named Creature: Corrow), who demand coin, goods, or information as a toll from anyone they spot.
**Hidden Cache:** Beneath one lean-to's floorboards - sitting slightly proud of the others, worth a careful eye - lies their hoard; prying them up reveals it (Treasure II, d20).
**Exits:** trampled dirt path -> B.1 Buried Road, trampled dirt path -> B.8 Salt Flat Overlook
