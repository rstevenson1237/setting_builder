B.3 **Tannaskar Sawpits** (landmark) - *worked, sap-slick, exposed*
A cluster of open **Sawpits** and rough log-skids marks where Khirengaard's loggers cut and buck timber close enough to the stockade to work in shifts. A narrow trail at the clearing's far edge drops toward a half-collapsed mineshaft nobody from town likes to talk about.
*A roughly 50 by 40 yard clearing of churned mud and sawdust, cut through with three long sawpits and stacked log-skids, sitting about 250 yards east of the stone line along the fringe road. The ground stays sap-slick even in dry weather, and the ring of distant axe-work carries here most of the working day.*
**Sawpits:** three pits, each roughly 12 feet long, where two-man crews buck felled logs into lengths; abandoned tools and a dropped coin purse from some hasty shift-change turn up for anyone willing to search the pits properly (Treasure I, d20).
**Log-Skids:** stacked timber awaiting haul-out along the clearing's north edge - a Fringe Wolf pack is occasionally glimpsed shadowing the treeline here, keeping well clear of the stone line and never approaching while workers are present.
**Exits:** trail, west back along the fringe road -> B.1 The Yrsk Stone-Line, muddy track, south toward wetter ground -> B.5 Hollmyr Fen, narrow trapper's path, north edge of the clearing -> B.9 The Long Trapline, half-collapsed mineshaft entrance, sagging timber frame at the clearing's southeast edge -> C.1 The Shaft-Head
