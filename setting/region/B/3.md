B.3 **The Burned Wagons** (Ruin) - *charred, scattered, picked-over*
Charred **frames** of wagons lie along the ditch with their **casks** stove in, and black **thumbprints** stain the blackened timbers. A thin smoke rises beyond the grass.
*Wreckage in a line 60 ft long; the second convoy was struck here. Raiders watch the road from the grass and hide from a large party.*
**Frames:** Axles, tyres and a burnt tailboard lie where they fell, a green wheel painted on one.
**Casks:** Stove in and emptied; salt and grain crusted in the mud.
**Thumbprints:** Tar thumbprints on timbers and bales, fresh over cracked old ones.
**Raiders:** Six goblins lie in the grass with spears and slings, watching the road for the next wagon (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Goblin).
