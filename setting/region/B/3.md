B.3 **Burial Mounds** (landmark) - *Burial Mound, Mound-Tender*
A field of grave-mounds on drier ground, south of the causeway, tended by **Barrow-Kin** who offer nothing and approach no one. One **untried mound** stands apart at the northern edge, bare of offerings.
*Two miles south of the causeway, a low rise holding perhaps a dozen mounds, across sixty yards of dry ground, the grass between them cropped short. Smells of turned earth and cold smoke, from small fire-rings, and quieter than the marsh around it.*
**Barrow-Kin:** A silent kin-group keeps this ground, taking nothing from the graves, digging into a tended mound -> their silence ends (Wary, 4, Bestiary : Barrow-Kin).
**Tended Mounds:** Most of the field, kept clear of brush, marked with small fresh offerings, opening one -> old grave-goods and Barrow-Kin attention (Treasure III, d20).
**Untried Mound:** Set apart at the field's northern edge, bare of offerings, unmentioned by the Barrow-Kin, digging into it -> something inside answers before the dig is finished.
**Worn Track South:** A packed-earth path leaves the tended ground, at its southern edge, cut by no one here, and the Barrow-Kin say it is not theirs.
**Exits:** open path - reached along the causeway itself -> B.1 Sunk Bridge Ford, open track - the worn path south, old and packed hard -> E.11 The Old Procession Track, open ground, no marked path -> the drier ground south of the causeway
