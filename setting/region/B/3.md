B.3 **Ghutrrun** (landmark) - *fastness, cord, elder*
A platform of lashed reed and withy standing on driven stakes over water too deep to wade, with four low **reed houses** on it and a **stake forest** of channel markers running out from it in every direction. An old one of the fen people sits at the platform's edge with **nine iron rings** through her ears and the knotted cord of nine channels at her belt, and she has been expecting somebody for a while.
*A staked platform about 30 by 25 yards standing three feet above water that is eleven feet deep, in the north-west of Lindmarn, some five miles north-west of the causeway's slumped section and reachable only by punt. It is one of three Vingash deep-water fastnesses and the only one a Marchman has ever been invited onto. It smells of eel smoke and wet withy, and it is startlingly quiet - the platform absorbs footfalls, and the Vingash do not raise their voices.*
**Stake Forest:** Some two hundred channel stakes driven in a fan out from the platform across half a mile of water, each carrying knotted reed cord, in a knotting that is genuinely readable and genuinely teachable. Read correctly it gives the depth of every reach in the north-west quarter. This is the most valuable thing in Lindmarn to be given, and it is given rather than taken - a party that cuts a cord here has ended the conversation and will find its route home retied wrong.
**Reed Houses:** Four low houses of lashed reed on the platform's landward side, each about 12 by 10 feet, built to be taken apart and moved rather than repaired. Eleven Vingash live here at present, smoking eel and mending traps and not attending to visitors. Nobody here will speak Ghaunt in front of a Marchman, which reads as hostility and is in fact a settled judgement about who is worth speaking to.
**Nine Rings:** The elder wears bitter iron through both outer ears, nine rings, one for each channel she has held; she is called Zhakur-of-Nine and she is the reason the Vingash position on the stair is a position rather than a reflex. She will talk, at length, and will trade what the Vingash know about Nessekire - that the barrier did not fail - for a commitment about Siwapelu. She is not difficult to reach and she is very difficult to satisfy (Named Creature: Zhakur-of-Nine). The knotted record of nine reaches hangs at her belt and she will let it be examined without being asked twice (Lore: Channel Reckoning of Nine Reaches).
**Iron Rings on the Boards:** Set out in a row along the platform's western edge, facing the open water, are eleven bitter-iron ear-rings laid on the boards above the waterline. They are not stored there; they are placed, deliberately, and replaced when weather takes them. **Clue**: the row faces west and there is nothing to the west but flooded pans the Vingash will not go to, and no watcher on this platform ever looks that way. **Trigger**: asking Zhakur-of-Nine directly what the rings are counting, or picking one up in her sight and offering it back to her. **Payload**: they are a count of the Vingash who went west during the Gell Year and did not come out. She will name the reach, describe the channel through the drowned pan walls that reaches it, and ask the party not to go - and she will describe it accurately anyway, because she has decided that somebody who has seen it is worth more to her than somebody who has not.
**Exits:** open water on every side, deep, punt only; the staked channels fan out north, east and south from the platform edge into the reed and Lindmarn is roamed rather than routed - no fixed way leads on from here
