Tags of B The Borderland

- **Washed Verge** - the cleared ground nearest the gate floods every spring, and the road across it goes soft enough to swallow a cart wheel.
- **True Treeline** - past the cleared verge, the wood closes in fully; nothing built here is visible from the keep's own walls.
- **Boundary Stones** - old-tongue-carved markers standing at irregular intervals, worn past reading by anyone at the keep now.
- **Fen Ground** - low, wet ground off the road's edge, where nothing built stands solid without being raised on its own mound.
- **Hermit's Trade** - whoever lives past the last farm trades worked goods for coin, but never comes as far as the gate to spend it.
- **Charcoal Stack** - a burner's mound smoulders somewhere off the road most seasons, tended by no one anyone at the keep can name.
- **Old Ford** - a river crossing shallow enough to wade at low water, and impassable without a detour when it isn't.
- **Night Silence** - past dusk, the wood goes quiet all at once rather than gradually, in a way every local claims to have noticed and none can explain.
- **Raider Sign** - hoofprints crossing the road at an angle no trader's cart would take, left wherever a band's passed through recently.
- **Warded Stretch** - one length of ground where nothing planted takes root, and travellers route around it rather than test why.
- **Game Trail** - a trodden path worn by beasts rather than people, wide enough to walk but leading nowhere a person means to go.
- **High Rock** - broken high ground rising clear of the treeline, the one place a party can see any distance across the region.
- **Dead Ground** - a stretch where old bones surface every spring thaw, and the local stock won't graze past its edge.
- **Trapper's Line** - a run of snares and deadfalls someone's set and re-checks on a schedule nobody at the keep is told.
- **Waystone Cairn** - a heap of stones added to by anyone who passes it safely, though nobody agrees on what it's actually for.
- **Fording Bell** - a bell hung from a dead tree at the old ford, rung by whoever's crossing so the far bank knows to expect them.
- **Squatter's Forge** - a lean-to forge past the last farm, worked by one hand and cold more often than lit.
- **Mire Path** - the one dry footing through the fen ground, known only to whoever's crossed it more than once.
- **Storm Shelter** - a fallen-in structure old enough that nobody living built it, used now only to wait out weather.
- **Herd Sign** - torn bark and rooted ground at exactly the height of a feeding animal, left along the tree line every night.
- **Watcher's Blind** - a screen of cut brush overlooking the road, built and rebuilt by whoever's currently using it to watch it unseen.
- **Old Camp** - a ring of cold fire-stones and a collapsed lean-to, abandoned long enough that nobody claims it.
- **Root Cellar** - a dug-in store off the road, stocked and re-stocked by someone who never comes near the gate for supplies.
- **Broken Bridge** - a span fallen at one end, forcing a ford or a long way round wherever the road once crossed it directly.
- **Marker Line** - a row of cut stakes some tribe has driven into the ground, claiming a stretch none of them actually holds.
