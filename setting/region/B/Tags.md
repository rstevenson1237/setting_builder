Tags of B Marfen

- **Sunk Bridge** - the ford itself, drowned at the old crossing point and held by Brekar.
- **Red Knot** - Brekar's own passage-mark, a red-dyed cloth tied in a three-loop hitch.
- **Boundary Cairn** - stone markers where three kingdoms' claims still physically overlap.
- **Old Causeway** - the one dry crossing through the marsh besides the ford itself.
- **Bog Iron** - low-grade ore drawn from the mud, valued by tanners more than smiths.
- **Reed-Root** - a bland, always-available marsh staple, edible raw or cooked.
- **Silver Dace** - a small river fish, easy to net where the current slows.
- **Flood Line** - the water mark showing how high the river rose fifteen years ago.
- **Bared Stones** - the drowned temple's upper courses, visible only at low water.
- **Weed-Choked Road** - the old approach north of the cairns, unused and unmarked now.
- **Iron Truce** - the unspoken bargain that spares anyone wearing worked iron above the
  elbow crossing Man-Ape ground.
- **Mudhold Print** - a footprint that lingers in the mud for days after it's made.
- **Watch Rider** - a mounted Brekar sentry posted in sight of the ford at all hours.
- **Mound-Tender** - the Barrow-Kin who keep the burial ground south of the causeway, never
  approaching first.
- **Untried Mound** - the one grave-mound nobody has opened; everyone who's tried has
  turned back the same night.
- **Standing Water** - still pools that hide a Ford-Eel as easily as they hide nothing at
  all.
- **Detour Track** - the week-long bypass route around the ford, muddy and barely marked.
- **Toll Camp** - Brekar's own barrier and camp at the ford, visible from the causeway.
- **Empty Seat** - what's left of the old crown's capital, watched by three armies and held
  by none.
- **Overlapping Claim** - ground two or three kingdoms' patrols separately insist is theirs.
- **Marsh Haze** - low mist that sits over the standing water past dusk, cutting sightlines
  to nothing.
- **Cropped Verge** - open ground at the marsh's edge grazed by wild aurochs herds.
- **Hollow Crossing** - a ford besides the Sunk Bridge, known only to locals, deep enough to
  drown a horse.
- **Confiscated Cargo** - goods Brekar has taken and not yet sold, stacked openly at their
  camp.
- **Split Loyalty** - a garrison patrol that won't say which kingdom actually pays it this
  season.
