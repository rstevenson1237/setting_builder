B.6 **Old Approach Road** (secret) - *Old Road, Weed-Choked Road*
A quarter mile north of the middle cairn, the weeds thin and show **buried paving** beneath. The road runs on toward the empty crown seat, which three armies watch from a distance.
*A road bed twelve feet wide, paved and grown over, running arrow-straight through ground the marsh has otherwise reclaimed. Smells of crushed weed and old stone, and a footfall here lands hard on stone instead of mud.*
**Buried Paving:** Dressed stone, a road bed rather than a rise, visible only from on top of it.
**Sinking Verge:** Where the paving has broken, a weed mat covers open marsh, stepping onto it -> the mat gives and the leg goes through (Test of Fate, Impact).
**Old Milestone:** A cut stone at the road's one bend, naming the distance to the crown seat (Lore: The Old Seat Milestone).
**Exits:** open road, paved, south end -> B.2 Boundary Cairns
