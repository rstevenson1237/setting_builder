B.6 **Kkithik** (secret) - *soured, flooded, watched*
Forty rectangular pans standing full to their coping in still yellow water, laid out in a grid to the horizon with the **pan walls** just breaking the surface between them, and a **crust** of sour yellow salt standing on every wall like frost. Nothing moves on the water, there are **iron rings** laid in rows on the copings, and at some point a party becomes certain they are being counted.
*The flooded pans run some 1200 by 800 yards, a hard rectilinear grid entirely unlike the rest of Lindmarn, lying west of the causeway and reached only through the channel Zhakur-of-Nine describes. These are the forty pans abandoned in the Gell Year, and the Vingash have not come here in eighteen years. The smell is the sour-sweet gorm, constant and strong, rising off standing water in an open place where it has no business collecting. There is no birdsong at all, which is what a party notices first.*
**Pan Walls:** The Odarath pan walls run the whole grid, knee-deep under the surface, so that the entire location is walkable by someone who knows where the walls are and drowning to someone who does not - the cuts between them are eleven feet deep and the yellow water shows nothing. This is Lindmarn's central fact in its purest form. There is no cord here and there are no stakes; the Vingash withdrew and took them.
**Yellow Crust:** A rind of sour salt standing a hand's breadth proud on every coping, pale yellow, crumbling, and quite dry despite the water an inch below it. It is what the Gell Year produced and it is why the pans were abandoned - it will not keep meat and what is packed in it goes off inside a month. The gorm comes off the water rather than the crust, and it collects here in the open in a way that it does nowhere else above ground: the Gorm track from `setting/Procedures.md` runs here, in daylight, in the open air, which no Marchman would credit and which is the whole reason the Vingash will not come.
**Iron Rings:** Bitter-iron ear-rings laid in rows along the copings of the eastern pans, above the waterline - sixty or seventy of them, in ones and twos, placed and replaced. They match the eleven laid out on the boards at Ghutrrun and they are the other half of that count. Nobody has been able to say whether this is an offering, a boundary, or a tally.
**Kkith-Moss:** Pale weed growing thick on the north face of every coping, the only living thing in the location, which is said in Torsgaard to close a wound and does, and which is also said to close it without the wound healing underneath, and does that too. It is abundant here and grows nowhere else in Brackvaen. Harvesting it costs an action and a Gorm step, and it is worth real coin to Naugen Gelling at Gellhalm, who will not ask where it came from and will not use it on anyone.
**Sump-Kin:** Perhaps sixty of them, in the flooded cuts, and they surface. They do not approach a party that stays on the walls and out of the water; anything in the water is a different matter entirely. Their iron rings have grown over. They were Vingash eighteen years ago and Zhakur-of-Nine's count is a count of these, which she knows and has never said aloud, and which is the reason the Vingash position on Siwapelu is held with the conviction it is.
**Exits:** a channel through the drowned pan walls running east, unstaked and unmarked, five miles to open water and the fastness beyond -> B.3 Ghutrrun
