B.6 **The Ford-Marker** (hidden) - *Submerged, Seasonal, Waystone*
The **Ford-Marker** is a second waystone, visible only when the river runs low, half a mile out from the Causeway's main span. Like the kerbstone above, it carries lettering nobody here can read.
*A single squared stone standing in the shallows, submerged except at low water; the ford beside it is shallower than the main causeway but far less even underfoot.*
**Waystone:** Old-tongue lettering, shorter than the Causeway's marker - likely a boundary rather than a name.
**Uneven Ford:** The riverbed here is slick and uneven; a careless crossing risks a fall in open water (Nuisance).
**Exits:** the shallows, back toward the main span -> B.1 The Causeway
