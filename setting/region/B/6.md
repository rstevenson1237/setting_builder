B.6 **The Fungus Gardens** (Natural) - *glowing, damp, spore-heavy*

Terraced beds of fungus step down the rock toward the lake, glowing pale green under a haze of spore. A faint wet creaking comes from the caps as they swell.

*Terraces about 150 yards long and 60 wide, tended by the dreamers and now spreading. Breathing the haze over a bed costs a Test of Sanity.*

**The Beds:** Caps as high as a knee, wet and glowing, each bed edged in stone. They are picked in baskets and left to grow.
**The Haze:** A glowing mist hangs over the beds (Test of Sanity, 2d). It drifts toward the quarters when the beds go untended.
**The Path:** A narrow stair between terraces leads down to the lake shore (B.7).
