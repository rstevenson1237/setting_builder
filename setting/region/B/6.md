B.6 **The Trapper's Cache** (hidden) - *trapline markers, salt-and-iron trade, avoid-this-ground warning*
A line of notched trail-marks leads off from the wallows to a dry rise where the **Trapper Clans** keep a standing cache. Someone's usually near enough to notice a stranger reading the marks.
*A dry hummock roughly 10 yards across in the middle of the wet ground, screened by reed on three sides; smells of the Trapper Clans' cured hide and woodsmoke.*
**Trapper Clans:** Trades knowledge of safe ground for coin, salt, or iron (see setting/Bestiary.md).
**Marked Trail:** Damaging a marker or trapline declares yourself the Clans' enemy.
**Missing Partner:** Trades safe-ground knowledge for word of the missing trapper (Quest: The Trapper's Partner).
**Exits:** cut trailmarks, west -> back to the Fen Wallows
