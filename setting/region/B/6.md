B.6 **The Wold-Marked Den** (hidden) - *blood-marked, denned, watchful*
Bones and torn hide litter the mouth of a shallow **Den** dug beneath a long-fallen log here, marking where the Wold-Marked Wolf actually beds down between hunts. Deep claw-scores run the length of the log's broken end, far too deliberate to be anything but a boundary marker of its own.
*A small clearing roughly 15 by 15 yards around the fallen log, reached by the trampled game-trail leading north from the toppled stone. The ground near the log's broken end is churned and blood-marked, and the air carries a thick, musky reek of wet fur.*
**Den Mouth:** a shallow burrow beneath the log's broken end, roughly 3 feet across - home to the Wold-Marked Wolf when it isn't ranging the toppled stone's clearing; scattered among the bones and torn hide at the entrance is gear from whatever it's already run down (Treasure II, d20).
**Broken Log:** the fallen trunk itself, roughly 20 feet long, deeply scored with claw-marks along one side where the wolf sharpens its claws and marks the den as its own.
**Exits:** trampled game-trail, leading back south through the brush -> B.4 The Toppled Stone
