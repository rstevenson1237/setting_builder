B.6 **Old Approach Road** (secret) - *Old Road, Weed-Choked Road*
The line of firmer ground behind the middle cairn is the old crown seat's own approach road, reached by walking it north for a quarter mile until the weeds thin enough to show the paving beneath. Nobody currently uses it, and three armies watching the empty seat it leads to would each have questions about anyone who did.
*A road bed twelve feet wide, paved and long since grown over, running arrow-straight through ground the marsh has otherwise reclaimed. Smells of crushed weed and old stone; footsteps here sound wrong against a road bed nobody has walked in a generation.*
**Buried Paving:** Dressed stone, unmistakably a road rather than a natural rise, showing through wherever the marsh hasn't fully swallowed it - the clue that this was ever a road at all is visible only once a party has already committed to following the firm ground this far.
**Sinking Verge:** Where the paving has broken, the marsh has reclaimed the road bed beneath a deceptively solid-looking weed mat - a wrong step here costs a boot, a fall, or worse before the party even reaches the milestone ahead.
**Old Milestone:** A cut stone at the road's one bend, naming the distance remaining to the crown seat in a hand older than any of the three kingdoms' own writing (Lore: The Old Seat Milestone).
**Exits:** open road, paved, south end -> B.2 Boundary Cairns
