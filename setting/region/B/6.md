B.6 **The Drowned Grove** (Natural) - *half-sunk, silent, hung*
Dead trees stand half sunk in black water, hung with grey moss, no bird calling, and bubbles rising round the trunks.
*500 by 400 feet. The footing under the water is soft mud.*
**The sunken mud:** bubbles round the sunken trunks mark soft mud; a step into it sinks (Test of Constitution, 2d, Suffocation).
**The moss-hung trunks:** grey moss drips from every trunk, and the silence is complete.
