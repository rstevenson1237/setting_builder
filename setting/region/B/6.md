B.6 **The Sinkhole** - *collapsed earth, hidden drop, second way in*
A fresh collapse in the ground drops into darkness, **loose earth** still sliding at its ragged edges. It sits just off the buried road.
*A vertical drop of roughly twenty feet into an unmapped passage, ragged-edged and clearly recent rather than an old feature of the Hills; a smell of damp earth and stone rises from below. Climbing down is straightforward with rope, climbing back out without one is not.*
**Collapsed Opening:** Climbing down (rope recommended) leads to C.19 Sinkhole Junction, deep in the Hollow Cairn - a faster but riskier entrance than the Cairn Mouth.
**Loose Rim:** A careless approach risks widening the collapse further.
**Exits:** trampled dirt path -> B.1 Buried Road, trampled dirt path -> B.5 Wolf Den, ragged sinkhole opening, loose earth and roots -> C.19 Sinkhole Junction
