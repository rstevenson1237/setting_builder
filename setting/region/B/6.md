B.6 **Waystone Cairn** (landmark) - *Unstocked, Crossroads, Mortared*
A waist-high cairn of stacked stones stands where two faint paths cross, added to by generations of travelers who've passed it safely, though nobody agrees anymore what it's actually for.
*A cairn eight feet across at the base, at a crossroads of faint paths roughly midway between the Old Ford and the fen, stones of every size and age mixed together. Left deliberately unstocked: no concealed detail, no challenge, no treasure, and no second name - a referee's own site to fill.*
**Cairn:** Every stone here was added by a traveler who passed safely and chose to leave one, and no two travelers asked about it agree on why the custom started or what stopping it would cost.
**Sealed Cavity:** A gap between two of the lowest stones has been mortared shut with something other than mud, and a strongbox key turned up at Greywatch's own quartermaster's store matches no lock anyone there could name (Keys: Unclaimed Strongbox Key).
**Exits:** faint path, crossing here from every direction -> the wood beyond
