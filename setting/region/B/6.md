B.6 **The Trapper's Cache** (hidden) - *trapline markers, salt-and-iron trade, avoid-this-ground warning*
A line of notched trail-marks leads off from the wallows to a dry rise where the **Trapper Clans** keep a standing cache. Someone's usually near enough to notice a stranger reading the marks.
*A dry hummock roughly 10 yards across in the middle of the wet ground, screened by reed on three sides; smells of cured hide and woodsmoke, and stays drier than anywhere else nearby even in a wet season.*
**Trapper Clans:** A small, self-sufficient band works a network of traplines through this stretch of country (see setting/Bestiary.md), moving too lightly to be found twice in the same season - though the cache itself is a fixed point they return to. They'll trade what they know about safe ground through here for coin, salt, or iron - the one thing had here and nowhere else nearby.
**Marked Trail:** Trail-marks cut into bark in a pattern the keep doesn't recognize lead from here back toward the wallows and, less obviously, onward past them. Damaging a marker or a trapline is treated as declaring yourself its enemy.
**Missing Partner:** One of the trapline crew hasn't come back from following a stray line too far past the wallows, toward the old foundations, weeks now. Whoever's here will trade real knowledge of safe ground for word of what happened to them, alive or dead (Quest: The Trapper's Partner).
**Exits:** cut trailmarks, west -> back to the Fen Wallows
