B.8 **The Scouted Barrow Mouth** (secret) - *fresh cart-ruts, shored entrance, leads on to Ingaraz*
Timber shoring holds open a freshly cut passage mouth in the hillside, dug with real care and covered over with brush when it's not being used. Whoever's working it clearly doesn't want company.
*A cut opening roughly 4 feet wide and 5 feet high, framed with new-sawn timber and screened by cut brush stacked to look undisturbed, hidden from the old watchtower shell above; smells of fresh-turned earth from the Diggers' Camp.*
**Digger's Camp:** Diggers work in bursts, usually elsewhere, racing rivals here (see setting/Factions.md, The Diggers).
**Missing Trapper's Sign:** Worn trapping-knife, oddly bound, half-buried inside - not Diggers' work (Quest: The Trapper's Partner).
**Exits:** shored passage, down and in -> C.10 The Outer Mound Path, cut brush screen out -> back to the old watchtower above
