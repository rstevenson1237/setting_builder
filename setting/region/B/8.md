B.8 **The Denning Cave** (hidden) - *Hidden, Deep, Guarded*
The **Denning Cave** is a shallow rock cleft barely tall enough to crouch in, floored with old bones and shed fur. Four pups huddle at the back, and at least one adult always stays close.
*A narrow cave twelve feet deep behind Wolf Hollow's rock shelter, low-roofed and close, smelling strongly of den and old kills; the pups are loud enough to hear before the cave is reached.*
**Pups:** Four Steppe Wolf pups, too young to run; the pack fights hard rather than abandon them (Territorial, 4, Bestiary : Steppe Wolf).
**Guardian:** At least one adult stays with the pups at all times, even while the rest of the pack hunts.
**Bone Litter:** Old kills dragged in and stripped bare, some predating this season - the den has held wolves for years.
**Exits:** the narrow cleft, out -> B.4 Wolf Hollow
