B.8 **The Last Dry Rise** (Natural) - *rooted, raised, watched*
Tall trees stand on the last dry rise before the hill's foot, an ochre handprint on the biggest trunk, and calls carry from the canopy.
*300 by 250 feet. A lizard men's lookout watches from the tall tree by day and a canoe patrol passes by night; the way north climbs the hill's foot.*
**The tall tree:** the tallest on the rise, an ochre handprint on its trunk, where a lizard man watches the swamp by day.
**The python:** a thirty-foot mottled snake hanging in the lookout's tree, and a sloughed skin on the trunk (Hostile, 1, Bestiary : Giant Python); it drops on what passes below.
**The hill-foot trail:** a path climbing from the rise into the foothills.
**Exits:** a trail up the hill's foot -> D.1 The Hill Trail
