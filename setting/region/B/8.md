B.8 **The Quay Walk** (Route) - *stone, curving, lit*

A curving stone quay hung with lamps runs round the lake's north shore, oars knocking and song carrying along it. Under the lamps small shapes cling and flutter.

*A quay about 400 yards long from the gate to the gardens, with four stops. Stirges hang under the lamps.*

**The First Lamp-post:** 30 yards south-east of the gate, lamps hung with spore jars and a bench.
**The Boat Steps:** 60 yards south, stone steps down to the water; reed boats tied, a dreamer asleep in one.
**The Singing Rail:** 90 yards east, a carved balustrade with masks on the rail, one worn; a path leads down to B.7.
**The Far Landing:** 120 yards south-west, where the fungus beds begin and a narrow path leads into them (B.6).
**The Stirges:** Six hang under the lamps (Hostile, 6, Bestiary : Stirge). Thin wings beat under the lamps; they drop on the warmest target.
