B.8 **The Salt Flat Overlook** (landmark) - *high vantage, sea view, storm signal*
A rocky rise offers a clear view down to **Saltmere's rooftops** and the gray line of the coast beyond. Wind here carries sound a long way.
*An exposed, roughly circular vantage point about seven yards across, a natural rocky rise rather than anything built; wind carries sound a long way up here, further than anywhere else in the Hills. From this height a party can spot the Watch Cairn's (A.6) signal fire, gauge incoming weather, or watch the Bandit Watch (B.3) at a safe distance.*
**Vantage:** Spending time here grants advance warning of weather or movement in the hills below.
**Signal-Marker Stone:** Weathered and unlit, matching the one at A.6.
**Exits:** trampled dirt path -> B.3 Bandit Watch, trampled dirt path -> B.7 Windbreak Hollow, trampled dirt path -> B.9 Old Boundary Stones
