B.8 **The Elk Wallow** (hidden) - *trampled, muddy, baited*
Reeds give way to a churned, muddy **Wallow** here, trampled flat by something far heavier than the fen's usual traffic. A Wold Elk stands knee-deep in the mud, unnaturally large and antlered, watching without much concern for anyone who's found their way in.
*A trampled clearing roughly 20 by 20 yards within the fen's reeds, the mud churned knee-deep at its center and firmer along the edges. The smell of wet hide and crushed reed is heavy here, and the fen's usual insect drone goes quiet within it.*
**Wallow:** the muddy center of the clearing, roughly 15 feet across, where a Wold Elk beds between forays deeper into the fringe - placid unless cornered, though a single kick can break a leg.
**Reed Wall:** the dense reed growth ringing the wallow, tall enough to hide anyone crouching at its edge - the only warning of the elk's presence before stepping into the clearing itself is the sudden hush in the fen's insect noise.
**Exits:** half-hidden animal trail, back through the reeds -> B.5 Hollmyr Fen
