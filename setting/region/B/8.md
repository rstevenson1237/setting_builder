B.8 **The Scouted Barrow Mouth** (secret) - *fresh cart-ruts, shored entrance, leads on to Ingaraz*
Timber shoring holds open a freshly cut passage mouth in the hillside, dug with real care and covered over with brush when it's not being used. Whoever's working it clearly doesn't want company.
*A cut opening roughly 4 feet wide and 5 feet high, framed with new-sawn timber and screened by cut brush stacked to look undisturbed; smells of fresh-turned earth over the older, colder air breathing up from within, and stays out of sight of the old watchtower shell above unless a party's already standing on top of it.*
**Digger's Camp:** A cold fire-ring and a scatter of tool-marks say a work crew has been cutting here in bursts rather than living on-site; more often than not the Diggers themselves are elsewhere when a party arrives, working another stretch of Ingaraz and meaning to come back to this one (see setting/Factions.md, The Diggers). Concealing the entrance this carefully is deliberate - the Diggers are racing the Old Tongue Grave-Robbers to whatever's sealed further in, and a found entrance is a lost head start.
**Missing Trapper's Sign:** A worn trapping-knife, its handle bound in a pattern no Fellward soldier ties, lies half-buried just inside the shoring - not Diggers' work, and not old (Quest: The Trapper's Partner).
**Exits:** shored passage, down and in -> C.10 The Outer Mound Path, cut brush screen out -> back to the old watchtower above
