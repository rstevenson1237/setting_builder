History of Kordath

900 years ago - The Khaggur ape-kin cut sea-stairs and terraces into Kordath's coastal cliffs.

150 years ago - Two horse-clan chiefs fought over the inland trade road, and the losing chief burned a day's ride of it rather than let the other hold it.
