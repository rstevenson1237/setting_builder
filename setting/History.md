History of Greywatch

900 years ago - Founding. Speakers of what Greywatch now calls the Old Tongue cut and
dressed the ravine's deepest tunnels and raised a shrine at its head, before any tribe now
living moved in. Outside living memory - nothing of who they were survives except what they
cut into the stone.
Left: chisel-work and inscriptions in the ravine's oldest stone, in a tongue no living
scholar reads - [pending 4d]

800 years ago - Working. Before they were gone, the shrine's builders bound something in
its deepest chamber to a task and set it to keep that task after they left. Outside living
memory.
Left: the working's own mark, cut into the stone where it was done - [pending 4d]

700 years ago - Withdrawal. The Old Tongue's people left the ravine, or died out; shrine
and tunnels were left standing, with no second builder's work cut anywhere into the stone
between theirs and what the tribes have since added. Outside living memory.
Left: the abandoned tunnels and shrine chamber, cut stone with nothing living in it -
[pending 4d]

100 years ago - Arrival. One of the ravine's Chaos tribes moved into the abandoned shrine
and made its chambers a den. Outside living memory.
Left: the tribe's own marks laid over the older cutting - [pending 4d]

35 years ago - Founding. The realm raised Greywatch at the road's end to hold this stretch
of frontier. Inside living memory: the keep's old-timers disagree whether the realm built
it to watch the ravine, or simply because the road gave out here first and a keep belonged
wherever it ended.
Left: Greywatch itself

6 years ago - Arrival. A priesthood serving Chaos took the shrine at the ravine's head from
the tribe holding it and began calling the ravine's scattered tribes toward one purpose.
Inside living memory: some at Greywatch say the raids on the road only turned organized
afterward, others say the ravine was always this bad and nothing has actually changed.
