History of The Isle of the Serpent Crown

### The Snake People Raise the Pyramid
When: ancient - a thousand years ago and more
Event: The snake people raised a stepped pyramid in the eastern jungle and ruled the island from it, with the lizard men for soldiers and the tribes for labour and sacrifice.
Left: A stepped pyramid standing over the canopy, and serpent-carved roads and boundary posts running out from it through the jungle.
Codes: none

### The Tribes Rise
When: old - some three centuries ago
Event: The tribes rose and burned the snake people's towns, and took the egg-stone of the lizard men's god from its altar. The snake people withdrew into the pyramid and dwindled, and the lizard men went back to the swamps they had been set to hold.
Left: Burned and vine-choked stone towns round the pyramid, and an egg-stone kept in a southern chief's house.
Codes: none

### The Victors Divide
When: old - some two centuries ago
Event: The victors split over the spoils into a northern people and a southern one, and the southern people built a great wall of stone and logs across the neck of their peninsula. The hill jungle between the shores was left to the beasts and the cave men.
Left: A wall across a peninsula's neck with one great gate, and no road through the hills between the two shores.
Codes: none

### The Ships Break on the Reef
When: living - within the memory of the oldest living
Event: Ships began to break on the western reef, and the mer people took the wrecks for their own. Pirates and treasure hunters followed the tales of the gold the reef and the pyramid hold.
Left: Hulls on the reef, a pirate anchorage in a western cove, and a treasure hunters' camp on the beach.
Codes: none
