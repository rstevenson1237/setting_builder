History of Thornmarch

### The Makers Cut the Ravine
When: ancient - many centuries ago
Event: The makers cut halls, a first shrine and passages joining what are now separate caves into the ravine's rock.
Left: Square-cut halls, a stair up the cliff and low passages between caves, all cut to one pattern and carved with the makers' script.
Codes: E.9, G.5, H.5, L.2, M.13

### The Makers Fade
When: old - longer ago than any tribe remembers
Event: A sickness emptied the makers' halls over a few years, and the last of them left or died. The tribes came after, into works already empty.
Left: Rows of burial niches cut into hall walls, most of them empty, and doors the makers sealed behind them.
Codes: M.8

### The Realm Raises the Keep
When: living - thirty years ago
Event: The realm raised a keep at the end of its road to hold the frontier, and has kept a garrison in it since.
Left: A founding stone over the main gate, cut with the realm's arms and the year.
Codes: A.1

### The Shrine Reopens
When: recent - five years ago
Event: Priests of Chaos found the makers' first shrine and reopened it. The tribes began to pay it a tithe.
Left: Fresh black stains on the old altar, and a trodden path from each cave mouth to the stair at the ravine head.
Codes: E.9, E.12, M.2
