History of Fellward

~300 years ago - Someone raised a wall across this ground long before there was a crown to
claim it; nobody now living knows who, or why here.
Left: foundation courses of a different, older-cut stone than the rest of the keep, exposed
where the cellar wall meets bedrock - C.13 The Old Foundation Course

~90 years ago - The crown chartered Fellward properly: a full garrison, a paved supply
road, and regular pay.
Left: the paved road itself, most of it grown over or washed out past the first mile out -
B.1 The Washed-Out Ford

~40 years ago - The garrison's official strength was cut by more than half. Whether the
crown ordered a real withdrawal or simply stopped paying depends who is asked - one story
says a treasury clerk crossed Fellward off a ledger by mistake and nobody with the standing
to correct it ever tried.
Left: the original barracks block, built for three times the garrison it now holds, half of
it shuttered and stripped for timber - A.3 The Barracks Block (Shuttered Half)

~12 years ago - Something moved through the treeline in numbers one bad season and took two
outer watchtowers before withdrawing on its own; the commander at the time died refusing to
admit the line couldn't hold both.
Left: the two collapsed watchtowers, still standing as broken shells past the current
watch-line - B.7 The Old Watch Stump (the second shell is only seen from B.2's Watchtower
Shell, not yet its own location)

Still resolving - The relief column requisitioned three seasons ago has not arrived, and no
word has come explaining why. The granary is already being rationed on the assumption it
never will.
Left: the requisition ledger itself, its most recent entries unanswered - A.2 The
Quartermaster's Stores (Ledger), A.1 The Granary (Counting Slate), Quest: The Missing
Dispatch (A.1 -> A.3)
