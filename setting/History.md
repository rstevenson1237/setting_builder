History of Khirengaard

Uncounted years ago - The Yrsk Wold takes root; no chronicle, human or otherwise, records a time before it.

Uncounted years ago - The standing stones are raised along the wold's edge by hands unknown. No tool-mark, quarry, or legend accounts for their placement.

400 years ago - The Marrgath, a confederation of hunter-clans, settle the wold's fringe and learn to read the stones as boundaries, not monuments - the first people known to have crossed the wold's edge and returned to say what the crossing cost.

160 years ago - The Marrgath vanish from the fringe within a single generation. Their longhouses stand roofless and empty; no siege, plague, or migration is recorded to explain it. The wold does not answer for it.

40 years ago - The Vansker Concern, a chartered logging company, stakes the fringe under a claim bought sight-unseen from a distant crown, and fells the first stand of timber outside the stone line.

23 years ago - A prospecting crew breaches an ore vein inside the stone line against the boundary-cutters' warning. Nine go in; two come out, neither of them able to say what happened to the rest.

12 years ago - Khirengaard is founded as a permanent camp at the fringe, built around the Vansker Concern's original stockade, as loggers, trappers, and prospectors settle for good rather than working the wold in shifts.

3 years ago - The boundary-cutters' guild is formally chartered after a second breach costs the town its founding factor. Since then, no stone line has been crossed without a cutter's blessing and no one has openly defied that rule.
