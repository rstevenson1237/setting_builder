History of Carrdun

Roughly six hundred years ago - the river shifted course in a single flood-season and
drowned Nemhet's capital whole, ending the priest-kings' dynasty with it. Nobody now living
knows whether it was the river's doing alone.
Left: the old causeway - a raised stone road that runs out into the current riverbed,
walkable only when the river runs low - [pending 4d]

Forty-five years ago - the frontier crown's border company built Carrdun directly atop
Nemhet's exposed footings, salvaging tomb-stone for the wall since the nearest quarry was
three weeks off. Old soldiers still disagree about whether the builders knew whose stone
they were breaking, or found out only after the first deaths on the wall crew.
Left: the wall itself, its lower courses funerary stone and its upper courses common
quarried block, the seam between them plain to anyone who looks for it - [pending 4d]

Also forty-five years ago - the garrison's granary, a squat stepped tower already standing
on the site when the walls went up, was walled in and filled with grain because nobody
building Carrdun could agree on what it had actually been raised for.
Left: the granary itself, its interior stepped shape not matching its plain outer face -

Three years ago - the quartermaster breached a sealed chamber of grave-goods while
repairing a collapsed footing, and reported it to the commander rather than the crown; the
commander has been selling excavation rights into the exposed ruin ever since, and the
crown has not yet heard a word of it.
Left: the reopened footing-shaft, boarded over, inside the garrison's own foundations -

Two winters ago - a work-gang digging on the commander's contract vanished in a single
night from a sealed section of the ruins. The handful who ran disagree about whether they
heard singing or hissing first.
Left: the sealed section, still ringed by the gang's own warning stakes - [pending 4d]

Ongoing - each spring's flood-recession has bared more of the tomb-city than the season
before, and this year's recession has not stopped yet.
