History of The Ashwrack Coast

900 years ago - The empire of Vael, ruler of the coastline and its deep harbors, offends the Drowned Court in its pursuit of forbidden pacts with the tide.

860 years ago - In a single black night, the sea rises without storm or warning and swallows Vael's capital whole; the empire's towers become reefs and its people are lost or scattered.

400 years ago - Survivors' descendants resettle the coast as scattered fishing holds, learning to read the tides that expose the drowned crypts twice a year.

150 years ago - The first salt-witches emerge among the coastal holds, women and men marked by visions of Vael's last king and able to bargain with the deep for calm water and safe passage - at a price.

40 years ago - A pirate fleet loots the Sunken Spire at an unusually low tide and returns with iron treasure and a plague of restless dreams; half the crew is dead within a year, and the Spire has not surfaced since.

3 years ago - Fishing holds up and down the coast begin reporting the same recurring dream: a bell tolling beneath the water, closer some nights than others.
