Factions of Fellward

The Quartermaster's Ring - AD: 5d6
- Resources: hoarded grain and tools skimmed off before they're logged, a handful of loyal
  quartermasters and teamsters, altered copies of the requisition ledger.
- Knowledge: the real state of the granary and what's actually left, against what the
  official ledger claims; which names on the duty roster are being carried past their term
  for a price. Has no dealings with, and no idea exists, whoever is actually living in the
  old foundations beyond the treeline.
- Tactics: controls access through rationing and paperwork rather than force - what gets
  issued, delayed, or quietly redirected, and to whom. Takes on outside hands for errands
  too visible to run with its own people, which is usually how someone new at the keep
  first meets them.
- Reactions: Noticed - tightens what the observer can access, nothing more. Crossed - the
  observer's own requisitions start going missing or arriving short. Injured - moves to
  have the interferer struck from the roster entirely, using a forged entry if a real
  reason isn't handy.
- Identity: every crate and sack that's passed through their hands carries the same chalk
  mark - a tally-stroke crossed twice - scrawled where only someone looking for it notices.
- Goals: keep the true state of Fellward's stores from reaching anyone who could act on it;
  expand what routes through their hands rather than the official stores.

The Diggers - AD: 3d6
- Resources: proper digging tools, timber for shoring, a fenced buyer for old-tongue relics
  somewhere off the border, and a quiet standing arrangement with the Quartermaster's Ring
  to move what they find without questions asked.
- Knowledge: which of the old foundations and barrows are worth the risk, and which ones
  the people living out there will actually fight to defend rather than just warn off.
- Tactics: works one site at a time, fast and thorough, and is gone before anyone can
  organize a response to it.
- Reactions: Noticed - relocates the dig, losing time rather than risking exposure.
  Crossed - posts armed watch on the current dig and treats further interference as a
  threat. Injured - abandons subtlety, takes what they can carry, and never comes back to
  that stretch of ruin again.
- Identity: marks a worked-out or claimed site with a spiral scratched into stone at knee
  height - never explained, always the same shape.
- Goals: clear one particular barrow they believe holds a sealed chamber, before anyone
  else finds the entrance they've already located.

The Barrow-Kin - AD: 2d6
- Resources: the ground itself - every safe path through the old foundations that isn't on
  any keep map - plus what their dead are buried with, which is never for sale.
- Knowledge: which graves and chambers the Diggers haven't found yet, and what's actually
  in the ones they have.
- Tactics: warns before it acts - a boundary marker moved closer, a grave re-tended visibly
  overnight - and escalates only once a warning has been ignored.
- Reactions: Noticed - moves the boundary marker again, closer still. Crossed - stops
  warning and starts making the disturbed ground unusable instead: collapsing an entrance,
  salting a campsite. Injured - treats it as a blood matter and answers in kind, at a place
  and time of their own choosing.
- Identity: every boundary they hold is marked the same way - a closed circle cut around a
  single mark on old-tongue stone - repeated nowhere else.
- Goals: drive the Diggers out of the foundations before the sealed chamber is found; keep
  the keep itself from ever learning how many of them there actually are.
