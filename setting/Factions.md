Factions of Roadend

### The Keep
AD: 4d6
Want: legitimacy - the realm's claim to the road and the land along it, acknowledged by whoever travels there
Identity: a grey tower on a white field, painted on shields, wagon-boards and the post at every milestone; patrols hail with "Road open?" and expect "Road open" back; the garrison keeps its spears with the butt-spike painted white.
Resources: the keep's walls and stores, a garrison of soldiers drawing pay from the realm, the toll-gate, and the commander's authority to grant a writ to travel.
Knowledge: which wagons left and did not arrive, and on what days; the road's whole length, and every place along it a wagon can be taken. It does not know how many goblins there are.
Tactics: tightens the gate and doubles the road patrols, and posts a bounty at the gate for proof - a tally of ears, a marked helm - rather than sending the garrison out of sight of its own walls.
Reactions: noticed - a soldier asks the party's business and its name; crossed - the gate is shut to them and the inn is told; injured - they are outlawed to the realm's writ, and any soldier on the road may arrest them.
Goals: keep the road open to wagons; learn where the raiders sleep; hold the keep without calling for more men from the realm.
Fields: Soldier

### The Goblin Tribe
AD: 3d6
Want: resource - what the road carries, taken by those who live nearest it and no one else
Identity: a chevron scratched with a knife into every wagon-board and strongbox they have stripped; helms cut from cooking pots and kettles; a dry clicking passed from throat to throat ahead of a raid; the tongue of Gob among themselves.
Resources: a cave with one door and many dark ways out, dozens of goblins, a heap of stripped goods, and the road's long reach past the keep's patrols.
Knowledge: the ravine's every ledge and hollow, the hours the patrols change, and the cave's older workings and where they lead.
Tactics: strikes a wagon at its slowest, strips it in minutes and is back in the ravine before a patrol can reach it; never meets a patrol it cannot outnumber.
Reactions: noticed - the clicking stops and the watchers slip away; crossed - the next wagon on the road is hit harder; injured - the tribe sends every goblin it has against whoever did it, and keeps coming until they are dead or gone.
Goals: strip every wagon that leaves the keep; learn what the keep will pay to stop them; keep the cave's door unwatched.
Fields: Goblin
