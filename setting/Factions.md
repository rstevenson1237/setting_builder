Factions of The Buried City

| Level | Relation | Die | Stance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Friendly | d12 | Will look to advance the causes of their allies. |
| 4 | Tolerated | d10 | Will look for equal gain between parties. |
| 3 | Neutral (or Ambivalence) | d8 | Will only cooperate with some advantage to themselves. |
| 2 | Antipathy | d6 | Will only cooperate with great advantage to themselves. |
| 1 | Hatred (or Fear) | d4 | Will only cooperate under duress, may take an opportunity to disadvantage one of their opponents, otherwise Hostility or Escape are preferred. |

### Kurumisa
Form: tongue
AD: 4d6
Want: cleared - the pyramid below level 5 cleared of the demon's cult, floor by floor.
Identity: Bronze scale over white linen, a red hawk painted on every shield and doorpost; the challenge "Who holds the gate?", answered "The gate holds."
Resources: A hall of arms on level 3, forty soldiers, stores of grain and oil, and a smith.
Knowledge: The cult's patrols below level 5 and the stair that leads to them. Not the demon's name, or what it feeds on.
Tactics: Holds its halls in ranks, sends sixes to clear a stair, and trades a guide for supplies rather than marching out.
Reactions: Noticed: challenged at spear's length. Crossed: refused the hall and its guides. Injured: hunted by sixes on every stair.
Goals: Clear the way to level 6. Recover the captain lost on level 4.
Relations:
- Ruhiahe - Antipathy
- Sihuahet - Antipathy
- Voxkroth - Hatred (fear)
- The Tomb Robbers - Neutral
Fields: Soldier

### Ruhiahe
Form: tongue
AD: 3d6
Want: sealed - the way down shut with the old rites, so the demon's servants cannot rise.
Identity: Pale robes and a bronze bell at the belt, a sun-disc painted over every doorway; the greeting "The bell is rung", answered "And the way is shut."
Resources: A temple on level 3, a cistern of clean water, a dozen acolytes and a store of oil and incense.
Knowledge: The rites that seal a door and the order they are said in. Not where the lost king lies.
Tactics: Rings the bell to call help, seals a door behind a retreat, and trades water for a service.
Reactions: Noticed: asked to wash and state its purpose. Crossed: barred from the temple. Injured: the bell rung, and every priest on the level comes.
Goals: Seal the stair to level 6. Find the ritual lost with the last queen.
Relations:
- Kurumisa - Antipathy
- Sihuahet - Antipathy
- Voxkroth - Hatred (fear)
- The Tomb Robbers - Neutral
Fields: Acolyte

### Sihuahet
Form: tongue
AD: 3d6
Want: knowing - how the demon was bound to the royal line, and how to unbind it.
Identity: Ink-stained grey robes and a reed pen worn behind the ear, an eye drawn over every door; the challenge "What do you know?", answered with a question.
Resources: A library and a workshop on level 3, a hundred scrolls, a stock of strange reagents and a score of adepts.
Knowledge: The demon's history, the cult's rites and the language of the carved walls. Not where the fighting is strongest.
Tactics: Asks questions, trades knowledge for knowledge, and reads a scroll at a party that gives a wrong answer.
Reactions: Noticed: asked what it knows. Crossed: refused the library. Injured: the adepts hold the door and read.
Goals: Learn the demon's name. Prove what feeds it.
Relations:
- Kurumisa - Antipathy
- Ruhiahe - Antipathy
- Voxkroth - Hatred (fear)
- The Tomb Robbers - Neutral
Fields: Adept

### Voxkroth
Form: tongue
AD: 4d6
Want: resource - the keys the last king hid, to open what the demon wants opened.
Identity: Black cloth and a bronze mask, ash smeared on the hands, a black hand painted on a wall to claim it; the chant "It is fed", answered "It is fed."
Resources: Cells and altars on levels 6 and 7, a score of cultists, the lieutenants' halls on level 8 and the demon's favour.
Knowledge: Where the demon is and what it wants. Not what the three branches will do.
Tactics: Chants to weaken a stranger, drags captives to an altar, and trades with the tomb robbers for the keys.
Reactions: Noticed: watched from the dark. Crossed: hunted down the stairs. Injured: the chant rises and every cell empties.
Goals: Find the lost king's keys. Feed the demon.
Relations:
- Kurumisa - Hatred
- Ruhiahe - Hatred
- Sihuahet - Hatred
- The Tomb Robbers - Tolerated
Fields: Cultist

### The Tomb Robbers
Form: common
AD: 2d6
Want: route - a way out of the buried city, and out of the pyramid with what they have taken.
Identity: Sand-browned leather, a rope coil on the shoulder and a notch cut in a doorframe for each haul; the hail "Fair split?", answered "Fair split."
Resources: A camp on level 2, a dozen delvers, a cache of rope, oil and stolen plate, and a few tricks for opening doors.
Knowledge: Which rooms are trapped, what the upper levels hold, and where the three branches do not go.
Tactics: Trades what it takes, ambushes a party that has been robbing it, and runs rather than fights the branches.
Reactions: Noticed: asked for a coin or a name. Crossed: ambushed in the dark. Injured: a price on the offender in every camp.
Goals: Find a way out of the pyramid. Sell what they have found.
Relations:
- Kurumisa - Neutral
- Ruhiahe - Neutral
- Sihuahet - Neutral
- Voxkroth - Hatred (fear)
Fields: Delver
