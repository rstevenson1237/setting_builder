Factions of Greywatch

Greywatch Garrison - AD: 5d6
- Resources: the keep's own walls and gate, its stores of grain and toll-coin, and every Greywatch Regular it can put on the wall or the road.
- Knowledge: every patrol's own schedule and every stretch of road it currently holds safe - and, wrongly as often as rightly, which tribe it credits for the last raid.
- Tactics: closes the gate and doubles the watch rather than marching out; answers a threat to the road with a contract offered at the gate instead of a company sent into the ravine.
- Reactions: noticed - the watch doubles on whatever stretch drew attention. Crossed - the gate is barred to whoever crossed it, and to anyone travelling with them. Injured - the garrison rides out once, in force, and does not again soon after.
- Identity: grey and white worn on every cloak and painted on every shield, under a tower-and-star device stamped into anything the garrison issues.
- Goals: keep the road passable between here and the realm; find whoever inside the gate is passing word out, without yet knowing there is a shrine on the other end of it.
- Fields in a fight: Greywatch Regular

The Shrine - AD: 6d6
- Resources: every bound and undead thing kept at the shrine itself, and whatever a tribe owes it for the shrine's own favour.
- Knowledge: Greywatch's patrol schedule and gate procedure, passed out through its own planted agent - knowledge no tribe on its own could get.
- Tactics: never risks its own bound servants where a tribe's warband can be spent instead; times a raid to a gap in the watch its agent has already reported.
- Reactions: noticed - the agent's own signals change, and nothing else visibly moves. Crossed - the tribe nearest the offence is set on whoever caused it. Injured - the shrine's own bound servants are sent out for the first time in living memory.
- Identity: Grozzgur spoken over every rite, and the same mark - three lines cut through a circle - left wherever the shrine's own hand has been.
- Goals: bind every tribe in the ravine to one purpose rather than its own; keep the agent inside Greywatch's gate from being found; keep whatever it inherited from the ravine's older workings running undisturbed.
- Fields in a fight: Shrine Wraith

Gorzgur - AD: 3d6
- Resources: its own warband and the cave complex it holds as a household, and whatever it's taken in a raid and not yet traded up the ravine.
- Knowledge: the ravine's own paths better than Greywatch will ever chart them, and exactly which entrance a rival tribe is currently holding too thin.
- Tactics: raids where it's weak and retreats to where it's strong, and won't stand a fight it isn't sure of winning.
- Reactions: noticed - posts more sentries at whichever entrance drew the attention. Crossed - raids whoever crossed it, or whoever that party is next seen with. Injured - calls a truce with whichever rival tribe it's fighting, just long enough to deal with the bigger threat.
- Identity: the red paint pattern worn identically by every warband member - Grozzgur for the pattern is Gorzgur itself, "the red band" - and one horn-call, always the same notes, answered from deeper in before the band itself is seen.
- Goals: hold its own household and take ground from the rival tribe pressing it hardest; give the shrine just enough to be left alone rather than compelled outright.
- Fields in a fight: Gorzgur
