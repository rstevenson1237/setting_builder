Factions of Khirengaard

Vansker Concern - AD: 4d6 +2
- Resources: the original stockade and timber rights, hired enforcers, coin enough to buy contracts outright, and a crown charter whose unsurveyed boundaries have never been challenged.
- Knowledge: which boundary-cutters can be bought, and which stretches of the survey line the guild has quietly refused to bless - and why.
- Tactics: buys up individual cutter contracts and leans on desperate prospectors to push claims past the stone line without a proper blessing, then disputes the consequences as bad luck rather than trespass.
- Reactions: closes ranks and produces paperwork when confronted - a writ, a contract, a factor's signature - rather than admitting fault, and quietly replaces whoever caused the exposure.
- Goals: extend the surveyed claim deeper into the wold before a rival company or the crown itself reasserts interest in the charter; keep the guild dependent on Concern coin rather than town coin.

Boundary-Cutters' Guild - AD: 3d6 +1
- Resources: sole legitimate knowledge of the stone lines, wold-iron tools and blades, and the trust (however eroding) of Khirengaard's founding families.
- Knowledge: the true circumstances of the founding factor's death three years ago, and the identity of at least one cutter currently taking Concern coin for false blessings.
- Tactics: withholds a blessing rather than confronts a client outright, letting the wold itself deliver consequences the guild would rather not be blamed for.
- Reactions: closes an offending cutter's practice quietly rather than publicly, preferring the town believe the guild's discipline is unbroken.
- Goals: root out the bribed cutter before another crossing goes wrong; keep the guild's authority over crossings independent of both the Concern and the town council.

Grey Charcoalers - AD: 2d6 +3
- Resources: deep-fringe kiln sites no outsider has located, a trade network of ash-marked stones where goods appear and vanish without a word exchanged, and an intimacy with the wold's rhythms no fringe-dweller can match.
- Knowledge: where the Marrgath actually went, and which stretches of the fringe the wold is quietly reclaiming next.
- Tactics: trade in silence at agreed drop-stones and vanish before contact; when followed, lead the follower in a long circle back to the stone line rather than confronting them directly.
- Reactions: abandon a kiln site entirely and burn what can't be carried the moment its location is compromised, cutting all trade with whoever caused the breach.
- Goals: keep their kiln sites and the true fate of the Marrgath unknown to both the Concern and the guild; continue trading fringe goods for what the wold provides only to them.
