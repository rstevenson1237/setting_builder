Factions of Thornmarch

| Level | Relation | Die | Stance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Friendly | d12 | Will look to advance the causes of their allies. |
| 4 | Tolerated | d10 | Will look for equal gain between parties. |
| 3 | Neutral (or Ambivalence) | d8 | Will only cooperate with some advantage to themselves. |
| 2 | Antipathy | d6 | Will only cooperate with great advantage to themselves. |
| 1 | Hatred (or Fear) | d4 | Will only cooperate under duress, may take an opportunity to disadvantage one of their opponents, otherwise Hostility or Escape are preferred. |

### The Keep
Form: common
AD: 4d6
Want: route - the road to the realm held open, wagon after wagon.
Identity: A white tower on blue, worn as a tabard and stamped on every crate and seal; the challenge "Who holds the road?", answered "The road holds."
Resources: Forty soldiers, the walls, stores of grain and arms, a wagon from the realm every fortnight, a chapel that heals for a price, and coin for what a party brings back.
Knowledge: The wagon days, and that wagons have been lost near the wood's edge for two months. Not how many raiders there are, or who commands them.
Tactics: Patrols the road by day in sixes, shuts the gate at dusk, and pays rewards rather than marching out.
Reactions: Noticed: challenged and asked its business. Crossed: refused the gate and its trade. Injured: put out of the keep and left to the road.
Goals: Get each wagon through. Learn who commands the raiders and where they sleep.
Relations:
- The Carters' Guild - Friendly
- The Wood Outlaws - Hatred
- The Sellaca - Antipathy
- The Gubak - Antipathy
- The Zhakuk - Antipathy
- The Rokmok - Antipathy
- The Burgnaz - Antipathy
- The Kurzun - Antipathy
- The Zurnaz - Antipathy
- The Black Altar - Hatred
Fields: Soldier

### The Carters' Guild
Form: common
AD: 1d6
Want: debt - the losses the guild owes its shippers repaid.
Identity: A green wheel painted on tailboards and gateposts; three knots in a cord tied round a post, which marks a safe stop.
Resources: Eight wagons and their drivers, who fight when pressed, a hall at the keep's market, and the shippers' coin.
Knowledge: The state of the road, which drivers have vanished and where their wagons were found, and what every wagon carried.
Tactics: Runs convoys on the keep's wagon days, hires guards at the market, and posts a bounty for lost cargo.
Reactions: Noticed: asked for news of the road. Crossed: refused a seat on the wagons. Injured: shut out of the market's trade.
Goals: Recover the lost wagons' cargo. Keep the convoys running.
Relations:
- The Keep - Friendly
- The Wood Outlaws - Hatred
- The Sellaca - Neutral
- The Gubak - Neutral
- The Zhakuk - Neutral
- The Rokmok - Neutral
- The Burgnaz - Neutral
- The Kurzun - Neutral
- The Zurnaz - Neutral
- The Black Altar - Neutral
Fields: none

### The Wood Outlaws
Form: common
AD: 2d6
Want: resource - coin and goods from whoever uses the trail.
Identity: A hare's foot hung from a branch; a ring of bark slashed at head height round a trunk.
Resources: A camp of twenty with bows, a hidden cache, poached game, and a fence who buys at the keep's market.
Knowledge: Every path in the wood, which carters pay a toll, and the route the tribes' tithe takes.
Tactics: Takes a toll with bows drawn from cover, and lets a party that pays go on.
Reactions: Noticed: watched from cover. Crossed: shot at from the trees. Injured: burn the trail's bridges and lay false paths.
Goals: Keep the trail's tolls. Stay clear of the keep's patrols.
Relations:
- The Keep - Hatred (fear)
- The Carters' Guild - Neutral
- The Sellaca - Antipathy
- The Gubak - Neutral
- The Zhakuk - Neutral
- The Rokmok - Neutral

- The Black Altar - Neutral
Fields: Soldier

### The Sellaca
Form: tongue
AD: 2d6
Want: sealed - the mere closed to strangers.
Identity: Bundles of reed tied with red thread on causeway posts; fish-bone spear points set upright in the mud.
Resources: A stilt village of thirty on the mere, pole boats, spears, and knowledge of the firm ground.
Knowledge: The one safe causeway, the sunken shrine's place, and where the drowned lie.
Tactics: Watches from the reeds, and strikes anyone who leaves the causeway.
Reactions: Noticed: followed along the reeds. Crossed: the causeway posts are pulled and the planks sunk. Injured: boats come out at night in force.
Goals: Keep the mere clear of strangers. Keep the causeway theirs.
Relations:
- The Keep - Neutral

### The Gubak
Form: tongue
AD: 1d6
Want: debt - the tribute owed to the goblins paid before it is collected.
Identity: A grinning dog-face chalked on rock; strings of tin and bone hung across passages as rattles.
Resources: Thirty or so kobolds, pits and dart traps, and a small hoard set aside for tribute.
Knowledge: The trap-ways of their own warren, what the goblins above take and when, and when the bugbears come for the tithe.
Tactics: Lures pursuers over its traps and runs for the deep passages.
Reactions: Noticed: rattles sound down the passage. Crossed: every trap is set. Injured: the warren empties into the goblins' hold.
Goals: Pay the tribute on time. Stay alive.
Relations:
- The Keep - Neutral
- The Carters' Guild - Neutral
- The Wood Outlaws - Neutral
- The Sellaca - Neutral
- The Zhakuk - Hatred (fear)

### The Zhakuk
Form: tongue
AD: 2d6
Want: resource - what the road carries.
Identity: A black tar thumbprint on doors and on every bale taken; a wolf skull on a pole at the cave mouth.
Resources: Twenty to thirty goblins, a dozen wolves, the kobolds' tribute, and loot from the road.
Knowledge: The wagon days, learned by watching the road, and the traps of the warren below.
Tactics: Strikes small wagons and lone travellers from cover at the wood's edge and the ravine banks at dusk, and withdraws before a patrol arrives.
Reactions: Noticed: watched, and followed to the wood's edge. Crossed: ambushes laid on the stream path. Injured: sally at night in force.
Goals: Fill the hold with the road's stores. Break the orcs beside them.
Relations:
- The Keep - Neutral

### The Rokmok
Form: tongue
AD: 3d6
Want: rival - the goblin tribe broken.
Identity: A notched tusk nailed above a door; a cairn of skulls at the cave mouth.
Resources: Twenty-odd orcs, a feast hall's stores, heavy axes, and a still that brews more than the tribe can drink.
Knowledge: The goblins' habits, and the passage that joins their stores to the orcs' own.
Tactics: Charges at once in a mass and drives what it strikes before it.
Reactions: Noticed: roared at from the ledge. Crossed: a raid at dawn on whoever crossed them. Injured: the whole tribe comes out, drunk or not.
Goals: Break the goblins. Take the goblins' stores.
Relations:

### The Burgnaz
Form: tongue
AD: 3d6
Want: legitimacy - the shrine's favour, and a name above the other tribes.
Identity: A red-dyed sash worn by every warrior; a red hand stencilled beside each gate and spears planted upright in ranks before it.
Resources: Twelve to fifteen hobgoblins in mail, arms and a store of grain, and a key to the shrine.
Knowledge: The tithe days, the shrine's sanctum door, and what the gnolls sell and to whom.
Tactics: Holds the gate in a shield line and advances only in step.
Reactions: Noticed: challenged at the gate. Crossed: a patrol shadows the party until it leaves. Injured: a column marches on the one who struck.
Goals: Be the shrine's captains. Break the gnolls beside them.
Relations:
- The Keep - Neutral

### The Kurzun
Form: tongue
AD: 2d6
Want: person - captives to sell, and a buyer who pays.
Identity: Strips of spotted hide hung from door lintels; iron collars hung at the den mouth.
Resources: A dozen gnolls, pens of captives, barbed spears, and a key to the maze.
Knowledge: The maze's way through, which buyers pay best, and who goes missing from the road.
Tactics: Circles a party, hamstrings the slow, and drags off whoever falls.
Reactions: Noticed: trailed at a distance, laughing. Crossed: a pack hunts the party at night. Injured: sell its captives and burn the den.
Goals: Sell the captives for coin. Break the hobgoblins beside them.

### The Zurnaz
Form: tongue
AD: 2d6
Want: route - the tithe path from every cave to the shrine kept open and paid.
Identity: A soot handprint at head height on rock; a row of sticks stuck in the ground, the tops smeared dark.
Resources: Six to ten bugbears, the tithe in store, a back stair to the shrine, and the shrine's pay in meat.
Knowledge: Where each tribe keeps its tithe, and the back stair's door.
Tactics: Waits hidden along the path and takes the last of a party or a late tithe.
Reactions: Noticed: watched from above. Crossed: a tithe taken from whoever carried it. Injured: they do not retaliate; the Black Altar is told.
Goals: Collect every tithe. Keep the tribes in the shrine's debt.
Relations:
- The Keep - Neutral
- The Carters' Guild - Neutral
- The Wood Outlaws - Neutral
- The Sellaca - Neutral
- The Gubak - Neutral

### The Black Altar
Form: common
AD: 3d6
Want: hiding - its hand unseen at the keep while the tribes are gathered.
Identity: A black candle burned to a stub; an inverted triangle cut into stone or wood.
Resources: A high priest, a dozen acolytes and guards, the dead, the tithe, and two agents inside the keep.
Knowledge: The keep's wagon days and patrol rounds, from its agents; the makers' sanctum and what it was built to hold.
Tactics: Works through its agents at the keep and through the bugbears in the ravine; raises the dead where a hold is lost.
Reactions: Noticed: nothing is done, and the agents are told. Crossed: the bugbears are sent. Injured: the dead are raised against the party.
Goals: Bring every tribe under the tithe. Learn the keep's wagon days.
Relations:
- The Keep - Neutral
- The Carters' Guild - Neutral
- The Wood Outlaws - Neutral
- The Sellaca - Neutral
- The Gubak - Tolerated
