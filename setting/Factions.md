Factions of Telar

Brekar - AD: 5d6
- Resources: the Sunk Bridge ford itself, held as a chokepoint; horses; a growing stockpile
  of confiscated cargo they haven't yet found a buyer for.
- Knowledge: every bypass route around the ford, scouted before they ever took it; which
  caravans travel with guards worth avoiding and which are soft.
- Tactics: hit a soft target fast and fall back to the ford before pursuit forms - they do
  not fight to keep ground they didn't already hold.
- Reactions: noticed - raise the toll, as a message rather than a threat. Crossed - the next
  caravan belonging to whoever crossed them is the one they take everything from. Injured -
  burn what they're holding rather than let it be retaken.
- Identity: a strip of red-dyed cloth, knotted in a distinctive three-loop hitch, left on
  anything they've let pass and anyone they've let go - the same knot at every crossing they
  work, and copying it wrong is worse than not copying it at all.
- Goals: hold the ford exactly as long as the toll outpays the risk of a garrison finally
  organizing to take it back.

The Nine Wagons - AD: 4d6
- Resources: coin; warehouses in each of the three kingdoms' walled cities; standing
  contracts with mercenary sellswords for guard work.
- Knowledge: exactly what the Sunk Bridge detour is costing each of the three kingdoms in
  lost toll, to the coin - and that running the alternate route at a markup is currently
  their single best-earning line. Also knows which garrison officers are underpaid enough to
  buy.
- Tactics: buys safe passage and information rather than fighting for either; where a route
  turns unsafe, raises prices before it lifts a finger to fix anything.
- Reactions: noticed - offer the interferer a cut. Crossed - undercut them in every market
  the Wagons touch until they can't afford to keep interfering. Injured - call in every
  mercenary contract currently on their books, all at once, against whoever did it.
- Identity: every wagon in the company is painted the same blue-grey and carries a small
  brass bell tied to the brake lever - recognisable by sound before it's recognisable by
  sight, which is deliberate.
- Goals: keep the Sunk Bridge detour profitable for as long as it lasts - and, quietly, keep
  it lasting. A party that retakes the ford for the "right" reasons is working directly
  against this faction's interest without necessarily knowing it.

The Serpent Cult - AD: 3d6
- Resources: hidden safehouses inside otherwise ordinary settlements; blackmail material on
  people of standing; a little real sorcery, at the price every working here costs.
- Knowledge: Essath, read and spoken better than almost anyone else living; which of the
  three kingdoms' courts already has a cultist seated inside it; at least one unspent Essath
  name, hoarded rather than used.
- Tactics: never confronts directly - exposes what a target most wants hidden, or removes an
  obstacle quietly, rather than risk anything open.
- Reactions: noticed - the cell goes quiet and relocates. Crossed - exposes whatever the one
  who crossed them most wants hidden, the party included. Injured - spends a hoarded Essath
  name against whoever hurt them, once, at the full and permanent cost that spending one
  always carries.
- Identity: a small coiled-serpent mark, tattooed or scarred, kept under clothing where it
  will not be seen by accident - and among themselves, a cultist identifies safely by
  invoking "the old debt" in ordinary conversation.
- Goals: keep Nirguk's enclaves undiscovered; place a cultist into a position of standing in
  each of the three kingdoms' courts, one seat at a time.
