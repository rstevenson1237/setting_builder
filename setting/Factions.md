Factions of The Isle of the Serpent Crown

| Level | Relation | Die | Stance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Friendly | d12 | Will look to advance the causes of their allies. |
| 4 | Tolerated | d10 | Will look for equal gain between parties. |
| 3 | Neutral (or Ambivalence) | d8 | Will only cooperate with some advantage to themselves. |
| 2 | Antipathy | d6 | Will only cooperate with great advantage to themselves. |
| 1 | Hatred (or Fear) | d4 | Will only cooperate under duress, may take an opportunity to disadvantage one of their opponents, otherwise Hostility or Escape are preferred. |

### Pulunita
Form: tongue
Gloss: the southern villages
AD: 4d6
Want: person - the captives taken in the swamp raids brought home, and the raids stopped.
Identity: White clay stripes across the brow and cheeks; the greeting "Who keeps the wall?", answered "The wall keeps us."
Resources: Three stockaded villages and a great wall with one gate, a hundred spears, canoes on the lagoon, gardens and guest houses, and the egg-stone in the chiefs' house.
Knowledge: The lagoon's channels and the swamp's edges, the ways round the wall's ends, and what comes to the gate at night. Not what the lizard men do with their captives.
Tactics: Holds the wall and the lagoon, sends canoe patrols to meet raiders in the shallows, and trades guides and canoes for a party's help.
Reactions: Noticed: hailed from the wall and asked what the party carries. Crossed: refused the gate and the market. Injured: every village told, and a spear on every path.
Goals: Win back the captives of the last raid. Keep the egg-stone in the chiefs' house.
Relations:
- Lemunita - Antipathy
- Theshik - Hatred
- Nealeu - Neutral
- Tuuzesh - Hatred (fear)
- The Cove Pirates - Neutral
- The Treasure Hunters - Neutral
Fields: Tribesman

### Lemunita
Form: tongue
Gloss: the northern villages
AD: 3d6
Want: knowing - what is taking their people, before they send a war party into the hills.
Identity: A net pattern of blue-black pigment on the forearms; a fish laid on the threshold of a guest's door.
Resources: Two villages at a river mouth and a hunters' camp up the river, fishing boats, forty bows, smoked fish and hill guides.
Knowledge: The river and the hill trails behind the north shore, and where the hunters have seen the cave men's fires. Not the south's wall or what is kept behind it.
Tactics: Watches from cover, trades fish and guides at a higher price to anyone come from the south, and sends hunters to see before a council decides.
Reactions: Noticed: watched from the trees until the party is near a village. Crossed: the fish taken from the door, and no guide to be had. Injured: the hunters turn out and the hill trails close.
Goals: Learn what happened to the lost hunting party. Learn what the south is feeding.
Relations:
- Pulunita - Antipathy
- Theshik - Neutral
- Nealeu - Neutral
- Tuuzesh - Hatred (fear)
- The Cove Pirates - Neutral
- The Treasure Hunters - Neutral
Fields: Hill Hunter

### Theshik
Form: tongue
Gloss: the lizard men
AD: 3d6
Want: debt - the egg-stone of their god, taken in the rising, returned to them.
Identity: An ochre band painted across the throat; the question "Where is the egg?" put to every stranger.
Resources: Enclaves on hummocks in the southern mangroves, war canoes in the channels, nets, and captives held for sale.
Knowledge: Every channel and hummock of the swamp, the southern villages' raiding paths, and the way into the pyramid's towns.
Tactics: Raids by water at night, takes captives rather than corpses, sells the captives on, and breaks off a fight on open ground.
Reactions: Noticed: watched from the water without a sound. Crossed: the channels close behind a party. Injured: war canoes follow it to the swamp's edge.
Goals: Win the egg-stone back. Take enough captives to keep the snake people's goodwill.
Relations:
- Pulunita - Hatred
- Lemunita - Neutral
- Nealeu - Neutral
- Tuuzesh - Neutral
- The Cove Pirates - Neutral
- The Treasure Hunters - Neutral
Fields: Lizard Man

### Nealeu
Form: tongue
Gloss: the mer people
AD: 3d6
Want: cleared - the pirates' anchorage gone from their water.
Identity: A belt and collar of green weed and shell; three falling notes sung before any approach.
Resources: Sea caves and the wrecks of the western reef, the reef's passages, nets, tridents, and the wrecks' treasure.
Knowledge: The reef's passages and every wreck on it, what the sea has taken in a century, and the pirates' habits at the cove.
Tactics: Keeps to the water and the reef's passages, trades for iron and fire-worked things, and drowns whoever takes from a wreck unasked.
Reactions: Noticed: followed under the water by a song. Crossed: a boat holed on the reef. Injured: the passages closed and the song changed.
Goals: Clear the anchorage. Get iron and fire-worked goods without going ashore.
Relations:
- Pulunita - Neutral
- Lemunita - Neutral
- Theshik - Neutral
- Tuuzesh - Neutral
- The Cove Pirates - Hatred
- The Treasure Hunters - Neutral
Fields: Merman

### Tuuzesh
Form: tongue
Gloss: the snake people's cult
AD: 4d6
Want: resource - captives and blood to feed the god until it wakes.
Identity: A green scale-mark inked over the heart and hidden under clothes; the words "The sleeper turns", answered "And we are the cradle."
Resources: The stepped pyramid, its halls and treasury, the first of the snake people, men bound in both peoples' villages, and captives bought from the lizard men and the pirates.
Knowledge: The pyramid's every way and what the god under it eats, the bound men in the chiefs' councils, and what each village's chief fears.
Tactics: Buys captives, binds a few men and sends them home, keeps patrols and bound men on the eastern roads, and lets the villages blame each other.
Reactions: Noticed: followed by bound men and patrols. Crossed: the bound men in the councils turn the villages against the party. Injured: the pyramid's patrols fill the eastern roads.
Goals: Feed the god enough to wake it. Keep the bound men in both councils.
Relations:
- Pulunita - Hatred
- Lemunita - Hatred
- Theshik - Tolerated
- Nealeu - Hatred
- The Cove Pirates - Tolerated
- The Treasure Hunters - Hatred
Fields: Snake Man

### The Cove Pirates
Form: common
AD: 2d6
Want: resource - the reef's gold and a buyer for every captive taken.
Identity: A red sash and a black cloth tied on the left arm; the toast "Share and share", answered "And the cove keeps the rest."
Resources: A ship at an anchorage in a western cove, a crew of a dozen, rum, a prize crew's loot and a boat for the reef.
Knowledge: The coast's landings, the reef's outer wrecks, and what the snake people pay a head.
Tactics: Hails a party, bargains until the odds are bad, takes a prize by boarding at night, and sells prisoners on to the pyramid.
Reactions: Noticed: hailed in the common tongue and offered a drink. Crossed: boarded at night. Injured: the ship hunts the party's boat.
Goals: Take the reef's gold. Sell captives to the snake people. Take the treasure hunters' map.
Relations:
- Pulunita - Neutral
- Lemunita - Neutral
- Theshik - Neutral
- Nealeu - Neutral
- Tuuzesh - Neutral
- The Treasure Hunters - Antipathy
Fields: Pirate

### The Treasure Hunters
Form: common
AD: 2d6
Want: route - a way to the pyramid's gold by a map of their own.
Identity: Grey canvas coats with brass buttons; a copper compass on a chain at the neck.
Resources: A beach camp and a second landing on the north shore, crossbows, a ship's boat, tinned stores and a half-copy of the party's map.
Knowledge: A copy of the route to the pyramid, a surveyed stretch of the eastern jungle's edge, and the reef's anchorages.
Tactics: Greets a party as a rival, trades news for news, follows the party's boat, and bargains with whoever holds a map.
Reactions: Noticed: greeted and traded with. Crossed: followed and robbed in the jungle. Injured: the camp turns out with crossbows.
Goals: Reach the pyramid first. Find the deserter who took half their map.
Relations:
- Pulunita - Neutral
- Lemunita - Neutral
- Theshik - Neutral
- Nealeu - Neutral
- Tuuzesh - Neutral
- The Cove Pirates - Antipathy
Fields: Treasure Hunter
