Factions of Carrdun

The Salvage Concern - AD: 4d6
- Resources: the commander's contracts, a rotating crew of licensed and unlicensed
  diggers, and the only reliable cart-route between the diggings and the fort.
- Knowledge: which sealed sections have been surveyed and which merely marked off, and
  exactly what the crown has not yet been told.
- Tactics: buys silence and labour before it resorts to force, and works fast on whatever
  it has just paid to secure before anyone with a prior claim finds out.
- Reactions: noticed - offers a cut to be let alone; crossed - has the offender's contract
  and standing with the commander quietly pulled; injured - hires outside muscle from the
  river-towns to settle it, no longer bothering with the commander's cover.
- Identity: a triangular tin tag stamped with a stepped pyramid, worn on a cord by every
  crew member the Concern actually trusts.
- Goals: keep digging the richest unopened sections before the crown learns their scale;
  find whatever the quartermaster's original chamber was guarding before anyone else does.

The Coil - AD: 2d6
- Resources: fragments of the Xoss tongue, a handful of Coil-Marked kin scattered through
  Carrdun and the dig crews, and total familiarity with the sealed sections nobody else has
  fully surveyed.
- Knowledge: which sealed sections are genuinely dangerous to open and which are merely
  marked to look that way, and what happened to the work-gang that vanished two winters ago.
- Tactics: never fights in the open - steers digs away from what it wants left alone and
  toward what it wants found, using planted rumours and a placed initiate rather than force.
- Reactions: noticed - withdraws its people and waits; crossed - the coil-mark starts
  appearing on the offender's own door; injured - answers with whatever the sealed section
  in question was built to hold.
- Identity: a coil scratched into stone or wood at knee height, always pointing the same
  direction, and a scale-patch worn hidden rather than displayed.
- Goals: keep one particular sealed section from ever being opened by outsiders; recover
  anything of Nemhet's that the Concern's diggers turn up before it leaves the ground.

The Ashward Riders - AD: 6d6
- Resources: numbers, horses, and total command of the open steppe beyond the ford - no
  cart or courier crosses it without their leave or their notice.
- Knowledge: every ford, water source, and dead ground for two days' ride in any direction,
  and which digging crews have pushed past the causeway this season.
- Tactics: shows itself first as a raid or a toll demand, and only escalates to a real
  fight if refused outright or if the ground in question is the causeway itself.
- Reactions: noticed - a rider watches openly from the far bank until whoever it's watching
  leaves; crossed - the next caravan or cart on that route pays for it; injured - a raid in
  strength on the offending party's own camp or crew, not the fort.
- Identity: a red-dyed horsehair tassel knotted to the bridle, and a spiral brand burned
  into the shoulder of every horse they've raised themselves.
- Goals: keep diggers from working the ground past the causeway, which the Riders hold as
  burial ground regardless of whose dead lie in it; drive the price of safe passage on the
  ford higher every season.

The Salvage Concern does not know the Coil exists inside its own crews - a planted
initiate looks, on any manifest, like an ordinary hire.
