Factions of The Ashwrack Coast

Saltmere Hold - AD: 3d6 +1
- Resources: a working fishing fleet, the bronze diving-bell that grants its keeper authority along the coast, stockpiled salvage, and a modest militia of hold-guards.
- Knowledge: keeps the tallies of every debt its people owe the sea, and holds fragments of oral history tying the drowned crypts to Vael's fall.
- Tactics: leverages the diving-bell's authority to rally neighboring holds, trading protection and salvage rights for loyalty and grain.
- Reactions: closes ranks and calls in favors when threatened; if the bell itself is lost or destroyed, the hold's authority fractures and rival claimants emerge within the season.
- Goals: recover its stolen diving-bell; keep outsiders from stirring the drowned crypts and drawing the sea's attention back to the coast. Suspects the Tallow Fleet of the theft, though it has no proof.

Tallow Fleet - AD: 4d6 +1
- Resources: a scavenged flotilla of raider ships, a hidden cove, and plundered stores of iron and widow-glass.
- Knowledge: knows the tide-windows safe enough to reach the Sunken Spire, and knows something beneath it expects a cut of every haul - though not what that something truly is.
- Tactics: raids shipping and isolated holds for iron and coin, then delivers a share of the take to the waters beneath the Spire to keep it quiet.
- Reactions: if its tribute run is disrupted or its cut stolen, the Fleet turns openly hostile and raises the pace of its raids to make up the loss before the debt comes due.
- Goals: keep whatever lives beneath the Spire satisfied without ever learning what it wants; expand its raiding range now that the Spire has resurfaced. Views Saltmere Hold as a soft target and a rival for salvage rights, with no particular malice behind the raids beyond profit.

The Drowned Court - AD: 6d6 +3
- Resources: the flooded ruins of Vael itself, command over the deep's creatures (the Drowned Folk, the Tide Serpent, the Deep Whisperer), and the Court Herald to speak on its behalf.
- Knowledge: remembers the true history of Vael's fall and holds the tally of every debt owed to it by every soul on the coast - including debts Saltmere Hold and the Tallow Fleet don't know they carry.
- Tactics: acts indirectly through omens, bargains offered by a Deep Whisperer, and tribute quietly demanded of anyone who disturbs its ruins, rather than open assault.
- Reactions: escalates in stages - omens first, then a Court Herald's visit, then open predation - as debts against it go longer unpaid or its ruins are looted further.
- Goals: collect on every debt it can reach, one bargain and one trespasser at a time, and keep its ruins from being looted further. Knows both Saltmere Hold and the Tallow Fleet by name and behavior; neither knows it in return.
