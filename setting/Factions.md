Factions of Roadsend

### The Keep
AD: 3d6
Want: route - the wagon road to the realm kept open, every fortnight.
Identity: A white tower on blue cloth, worn as a tabard and sewn on crates and seals; the challenge "Who holds the road?", answered "The road holds."
Resources: Forty soldiers, the walls, stores of grain and arms, a wagon from the realm every fortnight, and coin to pay for what a party brings back.
Knowledge: The wagon days, and that wagons have been lost near the ravine's mouth for two months. Not how many raiders there are, or where they sleep.
Tactics: Patrols the road by day in sixes, shuts the gate at dusk, and pays rewards rather than marching out.
Reactions: Noticed: challenged and asked its business. Crossed: refused the gate and its trade. Injured: put out of the keep and left to the road alone.
Goals: Get each wagon through. Learn where the raiders sleep.
Fields: Soldier

### The Zhakuk
AD: 2d6
Want: resource - what the road carries, wagons and travellers alike.
Identity: A red clay handprint daubed on stones, doors and every bale taken; a string of knucklebones worn at a chief's belt.
Resources: The cave and its workings, loot from the road, and a few dozen goblins whose number no one outside knows.
Knowledge: Every way into and out of the cave, and the days the wagons run, learned by watching the road.
Tactics: Strikes small wagons and lone travellers from the ravine banks and withdraws into the cave before a patrol arrives.
Reactions: Noticed: watched, and followed to the ravine's mouth. Crossed: ambushes set on the stream path. Injured: sallies at night in force against whoever struck.
Goals: Fill the cave with the keep's stores. Keep the cave against the keep.
Fields: Goblin
