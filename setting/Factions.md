Factions of Duen

The Dutor Wardens - AD: 5d6
- Resources: the Dutor quarry's tools, timber, and carts; a paid roster of overseers and
  toll-clerks; the only forge in Duen big enough to reshoe a full team.
- Knowledge: which galleries are sound and which are close to going, including the sealed
  lower gallery's real condition; who's paid a toll and who's dodged one, going back years.
- Tactics: leans on the toll rather than the sword - a wagon that won't pay finds its axle
  "inspected" until it will.
- Reactions: noticed - a Warden starts asking pointed questions about your business.
  Crossed - your tolls double and your carts get searched at every checkpoint. Injured -
  they stop extending credit on tolls to anyone, and name names to the garrison.
- Identity: a chalk-dust handprint pressed to the left of any door they've inspected or
  claimed, renewed whenever it fades.
- Goals: keep the quarry road the only viable crossing for as long as the low ford stays
  down; reopen the sealed lower gallery, quietly, before anyone else notices what's in it.

The Salir - AD: 3d6
- Resources: rope, rigging, and rafts enough to run a crossing nobody else controls;
  intimate knowledge of the gorge's currents and hidden trails down to the water.
- Knowledge: every crossing point along the Durad that isn't the ford or the quarry road,
  and which ones the garrison doesn't know exist.
- Tactics: names a price before anything else is discussed, and raises it mid-crossing if
  a party has no way to back out.
- Reactions: noticed - the price goes up. Crossed - the crossing "isn't running today,"
  indefinitely. Injured - they cut the ropes and relocate the whole operation overnight.
- Identity: a length of red-dyed cord, worn openly - braided into hair, tied at the wrist,
  looped through a belt.
- Goals: keep their crossing point secret from the Wardens for as long as the quarry road
  stays crowded and profitable; recruit anyone who knows the gorge better than they do.

The Xarask - AD: 4d6
- Resources: the flooded lower workings themselves - tunnels, standing water, and
  darkness no lantern crew wants to work in; numbers nobody above ground has counted.
- Knowledge: what's actually down past the Old Cut's third level, and how far the water
  has spread since the cave-in nineteen years back.
- Tactics: never meets force with force in the open - draws intruders deeper along
  half-flooded passage until the way back is no longer obvious.
- Reactions: noticed - broodlings are seen closer to the surface than usual, for days.
  Crossed - work stops in whatever gallery caused it, with no warning why. Injured - the
  flooding gets worse in that gallery within the week, by no mechanism anyone can find.
- Identity: a smeared, foul-smelling residue drawn in a rough spiral wherever they've
  passed.
- Goals: keep the sealed lower gallery sealed; expand the flooded workings outward at a
  pace nobody watching from above has yet noticed.

Ignorance: the Salir have never worked the quarry and don't know the Xarask exist; the
Xarask have never breached the surface and don't know the Salir exist either. The Wardens
suspect something is wrong with the lower gallery and know nothing about the rope-crossing
downriver at all.
