Factions of Sundfall

The Tallyhouse - AD: 2d6
- Resources: nearly every panner-crew's bond-paper, a fortified counting-house, hired ledger
  enforcers, and the unlegislated but unchallenged right to weigh and price salt at the
  scales.
- Knowledge: knows exactly which crews are close to working off their bonds and which are
  sinking further in, and moves fast to buy the paper of any crew that finds something
  valuable before word of the find gets out.
- Tactics: never seizes by force first - buys the debt of whoever has crossed it, then calls
  the whole bond due at once.
- Reactions: noticed - an enforcer pair starts "checking the scales" nearby; crossed - buys
  out whoever else holds paper on the offender and calls it in immediately; injured - hires
  the Wrecker militia, or posts a standing bounty redeemable in coin against any bond-paper
  naming the responsible party.
- Identity: a black-waxed seal stamped with a single tipped scale-pan; every enforcer
  carries the same scale-pan brand for marking a defaulter's gear.
- Goals: buy out the last two independent fisher-holds' bond-paper before next season's
  catch is weighed; find out who has been forging tally-marks on the west-bank scales.

The Fensal - AD: 1d6
- Resources: a scattering of salt-stores kept for ritual use rather than trade, a handful of
  members who still know the proper rites, and unspoken welcome at any hold that keeps its
  broken iron key over the door. Does not know, or does not act as if it knows, that the
  Tallyhouse holds bond-paper on more than half its own tithers.
- Knowledge: knows which of the recently disappeared were properly salted before the tide
  took them, and which were not - and therefore which will not stay under.
- Tactics: acts quietly and directly - salts a body before anyone else reaches it, or unsalts
  one deliberately when something needs to be made to answer for itself.
- Reactions: noticed - a member simply isn't there next time you look; crossed - stops
  salting your dead specifically, and lets it be known why; injured - unsalts something that
  was better left held.
- Identity: a fold of grey, undyed cloth worn at the wrist, and a single word spoken over
  the newly dead that no one outside the order writes down.
- Goals: find out what has been taking the recently disappeared before they can be salted at
  all; recruit a few more hands before the Tallyhouse's enforcers start checking funerals
  the way they check the scales.

The Wrack-Buyers - AD: 3d6
- Resources: real coin from outside the delta, a river-barge for hauling bulk salvage, and a
  standing offer no one local can match.
- Knowledge: knows what Old Rede salvage actually sells for outside the delta, and which
  pieces are worth more intact and unsold to particular buyers than broken up for scrap.
- Tactics: pays fast, pays first, asks where a find came from later - and remembers exactly
  who sold them what.
- Reactions: noticed - offers better terms to whoever's watching; crossed - simply stops
  buying from the offender, delta-wide, without explanation; injured - the barge and its
  coin leave for the season and do not return until someone fills the gap.
- Identity: a river-barge painted a color no local dye produces, and buyers who pay in a
  foreign coin stamped with a device nobody delta-side recognizes.
- Goals: fill the barge before the autumn tides close the channel; learn exactly what lies
  under Durgath before the Tallyhouse, or anyone else, gets there first.
