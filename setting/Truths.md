Truths of Sundfall

- **Salt packed fully around a wound, a corpse, or a find holds off whatever was going to
  happen to it - decay, bleeding-out, rot - entirely, and it resumes the instant the packing
  is broken or drawn back out.**
  Handle: break or draw the salt-packing from a specific packed thing to force whatever it
  held off to resume at once - [pending 4d]

- **A salt-bond is transferable proof of debt, not of the debtor's word, so whoever holds the
  paper owns the labor no matter who struck the original bargain.**
  Handle: buy, steal, or forge a specific panner's bond-paper to own their labor outright -
  [pending 4d]

- **The delta's drowned dead do not stay under: a body given to the flood without salt on it
  rises with the next low tide remembering just enough of its own life to be dangerous.**
  Handle: salt-pack a corpse before the tide takes it, or salt-pack one that has already
  risen, to still it - [pending 4d]

- **The buoys marking safe tide-ground are re-set by whichever panner-crew last dragged the
  channel, not by any authority, so ground that was safe last season is only a rumour of one
  now.**
  Handle: drag and re-sight a stretch of channel to move its buoy-line, redefining what
  counts as safe ground for whoever still trusts the old one - [pending 4d]

- **Every fisher-hold nails an iron key over its door, blade snapped off, and no one now
  living can say against what - only that a hold without one is the one the tide visits
  first.**
  Handle: examine or acquire a hold whose broken-key has been pried off or stolen, and learn
  what the ward was actually keeping out - [pending 4d]
