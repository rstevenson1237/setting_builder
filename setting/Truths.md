Truths of Greywatch

- **The dead - Whatever the shrine has bound doesn't rise on its own account; it stays at
  the one task it was set and does nothing else, not even to save itself.**
  Costs: whoever meets one and expects a fight to the death - a bound thing breaks off the
  moment its task ends or its post is passed, mid-fight or not.
  Learned: only by acting and seeing what happens - nothing at Greywatch can tell a party
  this outright, since nobody living was there when the binding was set.
  Handle: watch where a guardian stops chasing, and cross exactly there - [pending 4d]
  Shows: at Greywatch, a Law priest is kept on retainer just for putting down things that
  don't stay where they're bound; in the wilderness, stock graze right up to the edge of a
  battlefield the dead there never cross; in the ravine, a block's own dead hold one post
  and no further, which a party can use once they've seen it happen once.

- **Object - Blades forged from the ravine's cut stone never dull, but ring when swung in
  cold air loud enough to carry.**
  Costs: whoever carries one into the ravine's cold tunnels - the ring gives away a
  position no whisper would.
  Learned: told - Greywatch's own smith says as much to anyone who brings the raw stone in.
  Handle: leave the ravine-steel blade behind, or trade the noise for the edge on purpose -
  [pending 4d]
  Shows: at Greywatch, the smith buys the raw stone but won't forge it himself and sends
  it back out to whoever will; in the wilderness, a lone squatter past the road's edge is
  the one who forges it, trading finished blades for what the wild gives up; in the ravine,
  a block armed with it gives its own patrol route away by sound alone.

- **Custom - Every raiding band cuts an ear from its own dead before a body is found, and
  nobody living remembers why.**
  Costs: whoever tries to read a raid from its bodies - the mark says which tribe struck,
  but a wrongly-cut ear or a second tribe's dead mixed in reads the wrong story.
  Learned: told - it's common enough knowledge at Greywatch that nobody bothers hiding it.
  Handle: check the ears on a body before reporting whose raid it was - [pending 4d]
  Shows: at Greywatch, the garrison catalogs cut ears against which tribe last raided,
  and gets it wrong more than they'd admit; in the wilderness, travelers who find a body
  check the ears before they check the purse; in the ravine, a block keeps the ears it's
  taken from outsiders somewhere, a trophy rather than a warning.

- **Limit - Nothing spoken in Common or in Law's own rites unmakes a working laid in
  Grozzgur; only another working spoken in Grozzgur does that.**
  Costs: Greywatch's own priest of Law, whose rites stop at any ward the tribes or the
  shrine have laid, and anyone who counted on him to lift one.
  Learned: only by acting and seeing what happens - a party has to try a counter-rite
  against a Grozzgur ward and watch it fail before anyone believes it will.
  Handle: take or coerce a source that speaks Grozzgur rather than paying Greywatch's own
  priest - [pending 4d]
  Shows: at Greywatch, the priest of Law admits his own rites don't touch a Grozzgur curse,
  so the keep pays out for whoever can speak it instead; in the wilderness, a hex laid on a
  stretch of ground stays put no matter what Law tries against it, and travelers simply
  route around it; in the ravine, a block's wards are Grozzgur-laid, so only a captured or
  allied speaker of it can lift one.

- **Open - Waystone Cairn (B.6) is left unstocked on purpose: what it is for, and what its
  mortared cavity holds, are the referee's to decide.**
  Costs: whoever expects the setting to have an answer - there is none written down.
  Learned: by going there; nobody at Greywatch agrees on what the cairn is for.
  Handle: open the mortared cavity at the cairn's base with the strongbox key from the
  Quartermaster's Store (A.26), and find whatever the referee has put there - B.6
  Shows: at Greywatch, a key that fits no lock in the keep; in the wilderness, a cairn
  added to by every traveller who passes safely.
