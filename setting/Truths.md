Truths of The Buried City

| Truth | Handle | Codes |
|---|---|---|
| Every way down the pyramid is opened by something done in the room above: which way a party takes depends on what it solves, breaks or leaves alone. | A party can solve a room's puzzle, break its mechanism, or leave it alone, and each leaves a different way down. | C.3, C.4, D.6, E.2, G.6, G.19 |
| The three branches of the cynidiceans share one aim, to break the demon's hold and restore their people, and each holds that it alone has the strength and foresight to do it; none will work with the others, and each offers membership to a party that serves it. | A party can serve one branch for its standing, carry one branch's message to another, or refuse them all. | E.3, E.4, E.5 |
| The lake city's people have no shared leadership and are no faction: they chose the fungus, the masks and the dream, and will not be rescued. | A party can trade with the lake city, join its revels, or try to take someone out of it. | B.2, B.4, B.6 |
| The demon below is the cause of the corruption and a thing still being fed: it is the end of the descent, not a mistake that can be undone. | A party can follow what feeds it, cut off what feeds it, or go down to meet it. | H.7, L.3, L.4 |
