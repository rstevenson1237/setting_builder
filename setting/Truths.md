Truths of The Isle of the Serpent Crown

| Truth | Handle | Codes |
|---|---|---|
| The northern and southern villages speak one tongue and keep one story of the rising, and each blames the other for what was lost after it; each trades with strangers, and neither carries a stranger's word to the other. | A party trades at one shore's villages and then the other's, or tries to carry word from one shore to the other. | none |
| The lizard men's war is for an egg-stone of their god that the southern villages keep in the chiefs' house: returned, it ends the raids, and the southern chiefs will not give it up. | A party takes the egg-stone from the chiefs' house, or carries it to a lizard men's enclave. | none |
| The mer people keep what the reef takes and trade only for what the sea cannot give them, iron and fire-worked things, and they want the pirates' anchorage gone from their water. | A party offers iron or worked goods for a wreck's treasure, or brings word that the anchorage is cleared. | none |
| The eastern jungle is forbidden ground to both peoples: no guide crosses into it, a villager who does is cast out, and a party known to have gone there loses its standing in every village that learns of it. | A party asks a guide to cross into the eastern jungle, or is seen coming back from it. | none |
| The snake people's god is a living thing asleep under the pyramid, and each sacrifice brings it nearer waking; it is the end of the pyramid, not a thing a party can bargain with. | A party feeds what lies under the pyramid, cuts off what feeds it, or goes down to meet it. | none |
