Truths of Brackvaen

- **Anything left in the pans long enough keeps, and does not entirely finish what it was
  doing** - flesh, timber, rope, ink, an animal, a man.
  Handle: establish what a cured thing was doing and you have the way past it, because it
  is still doing that and nothing else - C.10 Wosaora, and any Cured Man on the first two
  courses. Everything Brackvaen sinks, it weights first.

- **Debt is reckoned in salt-weight on a cut board and attaches to goods rather than
  persons, so it passes to whoever takes up a dead man's kit.**
  Handle: strip a corpse and you have bought the weight recorded against the boots, payable
  when the party next uses the scales - A.1 Thrapstad, and the whole of the quest at
  B.5 Naugfast. The fourth tier of the rack at A.9 Vethgaard is debts taken up from the
  dead rather than issued to the living.

- **A thing cut in Old Rede on stone is obeyed - a boundary, a prohibition, a name - by
  people who cannot read it and by some things that are not people.**
  Handle: deface one and whatever it was holding is no longer held, immediately and
  locally, per `setting/Procedures.md` - B.4 Nuthora, C.7 Nuthorath. The reverse also
  works: set the fitting from B.4 into the twelfth socket at C.7 and every Oathkeeper in
  Brackvaen stands down at once, which is the enforcement behind half of Torsgaard's
  arithmetic.

- **Standing water below the waterline gives off a sour heavy air - the gorm - that puts
  out a lamp before it puts down a man, worsens through the day, and clears in wind.**
  Handle: the Gorm track in `setting/Procedures.md`, six steps, one per action below the
  waterline and one more for a general search. Carry a bird. C.17 Kireath is where a party
  working by torch finds out; B.6 Kkithik is where it collects in open daylight, which no
  Marchman will credit.

- **Anyone the salt has taken into their skin is Brined: they cannot inherit, cannot be
  sworn against, and are not turned out.**
  Handle: a debt cannot attach to a Brined person, which is the arithmetic that undoes the
  creditor at A.9 Vethgaard the moment a party does it. Forty of them work the stair and
  are the only reliable way anything comes up out of it - A.10 Ondtstad, C.1 Immorael.
