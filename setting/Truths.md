Truths of Thornmarch

| Truth | Handle | Codes |
|---|---|---|
| The tribes distrust one another more than they fear the keep. | A party can pay one tribe to move against another, carry a tribe's insult to its rival, or leave them to fight. | none |
| The makers' passages join caves each tribe believes are separate, and each tribe knows only its own end. | A party that finds a passage can cross between caves unseen, or bring one tribe's enemy through it. | none |
| Every tribe pays the shrine a tithe of what it raids, and a tithe unpaid is collected by the bugbears. | A party can take a tithe on the ravine path, or follow one to the shrine. | none |
| Chaos is inside the walls: the shrine has agents at the keep, and the keep's own people do not know it. | A party can find the agents' meeting place, tell the commander, or use the agents' routes. | A.1, A.4, A.12 |
