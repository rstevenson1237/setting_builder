Truths of Telar

- **An Essath name, once spoken aloud to bind what it names, is spent - it can never bind
  that thing again, by anyone.**
  Handle: a party who recovers an Essath name - from an inscription, a captive, an old
  record - can force one true answer or one bound act from whatever it names, but a second
  attempt on the same name gets nothing from anyone who tries it after. Names are readable at
  D.9 Inscribed Gallery and D.13 Chamber of the Coiled Serpent, and D.12 Sealed Archive
  records which ones the priest-kings had already spent.

- **Every act of sorcery is paid in years taken off the caster's own life, and the toll
  always shows on the body.**
  Handle: a party who examines a known sorcerer's remains, or watches one work, can read
  years-per-casting straight off a stated physical toll at the location - a hand gone
  translucent, a patch of hair gone white to the root, a wound years old that has never
  once closed. D.9 Inscribed Gallery's unworn panel is where a party can watch the toll taken
  from one of their own, and D.13's Vaalsenn Tablet is where it is charged deliberately.

- **The Man-Apes will not strike first at anyone wearing worked iron above the elbow,
  honouring a bargain neither side now remembers the terms of.**
  Handle: a party wearing, or convincingly faking, an iron armband can cross Man-Ape ground
  unmolested at a stated location until the band is removed, lost, or seen through as a
  sham - after which the truce is only ever owed to that person once. The ground is B.5
  Man-Ape Grounds, and the sign that it begins is at B.2 Boundary Cairns.

- **An Essath waystone's binding breaks the moment the stone is defaced, not merely when it
  is disobeyed.**
  Handle: a party who deliberately damages a named waystone releases whatever crossing,
  ward, or boundary that stone was holding - permanently, and everyone within sight of it
  will know exactly why. The named stones are the three at B.2 Boundary Cairns, and D.17
  Sealed Door is what the same binding-line holds when it is a door instead of a crossing.
