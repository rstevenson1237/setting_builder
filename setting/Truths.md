Truths of Kordath

- **Black lotus binds to whoever first cuts it; a second hand's cut turns the flower to ash before it clears the stem.**
  Handle: send someone other than the first cutter to harvest an already-claimed patch and watch it ash in their grip - [pending 4d]

- **The sea keeps its dead as they went under here; a body pulled from Kordath's water a year on looks a day dead, whatever the tide has done to the wreck around it.**
  Handle: read a drowned corpse's wear against its gear and clothing to learn how long it has really been down - [pending 4d]

- **Horse clans call anyone who has crossed the Ash Road on foot, without clan escort, an ash-walker, and no clan will share salt or shelter with one again.**
  Handle: cross the burned stretch without a clan rider alongside, then try to trade or lodge at any horse-clan camp - [pending 4d]

- **Every autumn storm redraws the safe channel into a Kordath harbor, and only whoever currently holds that harbor's point has this season's line through the rocks.**
  Handle: sail into a harbor that changed hands this season without hiring its new holder's pilot, and run the rocks that used to be clear - [pending 4d]

- **Sorcery worked on this coast draws its price from the sea rather than the caster, emptying a boat, a net, or a catch belonging to whoever nearby depends on the water.**
  Handle: trace a stretch of ruined catches or wrecked nets back to a working cast nearby, and find out who paid for it - [pending 4d]
