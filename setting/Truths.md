Truths of Carrdun

- **Anything sealed inside Nemhet stone does not decay - grain, flesh, or a body alike -
  but nothing kept there breeds, hatches, or heals as it should.**
  Handle: pen livestock, plant seed, or nurse a wound inside a room walled in old tomb-stone
  and watch what comes out changed - [pending 4d]

- **Stone quarried from a Nemhet tomb keeps whatever was sealed with the dead beside it;
  defacing the old-tongue carving on a re-used block breaks that hold.**
  Handle: chip or deface a wall block bearing old-tongue carving and whatever it was
  warding is no longer held - [pending 4d]

- **Hearing a Xoss working through to its end fixes three words of it in the listener
  whether they wanted them or not, and speaking those words aloud again is noticed by
  whatever the working was for.**
  Handle: repeat a remembered Xoss phrase aloud and something nearby takes notice -
  [pending 4d]

- **The crown's law runs exactly as far as Carrdun's walls and the old causeway beyond
  them, and not one stone further.**
  Handle: pick a fight past the causeway-line and watch that nobody from the garrison
  comes - [pending 4d]

- **Every excavation contract the commander sells is paid in salvage split three ways -
  crown, commander, digger - and a digger caught hiding a find outright forfeits the split
  and the commander's protection both.**
  Handle: report a find honestly for a third share, or hide it and lose the commander's
  cover the next time it matters - [pending 4d]
