# Setting Judgement Check - build 1, Roadsend, 2026-10-15

Recorded against the content as generated, before anything is fixed. Reviewed by the context that wrote it, so the finding that a fresh reader would catch and an author would not is the one most likely missing.

## Setting-level
- The brief was delivered: Confirmed, with one Needs Attention. Counts all measure: 3 tongues, 3 up-front Bestiary entries, 2 factions with 2 marks each, 6 rumours (2 T, 2 P, 1 F, 1 U) with one pointing at the cave and one true and sounding false (2), 1 quest given at the keep, A with 4 people, B with 3 landmarks and 1 route, C with 6 rooms in a 1/3/2 mix, one entrance, one cave mouth. Needs Attention: the outline says one frontier crossed on foot in half a day, but B's Approach and `setting/Procedures.md` Time put it at 12 miles, two of a day's three travel watches. Source: BRIEF (outline wording) against Procedures (generator).
- Genre held across levels: Confirmed for Reference, Authority, Allegiance, Population, Decay, Player characters and Naming (plain names; one coinage, the tribe's). Needs Attention on Lethality: the genre makes poison save-or-die and a first-level character fall to one or two blows, but the hazards are two 1d falls and no creature poisons; Procedures defines Lethal poison and nothing uses it. Source: BRIEF (thin test setting) and generator. Magic level held: one potion, one scroll, a +1 sword, +1 arrows, a +1 ring and a lamp.
- The tests, hardest at the top: Confirmed. The seeds and briefs each carry usable names, counts and prices; no seed line tells the party what to conclude. The Brief tables' d6 events are invented colour, but each can be put to a party.
- Discrete and discoverable, not vague: Confirmed. Every room holds named, findable things (the Wagon Board, the Loss Tally, the tally post, the central spiral, the crusted midden).
- Every secret has its answer: Needs Attention. Wolf tracks stop at the Broken Arch (B.4) and Rumour 6 is marked U with "no entry says why", but `setting/Truths.md` holds nothing, so nothing marks the gap as deliberate. Source: BRIEF (`truths count=0`) and Truths template's allowance for a standing mystery, which no step prompts. All other secrets carry their answer.
- Nothing contradicts: Needs Attention, two small ones. C.6's Chest Feature says the chest holds "the tribe's silver" where The Chief's Hoard lists one silver chalice. C.5 holds "the makers' pay" as a Magic roll, where the Hall Tally says the lord's pay was sealed in the strongroom, which reads as coin. Source: generator (5b and 5a).
- Everything recurs, nothing is orphaned: Needs Attention. Goblin, Wolf, Soldier, both factions, the Lore, the Key and the Hoard are met in rooms. Treasure Tables II, III and V are cited by no room, and only one Magic roll is cited. The Zhakuk's second mark (knucklebones at a chief's belt) appears in no room, and the Keep's own device (a white tower on blue) appears in none of A's four rooms though it is stamped on crates, a strongbox and a tabard elsewhere. Source: schema (no field places a faction's identity mark) and generator.
- Vocabulary precise, not decorative: Confirmed. Ford, pier, scree, murder-hole, tabard, midden and strongroom each name what they describe. No glosses remain after 5c.

- Locations reinforce this region: Confirmed. The Stream Path has no entry linking it to the three landmarks, so their order along it is carried only by Approach and Terrain prose. Source: Wild_Schema (no position on a Route).

- Locations reinforce this region: Confirmed. Alarm timings in the Brief come from Referee Notes lines, not from an entry; Needs Attention at the schema level. Source: Dangerous_Schema (nothing records how far a noise carries).

## Room to Grow
- Lethal poison - Procedures defines it; no hazard or creature uses it. A later pass could give the Midden or a goblin weapon a Poison hazard.
- Wolf tracks stopping at the arch - an open question with no entry in `setting/Truths.md`; a later revision could list it there as a standing mystery or give it an answer.
- Treasure II, III and V, and five of six Magic rolls - cited nowhere; A.3's stock is the natural place for Table II, a second reward in C.5 or C.3 for III and V.
- The Zhakuk's knucklebones and the Keep's white tower on blue - the marks each appears with elsewhere but not here: A.1, A.4 and C.6 are where they would pay.
- The cave-makers' tongue - no name coined from its roots; the Broken Arch, the Carved Hall or the makers themselves could take one, recorded in `setting/Language.md`.
- The Zhakuk is the only coined name; it is used in Factions and in three Encounters.
