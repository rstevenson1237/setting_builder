Duen *the fallen ford, the Dutor cut, an unanswered writ*

*Duen holds the only crossing of the Durad gorge for two days either way, on a charter from
a throne no letter has answered in five years - and since the low ford collapsed into the
gorge last spring, every cart, herd, and column crosses by the reopened Dutor quarry road
instead, whatever the garrison would rather. Stone cut from that quarry is said to remember
what's sworn on it, and nobody who has tested this walks away without a mark to show for
it. The party are Duen's garrison and the hangers-on camped against its walls - not
reinforcements, not heroes, just what's left to make the crossing hold.*
