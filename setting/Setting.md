Roadsend
Where the king's road ends and the goblins begin

*A party out of the keep at Roadsend crosses a ravine to a single cave and brings back what it can carry. A goblin tribe in that cave is raiding the road behind the keep, and nobody at the keep knows how many goblins there are. The cave is older than the tribe: its workings were cut by makers whose dead tongue is still carved into the walls.*
