Fellward *press-ganged watch, requisition ledger three seasons overdue, endless treeline*

*Fellward is a border keep held past the point it should have been abandoned, garrisoned by
whoever the last wagon left behind rather than by anyone sent to do it on purpose. Its
charter says the crown backs it; its granary and duty roster say otherwise, and the keep
runs on whichever of the two a given day needs more. Nothing beyond the treeline has a name
or a court to answer to, so every watch is a guess about what's coming, not a defense
against something known.*
