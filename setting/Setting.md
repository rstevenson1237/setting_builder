Brackvaen *salt, oath, subsidence*

*A hundred and twenty years ago the Marchfolk built Torsgaard on the only rise that stays dry and began cutting salt from the pans a drowned realm left behind; the pans are worth working, the fen between them is not a road, and three years ago the eastern water dropped and uncovered a stair going down into the Odarath's under-work. Salt here does not let go of what is put in it - meat, timber, oath-boards, or people - which is why debt is weighed in salt, why the boundary stones cut in Old Rede are still obeyed by men who cannot read them, and why the ones who come back up from the stair come back cured rather than dead. The party are here because the pans pay in coin, the stair pays better, and nobody who can afford to refuse the work is offering.*
