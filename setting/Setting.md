Thornmarch
Form: common
Where the realm's road ends and the ravine begins

*A party outfits at the keep at the road's end, crosses wood and mere to a ravine lined with the lairs of six rival tribes, and carries out what it can. Chaos is gathering there: tribes that fought one another for years now pay a shrine at the ravine's head, and the road is where the keep first feels it, with two of the shrine's agents already inside the walls. The tribes hate one another, and the makers who cut the ravine's halls left passages that join caves each tribe thinks are separate.*
