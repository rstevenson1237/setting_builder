Roadend
Every wagon that leaves the keep pays the cave first.

*Newcomers with empty purses reach Roadend Keep, the last garrisoned stone before the wilderness, and go out through the ravine behind it for the gold the cave holds. A goblin tribe in that cave has been raiding the road, and nobody at the keep knows how many there are. The cave was cut long before the goblins came, and its walls still carry the carvings of the people who cut it.*
