Sundfall *salt, debt, the breach*

*Two centuries ago the Old Rede diked this delta for grain; forty years ago the wall broke,
and the tide has sorted through the old grainland and the ruins beneath it twice a day
since. No one governs the delta outright - panners' debts are bonded on ledgers that trade
like coin, and thinning catches are pushing bonded crews further into ground the tide hasn't
finished with. Salt cured here does not merely preserve: packed against a wound, a corpse,
or a find it holds decay off entirely until drawn back out, and the party works the
tide-line same as any panner, except unbonded, and past the buoys.*
