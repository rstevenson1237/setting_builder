Telar

Tags: see setting/Tags.md

*Telar is the old road binding three kingdoms that broke apart a generation ago, and
whoever holds a crossing taxes whoever needs to pass - the party is here on hire, guarding a
crossing, a caravan, or taking a job nobody respectable would touch. A border-reaver band
seized the river ford at the Sunk Bridge three months back and holds it still; traffic now
runs a week's extra travel around them, and no garrison has moved to take it back. Every
waystone on the true road is cut in Essath, the tongue of whoever built it, and every
crossing still keeps to what its stone says regardless of who is collecting toll that week -
the one law older than any of the three kingdoms, and the only one every reaver, garrison
and merchant company still honours.*
