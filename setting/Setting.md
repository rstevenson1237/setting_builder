Brackvaen *salt, oath, subsidence*

*Eighty square miles of brackish fen over a drowned realm's salt pans, worked out of one rise at the eastern edge and answerable to no authority but a written settlement about who owns which pan. Everything here is weighed - salt, debt, and, since a cut stair opened under the eastern pans three winters ago, whatever comes back up it. The party are here because the pans pay in coin, the stair pays better, and nobody who can afford to refuse the work is offering.*
