Carrdun *Salvaged, Border, Waking*

*Carrdun is a border garrison raised from the salvaged stone of Nemhet, the drowned
river-kingdom whose flood-plain it now holds - and every season the receding river opens
more of the tomb-city it was quarried from. Its granary is a hollowed step-pyramid where
grain never rots, and nobody who works there will say why; its commander sells excavation
rights into the exposed ruins faster than anyone can account for what is coming back up.
The party has taken the wall's coin because no respectable company would.*
