The Ashwrack Coast *drowned ruins, salt-witches, iron hunger*

*A thousand years ago the empire of Vael sank beneath a black tide, leaving its broken towers as reefs and islands along a storm-lashed coastline. Scattered fishing holds and pirate camps cling to the shore, trading rumor and salvage while avoiding the drowned crypts that surface at low tide. Something in the deep water is still awake, and it remembers the old empire's name.*
