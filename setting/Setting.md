The Buried City
Form: common
A city under the dunes, and under it a pyramid that goes down to a demon

*A party caught in a sandstorm shelters in a ruin that the sand has buried, and finds no way back to the world: the only way is down, through the pyramid at the city's heart. Water and food run short from the first night, and three branches of the pyramid's own people, each claiming to speak for the whole, will bargain with a party they cannot do without and cannot trust. Beneath them a demon is still being fed, the pyramid grows wider for five levels and narrower for five more, and halfway down lies a lake city whose people have chosen a dream over their rescue.*
