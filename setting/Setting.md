The Isle of the Serpent Crown
Form: common
A jungle island where a sleeping god is fed in a stepped pyramid, and the ship home will not wait

*A party landed at the southern villages with a partial map and a ship that sails on a day set at landing crosses swamp, hill jungle and forbidden ground to a stepped pyramid, past cave men, dinosaurs, lizard men, mer people and pirates, to take the serpent crown from the god's sanctum and carry it back to the ship. Beneath it the swamp war is bleeding the southern villages of captives, who are sold on to the snake people to feed their god, and bound men already sit in the chiefs' councils on both shores. The island's two peoples will trade with a stranger and will not trust a stranger with each other, and each night something huge comes to the southern wall's gate and is fed.*
