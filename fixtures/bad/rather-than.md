X.2 **The Cistern Head** - *stone, standing water*
A barrel-vaulted room over black water, its **wellhead** capped with a pegged timber **lid**. A **windlass** stands over it, rope down the shaft and crank gone.
*Twenty feet square, vault ten at the crown, the water two feet below the lip. Built to draw for the yard above and capped when the yard burned.*
**Timber Lid:** Three elm planks pegged to a cross frame, sized to the shaft rather than to the wellhead. Levered at the south edge -> it slides free and drops into the water.
**Wellhead:** A drum of dressed stone a foot proud of the floor, its coping cracked across. The crack runs the full width rather than stopping at the joint.
**Windlass:** Bolted to the east wall, drum sound, crank socket empty. A spear shaft fits the socket, and two turns bring the bucket up.
**Exits:** plank door - oak, swollen, dragging on its foot, north wall -> X.1 The Burnt Yard, low arch - brick, sooted, knee height, west wall -> a drain out to the river
