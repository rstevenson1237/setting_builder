X.4 **The Forced Hatch** - *timber, iron*
A trap **hatch** in the boarded floor stands off two of its hinges, and a bent **pry bar** lies beside it.
*Ten feet by twelve, boarded floor, the hatch at the north end over a ladder shaft. Cut for the works below and boarded again when the lease changed hands.*
**Floor Hatch:** Oak leaves in an iron frame, the upper hinge torn out of the timber with its screws. The tear runs with the grain, which is why the leaf still sits in the frame.
**Pry Bar:** Iron, two feet, bent a hand's width out of true at the claw. The bend matches the hinge plate, and anyone can tell the bar did the work.
**Ladder Shaft:** Six feet square below the hatch, its top three rungs gone. A lantern lowered on a cord shows water twelve feet down.
**Exits:** trap hatch - oak, two leaves, off its upper hinges, north end -> X.5 The Corbel Room, plank door - elm, braced, south wall -> the stair to the yard
