X.3 **The Tally Room** - *timber, ledgers*
A clerk's room of empty shelving and one standing **desk**, with a split **tally stick** on its slope and the window **shutter** barred from inside.
*Fifteen feet square, plastered, the floor sanded and swept. The works' count was kept here until the crew walked off; the barred shutter is the only thing that has been touched since.*
**Standing Desk:** Oak, chest high, its slope scarred with knife cuts for ruling lines. Nothing has been written at it in years, and the inkwell has dried to a crust.
**Split Tally:** A hazel stick cut through its notches and laid in two halves. Its matching half is nowhere in the works, and the notches run to sixty-one.
**Barred Shutter:** Two leaves and a drop bar, barred from the inside of the room. Lifted -> the bar clears its brackets and the leaves swing out over the drop.
**Exits:** doorway - no door, frame charred at the head, east wall -> X.2 The Cistern Head, shuttered window - two leaves, barred inside, south wall -> a ledge above the yard
