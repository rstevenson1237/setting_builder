X.5 **The Corbel Room** - *stone, dressed*
A square chamber whose **ceiling** narrows overhead in stepped courses, with a barred **door** at the far end and a stone **bench** along one wall.
*Eighteen feet square, the ceiling twenty at the crown. Cut as a strongroom for the works above and emptied when the lease ran out.*
**Corbelled Ceiling:** The ceiling steps inward in courses of dressed stone. Each course oversails the one below by a hand, and the topmost pair are one slab.
**Drop Bar:** A bar that drops into two iron brackets on the door's inner face. Lifted from the bench -> it clears the brackets and the leaves swing in.
**Stone Bench:** Cut from the wall in one piece and worn hollow at its middle. The hollow holds an inch of water for a day after rain.
**Exits:** barred door - oak, two leaves, drop bar on the inner face, north wall -> X.4 The Forced Hatch, hatch - boarded, iron ring, floor's centre -> X.9 The Shaft Head
