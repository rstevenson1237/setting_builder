## REFEREE FRONT MATTER

- **Light:** none anywhere. Torches gutter in room 7. Track fuel.
- **Wandering:** 1 in 6 per 2 turns. 1-3 Rats (2d6), 4-5 Cinder Monks (1d3), 6 The Auditor.
- **Reaction:** roll 2d6 normally. The Cinder Monks *can* be talked to. Nothing else can.
- **Listening:** doors are oak, swollen. Listen on 1-2 in 6.
- **Secret doors:** 1-2 in 6 searching, 1 turn. Rooms 3/4 and 11/12.
- **Standing fact:** the chapterhouse floods from the west. Room 7 is the low point.

**TAG KEY** — `[dark]` `[wet]` `[unstable]` `[loud]` `[watched]` `[valuable]` `[trapped]` `[locked]` `[negotiable]` `[timed]`

## THE ROOMS

### 1. THE PILLARED LANDING — 20x50
`[dark] [watched]`

- Stairs down from the north. Two pillars, one west, one east.
- Each pillar is carved with a tally of notches. West: 41. East: 41. Fresh chalk adds one more to the east.
- Someone is keeping count. Someone is still here.
- Openings: west->2. Doors: south->corridor, east->9.

**Scenario — the count.** Whoever marks the east pillar adds a notch each time a living
thing passes into the chapterhouse. The party is 42. If they exit and re-enter, 43.
The Auditor (room 8) knows the number and will say it aloud.

### 2. THE WRECK — 30x30
`[unstable] [loud]`

- Ruined furniture, chest-high, piled against the north opening.
- Climbing it is noisy: automatic wandering check, and 1 in 6 a collapse (1d4, save vs. Death for half).
- Pulling it apart quietly takes 3 turns and two people.
- Searching (1 turn): a brass censer `[valuable]` (15 gp), a hand still wearing a ring of office.
- Openings: north->5, south->3, east->1.

### 3. THE HUNT TAPESTRY — 30x30
`[dark]`

- Tapestry over the south wall: a boar hunt, rotten, tears if pulled (it will).
- Behind the tapestry, low on the wall, forty-one names are scratched into the plaster.
- Debris. Searching turns up nothing. Say so.
- Secret door: west->4. Opening: north->2. Door: east->corridor.

### 4. THE DRY GALLERY — 30x70
`[valuable] [trapped]`

- The only dry room. Tapestry over the east wall — the same boar hunt, reverse side, and a
  figure stands among the trees who is not in the room-3 version.
- Three iron-bound chests. Two hold the chapter silver: 1,400 sp, 60 lbs, awkward.
- **The third chest is the trap.** Lid is unlocked and unhinged; lifting it releases the
  counterweight that holds the gallery's ceiling beam. Save vs. Death or 2d6 falling
  masonry; the room becomes `[unstable]` and the secret door is buried on 1-3 in 6.
- Spiking the lid shut first (1 iron spike) disarms it. So does removing the silver
  chests' weight from the floor plates *before* touching the third.

### 5. THE COUNCIL TAPESTRY — 30x30
`[watched]`

- Tapestry over the north wall: the chapter in session, twelve seated figures.
- The central figure's face has been cut out. The cut edge is clean and **warm**.
- If the party has already been to room 8, there are now two faces missing.
- Opening: south->2. Door: west->6.

### 6. THE SWOLLEN DOORS — 30x40
`[loud]`

- Empty. Wet footprints lead west, barefoot, more than one set.
- The double door west is swollen: two characters, 1 round, and the noise carries to
  rooms 7 and 8. Automatic wandering check.
- Prying it with a crowbar is quiet but takes 3 rounds.
- Doors: double west->7, door east->5.

### 7. THE COLONNADE — 50x50
`[wet] [dark] [timed]`

- Partially flooded: 1 ft standing water, black, opaque. Movement halved. No surprise.
- Two rows of columns, north half and south half. Every column is scored with claw marks
  at chest height.
- Under the water: the chapter's dead, about twenty, and 300 cp scattered.
- **Torches gutter here.** Each round in the room, 1 in 6 a torch goes out.
- Splashing draws the Drowned. They rise on the second round of any loud crossing.
- Doors: double east->6, door north->8.

> **Drowned Brothers (1d4+2):** AC 8, HD 2, hp 9 each, MV 60'(20'), #AT 1 grapple, D
> special, Sv F2, ML 12, AL C, XP 20. A hit means grappled; grappled characters are
> dragged under next round (drowning rules). Cannot leave the water. **Wading the
> colonnade edge, against the wall, is only waist-deep and they cannot reach.**

### 8. THE THRONE AND THE LEDGER — 30x30
`[negotiable] [watched]`

- Raised dais, stone throne. On it sits the Auditor, robed, holding an open ledger.
- It does not attack first. It asks the party how many of them there are.
- **It wants the number to come out even.** Forty-one names went in. Forty-one must
  come out. It will trade: the party may take anything they can carry, and leave, if
  they leave one of their number behind — or one body, living or dead, of equal count.
- It accepts a corpse. It accepts the hand from room 2. It does not accept a rat.
- It will not follow beyond room 7.
- Door: south->7.

> **The Auditor:** AC 3, HD 5, hp 24, MV 90'(30'), #AT 1 touch, D 1d8 + 1 point
> permanent CON, Sv F5, ML 11, AL L, XP 400. Turned as a Wraith. **Lawful** — it keeps
> every bargain exactly as stated, and no further.

### 9. THE SPEAR RACKS — 50x30
`[dark]`

- Empty weapon racks, all forty-one sockets.
- Three spear butts remain, snapped off at the same height, as if all pulled at once.
- Doors: west->1, east->corridor, north->10.

### 10. THE CLEAN WELL — 40x30
`[wet] [valuable]`

- Tapestry over the north wall: a heron, unfaded, undamaged. It is the only clean thing here.
- A well or fountain in the center. The water is **clear** — the only clear water below ground.
- Dropped objects make no splash. A lowered rope comes back up 30 ft shorter each time.
- **Drinking it:** save vs. Spells. Success — heal 1d6, once per character, ever.
  Failure — the drinker's name is now in the room-3 plaster, and the Auditor counts them
  as chapter, not as visitor.
- Doors: south->9, east->corridor.

### 11. THE SCRATCHED ROOM — 30x30
`[dark]`

- Bare. Cold enough to see breath.
- The north wall's seam is scored with fingernail scratches — from *this* side.
- Secret door: north->12. Opening: east->corridor.

### 12. THE SALT CIRCLE — 40x50
`[locked] [trapped] [valuable]`

- Sealed since the flood. Air is foul: 1 point CON per turn until aired (open the door
  and wait 3 turns).
- Bones stacked against every wall, stripped and sorted by type. Careful work.
- Floor center: an unbroken circle of salt, 10 ft across. Inside it, a closed ledger —
  the **first** ledger, the one with the true forty-one names.
- **Breaking the circle** frees the Cinder Monks (1d3+2 appear at once, and all wandering
  rolls are now Cinder Monks).
- **Reaching in without breaking it** is possible with a 10 ft pole and patience, or by
  a character of CHA 13+ who states aloud what they intend to do with the book.
- The true ledger, shown to the Auditor, ends it: its count was wrong by one from the
  start, and it knows.

> **Cinder Monks (1d3+2):** AC 6, HD 1+1, hp 6 each, MV 120'(40'), #AT 1 grasp, D 1d4 +
> 1d4 heat, Sv F1, ML 8, AL C, XP 21. `[negotiable]` — reaction roll applies. They want
> out, they know the way, and they will guide the party for the price of the censer
> from room 2, which was theirs.

## WHAT THE PARTY CAN CARRY OUT

- Chapter silver, 1,400 sp, 60 lbs (room 4). Brass censer, 15 gp (room 2). 300 cp under
  the water (room 7). The ring of office, 80 gp, marks the wearer as chapter (rooms 2/10).
- The true ledger (room 12) is worth nothing to a fence and everything to the Auditor.
