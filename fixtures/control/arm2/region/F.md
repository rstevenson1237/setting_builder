F Sarnar

Overview: Sarnar is the crown seat one warlord dynasty ruled the whole road from, standing
empty on its hill above the old approach road since the succession broke, and a Nine Wagons
stone-agent is up there now pricing its dressed courses rather than its contents. All three
kingdoms keep watchers on the surrounding ground and count who goes up the hill and who
comes down, so whatever is carried out of Sarnar is carried out in front of people who
answer to somebody. What is true here and nowhere else in Telar: the seat's lower courses
were quarried out of Daghash and carted a week west, and the Essath bindings cut into that
stone came with it.

Ambiance: Cold at a fixed depth all year, and dry at head height while the
floor stays damp in every room below the landing. The hill's spring runs audibly under the
whole floor, loudest toward the western rooms and inaudible in the south. Wet lime, cold
stone dust, and old lamp smoke; nothing here is rotting.

Architecture: Two masonries in every wall - Telar ashlar of local grey sandstone above, and
beneath it two to four courses of Essath qorth, black, harder, and cut for a building a
week's cart east. The motif that ties the seat together as one work is what the masons did
with that stone: they laid it for fit rather than for sense, so the binding-lines cut into
it run upside down, reversed, or a word short in room after room, and no two rooms carry
the same fragment. Ceilings run twelve feet in the court rooms and nine feet elsewhere;
passages run five feet wide.

Layout: A collection - twelve rooms in a single quarter, the court itself, cut into the
hill's north face. One entrance, a stair down from the hillside into the muster landing.
Two arms run off that landing: west through the robing rooms to the flooded audience hall
and the seat beyond it, and a corridor south serving the register, the taper store, the
arms-check and the washing court. No room sits more than eighty feet from the landing. The
Wagons' survey party is two rooms in, on the western arm; the water is at the western arm's
far end; the closed court is behind the taper store at the south. Time here runs on the
Danger countdown below, not the clock.

Features: The spring under the floor keeps every room below the landing damp at boot level,
and the western rooms wet. Sound carries along the corridor far better than through the
doors, so a room's noise reaches the corridor's other mouths before it reaches the room
next door. The hangings are damp through - a torch held to one smoulders and smokes rather
than catching, and the smoke stays at the ceiling and does not clear.

Dangers: The seat itself sleeps. The hauled courses do nothing at all until one is levered
out, read aloud, or broken, and every binding fragment in the place is already spoiled but
one. What does not sleep is who is here and who is outside: the Wagons party reacts to
anyone on the stair below them, and the three kingdoms' watchers on the hillside report
every party that goes up, by horn, the same day.

Creatures: A Bound Dead stands in the gift-store behind the register's west seam, bound
against anything lifted from its shelves and indifferent to anything else (setting/Bestiary.md).
The Unlettered Dead lie in the fill under the audience hall's floor, which was carted from
Daghash with the stone, and they rise out of the silt where the water is walked
(setting/Bestiary.md). A sheet of Grave-Mould holds the closed court, which has been shut
since the seat was built over it. No other population: the spring water
carries no fish, and the hill's vermin keep to the landing and the stair.

Factions: The Nine Wagons holds the robing room as a survey position - one stone-agent and
two hired masons, a blue-grey handcart with a brass bell tied to its brake lever standing
at the stair head. What the position is for is a price: what the seat's dressed courses are
worth lifted and carted, and whether the black stone runs under the whole hill. Neither
Brekar nor the Serpent Cult has any presence here, and neither has had reason to come this
far west of the ford.

Secrets: What Sarnar's lower courses are, and where they were cut, is legible to anyone who
reads Essath, and nobody who built the seat could. The closed court behind the taper store
was not on the seat's plan - it was already in the hill, and the seat was walled onto it.
The bronze socket in its floor takes the petitioner's token fitted into the gift-store's
vault keystone.

Treasure: setting/Treasure2.md (equipment and armaments) is what the claimants' men left
behind them in the arms-check and dropped crossing the flooded hall.
setting/Treasure4.md (luxury and trade goods) is the tribute still on the gift-store's
shelves, heavy and mostly not worth the carry. setting/Treasure5.md (treasure cache) is
what was paid into the closed court over a century and entered on no register.
