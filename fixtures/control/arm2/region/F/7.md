F.7 **The Flooded Audience Hall** (high) - *Drowned Temple, Colonnade*
**Standing water** covers the floor a foot deep, and two rows of **blind colonnades** run the hall's length, one row in each half. A **choked drain** stands silted in the north-west corner.
*Fifty feet square, ceiling twelve feet in one unbroken span, the hall the seat gave audience in. The spring is loudest here and comes up through the floor.*
**Standing Water:** A foot deep across the floor, deepest at the centre, crossed at speed or under load -> footing goes in the silt (Test of Fate, Slowed).
**Blind Colonnades:** Two rows of columns, one in each half, carrying nothing under a single-span ceiling.
**Unlettered Dead:** Lying in the fill under the floor, carted here with the stone, rising out of the silt, where the water is walked (Aimless, 2 dozen, Bestiary : The Unlettered Dead).
**Choked Drain:** A cut mouth a foot across, in the north-west corner, packed solid with silt, dug clear over a turn -> the water falls to ankle depth.
**Dropped Armament:** Under the silt at the south colonnade's foot, found by dragging a boot along it (Treasure II, d20).
**Exits:** double door - oak, bronze-shod, swollen at the foot, wanting two people to shift the leaves, east side -> F.6 The Presence Antechamber, plank door - oak, iron-strapped, standing open on a silt-locked leaf, north side -> F.8 The Sworn Seat
