F.12 **The Closed Court** (high) - *Elder Sorcery, Closed Court*
A **whole Essath chamber**, black stone floor to ceiling, with no sandstone in it anywhere. A **grave-mould sheet** covers the north-east corner and the floor under it, and a **bronze socket** is set into the middle of the floor.
*Forty feet by fifty, ceiling eleven feet, corbelled, the room judgments were given in that nobody outside was to hear. Colder than the rest of the seat by several degrees, dry, and the mould is still living, which dates the seal to the seat's own building.*
**Whole Essath Chamber:** Cut stone throughout, no hauled course and no ashlar, the one room the seat was built onto.
**Grave-Mould Sheet:** Grown over the north-east corner, and a body-length of floor, soft and cold, handled or crossed -> it takes to leather, rope and cloth (Ancient, 1, Bestiary : Grave-Mould).
**Bronze Socket:** Set flush in the centre of the floor, cut to a disc profile, the gift-store's token fitted in -> a recess opens in the north wall (Keys: The Closed Court Token).
**Forced Socket:** A bar put to the bronze instead -> the bronze shears, and the recess never opens after.
**Wall Recess:** Opened a course deep behind the north wall, holding what the closed court wrote down (Lore: The Closed Court's Record).
**Swept Corner:** Under the mould sheet, in the north-east corner, swept into the corner (Treasure V, d20).
**Exits:** hidden passage - a back slab behind the taper store's blank bay, south wall -> F.11 The Taper Store
