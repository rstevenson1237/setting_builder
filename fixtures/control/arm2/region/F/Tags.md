Tags of F Sarnar

- **Hauled Course** - a course of masonry cut for somewhere else and laid here by men who
  could not read it.
- **Reversed Script** - an Essath binding-line laid face-out, upside down, or a word short,
  in wall after wall.
- **Crown Seat** - the hall one warlord dynasty ruled the whole road from, for a little
  over a century.
- **Three Watchers** - each of the three kingdoms keeps eyes on the hill and none of them
  holds it.
- **Empty Racks** - weapon fittings stripped out and carried off, the sockets left in the
  wall.
- **Petitioner's Token** - a numbered disc handed out at the door and handed back at the
  presence.
- **Closed Court** - a judgment given where nobody outside the room was to hear it.
- **Spring Under Stone** - the hill's own water, which the seat was sited on and then had
  to manage.
- **Silted Drain** - a channel cut to carry water out of a room, no longer doing it.
- **Colonnade** - parallel rows of columns that divide a hall and carry no roof.
- **Standard Socket** - an iron-lined hole in a pillar base where a pole was stepped.
- **Stone Price** - dressed courses worth more sold off the hill than standing on it.
- **Chisel Test** - a fresh square cut into old stone to see how deep the good stone runs.
- **Bound Stone** - Essath masonry that came here with its binding still cut into it.
- **Court Register** - the written record of what the seat decided, and who it decided
  against.
- **Sworn Hall** - Sarnar's own name, from sarn: the place oaths were given, and orders
  only after.
- **Dais** - a raised step that says where a room's attention was meant to go.
- **Lustration Basin** - water kept for washing before the presence, never for drinking.
- **Tapestry Wall** - heavy hangings kept on the walls, most of them still up and damp
  through.
- **Robing Wreck** - the smashed furniture of the room petitioners left their cloaks in.
- **Arms-Check** - where a visitor's weapons were taken and counted before they went in.
- **Taper Shelf** - a wall-run of stone brackets where the hall's lamps were kept and lit.
- **Grave-Mould** - a grey-green growth that takes to worked Essath stone alone.
- **Succession Break** - twenty-eight years ago: three heirs, and no named one.
- **Ziggurat Spoil** - stone quarried out of Daghash and carted a week west by ox.
