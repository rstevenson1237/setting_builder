F.1 **The Muster Landing** (medium) - *Old Road, Standard Socket*
A long landing at the foot of the stair, a squared **pillar** standing in each half, the west one cut about at its base and wedged. A **black course** runs along the west wall at ankle height, cut with script upside down.
*Twenty feet by fifty, ceiling twelve feet, at the foot of the stair down from the hillside. Fresh stone dust lies on old floor grit under the west pillar.*
**Undercut Pillar:** Square test holes cut around its base, an oak wedge still seated in one, spoil heaped at its foot, weight put on the shaft -> a course sheds off it (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**Black Course:** Laid face-in along the west wall, three Essath words legible upside down, at ankle height.
**Standard Sockets:** Iron-lined holes cut into both pillar bases, each facing the east door, both empty.
**Exits:** open archway - sandstone, jambs rubbed smooth at shoulder height, west side -> F.2 The Robing Wreck, plank door - oak, iron-strapped, hinges seized half open, south side, the corridor's west end -> F.3 The Register Room, plank door - oak, one leaf off its pintles, east side -> F.9 The Arms-Check, stair - dressed sandstone, treads worn hollow, north end -> the hillside above the old approach road (B.6 Old Approach Road)
