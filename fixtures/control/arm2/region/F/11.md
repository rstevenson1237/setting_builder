F.11 **The Taper Store** (low) - *Old Road, Taper Shelf*
A bare store room, a **bracket run** of stone along the north wall with soot on the wall above each socket. The run stops one **blank bay** short of the corner, and the soot above that bay is the same.
*Thirty feet square, ceiling nine feet, where the hall's lamps were kept, trimmed and lit. The spring cannot be heard from this room at all.*
**Blank Bay:** The shelf run stopping short of the corner, its soot unbroken above the gap, the flanking brackets drawn down together -> the bay's back slab swings inward.
**Bracket Run:** Stone brackets at chest height, along the north wall, each worn into a shallow dish.
**Exits:** open doorway - sandstone, jambs sooted black to the lintel, east side, west along the corridor -> F.3 The Register Room, open doorway - the same opening, east along the corridor -> F.9 The Arms-Check, hidden passage - a back slab behind the blank bay, swung inward once its flanking brackets are drawn down, north wall -> F.12 The Closed Court
