F.6 **The Presence Antechamber** (low) - *Old Road, Reversed Script*
A small empty room between two doors, a **summoner's bell** hung on an iron bracket in the north wall. **Silt at the sill** is banked against the far face of the double doors west, ridged in low furrows.
*Thirty feet by forty, ceiling nine feet, the last room before the hall and the room the seat's usher stood in. Cold and wet at the floor, and the spring is loud enough here to talk over.*
**Summoner's Bell:** A hand-bell on an iron bracket, the course behind it cut reversed, lifted clear of the bracket -> a niche opens behind it (Lore: The Usher's Tally).
**Silt at the Sill:** Banked a hand deep, against the west doors' far face, ridged in low furrows, pushed from the hall side.
**Exits:** plank door - oak, panelled, the panels sprung out of their grooves, east side -> F.5 The Waiting Chamber, double door - oak, bronze-shod, swollen at the foot and silted against, wanting two people to shift the leaves, west side -> F.7 The Flooded Audience Hall
