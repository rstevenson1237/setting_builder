F.4 **The Gift-Store** (high) - *Tribute, Hauled Course*
A long barrel **numbered vault** of black stone, every stone in it cut with a number in Essath and the numbers out of order. **Tribute shelves** run both long walls and still carry crates and bundles, and a dried **bound dead** stands among them facing the way in.
*Thirty feet by seventy, the vault springing at seven feet and closing at eleven, the store tribute presented at court was held in. Dry where every other room below the landing is damp, and it smells of cedar and old sacking.*
**Numbered Vault:** Essath stone throughout, reassembled course by course, its setting-numbers cut and running out of sequence.
**Bound Dead:** Standing between the shelves and the way in, unmoving, acting only where a shelf is lifted from (Ancient, 1, Bestiary : Bound Dead).
**Tribute Shelves:** Crates, bolts and bundles along both long walls, sound where the sacking has gone, taken off the shelf -> the Bound Dead acts (Treasure IV, d20).
**Keystone Plug:** A disc of pale stone set flush, in the vault's keystone, filling a socket cut for a numbered stone, prised out by hand (Keys: The Closed Court Token).
**Exits:** hidden pivot slab - dressed sandstone on a socket, flush and unmarked from this side, at the end of the short corridor, east wall -> F.3 The Register Room
