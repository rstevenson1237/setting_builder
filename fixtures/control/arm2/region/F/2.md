F.2 **The Robing Wreck** (medium) - *Caravans, Chisel Test*
Smashed cloak-presses and benches fill the room waist-high, and three people in working clothes have cleared a space at the west wall where a **prised facing slab** has come off. A **blue-grey handcart** stands against the debris with a small brass bell tied to its brake lever.
*Thirty feet square, ceiling nine feet, the room petitioners left their cloaks and arms in before going west. Chisel ring carries from here up the stair and down the corridor both.*
**Ranik and Two Masons:** A stone-agent directing two hired men, at the west wall, none of them armoured, and she talks first (Named Creature: Ranik).
**Blue-Grey Handcart:** Painted the one colour, a brass bell tied at the brake lever, loaded with rule, plumb and dressed samples.
**Prised Facing Slab:** Levered off the west wall and propped, the black course behind it bared, its script running reversed.
**Standing Order:** Folded in the agent's satchel and readable, price the courses and the depth, take nothing, be off by the fourteenth.
**If This Position Is Lost:** Word is due at Tolkar's toll-house, on the fourteenth, unsent -> the Wagons sell the hill where it lies (setting/Factions.md).
**Younger Mason:** Works facing the west opening, not the wall, and will not go past it, having been west once.
**Spilled Tokens:** Numbered discs trodden into the wreck, under the benches, a handful still legible (Treasure I, d20).
**Exits:** open archway - sandstone, jambs rubbed smooth at shoulder height, east side -> F.1 The Muster Landing, open archway - sandstone, lintel cracked and shimmed, north side -> F.5 The Waiting Chamber, open archway - sandstone, threshold worn into a trough, south side -> F.3 The Register Room
