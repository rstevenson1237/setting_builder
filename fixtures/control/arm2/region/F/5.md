F.5 **The Waiting Chamber** (medium) - *Decadent Court, Tapestry Wall*
A bare room with benches along two walls and a **woven hall** hung over the north wall, showing this seat's own audience chamber. An **usher's bell-cord** runs out from under the hanging's rail and into the wall.
*Thirty feet square, ceiling nine feet, where petitioners waited to be called west. Anything that rings in here is heard through the wall in the antechamber and past it.*
**Usher's Bell-Cord:** A waxed cord from the tapestry rail, into the north wall, the hanging brushed or pulled -> a bell sounds two rooms off (Test of Fate, Impact).
**Woven Hall:** The hanging shows this chamber in thread, its columns doubled in two rows, the raised seat at the far end empty.
**Exits:** open archway - sandstone, lintel cracked and shimmed, south side -> F.2 The Robing Wreck, plank door - oak, panelled, the panels sprung out of their grooves, west side -> F.6 The Presence Antechamber
