F.3 **The Register Room** (medium) - *Decadent Court, Court Register*
The floor has **dropped** a foot in the south-west quarter and all the room's debris has slid into it. A **south tapestry** covers the far wall, and a **swept arc** of bare floor stands against the west wall where nothing has been swept.
*Thirty feet square, ceiling nine feet, the room the court's judgments were written and filed in. An under-vault runs beneath the south-west quarter, and that quarter sits a foot low.*
**Dropped Floor:** Flagstones tilted into the south-west quarter, the debris slid against them, weight put on the tilt -> the floor gives into the vault (Test of Constitution, 1d, Crushing).
**South Tapestry:** Heavy cloth on a rail, against the south wall, damp through, drawn aside -> a black course laid face-out, its line a word short.
**Swept Arc:** A quarter-circle of bare floor, at the west wall, scored by a slab's edge, the seam prised at its top corner -> the slab pivots inward.
**Threshold Dust Beyond:** Past the pivoted slab, dust lies unbroken on the sill, in every direction but one.
**Register Leaf:** A written sheet wedged flat, under a fallen slab, at the vault's lip (Lore: The Third Winter's Register).
**Exits:** open archway - sandstone, threshold worn into a trough, north side -> F.2 The Robing Wreck, hidden pivot slab - dressed sandstone on a socket, flush and unmarked until the seam behind the swept arc is prised, west wall -> F.4 The Gift-Store, plank door - oak, plank-and-batten, sprung and binding at the foot, east side, north along the corridor -> F.1 The Muster Landing, plank door - the same opening, south along the corridor -> F.11 The Taper Store
