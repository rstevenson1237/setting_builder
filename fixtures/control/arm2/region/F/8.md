F.8 **The Sworn Seat** (high) - *Decadent Court, Crown Seat*
A **dais slab** of one black stone fills the room's north end, and a stone seat stands on it with one **scoured arm**. A channel is cut around the dais's edge and runs to a socket at its north foot, and the **complete line** of script on its south edge is unbroken.
*Thirty feet square, ceiling twelve feet, the room the road was ruled from for a little over a century. The dais slab is the largest hauled course in the seat and is laid whole, its south line unbroken.*
**Dais Slab:** One Essath threshold-stone laid flat, a channel cut round its four edges, running to a socket at its north foot, its south edge cut with a whole binding-line.
**Complete Line:** The one unspoiled binding in Sarnar, read aloud from the dais's south edge, by someone standing on it -> the line is spent, and the seat settles an inch.
**Spoiling It:** An Essath word said wrong on the slab -> what the line holds answers the attempt, not the reader (Test of Sanity, 1d).
**Scoured Arm:** Rubbed pale on the seat's right, down to clean stone, rough and unworn on the left.
**Void Under the Seat:** Opened at the channel's socket, where the seat settles, reached by hand from the dais (Treasure III, d20).
**Last Sworn Roll:** Hide, tied and dropped behind the dais, at its north foot, out of the channel's reach (Lore: The Last Sworn Roll).
**Whose Seat It Was:** What lies under the seat, names which of the three took it last, wanted at Tolkar and at Velar both (Quest: Who Last Sat).
**Exits:** plank door - oak, iron-strapped, standing open on a silt-locked leaf, south side -> F.7 The Flooded Audience Hall
