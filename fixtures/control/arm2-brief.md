# Arm 2 brief

Sarnar, the old crown seat on the hill above the old approach road - the Left mark
`setting/History.md` names for the unified crown and the one no location has ever carried,
empty since the succession broke twenty-eight years ago and watched from a distance by all
three kingdoms since.

Twelve rooms, one quarter, DANGEROUS: its lower courses are ziggurat stone hauled west out
of Daghash, and what was cut into that stone came with it.
