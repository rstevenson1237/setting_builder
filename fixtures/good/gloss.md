X.8 **The Weighing Floor** - *iron, dust*
A boarded floor with a **beam scale** hung from the tie above it and a **tally board** pegged to the post beside it.
*Thirty feet by twenty, boarded over the shaft head below, dust to the ankle in the corners. Ore was weighed here before it went down to the road.*
**Beam Scale:** The arm hangs on a stirrup from the tie, its pans off and stacked beneath. A set of iron weights sits in a rack on the post, one of them short.
**Tally Board:** The marks run in fives, cut deep and then scored through in threes. The last column is cut bright and stops halfway across.
**Dust Corner:** A drift of waste banked against the north wall, ankle deep and dry. A boot through it turns up a bronze token, squared at one end.
