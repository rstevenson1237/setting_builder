X.6 **The Drift Mouth** - *timber, spoil*
A timbered **adit** opens in the hillside behind a spoil bank, with a plate **rail** running out of it to a tipping stage.
*Eight feet by six at the mouth, running back level into the hill. Worked for ore and left standing when the seam turned to water.*
**Adit Timbering:** Sets of props and caps at four-foot spacing, sound for the first thirty feet. Nothing bars the way in, and none of the sets has taken a fall.
**Plate Rail:** Cast plates on oak sleepers, running from the face out to the stage over the bank. The same gauge is laid elsewhere on this hillside, and a tub still stands on it.
**Tipping Stage:** Trestles and planks over the spoil, the far end broken away. A tub tipped here empties down the bank and never comes back up.
**Exits:** adit mouth - timbered, eight feet by six, hillside -> X.7 The Wet Face, cart track - rutted, running down the spoil -> the valley road
