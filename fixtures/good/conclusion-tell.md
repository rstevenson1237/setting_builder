X.7 **The Wet Face** - *water, ore*
The drift ends at a working **face** standing in a foot of water, with a **pump rod** down the sump beside it and a loaded **tub** on the rail.
*The drift's last thirty feet, the floor falling away to a sump at the face. The seam was cut into water and the crew drew off what they could carry.*
**Working Face:** Ore cut back in benches to head height, the lowest bench under water. Two shot holes stand drilled and unfired at the left edge. Do not prompt them about the holes.
**Pump Rod:** Ash, four inches square, dropping into the sump on a pivot at the wall. Worked by hand -> the water falls a foot for as long as two people keep at it. Let them argue about who works it.
**Standing Tub:** Iron, four-wheeled, loaded and chocked on the rail. Its chock is a wedge of ash, and pulling it starts the tub down the grade. Roll this where they can see it.
**Exits:** drift - timbered, water ankle deep, running out -> X.6 The Drift Mouth
