# Drakenhold, the original user submission

The brief the predecessor project `rstevenson1237/drakenhold` was built from, committed
here verbatim as the campaign-scale worked example of what a user hands the generator
(see `INTROSPECTIVE.md` P0.2 and P5.1). It is a control input, not setting content:
nothing in it enters `setting/` unless a build under a brief of its own draws it.

---

## Region Overview
The Breakstone mountains run Northwards and slightly West for hundreds of miles with open grasslands on their east and the Ironwood on their West. At the northern edge of the Ironwoods, where they transition into broken hills and scrub, the mountain range shoots directly Eastward for three single peaks, forming what is now known as Drakenhold before continuing Northwards again into the unpassable Broken Teeth mountains. As such, Drakenhold was the northernmost passable route from the West, through the trail navigating the Ironwoods to Thornhaven where the river would take goods to civilization South or roads would take goods to the quiet farming communities of the West.

## The Truths
Runic Script, communicates basic features from the original inhabitants, used for puzzles and traps, used by the Runemasters for magical wards and incantations, clues hidden throughout to assist with translation.
Complex carved stone tiles that when fitted to appropriate receptacles grant access to various areas of Drakenhold.
Metallic rods control access to secured portals within Drakenhold instead of simple keys. These are precious metal rods carved with magical runes and one or more may be required to open portals into vaults, secured areas or private residences. Some rods may grant access to many areas while the highest level of security may require multiple very specific rods to access.
The Dwarves were inventors of complex machinery and earth powered magics. They created complex traps, ritual sites and marvelous methods of moving people and things throughout the hold.
Political Power in Drakenhold, 7 seats on the council were filled with 2 from the Trade Guilds, 2 from the Runemasters, the Steward of Drakenhold, the Leader of the Guard and the King.
Names hold power, speaking the names from the Dwarven past allows the opening of secret doors, activating of magical effects and brings the ability to speak with encountered wraiths of long dead Dwarves.
The Dragon's name is Vermakith. The final King is a polarizing figure and his name has been stricken from many areas while others hold him still in high regard as trying to protect the Dwarven peoples. The Dwarven Knight does not have his name written officially but can be found in graffiti and personal writings.
In addition to the Runemasters, the Guildmasters and the Royal Family, there was an underclass of Dwarf that acted as servants, staff and slaves. A few poor souls have survived and eke out a meager existence in the servants' passages deep within Drakenhold.
In the burial tomb of the Knight of Drakenhold, there is a magical lance that may bring ruin upon the Dragon.

## History of Drakenhold
1273 Years Ago - Drakenhold is founded as an outpost of a now forgotten Dwarven lineage. Over the next 300 years the three large halls are carved into each of the peaks, expanding down into the earth until ancient caverns are uncovered.
973 Years Ago - Minor Wyrms, crawling in the inky blackness of the below caverns are discovered, living off the heats of volcanic vents deep within the earth. The Wyrms are hateful and strike back against Dwarvish efforts to plunge the depths for riches and much bloodshed ensues.
960 Years Ago - The clans are united under their first King, who organizes a final push against the Wyrms that pushes them back into darkness permanently and leaves the Dwarves as sole rulers of Drakenhold.
900 Years Ago - A period of relative peace sees the establishment of the Guild Hall and the formation of its founding traditions, laying the groundwork for the council that would one day govern Drakenhold.
752 Years Ago - Nearby veins of precious metals are discovered, bringing the Dwarves into prosperity and expansion as each of the peaks is expanded upwards into the three upper levels. The Skybridge is constructed during this era, connecting the peaks and cementing Drakenhold as a unified hold rather than three separate outposts.
311 Years Ago - Increasing attacks from Dragons leads to open conflict, ancient magic is used to repel and eventually defeat the Dragons which leads to the establishment of the Runemasters who are given the Third Peak to reside within as a reward for their contributions, during this conflict the watchtowers are constructed and the Skybridge is fortified.
40 Years Ago - An ancient artifact was discovered, granting access to a demonic entity with connections to the earth. This artifact is used to expand the mining operations of the Dwarves of Drakenhold and leads to even greater prosperity for the Dwarven Kingdom.
35 Years Ago - Increasing intrusions of evil beings coming from the caverns beneath Drakenhold, the King grows fearful of the evil power contained within the artifact and bans its use despite outcries from the Runemasters.
33 Years Ago - The artifact is stolen, a schism is formed between the King and the Runemasters.
32 Years Ago - The Breaking, the King is assassinated leading to open civil war, forces loyal to the royal family make a last stand in the Armory.
31 Years Ago - A young Dwarven Knight casts off his sword and leaves his wealth behind, preaching of peace and humility and brotherhood to the broken Dwarven clans, igniting an urge to come together and begin rebuilding. The scattered clans begin returning to Drakenhold, drawn by his words, the hold stirring with fragile hope for the first time since the Breaking.
30 Years Ago - A mighty Red Dragon descended upon the Dwarven mountain home of Drakenhold. A massive habitation built into three mountain peaks running against the Ironwood forest. The reuniting clans are scattered once more, the young Knight of Drakenhold's fate unknown.
10 Years Ago - The Dragon's sightings away from its conquered home have stopped completely and Drakenhold lies silent.

## Factions
The Dragon - AD: 6d6, Vermakith, a great red dragon from the north
Kobolds - AD: 2d6, Both drawn to and fearful of the Dragon and the Lizardmen
Lizardmen - AD: 3d6, Direct servants to Vermakith. They exist to do his bidding and strike fear in others in his name, especially with intruders and the kobolds
Dwarven Survivors - AD: 1d6, small groups of surviving underclass Dwarves, hiding in fear in the servants quarters of Drakenhold
Goblins - AD: 1d6, decrepit bandits trying to make an existence in their small camps throughout the Ironwood
Hobgoblins - AD: 2d6, raiders and scavengers around Drakenhold, they will occasionally send exploratory parties into the ruined city and Drakenhold itself as well as occasionally terrorizing Thornhaven

## Bestiary
Wolf (Beasts) - AD: 2d6 Description: Lean, pack-hunting predators that test a target's footing and numbers before committing, peeling off the moment the odds turn against them.
Automaton (Constructs) - AD: 3d6+2 MA: 1 Description: A dwarven war-machine, mostly engineering with a thread of rune-work woven through its mechanism, still executing whatever drill or order it was last given centuries ago. Mindless and literal, it can not be reasoned with, only redirected by satisfying the letter of its last command.
Living Statue (Constructs) - AD: 3d6+1 Description: A stone guardian animated purely by rune magic rather than any mechanism, holding a single post or threshold with total indifference to anything beyond the bounds of its watch. Slow and nearly silent, unsettling mainly for how long it stays motionless before moving at all.
Skeletal Warrior (Undead) - AD: 2d6+1 Description: The drilled remains of a fallen soldier, still moving through old formation and weapon drills with no fear, fatigue, or judgment left to interrupt them. Holds a post or patrol route with total indifference to the cost of doing so.
Shadow (Undead) - AD: 2d6-1 Description: A sliver of negative life-force clinging to total darkness, cold and nearly silent until it's already touching you. Flinches from real light rather than press a losing fight, and drains warmth from anything it gets a grip on.
Dwarven Wraith (Undead) - AD: 4d6+2 Description: The vengeful spirit of a dwarven defender who died holding this place, bound here by oath, grudge, or both. Recognizes every intruder as the next in a long line of looters but may still parley in old Dwarven for someone who shows the hold real respect.
Ancient Wyrm (Wyrms) - AD: 5d6+2 Description: Distant, diminished kin of the same Wyrm bloodline that produced Vermakith. They are far lesser in power and horrible to look upon. Functions more as an environmental hazard than a singular threat in the lower megadungeon levels; multiple individuals may lurk at once, each rolled fresh.
Ooze (Fantasy Creatures) - AD: 2d6 Description: A mindless, corrosive mass that's pooled and fed in the hold's flooded lower halls for decades, swollen on collapsed dwarven stores and whatever's drowned alongside them. Flows toward movement, warmth, or noise with no intelligence behind it, and eats through cheap gear left sitting in it. Nothing to bargain with, only avoid or destroy.
The Dragon Vermakith (Wyrms) - AD: 15d6+5 MA: 4 Description: A great red dragon from the north whose descent on Drakenhold thirty years ago shattered the reunited dwarven clans outright. Less a combatant to be fought than a cataclysm to be survived.
Kobold (Humanoids) - AD: 1d6-2 Description: Small, cowardly reptilian opportunists who fight only in numbers and flee the instant those numbers thin. Favor traps, tunnels, and noise over any direct confrontation.
Kobold Warrior (Humanoids) - AD: 1d6 Description: A kobold given crude armor, a spear, and just enough drilling to hold a line but will quickly fall back to trickery and striking from shadows as soon as possible.
Kobold Chieftain (Humanoids) - AD: 3d6+1 Description: Rules the warren through fear and a stolen trophy weapon, always flanked by its most loyal warriors. Quick to trade information for its life if ever caught alone.
Goblin (Humanoid) - AD: 1d6-1 Description: Small, wiry scavengers with slimy green hide, breeding fast and roaming in ragged tribal packs. Skittish around real threats but turn vicious the moment they sense an advantage. They seek after easy loot above all, and desperate to stay off the radar of bigger things in the dark.
Goblin Chieftain (Humanoids) - AD: 3d6+1 Description: Clawed its way to the top of the tribe through cruelty and cunning, hoarding the tribe's best scavenged loot personally. Sacrifices underlings without hesitation to save its own skin.
Owlbear (Beasts) - AD: 5d6+3 MA: 2 Description: A hulking fusion of owl and bear, silent until the moment it isn't. Hunts by sound in total dark and territorial well past the point of reason, tearing into anything that lingers in its hunting grounds.
Dwarven Survivor (Humanoids) - AD: 2d6 Description: One of the last dwarves of the original hold, alive only because it's spent years hiding in passages even the Automatons don't patrol. Avoidant and suspicious by default, they will avoid combat unless cornered or provoked.
Giant Spider (Beasts) - AD: 2d6 Description: Ambush predators that lurk in webbed thickets just off the trail, dropping from cover onto anything that lingers too long. Their bite carries a paralytic venom, and a patient eye can spot the husks of prior prey wrapped and stored nearby.
Wood Sprite (Fey) - AD: 1d6-2 Description: Palm-sized, light-bending fae bound to the Ironwood and unable to stray beyond its edge. More trickster than threat. They will scatter trail markers, mimic voices, and lead travelers in circles, preferring riddles and petty bargains to any kind of fight.
