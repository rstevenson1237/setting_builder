# Genre

The answers to `patterns/Genre.md`'s GENRE block, one per question, in its order.

- **Reference** - 1981 Basic/Expert Dungeons & Dragons, and its introductory module *Keep
  on the Borderlands*: one fortified outpost of the civilized realm at the edge of the
  wilderness, set against the forces of Chaos gathering in the caves and ruins beyond it.
  Play goes out from a safe base into wilderness and dungeon, and comes back with what it
  can carry.
- **Lethality** - High at the start and easing with experience: a first-level character
  falls to one or two blows, poison is save or die, and a party that does not know when to
  retreat does not come back. Death is permanent at any level a party begins at.
- **Magic level** - Present but scarce at the level play begins. A few characters cast,
  each holding a spell or two a day that is gone once spoken until they rest. Magic items
  are found, never bought; most finds are a potion, a scroll or a weapon a little better
  than it should be, and anything greater is rare and remembered.
- **Authority** - Distant: the realm lies behind the characters, and its writ holds
  inside the keep's walls and along the road back to it and nowhere past them. The keep's
  commander governs what can be seen from its towers.
- **Allegiance** - Law, Neutrality and Chaos. Law is the keep and the realm behind it;
  Chaos is the humanoid tribes, the evil priests, and what they serve; Neutrality is most
  beasts and anyone who looks after themselves. Each alignment is also a spoken tongue, so
  a stranger's side is read from the words they know, the company they keep, and what they
  do on meeting - never from a label.
- **Population density** - Scarce past the border: one fortified settlement at the
  frontier, a road back to the realm, and beyond it wilderness holding hermits, raiders,
  lairs and ruins, with the dungeon a day's walk or less from the gate.
- **Decay** - The realm behind is whole, and the keep is new, garrisoned and kept in
  repair. What is past its peak lies out in the borderlands: works, tombs and holds left by
  whoever held this land before, now occupied by Chaos.
- **Player characters** - Adventurers of the seven classes - fighter, cleric, magic-user,
  thief, dwarf, elf, halfling - newly arrived at the keep with little money, come to make a
  fortune in the caves. Nobody sent them; the keep tolerates them, trades with them and
  buys what they bring back, and will not march out to rescue them.
- **Reward** - Treasure, and carrying it home: gold brought back to safety is what makes a
  character more than they were, so every find is weighed against what getting it out
  costs. Magic items are the rarer prize.
- **Naming** - Plain and descriptive, in the common tongue, for nearly everything a party
  meets: the keep, the caves, the raiders' camp. Coined names, drawn from the tongues
  seeded in `setting/Language.md`, belong to the old - ruins, tombs, what the builders
  called their works - and to the non-human, what a tribe calls itself.

`STYLE.md` carries the three tests every line at every level must pass; it outranks this
file and every template.
