# Tags - Fallout

Proof-of-concept target for `PLANS.md` Plan 1 - not wired into generation yet.
Retro-futuristic post-nuclear collapse: pre-war ruins and technology scavenged by
settlements squabbling over what's left, radiation and mutation as ambient facts of life,
dark comedy sitting right next to real desperation.

Same shape as `tags_fantasy.md` and `tags_scifi.md` - Thematic and Hazard/Room-Type
facets per rating, plus People and Creatures - deliberately kept structurally identical
so the comparison is about vocabulary, not format.

## SAFE

### Thematic
- **Vault Dwellers** - people who rode out the end underground, and it shows
- **Water Rights** - who controls the purification, and what they charge for it
- **Scrip Economy** - a settlement's own currency, worthless three towns over
- **Old-World Relic Trade** - pre-war tech bought and sold like scripture
- **Settlement Militia** - armed amateurs standing in for a government that isn't coming
- **Rad-Free Zone** - a settlement clean enough to raise children in, and jealous of it
- **Trade Caravan Route** - the road that keeps a settlement alive, and its biggest liability
- **Company Town** - a settlement still run like the corporation that built it never left
- **Cult of the Old World** - worship built around pre-war artifacts nobody understands
- **Mutant Tolerance Line** - how far a settlement will go before turning away the changed
- **Scavenger Guild** - organized looters with their own rules about who claims what
- **Generator Dependency** - a settlement's whole existence riding on one aging machine

### Hazard/Room-Type
- **Purifier** - the settlement's water source, guarded like the treasury it is
- **Scrap Yard** - salvage sorted, hoarded, and fought over
- **Vault Door** - a blast door that still, mostly, works
- **Trading Post** - where caps, scrip, and rumor change hands
- **Clinic** - understocked, overworked, and never turning away a paying patient
- **Broadcast Tower** - the settlement's one link to anyone else still out there
- **Armory** - what's left of the settlement's teeth
- **Farm Dome** - growing food under glass, jealously protected
- **Holding Cell** - wherever a settlement keeps people it hasn't decided about yet
- **Old Terminal Room** - pre-war systems still half-running, half-understood
- **Checkpoint** - the settlement's actual border, manned or not
- **Bunkhouse** - shared shelter, thin walls, and everyone's business known

## WILD

### Thematic
- **Raider Territory** - ground a gang has claimed by force and holds by reputation
- **Rad Storm Corridor** - a stretch where the weather itself is the threat
- **Caravan Ambush Point** - a chokepoint every raider band already knows about
- **Feral Ghoul Nest** - the wasteland's own version of a predator's den
- **Pre-War Highway** - a road that still connects places, if you can survive it
- **Mutant Herd Range** - territory a pack of changed creatures treats as home
- **Scrap Rush** - a stretch of wasteland everyone's currently picking clean
- **No-Man's Land** - ground so contested nobody actually controls it
- **Old Border Checkpoint** - a pre-war boundary marker nobody enforces anymore
- **Trader's Truce Ground** - neutral territory kept peaceful because everyone needs it
- **Bleed Zone** - land still radioactive enough to matter, decades later
- **Wandering Cult** - a group that's made a religion out of surviving the wasteland

### Hazard/Room-Type
- **Crater** - ground zero, still wrong in ways that show
- **Overpass Camp** - a raider or refugee camp built into old infrastructure
- **Rusted Convoy** - a caravan that never made it, picked over or not
- **Sink Hole** - collapsed ground hiding whatever's underneath
- **Broadcast Ruin** - a dead transmitter, and whoever's still guarding it
- **Rad Pool** - standing water or ground hot enough to matter
- **Scrap Heap** - a landmark-sized pile of pre-war debris
- **Overgrown Lot** - nature reclaiming a pre-war structure, roots and all
- **Watchtower** - built from scrap, manned by whoever controls this stretch
- **Dead Convoy Line** - a road marked by wrecks nobody's cleared
- **Signal Relay** - a working pre-war tower, valuable to whoever holds it
- **Bone Field** - a battle or massacre site, still marked by what's left

## DANGEROUS

### Thematic
- **Pre-War Military Cache** - a stockpile nobody's supposed to still know about
- **Failed Vault Experiment** - an underground shelter whose real purpose wasn't survival
- **Rogue Protectron** - a security system still enforcing orders nobody gave it recently
- **Super Mutant Warband** - the old world's failed answer to a problem, now a new one
- **Rad-Mutated Ecosystem** - a place where the fallout changed more than the people
- **Corporate Black-Budget Site** - research the pre-war company never disclosed
- **Feral Ghoul Colony** - what's left of people who didn't get better
- **Automated Defense Grid** - old-world weapons still technically operational
- **Cult of the Bomb** - worshippers who think the apocalypse was the point
- **Sealed Research Wing** - a lab nobody finished evacuating
- **Legacy AI** - a pre-war system still executing its last understood orders
- **Buried Ordnance** - weapons the old world left behind, some of them still armed

### Hazard/Room-Type
- **Overseer's Office** - command center for whatever this shelter actually was
- **Reactor Chamber** - the power source, and the reason this place still has lights
- **Sealed Armory** - pre-war weapons, still racked, still dangerous to disturb
- **Med Wing** - where the vault tried, and maybe failed, to treat something
- **Server Room** - pre-war data, worth more to some factions than the guns
- **Abandoned Barracks** - military housing, empty in ways that raise questions
- **Containment Cell** - built to hold something the old world didn't want loose
- **Frozen Mess Hall** - a room that stopped mid-routine and never restarted
- **Command Bunker** - deep, hardened, and built for people who never made it there
- **Radiation Lab** - research into the thing that ended everything
- **Cryo Storage** - people or specimens on ice, decades past their intended wake time
- **Access Shaft** - the only way down, and the only way anything gets back up

## People (personalities)
- **Old-World Nostalgic** - romanticizes a world they never actually lived in
- **Survivalist** - has out-planned everyone else in the room, and it shows
- **Scavenger-Minded** - measures every room by what's worth stripping from it
- **Rad-Hardened** - has stopped being afraid of the thing that's killing them slowly
- **Faction-Loyal** - the settlement, gang, or cause comes before anything personal
- **Cynical** - assumes every kindness is a setup
- **Hopeful** - still building as if the world's coming back
- **Ruthless Trader** - will deal with anyone, on any terms, if the price is right
- **Haunted** - carries a specific, named loss from before or during the end
- **Territorial** - has claimed something and will defend it past reason
- **Superstitious** - treats pre-war tech as either miracle or curse, nothing between
- **Institution-Broken** - was inside a vault or bunker system, and it left a mark

## Creatures (demeanors)
- **Mutated** - changed by exposure into something the old world wouldn't recognize
- **Feral** - lost whatever higher function it had, running on instinct alone
- **Territorial** - has claimed a ruin or stretch of wasteland as exclusively its own
- **Pack-Hunting** - coordinates with others of its kind against isolated prey
- **Radiation-Fed** - draws sustenance or strength from exposure that would kill anything else
- **Ambush-Oriented** - waits in wreckage or rubble rather than hunting openly
- **Aggressive** - attacks on sight, no negotiation phase
- **Cautious** - has learned, the hard way, to test before committing
- **Swarm-Forming** - individually weak, dangerous in numbers
- **Armored** - old-world exposure or mutation left it hard to put down
- **Erratic** - behavior that doesn't track normal animal logic, unsettling as much as dangerous
- **Scavenging** - competes with humans for the same salvage, and doesn't share
