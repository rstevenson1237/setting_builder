# Spec - judgement checks

Tick a line to approve it.

- [ ] **J1. A check records findings, and nothing else.** An item with nothing to report is not written; a
  region with none is one line. The checklist stays in the template as what to read for. The setting check's 104
  lines become its 13.
- [ ] **J2. A fifth check: the walk** (`templates/checks/Walk_Judgement_Check.md`, step 5e). In a context that
  wrote none of the setting, take `BRIEF.md`'s `test` line as the route and walk it three times, each making
  different choices where the text offers one. At each location read only what a party is given - the read-aloud,
  then each Feature it would look at, then the Exits - and the map. Record, with the location's code:
  - where the text gives a party no next step, or only one where the seed promises a choice;
  - a fact a party needs and cannot find: a way in, a price, who to ask, what a key opens;
  - where the map, the text and the brief disagree about what is there or which way it lies;
  - what a referee must invent to keep the walk going.

  Its output is the three routes as code lists, then the findings, each with its source and the step that owes it.
- [ ] **J3. The Map Judgement Check reads every block's map** (with the wild-maps plan, W6).
- [ ] **J4. A finding a tool could make is sent to the tool.** Each check closes with the findings that are
  mechanical - a count, an overlap, a missing citation - named as owed to `validate_setting.py`, `metrics.py`
  or `space.py`, so the next pass does not need a reader for them.

- [ ] **J5. The checklists gain what this build's read-through needed**, as #64 added Greywatch's:
  - Map check: a block's map shows what its seed says is there (streets, a wall, the thing it is named for);
    nothing a sheet draws at a region's edge contradicts `GENRE.md`.
  - Setting check: each faction a party is meant to deal with can be dealt with through someone or something a
    table places; each way between two regions is one a party could walk, by the section.
